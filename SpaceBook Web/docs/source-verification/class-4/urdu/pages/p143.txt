Suddenly his axe slipped from his hand and fell into the river. He was very worried and sat on the bank crying, for this axe was his only means of living. Then a radiant old man rose from the river and asked why he was sad. The woodcutter told him everything. The old man dived into the water, brought out a silver axe and asked, "Is this yours?" The woodcutter said, "No, it is not mine." Then he brought a golden axe; again the woodcutter refused. At last he brought an iron axe, and the woodcutter said happily, "Yes, that is my axe!" The old man was very pleased with his honesty and gave him all three axes as a reward. Moral: honesty always bears sweet fruit.
