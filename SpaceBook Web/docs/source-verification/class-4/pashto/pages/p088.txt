He fought many wars in his time, and through his firm resolve and swordsmanship he always won victory over the enemy.
p دغه شان یوه ورځ د یو جنګ پهٔ موقع دهٔ خپل کوشش وکړو خو بیا ئې هم شکست وخوړو او د دښمن فوځ پرې غلبه بیاموندله. || اسی طرح ایک دن ایک جنگ کے موقع پر اس نے پوری کوشش کی، لیکن پھر بھی شکست کھائی اور دشمن کی فوج اس پر غالب آ گئی۔ || Likewise, one day in a battle he made every effort, yet still he was defeated and the enemy army overcame him.

Now I will tell you the whole story." When evening came, King Timur wondered why he had been defeated like this for the first time. In the morning he again fought in the field with his whole army, but unfortunately the enemy army once again won victory over him.
