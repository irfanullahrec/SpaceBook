As he passed through the bazaar, he first saw an old and feeble man. The prince asked why this man was in such a poor state. He was told that when a person grows old, this is what happens. Further on he saw a sick man in great pain from his illness. The prince asked why; he was told that when a person falls ill, this is how he becomes. Then on the road he saw a funeral. He was told that this is the final state of a person, when his connection with the world ends. On the way back he saw a dervish sitting silently. The servants told him that these are people who have given up the comforts and pleasures of the world and control their desires.
