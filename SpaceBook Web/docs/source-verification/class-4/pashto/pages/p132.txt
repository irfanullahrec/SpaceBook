He ran towards the village and began shouting that the wolf had come. When the village people heard him, they all left their work and ran to help. When they arrived, he laughed loudly that he had only been joking. The people went back very disappointed. The next day he did the same thing again, shouting that the wolf had come. The people ran to help him as before — but where was the wolf? The shepherd laughed heartily at them again. The people were very angry at this second trick and went back to their homes.
