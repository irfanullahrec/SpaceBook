q 3 | Constructed Response Questions: Look at the picture of drinking water filtration plant for the public and write the answers. || تعمیری جوابی سوالات: عوام کے لیے پینے کے پانی کے فلٹریشن پلانٹ کی تصویر دیکھیں اور جوابات لکھیں۔ || جوړښتي ځوابي پوښتنې: د خلکو لپاره د څښلو د اوبو د فلټر پلانټ انځور وګورئ او ځوابونه ولیکئ.
sol (i) A filter separates dirt and particles from water so that we get clean water. (ii) A simple filter cannot stop most germs because they are very small; chlorine is added or the water is boiled to kill them. (iii) Germs can be killed by boiling the water for 5 to 10 minutes after it starts boiling, or by adding chlorine. (iv) Water is made suitable for drinking by filtering it to remove dirt and particles and then boiling it (or adding chlorine) to kill the germs. ## Answers of the four parts of Question 3.
