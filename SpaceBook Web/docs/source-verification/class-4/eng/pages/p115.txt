sol Sample — Character: a boy, Hamza, and his dog. Setting: a village by a river, one summer evening. In the beginning: Hamza takes his dog for a walk along the river. Conflict: the dog falls into the water and cannot climb out. In the middle: Hamza shouts for help and runs to a farmer nearby. Solution: the farmer pulls the dog out with a rope, and Hamza thanks him. ## Agent sample answer. || ایجنٹ کا نمونہ جواب۔ || د اجنټ د بېلګې ځواب.
q ii | Now write a story of your own using the information above in your notebook. || اب اوپر دی گئی معلومات استعمال کرتے ہوئے اپنی ایک کہانی اپنی کاپی میں لکھیں۔ || اوس د پورته معلوماتو په کارولو خپله یوه کیسه په کتابچه کې ولیکئ.
sol A story of friendship: Once there lived two friends, Ali and Hamza. One day Ali lost his pencil box and Hamza gave him his own. Ali was thankful and promised to help him too. After some days Hamza was ill, and Ali took notes for him in class. The two friends learnt that true friends help each other. ## Sample answer: answers will vary.
## 2. Creative Writing || 2۔ تخلیقی تحریر || 2. تخلیقي لیکنه
q 2 | Write some rules of staying safe. || محفوظ رہنے کے چند اصول لکھیں۔ || د خوندي پاتې کېدو ځینې قاعدې ولیکئ.
sol Sample: 1. Never take gifts or sweets from strangers. 2. Do not go anywhere with a stranger. 3. Do not leave school without telling your teacher. 4. If someone makes you uncomfortable, walk away at once. 5. Tell your parents about your day. ## Agent sample answer based on the lesson. || سبق پر مبنی ایجنٹ کا نمونہ جواب۔ || پر لوست ولاړ د اجنټ د بېلګې ځواب.
