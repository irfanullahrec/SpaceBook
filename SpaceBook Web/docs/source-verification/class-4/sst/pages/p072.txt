p In order to get freedom from British in Subcontinent, Muslim leaders of India demanded a separate country for the Muslims. It was decided about the province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa that people of this province would decide through referendum to join Pakistan. The Movement of Khudai Khidmatgar boycotted the referendum but the people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa decided through referendum to join Pakistan. ### Afghan Refugees in the Province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa || صوبہ خیبر پختونخوا میں افغان مہاجرین || د خیبر پښتونخوا په ولایت کې افغان کډوال
p The Soviet Union (Russia) did military intervention in Afghanistan in 1979. The Soviet Union established a puppet government in Afghanistan. People of Afghanistan were not happy with the intervention of Russia. Therefore, millions of Afghan refugees migrated to the province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The province faced many problems due to arrival of Afghan refugees.
