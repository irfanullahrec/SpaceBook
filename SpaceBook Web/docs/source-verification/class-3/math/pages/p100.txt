q 6 | Colour the figures according to the given fractions. || دیے گئے کسور کے مطابق اشکال میں رنگ بھریں۔ || د ورکړل شویو کسرونو سره سم شکلونه رنګ کړئ.
qp i | $\frac{4}{8}$ + $\frac{3}{8}$ = $\frac{7}{8}$
fig frow fpie 8 0 ;; ftext + ;; fpie 8 0 ;; ftext = ;; fpie 8 0
sol Colour 4 parts, then 3 parts, then 7 parts of the last circle. ## 4/8 + 3/8 = 7/8 || 4/8 + 3/8 = 7/8 || 4/8 + 3/8 = 7/8
qp ii | $\frac{3}{10}$ + $\frac{5}{10}$ = $\frac{8}{10}$
fig frow fgrid 2 5 0 ;; ftext + ;; fgrid 2 5 0 ;; ftext = ;; fgrid 2 5 0
sol Colour 3 parts, then 5 parts, then 8 parts of the last rectangle. ## 3/10 + 5/10 = 8/10 || 3/10 + 5/10 = 8/10 || 3/10 + 5/10 = 8/10
qp iii | $\frac{1}{4}$ + $\frac{2}{4}$ = $\frac{3}{4}$
fig frow fxsq - ;; ftext + ;; fxsq - ;; ftext = ;; fxsq -
sol Colour 1 triangle, then 2 triangles, then 3 triangles of the last square. ## 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4 || 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4 || 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4
q 7 | Show the following figures by fractions. || درج ذیل اشکال کو کسور سے ظاہر کریں۔ || لاندې شکلونه په کسرونو وښایئ.
qp i | Rectangles in 3 parts
fig frow fgrid 1 3 1,2 #8b5cf6 ;; ftext − ;; fgrid 1 3 3 #8b5cf6 ;; ftext = ;; fgrid 1 3 3 #8b5cf6
sol $\frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{3}$ ## 2 parts shaded, minus 1 part, leaves 1 part. || 2 رنگے حصے، 1 حصہ کم، 1 حصہ بچا۔ || 2 رنګ شوې برخې، منفي 1 برخه، 1 برخه پاتې.
qp ii | Squares cut by their diagonals into 4 parts
fig frow fxsq tlb #8b5cf6 ;; ftext − ;; fxsq tl #8b5cf6 ;; ftext = ;; fxsq b #8b5cf6
sol $\frac{3}{4} - \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$ ## 3 triangles shaded, minus 2 triangles, leaves 1 triangle. || 3 مثلث رنگی، 2 کم، 1 بچی۔ || 3 مثلثونه رنګ شوي، 2 کم، 1 پاتې.
endexercise
