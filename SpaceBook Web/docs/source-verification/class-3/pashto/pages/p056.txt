Most irrigation is by canals and streams; in some places tube wells and Persian wheels also water the land. The plains have big cities and big villages, with many people. Likewise most factories, railway lines and wide roads are also in the plains.

In the village there is less pollution. People mostly live in mud houses. There is greenery, trees and plants all around, but people do not take much care of cleanliness. Near the houses there are rubbish heaps and ponds of dirty water, and drains are often blocked. Transport is not very good. The people are hard-working, but because there are no health facilities many people are often ill.
