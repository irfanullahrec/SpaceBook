The woman went away and a little later came back after asking, saying, "This bread was sent for you." This time when Bibi Rabia counted the loaves there were exactly twenty. The first time, the maid had hidden two for herself, and now she had put them back. Bibi Rabia said, "Truly this bread has been sent for me."

Why did you return eighteen loaves and accept twenty?" She answered, "When I gave the beggar two loaves at the door, I prayed to God: 'I have nothing else at this time; now You Yourself provide for my guests.' Allah Ta'ala says in the Holy Quran that whoever does one good deed, He gives ten times as much in return. By that count I was owed exactly twenty loaves. That is why I did not accept eighteen, and accepted twenty, because that was the full reward of my good deed."
