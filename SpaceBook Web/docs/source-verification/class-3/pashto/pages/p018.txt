Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ was known as truthful and honest. When Hazrat Khadijah (R.A.) heard of his honesty and integrity, she herself asked him to take her trade goods to Syria. He ﷺ agreed and took the goods there. One of Hazrat Khadijah's servants went with him on the journey. He ﷺ completed the trading journey with great honesty and hard work, and on returning showed a very large profit. Hearing of such profit, Hazrat Khadijah (R.A.) was amazed, and her servant Maisarah told her truthfully all about his ﷺ good habits, kindness and respect for others. She liked all this very much and sent him a proposal of marriage. He ﷺ consulted his uncle Abu Talib and accepted the proposal on his advice.
