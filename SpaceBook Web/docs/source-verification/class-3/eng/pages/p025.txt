qp a | Sara-------hard.
sol Subject: Sara · Sara works hard. ## With he/she/it the verb takes s: work → works. || he/she/it کے ساتھ فعل میں s لگتا ہے۔ || له he/she/it سره فعل s اخلي.
qp b | The teacher-------a question.
sol Subject: The teacher · The teacher asks a question. ## ask → asks. || ask → asks۔ || ask → asks.
qp c | We-------cricket.
sol Subject: We · We play cricket. ## With "we" the verb has no s. || "we" کے ساتھ فعل میں s نہیں۔ || له "we" سره فعل s نه اخلي.
qp d | She-------my class teacher.
sol Subject: She · She is my class teacher. ## "is" is used with she. || she کے ساتھ "is"۔ || له she سره "is".
