# A) Oral Communication || A) زبانی ابلاغ || A) شفاهي اړیکه
## 1. Learning the Sounds || 1. آوازیں سیکھنا || 1. غږونه زده کول
p Practise saying the following contractions. || درج ذیل مختصر الفاظ بولنے کی مشق کریں۔ || لاندې لنډیزونه د ویلو تمرین وکړئ.
box info | Read Me || مجھے پڑھیں || ما ولولئ
p Contractions are mostly weak sounds and are unstressed. For example I do not – I don't, he does not – he doesn't. The present form of the verb is used with do and does, example; eat, like, walk. || مختصر الفاظ زیادہ تر کمزور اور بغیر زور کے ہوتے ہیں۔ مثال: I do not – I don't، he does not – he doesn't۔ do اور does کے ساتھ فعل کی حال کی شکل آتی ہے، مثلاً eat، like، walk۔ || لنډیزونه اکثره کمزوري او بې ټینګاره وي. بېلګه: I do not – I don't، he does not – he doesn't. له do او does سره د فعل اوسنۍ بڼه راځي، لکه eat، like، walk.
endbox
q i | do not / don't · does not / doesn't || do not / don't · does not / doesn't || do not / don't · does not / doesn't
sol do not = don’t; does not = doesn’t. ## These are the contractions of the negative forms.
p The present form of the verb is used with do and does, eg eat, like, etc. || do اور does کے ساتھ فعل کی حال کی شکل آتی ہے، مثلاً eat، like وغیرہ۔ || له do او does سره د فعل اوسنۍ بڼه کارول کېږي، لکه eat، like او نور.
q ii | Speak the underlined using contractions. An example is given to help you. || خط کشیدہ حصے کو مختصر شکل میں بولیں۔ مدد کے لیے مثال دی گئی ہے۔ || لاندې کرښه لرونکې برخه په لنډیز ووایاست. د مرستې لپاره بېلګه ورکړل شوې.
qp a | I do not like a banana. → I don't like a banana. (done for you)
sol I don’t like a banana. ## do not = don’t.
qp b | We do not enjoy cricket.
sol We don't enjoy cricket. ## do not → don't || do not → don't || do not → don't
qp c | You do not run fast.
sol You don't run fast. ## do not → don't || do not → don't || do not → don't
qp d | They do not live in China.
sol They don't live in China. ## do not → don't || do not → don't || do not → don't
qp e | He does not play football.
sol He doesn't play football. ## does not → doesn't || does not → doesn't || does not → doesn't
qp f | She does not go to her village.
sol She doesn't go to her village. ## does not → doesn't || does not → doesn't || does not → doesn't
qp g | It does not fly high.
sol It doesn't fly high. ## does not → doesn't || does not → doesn't || does not → doesn't
q iii | Answer the following questions by stating “yes and no” and the contraction. || ”ہاں اور نہیں“ اور مختصر شکل سے درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || د ”هو او نه“ او لنډیز په ویلو لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
qp a | Do I like a banana? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't. (done for you)
sol Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. ## Short answers with do.
qp b | Do we enjoy cricket?
sol Yes, we do. / No, we don't. ## Short answer with do. || do کے ساتھ مختصر جواب۔ || له do سره لنډ ځواب.
qp c | Do you run fast?
sol Yes, I do. / No, I don't. ## "you" is answered with "I". || "you" کا جواب "I" سے۔ || "you" ته ځواب په "I".

qp f | Does she go to her village?
