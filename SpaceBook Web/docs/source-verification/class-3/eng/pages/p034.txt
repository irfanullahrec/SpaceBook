## 3. Grammar || 3. گرامر || 3. ګرامر
### Contractions || مختصر الفاظ || لنډیزونه
box info | Read Me || مجھے پڑھیں || ما ولولئ
p “Have” is used with I, we, you, they. “Has” is used with he, she, it. || “Have” کو I، we، you، they کے ساتھ اور “Has” کو he، she، it کے ساتھ استعمال کرتے ہیں۔ || “Have” له I، we، you، they سره او “Has” له he، she، it سره کارول کېږي.
endbox
q i | Write the following statements using contractions. An example is given to help you. || درج ذیل جملوں کو مختصر شکل میں لکھیں۔ مدد کے لیے مثال دی گئی ہے۔ || لاندې جملې د لنډیزونو په کارولو ولیکئ. د مرستې لپاره بېلګه ورکړل شوې.
qp a | I have eaten an apple. → I've eaten an apple. (done for you)
qp b | We have liked the game.
sol We've liked the game. ## have → 've || have → 've || have → 've
qp c | You have finished your work.
sol You've finished your work. ## have → 've || have → 've || have → 've
qp d | They have gone home.
sol They've gone home. ## have → 've || have → 've || have → 've
qp e | He has written a letter.
sol He's written a letter. ## has → 's || has → 's || has → 's
q ii | Write the following statements using negative contractions. An example is given to help you. || درج ذیل جملوں کو منفی مختصر شکل میں لکھیں۔ مدد کے لیے مثال دی گئی ہے۔ || لاندې جملې د منفي لنډیزونو په کارولو ولیکئ. د مرستې لپاره بېلګه ورکړل شوې.
qp a | I have not eaten an apple. → I haven't eaten an apple. (done for you)
qp b | We have not liked the game.
sol We haven't liked the game. ## have not → haven't || have not → haven't || have not → haven't
qp c | You have not finished your work.
sol You haven't finished your work. ## have not → haven't || have not → haven't || have not → haven't
qp d | They have not gone home.
sol They haven't gone home. ## have not → haven't || have not → haven't || have not → haven't
qp e | He has not written a letter.
sol He hasn't written a letter. ## has not → hasn't || has not → hasn't || has not → hasn't
qp f | She has not taken part in a game.
sol She hasn't taken part in a game. ## has not → hasn't || has not → hasn't || has not → hasn't
qp g | It has not flown away.
sol It hasn't flown away. ## has not → hasn't || has not → hasn't || has not → hasn't
box teach | Teaching Point || تدریسی نکتہ || تدریسي ټکی
p Practise contractions in the class and encourage students to answer simple questions. This will enable them to express themselves fluently. || جماعت میں مختصر الفاظ کی مشق کرائیں اور طلبہ کو سادہ سوالات کے جواب دینے کی ترغیب دیں۔ اس سے وہ روانی سے اظہار کر سکیں گے۔ || په ټولګي کې د لنډیزونو تمرین وکړئ او زده کوونکي ساده پوښتنو ځوابولو ته وهڅوئ. دا به یې په روانۍ د ځان څرګندولو وړ کړي.
endbox
