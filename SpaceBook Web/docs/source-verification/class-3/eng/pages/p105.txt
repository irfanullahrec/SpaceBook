p **Tenses – Present, Past, Future.** Tenses show the time of action (verb). Read the sentences in the table below to see the use of verbs. || **زمانے – حال، ماضی، مستقبل۔** زمانے کام (فعل) کا وقت بتاتے ہیں۔ افعال کا استعمال دیکھنے کے لیے نیچے جدول کے جملے پڑھیں۔ || **زمانې – حال، تېر، راتلونکی.** زمانې د کار (فعل) وخت ښيي. د فعلونو کارونې لیدو لپاره د لاندې جدول جملې ولولئ.
table Tenses || زمانے || زمانې
th Example | Example | Example
tr I am a teacher | I was a teacher | I will be a teacher
tr He is a teacher | He was a teacher | He will be a teacher
tr They are teachers | They were teachers | They will be teachers
tr He teaches English | He taught English | He will teach English
tr They teach English | They taught English | They will teach English
tr **Simple Present Tense:** We use first form of the verb e.g. Go, cook, show etc | **Simple Past Tense:** We use second form of the verb e.g. went, cooked, showed etc | **Simple Future Tense:** We use will and shall with first form of verb example; shall go etc
endtable
