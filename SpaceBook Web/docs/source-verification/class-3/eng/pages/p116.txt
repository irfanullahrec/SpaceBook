p Title: ______ Humayun came home after playing the match. || عنوان: ______ ہمایوں میچ کھیل کر گھر آیا۔ || سرلیک: ______ همایون له لوبې وروسته کور ته راغی.
sol Title: Hot Soup. Humayun came home after playing the match. He was very hungry. His mother gave him a bowl of soup, but it was too hot. He took a sip and his tongue was burnt. He went to the freezer and grabbed some ice cubes. He put them and some bread pieces in the soup. Soon the soup cooled down and Humayun could eat it happily. ## SpaceBook sample paragraph. || SpaceBook نمونہ پیراگراف۔ || د SpaceBook نمونه پاراګراف.
