# Author Profile || مصنفہ کا تعارف || د لیکوالې پېژندنه
p Mrs. Sadia Jabeen is currently working as Content Manager at Ulearn Education Pvt. Limited. She has completed her Master in Mathematics from University of Gujrat and did her B.Ed. from Allama Iqbal Open University Islamabad. She has more than 14 years experience in the field of education. She has also worked as Senior Mathematics Teacher in renowned educational institutes including Army Public School & College and Aligarh Public School & College. Her publications include more than 56 textbooks and Workbooks (National and International Series) of Mathematics for Pre-primary to Grade-VII. Most recently the author has developed Mathematics Textbooks Grade-1 under the Singal National Curriculum with National Curriculum Council Islamabad.
