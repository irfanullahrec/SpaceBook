qp 9 | Give reason that why the structure of BF₃ is trigonal planar and NH₃ is trigonal pyramidal. || وجہ بیان کریں کہ BF₃ کی ساخت مثلثی مستوی جبکہ NH₃ کی مثلثی اہرامی کیوں ہے؟ || دلیل ورکړئ چې د BF₃ جوړښت ولې مثلثي هوار او د NH₃ مثلثي هرمي دی؟
sol BF₃: boron has three valence electrons forming three bond pairs and no lone pair, so VSEPR predicts a trigonal planar shape (sp², 120°). NH₃: nitrogen has three bond pairs and one lone pair (four electron pairs, sp³), so the lone pair pushes the bond pairs together, giving a trigonal pyramidal shape (about 107°). ## VSEPR theory.
qp 10 | Why are the bond energies of polar molecules higher than those of non-polar molecules? || قطبی سالموں کی بندشی توانائی غیر قطبی سالموں سے زیادہ کیوں ہوتی ہے؟ || د قطبي مالیکولونو د تړون انرژي ولې د غیرقطبي مالیکولونو تر هغې لوړه وي؟
sol The bond energies of polar molecules are higher because the electronegativity difference gives partial ionic character: the attraction between the partial charges (δ+ and δ−) adds to the covalent bond strength, so more energy is needed to break the bond than in a pure non-polar covalent bond. ## Polar and non-polar bonds.

qp • | 2(a). Explain how σ and π bonds are similar and how they are different. || 2(الف) وضاحت کریں کہ σ اور π بندشیں کس طرح ملتی جلتی اور مختلف ہیں۔ || 2(الف) تشریح کړئ چې σ او π تړونونه څنګه ورته او څنګه توپیر لري.
sol Similarities: both σ and π bonds are covalent bonds formed by the overlap of atomic orbitals, each with a pair of electrons shared between two atoms. Differences: a σ bond is formed by head-on overlap, is stronger, has electron density along the bond axis and rotation is free; a π bond is formed by sideways overlap, is weaker, has electron density above and below the axis and exists only along with a σ bond (double and triple bonds contain π bonds). ## σ and π bonds.
q  | 2(b). Define and explain resonance with a suitable example. || 2(ب) ریزونینس کی تعریف کریں اور مناسب مثال سے وضاحت کریں۔ || 2(ب) رېزونانس تعریف او په مناسبه بېلګه یې تشریح کړئ.
sol Resonance is the representation of a molecule by two or more Lewis structures that differ only in the position of electrons; the real molecule is a resonance hybrid of all of them and is more stable than any contributor. Example: ozone O₃ (O=O–O ↔ O–O=O), or benzene (two Kekulé structures), or the carbonate ion; the bonds have equal lengths intermediate between single and double. ## Resonance.

qp • | 3(a). Explain the HCl, H₂O and N₂ molecules on the basis of valence-bond theory. || 3(الف) ویلنس بندش نظریے کی بنیاد پر HCl، H₂O اور N₂ سالموں کی وضاحت کریں۔ || 3(الف) د والنس تړون تیورۍ پر بنسټ د HCl، H₂O او N₂ مالیکولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol HCl: the 1s orbital of H (half-filled) overlaps head-on with the half-filled 3p orbital of Cl to form one σ bond. H₂O: the two half-filled 2p orbitals of O overlap with the 1s orbitals of two H atoms to form two σ bonds; angle about 90° by VBT (actual 104.5°). N₂: the three half-filled 2p orbitals of each N overlap: one head-on σ bond and two sideways π bonds, forming a triple bond N≡N. ## Valence bond theory.
q  | 3(b). Define and explain hybridization with reference to the sp model of hybridization. || 3(ب) sp ہائبرڈائزیشن ماڈل کے حوالے سے ہائبرڈائزیشن کی تعریف اور وضاحت کریں۔ || 3(ب) د sp هایبرډایزېشن ماډل په تړاو هایبرډایزېشن تعریف او تشریح کړئ.
sol Hybridization is the mixing of orbitals of nearly the same energy of the same atom to give an equal number of equivalent hybrid orbitals. In sp hybridization, one s and one p orbital mix to give two equivalent sp hybrid orbitals, at 180° (linear), with 50 % s-character; e.g. BeCl₂, C₂H₂ (the two unhybridized p orbitals on each carbon form the two π bonds). ## sp hybridization.

qp • | 4(a). Write down the main points of molecular-orbital theory. || 4(الف) سالماتی اوربیٹل نظریے کے اہم نکات لکھیں۔ || 4(الف) د مالیکولي اوربیټال تیورۍ مهم ټکي ولیکئ.
sol Main points of molecular orbital theory: (1) atomic orbitals combine to form the same number of molecular orbitals spread over the whole molecule; (2) in-phase combination gives lower energy bonding MOs and out-of-phase combination gives higher energy antibonding MOs; (3) electrons fill the MOs by the Aufbau principle, Pauli's principle and Hund's rule; (4) bond order = (Nb − Na)/2; (5) a stable molecule needs bond order > 0. ## MOT.
q  | 4(b). Draw the bonding in the oxygen molecule with the help of molecular-orbital theory. || 4(ب) سالماتی اوربیٹل نظریے کی مدد سے آکسیجن سالمے کی بندشیں بنائیں۔ || 4(ب) د مالیکولي اوربیټال تیورۍ په مرسته د اکسیجن مالیکول تړونونه رسم کړئ.
sol O₂ has 16 electrons. MO configuration: σ1s² σ*1s² σ2s² σ*2s² σ2p_z² (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) (π*2p_x¹ = π*2p_y¹). There are 10 bonding and 6 antibonding electrons, bond order = (10 − 6)/2 = 2 (double bond), with two unpaired electrons in the π* orbitals, so oxygen is paramagnetic. ## MO diagram of O₂.

qp • | 5(a). How can the geometry of a molecule be determined by using the dipole moment? || 5(الف) ڈائی پول مومنٹ استعمال کرکے سالمے کی جیومیٹری کیسے متعین کی جا سکتی ہے؟ || 5(الف) د ډایپول مومنټ په کارولو د مالیکول هندسه څنګه ټاکل کېدای شي؟
sol The dipole moment shows the shape: a molecule with zero dipole moment despite polar bonds must be symmetrical (linear CO₂, trigonal planar BF₃, tetrahedral CCl₄), while a molecule with a net dipole moment is not symmetrical (angular H₂O, pyramidal NH₃). A non-zero value of H₂O (1.85 D) proves the bent structure. ## Dipole moment and geometry.
q  | 5(b). How could you measure the dipole moment of a molecule? || 5(ب) آپ کسی سالمے کا ڈائی پول مومنٹ کیسے ناپیں گے؟ || 5(ب) د مالیکول ډایپول مومنټ څنګه اندازه کولای شئ؟
sol The dipole moment is measured from the effect of an electric field on the substance: the dielectric constant (capacitance) of the sample in a condenser is measured at different temperatures, and the dipole moment is calculated using the Debye equation; it is expressed in debye (D). ## Experimental determination.
q  | 5(c). Write down the difference among molecular, network and metallic solids. || 5(ج) سالماتی، نیٹ ورک اور دھاتی ٹھوسوں کے درمیان فرق لکھیں۔ || 5(ج) د مالیکولي، شبکوي او فلزي جامدونو ترمنځ توپیر ولیکئ.
sol Molecular solids (ice, iodine, sugar) consist of molecules held by weak van der Waals forces or hydrogen bonds, have low melting points, are soft and non-conductors. Network (covalent) solids (diamond, SiO₂) are held by a continuous network of covalent bonds, very hard with very high melting points, non-conductors (graphite conducts). Metallic solids (Na, Cu) consist of positive ions in a sea of delocalised electrons, are malleable, ductile, shiny and conduct electricity. ## Types of solids.
