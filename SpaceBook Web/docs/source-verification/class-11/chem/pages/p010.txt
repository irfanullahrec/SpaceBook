sol Relative atomic mass is the mass of one atom of an element compared with 1/12 of the mass of one atom of carbon-12 (it has no unit; when expressed in amu or g/mol it is the atomic mass). ## Definition.
q 2 | What is gram atom? || گرام ایٹم کیا ہے؟ || ګرام اتوم څه شی دی؟
sol A gram atom is the atomic mass of an element expressed in grams. It contains one mole (6.022 × 10²³) of atoms, e.g. 12 g of carbon or 23 g of sodium is one gram atom. ## Definition.
