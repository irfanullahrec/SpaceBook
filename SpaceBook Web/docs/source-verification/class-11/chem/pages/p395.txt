q 6 | What are electrochemical cells? Explain it in detail. || برق کیمیائی خلیے کیا ہیں؟ اس کی تفصیل سے وضاحت کریں۔ || الکتروکيمياوي حجرې څه دي؟ دا په تفصیل سره تشریح کړئ.
sol Electrochemical cells are devices that convert chemical energy into electrical energy (voltaic or galvanic cells) or electrical energy into chemical energy (electrolytic cells). A cell consists of two half cells (electrodes in electrolytes), joined by a wire and a salt bridge. In a voltaic cell the oxidation occurs at the anode (−) and the reduction at the cathode (+), and E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode (Daniell cell: Zn | Zn²⁺ ‖ Cu²⁺ | Cu, 1.10 V). Types are primary cells, secondary cells and fuel cells. ## Electrochemical cells.
q 7 | Write down the types of batteries. Explain fuel cells? || بیٹریوں کی اقسام لکھیں۔ ایندھن خلیوں کی وضاحت کریں؟ || د بیټریو ډولونه ولیکئ. د سونګ حجرې تشریح کړئ؟
sol Types of batteries: primary batteries (dry cell, alkaline cell, mercury cell; not rechargeable), secondary batteries (lead storage battery, Ni-Cd and lithium-ion; rechargeable) and fuel cells. A fuel cell converts the chemical energy of a fuel (H₂) and an oxidant (O₂) directly into electricity: at the anode H₂ + 2OH⁻ → 2H₂O + 2e⁻; at the cathode O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻; overall 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. It is efficient, produces only water, and works as long as the fuel is supplied (used in space vehicles). ## Batteries and fuel cells.

Connect the pencil cores with electrical wires with the terminals of a 9 volt battery, one pencil with the positive pole and other with the negative pole of the battery. When the battery is switched on you will see bubbles coming from the lower ends of the pencil cores. What do you think about the nature of the bubbles?
