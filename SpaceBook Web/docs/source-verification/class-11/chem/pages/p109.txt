## Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
qp • | What evidence can you find that the CO₂ molecule has a linear but the H₂O molecule has an angular structure? || آپ کیا ثبوت دے سکتے ہیں کہ CO₂ سالمہ خطی جبکہ H₂O سالمہ زاویائی ساخت رکھتا ہے؟ || کوم شواهد ښيي چې CO₂ خطي او H₂O زاویوي جوړښت لري؟
sol CO₂ has no net dipole moment (zero), because the two C=O bond dipoles cancel each other in a linear molecule, while H₂O has a net dipole moment (about 1.85 D) because its two O-H dipoles do not cancel in a bent molecule. Their shapes also follow from VSEPR theory: CO₂ has two bond pairs and no lone pair (180°); H₂O has two bond pairs and two lone pairs (104.5°). ## Dipole moment evidence.
qp • | Give the reason why the O₂ molecule is paramagnetic. || وجہ بتائیں کہ O₂ سالمہ پیرا میگنیٹک کیوں ہے۔ || دلیل ورکړئ چې O₂ مالیکول ولې پارامقناطیسي دی.
sol In the molecular orbital configuration of O₂, the last two electrons occupy two separate degenerate π* antibonding orbitals with parallel spins (Hund's rule), so the molecule has two unpaired electrons and is paramagnetic. ## Molecular orbital theory.
qp • | What ideas justify that the bond angle in NH₃ is 107.5° but in H₂O it is 104.5°? || کون سے تصورات واضح کرتے ہیں کہ NH₃ میں بندشی زاویہ 107.5° جبکہ H₂O میں 104.5° ہے؟ || کوم مفاهیم دا توجیه کوي چې په NH₃ کې د تړون زاویه 107.5° او په H₂O کې 104.5° ده؟
sol NH₃ has three bond pairs and one lone pair, H₂O has two bond pairs and two lone pairs. Lone pair-bond pair repulsion is greater than bond pair-bond pair repulsion, and two lone pairs repel the bond pairs more strongly, so the angle decreases from 109.5° to 107.5° in NH₃ and 104.5° in H₂O. ## VSEPR theory.
qp • | Differentiate between atomic and molecular orbitals. || ایٹمی اور سالماتی اوربیٹلوں میں فرق کریں۔ || د اټومي او مالیکولي اوربیټالونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol An atomic orbital belongs to a single atom (one nucleus) and can hold up to two electrons; a molecular orbital is formed by the combination of atomic orbitals of different atoms and belongs to the whole molecule (two or more nuclei). Atomic orbitals are s, p, d and f; molecular orbitals are bonding or antibonding (σ, π, σ*, π*). ## Difference.
qp • | What data was used to conclude that the calculated bond lengths of heteronuclear molecules are always higher than experimental values? || یہ نتیجہ اخذ کرنے کے لیے کون سا ڈیٹا استعمال ہوا کہ مختلف ایٹمی سالموں کی حسابی بندشی لمبائیاں ہمیشہ تجرباتی قدروں سے زیادہ ہیں؟ || د دې پایلې لپاره کوم معلومات کارول شوي چې د ناهم‌هسته‌يي مالیکولونو حساب شوي تړون اوږدوالی تل له تجربه شوو ارزښتونو لوړ وي؟
sol Experimental bond lengths of heteronuclear molecules (e.g. HCl, HF) are shorter than the calculated (sum of covalent radii) values, because of the polarity of the bond: the partial ionic character (electronegativity difference) adds attraction and shortens the bond. The data used are the electronegativity values and the measured bond lengths. ## Schomaker-Stevenson correction.
qp • | How would you prove that a sigma (σ)-bond is stronger than a pi (π)-bond? || آپ کیسے ثابت کریں گے کہ سگما (σ) بندش پائی (π) بندش سے زیادہ مضبوط ہے؟ || څنګه به ثابته کړئ چې سیګما (σ) تړون تر پای (π) تړون پیاوړی دی؟
sol A σ bond is stronger because its overlap is head-on along the axis, giving the maximum overlap; a π bond is formed by sideways overlap, which is smaller. Evidence: the bond energy of a C-C single bond (σ, 347 kJ/mol) and of the C=C double bond (σ + π, 614 kJ/mol), the π part being about 267 kJ/mol only, which is less than the σ bond; π bonds break more easily in reactions (addition). ## Bond energies.
qp • | What information would you use to support the view that the He₂ molecule does not exist? || He₂ سالمہ وجود نہیں رکھتا، اس خیال کی تائید کے لیے آپ کون سی معلومات استعمال کریں گے؟ || د دې نظر د ملاتړ لپاره به کوم معلومات وکاروئ چې He₂ مالیکول شتون نه لري؟
sol He₂ does not exist because its bond order is zero: He₂ would have two electrons in the bonding σ(1s) and two in the antibonding σ*(1s) orbitals, so the stabilisation of the bonding is cancelled by the destabilisation of the antibonding. Bond order = (2 − 2)/2 = 0; noble gas helium exists as single atoms. ## Molecular orbital theory.
qp • | Differentiate between bonding and anti-bonding orbitals. || بندشی اور ضدِ بندشی اوربیٹلوں میں فرق کریں۔ || د تړونکو او ضد تړونکو اوربیټالونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol A bonding molecular orbital is formed by the in-phase (constructive) overlap of atomic orbitals, has a high electron density between the nuclei, and lower energy than the atomic orbitals, so it stabilises the molecule. An antibonding molecular orbital is formed by out-of-phase (destructive) overlap, has a node between the nuclei, higher energy, and destabilises the molecule (marked with *). ## MOT.
