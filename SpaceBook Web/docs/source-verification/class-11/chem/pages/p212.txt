q 3 | Differentiate between the following pairs: || درج ذیل جوڑوں میں فرق کریں: || د لاندې جوړو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ:
q i | Ionic solid and molecular solid || آئنی ٹھوس اور سالماتی ٹھوس || آیوني جامد او مالیکولي جامد
sol An ionic solid is made of ions held by strong electrostatic forces (NaCl), hard, with a high melting point, non-conducting when solid; a molecular solid consists of molecules held by weak forces (ice, iodine), soft, with a low melting point. ## Difference.
q ii | Lattice energy and crystal lattice || جالی توانائی اور قلمی جالی || د جالۍ انرژي او کرسټالي جالۍ
sol Lattice energy is the energy released when gaseous ions combine to form one mole of the ionic solid; crystal lattice is the regular three-dimensional arrangement of particles in the crystal. ## Difference.
q iii | Plane of symmetry and axis of symmetry || سطحِ تشاکل اور محورِ تشاکل || د تناظر مستوي او د تناظر محور
sol A plane of symmetry is an imaginary plane dividing a crystal into two halves which are mirror images of each other; an axis of symmetry is an imaginary line through the crystal about which, on rotating by 360°/n, the crystal looks the same (two-, three-, four- and six-fold). A cube has 9 planes (3 + 6) and 13 axes (3 + 4 + 6) of symmetry. ## Elements of symmetry.
q 4 | (a) Define allotropy. Use oxygen and sulphur as an example to[scan obscured] allotropes. || (الف) دوسرے روپ (ایلوٹروپی) کی تعریف کریں۔ آکسیجن اور سلفر کو مثال کے طور پر استعمال کریں [scan obscured] دوسرے روپ۔ || (الف) المورفي تعریف کړئ. اکسیجن او سلفر د بېلګې په توګه وکاروئ [scan obscured] المورفونه.
sol Allotropy is the existence of an element in two or more crystalline or molecular forms in the same state with different physical properties. Oxygen exists as O₂ and O₃ (ozone); sulphur exists as rhombic (octahedral, α) and monoclinic (needle, β) sulphur, and as amorphous plastic sulphur. ## Allotropes.
q  | (b) Explain the low density and high heat of fusion of ice. || (ب) برف کی کم کثافت اور بلند حرارتِ پگھلاؤ کی وضاحت کریں۔ || (ب) د یخ ټیټه کثافت او لوړه د ویلې کېدو تودوخه تشریح کړئ.
sol Ice has a low density because of its open hydrogen-bonded structure; each O atom is bonded to four H atoms in a tetrahedral arrangement with empty hexagonal spaces. Its heat of fusion is high (6.01 kJ/mol) because a lot of energy is needed to break these hydrogen bonds when it melts. ## Hydrogen bonding.
q 5 | (a) Define and explain molecular and metallic solids || (الف) سالماتی اور دھاتی ٹھوس کی تعریف کریں اور وضاحت کریں || (الف) مالیکولي او فلزي جامدات تعریف او تشریح کړئ
sol Molecular solids have molecules at the lattice points held by weak intermolecular forces (iodine, dry ice, ice); they are soft, volatile, with low melting points, and are non-conductors. Metallic solids have metal cations in a sea of delocalised electrons (metallic bond), such as Cu or Na; they are malleable, ductile, lustrous and conduct heat and electricity. ## Definitions.
q  | (b) What are the features of three types of packing arrangements[scan obscured] system and construct models of them? || (ب) بھراؤ کی تین ترتیبوں کی کیا خصوصیات ہیں [scan obscured] نظام اور ان کے ماڈل تیار کریں؟ || (ب) د ډکولو د درېیو ډولونو ترتیبونو ځانګړنې څه دي [scan obscured] سیسټم او د هغوی ماډلونه جوړ کړئ؟
sol The three types of packing arrangements are: simple cubic (each sphere touches 6 others, coordination number 6, packing efficiency 52 %), body-centred cubic (coordination number 8, 68 %) and closest packing which can be hexagonal (ABAB) or cubic (ABCABC) (coordination number 12, 74 %). Models can be made with marbles, polystyrene balls or clay balls arranged in layers. ## Packing in crystals.
q 6 | (a) Write down the three factors that affect the shape of an ionic cr[scan obscured] || (الف) وہ تین عوامل لکھیں جو آئنی قلم [scan obscured] کی شکل پر اثر انداز ہوتے ہیں || (الف) هغه درې فکتورونه ولیکئ چې د آیوني کر [scan obscured] په شکل اغېز کوي
sol Three factors affecting the shape of an ionic crystal: (1) the radius ratio r⁺/r⁻ of the cation and anion; (2) the relative number of cations and anions (the charge balance); (3) the packing and coordination number which are the result of the size and charge of the ions (also temperature and pressure). ## Ionic crystal shape.
q  | (b) What is meant by unit cell of crystal? Sketch the unit cell of NaC[scan obscured] || (ب) قلم کے اکائی خانے سے کیا مراد ہے؟ NaC[scan obscured] کے اکائی خانے کا خاکہ بنائیں || (ب) د کرسټال د واحد حجرې څخه څه مراد دی؟ د NaC[scan obscured] د واحد حجرې انځور رسم کړئ
sol The unit cell is the smallest repeating unit of the crystal lattice which, repeated in all three directions, gives the complete crystal. The unit cell of NaCl is a face-centred cube: Cl⁻ ions at the eight corners and the six face centres, with Na⁺ ions at the edge centres and the body centre; each unit cell contains four Na⁺ and four Cl⁻ ions. ## Sketch the cube with alternating ions.
