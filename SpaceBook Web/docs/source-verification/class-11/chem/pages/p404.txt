## GLOSSARY || فرہنگ || وييپانګه
table فرہنگ || Glossary || وييپانګه
th | Term | Definition
tr | Molar heat capacity | The energy required to raise the temperature of one mole of a substance by one degree Celsius
tr | Molar mass | The mass in grams of one mole of molecules or formula units of a substance; also called molecular weight
tr | Molar solution | A solution containing 1 mole of solute per dm³ (liter) of solution
tr | Molar volume | The volume occupied by one mole of an ideal gas at standard temperature and pressure is called molar volume. Its value is approximately 22.4 dm3.
tr | Molarity | Concentration unit of a solution expressed as moles of solute dissolved per dm³ of solution
tr | Mole | The chemical unit of quantity for any substance; equal to 6.02 x1023 individual atoms, molecules, or formula units of the substance; abbreviated mol.
tr | Mole fraction | The ratio of the number of moles of a given component in a mixture to the total number of moles in the mixture.
tr | Monoatomic molecules | A molecule which consist of only one atom e.g. neon, argon.
tr | Moseley's Law | The square root of the frequency (√v) is directly proportional to the atomic number (Z) of an element.
tr | Network solids | Solids in which atoms are bonded covalently into continuous two-dimensional or three dimensional arrays with a wide range of properties
tr | Orbital | A cloudlike region around the nucleus where electrons are located. Orbitals are considered to be energy sublevels (s, p, d, f) within the principal energy levels.
tr | Oxidizing agent | A substance which brings about oxidation.
tr | Partial pressure | The pressure exerted by individual gas in a gaseous mixture is called the partial pressure of that gas.
tr | Parts per million (ppm) | A measurement of the concentration of dilute solutions commonly used by chemists in place of mass percent.
tr | Pascal | The SI unit of pressure; equal to Newton per meter squared.
tr | Pauli Exclusion Principle | In a given atom no two electrons can have the same set of four quantum numbers (n, l, m, and s).
tr | Percent composition of a compound | The mass percent represented by each element in a compound
