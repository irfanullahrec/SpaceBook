q 5 | (a) Define quantum numbers. What information is given by the … quantum number? What are the possible values for this quant… number? || (a) کوانٹم اعداد کی تعریف کریں۔ … کوانٹم عدد کیا معلومات دیتا ہے؟ اس کوانٹ… عدد کی ممکنہ قدریں کیا ہیں؟ || (a) کوانتمي شمېرې تعریف کړئ. … کوانتمي شمېره څه معلومات ورکوي؟ د دې کوانټ… شمېرې ممکنه ارزښتونه څه دي؟
sol (a) Quantum numbers are the set of numbers which describe an electron in an atom. The azimuthal (angular momentum) quantum number ℓ gives the shape of the sub-shell/orbital and the angular momentum; its possible values are 0 to n − 1 (ℓ = 0 for s, 1 for p, 2 for d, 3 for f). ## Quantum numbers.
q  | (b) What information is given by the magnetic quantum number? || (b) مقناطیسی کوانٹم عدد کیا معلومات دیتا ہے؟ || (b) مقناطیسي کوانتمي شمېره څه معلومات ورکوي؟
sol (b) The magnetic quantum number (m) gives the orientation of the orbital in space (the number of orbitals in the sub-shell). Its values are −ℓ ... 0 ... +ℓ, i.e. 2ℓ + 1 values (1 for s, 3 for p, 5 for d, 7 for f). ## Magnetic quantum number.
q 6 | (a) What is an orbital? How does it differ from an orbit? || (a) اوربیٹل کیا ہے؟ یہ مدار سے کیسے مختلف ہے؟ || (a) اوربیټال څه دی؟ له مدار څخه څنګه توپیر لري؟
sol (a) An orbital is the region of space around the nucleus in which the probability of finding the electron is maximum (about 90-95 %), given by a wave function ψ. An orbit (Bohr) is a definite circular path around the nucleus along which the electron is assumed to revolve; an orbital has shape and is a probability region, while an orbit is a fixed path (two-dimensional); an orbital can hold at most two electrons. ## Difference between orbit and orbital.
q  | (b) Discuss the shapes of s, p and d orbitals. || (b) s، p اور d اوربیٹلوں کی شکلیں بیان کریں۔ || (b) د s، p او d اوربیټالونو بڼې بیان کړئ.
sol (b) s orbitals are spherical, with an increasing size from 1s to 2s (with nodes); the three p orbitals (pₓ, p_y, p_z) are dumb-bell shaped, along the three axes at right angles, with a node at the nucleus; the five d orbitals have double dumb-bell shapes (d_xy, d_yz, d_zx, d_x²−y²), and d_z² has a dumb-bell with a doughnut (ring) in the middle. ## Shapes of orbitals.
