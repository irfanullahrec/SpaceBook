## (ii) Freezing Point Depression || (ii) نقطۂ انجماد میں کمی || (ii) د ځمبېدو د نقطې ښکته کېدل
p The freezing point of a liquid is the temperature at which the solid phase begins to separate out from the liquid phase. At this temperature solid and liquid phases are in equilibrium and have equal vapour pressure. A solution freezes at lower temperature as compared to its pure solvent. This lowering of freezing point of solution as compared to its pure solvent is called depression of freezing point. This lowering is due to lowering in vapour pressure of solution as a result of addition of small amount of non-volatile solute.
