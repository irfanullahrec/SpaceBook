## Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
q 1 | Give at least two of the effects on our lives if water had weak hydrogen bonding among its molecules. || اگر پانی کے مالیکیولز میں ہائیڈروجن بندش کمزور ہوتی تو ہماری زندگیوں پر کم از کم دو اثرات بیان کریں۔ || که د اوبو په مالیکولونو کې هایډروجن بانډ کمزوری وای نو زموږ په ژوند باندې لږترلږه دوه اغېزې ورکړئ.
sol If water had weak hydrogen bonding: (1) it would be a gas at room temperature with a low boiling point (like H₂S), so there would be no oceans and lakes and life would be impossible; (2) ice would not float, nor would water have its high specific heat, so the climate would be extreme and aquatic life would be killed in winter. ## Importance of hydrogen bonding.
q 2 | Give the reason that why HF is a liquid at ordinary temperature while HCl is a gas. || وجہ بیان کریں کہ HF عام درجہ حرارت پر مائع کیوں ہے جبکہ HCl گیس ہے۔ || لامل ورکړئ چې ولې HF په عادي تودوخه کې مایع دی پداسې حال کې چې HCl ګاز دی.
sol HF molecules form strong hydrogen bonds with each other because fluorine is highly electronegative and small, and the H-F bond is very polar, so HF is a liquid (boiling point 19.5 °C). HCl has a weak dipole-dipole attraction and no hydrogen bonding (Cl is bigger and less electronegative), so it is a gas at room temperature (b.p. −85 °C). ## Hydrogen bonding.
q 3 | Why H₂O has high boiling point than HF, although fluorine is more electronegative than oxygen. || H₂O کا نقطۂ ابال HF سے زیادہ کیوں ہے حالانکہ فلورین آکسیجن سے زیادہ برق منفی ہے۔ || ولې H₂O د HF څخه د اېشېدو لوړ ټکی لري که څه هم فلورین له اکسیجن څخه ډېر برېښنا منفي دی.
sol Each water molecule has two hydrogen atoms and two lone pairs, so it can form up to four hydrogen bonds (two as donor and two as acceptor), giving a three-dimensional network; HF has only one hydrogen and forms only a chain of about two bonds per molecule. More hydrogen bonds per molecule require more energy to break, so water has a higher boiling point than HF. ## Hydrogen bonding.
q 4 | How would you justify that water and ethanol can mix easily in all proportions? || آپ اس بات کی کیا توجیہ کریں گے کہ پانی اور ایتھانول تمام تناسب میں آسانی سے مل سکتے ہیں؟ || تاسو به څنګه توجیه کړئ چې اوبه او ایتانول په ټولو تناسبونو کې په اسانۍ سره ګډېدای شي؟
sol Water and ethanol are both polar and form hydrogen bonds with each other: the -OH group of ethanol makes hydrogen bonds with water, and the attractions between the two kinds of molecules are as strong as those between like molecules, so they mix in all proportions (miscible). ## Like dissolves like.
q 5 | How can you explain that neon and argon both are mono-atomic noble gases of the same group, neon has boiling of -248°C, while argon has -189°C? || آپ کیسے وضاحت کریں گے کہ نیون اور آرگن دونوں ایک ہی گروپ کی یک ایٹمی نوبل گیسیں ہیں، نیون کا نقطۂ ابال -248°C ہے جبکہ آرگن کا -189°C ہے؟ || تاسو به څنګه تشریح کړئ چې نیون او ارګون دواړه د یوې ډلې یو اتومي نوبل ګازونه دي، نیون د اېشېدو ټکی -248°C لري پداسې حال کې چې ارګون -189°C لري؟
sol Boiling point depends on London dispersion forces, which increase with the size of the atom and the number of electrons. Argon (atomic number 18) has more electrons and a larger electron cloud than neon (10), so its dispersion forces are stronger and it has a higher boiling point (−189 °C against −248 °C). ## Dispersion forces.
q 6 | How would you justify that different liquids have different rates of evaporation even at the same temperature. || آپ اس بات کی کیا توجیہ کریں گے کہ مختلف مائعات ایک ہی درجہ حرارت پر بھی تبخیر کی مختلف شرحیں رکھتے ہیں۔ || تاسو به څنګه توجیه کړئ چې بېلابېل مایعات حتی په یوه تودوخه کې هم د تبخیر بېلابېلې کچې لري.
sol Different liquids evaporate at different rates at the same temperature because of different intermolecular forces: liquids with weak forces (ether, acetone, petrol) have molecules which escape easily and are volatile, while those with strong forces (water, glycerine) evaporate slowly. ## Intermolecular forces.
q 7 | Justify the statement that earthenware vessels keep water cool even in summer days. || اس بیان کی توجیہ کریں کہ مٹی کے برتن گرمی کے دنوں میں بھی پانی ٹھنڈا رکھتے ہیں۔ || د دې بیان توجیه وکړئ چې د خټو لوښي حتی د دوبي په ورځو کې هم اوبه یخې ساتي.
sol Earthenware vessels are porous, so water slowly seeps through the walls to the outer surface and evaporates; the evaporation absorbs latent heat from the pot and the water inside, cooling it even in hot summer. ## Evaporation causes cooling.
q 8 | How vacuum distillation can be used to avoid decomposition of sensitive liquids? || حساس مائعات کے تحلیل سے بچنے کے لیے خلائی کشید کیسے استعمال ہو سکتی ہے؟ || ویکیوم تقطیر د حساسو مایعاتو له تجزیې د مخنیوي لپاره څنګه کارول کېدای شي؟
sol In vacuum distillation the pressure above the liquid is greatly reduced, so the liquid boils at a much lower temperature than its normal boiling point. Heat-sensitive liquids which decompose at their normal boiling points can then be distilled without decomposition. ## Vacuum distillation.
q 9 | Why evaporation of a liquid causes cooling? || مائع کا تبخیر ٹھنڈک کیوں پیدا کرتا ہے؟ || د مایع تبخیر ولې یخوالی رامنځته کوي؟
sol During evaporation the faster (higher energy) molecules escape from the liquid, so the average kinetic energy of the remaining molecules falls and the temperature of the liquid decreases; the heat required for evaporation is taken from the liquid and its surroundings, which causes cooling. ## Evaporation is endothermic.
q 10 | Explain that why a liquid boils at different temperature at sea level and mountains. || وضاحت کریں کہ مائع سطحِ سمندر اور پہاڑوں پر مختلف درجہ حرارت پر کیوں ابلتا ہے۔ || تشریح کړئ چې ولې یو مایع د سمندر په کچه او غرونو کې په بېلابېلو تودوخو اېشي.
sol A liquid boils when its vapour pressure equals the external pressure. At sea level the atmospheric pressure is 1 atm, so water boils at 100 °C; on mountains the atmospheric pressure is lower, so the vapour pressure reaches it at a lower temperature and water boils at less than 100 °C. ## Boiling point and pressure.
q 11 | Why water droplet is spherical? || پانی کا قطرہ کروی کیوں ہوتا ہے؟ || د اوبو څاڅکی ولې ګردی وي؟
sol A water drop is spherical because surface tension makes the liquid surface contract to the smallest possible area, and a sphere has the minimum surface area for a given volume. ## Surface tension.
q 12 | Temperature of a liquid remains constant during boiling although heat being supplied continuously, why? || ابلنے کے دوران مائع کا درجہ حرارت مستقل رہتا ہے حالانکہ حرارت مسلسل فراہم کی جا رہی ہوتی ہے، کیوں؟ || د اېشېدو پر مهال د مایع تودوخه ثابته پاتې کېږي که څه هم تودوخه په دوام ورکول کېږي، ولې؟
sol During boiling, the heat supplied is used to overcome the intermolecular forces and convert liquid to vapour (latent heat of vaporization) and not to raise the kinetic energy of the molecules, so the temperature remains constant at the boiling point. ## Latent heat.
