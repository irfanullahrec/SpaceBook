## Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
## 1۔ Explain the following short questions with reasons. || درج ذیل مختصر سوالات کی وجوہات کے ساتھ وضاحت کریں۔ || لاندې لنډې پوښتنې د دلایلو سره تشریح کړئ.
q i | Total energy of the system and its surroundings remains constant. || نظام اور اس کے ماحول کی کل توانائی مستقل رہتی ہے۔ || د سیسټم او د هغه د شاوخوا ټوله انرژي ثابته پاتې کېږي.
sol Energy can neither be created nor destroyed but only transferred or changed from one form to another (first law of thermodynamics), so the energy lost by the system is gained by the surroundings and the total energy of the system plus surroundings (the universe) remains constant. ## First law.
q ii | Ionic reactions are very fast. || آئنی تعاملات بہت تیز ہوتے ہیں۔ || آیوني تعاملات ډېر ګړندي دي.
sol Ionic reactions are very fast because the ions are already present in solution and only combine by electrostatic attraction (no bonds are broken), so the activation energy is very small and the reaction is almost instantaneous. ## Ionic reactions.
q iii | The work done has positive and negative values. || کیے گئے کام کی مثبت اور منفی قیمتیں ہوتی ہیں۔ || ترسره شوی کار مثبت او منفي ارزښتونه لري.
sol The work done is positive when work is done on the system (compression, the energy of the system increases) and negative when work is done by the system (expansion). It is defined by the sign convention (w = −PΔV): in expansion ΔV is positive so w is negative. ## Sign convention.
q iv | Heat of combustion is always negative. || احتراق کی حرارت ہمیشہ منفی ہوتی ہے۔ || د سوځېدو تودوخه تل منفي وي.
sol The heat of combustion is always negative because combustion is exothermic: the heat is evolved to the surroundings, so the enthalpy of the system decreases (ΔH < 0). ## Combustion.
q v | Enthalpy change is a state function but heat is not. || انتھالپی تبدیلی حالت کا تفاعل ہے لیکن حرارت نہیں۔ || د انتالپي بدلون د حالت تابع ده خو تودوخه نه ده.
sol Enthalpy change depends only on the initial and final states of the system and not on the path, so it is a state function. Heat is a form of energy in transit that depends on the path taken (for example, the reaction in an open vessel or at constant volume), so it is not a state function. ## State functions.
q vi | ΔH for solids and liquids becomes equal to ΔE. || ٹھوس اور مائعات کے لیے ΔH، ΔE کے برابر ہو جاتا ہے۔ || د جامداتو او مایعاتو لپاره ΔH له ΔE سره مساوي کېږي.
sol ΔH = ΔE + PΔV. In solids and liquids the volume change ΔV is negligible, so PΔV ≈ 0 and ΔH ≈ ΔE. ## Enthalpy and internal energy.
q vii | Enthalpy of neutralization of strong acids and strong bases has always the same value. || مضبوط تیزابوں اور مضبوط اساسوں کی تعدیل کی انتھالپی ہمیشہ ایک ہی قیمت رکھتی ہے۔ || د قوي اسیدونو او قوي القليو د خنثی کېدو انتالپي تل یو شان ارزښت لري.
sol The neutralization of a strong acid with a strong base is essentially H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O, since both are completely ionized, so the heat released per mole of water formed (about −57.3 kJ mol⁻¹) is the same for every strong acid and strong base. ## Heat of neutralization.
q 2 | What do you mean by state of a system? What are state functions? || نظام کی حالت سے آپ کیا مراد لیتے ہیں؟ حالت کے تفاعل کیا ہیں؟ || د سیسټم له حالت څخه تاسو څه مراد اخلئ؟ د حالت تابع څه دي؟
sol The state of a system is the condition of the system described by its measurable properties (temperature, pressure, volume, composition). State functions are properties that depend only on the present state of the system and not on how it reached that state, e.g. internal energy, enthalpy, entropy, pressure, temperature and volume. ## State functions.
## Long Questions || تفصیلی سوالات || اوږدې پوښتنې
q 1 | State and explain Hess's law of constant heat summation. Show that it is a direct consequence of the first law of thermodynamics. || ہیس کے مستقل حرارتی مجموعے کے قانون کو بیان کریں اور وضاحت کریں۔ ثابت کریں کہ یہ تھرموڈائنامکس کے پہلے قانون کا براہِ راست نتیجہ ہے۔ || د هېس د ثابت تودوخې د مجموعې قانون بیان او تشریح کړئ. وښیئ چې دا د ترموډینامیکس د لومړي قانون مستقیمه پایله ده.
sol Hess's law: the heat change of a reaction is the same whether it takes place in one step or in several steps. It is a consequence of the first law: since energy is conserved and ΔH is a state function, if two paths between the same reactants and products had different ΔH, energy could be created or destroyed by going around the cycle, which is impossible. So ΔH(one step) = ΔH₁ + ΔH₂ + ΔH₃ ... Example: C + O₂ → CO₂ (−393.5) equals C + ½O₂ → CO (−110.5) plus CO + ½O₂ → CO₂ (−283). ## Hess's law.
q 2 | How does Born Haber cycle help in calculating lattice energy? || بورن ہیبر چکر جالی توانائی کا حساب لگانے میں کیسے مدد دیتا ہے؟ || بورن-هابر دوره د جالۍ د انرژۍ په محاسبه کې څنګه مرسته کوي؟
sol In the Born-Haber cycle, the formation of an ionic solid from its elements is split into steps: sublimation of the metal, ionization of the metal atom, dissociation of the non-metal molecule into atoms, electron affinity of the non-metal atom and the lattice energy. By Hess's law, ΔH°f = ΔH(sub) + IE + ½ΔH(diss) + EA + U. Knowing all the other quantities, the lattice energy U can be calculated. ## Born-Haber cycle (e.g. NaCl).
q 3 | What is meant by heat of a reaction? Explain how the heat of a reaction at constant volume differs from the heat of a reaction at constant pressure? || تعامل کی حرارت سے کیا مراد ہے؟ وضاحت کریں کہ مستقل حجم پر تعامل کی حرارت مستقل دباؤ پر تعامل کی حرارت سے کیسے مختلف ہے؟ || د تعامل له تودوخې څخه څه مراد دی؟ تشریح کړئ چې په ثابت حجم کې د تعامل تودوخه په ثابت فشار کې د تعامل له تودوخې څخه څنګه توپیر لري؟
sol The heat of a reaction is the amount of heat absorbed or evolved when the reaction takes place under given conditions. At constant volume the heat change equals the change in internal energy (q_v = ΔE), because no work is done; at constant pressure it equals the enthalpy change (q_p = ΔH = ΔE + PΔV), which includes the work of expansion. The two are different when the number of moles of gas changes (ΔH = ΔE + ΔnRT). ## Heat of reaction.
q 4 | What is meant by pressure volume work? How it is related to the first law of thermodynamics? || دباؤ حجم کام سے کیا مراد ہے؟ اس کا تھرموڈائنامکس کے پہلے قانون سے کیا تعلق ہے؟ || د فشار حجم کار څخه څه مراد دی؟ دا د ترموډینامیکس له لومړي قانون سره څنګه اړوند دی؟
sol Pressure-volume work is the work done when a gas expands or is compressed against an external pressure: w = −P ΔV. By the first law ΔE = q + w = q − PΔV, so at constant pressure q_p = ΔE + PΔV = ΔH. ## PV work.

q 1 | Apply Hess's law to calculate ΔH° for the sublimation of one mole of iodine from the following equations. (i) H₂(g) + I₂(s) → 2HI(g) ΔH° = 51.8 kJ mol⁻¹ (ii) H₂(g) + I₂(g) → 2HI(g) ΔH° = −10.5 kJ mol⁻¹ (Ans. 62.3 kJ mol⁻¹) || درج ذیل مساواتوں سے آیوڈین کے ایک مول کی تصعید کے لیے ΔH° معلوم کرنے کے لیے ہیس کا قانون لاگو کریں۔ (i) H₂(g) + I₂(s) → 2HI(g) ΔH° = 51.8 kJ mol⁻¹ (ii) H₂(g) + I₂(g) → 2HI(g) ΔH° = −10.5 kJ mol⁻¹ (جواب: 62.3 kJ mol⁻¹) || د لاندې معادلو څخه د آیوډین د یو مول د تصعید لپاره ΔH° د محاسبې لپاره د هېس قانون تطبیق کړئ. (i) H₂(g) + I₂(s) → 2HI(g) ΔH° = 51.8 kJ mol⁻¹ (ii) H₂(g) + I₂(g) → 2HI(g) ΔH° = −10.5 kJ mol⁻¹ (ځواب: 62.3 kJ mol⁻¹)
sol Sublimation: I₂(s) → I₂(g). Subtract equation (ii) from (i): ΔH° = 51.8 − (−10.5) = 62.3 kJ mol⁻¹. ## Answer 62.3 kJ mol⁻¹.
q 2 | Calculate heat of formation of an aqueous solution of NH₄Cl from the following data. (i) NH₃(g) + aq → NH₃(aq) ΔH° = −35.16 kJ mol⁻¹ (ii) HCl(g) + aq → HCl(aq) ΔH° = −72.41 kJ mol⁻¹ (iii) NH₃(aq) + HCl(aq) → NH₄Cl(aq) ΔH° = −51.48 kJ mol⁻¹ (Ans. −159.08 kJ mol⁻¹) || درج ذیل اعداد و شمار سے NH₄Cl کے آبی محلول کی تشکیل کی حرارت معلوم کریں۔ (i) NH₃(g) + aq → NH₃(aq) ΔH° = −35.16 kJ mol⁻¹ (ii) HCl(g) + aq → HCl(aq) ΔH° = −72.41 kJ mol⁻¹ (iii) NH₃(aq) + HCl(aq) → NH₄Cl(aq) ΔH° = −51.48 kJ mol⁻¹ (جواب: −159.08 kJ mol⁻¹) || د لاندې معلوماتو څخه د NH₄Cl د اوبیز محلول د جوړېدو تودوخه محاسبه کړئ. (i) NH₃(g) + aq → NH₃(aq) ΔH° = −35.16 kJ mol⁻¹ (ii) HCl(g) + aq → HCl(aq) ΔH° = −72.41 kJ mol⁻¹ (iii) NH₃(aq) + HCl(aq) → NH₄Cl(aq) ΔH° = −51.48 kJ mol⁻¹ (ځواب: −159.08 kJ mol⁻¹)
sol Adding equations (i), (ii) and (iii): NH₃(g) + HCl(g) + aq → NH₄Cl(aq), ΔH = −35.16 − 72.41 − 51.48 = −159.05 kJ mol⁻¹ (about −159.08 as printed in the book; the small difference is rounding). ## Hess's law.
