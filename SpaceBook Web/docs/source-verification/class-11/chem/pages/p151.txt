## Descriptive Questions || تفصیلی سوالات || تشریحي پوښتنې
q 1 | (a) Explain the concept of diffusion and effusion of gases. || (a) گیسوں کے پھیلاؤ اور باریک سوراخ سے اخراج کے تصورات کی وضاحت کریں۔ || (a) د ګازونو د خپرېدو او له کوچني سوري څخه د وتلو مفهوم تشریح کړئ.
sol Diffusion is the spontaneous mixing of gases (or spreading from a high to a low concentration) due to the random motion of molecules, e.g. perfume spreading in a room. Effusion is the escape of a gas through a very small hole into a vacuum or a low pressure region, e.g. air leaking out of a punctured tyre. ## Definitions.
q  | (b) Apply the knowledge of the kinetic theory of gases and derive an expression of Graham’s law of diffusion. || (b) گیسوں کے حرکی نظریے کے علم کو استعمال کرتے ہوئے گراہم کے قانونِ پھیلاؤ کی مساوات اخذ کریں۔ || (b) د ګازونو د کینټیک تیورۍ پوهه وکاروئ او د ګراهام د خپرېدو د قانون یوه رابطه ترلاسه کړئ.
sol From the kinetic theory, KE = ½mv² is the same for all gases at the same temperature, so for two gases ½m₁v₁² = ½m₂v₂² and v₁/v₂ = √(m₂/m₁). The rate of diffusion is proportional to the average speed, therefore r₁/r₂ = √(M₂/M₁) = √(d₂/d₁): the rate of diffusion of a gas is inversely proportional to the square root of its density (molar mass), which is Graham's law. ## Derivation.

q 2 | (a) State Dalton’s law of partial pressure. Write down its mathematical form. || (a) ڈالٹن کا قانونِ جزوی دباؤ بیان کریں۔ اس کی ریاضیاتی صورت لکھیں۔ || (a) د ډالټن د جزوي فشار قانون بیان کړئ. ریاضيکي بڼه یې ولیکئ.
sol Dalton's law of partial pressures: the total pressure of a mixture of non-reacting gases is equal to the sum of the partial pressures of the individual gases. Mathematically P_total = P₁ + P₂ + P₃ + ..., where each partial pressure is the pressure the gas would exert if it alone occupied the container. ## Dalton's law.
q  | (b) Explain the significance of absolute zero, giving its value in degree Celsius and Kelvin. || (b) مطلق صفر کی اہمیت بیان کریں اور اس کی قدر ڈگری سیلسیس اور کیلون میں دیں۔ || (b) د مطلق صفر اهمیت تشریح کړئ او ارزښت یې په سیلسیس درجو او کیلوین کې ورکړئ.
sol Absolute zero is the lowest possible temperature, at which the volume (and pressure) of an ideal gas would become zero and molecular motion stops: 0 K = −273.15 °C. It is the zero of the Kelvin scale, which cannot be reached in practice; it follows from Charles' law because the volume of a gas decreases by 1/273 of its volume at 0 °C for each degree fall. ## Kelvin scale.

q 3 | (a) How would you relate temperature to the average kinetic energy of the particles in a substance? || (a) آپ کسی مادے کے ذرات کی اوسط حرکی توانائی اور درجہ حرارت کے درمیان تعلق کیسے بیان کریں گے؟ || (a) تاسو به تودوخه په یوه ماده کې د ذرو له منځنۍ حرکي انرژۍ سره څنګه وتړئ؟
sol The average kinetic energy of the particles of a substance is directly proportional to its absolute temperature (KE ∝ T): the higher the temperature, the faster the particles move. At the same temperature all substances have the same average kinetic energy. ## Kinetic molecular theory.
q  | (b) Explain Linde’s method for the liquefaction of gases. || (b) گیسوں کو مائع بنانے کے لیے لنڈے کے طریقے کی وضاحت کریں۔ || (b) د ګازونو د مایع کولو لپاره د لینډې طریقه تشریح کړئ.
sol Linde's method: the gas is compressed to a high pressure (about 200 atm), cooled by circulating water to remove the heat of compression, and passed through a spiral pipe to a jet where it expands to low pressure; the expansion cools the gas (Joule-Thomson effect). The cooled gas returns to cool the incoming compressed gas, so the cooling continues until the temperature drops below the critical temperature and the gas liquefies. ## Liquefaction of gases.
q c | Give two points of evidence to show that gases do not behave ideally under all conditions of temperature and pressure. || دو شواہد دیں جو ظاہر کریں کہ گیسیں درجہ حرارت اور دباؤ کی تمام حالتوں میں مثالی برتاؤ نہیں کرتی ہیں۔ || د شواهدو دوه ټکي وړاندې کړئ چې وښيي ګازونه د تودوخې او فشار په ټولو شرایطو کې مثالي چلند نه کوي.
sol (1) At high pressure and low temperature the gas deviates in its volume: the PV/nRT ratio is not 1, but greater than 1 at high pressure and less than 1 at moderate pressure. (2) Gases liquefy when cooled and compressed, which is impossible for an ideal gas, showing the molecules have attractions and finite volume. ## Evidence of non-ideal behaviour.
q 4 | (a) Write the Van der Waals equation for a real gas. Clearly explain the meaning of the corrective terms for pressure and volume. || (a) حقیقی گیس کے لیے وان ڈر والز مساوات لکھیں۔ دباؤ اور حجم کی تصحیحی حدوں کا مطلب واضح طور پر بیان کریں۔ || (a) د یوه حقیقي ګاز لپاره د وان در والس معادله ولیکئ. د فشار او حجم د اصلاحي حدونو مانا په څرګنده تشریح کړئ.
sol Van der Waals equation for n moles: (P + an²/V²)(V − nb) = nRT. The term an²/V² is the pressure correction for the attraction between molecules, which reduces the pressure on the walls (a is a constant for the attraction); the term nb is the volume correction for the finite volume of the molecules, so the free volume is V − nb (b is the excluded volume). ## Real gases.
q  | (b) The temperature of a real gas usually drops when it is allowed to enter into a low pressure (vacuum). Explain. || (b) حقیقی گیس کو کم دباؤ (خلا) میں داخل ہونے دیا جائے تو عموماً اس کا درجہ حرارت گر جاتا ہے۔ وضاحت کریں۔ || (b) کله چې یوه حقیقي ګاز ته ټیټ فشار (خلا) ته د ننوتلو اجازه ورکړل شي، عموماً یې تودوخه ټیټېږي. تشریح یې کړئ.
sol When a real gas enters a vacuum, the molecules move apart and must overcome the attractions between them (a large increase of the average distance). The work done against these forces is taken from the kinetic energy of the molecules, so the temperature decreases. ## Joule-Thomson effect.
q c | Define and describe the properties of plasma. || پلازما کی تعریف کریں اور اس کی خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ || پلازما تعریف کړئ او ځانګړنې یې بیان کړئ.
sol Plasma is the fourth state of matter: a hot, ionised gas consisting of free electrons, positive ions and neutral particles, electrically neutral as a whole. Properties: it conducts electricity and is affected by magnetic fields; it emits light; it is found at very high temperatures (the sun and stars, lightning, neon signs, fluorescent tubes); it has no fixed shape or volume. ## State of matter.
