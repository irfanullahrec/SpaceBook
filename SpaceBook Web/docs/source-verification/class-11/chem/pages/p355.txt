q 1 | Define calorimetry and how to measure enthalpy change with the help of calorimeter. || کیلوری میٹری کی تعریف کریں اور کیلوری میٹر کی مدد سے انتھالپی تبدیلی کیسے ناپی جاتی ہے۔ || کیلوري میټري تعریف کړئ او د کیلوري میټر په مرسته د انتالپي بدلون څنګه اندازه کېږي.
sol Calorimetry is the measurement of the heat absorbed or released in a chemical or physical change. In a calorimeter, the reaction is carried out in an insulated vessel containing water; from the rise in temperature the heat is calculated by q = m × c × ΔT (or C × ΔT), and ΔH is q per mole; a bomb calorimeter measures ΔE at constant volume. ## Calorimetry.
q 2 | State the Hess's law. || ہیس کا قانون بیان کریں۔ || د هېس قانون بیان کړئ.
sol Hess's law of constant heat summation: the total enthalpy change of a reaction is the same whether the reaction takes place in one step or in several steps, since it depends only on the initial and the final states. ## Definition.
q 3 | On which principle Born Harber cycle is based? || بورن ہیبر چکر کس اصول پر مبنی ہے؟ || بورن-هابر دوره په کوم اصل ولاړه ده؟
sol The Born-Haber cycle is based on Hess's law of constant heat summation: the enthalpy of formation of an ionic compound equals the sum of the enthalpy changes of the individual steps (sublimation, ionization, dissociation, electron affinity and lattice energy), because enthalpy is a state function. ## Principle.
