## ii. Effect of Concentration on Rate of the Reaction || ii. تعامل کی شرح پر ارتکاز کا اثر || ii. د تعامل په چټکتیا د غلظت اغېز
p Reactions occurring in gaseous mixtures or in solutions are said to be homogeneous if they occur only in one phase.
