th | Property | Amorphous Solids | Crystalline Solids
tr | Shape | They do not have definite geometrical shape. | They have characteristic geometrical shape.
tr | Melting point | Amorphous solids do not have particular melting point. They melt over a wide range of temperature. | They have sharp melting point.

exercise Self-Assessment
q 1 | Differentiate between amorphous solids and crystalline solids. || بے قلمی ٹھوس اور قلمی ٹھوس میں فرق کریں۔ || بې بڼې جامد او کرسټالي جامد توپیر کړئ.
sol Crystalline solids have a regular, repeating arrangement of particles (long-range order), a sharp melting point, a definite geometry with flat faces and are anisotropic (e.g. NaCl, diamond). Amorphous solids have a random arrangement (short-range order), melt gradually over a range of temperature, have no definite geometry, and are isotropic (e.g. glass, rubber, plastics). ## Comparison.
q 2 | Define symmetry. What are elements of symmetry? || تشاکل کی تعریف کریں۔ تشاکل کے عناصر کیا ہیں؟ || سیمټري تعریف کړئ. د سیمټري عناصر کوم دي؟
sol Symmetry is the similarity of the parts of a crystal on both sides of an imaginary line, plane or point. Elements of symmetry: plane of symmetry (divides the crystal into two mirror-image halves), axis of symmetry (rotation by 360°/n gives the same appearance), and centre of symmetry (a point such that a line through it meets equal faces at equal distance). ## Symmetry.
q 3 | Explain the terms polymorphism and allotropy. || کثیر شکلی اور ایلوٹروپی کی اصطلاحات کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د پولي مورفیزم او ایلوټروپي اصطلاحات تشریح کړئ.
sol Polymorphism is the ability of a substance to exist in more than one crystalline form (e.g. CaCO₃ as calcite and aragonite). Allotropy is the existence of an element in two or more different forms in the same physical state (e.g. carbon as diamond and graphite, sulphur as rhombic and monoclinic). Both are the same phenomenon: allotropy is polymorphism of an element. ## Polymorphism and allotropy.
q 4 | Differentiate between habit of crystal and crystal growth, isotropy and anisotropy. || قلم کی عادت اور قلم کی نشوونما، ہم سمتیت اور غیر ہم سمتیت میں فرق کریں۔ || د کرسټال عادت او د کرسټال وده، ایزوټروپي او انایزوټروپي توپیر کړئ.
sol Habit of a crystal is its external shape, which depends on the relative rate of growth of the faces; crystal growth is the process in which a crystal gets larger by the layer-wise addition of particles. Isotropy means that physical properties are the same in all directions (amorphous solids); anisotropy means that properties such as refractive index and electrical conductivity vary with direction (crystals). ## Crystal terms.
q 5 | Define allotropy. Use oxygen and sulphur as an example. || ایلوٹروپی کی تعریف کریں۔ آکسیجن اور سلفر کو بطور مثال استعمال کریں۔ || ایلوټروپي تعریف کړئ. اکسیجن او سلفر د بېلګې په توګه وکاروئ.
sol Allotropy is the existence of an element in two or more forms with different physical properties in the same physical state. Oxygen: dioxygen O₂ and ozone O₃. Sulphur: rhombic (α) and monoclinic (β) sulphur (stable below and above 95.5 °C), with the transition temperature 95.5 °C. ## Allotropy.
