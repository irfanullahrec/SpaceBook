sol A sigma (σ) bond is formed by the head-on (end-to-end) overlap of two atomic orbitals along the line joining the nuclei; it is strong and allows free rotation. A pi (π) bond is formed by the sideways (parallel) overlap of p orbitals above and below the bond axis; it is weaker and prevents rotation. ## Definitions.
