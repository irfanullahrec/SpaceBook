exercise 10.1.5. Solubility and Heat of Solution
p The change in solubility with temperature is closely related to the heat of[scan obscured] solution of the substance. The heat of solution can be determined by noting the[scan obscured] rise or fall of the temperature of the solution. If the temperature of the solution[scan obscured] increases, the heat is evolved and therefore, heat of solution is negative and if the[scan obscured] temperature decreases the heat of solution is positive. Thus, for silver nitrate the[scan obscured] heat of solution is positive. ## Self-Assessment || خود جائزہ || ځان ارزونه
q 1 | Differentiate between solution, suspension and colloids. || محلول، معلق اور کولائیڈز میں فرق کریں۔ || د محلول، معلق او کلوایډونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Solution: a homogeneous mixture with particles smaller than 1 nm (ions or molecules), transparent, does not scatter light, does not settle and cannot be separated by filtration (salt in water). Colloid: a heterogeneous-looking mixture with particles of 1 to 1000 nm; scatters light (Tyndall effect), does not settle, passes through ordinary filter paper but not through parchment (milk, fog, ink). Suspension: particles larger than 1000 nm, visible, settles on standing, scatters or blocks light and is filtered (muddy water, chalk in water). ## Comparison.
q 2 | Give example of each type of solutions of liquids in liquids. || مائعات کے مائعات میں محلولوں کی ہر قسم کی مثال دیں۔ || د مایعاتو په مایعاتو کې د محلولونو د هر ډول بېلګه ورکړئ.
sol Examples of solutions of liquids in liquids: completely miscible liquids (alcohol in water, benzene in toluene); partially miscible liquids (phenol in water, ether in water); immiscible liquids (oil and water, which do not form a solution). ## Types.
q 3 | Explain the effect of temperature and pressure on solubility. || حلالیت پر درجہ حرارت اور دباؤ کے اثر کی وضاحت کریں۔ || په حلالیت د تودوخې او فشار اغېز تشریح کړئ.
sol Effect of temperature: for most solids the solubility increases with temperature (endothermic dissolution), e.g. KNO₃; a few, such as Ce₂(SO₄)₃ and Ca(OH)₂, are less soluble when hot; the solubility of gases in liquids decreases when the temperature rises. Effect of pressure: it has almost no effect on solids and liquids, but the solubility of a gas in a liquid is directly proportional to the pressure (Henry's law), e.g. carbonated drinks. ## Solubility.
