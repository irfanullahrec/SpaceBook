q 1 | Define dynamic equilibrium between two physical states. || دو طبعی حالتوں کے درمیان حرکی توازن کی تعریف کریں۔ || د دوو فزیکي حالتونو ترمنځ متحرک تعادل تعریف کړئ.
sol A dynamic equilibrium between two physical states (e.g. liquid and vapour) is the state in which the rate of one change (evaporation) is equal to the rate of the opposite change (condensation), so no net change is seen though both processes continue. ## Definition.
q 2 | Define liquid crystals. || مائع قلموں کی تعریف کریں۔ || مایع کرسټالونه تعریف کړئ.
sol Liquid crystals are substances which show properties of both liquids and crystalline solids: they flow like a liquid but their molecules have an ordered arrangement in one or two dimensions, e.g. cholesteryl benzoate. ## Definition.
q 3 | Differentiate liquid crystals from pure liquids and crystalline solids. || مائع قلموں کو خالص مائعات اور قلمی ٹھوس سے ممتاز کریں۔ || مایع کرسټالونه له خالصو مایعاتو او کرسټالي جامدو څخه توپیر کړئ.
sol Liquid crystals flow like liquids but have molecules oriented in a definite direction (anisotropic), whereas pure liquids are isotropic with random molecular arrangement. Crystalline solids have a rigid three-dimensional order and sharp melting points; liquid crystals have partial order and turn cloudy at the first melting point and clear at the second (clearing point). ## Comparison.
q 4 | What is energetics of phase change? || حالت کی تبدیلی کی توانائیات کیا ہیں؟ || د فاز بدلون انرژیټیکس څه دی؟
sol The energetics of phase change is the study of the heat absorbed or released when a substance changes from one state to another: melting and vaporisation absorb heat (endothermic, heat of fusion and vaporisation), while freezing and condensation release it (exothermic). During the change the temperature stays constant because the energy is used to overcome the intermolecular forces. ## Definition.
q 5 | Briefly explain the uses of liquid crystals in daily life. || روزمرہ زندگی میں مائع قلموں کے استعمالات مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || په ورځني ژوند کې د مایع کرسټالونو کارونې لنډ تشریح کړئ.
sol Uses of liquid crystals: liquid crystal displays (LCD) in watches, calculators, mobile phones, computers and TVs; thermometers and temperature sensors (colour changes with temperature); medical diagnosis (detecting tumours by temperature); and detecting cracks and leakage in electronics. ## Applications.
