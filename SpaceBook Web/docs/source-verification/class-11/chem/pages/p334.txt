q 4 | In an experiment 6.4g of an organic compound was dissolved in 25.6g of benzene and 1.0 °C depression of freezing point was observed. Calculate the molecular weight of the substance. (k_f of benzene=5.12) Ans. 1280 g/mol || ایک تجربے میں 6.4g نامیاتی مرکب 25.6g بینزین میں حل کیا گیا اور نقطۂ انجماد میں 1.0 °C کمی دیکھی گئی۔ مادے کا سالماتی وزن معلوم کریں۔ (بینزین کا k_f=5.12) جواب: 1280 g/mol || په یوه تجربه کې 6.4g عضوي مرکب په 25.6g بنزین کې حل شو او د ځمبېدو د نقطې 1.0 °C ښکته کېدل ولیدل شول. د مادې مالیکولي وزن محاسبه کړئ. q 1 | How a given mixture can be differentiated into a true solution or coarse suspension. || کسی دیے گئے آمیزے کو حقیقی محلول یا موٹے معلق میں کیسے ممیز کیا جا سکتا ہے۔ || یو ورکړل شوی مخلوط څنګه په حقیقي محلول یا غټ معلق کې توپیر کېدای شي.
sol A true solution is clear, the particles cannot be seen, it does not settle, does not scatter a beam of light and passes through filter paper. A coarse suspension is cloudy, the particles are visible, they settle on standing, they are removed by filtration and may scatter light. (A colloid shows the Tyndall effect.) ## Tests of a mixture.
q 2 | Define solubility, what are the factors that affect the solubility of substances? || حلالیت کی تعریف کریں، وہ کون سے عوامل ہیں جو مادوں کی حلالیت کو متاثر کرتے ہیں؟ || حلالیت تعریف کړئ، هغه کوم فکتورونه دي چې د موادو په حلالیت اغېز کوي؟
sol Solubility is the maximum amount of a solute that dissolves in a given amount of solvent (usually 100 g) at a given temperature to form a saturated solution. Factors: (1) nature of the solute and solvent (like dissolves like); (2) temperature; (3) pressure (for gases, Henry's law); (4) the size of the particles and stirring, which affect the rate of dissolving; (5) presence of other (common) ions. ## Solubility.
q 3 | Classify colloids on the basis of their behaviour towards medium and the physical states of matter. || کولائیڈز کی درجہ بندی منتشر واسطے کی طرف ان کے رویے اور مادے کی طبعی حالتوں کی بنیاد پر کریں۔ || کلوایډونه د منځګړي په لور د هغوی د چلند او د مادې د فزیکي حالتونو په بنسټ طبقه بندي کړئ.
sol On the basis of behaviour towards the medium: lyophilic (solvent-loving, e.g. starch in water) and lyophobic colloids (e.g. gold sol). On the basis of the physical states: solid in liquid (sol, e.g. paint), liquid in liquid (emulsion, e.g. milk), gas in liquid (foam, e.g. whipped cream), liquid in gas (aerosol, e.g. fog, mist), solid in gas (smoke), liquid in solid (gel, e.g. butter, cheese), gas in solid (solid foam, e.g. pumice). ## Classification.
q 4 | Give the statement of Raoult's law. Explain the lowering of vapour pressure of a solution based on this law. || راؤلٹ کے قانون کا بیان دیں۔ اس قانون کی بنیاد پر محلول کے بخارات دباؤ میں کمی کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د راؤلټ د قانون بیان ورکړئ. د دې قانون پر بنسټ د محلول د بخار فشار کمښت تشریح کړئ.
sol Raoult's law: the lowering of the vapour pressure of a solvent by a non-volatile solute is directly proportional to the mole fraction of the solute: (P° − P)/P° = x(solute), where P° is the vapour pressure of the pure solvent and P of the solution. The solute particles occupy some places on the surface of the liquid and reduce the number of solvent molecules which escape, so the vapour pressure is lowered. ## Raoult's law.
q 5 | Explain the phenomenon of osmosis. || اسموسس کے مظہر کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د اسموسس پدیده تشریح کړئ.
sol Osmosis is the flow of solvent molecules through a semi-permeable membrane from a solution of lower concentration (or pure solvent) to a solution of higher concentration until equilibrium. The pressure that must be applied to stop the flow is the osmotic pressure π, given by π = MRT. Example: water enters a raisin or potato slice placed in water. ## Osmosis.
q 6 | Explain the phenomenon of depression of freezing point. How molecular mass of solute can be calculated on the basis of depression in freezing point? || نقطۂ انجماد میں کمی کے مظہر کی وضاحت کریں۔ نقطۂ انجماد میں کمی کی بنیاد پر محلول شدہ شے کی سالماتی کمیت کیسے معلوم کی جا سکتی ہے؟ || د ځمبېدو د نقطې د ښکته کېدو پدیده تشریح کړئ. د ځمبېدو د نقطې د ښکته کېدو پر بنسټ د محلول شوې مادې مالیکولي کتله څنګه محاسبه کېدای شي؟
sol When a non-volatile solute is dissolved in a solvent, the freezing point of the solution is lower than that of the pure solvent, because solute particles interfere with the formation of the solid. ΔT_f = K_f × m, where m is the molality and K_f the molal depression constant. The molar mass is M = (K_f × w₂ × 1000)/(ΔT_f × w₁), where w₂ is the mass of the solute and w₁ that of the solvent in g. ## Depression of freezing point.
q 7 | Explain the elevation of boiling point, how molecular mass of solute can be calculated on the basis of elevation of boiling point? || نقطۂ جوش میں اضافے کی وضاحت کریں، نقطۂ جوش میں اضافے کی بنیاد پر محلول شدہ شے کی سالماتی کمیت کیسے معلوم کی جا سکتی ہے؟ || د جوش د نقطې لوړېدل تشریح کړئ، د جوش د نقطې د لوړېدو پر بنسټ د محلول شوې مادې مالیکولي کتله څنګه محاسبه کېدای شي؟
sol The boiling point of a solution of a non-volatile solute is higher than that of the pure solvent because the vapour pressure is lowered, so a higher temperature is needed to equal the external pressure. ΔT_b = K_b × m. The molar mass is M = (K_b × w₂ × 1000)/(ΔT_b × w₁), where w₂ is the mass of the solute and w₁ that of the solvent in g. ## Elevation of boiling point.
