q 1 | The change in temperature changes the equilibrium position of the reaction N₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2NO(g) but change in pressure does not, why? || درجہ حرارت کی تبدیلی تعامل N₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2NO(g) کے توازن کا مقام بدلتی ہے لیکن دباؤ کی تبدیلی نہیں بدلتی، کیوں؟ || د تودوخې بدلون د N₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2NO(g) تعامل د تعادل موقعیت بدلوي خو د فشار بدلون نه بدلوي، ولې؟
sol In N₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2NO(g) the number of moles of gas is the same on both sides (Δn = 0), so a change of pressure does not shift the equilibrium. The reaction is endothermic (ΔH positive), so a change in temperature changes the position (and value of K_c), more NO is formed at high temperature. ## Le Chatelier's principle.
eq \mathrm{N_{2(g)}+O_{2(g)}\rightleftharpoons 2NO_{(g)}}
q 2 | Give the units of K_p for the following reversible reactions. || درج ذیل الٹنے والے تعاملات کے لیے K_p کی اکائیاں دیں۔ || د لاندې بېرته کېدونکو تعاملاتو لپاره د K_p واحدونه ورکړئ.
sol Unit of K_p = (atm)^Δn, where Δn = moles of gaseous products − moles of gaseous reactants; for example N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃: Δn = −2, K_p has the unit atm⁻²; if Δn = 0, K_p has no unit; if Δn = +1 (e.g. PCl₅ ⇌ PCl₃ + Cl₂), the unit is atm. ## The reactions are not legible in the scan, so the rule is given.
eq \text{a)}\ \mathrm{PCl_{5(g)}\rightleftharpoons PCl_{3(g)}+Cl_{2(g)}}
eq \text{b)}\ \mathrm{N_{2(g)}+3H_{2(g)}\rightleftharpoons 2NH_{3(g)}}
eq \text{c)}\ \mathrm{H_{2(g)}+CO_{2(g)}\rightleftharpoons CO_{(g)}+H_2O_{(g)}}
eq \text{d)}\ 4\mathrm{NH_{3(g)}}+5\mathrm{O_{2(g)}}\rightleftharpoons 4\mathrm{NO_{(g)}}+6\mathrm{H_2O_{(g)}}
q 3 | Why does the value of K_c fall with the rise in temperature for the synthesis of SO₃? || SO₃ کی تالیف کے لیے درجہ حرارت بڑھنے سے K_c کی قیمت کیوں گھٹ جاتی ہے؟ || د SO₃ د ترکیب لپاره د تودوخې په لوړېدو سره د K_c ارزښت ولې کمېږي؟
sol The synthesis of SO₃, 2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃, is exothermic. By Le Chatelier's principle, a rise in temperature shifts the equilibrium backward (the endothermic direction), so less SO₃ is present at equilibrium and the value of K_c = [SO₃]²/([SO₂]²[O₂]) falls. ## Effect of temperature on K_c.

q 4 | There is a dynamic rather than static equilibrium present between liquid[scan obscured] and vapour at constant temperature; explain. || مستقل درجہ حرارت پر مائع [scan obscured] اور بخارات کے درمیان ساکن کے بجائے حرکی توازن موجود ہوتا ہے؛ وضاحت کریں۔ || په ثابته تودوخه کې د مایع [scan obscured] او بخار ترمنځ د ساکن په ځای دیناميکي تعادل شتون لري؛ تشریح کړئ.
sol At constant temperature, liquid and vapour are in dynamic equilibrium: molecules continue to evaporate from the liquid surface and molecules of the vapour continue to condense, with equal rates. The two processes do not stop, so there is no net change, and the equilibrium is dynamic, not static. ## Dynamic equilibrium.
q 5 | The change in concentration of reactants does not change the value of[scan obscured] equilibrium constant permanently, elaborate. || تعامل کنندگان کے ارتکاز کی تبدیلی توازنی مستقلہ [scan obscured] کی قیمت کو مستقل طور پر نہیں بدلتی، وضاحت کریں۔ || د متعاملینو د غلظت بدلون د تعادل ثابت [scan obscured] ارزښت په دایمي ډول نه بدلوي، تفصیل ورکړئ.
sol A change in concentration only shifts the position of equilibrium: the system adjusts to the new concentrations, and when equilibrium is re-established the ratio of the concentrations (the equilibrium constant) has the same value, because K_c depends only on temperature. ## K_c depends only on the temperature.
q 6 | Discuss the equilibrium of a sparingly soluble salt. || کم حل پذیر نمک کے توازن پر بحث کریں۔ || د یوې لږ حل کېدونکې مالګې د تعادل په اړه بحث وکړئ.
sol A sparingly soluble salt in contact with its saturated solution is in equilibrium: AB(s) ⇌ A⁺(aq) + B⁻(aq). The rate of dissolution equals the rate of precipitation; the equilibrium constant is the solubility product K_sp = [A⁺][B⁻]. If the ionic product is less than K_sp the solution is unsaturated; if greater, the salt precipitates. ## Solubility equilibrium.
q 7 | Common ion effect operates best in the purification of certain substances[scan obscured], explain. || مشترک آئن اثر بعض مادوں کی تطہیر میں بہترین کام کرتا ہے [scan obscured]، وضاحت کریں۔ || ګډ آیون اغېز د ځینو موادو په پاکولو کې غوره کار کوي [scan obscured]، تشریح کړئ.
sol In purification (for example of NaCl or in qualitative analysis), a common ion lowers the solubility of a salt and so precipitates it: passing HCl gas into a saturated NaCl solution precipitates pure NaCl, and in the analysis groups, group II sulphides are precipitated in the presence of HCl. The common ion effect thus makes the precipitation more complete. ## Common ion effect.
q 8 | The ionization of calcium oxalate is suppressed by adding CaCl₂ to it, why? || کیلشیم آکسالیٹ کی آئنائزیشن CaCl₂ شامل کرنے سے دب جاتی ہے، کیوں؟ || د کلسیم اوکزالیټ آیونایزیشن د CaCl₂ په اضافه کولو سره ځپل کېږي، ولې؟
sol Calcium oxalate ionizes as CaC₂O₄ ⇌ Ca²⁺ + C₂O₄²⁻. Adding CaCl₂ increases the concentration of Ca²⁺ (common ion), so by Le Chatelier's principle the equilibrium shifts backward and the ionization (solubility) of calcium oxalate decreases. ## Common ion effect.
q 9 | The solubility of a sparingly soluble substance is calculated from the[scan obscured] solubility product data, explain. || کم حل پذیر مادے کی حلالیت حلالیت حاصل ضرب کے اعداد و شمار سے معلوم کی جاتی ہے [scan obscured]، وضاحت کریں۔ || د یوې لږ حل کېدونکې مادې حلالیت د حلالیت حاصل ضرب له معلوماتو څخه محاسبه کېږي [scan obscured]، تشریح کړئ.
sol For a salt such as AgCl, K_sp = s² (for a 1 : 1 salt, s = √K_sp), and for a 1 : 2 salt like CaF₂, K_sp = 4s³ (s = (K_sp/4)^1/3). Thus the molar solubility s of a sparingly soluble salt can be calculated from its K_sp. ## Solubility from the solubility product.
