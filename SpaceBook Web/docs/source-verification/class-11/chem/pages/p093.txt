## Self-Assessment || خود جانچ || ځان ارزونه
q 1 | Discuss the structure of BF₃, CH₄ and H₂O on the basis of VSEPR theory. || VSEPR نظریے کی بنیاد پر BF₃، CH₄ اور H₂O کی ساخت پر بحث کریں۔ || د VSEPR نظریې پر بنسټ د BF₃، CH₄ او H₂O جوړښت وڅېړئ.
sol BF₃: boron has three bond pairs and no lone pair, so the molecule is trigonal planar with 120° angles. CH₄: four bond pairs and no lone pairs, so it is tetrahedral with 109.5°. H₂O: two bond pairs and two lone pairs (four electron pairs), so the shape is bent (angular) with a bond angle of 104.5°, smaller than the tetrahedral angle because the lone pairs repel more strongly. ## VSEPR theory.
q 2 | Define resonance? What are the basic conditions for resonance? Discuss the resonance in ozone molecule. || ریزونینس کی تعریف کریں۔ ریزونینس کی بنیادی شرائط کیا ہیں؟ اوزون سالمے میں ریزونینس پر بحث کریں۔ || ریزونانس تعریف کړئ. د ریزونانس بنسټیز شرطونه کوم دي؟ د اوزون په مالیکول کې ریزونانس وڅېړئ.
sol Resonance is the phenomenon in which a molecule or ion can be represented by two or more Lewis structures that differ only in the positions of electrons, none of which fully describes the molecule; the true structure is a hybrid. Conditions: the contributing structures must have the same positions of atoms, the same number of paired and unpaired electrons, and similar energies. In ozone (O₃), the O=O–O and O–O=O structures are contributors; the two O–O bonds are equal in length (128 pm), intermediate between a single and a double bond. ## Resonance in O₃.
q 3 | Write down at least four postulates of the valence bond theory. || ویلنسی بندش نظریے کے کم از کم چار مسلمے لکھیں۔ || د ظرفیتي تړون نظریې لږ تر لږه څلور اصول ولیکئ.
sol Postulates of valence bond theory: (1) a covalent bond forms by the overlap of half-filled atomic orbitals of two atoms; (2) each overlapping orbital contains one electron, and the electrons have opposite spins; (3) the greater the overlap, the stronger the bond; (4) the bond lies in the direction of maximum overlap, which gives the molecule its shape; (5) the atoms of the bond share the pair of electrons. ## Valence bond theory (VBT).
q 4 | How sigma bond is different from pi-bond. Explain bonding in the molecules of HCl, Cl₂ and O₂. || سگما بندش، پائی بندش سے کس طرح مختلف ہے؟ HCl، Cl₂ اور O₂ سالمات میں بندش کی وضاحت کریں۔ || سیګما تړون له پای تړون څخه څنګه توپیر لري؟ په HCl، Cl₂ او O₂ مالیکولونو کې تړون تشریح کړئ.
sol A σ bond is formed by head-on overlap along the internuclear axis and is stronger, with free rotation; a π bond is formed by sideways overlap of p orbitals, is weaker and restricts rotation. HCl: the 1s orbital of H overlaps with the 3p orbital of Cl, giving one σ bond. Cl₂: two 3p orbitals overlap head-on, giving one σ bond. O₂: two 2p orbitals form a σ bond by head-on overlap and the other two (parallel) 2p orbitals form a π bond (double bond). ## Sigma and pi bonding.
q 5 | What is hybridization? Discuss the sp³, sp² and sp hybridization in different molecules in detail. || ہائبرڈائزیشن کیا ہے؟ مختلف سالمات میں sp³، sp² اور sp ہائبرڈائزیشن پر تفصیل سے بحث کریں۔ || هایبرډ کېدل څه دي؟ په بېلابېلو مالیکولونو کې sp³، sp² او sp هایبرډ کېدل په تفصیل وڅېړئ.
sol Hybridization is the mixing of atomic orbitals of the same atom of nearly equal energy to form an equal number of equivalent hybrid orbitals. sp³ (one s + three p): four equivalent orbitals at 109.5° (tetrahedral), e.g. CH₄. sp² (one s + two p): three orbitals at 120° (trigonal planar) with one unhybridized p orbital left for the π bond, e.g. BF₃, C₂H₄. sp (one s + one p): two orbitals at 180° (linear), two unhybridized p orbitals, e.g. BeCl₂, C₂H₂. ## Hybridization.
q 6 | What are the shortcomings of VBT? || VBT کی خامیاں کیا ہیں؟ || د VBT نیمګړتیاوې کومې دي؟
sol Shortcomings of VBT: it cannot explain the paramagnetism of oxygen; it does not explain the bonding in electron-deficient molecules; it considers the electron localised between two atoms (cannot explain delocalised bonding); it does not explain the energy of molecular orbitals, spectra or the colour of compounds. ## Limits of the valence bond theory.
