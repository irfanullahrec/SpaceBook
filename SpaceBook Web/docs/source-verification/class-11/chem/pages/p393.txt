## Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
q 1 | What is the oxidation number of N in N₂O, NO₂, N₂O₃, HNO₃, and NH₄⁺. || N₂O، NO₂، N₂O₃، HNO₃ اور NH₄⁺ میں N کا تکسیدی عدد کیا ہے۔ || په N₂O، NO₂، N₂O₃، HNO₃ او NH₄⁺ کې د N د اکسیډیشن شمېره څومره ده.
sol N₂O: +1; NO₂: +4; N₂O₃: +3; HNO₃: +5; NH₄⁺: −3. ## Using the sum of the oxidation numbers (zero for molecules, the charge for ions).
q 2 | Na⁺ is an oxidizing but Na is a reducing agent, why? || Na⁺ تکسیدی ہے لیکن Na تخفیفی ایجنٹ ہے، کیوں؟ || Na⁺ اکسیډېزنګ دی خو Na ریډیوسینګ اجنټ دی، ولې؟
sol Na⁺ has already lost its outer electron and can only gain an electron to become Na, so it acts as an oxidizing agent (it is reduced); sodium metal can easily lose its outer electron to become Na⁺, so it acts as a reducing agent (it is oxidised). ## Redox behaviour.
q 3 | The lead storage battery is rechargeable. Why? || سیسہ ذخیرہ بیٹری دوبارہ چارج ہو سکتی ہے۔ کیوں؟ || د سرب ذخیره کوونکې بیټرۍ بیا چارج کېدونکې ده. ولې؟
sol The lead storage battery is rechargeable because the products of discharge (PbSO₄) stay stuck on the electrodes, and by passing a current in the reverse direction (charging) the reaction is reversed: PbSO₄ is changed back into Pb, PbO₂ and H₂SO₄. ## Secondary cell.
q 4 | Why a solution of sugar is non-conductor but that of table salt is a good conductor of electricity? || چینی کا محلول غیر موصل کیوں ہے جبکہ کھانے کے نمک کا محلول بجلی کا اچھا موصل ہے؟ || د بوره محلول غیرهادي ولې دی پداسې حال کې چې د خوړو مالګې محلول د برېښنا ښه هادي دی؟
sol Sugar is a covalent compound which dissolves in water as neutral molecules and no ions are produced, so it does not conduct electricity. Table salt (NaCl) is ionic and gives mobile Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions in water that carry the current. ## Electrolytes and non-electrolytes.
q 5 | NaCl is non-conductor in the solid state but is a good conductor in molten form. Why? || NaCl ٹھوس حالت میں غیر موصل ہے لیکن پگھلی حالت میں اچھا موصل ہے۔ کیوں؟ || NaCl په جامد حالت کې غیرهادي دی خو په ویلې شوې بڼه کې ښه هادي دی. ولې؟
sol In solid NaCl the ions are held in fixed positions in the crystal lattice and cannot move, so it does not conduct. In the molten state the lattice is broken and the ions are free to move and carry the current. ## Conduction by ions.
q 6 | During electrolysis of fused NaCl, sodium metal is collected at cathode and not at anode, why? || پگھلے ہوئے NaCl کی برق پاشی کے دوران سوڈیم دھات کیتھوڈ پر جمع ہوتی ہے نہ کہ اینوڈ پر، کیوں؟ || د ویلې شوي NaCl د الکترولیز پر مهال سوډیم فلز په کیتوډ کې راټولېږي نه په انوډ کې، ولې؟
sol Na⁺ ions are positive and are attracted to the negatively charged cathode, where they gain electrons (Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na); the negative Cl⁻ ions move to the anode (2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻). So sodium is collected at the cathode. ## Electrolysis of fused NaCl.
q 7 | SHE acts as anode with Cu electrode but acts as cathode when connected to Zn electrode. || SHE، Cu الیکٹروڈ کے ساتھ اینوڈ کا کام کرتا ہے لیکن Zn الیکٹروڈ سے جوڑنے پر کیتھوڈ کا کام کرتا ہے۔ || SHE له Cu الکترود سره د انوډ په توګه عمل کوي خو کله چې له Zn الکترود سره وصل شي د کیتوډ په توګه عمل کوي.
sol The standard hydrogen electrode has a reduction potential of zero. Cu has a higher reduction potential (+0.34 V) than hydrogen, so Cu is the cathode and SHE is the anode (hydrogen is oxidised). Zn has a lower reduction potential (−0.76 V) than hydrogen, so Zn is the anode and SHE is the cathode (H⁺ is reduced). ## Electrode potentials.
q 8 | In electroplating, the article to be plated is made cathode, why? || برق ملمع کاری میں جس چیز پر ملمع کرنا ہو اسے کیتھوڈ بنایا جاتا ہے، کیوں؟ || په برېښنایي پوښښ کې هغه شی چې پوښل کېږي کیتوډ جوړېږي، ولې؟
sol In electroplating the object to be plated is made the cathode so that the metal ions of the electrolyte (e.g. Ag⁺) gain electrons there (reduction) and are deposited as a layer of metal on its surface. ## Electroplating.
