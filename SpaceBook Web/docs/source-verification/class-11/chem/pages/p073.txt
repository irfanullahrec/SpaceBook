## Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
q 1 | Name any four properties of cathode rays. || کیتھوڈ شعاعوں کی کوئی چار خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ || د کاتوډ وړانګو هرې څلور ځانګړنې بیان کړئ.
sol Four properties of cathode rays: (1) they travel in straight lines, from the cathode; (2) they carry negative charge, being deflected towards the positive plate in electric and magnetic fields; (3) they produce fluorescence and heat and produce X-rays when they strike a metal; (4) they are deflected by magnetic fields and have momentum (they turn a small paddle wheel), and their e/m (1.76 × 10¹¹ C/kg) is the same for every gas. ## Cathode ray properties.
q 2 | How will you differentiate between a continuous and a line spectrum? || مسلسل اور خطی طیف میں کیسے فرق کریں گے؟ || د پرله پسې او خطي طیف ترمنځ څنګه توپیر کوئ؟
sol A continuous spectrum contains all the wavelengths (colours) of a range merging into each other without gaps, e.g. white light or the light of a heated solid. A line spectrum consists of separate bright lines of definite wavelengths on a dark background, produced by excited atoms, and is characteristic of the element (e.g. hydrogen). ## Difference between spectra.
q 3 | How did Moseley discover that the atomic number (Z) is the fundamental property of an element? || موزلے نے کیسے دریافت کیا کہ ایٹمی نمبر (Z) عنصر کی بنیادی خصوصیت ہے؟ || موزلي څنګه کشف کړه چې اټومي شمېره (Z) د عنصر اساسي ځانګړنه ده؟
sol Moseley measured the frequencies of the characteristic X-rays of many elements and found that the square root of the frequency varied regularly with a whole-number: the position of the element in the periodic table (atomic number Z), not atomic mass. A graph of √ν against Z for the K-lines was a straight line, so Z (nuclear charge) is the fundamental property. ## Moseley's discovery.
q 4 | What are the shortcomings of Bohr’s atomic model? || بوہر کے ایٹمی ماڈل کی کمزوریاں کیا ہیں؟ || د بوهر د اټومي ماډل نیمګړتیاوې څه دي؟
sol Shortcomings of Bohr's model: it explains only the hydrogen-like (one-electron) species; cannot explain the spectra of multi-electron atoms, the fine structure and the Zeeman and Stark effects; it treats the electron as a particle on a fixed orbit, disregarding the wave nature of the electron and Heisenberg's uncertainty principle; and it gives no explanation of chemical bonding. ## Limits of Bohr's model.
q 5 | Can you give the reason that 4s orbital are written first than the 3d orbital. || کیا آپ وجہ بتا سکتے ہیں کہ 4s اوربیٹل 3d اوربیٹل سے پہلے کیوں لکھا جاتا ہے۔ || ایا دلیل بیانولای شئ چې 4s اوربیټال ولې له 3d اوربیټال څخه مخکې لیکل کېږي.
sol The 4s orbital is filled before 3d because its energy is lower: by the (n + ℓ) rule, 4s has n + ℓ = 4 + 0 = 4 while 3d has 3 + 2 = 5; the 4s orbital penetrates closer to the nucleus and so is more stable at the point of filling. ## Aufbau (n + ℓ) rule.

## Descriptive Questions || تفصیلی سوالات || تشریحي پوښتنې
q 1 | (a) What is Planck’s quantum theory? What are the postulates of this theory? || (a) پلانک کا کوانٹم نظریہ کیا ہے؟ اس کے مفروضات کیا ہیں؟ || (a) د پلانک کوانتمي نظریه څه ده؟ د دې نظریې فرضیې څه دي؟
sol (a) Planck's quantum theory: a body emits or absorbs energy discontinuously in small packets called quanta (photons). Postulates: (1) energy is emitted or absorbed in whole-number multiples of a quantum; (2) the energy of one quantum is proportional to the frequency of the radiation, E = hν (h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J s); (3) a body emits or absorbs energy as nhν where n = 1, 2, 3 ... ## Planck.
q  | (b) Prove that E = hcν̄, where E = energy, h= Planck’s constant, c = velocity of light, and ν̄ = wave number. || (b) ثابت کریں کہ E = hcν̄، جہاں E = توانائی، h= پلانک کا مستقل، c = روشنی کی رفتار اور ν̄ = عدد موج ہے۔ || (b) ثابته کړئ چې E = hcν̄، دلته E = انرژي، h= د پلانک ثابت، c = د رڼا سرعت او ν̄ = د څپو شمېر دی.
sol Since frequency ν = c/λ, E = hν = hc/λ. The wave number is ν̄ = 1/λ, so E = hcν̄. ## Proof.
q c | What will be energy of a radiation with λ=2×10⁻⁸m? || λ=2×10⁻⁸m والی تابکاری کی توانائی کیا ہوگی؟ || د λ=2×10⁻⁸m لرونکو وړانګو انرژي به څه وي؟
sol Ans.: 9.939 ×10⁻¹⁸J ## Ans.: 9.939 ×10⁻¹⁸J || جواب: 9.939 ×10⁻¹⁸J || ځواب: 9.939 ×10⁻¹⁸J
q 2 | (a) Write down the main postulates of Bohr’s atomic model. || (a) بوہر کے ایٹمی ماڈل کے بنیادی مفروضات لکھیں۔ || (a) د بوهر د اټومي ماډل اساسي فرضیې ولیکئ.
sol Postulates of Bohr's model: (1) an electron revolves around the nucleus in definite circular orbits (stationary states) without radiating energy; (2) an electron can move only in orbits where its angular momentum is an integral multiple of h/2π, mvr = nh/2π; (3) energy is emitted or absorbed only when the electron jumps from one orbit to another, ΔE = E₂ − E₁ = hν. ## Bohr's postulates.
q  | (b) How can Bohr’s model of atom be applied to hydrogen atom to calculate the radius of nᵗʰ shell? || (b) nویں خول کا نصف قطر نکالنے کے لیے بوہر کا ایٹمی ماڈل ہائیڈروجن ایٹم پر کیسے لاگو کیا جا سکتا ہے؟ || (b) د nم پوښ د شعاع د محاسبې لپاره د بوهر اټومي ماډل د هایدروجن پر اټوم څنګه پلي کېدای شي؟
sol Ans: ν̄=1.028×10⁷m⁻¹, λ=9.72×10⁻⁸m ## Ans: ν̄=1.028×10⁷m⁻¹, λ=9.72×10⁻⁸m || جواب: ν̄=1.028×10⁷m⁻¹, λ=9.72×10⁻⁸m || ځواب: ν̄=1.028×10⁷m⁻¹, λ=9.72×10⁻⁸m
q 3 | (a) Derive expression using Bohr’s model, for the energy difference (ΔE), frequency (ν) and wave number (ν̄) in hydrogen atom. || (a) بوہر ماڈل استعمال کرتے ہوئے ہائیڈروجن ایٹم میں توانائی کے فرق (ΔE)، تعدد (ν) اور عدد موج (ν̄) کے لیے عبارت اخذ کریں۔ || (a) د بوهر د ماډل په کارولو په هایدروجن اټوم کې د انرژۍ د توپیر (ΔE)، فریکونسۍ (ν) او د څپو شمېر (ν̄) لپاره عبارت راوباسئ.
sol With Eₙ = −2π²me⁴k²/n²h², ΔE = E₂ − E₁ = 2π²me⁴k²/h² (1/n₁² − 1/n₂²). Frequency ν = ΔE/h = (2π²me⁴k²/h³)(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²). Wave number ν̄ = ν/c = R_H (1/n₁² − 1/n₂²), where R_H = 1.097 × 10⁷ m⁻¹ (Rydberg constant). ## Derivation from Bohr's energy levels.
q  | (b) How does Bohr’s model explain the hydrogen spectrum? || (b) بوہر ماڈل ہائیڈروجن کے طیف کی وضاحت کیسے کرتا ہے؟ || (b) د بوهر ماډل د هایدروجن طیف څنګه تشریح کوي؟
sol When the hydrogen gas is excited, electrons jump to higher orbits; when they fall back to lower orbits they emit radiation of energy ΔE = hν equal to the difference between the two orbits. Because the orbits have fixed energies, only certain frequencies appear, giving the line spectrum, and the different series (Lyman, Balmer, Paschen, Brackett, Pfund) correspond to drops to n = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. ## Bohr's explanation of the hydrogen spectrum.
q 4 | (a) What are X-rays? How are these rays produced? || (a) ایکس شعاعیں کیا ہیں؟ یہ کیسے پیدا ہوتی ہیں؟ || (a) ایکس وړانګې څه دي؟ دا څنګه تولیدېږي؟
sol (a) X-rays are high-energy electromagnetic radiations of very short wavelength. They are produced in an X-ray tube when fast moving electrons (cathode rays) strike a heavy metal target (anode, e.g. tungsten, copper or molybdenum) at a very high voltage: some energy is converted into X-rays. ## Röntgen.
q  | (b) Enlist some characteristics of X – rays. || (b) ایکس شعاعوں کی کچھ خصوصیات درج کریں۔ || (b) د ایکس وړانګو ځینې ځانګړنې ولیکئ.
sol Characteristics of X-rays: (1) they travel in straight lines at the speed of light; (2) they are not deflected by electric or magnetic fields (no charge); (3) they have high penetrating power; (4) they affect photographic plates and cause fluorescence; (5) they ionise gases; (6) they harm living tissue. ## Properties of X-rays.
