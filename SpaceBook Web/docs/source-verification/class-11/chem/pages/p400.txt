## GLOSSARY || فرہنگ || وييپانګه
table فرہنگ || Glossary || وييپانګه
th | Term | Definition
tr | Dipole | A molecule or a part of a molecule that contains both positively and negatively charged regions
tr | Discontinuous Spectrum or Line Spectrum | The spectrum consists of lines with dark or bright spaces between them.
tr | Ductile | The property of a metal which enables it to be drawn out into a wire.
tr | Effusion | The process by which gas molecules escapes from one compartment of a container to another by passing through a small opening without collision
tr | Electrical potential | An electrode in contact with a solution of its ions exhibits a potential.
tr | Electrochemical series | A series in which the elements are arranged in decreasing value of their standard oxidation potential. Thus higher the metal in the series the greater is its tendency to be oxidized.
tr | Electrochemistry | The branch of chemistry that is the study of the inter-conversion of chemical energy into electrical energy and vice versa.
tr | Electrodes | These are metallic plates, wires or rods through which the current enters or leaves the electrolyte in a cell.
tr | Electrolysis | A process in which a chemical reaction is caused by the passage of an electric current.
tr | Electrolytic cell | It is a device in which a non-spontaneous chemical reaction is carried out by passing electric current.
tr | Electrostatic forces of attraction | A strong force of attraction between unlike charges.
tr | Endothermic reaction | A chemical reaction in which heat is absorbed from the environment. ΔH value is positive for endothermic reactions.
tr | Enthalpy | The sum of the internal energy of a system plus the product of the system's volume multiplied by the pressure that the system exerts on its surroundings
tr | Enthalpy of combustion | The enthalpy change which takes place when one mole of a substance is completely burned in oxygen.
tr | Equilibrium constant, Kc | It is defined as the ratio of the product of the molar concentration of the products to that of the reaction.
endtable
