sol Take a buffer made of a weak acid HA and its salt A⁻. On adding a small amount of acid (H⁺): A⁻ + H⁺ → HA, so H⁺ is used up and the pH changes only slightly. On adding a small amount of base (OH⁻): HA + OH⁻ → A⁻ + H₂O, so OH⁻ is removed. As the buffer contains a large reserve of both HA and A⁻ the ratio [A⁻]/[HA] hardly changes, so the pH remains almost constant. ## Henderson equation: pH = pK_a + log([salt]/[acid]).
q 3 | (a) Briefly describe the leveling effect. || (الف) برابری کے اثر کو مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || (الف) د برابرولو اغېز په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ.
sol Leveling effect: in a given solvent all acids stronger than the solvent's conjugate acid appear to have the same strength. In water, HCl, HBr and HNO₃ are completely ionized and give H₃O⁺, so their strength is levelled to that of H₃O⁺. ## Leveling effect.
q  | (b) What is the relationship between K_a and K_b? || (ب) K_a اور K_b کے درمیان کیا تعلق ہے؟ || (ب) د K_a او K_b ترمنځ اړیکه څه ده؟
sol K_a × K_b = K_w for a conjugate pair; at 25 °C K_a × K_b = 10⁻¹⁴, so a strong acid has a weak conjugate base. ## Relationship.

sol For NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻: K_b = [NH₄⁺][OH⁻]/[NH₃]. For NH₄⁺ + H₂O ⇌ NH₃ + H₃O⁺: K_a = [NH₃][H₃O⁺]/[NH₄⁺]. Multiplying: K_a × K_b = [H₃O⁺][OH⁻] = K_w = 1 × 10⁻¹⁴. ## Relationship between K_a and K_b.
q 4 | (a) What is meant by the term amphoteric? Give an example of a substanc[scan obscured] or ion with amphoteric characteristics. || (الف) ایمفوٹیرک کی اصطلاح سے کیا مراد ہے؟ ایمفوٹیرک خصوصیات والے مادے [scan obscured] یا آئن کی مثال دیں۔ || (الف) د امفوټریک له اصطلاح څخه څه مراد دی؟ د امفوټریک ځانګړنو لرونکي مادې [scan obscured] یا آیون یوه بېلګه ورکړئ.
sol An amphoteric substance can act as an acid or a base. Example: water (H₂O + HCl → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻; H₂O + NH₃ ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻); bicarbonate ion HCO₃⁻ (donates H⁺ to form CO₃²⁻ or accepts H⁺ to form H₂CO₃); also Al(OH)₃ and ZnO. ## Amphoteric.
q  | (b) Define pH, pOH, pK_a and pK_b. || (ب) pH، pOH، pK_a اور pK_b کی تعریف کریں۔ || (ب) pH، pOH، pK_a او pK_b تعریف کړئ.
sol pH = −log[H⁺]; pOH = −log[OH⁻]; pK_a = −log K_a (of an acid); pK_b = −log K_b (of a base). At 25 °C, pH + pOH = 14. ## Definitions.
q c | Explain ionization constant of water and calculate pH and pOH in aqueous[scan obscured] medium using given K_w values. || (ج) پانی کے آئنائزیشن مستقلہ کی وضاحت کریں اور دی گئی K_w قیمتیں استعمال کر کے آبی [scan obscured] واسطے میں pH اور pOH معلوم کریں۔ || (ج) د اوبو د آیونایزیشن ثابت تشریح کړئ او د ورکړل شویو K_w ارزښتونو په کارولو سره په اوبیز [scan obscured] منځګړي کې pH او pOH محاسبه کړئ.
sol Water ionizes slightly: 2H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + OH⁻, with K_w = [H₃O⁺][OH⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴ at 25 °C. In pure water [H⁺] = [OH⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻⁷ M, so pH = 7 and pOH = 7 (neutral). For other K_w (e.g. 5.5 × 10⁻¹⁴ at 50 °C), [H⁺] = √K_w and pH = ½ pK_w. ## Ion product of water.
q 5 | (a) Define salt hydrolysis. Categorize salts according to how they affect the pH[scan obscured] of a solution. || (الف) نمک کی ہائیڈرولائسس کی تعریف کریں۔ نمکوں کو اس لحاظ سے درجہ بند کریں کہ وہ محلول کے pH [scan obscured] پر کیسے اثر انداز ہوتے ہیں۔ || (الف) د مالګې هایدرولیز تعریف کړئ. مالګې د دې له مخې طبقه بندي کړئ چې د محلول pH [scan obscured] باندې څنګه اغېز کوي.
sol Salt hydrolysis is the reaction of the cation or anion of a salt with water, changing the pH of the solution. Categories: (1) strong acid + strong base (NaCl): neutral, pH 7; (2) strong acid + weak base (NH₄Cl): acidic, pH < 7; (3) weak acid + strong base (CH₃COONa): basic, pH > 7; (4) weak acid + weak base (CH₃COONH₄): pH depends on K_a and K_b. ## Types of salts.

Give the conjugate bases of the[scan obscured] following acids; HClO₄, HCN, H₂CO₃, NH₄⁺ || (ب) (i) مزدوج تیزاب اور اساس کیا ہیں؟ درج ذیل تیزابوں کے مزدوج اساس بتائیں [scan obscured]؛ HClO₄، HCN، H₂CO₃، NH₄⁺ || (ب) (i) مزدوج اسیدونه او القلي څه دي؟ د لاندې اسیدونو مزدوج القلي ورکړئ [scan obscured]؛ HClO₄، HCN، H₂CO₃، NH₄⁺

q ii | Classify as acids and bases giving reasons; BF₃, NH₃, NH₄⁺, Ag⁺, CaO, KC[scan obscured] H₂S, SO₄²⁻, Na⁺, Cl⁻ || وجوہات کے ساتھ تیزابوں اور اساسوں میں درجہ بند کریں؛ BF₃، NH₃، NH₄⁺، Ag⁺، CaO، KC[scan obscured] H₂S، SO₄²⁻، Na⁺، Cl⁻ || د دلایلو سره په اسیدونو او القليو طبقه بندي کړئ؛ BF₃، NH₃، NH₄⁺، Ag⁺، CaO، KC[scan obscured] H₂S، SO₄²⁻، Na⁺، Cl⁻
sol Lewis acids (electron-pair acceptors): BF₃ (incomplete octet), NH₄⁺ (can give H⁺, a Bronsted acid), Ag⁺, Na⁺ (metal cations accept pairs). Bases: NH₃ (lone pair), CaO (O²⁻ ion), SO₄²⁻, Cl⁻, KCl (Cl⁻ is a weak base), H₂S can act as an acid (donate H⁺). ## Classification by reasoning; the list is partly hidden in the scan.

sol Lewis acids: CO₂, SO₂, BCl₃ (they accept a pair of electrons). Lewis bases: H₂O, I⁻, NH₃, OH⁻ (they have lone pairs to donate). ## Lewis classification.
