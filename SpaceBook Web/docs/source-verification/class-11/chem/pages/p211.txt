## Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
q 1 | Briefly explains the term unit cell and crystal lattice. || اکائی خانہ اور قلمی جالی کی اصطلاحات مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || د واحد حجرې او کرسټالي جالۍ اصطلاحات په لنډه توګه تشریح کړئ.
sol Unit cell: the smallest repeating unit of the crystal lattice which, repeated in space in three directions, builds the entire crystal. Crystal lattice: the regular three-dimensional arrangement of the particles (points) in the crystal. ## Definitions.
q 2 | Why crystalline solids are anisotropic? || قلمی ٹھوس غیر ہم سانی کیوں ہوتے ہیں؟ || کرسټالي جامدات ولې انایسوټروپیک دي؟
sol Crystalline solids are anisotropic because the arrangement of particles is different along different directions, so some physical properties (electrical conductivity, thermal conductivity, refractive index, cleavage) vary with the direction in which they are measured. ## Anisotropy.
q 3 | Give the reason that metallic crystals are good conductors of electricity but ionic crystals are not. || وجہ بیان کریں کہ دھاتی قلم بجلی کے اچھے موصل ہیں لیکن آئنی قلم نہیں۔ || دلیل ورکړئ چې فلزي کرسټالونه د برېښنا ښه تېرونکي دي خو آیوني کرسټالونه نه دي.
sol Metallic crystals contain free (mobile) electrons which move under an applied voltage, so they conduct. In ionic crystals the ions are held rigidly at their lattice points by strong electrostatic forces and cannot move, so solid ionic crystals do not conduct (they conduct in solution or in the molten state). ## Conduction.
q 4 | Differentiate between crystalline and amorphous solids. || قلمی اور بے قلمی ٹھوس میں فرق کریں۔ || د کرسټالي او بې بڼې جامداتو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Crystalline solids: regular arrangement, definite geometric shape, sharp melting point, anisotropic, true solids (NaCl). Amorphous solids: irregular arrangement, no definite shape, melt over a range of temperature, isotropic, supercooled liquids (glass). ## Comparison.
q 5 | Justify that crystalline solids have sharp melting point. || ثابت کریں کہ قلمی ٹھوس کا نقطۂ پگھلاؤ تیز (معین) ہوتا ہے۔ || ثابته کړئ چې کرسټالي جامدات تېزه د ویلې کېدو نقطه لري.
sol In a crystalline solid all the particles are arranged in a regular pattern with the same forces and strength of attraction in the whole crystal, so all the bonds break at the same temperature; hence the solid melts at a sharp, definite temperature. ## Melting point.
q 6 | What is transition temperature? Give examples. || تبدیلی درجہ حرارت کیا ہے؟ مثالیں دیں۔ || د انتقال تودوخه څه ده؟ بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Transition temperature is the temperature at which two polymorphic (allotropic) crystalline forms of a substance can exist in equilibrium with each other. Examples: rhombic sulphur ⇌ monoclinic sulphur at 95.5 °C; white tin ⇌ grey tin at 13 °C. ## Definition and examples.
q 7 | Explain that isomorphic substances have usually the same atomic ratio. || وضاحت کریں کہ ہم شکل مادوں کا ایٹمی تناسب عموماً ایک ہی ہوتا ہے۔ || تشریح کړئ چې ایزومورفیک مواد معمولاً یو شان اتومي نسبت لري.
sol Isomorphous substances have the same atomic ratio (the same type of formula) and thus the same number of positive and negative ions of similar size, so they pack in the same way and form the same crystal shape. Example: NaNO₃ and KNO₃ (1 : 1 : 3). ## Isomorphism.
q 8 | Give the reason that covalent crystals are hard while molecular crystals are soft. || وجہ بیان کریں کہ ہم ذرّی قلم سخت اور سالماتی قلم نرم کیوں ہوتے ہیں۔ || دلیل ورکړئ چې کوولنټ کرسټالونه ولې کلک او مالیکولي کرسټالونه ولې نرم دي.
sol Covalent crystals are hard because the atoms are joined in a continuous network by strong covalent bonds (diamond), which are difficult to break. Molecular crystals consist of molecules held by weak van der Waals or hydrogen bonds, which are easily overcome, so they are soft with low melting points. ## Bonding.
q 9 | Define lattice energy and give examples. || جالی توانائی کی تعریف کریں اور مثالیں دیں۔ || د جالۍ انرژي تعریف کړئ او بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Lattice energy is the energy released when one mole of an ionic crystal is formed from its gaseous ions (or the energy required to break the lattice into gaseous ions), e.g. NaCl = 787 kJ/mol, MgO = 3791 kJ/mol. ## Definition.
q 10 | Differentiate between polymorphism and isomorphism. || کثیر شکلی اور ہم شکلی میں فرق کریں۔ || د پولیمورفیزم او ایزومورفیزم ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Polymorphism: a single substance exists in more than one crystalline form (e.g. calcite and aragonite, CaCO₃). Isomorphism: different substances with the same atomic ratio crystallise in the same shape (e.g. NaNO₃ and KNO₃, ZnSO₄ and MgSO₄). ## Comparison.

q 1 | (a) Explain the kinetic molecular interpretation of solids. || (الف) ٹھوس کی حرکی سالماتی توضیح بیان کریں۔ || (الف) د جامداتو حرکي مالیکولي تفسیر تشریح کړئ.
sol Kinetic molecular interpretation of solids: the particles (atoms, molecules or ions) are very close together and held at fixed positions by strong forces; they can only vibrate about their mean positions, so solids have a definite shape and volume, are almost incompressible, do not diffuse appreciably, expand slightly on heating (greater vibration), and melt when the vibration overcomes the forces. ## KMT of solids.
q  | (b) Discuss in detail the following properties, keeping in view the Kinetic Molecular Theory. || (ب) حرکی سالماتی نظریے کو مدنظر رکھتے ہوئے درج ذیل خصوصیات پر تفصیل سے بحث کریں۔ || (ب) د حرکي مالیکولي تیورۍ په پام کې نیولو سره لاندې ځانګړنې په تفصیل سره وڅېړئ.
q i | Diffusion (ii) Compression (iii) Expansion (iv) Motion of molecules || نفوذ (ii) دباؤ سے سکڑنا (iii) پھیلاؤ (iv) سالموں کی حرکت || خپرېدل (ii) فشار (iii) پراخېدل (iv) د مالیکولونو حرکت
sol (i) Diffusion: solid particles hardly diffuse because they are in fixed positions and held strongly. (ii) Compression: solids are practically incompressible because the particles are in contact with no empty space. (iii) Expansion: on heating the vibrations increase and the particles move slightly further apart, so solids expand only a little. (iv) Motion: particles only vibrate about their mean positions; there is no translation. ## Kinetic molecular interpretation of solids.
q 2 | (a) Discuss in detail a sodium chloride lattice. || (الف) سوڈیم کلورائیڈ جالی پر تفصیل سے بحث کریں۔ || (الف) د سوډیم کلوراید جالۍ په تفصیل سره وڅېړئ.
sol In the NaCl lattice, the Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions alternate at the corners and faces of a face-centred cubic unit cell. Each Na⁺ is surrounded by six Cl⁻ and each Cl⁻ by six Na⁺ (coordination number 6 : 6, octahedral). There are four Na⁺ and four Cl⁻ ions per unit cell (4 formula units); the edge length is 564 pm, with an ionic radius ratio of 0.52. ## Rock-salt structure.

q i | Habit of a crystal (ii) Cleavage planes (iii) Crystal growth (iv) Anisotropy (v) Symmetry. || قلم کی عادت (ii) سطحِ شگاف (iii) قلم کی نمو (iv) غیر ہم سانی (v) تشاکل۔ || د کرسټال عادت (ii) د ماتېدو سطحې (iii) د کرسټال وده (iv) انایسوټروپي (v) تناظر.
sol Habit of a crystal is the external shape of the crystal depending on the relative growth of faces. Cleavage planes are the planes along which a crystal splits easily into smooth surfaces. Crystal growth is the build-up of a crystal by adding particles layer by layer. Anisotropy is the dependence of physical properties on direction. Symmetry is the similarity of faces about a plane, an axis or a centre. ## Crystal properties.
