When the battery is turned on, zinc metal from the anode surface oxidizes and is dissolved as Zn²⁺ ions and at the same time zinc ions from the electrolyte solution are reduced at the cathode surface as zinc metal. Thus, the concentration of zinc in the solution remains the same and zinc electrode losses its weight and cathode gains weight due to deposition of zinc on it.
