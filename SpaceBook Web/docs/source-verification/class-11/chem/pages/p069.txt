qp 1 | Briefly write the Moseley experiment. || موزلے کا تجربہ مختصراً لکھیں۔ || د موزلي تجربه په لنډ ډول ولیکئ.
sol Moseley bombarded different metals as targets in an X-ray tube with cathode rays and measured the frequencies of the X-rays emitted. He plotted √ν against the atomic number Z and obtained a straight line (√ν = a(Z − b)). He concluded that atomic number, the nuclear charge, is the fundamental property determining an element's characteristic X-rays, and used it to arrange the elements and to predict missing elements. ## Moseley's experiment.
qp 2 | Explain the magnetic quantum number in detail. || مقناطیسی کوانٹم عدد کی تفصیل سے وضاحت کریں۔ || مقناطیسي کوانتمي شمېره په تفصیل تشریح کړئ.
sol The magnetic quantum number (m) describes the orientation of an orbital in space (the direction of its axis) in an external magnetic field. For a given ℓ it can have (2ℓ + 1) values from −ℓ to +ℓ including 0; e.g. for p (ℓ = 1), m = −1, 0, +1 (three orbitals pₓ, p_y, p_z), for d (ℓ = 2) five values. It explains the splitting of lines in a magnetic field (Zeeman effect). ## Magnetic quantum number.
qp 3 | What are the shapes of s, p and d orbitals? || s، p اور d اوربیٹلوں کی شکلیں کیا ہیں؟ || د s، p او d اوربیټالونو بڼې څه دي؟
sol s orbital: spherical, non-directional; p orbital: dumb-bell shaped, along the x, y or z axis (three orbitals pₓ, p_y, p_z) with a node at the nucleus; d orbitals: mostly double dumb-bell (clover-leaf) shapes, five orbitals (d_xy, d_yz, d_xz, d_x²−y², d_z²). ## Shapes of orbitals.
qp 4 | Define Pauli’s exclusion principle and Hund’s rule. || پاؤلی کے اصول اخراج اور ہنڈ کے اصول کی تعریف کریں۔ || د پاولي د منع اصل او د هونډ اصل تعریف کړئ.
sol Pauli's exclusion principle: no two electrons in an atom can have the same set of four quantum numbers, so an orbital can hold at most two electrons with opposite spins. Hund's rule: in degenerate orbitals (same energy, e.g. p, d) electrons occupy them singly with parallel spins first, and pair up only after each orbital has one electron. ## Principles for filling orbitals.
qp 5 | What is mean by Aufbau principle? || آفباؤ اصول سے کیا مراد ہے؟ || د افباو له اصل څخه څه مراد ده؟
sol Aufbau principle: electrons occupy the orbitals in the order of increasing energy, filling the lowest available energy orbitals first (1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p ...), as given by the (n + ℓ) rule. ## Building-up principle.
