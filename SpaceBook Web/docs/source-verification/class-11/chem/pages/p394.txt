q 2 | Balance the following redox equations by the half reaction method || درج ذیل ریڈاکس مساواتوں کو نیم تعامل کے طریقے سے متوازن کریں || لاندې ریډاکس معادلې د نیمایي تعامل په طریقه انډول کړئ
sol Half reaction (ion-electron) method: (1) split the equation into an oxidation and a reduction half reaction; (2) balance the atoms other than H and O; (3) balance O with H₂O and H with H⁺ (in acid; with OH⁻ in a base); (4) balance the charge with electrons; (5) multiply to equalise the electrons and add the two halves, cancelling the common species. The equations are not legible in the scan. ## Method.
eq \text{i.}\ \mathrm{Sn^{2+}+Fe^{3+}\longrightarrow Sn^{4+}+Fe^{2+}}\quad\text{(Acidic medium)}
eq \text{ii.}\ \mathrm{Zn+Cr_2O_7^{2-}+H^+\longrightarrow Zn^{2+}+Cr^{3+}+H_2O}\quad\text{(Acidic medium)}
eq \text{iii.}\ \mathrm{H_2O_2+MnO_4^-+H^+\longrightarrow Mn^{2+}+O_2+H_2O}\quad\text{(Acidic medium)}
eq \text{iv.}\ \mathrm{Sn^{2+}+I_2\longrightarrow Sn^{4+}+I^-}\quad\text{(Neutral)}
q 3 | Given the following cells at 25°C. || 25°C پر درج ذیل خلیے دیے گئے ہیں۔ || په 25°C کې لاندې حجرې ورکړل شوي.
sol Cell reactions: at the anode the metal with the lower reduction potential is oxidised, M → M^n+ + ne⁻; at the cathode the ion of the metal with the higher reduction potential is reduced, N^m+ + me⁻ → N. sol E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode, using the standard reduction potentials of the two half cells (e.g. Zn²⁺/Zn = −0.76 V, Cu²⁺/Cu = +0.34 V gives E°cell = 0.34 − (−0.76) = 1.10 V). The cells are not legible in the scan, so the formula and an example are given. ## Method.

q 1 | (a) Define the term electrolysis. State and explain Faraday's laws of electrolysis. || (الف) برق پاشی کی اصطلاح کی تعریف کریں۔ فیراڈے کے قوانینِ برق پاشی بیان کریں اور ان کی وضاحت کریں۔ || (الف) د الکترولیز اصطلاح تعریف کړئ. د فاراډې د الکترولیز قوانین بیان او تشریح کړئ.
sol (a) Electrolysis is the chemical decomposition of an electrolyte (molten or in solution) by passing an electric current through it. Faraday's first law: the mass of a substance deposited or liberated at an electrode is directly proportional to the quantity of electricity passed: W = Z I t (Z is the electrochemical equivalent). Faraday's second law: when the same quantity of electricity is passed through different electrolytes, the masses of the substances liberated are proportional to their chemical equivalents (equivalent masses): W₁/W₂ = e₁/e₂; one faraday (96500 C) liberates one gram equivalent of any substance. ## Faraday's laws.
q  | (b) A certain amount of current is passed through CuSO₄ solution for fifty minutes. The net amount of Cu deposited was found to be 25g. Calculate the current when Z=1 and At. Mass of Cu is 63.5g. || (ب) CuSO₄ محلول میں سے پچاس منٹ تک ایک خاص مقدار کی رو گزاری گئی۔ جمع ہونے والے Cu کی خالص مقدار 25g پائی گئی۔ رو کا حساب لگائیں جب Z=1 ہے اور Cu کی ایٹمی کمیت 63.5g ہے۔ || (ب) د CuSO₄ محلول له لارې د جریان یو ټاکلی مقدار پنځوس دقیقې تېر شو. د ترسب شوي Cu خالص مقدار 25g وموندل شو. جریان محاسبه کړئ کله چې Z=1 او د Cu اټومي کتله 63.5g ده.
sol W = (e × I × t)/96500 for Cu²⁺ (equivalent mass e = 63.5/2 = 31.75 g): I = (W × 96500)/(e × t) = (25 × 96500)/(31.75 × 50 × 60) = 2,412,500/95,250 = 25.3 A. (If the stated value Z = 1 is used as a valency of 1, e = 63.5 and I = 12.7 A; for Cu²⁺ the correct value is 25.3 A.) ## t = 50 min = 3000 s.
q 2 | What is an electrode potential? How it can be measured? || الیکٹروڈ پوٹینشل کیا ہے؟ اسے کیسے ناپا جا سکتا ہے؟ || د الکترود پوټنشیل څه دی؟ دا څنګه اندازه کېدای شي؟
sol Electrode potential is the potential difference developed between a metal electrode and the solution of its own ions (the tendency of the metal to lose or gain electrons). It cannot be measured alone, so it is measured relative to a reference, the standard hydrogen electrode (SHE, pH 0, 1 atm H₂, 298 K) whose potential is taken as 0 V: a cell is made of the electrode (1 M ion solution) with SHE and the voltage read with a voltmeter gives the electrode potential. ## Measurement.
q 3 | What is the difference between the following? Give examples. || درج ذیل میں کیا فرق ہے؟ مثالیں دیں۔ || د لاندې ترمنځ څه توپیر دی؟ بېلګې ورکړئ.
q i | Reversible and irreversible cells || الٹ پذیر اور ناقابلِ واپسی خلیے || بیرته راګرځېدونکې او بیرته نه راګرځېدونکې حجرې
sol A reversible cell is one in which the cell reaction can be reversed by applying an opposing voltage slightly higher than the cell potential (Daniell cell, lead storage battery), so the cell can be recharged. An irreversible cell is one in which the reaction cannot be reversed by an external supply (the dry cell, or Zn in dilute H₂SO₄), so it cannot be recharged. ## Difference.
q ii | Electrolytic and voltaic cell || برق پاشی اور وولٹائی خلیہ || الکترولایټیک او وولټایک حجره
sol A voltaic (galvanic) cell converts the chemical energy of a spontaneous redox reaction into electrical energy; the anode is negative and the cathode positive (Daniell cell). An electrolytic cell uses electrical energy to cause a non-spontaneous chemical reaction; the anode is positive and the cathode negative (electrolysis of NaCl, electroplating). ## Difference.
q 4 | i) Explain the term oxidation and reduction with example. || i) مثال کے ساتھ تکسید اور تخفیف کی اصطلاحات کی وضاحت کریں۔ || i) په بېلګې سره د اکسیډیشن او ریډکشن اصطلاحات تشریح کړئ.
sol Oxidation is the loss of electrons (increase in oxidation number), e.g. Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻; reduction is the gain of electrons (decrease in oxidation number), e.g. Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu. Both occur together in a redox reaction: Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu. ## Definitions.
q ii | What is oxidation state? Give the oxidation number of Mn in KMnO₄, MnO₂ and Mn₂O₇. || تکسیدی حالت کیا ہے؟ KMnO₄، MnO₂ اور Mn₂O₇ میں Mn کا تکسیدی عدد بتائیں۔ || د اکسیډیشن حالت څه دی؟ په KMnO₄، MnO₂ او Mn₂O₇ کې د Mn د اکسیډیشن شمېره ورکړئ.
sol Oxidation state (oxidation number) is the charge that an atom would have if all the bonds were ionic. Mn in KMnO₄: +1 + x − 8 = 0, x = +7. Mn in MnO₂: x − 4 = 0, x = +4. Mn in Mn₂O₇: 2x − 14 = 0, x = +7. ## Calculated from the sum of the oxidation numbers.
q 5 | What is an electrolytic cell? Explain Daniell cell in detail. || برق پاشی خلیہ کیا ہے؟ ڈینیل خلیے کی تفصیل سے وضاحت کریں۔ || الکترولایټیک حجره څه ده؟ د ډینیل حجره په تفصیل سره تشریح کړئ.
sol An electrolytic cell uses electrical energy to bring about a non-spontaneous chemical change (electrolysis). The Daniell cell is a voltaic cell: a zinc rod dips in ZnSO₄ solution (anode, oxidation Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻) and a copper rod in CuSO₄ solution (cathode, reduction Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu), joined by a wire and a salt bridge (KCl) which completes the circuit and keeps the solutions neutral. Overall reaction: Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu, E°cell = 0.34 − (−0.76) = 1.10 V. ## Daniell cell.
q  | a) Calculate the electrical energy obtained from a Daniell cell. || الف) ڈینیل خلیے سے حاصل ہونے والی برقی توانائی کا حساب لگائیں۔ || الف) له ډینیل حجرې څخه ترلاسه شوې برېښنایي انرژي محاسبه کړئ.
sol E°cell = 1.10 V and two electrons are transferred (n = 2). Electrical energy = nFE°cell = 2 × 96500 C × 1.10 V = 2.12 × 10⁵ J = 212 kJ per mole of zinc (the maximum useful work, −ΔG°). ## Electrical energy of the Daniell cell.
q  | b) What are the half-cell reaction? Give the half-cell reactions of the Nelson's cell for the production of NaOH. || ب) نیم خلوی تعامل کیا ہیں؟ NaOH کی تیاری کے لیے نیلسن خلیے کے نیم خلوی تعاملات بتائیں۔ || ب) نیمه حجروي تعامل څه دي؟ د NaOH د جوړولو لپاره د نیلسن حجرې نیمه حجروي تعاملات ورکړئ.
sol A half-cell reaction is the oxidation or the reduction process that occurs at one electrode. Nelson cell (production of NaOH by electrolysis of brine, with a steel cathode and an asbestos diaphragm): at the anode (graphite): 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻ (oxidation); at the cathode (iron gauze): 2H₂O + 2e⁻ → H₂ + 2OH⁻ (reduction); the Na⁺ and OH⁻ ions form NaOH solution. ## Nelson's cell.
