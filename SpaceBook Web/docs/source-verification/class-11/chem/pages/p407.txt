## GLOSSARY || فرہنگ || وييپانګه
table فرہنگ || Glossary || وييپانګه
th | Term | Definition
tr | Spin quantum number (ms) | The quantum number that specifies the direction in which the electron is spinning with values of +1/2 and -1/2
tr | Standard electrode potential | The potential developed by a metal or other material immersed in an electrolyte solution relative to the potential of the hydrogen electrode, which is set at zero
tr | Standard enthalpy of neutralization (ΔH°n) | It is the enthalpy change when one mole of an acid is neutralized by one mole of a base.
tr | Standard Enthalpy of solution (ΔH°s) | It is the change in enthalpy when one mole of a substance is dissolved in a solvent so that further dissolution does not give any change in enthalpy.
tr | Standard solution | A solution of known concentration
tr | Standard temperature and pressure, STP | The standard temperature is 0°C(273K) and the standard pressure is 1 atm or 760mm of Hg or 760 torr or 101325Nm⁻².
tr | State | A complete description of the system is called its state. It is a particular set of conditions of pressure, temperature, volume and volume amount of each component of a system.
tr | State function | The properties of a system which uniquely define the state of a system are called state functions. A state function depends on initial and final states of a system and is independent of the path fowled.
tr | Stoichiometry | The area of chemistry that deals with the quantitative relationships among reactants and products in a chemical reaction
tr | Strong electrolytes | An electrolyte that is 100% ionized in solution or in molten state is called a strong electrolyte e.g. NaCl, NaOH and HCl
tr | Substance | Matter that is homogeneous and has a definite, fixed composition; substances occur in two forms—as elements and as compounds.
tr | Substrate | In biochemical reactions, the substrate is the unit acted upon by an enzyme.
tr | Surface tension | The resistance of a liquid to an increase in its surface area.
tr | System (thermodynamic) | That part of the universe on which attention is to be focused.
endtable
