p According to Hess's law the sum of all the five steps lead to the heat of formation of NaCl(s). || ہیس کے قانون کے مطابق تمام پانچ مراحل کا مجموعہ NaCl(s) کی تشکیل کی حرارت تک پہنچاتا ہے۔ || د هېس د قانون له مخې د ټولو پنځو ګامونو مجموعه د NaCl(s) د جوړېدو تودوخې ته رسوي.
eq \Delta H^{\circ}_f=\Delta H^{\circ}_{Sub}+\Delta H^{\circ}_{IE}+\tfrac12\Delta H^{\circ}_{Diss}+\Delta H^{\circ}_{EA}+\Delta H^{\circ}_{Lat}
eq \Delta H^{\circ}_{Lat}=\Delta H^{\circ}_f-\Delta H^{\circ}_{Sub}-\Delta H^{\circ}_{IE}-\tfrac12\Delta H^{\circ}_{Diss}-\Delta H^{\circ}_{EA}
eq =-411\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}-109\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}-496\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}-121\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}-(-348\ \mathrm{kJ/mol})
eq \Delta H^{\circ}_{Lat}\ \text{of NaCl}_{(s)}=-789\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}
