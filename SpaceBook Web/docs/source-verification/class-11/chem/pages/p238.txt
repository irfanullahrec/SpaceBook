q ii | (a) State Le-Chatelier's principle and discuss its application to ammonia system. || (الف) لی شیٹیلیئر کا اصول بیان کریں اور امونیا کے نظام پر اس کے اطلاق پر بحث کریں۔ || (الف) د لی-شاټیلیر اصل بیان کړئ او د امونیا په سیسټم باندې د هغه د کارونې په اړه بحث وکړئ.
sol (a) Le Chatelier's principle: if a stress is applied to a system at equilibrium, the system adjusts to reduce the stress. Ammonia system (Haber process) N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, ΔH = −92 kJ/mol: increasing the pressure, adding more N₂ or H₂ or removing NH₃ shifts the equilibrium forward (more NH₃); lowering the temperature favours NH₃ (exothermic), but the rate is slow, so about 450 °C with an iron catalyst and 200 atm is the compromise. ## Application.
q  | (b) What is the effect of raising temperature on each of the following equilibria. || (ب) درج ذیل توازنوں میں سے ہر ایک پر درجہ حرارت بڑھانے کا کیا اثر ہے۔ || (ب) د لاندې تعادلونو له هر یوه باندې د تودوخې د لوړولو اغېز څه دی.
sol Raising the temperature favours the endothermic direction: for an endothermic forward reaction (ΔH positive) the equilibrium shifts forward and K_c increases; for an exothermic forward reaction (ΔH negative) it shifts backward and K_c decreases. ## The reactions are not legible in the scan, so the rule is given.

q iv | (a) With the help of chemical equilibrium constant expression, how will you predict the direction and the extent of a chemical reaction? || (الف) کیمیائی توازنی مستقلہ کے اظہار کی مدد سے آپ کیمیائی تعامل کی سمت اور حد کی پیش گوئی کیسے کریں گے؟ || (الف) د کيمياوي تعادل د ثابت د بیان په مرسته به تاسو د کيمياوي تعامل لوری او اندازه څنګه وړاندوینه کوئ؟
sol (a) Compare the reaction quotient Q (the expression of K_c with the present concentrations) with K_c: if Q < K_c the reaction goes forward; if Q > K_c it goes backward; if Q = K_c the system is at equilibrium. The size of K_c shows the extent: K_c very large (> 10³) means the reaction is almost complete; very small (< 10⁻³) means hardly any product; intermediate means appreciable amounts of both. ## Prediction using K_c.

Predict the extent of the formation of O₂ at room temperature. || (ب) تعامل 2O₃ ⇌ 3O₂ کا توازنی مستقلہ 25°C پر 1.0 × 10[scan obscured] ہے۔ کمرے کے درجہ حرارت پر O₂ کی تشکیل کی حد کی پیش گوئی کریں۔ || (ب) د 2O₃ ⇌ 3O₂ تعامل د تعادل ثابت په 25°C کې 1.0 × 10[scan obscured] دی. د خونې په تودوخه کې د O₂ د جوړېدو اندازه وړاندوینه کړئ.
sol (b) For 2O₃ ⇌ 3O₂, K_c = [O₂]³/[O₃]² is enormous (about 10⁵⁵ or more at 25 °C), so the reaction goes practically to completion in the forward direction: at room temperature ozone is thermodynamically unstable and changes almost completely into oxygen (the rate may be slow without a catalyst). ## The exponent is partly hidden in the scan.
