## GLOSSARY || فرہنگ || وييپانګه
table فرہنگ || Glossary || وييپانګه
th | Term | Definition
tr | Reaction quotient | It is the form of Kc or Kp in which the reactants and products may have any concentration before the reaction attains equilibrium.
tr | Real gas | A gas, which does not obey he gas laws at all temperature and pressure.
tr | Redox | A term applied to any chemical process which involves both reduction and oxidation.
tr | Reducing agent | A substance which brings about reduction.
tr | Reduction | A decrease in the oxidation number of an element as a result of gaining electrons.
tr | Relative atomic mass | The mass of one atom of the element compared with one-twelfth the mass of one atom of carbon ¹²₆C.
tr | Relative formula mass (relative molecular mass) | This is the sum of the relative atomic masses of all those elements shown in the formula of the substances. This is often referred to as the relative molecular mass.
tr | Resonance structures | Two or more Lewis structures that show the same relative position of atoms but different positions of electron pairs
tr | Reversible reaction | A chemical reaction which is said to be reversible can go both ways. This means that once some of the products have been formed they will undergo a chemical change once more to reform the reactants. The reaction from left to right, as the equation for the reaction is written is known as the forward reaction and the reaction from right to left is known as the reverse reaction.
tr | Salt bridge | A U-tube containing an electrolyte that connects the two compartments of a galvanic cell, allowing ion flow without extensive mixing of the different solutions.
tr | Solubility product, Ksp | Ksp is the product of molar concentrations of ions in the saturated solution each raised to an exponent equal to the co-efficient of the balanced equation.
tr | Spectrum | A band or series of radiations in increasing or decreasing order of wavelengths or frequencies when light is resolved into it constituent radiations.
tr | Speed (c) | The speed (or velocity) of a wave is the distance through which a particular wave travels in one second.
endtable
