q 1 | Define activation energy and transition state. || توانائی دہلیز اور عبوری حالت کی تعریف کریں۔ || د فعالولو انرژي او انتقالي حالت تعریف کړئ.
sol Activation energy is the minimum energy that colliding molecules must have for a collision to result in a reaction. The transition state (activated complex) is the unstable, high-energy intermediate arrangement of atoms formed at the top of the energy barrier, which either breaks into the products or goes back to the reactants. ## Definitions.
q 2 | Differentiate between homogenous and heterogenous catalysis. || ہم جنس اور غیر ہم جنس اتپریری عمل میں فرق کریں۔ || د همجنس او ناهمجنس کتلیسس ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Homogeneous catalysis: the catalyst is in the same phase as the reactants (e.g. NO gas catalysing SO₂ to SO₃ in the lead chamber process, H⁺ in ester hydrolysis). Heterogeneous catalysis: the catalyst is in a different phase (e.g. solid iron in the Haber process, solid Pt in the catalytic converter, V₂O₅ in the contact process). ## Types of catalysis.
