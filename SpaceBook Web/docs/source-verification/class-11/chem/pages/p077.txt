sol Five postulates of VSEPR theory: (1) the shape of a molecule depends on the number of electron pairs (bonding and lone pairs) around the central atom; (2) electron pairs repel each other and arrange themselves as far apart as possible; (3) lone pair-lone pair repulsion > lone pair-bond pair > bond pair-bond pair; (4) multiple bonds are treated as one electron group; (5) the molecular shape is determined by the positions of the atoms, not the lone pairs. ## VSEPR postulates.
