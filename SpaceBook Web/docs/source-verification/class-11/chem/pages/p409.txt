## GLOSSARY || فرہنگ || وييپانګه
table فرہنگ || Glossary || وييپانګه
th | Term | Definition
tr | Wave number (ṽ) | The number of waves per unit length. This is reciprocal of the wavelength and is given the symbol ṽ (nu bar).
tr | Wavelength (λ) | The distance between two adjacent crests or troughs of a wave.
tr | Weak electrolytes | The substance which is partially dissociated into its ions is called a weak electrolyte e.g. NH₄Cl and H₂CO₃ etc.
tr | X-Rays | When high energy electrons (cathode rays) strikes with the heavy metal used as an anode, x-rays are produced.
tr | Yield | Yield of a chemical reaction is the amount of product that is produced from given amounts of reactants.
