## 5.2.2 Properties of coefficient of correlation || 5.2.2 ارتباطی عددی سر کی خصوصیات || 5.2.2 د همتړاو د ضریب ځانګړتیاوې
p (i) The coefficient of correlation is symmetrical with respect to the variables x and y i.e. r_xy = r_yx. || (i) ارتباطی عددی سر متغیرات x اور y کے لحاظ سے متشاکل ہے یعنی r_xy = r_yx۔ || (i) د همتړاو ضریب د x او y متغیرونو په نسبت متناسب دی یعنې r_xy = r_yx.

## 5.2.3 Correlation and causation || 5.2.3 ارتباط اور علت || 5.2.3 همتړاو او علت
p Correlation does not establish a cause and effect relationship between two variables. It does not provide any information about the size of the change in one variable as a result of a certain change in the other variable. In correlation both variables are interdependent. Relationship between them may be zero, weak or strong. Sometimes correlation between two unconnected variables will be nonsense or spurious.
