## 3.6.2 Sheppard's correction || 3.6.2 شیپرڈ کی تصحیح || 3.6.2 د شیپرډ سمون
p When summary statistics like mean, standard deviation and moments etc. are computed from a continuous grouped frequency distribution, the frequencies are allotted to the mid-points of the classes, which surely create error and need correction. This point was first noticed by W.F Sheppard and suggested the following corrected formulas known as Sheppard's correction.
