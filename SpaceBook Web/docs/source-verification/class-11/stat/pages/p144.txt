## 3.4 Explain the terms: || 3.4 اصطلاحات کی وضاحت کریں: || 3.4 اصطلاحات تشریح کړئ:
q i | Dispersion || انتشار || خپراوی
sol Dispersion is the degree to which the observations of a data set are spread (scattered) about a central value such as the mean or median. Data with a small dispersion are close together; data with a large dispersion are widely scattered. ## Definition.
q ii | Measures of dispersion || انتشار کے پیمانے || د خپراوي اندازې
sol Measures of dispersion are numerical values that describe the amount of variation or scatter in the data. The main ones are the range, the quartile deviation, the mean deviation, the variance and the standard deviation (and their coefficients). ## Definition and list.
q iii | Absolute and relative measures of dispersion. || انتشار کے مطلق اور اضافی پیمانے۔ || د خپراوي مطلقې او نسبي اندازې.
sol An absolute measure of dispersion is expressed in the same units as the data (range, Q.D, M.D, S.D, variance), so it cannot compare data in different units. A relative measure (a coefficient of dispersion) is the absolute measure divided by an average, so it has no units and is used to compare the variation of different series; examples are the coefficient of range, of Q.D, of M.D and the coefficient of variation. ## Absolute vs relative.
