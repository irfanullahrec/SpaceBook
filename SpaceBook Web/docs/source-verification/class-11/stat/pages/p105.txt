eq \text{M.D}=\dfrac{\sum f|x-\bar{x}|}{\sum f}\ \text{ where, }\ \bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}=\dfrac{5879}{40}=146.975
eq \therefore\ \text{M.D}=\dfrac{435.35}{40}=10.88\qquad\text{(Absolute dispersion)}
