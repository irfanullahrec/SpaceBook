q 4 | 7 Explain the following concepts: (i) Simple and composite index numbers. (ii) Weighted and un-weighted index numbers. (iii) Fixed base method and chain base method. || 7 درج ذیل تصورات کی وضاحت کریں: (i) سادہ اور مرکب اشاریہ اعداد۔ (ii) وزنی اور غیر وزنی اشاریہ اعداد۔ (iii) مقررہ بنیاد کا طریقہ اور زنجیری بنیاد کا طریقہ۔ || 7 لاندې مفهومونه تشریح کړئ: (i) ساده او مرکب شاخص شمېرې. (ii) وزني او نه‌وزني شاخص شمېرې. (iii) ټاکلې بنسټ طریقه او زنځیري بنسټ طریقه.
sol (i) A simple index number is formed for one commodity; a composite index number for a group of commodities. (ii) An unweighted index gives every item the same importance (simple aggregative method, simple average of price relatives); a weighted index gives each item a weight according to its importance (e.g. quantities consumed), as in Laspeyre, Paasche and Fisher. (iii) In the fixed base method every period is compared with one fixed base period; in the chain base method each period is compared with the one before it (link relatives), and the link relatives are chained: chain index = (link relative x previous chain index)/100. ## The three pairs of concepts.

q 4 | 9 (a) Distinguish between fixed base and chain base methods of constructing index numbers. (b) Compute the chain index numbers (link relatives) from the following data: || 9 (الف) اشاریہ اعداد بنانے کے مقررہ بنیاد اور زنجیری بنیاد کے طریقوں میں فرق کریں۔ (ب) درج ذیل اعداد و شمار سے زنجیری اشاریہ اعداد (کڑی اضافتیں) حساب کریں: || 9 (a) د شاخص شمېرو د جوړولو په ټاکلې بنسټ او زنځیري بنسټ طریقو کې توپیر وکړئ. (b) له لاندې معلوماتو څخه زنځیري شاخص شمېرې (پیوندي نسبتونه) محاسبه کړئ:
sol (a) In the fixed base method all the years are compared with one fixed base year, so the indices are directly comparable with each other and are easy to compute, but the base may become out of date and new commodities cannot be added. In the chain base method each year is compared with the preceding year (link relative = (current price/previous price) x 100) and the links are chained; it allows commodities and weights to be changed easily, but errors accumulate and the long-term comparison is less reliable. (b) Link relatives for 1960-1970 (1960 = 100): 100.0, 104.2, 112.0, 103.6, 106.9, 106.5, 106.1, 120.0, 121.4, 125.5, 89.1. Chain indices (chain = link x previous chain/100): 100.0, 104.2, 116.7, 120.8, 129.2, 137.5, 145.8, 175.0, 212.5, 266.7, 237.5. ## Chain base method on the 1960 price.
