## 6 Differentiate between: || 6 فرق بیان کریں: || 6 توپیر وښیئ:
q i | Descriptive and inferential statistics. || بیانیہ اور استنباطی شماریات۔ || تشریحي او استنباطي احصایه.
sol Descriptive statistics collects, organises, summarises and presents data (mean, tables, graphs) for the group studied. Inferential statistics uses the sample data to draw conclusions, make estimates and test hypotheses about the population (with probability). ## Difference.
q ii | Qualitative and quantitative data. || کیفی اور مقداری اعداد و شمار۔ || کیفي او کمیتي معلومات.
sol Qualitative data describe quality or attribute and cannot be measured numerically (colour, gender, religion, marital status). Quantitative data are measured numerically (height, weight, income, number of students). ## Difference.
q iii | Discrete and continuous variable. || منفصل اور مسلسل متغیر۔ || منفصل او دوامداره متغیر.
sol A discrete variable takes only isolated (countable) values, usually whole numbers (number of children, number of cars). A continuous variable can take any value within an interval, including fractions and decimals (height, weight, temperature, time). ## Difference.
q iv | Primary and secondary data. || ابتدائی اور ثانوی اعداد و شمار۔ || لومړني او ثانوي معلومات.

(x) په مایل په ساعت کې د موټر چټکتیا.
sol Continuous: (i) height of a girl, (ii) weight of a boy, (iii) distance travelled by a train, (x) speed of an automobile, (viii) age of a shopkeeper. Discrete: (iv) scores on an achievement test, (v) number of poles in a colony, (vi) number of individuals sitting in a bus, (ix) number of cars in a showroom; (vii) intelligence test scores are also discrete (whole-number scores). ## Counts and whole-number scores are discrete; measurements are continuous.
q 1 | 8 Classify the following measurements as interval, ratio, ordinal or nominal measurements: (i) gender of a rabbit (ii) eye colour (iii) temperature (iv) height (v) weight (vi) rating of performance in a task (vii) scores on an achievement test (viii) scores on an intelligence test (ix) calendar time (x) length || 8 درج ذیل پیمائشوں کو وقفہ جاتی، تناسبی، ترتیبی یا اسمی پیمائشوں میں درجہ بند کریں: (i) خرگوش کی جنس (ii) آنکھ کا رنگ (iii) درجہ حرارت (iv) قد (v) وزن (vi) کسی کام میں کارکردگی کی درجہ بندی (vii) کارکردگی ٹیسٹ کے نمبر (viii) ذہانت ٹیسٹ کے نمبر (ix) تقویمی وقت (x) لمبائی || 8 لاندې اندازې په انټروال، تناسبي، ترتیبي یا نومي اندازو طبقه‌بندي کړئ: (i) د خرګوش جنس (ii) د سترګو رنګ (iii) تودوخه (iv) قد (v) وزن (vi) په یوه کار کې د فعالیت درجه‌بندي (vii) د السته راوړنې ازموینې نمرې (viii) د هوښیارتیا ازموینې نمرې (ix) تقویمي وخت (x) اوږدوالی
sol (i) gender of a rabbit: nominal; (ii) eye colour: nominal; (iii) temperature (°C or °F): interval; (iv) height: ratio; (v) weight: ratio; (vi) rating of performance in a task: ordinal; (vii) scores on an achievement test: interval; (viii) scores on an intelligence test: interval; (ix) calendar time (year): interval; (x) length: ratio. ## Scales of measurement.
q 1 | 9 Discuss the various methods used for the collection of primary data. || 9 ابتدائی اعداد و شمار کے اکٹھا کرنے کے لیے استعمال ہونے والے مختلف طریقوں پر بحث کریں۔ || 9 د لومړنیو معلوماتو د راټولولو لپاره کارېدونکې بېلابېلې طریقې وڅېړئ.
sol Methods of collecting primary data: (1) direct personal investigation (interview), (2) indirect oral investigation, (3) questionnaires sent by mail or filled by enumerators, (4) schedules filled by trained enumerators, (5) observation and experiments, (6) telephone and internet surveys, (7) local sources/correspondents. Each has advantages (accuracy, originality) and limitations (cost, time, bias). ## Primary data.

(i) 8.675 (ii) 23.72558 (iii) 52.726 (iv) 0.315 (v) 0.845 (vi) 2.374 || 10 درج ذیل اعداد کو دو اعشاری مقامات تک گول کریں۔ (i) 8.675 (ii) 23.72558 (iii) 52.726 (iv) 0.315 (v) 0.845 (vi) 2.374 || 10 لاندې شمېرې دوو اعشاري ځایونو ته وګردوئ. (i) 8.675 (ii) 23.72558 (iii) 52.726 (iv) 0.315 (v) 0.845 (vi) 2.374
sol (i) 8.675 → 8.68; (ii) 23.72558 → 23.73; (iii) 52.726 → 52.73; (iv) 0.315 → 0.32; (v) 0.845 → 0.84 (the digit before the 5 is even, so it is left unchanged); (vi) 2.374 → 2.37. ## Rounding: if the dropped digit is greater than 5 round up, less than 5 leave; if exactly 5, round to make the last kept digit even.
q 1 | 11 What is the scale of measurement? Define nominal scale, ordinal scale, interval scale and ratio scale. || 11 پیمائش کا پیمانہ کیا ہے؟ اسمی پیمانے، ترتیبی پیمانے، وقفہ جاتی پیمانے اور تناسبی پیمانے کی تعریف کریں۔ || 11 د اندازې پیمانه څه ده؟ نومي پیمانه، ترتیبي پیمانه، انټروال پیمانه او تناسبي پیمانه تعریف کړئ.
sol The scale of measurement is the way in which a variable is measured. Nominal scale: only names or categories without order (gender, colour). Ordinal scale: categories with a meaningful order but unequal gaps (grades, rank). Interval scale: ordered with equal intervals but no true zero (temperature in °C, calendar year). Ratio scale: has equal intervals and a true zero, so ratios are meaningful (height, weight, income). ## Scales of measurement.
q 1 | 12 What do you understand by classification and tabulation of data? State the basic principles of classification. || 12 اعداد و شمار کی درجہ بندی اور جدول بندی سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ درجہ بندی کے بنیادی اصول بیان کریں۔ || 12 د معلوماتو د طبقه‌بندۍ او جدول‌جوړونې څخه تاسو څه پوهېږئ؟ د طبقه‌بندۍ بنسټیز اصول بیان کړئ.
sol Classification is the process of arranging data into groups or classes according to their common characteristics (similarities and dissimilarities). Tabulation is the systematic arrangement of classified data in rows and columns. Basic principles of classification: it should be unambiguous (classes are exclusive), exhaustive (all items included), stable, flexible, suited to the purpose and homogeneous in each class, and of suitable number and size. ## Classification and tabulation.
q 1 | 13 Define a frequency distribution. Describe briefly the main steps involved in the construction of a grouped frequency table from the raw data. || 13 تعدد تقسیم کی تعریف کریں۔ خام اعداد و شمار سے گروہی تعدد جدول بنانے کے اہم مراحل مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || 13 فریکونسي ویش تعریف کړئ. له خامو معلوماتو څخه د ګروپي فریکونسي جدول د جوړولو مهم پړاوونه په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ.
sol A frequency distribution is a table that shows the values of a variable (or classes) and the number of times (frequencies) each occurs. Steps of a grouped table: (1) find the range (largest − smallest); (2) decide the number of classes (Sturges: k = 1 + 3.3 log n); (3) find the class width h = range/k and round it; (4) fix class limits and boundaries without overlap; (5) tally the data into classes; (6) count the tallies to get the frequencies and check the total. ## Frequency distribution.
