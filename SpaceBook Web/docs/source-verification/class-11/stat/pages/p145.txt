q 3 | 5 Describe the role of measures of central tendency and measures of dispersion which they play in the statistical analysis. || 5 شماریاتی تجزیے میں مرکزی رجحان کے پیمانوں اور انتشار کے پیمانوں کے کردار کو بیان کریں۔ || 5 په احصایوي تحلیل کې د مرکزي تمایل د اندازو او د خپراوي د اندازو رول بیان کړئ.
sol A measure of central tendency gives the single value around which the data cluster; a measure of dispersion shows how closely the data are grouped about it. An average alone can mislead, e.g. two series can have the same mean but very different spread. Together they describe a distribution: the average locates it, the dispersion shows its reliability and consistency (a small dispersion means the average is representative), and dispersion is also used to compare series and in further statistical analysis such as control of quality and sampling. ## Role of averages and dispersion.
q 3 | 6 Define range, coefficient of range and Semi-inter quartile range or quartile deviation. || 6 حد، حد کا عددی سر اور نیم بین الربعی حد یا چوتھائی انحراف کی تعریف کریں۔ || 6 رېنج، د رېنج ضریب او نیمه‌منځ څلورمه رېنج یا څلورمه انحراف تعریف کړئ.
sol Range = largest value - smallest value (R = xL - xS). Coefficient of range = (xL - xS)/(xL + xS). Quartile deviation (semi-inter-quartile range) Q.D = (Q3 - Q1)/2, and the coefficient of Q.D = (Q3 - Q1)/(Q3 + Q1). The range is an absolute measure and its coefficient a relative one; likewise for Q.D. ## Definitions and formulas.

sol (a) The mean deviation (M.D) is the mean of the absolute deviations of the observations from an average (mean or median): M.D = sum of |x - a|/n; its coefficient is M.D divided by that average. Disadvantages: ignoring the signs of the deviations is mathematically unsound, the absolute values cannot be handled algebraically, and so it is little used in further statistical work. (b) n = 15; mean = 363/15 = 24.20; median = 24. M.D about mean = 59.20/15 = 3.95, coefficient = 3.95/24.20 = 0.163. M.D about median = 59.0/15 = 3.93, coefficient = 3.93/24 = 0.164. ## Mean deviation from mean and from median.
