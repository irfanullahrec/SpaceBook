q 3 | 17 (a) Explain in detail the coefficient of variation. What for we use it? (b) Consider the following data || 17 (الف) تغیر کے عددی سر کی تفصیل سے وضاحت کریں۔ ہم اسے کس لیے استعمال کرتے ہیں؟ (ب) درج ذیل اعداد و شمار پر غور کریں || 17 (a) د تغیر ضریب په تفصیل تشریح کړئ. موږ یې د څه لپاره کاروو؟ (b) لاندې معلومات په پام کې ونیسئ
sol (a) The coefficient of variation is the standard deviation expressed as a percentage of the mean: C.V = (S.D/mean) x 100. It has no units, so it is used to compare the variability of two or more series measured in different units or with different means; the series with the smaller C.V is more consistent (uniform, stable). (b) The data are used in the next question, where the C.V of each factory is computed. ## Definition and use of the C.V.
