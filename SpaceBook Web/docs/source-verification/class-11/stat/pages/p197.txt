q 5 | 4 Explain the concepts of the following (i) Regression (ii) Linear and nonlinear regression (iii) Dependent and independent variables || 4 درج ذیل کے تصورات کی وضاحت کریں (i) ریگریشن (ii) خطی اور غیر خطی ریگریشن (iii) تابع اور آزاد متغیرات || 4 د لاندې مفهومونه تشریح کړئ (i) ریګرېشن (ii) خطي او غیر خطي ریګرېشن (iii) تابع او خپلواک متغیرونه
sol (i) Regression is the statistical technique of estimating (predicting) the value of one variable, the dependent variable, from the known value(s) of another variable, the independent variable, through an equation. (ii) A regression is linear when the relation between the variables is a straight line (y = a + bx) and non-linear when it is a curve (e.g. y = a + bx + cx²). (iii) The dependent variable (y) is the one whose value is estimated or explained; the independent variable (x) is the one whose value is known or given and is used to estimate y. ## Definitions.
q 5 | 5 What do you mean by (i) Regression lines (ii) Regression coefficient (iii) Least squares principle || 5 آپ کی کیا مراد ہے (i) ریگریشن لکیریں (ii) ریگریشن عددی سر (iii) کم از کم مربعات کا اصول || 5 تاسو څه مطلب لرئ (i) د ریګرېشن کرښې (ii) ریګرېشن ضریب (iii) د لږ تر لږه مربعاتو اصل
sol (i) A regression line is the straight line that best fits the points of the scatter diagram and is used to estimate one variable from the other; there are two, y on x and x on y. (ii) A regression coefficient is the slope b of the regression line: the average change in the dependent variable for a one-unit change in the independent variable (b_y.x = Σ(x - x̄)(y - ȳ)/Σ(x - x̄)²). (iii) The least squares principle chooses the line for which the sum of the squares of the vertical deviations Σ(y - ŷ)² is the smallest. ## Definitions.
q 5 | 6 Distinguish between the following. (i) Simple and multiple regression. (ii) Scatter diagram and curve fitting. || 6 درج ذیل میں فرق کریں۔ (i) سادہ اور متعدد ریگریشن۔ (ii) بکھراؤ خاکہ اور منحنی فٹ کرنا۔ || 6 په لاندې کې توپیر وکړئ. (i) ساده او څو‌ګونی ریګرېشن. (ii) د خپرېدو ډیاګرام او د منحني برابرول.
sol (i) Simple regression has one independent variable (y = a + bx); multiple regression has two or more (y = a + b1x1 + b2x2 + ...). (ii) A scatter diagram is a graph of the plotted pairs (x, y) that shows the kind of relationship; curve fitting finds the equation of the line or curve that best represents the points, e.g. by the method of least squares. ## Distinctions.

q 5 | 8 For the following data, find the linear regression equation for predicting y from x || 8 درج ذیل اعداد و شمار کے لیے x سے y کی پیش گوئی کے لیے خطی ریگریشن مساوات معلوم کریں || 8 د لاندې معلوماتو لپاره د x څخه د y د وړاندوینې لپاره خطي ریګرېشن معادله ومومئ

q i | Fit a linear regression equation of y on x and estimate the blood pressure of a 70 years old person. || x پر y کی خطی ریگریشن مساوات فٹ کریں اور 70 سال کی عمر کے شخص کے بلڈ پریشر کا تخمینہ لگائیں۔ || د x پر y خطي ریګرېشن معادله برابره کړئ او د 70 کلن کس د وینې فشار اټکل کړئ.
sol n = 8; Σx = 395; Σy = 1070; Σxy = 52995; Σx² = 19863. b = (nΣxy - ΣxΣy)/(nΣx² - (Σx)²) = 1310/2879 = 0.4550; a = ȳ - b x̄ = 133.750 - 0.4550(49.375) = 111.283. Regression equation: ŷ = 111.283 + 0.4550 x. For x = 70 years: ŷ = 111.283 + 0.4550(70) = 143.1, so the estimated systolic blood pressure of a 70-year-old person is about 143. ## Regression of blood pressure on age.

sol The regression coefficient b is the slope of the regression line: the average change in y for a one-unit increase in x. Here b = 0.4550, which means that, on average, systolic blood pressure rises by about 0.46 units for each additional year of age. ## Interpretation of the slope found in the previous question.
