q 1 | 23 Differentiate between historigram and histogram. || 23 ہسٹوریگرام اور ہسٹوگرام میں فرق کریں۔ || 23 د هیسټوریګرام او هیسټوګرام ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol A histogram is the graph of a continuous frequency distribution: the class boundaries are on the x-axis and the frequencies on the y-axis, and the adjacent rectangles show the frequencies. A historigram is the graph of a time series: time (year, month) on the x-axis and the value of the variable on the y-axis, joined by line segments. ## Difference.
