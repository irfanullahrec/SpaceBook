q 2 | 17 Explain the relationship among the mean median and mode for symmetrical and skewed distributions. || 17 متناسب اور کج تقسیمات کے لیے اوسط وسطانیہ اور عادہ کے درمیان تعلق کی وضاحت کریں۔ || 17 د متناسبو او کوړو ویشونو لپاره د اوسط میانې او مود ترمنځ اړیکه تشریح کړئ.
sol In a symmetrical distribution the mean, median and mode are equal (mean = median = mode). In a positively skewed distribution (tail to the right) mean > median > mode; in a negatively skewed distribution (tail to the left) mean < median < mode. For moderately skewed distributions the empirical relation is mode = 3 median − 2 mean. ## Relationship among the averages.
q 2 | 18 For a skewed distribution median = 36, mean = 40.5. What will be the value of mode? || 18 ایک کج تقسیم کے لیے وسطانیہ = 36، اوسط = 40.5۔ عادہ کی قدر کیا ہوگی؟ || 18 د یوه کوړ ویش لپاره میانه = 36، اوسط = 40.5. د مود ارزښت به څومره وي؟
sol Mode = 3 median − 2 mean = 3(36) − 2(40.5) = 108 − 81 = 27. ## Empirical relationship.

q 2 | 22 Define geometric mean and harmonic mean. What is the empirical relationship among G.M, H.M and A.M.? || 22 ہندسی اوسط اور ہارمونی اوسط کی تعریف کریں۔ G.M، H.M اور A.M کے درمیان تجرباتی تعلق کیا ہے؟ || 22 هندسي اوسط او هارمونیک اوسط تعریف کړئ. د G.M، H.M او A.M ترمنځ تجربوي اړیکه څه ده؟
sol Geometric mean: the n-th root of the product of n positive values, G.M = (x₁x₂...xₙ)^(1/n) (antilog of the mean of logs). Harmonic mean: the reciprocal of the arithmetic mean of the reciprocals, H.M = n/Σ(1/x). For positive unequal values A.M > G.M > H.M, and G.M² = A.M × H.M for two values; they are equal only when all values are equal. ## Definitions and relationship.
