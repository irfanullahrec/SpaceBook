q 5 | 12 Define the term correlation, simple correlation and multiple correlation. || 12 اصطلاح ارتباط، سادہ ارتباط اور متعدد ارتباط کی تعریف کریں۔ || 12 د همتړاو، ساده همتړاو او څو‌ګونی همتړاو اصطلاح تعریف کړئ.
sol Correlation is the statistical measure of the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two (or more) variables, i.e. how far a change in one variable is accompanied by a change in the other. Simple correlation is the correlation between two variables only (e.g. height and weight); multiple correlation is the relationship of one variable with two or more other variables taken together (e.g. a crop yield with rainfall and fertilizer). ## Definitions.
q 5 | 13 Explain the following. (i) Correlation (ii) Positive correlation (iii) Negative correlation (iv) Coefficient of correlation. || 13 درج ذیل کی وضاحت کریں۔ (i) ارتباط (ii) مثبت ارتباط (iii) منفی ارتباط (iv) ارتباطی عددی سر۔ || 13 لاندې تشریح کړئ. (i) همتړاو (ii) مثبت همتړاو (iii) منفي همتړاو (iv) د همتړاو ضریب.
sol (i) Correlation: the degree of linear association between two variables. (ii) Positive correlation: the variables move in the same direction (both increase or both decrease), 0 < r <= 1. (iii) Negative correlation: the variables move in opposite directions (one increases when the other decreases), -1 <= r < 0. (iv) Coefficient of correlation: the number r = Σ(x - x̄)(y - ȳ)/sqrt(Σ(x - x̄)²Σ(y - ȳ)²), always between -1 and +1, which measures the strength and direction of the linear relation; r = 0 means no linear relation. ## Definitions.

sol (a) Rank correlation measures the strength of agreement between two sets of ranks (Spearman's rho = 1 - 6Σd²/(n(n² - 1))). The product moment (Pearson) coefficient is used when the data are quantitative and the relation is linear; the rank correlation coefficient is used when the data are only ranks (qualitative or ordinal), when exact values are not reliable, or when there are extreme values. (b) Ranks of x (largest = 1): 8, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7; ranks of y: 1, 8, 6, 5, 7, 3, 4, 2; d² = 49, 49, 16, 4, 9, 4, 4, 25; Σd² = 160; rho = 1 - 6(160)/(8 x 63) = -0.905. ## Spearman rank correlation.

q 5 | 19 (a) what do you mean by ranking of tied observations? Explain how you would find the coefficient of rank correlation for tied ranks. (b) Calculate rank correlation coefficient for the following data. || 19 (الف) برابر مشاہدات کی درجہ بندی سے آپ کی کیا مراد ہے؟ وضاحت کریں کہ آپ برابر درجوں کے لیے درجاتی ارتباط کا عددی سر کیسے معلوم کریں گے۔ (ب) درج ذیل اعداد و شمار کے لیے درجاتی ارتباطی عددی سر حساب کریں۔ || 19 (a) د برابرو کتنو له رتبه‌بندۍ څخه تاسو څه مطلب لرئ؟ تشریح کړئ چې تاسو به د برابرو رتبو لپاره د رتبه‌یي همتړاو ضریب څنګه ومومئ. (b) د لاندې معلوماتو لپاره رتبه‌یي همتړاو ضریب محاسبه کړئ.
sol (a) When two or more observations have the same value they are tied; each is given the average of the ranks they would have occupied (e.g. two values tied for 3rd and 4th place each get 3.5). Then Spearman's coefficient is corrected by adding the term (m³ - m)/12 for each group of m tied values to Σd²: rho = 1 - 6[Σd² + Σ(m³ - m)/12]/(n(n² - 1)). (b) Ranks of x (largest = 1): 7.0, 9.0, 8.0, 4.5, 10.0, 2.0, 6.0, 4.5, 3.0, 1.0; ranks of y: 5.5, 10.0, 5.5, 2.0, 8.0, 4.0, 9.0, 1.0, 7.0, 3.0; Σd² = 65.0; x has the tie 68, 68 (m = 2) and y the tie 66, 66 (m = 2), so the correction is (8 - 2)/12 + (8 - 2)/12 = 1; rho = 1 - 6(65.0 + 1)/(10 x 99) = 0.600. ## Rank correlation with tied ranks.
