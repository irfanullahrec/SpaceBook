q 6 | 3 Explain time series and describe it different components. || 3 وقتی سلسلے کی وضاحت کریں اور اس کے مختلف اجزاء بیان کریں۔ || 3 د وخت لړۍ تشریح کړئ او د هغې بېلابېلې برخې بیان کړئ.
sol A time series is a set of observations of a variable arranged in order of time (e.g. yearly production). Components: (1) secular trend, the long-term movement upward or downward; (2) seasonal variations, regular changes that repeat within a year because of weather, customs or festivals; (3) cyclical variations, wave-like movements about the trend that last more than a year, such as business cycles; (4) irregular (random) variations, unpredictable changes due to strikes, floods, war or fire. ## Definition and components.
q 6 | 4 Define time series analysis. Write various components of a time series. || 4 وقتی سلسلے کے تجزیے کی تعریف کریں۔ وقتی سلسلے کے مختلف اجزاء لکھیں۔ || 4 د وخت لړۍ تحلیل تعریف کړئ. د وخت لړۍ بېلابېلې برخې ولیکئ.
sol Time series analysis is the study of a time series by separating it into its components in order to understand its past behaviour, to compare it with other series, and to forecast. The components are the secular trend (T), the seasonal variation (S), the cyclical variation (C) and the irregular variation (I). ## Definition and components.
q 6 | 5 Describe the various methods of measuring secular trend in a time series. || 5 وقتی سلسلے میں صدیوی رجحان ناپنے کے مختلف طریقے بیان کریں۔ || 5 په وخت لړۍ کې د عمري تمایل د اندازه کولو بېلابېلې طریقې بیان کړئ.
sol The secular trend is measured by (1) the freehand (graphic) method: plot the data and draw a smooth curve by eye; (2) the method of semi-averages: divide the data into two equal parts, find the average of each part and draw the line through the two averages; (3) the method of moving averages: replace each value by the mean of a fixed number of consecutive values; (4) the method of least squares: fit a straight line (or parabola) so that Σ(y - ŷ)² is a minimum. ## Methods of measuring trend.
q 6 | 6 Illustrate the following. (i) Cyclical variations (ii) Irregular variation (iii) The method of semi-average (iv) The least square method || 6 درج ذیل کی مثال دے کر وضاحت کریں۔ (i) دوری تغیرات (ii) بے قاعدہ تغیر (iii) نیم اوسط کا طریقہ (iv) کم از کم مربعات کا طریقہ || 6 لاندې په بېلګې سره تشریح کړئ. (i) دوري تغیرات (ii) بې‌قاعده تغیر (iii) د نیمه اوسط طریقه (iv) د لږ تر لږه مربعاتو طریقه
sol (i) Cyclical variations are wave-like movements lasting more than a year, e.g. the business cycle of prosperity, recession, depression and recovery. (ii) Irregular variations are unpredictable changes due to chance events such as a flood, a strike or a war. (iii) The method of semi-averages divides the data into two equal halves, finds the mean of each, plots the means against the mid-years and joins them by a straight line, which is the trend. (iv) The method of least squares fits ŷ = a + bx so that Σ(y - ŷ)² is the least; with Σx = 0, a = Σy/n and b = Σxy/Σx². ## Illustrations.

q 6 | 8 Distinguish between the additive and multiplicative models in time series analysis. || 8 وقتی سلسلے کے تجزیے میں جمعی اور ضربی ماڈلز میں فرق کریں۔ || 8 د وخت لړۍ په تحلیل کې د جمعي او ضربي ماډلونو تر منځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Additive model: Y = T + S + C + I, the components are added and are assumed to be independent of each other (all in the units of Y). Multiplicative model: Y = T x S x C x I, the components are multiplied, which assumes that the seasonal, cyclical and irregular movements are proportions of the trend; it is used when the variations grow with the level of the trend, and taking logarithms turns it into an additive model. ## Additive vs multiplicative.
