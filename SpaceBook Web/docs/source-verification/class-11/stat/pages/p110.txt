sol (i) By direct method ## (i) By direct method || (i) براہِ راست طریقے سے || (i) په مستقیم طریقه
eq S=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sum f(x-\bar{x})^2}{\sum f}}
eq S^2=\dfrac{\sum f(x-\bar{x})^2}{\sum f}

eq \bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}=\dfrac{158}{50}=3.16
eq S=\sqrt{\dfrac{72.72}{50}}=\sqrt{1.4544}=1.21\qquad\text{(Absolute dispersion)}
eq S^2=\dfrac{72.72}{50}=1.4545
eq \text{Coefficient of S.D}=\dfrac{S}{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{1.22}{3.16}=0.39\qquad\text{(Relative dispersion)}
