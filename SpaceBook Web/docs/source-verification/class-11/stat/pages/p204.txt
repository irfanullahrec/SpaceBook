## Solution: || حل: || حل:
sol (a) Here the number of years is even, the data is divided in to two equal parts, each having four observations. Simple average of each part is computed and is written at the middle of the group. These averages are plotted on the graph paper. Then a line is drawn crossing these two points, which is the required semi-average trend line. ## (a) Here the number of years is even, the data is divided in to two equal parts, each having four observations.
