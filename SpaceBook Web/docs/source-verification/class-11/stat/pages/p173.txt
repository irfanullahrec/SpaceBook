q 4 | 4 Explain the concept of an index number. Differentiate between simple and composite index numbers? || 4 اشاریہ عدد کے تصور کی وضاحت کریں۔ سادہ اور مرکب اشاریہ اعداد میں فرق کریں؟ || 4 د شاخص شمېرې مفهوم تشریح کړئ. په ساده او مرکب شاخص شمېرو کې توپیر وکړئ؟
sol An index number is a statistical measure (a special kind of average) that shows the relative change in a variable or a group of related variables, such as prices, quantities or values, over time, place or another characteristic, with the base period equal to 100. A simple index number measures the change of a single commodity, e.g. the price relative (p1/p0) x 100. A composite index number measures the combined change of a group of commodities and is obtained by combining their prices or quantities (simple aggregative, average of relatives, or weighted), e.g. the consumer price index. ## Concept, simple vs composite.
q 4 | 5 Define an index number. Describe the types of an index number. || 5 اشاریہ عدد کی تعریف کریں۔ اشاریہ عدد کی اقسام بیان کریں۔ || 5 شاخص شمېره تعریف کړئ. د شاخص شمېرې ډولونه بیان کړئ.
sol An index number is a ratio, expressed as a percentage, which measures the average change in a variable or group of variables between two periods or places. Types: (i) by what is measured: price index, quantity (volume) index and value index numbers; (ii) by the number of items: simple and composite; (iii) by weighting: unweighted and weighted (Laspeyre, Paasche, Fisher); (iv) by the base: fixed base and chain base; (v) by use: wholesale price, consumer price (cost of living), industrial production, share price index numbers, etc. ## Definition and types.
q 4 | 6 Discuss the main steps involved in the construction of index numbers of wholesale prices. || 6 تھوک قیمتوں کے اشاریہ اعداد کی تعمیر کے اہم مراحل پر بحث کریں۔ || 6 د عمده بیو د شاخص شمېرو د جوړولو اصلي مرحلې وڅېړئ.
sol Main steps: (1) fix the purpose and scope of the index; (2) select the commodities (important, representative, staple and of standard quality); (3) choose the base period (a normal year, not too far back); (4) collect the wholesale prices of the commodities from the principal markets, at the same time and for the same quality; (5) choose the average (geometric or arithmetic mean, or the median); (6) assign weights according to the importance of each commodity; (7) choose the formula (e.g. Laspeyre, Paasche or Fisher); (8) compute the index and publish it. ## Steps in the construction of a wholesale price index.
