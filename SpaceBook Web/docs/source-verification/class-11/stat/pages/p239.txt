q 7 | 3 Explain vital statistics. Bring out the uses of vital statistics. || 3 حیاتیاتی شماریات کی وضاحت کریں۔ حیاتیاتی شماریات کے استعمالات بیان کریں۔ || 3 حیاتي احصایې تشریح کړئ. د حیاتي احصایو کارونې بیان کړئ.
sol Vital statistics are the statistics of vital events of human life: births, deaths, marriages, divorces and migration. Uses: they show the growth of the population, the birth, death and fertility rates; they are used in planning health, education, housing and food supply; in estimating the population between two censuses; in insurance and pension calculations; in comparing the health and social conditions of different regions; and in demographic research. ## Meaning and uses of vital statistics.
q 7 | 4 Describe the methods of collecting vital statistics. || 4 حیاتیاتی شماریات جمع کرنے کے طریقے بیان کریں۔ || 4 د حیاتي احصایو د راټولولو طریقې بیان کړئ.
sol Vital statistics are collected by (1) the registration system: births, deaths and marriages are registered with the local authorities, which is the main continuous source; (2) the census: a complete count of the population at a fixed date, usually every ten years; (3) sample surveys: a sample of households is interviewed about births, deaths and migration; (4) other records such as hospital, school and police records. ## Methods of collection.
q 7 | 5 Define the following: (i) Rates and ratios (ii) Crude death rate (iii) Age-specific death rate (iv) Standardized death rate. (v) Sex-ratio. || 5 درج ذیل کی تعریف کریں: (i) شرحیں اور تناسب (ii) خام شرحِ اموات (iii) عمر کے لحاظ سے مخصوص شرحِ اموات (iv) معیاری شرحِ اموات۔ (v) جنسی تناسب۔ || 5 لاندې تعریف کړئ: (i) کچې او نسبتونه (ii) خامه د مړینې کچه (iii) د عمر له مخې ځانګړې د مړینې کچه (iv) معیاري د مړینې کچه. (v) د جنس نسبت.
sol (i) A rate is the number of events (e.g. births) per unit (usually 1000) of the population exposed to the risk; a ratio compares two quantities of the same kind, one not necessarily part of the other (e.g. males to females). (ii) Crude death rate = (total deaths in the year/mid-year population) x 1000. (iii) Age-specific death rate = (deaths in an age group/mid-year population of that group) x 1000. (iv) Standardized death rate is the adjusted death rate that removes the effect of different age composition; by the direct method S.T.D.R = Σ(d_i/p_i)P_i/ΣP_i x 1000, and by the indirect method S.T.D.R = Σd_i/Σ(D_i/P_i)p_i x C.D.R of the standard population. (v) Sex ratio = (number of males/number of females) x 100. ## Definitions.
q 7 | 6 Differentiate between: (i) Crude birth rate and age-specific fertility rate. (ii) Gross reproduction rate and net reproduction rate. (iii) Total fertility rate and standardized birth rate. || 6 فرق کریں: (i) خام شرحِ پیدائش اور عمر کے لحاظ سے مخصوص شرحِ زرخیزی۔ (ii) مجموعی شرحِ تولید اور خالص شرحِ تولید۔ (iii) کل شرحِ زرخیزی اور معیاری شرحِ پیدائش۔ || 6 توپیر وکړئ: (i) خام د زېږون کچه او د عمر له مخې ځانګړې د زېږون‌توان کچه. (ii) ناخالصه د بیا زېږون کچه او خالصه د بیا زېږون کچه. (iii) ټوله د زېږون‌توان کچه او معیاري د زېږون کچه.
sol (i) The crude birth rate is the live births of the year per 1000 of the total mid-year population (B/P x 1000); the age-specific fertility rate is the births to women of one age group per 1000 women of that group (B_i/P_i x 1000), so it allows for the age of the mothers. (ii) The gross reproduction rate counts the female children a woman would bear in her life, ignoring their mortality; the net reproduction rate allows for the mortality of the girls until they reach child-bearing age, so N.R.R <= G.R.R. (iii) The total fertility rate is the sum of the age-specific fertility rates times the class width (the average number of children per woman, per 1000 women); the standardized birth rate is the birth rate adjusted to a standard age composition so that populations can be compared. ## Differences.

sol Crude death rate X = ΣD/ΣP x 1000 = 2176/200000 x 1000 = 10.88 per 1000; Y = 1239/100000 x 1000 = 12.39 per 1000. No standard population is given, so the combined population of the two cities (21000, 77000, 182000, 20000) is taken as the standard. Direct method S.T.D.R = Σ(d_i/p_i)P_i/ΣP_i x 1000: city X = 10.63 per 1000, city Y = 13.18 per 1000. So after allowing for the age composition city Y has the higher death rate. ## Crude and standardized rates.

q 7 | 8 Write short notes on the following: (i) Crude birth rate (ii) Crude death rate (iii) Total fertility rate (iv) Sex ratio (v) Vital index || 8 درج ذیل پر مختصر نوٹ لکھیں: (i) خام شرحِ پیدائش (ii) خام شرحِ اموات (iii) کل شرحِ زرخیزی (iv) جنسی تناسب (v) حیاتیاتی اشاریہ || 8 پر لاندې لنډې یادښتونه ولیکئ: (i) خام د زېږون کچه (ii) خامه د مړینې کچه (iii) ټوله د زېږون‌توان کچه (iv) د جنس نسبت (v) حیاتي شاخص
sol (i) Crude birth rate = (live births in a year/mid-year population) x 1000. (ii) Crude death rate = (deaths in a year/mid-year population) x 1000. (iii) Total fertility rate = Σ(age-specific fertility rates) x class interval, the number of children a woman would bear in the child-bearing years (divided by 1000). (iv) Sex ratio = (males/females) x 100, the number of males per 100 females. (v) Vital index = (live births/deaths) x 100; a value above 100 means that births exceed deaths and the population is growing. ## Short notes.
