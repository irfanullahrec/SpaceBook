## Solution: || حل: || حل:
eq \text{As }x_L=18,\ x_S=10\ \ \therefore\ R=x_L-x_S=18-10=8\qquad\text{(Absolute dispersion)}
eq \text{Coefficient of range}=\dfrac{x_L-x_S}{x_L+x_S}=\dfrac{18-10}{18+10}=\dfrac{8}{28}=0.29\ (\text{Relative dispersion})=29\%

eq R=x_L-x_S=74-60=14\qquad\text{(Absolute dispersion)}
eq \text{Coefficient of range}=\dfrac{x_L-x_S}{x_L+x_S}=\dfrac{74-60}{74+60}=\dfrac{14}{134}=0.10=10\%\ (\text{Relative dispersion})
