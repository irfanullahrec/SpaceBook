eq \sum(x-\bar{x})=0\quad\text{(In case of ungrouped data) and}

p Proof: || ثبوت: || ثبوت:
eq \text{Consider }\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar{x})=\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i-n\bar{x}\quad\text{(as }\bar{x}\text{ is constant)}
eq =\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i-n\,\dfrac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i}{n}\quad\left(\text{as }\bar{x}=\dfrac{\sum x}{n}\right)
eq =\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i-\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i=0
