q 15 | How was Eddie killed? || ایڈی کیسے مارا گیا؟ || ایډي څنګه ووژل شو؟
sol Model answer: Eddie was a nineteen-year-old soldier killed when a shell fell into a shallow trench where he and his men had been waiting for eight days and nights; the shell destroyed five men completely and nothing was left to bury. ## Mrs. Meldon's account, from a young man of the same battalion.
q 16 | Describe the physical and mental conditions of Mrs. Meldon after the death of her son Eddie. || اپنے بیٹے ایڈی کی موت کے بعد مسز میلڈن کی جسمانی اور ذہنی حالت بیان کریں۔ || د خپل زوی ایډي له مړینې وروسته د مېرمن میلډن بدني او ذهني حالت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: After Eddie's death Mrs. Meldon is physically weak and nervous, dressed in black, "plainly suffering deeply", lonely (her husband Tom died of a broken heart) and mentally restless and haunted by the memory of her son; she cannot forget him and wishes she had died. ## Based on the play.
q 17 | Why does Mrs. Meldon hate war? || مسز میلڈن جنگ سے نفرت کیوں کرتی ہے؟ || مېرمن میلډن ولې له جګړې کرکه لري؟
sol Model answer: Mrs. Meldon hates war because it took her only son, Eddie, just when his life was beginning, and destroyed him so that nothing was left to bury; her husband died of a broken heart; she is left lonely. For her, war is "organised butchery of boys". ## Based on the play.
q 18 | What is Mrs. Meldon’s view about war? || جنگ کے بارے میں مسز میلڈن کا نقطۂ نظر کیا ہے؟ || د جګړې په اړه د مېرمن میلډن لیدلوری څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Mrs. Meldon thinks that war is cruel, meaningless and horrible, an organised killing of young men that brings only sorrow to mothers and wives. She wants to abolish it, not to make it more efficient. ## Based on the play.
q 19 | What is Prof. Corrie’s view about war? || جنگ کے بارے میں پروفیسر کوری کا نقطۂ نظر کیا ہے؟ || د جګړې په اړه د پروفیسر کوري لیدلوری څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Professor Corrie thinks that war is inevitable ("we're altogether too pugnacious") and that the way is to make it so horrible and quick that nations will not fight unless absolutely forced; he sees war from the broad point of view of statesmen and science, ignoring human suffering. ## Based on the play.
q 20 | Bring out the ideological conflict between Mrs. Meldon and Prof. Corrie. || مسز میلڈن اور پروفیسر کوری کے درمیان نظریاتی تنازع واضح کریں۔ || د مېرمن میلډن او پروفیسر کوري ترمنځ ایډیالوژیکي شخړه روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer: Mrs. Meldon represents the humanitarian view: human life and peace are most important, war must be abolished and science must serve life. Corrie represents the scientific-nationalist view: progress means more powerful weapons, national power and fame; everything else is sentiment. Their quarrel is between compassion and cold reason. ## Based on the play.
