p Summary of paragraph 1: The narrator grew up in Estepona, in the south of Spain. When he was sixteen his father said he could drive him to a remote village, Mijas, about 18 miles away, on condition that he take the car to a nearby garage to be serviced. Having just learned to drive and rarely getting the chance, he agreed gladly, dropped his father in Mijas, promised to pick him up at 4 p.m., and left the car at the garage.
