exercise Reading Comprehension
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | Why did the young Seagull have to join the company of his family? || نوجوان سمندری چیل کو اپنے خاندان کی صحبت میں کیوں شامل ہونا پڑا؟ || ځوان سمندري ټوپک ولې باید د خپلې کورنۍ ملګرتیا ته ورشي؟
sol Model answer: The young seagull was the only one of the family who had not yet flown. His brothers and sister had already flown away, but he was afraid, so he stayed alone on the ledge. Hunger and the call of his family made him join them, because it was time for him to learn to fly. ## From the story 'His First Flight' by Liam O'Flaherty.
q 2 | Why don't his parents, his brothers and sister give him food? || اس کے والدین، بھائی اور بہن اسے کھانا کیوں نہیں دیتے؟ || د هغه مور پلار، ورونه او خور ولې هغه ته خواړه نه ورکوي؟
sol Model answer: His parents, brothers and sister did not give him food because they wanted to force him to fly. They knew his hunger would push him to overcome his fear, so they ate in front of him and left him hungry. ## Based on the story.
q 3 | What was the attitude of the family towards the young Seagull? Was it agreeable to you or not? Explain || خاندان کا نوجوان سمندری چیل کے ساتھ رویہ کیا تھا؟ کیا یہ آپ کے لیے قابلِ قبول تھا یا نہیں؟ وضاحت کریں || د کورنۍ چلند د ځوان سمندري ټوپک سره څنګه و؟ ایا دا ستاسو لپاره منلو وړ و که نه؟ تشریح کړئ
sol Model answer: The family was strict and harsh: they mocked him, scolded him, ignored him and kept food from him because they wanted him to learn to fly. Their aim was good, but I do not fully agree with the method: encouragement and patience would have been kinder, though a little pressure can help a frightened learner to try. ## Open-ended; other valid opinions are possible if explained.
q 4 | Describe in your own words the feelings, actions and thoughts of the young Seagull soon after his fall. || نوجوان سمندری چیل کے گرنے کے فوراً بعد اس کے احساسات، اعمال اور خیالات کو اپنے الفاظ میں بیان کریں۔ || د ځوان سمندري ټوپک د غورځېدو سمدستي وروسته احساسات، عملونه او فکرونه په خپلو کلمو کې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: After his fall the young seagull was first terrified and sure he would die. Then, to his surprise, he found that his wings supported him; he floated and glided, felt joy and wonder, and flew down to the sea and the others. He realised he could fly and that his fear had been unnecessary. ## Based on the story.
q 5 | What could be the author's purpose behind writing the story 'His First Flight'? || کہانی ‘اس کی پہلی پرواز’ لکھنے کے پیچھے مصنف کا مقصد کیا ہو سکتا ہے؟ || د کیسې ‘د هغه لومړنۍ الوتنه’ لیکلو شاته د لیکوال موخه څه کېدای شي؟
sol Model answer: The author wants to show that fear is often greater than the danger and that we discover our strength only when we try. It also shows how parents' firm guidance, and the push of necessity (hunger), help the young to become independent. ## Open-ended; other valid purposes are possible.
q 6 | Compare and contrast the attitude of the family members before and after the flight of the young sea gull in 'His First Flight'. || ‘اس کی پہلی پرواز’ میں نوجوان سمندری چیل کی پرواز سے پہلے اور بعد خاندان کے افراد کے رویے کا موازنہ اور تضاد کریں۔ || په ‘د هغه لومړنۍ الوتنه’ کې د ځوان سمندري ټوپک له الوتنې مخکې او وروسته د کورنۍ د غړو چلند پرتله کړئ او توپیر یې ښودل.
sol Model answer: Before the flight the family were harsh: they shouted, taunted, ignored him and starved him. After he flew, they were proud and joyful: they praised him, flew around him and gave him food. Their goal throughout was to make him independent, but their treatment changed from pressure to celebration. ## Based on the story.
q 7 | Identify the sentence in the first paragraph of ‘His First Flight’ which shows cause and effect. || ‘اس کی پہلی پرواز’ کے پہلے پیراگراف میں وہ جملہ پہچانیں جو علت اور معلول ظاہر کرتا ہے۔ || د ‘د هغه لومړنۍ الوتنه’ په لومړي پراګراف کې هغه جمله وپېژنئ چې علت او معلول ښيي.
sol Model answer: "He felt certain that his wings would never support him; so he bent his head and ran away back to the little hole under the ledge." The cause is his certainty (fear) that his wings would fail; the effect is that he ran away and did not fly. ## Another cause-effect sentence in the first paragraph: he was afraid, so he did not follow his brothers.
q 8 | Read any paragraph from the text to identify the main idea and the sentences that support main idea. || متن سے کوئی بھی پیراگراف پڑھیں تاکہ مرکزی خیال اور مرکزی خیال کی تائید کرنے والے جملے پہچانیں۔ || له متن څخه هر پراګراف ولولئ ترڅو اصلي نظر او هغه جملې وپېژنئ چې اصلي نظر ملاتړ کوي.
sol Model answer: Main idea of the first paragraph: the young seagull was left alone on the ledge because he was too afraid to fly. Supporting sentences: his brothers and sister had already flown; he had been afraid to fly with them; the sea looked miles below; he felt his wings would not support him. ## Open-ended; any paragraph with a correct main idea and supporting details is acceptable.
q 9 | Read the second paragraph of the text to identify transitional devices and state their functions. || متن کا دوسرا پیراگراف پڑھیں تاکہ انتقالی ذرائع پہچانیں اور ان کے افعال بیان کریں۔ || د متن دویم پراګراف ولولئ ترڅو انتقالي وسایل وپېژنئ او د هغو دندې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Transitional words such as "even when", "and when", "so" and "then" connect the ideas: "even when" adds contrast (he did not follow although the others did), "and when" shows time/sequence, "so" shows result and "then" shows the next step. ## Open-ended; the transitions in the paragraph the student reads may be listed.
