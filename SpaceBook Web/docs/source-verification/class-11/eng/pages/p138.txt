## Reading Comprehension || مطالعے کا فہم || د لوست پوهه
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | Write the central idea of ‘A Long Walk Home’. || ‘گھر تک ایک طویل پیدل سفر’ کا مرکزی خیال لکھیں۔ || د ‘کور ته یو اوږد پیاده سفر’ مرکزي مفکوره ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: The central idea is that honesty is the basis of trust and that lying hurts those who love us. The narrator lies to his father about being late, and his father's silent walk of eighteen miles teaches him a lesson about telling the truth that he never forgets. ## 'A Long Walk Home' by Jason Bocarro.
q 2 | Analyze the pattern of organizing paragraphs in the text and tell what pattern has been used? || متن میں پیراگرافوں کی ترتیب کے نمونے کا تجزیہ کریں اور بتائیں کہ کون سا نمونہ استعمال کیا گیا ہے؟ || په متن کې د پاراګرافونو د ترتیب بڼه تحلیل کړئ او ووایئ چې کومه بڼه کارول شوې؟
sol Model answer: The paragraphs follow the chronological (time-sequence) pattern: the story is told from the day the boy drives his father to Mijas, through the cinema, the lie, the discovery and the walk, to the lesson learned. ## Chronological order.
q 3 | What was the most distressing and painful experience for Jason? || جیسن کے لیے سب سے تکلیف دہ اور اذیت ناک تجربہ کون سا تھا؟ || د جیسن لپاره تر ټولو دردناک او ستړی کوونکی تجربه کومه وه؟
sol Model answer: The most distressing and painful experience for Jason was watching his father walk for eighteen miles in silence along the dusty roads in pain and disappointment, while he followed in the car pleading for forgiveness. ## From the last paragraphs of the story.
