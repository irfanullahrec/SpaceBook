exercise Reading Comprehension
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What is the central idea of Keen’s essay and how is it related to the title? || کین کے مضمون کا مرکزی خیال کیا ہے اور اس کا عنوان سے کیا تعلق ہے؟ || د کین د مقالې مرکزي مفکوره څه ده او د سرلیک سره یې څه اړیکه ده؟
sol Model answer: Keen's central idea is that the strength of the family is essential for a free civilization: it is the first line of defence against dehumanisation, it teaches love, values and freedom, and it must be put back at the centre of our lives. The title "The Importance of Family" states exactly this subject. ## Essay by Sam Keen.
q 2 | Analyze any two paragraphs of the essay ‘The Importance of Family’ and classify the sentences that support the main idea under the headings of definition, example, cause and effect, facts and opinions. || مضمون ‘خاندان کی اہمیت’ کے کوئی دو پیراگراف تجزیہ کریں اور مرکزی خیال کی تائید کرنے والے جملوں کو تعریف، مثال، سبب اور اثر، حقائق اور آراء کے عنوانات کے تحت درجہ بند کریں۔ || د مقالې ‘د کورنۍ اهمیت’ کوم دوه پاراګرافونه وڅېړئ او هغه جملې چې اصلي مفکوره ملاتړ کوي د تعریف، بېلګې، لامل او اغېز، حقایقو او نظرونو په سرلیکونو لاندې طبقه‌بندي کړئ.
sol Model answer: Paragraph 1 (main idea: the family is essential to a free civilization). Definition/explanation: tyrants and utopian thinkers have always attacked the family. Examples: Plato, Marx and Mao tried to replace it with state institutions. Cause and effect: because the family trains free persons, tyrants first target it. Paragraph 4 (main idea: the word "economics" tells a parable). Fact: "economics" originally meant the management of a household. Cause and effect: after the industrial revolution, factories and banks took over loyalties and displaced the home. Opinion: the loss of home values harmed freedom. ## Open-ended; any two paragraphs analysed correctly are acceptable.
q 3 | What according to Keen is the ‘only revolution’ that will heal the ills of our society? || کین کے مطابق وہ ‘واحد انقلاب’ کون سا ہے جو ہمارے معاشرے کی بیماریوں کا علاج کرے گا؟ || د کین په وینا هغه ‘یوازېنی انقلاب’ کوم دی چې زموږ د ټولنې ناروغۍ ښه کوي؟
sol Model answer: According to Keen, the only revolution that will heal our society is one in which men and women together put the creation of a rich family life back at the centre of their values, and men realise that they are equally responsible with women for creating and nurturing the family and children. ## Paragraph 5 of the essay.
q 4 | Is the thesis of the essay ‘The Importance of Family’ implicitly or explicitly stated? Explain. || کیا مضمون ‘خاندان کی اہمیت’ کا مرکزی دعویٰ (تھیسس) ضمنی طور پر بیان کیا گیا ہے یا صریح طور پر؟ وضاحت کریں۔ || ایا د مقالې ‘د کورنۍ اهمیت’ مرکزي ادعا ضمني ډول بیان شوې که په څرګند ډول؟ تشریح یې کړئ.
sol Model answer: The thesis is explicitly stated at the beginning of the essay: Keen says that "the strength of the family is essential to a civilization of free people", and the rest of the essay supports it with explanations, history and advice. ## Paragraph 1.
q 5 | What is the point of the 4th paragraph? What does its contents have to do with thesis of the essay? || چوتھے پیراگراف کا نکتہ کیا ہے؟ اس کے مندرجات کا مضمون کے مرکزی دعوے سے کیا تعلق ہے؟ || د ۴ پاراګراف ټکی څه دی؟ د هغه منځپانګه د مقالې د مرکزي ادعا سره څه اړیکه لري؟
sol Model answer: The fourth paragraph explains the history of the word "economics": it originally meant the art of managing a household with thrift and simplicity, but after the industrial revolution it came to mean the production and consumption system. When factory, store and bank took over men's loyalties, the home was displaced. This supports the thesis by showing how the modern economic order has weakened the family and how we have shifted our values. ## Paragraph 4.
q 6 | Keen says, “We heal ourselves by learning to give to our children what we did not receive.” What does he mean? Do you agree? || کین کہتا ہے، “ہم اپنے بچوں کو وہ دینا سیکھ کر شفا پاتے ہیں جو ہمیں نہیں ملا۔” اس کا کیا مطلب ہے؟ کیا آپ متفق ہیں؟ || کین وايي، “موږ ځان ښه کوو د دې زده کړې سره چې خپلو ماشومانو ته هغه ورکړو چې موږ نه دي ترلاسه کړي.” د هغه مطلب څه دی؟ ایا تاسو موافق یاست؟
sol Model answer: He means that the best way to heal our childhood wounds (for example, absent or uncaring parents) is to give our own children the love, time and care we did not get; turning pain into care. I agree, because people who were neglected often become better parents when they decide to break the cycle. ## Open-ended; a reasoned disagreement is also acceptable.
