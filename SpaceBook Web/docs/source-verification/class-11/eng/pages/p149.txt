q 3 | What is the thesis of the ‘University Days’? Is it stated or implied? || ‘یونیورسٹی کے دن’ کا مرکزی دعویٰ (تھیسس) کیا ہے؟ کیا یہ صراحتاً بیان ہوا ہے یا ضمنی ہے؟ || د ‘د پوهنتون ورځې’ مرکزي ادعا (تیسس) څه ده؟ ایا دا څرګنده بیان شوې یا ضمني ده؟
sol Model answer: The thesis is implied, not directly stated: it is that rigid teaching methods fail students who learn differently, and that failing at something does not make a person a failure; a humorous look at one's own failures. ## 'University Days' by James Thurber.
q 4 | What is the implied message of ‘University Days’? || ‘یونیورسٹی کے دن’ کا ضمنی پیغام کیا ہے؟ || د ‘د پوهنتون ورځې’ ضمني پیغام څه دی؟
sol Model answer: The implied message is that teachers should be patient and flexible with students who learn differently, that each person has his own strengths and weaknesses, and that we can laugh at our own failures. ## Implied meaning of the essay.
q 5 | Why was the instructor irritated with Thurber? || استاد تھربر سے کیوں چڑ گیا تھا؟ || ښوونکی ولې له ترېبر نه ځورېدلی و؟
sol Model answer: The instructor was irritated because Thurber could not see the cell through the microscope, kept saying he saw only milk, and told him that flowers lose their beauty; the instructor believed Thurber was only pretending and not trying. His own method did not work with Thurber. ## Based on the text.
