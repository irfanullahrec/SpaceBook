exercise Reading Comprehension
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What is the central idea of essay ‘Drug Abuse in the Youth of Pakistan’? || مضمون ‘پاکستان کے نوجوانوں میں منشیات کا استعمال’ کا مرکزی خیال کیا ہے؟ || د مقالې ‘په پاکستان کې د ځوانانو ترمنځ د مخدره توکو ناوړه کارول’ اصلي مفکوره څه ده؟
sol Model answer: The central idea is that drug abuse is a growing menace among the educated youth of Pakistan, caused by academic pressure, over-expectation of parents, peer pressure, easy availability of drugs and lack of counselling, and that parents, educational institutions and the government must work together to eradicate it. ## 'Drug Abuse in the Youth of Pakistan'.
q 2 | Analyze the pattern of writing and the order of arranging paragraphs in the essay ‘Drug Abuse in the Youth of Pakistan’ || مضمون ‘پاکستان کے نوجوانوں میں منشیات کا استعمال’ میں تحریر کے نمونے اور پیراگرافوں کی ترتیب کا تجزیہ کریں || د مقالې ‘په پاکستان کې د ځوانانو ترمنځ د مخدره توکو ناوړه کارول’ کې د لیکنې بڼه او د پاراګرافونو ترتیب تحلیل کړئ
sol Model answer: The essay follows a problem-solution pattern with cause and effect. It opens with the alarming statistics (general), then explains the causes (freedom, pressure of studies and parents, easy availability, nexus with drug mafia), then the signs of addiction, and finally the solutions (counselling, education, controlling tobacco, awareness campaigns). The paragraphs are arranged logically from the problem to the remedy. ## Based on the text.
q 3 | Why are the educated youth resorting to drug abuse? Explain it in five lines. || تعلیم یافتہ نوجوان منشیات کے استعمال کی طرف کیوں جا رہے ہیں؟ اسے پانچ سطروں میں بیان کریں۔ || زده‌کړي ځوانان د مخدره توکو ناوړه کارونې ته ولې مخه کوي؟ دا په پنځو کرښو کې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Educated youth turn to drugs because of academic pressure and fear of failure. Parents' high expectations add stress. They also face new freedom and peer pressure at college without guidance. Drugs are cheaply and easily available. Instead of exercise or counselling, some choose drugs as an escape. ## About five lines, as required.
q 4 | Compare and contrast college’s life with that of school’s. Highlights the points of similarities and differences in the light of the essay. || کالج کی زندگی کا اسکول کی زندگی سے موازنہ اور تقابل کریں۔ مضمون کی روشنی میں مماثلت اور فرق کے نکات نمایاں کریں۔ || د کالج ژوند له ښوونځي ژوند سره پرتله او توپیر وکړئ. د مقالې په رڼا کې د ورته‌والي او توپیر ټکي روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer: Similarities: both involve studies, exams, pressure to perform and friendships. Differences: school life is controlled with parents and teachers closely supervising, while college offers freedom to move and choose, living away from family, heavier academic work, less guidance, and stronger peer influence. The freedom and pressure of college make students more exposed to drugs. ## Based on the essay.
q 5 | What should be the role of parents in managing the future goals of their children? || والدین کا اپنے بچوں کے مستقبل کے اہداف سنبھالنے میں کیا کردار ہونا چاہیے؟ || د مورپلار رول د خپلو ماشومانو د راتلونکو هدفونو په سمبالولو کې باید څه وي؟
sol Model answer: Parents should set realistic expectations and not push children beyond their ability, talk openly with them about stress and the dangers of drugs, set a positive example by coping with pressure in healthy ways, watch for signs of addiction, keep a warm and trusting relationship, and seek medical help early. ## Based on the essay.
q 6 | What role can academia play in control of drug abuse? || تعلیمی حلقے منشیات کے استعمال پر قابو پانے میں کیا کردار ادا کر سکتے ہیں؟ || علمي حلقې د مخدره توکو ناوړه کارونې په کنټرول کې کوم رول لوبولی شي؟
sol Model answer: Educational institutions can teach students how to manage stress, offer counselling and exercise/sports programmes under trained instructors, make drug education compulsory, hold orientation weekends and awareness campaigns, control tobacco and drugs on campus, and set up anti-drug youth cells. ## Based on the essay.
q 7 | How important is healthy relationships between parents and children for the overall development of child? || بچے کی مجموعی نشوونما کے لیے والدین اور بچوں کے درمیان صحت مند تعلقات کتنے اہم ہیں؟ || د ماشوم د ټولیزې ودې لپاره د مورپلار او ماشومانو ترمنځ روغې اړیکې څومره مهمې دي؟
sol Model answer: Healthy parent-child relationships are vital because children who feel secure, trusted and loved can talk about problems, learn healthy ways of coping, and have confidence and self-respect. Lack of warmth, trust and structure puts them at greater risk of drug abuse and mental problems. ## Based on the essay.
q 8 | Why should the use of soft drugs be controlled? What are they “gate way” to? || نرم منشیات کے استعمال کو کیوں قابو کیا جانا چاہیے؟ یہ کس چیز کا “دروازہ” ہیں؟ || د نرمو مخدره توکو کارونه ولې باید کنټرول شي؟ دا د څه “دروازه” دي؟
sol Model answer: Soft drugs such as cigarettes, charas, gutka, naswar and pan should be controlled because they are the "gateway" to hard drugs like heroin, opium, cocaine, ice and sheesha: those who start smoking or drinking young are much more likely to try illegal drugs. ## Paragraph 10 of the essay.
