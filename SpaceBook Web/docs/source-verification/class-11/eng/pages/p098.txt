exercise Reading Comprehension
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What arrangements were made by Navasard for the coming home Arshak? || نواسرد نے ارشک کے گھر آنے کے لیے کیا انتظامات کیے؟ || نواسرد د ارشک د کور ته د راتګ لپاره کوم تدارک وکړ؟
sol Model answer: Navasard cleaned the yard and swept the dirt, hammered in a protruding nail, fetched water and sprinkled the yard and floor, cut the biggest watermelon and picked ripe figs and other fruit, borrowed a new tablecloth, covered the table and the couch, and brought kindling for the fire. He kept the white lamb to be killed for shashlyk (roast meat) and waited for Arshak all night. ## 'The White Lamb' by Sero Khanzadian.
q 2 | Why was Navasard so excited at the arrival of Arshak? || نواسرد ارشک کی آمد پر اتنا پرجوش کیوں تھا؟ || نواسرد د ارشک په راتګ ولې دومره په ولوله کې و؟
sol Model answer: Navasard was excited because he had not seen Arshak for ten years, he loved him like his own son (he had brought him up after his parents died), he had sold his belongings to send him to study, and he was proud of the important man Arshak had become. He believed the long wait was finally over. ## Based on the story.
q 3 | Describe the plight of Navasard after the departure of Arshak without meeting him. || ارشک کے اس سے ملے بغیر چلے جانے کے بعد نواسرد کی حالتِ زار بیان کریں۔ || د ارشک له هغه سره له لیدلو پرته د تللو وروسته د نواسرد حال بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: After Arshak left without meeting him, Navasard was heartbroken and ashamed. He had waited all night with the food and the lamb prepared, he fell asleep at the table, and when he woke the neighbour told him Arshak was leaving. He climbed to the roof and watched the car vanish, then walked slowly with sunken eyes and a more stooped back, the white lamb following him. His plight shows bitter loneliness and disappointment. ## Based on the end of the story.
q 4 | What is the central idea of the story ‘The White Lamb’. Is this theme relevant to our culture as well? || کہانی ‘سفید برہ’ کا مرکزی خیال کیا ہے؟ کیا یہ موضوع ہماری ثقافت سے بھی متعلق ہے؟ || د کیسې ‘سپینه وری’ مرکزي مفکوره څه ده؟ ایا دا موضوع زموږ له کلتور سره هم تړاو لري؟
sol Model answer: The central idea is the ingratitude and neglect of a successful child toward an elderly guardian who sacrificed everything, and the pain of the old man's love going unanswered. It is also relevant to our culture, where many young people go to cities or abroad for success, forget their rural parents and elders, and leave them lonely, although our religion and culture teach respect for parents and elders. ## Open-ended; other valid views are possible.
q 5 | Write down the plot of the story ‘The White Lamb’. || کہانی ‘سفید برہ’ کا پلاٹ لکھیں۔ || د کیسې ‘سپینه وری’ پلاټ ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Exposition: old gardener Navasard hears from the team leader that his nephew Arshak, whom he raised, has come back after ten years. Rising action: he joyfully prepares gifts, melons, figs and a white lamb, cleans the house and waits. Climax: Arshak does not come but stays with the chairman, and Navasard waits all night. Falling action and resolution: in the morning he finds that Arshak is leaving in his car. The old man walks sadly down the path, the lamb following him. ## Plot of 'The White Lamb'.
q 6 | Is there any conflict in the story ‘The White Lamb’? If yes, how is it resolved? || کیا کہانی ‘سفید برہ’ میں کوئی تنازع ہے؟ اگر ہاں تو وہ کیسے حل ہوتا ہے؟ || ایا په کیسه ‘سپینه وری’ کې کومه شخړه شته؟ که هو، نو څنګه حل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: Yes. The conflict is between Navasard's deep love and expectation and Arshak's indifference (and pride in his social status). It is also an internal conflict of the old man between hope and disappointment. The conflict is not resolved happily: Arshak leaves without meeting him and Navasard is left alone with his sorrow, which makes the story tragic. ## Conflict type: person vs person and person vs self.
q 7 | How would you resolve the conflict if you were the author of ‘The White Lamb’. || اگر آپ ‘سفید برہ’ کے مصنف ہوتے تو آپ تنازع کو کیسے حل کرتے؟ || که ته د ‘سپینه وری’ لیکوال وای نو دې شخړه به دې څنګه حل کړې وای؟
sol Model answer: I would end the story with Arshak realising his duty. After the chairman's meeting he would hurry to the village, embrace his uncle, eat the shashlyk and promise to visit often or take him to the city. The lamb would be saved as a symbol of love. This resolution would show gratitude and reward the uncle's sacrifice. ## Open-ended; other resolutions are possible.
q 8 | Compare and contrast the character of Navasard and Arshak. || نواسرد اور ارشک کے کردار کا موازنہ اور تقابل کریں۔ || د نواسرد او ارشک کرکټر پرتله او توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Navasard is simple, poor, loving, selfless, patient, humble and loyal; he sacrifices everything for Arshak and finds happiness in giving. Arshak is educated, successful and prosperous, but proud, selfish and ungrateful; he neglects the uncle who raised him. Both are connected by family, but Navasard is rich in feeling, Arshak is rich only in wealth. ## Based on the story.
q 9 | ‘How sharper than a serpent’s tooth it is to have thankless child.’ (Shakespeare). Critically evaluate this statement in the light of the text of lesson ‘The White Lamb’. || ‘ناشکرا اولاد رکھنا سانپ کے دانت سے کتنا زیادہ تیز ہے۔’ (شیکسپیئر)۔ سبق ‘سفید برہ’ کے متن کی روشنی میں اس بیان کا تنقیدی جائزہ لیں۔ || ‘ناشکره اولاد لرل د مار د غاښ نه څومره تېز دی.’ (شکسپیر). د درس ‘سپینه وری’ د متن په رڼا کې دا بیان په انتقادي ډول وارزوئ.
sol Model answer: Shakespeare's line (King Lear) means that the hurt caused by a thankless child is sharper than a snake's bite. The story proves it: Navasard gave all he had, sold his things for Arshak's education and waited ten years, yet Arshak did not even visit his home, and the old man was crushed. The statement is largely true for this story, though we should remember that Arshak's reasons are not given and that ingratitude is one among many human responses. ## Open-ended critical evaluation.
