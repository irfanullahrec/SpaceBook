exercise Reading Comprehension
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What is job interview? || جاب انٹرویو کیا ہے؟ || د دندې مرکه څه ده؟
sol Model answer: A job interview is a formal meeting between an employer (or a panel) and a candidate in which the employer asks questions to judge the candidate's skills, experience, character and suitability for a post, and the candidate learns about the job. ## 'How to Take a Job Interview'.
q 2 | Describe some types of job interviews and their purposes. || جاب انٹرویو کی کچھ اقسام اور ان کے مقاصد بیان کریں۔ || د دندې د مرکو ځینې ډولونه او د هغو موخې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Types: (1) one-to-one interview with one interviewer, to judge personality and skills; (2) panel interview with several interviewers, to get different views and a fair decision; (3) group interview, to see how candidates work with others; (4) telephone/video interview, to screen candidates at a distance; (5) behavioural or stress interviews, to test how a candidate acts under pressure. ## Based on the lesson; the exact list may differ.
q 3 | How should one dress for job interviews? || جاب انٹرویو کے لیے کسی کو کیسا لباس پہننا چاہیے؟ || د دندې مرکو لپاره باید یو څوک څنګه جامې واغوندي؟
sol Model answer: A candidate should dress neatly and formally, in clean, well-pressed, conservative clothes (for men a suit or shirt and trousers, for women a modest professional dress), simple colours, polished shoes, good grooming and little jewellery or perfume, and in a way suitable for the job. ## Based on the lesson.
q 4 | What is the impact of dressing on interview? || انٹرویو پر لباس کا کیا اثر ہوتا ہے؟ || پر مرکه د جامو اغېز څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Dressing creates the first impression and shows seriousness, respect and professionalism; it affects both the interviewer's judgement and the candidate's own confidence. Poor or careless dress may suggest carelessness, while appropriate dress helps the candidate to be taken seriously. ## Based on the lesson.
q 5 | How can a person keep nerves calm during interviews? || انٹرویو کے دوران کوئی شخص اپنے اعصاب کو کیسے پرسکون رکھ سکتا ہے؟ || یو کس د مرکو پر مهال خپل اعصاب څنګه آرام ساتلی شي؟
sol Model answer: A person can keep calm by preparing well (knowing about the job and organisation and practising likely questions), sleeping well, arriving early, breathing slowly and deeply, thinking positively, sitting comfortably and speaking slowly, and treating the interview as a conversation. ## Based on the lesson.
q 6 | What is the importance of first five minutes in job interview? How should the opening of the interview be planned ? || جاب انٹرویو میں پہلے پانچ منٹ کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ انٹرویو کے آغاز کی منصوبہ بندی کیسے کی جانی چاہیے؟ || د دندې په مرکه کې د لومړیو پنځو دقیقو اهمیت څه دی؟ د مرکې پیل څنګه پلان شي؟
sol Model answer: The first five minutes are very important because the interviewer forms a strong first impression of the candidate. The opening should be planned: arrive early, greet politely, shake hands firmly, smile, keep eye contact, sit when asked, and have a short confident answer ready for "tell me about yourself". ## Based on the lesson.
q 7 | What is the role of self confidence in job interview and how can it be boosted? || جاب انٹرویو میں خود اعتمادی کا کیا کردار ہے اور اسے کیسے بڑھایا جا سکتا ہے؟ || د دندې په مرکه کې د ځان‌باور رول څه دی او دا څنګه زیاتېدای شي؟
sol Model answer: Self-confidence shows that the candidate believes in his abilities and persuades the interviewer to believe in him. It can be boosted by thorough preparation, knowing one's strengths, positive self-talk, practising mock interviews, good posture, and by remembering past successes. ## Based on the lesson.
q 8 | What type of text is used in the lesson ‘How To Take Job Interview’? Illustrate it with examples from the text. || سبق ‘جاب انٹرویو کیسے دیں’ میں کس قسم کا متن استعمال ہوا ہے؟ متن سے مثالوں کے ذریعے اس کی وضاحت کریں۔ || په درس ‘د دندې مرکه څنګه ورکړو’ کې کوم ډول متن کارول شوی؟ د متن له بېلګو سره یې روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer: The text is an informational and instructional (how-to) text: it gives advice and steps in a clear, organised way with headings, lists and the use of imperatives and examples such as "arrive early", "dress appropriately" and "make eye contact", and also includes sample job advertisements, a CV and a cover letter. ## Based on the lesson.
q 9 | What is the central idea of the lesson ‘How To Take Job Interview ’? || سبق ‘جاب انٹرویو کیسے دیں’ کا مرکزی خیال کیا ہے؟ || د درس ‘د دندې مرکه څنګه ورکړو’ مرکزي مفکوره څه ده؟
sol Model answer: The central idea is that success in a job interview depends on careful preparation: knowing the job, preparing the CV and cover letter, dressing well, controlling nerves, making a good first impression, answering confidently and practising beforehand. ## 'How to Take a Job Interview'.
q 10 | What is mock interview? How can it be helpful for preparation of job interviews? || نقلی (mock) انٹرویو کیا ہے؟ یہ جاب انٹرویو کی تیاری کے لیے کیسے مددگار ہو سکتا ہے؟ || مصنوعي (mock) مرکه څه ده؟ دا د دندې د مرکو د چمتو کولو لپاره څنګه ګټوره کېدای شي؟
sol Model answer: A mock interview is a practice interview in which a friend, teacher or mentor plays the interviewer and asks typical questions. It helps because the candidate gets used to the situation, improves answers and body language, receives feedback on weak points and loses fear before the real interview. ## Based on the lesson.
