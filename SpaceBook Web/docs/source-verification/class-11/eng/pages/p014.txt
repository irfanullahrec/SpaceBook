exercise Reading Comprehension
p Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | Why does the Quaid stress on youth to be continuously vigilant? || قائد نوجوانوں پر مسلسل چوکس رہنے پر کیوں زور دیتے ہیں؟ || قایدِ اعظم ولې پر ځوانانو ټینګار کوي چې تل هوښیار اوسي؟
sol Model answer: The Quaid tells the youth to stay vigilant because the enemies of Pakistan, who failed to stop its birth, kept trying to weaken and destroy it: the Punjab and Delhi killings and the refugee crisis, India withholding Pakistan's cash balances and share of military equipment, and an economic blockade. The young must watch for such dangers, beware of fifth columnists and selfish exploiters, and judge honest servants of the State. ## The speech, Dhaka University Convocation, 24 March 1948.
q 2 | What, according to the Quaid, is the prime responsibility of youth? || قائد کے مطابق نوجوانوں کی بنیادی ذمہ داری کیا ہے؟ || د قاید په وینا د ځوانانو لومړنۍ مسؤولیت څه دی؟
sol Model answer: According to the Quaid, the main responsibility of the youth is to devote their attention solely to their studies, in fairness to themselves, their parents and the State, so that they can equip themselves for the battle of life and become an asset and source of strength for Pakistan. ## "Your main occupation should be ... to devote your attention solely to your studies."
q 3 | Having read the text of the Quaid's speech, what traits of character the Quaid wants to see in the educated youth? || قائد کی تقریر کا متن پڑھنے کے بعد، قائد تعلیم یافتہ نوجوانوں میں کردار کی کون سی خصوصیات دیکھنا چاہتے ہیں؟ || د قاید د وینا متن لوستلو وروسته، قاید په زده‌کړیالو ځوانانو کې د کردار کومې ځانګړتیاوې لیدل غواړي؟
sol Model answer: He wants educated youth who are honest, selfless and dedicated to serving the people; disciplined and united; hard-working and constructive rather than merely militant; vigilant against selfish and disloyal people; independent and self-reliant, not looking only for government jobs; and proud of their country. ## Based on the speech.
q 4 | What advice does the Quaid give to the youth about the choice of career? || قائد نوجوانوں کو پیشے کے انتخاب کے بارے میں کیا مشورہ دیتے ہیں؟ || قاید ځوانانو ته د مسلک د غوراولو په اړه کوم مشوره ورکوي؟
sol Model answer: The Quaid advises the youth to get out of the old rut of seeking only government service, which the colonial system produced (well-trained clerks), and to take up other careers such as trade, commerce, industry, agriculture, science and technology, so that they can build the economy of the new country. ## "Hitherto, you have been following the rut ... get out of that rut." Other careers are also acceptable.
q 5 | Compare and contrast the youth of today with ideal youth of Quaid -e- Azam as described in his speech. || آج کے نوجوانوں کا قائدِ اعظم کی تقریر میں بیان کیے گئے مثالی نوجوانوں سے موازنہ اور تضاد کریں۔ || نن ورځې ځوانان د قایدِ اعظم له هغو مثالي ځوانانو سره پرتله کړئ او توپیر یې ښودل چې په وینا کې بیان شوي دي.
sol Model answer: The ideal youth of the Quaid's speech are disciplined, united, honest, serious about their studies, alert to the nation's enemies and ready to serve with heart and soul, and they look beyond clerical jobs to build the country. Many young people today are often distracted by social media, entertainment and shortcuts, run after degrees and jobs instead of skills and service, and are less united or disciplined; but there are many hard-working students today too. To match the ideal, today's youth should combine study, character and national service. ## Open-ended; other valid comparisons are possible.
q 6 | Having read the text of ‘Responsibilities of Youth’, what, in your view could be the possible role of youth in crisis management of the country? || ‘نوجوانوں کی ذمہ داریاں’ کا متن پڑھنے کے بعد آپ کی رائے میں ملک کے بحران کے انتظام میں نوجوانوں کا ممکنہ کردار کیا ہو سکتا ہے؟ || د ‘د ځوانانو مسؤولیتونه’ متن لوستلو وروسته ستاسو په نظر د هېواد د بحران په مدیریت کې د ځوانانو ممکن رول څه کېدای شي؟
sol Model answer: In a crisis the youth can help by staying calm and informed, avoiding rumours and panic, volunteering in relief work (first aid, blood donation, collecting and distributing supplies), using technology to share correct information, keeping unity and discipline, helping the elderly and the needy, and preparing themselves with skills so they can help rebuild afterwards. ## Open-ended; other valid answers are possible.
q 7 | Quaid says “Not only has Pakistan survived the shock of that upheaval [scan obscured] than ever.” Which upheaval is he talking about? || قائد کہتے ہیں “پاکستان نہ صرف اس اتھل پتھل کے صدمے سے بچ نکلا ہے [scan obscured] پہلے سے زیادہ۔” وہ کس اتھل پتھل کی بات کر رہے ہیں؟ || قاید وايي “پاکستان نه یوازې د هغې ګډوډۍ له ټکان څخه ژوندی پاتې شو [scan obscured] تر پخوا.” هغه د کومې ګډوډۍ خبره کوي؟
sol Model answer: He is talking about the upheaval of the Partition of 1947: the Punjab and Delhi holocaust in which thousands of men, women and children were killed and millions were uprooted, and over fifty lakh refugees came into the Punjab within weeks. ## Pakistan survived that shock and "emerged stronger".
