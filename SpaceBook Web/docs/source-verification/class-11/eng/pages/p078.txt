## Reading Comprehension || مطالعے کا فہم || د لوست پوهه
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What is the dominant theme of the letter? || خط کا غالب موضوع کیا ہے؟ || د لیک غالب موضوع څه دی؟
sol Model answer: The dominant theme is that choosing a career is one of the most important decisions in life and should be made carefully by the person himself, through self-assessment, exploring the options, learning the requirements and then planning and acting. ## From the letter to Kashif about career choice.
q 2 | Why doesn’t the career expert mention any specific profession as career? || کیریئر ماہر کسی مخصوص پیشے کو بطور کیریئر کیوں بیان نہیں کرتا؟ || د مسلک پوه ولې کوم ځانګړی مسلک د مسلک په توګه نه یادوي؟
sol Model answer: The career expert does not name any particular profession because he thinks the decision should be made by Kashif himself: every person has different interests, skills and values, so the expert gives a process (self-assessment, searching for occupations, checking education needs) instead of choosing for him. ## "I, however, think that this is the decision you should make on your own."
q 3 | Why is the decision of choice of career considered the toughest? Give examples from the text of the letter. || پیشے کے انتخاب کا فیصلہ سب سے مشکل کیوں سمجھا جاتا ہے؟ خط کے متن سے مثالیں دیں۔ || د مسلک د غوراوي پرېکړه ولې تر ټولو ستونزمنه ګڼل کېږي؟ د لیک له متن څخه بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: The choice is the toughest because it affects the whole of one's life: we spend about 25% of each year, roughly 11½ of 35 working years, at work; many people choose careers that only look secure or well-paid and end up unhappy; interests also change over time, and a person needs to know himself, the occupations and their requirements. ## From the text of the letter.
q 4 | What is self-assessment? How far is it helpful in the choice of career? || خود جائزہ کیا ہے؟ یہ پیشے کے انتخاب میں کس حد تک مددگار ہے؟ || ځان‌ارزونه څه ده؟ دا د مسلک په غوراوي کې تر کومې کچې ګټوره ده؟
sol Model answer: Self-assessment means knowing yourself: your skills, interests, likes and dislikes, values and needs, including your hobbies. It is helpful because it shows which occupations suit you, keeps you from choosing a career for the wrong reasons (money, security), and helps you to find satisfaction in your work. ## From the text of the letter.
q 5 | Summarize the text of the lesson and extract the main points of the process of career choice. Now transfer the salient points of the process of career choice into flow chart and critically analyze the process. || سبق کے متن کا خلاصہ کریں اور پیشے کے انتخاب کے عمل کے اہم نکات نکالیں۔ اب پیشے کے انتخاب کے عمل کے نمایاں نکات کو بہاؤ کے چارٹ میں منتقل کریں اور عمل کا تنقیدی تجزیہ کریں۔ || د درس متن لنډ کړئ او د مسلک د غوراوي د پروسې مهم ټکي را وباسئ. اوس د مسلک د غوراوي د پروسې مهم ټکي په فلوچارټ کې ولیږدوئ او پروسه په انتقادي ډول تحلیل کړئ.
sol Model answer: Summary: choosing a career is vital; the writer advises (1) self-assessment of skills, interests and values; (2) reflecting on likes and dislikes and on hobbies; (3) searching for occupations that match the interests (e.g. teaching, medicine, accounting); (4) finding out the education, certification and skills needed; (5) making a plan and following it. Flow chart: Self-assessment -> Reflect on likes/dislikes -> Search occupations -> Check requirements -> Plan & decide -> Review. Critical analysis: the process is logical and practical, but it should also include advice from family and mentors, real work experience (internships), job market demand and the ability to change later. ## Open-ended; other flow charts are acceptable.
q 6 | Evaluate the steps of career choice and tell how effective or otherwise they are for your choice of career. || پیشے کے انتخاب کے مراحل کا جائزہ لیں اور بتائیں کہ آپ کے پیشے کے انتخاب کے لیے وہ کتنے مؤثر ہیں یا نہیں۔ || د مسلک د غوراوي مرحلې ارزونه وکړئ او ووايئ چې ستاسو د مسلک د غوراوي لپاره دوی څومره اغېزمن دي یا نه.
sol Model answer: The steps are effective because they begin with me (my abilities and interests), then move to options and requirements. For my own career choice, self-assessment shows that I like problem solving and helping others, so I can explore medicine or engineering; the requirement step tells me which subjects and degrees are needed. A weakness is that the steps do not mention the job market and finance, so I would add counselling and internships. ## Open-ended personal evaluation; other valid answers are possible.
