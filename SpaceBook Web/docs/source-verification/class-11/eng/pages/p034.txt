exercise About the poet
p Douglas Malloch (May 5, 1877 – July 2, 1938) was an American poet, short-story writer and Associate Editor of American Lumberman, a trade paper in Chicago. He was known as a ‘Lumberman’s poet’ both locally and nationally. He is noted for writing ‘Round River Drive’ and ‘Be the Best of Whatever You Are’ in addition to many other creations. || ڈگلس میلوک (5 مئی 1877 – 2 جولائی 1938) ایک امریکی شاعر، افسانہ نگار اور شکاگو کے ایک تجارتی اخبار امریکن لمبرمین کے ایسوسی ایٹ ایڈیٹر تھے۔ وہ مقامی اور قومی دونوں سطحوں پر ‘لکڑہاروں کے شاعر’ کے طور پر جانے جاتے تھے۔ وہ دیگر بہت سی تخلیقات کے علاوہ ‘Round River Drive’ اور ‘Be the Best of Whatever You Are’ لکھنے کے لیے مشہور ہیں۔ || ډګلس ملوچ (د 1877 کال د مۍ 5 – د 1938 کال د جولای 2) یو امریکايي شاعر، لنډه‌کیسه‌لیکوال او د شیکاګو د یوې سوداګریزې ورځپاڼې امریکن لمبرمین مرستیال مدیر و. هغه په محلي او ملي دواړو کچو د ‘د لرګي‌کارانو شاعر’ په توګه پېژندل کېده. هغه د نورو ډېرو آثارو سربېره د ‘Round River Drive’ او ‘Be the Best of Whatever You Are’ په لیکلو مشهور دی.

## Reading Comprehension || مطالعے کا فہم || د لوست پوهه
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What is the significance of the title of the poem 'Good Timber'? || نظم ‘اچھی لکڑی’ کے عنوان کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د نظم ‘ښه لرګی’ د سرلیک اهمیت څه دی؟
sol Model answer: "Good timber" is strong, useful wood. The title is a metaphor: just as a tree becomes strong timber only by fighting wind, storm, cold and shade, a person becomes strong in character only by facing difficulties. The title therefore sums up the poet's message that hardship and struggle build strength. ## Good Timber by Douglas Malloch.
q 2 | What is good timber? How does tree grow into good timber? || اچھی لکڑی کیا ہے؟ درخت اچھی لکڑی میں کیسے بدلتا ہے؟ || ښه لرګی څه دی؟ ونه څنګه په ښه لرګي بدلېږي؟
sol Model answer: Good timber is strong, sound wood from a tree that has grown tall and firm. A tree grows into good timber not with ease but through struggle: it must fight for sun, sky, air and light, and face strong winds, storms, snow, rain and cold. The harder the wind, the stronger the tree grows. ## "Good timber does not grow with ease; the stronger wind, the stronger trees."
q 3 | What, according to Douglas Malloch, is the fate of those people who do not work hard in life? || ڈگلس میلوک کے مطابق ان لوگوں کا انجام کیا ہے جو زندگی میں محنت نہیں کرتے؟ || د ډګلس ملوچ په وینا د هغو خلکو برخلیک څه دی چې په ژوند کې سخت کار نه کوي؟
sol Model answer: According to the poet, people who never work hard or struggle never grow into strong, capable persons. Like the tree that stands alone in the open plain and never has to fight for light and rain, they stay weak and unimportant: they "live and die as they began" without achieving anything. ## The tree that never fights remains "a scrubby thing"; the man who never toils never becomes "a manly man".
q 4 | How can a person achieve his/her true potential in life? Illustrate it with examples from the poem 'Good Timber'. || انسان زندگی میں اپنی حقیقی صلاحیت کیسے حاصل کر سکتا ہے؟ نظم ‘اچھی لکڑی’ سے مثالوں کے ساتھ واضح کریں۔ || یو انسان په ژوند کې خپله ریښتینې وړتیا څنګه ترلاسه کولای شي؟ د نظم ‘ښه لرګی’ له مثالونو سره یې روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer: A person can reach his true potential by working hard, facing difficulties bravely and not avoiding struggle. In the poem, the tree that has fought sun, wind and storm becomes a forest king, while the tree in the open plain that gets everything easily stays a scrubby thing. In the same way, a man who has to toil, to earn his share and overcome hardship becomes a strong, manly person. ## "The more the storm, the more the strength."
q 5 | What is the central idea of the poem ' Good Timber'? || نظم ‘اچھی لکڑی’ کا مرکزی خیال کیا ہے؟ || د نظم ‘ښه لرګی’ مرکزي مفکوره څه ده؟
sol Model answer: The central idea is that struggle and hardship make people and trees strong: no one grows great without facing difficulty. Success and strong character come from effort and perseverance, not from an easy life; this is "the common law of life". ## Good Timber by Douglas Malloch.
q 6 | What is the rhyme scheme of the poem 'Good Timber'? || نظم ‘اچھی لکڑی’ کی قافیہ بندی کی ترتیب کیا ہے؟ || د نظم ‘ښه لرګی’ د قافیې ترتیب څه دی؟
sol Model answer: The poem is written in rhyming couplets, so the rhyme scheme is aabbcc in each stanza (e.g. fight/light, plain/rain, king/thing). ## Each stanza has six lines in iambic tetrameter couplets.
q 7 | An extended metaphor is a poetic device in which two unlike things are compared in a series of lines of a poem. Give the example of extended metaphor from poem 'Good Timber'. || توسیع شدہ استعارہ ایک شعری حربہ ہے جس میں نظم کے مصرعوں کے ایک سلسلے میں دو غیر مشابہ چیزوں کا موازنہ کیا جاتا ہے۔ نظم ‘اچھی لکڑی’ سے توسیع شدہ استعارے کی مثال دیں۔ || غځېدلی استعاره یو شعري وسیله ده چې په کې د نظم د کرښو په لړۍ کې دوه نامشابهې شیان پرتله کېږي. د نظم ‘ښه لرګی’ څخه د غځېدلې استعارې بېلګه ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: The whole poem is an extended metaphor comparing a tree with a man. The tree that struggles against wind, rain and shade becomes strong timber just as a man who toils and overcomes hardship becomes a strong, successful person: "In trees and men good timbers grow." ## Extended metaphor: tree = man throughout the poem.
q 8 | What is alliteration ?Give its examples from the poem 'Good Timber' . || حرفی تکرار (تجنیسِ صوتی) کیا ہے؟ نظم ‘اچھی لکڑی’ سے اس کی مثالیں دیں۔ || د غږ تکرار څه دی؟ د نظم ‘ښه لرګی’ څخه یې بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Alliteration is the repetition of the same consonant sound at the beginning of nearby words. Examples in the poem: "sun and sky", "stronger wind, the stronger trees", "the more the storm, the more the strength", "broken branches", "forest king". ## Alliteration adds rhythm and emphasis.
