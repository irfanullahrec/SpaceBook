q 2 | How coherence and cohesion is achieved in the essay ‘It’s Country For Me’. Illustrate with examples from the text. || مضمون ‘یہ میرے لیے ملک ہے’ میں ربط اور ہم آہنگی کیسے حاصل کی گئی ہے۔ متن سے مثالوں کے ساتھ واضح کریں۔ || په مقاله ‘دا زما لپاره هېواد دی’ کې اړیکه او همغږي څنګه ترلاسه شوې ده. له متن څخه د مثالونو سره روښانه یې کړئ.
sol Model answer: Coherence (ideas flow logically) is achieved by moving from the opening night scene to Joel's background, school, chores, family and goals, with each paragraph supporting the main idea that Joel loves farm life. Cohesion (links between sentences) comes from repetition of key words (farm, Joel), pronouns (he, his), transitions ("But when the school bus ...", "Yet he is a boy still", "In fact", "Now, five generations later") and time markers. ## Based on the text.
q 3 | Explain the significance of the title, ‘It’s Country For Me’ and its connection to the theme of the lesson. || سرخی ‘یہ میرے لیے ملک ہے’ کی اہمیت اور سبق کے مرکزی خیال سے اس کے تعلق کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د سرلیک ‘دا زما لپاره هېواد دی’ اهمیت او د درس له مرکزي مفکورې سره د هغه اړیکه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: The title expresses Joel's preference for country life over city life: "It's country for me." It connects to the theme that farm life, with its hard work, responsibility, family and heritage, gives a boy purpose, happiness and skills, and Joel chooses to be a farmer. ## Based on the text.
q 4 | Describe Joel’s treatment of ‘Lamby’, the orphaned lamb and Jessica the small dog; what does this show? || یتیم میمنے ‘لیمبی’ اور چھوٹی کتیا جیسیکا کے ساتھ جوئل کے سلوک کو بیان کریں؛ یہ کیا ظاہر کرتا ہے؟ || د یتیم وري ‘لیمبي’ او کوچنۍ سپۍ جېسیکا سره د جوئل چلند بیان کړئ؛ دا څه ښيي؟
sol Model answer: Joel feeds Lamby at night, cares for her like a mother, and plays and works easily with Jessica. This shows that he is kind, patient, responsible and loving towards animals, and that he treats his chores as a duty of care, not a burden. ## Based on the text.
q 5 | Compare and contrast your daily routine with that of Joel’s. How would you rate the motivational level of your doing work with that of Joel’s? || اپنے روزمرہ معمول کا جوئل کے معمول سے موازنہ اور تضاد کریں۔ آپ اپنے کام کرنے کی ترغیب کی سطح کا جوئل کی سطح سے کیسے موازنہ کریں گے؟ || خپل ورځنی معمول د جوئل له معمول سره پرتله کړئ او توپیر یې ښودل. تاسو به د خپل کار کولو د هڅونې کچه د جوئل له کچې سره څنګه پرتله کړئ؟
sol Model answer: My routine includes school, homework, some chores and leisure time, with fixed hours and fewer physical duties. Joel's day begins with school and then includes boot work: chores every evening and for hours on weekends, caring for animals and machines. Joel does the work of an adult, while I mostly study and relax. His motivation is high because the farm is his heritage and future goal; mine is moderate and I should work on setting clearer goals. ## Open-ended personal comparison.
q 6 | What skills one is required to learn if he wants to be a full-fledged farmer? || اگر کوئی پورا کاشتکار بننا چاہتا ہو تو اسے کون سی مہارتیں سیکھنی ہوں گی؟ || که څوک یو بشپړ بزګر کېدل غواړي نو کومې مهارتونه باید زده کړي؟
sol Model answer: To be a full-fledged farmer one must learn to be a machine operator and mechanic, a husbandman (raising livestock), a veterinarian (caring for sick animals), an agriculturist (growing food on a large scale), and a businessperson (managing the farm's finances and sales). ## From the text.
q 7 | In what way does Joel play the role of an adult? || جوئل کس طرح بالغ کا کردار ادا کرتا ہے؟ || جوئل څنګه د یو بالغ رول لوبوي؟
sol Model answer: Joel plays the adult by doing hard chores every day, feeding and caring for animals, operating machinery, helping his father and brothers, listening and learning about business, and carrying real responsibility for the family's income and well-being. ## "Joel is a teenager, but he does the work of an adult."

q  | Summarize the lesson ‘Its Country for Me’ in your own words. || سبق ‘یہ میرے لیے ملک ہے’ کا اپنے الفاظ میں خلاصہ لکھیں۔ || د درس ‘دا زما لپاره هېواد دی’ لنډیز په خپلو کلمو ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: The lesson describes thirteen-year-old Joel Holland, who lives on a 245-acre family farm in Illinois that his ancestors started. He goes to a small school, does well at his studies, and every evening and weekend does heavy chores. The family works together, raising cattle and sheep and growing corn, oats and hay. Joel learns many skills and wants to be a farmer, because he loves the life and the heritage. ## Summary in my own words.
