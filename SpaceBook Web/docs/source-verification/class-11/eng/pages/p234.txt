exercise Reading Comprehension
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What is the significance of the title of the play ‘Progress’? || ڈرامے ‘ترقی’ کے عنوان کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د ډرامې ‘پرمختګ’ د سرلیک اهمیت څه دی؟
sol Model answer: The title 'Progress' is ironic. Professor Corrie thinks his deadly bomb is progress, but his sister Mrs. Meldon shows that true progress means saving life and destroying the means of destruction. The title stresses the difference between scientific advancement and moral progress. ## 'Progress' by St. John Ervine.
q 2 | What is the central idea of the play ‘Progress’? || ڈرامے ‘ترقی’ کا مرکزی خیال کیا ہے؟ || د ډرامې ‘پرمختګ’ مرکزي مفکوره څه ده؟
sol Model answer: The central idea is that science and invention without humanity lead to destruction: the so-called progress of making weapons that can wipe out whole cities is really a disaster, and real progress is to use knowledge to protect and preserve human life. It also shows the grief of a mother and the horror of war. ## 'Progress', a one-act play.
q 3 | Describe the climax of the play ‘Progress’ in few lines. || ڈرامے ‘ترقی’ کے نقطۂ عروج کو چند سطروں میں بیان کریں۔ || د ډرامې ‘پرمختګ’ اوج په څو کرښو کې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: The climax comes when Mrs. Meldon, having heard that her brother's invention will obliterate thousands of people and that he refuses to abandon it, decides to stop him. She poisons his tea and, as he dies, takes the papers with the formula and destroys the means of destruction. ## Climax: Mrs. Meldon's decisive act.
q 4 | What is one act play? illustrate it with examples from the play ‘Progress’? || ایک ایکٹ ڈراما کیا ہے؟ ڈرامے ‘ترقی’ سے مثالوں کے ذریعے اس کی وضاحت کریں۔ || یو اکټیزه ډرامه څه ده؟ د ډرامې ‘پرمختګ’ له بېلګو سره یې روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer: A one-act play is a short play with a single act, few characters, one setting and one main action. 'Progress' is a one-act play: it has three characters (Prof. Corrie, Mrs. Meldon and Hannah), one scene (the Professor's study in a village in the North of England in 1919), and a single rapidly developing conflict that ends in the climax. ## Stage directions and dialogue are used.
q 5 | What is plot? Write down the plot of the play ‘Progress’. || پلاٹ کیا ہے؟ ڈرامے ‘ترقی’ کا پلاٹ لکھیں۔ || پلاټ څه دی؟ د ډرامې ‘پرمختګ’ پلاټ ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: The plot is the sequence of events that make up a story. Plot of 'Progress': Exposition: Professor Corrie has succeeded in his experiment and wants to sell the formula of a bomb; his sister Mrs. Meldon, whose son Eddie was killed in the war, returns home grieving. Rising action: He explains that the bomb will destroy whole cities; she tells him how Eddie died. Climax: she pleads with him to destroy the formula, he refuses, and she poisons his tea. Falling action and resolution: he dies and she destroys the formula. ## Summary of the plot.
q 6 | Write the character sketch of Mrs. Meldon. || مسز میلڈن کا کردار نگاری خاکہ لکھیں۔ || د مېرمن میلډن د کرکټر انځور ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Mrs. Meldon is a widow of forty-three, dressed in black, sensitive, dignified and deeply grieving for her husband and her only son Eddie. She is not clever, but is wise in her feelings. She loves peace and hates war; she is gentle and patient with her brother, yet determined and strong enough, when it is necessary, to stop him from unleashing a horror. She is a typical tragic mother. ## Based on the play.
q 7 | Draw the character of Prof. Corrie as despiser/hater of humanity. || پروفیسر کوری کے کردار کو انسانیت سے نفرت کرنے والے/حقیر سمجھنے والے کے طور پر پیش کریں۔ || د پروفیسر کوري کرکټر د انسانیت سپکوونکي/کرکې کوونکي په توګه انځور کړئ.
sol Model answer: Professor Corrie is cold, proud and obsessed with his work; his eyes are "cold and humourless" and he is indifferent to others' feelings (he forgets to meet his sister and forgets Eddie's anniversary). He is happy to make a bomb that would kill whole cities and sees no difference between soldiers and civilians. He considers war as unavoidable and wants only fame and money. He thus despises and hates humanity in the name of science. ## Based on the play.
q 8 | What type of mother is Mrs. Meldon? Illustrate through her character that themes of literature are universal and exist across all cultures and societies. || مسز میلڈن کس قسم کی ماں ہے؟ اس کے کردار کے ذریعے وضاحت کریں کہ ادب کے موضوعات عالمگیر ہیں اور تمام ثقافتوں اور معاشروں میں موجود ہیں۔ || مېرمن میلډن کوم ډول مور ده؟ د هغې د کرکټر له لارې روښانه کړئ چې د ادب موضوعګانې نړیوالې دي او په ټولو کلتورونو او ټولنو کې شته.
sol Model answer: Mrs. Meldon is a loving, devoted and grieving mother who treasured her only son and is shattered by his death. Her love and sorrow, the cruelty of war and the pain of losing a child are felt in all cultures and societies, so the play shows that the theme of motherly love and the horror of war is universal in literature. ## Based on the play.
q 9 | Was Mrs. Meldon justified in killing her brother? Give your considered view. || کیا مسز میلڈن اپنے بھائی کو قتل کرنے میں حق بجانب تھی؟ اپنی سوچی سمجھی رائے دیں۔ || ایا مېرمن میلډن د خپل ورور په وژلو کې حق ته وه؟ خپل سنجول شوی نظر ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Her action can be viewed in two ways. She was morally justified in the sense that she acted to save thousands of lives and prevent a horrible weapon; she tried to reason with him first, and the war had destroyed her family. But killing a person, even one's own brother, is murder and no one has the right to take another's life. In my view the motive was noble but the act was wrong; she could have destroyed the formula or called the authorities. ## Open-ended; either reasoned view is acceptable.
q 10 | What was the cause of quarrel between Mrs. Meldon and Prof. Corrie? || مسز میلڈن اور پروفیسر کوری کے درمیان جھگڑے کی وجہ کیا تھی؟ || د مېرمن میلډن او پروفیسر کوري ترمنځ د شخړې لامل څه و؟
sol Model answer: The quarrel arises from the brother's invention, a bomb that would destroy a whole city: Professor Corrie sees it as progress and patriotic, while Mrs. Meldon, who lost her son in the war, sees it as monstrous and begs him to give it up; he refuses and wants to sell it to the Government. ## The dispute about the use of the invention.
q 11 | Contrast the characters of Mrs. Meldon and Prof. Corrie, highlighting the dominant traits of their personalities. || مسز میلڈن اور پروفیسر کوری کے کرداروں کا تقابل کریں، ان کی شخصیتوں کی غالب خصوصیات کو نمایاں کرتے ہوئے۔ || د مېرمن میلډن او پروفیسر کوري کرکټرونه پرتله کړئ، د هغوی د شخصیتونو غالبې ځانګړتیاوې روښانه کولو سره.
sol Model answer: Mrs. Meldon is loving, emotional, compassionate, humane, simple and brave; Prof. Corrie is cold, rational, selfish, ambitious, proud and heartless. She values human life; he values science and fame. She feels the war through her son, he looks at it as an abstract problem. ## Based on the play.
q 12 | What is the conflict in the play ‘Progress’? How is it resolved? || ڈرامے ‘ترقی’ میں تنازع کیا ہے؟ یہ کیسے حل ہوتا ہے؟ || په ډرامه ‘پرمختګ’ کې شخړه څه ده؟ دا څنګه حل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: The conflict is between Mrs. Meldon, who values human life and hates war, and Professor Corrie, who values scientific progress and fame and does not care about the human cost. It is an ideological conflict (and also person against person). It is resolved when Mrs. Meldon poisons her brother and destroys his formula. ## 'Progress'.
q 13 | Suggest another ending for the play ‘Progress’. || ڈرامے ‘ترقی’ کے لیے ایک اور اختتام تجویز کریں۔ || د ډرامې ‘پرمختګ’ لپاره بل پای وړاندیز کړئ.
sol Model answer: Another ending: after hearing about Eddie's death and the effect of the bomb, Corrie, moved by his sister's grief and his conscience, burns the formula himself and decides to use his knowledge for medicine and peace. Mrs. Meldon then lays the wreath on the War Memorial with him. ## Open-ended; other endings are possible.
q 14 | True progress means “The destroying of the means of destruction”. Elaborate this statement in light of the play ‘Progress’. || حقیقی ترقی کا مطلب ہے “تباہی کے ذرائع کو تباہ کرنا”۔ ڈرامے ‘ترقی’ کی روشنی میں اس بیان کی وضاحت کریں۔ || ریښتینی پرمختګ معنا لري “د ویجاړۍ د وسایلو ویجاړول”. د ډرامې ‘پرمختګ’ په رڼا کې دا بیان تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: The statement means that true progress is not the invention of more powerful weapons but the removal of the instruments of destruction. In the play, Corrie's formula would destroy thousands, so Mrs. Meldon destroys it, and by doing so she shows real progress: respecting human life and using knowledge for protection, not for killing. ## 'Progress'.
