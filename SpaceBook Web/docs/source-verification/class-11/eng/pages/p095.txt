p Arshak had still not retuned. The fire died down, leaving a mound of ashes in the fireplace, while the lamb lay on the grass, chewing its cud. Navasard was all ears as he peered into the darkness. He should come to me.” He began to grumble, but then consoled himself once again by saying: “Well, he’s an important man. Maybe he has some important business to discuss with the chairman.
