## Reading Comprehension || مطالعے کا فہم || د لوست پوهه
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What does Baker see as the difference between school and education? || بیکر اسکول اور تعلیم کے درمیان کیا فرق دیکھتا ہے؟ || بیکر د ښوونځي او زده کړې ترمنځ کوم توپیر ګوري؟
sol Model answer: For Baker, school is the formal, institutional process of twelve years of tests, competition and getting into college and graduate school, where children learn to tell the testers what they want to hear. Education is the real learning: what children pick up from television and parents before school, and the curious, open-minded reading and thinking that a person may begin only after school and success. The two are not the same. ## 'School vs. Education' by Russell Baker.
q 2 | What can we infer about Baker’s attitude toward television? || ٹیلی ویژن کے بارے میں بیکر کے رویے سے ہم کیا نتیجہ اخذ کر سکتے ہیں؟ || د ټلویزیون په اړه د بیکر له چلند څخه موږ څه پایله اخیستلای شو؟
sol Model answer: Baker's attitude toward television is critical and ironic: he shows that, before school, the child learns from it (with sharp irony) how to pick a lock, commit a bank robbery and kill people with sophisticated weapons, so it teaches harmful and trivial things instead of real values. He treats it as an unreliable and negative teacher. ## Paragraph 2 of the essay.
q 3 | Baker’s tone is very cynical in ‘School vs. Education’. To what extent do you think he means what he says? Explain. || ‘اسکول بمقابلہ تعلیم’ میں بیکر کا لہجہ بہت تلخ طنزیہ ہے۔ آپ کے خیال میں وہ جو کہتا ہے اس کا کس حد تک مطلب بھی لیتا ہے؟ وضاحت کریں۔ || په ‘ښوونځی پر وړاندې زده کړه’ کې د بیکر لهجه ډېره تروې ملنډه‌ییزه ده. ستاسو په فکر هغه څومره هغه څه چې وايي مطلب یې هم لري؟ تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Baker's tone is cynical and satirical, but he does not mean everything literally: he uses irony and exaggeration to criticise a system that rewards cunning and test-taking instead of true learning. He really means that schools and society make success the aim, not knowledge, and that real education is lost. ## Open-ended; other valid views are possible.
q 4 | What is the main idea of Baker’s essay? || بیکر کے مضمون کا مرکزی خیال کیا ہے؟ || د بیکر د مقالې اصلي مفکوره څه ده؟
sol Model answer: The main idea is that schooling is not the same as education: school trains children to pass tests, compete and succeed for rewards, while true education means curiosity, understanding and values, which many people acquire only after (or despite) schooling. ## Russell Baker, 'School vs. Education'.
q 5 | Pick out some transitional devices from the text ‘School Vs Education’. What purpose do they serve? || متن ‘اسکول بمقابلہ تعلیم’ سے کچھ انتقالی ذرائع چنیں۔ وہ کیا مقصد پورا کرتے ہیں؟ || له متن ‘ښوونځی پر وړاندې زده کړه’ څخه څو انتقالي وسایل وټاکئ. دوی کومه موخه پوره کوي؟
sol Model answer: Transitional devices include: "By the age of six", "At this stage", "At this point", "During", "From this", "Thus", "Afterwards", "Those", "a fresh question arises". They show sequence in time (the stages of education), cause and result ("Thus the student learns ...") and link one paragraph to the next so the essay moves logically from early childhood to adulthood. ## Based on the text.
