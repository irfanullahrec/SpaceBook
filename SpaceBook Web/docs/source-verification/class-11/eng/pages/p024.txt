Then he flapped his wings once and he soared upwards. He uttered a joyous scream and flapped them again. He soared higher. He raised his breast and banked against the wind. “Ga, ga, ga. Ga, ga, ga. ## About the Author || مصنف کے بارے میں || د لیکوال په اړه
p Liam O’Flaherty (1896-1984), Irish novelist, was born in the Arran Islands, County Galway, and educated at University College, Dublin. He was a leading Irish novelist of the early 20th century. His works are characterized by stark Naturalism, psychological analysis, and powerful drama. Among his books are Thy Neighbour’s Wife (1924), The Informer (1925; film, 1935), Mr. Gilhooley (1926), Short Stories (1937; revised 1956), Land (1946), Two Lovely Beasts and Other Stories (1950), Insurrection (1951), and The Pedlar’s Revenge and Other Stories (1976).
