Your responses will be stronger if you prepare in advance, know what to expect during the interview and have a sense of what you want to focus on. Rehearsing is one of the best ways to prepare for a job interview. A mock interview, also known as a practice interview, is a simulation of an actual job interview that gives job seekers an opportunity to practise and receive feedback on their interviewing skills; it is typically held with a professional career counsellor, friends or family members. A mock interview helps you learn how to answer difficult questions, develop interview strategies, improve your communication skills and reduce your stress before an actual job interview.
