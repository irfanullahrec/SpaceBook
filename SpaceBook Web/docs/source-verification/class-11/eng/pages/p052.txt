exercise Reading Comprehension
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | How is the theme of ‘From Mother . . . With Love’ a universal theme present in literature across all cultures? || ‘ماں کی طرف سے . . . محبت کے ساتھ’ کا موضوع تمام ثقافتوں کے ادب میں موجود عالمگیر موضوع کیسے ہے؟ || د ‘له مور څخه . . . په مینه’ موضوع څنګه په ټولو کلتورونو کې په ادب کې شته نړیوال موضوع دی؟
sol Model answer: The central theme is a parent's selfless love and the way a family faces death with courage. Every culture knows the love of mothers and fathers, the pain of losing someone and the idea of loving someone enough to protect them from pain, so readers everywhere can relate to Minta's story. Themes of love, sacrifice, loss and growing up are universal. ## Story by Zoa Sherburne.
q 2 | Comment on the plot of the story ‘From Mother . . . With Love’. || کہانی ‘ماں کی طرف سے . . . محبت کے ساتھ’ کے پلاٹ پر تبصرہ کریں۔ || د کیسې ‘له مور څخه . . . په مینه’ پر پلاټ تبصره وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Minta Hawley, a teenage girl, learns from her father at the beach that her mother is going to die within three months. They decide not to tell the mother and to keep the family happy. Minta hides her pain and carries on, until her mother dies. While clearing her clothes, Minta finds suitcases full of Christmas gifts that her mother had prepared for everyone: the mother knew all along and kept up the pretence too. Moved, Minta accepts her loss, takes her mother's place (vitamin pills, blue chair) and begins to grow up. ## Plot: exposition, rising action (secret), climax (gifts), resolution (blue chair).
q 3 | Identify the narrator or speaker of the story ‘From Mother. . . With Love’. || کہانی ‘ماں کی طرف سے . . . محبت کے ساتھ’ کے راوی یا بولنے والے کی شناخت کریں۔ || د کیسې ‘له مور څخه . . . په مینه’ راوي یا خبرې کوونکی وپېژنئ.
sol Model answer: The story is told by an unnamed third-person narrator who knows Minta's thoughts and feelings (limited omniscient point of view), so we see events through Minta's eyes. ## It is not told by Minta herself in the first person.
q 4 | Analyze and comment on the character of Minta? || منٹا کے کردار کا تجزیہ اور اس پر تبصرہ کریں؟ || د منټا د کردار تحلیل وکړئ او تبصره پرې وکړئ؟
sol Model answer: Minta is loving, sensitive, mature and strong. She has a close bond with her parents; she keeps her grief hidden to protect her mother and father, and stays home to help, putting the family before her own wish to go to school. She is also lively and humorous (teasing her mother), observant, and capable of change: after discovering her mother's gifts she grows from a girl into a responsible young woman. ## Based on the text.
q 5 | What is the conflict in the story? How is it resolved? || کہانی میں تنازع کیا ہے؟ وہ کیسے حل ہوتا ہے؟ || په کیسه کې شخړه څه ده؟ دا څنګه حل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: The main conflict is internal and emotional: Minta struggles between her grief at the coming death of her mother and her need to behave normally so that her mother is not distressed. There is also the conflict between truth and pretence in the family. It is resolved when Minta discovers the prepared Christmas gifts, understands that her mother knew the truth and accepted it, and she then finds peace and takes up her mother's role. ## Conflict: person vs self/circumstance.
q 6 | Where does Minta’s father take her to discuss her mother’s illness? || منٹا کے والد اسے اس کی ماں کی بیماری پر بات کرنے کے لیے کہاں لے جاتے ہیں؟ || د منټا پلار هغه د هغې د مور د ناروغۍ د خبرو لپاره چېرته وړي؟
sol Model answer: Minta's father takes her to the beach, saying they will dig for clams at low tide. They walk away from the other people towards the rocks, and there he tells her that her mother is dying. ## The beach scene opens the story.
q 7 | Which approach would you have preferred — deception or honesty — if you had to deal with similar circumstances like Minta’s mother ? Give reasons for your choice. || اگر آپ کو منٹا کی ماں جیسے حالات سے نمٹنا پڑتا تو آپ کون سا طریقہ پسند کرتے — فریب یا دیانت؟ اپنے انتخاب کی وجوہات بتائیں۔ || که تاسو باید د منټا د مور په څېر شرایطو سره معامله کړې وای نو کوم طریقه به مو خوښه کړې وای — دوکه که صداقت؟ د خپل انتخاب دلایل ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: I would prefer honesty, because a person has the right to know the truth about her own life and to say goodbye, settle her affairs and spend the last days as she wishes. Pretending causes loneliness for everyone and prevents sharing grief. However, deception can protect someone from fear, and in the story it let the family enjoy a happy last season. Therefore I would tell the truth gently and with love. ## Open-ended; the opposite choice with good reasons is also acceptable.
q 8 | How did the vitamin pills become the symbol of responsibility? || وٹامن کی گولیاں ذمہ داری کی علامت کیسے بن گئیں؟ || د ویټامین ګولۍ د مسؤولیت نښه څنګه شوې؟
sol Model answer: Before going to hospital Mother asked Minta to see that Father took his vitamin pills because he was careless. After her death, Minta tidies the house and puts the pills where she will see them. The pills thus become a symbol of care and responsibility passing from mother to daughter: Minta now looks after her father as her mother did. ## Based on the text.
q 9 | How did Minta change over the course of the story? Limit your answer to just five sentences. || کہانی کے دوران منٹا کیسے بدلی؟ اپنا جواب صرف پانچ جملوں تک محدود رکھیں۔ || د کیسې په اوږدو کې منټا څنګه بدله شوه؟ خپل ځواب یوازې پنځو جملو ته محدود کړئ.
sol Model answer: At the start Minta is a carefree girl looking forward to boarding school. After learning the truth she becomes serious and quietly strong, hiding her sorrow. After her mother's death she is numb and cannot cry, avoiding people. Finding the hidden gifts breaks her shell and she cries and understands her mother's courage. At the end she takes over her mother's role and smiles at her father, showing she has grown up. ## Five sentences, as the question asks.
q 10 | Why was the blue chair significant? Explain. || نیلی کرسی کیوں اہم تھی؟ وضاحت کریں۔ || شین چوکۍ ولې مهمه وه؟ تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: The blue chair by the fireplace was her mother's place, so when it is empty it reminds the family of her absence. At the end Minta sits in the chair "as if she belonged there", which shows that she accepts her mother's death and takes her place as the heart of the home, comforting her father and moving on to a new life. ## Symbol of the mother's place in the family.
q 11 | Explain how Minta knows that her mother accepted death. Why is that knowledge comforting to her? || وضاحت کریں کہ منٹا کیسے جانتی ہے کہ اس کی ماں نے موت کو قبول کر لیا تھا۔ وہ علم اس کے لیے تسلی بخش کیوں ہے؟ || تشریح کړئ چې منټا څنګه پوهېږي چې د هغې مور مړینه منلې وه. دا پوهه د هغې لپاره ولې تسلي بښونکې ده؟
sol Model answer: Minta finds suitcases full of Christmas gifts, each tagged in her mother's writing ("To Minta from Mother ... with love"), which Mother had prepared for everyone before dying. This shows that she knew she was dying, faced it calmly, and still loved and cared for her family. This comforts Minta because her mother was not afraid or alone, and had peace and prepared the family with love. ## Based on the text.
