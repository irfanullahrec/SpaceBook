p James Mercer Langston Hughes (February 1, 1902 – May 22, 1967) was an American poet, social activist, novelist, playwright, and columnist. Hughes is best known as a leader of the Harlem Renaissance. His work, first published in 1921, continue to enrich and inspire decades later. ## Reading Comprehension || مطالعے کا فہم || د لوست پوهه
q  | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
q 1 | What is the dominant theme of the poem ‘Mother to Son’? || نظم ‘ماں کا بیٹے کے نام’ کا غالب موضوع کیا ہے؟ || د نظم ‘مور له زوی ته’ غالب موضوع څه دی؟
sol Model answer: The dominant theme is perseverance in the face of hardship. A mother tells her son that her life has not been easy ("no crystal stair") but she kept climbing, and she urges him not to give up or turn back, whatever difficulties he faces. It also shows a mother's love, wisdom and hope. ## 'Mother to Son' by Langston Hughes.
q 2 | What are some of the literary techniques used in the poem ‘Mother to Son’? || نظم ‘ماں کا بیٹے کے نام’ میں استعمال ہونے والی کچھ ادبی تکنیکیں کیا ہیں؟ || په نظم ‘مور له زوی ته’ کې کارېدونکې ځینې ادبي تخنیکونه کوم دي؟
sol Model answer: The poem uses extended metaphor (life as a staircase), imagery (tacks, splinters, boards torn up, bare floors), repetition ("And ... And ..."), direct address (the mother speaking to "son"), symbolism (stairs/landings = progress and rest), dialect (non-standard speech) to give authenticity, free verse with irregular line length, and a conversational, dramatic-monologue tone. ## Langston Hughes, 'Mother to Son'.
q 3 | What is an extended metaphor? How does Hughes use this literary technique to illustrate the consistent struggle of mother in her life? Give examples from the poem. || توسیع شدہ استعارہ کیا ہے؟ ہیوز اس ادبی تکنیک کو ماں کی زندگی میں مسلسل جدوجہد کو واضح کرنے کے لیے کیسے استعمال کرتے ہیں؟ نظم سے مثالیں دیں۔ || غځېدلې استعاره څه ده؟ هیوز دا ادبي تخنیک د مور په ژوند کې د دوامدارې مبارزې د روښانه کولو لپاره څنګه کاروي؟ له نظم څخه بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: An extended metaphor compares two unlike things throughout the whole poem. Hughes compares the mother's life to a staircase: it is not a "crystal stair" (smooth and luxurious) but has "tacks", "splinters", "boards torn up" and places with "no carpet on the floor", which stand for pain, poverty and obstacles. Yet she keeps "reachin' landin's, turnin' corners", meaning she has made progress; she is still climbing, showing her constant struggle. ## 'Mother to Son'.
q 4 | In the poem ‘Mother to Son’, Hughes repeats some lines. What function does it serve in poem? Explain. || نظم ‘ماں کا بیٹے کے نام’ میں ہیوز کچھ مصرعے دہراتے ہیں۔ نظم میں اس کا کیا کام ہے؟ وضاحت کریں۔ || په نظم ‘مور له زوی ته’ کې هیوز ځینې کرښې تکراروي. په نظم کې دا کومه دنده ترسره کوي؟ تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Repetition of lines such as "I'se been a-climbin' on" and of "And" at the start of lines creates rhythm and a sense of continuous effort, imitating the steady climb. It stresses the mother's persistence and emphasises her advice not to stop or sit down on the steps. It also gives the poem the feeling of spoken speech. ## 'Mother to Son'.
q 5 | What type of imagery is used in the poem ‘Mother to Son’? What does it suggest? || نظم ‘ماں کا بیٹے کے نام’ میں کس قسم کی پیکر تراشی استعمال ہوئی ہے؟ یہ کیا ظاہر کرتی ہے؟ || په نظم ‘مور له زوی ته’ کې کوم ډول انځورګري کارول شوې؟ دا څه ښيي؟
sol Model answer: The poem mainly uses visual and tactile imagery: tacks, splinters, torn boards, bare floors, dark stairs with no light ("it's dark"). This imagery suggests a hard, painful and uncertain life full of obstacles, but also an endless, upward journey in which the mother moves ahead in the dark. ## 'Mother to Son'.
q 6 | What is the tone of the poem ‘Mother to Son’? Support your view with instances from the poem. || نظم ‘ماں کا بیٹے کے نام’ کا لہجہ کیا ہے؟ نظم سے مثالوں کے ساتھ اپنی رائے کی تائید کریں۔ || د نظم ‘مور له زوی ته’ لهجه څه ده؟ له نظم څخه د مثالونو سره خپل نظر ملاتړ کړئ.
sol Model answer: The tone is serious, honest and encouraging: the mother speaks frankly ("Life for me ain't been no crystal stair"), but also firmly and lovingly urges her son: "So boy, don't you turn back. Don't you set down on the steps." The tone is also determined and hopeful. ## 'Mother to Son'.
