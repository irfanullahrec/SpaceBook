p Summary of the rest of paragraph 3: … for the present. There are a lot of people who are “just” something; “just” a housewife immediately and painfully comes to mind, and we still hear it all the time: women who have kept a house and reared six children refer to themselves as “just” a housewife. “Just” a bum, “just” a kid, bag lady, old man, student, punk are some others, and the reader can probably add to the list. The “just” category contains present non-earners, people with no past job history highly valued by society and people whose present jobs are low-end on the pay and prestige scale.
