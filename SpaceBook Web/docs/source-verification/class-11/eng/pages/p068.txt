q  | Now write an explanation of the following lines with reference to the context. || اب سیاق و سباق کے حوالے سے درج ذیل مصرعوں کی تشریح لکھیں۔ || اوس د شرایطو په حواله د لاندې کرښو تشریح ولیکئ.
sol Reference to the context: These lines are taken from the poem 'Mother to Son' written by Langston Hughes. They are the opening lines of this dramatic monologue, in which a mother speaks to her son. Explanation: The mother tells her son that her life has not been easy or smooth like a "crystal stair"; it has had "tacks" (small sharp problems), "splinters" (small hurts) and "boards torn up" (breaks and failures), and places with no carpet, that is, bare, poor and uncomfortable. The staircase is an extended metaphor for life, and the mother wants to say honestly that life is full of difficulty, so that her son will understand that struggle is normal and that she has survived it. ## Model answer; other valid wording is possible.
p Well, son, I’ll tell you: Life for me ain’t been no crystal stair.
