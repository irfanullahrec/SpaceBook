p sight of the receiver. Electronic waves with frequencies between 1 GHz to 300 GHz are normally called microwaves. Micro waves are used to transmit wireless signals across a few miles. Unlike radio waves, microwaves are unidirectional, in which the sending and receiving antennas need to be aligned. Microwave stations or antennas are usually installed on the high towers or buildings. Microwaves propagation is line-of-sight therefore towers with mounted antennas need to be in direct sight of each other.
