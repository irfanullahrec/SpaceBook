q  | Q3. Give detailed answers of the following questions. || سوال ۳: درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات دیں۔ || ۳ پوښتنه: د لاندې پوښتنو تفصیلي ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp i | Explain different types of database models. || ڈیٹا بیس ماڈلز کی مختلف اقسام بیان کریں۔ || د ډېټابېس ماډلونو مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Database models: (1) Hierarchical model: data in a tree of parent and child segments, one parent for each child (fast but rigid). (2) Network model: records linked in a graph, a child may have many parents. (3) Relational model: data in tables (relations) related through keys; the most widely used (Oracle, MS Access, MySQL). (4) Object-oriented model: data stored as objects with attributes and methods, used for multimedia and complex data. ## Unit 7 database models.
qp ii | What is SQL? Explain its types. || ایس کیو ایل کیا ہے؟ اس کی اقسام بیان کریں۔ || SQL څه دی؟ ډولونه یې تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: SQL (Structured Query Language) is the standard language used to create, manage and query relational databases. Types of SQL commands: DDL (Data Definition Language): CREATE, ALTER, DROP. DML (Data Manipulation Language): SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE. DCL (Data Control Language): GRANT, REVOKE (access rights). TCL (Transaction Control Language): COMMIT, ROLLBACK. ## Unit 7 SQL.
qp iii | Explain various steps of planning a database. || ڈیٹا بیس کی منصوبہ بندی کے مختلف مراحل بیان کریں۔ || د ډېټابېس د پلانولو مختلف ګامونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Steps in planning a database: (1) Decide the purpose of the database and what information it must give. (2) Collect and study the data and find the entities (tables). (3) Decide the fields (attributes) of each table. (4) Choose the primary key of each table. (5) Find the relationships between the tables (1:1, 1:M, M:N) and add foreign keys. (6) Normalise the design to remove redundancy. (7) Create the database, enter sample data, test it and refine. ## Unit 7 database planning.
qp iv | What is a relation? Explain the degree of relationships with examples. || ریلیشن کیا ہے؟ ریلیشن شپس کی ڈگری مثالوں کے ساتھ بیان کریں۔ || اړیکه څه ده؟ د اړیکو درجه په بېلګو سره تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: A relation (relationship) is an association between entities, for example Teacher teaches Student. Degree of a relationship is the number of entity types that take part: (1) Unary (degree 1): an entity related to itself, e.g. an Employee manages Employee. (2) Binary (degree 2): two entities, e.g. Student enrols in Course. (3) Ternary (degree 3): three entities, e.g. Doctor prescribes Medicine to Patient. ## Unit 7 relationships; the degree examples can be changed.
qp v | What is ERD? Draw an ERD for the following systems. || ای آر ڈی کیا ہے؟ درج ذیل نظاموں کے لیے ای آر ڈی بنائیں۔ || ERD څه دی؟ د لاندې سیسټمونو لپاره ERD رسم کړئ.
sol Model answer: An ERD (entity relationship diagram) is a picture of a database design: rectangles are entities, ovals are attributes (the primary key underlined), diamonds are relationships, and lines with 1, M or N show cardinality. It shows the entities and how they are connected before the tables are made. The ERDs of the hospital and airline systems are described in the next two answers. ## Unit 7 ERD.
qp vi | Hospital management system || ہسپتال مینجمنٹ سسٹم || د روغتون مدیریت سیسټم
sol Model answer: Hospital management ERD (to be drawn): Entities and key attributes: PATIENT (Patient_ID, name, age, address, phone), DOCTOR (Doctor_ID, name, specialty, phone), WARD/ROOM (Room_No, type, beds), APPOINTMENT/TREATMENT (date, diagnosis), MEDICINE (Medicine_ID, name, price), BILL (Bill_No, amount, date), NURSE (Nurse_ID, name). Relationships: DOCTOR treats PATIENT (many-to-many, through APPOINTMENT); PATIENT admitted to ROOM (many-to-one); NURSE works in WARD (many-to-one); PATIENT receives MEDICINE (many-to-many); PATIENT has BILL (one-to-many). Rectangles for entities, ovals for attributes, diamonds for relationships. ## Unit 7 ERD; a model design, other correct designs are possible.
qp vii | Airline booking system || ایئر لائن بکنگ سسٹم || د هوایي کرښې بکینګ سیسټم
sol Model answer: Airline booking ERD (to be drawn): Entities and key attributes: PASSENGER (Passenger_ID, name, CNIC/passport, phone), FLIGHT (Flight_No, departure, arrival, date, time), AIRCRAFT (Aircraft_ID, model, seats), AIRPORT (Airport_Code, city), TICKET/BOOKING (Ticket_No, seat_no, fare, date), CREW/EMPLOYEE (Emp_ID, name, role). Relationships: PASSENGER books TICKET (one-to-many); TICKET is for FLIGHT (many-to-one); AIRCRAFT operates FLIGHT (one-to-many); FLIGHT departs from / arrives at AIRPORT (many-to-one each); CREW assigned to FLIGHT (many-to-many). ## Unit 7 ERD; a model design, other correct designs are possible.
qp viii | Describe different steps of Transformation of E-R Model into Relational Schema. || ای-آر ماڈل کی ریلیشنل اسکیما میں تبدیلی کے مختلف مراحل بیان کریں۔ || د E-R ماډل اړوندې طرحې ته د بدلولو مختلف ګامونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Transformation of the E-R model into a relational schema: (1) Each strong entity becomes a table; its attributes become columns and its key attribute the primary key. (2) A weak entity becomes a table that includes the key of its owner entity as a foreign key and as part of its primary key. (3) A one-to-one relationship: put the primary key of one table as a foreign key in the other. (4) A one-to-many relationship: put the primary key of the "one" side as a foreign key in the "many" side table. (5) A many-to-many relationship becomes a new table whose primary key is made of the two foreign keys, plus any attributes of the relationship. (6) A multi-valued attribute becomes a separate table linked by a foreign key. ## Unit 7 ER to relational.
qp ix | What is Normalization? Explain the following Normal Forms 1NF, 2NF, 3NF. || نارملائزیشن کیا ہے؟ درج ذیل نارمل فارمز ۱ این ایف، ۲ این ایف، ۳ این ایف کی وضاحت کریں۔ || نورمال کول څه دی؟ لاندې نورمال بڼې 1NF، 2NF، 3NF تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Normalization is the process of organising the tables of a database to remove redundancy and avoid insertion, update and deletion anomalies by splitting big tables into smaller related ones. 1NF (first normal form): every field holds only a single (atomic) value, no repeating groups, and each row is unique. 2NF: the table is in 1NF and every non-key attribute depends on the whole primary key (no partial dependency on a part of a composite key). 3NF: the table is in 2NF and no non-key attribute depends on another non-key attribute (no transitive dependency). ## Unit 7 normalization.
