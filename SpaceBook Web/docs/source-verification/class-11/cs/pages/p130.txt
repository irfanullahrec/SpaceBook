q  | Q3. Give detailed answers to the following questions. || سوال ۳: درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جواب دیں۔ || ۳ پوښتنه: د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp i | Discuss different types of expansion cards. || ایکسپینشن کارڈز کی مختلف اقسام پر بحث کریں۔ || د پراختیا کارتونو مختلف ډولونه وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Expansion cards are circuit boards inserted into expansion slots to add features. Types: (1) Video (graphics) card: produces the picture for the monitor. (2) Sound card: input and output of audio. (3) Network interface card (NIC): connects the computer to a network. (4) Modem card: connects to the Internet through a telephone line. (5) TV tuner and capture cards: receive TV signals or capture video. ## Unit 4 expansion cards.
qp ii | What is port? Explain different types of ports in computers. || پورٹ کیا ہے؟ کمپیوٹرز میں پورٹس کی مختلف اقسام کی وضاحت کریں۔ || پورټ څه دی؟ په کمپیوټرونو کې د پورټونو مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: A port is a socket on the system unit through which peripheral devices are connected to the computer. Types: (1) USB port: for keyboard, mouse, printer, flash drives and many other devices. (2) Serial port (COM): sends data one bit at a time (old modems and mice). (3) Parallel port (LPT): sends many bits at once (old printers). (4) PS/2 port: keyboard and mouse. (5) VGA/HDMI: monitor and projector. (6) FireWire (IEEE 1394): fast transfer from cameras. (7) Audio and network (Ethernet/RJ45) ports. ## Unit 4 ports.
qp iii | Explain different types of ribbon cables. || ربن کیبلز کی مختلف اقسام کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د ریبن کېبلونو مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Ribbon cables are flat cables with many parallel wires side by side. Types: (1) IDE (PATA) ribbon cable with 40 wires (80-wire version for faster drives) connecting hard disks and CD/DVD drives to the motherboard. (2) Floppy disk drive ribbon cable with 34 wires. (3) SCSI ribbon cable for SCSI drives. (4) SATA is a thin serial cable that replaced them. ## Unit 4 cables.
qp iv | Discuss different types of memory chips. || میموری چپس کی مختلف اقسام پر بحث کریں۔ || د حافظې چپونو مختلف ډولونه وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Memory chips on the motherboard are of two main kinds. RAM chips: DRAM (dynamic, needs refreshing), SRAM (static, faster, used in cache), SDRAM and DDR SDRAM (synchronised with the system clock). ROM chips: MROM, PROM, EPROM, EEPROM and flash. Memory chips are mounted on modules, the SIMM and the DIMM. ## Unit 4 memory chips.
qp v | What is Bus? Explain different types of buses in computers. || بس کیا ہے؟ کمپیوٹرز میں بسوں کی مختلف اقسام کی وضاحت کریں۔ || بس څه دی؟ په کمپیوټرونو کې د بسونو مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: A bus is a set of wires that carries data, addresses and control signals between the parts of the computer. Types: (1) Data bus: carries the data. (2) Address bus: carries memory or device addresses. (3) Control bus: carries timing and control signals. By use there are also the system (front-side) bus connecting CPU and memory, and expansion or I/O buses such as PCI, AGP, PCIe and USB. ## Unit 4 buses.
qp vi | Write note on SDRAM and DDR SDRAM. || ایس ڈی ریم اور ڈی ڈی آر ایس ڈی ریم پر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || په SDRAM او DDR SDRAM نوټ ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: SDRAM (synchronous DRAM) is dynamic RAM that is synchronised with the system clock of the CPU, so it can accept a new instruction before the earlier one is finished; this makes it faster than older asynchronous DRAM. DDR SDRAM (double data rate SDRAM) transfers data on both the rising and the falling edge of the clock, so it gives nearly twice the data rate of ordinary SDRAM. DDR2, DDR3 and DDR4 are later, faster versions. ## Unit 4 SDRAM and DDR.
