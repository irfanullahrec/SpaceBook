q  | Q3. Give detailed answers of the following questions. || سوال ۳: درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات دیں۔ || ۳ پوښتنه: د لاندې پوښتنو تفصیلي ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp i | What are the advantages and disadvantages of wireless networks? || وائرلیس نیٹ ورکس کے فوائد اور نقصانات کیا ہیں؟ || د بې سیمو شبکو ګټې او زیانونه څه دي؟
sol Model answer: Advantages of wireless networks: no cables, so easy and cheap installation; mobility and anywhere access; easy to add new devices; useful in places where cabling is difficult; quick set-up for temporary networks. Disadvantages: lower speed than wired networks; interference from other devices and walls; weaker security (signals can be intercepted); limited range; affected by weather and obstacles; health concerns about radiation are discussed. ## Unit 6 wireless networks.
qp ii | Explain different types of short distance communications technologies. || مختصر فاصلے کی مواصلاتی ٹیکنالوجیز کی مختلف اقسام بیان کریں۔ || د لنډ واټن اړیکو ټکنالوجیو مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Short-distance technologies: (1) Bluetooth: low-power radio, about 10 metres, connects phones, headsets and keyboards. (2) Wi-Fi: IEEE 802.11 standard, about 100 metres, wireless LAN and Internet access through access points. (3) Infrared (IrDA): light signals, a few metres, line of sight, for remote controls and file exchange. (4) Others: NFC for a few centimetres (contactless payment) and ZigBee for low-power sensors. ## Unit 6 short-distance communication.
qp iii | Describe long distance communications technologies in detail. || طویل فاصلے کی مواصلاتی ٹیکنالوجیز کو تفصیل سے بیان کریں۔ || د اوږد واټن اړیکو ټکنالوجۍ په تفصیل بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Long-distance technologies: (1) Cellular mobile networks (GSM, CDMA, 3G, 4G, 5G): divide an area into cells, each with a base station; voice, SMS and data for mobile users over wide areas. (2) WiMAX: high-speed broadband wireless over several kilometres. (3) Microwave links: towers in line of sight, up to about 50 km each. (4) Satellite communication: signals pass through a satellite for TV, telephone, Internet and GPS anywhere on Earth. ## Unit 6 long-distance communication.
qp iv | Explain different classifications of Satellite Systems. || سیٹلائٹ سسٹمز کی مختلف درجہ بندیوں کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د سپوږمکیو سیسټمونو مختلفې طبقه بندۍ تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Satellite systems are classified by their orbit: (1) LEO (Low Earth Orbit), about 160 to 2,000 km, fast moving, short delay, used for imaging, Iridium phones and Starlink. (2) MEO (Medium Earth Orbit), about 2,000 to 35,000 km, used for navigation (GPS). (3) GEO (Geostationary Earth Orbit), about 35,800 km (22,000 miles) above the equator, moves with the Earth so it stays over one spot, used for TV broadcast and communication. (HEO, the highly elliptical orbit, is used for coverage near the poles.) ## Unit 6 satellites.
qp v | What are the Requirements of Mobile Communication System? || موبائل مواصلاتی نظام کی ضروریات کیا ہیں؟ || د ګرځنده اړیکو سیسټم اړتیاوې څه دي؟
sol Model answer: Requirements of a mobile communication system: (1) Mobile terminals (phones, laptops) small, light and low in power use. (2) Wireless link with enough bandwidth and a reliable radio channel. (3) Base stations or access points to cover the area (cells). (4) Handoff/handover: keep the call when a user moves from one cell to another. (5) Roaming and location tracking, so the user can be reached anywhere. (6) Security and authentication of users. (7) Interconnection with wired networks and the Internet. ## Unit 6 mobile communication systems.
qp vi | Give important features and limitations of Mobile Communication System. || موبائل مواصلاتی نظام کی اہم خصوصیات اور حدود بیان کریں۔ || د ګرځنده اړیکو سیسټم مهمې ځانګړتیاوې او محدودیتونه ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Features of mobile communication: mobility and portability (use anywhere), wireless connection, voice, text and data services, wide coverage through cells, roaming, low cost compared with fixed lines. Limitations: limited battery life, lower bandwidth and speed than wired links, signal loss and interference in some places, dropped calls during handoff, security risks, limited coverage in remote areas, and small screens on devices. ## Unit 6 mobile communication systems.
