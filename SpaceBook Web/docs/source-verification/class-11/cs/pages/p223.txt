## a. Hierarchal Database Model || الف۔ ہائیرارکل ڈیٹا بیس ماڈل || الف. درجه بندي ډېټابېس ماډل
p In this type of model, data is organized into a tree-like structure. There is a hierarchy of parent and child segments.
