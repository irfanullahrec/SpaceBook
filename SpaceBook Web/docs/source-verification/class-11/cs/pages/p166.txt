## 5.3 TCP/IP || ۵.۳ ٹی سی پی / آئی پی || ۵.۳ TCP/IP
p Communications between computers on a network is done through protocol suits. The most widely used and most widely available protocol suite is TCP/IP protocol suite. TCP/IP is an industry standard suite of protocols designed for local and wide area networks.
