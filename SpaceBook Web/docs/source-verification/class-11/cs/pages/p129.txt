qp i | Differentiate between CPU and System unit. || سی پی یو اور سسٹم یونٹ میں فرق کریں۔ || د CPU او سیسټم واحد ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: The CPU is the processor chip, the part that executes instructions (ALU, control unit and registers). The system unit is the whole box (case) that houses the CPU together with the motherboard, memory, power supply, disk drives, expansion cards and other internal parts. So the CPU is one component inside the system unit. ## Unit 4 system unit.
qp ii | Define two main types of computer casings. || کمپیوٹر کیسنگز کی دو بنیادی اقسام کی تعریف کریں۔ || د کمپیوټر کېسنګونو دوه اصلي ډولونه تعریف کړئ.
sol Model answer: The two main types of computer casing are: (1) Desktop case, which is laid flat on the desk and the monitor is placed on top. (2) Tower case, which stands upright and is of three kinds: mini, mid and full tower, giving more space for drives and expansion cards and easier cooling. ## Unit 4 casings.
qp iii | List different parts of a motherboard. || مدر بورڈ کے مختلف حصوں کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د مدربورډ مختلفې برخې لست کړئ.
sol Model answer: Main parts of a motherboard: CPU socket, RAM slots (DIMM), chipset, BIOS/CMOS chip and battery, expansion slots (PCI, PCIe, AGP), IDE/SATA connectors for drives, power connector, buses, and ports/connectors (USB, keyboard, mouse, audio, network, video). ## Unit 4 motherboard.
qp iv | What is role of BIOS in computer system? || کمپیوٹر سسٹم میں بائیوس کا کیا کردار ہے؟ || په کمپیوټر سیسټم کې د BIOS څه کردار دی؟
sol Model answer: BIOS (Basic Input Output System) is the firmware stored in a ROM/flash chip on the motherboard. When the computer is switched on it runs the power-on self test (POST) to check the hardware, finds and loads the operating system from the disk (boot), and provides basic input/output services to the hardware. ## Unit 4 BIOS.
qp v | Differentiate between a port and a slot. || پورٹ اور سلاٹ میں فرق کریں۔ || د پورټ او سلاټ ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: A port is an external socket or connector on the case where a peripheral device (keyboard, mouse, printer, USB drive, monitor) is plugged in. A slot is an opening on the motherboard inside the system unit where an expansion card (sound card, graphics card, network card) or memory module is inserted. ## Unit 4 ports and slots.
qp vi | Name different types of data cables with their purpose of use. || ڈیٹا کیبلز کی مختلف اقسام کے نام ان کے استعمال کے مقصد کے ساتھ لکھیں۔ || د ډېټا د کېبلونو مختلف ډولونو نومونه د هغو د کارونې د موخې سره ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Data cables: (1) Ribbon cable (IDE/PATA): wide flat cable with many parallel wires that connects hard disks and optical drives to the motherboard. (2) SATA cable: thin serial cable for connecting storage drives; faster than IDE. (3) Floppy drive cable: ribbon cable for the floppy disk drive. (4) Power cables: carry power from the power supply to the motherboard and drives. ## Unit 4 cables.
qp vii | Why cooling system is important for a computer? || کمپیوٹر کے لیے کولنگ سسٹم کیوں اہم ہے؟ || د کمپیوټر لپاره د یخولو سیسټم ولې مهم دی؟
sol Model answer: Components such as the processor, power supply and graphics card produce heat. Too much heat slows the computer, causes errors and crashes, and can permanently damage chips and shorten their life. A cooling system (heat sinks, fans, liquid cooling and case ventilation) removes the heat and keeps the temperature in the safe range. ## Unit 4 cooling system.
qp viii | Differentiate between SIMM and DIMM. || ایس آئی ایم ایم اور ڈی آئی ایم ایم میں فرق کریں۔ || د SIMM او DIMM ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: SIMM (Single In-line Memory Module) has contacts on both sides of the module joined together so they carry the same signal, giving a 32-bit data path (older computers). DIMM (Dual In-line Memory Module) has separate contacts on the two sides, giving a 64-bit data path, higher speed and capacity (modern computers). ## Unit 4 memory modules.
