q  | Q2. Give short answers to the following questions. || سوال ۲: درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات دیں۔ || ۲ پوښتنه: د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp i | What is Radio Transceiver? || ریڈیو ٹرانسیور کیا ہے؟ || راډیو ټرانسیور څه دی؟
sol Model answer: A radio transceiver is a device that contains both a radio transmitter and a radio receiver in one unit, so it can send as well as receive data through radio signals. Examples are walkie-talkies, mobile phones and the radio in a Wi-Fi router or Bluetooth device. ## Unit 6 radio signals.
qp ii | What is ‘Hotspot’? || ’’ہاٹ اسپاٹ‘‘ کیا ہے؟ || ‘ګرم ځای’ څه دی؟
sol Model answer: A hotspot is a public or private place where a wireless access point gives Wi-Fi Internet access to nearby devices such as laptops and phones, for example in an airport, hotel, cafe or on a phone shared as a hotspot. ## Unit 6 Wi-Fi.
qp iii | What does IEEE stand for? || آئی ای ای ای کا مطلب کیا ہے؟ || IEEE د څه لنډیز دی؟
sol Model answer: IEEE stands for the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, a professional organisation that sets standards in electrical, electronic and computer fields (for example 802.11 for Wi-Fi). ## Unit 6 Wi-Fi standards.
qp iv | What is meant by line of sight communication? || خطِ نظر مواصلات سے کیا مراد ہے؟ || د لیدو کرښې اړیکو څخه څه مراد دی؟
sol Model answer: Line of sight communication means that the sending and receiving devices must be in direct view of each other, with no obstacle such as walls or hills between them, because the signal travels in a straight line. Infrared remote controls and microwave towers use it. ## Unit 6 infrared and microwave.
qp v | Differentiate between short distance and long distance wireless communication. || مختصر فاصلے اور طویل فاصلے کی وائرلیس مواصلات میں فرق کریں۔ || د لنډ واټن او اوږد واټن بې سیمو اړیکو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Short-distance wireless communication works over a few centimetres to about 100 metres, for example Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, infrared and NFC, and is used inside a room, a building or a campus. Long-distance wireless communication covers many kilometres up to the whole world, for example cellular (GSM/CDMA) networks, WiMAX, microwave links and satellite communication. ## Unit 6 types of wireless communication.
qp vi | What is Global Positioning System (GPS)? || گلوبل پوزیشننگ سسٹم (جی پی ایس) کیا ہے؟ || نړیوال موقعیت سیسټم (GPS) څه دی؟
sol Model answer: The Global Positioning System (GPS) is a satellite-based navigation system made of 24 satellites operated by the U.S. Department of Defense. A GPS receiver gets signals from several satellites and calculates its exact position, speed and time. It is used for land, sea and air navigation, maps, tracking vehicles and ships, and works in any weather. ## Unit 6 GPS.
qp vii | What is Wireless Markup Language? || وائرلیس مارک اپ لینگویج کیا ہے؟ || بې سیمه نښه کولو ژبه څه ده؟
sol Model answer: Wireless Markup Language (WML) is a markup language based on XML made for devices that use the Wireless Application Protocol, such as mobile phones. Like HTML, it supports text and image presentation, hyperlinks, forms and data input, and navigation, and it was the first markup language standard for wireless devices. ## Unit 6 WML.
qp viii | What is Wireless Application Protocol? || وائرلیس ایپلیکیشن پروٹوکول کیا ہے؟ || بې سیمه اپلېکېشن پروتوکول څه دی؟
sol Model answer: The Wireless Application Protocol (WAP) is an open standard protocol that gives Internet access and information services to mobile users of wireless phones, pagers and PDAs. A WAP gateway connects the wireless network to the Internet, and pages are written in WML. ## Unit 6 WAP.
qp ix | Write short note on Web Protocol Stack? || ویب پروٹوکول اسٹیک پر مختصر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || د ویب پروتوکول ټولګې په اړه لنډ یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: The Web (WAP) protocol stack is the group of layers used for web communication from a wireless device: Application layer (WAE, the environment with WML and scripts), Session layer (WSP), Transaction layer (WTP), Security layer (WTLS, encryption), Transport layer (WDP, datagrams) and the bearer networks (GSM, CDMA, SMS) at the bottom. It is similar to the TCP/IP and OSI stacks but made for low bandwidth and small devices. ## Unit 6 web protocol stack; layer names can be matched with the book's figure.
