q  | Q3. Give detailed answers to the following questions. || سوال ۳: درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جواب دیں۔ || ۳ پوښتنه: د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp i | What are computing devices? Explain early and modern computing devices. || کمپیوٹنگ آلات کیا ہیں؟ ابتدائی اور جدید کمپیوٹنگ آلات کی وضاحت کریں۔ || کمپیوټینګ وسایل څه دي؟ لومړني او مدرن کمپیوټینګ وسایل تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Computing devices are tools used to perform calculations or process data. Early computing devices: abacus (beads on rods), Napier's bones, slide rule, Pascaline (Blaise Pascal's mechanical adding machine), Leibniz's stepped reckoner, Babbage's Difference Engine and Analytical Engine, Hollerith's tabulating machine, and Mark 1 (electromechanical). Modern computing devices: desktop and laptop computers, notebooks, tablets, smartphones, PDAs, servers, mainframes and supercomputers, all electronic and programmable. ## Unit 1 sections on early and modern computing devices.
qp ii | Explain different classifications of computers. || کمپیوٹرز کی مختلف درجہ بندیوں کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د کمپیوټرونو مختلفې درجه بندۍ تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Classification of computers: (1) By type of data: analog (work on continuous quantities such as temperature), digital (work on discrete 0/1 data) and hybrid (both). (2) By purpose: general-purpose (can do many jobs) and special-purpose (made for one job). (3) By size and power: supercomputers (fastest, used for weather and space research), mainframe computers (large organisations, thousands of users), minicomputers (medium business, tens to hundreds of users) and microcomputers (personal computers: desktop, laptop, notebook, tablet, PDA). ## Unit 1 classification of computers.
qp iii | What is software? Explain its two main types with examples. || سافٹ ویئر کیا ہے؟ اس کی دو بنیادی اقسام کی مثالوں کے ساتھ وضاحت کریں۔ || سافټویر څه دی؟ د هغه دوه اصلي ډولونه د بېلګو سره تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Software is the set of programs and instructions that tell the hardware what to do. Two main types: (1) System software operates and controls the computer system, for example the operating system (Windows, Linux), device drivers, utility software and language processors (compiler, interpreter, assembler). (2) Application software helps users do particular jobs, for example MS Word, Excel, a web browser or a game. ## Unit 1 software section.
qp iv | Explain different types of General Purpose Application Software. || عمومی مقصد کے ایپلیکیشن سافٹ ویئر کی مختلف اقسام کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د عمومي موخې اپلیکېشن سافټویر مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: General-purpose application software: (1) Word processing (MS Word) for documents. (2) Spreadsheet (MS Excel) for calculations, tables and charts. (3) Database management software (MS Access) for storing and finding records. (4) Presentation software (PowerPoint) for slide shows. (5) Desktop publishing (Publisher) for books and brochures. (6) Graphics and image editing (Paint, Photoshop). (7) Web browser and email programs. (8) Multimedia players. ## Unit 1 application software section.
qp v | Write short note on any five input devices. || کسی بھی پانچ ان پٹ آلات پر مختصر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || په هر پنځو د دخلولو وسایلو لنډ نوټ ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Five input devices: (1) Keyboard: enters text, numbers and commands with keys. (2) Mouse: pointing device that moves the cursor and selects with clicks. (3) Scanner: converts printed pictures or text into digital form. (4) Digital camera: captures pictures and video in digital form. (5) Microphone: converts sound into digital signals. (Light pen, joystick, touch screen and barcode reader are other input devices.) ## Unit 1 input devices.
qp vi | What is output? Briefly explain softcopy output devices. || آؤٹ پٹ کیا ہے؟ سافٹ کاپی آؤٹ پٹ آلات کی مختصراً وضاحت کریں۔ || وتل څه دی؟ د سافټ کاپي وتلو وسایل په لنډه توګه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Output is the result or information that the computer gives after processing. Softcopy output is output that appears on a screen or is heard, and it is not permanent. Softcopy output devices: (1) Monitor (CRT, LCD, LED) displays text, pictures and video; (2) Speakers and headphones give sound output; (3) Multimedia projector shows the screen on a big wall or screen. ## Unit 1 output devices.
qp vii | What is the importance of magnetic cards/devices based systems? Explain different types of magnetic cards. || مقناطیسی کارڈز / آلات پر مبنی نظاموں کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ مقناطیسی کارڈز کی مختلف اقسام کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د مقناطیسي کارتونو / وسایلو پر بنسټ سیسټمونو څه اهمیت دی؟ د مقناطیسي کارتونو مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Magnetic cards store data on a magnetic stripe or chip on a plastic card. Importance: they are cheap, small, easy to carry and can hold identification and account data so that a person can be recognised and a transaction done quickly and securely. Types: (1) Magnetic stripe cards (ATM, debit/credit, ID, hotel key cards), read by swiping; (2) Smart cards, with a built-in chip and memory (SIM card, bank chip cards, e-ID); (3) Prepaid and membership cards (phone cards, transport cards). ## Unit 1 magnetic card / device based systems.
qp viii | What are impact and non-impact printers? Explain any two types of printers in each category. || امپیکٹ اور نان امپیکٹ پرنٹرز کیا ہیں؟ ہر زمرے میں سے کسی دو اقسام کے پرنٹرز کی وضاحت کریں۔ || ټکر او بې ټکره پرنټرونه څه دي؟ په هره ډله کې د هر دوو ډولونو پرنټرونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Impact printers print by striking an inked ribbon against the paper with pins or hammers; they are noisy but can make carbon copies. Examples: (1) Dot-matrix printer, which strikes the ribbon with a matrix of pins; (2) Daisy-wheel or line printer. Non-impact printers print without striking the paper, so they are quiet and give better quality. Examples: (1) Inkjet printer, which sprays tiny drops of ink; (2) Laser printer, which uses a laser beam, toner and heat to form the image. ## Unit 1 printers.
qp ix | How a plotter is different from a printer? Explain different types of plotters. || پلاٹر پرنٹر سے کیسے مختلف ہے؟ پلاٹرز کی مختلف اقسام کی وضاحت کریں۔ || پلاټر له پرنټر نه څنګه توپیر لري؟ د پلاټرونو مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: A printer prints text and small graphics on normal sized paper, while a plotter draws large, high-quality drawings and designs such as maps, building plans and engineering diagrams, using pens or inkjets over big sheets. Types of plotters: (1) Drum plotter, where paper moves over a drum while pens move sideways; (2) Flatbed plotter, where paper is fixed on a flat table and the pen moves over it; (3) Inkjet plotter, which sprays ink for large colour prints. ## Unit 1 plotters.
