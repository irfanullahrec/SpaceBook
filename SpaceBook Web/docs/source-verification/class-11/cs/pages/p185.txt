qp i | Show all the modes of data communication with the help of a diagram. || ڈیٹا مواصلات کے تمام طریقے خاکے کی مدد سے دکھائیں۔ || د ډېټا اړیکو ټول حالتونه د انځور په مرسته وښیئ.
sol Model answer: Modes of data communication (diagram described): (1) Simplex: A → B. Data flows in one direction only; A is always the sender and B always the receiver (keyboard to computer, radio or TV broadcast). (2) Half-duplex: A ↔ B, both directions but only one at a time (walkie-talkie). (3) Full-duplex: A ⇄ B, both directions at the same time on separate paths (telephone call). The diagram is drawn with two boxes A and B and arrows as shown. ## Unit 5 data communication modes.
qp ii | Differentiate between synchronous and asynchronous transmission. || ہم وقت اور غیر ہم وقت ترسیل میں فرق کریں۔ || په همغږي او ناهمغږي لېږد کې توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: In synchronous transmission data is sent as a continuous block of many bytes (frame) controlled by a common clock, with no gaps between characters; it is fast and used for large transfers between computers. In asynchronous transmission data is sent one byte (character) at a time, each with a start bit and a stop bit, with gaps in between and no common clock; it is simple and cheap but slower, used for keyboards and serial links. ## Unit 5 transmission types.
qp iii | Differentiate between guided and unguided media. || رہنمائی یافتہ اور غیر رہنمائی یافتہ وسائط میں فرق کریں۔ || په لارښوونه شوې او بې لارښوونې رسنیو کې توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Guided media carry the signal through a physical path (cable): twisted pair, coaxial cable and fibre optic; they give a more secure link but need laying. Unguided media carry the signal through the air or space without a physical path: radio waves, microwaves, infrared and satellite; they allow mobility and wide coverage but are less secure and more affected by weather and interference. ## Unit 5 transmission media.
qp iv | Differentiate between LAN and WAN. || لین اور وین میں فرق کریں۔ || په LAN او WAN کې توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: A LAN (local area network) covers a small area such as a room, building or campus, is usually owned by one organisation, has high speed (up to Gbps), low error rate and low cost, and uses cables or Wi-Fi. A WAN (wide area network) covers a large area such as cities or countries and joins many LANs, uses leased lines, satellites or the Internet, is slower, costs more and has more errors. The Internet is the largest WAN. ## Unit 5 network types.
qp v | What is OSI Model? || او ایس آئی ماڈل کیا ہے؟ || د OSI ماډل څه دی؟
sol Model answer: The OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) model is a reference model of the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) that divides network communication into seven layers, so that different systems and vendors can work together. The layers from the top are: Application, Presentation, Session, Transport, Network, Data Link and Physical. Each layer has its own job and talks to the layer above and below. ## Unit 5 OSI model.
qp vi | Compare TCP/IP Model with OSI Model. || ٹی سی پی / آئی پی ماڈل کا او ایس آئی ماڈل سے موازنہ کریں۔ || د TCP/IP ماډل له OSI ماډل سره پرتله کړئ.
sol Model answer: TCP/IP vs OSI: TCP/IP has four layers (Application, Transport, Internet, Network Access) while OSI has seven (the OSI Application, Presentation and Session layers are joined in the TCP/IP Application layer, and Data Link with Physical form the Network Access layer). TCP/IP was developed first, for the Internet, and is the protocol suite really in use; OSI is a theoretical reference model used for teaching and design. OSI separates services, interfaces and protocols clearly, TCP/IP does not. ## Unit 5 TCP/IP and OSI.
qp vii | Differentiate between circuit switched and packet switched networks. || سرکٹ سوئچڈ اور پیکٹ سوئچڈ نیٹ ورکس میں فرق کریں۔ || په سرکټ سویچ شویو او کڅوړه سویچ شویو شبکو کې توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: In a circuit-switched network a dedicated path between sender and receiver is set up before communication and kept during the whole call; the bandwidth is reserved even if nothing is sent, delay is constant, and the example is the telephone network. In a packet-switched network the message is split into packets that travel independently over different paths and are put together at the destination; the line is shared, so it is efficient and cheap, the delay can vary, and the example is the Internet. ## Unit 5 switching.
qp viii | Briefly describe IP Addressing. || آئی پی ایڈریسنگ کو مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || د IP پتې کولو لنډه تشریح وکړئ.
sol Model answer: An IP address is a unique number that identifies each device on a network. IPv4 uses 32 bits written as four decimal numbers separated by dots (for example 192.168.1.10); it has a network part and a host part, and is divided into classes A, B, C (and D, E for special uses). IPv6 uses 128 bits written in hexadecimal to provide many more addresses. Addresses can be static (fixed) or dynamic (given by DHCP), and public or private. ## Unit 5 IP addressing.

qp i | Explain various modes of data communication. || ڈیٹا مواصلات کے مختلف طریقوں کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د ډېټا اړیکو مختلف حالتونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Modes of data communication are: (1) Simplex: one-way only, the sender can only send and the receiver can only receive (keyboard to computer, TV and radio broadcast). (2) Half-duplex: two-way, but one direction at a time (walkie-talkie, CB radio). (3) Full-duplex: two-way at the same time, using two channels (telephone, mobile call). ## Unit 5 data communication modes.
qp ii | What is guided media? Explain different types of guided media. || رہنمائی یافتہ وسائط کیا ہیں؟ رہنمائی یافتہ وسائط کی مختلف اقسام بیان کریں۔ || لارښوونه شوې رسنۍ څه دي؟ د لارښوونه شویو رسنیو مختلف ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Guided media (bounded media) carry the signal along a physical cable. Types: (1) Twisted pair cable: two insulated copper wires twisted together to reduce interference; UTP (unshielded) and STP (shielded); cheap, used in telephone and LAN cables (CAT5). (2) Coaxial cable: a central copper conductor with insulation and a metal shield; carries higher frequencies, used for TV and older LANs. (3) Fibre optic cable: thin glass or plastic strands that carry light signals; very high speed and bandwidth, no electrical interference, secure, but costly and difficult to install. ## Unit 5 guided media.
qp iii | Explain Radio wave and Microwave communications. || ریڈیو ویو اور مائیکرو ویو مواصلات کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د راډیو څپې او مایکرو څپې اړیکې تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Radio waves are electromagnetic waves with frequency from about 3 KHz to 1 GHz; they travel in all directions (omnidirectional), can pass through walls, and are used for radio, TV, cordless phones and mobile phones. Microwaves have higher frequencies (above 1 GHz), travel in a straight narrow beam (unidirectional) and need line of sight, so transmitting and receiving towers or satellite dishes must face each other; they are used for long-distance telephone links, satellite and mobile networks. ## Unit 5 unguided media.
qp iv | Write notes on switch, router and gateway. || سوئچ، راؤٹر اور گیٹ وے پر نوٹس لکھیں۔ || د سویچ، روټر او ګیټ وې په اړه یادښتونه ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: A switch connects devices inside one network (LAN) and sends the data only to the port of the device it is meant for, using MAC addresses; it reduces traffic and collisions. A router connects different networks (for example a LAN to the Internet) and finds the best path for each data packet using IP addresses. A gateway connects networks that use different protocols and translates between them; it works as an entry and exit point of a network. ## Unit 5 network devices.
qp v | Explain in detail Client/Server and Peer-to-Peer networks. || کلائنٹ/سرور اور پیئر ٹو پیئر نیٹ ورکس کی تفصیل سے وضاحت کریں۔ || د پېرېدونکي/خدمتګار او همتا-په-همتا شبکې په تفصیل تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: In a client/server network one or more powerful computers (servers) provide files, printers, email or applications to many client computers; the server controls security, backup and users, so it is easy to manage and secure but costs more and depends on the server. In a peer-to-peer network all computers are equal; each can share its own files and devices with the others, with no dedicated server, so it is cheap and easy to set up for small groups but is less secure, harder to manage and slower with many users. ## Unit 5 network models.
qp vi | Define network topology and explain its types. || نیٹ ورک ٹوپولوجی کی تعریف کریں اور اس کی اقسام بیان کریں۔ || د شبکې توپولوژي تعریف کړئ او ډولونه یې تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Network topology is the physical or logical arrangement of the devices (nodes) and links of a network. Types: (1) Bus: all nodes on one common cable; cheap, but a break stops the network. (2) Star: all nodes connect to a central hub or switch; easy to manage and fault-finding is easy, but the centre is a single point of failure. (3) Ring: each node connects to two neighbours in a closed loop; data moves in one direction. (4) Mesh: every device is connected to every other; very reliable but most expensive. (5) Tree (hybrid): star networks connected to a bus backbone. ## Unit 5 topologies.
qp vii | Describe the seven layers of OSI Model. || او ایس آئی ماڈل کی سات تہوں کو بیان کریں۔ || د OSI ماډل اوه طبقې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: The seven OSI layers (top to bottom): (7) Application: gives network services to the user (email, file transfer, web). (6) Presentation: translates, encrypts and compresses data. (5) Session: opens, manages and closes sessions between applications; security, name recognition and logging. (4) Transport: end-to-end delivery, error recovery and flow control (TCP, UDP). (3) Network: logical addressing and choosing the path (routing). (2) Data Link: forms frames, physical addressing (MAC), error detection on a link. (1) Physical: sends raw bits over the cable or air; cables, voltages, connectors. ## Unit 5 OSI layers.
qp viii | What is TCP/IP? Explain TCP/IP Protocol Suite. || ٹی سی پی / آئی پی کیا ہے؟ ٹی سی پی / آئی پی پروٹوکول سوئٹ کی وضاحت کریں۔ || TCP/IP څه دی؟ د TCP/IP پروتوکول ټولګه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: TCP/IP (Transmission Control Protocol / Internet Protocol) is the suite of protocols used on the Internet. Its layers: (1) Application: protocols used by programs: HTTP, FTP, SMTP, POP3, DNS, Telnet. (2) Transport: TCP gives reliable, connection-oriented delivery; UDP is fast and connectionless. (3) Internet: IP addresses and routes packets (IP, ICMP, ARP). (4) Network access: sends the frames over the physical network (Ethernet, Wi-Fi). ## Unit 5 TCP/IP.
