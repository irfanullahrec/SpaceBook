In those days we studied in the early classes of school. On reading about the defeat and destruction of Darius at the hands of Alexander the Great we did not feel much sorrow because we considered Alexander the Great a Muslim … and what is peculiar to Alexander the Great? Whatever names contained f, q, gh, z, etc., could not in any case be Hindus, for example: Philip, Aristotle, Plato, Pythagoras, Socrates, Hippocrates; and in those days in our view there were only two nations: Hindu and Muslim.
