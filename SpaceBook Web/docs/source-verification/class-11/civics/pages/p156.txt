It fixes a professional standard for newspapers and news agencies and makes them answerable to public feelings, fears and problems. This law provides newspapers and news agencies with opportunities to ensure their freedom. This law is relaxing the procedure for permission for any newly published material. News agencies can be set up very easily. The powers of the institutions and authorities that keep watch over the media are being reduced. Now the media will itself keep watch on itself under a code of ethics through a system of checks and balances. Under this law newspapers will have to pay a nominal and symbolic fine for a violation.
