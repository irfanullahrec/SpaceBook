For example social security, family life, the abolition of slavery, the rights of women and the rights of minorities and so on. In the early days Islam ended the special privileges of the elite and opened paths for ability. The Prophet ﷺ ended the inhuman treatment of girls among the Arab tribes, stopped the exploitation of the poor, and discouraged fraudulent contracts and theft. In 622 AD he made an agreement among the Muslims, Jews and other tribes of Medina called the Charter of Medina. By this agreement the fighting in Medina ended and tolerance and forbearance among the tribes were promoted. In this way the concept of the rights of minorities was presented and protection was provided to them. Through the Charter of Medina protection was given to the people of Medina, women received protection, freedom of religion was obtained and a judicial system was introduced for the settlement of disputes. The Prophet ﷺ made sure that bread and clothing were provided to prisoners without distinction.
