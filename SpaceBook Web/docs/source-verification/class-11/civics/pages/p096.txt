Brotherly and close relations have existed between the two countries from the first day. In 1956 the Pakistan–Iran border was settled in a peaceful manner. Both countries were members of the Baghdad Pact and when Iraq announced withdrawal from this pact and it was named CENTO, Iran and Pakistan were among the important members of CENTO. The two neighbours together with Turkey took part in the Istanbul agreement and laid the foundation of the organisation R.C.D., Regional Cooperation for Development. This same organisation has today been converted into the Economic Cooperation Organisation (E.C.O.). After the tragedy of East Pakistan Iran supported Pakistan’s needed economic condition. It gave Pakistan financial and technical help in establishing several industries. Iran has helped to establish a medical college and an engineering college in Balochistan. Iran gave foreign exchange of lakhs of dollars for the irrigation system in Balochistan. The government of Iran restored diplomatic relations between Afghanistan and Pakistan in 1963.
