example 24 | Show that $\cos20^\circ\cos40^\circ\cos80^\circ=\dfrac18$ || دکھائیں کہ $\cos20^\circ\cos40^\circ\cos80^\circ=\dfrac18$ || وښایاست چې $\cos20^\circ\cos40^\circ\cos80^\circ=\dfrac18$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $\text{L.H.S.}=\cos20^\circ\cos40^\circ\cos80^\circ=\dfrac14[4\cos20^\circ\cos40^\circ\cos80^\circ]=\dfrac14[(2\cos40^\circ\cos20^\circ)\,2\cos80^\circ]$
m $=\dfrac14[(\cos60^\circ+\cos20^\circ)\,2\cos80^\circ]=\dfrac14[(1/2+\cos20^\circ)\,2\cos80^\circ]=\dfrac14[\cos80^\circ+2\cos80^\circ\cos20^\circ]$
m $=\dfrac14[\cos80^\circ+\cos100^\circ+\cos60^\circ]=\dfrac14[\cos80^\circ+\cos(180^\circ-80^\circ)+\cos60^\circ]$
m $=\dfrac14\left[\cos80^\circ-\cos80^\circ+\dfrac12\right]\quad\because\cos(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta$
m $=\dfrac14\left[\dfrac12\right]=\dfrac18=\text{R.H.S}$
endexample
