example 13 | Find the sum of the series: $\dfrac{1}{1\cdot4}+\dfrac{1}{4\cdot7}+\dfrac{1}{7\cdot10}+\cdots$ to infinity. || سلسلے کا مجموعہ معلوم کریں: $\dfrac{1}{1\cdot4}+\dfrac{1}{4\cdot7}+\dfrac{1}{7\cdot10}+\cdots$ لامتناہی تک۔ || د لړۍ مجموعه پیدا کړئ: $\dfrac{1}{1\cdot4}+\dfrac{1}{4\cdot7}+\dfrac{1}{7\cdot10}+\cdots$ تر بې‌نهایته.
m **Solution:** Here $T_n=\dfrac{1}{(3n-2)(3n+1)}$
p Breaking it into Partial Fractions, we have || اسے جزوی کسور میں توڑنے سے ہمیں ملتا ہے || په جزوي کسرونو یې د وېشلو سره لرو
m $\dfrac{1}{(3n-2)(3n+1)}=\dfrac{A}{3n-2}+\dfrac{B}{3n+1}$
m Multiplying both sides by $(3n-2)(3n+1)$, we have $1=A(3n+1)+B(3n-2)$
m Comparing the coefficient of n and the constants both sides, we get $0=3A+3B$ (i), $1=A-2B$ (ii)
m Solving (i) and (ii) we get $A=\dfrac{1}{3},\ B=-\dfrac{1}{3}$
m $\therefore\ T_n=\dfrac{1}{3(3n-2)}-\dfrac{1}{3(3n+1)}=\dfrac{1}{3}\left[\dfrac{1}{3n-2}-\dfrac{1}{3n+1}\right]$
m and $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}T_k=\dfrac{1}{3}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\left(\dfrac{1}{3k-2}-\dfrac{1}{3k+1}\right)=\dfrac{1}{3}\left[\left(1-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{7}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{7}-\dfrac{1}{10}\right)+\cdots\right]=\dfrac{1}{3}(1)=\dfrac{1}{3}$
endexample
