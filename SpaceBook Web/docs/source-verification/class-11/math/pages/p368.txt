example 11 | Solve the equation $\sin\theta=\dfrac12$. || مساوات $\sin\theta=\dfrac12$ حل کریں۔ || معادله $\sin\theta=\dfrac12$ حل کړئ.
p **Solution:** We have $\sin\dfrac\pi6=\dfrac12$, so the reference angle is $\theta=\dfrac\pi6$. Since sine is positive in quadrant I and quadrant II, so the equation has two solutions in the interval $0\le\theta<2\pi$, one in quadrant I and the other in quadrant II i.e. $\theta=\dfrac\pi6$ or $\theta=\pi-\dfrac\pi6=\dfrac{5\pi}6$. example 12 | Solve the equation $\cos\theta=\dfrac12$. || مساوات $\cos\theta=\dfrac12$ حل کریں۔ || معادله $\cos\theta=\dfrac12$ حل کړئ.
p **Solution:** We have $\cos\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac12$, so the reference angle is $\theta=\dfrac\pi3$. Thus, the equation has two solutions in the interval $0\le\theta<2\pi$, one in quadrant I and the other in quadrant II i.e. $\theta=\dfrac\pi3$ or $\theta=2\pi-\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac{5\pi}3$ (the printed text says "quadrant II"; the second solution is in quadrant IV).
