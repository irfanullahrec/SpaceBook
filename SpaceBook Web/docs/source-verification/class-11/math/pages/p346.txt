example 2 | Find the range of each function. (i) $\cos3x$ (ii) $3\tan2x$ (iii) $2\csc\dfrac13x$ || ہر تفاعل کی سعت معلوم کریں۔ (i) $\cos3x$ (ii) $3\tan2x$ (iii) $2\csc\dfrac13x$ || د هرې تابع رینج پیدا کړئ. (i) $\cos3x$ (ii) $3\tan2x$ (iii) $2\csc\dfrac13x$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $\text{(i) We know that for all }t\in\text{dom }\cos t,\ -1\le\cos t\le1.\ \text{Let }t=3x\text{ then }-1\le\cos3x\le1.\ \text{Hence range }\cos3x\text{ is the closed interval }[-1,1]$
m $\text{(ii) Since for all }t\in\text{dom }\tan t,\ -\infty<\tan t<\infty.\ \text{Let }t=2x\text{ then }-\infty<\tan2x<\infty.\ \text{Hence }-\infty<3\tan2x<\infty.\text{ Thus range of }3\tan2x\text{ is }R.$
m $\text{(iii) Since for all }t\in\text{dom }\csc t:\ \csc t\le-1\text{ or }\csc t\ge1.\ \text{Let }t=\dfrac13x.\ \text{Then }\csc\dfrac13x\le-1\text{ or }\csc\dfrac13x\ge1.$
m $\text{Hence }2\csc\dfrac13x\le-2\text{ or }2\csc\dfrac13x\ge2.\ \text{Hence range of }2\csc\dfrac13x=R-\{p\mid-2<p<2\}.$
endexample
