p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $S=\dfrac12(a+b+c)=\dfrac12(75+55+50)=90$
m $S-a=90-75=15,\qquad S-b=90-55=35,\qquad S-c=90-50=40$
m $\text{Using half angle formula }\cos\dfrac\alpha2=\sqrt{\dfrac{S(S-a)}{bc}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{90(15)}{(55)(50)}}=0.700649\Rightarrow\dfrac\alpha2=45^\circ31′\text{ or }\alpha=91^\circ2′$
m $\text{Also }\cos\dfrac\beta2=\sqrt{\dfrac{S(S-b)}{ac}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{90\times35}{75\times50}}=0.9165\Rightarrow\dfrac\beta2=23^\circ35′,\ \beta=47^\circ10′$
m $\text{Hence }\gamma=180^\circ-(\alpha+\beta)=180^\circ-138^\circ12′=41^\circ48′$
endexample
