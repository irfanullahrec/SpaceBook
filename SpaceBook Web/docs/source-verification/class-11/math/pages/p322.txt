example 12 | Solve the triangle in which $\alpha=38^\circ,\ \beta=121^\circ$ and $a=20$ || وہ مثلث حل کریں جس میں $\alpha=38^\circ,\ \beta=121^\circ$ اور $a=20$ ہے || هغه درې ګونی حل کړئ چې په کې $\alpha=38^\circ,\ \beta=121^\circ$ او $a=20$ دي
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $\text{Since }\alpha+\beta+\gamma=180^\circ:\ \gamma=180^\circ-121^\circ-38^\circ=21^\circ$
m $\text{Use the law of sines to get }b:\ \dfrac b{\sin\beta}=\dfrac a{\sin\alpha}\Rightarrow b=\dfrac{a\sin\beta}{\sin\alpha}=20\times\dfrac{\sin121^\circ}{\sin38^\circ}=28\text{ approximately.}$
m $\text{Use the law of sines again but this time using }\alpha,\gamma\text{ to get }c:\ \dfrac c{\sin\gamma}=\dfrac a{\sin\alpha}\Rightarrow c=\dfrac a{\sin\alpha}\times\sin\gamma=20\times\dfrac{\sin21^\circ}{\sin38^\circ}=11.6\approx12\text{ approximately.}$
endexample
### (c) The Law of Tangents || (c) قانونِ ٹینجنٹ || (c) د ټینجنټو قانون
box theorem | Theorem || مسئلہ || قضیه
p In any triangle ABC, show that (i) $\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\alpha+\beta)}{\tan\frac12(\alpha-\beta)}$ (ii) $\dfrac{b+c}{b-c}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\beta+\gamma)}{\tan\frac12(\beta-\gamma)}$ (iii) $\dfrac{c+a}{c-a}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\gamma+\alpha)}{\tan\frac12(\gamma-\alpha)}$ || کسی بھی مثلث ABC میں دکھائیں کہ (i) $\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\alpha+\beta)}{\tan\frac12(\alpha-\beta)}$ (ii) $\dfrac{b+c}{b-c}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\beta+\gamma)}{\tan\frac12(\beta-\gamma)}$ (iii) $\dfrac{c+a}{c-a}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\gamma+\alpha)}{\tan\frac12(\gamma-\alpha)}$ || په هر درې ګوني ABC کې وښایاست چې (i) $\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\alpha+\beta)}{\tan\frac12(\alpha-\beta)}$ (ii) $\dfrac{b+c}{b-c}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\beta+\gamma)}{\tan\frac12(\beta-\gamma)}$ (iii) $\dfrac{c+a}{c-a}=\dfrac{\tan\frac12(\gamma+\alpha)}{\tan\frac12(\gamma-\alpha)}$
endbox
p **Proof:** By the law of sines in any triangle ABC $\dfrac a{\sin\alpha}=\dfrac b{\sin\beta}=\dfrac c{\sin\gamma}=D$ (say). We have $a=D\sin\alpha$ and $b=D\sin\beta$. || **ثبوت:** کسی بھی مثلث ABC میں قانونِ سائن سے $\dfrac a{\sin\alpha}=\dfrac b{\sin\beta}=\dfrac c{\sin\gamma}=D$ (فرض کریں)۔ ہمارے پاس $a=D\sin\alpha$ اور $b=D\sin\beta$ ہے۔ || **ثبوت:** په هر درې ګوني ABC کې د ساینو د قانون له مخې $\dfrac a{\sin\alpha}=\dfrac b{\sin\beta}=\dfrac c{\sin\gamma}=D$ (ووایو). موږ لرو $a=D\sin\alpha$ او $b=D\sin\beta$.

m $a+b=D(\sin\alpha+\sin\beta)\qquad(1)$
m $a-b=D(\sin\alpha-\sin\beta)\qquad(2)$
m $\text{From (1) and (2): }\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}=\dfrac{\sin\alpha+\sin\beta}{\sin\alpha-\sin\beta}$
p Using the formulae $\sin\alpha+\sin\beta=2\sin\dfrac{\alpha+\beta}2\cos\dfrac{\alpha-\beta}2$ || فارمولے استعمال کرتے ہوئے $\sin\alpha+\sin\beta=2\sin\dfrac{\alpha+\beta}2\cos\dfrac{\alpha-\beta}2$ || فورمولونه کارولو سره $\sin\alpha+\sin\beta=2\sin\dfrac{\alpha+\beta}2\cos\dfrac{\alpha-\beta}2$
