example 23 | Evaluate: (i) $\tan\left[\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (ii) $\tan\left[\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (iii) $\text{Tan}\left[\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (iv) $\text{Tan}\left[\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ || قیمت معلوم کریں: (i) $\tan\left[\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (ii) $\tan\left[\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (iii) $\text{Tan}\left[\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (iv) $\text{Tan}\left[\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ || ارزښت پیدا کړئ: (i) $\tan\left[\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (ii) $\tan\left[\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (iii) $\text{Tan}\left[\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$ (iv) $\text{Tan}\left[\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right]$
p **Solution:** (i) For $\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)$, we seek an angle whose cosine is $\left(-\dfrac12\right)$. The reference point is $\dfrac\pi3$. But cosine is negative in the II and III quadrants. Hence the required angles are $\left(\pi-\dfrac\pi3\right)$ and $\left(\pi+\dfrac\pi3\right)$. Adding the period $2n\pi$ we get, $\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\in\left\{\dfrac{2\pi}3+2n\pi\right\}\cup\left\{\dfrac{4\pi}3+2n\pi\right\},\ n\in Z$. Now $\tan\left(\dfrac{2\pi}3+2n\pi\right)=\tan\dfrac{2\pi}3=-\sqrt3$ and $\tan\left(\dfrac{4\pi}3+2n\pi\right)=\tan\dfrac{4\pi}3=+\sqrt3$ $\therefore\tan\left(\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right)=\tan\dfrac{2\pi}3\cup\left\{\tan\dfrac{4\pi}3\right\}=\{-\sqrt3,+\sqrt3\}$. (ii) $\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\text{Arc}\cos\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac{2\pi}3\in[0,\pi]$ (printed as $2\pi$). But $\tan\left(\dfrac{2\pi}3\right)=-\sqrt3$. Therefore $\tan\left(\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right)=\{-\sqrt3\}$ only. || **حل:** (i) $\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)$ کے لیے ہم ایسا زاویہ تلاش کرتے ہیں جس کا کوسائن $\left(-\dfrac12\right)$ ہو۔ حوالہ نقطہ $\dfrac\pi3$ ہے۔ لیکن کوسائن ربع II اور III میں منفی ہے۔ لہٰذا مطلوبہ زاویے $\left(\pi-\dfrac\pi3\right)$ اور $\left(\pi+\dfrac\pi3\right)$ ہیں۔ دور $2n\pi$ جمع کرنے پر ہمیں ملتا ہے $\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\in\left\{\dfrac{2\pi}3+2n\pi\right\}\cup\left\{\dfrac{4\pi}3+2n\pi\right\},\ n\in Z$۔ اب $\tan\left(\dfrac{2\pi}3+2n\pi\right)=\tan\dfrac{2\pi}3=-\sqrt3$ اور $\tan\left(\dfrac{4\pi}3+2n\pi\right)=\tan\dfrac{4\pi}3=+\sqrt3$ $\therefore\tan\left(\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right)=\tan\dfrac{2\pi}3\cup\left\{\tan\dfrac{4\pi}3\right\}=\{-\sqrt3,+\sqrt3\}$۔ (ii) $\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\text{Arc}\cos\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac{2\pi}3\in[0,\pi]$ (چھپا ہوا $2\pi$)۔ لیکن $\tan\left(\dfrac{2\pi}3\right)=-\sqrt3$۔ لہٰذا $\tan\left(\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right)=\{-\sqrt3\}$ صرف۔ || **حل:** (i) د $\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)$ لپاره موږ داسې زاویه لټوو چې کوساین یې $\left(-\dfrac12\right)$ وي. مرجع ټکی $\dfrac\pi3$ دی. خو کوساین په II او III ربع کې منفي دی. له دې امله غوښتل شوې زاویې $\left(\pi-\dfrac\pi3\right)$ او $\left(\pi+\dfrac\pi3\right)$ دي. دوره $2n\pi$ جمع کولو سره موږ ترلاسه کوو $\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\in\left\{\dfrac{2\pi}3+2n\pi\right\}\cup\left\{\dfrac{4\pi}3+2n\pi\right\},\ n\in Z$. اوس $\tan\left(\dfrac{2\pi}3+2n\pi\right)=\tan\dfrac{2\pi}3=-\sqrt3$ او $\tan\left(\dfrac{4\pi}3+2n\pi\right)=\tan\dfrac{4\pi}3=+\sqrt3$ $\therefore\tan\left(\cos^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)\right)=\tan\dfrac{2\pi}3\cup\left\{\tan\dfrac{4\pi}3\right\}=\{-\sqrt3,+\sqrt3\}$.
