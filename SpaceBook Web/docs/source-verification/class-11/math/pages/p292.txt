### 10.2 Trigonometric ratios of allied angles || 10.2 ہم رشتہ زاویوں کی مثلثیاتی نسبتیں || 10.2 د اړوندو زاویو مثلثاتي نسبتونه
p **10.2.1** The angles of measure $\dfrac\pi2\pm\theta,\ \pi\pm\theta,\ \dfrac{3\pi}2\pm\theta,\ 2\pi\pm\theta$ are called **allied angles**. Thus the angles which are connected with basic angles of measure $\theta$ by a right angle or its multiple are known as allied angles. || **10.2.1** پیمائش $\dfrac\pi2\pm\theta,\ \pi\pm\theta,\ \dfrac{3\pi}2\pm\theta,\ 2\pi\pm\theta$ والے زاویے **ہم رشتہ زاویے** کہلاتے ہیں۔ یوں وہ زاویے جو پیمائش $\theta$ کے بنیادی زاویوں سے قائمہ زاویے یا اس کے ضعف کے ذریعے جڑے ہوں ہم رشتہ زاویے کہلاتے ہیں۔ || **10.2.1** د اندازې $\dfrac\pi2\pm\theta,\ \pi\pm\theta,\ \dfrac{3\pi}2\pm\theta,\ 2\pi\pm\theta$ زاویې **اړوندې زاویې** بلل کېږي. په دې توګه هغه زاویې چې د اندازې $\theta$ له بنسټیزو زاویو سره د قایمې زاویې یا د هغې د ضعف په واسطه تړلې وي اړوندې زاویې بلل کېږي.
p **10.2.2 Derivation of trigonometric ratios of allied angles.** All the following trigonometric ratios of allied angles can be derived from the fundamental theorem of trigonometry and thus has been left for the students as an exercise. || **10.2.2 ہم رشتہ زاویوں کی مثلثیاتی نسبتوں کا اخذ۔** ہم رشتہ زاویوں کی درج ذیل تمام مثلثیاتی نسبتیں مثلثیات کے بنیادی مسئلے سے اخذ کی جا سکتی ہیں اور اس لیے طلبہ کے لیے بطور مشق چھوڑ دی گئی ہیں۔ || **10.2.2 د اړوندو زاویو د مثلثاتي نسبتونو پیدا کول۔** د اړوندو زاویو ټولې لاندې مثلثاتي نسبتونه د مثلثاتو له بنسټیز قضیې نه پیدا کېدای شي او له دې امله د زده کوونکو لپاره د تمرین په توګه پرېښودل شوې دي.
- i. $\sin\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\cos\theta,\qquad\sin\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=\cos\theta$ || i. $\sin\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\cos\theta,\qquad\sin\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=\cos\theta$ || i. $\sin\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\cos\theta,\qquad\sin\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=\cos\theta$
- ii. $\cos\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=-\sin\theta$ || ii. $\cos\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=-\sin\theta$ || ii. $\cos\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=-\sin\theta$
- iii. $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\cot\theta,\qquad\tan\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=-\cot\theta$ || iii. $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\cot\theta,\qquad\tan\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=-\cot\theta$ || iii. $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi2-\theta\right)=\cot\theta,\qquad\tan\left(\dfrac\pi2+\theta\right)=-\cot\theta$
- iv. $\sin(\pi-\theta)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(\pi-\theta)=-\cos\theta$ || iv. $\sin(\pi-\theta)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(\pi-\theta)=-\cos\theta$ || iv. $\sin(\pi-\theta)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(\pi-\theta)=-\cos\theta$
- v. $\sin(\pi+\theta)=-\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(\pi+\theta)=-\cos\theta$ || v. $\sin(\pi+\theta)=-\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(\pi+\theta)=-\cos\theta$ || v. $\sin(\pi+\theta)=-\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(\pi+\theta)=-\cos\theta$
- vi. $\tan(\pi-\theta)=-\tan\theta,\qquad\tan(\pi+\theta)=\tan\theta$ || vi. $\tan(\pi-\theta)=-\tan\theta,\qquad\tan(\pi+\theta)=\tan\theta$ || vi. $\tan(\pi-\theta)=-\tan\theta,\qquad\tan(\pi+\theta)=\tan\theta$
- vii. $\sin\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cos\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2-\theta\right)=-\sin\theta$ || vii. $\sin\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cos\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2-\theta\right)=-\sin\theta$ || vii. $\sin\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cos\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2-\theta\right)=-\sin\theta$
- viii. $\sin\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cos\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=\sin\theta$ || viii. $\sin\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cos\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=\sin\theta$ || viii. $\sin\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cos\theta,\qquad\cos\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=\sin\theta$
- ix. $\tan\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=\cot\theta,\qquad\tan\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cot\theta$ || ix. $\tan\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=\cot\theta,\qquad\tan\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cot\theta$ || ix. $\tan\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=\cot\theta,\qquad\tan\left(\dfrac{3\pi}2+\theta\right)=-\cot\theta$
- x. $\sin(2\pi-\theta)=-\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(2\pi-\theta)=\cos\theta$ || x. $\sin(2\pi-\theta)=-\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(2\pi-\theta)=\cos\theta$ || x. $\sin(2\pi-\theta)=-\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(2\pi-\theta)=\cos\theta$
- xi. $\sin(2\pi+\theta)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(2\pi+\theta)=\cos\theta$ || xi. $\sin(2\pi+\theta)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(2\pi+\theta)=\cos\theta$ || xi. $\sin(2\pi+\theta)=\sin\theta,\qquad\cos(2\pi+\theta)=\cos\theta$
- xii. $\tan(2\pi-\theta)=-\tan\theta,\qquad\tan(2\pi+\theta)=\tan\theta$ || xii. $\tan(2\pi-\theta)=-\tan\theta,\qquad\tan(2\pi+\theta)=\tan\theta$ || xii. $\tan(2\pi-\theta)=-\tan\theta,\qquad\tan(2\pi+\theta)=\tan\theta$
