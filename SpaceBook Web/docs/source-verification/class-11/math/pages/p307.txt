example 20 | Express $\cos4\theta-\cos2\theta$ as a product. || $\cos4\theta-\cos2\theta$ کو حاصل ضرب کی صورت میں ظاہر کریں۔ || $\cos4\theta-\cos2\theta$ د ضرب په بڼه وښیئ.
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $\text{We have }\cos\theta-\cos\phi=-2\sin\dfrac{\theta+\phi}2\sin\dfrac{\theta-\phi}2$
m $\therefore\ \cos4\theta-\cos2\theta=-2\sin\dfrac{4\theta+2\theta}2\sin\dfrac{4\theta-2\theta}2=-2\sin\dfrac{6\theta}2\sin\dfrac{2\theta}2=-2\sin3\theta\sin\theta$
endexample
example 21 | Show that $\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\cos\beta}{\sin\alpha+\sin\beta}=-\tan\dfrac12(\alpha-\beta)$ || دکھائیں کہ $\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\cos\beta}{\sin\alpha+\sin\beta}=-\tan\dfrac12(\alpha-\beta)$ || وښایاست چې $\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\cos\beta}{\sin\alpha+\sin\beta}=-\tan\dfrac12(\alpha-\beta)$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $\text{L.H.S}=\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\cos\beta}{\sin\alpha+\sin\beta}=\dfrac{-2\sin\frac{\alpha+\beta}2\sin\frac{\alpha-\beta}2}{2\sin\frac{\alpha+\beta}2\cos\frac{\alpha-\beta}2}=\dfrac{-\sin\frac{\alpha-\beta}2}{\cos\frac{\alpha-\beta}2}=-\tan\dfrac{\alpha-\beta}2=-\tan\dfrac12(\alpha-\beta)=\text{R.H.S}$
endexample
example 22 | Show that $\sin5\theta+2\sin3\theta+\sin\theta=4\sin3\theta\cos^2\theta$ || دکھائیں کہ $\sin5\theta+2\sin3\theta+\sin\theta=4\sin3\theta\cos^2\theta$ || وښایاست چې $\sin5\theta+2\sin3\theta+\sin\theta=4\sin3\theta\cos^2\theta$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $\text{L.H.S}=\sin5\theta+2\sin3\theta+\sin\theta=(\sin5\theta+\sin3\theta)+(\sin3\theta+\sin\theta)$
m $=2\sin\left(\dfrac{5\theta+3\theta}2\right)\cos\left(\dfrac{5\theta-3\theta}2\right)+2\sin\left(\dfrac{3\theta+\theta}2\right)\cos\left(\dfrac{3\theta-\theta}2\right)$
m $=2\sin\dfrac{8\theta}2\cos\dfrac{2\theta}2+2\sin\dfrac{4\theta}2\cos\dfrac{2\theta}2=2\sin4\theta\cos\theta+2\sin2\theta\cos\theta$
m $=2\cos\theta(\sin4\theta+\sin2\theta)=2\cos\theta\left[2\sin\left(\dfrac{4\theta+2\theta}2\right)\cos\left(\dfrac{4\theta-2\theta}2\right)\right]$
m $=2\cos\theta(2\sin3\theta\cos\theta)=4\sin3\theta\cos^2\theta=\text{R.H.S.}$
endexample
