example 28 | Sum the series $\frac{2}{3}, -1, \frac{3}{2},\ldots$ to 7 terms. || سلسلے $\frac{2}{3}, -1, \frac{3}{2},\ldots$ کو 7 ارکان تک جمع کریں۔ || لړۍ $\frac{2}{3}, -1, \frac{3}{2},\ldots$ تر 7 حدونو پورې جمع کړئ.
p **Solution:** The common ratio $=-\frac{3}{2}$; hence by formula $\dfrac{a_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$ || **حل:** مشترک نسبت $=-\frac{3}{2}$ ؛ لہٰذا فارمولے $\dfrac{a_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$ سے || **حل:** ګډ نسبت $=-\frac{3}{2}$؛ نو د فورمول $\dfrac{a_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$ له مخې
m The sum $=\dfrac{\frac{2}{3}\left\{1-\left(-\frac{3}{2}\right)^7\right\}}{1+\frac{3}{2}}=\dfrac{\frac{2}{3}\left\{1+\frac{2187}{128}\right\}}{\frac{5}{2}}=\dfrac{2}{3}\times\dfrac{2315}{128}\times\dfrac{2}{5}=\dfrac{463}{95}$
endexample
