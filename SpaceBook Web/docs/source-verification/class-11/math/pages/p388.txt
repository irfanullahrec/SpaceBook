example 27 | Evaluate $\cos\left(\text{Sin}^{-1}\dfrac45+\text{Cos}^{-1}\dfrac35\right)$ without tables or a calculator. || جدول یا کیلکولیٹر کے بغیر قیمت معلوم کریں $\cos\left(\text{Sin}^{-1}\dfrac45+\text{Cos}^{-1}\dfrac35\right)$۔ || پرته له جدول یا کلکولیټر ارزښت پیدا کړئ $\cos\left(\text{Sin}^{-1}\dfrac45+\text{Cos}^{-1}\dfrac35\right)$.
p **Solution:** Let $x=\text{Sin}^{-1}\dfrac45$ and $y=\text{Cos}^{-1}\dfrac35$ then $\sin x=\dfrac45$ and $\cos y=\dfrac35$, where $x$ and $y$ are in the 1st quadrant. We have $\cos(x+y)=\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y$. We know $\sin x$ but need $\cos x$: $\cos x=\sqrt{1-\sin^2x}$, as $\cos x$ is $+$ve in the 1st quadrant, $=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{16}{25}}=\dfrac35$. Again we know $\cos y$ but need $\sin y$: $\sin y=\sqrt{1-\cos^2y}$, $\sin y$ is $+$ve in quadrant 1, $=\sqrt{1-\dfrac9{25}}=\dfrac45$. || **حل:** فرض کریں $x=\text{Sin}^{-1}\dfrac45$ اور $y=\text{Cos}^{-1}\dfrac35$ تب $\sin x=\dfrac45$ اور $\cos y=\dfrac35$ جہاں $x$ اور $y$ پہلے ربع میں ہیں۔ ہمارے پاس $\cos(x+y)=\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y$ ہے۔ $\sin x$ معلوم ہے مگر $\cos x$ چاہیے: $\cos x=\sqrt{1-\sin^2x}$ کیونکہ پہلے ربع میں $\cos x$ مثبت ہے، $=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{16}{25}}=\dfrac35$۔ پھر $\cos y$ معلوم ہے مگر $\sin y$ چاہیے: $\sin y=\sqrt{1-\cos^2y}$، ربع 1 میں $\sin y$ مثبت ہے، $=\sqrt{1-\dfrac9{25}}=\dfrac45$۔ || **حل:** فرض کړئ $x=\text{Sin}^{-1}\dfrac45$ او $y=\text{Cos}^{-1}\dfrac35$ بیا $\sin x=\dfrac45$ او $\cos y=\dfrac35$ چېرته چې $x$ او $y$ په لومړي ربع کې دي. موږ لرو $\cos(x+y)=\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y$. $\sin x$ مو معلوم دی خو $\cos x$ پکار دی: $\cos x=\sqrt{1-\sin^2x}$ ځکه چې په لومړي ربع کې $\cos x$ مثبت دی، $=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{16}{25}}=\dfrac35$. بیا $\cos y$ معلوم دی خو $\sin y$ پکار دی: $\sin y=\sqrt{1-\cos^2y}$، په ربع 1 کې $\sin y$ مثبت دی، $=\sqrt{1-\dfrac9{25}}=\dfrac45$.
p Therefore || لہٰذا || له دې امله
m \cos\left[\text{Sin}^{-1}\dfrac45+\text{Cos}^{-1}\dfrac35\right]=\cos(x+y)=\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y=\dfrac35\times\dfrac35-\dfrac45\times\dfrac45=-\dfrac7{25}
endexample
example 28 | Evaluate $\sin\left(\text{Arc}\tan\dfrac12-\text{Arc}\cos\dfrac45\right)$ || قیمت معلوم کریں $\sin\left(\text{Arc}\tan\dfrac12-\text{Arc}\cos\dfrac45\right)$ || ارزښت پیدا کړئ $\sin\left(\text{Arc}\tan\dfrac12-\text{Arc}\cos\dfrac45\right)$
p **Solution:** Let $u=\text{Arc}\tan\dfrac12$ and $v=\text{Arc}\cos\dfrac45$, then $\tan u=\dfrac12$ and $\cos v=\dfrac45$. As $\tan u$ is $+$ve, and $u\in\left[-\dfrac\pi2,\dfrac\pi2\right]$ hence $u$ must be positive i.e. $u\in\left[0,\dfrac\pi2\right]$. Similarly $\cos v$ being positive means $v\in\left[0,\dfrac\pi2\right]$. We wish to find $\sin(u-v)$. Since $u$ and $v$ are in the interval $\left[0,\dfrac\pi2\right]$. They can be considered as the radian measure of positive acute angles and we may construct right angled triangles for $u$ and $v$ as shown in Fig 12.43.
