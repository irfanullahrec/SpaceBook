p The sequences given in example 3(a), 3(b) and 3(c) are special type of sequences, called the triangle number sequence, the factorial sequence and Pascal sequence respectively. These sequences play an important role in the expansion of binomial expressions like $(x+y)^n$. The complete Pascal sequence in 3(c) is $1, 4, 6, 4, 1, 0, 0, 0, 0,\ldots$ This is only one of the families of Pascal sequences.
