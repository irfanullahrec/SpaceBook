example 4 | Find the sum of the n terms of the series $1\cdot2^2+2\cdot3^2+3\cdot4^2+\cdots$ || سلسلے $1\cdot2^2+2\cdot3^2+3\cdot4^2+\cdots$ کے n ارکان کا مجموعہ معلوم کریں || د لړۍ $1\cdot2^2+2\cdot3^2+3\cdot4^2+\cdots$ د n حدونو مجموعه پیدا کړئ
m **Solution:** Here $T_j=j(j+1)^2$
m then $\sum_{j=1}^{n}T_j=\sum_{j=1}^{n}(j^3+2j^2+j)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}j^3+2\sum_{j=1}^{n}j^2+\sum_{j=1}^{n}j$
m $=\dfrac{n^2(n+1)^2}{4}+2\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}+\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$
m $=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{12}\left[3n^2+3n+8n+4+6\right]=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{12}\left[3n^2+11n+10\right]$
m $=\dfrac{1}{12}n(n+1)(n+2)(3n+5)$
endexample
example 5 | Find the sum of n terms of the series whose nth term is $2^{n-1}+8n^3-6n^2$. || اس سلسلے کے n ارکان کا مجموعہ معلوم کریں جس کا nواں رکن $2^{n-1}+8n^3-6n^2$ ہے۔ || د هغې لړۍ د n حدونو مجموعه پیدا کړئ چې nم حد یې $2^{n-1}+8n^3-6n^2$ دی.
m **Solution:** Given that $T_n=2^{n-1}+8n^3-6n^2$ then $T_j=2^{j-1}+8j^3-6j^2$
m $\sum_{j=1}^{n}T_j=\sum_{j=1}^{n}(2^{j-1}+8j^3-6j^2)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}2^{j-1}+8\sum_{j=1}^{n}j^3-6\sum_{j=1}^{n}j^2$
