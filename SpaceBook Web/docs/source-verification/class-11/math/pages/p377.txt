p (iii) Let $y=\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)$, we seek the angle whose cosine equals $-\dfrac12$. The reference point in the first quadrant is $\dfrac\pi3$. Hence for negative (cosine) we go to II quadrant by finding supplementary angle. $y=\pi-\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac{2\pi}3\in[0,\pi]$. Hence $\text{Cos}\,y=-\dfrac12\Rightarrow y=\dfrac{2\pi}3$. Thus $\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac{2\pi}3$. || (iii) فرض کریں $y=\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)$، ہم وہ زاویہ تلاش کرتے ہیں جس کا کوسائن $-\dfrac12$ کے برابر ہو۔ پہلے ربع میں حوالہ نقطہ $\dfrac\pi3$ ہے۔ لہٰذا منفی (کوسائن) کے لیے ہم تکملی زاویہ معلوم کر کے ربع II میں جاتے ہیں۔ $y=\pi-\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac{2\pi}3\in[0,\pi]$۔ لہٰذا $\text{Cos}\,y=-\dfrac12\Rightarrow y=\dfrac{2\pi}3$۔ یوں $\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac{2\pi}3$۔ || (iii) فرض کړئ $y=\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)$، موږ هغه زاویه لټوو چې کوساین یې د $-\dfrac12$ سره برابر وي. په لومړي ربع کې مرجع ټکی $\dfrac\pi3$ دی. له دې امله د منفي (کوساین) لپاره موږ بشپړوونکې زاویه پیدا کولو سره II ربع ته ځو. $y=\pi-\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac{2\pi}3\in[0,\pi]$. له دې امله $\text{Cos}\,y=-\dfrac12\Rightarrow y=\dfrac{2\pi}3$. په دې توګه $\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac{2\pi}3$.
p (iv) By definition $y=\text{Cos}^{-1}\left(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}2\right)$, if and only if $\text{Cos}\,y=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}2$, and $0\le y\le\pi$. The reference angle (1st quadrant) is $\dfrac\pi6$. But for negative cosine, $y$ lies in the second quadrant (as $0\le y\le\pi$). Thus $y=\pi-\dfrac\pi6=\dfrac{5\pi}6$.
