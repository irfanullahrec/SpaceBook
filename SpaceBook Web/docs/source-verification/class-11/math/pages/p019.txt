p When $n=-1$, $\left(\dfrac{x_1+iy_1}{x_2+iy_2}\right)^n$ reduces to $\left(\dfrac{x_1+iy_1}{x_2+iy_2}\right)^{-1}$ || جب $n=-1$ ہو تو یہ $\left(\dfrac{x_1+iy_1}{x_2+iy_2}\right)^{-1}$ بن جاتا ہے || کله چې $n=-1$ وي، دا په $\left(\dfrac{x_1+iy_1}{x_2+iy_2}\right)^{-1}$ بدلېږي
m We have $\left(\dfrac{x_1+iy_1}{x_2+iy_2}\right)^{-1}=\dfrac{x_2+iy_2}{x_1+iy_1}=\dfrac{x_2+iy_2}{x_1+iy_1}\times\dfrac{x_1-iy_1}{x_1-iy_1}$ (by rationalization)
m $=\dfrac{x_2x_1+y_2y_1}{x_1^2+y_1^2}+i\dfrac{y_2x_1-x_2y_1}{x_1^2+y_1^2}$ (by routine calculation)
m Therefore, real part $=\dfrac{x_2x_1+y_2y_1}{x_1^2+y_1^2}$ and imaginary part $=\dfrac{y_2x_1-x_2y_1}{x_1^2+y_1^2}$
