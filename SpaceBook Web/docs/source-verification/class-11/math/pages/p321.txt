p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $\text{Using law of sine }\dfrac{\sin\alpha}a=\dfrac{\sin\beta}b\Rightarrow\sin\alpha=30\times\dfrac{\sin85^\circ}{70}=0.4269\Rightarrow\alpha=25^\circ16'25''$
