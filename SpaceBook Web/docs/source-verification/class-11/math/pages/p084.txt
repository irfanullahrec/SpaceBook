q 1 | ABCDEF is a regular hexagon $\overrightarrow{AB}=\mathbf{a},\ \overrightarrow{BC}=\mathbf{b}$ and $\overrightarrow{CD}=\mathbf{c}$, state the following vectors as scalar multiple of $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}$ or $\mathbf{c}$. (i) $\overrightarrow{DE}$ (ii) $\overrightarrow{EF}$ (iii) $\overrightarrow{FA}$ (iv) $\overrightarrow{AD}$ (v) $\overrightarrow{BE}$. (i) $\overrightarrow{DE}$ (ii) $\overrightarrow{EF}$ (iii) $\overrightarrow{FA}$ (iv) $\overrightarrow{AD}$ (v) $\overrightarrow{BE}$. اشاره: په منظم شپږ ضلعي کې لوی قطر $\overline{AD}$ د ضلعې $\overline{BC}$ دوه چنده او ورسره موازي دی.
