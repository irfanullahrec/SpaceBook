q 1 | Write each of the following as a trigonometric function of a single angle. (i) $\sin37^\circ\cos22^\circ+\cos37^\circ\sin22^\circ$ (ii) $\cos83^\circ\cos53^\circ+\sin83^\circ\sin53^\circ$ (iii) $\cos19^\circ\cos5^\circ-\sin19^\circ\sin5^\circ$ (iv) $\sin40^\circ\cos15^\circ-\cos40^\circ\sin15^\circ$ (v) $\dfrac{\tan20^\circ+\tan32^\circ}{1-\tan20^\circ\tan32^\circ}$ (vi) $\dfrac{\tan35^\circ-\tan12^\circ}{1+\tan35^\circ\tan12^\circ}$

(i) $\cos(u+v)$ (ii) $\tan(u-v)$ (iii) $\sin(u-v)$ (iv) $\cos(u-v)$
q 4 | If $\sin\alpha=-\dfrac45$ and $\cos\beta=-\dfrac{12}{13}$, $\alpha$ in Quadrant III and $\beta$ in Quadrant II, find the exact value of: (i) $\sin(\alpha-\beta)$ (ii) $\cos(\alpha+\beta)$ (iii) $\tan(\alpha+\beta)$ || اگر $\sin\alpha=-\dfrac45$ اور $\cos\beta=-\dfrac{12}{13}$، $\alpha$ تیسرے ربع میں اور $\beta$ دوسرے ربع میں ہو تو صحیح قیمت معلوم کریں: (i) $\sin(\alpha-\beta)$ (ii) $\cos(\alpha+\beta)$ (iii) $\tan(\alpha+\beta)$ || که $\sin\alpha=-\dfrac45$ او $\cos\beta=-\dfrac{12}{13}$، $\alpha$ په دریم ربع او $\beta$ په دویم ربع کې وي نو دقیق ارزښت پیدا کړئ: (i) $\sin(\alpha-\beta)$ (ii) $\cos(\alpha+\beta)$ (iii) $\tan(\alpha+\beta)$
q 5 | If $\tan\alpha=\dfrac34$, $\sec\beta=\dfrac{13}5$, and neither the terminal side of the angle of measure $\alpha$ nor $\beta$ in the first quadrant, then find: (i) $\sin(\alpha+\beta)$ (ii) $\cos(\alpha+\beta)$ (iii) $\tan(\alpha+\beta)$ || اگر $\tan\alpha=\dfrac34$، $\sec\beta=\dfrac{13}5$ ہو اور نہ $\alpha$ اور نہ $\beta$ کا انتہائی بازو پہلے ربع میں ہو تو معلوم کریں: (i) $\sin(\alpha+\beta)$ (ii) $\cos(\alpha+\beta)$ (iii) $\tan(\alpha+\beta)$ || که $\tan\alpha=\dfrac34$، $\sec\beta=\dfrac{13}5$ وي او نه د $\alpha$ او نه د $\beta$ وروستی لوری په لومړي ربع کې وي نو پیدا کړئ: (i) $\sin(\alpha+\beta)$ (ii) $\cos(\alpha+\beta)$ (iii) $\tan(\alpha+\beta)$
q 6 | Show that: (i) $\cos\alpha=2\cos^2\dfrac\alpha2-1=1-2\sin^2\dfrac\alpha2$ (ii) $\sin(\alpha+\beta)\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\cos^2\beta-\cos^2\alpha$ || دکھائیں کہ: (i) $\cos\alpha=2\cos^2\dfrac\alpha2-1=1-2\sin^2\dfrac\alpha2$ (ii) $\sin(\alpha+\beta)\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\cos^2\beta-\cos^2\alpha$ || وښایاست چې: (i) $\cos\alpha=2\cos^2\dfrac\alpha2-1=1-2\sin^2\dfrac\alpha2$ (ii) $\sin(\alpha+\beta)\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\cos^2\beta-\cos^2\alpha$
q 7 | Show that: (i) $\cot(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{\cot\alpha\cot\beta-1}{\cot\alpha+\cot\beta}$ (ii) $\dfrac{\sin(\alpha+\beta)}{\cos\alpha\cos\beta}=\tan\alpha+\tan\beta$ || دکھائیں کہ: (i) $\cot(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{\cot\alpha\cot\beta-1}{\cot\alpha+\cot\beta}$ (ii) $\dfrac{\sin(\alpha+\beta)}{\cos\alpha\cos\beta}=\tan\alpha+\tan\beta$ || وښایاست چې: (i) $\cot(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{\cot\alpha\cot\beta-1}{\cot\alpha+\cot\beta}$ (ii) $\dfrac{\sin(\alpha+\beta)}{\cos\alpha\cos\beta}=\tan\alpha+\tan\beta$
q 8 | Prove that: (i) $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi4+\theta\right)=\dfrac{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}{\cos\theta-\sin\theta}$ (ii) $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi4-\theta\right)=\dfrac{1-\tan\theta}{1+\tan\theta}$ (iii) $\dfrac{\tan(\alpha+\beta)}{\cot(\alpha-\beta)}=\dfrac{\tan^2\alpha-\tan^2\beta}{1-\tan^2\alpha\tan^2\beta}$ (iv) $\dfrac{1-\tan\theta\tan\phi}{1+\tan\theta\tan\phi}=\dfrac{\cos(\theta+\phi)}{\cos(\theta-\phi)}$ || ثابت کریں کہ: (i) $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi4+\theta\right)=\dfrac{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}{\cos\theta-\sin\theta}$ (ii) $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi4-\theta\right)=\dfrac{1-\tan\theta}{1+\tan\theta}$ (iii) $\dfrac{\tan(\alpha+\beta)}{\cot(\alpha-\beta)}=\dfrac{\tan^2\alpha-\tan^2\beta}{1-\tan^2\alpha\tan^2\beta}$ (iv) $\dfrac{1-\tan\theta\tan\phi}{1+\tan\theta\tan\phi}=\dfrac{\cos(\theta+\phi)}{\cos(\theta-\phi)}$ || ثابته کړئ چې: (i) $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi4+\theta\right)=\dfrac{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}{\cos\theta-\sin\theta}$ (ii) $\tan\left(\dfrac\pi4-\theta\right)=\dfrac{1-\tan\theta}{1+\tan\theta}$ (iii) $\dfrac{\tan(\alpha+\beta)}{\cot(\alpha-\beta)}=\dfrac{\tan^2\alpha-\tan^2\beta}{1-\tan^2\alpha\tan^2\beta}$ (iv) $\dfrac{1-\tan\theta\tan\phi}{1+\tan\theta\tan\phi}=\dfrac{\cos(\theta+\phi)}{\cos(\theta-\phi)}$
