example 10 | Simplify each of the following. a) $\dfrac{\sin x\cos x}{\frac12\cos2x}$ b) $2\sin^2\dfrac x2+\cos x$ || ہر ایک کو سادہ کریں۔ a) $\dfrac{\sin x\cos x}{\frac12\cos2x}$ b) $2\sin^2\dfrac x2+\cos x$ || هر یو ساده کړئ. a) $\dfrac{\sin x\cos x}{\frac12\cos2x}$ b) $2\sin^2\dfrac x2+\cos x$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $\text{a) }\dfrac{\sin x\cos x}{\frac12\cos2x}=\dfrac22\cdot\dfrac{\sin x\cos x}{\frac12\cos2x}=\dfrac{2\sin x\cos x}{\cos2x}=\dfrac{\sin2x}{\cos2x}=\tan2x\quad(\text{using }\sin2x=2\sin x\cos x)$
m $\text{b) }2\sin^2\dfrac x2+\cos x=2\left(\dfrac{1-\cos x}2\right)+\cos x=1-\cos x+\cos x=1\quad\left(\text{using }\sin^2\dfrac x2=\dfrac{1-\cos x}2\right)$
endexample
