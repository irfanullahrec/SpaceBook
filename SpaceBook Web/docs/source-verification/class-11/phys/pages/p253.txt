p To understand how a wave moves along a vibrating cord, consider Figure 8.6: a wave moves to the left along a stretched string as end A makes one complete vibration. (i) At t = 0, A is at the mean position and point B is at a crest. (ii) After t = T/4, A is at the trough while B is at equilibrium; during this time the crest has moved right. (iii) After t = T/2, A passes through equilibrium while B is at the trough and the crest has moved further right. (iv) At t = 3T/4, A is at the crest while B is at the mean position and the crest has moved further. (v) After period T, A and B have each completed one oscillation and the crest has moved one wavelength λ.
