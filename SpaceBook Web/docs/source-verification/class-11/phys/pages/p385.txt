p GLOSSARY: Spherical wavefront—When a disturbance propagates in all directions from a point source. Stationary wave—Resultant wave arising from interference of two identical oppositely directed waves. System international—Printed definition: “The international agreed system of units used almost over.” [source wording appears malformed; preserved]. Systematic error—Error due to incorrect design or calibration of the measuring device. Terminal velocity—Maximum constant velocity of an object falling vertically downward. Torque—Turning effect of a force. Total internal reflection—When the angle of incidence increases by the critical angle, incident light is reflected back in the same material [definition retained as printed]. Trajectory—Path through space followed by a projectile. Translational equilibrium—Printed definition: “A body having zero linear acceleration.” Transverse wave—Wave in which medium particles vibrate perpendicular to the direction of propagation. Trough—Lower portion of a wave below the mean level. Turbulent flow—Disorderly and changing flow pattern of fluids. Unit vector—Vector of magnitude one used to denote direction. Vector quantity—Physical quantity having both magnitude and direction. Vector product—Product of two vectors that results in another vector. Wavefront—Surface passing through all points undergoing a similar disturbance (same phase) at a given instant. Wavelength—Distance between two consecutive wavefronts.
