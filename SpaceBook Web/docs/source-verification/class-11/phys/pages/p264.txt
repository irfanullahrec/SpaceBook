p The corrected elastic modulus for the isothermal process is γP = E. The speed of sound in air or gas becomes v = √(γP/ρ) (8.9), or v = √(γρₘgh/ρ) (8.10). For air, γ = 1.42; using mercury density at S.T.P. ρₘ = 13.6 g cm⁻³, g = 980 cm s⁻², h = 76 cm, and air density ρ = 0.001293 g cm⁻³, the book calculates v = 33310 cm s⁻¹ = 333 m s⁻¹. This agrees reasonably with experimental value 332 m s⁻¹, so the Laplace correction is supported. 8.4.2 EFFECTS OF VARIOUS FACTORS ON SPEED OF SOUND IN AIR. Sound waves are compressional mechanical waves in gas/air with speed v = √(γP/ρ). (1) Density: speed varies inversely with the square root of gas density. (2) Moisture: moisture reduces air’s resultant density, so speed increases with humidity; sound travels faster in damp than dry air. (3) Pressure: for one mole of an ideal gas of volume V and pressure P at temperature T, PV = RT (8.11). R is the general gas constant.
