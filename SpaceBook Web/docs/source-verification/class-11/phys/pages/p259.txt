p WAVELENGTH λ. The distance between two successive particles exactly in the same state of vibration is the wavelength λ. In Figure 8.11, A and C, P and Q, or B and D are in the same phase because they have the same state of vibration. One wavelength is emitted as the wave source completes one vibration. During time period T, the wave travels distance λ, so v = distance/time = λ/T = fλ (8.4). This relation is true for all waves. AMPLITUDE OF WAVE. The maximum displacement of a vibrating particle from its equilibrium position on either side is the amplitude, indicated by y₀. INTENSITY OF WAVE. Energy transmitted per second per unit area placed perpendicular to wave propagation is wave intensity, indicated by I. EXAMPLE 8.1 · A WAVE GENERATOR. A wave generator produces 500 pulses in 10 s.
