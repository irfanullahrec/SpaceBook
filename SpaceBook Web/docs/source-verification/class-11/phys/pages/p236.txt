p Where ω = √(k/m). The force required to displace the mass by x is F = −F_r = −(−mω²x) = mω²x (2). At displacement x the force is mω²x, while at x = 0 the force is zero. Average force during displacement x is F_av = (F_i+F_f)/2 = (0+kx)/2 = ½mω²x (7.15). Work done against restoring force: Work = F_av·x = ½mω²x² (7.16). By the work-energy principle, this work is stored as elastic potential energy in the oscillating mass-spring system: P.E. = ½mω²x² = ½kx² (7.17). P.E. is maximum at the extreme position x = x₀: (P.E.)max = ½kx₀² (7.18). P.E. is zero at the mean position x = 0: P.E. = ½k(0) = 0. 7.5.2 TOTAL ENERGY IN S.H.M. AND ENERGY CONSERVATION. At displacement x, the oscillator’s energy is partly kinetic and partly potential.
