p D. RANGE. The horizontal distance from point of projection to point of impact is called range of projectile. Consider a projectile thrown with velocity v₀ at angle θ with horizontal, as shown in Figure 3.18. To find maximum range, use second equation of motion along x-axis: sₓ = vᵢₓt + ½aₓt². Here vᵢₓ = v₀ cos θ, sₓ = R, aₓ = 0, and t = T = 2v₀ sin θ/g. Putting values: R = v₀ cos θ(2v₀ sin θ/g) + ½(0)(2v₀ sin θ/g)², or R = v₀ cos θ(2v₀ sin θ/g), or R = v₀²/g(2 sin θ cos θ). Since 2 sin θ cos θ = sin 2θ, therefore R = v₀²/g sin 2θ (3.25).
