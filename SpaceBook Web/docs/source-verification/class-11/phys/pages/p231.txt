p If a_c is the centripetal acceleration of the body directed toward mean position O, its component along the diameter is a_x = a_c cosθ (1). Since a_c = v²/r = rω², where ω is angular velocity, equation (1) becomes a_x = rω² cosθ (2). From Figure 7.2(a), triangle OQP is right-angled, so x/r = cosθ and x = r cosθ. Putting this in equation (2), a_x = rω²(x/r) = ω²x (3). As a_x is directed toward the mean position, take its sign negative: a_x = ω²(−x), or a_x = −constant × x, where ω² is constant; a_x ∝ −x. This is the equation of S.H.M. TIME PERIOD. The time required by a body to complete one vibration is called time period.
