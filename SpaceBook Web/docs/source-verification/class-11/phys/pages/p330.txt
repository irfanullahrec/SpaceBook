p EXAMPLE 9.4 · DIFFRACTED IMAGE. An optical grating has 5000 lines per centimetre; the deviation angle of the second-order image is 32°. Find wavelength λ. GIVEN: N = 5000 lines/cm, θ = 32°, m = 2. Grating element d = 1 cm/5000 = 0.00020 cm. From d sinθ = mλ, λ = d sinθ/m = (0.00020 cm × sin32°)/2 = (0.00020 × 0.53)/2 = 5.3 × 10⁻⁵ cm. p 9.12 THE DIFFRACTION OF X-RAYS BY CRYSTAL. Interference and diffraction are important for studying crystal structure. Atomic layers in crystals are less than 1 nm apart, so very short-wavelength radiation is needed. X-rays are electromagnetic waves with wavelength about 10⁻¹⁰ m. Their interference fringes cannot be produced by Young’s double-slit experiment or by the thin-film method.
