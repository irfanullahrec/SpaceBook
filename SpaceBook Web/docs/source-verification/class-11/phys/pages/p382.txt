p GLOSSARY: Adiabatic process—A completely isolated process in which no heat transfer can take place. Angular acceleration—Rate of change of angular velocity with time. Angular displacement—Angle subtended at the centre of a circle by a particle moving along the circumference in a given time. Angular momentum—Cross product of position vector and linear momentum. Angular velocity—Angular displacement per second. Antinode—Point of maximum displacement on a stationary wave. Artificial gravity—Gravity-like effect produced in an orbiting spaceship to overcome weightlessness. Average velocity—Average rate at which displacement vector changes with time. Base quantities—Physical quantities such as length, mass and time. Centre of mass—Point at which all the body’s mass is assumed concentrated. Centripetal force—Force needed to move a body around a circular path. Compression—Region of maximum density of a wave. Conservative field—Field in which work done along a closed path is zero. Crest—Portion of a wave above the mean level. Damping—Process whereby energy is dissipated from an oscillatory system. Denser medium—Medium with greater density. Derived quantities—Physical quantities defined in terms of base quantities. Destructive interference—When two waves overlap in opposite phases. Diffraction—Bending of light around obstacles. Dimension—One of the basic measurable physical properties, such as length, mass and time. Displacement—Change in a body’s position from initial to final position.
