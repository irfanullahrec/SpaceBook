p QUIZ: What is the significance of Joule’s experiment for determining J? 10.5 THERMODYNAMIC SYSTEM. A thermodynamic system includes whatever has properties of interest. It is enclosed by surroundings or environment and can exchange heat with, and do work on, its environment through a boundary—an imagined wall separating system and environment. Immediate surroundings interact directly and strongly influence its behavior. Example: for a car engine, gasoline burning inside the cylinder is the system; piston, exhaust system, radiator, and outside air are surroundings. The boundary consists of the inner surfaces of cylinder and piston. Any change or series of changes in a thermodynamic system is a thermodynamic process. System: quantity of matter or region of space being studied (for example, gas in a cylinder). Surroundings: everything in the universe other than the system.
