p Football or cricket ball hit into air, a shell fired from cannon and a stone thrown down the hill are all examples of projectile motion. Projectile motion only occurs when there is one force applied at the beginning of the trajectory after which there is no force apart from gravity. The vertical component aᵧ of the acceleration has a magnitude of 9.80 m/s² while the horizontal component aₓ has the magnitude of 0 m/s² as shown in Figure 3.14. p IDEAL PROJECTILE MOTION. Ideal projectile motion neglects air resistance and wind speed, spin of the projectile, and other effects influencing the flight of real-life projectiles. For realistic situations in which a football or cricket ball moves in air, the actual trajectory is not well described by ideal projectile motion and requires a more sophisticated analysis. Here we will consider only ideal projectile motion.
