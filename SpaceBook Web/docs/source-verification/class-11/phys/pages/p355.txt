p Equation 10.1: ΔQ=ΔU+ΔW. ΔQ is positive when heat enters the system and negative when it leaves. ΔU is positive when temperature rises and negative when it falls. The first law combines (1) internal energy U as a state variable, (2) conservation of energy, and (3) heat as energy in transit. Internal energy is the sum of kinetic (translational, rotational, vibrational) and potential energies associated with random atomic motion. POINT TO PONDER: Earth continuously receives heat from the Sun. Why does it not become as hot as a furnace; what compensates to maintain Earth’s temperature? Rearranging gives ΔU=ΔQ−ΔW: internal-energy change equals heat flowing in minus energy flowing out as work. Molecular translational, vibrational, rotational kinetic energy and intermolecular potential energy contribute. Internal-energy change depends only on initial and final states, not path.
