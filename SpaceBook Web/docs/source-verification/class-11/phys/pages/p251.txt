p If the free end of a rope is moved up and down periodically, a train of transverse waves is produced and moves down the rope. Each portion of the rope moves up and down periodically (Figure 8.1). A more effective periodic oscillating source is an electromagnetic vibrator (Figure 8.2). Increasing its frequency produces transverse pulses in the string; the waves travel down the cord from the vibrator to the clamped end. Circular periodic waves can similarly be generated in a ripple tank by spherical dippers just touching the water surface. A small electric motor drives the mechanical arrangement; the dipping rate is controlled by changing motor speed. Figure 8.3 shows the wave pattern at an instant.
