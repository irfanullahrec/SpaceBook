p TIME PERIOD. T = 2π/ω (2). FREQUENCY f: by definition f = 1/T. Putting in T gives f = ω/(2π), so ω = 2πf (3), the frequency of a body in S.H.M. VELOCITY OF PROJECTION. Resolving the body’s velocity in Figure 7.2(a): v_x = v sinθ and v_y = v cosθ. As Q vibrates in the direction of v_x, take v_x only: v_x = v sinθ (7.6). By trigonometry cos²θ + sin²θ = 1, so sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ. From the figure x/r = cosθ; therefore sinθ = √(r²−x²)/r. Putting this in equation (7.6), v_x = (v/r)√(r²−x²). Since v = rω, v_x = ω√(r²−x²) (7.7). Here r is the circle’s radius, x is the displacement of projection Q from mean position O, and ω is the angular velocity of the particle. FIGURE 7.3(b).
