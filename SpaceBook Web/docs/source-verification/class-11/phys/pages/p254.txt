No particle in the disturbance moves far from its initial position; only the disturbance moves through the cord. This is characteristic of wave motion. NECESSARY CONDITIONS FOR WAVE MOTION. Wave propagation occurs through interaction of medium particles, so: (i) the medium must be elastic; (ii) its particles must interact and exert forces on one another. Transverse and longitudinal waves can be set up in solids. In fluids, transverse waves die out very quickly and usually cannot be produced. 8.3 CLASSIFICATION OF PROGRESSIVE WAVES. Waves that transmit energy from one place to another are called progressive or traveling waves. They are classified as (a) transverse and (b) longitudinal waves. 8.3.1 TRANSVERSE WAVES. In transverse waves, particles of the medium vibrate along a line perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation. When a disturbance propagates through a medium, its particles are disturbed and start vibrating. If particle vibration is perpendicular to wave propagation, it is a transverse wave pulse. The particles vibrate with the source’s period and frequency. WAVE CREST.
