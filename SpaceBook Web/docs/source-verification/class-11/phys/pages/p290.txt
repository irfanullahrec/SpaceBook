p As f′ < f, the pitch of sound decreases when the sounding source moves away from the stationary listener. (II-A) LISTENER MOVES TOWARD A STATIONARY SOUNDING SOURCE (continued). The apparent frequency is f″ = (v + b)/λ. But λ = v/f. Therefore f″ = [(v + b)/v]f (8.29). Since f″ > f, the pitch of sound increases when the listener moves toward a stationary sounding source.
