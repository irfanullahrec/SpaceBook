The sound wave reflected from the bottom interferes with the incoming wave to produce a standing wave. Since the bottle is closed at one end, there is a displacement node at the bottom. Add varying amounts of water and listen for other resonances. Resonant sound is noticeably louder than non-resonant sound; the longer the air column, the lower the pitch. EXAMPLE 8.9 · OPEN PIPE. A pipe open at one end and closed at the other is 82 cm long. Find its three lowest resonant frequencies; sound speed is 340 m s⁻¹. L = 82 cm = 0.82 m. For lowest frequency λ₁ = 4L and f₁ = v/(4L) = 340/(4 × 0.82) = 104 Hz. As in a closed pipe, only odd harmonics are present: f₂ = 3f₁ = 312 Hz; f₃ = 5f₁ = 520 Hz. Answer: 104 Hz, 312 Hz and 520 Hz. ASSIGNMENT 8.5. What length closed pipe produces fundamental frequency 256 Hz at 20°C? (33.5 cm.) 8.14 DOPPLER EFFECT. The apparent change in sound frequency caused by relative motion of the source, listener or both is the Doppler effect. It relates measured wave frequency to the relative velocity of source and receiver. Christian Johann Doppler showed in 1842 that relative motion between source and observer shifts frequencies of sound and light. A source S emits sound waves of speed v, frequency f and wavelength λ. If S and listener A are at rest, the listener receives f waves per second. Since f waves occupy distance v, wavelength λ = v/f, so f = v/λ. 8.15.1 SOURCE MOVING, LISTENER AT REST.
