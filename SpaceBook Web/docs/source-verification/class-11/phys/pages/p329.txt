p From Δr = λ and Δr = d sinθ, d sinθ = λ (iii). Here d is separation between adjacent slits, called the grating element: d = (unit length of grating)/(total number N of ruled lines); for a 1 cm unit, d = 1 cm/N. In other directions on either side of line OC, adjacent waves may differ in path by 2λ, 3λ, …, producing second-, third-order, etc. bright images. p FOR YOUR INFORMATION. A grating spectrometer measures wavelength. Monochromatic light enters the grating normally through the collimator; diffracted light leaves at angle θ and a telescope views the image. Measuring angles of several orders on each side of the central maximum allows wavelength calculation.
