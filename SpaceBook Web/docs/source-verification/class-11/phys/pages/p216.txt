p This is the solution for speed in a pipe by Venturi’s meter when any barometer (device used to measure pressure) is used. However, when no barometer is used, the height of the fluid in smaller equal-diameter pipes attached to both the pipe and the neck is used as the reference, as shown in Figure 6.13. By the first condition of equilibrium, the pressure difference is related to the height difference. Putting (P₁−P₂) = ρgh in equation 6.8 gives v₁ = A₂√[2(ρgh)/{ρ(A₁²−A₂²)}].
