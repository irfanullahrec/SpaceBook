p Resonance occurs when tuning-fork frequency fₑ equals the air-column fundamental frequency f₀; several tube heights usually resonate. Stationary longitudinal waves in pipes are the sound source in wind instruments such as flute, organ, trombone and clarinet (Figure 8.28). ORGAN PIPES. An organ pipe produces sound through a vibrating air column. There are two types: closed and open organ pipes. In each, air is set vibrating by blowing into the whistle end. CLOSED ORGAN PIPE. A pipe closed opposite the whistle end is a closed organ pipe. Its air column is vibrated by sending a narrow air jet toward the lip at the open end. Air striking the lip sends a compression into the pipe; it reflects from the closed end toward the open end.
