p EXPERIMENT WITH TRANSVERSE WAVES. To show that only transverse waves exhibit polarization, let a transverse wave on a string pass through slot P in a board (Figure 9.21). A slot parallel to the transverse vibrations lets the wave pass undisturbed. If a second slot is at right angles to the first, no wave passes through it. Replacing the string by a spring carrying a longitudinal (compressional) wave lets the wave pass regardless of slot orientation; longitudinal waves therefore do not show polarization. p 9.13.1 ORDINARY LIGHT. Why is ordinary light unpolarized? Light is emitted when an orbiting electron jumps from a higher to a lower energy state. A transition involving an electron in a vertical plane emits vertically polarized light; a horizontal orbit emits horizontally polarized light. Light from a candle flame, bulb filament, or Sun is unpolarized because it comes from many atoms whose electrons undergo transitions in different planes in all directions.
