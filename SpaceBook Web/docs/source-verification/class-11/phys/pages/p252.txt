p Many vibrating objects generate waves. A vibrating object does work on particles of the medium and gives them energy; the particles begin to vibrate, so energy is transmitted from one point to another. Sound waves may originate from a vibrating tuning fork in a laboratory or from a vibrating guitar string. These produce mechanical progressive waves (Figure 8.4). Consider a rope attached at one end to a rigid support. A sudden up-and-down movement at the free end sets up a wave that travels along the string at speed v. If the hand continues moving, a series of waves travels along the string.
