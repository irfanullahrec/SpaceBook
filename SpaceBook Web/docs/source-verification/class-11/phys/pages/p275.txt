p For a wave reflected at a fixed end, an upward displacement pulse returns downward: the incident and reflected pulses have a 180° or π-radian phase change, equivalent to half a wavelength (λ/2). If the string end is attached to a ring that moves freely up and down, an arriving pulse lifts the ring and its return produces an upward pulse; there is no phase change. Thus, reflection from a denser medium causes a 180° (π rad) phase change, while reflection at the boundary of a rarer medium causes no phase change. The same principle applies to longitudinal waves. ELECTROMAGNETIC WAVES. A phase change also occurs when electromagnetic waves such as light reflect from a denser medium. Radar (radio detection and ranging) waves locate objects by reflecting waves of known speed from them. REFLECTION OF SOUND WAVES.
