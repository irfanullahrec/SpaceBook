p GLOSSARY: Drag force—Retarding force experienced by an object moving through a fluid. Elastic collision—Interaction in which both momentum and kinetic energy are conserved. Entropy—The printed line reads “Measure of increase in disorder of a thermodynamics system of degradation of energy”; the wording after “system” is malformed in the source and is retained. Forced oscillations—Oscillations of a body subjected to an external force. Free oscillations—Oscillations of a body at its own frequency without interference from an external force. Freely falling body—Body moving under gravity alone. Geo-stationary satellite—Satellite whose orbital motion is synchronized with Earth’s rotation. Harmonics—Stationary waves set up with integral multiples of the fundamental frequency. Heat engine—Device converting part of input heat energy into mechanical work. Ideal fluid—Incompressible fluid with no viscosity. Impulse—Product of force and time for which it acts on a body. Inelastic collision—Interaction in which kinetic energy is not conserved. Instantaneous acceleration—Acceleration at a particular instant. Instantaneous velocity—Velocity at a particular instant. Isothermal process—Process in which Boyle’s law applies. Kinetic energy—Energy a body possesses due to motion. Laminar flow—Smooth sliding of fluid layers past each other. Longitudinal wave—Wave in which medium particles vibrate parallel to wave propagation. Modulus of elasticity—Printed as “Ration of stress and the strain” [source wording retained]. Molar specific heat at constant pressure—Heat needed to change temperature of one mole of gas through 1 K while pressure is constant. Molar specific heat at constant volume—Heat needed to change temperature of one mole of gas through 1 K while volume is constant. Moment arm—Perpendicular distance between axis of rotation and line of action of force. Moment of inertia—Rotational analogue of mass in linear motion. Momentum—Product of mass and velocity of an object. Elastic collision—هغه تعامل چې momentum او kinetic energy دواړه پکې ساتل کېږي. Entropy—سرچينه داسې چاپوي: “Measure of increase in disorder of a thermodynamics system of degradation of energy”؛ له “system” وروسته عبارت په سرچينه کې ناسم دی او هماغسې ساتل شوی. Forced oscillations—د بهرني ځواک تابع د جسم oscillations. Free oscillations—د بهرني ځواک له مداخلې پرته د جسم oscillations په خپل frequency. Freely falling body—هغه جسم چې يوازې د جاذبې تر اغېز لاندې خوځېږي. Geo-stationary satellite—هغه satellite چې مداري حرکت يې د ځمکې له څرخي حرکت سره هممهاله وي. Harmonics—په بنسټيز frequency کې د صحيح عددونو په مضاربو جوړې شوې څپې. Heat engine—آله چې د input heat energy يوه برخه په ميخانيکي کار بدلوي. Ideal fluid—نه‌فشرده کېدونکی او بې viscosity fluid. Impulse—د ځواک او د هغه د اغېز وخت حاصل‌ضرب. Inelastic collision—تعامل چې kinetic energy پکې نه ساتل کېږي. Instantaneous acceleration—په يوه ځانګړې شېبه کې acceleration. Instantaneous velocity—په يوه ځانګړې شېبه کې velocity. Isothermal process—هغه بهير چې Boyle’s law پرې عملي کېږي. Kinetic energy—د حرکت له امله د جسم انرژي. Laminar flow—د fluid د پوړونو يو د بل تر څنګ نرم ښويېدل. Longitudinal wave—څپه چې د منځني ذرات يې د څپې د خپرېدو له لوري سره موازي رپيږي. Modulus of elasticity—سرچينه “Ration of stress and the strain” چاپوي [عبارت هماغسې ساتل شوی]. په ثابت فشار کې molar specific heat—هغه تودوخه چې د ګاز د يو mole تودوخه 1 K بدل کړي، په داسې حال کې چې فشار ثابت وي. په ثابت حجم کې molar specific heat—هغه تودوخه چې د ګاز د يو mole تودوخه 1 K بدل کړي، په داسې حال کې چې حجم ثابت وي. Moment arm—د څرخي محور او د ځواک د عمل کرښې ترمنځ عمودي واټن. Moment of inertia—په خطي حرکت کې د کتلو څرخي مشابه. Momentum—د کتلې او velocity حاصل‌ضرب. Node—د صفر displacement ټکی.
