p OPEN SYSTEM: mass can cross the boundary; heat can also transfer between it and surroundings (example: boiling tea kettle). ISOLATED SYSTEM: neither mass nor heat energy crosses its boundary; tea in a well-insulated thermos flask is an example. THERMODYNAMIC STATE AND STATE VARIABLES: a system’s particular condition, specified by pressure P, volume V, temperature T, etc., is its state. Variables/functions determining its physical state are state variables/state functions. For a homogeneous system, volume, pressure and temperature usually suffice; their mathematical relationship is its equation of state. For an ideal gas, PV=nRT, where n is moles and R the universal gas constant; P, V, T are pressure, volume and temperature. Other state variables include internal energy U and entropy S. p 10.6 REVERSIBLE AND IRREVERSIBLE PROCESSES. In thermodynamics, a process is a change in a system’s state caused by a change in state variables. A process occurs when a system interacts and exchanges energy with surroundings. || 10.6 قابلِ واپسی اور ناقابلِ واپسی عمل۔ thermodynamics میں process، state variables کی تبدیلی سے system کی حالت میں آنے والی تبدیلی ہے۔ process اس وقت ہوتا ہے جب system، surroundings سے تعامل اور توانائی کا تبادلہ کرتا ہے۔ || 10.6 بېرته‌راګرځېدونکې او نه‌راګرځېدونکې پروسې. په ترموديناميک کې process د state variables د بدلون له امله د سيستم د حالت بدلون دی. پروسه هغه وخت کېږي چې سيستم له چاپېريال سره تعامل او انرژي تبادله کړي.
