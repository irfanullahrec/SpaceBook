p The resulting effect is an interference pattern. Coherent sources have constant phase difference, so they must have the same frequency and amplitude. There are two types. (i) CONSTRUCTIVE INTERFERENCE. When two waves arrive at the same place at the same time in phase, they reinforce. Resultant displacement at the superposition point equals the vector sum of individual displacements. For transverse waves, a crest meets a crest and a trough meets a trough. The figure gives PS = QS + RS and BC = BD − AB. For longitudinal waves, compression meets compression and rarefaction meets rarefaction. (ii) DESTRUCTIVE INTERFERENCE. If two waves arrive at the same place at the same time out of phase by 180°, destructive interference occurs.
