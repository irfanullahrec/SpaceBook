p The first tourmaline transmits vibrations in one plane, producing plane-polarized light. A second tourmaline with its crystallographic axis parallel to the first transmits all the polarized light. Rotating the second crystal inclines the axes and decreases transmitted intensity; at 90° no light passes. Rotating it farther makes emergent light reappear. Thus the second crystal is an analyzer, and the arrangement produces a polarized beam. p 9.13.3 POLARIZATION BY REFLECTION. Unpolarized light reflected from glass, water, etc. is generally partially plane-polarized. At a particular angle of incidence, the polarizing angle, polarization is complete. At this angle the reflected ray and refracted ray in the transmitted medium are at right angles. Vibrations in the reflected ray are parallel to the surface.
