p KEY POINTS. FLUID FLOW: The basic property of a fluid is that it can flow. The fluid does not have any resistance to change of its shape. Thus, the shape of a fluid is governed by the shape of its container. VISCOSITY: The resistance to flow of a fluid. DRAG FORCE: Retarding force experienced by an object moving through a fluid. TERMINAL VELOCITY: The maximum velocity attained and maintained by an object while falling through a fluid. STREAMLINE, STEADY, OR LAMINAR FLOW: Every particle of a fluid moving along exactly the same path as followed by particles that have passed that point earlier. TURBULENT FLOW: Irregular flow characterized by small whirlpool-like regions. EQUATION OF CONTINUITY: The volume of an incompressible fluid passing any point in a pipe of nonuniform cross-section is the same in steady flow. vA = constant (v is velocity and A is area of cross-section). The equation is due to mass conservation for ideal flow. BERNOULLI’S EQUATION: Along a streamline, the sum of pressure P, kinetic energy per unit volume (ρv²/2), and potential energy per unit volume (ρgh) remains constant. P + ρv²/2 + ρgh = constant (ρ is density and g is acceleration due to gravity).
