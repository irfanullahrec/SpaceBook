p EXAMPLE 8.3 · STEEL RAILWAY TRACK. Find the speed of sound in a steel railway track if steel density is 7800 kg m⁻³ and elastic modulus is 2.0 × 10¹¹ N m⁻². GIVEN: ρ = 7800 kg m⁻³; E = 2.0 × 10¹¹ N m⁻². REQUIRED: sound speed v. Formula v = √(E/ρ). Substitution: v = √[(2.0 × 10¹¹ N m⁻²)/(7800 kg m⁻³)] = 5.06 × 10³ m s⁻¹. Answer: 5.06 × 10³ m s⁻¹. ASSIGNMENT 8.1 · SEA WATER.
