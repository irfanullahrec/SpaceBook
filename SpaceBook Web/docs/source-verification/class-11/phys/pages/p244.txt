p Damping is used in the shock absorbers of a car to prevent excessive vibrations. Its effect can be observed by attaching a very light mass, such as a pith ball, and a same-size heavy lead bob to a rod, then setting them into vibration with a third pendulum of equal length attached to the same rod. The amplitude of the lead bob is much greater than that of the pith ball. Air resistance damps the pith ball more than the lead bob. Therefore, the sharpness of a resonance curve depends on frictional loss of energy.
