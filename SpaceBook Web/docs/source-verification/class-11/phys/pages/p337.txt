p Snell’s law at the polarizing angle iₚ is n₁ sin iₚ = n₂ sin r (9.12), where n₁ and n₂ are the absolute refractive indices of media 1 and 2. Figure 9.24 gives iₚ + 90° + r = 180°, hence r = 90° − iₚ. Substituting: n₁ sin iₚ = n₂ sin(90°−iₚ)=n₂ cos iₚ, so n₂/n₁ = sin iₚ/cos iₚ = tan iₚ (9.13), Brewster’s law. For glass of refractive index 1.55, the source gives iₚ = 57°.
