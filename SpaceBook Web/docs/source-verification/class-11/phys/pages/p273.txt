p When two waves travel along the same line simultaneously, the resultant displacement of a particle is the vector sum of the displacements due to each wave. Their resultant wave amplitude varies with time, causing loudness variation called beats. BEAT FREQUENCY. The difference between the frequencies of two waves is beat frequency N. The interval between successive loud sounds is beat period T. During T, the waves complete f₁T and f₂T oscillations, differing by one: f₁T − f₂T = 1. Therefore N = f₁ − f₂ = 1/T (8.19), where f₁ − f₂ is beat frequency and T is beat period. Beats are used to find unknown frequencies and tune musical instruments. Tuning adjusts the pitch of one or more tones until they form the desired arrangement. Pitch is the perceived fundamental frequency of sound.
