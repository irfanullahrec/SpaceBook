p (C.II) SOURCE AND LISTENER MOVE AWAY FROM EACH OTHER. Apparent wavelength λ′ = (v + a)/f. Speed of sound relative to the listener is v′ = v − b. Apparent frequency f′ = v′/λ′ = [(v − b)/(v + a)]f (8.32). Since f′ < f, pitch decreases when source and listener move away from each other. || (C.II) منبع اور سامع ایک دوسرے سے دور حرکت کرتے ہیں۔ ظاہری wavelength λ′ = (v + a)/f ہے۔ سامع کے لحاظ سے آواز کی رفتار v′ = v − b ہے۔ ظاہری frequency f′ = v′/λ′ = [(v − b)/(v + a)]f (8.32) ہے۔ چونکہ f′ < f، ایک دوسرے سے دور جاتے منبع اور سامع کے لیے pitch کم ہوتی ہے۔ || (C.II) سرچينه او اورېدونکی يو له بله لېرې خوځېږي. ظاهري wavelength λ′ = (v + a)/f ده. د اورېدونکي په نسبت د غږ سرعت v′ = v − b دی. ظاهري frequency f′ = v′/λ′ = [(v − b)/(v + a)]f (8.32) ده. ځکه f′ < f، د سرچينې او اورېدونکي په لېرې کېدو pitch کمېږي.

p APPLICATIONS OF DOPPLER EFFECT. Important applications of Doppler frequency shift include: (i) Doppler effect also applies to light waves. The frequency received from stars moving toward or away from Earth differs slightly from the frequency of the same light emitted by a source on Earth. (ii) Radar waves reflected from an aeroplane provide another application. Reflected-wave frequency decreases when the plane moves away from the source and increases when it moves toward the source. The frequency shift determines the plane’s speed and direction.
