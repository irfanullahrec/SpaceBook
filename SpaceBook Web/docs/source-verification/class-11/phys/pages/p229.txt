p When the body is released it moves toward O, crosses the mean position due to inertia and reaches B, compressing the spring; it returns and starts oscillation between A and B. At point A, F_applied = −F_restoring; kx = −F_restoring (by Hooke’s law); kx = −ma; therefore a = −(k/m)x. During motion k and m remain constant. Then a = (k/m)(−x). Since k/m is constant, a ∝ −x (equations 7.1 and 7.2). This is the equation of S.H.M. The type of motion in which acceleration is always directly proportional to displacement from the mean position and always directed toward the mean position is called simple harmonic motion. TIME PERIOD. The time for one vibration of a simple harmonic oscillator is T = 2π/ω (3). We know a = −xω², so −(k/m)x = −xω²; therefore ω = √(k/m) (7.3). Putting this in equation (3), T = 2π√(m/k) (7.4). FREQUENCY.
