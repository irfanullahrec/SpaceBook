p NUMERICAL QUESTIONS. 1. Eight equal drops of oil are falling through air with steady velocity 0.1 m s⁻¹. The drops recombine to form a single drop. What should be the new terminal velocity? (0.4 m/s) 2. Water travels through a 9.6 cm diameter fire hose at 1.3 m/s. At the end of the hose, water flows out through a nozzle whose diameter is 2.5 cm. (a) What is the speed of water coming out of the nozzle? (b) What diameter nozzle is required to give water speed of 21 m/s? ((a) 19 m/s, (b) 2.4 cm) 3. A fish tank is 0.30 m wide by 1.0 m long by 0.60 m high. If the filter should process all the water once every 3.0 h, what should the flow speed be in the 3.0 cm diameter input tube for the filter? (2.4 cm/s) 4. A Venturi meter measures water flow; it has a main diameter of 3.5 cm tapering down to a throat diameter of 1.0 cm. If the pressure difference is 18 mm-Hg, what is the speed of water entering the Venturi throat? (0.18 m/s) 5. A small circular hole 6.00 mm in diameter is cut in the side of a large water tank, 14.0 m below the water level. The top of the tank is open to air. Find (a) the speed of efflux and (b) the volume discharged per second. ((a) 16.6 m/s, (b) 0.0469 cm³ s⁻¹) 6. What is the aerofoil lift (in newtons) due to Bernoulli’s principle on a paper plane with wing area 0.01 m² if air passes over the top and bottom surfaces at 9 m/s and 7 m/s, respectively? Take density of air as 1.28 kg/m³. (0.2 N) 7. During a windstorm, a 25 m/s wind blows across the flat roof of a small home. Find the pressure difference between the air inside the home and the air just above the roof, assuming doors and windows are closed. Density of air is 1.28 kg/m³.
