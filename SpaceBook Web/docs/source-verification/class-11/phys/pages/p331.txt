p The fringe spacing is Lλ/d; unless crystal-plane separation is of order 10⁻¹⁰ m, the fringes are too close to observe. X-ray diffraction can be obtained with sodium chloride (NaCl), common table salt. Crystal atoms are uniformly spaced in planes separated by distance d. FIGURE 9.18 shows X-ray diffraction patterns. In a typical Laue experiment (Figure 9.19), a narrow X-ray beam is collimated through a slit onto a crystal such as NaCl; the transmitted beam enters detector D. The Laue photograph has a central spot surrounded by many spots in a defined pattern, called Laue spots. Their arrangement differs with crystal structure.
