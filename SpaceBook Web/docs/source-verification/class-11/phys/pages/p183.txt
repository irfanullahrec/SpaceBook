p 5.12 THE ARTIFICIAL SATELLITES. A satellite is anything that orbits around another object. Moons are natural satellites that orbit planets, whereas artificial satellites are human-built objects orbiting Earth and other planets in the Solar System and launched into orbit using rockets. There are currently over a thousand active satellites orbiting Earth. The size, altitude, and design of a satellite depend on its purpose. Artificial satellites are spacecraft that stay in orbit around Earth. p Navigation satellites. The GPS (Global Positioning System) is made up of 24 satellites that orbit at an altitude of 20,000 km above Earth’s surface. p Communication satellites. These satellites are used for television, phone, or internet transmissions. For example, the Optus D1 satellite is in a geostationary orbit above the equator and has a coverage footprint providing signals to all of Australia and New Zealand.
