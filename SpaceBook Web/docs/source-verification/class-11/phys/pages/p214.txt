p (a) By Torricelli’s theorem v = √(2gh). Putting values: v = √(2 × 9.8 m s⁻² × 1.2 m), hence v = 4.85 m s⁻¹. (b) From the equation of continuity the volume flow rate is ΔV/Δt = Av. Since A = πr², therefore ΔV/Δt = πr² × v. Putting values: ΔV/Δt = 3.14 × (6.0 × 10⁻³ m)² × 4.85 m s⁻¹. Hence ΔV/Δt = 5.48 × 10⁻⁴ m³ s⁻¹. p D. VENTURI METRE (FLOW METRE). Venturi metre is a device used to measure the flow speed or flow rate through a piping system. It works on the principle of pressure difference between restricted and unrestricted flow regions. We consider the flow to be steady, and assume the fluid is incompressible and has negligible internal friction. Hence we can use Bernoulli’s equation.
