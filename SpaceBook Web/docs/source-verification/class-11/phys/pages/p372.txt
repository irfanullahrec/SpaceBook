p Example 10.5 solution: η=1−T₂/T₁, so T₂/T₁=1−0.55=0.45. With T₁=600 K, T₂=600×0.45=270 K. Printed answer: T₂=270 K. 10.12 REFRIGERATOR. A refrigerating machine maintains a body below the surrounding temperature. Heat must be made to flow from a low-temperature body to higher-temperature surroundings, opposite the spontaneous flow from hot to cold. The reverse cycle, in which heat Q flows back from a cold body to a hot body, is not possible without work. Used for summer cooling, the device is a refrigerator; for winter heating, it is a heat pump. A refrigerator runs the working substance through a cycle opposite to a heat engine; the working substance is the refrigerant. A system removes heat Q₂ from a low-temperature source at T₂; compressor work W is performed on the refrigerant. Heat Q₁ rejected to the high-temperature source satisfies Q₁=W+Q₂ (10.16).
