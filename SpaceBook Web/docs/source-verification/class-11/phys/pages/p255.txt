p WAVE TROUGH. The portion of a wave below the mean level is a wave trough. EXAMPLES. If one end of a string is attached to a strong support and the other end is free, periodically moving the free end up and down produces transverse waves (Figure 8.7). Each cord particle vibrates perpendicular to the direction of propagation. When a pebble is dropped into a quiet pond, a circular pattern spreads from the impact point (Figure 8.8). To investigate whether water molecules at the surface move as a wave passes, drop a piece of paper on the surface and watch it move. A piece of cork does not travel along with the wave.
