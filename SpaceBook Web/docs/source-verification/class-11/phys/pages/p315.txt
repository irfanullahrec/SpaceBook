p For destructive interference, d sin θ = λ/2, 3λ/2, 5λ/2, …, equivalently d sin θ = (m + 1/2)λ, where m = 0, ±1, ±2, … . POSITION OF FRINGES ON THE SCREEN. To find the vertical distance y of the mth-order bright or dark fringe from the central point O, consider similar right-angled triangles ABQ and PEO: s/y = AB/PE = d/PE (i). p FOR YOUR INFORMATION — BLUE SKY. Short wavelengths are scattered from the Sun’s direct beam more effectively at midday than longer wavelengths. These scattered waves produce the blue sky. We do not look directly at the Sun when seeing the sky; we see light scattered multiple times, concentrating shorter blue and violet wavelengths in the light reaching our eyes. Sunlight contains more blue than violet, and our eyes respond more strongly to blue than violet, so the colour we identify is blue.
