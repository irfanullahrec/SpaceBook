p U_A and U_B are initial and final internal energies. In a cyclic process the system returns to its initial state, so U_B=U_A. The first law gives 0=ΔQ−ΔW, hence ΔQ=ΔW (equation 10.2): in a cycle all heat absorbed is used to do useful work. QUIZ: Two ice blocks pressed together combine into one piece. Explain how. 10.7.1 APPLICATIONS OF THE FIRST LAW. Internal energy, heat and work can convert into one another. Practical cases include processes where one of the three terms in ΔQ=ΔU+ΔW is zero, or one of the state variables P,V,T is held constant. ISCHORIC PROCESS: volume remains constant. A gas in a cylinder with conducting base, nonconducting walls and fixed piston is heated by ΔQ at constant volume; pressure rises P₁→P₂ and temperature T₁→T₂. Since the system neither expands nor contracts, ΔW=0; therefore ΔQ=ΔU (equation 10.3).
