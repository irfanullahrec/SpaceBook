p 9.6 YOUNG’S DOUBLE SLITS EXPERIMENT. In 1801, Thomas Young performed his famous double-slit experiment, providing experimental evidence for the wave theory of light. The principle is division of a wavefront. To obtain phase coherence between interfering light beams, Young split the wavefront of the same monochromatic light into two parts and allowed them to interfere.
