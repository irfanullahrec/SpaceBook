p NUCLEAR ENERGY. When atoms are split apart, energy is released; it can be used to make electricity. This process is called nuclear fission. In a nuclear power plant, fission takes place inside a reactor. Nuclear power in Pakistan makes a small contribution to electricity production, supplying only 6.1 terawatt-hours (5.5%) in 2015. 4.11 RENEWABLE RESOURCES. Non-conventional sources are also called renewable resources. Resources continuously renewed by nature are renewable. The sun, winds, tides, geothermal energy, biomass, farm and animal waste including human excreta are non-conventional sources. They are inexpensive in nature and not very common these days; however, they are expected to contribute substantially to future world energy demand.
