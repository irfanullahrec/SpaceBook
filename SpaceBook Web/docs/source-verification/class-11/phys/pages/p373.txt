p 10.12.1 COEFFICIENT OF PERFORMANCE (ENERGY RATIO) OF REFRIGERATOR. A refrigerator’s performance is described by its coefficient of performance, defined as heat removed from the cold sink divided by work required. Cooling coefficient: E_cooling=Q₂/W. From Q₁=W+Q₂, W=Q₁−Q₂, so E_cooling=Q₂/(Q₁−Q₂) (10.17). Using Q₁/Q₂=T₁/T₂ gives E_cooling=T₂/(T₁−T₂). Heating coefficient: E_heating=Q₁/W=Q₁/(Q₁−Q₂)=T₁/(T₁−T₂). No cyclic device can extract heat Q₂ from a reservoir and reject it entirely to a hotter reservoir without work; this is the Clausius statement of the second law.
