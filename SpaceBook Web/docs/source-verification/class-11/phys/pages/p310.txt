p Two coherent monochromatic light waves from the two sources interfere constructively at some screen points and destructively at others, producing bright and dark fringes (Figure 9.5). CONSTRUCTIVE INTERFERENCE. Where coherent light waves meet in phase and reinforce, constructive interference occurs and brightness is observed.
