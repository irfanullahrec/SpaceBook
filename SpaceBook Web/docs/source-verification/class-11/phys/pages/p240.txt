p The waveform of harmonic motion can also be traced experimentally. Hang a small mass m from a spring; place a sheet of paper behind it and move the paper at constant speed. A pen attached to the mass lightly touches the paper and marks the mass position at each instant. Raise the mass a distance y and release it. It executes S.H.M. with amplitude A and the pen traces the waveform on the paper, as in Figure 7.12. 7.9 PHASE. Phase is the quantity that shows the state of motion of an oscillator. For circular motion, displacement of the projection executing S.H.M. on the circle’s diameter is x = x₀ cosθ = x₀ cosωt (7.21). Here x is instantaneous displacement, x₀ maximum displacement, and ω angular velocity. The general form is x = x₀ cos(ωt + φ) (7.22). The time-varying quantity (ωt + φ) is the phase of motion; it describes the state at a given time.
