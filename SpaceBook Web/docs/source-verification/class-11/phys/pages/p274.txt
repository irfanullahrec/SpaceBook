p TUNING MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS. Tuning is performed by sounding two pitches and adjusting one to match or relate to the other. Tuning forks, pianos and other devices can provide a reference pitch. If two played pitches have different frequencies they produce a beating sound called “interference beats.” As two notes approach a harmonic relationship, beat frequency decreases. Adjust the instrument until beating slows so much it cannot be detected. EXAMPLE 8.5. Two pianos sound the same note. One vibrates at 221.60 Hz and the other at 221.40 Hz. Find beat frequency. GIVEN: f₁ = 221.60 Hz; f₂ = 221.40 Hz. Beat frequency f = f₁ − f₂ = 0.20 Hz. Answer: 0.20 Hz. ASSIGNMENT 8.2.
