INTRODUCTION. The idea of a wave is useful for a wide range of phenomena and is a basic concept in physics; wave behavior is also important to engineers. A progressive wave is a disturbance moving from its source to surrounding places, transmitting energy from one point to another. Wave motion is the mechanism by which energy is transferred from one point to another. Some waves require a medium for propagation, such as sound in air, water waves and waves in a string or spring; these are mechanical waves. Other waves, including heat, light and radio waves, need no medium and are electromagnetic waves. 8.1 PERIODIC WAVES. A source oscillating periodically in a medium produces continuous periodic waves. As the source oscillates, it disturbs particles of the medium and sets them vibrating with the same amplitude and frequency, causing wave motion.
