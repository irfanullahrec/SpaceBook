p When two or more waves pass through the same region at the same time, total displacement at an interaction point equals the vector sum of individual displacements. If n waves displace a particle by y₁, y₂, …, yₙ, the resultant is y = y₁ + y₂ + … + yₙ (8.17). This is the principle of superposition. 8.6 INTERFERENCE OF WAVES. The effect produced by superposition of waves from two coherent sources passing through the same region is interference. Sources are coherent if their phase difference is constant; they have the same frequency and amplitude. Where wave trains from coherent sources meet, superposition reinforces waves at some points and cancels them at others.
