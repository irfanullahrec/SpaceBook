Assume the shock absorbers are poor, so the car really oscillates up and down. (0.9 s, 1.1 Hz). 7.6 FREE AND FORCED OSCILLATIONS. A body executes free vibrations if it oscillates with its natural frequency without interference of an external force. For example, a simple pendulum vibrates freely with its natural frequency, which depends only on its length when slightly displaced from its mean position. If a freely oscillating system is subjected to an external force, forced vibrations take place. If the mass of a vibrating pendulum is struck repeatedly, forced vibrations are produced. Vibrations of a factory floor caused by heavy machinery are an example of forced vibration. Loud music produced by sounding wooden boards of string instruments is another example. 7.7 RESONANCE.
