Why do we not keep bananas in the refrigerator? Why does the refrigerator switch itself OFF intermittently with some noise? 10.13 ENTROPY. Rudolph Clausius introduced entropy in 1856 to give a quantitative basis for the second law. Entropy is a state variable alongside pressure, volume, temperature and internal energy. In a reversible process where a system absorbs heat ΔQ at absolute temperature T, its entropy increase is ΔS=ΔQ/T (10.18). As with potential or internal energy, the change in entropy is important. Entropy change is positive when heat is added and negative when heat is removed. If heat Q flows from a reservoir at T₁ through a conducting rod to a reservoir at T₂, where T₁>T₂, the hot reservoir loses entropy Q/T₁ and the cold reservoir gains Q/T₂. Since T₁>T₂, Q/T₂>Q/T₁; net entropy change is ΔS_net=Q/T₂−Q/T₁>0.
