A system is separated from surroundings by its boundary. Energy can cross it as work or heat. CLOSED SYSTEM: no mass transfers across its boundary. Heat energy can transfer between a closed system and surroundings in either direction. Figure 10.4(a): a boiling tea kettle is an open system, transferring heat and matter (steam) to surroundings. (b) A pressure cooker approximates a closed system; a little steam escapes through the top valve to prevent explosion. POINT TO PONDER: an ink dot on a white porcelain dish looks dark, but when the dish is heated to a very high temperature the dot appears brighter than its surroundings. Why?
