p CONCEPTUAL QUESTIONS—Give a short response. 1. Why is the Earth not in thermal equilibrium with the Sun? 2. When a block with a hole is heated, why does the material around the hole not expand into the hole and make it small? 3. A thermometer is placed in direct sunlight. Will it read the temperature of the air, the Sun, or something else? 4. The pressure in a gas cylinder containing hydrogen leaks more quickly than if it contains oxygen. Why? 5. What happens to the temperature of a room in which an air conditioner is left running on a table in the middle of the room? 6. Why does air pressure in an automobile tyre increase if the automobile is driven for a while? 7. On removing the valve, the air escaping from a cycle tube cools. Why? 8. Write the limitations of the first law of thermodynamics. 9. According to the second law, is it possible to construct a heat engine free from thermal pollution? 10. Can the specific heat of a gas be zero or infinity? Can specific heat be negative? 11. An inventor claims a heat engine working between 27°C and 227°C has efficiency 45%. Is the claim valid? Why?
