p From PV = RT, V = RT/P (8.11). For gas mass m, density is ρ = m/V (8.12). Therefore sound speed is v = √(γRT/m) (8.13), and is independent of gas pressure. TEMPERATURE. For solids and liquids, the change in sound speed with temperature is very small and may be neglected. For gases it is large: speed increases about 0.6 m s⁻¹ per 1°C rise. Since v depends on the square root of the gas’s absolute temperature, if v₀ and v are sound speeds at temperatures T₀ and T, then v/v₀ = √(T/T₀) (8.14). For 0°C, T₀ = (0 + 273) K and for t°C, T = (t + 273) K. Applying the binomial theorem and neglecting higher powers gives √[(t + 273)/273] ≈ 1 + t/546 (8.15). Since at 0°C, v₀ = 332 m s⁻¹, v = 332(1 + t/546) = 332 + 0.61t m s⁻¹ (8.16).
