p KEY POINTS continued: Reversible process—can be retraced exactly in reverse without changing surroundings. Irreversible process—cannot be retraced backward by reversing controlling factors. Heat engine converts heat energy into mechanical work. Reversible heat engine can be retraced at any stage by reversing boundary conditions. Heat source/reservoir has effectively unlimited heat capacity and can absorb or reject unlimited heat without temperature change. Principle of increase of entropy: in a thermally isolated system, entropy either increases or remains constant. Degradation of energy: conversion from a highly ordered state to a less ordered state.
