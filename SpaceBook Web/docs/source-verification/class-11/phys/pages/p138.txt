p EXAMPLE 4.6 · ESCAPE SPEED. Compare the escape speed of a rocket launched from the Moon with Earth. The Moon’s mass is 7.35 × 10²² kg and radius is 1.74 × 10⁶ m. REQUIRED: speed v = ? SOLUTION: v = √(2GMₘ/Rₘ) = √([2(6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻²)(7.35 × 10²² kg)]/[1.74 × 10⁶ m]) = 2370 m s⁻¹. You can escape from the Moon by travelling much more slowly than to escape Earth’s gravitational pull. This is why launching a Lunar Module from the Moon’s surface was much easier than launching an Apollo spacecraft from Earth.
