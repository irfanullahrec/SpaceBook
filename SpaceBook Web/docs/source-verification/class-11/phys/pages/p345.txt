p LEARNING OUTCOMES (continued). Explain that internal energy is determined by the state of the system and is the sum of random-distribution kinetic and potential energies associated with its molecules. Calculate work done by a thermodynamic system during volume change. Describe the first law using change in internal energy, heating of the system and work done on it; explain that it expresses conservation of energy. Define specific heat and molar specific heats of a gas; apply the first law to derive Cₚ−Cᵥ=R. State the working principle of a heat engine; describe reversible and irreversible processes; state and explain the second law; explain Carnot-engine operation and that its efficiency is independent of working substance but depends on hot- and cold-reservoir temperatures. Describe a refrigerator as a heat engine operating in reverse; derive its coefficient of performance. Describe entropy change as positive when heat is added and negative when removed; explain that rising temperature increases disorder, increasing entropy means energy degradation, energy is degraded
