p EXAMPLE 8.1 SOLUTION. GIVEN: 500 pulses in 10 s. Required: (a) period T; (b) frequency f. (a) T = 10/500 = 1/50 s. (b) f = 1/T = 50 pulses s⁻¹. Answer: T = 1/50 s, f = 50 pulses s⁻¹. POINT TO PONDER. Sun explosions occur at the Sun’s surface due to fission and fusion reactions, but we cannot hear them. Why? EXAMPLE 8.2 · RIPPLE TANK. In a ripple tank, 40 waves pass a point in one second. If wavelength is 5 cm, find wave speed. GIVEN: f = 40 waves s⁻¹; λ = 5 cm. Required: v. Formula v = fλ. Therefore v = 40 waves s⁻¹ × 5 cm = 200 cm s⁻¹ = 2 m s⁻¹.
