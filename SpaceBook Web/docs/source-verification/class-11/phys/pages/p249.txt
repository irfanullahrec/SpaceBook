p UNIT 8 · WAVES. How can a surfer capture some of a wave’s energy and take it for a ride? After studying this unit, students will be able to: describe wave motion as illustrated by vibrations in ropes, springs and a ripple tank; demonstrate that mechanical waves require a medium for propagation while electromagnetic waves do not; define and apply medium, displacement, amplitude, period, compression, rarefaction, crest, trough, wavelength and velocity; solve problems using v = fλ; describe energy transfer by a progressive wave; identify sound waves as vibrations of particles in a medium; compare transverse and longitudinal waves; explain that the speed of sound depends on medium properties and describe Newton’s formula; describe the Laplace correction to Newton’s formula for sound in air; identify factors on which the speed of sound in air depends.
