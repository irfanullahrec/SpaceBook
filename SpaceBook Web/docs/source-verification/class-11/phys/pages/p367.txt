p Example 10.4 solution: Given T₁−T₂=100°C=100 K, Q₁=746 J and Q₂=546 J. For a reversible engine η=(Q₁−Q₂)/Q₁=(T₁−T₂)/T₁. Thus 200/746=100/T₁, T₁=746×100/200=373 K=100°C, and T₂=T₁−100°C=0°C. Printed answer: T₁=100°C and T₂=0°C. ASSIGNMENT 10.2: During one cycle, an engine extracts 2.00×10³ J from a hot reservoir and transfers 1.50×10³ J to a cold reservoir. (a) Find the thermal efficiency. (b) Find the work per cycle. (c) Find average power if it completes four cycles in 2.50 s. Printed answers: (a) 0.250 or 25.0%; (b) 5.00×10² J; (c) 8.00×10² W [right edge clipped]. 10.10 SECOND LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS. The first law generalizes conservation of energy and says heat and mechanical work are mutually interconvertible.
