p We consider terminal velocity for the simplest case: a spherical object of uniform density falling through a consistent medium, as in Figure 6.2. Newton’s laws apply whether objects fall freely or in the presence of resistive forces; acceleration differs because the net force differs. In a vacuum, net force is weight alone.
