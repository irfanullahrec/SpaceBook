p 9.7 INTERFERENCE IN THIN FILM. Colour bands on the surface of slightly oily water and soap bubbles on water are interference fringes caused by rays reflected from the upper and lower surfaces of a thin oil film on water. The principle uses division of amplitude by partial reflection and transmission at the boundary of two media.
