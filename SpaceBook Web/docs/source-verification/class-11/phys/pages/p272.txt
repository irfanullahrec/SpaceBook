p As time passes, fork B has slightly lower frequency than A and falls behind. After t₂ = 1/4 s, fork A completes 64 vibrations and sends a compression, while fork B completes 63½ vibrations and sends a rarefaction; compression and rarefaction cancel at the ear, so no sound is heard. At t₃ = 1/2 s both send compressions together and loud sound returns. At t₄ = 3/4 s, A sends a compression while B sends a rarefaction; they cancel and sound is faint. At t₅ = 1 s, A completes 256 vibrations and B 254; both send compressions together and loud sound returns. Thus two beats per second are produced, equal to the difference between fork frequencies. Number of beats per second N = f₁ − f₂ (8.17).
