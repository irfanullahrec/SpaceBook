p Stationary waves with a discrete set of frequencies can form in an air column, as in a stretched string, spring or elastic membrane. A sounding tuning fork held above the open end of a glass tube filled with water can produce greatly amplified sound under suitable conditions. Slowly lower the water surface in the tube with a reservoir. At a certain water height, the air column resonates loudly with the tuning-fork sound (Figure 8.27).
