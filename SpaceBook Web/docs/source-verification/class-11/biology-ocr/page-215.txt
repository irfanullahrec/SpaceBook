Respiration takes place in aquatic forms through gills and in terrestrial forms
(especially insects) through trachea, Trachea are air tubes which divide repeatedly to
form numerous fine branches distributed to all the tissues of the body. They
communicate with exterior through openings on each side of the body known as
Spiracles. Arachinids, a group of arthropods including scorpion, spider etc. respire
through special structures, arranged side by side like books in a book shelf, hence
called book lungs.

Excretion takes place either through malpighian tubules (as in insects) or
green gland or coxal gland (as in crustacean). Fertilization is internal. Sexes are
generally separate. Sexual dimorphism is generally present i.e. male and female can
be differentiated from each other on the basis of their morphology. During
development they exhibit either complete metamorphosis or incomplete
_ metamorphosis. Some insects like honey bees, ants, termites etc. show social
