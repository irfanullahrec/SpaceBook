2.6.2 Polynucleotides (Poly:many)

When many nucleotides are linked together they form a structure called
polynucleotide. DNA and RNA molecules are polynucleotide chains (strand).
There are different types of RNA molecules, each type performs specific function
under the instructions of DNA. Nucleic acids are of two types:

1. DNA: Deoxyribonucleic Acid — made of deoxyribonucleotides
2. RNA: Ribonucleic Acid - made of ribonucleotides
