2.5.5 Terpenoids

Terpenoids are lipids that like the steroids do not contain fatty acids, They are
lipid soluble and water insoluble substances The terpenoids are formed of units called
isoprenoid units. They join by the process of condensation and give rise to different
types of compounds such as rubber, carotenoids, etc.
