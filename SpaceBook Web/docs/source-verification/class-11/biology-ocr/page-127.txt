4. Bacterial galls :
Crown gall is caused by a soil-inhabiting bacterium, Agrobacterium
tumefaciens, which occurs worldwide and attacks over 600 plant species in more than
’ 90 plant families. The most obvious symptoms are the galls or growths that usually occur on
the twigs, stems, and roots near the base of the plant at the soil line. Gall size can vary
from small to large and are usually spongy when young, but then become hard and
woody with age.
