5. Phylum Arthropoda

The word arthropoda
is derived from two Greek
words; Arthoros means
Jointed and Podos means
limbs or legs hence,
arthropoda includes all those
animals having jointed
appendages. This is the most
successful group and the
largest phylum of the animals.

They are triploblastic
and bilaterally symmetrical.
The body is metamerically
segmented. They have jointed
limbs and generally every
segment of the body has a pair
of legs (also called limbs or
appendages). The body is
covered with an exoskeleton
in the form of thick cuticle
chemically made of chitin.
