based on morphology and the type of host
they infect. On the basis of morphology
there are three classes of viruses,

a. Spherical Virus. e. g. Polio virus
b. Tadpole shaped virus. e.g. Bacteriophage.
c. Rod shaped virus. e.g. tobacco mosaic
virus.
