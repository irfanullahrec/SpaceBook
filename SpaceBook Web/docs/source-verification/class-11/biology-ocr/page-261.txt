: The inhibition or control of lateral buds to develop by the activity of apical bud is
illed inhibitory correlation or apical dominance. This is the most important and the
most common example of correlation in plants. The active apex of the shoot controls
the development of lateral buds. This is proved by the fact that if the apical bud is cut
off, then one or more of the axillary buds grow out and exert inhibitory effect on the
buds below. Apical dominance may be complete or incomplete.
