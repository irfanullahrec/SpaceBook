a. Macronutrients
Macronutrients can be divided into two more groups: primary and secondary
nutrients. The primary nutrients are nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), and potassium
(K). These major nutrients are usually less in soil because plants use these in large
amounts for their growth and survival. The secondary nutrients are calcium (Ca),
magnesium (Mg) and sulfur (S). These are usually present in reasonable amounts
Large amounts of Calcium and Magnesium are added when lime is applied to acidic
soils. Sulfur is usually found in sufficient amounts from the slow decomposition of
soil organic matter.
