2.5.6 Functions of lipids

usually store lipids in seeds as oils (unsaturated fatty acids) while animals store
them as fats (saturated fatty acids), Lipids store greater amount of energy than
carbohydrates because they contain less oxygen and more C-H bonds than

lipo-protein membranes. Lipids provide insulation to various organs and to the
