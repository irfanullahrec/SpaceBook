Arterioles are the smallest arteries with a diameter ranging from 3 i to 10

Arterioles play an important role
in vasoconstriction and vasodilation.
Innervations (stimulus) of sympathetic
nervous system cause contraction in the
muscular layer of the arteriole, resulting in
vasoconstriction of arteriole. This
decreases the flow of blood into
capillaries, resulting a rise in blood
pressure. In contrast, the vasodilation of
arterioles increases the blood flow in the
capillaries which result in a decrease in

blood pressure.
