Fungi in pharmaceutical industry

pencillium chrysogenum and other related species. Cephalosporin is most widely
used broad spectrum antibiotic, obtained from cephalosporium acremonium and
related species. Griseofulvin is an antibiotic used effectively against fungal
infections of hair, nails, skin, athlete's foot and ringworm.
