b. Ascomycota

The characteristic feature of ascomycota is the production of sexual spores
called the ascospores within saclike asci (singular, ascus); thus, they are commonly
called sac fungi. Unlike zygomycota, the most ascomycota bear their sexual states in
fruit bodies called ascocarps which range in size from microscopic to macroscopic.
Asci are produced in ascopcarps. Most of the ascomycota also reproduce asexually
by means of conidia, produced in chains at the end ofa conidiophores. ;
