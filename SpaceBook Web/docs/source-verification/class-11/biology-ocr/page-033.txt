The bonding of hydrogen, Oxygen, nitrogen and other atoms to carbon atom
forms the compounds called organic compounds. The organic molecules play most
important role that characterize the structure and function of the cell organelles.
Carbon-carbon bonds make chain of carbon atoms which form the skeleton or
backbone of large organic molecules: The chemical analysis of protoplasm shows that
it is composed of two types of compounds, i.e. organic compounds and inorganic
compounds. These compounds are present in somewhat different proportions in
different organisms and even in different types of cells of the same organism. Water is
the most abundant of all compounds of protoplasm. It forms almost three-fourth ofthe
body by weight, next to water are proteins. Proteins are the most abundant organic
compounds present in the cell. Proteins have structural functional roles in the cell.
Other organic compounds include carbohydrates, lipids and nucleic acids. Enzymes
which are proteins in nature are also present. Hormones which play very important
role and inorganic salts are present in small proportion.
