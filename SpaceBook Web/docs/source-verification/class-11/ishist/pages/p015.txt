In 440 CE Qusayy seized Makkah and laid the foundation of the rule of Quraysh. In his fifth generation, fifty-five days after the failure of Abrahah, the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) came into this world. Historians hold different views about the date of the birth of the Holy Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him); most historians agree on 20 April 571 CE. The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) was born in Makkah a few months after the death of his respected father Hazrat Abdullah bin Abdul Muttalib. His mother Hazrat Aminah belonged to the Quraysh tribe of Banu Zuhrah, and his father was the chief of this tribe (as printed).
