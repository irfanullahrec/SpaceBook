The Kharijites went back and raised revolts. The Kharijite chief Nafi’ bin Azraq expelled the official of Ahwaz and took possession of the city; after this, conquering other cities, they reached as far as Basrah. Ibn Zubayr appointed Muhallab to crush the Kharijites; Muhallab suppressed them by defeating them one after another. After the martyrdom of Mus’ab, Muhallab pledged allegiance to Abd al-Malik. Khalid bin Abdullah, the ruler of Basrah, dismissed Muhallab after appointing his brother Abd al-Aziz as commander. The Kharijites defeated and killed Abd al-Aziz, and Muhallab was restored. Abd al-Malik appointed Hajjaj bin Yusuf governor of Iraq. Hajjaj sent many expeditions to help Muhallab, but no particular success was achieved. Fortunately dissension broke out among the Kharijites and they began to fight among themselves. Now Muhallab succeeded in defeating them. Muhallab was honoured with rewards.
