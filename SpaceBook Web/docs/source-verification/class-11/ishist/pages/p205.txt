A third brought and heaped up the whole property of his house in service … Someone thought, “Why should I remain behind in the competition?” and he went on irrigating someone’s field all night. In the morning he got a few dates in return for his service; he tried to present those very dates, hiding them, only with the thought that they were a trifling thing and might not be accepted; but the Accepter honoured his offering so that He placed those dates in the highest place. The truth is that with Him sincerity has its price, and that is all. Among the women such a … change had been created that a woman is told, “Your brother has been martyred.” Hearing this she asks, “How is the Holy Prophet?” After a little while she is told, “Your son has been martyred.” She again asks, “In what condition is the Holy Prophet?” Then she is told, “Your father has been martyred.” Hearing this she runs towards the hill of Uhud.
