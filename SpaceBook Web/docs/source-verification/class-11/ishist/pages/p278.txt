When Jarrah bin Abdullah was appointed governor of Khurasan he released the new ruler and expressed displeasure with Jahm. Hazrat Umar bin Abd al-Aziz learnt that Jarrah collected jizyah from those among the dhimmis who became Muslims and acted oppressively like Hajjaj. Umar bin Abd al-Aziz wrote to Jarrah that jizyah should not be collected from those who offer prayer. By this decree people began to accept Islam in crowds. Later Jarrah was dismissed. After him Abd al-Rahman bin Nu’aym was appointed governor of Khurasan. In the time of Jarrah’s rule the preachers and envoys of Muhammad bin Ali began to reach Khurasan and the Abbasid propaganda began, which in the end deprived Banu Umayyah of power and gave power to Banu Abbas. Hajjaj had got Walid to dismiss Hazrat Umar bin Abd al-Aziz from the governorship of Madinah. After the death of Sulayman he ascended the throne; he began the caliphate in a new manner. Departing from the traditional way of the caliphs of Banu Umayyah he adopted the ways of Hazrat Umar Farooq. In this connection the reforms enforced by him have a revolutionary status. For this very reason he is counted among the Rightly Guided Caliphs and is called the fifth caliph.
