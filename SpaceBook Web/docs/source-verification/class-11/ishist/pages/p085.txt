In 10 AH the Prophet made up his mind to perform his last Hajj. When this news spread in Madinah, people began to join this caravan for Hajj in group after group. The Prophet set out from Madinah on 26 Dhul Qa’dah 10 AH. The number of those performing Hajj with him was ninety thousand, but when he reached the place Dhul Hulayfah near Makkah, the number of Muslims reached one hundred and forty-four thousand. All the blessed wives of the Prophet were also included. Clad in the dress of Hajj, glorifying Allah the Almighty, when he entered the valley of Makkah, the voice of Allahu Akbar was echoing in the whole area. Hazrat Ali, who had gone to Yemen, joined him. On 5 Dhul Hijjah he entered Makkah. On 9 Dhul Hijjah, after offering the Fajr prayer, preparations began to go to the valley of Arafat. At noon the Prophet was seated on the she-camel named Qaswa in the very middle of the valley of Arafat. The sermon he gave on this historic occasion is called the Khutbah of the Farewell. This sermon is in reality the essence of the Islamic teachings.
