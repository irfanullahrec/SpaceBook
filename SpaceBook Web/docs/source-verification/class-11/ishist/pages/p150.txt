Strengthen the religion through Umar.” In childhood Hazrat Umar used to graze sheep. When he grew up he learnt genealogy and gained skill in reading and writing. He often went on the embassies of the Quraysh. He had a great attachment to poetry and literature. He also used to wrestle. He also traded. When he accepted Islam, he took the Holy Prophet with great force and fervour to the Ka’bah and offered prayer in congregation there. Truly Islam received strength by the acceptance of Islam by Hazrat Umar. Hazrat Umar was counted among the special advisers of the Prophet. He remained a special adviser of Hazrat Abu Bakr. Before becoming a Muslim he was famous for his very hard temper. But when he accepted Islam and later became caliph, gentleness came into his temper. At night he used to go around the streets of Madinah to learn the condition of the people. He helped the poor with his own hands. He had given the subjects full right to criticise him. People could criticise the caliph without any fear. He had instructed the governors and other officials not to post doorkeepers at their doors, so that complainants could lodge their complaints with them without any obstacle. He lived a simple life. His clothes had many patches. Hazrat Abdullah bin Mas’ud used to say, “The acceptance of Islam by Umar was a conquest.
