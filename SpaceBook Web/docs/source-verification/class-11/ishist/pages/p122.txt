The Holy Prophet had not yet been buried when in the meantime the news came that the Ansar had gathered in the Saqifah of Banu Sa’idah and wished to elect Sa’d bin Ubadah, the chief of Khazraj, as caliph. At this delicate moment Hazrat Abu Bakr wished to save the Islamic state from destruction, so he went there taking Hazrat Umar and Hazrat Abu Ubaydah. There was a rather heated discussion. Sa’d bin Ubadah was lying in bed because of illness. Hazrat Abu Bakr said, “Whatever you say is right, but the people of Arabia will not accept the leadership of anyone except the Quraysh.” A proposal to make two caliphs was put forward, but this was impossible. Hazrat Abu Ubaydah said, “O Ansar! It is true that you took the lead in the service of Islam; do you want to take the lead in destruction as well?” A misunderstanding had arisen in Hazrat Ali. According to Allama Tabari, he pledged allegiance after forty days and remained a special adviser of Hazrat Abu Bakr. The people of the tribe of Khazraj were richer in money than the tribe of Aws. Sa’d bin Ubadah was the chief of the Khazraj. The Ansar decided unanimously that Sa’d bin Ubadah should be nominated as caliph. The Muhajirin objected. In this way the discussion and debate began. Hubab bin al-Mundhir put forward the proposal of making two caliphs, one from the Ansar and one from the Muhajirin.
