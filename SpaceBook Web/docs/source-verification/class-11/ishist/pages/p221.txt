Abdullah bin Umar and Abdullah bin Abbas pledged allegiance later. Hazrat Imam Husayn and Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubayr remained. Yazid had a serious danger from these two. He wrote to the governor of Madinah, Walid bin Uqbah, that the pledge be taken from these two at once. Walid consulted Marwan, who advised hurry in taking the pledge. Walid called Hazrat Imam Husayn and showed him the letter of Yazid. Hazrat Imam Husayn refused to pledge secretly and said that he would pledge in public. After that he went to Makkah with his family. Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubayr too, taking a day’s respite, went to Makkah. In Kufah the Shi’an-i-Ali were in the majority. When Hazrat Amir Mu’awiyah died they gathered at the house of their chief Sulayman bin Surad for mutual consultation and decided that the pledge to Yazid be refused and that Hazrat Imam Husayn be made Amir al-Mu’minin. They sent many letters to Hazrat Imam Husayn that he should come to Kufah; indeed some people themselves came into his presence and invited him to come to Kufah. Hazrat Imam Husayn sent his cousin Hazrat Muslim bin Aqil to Kufah to learn the situation. The ruler of Kufah was Nu’man bin Bashir. Nu’man did not like violence; he showed no kind of severity to Muslim bin Aqil either.
