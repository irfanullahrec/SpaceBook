He never even thought of collecting wealth. Whatever he had, he distributed among the poor and helpless. The concept of a pure life existed from ancient times. Many people before the Prophet too presented their own theories, but history is witness that the practical picture of a pure life that the Prophet presented, no one has been able to present till today and no one will be able to present. Many people believed after being impressed by his character. As long as he had not declared prophethood, the people of Makkah held him in the greatest esteem, but when he announced prophethood, these people turned against him. This difference was religious; otherwise all the people of Makkah accepted his great character. He bore the excesses of the people of Makkah with a smiling face. The ill-treatment shown by the chiefs of Taif will always be remembered in history. But even in these circumstances the Prophet gave proof of his being a mercy for the worlds. In the same way, his forgiving Hindah and Wahshi on the occasion of the Conquest of Makkah is also clear proof of the fact that he is a mercy for both worlds; otherwise what oppression of the people of Makkah was there that was not tried on him.
