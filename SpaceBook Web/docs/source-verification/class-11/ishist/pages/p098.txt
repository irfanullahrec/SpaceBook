The followers of Hazrat Isa were also very few. But the message of the Holy Prophet is universal. It is not limited to geographical boundaries or particular races, nations and languages. It has no restriction of time and era. It is in every respect complete for every age and will remain useful until the Day of Resurrection. The Creator of the universe has taken its protection upon Himself. It is the miracle of the Quran that in spite of the passing of centuries not even one letter of it can be changed. It is the only book in the world that can be memorised completely. It goes on being transferred from heart to heart. Papers can be altered, but it is not possible to erase it from hearts. The Prophet is the Seal of the Prophets. The chain of messengership has ended with him. No other prophet will come in the world now. The book revealed to him is the last book from Allah the Almighty. Its teachings are sufficient and curative for mankind until the Day of Resurrection. The Quran has declared the biography of the Prophet “the uswah hasanah”, that is, the best model. The foundation of Islam is itself on the Quran and the Sunnah of the Prophet. The Quran repeatedly stresses: obey Allah and His Messenger. It is the duty of all of us to adopt the teachings of the Quran and the Sunnah and to bring a revolution in our lives according to them.
