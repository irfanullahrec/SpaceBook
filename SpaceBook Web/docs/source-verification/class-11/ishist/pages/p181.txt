When she learnt of this tragedy she was very grieved. Hazrat Talhah and Hazrat Zubayr demanded of Hazrat Ali that retribution for Hazrat Uthman be taken at once. For Hazrat Ali there were two difficulties in doing so. The first difficulty was that there was no eyewitness of the martyrdom of Hazrat Uthman. Hazrat Naila was very old and her eyesight was weak, so she could not recognise anyone. The second difficulty was that the Muslim community was a victim of disorder and confusion; Hazrat Ali had no control over the killers. So Hazrat Ali said, “Let us be given so much opportunity that the condition of the Muslim community becomes right and discipline is established; then the killers will certainly be punished. I too am anxious in this matter.” Hazrat Talhah and Hazrat Zubayr were not satisfied and came to Makkah. Here, after persuading people to demand retribution, they set out towards Basrah. They defeated the Alawi governor of Basrah, Uthman bin Hunayf, and took possession of Basrah. When Hazrat Ali learnt of these circumstances he set out towards Basrah instead of Syria. The army of Kufah also reached to help him. The series of negotiations began from both sides. The negotiations succeeded.
