Hazrat Sa’d, on reaching Qadisiyyah, according to the instructions of Hazrat Umar, sent fourteen men to Yazdgird to invite him to accept Islam. Rustam came to the battlefield with an army of one hundred and twenty thousand, but he was avoiding starting the battle. Hazrat Sa’d was ill, so he went into an upper room. He addressed the mujahidin. The battle began. Hazrat Sa’d kept writing instructions for Khalid bin Urfutah on pieces of paper in the upper room and throwing them down, and he kept having them acted on. On the first day the Persians had the upper hand. Qa’qa’ played a prominent part in the battle. On the second day black masks were put on the horses of the Muslims, frightened by which the elephants began to bolt. The Muslims’ side began to look heavy. On the third day the decisive attack was made.
