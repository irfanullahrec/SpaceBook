The whole life of the Prophet is in reality a practical life. He completed humanity. His sincerity, steadfastness, simplicity, patience, contentment, self-sacrifice, trustworthiness, honesty, moral greatness and truthfulness will go on guiding humanity until the Day of Resurrection. He never cursed even those who gave him pain and his enemies; rather he always prayed for their guidance.
