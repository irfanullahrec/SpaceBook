No special resistance had to be faced; only Ikrimah bin Abu Jahl and a few of his companions challenged the Muslims. In the skirmish three companions were martyred. Thirteen of the polytheists were killed. They were faced by Hazrat Khalid bin Walid. This victory took place on 20 Ramadan 8 AH. After the victory the Prophet went to the Ka’bah and with his own hand cleared the holy house of Allah the Almighty of the false gods. All the idols were broken and the Ka’bah was purified. He offered two rak’ahs of prayer of thanksgiving. The Holy Prophet said, “The truth has come and falsehood has fled.” Hazrat Bilal gave the call to prayer, and the Holy Prophet with the noble companions offered the prayer in congregation without fear or danger. On the second day the Prophet stood at the door of the Ka’bah and addressed the people: “Allah is One. Only His worship is permissible. All the customs of the Age of Ignorance are abolished. The custodianship of the Ka’bah and the office of giving Zamzam water remain as before.” Then he said, “O Quraysh! What do you think about me?
