This house in reality played the role of an upper house. Important … matters were presented before this house. All these decisions were made in the Prophet’s Mosque … in the same way, if any problem requiring thought arose, for it the call “As-salatu jami’ah” was made in the streets of Madinah at the time of prayer. All the people turned towards the Prophet’s Mosque. … after the prayer the caliph stood on the pulpit and presented the problem. The aim of this call was that as many of the people of the city as possible should take part in it and express their opinion on the problem. Here the opinion of one another was respected. Every man could express his opinion in freedom. The problem was discussed in detail so that all aspects might come before the people. After deliberation a decision was made unanimously. The caliph in no case imposed his opinion on the people. In this way this house in reality performed the duty of a lower house.
