qp 1 | Why certain animals are called living fossils? || بعض جانوروں کو زندہ فوسل کیوں کہا جاتا ہے؟ || ځینې څاروي ولې ژوندي فوسیل بلل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: Living fossils are living species that have remained almost unchanged for millions of years and look like their ancestors known from fossils, e.g. Limulus (horseshoe crab), Latimeria (coelacanth), Sphenodon (tuatara), Peripatus, Ginkgo and Neopilina. They are called so because they show the features of earlier groups and help to understand evolution. ## Unit 9 evolution.
qp 2 | What is the reason for the formation of larva during the life cycle of many animals? || بہت سے جانوروں کے زندگی چکر کے دوران لاروا بننے کی کیا وجہ ہے؟ || د ډېرو څارویو د ژوند په دوره کې د لاروا د جوړېدو لامل څه دی؟
sol Model answer: A larva helps the species in several ways: it is a feeding stage that eats and grows while the adult spreads and reproduces; larva and adult use different food and habitat, so they do not compete; it can be a dispersal stage (free swimming larva of sessile animals like sponges and corals); and it helps to survive bad seasons. Examples: caterpillar of a butterfly, tadpole of a frog. ## Unit 9 life cycles.
qp 3 | What is the importance of polymorphism? || پولی مورفزم کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د پولي مورفیزم اهمیت څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Polymorphism (two or more distinct forms of the same species) enables division of labour: in Obelia the polyp feeds and the medusa reproduces; in honey bees and termites the queen, workers and drones do different jobs. It makes the colony efficient and improves survival and reproduction. ## Unit 9 polymorphism.
qp 4 | Cuttle fish, jelly fish, star fish are invertebrates and are not fishes. Why many invertebrates are called fish? || کٹل فش، جیلی فش، اسٹار فش غیر فقاریہ ہیں اور مچھلیاں نہیں ہیں۔ بہت سے غیر فقاریہ کو مچھلی کیوں کہا جاتا ہے؟ || کټل فش، جېلي فش، سټار فش بې ملا ټیري دي او کبان نه دي. ډېر بې ملا ټیري ولې کب بلل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: The word fish in cuttle fish, jelly fish and star fish is only a common name from the fact that they live in water, move in water and look like fishes at a glance. They are not fishes: they have no backbone, no fins with rays, no scales and no gills of fishes; cuttle fish is a mollusc, jelly fish a cnidarian and star fish an echinoderm. True fishes are vertebrates (chordates). ## Unit 9 invertebrates.
qp 5 | Why the reptiles excrete nitrogenous wastes in the form of uric acid crystals? || رینگنے والے جانور نائٹروجنی فضلات یورک ایسڈ کے قلموں کی شکل میں کیوں خارج کرتے ہیں؟ || خزندګان نایتروجني ضایعات د یوریک اسید د کرستالونو په بڼه ولې ایستل کوي؟
sol Model answer: Reptiles live in dry places and conserve water. Uric acid is almost insoluble, so it is excreted as a semi-solid paste or crystals with very little water, which saves the body water and is non-toxic. Also the embryo inside the shelled egg can store uric acid without being poisoned. ## Unit 9 excretion.
qp 6 | In what heamolymph is different from blood? || ہیمولمف خون سے کس طرح مختلف ہے؟ || هیمولمف له وینې څخه په څه توپیر لري؟
sol Model answer: Haemolymph is the fluid of the open circulatory system of arthropods and molluscs: it fills the haemocoel, bathes the organs directly, has no red blood cells and usually no respiratory pigment (it often does not carry oxygen in insects). Blood of vertebrates flows in closed vessels, has red blood cells with haemoglobin and carries oxygen. ## Unit 9 circulatory system.
