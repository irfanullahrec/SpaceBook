qp 4 | How the osmotic adjustments of plants in saline soils take place?. || نمکین مٹیوں میں پودوں کی آسموٹک ایڈجسٹمنٹ کیسے ہوتی ہے؟ || په مالګینو خاورو کې د بوټو اسموټیک تنظیمات څنګه کېږي؟
sol Model answer: In saline soils the water potential of the soil is low, so water tends to leave the root. Halophytes and other plants adjust osmotically by taking up ions (K+, Na+, Cl-) and making organic solutes such as proline, glycine betaine and sugars in the cell sap, which lowers the cell's water potential below that of the soil so that water can still enter. Some store salt in vacuoles, excrete it through salt glands, or exclude it at the roots. ## Unit 10 water relations.
qp 5 | Describe the structure of supporting tissues in plants. || پودوں میں سہارا دینے والی بافتوں کی ساخت بیان کریں۔ || په بوټو کې د ملاتړ نسجونو جوړښت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Supporting tissues: (1) Collenchyma: living cells with unevenly thickened walls (cellulose and pectin), usually below the epidermis of young stems and leaf stalks; gives flexible support and allows growth. (2) Sclerenchyma: dead cells with thick lignified walls and narrow cavities; fibres (long, pointed; in hemp, flax, jute) and sclereids (stone cells; in nut shells and pear); give rigidity and strength. (3) Xylem (tracheids, vessels) also supports the plant. Parenchyma supports young plants by turgor. ## Unit 10 plant tissues.
qp 6 | Discuss the role of important plant growth hormones. || پودوں کی نشوونما کے اہم ہارمونز کے کردار پر بحث کریں۔ || د بوټو د ودې د مهمو هورمونونو رول وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Plant hormones: (1) Auxins (IAA) are made in the shoot tip; they cause cell elongation, apical dominance, phototropism and gravitropism, root formation and fruit growth. (2) Gibberellins make the stem elongate, break seed dormancy and cause germination and flowering. (3) Cytokinins promote cell division, delay leaf ageing and with auxin control shoot and root formation. (4) Abscisic acid (ABA) is the stress hormone: closes stomata in drought, keeps seeds and buds dormant. (5) Ethylene is a gas that ripens fruit and causes leaf and fruit fall (abscission). ## Unit 10 growth regulators.
qp 7 | Classify plants on the basis of photoperiodism and give examples. || فوٹوپیریوڈزم کی بنیاد پر پودوں کی درجہ بندی کریں اور مثالیں دیں۔ || بوټي د فوټوپېریوډېزم پر بنسټ طبقه بندي کړئ او بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Photoperiodism is the response of plants to the length of day and night. Classification: (1) Short-day plants flower when the day is shorter than a critical length (night longer): chrysanthemum, rice, soybean, tobacco. (2) Long-day plants flower when the day is longer than a critical length: wheat, spinach, lettuce, barley, radish. (3) Day-neutral plants flower independent of day length: tomato, cotton, maize, sunflower. The night length is actually what the plant measures, with the pigment phytochrome. ## Unit 10 photoperiodism.
qp 8 | Describe the mechanism of opening and closing of stomata. || روزنوں کے کھلنے اور بند ہونے کے میکانزم کو بیان کریں۔ || د روزنو د خلاصېدو او تړلو میکانیزم بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Each stoma has two guard cells. Opening: in light, guard cells take in potassium ions (K+) (actively, by a proton pump) and chloride, and make organic acids and sugars; the water potential falls, water enters by osmosis, the guard cells become turgid and, since their inner walls are thick, they bend apart and the pore opens. Closing: in darkness or when the plant loses water, ABA causes K+ to leave, water leaves by osmosis, the guard cells become flaccid and the pore closes. ## Unit 10 transpiration.

qp • | Prepare a temporary slide, by cutting a T.S of a dicot stem. || دو دالی تنے کی مستعرض تراش (T.S) کاٹ کر ایک عارضی سلائیڈ تیار کریں۔ || د دوه لپکي ډډ د مقطع (T.S) پرې کولو سره یو لنډمهاله سلاید چمتو کړئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. T.S. of a dicot stem: take a young dicot stem (sunflower or Helianthus), hold it in a piece of carrot or pith, and cut very thin transverse sections with a sharp blade; place them in water in a watch glass, select the thinnest, stain with safranin (about 1 minute), wash, mount in glycerine on a slide, cover with a cover slip and see under the microscope. You see, from the outside: epidermis, cortex (collenchyma, parenchyma), endodermis, a ring of vascular bundles (each with xylem inside and phloem outside, with cambium between them) and the central pith. ## Practical work.
qp • | Collect and write taxonomic classification of at least five plants exhibiting xerophytic, mesophytic and hydrophytic characters respectively. || کم از کم پانچ پودوں کی جو بالترتیب زیروفائٹک، میزوفائٹک اور ہائیڈروفائٹک خصوصیات دکھاتے ہوں، جمع کر کے ان کی ٹیکسونومک درجہ بندی لکھیں۔ || لږترلږه پنځه بوټي چې په ترتیب سره زیروفایټیک، میزوفایټیک او هایډروفایټیک ځانګړتیاوې ښیي راټول کړئ او د هغو ټیکسونومیک طبقه بندي ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Examples with taxonomic classification (to be checked with the teacher): Xerophytes: Calotropis procera (Apocynaceae), Opuntia (cactus, Cactaceae), Acacia nilotica (Fabaceae), Capparis decidua (Capparaceae), Euphorbia (Euphorbiaceae). Mesophytes: Rosa indica (Rosaceae), Mangifera indica (Anacardiaceae), Triticum aestivum (Poaceae), Zea mays (Poaceae), Helianthus annuus (Asteraceae). Hydrophytes: Eichhornia crassipes (Pontederiaceae), Nymphaea (Nymphaeaceae), Hydrilla (Hydrocharitaceae), Lemna (Araceae), Typha (Typhaceae). Each is placed in Kingdom Plantae, Division Angiospermae. ## Practical work.
qp • | In your local area identify some major symptoms of mineral deficiencies in plants e.g. necrosis, chlorosis, stunted growth etc. || اپنے مقامی علاقے میں پودوں میں معدنی کمی کی کچھ بڑی علامات کی نشاندہی کریں مثلاً نیکروسس، کلوروسس، رکی ہوئی نشوونما وغیرہ۔ || په خپله ځايي سیمه کې په بوټو کې د معدني کمښت ځینې لوی نښې وپېژنئ لکه نیکروسیس، کلوروسیس، ودې کې درېدل او نور.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Major symptoms of mineral deficiency: nitrogen: pale-yellow older leaves (chlorosis) and stunted growth; phosphorus: purple or dark-green leaves and poor roots; potassium: yellow leaf margins that dry and die (necrosis); magnesium: yellowing between the veins of older leaves; iron: yellowing between veins of young leaves; calcium: death of growing tips and young leaves; zinc: small leaves (little leaf) and short internodes. Students look at crops in the field, record the symptoms, and suggest the fertiliser. ## Practical work.
