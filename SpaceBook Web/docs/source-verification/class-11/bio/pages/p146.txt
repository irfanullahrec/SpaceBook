## B. Write short answers of the following questions. || ب۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیں۔ || ب. د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Why has monera become obsolete? || مونیرا متروک کیوں ہو گیا ہے؟ || مونېرا ولې متروک شوی دی؟
sol Model answer: Monera was the kingdom of all prokaryotes (bacteria). It became obsolete because the studies of rRNA showed that prokaryotes are of two very different groups, bacteria and archaea, which differ from each other as much as from eukaryotes. So Monera was divided into the domains Bacteria and Archaea, and the kingdom name is no longer used. ## Unit 6 classification.
qp 2 | What is a domain? Why is this term coined? || ڈومین کیا ہے؟ یہ اصطلاح کیوں وضع کی گئی؟ || ډومېن څه دی؟ دا اصطلاح ولې جوړه شوې؟
sol Model answer: A domain is the highest taxonomic category, above kingdom. The term was coined by Carl Woese (1990) when he found, from rRNA sequences, that living things fall into three major groups: Bacteria, Archaea and Eukarya. It was coined to show that archaea differ from bacteria so deeply that they could not be placed in one kingdom. ## Unit 6 classification.
qp 3 | What is the role of prokaryotes in supporting life on earth? || زمین پر زندگی کو سہارا دینے میں پروکیریوٹس کا کیا کردار ہے؟ || په ځمکه کې د ژوند په ملاتړ کې د پروکاریوټونو رول څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Prokaryotes support life on earth: they recycle nutrients by decomposing dead matter, fix atmospheric nitrogen (Rhizobium, Azotobacter, cyanobacteria), take part in the carbon, sulphur and phosphorus cycles, produce oxygen (cyanobacteria), form the base of food chains, live in the gut of animals aiding digestion and vitamin production, and are used in making food (yoghurt, cheese), medicines and in genetic engineering and sewage treatment. ## Unit 6 importance of prokaryotes.
qp 4 | What are main differences between archaea and bacteria? || آرکیا اور بیکٹیریا کے درمیان اہم فرق کیا ہیں؟ || د آرکیا او باکتریا ترمنځ اصلي توپیرونه څه دي؟
sol Model answer: Archaea differ from bacteria in: cell wall (no peptidoglycan in archaea, murein in bacteria), cell membrane lipids (ether-linked branched lipids in archaea, ester-linked in bacteria), ribosomes and RNA polymerase (archaeal ones are like those of eukaryotes), introns and histones (found in some archaea, absent in bacteria), habitat (extreme environments for many archaea) and sensitivity to some antibiotics (archaea are not affected). Both are prokaryotes without a nucleus. ## Unit 6 archaea and bacteria.
qp 5 | What are plasmids and what are their importance? || پلازمڈز کیا ہیں اور ان کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || پلازمیډونه څه دي او د هغوی اهمیت څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Plasmids are small circular double-stranded DNA molecules in bacteria, separate from the main chromosome, that replicate independently. Importance: they carry useful genes such as resistance to antibiotics, genes for toxin production or nitrogen fixation, they can be passed between bacteria in conjugation, and, in genetic engineering, they are used as vectors to carry a foreign gene into bacterial cells to make insulin and other products. ## Unit 6 plasmids.
qp 6 | What is the function of endospores? || اینڈوسپورز کا کیا کام ہے؟ || د اندوسپورونو دنده څه ده؟
sol Model answer: Endospores are thick-walled resting cells that some bacteria (Bacillus, Clostridium) form inside the cell when conditions are bad (drought, heat, lack of food). They are not for reproduction; they protect the bacterium's DNA and allow it to survive extreme conditions for many years, and germinate into a new active cell when conditions are suitable. ## Unit 6 bacterial structure.
qp 7 | What do you mean by genetic recombination? || جینیاتی ری کمبی نیشن سے آپ کی کیا مراد ہے؟ || له جینیټیک ریکامبینېشن څخه ستاسو موخه څه ده؟
sol Model answer: Genetic recombination is the production of new combinations of genes by the exchange or transfer of genetic material from one cell to another. Bacteria do not reproduce sexually, but recombine genes by conjugation (through a pilus), transformation (picking up naked DNA from the surroundings) and transduction (transfer by a bacteriophage), which gives variation. ## Unit 6 reproduction in bacteria.
qp 8 | Why are the bacteria called nutrient recyclers? || بیکٹیریا کو غذائی اجزا کے ری سائیکلر کیوں کہا جاتا ہے؟ || باکتریا د مغذي موادو بیا کارونکي ولې بلل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: Bacteria are called nutrient recyclers because saprophytic bacteria decompose dead plants and animals and wastes, and release the minerals, carbon, nitrogen and phosphorus in them back into the soil and air to be used again by plants. Bacteria also convert nitrogen to nitrates (nitrification), and so keep the nutrients cycling in the ecosystem. ## Unit 6 role of bacteria.
qp 9 | Discuss the role of radiation in controlling bacterial growth. || بیکٹیریائی نشوونما کو قابو کرنے میں شعاعوں کے کردار پر بحث کریں۔ || د باکتریايي ودې په کنټرول کې د وړانګو رول وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Radiation controls bacterial growth by damaging them: ultraviolet light damages bacterial DNA (forming thymine dimers) and is used to sterilise the air and surfaces in operation rooms and water; ionising radiation (gamma rays and X-rays) breaks DNA and is used to sterilise medical instruments and food (irradiation); the radiation cannot penetrate deeply, and spores are more resistant. ## Unit 6 control of bacteria.
## C. Write answers of the following questions. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے جوابات لکھیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Give an account of the structure of a bacterium. || ایک بیکٹیریم کی ساخت کا بیان دیں۔ || د یوه باکتریم د جوړښت حساب ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Structure of a bacterium (a typical rod-shaped one): a capsule or slime layer (sometimes) outside; a cell wall of murein (peptidoglycan); the plasma membrane; cytoplasm without membrane-bound organelles but containing 70S ribosomes and food granules; a nucleoid, a single circular chromosome of DNA without a nuclear membrane, and often plasmids; mesosomes (infoldings of the membrane); flagella for movement (some); pili/fimbriae for attachment and conjugation. Size is about 1-5 micrometres. ## Unit 6 bacterial structure.
qp 2 | Discuss various methods of sexual reproduction of a bacteria. || ایک بیکٹیریا کی جنسی تولید کے مختلف طریقوں پر بحث کریں۔ || د یوه باکتریا د جنسي تکثیر بېلابېلې طریقې وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Bacteria reproduce asexually by binary fission; the sexual-like methods (genetic recombination) are: (1) Conjugation: two cells join by a pilus (sex pilus), and a copy of the F plasmid or part of the chromosome passes from the donor (F+) to the recipient. (2) Transformation: a bacterium takes up naked DNA of dead bacteria from its surroundings and includes it into its genome (Griffith's experiment). (3) Transduction: a bacteriophage carries a part of DNA from one bacterium to another. All give new combinations of genes. ## Unit 6 reproduction.
qp 3 | Describe the mode of nutrition in bacteria. || بیکٹیریا میں غذائیت کے طریقے کو بیان کریں۔ || په باکتریا کې د تغذیې طریقه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Modes of nutrition in bacteria: (1) Autotrophic: photoautotrophs use light (cyanobacteria with water, purple and green sulphur bacteria with H2S); chemoautotrophs use energy of inorganic chemicals (nitrifying bacteria, sulphur and iron bacteria). (2) Heterotrophic: saprotrophs (feed on dead matter), parasites (feed on living hosts, causing disease), and symbionts (live together with benefit, as Rhizobium in legume roots or gut bacteria). ## Unit 6 nutrition.
qp 4 | Give an account of bacterial diseases of plants. || پودوں کی بیکٹیریائی بیماریوں کا بیان دیں۔ || د بوټو د باکتریايي ناروغیو حساب ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Bacterial diseases of plants: citrus canker (Xanthomonas citri, brown raised spots on leaves and fruit), crown gall (Agrobacterium tumefaciens, tumours on stem and roots), bacterial wilt (Ralstonia and Erwinia: sudden wilting), soft rot of vegetables (Erwinia carotovora), blight of rice (Xanthomonas oryzae), and fire blight of apple and pear (Erwinia amylovora). Control: disease-free seeds, resistant varieties, removal and burning of infected plants, crop rotation, and copper-based sprays. ## Unit 6 plant diseases.
qp 5 | Describe the normal flora of some important organs of humans and also discuss its benefits || انسانوں کے کچھ اہم اعضا کی نارمل فلورا بیان کریں اور اس کے فوائد پر بھی بحث کریں || د انسانانو د ځینو مهمو غړو نارمل فلورا بیان کړئ او د هغې ګټې هم وڅېړئ
sol Model answer: Normal flora are the microorganisms that live on or in the human body without harm. Skin: Staphylococcus epidermidis, Corynebacterium. Mouth and throat: Streptococcus species. Large intestine: Escherichia coli, Bacteroides, Lactobacillus. Vagina: Lactobacillus. Benefits: they stop harmful microbes by competing for food and space and by making acids and antibiotic-like substances; they make vitamins B and K in the gut; they help digestion; and they train the immune system. ## Unit 6 normal flora.
