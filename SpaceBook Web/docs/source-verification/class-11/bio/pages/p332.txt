qp 4 | Describe the principles of angiography. || انجیوگرافی کے اصول بیان کریں۔ || د انجیوګرافۍ اصول بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Angiography is an X-ray method for taking pictures of the blood vessels. A thin tube (catheter) is passed into an artery (usually the femoral artery in the groin) and moved to the area to be examined; a contrast dye that shows up on X-rays is injected; X-ray pictures (angiograms) taken as the dye flows show narrowing or blocking of the vessels, aneurysm or clots. It is used to diagnose coronary artery disease, and angioplasty (balloon opening with a stent) can be done in the same way. ## Unit 12 diagnosis.
qp 5 | Describe the functions of lymph nodes and state the role of spleen as containing lymphoid tissue. || لمفی نوڈز کے افعال بیان کریں اور لمفاوی بافت پر مشتمل ہونے کے طور پر تلی کا کردار بیان کریں۔ || د لمفي نودونو دندې بیان کړئ او د توږي رول د لمفاوي نسج لرونکي په توګه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Lymph nodes are small bean-shaped structures along the lymph vessels (in the neck, armpit, groin). Functions: they filter the lymph and trap germs and foreign particles; macrophages destroy them; lymphocytes (B and T cells) are made and multiply in them to give immunity; they swell when there is an infection. The spleen is the largest lymphoid organ, in the upper left abdomen; its white pulp has lymphatic tissue with lymphocytes that respond to blood-borne germs and make antibodies, while the red pulp filters the blood and removes old red blood cells; it also stores blood and platelets. ## Unit 12 lymphatic system.
qp 6 | List the changes in life styles that can protect man from hypertension and cardiac problems. || طرزِ زندگی کی ان تبدیلیوں کی فہرست بنائیں جو انسان کو ہائپرٹینشن اور دل کے مسائل سے بچا سکتی ہیں۔ || د ژوند په طریقو کې هغه بدلونونه لیست کړئ چې کولای شي انسان له هایپرټنشن او د زړه له ستونزو وساتي.
sol Model answer: Changes in lifestyle to protect against hypertension and heart disease: stop smoking and avoid tobacco; reduce salt, saturated fat and cholesterol in the diet and eat fruit, vegetables and fibre; keep the weight normal; exercise regularly (for example 30 minutes of walking a day); limit alcohol; control stress with rest, sleep and relaxation; have regular check-ups of BP, sugar and cholesterol; control diabetes. ## Unit 12 heart disease.
qp 7 | Identify the factors causing atherosclerosis and arteriosclerosis. || ایتھروسکلیروسس اور آرٹیریوسکلیروسس کا سبب بننے والے عوامل کی نشاندہی کریں۔ || هغه لاملونه وپېژنئ چې اتیروسکلیروسیس او ارتیریوسکلیروسیس رامنځته کوي.
sol Model answer: Atherosclerosis is the build-up of fatty plaques (cholesterol, fat, calcium) inside arteries; arteriosclerosis is the thickening and hardening of artery walls. Causes: a diet high in saturated fat and cholesterol, high LDL, smoking, high blood pressure, diabetes, obesity, lack of exercise, stress, age, family history (genetic) and male sex. ## Unit 12 arterial diseases.

Draw a conclusion from this data for preventing cardiac issues and for sustaining healthy life. || کورونری بائی پاس سرجری سے گزرنے والے مریض کا انٹرویو ریکارڈ کریں۔ اس کے ماضی کے طرزِ زندگی، خوراکی عادات اور موروثی تاریخ سے متعلق معلومات جمع کریں۔ ان اعداد و شمار سے دل کے مسائل کی روک تھام اور صحت مند زندگی برقرار رکھنے کے لیے نتیجہ اخذ کریں۔ || له هغه ناروغ سره مرکه ثبت کړئ چې کورونري بایپاس جراحي یې کړې وي. د هغه/هغې د تېر ژوند طریقې، خوراکي عادتونو او جنیټیکي تاریخچې په اړه معلومات راټول کړئ. له دې معلوماتو څخه د زړه د ستونزو د مخنیوي او روغ ژوند د دوام لپاره پایله واخلئ.
sol Model answer: Interview task.
