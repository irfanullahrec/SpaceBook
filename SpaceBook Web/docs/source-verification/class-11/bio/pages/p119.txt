qp 1 | Give a brief status of viruses in classification. || درجہ بندی میں وائرسوں کی حیثیت کا مختصر بیان دیں۔ || په طبقه بندۍ کې د ویروسونو دریځ لنډ وړاندې کړئ.
sol Model answer: Viruses are acellular, non-living outside a host: they have no cytoplasm, organelles or metabolism, and they multiply only inside a living cell. Because they show characters of both living and non-living things, they are not included in the five kingdoms; they are classified separately (by the type of nucleic acid DNA/RNA, shape, host and disease) into groups such as DNA viruses, RNA viruses and retroviruses. ## Unit 5 introduction.
