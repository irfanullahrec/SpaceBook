## 2. Write short answers of the following questions. || ۲۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیں۔ || ۲. د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | What were the consequences of cells undergoing the process of endosymbiosis? || خلیوں کے اینڈوسمبایوسس کے عمل سے گزرنے کے کیا نتائج تھے؟ || د حجرو د اندوسمبایوسیس بهیر تېرولو پایلې څه وې؟
sol Model answer: Endosymbiosis allowed a host cell to take in other cells that lived inside it: aerobic bacteria became mitochondria, and cyanobacteria became chloroplasts. The consequences: eukaryotic cells with membrane-bound organelles evolved, with greater energy production by respiration and the ability of photosynthesis in algae and plants, and so a huge diversity of eukaryotes, including plants and animals. ## Unit 7 evolution of eukaryotes.
qp 2 | What do you understand by red tides? || سرخ مدّ (ریڈ ٹائیڈز) سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ || له سرو څپو (ریډ ټایډونو) څخه تاسو څه پوهېږئ؟
sol Model answer: Red tides are blooms (sudden increase) of dinoflagellates (e.g. Gonyaulax, Karenia) in the sea that colour the water red or brown. These algae release toxins that kill fish and shellfish and poison people who eat the contaminated shellfish; the dead blooms also use up the oxygen of the water. ## Unit 7 dinoflagellates.
qp 3 | Why asexual reproduction is critical for the survival of fungi? || فنجائی کی بقا کے لیے غیر جنسی تولید کیوں اہم ہے؟ || د فنجي د ژوندي پاتې کېدو لپاره غیر جنسي تکثیر ولې مهم دی؟
sol Model answer: Fungi reproduce asexually by spores, budding or fragmentation. This is critical because it is fast, it needs only one parent, it produces vast numbers of spores that spread by wind and water and colonise new food sources quickly, and the spores withstand bad conditions. Sexual reproduction occurs less often, when conditions need variation. ## Unit 7 reproduction in fungi.
qp 4 | Why fungi are considered a threat for crops? || فنجائی کو فصلوں کے لیے خطرہ کیوں سمجھا جاتا ہے؟ || فنجي د فصلونو لپاره ګواښ ولې ګڼل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: Fungi are a threat to crops because many parasitic fungi cause plant diseases: rusts and smuts of wheat, powdery mildew, late blight of potato (Phytophthora, a water mould), leaf spots and wilts. They spread by spores, damage leaves, stems and grain, lower yield and quality and cause losses of millions of rupees; stored grain also rots by fungi and some make aflatoxins that are poisonous. ## Unit 7 harmful fungi.
qp 5 | Discuss the role of algae in maintaining the oxygen balance in the biosphere. || حیاتی کرے میں آکسیجن کا توازن برقرار رکھنے میں الجی کے کردار پر بحث کریں۔ || په بایوسفیر کې د اکسیجن د توازن په ساتلو کې د الجي رول وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Algae are the main producers of oxygen in the biosphere: phytoplankton in oceans and lakes carry out photosynthesis and produce about half of the world's oxygen. They use up the carbon dioxide and release oxygen to the air and to water, which supports the aerobic life of aquatic animals and keeps the CO2-O2 balance. They also make the food that supports food chains. ## Unit 7 role of algae.
qp 6 | What do you understand by symbiosis? || ہم زیستی (سمبایوسس) سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ || له همژیوي ژوند (سمبایوسیس) څخه تاسو څه پوهېږئ؟
sol Model answer: Symbiosis is a close and long relationship between two different species living together. Types: mutualism (both benefit; lichen of an alga and a fungus, mycorrhiza), commensalism (one benefits, the other is not affected) and parasitism (one benefits at the cost of the other). ## Unit 7 symbiosis.
qp 7 | How will you justify the name scavengers given to fungi? || فنجائی کو دیے گئے نام صفائی کارکن (اسکیوینجرز) کا جواز آپ کیسے پیش کریں گے؟ || فنجي ته ورکړل شوي نوم پاکوونکي (سکیوینجر) توجیه به تاسو څنګه کوئ؟
sol Model answer: Fungi are called scavengers because most are saprotrophs: they secrete digestive enzymes on dead plants and animals, dung and waste, absorb the digested nutrients, and so decompose dead organic matter and clean the environment, returning minerals to the soil. ## Unit 7 role of fungi.
qp 8 | Discuss the medicinal importance of *claviceps purpurea*. || *claviceps purpurea* کی طبی اہمیت پر بحث کریں۔ || د *claviceps purpurea* طبي اهمیت وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Claviceps purpurea (ergot fungus) grows on rye and other cereals and forms hard black sclerotia (ergot). Ergot contains alkaloids, from which drugs are made: ergometrine is used to control bleeding after childbirth and to contract the uterus, ergotamine for migraine, and LSD is a derivative. Eating bread made from ergot-infected grain causes ergotism, a serious poisoning. ## Unit 7 Ascomycota.
qp 9 | Discuss the economic importance of yeast. || خمیر کی معاشی اہمیت پر بحث کریں۔ || د خمیر اقتصادي اهمیت وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Economic importance of yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae): it ferments sugar to alcohol and CO2, used in brewing, wine and industrial alcohol; baker's yeast makes dough rise in bread and cakes; yeast extract is rich in vitamin B and protein (Marmite) and is used in animal feed; it is used in genetic engineering and for making vaccines (hepatitis B) and enzymes (invertase). Some yeasts (Candida) cause disease. ## Unit 7 yeast.
## C. Write answers of the following questions in detail. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے جوابات تفصیل سے لکھیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو ځوابونه په تفصیل سره ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Describe the salient features of class ascomycota. || جماعت ایسکومائکوٹا کی نمایاں خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ || د اسکومایکوټا ټولګي مهمې ځانګړتیاوې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Class Ascomycota (sac fungi): the mycelium is septate; sexual spores (ascospores, usually eight) are formed in a sac-like ascus, often in a fruiting body (ascocarp); asexual reproduction is by conidia. Examples: yeast, Penicillium, Aspergillus, Neurospora, morels, truffles, Claviceps. Many are decomposers, some are parasites, and some form lichens. ## Unit 7 Ascomycota.
qp 2 | Discuss the general characteristics of fungi. || فنجائی کی عمومی خصوصیات پر بحث کریں۔ || د فنجي عمومي ځانګړتیاوې وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: General characteristics of fungi: eukaryotic, non-green, achlorophyllous; heterotrophic (saprotrophic, parasitic or symbiotic) with absorptive nutrition; body of thread-like hyphae forming a mycelium (unicellular in yeast); cell wall of chitin; reserve food as glycogen; reproduction by spores (sexual and asexual); no tissues; most are non-motile; found in soil, wood, food and water. ## Unit 7 fungi.
qp 3 | Given an account of the class basidiomycota. || جماعت بیسیڈیومائکوٹا کا بیان دیں۔ || د باسیډیومایکوټا ټولګي حساب ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Class Basidiomycota (club fungi): septate mycelium, with a dikaryotic phase that lasts long; sexual spores (basidiospores, usually four) are borne on a club-shaped basidium on a fruiting body (basidiocarp); asexual spores are rare. Examples: mushrooms (Agaricus), toadstools, puffballs, bracket fungi, and rusts and smuts of cereals. Many are edible or are decomposers of wood; some are poisonous (Amanita) or plant pathogens. ## Unit 7 Basidiomycota.
qp 4 | Discuss the beneficial aspect of fungi. || فنجائی کے مفید پہلو پر بحث کریں۔ || د فنجي ګټور اړخ وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: Beneficial aspects of fungi: decomposers recycling nutrients; food (mushrooms, yeast bread, cheese like Roquefort, soy sauce); antibiotics (penicillin from Penicillium); organic acids and enzymes (citric acid from Aspergillus); fermentation of alcohol; mycorrhiza which help plant roots take up water and minerals; biological control of insects; and as research organisms. ## Unit 7 importance of fungi.
qp 5 | Given an account of human and animals diseases caused by fungi. || فنجائی سے پیدا ہونے والی انسانی اور جانوروں کی بیماریوں کا بیان دیں۔ || د فنجي له امله د انسانانو او څارویو ناروغیو حساب ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Fungal diseases of humans and animals: ringworm (tinea) of skin, scalp and nails (Trichophyton, Microsporum); athlete's foot; candidiasis (thrush) by Candida albicans in mouth and vagina; aspergillosis of the lungs; histoplasmosis and cryptococcosis (lung and brain); and mycotoxin poisoning (aflatoxin). In animals: ringworm and lung infections of cattle, aspergillosis of birds. Treated with antifungal drugs (nystatin, clotrimazole, fluconazole). ## Unit 7 fungal diseases.

Edible ones have no ring-and-cup (volva), do not have a bitter smell; but only an expert can be sure, so identify with a field guide. ## Practical work; the list depends on the local collection.
qp • | Search some fungi which are source of antibiotic. || کچھ ایسے فنجائی تلاش کریں جو اینٹی بایوٹک کا ماخذ ہیں۔ || ځینې داسې فنجي ولټوئ چې د انټي بایوټیک سرچینه دي.
sol Model answer: Fungi that are sources of antibiotics: Penicillium notatum and P. chrysogenum (penicillin), Cephalosporium acremonium (cephalosporins), Streptomyces (an actinomycete bacterium, giving streptomycin and tetracycline), Aspergillus species (some antibiotics), Penicillium griseofulvum (griseofulvin, an antifungal drug). ## Research task.
