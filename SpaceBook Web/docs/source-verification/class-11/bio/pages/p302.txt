## C. Write in detail the answers of the following questions. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے جوابات تفصیل سے لکھیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو ځوابونه په تفصیل سره ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Explain the role of nervous system and gastrin hormone on the secretion of gastric juice. || معدے کے رس کے اخراج پر اعصابی نظام اور گیسٹرن ہارمون کے کردار کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د معدې د رس په افراز کې د عصبي سیستم او ګسټرین هورمون رول تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Gastric juice is controlled in three phases. Cephalic (nervous) phase: the sight, smell, taste or thought of food stimulates the brain, and the vagus nerve causes the stomach glands to make gastric juice and gastrin, before the food arrives. Gastric (hormonal) phase: food in the stomach stretches it and its protein breakdown products stimulate the G cells of the stomach to release the hormone gastrin into the blood; gastrin stimulates the gastric glands to secrete HCl and pepsinogen. Intestinal phase: food reaching the duodenum causes hormones (enterogastrone) that slow the secretion. ## Unit 11 control of digestion.
qp 2 | Describe the mechanism of digestion in the stomach. || معدے میں ہاضمے کے میکانزم کو بیان کریں۔ || په معده کې د هضم میکانیزم بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Digestion in the stomach: the stomach wall churns the food and mixes it with gastric juice (about 2-3 litres a day) to make a semi-liquid chyme. Hydrochloric acid (pH about 2) kills germs and activates pepsinogen to pepsin. Pepsin breaks proteins into peptones and proteoses. Rennin (in infants) curdles milk. Gastric lipase begins the digestion of fats. Mucus protects the wall. Small amounts of chyme pass through the pyloric sphincter into the duodenum. ## Unit 11 stomach.
qp 3 | Describe the composition of bile and relate the constituents with respective roles. || صفرا کی ترکیب بیان کریں اور اس کے اجزا کو متعلقہ کرداروں سے جوڑیں۔ || د صفرا ترکیب بیان کړئ او اجزا یې له اړوندو رولونو سره وتړئ.
sol Model answer: Bile is a green-yellow alkaline fluid made in the liver and stored in the gall bladder. Composition: water (about 97%); bile salts (sodium glycocholate and taurocholate) which emulsify fats and help absorb them; bile pigments (bilirubin and biliverdin) from the breakdown of haemoglobin, which are excreted and colour faeces; cholesterol and lecithin; and mineral salts (sodium bicarbonate) that neutralise stomach acid and give the alkaline medium for the enzymes. There is no digestive enzyme in bile. ## Unit 11 bile.
qp 4 | Discuss the mechanism of digestion and absorption of food in the small intestine. || چھوٹی آنت میں خوراک کے ہاضمے اور جذب کے میکانزم پر بحث کریں۔ || په کوچنۍ کولمه کې د خوراک د هضم او جذب میکانیزم وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer: In the small intestine (duodenum, jejunum, ileum) the chyme meets bile, pancreatic juice and intestinal juice (succus entericus). Pancreatic amylase digests starch to maltose; trypsin and chymotrypsin break proteins into peptides; lipase breaks fats into fatty acids and glycerol. Intestinal enzymes finish the work: maltase, sucrase and lactase make glucose, fructose and galactose; peptidases make amino acids; nucleases break nucleic acids. Absorption: the inner wall has folds, villi and microvilli which give a huge area; glucose and amino acids are absorbed by diffusion and active transport into the blood capillaries of the villi (and go to the liver by the hepatic portal vein); fatty acids and glycerol enter the lacteal (lymph). ## Unit 11 small intestine.
qp 5 | Explain the symptoms and treatment of bulimia nervosa? || بلیمیا نروسا کی علامات اور علاج کی وضاحت کریں؟ || د بولیمیا نیروزا نښې او درملنه تشریح کړئ؟
sol Model answer: Bulimia nervosa is an eating disorder of repeated binge eating followed by getting rid of the food by self-induced vomiting, laxatives or too much exercise. Symptoms: eating large amounts in a short time, vomiting afterwards, normal or slightly changed body weight, sore throat, swollen salivary glands, tooth decay (from stomach acid), irregular periods, tiredness, fear of fat and depression. Treatment: psychotherapy (cognitive behavioural therapy), nutritional counselling, antidepressant drugs, and treatment of complications, with the support of family. ## Unit 11 eating disorders.

sol Model answer: Practical/survey task. Digestive disorders and common home remedies: indigestion or dyspepsia (ajwain, fennel, a pinch of salt with warm water); constipation (isabgol husk, water, fibre-rich food, fruit); diarrhoea (oral rehydration solution of salt and sugar, yoghurt, rice water); acidity or heartburn (cold milk, banana, avoiding spicy food); gas or flatulence (cumin, ginger). Judgement: those that give water and salt (ORS) or fibre (isabgol) are scientifically supported; others may help in mild cases, but severe or lasting symptoms need a doctor. ## Survey task; the findings depend on the class survey.
