## B. Write short answers of the following questions. || ب۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیں۔ || ب. د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | List four main functions of a nucleus. || مرکزے کے چار اہم افعال کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د هستې څلور اصلي دندې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Four main functions of the nucleus: (1) It controls all the activities of the cell, because it contains the genetic information (DNA). (2) It stores the hereditary material as chromatin/chromosomes and passes it to daughter cells during cell division. (3) It makes ribosomal RNA and ribosome subunits in the nucleolus and controls protein synthesis by making mRNA. (4) It controls growth, metabolism and reproduction of the cell; its envelope with pores regulates the exchange of material with the cytoplasm. ## Nucleus section of Unit 1.
qp 2 | What do you know about peroxisomes? || پیروکسی سومز کے بارے میں آپ کیا جانتے ہیں؟ || د پیروکسیزومونو په اړه تاسو څه پوهېږئ؟
sol Model answer: Peroxisomes (microbodies) are small single-membrane organelles found in plant and animal cells, most common in the liver and kidney cells of animals. They are like lysosomes but denser and have different enzymes, such as catalase, which breaks down hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen and protects the cell from its harmful effect. In the leaves of green plants they take part in photorespiration, and in animals they help to break down fatty acids. ## Unit 1 peroxisomes.
qp 3 | Write two differences between rough endoplasmic reticulum and smooth endoplasmic reticulum. || کھردرے اینڈوپلازمک ریٹیکولم اور ہموار اینڈوپلازمک ریٹیکولم میں دو فرق لکھیں۔ || د زیږ انډوپلازمیک ریټیکولم او ښویه انډوپلازمیک ریټیکولم ترمنځ دوه توپیرونه ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Rough ER has ribosomes on its outer surface, is mainly made of flattened cisternae, and makes and transports proteins (it is plentiful in cells that secrete protein, like pancreatic cells). Smooth ER has no ribosomes, is mostly tubular, and makes lipids and steroids, detoxifies drugs and stores calcium (plentiful in liver and gland cells). ## Unit 1 endoplasmic reticulum.
qp 4 | What is cytoskeleton? Why it is consider to be important organelle in the cell. || سائٹوسکیلیٹن کیا ہے؟ اسے خلیے میں اہم عضویہ کیوں سمجھا جاتا ہے؟ || سایټوسکلیټون څه دی؟ ولې دا په حجره کې مهم ارګانیل ګڼل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: The cytoskeleton is a network of protein fibres in the cytoplasm made of microtubules, microfilaments and intermediate filaments. It is important because it gives the cell its shape and support, holds organelles in position, and enables movement of the cell and of organelles (cyclosis, vesicle transport), muscle contraction and cell division (spindle fibres), and forms cilia and flagella. ## Unit 1 cytoskeleton.
qp 5 | Cell is called structural and functional unit of life. Justify the statement. || خلیے کو زندگی کی ساختی اور فعلی اکائی کہا جاتا ہے۔ اس بیان کا جواز پیش کریں۔ || حجره د ژوند جوړښتي او دندیزه واحد بلل کېږي. دا بیان ثابت کړئ.
sol Model answer: The cell is the structural unit because all living organisms, from bacteria to humans, are built of one or more cells, and tissues, organs and organ systems are made of cells. It is the functional unit because every life process (respiration, digestion, synthesis of protein, excretion, reproduction) takes place within cells with the help of their organelles. A cell can live and reproduce independently when supplied with nutrients (a unicellular organism or a tissue culture), and new cells arise only from existing cells. ## Cell theory in Unit 1.
qp 6 | Why are chloroplast found only in plant cells? || کلوروپلاسٹ صرف پودوں کے خلیوں میں ہی کیوں پائے جاتے ہیں؟ || کلوروپلاسټونه ولې یوازې د بوټو په حجرو کې موندل کېږي؟
sol Model answer: Chloroplasts are found only in plant cells (and algae) because plants are autotrophs that make their own food by photosynthesis, and chloroplasts contain the chlorophyll and enzymes needed for it. Animals are heterotrophs that take ready-made food, so their cells have no need of chloroplasts. ## Unit 1 plastids.
qp 7 | What are the consequences of cell losing cell membrane? || خلیے کے خلوی جھلی کھو دینے کے کیا نتائج ہیں؟ || د حجرې د حجروي غشا له لاسه ورکولو پایلې څه دي؟
sol Model answer: Without the cell membrane the cell would die. The membrane controls what enters and leaves, so the cytoplasm would lose its contents and the ions, water and nutrients could not be balanced; the cytoplasm would leak out and the cell would swell or shrink by osmosis; it could not receive signals or keep its internal environment constant and would not be separated from its surroundings. ## Unit 1 plasma membrane.
