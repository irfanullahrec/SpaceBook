qp 2 | Differentiate between a retrovirus and a typ || ریٹرووائرس اور ایک عام (typ…) وائرس میں فرق کریں۔ || د ریټرو ویروس او یو عادي (typ…) ویروس ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: The question is cut off in the book data; it is answered as "retrovirus and a typical (RNA) virus". A typical RNA virus uses its RNA directly as mRNA or copies it into RNA with RNA replicase, and stays RNA throughout. A retrovirus (e.g. HIV) has RNA that is first copied into DNA by the enzyme reverse transcriptase; the DNA then enters the host chromosome (provirus) and uses the host's machinery to make new viruses. ## Unit 5 viruses. qp 3 | What are the consequences of a viral DNA becoming incorporated into a human egg or sperm cell? || وائرسی DNA کے انسانی بیضے یا نطفے کے خلیے میں شامل ہو جانے کے کیا نتائج ہیں؟ || د ویروسي DNA د انسان په هګۍ یا د نطفې په حجره کې شاملېدو پایلې څه دي؟
sol Model answer: If viral DNA becomes part of the DNA of an egg or sperm cell, it is passed to the offspring in every cell of the body (inherited), and may be copied for generations as an endogenous virus. It can disturb or switch on genes and cause diseases or cancer, or sometimes remain silent. It is part of the way in which viral genes became part of the human genome. ## Unit 5 viruses.
qp 4 | How do viruses suppresses the immune system in human body? || وائرس انسانی جسم میں مدافعتی نظام کو کیسے دباتے ہیں؟ || ویروسونه د انسان په بدن کې معافیتي سیستم څنګه ځپي؟
sol Model answer: Some viruses, like HIV, infect and destroy the helper T cells (CD4 lymphocytes), the cells that coordinate the immune response. When they fall in number, antibodies and killer cells are not stimulated properly, so the immune system becomes weak. Others hide inside cells, change their surface proteins (antigenic variation) or produce proteins that block the antigen display, so the immune system cannot find them. ## Unit 5 viruses and the immune system.
qp 5 | Why do viruses need a host cell? || وائرسوں کو میزبان خلیے کی ضرورت کیوں ہوتی ہے؟ || ویروسونه ولې کوربه حجرې ته اړتیا لري؟
sol Model answer: Viruses have no ribosomes, enzymes for metabolism or energy (ATP) of their own. They need a living host cell to supply amino acids, nucleotides, ribosomes, energy and enzymes for making new viral nucleic acid and protein, so they can reproduce only inside host cells (obligate parasites). ## Unit 5 viruses.
qp 6 | List down the main steps in the life cycle of HIV. || HIV کے زندگی چکر کے اہم مراحل کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د HIV د ژوند د دورې اصلي پړاوونه لیست کړئ.
sol Model answer: Life cycle of HIV: (1) Attachment: the viral glycoprotein (gp120) binds to the CD4 receptor on the helper T cell. (2) Fusion: the viral envelope fuses with the cell membrane and the capsid enters. (3) Reverse transcription: viral RNA is copied into DNA by reverse transcriptase. (4) Integration: the viral DNA enters the nucleus and is inserted into the host chromosome by integrase (provirus). (5) Transcription and translation: the host machinery makes viral RNA and proteins. (6) Assembly: new virus particles are assembled. (7) Budding and release: the viruses leave the cell, taking part of the membrane as envelope, and infect other cells. ## Unit 5 HIV.
qp 7 | How lymphocytes maintain a healthy immune system in human body? || لمفوسائٹس انسانی جسم میں صحت مند مدافعتی نظام کیسے برقرار رکھتے ہیں؟ || لمفوسایټونه د انسان په بدن کې روغ معافیتي سیستم څنګه ساتي؟
sol Model answer: Lymphocytes are white blood cells that recognise specific antigens. B lymphocytes become plasma cells and make antibodies (humoral immunity) and memory cells. T lymphocytes: helper T cells coordinate the response, cytotoxic T cells kill infected cells (cell-mediated immunity), suppressor T cells stop the response when over, and memory cells give a quick response on a second exposure. Together they remember invaders, destroy them and prevent illness. ## Unit 5 and Unit 13 immunity.

qp 1 | Explain how a virus manages to survive inside a host cell protected from the immune system? || وضاحت کریں کہ ایک وائرس مدافعتی نظام سے محفوظ میزبان خلیے کے اندر کیسے زندہ رہتا ہے؟ || تشریح کړئ چې یو ویروس د معافیتي سیستم څخه ساتل شوې کوربه حجرې دننه څنګه ژوندی پاتې کېږي؟
sol Model answer: A virus protects itself inside the host cell because the antibodies in the blood cannot enter cells. Some viruses (herpes) stay hidden and inactive (latent) in nerve cells, some (HIV) integrate into the host DNA as a provirus, and some change their surface antigens, so the immune system is always behind. Viruses also spread directly from cell to cell without being exposed in the blood. ## Unit 5 viruses.
qp 2 | Describe the role of bacteriophage in genetic engineering. || جینیاتی انجینئرنگ میں بیکٹیریوفیج کے کردار کو بیان کریں۔ || په جینیټیکي انجینرۍ کې د باکتریوفاژ رول بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Bacteriophages are used in genetic engineering as vectors (carriers) to carry foreign DNA into bacterial cells. The gene of interest is cut and inserted into the phage genome (for example lambda phage); when the phage infects bacteria it injects the DNA, and the gene is copied or expressed in the host (transduction). Phages are also used in studying genes, in phage display and in phage therapy against bacteria. ## Unit 5 bacteriophage.
qp 3 | Describe some common control measures against the transmission of HIV. || HIV کی منتقلی کے خلاف چند عام کنٹرول اقدامات بیان کریں۔ || د HIV د لېږد پر وړاندې ځینې عام کنټرول اقدامات بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Control measures against HIV transmission: avoid unsafe sex and keep to one faithful partner (use condoms); use only sterilised or disposable syringes and needles, never share needles or razors; screen all blood and organs before transfusion; infected mothers should take antiviral drugs during pregnancy and delivery and avoid breastfeeding if advised; use gloves when handling blood; health education and early testing. HIV is not spread by touching, sharing food or mosquito bites. ## Unit 5 HIV.
qp 4 | Describe the causative agent, symptoms, treatment and prevention of leaf curl virus disease of cotton. || کپاس کے پتوں کے مڑنے کی وائرسی بیماری کے سبب بننے والے عامل، علامات، علاج اور بچاؤ کو بیان کریں۔ || د پنبې د پاڼو د تاوېدو ویروسي ناروغۍ لامل، نښې، درملنه او مخنیوی بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Leaf curl virus of cotton (CLCuV): it is a begomovirus (single-stranded DNA virus) carried by the whitefly (Bemisia tabaci). Symptoms: upward or downward curling of leaves, thickening of veins, enation (small leaf-like outgrowths on the underside of the leaf), stunted growth and few bolls, so the yield falls greatly. Treatment: no cure for the diseased plant; infected plants are uprooted and burned and whiteflies are killed with insecticides. Prevention: grow resistant varieties, destroy weeds and alternate hosts, use clean seed, and sow at the right time. ## Unit 5 plant viral diseases.
qp 5 | Write a brief note on prions and viroids. || پرائیونز اور وائرائیڈز پر ایک مختصر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || په پرایونونو او ویرایډونو یو لنډ یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Prions are infectious proteins (proteinaceous infectious particles) with no nucleic acid, discovered by Prusiner. A normal protein of nerve cells (PrPc) is changed to a wrongly folded form (PrPsc) that makes other normal proteins fold in the same way; they form clumps and damage the brain (spongy brain), as in mad cow disease (BSE), scrapie in sheep and Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease in humans. Viroids are the smallest known infectious agents: small circular single-stranded RNA with no protein coat, found in plants, causing diseases like potato spindle tuber and cadang-cadang of coconut; they don't code for any protein and use host enzymes to copy themselves. ## Unit 5 prions and viroids.
