## C. Write detailed answers to the following questions. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات لکھیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Explain how the properties of water make it important for life? || وضاحت کریں کہ پانی کی خصوصیات اسے زندگی کے لیے کیسے اہم بناتی ہیں؟ || تشریح کړئ چې د اوبو ځانګړتیاوې دا د ژوند لپاره څنګه مهم ګرځوي؟
sol Model answer: Water is important for life because of its properties: it is a polar molecule that forms hydrogen bonds. (1) A universal solvent, so reactions and transport in cells occur in water. (2) High specific heat capacity, so temperature of cells and the body changes slowly. (3) High heat of vaporisation, so sweating and transpiration cool organisms. (4) Cohesion and adhesion, which pull water up the xylem and give surface tension. (5) It is a reactant in hydrolysis and photosynthesis. (6) Ice floats, so lakes freeze from the top and aquatic life survives. (7) Transparent, so light reaches aquatic plants. ## Unit 2 water.
qp 2 | Describe the properties and roles of disaccharides. || ڈائی سیکرائیڈز کی خصوصیات اور کرداروں کو بیان کریں۔ || د ډای سکرایډونو ځانګړتیاوې او رولونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Disaccharides are formed from two monosaccharides joined by a glycosidic bond. Properties: soluble in water, sweet, crystalline, can be hydrolysed to monosaccharides, and many are reducing sugars (maltose, lactose; sucrose is non-reducing). Roles: sucrose (glucose + fructose) is the transport sugar in plants and table sugar; maltose (glucose + glucose) is formed in the digestion of starch and in germinating seeds; lactose (glucose + galactose) is the sugar of milk, the main energy source for infants. ## Unit 2 disaccharides.
qp 3 | Classify proteins. List examples and roles of structural and functional proteins. || پروٹینوں کی درجہ بندی کریں۔ ساختی اور فعلی پروٹینوں کی مثالوں اور کرداروں کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || پروټینونه ډلبندي کړئ. د جوړښتي او دندیزو پروټینونو بېلګې او رولونه لړلیک کړئ.
sol Model answer: Proteins are classified by shape and composition: (1) Fibrous proteins (long, insoluble; structural), e.g. keratin, collagen, fibroin. (2) Globular proteins (round, soluble; functional), e.g. enzymes, haemoglobin, antibodies, insulin. (3) Conjugated proteins (protein + non-protein part), e.g. glycoproteins, lipoproteins. Structural proteins give shape and strength (collagen in tendons and bones, keratin in hair and nails, actin and myosin in muscle). Functional proteins do a job (enzymes catalyse reactions, haemoglobin carries oxygen, antibodies defend, hormones like insulin regulate, membrane carriers transport). ## Unit 2 proteins.
qp 4 | Describe the properties and roles of acylglycerol, terpenes and phospholipids. || ایسائل گلیسرول، ٹرپینز اور فاسفولپڈز کی خصوصیات اور کرداروں کو بیان کریں۔ || د اسایل ګلیسرول، ټرپینونو او فاسفولیپیډونو ځانګړتیاوې او رولونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Acylglycerols (fats and oils) are made of glycerol and fatty acids by ester bonds; a triglyceride has three. Properties: insoluble in water, high energy (about 38 kJ per gram), solid (fats, saturated) or liquid (oils, unsaturated). Roles: energy store, heat insulation, protection of organs and a solvent for vitamins. Terpenes are long molecules made of isoprene units; roles: carotenoids and chlorophyll's tail, vitamins A, E and K, plant oils, rubber and plant hormones (gibberellins). Phospholipids have glycerol, two fatty acids and a phosphate head: the head is hydrophilic and tails hydrophobic; they form the bilayer of all cell membranes. ## Unit 2 lipids.
qp 5 | Define conjugated molecule and describe the roles of common conjugated molecules. || کنجوگیٹڈ سالمے کی تعریف کریں اور عام کنجوگیٹڈ سالموں کے کرداروں کو بیان کریں۔ || کنجوګېټډ مالیکول تعریف کړئ او د عامو کنجوګېټډ مالیکولونو رولونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: A conjugated molecule is a molecule made by combining a protein, lipid or carbohydrate with another kind of molecule (the prosthetic group). Common ones: glycoproteins (protein + carbohydrate; cell recognition, mucus, antibodies); glycolipids (lipid + carbohydrate; cell membrane markers); lipoproteins (lipid + protein; transport of fats in blood, e.g. LDL and HDL); nucleoproteins (nucleic acid + protein; chromosomes, ribosomes); chromoproteins (protein + pigment; haemoglobin carries oxygen). ## Unit 2 conjugated molecules.
qp 6 | Explain the double helical structure of DNA as proposed by the Watson and Crick. || واٹسن اور کرک کی تجویز کردہ DNA کی دوہری مرغولہ ساخت کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د واټسن او کریک لخوا وړاندیز شوی د DNA دوه ګونی مارپیچ جوړښت تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Watson and Crick (1953) proposed that DNA is a double helix: two polynucleotide strands wound round each other like a twisted ladder with a right-handed turn. The sugar-phosphate backbones are on the outside, run in opposite directions (antiparallel), and the nitrogenous bases face inward and are joined by hydrogen bonds: adenine pairs with thymine (two bonds) and guanine with cytosine (three bonds), so one strand is complementary to the other. The helix is about 2 nm wide and makes one complete turn every 3.4 nm (10 base pairs). This structure explains how DNA copies itself (semi-conservative replication). ## Unit 2 DNA.
## Projects: || منصوبے: || پروژې:
qp • | Make a model exhibiting the hydrogen bonding. || ہائیڈروجن بانڈنگ کو ظاہر کرنے والا ایک ماڈل بنائیں۔ || یو ماډل جوړ کړئ چې هایډروجني تړاو ښيي.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Hydrogen-bonding model of water: make water molecules from three balls each (one big red ball for oxygen and two small white balls for hydrogen) fixed at an angle of 104.5 degrees with toothpicks. Mark the oxygen end as slightly negative and the hydrogens slightly positive. Join several molecules with dotted lines or thin springs between the oxygen of one molecule and the hydrogen of another to show the hydrogen bonds; label them. ## Practical work.
qp • | Make a simple model of ring forms of alpha and beta glucose. || الفا اور بیٹا گلوکوز کی حلقہ دار شکلوں کا ایک سادہ ماڈل بنائیں۔ || د الفا او بېټا ګلوکوز د حلقوي بڼو یو ساده ماډل جوړ کړئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Rings of alpha and beta glucose: cut a hexagonal ring from cardboard or use clay with five carbon atoms and one oxygen atom in the ring; number the carbons 1 to 6; carbon 6 (CH2OH) sticks up from carbon 5. Put the OH on carbon 1 downward (below the ring) for alpha glucose and upward for beta glucose; the other groups are the same in both. Label both models. ## Practical work.
qp • | Create a 3-Dimensional model of Watson - Crick Model of DNA. || واٹسن-کرک ماڈل برائے DNA کا سہ جہتی ماڈل تیار کریں۔ || د DNA د واټسن-کریک ماډل درې اړخیز ماډل جوړ کړئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. 3-D model of the Watson-Crick DNA: use two long wires or twisted ropes as the sugar-phosphate backbones, coloured beads or pieces of foam as nitrogenous bases (e.g. red adenine, blue thymine, green guanine, yellow cytosine) fixed as steps between them, A with T and G with C, hydrogen bonds shown by toothpicks (2 for A-T and 3 for G-C); twist the whole structure to a right-handed helix, mark the 5' and 3' ends and label the parts. ## Practical work.
