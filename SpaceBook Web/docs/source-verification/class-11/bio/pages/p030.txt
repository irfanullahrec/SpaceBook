## C. Write the answers of the following questions. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے جوابات لکھیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Differentiate between a prokaryotic and eukaryotic cell. || پروکیریوٹک اور یوکیریوٹک خلیے میں فرق کریں۔ || د پروکاریوټیک او یوکاریوټیک حجرې ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Prokaryotic cell: no true nucleus (DNA lies free in the nucleoid), no membrane-bound organelles, small ribosomes (70S), cell wall of murein, size 1-10 micrometres, single circular DNA, binary fission; examples bacteria and cyanobacteria. Eukaryotic cell: a true nucleus enclosed by a nuclear envelope, membrane-bound organelles (mitochondria, ER, Golgi), larger ribosomes (80S), size 10-100 micrometres, several linear chromosomes, mitosis/meiosis; examples are plants, animals, fungi and protists. ## Unit 1 cell types.
qp 2 | Describe the fluid mosaic model of plasma membrane. || پلازما جھلی کے سیال پچی کاری ماڈل کو بیان کریں۔ || د پلازما غشا د سیال موزاییک ماډل تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: The fluid mosaic model of Singer and Nicolson (1972) says the plasma membrane is a fluid double layer (bilayer) of phospholipids in which proteins float like a mosaic. The phospholipid heads are hydrophilic and face the water outside and inside, the tails are hydrophobic and face each other inside. Integral proteins go across the bilayer (channels, carriers) and peripheral proteins lie on the surface; cholesterol keeps the fluidity; carbohydrates attached to proteins or lipids (glycoproteins, glycolipids) lie on the outer surface for recognition. Lipids and proteins can move sideways, so the membrane is fluid. ## Unit 1 plasma membrane.
qp 3 | What do you know about Golgi apparatus. Describe its functions. || گولجی اپریٹس کے بارے میں آپ کیا جانتے ہیں۔ اس کے افعال بیان کریں۔ || د ګولجي اپارات په اړه تاسو څه پوهېږئ. دندې یې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: The Golgi apparatus (Golgi complex) is a stack of flattened membrane sacs (cisternae) with vesicles at the edges, with a cis (receiving) face near the ER and a trans (shipping) face. Functions: it modifies, sorts and packs proteins and lipids made in the ER (adds sugar to make glycoproteins), forms secretory vesicles for export (enzymes, hormones), forms lysosomes, makes cell wall material in plants, and forms the plasma membrane. ## Unit 1 Golgi apparatus.
qp 4 | Explain critically the role of lysosomes in human body. || انسانی جسم میں لائسوسومز کے کردار کی تنقیدی وضاحت کریں۔ || د انسان په بدن کې د لایزوزومونو رول په انتقادي ډول تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Lysosomes are single-membrane sacs full of hydrolytic enzymes. Roles in the human body: (1) Digest food particles and bacteria taken in by white blood cells (phagocytosis), helping defence. (2) Autophagy: break down worn-out organelles and recycle their parts. (3) Autolysis: they burst and digest the cell after its death (suicide bags), which helps in removing tissues, e.g. the tail of the tadpole or the tissue of the uterus after birth. (4) If their enzymes are missing, undigested material builds up and storage diseases (e.g. Tay-Sachs disease) occur; if they leak, they harm the cell, as in arthritis. ## Unit 1 lysosomes.
qp 5 | Write a note on cytoskeleton. || سائٹوسکیلیٹن پر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || په سایټوسکلیټون یوه یادداښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: The cytoskeleton is a network of protein filaments in the cytoplasm of eukaryotic cells. It has three parts: microtubules (tubulin, hollow tubes; form spindle fibres, cilia, flagella, centrioles), microfilaments (actin, thin solid rods; muscle contraction, cytoplasmic streaming and cell movement) and intermediate filaments (strong ropes that hold the nucleus and give strength). It gives shape and mechanical support, anchors organelles, moves materials and the cell itself, and separates chromosomes at cell division. ## Unit 1 cytoskeleton.
qp 6 | What do you mean by tissue culture? What are the main steps involved in the process of tissue culture? || بافتی کاشت سے آپ کی کیا مراد ہے؟ بافتی کاشت کے عمل میں شامل اہم مراحل کیا ہیں؟ || د نسج له کرنې څخه ستاسو موخه څه ده؟ د نسج د کرنې په بهیر کې کوم اصلي پړاوونه شامل دي؟
sol Model answer: Tissue culture is growing cells, tissues or organs of a plant or animal in a sterile artificial nutrient medium outside the organism. Main steps: (1) Choose and cut the explant (a small piece of tissue, e.g. a shoot tip) from a healthy plant. (2) Sterilise the explant and work in a sterile environment (laminar flow cabinet) with sterilised instruments. (3) Put it on a sterile nutrient medium (agar with minerals, sugar, vitamins and growth hormones) in a culture vessel. (4) Incubate under controlled temperature and light; the explant forms a mass of undifferentiated cells (callus). (5) Transfer the callus to a medium with suitable hormones to make shoots and roots (differentiation). (6) Harden the small plants and transfer them to soil. ## Unit 1 tissue culture.
qp 7 | How do the cell organelles work together to keep us alive? || خلوی عضویات ہمیں زندہ رکھنے کے لیے مل کر کیسے کام کرتے ہیں؟ || حجروي ارګانیلونه موږ ژوندي ساتلو لپاره څنګه یو ځای کار کوي؟
sol Model answer: The organelles work as a team. The nucleus holds the instructions (DNA) and makes mRNA; ribosomes make proteins; the ER and Golgi apparatus fold, modify, sort and ship them; mitochondria make ATP by respiration to power all the work; lysosomes and peroxisomes digest and remove waste; the plasma membrane controls entry and exit; in plants chloroplasts make food. Because they cooperate, the cell grows, repairs itself, takes in nutrients and removes waste, and all the cells together keep the body alive. ## Unit 1 cell organelles.
qp 8 | Discuss how the structure of specialized animal cells is related to their functions? || بحث کریں کہ مخصوص جانوری خلیوں کی ساخت ان کے افعال سے کیسے تعلق رکھتی ہے؟ || بحث وکړئ چې د ځانګړو څارویو حجرو جوړښت د هغوی له دندو سره څنګه تړاو لري؟
sol Model answer: Structure fits function in specialised animal cells: Red blood cells have no nucleus and are biconcave discs, giving more room for haemoglobin and a larger surface to carry oxygen. Nerve cells are long with branching dendrites and an axon to carry impulses over long distances. Muscle cells have many mitochondria and contractile protein filaments for movement and energy. Sperm cells have a tail (flagellum) for swimming, many mitochondria in the middle piece, and an acrosome with enzymes to enter the egg. Cells of the intestine have microvilli to increase the surface for absorption. ## Unit 1 specialised cells.
## Projects: || منصوبے: || پروژې:
qp • | Make a fluid mosaic model of plasma membrane. || پلازما جھلی کا سیال پچی کاری ماڈل بنائیں۔ || د پلازما غشا د سیال موزاییک ماډل جوړ کړئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Fluid mosaic model of the plasma membrane: take a base of cardboard or foam sheet; make two rows of phospholipid molecules from round beads (heads) with two wires or threads (tails), tails pointing inwards to form a bilayer; add large coloured clay shapes as integral proteins across the bilayer and smaller ones on the surface as peripheral proteins; add cholesterol as small yellow pieces among the tails and carbohydrate chains as threads with beads on the outer surface; label all parts. ## Practical work; the model is made by the students.
qp • | Take a material of your choice and measure the size of its cells by micrometry. || اپنی پسند کا کوئی مواد لیں اور مائیکرومیٹری سے اس کے خلیوں کا حجم ناپیں۔ || د خپلې خوښې یو مواد واخلئ او د مایکرومېټرۍ په وسیله د هغه د حجرو اندازه وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Measuring cell size by micrometry: fit an ocular micrometer into the eyepiece of the microscope and a stage micrometer on the stage (1 division = 0.01 mm). Calibrate: find how many eyepiece divisions equal a known stage distance, e.g. if 10 ocular divisions = 5 stage divisions (0.05 mm), then 1 ocular division = 0.005 mm. Make a slide of a thin onion epidermal peel, measure the length and width of 10 cells in ocular divisions, multiply by the calibration value, and take the average. Typical result: an onion epidermal cell is about 0.2 mm long (200 micrometres). ## Practical work; the numbers depend on the material and microscope.
qp • | Compare in tabular form, the functions of organelles with the processes occurring in animals and plants on a chart and share it in the class. || ایک چارٹ پر جدول کی صورت میں عضویات کے افعال کا جانوروں اور پودوں میں ہونے والے عوامل سے موازنہ کریں اور اسے کلاس میں پیش کریں۔ || په یوه چارټ کې په جدولي بڼه د ارګانیلونو دندې د څارویو او بوټو په ترسره کېدونکو بهیرونو سره پرتله کړئ او په ټولګي کې یې شریکه کړئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Chart (organelle - function - process in animals / plants): Nucleus - controls the cell - division and heredity in both; Mitochondria - ATP by respiration - energy for movement in animals and for growth in plants; Chloroplast - photosynthesis - only in plants (food production); Ribosomes - protein synthesis - enzymes, hormones and structural proteins in both; Rough ER - transport of protein; Golgi - packing and secretion - digestive enzymes in animals, cell wall material in plants; Lysosomes - digestion - mainly animal cells (defence by white blood cells); Vacuole - storage and turgor - large and central in plants; Cell wall - support - only plants. Students draw this in a table on a chart and share it. ## Practical work.
