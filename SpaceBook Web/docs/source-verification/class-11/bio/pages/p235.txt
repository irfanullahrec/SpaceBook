qp 7 | Differentiate between Proterostomes and Deuterostomes. || پروٹیروسٹومز اور ڈیوٹیروسٹومز میں فرق کریں۔ || د پروټیروسټومونو او ډیوټیروسټومونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Protostomes (annelids, molluscs, arthropods): the blastopore becomes the mouth, cleavage is spiral and determinate, and the coelom forms by splitting of mesoderm (schizocoelous). Deuterostomes (echinoderms, chordates): the blastopore becomes the anus and the mouth forms later, cleavage is radial and indeterminate, and the coelom forms from outpockets of the gut (enterocoelous). ## Unit 9 animal embryology.
qp 8 | Why alternation of generation is required . || نسلوں کا تبادل کیوں ضروری ہے۔ || د نسلونو تناوب ولې اړین دی.
sol Model answer: Alternation of generation, as in Obelia, is needed because the asexual polyp stage can increase the number of individuals rapidly, while the sexual medusa stage produces gametes and gives variation and spreads the species to new places. Both together help survival. ## Unit 9 Cnidaria.
qp 9 | What is the difference between haemocoel and coelom? || ہیموسیل اور سیلوم میں کیا فرق ہے؟ || د هیموسیل او سیلوم ترمنځ توپیر څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Coelom is a true body cavity completely lined with mesoderm, which contains the organs and is separate from the gut and body wall. A haemocoel is a body cavity filled with blood (haemolymph) that bathes the organs; it is a remnant of the coelom of arthropods and molluscs, not lined by mesoderm, and part of the open circulatory system. ## Unit 9 body cavities.
qp 10 | List down three organisms, each representing radial symmetry and bilateral symmetry? || شعاعی تشاکل اور دو طرفہ تشاکل کی نمائندگی کرنے والے تین تین جانداروں کی فہرست بنائیں؟ || درې موجودات لیست کړئ، هر یو چې وړانګیز تناسب او دوه اړخیز تناسب استازیتوب کوي؟
sol Model answer: Radial symmetry: Hydra, jellyfish (Aurelia) and sea anemone (also starfish). Bilateral symmetry: earthworm, cockroach and human being (also frog, fish). ## Unit 9 symmetry.
qp 11 | Differentiate between prototheria and metatheria. || پروٹوتھیریا اور میٹاتھیریا میں فرق کریں۔ || د پروټوتیریا او میټاتیریا ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Prototheria (monotremes) lay eggs, have a cloaca, mammary glands without nipples, e.g. duck-billed platypus and spiny anteater (echidna). Metatheria (marsupials) give birth to very immature young which complete development in the mother's pouch (marsupium), attached to the nipples, with a poorly developed placenta, e.g. kangaroo and opossum. ## Unit 9 mammals.

qp 1 | Describe the importance of classification in animal kingdom. What are different categories used for classification? || جانوروں کی بادشاہت میں درجہ بندی کی اہمیت بیان کریں۔ درجہ بندی کے لیے استعمال ہونے والے مختلف زمرے کون سے ہیں؟ || د څارویو په سلطنت کې د طبقه بندۍ اهمیت بیان کړئ. د طبقه بندۍ لپاره کارېدونکې بېلابېلې کټګورۍ کومې دي؟
sol Model answer: Classification is important because it arranges the very large number of animals into groups according to their similarities, makes it easy to study and identify them, shows their evolutionary relationships, gives each a universal scientific name, and helps to find and record new species. Categories (taxa) from the highest to the lowest: Domain, Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus and Species. ## Unit 9 classification.
qp 2 | What are the salient features of phylum Annelida? Describe their economic importance as well. || فائلم اینیلیڈا کی نمایاں خصوصیات کیا ہیں؟ ان کی معاشی اہمیت بھی بیان کریں۔ || د فایلم انیلیډا مهمې ځانګړتیاوې کومې دي؟ د هغوی اقتصادي اهمیت هم بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Phylum Annelida (segmented worms): bilateral symmetry; body in similar rings (metameric segmentation); true coelom; triploblastic; closed circulatory system with blood containing haemoglobin; nephridia for excretion; a nervous system with a brain and ventral nerve cord; bristles (setae) for movement; examples: earthworm, leech, Nereis. Economic importance: earthworms make soil fertile, aerate and drain it; they are used as bait and as feed for fish and poultry; leeches are used in medicine (hirudin, to take out blood); some are food for fish; some leeches are parasites. ## Unit 9 Annelida.
qp 3 | Describe the parasitic adaptations in phylum platyhelminths. || فائلم پلیٹی ہیلمنتھس میں طفیلی موافقتیں بیان کریں۔ || په فایلم پلاتي هیلمنتس کې پرازیتي تطابقونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Parasitic adaptations of flatworms (flukes and tapeworms): hooks and suckers for attachment to the host; a thick cuticle that protects against the host's digestive enzymes; loss of digestive system in tapeworms, and absorption of digested food from the host through the body surface; reduced sense organs and nervous system; very large numbers of eggs (high reproductive capacity, the whole body is a reproductive system); hermaphroditism (self-fertilisation); complex life cycles with intermediate hosts (e.g. snail in liver fluke); and ability to live without oxygen (anaerobic respiration). ## Unit 9 Platyhelminthes.
qp 4 | Compare the structure of heart in different phyla of animal kingdom and especially in different classes of vertebrates. || جانوروں کی بادشاہت کے مختلف فائلا میں اور خاص طور پر فقاریہ کی مختلف جماعتوں میں دل کی ساخت کا موازنہ کریں۔ || د څارویو د سلطنت په بېلابېلو فایلا کې او په ځانګړې توګه د ملا لرونکو په بېلابېلو ټولګیو کې د زړه جوړښت پرتله کړئ.
sol Model answer: Heart in animals: In invertebrates like annelids there are simple contractile blood vessels (pseudo-hearts); arthropods and molluscs have a tubular or chambered heart in an open system. Among vertebrates: fishes have a two-chambered heart (one atrium and one ventricle) with venous blood only; amphibians have a three-chambered heart (two atria, one ventricle) so oxygenated and deoxygenated blood mix; reptiles have three chambers with a partly divided ventricle (crocodiles have four); birds and mammals have a four-chambered heart (two atria and two ventricles) with complete separation of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood, which supports a high metabolic rate and a warm-blooded condition. ## Unit 9 and Unit 12.
qp 5 | Discuss the distinguishing characters of phylum arthropoda. How will you evaluate the economic importance of class insecta? || فائلم آرتھروپوڈا کی امتیازی خصوصیات پر بحث کریں۔ آپ جماعت انسیکٹا کی معاشی اہمیت کا اندازہ کیسے لگائیں گے؟ || د فایلم ارتروپوډا ځانګړې ځانګړتیاوې وڅېړئ. د انسیکټا ټولګي اقتصادي اهمیت به څنګه ارزوئ؟
sol Model answer: Phylum Arthropoda: jointed appendages; body covered by a chitinous exoskeleton, shed in moulting (ecdysis); segmented body in head, thorax, abdomen; open circulatory system with a haemocoel; striated muscles; compound eyes in many; respiration by gills, book lungs or tracheae; Malpighian tubules or green glands for excretion; bilateral symmetry; the largest phylum. Economic importance of insects: useful: honey bees (honey, wax), silkworm (silk), lac insects, pollinators of crops, predators of pests, food for fish and birds; harmful: pests of crops and stores (locusts, weevils), carriers of disease (mosquito for malaria and dengue, housefly for typhoid and cholera, flea for plague), and destruction of wood (termites) and cloth. ## Unit 9 Arthropoda.
qp 6 | What adaptations are found in birds for aerial life? Describe evolutionary history of birds with special reference to the fossil birds. || پرندوں میں فضائی زندگی کے لیے کون سی موافقتیں پائی جاتی ہیں؟ فوسل پرندوں کے خصوصی حوالے سے پرندوں کی ارتقائی تاریخ بیان کریں۔ || په مرغانو کې د هوايي ژوند لپاره کوم تطابقونه موندل کېږي؟ د فوسیل مرغانو په ځانګړې حواله د مرغانو تکاملي تاریخ بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Adaptations of birds for aerial life: feathers and wings for flight, light bones with air spaces (pneumatic bones), a streamlined body, a horny beak without teeth, a strong keel on the sternum for flight muscles, very efficient lungs with air sacs, a four-chambered heart, a high body temperature (warm-blooded), good vision, and no urinary bladder to reduce weight. Evolution: birds evolved from small theropod reptile ancestors (dinosaurs). The fossil Archaeopteryx (about 150 million years ago, Jurassic) had wings and feathers like birds and teeth, claws and a long bony tail like reptiles; later fossil birds include Confuciusornis and Hesperornis (a toothed diving bird). Modern birds have lost the teeth and have a short tail. ## Unit 9 Aves.
qp 7 | Write short notes on the following. || درج ذیل پر مختصر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || پر لاندې لنډ یادښتونه ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: This question has the three parts (a) Binomial nomenclature, (b) Parazoa and (c) Poikilothermic animals. Each part has its own answer under its own question. ## Heading of a multi-part question.
qp • | a. Binomial nomenclature || الف۔ دو نامی نظامِ نام گذاری || الف. دوه نومي نوم ایښودنه
sol Model answer: Binomial nomenclature is the system of naming each organism with two words, the genus name (capitalised) and the species name (small letter), in Latin, italicised or underlined, introduced by Carolus Linnaeus, e.g. Homo sapiens, Panthera leo. It gives each species a unique world-wide name, avoiding the confusion of local names. ## Unit 9 nomenclature.
qp • | b. Parazoa || ب۔ پیرازوا || ب. پارازوا
sol Model answer: Parazoa is the sub-kingdom of animals that includes the sponges (phylum Porifera). They have a cellular grade of organisation, no true tissues or organs and no definite symmetry; the body has pores (ostia) and a central cavity, and flagellated collar cells (choanocytes) create the water current. ## Unit 9 Porifera.
qp c | Poikilothemic animals || ج۔ بے ثبات حرارت والے جانور (پوئیکیلوتھرمک) || ج. بې ثباته تودوخې څاروي (پوییکیلوترمیک)
sol Model answer: Poikilothermic (cold-blooded) animals cannot keep a constant body temperature; it changes with the surroundings, as in fishes, amphibians and reptiles (and invertebrates). They depend on external heat, are active in warm weather and become inactive (hibernation or aestivation) in extreme cold or heat. Homeothermic (warm-blooded) animals, birds and mammals, keep a constant temperature. ## Unit 9 body temperature.

sol Model answer: Presentation task.
