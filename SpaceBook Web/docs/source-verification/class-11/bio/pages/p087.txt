## iii. Regeneration of RuBP || iii. RuBP کی تجدید || iii. د RuBP بیا جوړېدنه
p In this stage RuBP molecules are regenerated so as to continue the cycle. The PGAL molecules formed in the reduction stage have many alternatives. Out of every six molecules of PGAL formed, only one molecule leaves the cycle to be used by the plant for making glucose and other organic compounds.
