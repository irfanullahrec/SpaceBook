qp 1 | Define the following: (a) condensation (b) hydrolysis || درج ذیل کی تعریف کریں: (الف) تکثیف (ب) آبی تحلیل || لاندې تعریف کړئ: (الف) تراکم (ب) هایډرولایسس
sol Model answer: (a) Condensation (dehydration synthesis) is a reaction in which two small molecules join to form a larger molecule with the removal of a water molecule, for example two glucose molecules joined to form maltose. (b) Hydrolysis is the breaking of a larger molecule into smaller ones by adding water, for example maltose + water gives two glucose molecules; enzymes catalyse both. ## Unit 2 macromolecules.
qp 2 | What are different kinds of carbohydrates? Give two example of each. || کاربوہائیڈریٹس کی مختلف اقسام کیا ہیں؟ ہر ایک کی دو مثالیں دیں۔ || د کاربوهایډریټونو بېلابېل ډولونه کوم دي؟ د هر یوه دوه بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Carbohydrates have the general formula (CH2O)n. Kinds: (1) Monosaccharides, single sugars: glucose and fructose (also galactose, ribose). (2) Disaccharides, two sugars joined by a glycosidic bond: sucrose and maltose (also lactose). (3) Oligosaccharides, 3-10 sugars: raffinose and stachyose. (4) Polysaccharides, many sugars: starch and cellulose (also glycogen, chitin). ## Unit 2 carbohydrates.
qp 3 | Compare the isomers and stereoisomers of glucose. || گلوکوز کے آئسومرز اور سٹیریو آئسومرز کا موازنہ کریں۔ || د ګلوکوز آیسومرونه او سټیریوآیسومرونه پرتله کړئ.
sol Model answer: Isomers are compounds with the same molecular formula but different structural formulas; glucose, fructose and galactose (C6H12O6) are structural isomers (glucose is an aldehyde sugar, fructose a ketone sugar, galactose differs in the position of the OH on carbon 4). Stereoisomers have the same structure but a different arrangement of atoms in space: D- and L-glucose are mirror images (optical isomers), and alpha and beta glucose differ in the position of the OH group on carbon 1 of the ring (below the ring in alpha, above in beta). ## Unit 2 isomers of glucose.
qp 4 | Give the chemical nature of the glycosidic bond. || گلائیکوسیڈک بانڈ کی کیمیائی نوعیت بیان کریں۔ || د ګلایکوسیډیک تړون کیمیاوي طبیعت ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: The glycosidic bond is a covalent bond (C-O-C) that joins two monosaccharides: it forms between the hydroxyl group on carbon 1 of one sugar and the hydroxyl group on a carbon (usually 4 or 6) of another, with a water molecule removed (condensation). It is broken by hydrolysis. Examples are alpha 1-4 in maltose and starch, beta 1-4 in cellulose. ## Unit 2 glycosidic bond.
qp 5 | How dehydration-synthesis and hydrolysis reactions are used for the making and breaking of macromolecule polymers. || پانی کے اخراج سے تالیف اور آبی تحلیل کے تعاملات ماکرو مالیکیول پولیمرز کی تشکیل اور ٹوٹ پھوٹ کے لیے کیسے استعمال ہوتے ہیں۔ || د اوبو د ایستلو له لارې جوړونه او هایډرولایسس تعاملات د ماکرومالیکول پولیمرونو د جوړېدو او ماتېدو لپاره څنګه کارول کېږي.
sol Model answer: In dehydration synthesis (condensation), monomers are joined to make a polymer; each joining removes a water molecule (the OH from one monomer and H from the other), e.g. glucose units to starch, amino acids to protein. In hydrolysis, water is added across the bond between monomers, which breaks the polymer into monomers (digestion of starch to glucose, protein to amino acids), and enzymes speed both reactions. ## Unit 2 macromolecules.
qp 6 | Outline the synthesis and breakage of peptide linkages. || پیپٹائیڈ روابط کی تالیف اور ٹوٹنے کا خاکہ بیان کریں۔ || د پیپټایډ تړونو د جوړېدو او ماتېدو لیک ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: A peptide linkage (CO-NH bond) is formed by condensation between the carboxyl group (-COOH) of one amino acid and the amino group (-NH2) of the next, releasing a molecule of water; repeating gives a dipeptide, tripeptide and then a polypeptide. It is broken by hydrolysis: water is added across the bond and enzymes (peptidases) or acid separate the amino acids again. ## Unit 2 proteins.
qp 7 | Evaluate the role of steroids in human body. || انسانی جسم میں سٹیرائیڈز کے کردار کا جائزہ لیں۔ || د انسان په بدن کې د سټیرایډونو رول ارزوئ.
sol Model answer: Steroids are lipids with a four-ring carbon skeleton. Their roles in the human body: cholesterol is a part of cell membranes and the raw material for other steroids; sex hormones (testosterone, oestrogen, progesterone) control sexual development and reproduction; cortisol and aldosterone (adrenal hormones) control metabolism, stress responses and salt balance; bile salts help digest fats; vitamin D (a steroid) helps calcium absorption. Too much cholesterol, however, can build up in the arteries. ## Unit 2 lipids.
qp 8 | Illustrate the formation of phosphodiester bond. || فاسفو ڈائی ایسٹر بانڈ کی تشکیل کی مثال سے وضاحت کریں۔ || د فاسفو ډای ایسټر تړون جوړېدنه انځور کړئ.
sol Model answer: A phosphodiester bond joins nucleotides in nucleic acids. The phosphate group on carbon 5 of one nucleotide's pentose sugar forms an ester bond with the hydroxyl (-OH) on carbon 3 of the sugar of the next nucleotide, so the phosphate links the two sugars by two ester bonds (3'-5' linkage), with the loss of a water molecule. Repeating this builds the sugar-phosphate backbone of DNA and RNA. ## Unit 2 nucleic acids.
qp 9 | List some examples of structural proteins. || ساختی پروٹینوں کی کچھ مثالوں کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د جوړښتي پروټینونو څو بېلګې لړلیک کړئ.
sol Model answer: Examples of structural proteins: keratin (hair, nails, skin), collagen (tendons, bone, cartilage, skin), elastin (elastic tissue), fibroin (silk), actin and myosin (muscle), tubulin (microtubules) and proteins of cell membranes. ## Unit 2 proteins.
