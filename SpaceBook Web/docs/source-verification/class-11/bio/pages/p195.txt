## C. Answer the following questions in detail. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے جوابات تفصیل سے دیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو ځوابونه په تفصیل سره ورکړئ.
qp 1 | Describe the adaptations shown by bryophytes to land life. || برائیوفائٹس کی خشکی کی زندگی کے لیے موافقتیں بیان کریں۔ || د وچې ژوند ته د بریوفایټونو تطابقونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Adaptations of bryophytes to land life: a waxy cuticle on the aerial parts (in some) reduces water loss; rhizoids anchor the plant and absorb water and minerals; the sporophyte has stomata in mosses; sex organs have a jacket of sterile cells that stops drying; the zygote develops into an embryo inside the archegonium (protected, nourished); spores are covered by a tough wall (sporopollenin) and are spread by wind. They still need water for the sperms to swim, so they live in damp places. ## Unit 8 bryophytes.
qp 2 | Explain the life cycle of flowering plant. || پھول دار پودے کے زندگی چکر کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د ګل لرونکي بوټي د ژوند دوره تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Life cycle of a flowering plant: the sporophyte (the plant) bears flowers. In the anther, pollen mother cells divide by meiosis to form haploid microspores, which become pollen grains (male gametophyte). In the ovule, the megaspore mother cell divides by meiosis; one megaspore survives and by three mitotic divisions forms the embryo sac with eight nuclei (female gametophyte, including the egg). Pollination brings the pollen to the stigma; the pollen tube grows to the ovule. Double fertilisation: one sperm fuses with the egg (zygote, 2n) and the other with the two polar nuclei (endosperm, 3n). The zygote grows to an embryo and the ovule becomes a seed; the ovary becomes a fruit; the seed germinates into a new plant. ## Unit 8 angiosperms.
qp 3 | Write detailed note on any two of the following. || درج ذیل میں سے کسی دو پر تفصیلی نوٹ لکھیں۔ || د لاندې څخه پر هر دوو تفصیلي یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: This question asks for notes on any two of (a) Psilopsida, (b) Sphenopsida, (c) Lycopsida. Each of these has its own answer below; any two may be written. ## Heading of a multi-part question.
qp • | a. Psilopsida || الف۔ سائلوپسڈا || الف. سایلوپسیډا
sol Model answer: Psilopsida (psilophytes): the most primitive vascular plants, with a simple sporophyte: no true roots or leaves, only rhizoids and a branching leafless stem (dichotomous branching) with xylem and phloem; sporangia at the tips of branches; homosporous. Examples: Rhynia (extinct), Psilotum, Tmesipteris (living fossils). ## Unit 8 Psilopsida.
qp • | b. Sphenopsida || ب۔ اسفینوپسڈا || ب. سفینوپسیډا
sol Model answer: Sphenopsida (horsetails): the sporophyte has an underground rhizome and upright jointed stems with ribs and silica; whorls of small scale-like leaves at the nodes; sporangia on special cone-like strobili (sporangiophores); homosporous; sperms are spirally flagellate. The only living genus is Equisetum (horsetail), with fossil giant forms such as Calamites. ## Unit 8 Sphenopsida.
qp c | Lycopsida || ج۔ لائکوپسڈا || ج. لایکوپسیډا
sol Model answer: Lycopsida (club mosses): sporophyte with true roots, stems and small microphyllous leaves with a single vein; sporangia on the leaves, in cones (strobili) in many; some are homosporous (Lycopodium) and some heterosporous (Selaginella); gametophytes are small. Examples: Lycopodium, Selaginella; fossil Lepidodendron (giant trees of the coal age). ## Unit 8 Lycopsida.
qp 4 | Give important characters of liverworts and hornworts. || لیور ورٹس اور ہارن ورٹس کی اہم خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ || د لیور ورټونو او هارن ورټونو مهمې ځانګړتیاوې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Liverworts (Hepaticopsida): flat, ribbon-like, dichotomously branched thallus (e.g. Marchantia), dorsiventral; rhizoids on the lower surface; gemma cups in some for asexual reproduction; sporophyte has a foot, a short seta and a capsule with spores and elaters. Hornworts (Anthocerotopsida): small flat thallus with one large chloroplast per cell and pyrenoids; the sporophyte is a long, horn-like capsule that grows from its base continuously (e.g. Anthoceros). ## Unit 8 bryophytes.
## Projects || منصوبے || پروژې
qp • | Teacher may plan a trip to nearby area with natural vegetation and ask his/her students to collect plants in the following manner. || استاد قریبی علاقے کی سیر کا منصوبہ بنا سکتا ہے جہاں قدرتی نباتات ہوں اور اپنے طلبہ سے درج ذیل طریقے سے پودے جمع کرنے کو کہہ سکتا ہے۔ || ښوونکی کولای شي د طبیعي نباتاتو سره نږدې سیمې ته سفر پلان کړي او خپلو زده کوونکو ته ووايي چې په لاندې ډول بوټي راټول کړي.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. On the field trip, collect plant specimens (leaves, flowers, whole small plants) with a trowel and secateurs, press them between newspaper sheets, label each with its name, date and place, and sort them into the groups listed in the next four parts. ## Practical work.
qp 1 | Two examples of bryophytes. || برائیوفائٹس کی دو مثالیں۔ || د بریوفایټونو دوه بېلګې.
sol Model answer: Two examples of bryophytes: Marchantia (a liverwort) and Funaria (a moss); also Riccia and Anthoceros. ## Examples.
qp 2 | Two examples of fern. || فرن کی دو مثالیں۔ || د فرن دوه بېلګې.
sol Model answer: Two examples of ferns: Dryopteris (male fern) and Pteris; also Adiantum (maidenhair fern) and Marsilea. ## Examples.
qp 3 | Five examples of angiospermic plants. || اینجیوسپرمک پودوں کی پانچ مثالیں۔ || د انجیوسپرمیک بوټو پنځه بېلګې.
sol Model answer: Five examples of angiosperms: wheat (a monocot), maize (monocot), rose, mango, and mustard or gram (dicots); also sunflower, cotton, bean. ## Examples.
qp 4 | Three examples of gymnosperms || جمنوسپرمز کی تین مثالیں || د جمنوسپرمونو درې بېلګې
sol Model answer: Three examples of gymnosperms: Pinus (pine), Cedrus (deodar) and Cycas; also Ephedra, Ginkgo and Thuja. ## Examples.
qp • | Take guidance from the teacher for proper preservation of plants. || پودوں کے مناسب تحفظ کے لیے استاد سے رہنمائی لیں۔ || د بوټو د سم ساتلو لپاره له ښوونکي څخه لارښوونه واخلئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Preservation of plants: press the specimen flat between blotting paper or newspaper under a weight for some days, changing the paper daily until dry; mount it on a herbarium sheet with gum or tape; add a label (scientific name, family, place, date, collector); a treatment with mercuric chloride or a naphthalene ball is used under the teacher's guidance to protect it from insects and fungi. ## Practical work.
qp • | Make a medicinal plant collection present in your local environment. Paste them on cards and write their taxonomic classification for every collected plant. Take guidance from your teacher for identification and classification of your plant collection. || اپنے مقامی ماحول میں موجود طبی پودوں کا ذخیرہ بنائیں۔ انہیں کارڈز پر چپکائیں اور ہر جمع کردہ پودے کی درجہ بندی (ٹیکسونومک) درجہ بندی لکھیں۔ اپنے پودوں کے ذخیرے کی شناخت اور درجہ بندی کے لیے اپنے استاد سے رہنمائی لیں۔ || په خپل سیمه ییز چاپېریال کې شته د درملیزو بوټو ټولګه جوړه کړئ. دوی پر کارتونو ونښلوئ او د هر راټول شوي بوټي طبقه بندي ولیکئ. د خپلو بوټو ټولګې د پېژندنې او طبقه بندۍ لپاره له خپل ښوونکي څخه لارښوونه واخلئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Medicinal plant collection (examples that grow in Pakistan; the classification follows the teacher's help): Neem (Azadirachta indica, family Meliaceae) for skin diseases; Aloe vera (Aloe barbadensis, Asphodelaceae) for burns; Mint (Mentha, Lamiaceae) for digestion; Ephedra (Ephedra gerardiana, Ephedraceae) for asthma; Tulsi (Ocimum, Lamiaceae) for cough. Press each, paste on a card, and write Kingdom Plantae, division, class, order, family, genus and species. ## Practical work.
