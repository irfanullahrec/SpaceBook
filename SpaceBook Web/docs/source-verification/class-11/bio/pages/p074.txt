## B. Write short answers to the following questions. || ب۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیں۔ || ب. د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | What is a cofactor? Give examples. || ہم عامل (کو فیکٹر) کیا ہے؟ مثالیں دیں۔ || همفکټور څه دی؟ بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: A cofactor is a non-protein part (organic or inorganic) that an enzyme needs in order to work; the protein part alone is an apoenzyme, and apoenzyme + cofactor = holoenzyme. Kinds: metal ion activators (Mg2+, Zn2+, Fe2+, Cu2+, Ca2+), coenzymes (organic, loosely attached; NAD, FAD, coenzyme A) and prosthetic groups (organic, tightly attached; haem in catalase). ## Unit 3 cofactors.
qp 2 | What are metal activators? Give three examples. || دھاتی محرک (میٹل ایکٹیویٹرز) کیا ہیں؟ تین مثالیں دیں۔ || فلزي فعالوونکي (میټل اکټیویټرونه) څه دي؟ درې بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Metal activators are metal ions that bind to an enzyme or to its substrate and make the enzyme active or more effective. Three examples: magnesium (Mg2+) activates enzymes of ATP reactions (kinases); zinc (Zn2+) is needed by carbonic anhydrase and alcohol dehydrogenase; calcium (Ca2+) activates enzymes such as lipase and in blood clotting (also Fe2+ in catalase, Cu2+ in cytochrome oxidase). ## Unit 3 cofactors.
qp 3 | Differentiate the key difference between the Lock and Key Model and Induced Fit Hypothesis/model? || تالا اور چابی ماڈل اور انڈیوسڈ فٹ مفروضے/ماڈل کے درمیان بنیادی فرق واضح کریں۔ || د قفل او کیلي ماډل او هڅول شوي سمون (انډیوسډ فټ) فرضیې/ماډل ترمنځ اصلي توپیر وښایاست.
sol Model answer: In the Lock and Key Model (Emil Fischer) the active site has a fixed shape that exactly matches only one substrate, like a key in a lock; the enzyme is rigid. In the Induced Fit Hypothesis (Koshland) the active site is not exactly fitted at first; when the substrate binds, the enzyme changes shape to fit it closely, so it is flexible; this explains why an enzyme can act on a group of related substrates. ## Unit 3 mode of action.
qp 4 | How pH of a cell affects the enzyme activity? || خلیے کا pH خامرے کی سرگرمی کو کیسے متاثر کرتا ہے؟ || د حجرې pH د انزایم فعالیت څنګه اغېزمنوي؟
sol Model answer: Every enzyme has an optimum pH at which it works best (pepsin about 1.5-2, trypsin about 8, salivary amylase about 6.8). A change of pH changes the charges on the amino acids at the active site and the bonds that hold the protein's shape; the active site loses its shape, substrate cannot bind, and the rate falls. An extreme pH denatures the enzyme permanently. The cell therefore keeps its pH nearly constant with buffers. ## Unit 3 factors affecting enzyme action.
## C. Write detailed answers to the following questions. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات لکھیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Describe the characteristics of enzymes. || خامروں کی خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ || د انزایمونو ځانګړتیاوې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Characteristics of enzymes: (1) They are globular proteins. (2) They are biological catalysts: they speed up reactions by lowering the activation energy. (3) They are needed in very small quantity and are not used up. (4) They are specific: one enzyme acts on one substrate or a group of related substrates. (5) They work at an optimum temperature and pH and are denatured by heat. (6) They can work in both directions of a reversible reaction. (7) Their activity is controlled by inhibitors and activators. (8) They need cofactors or coenzymes in some cases. ## Unit 3 enzymes.
qp 2 | Explain the process of enzyme inhibition. Make a list of some common enzymes inhibitors. || خامرے کی روک (انہیبیشن) کے عمل کی وضاحت کریں۔ کچھ عام خامروی روکنے والوں کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د انزایم د ځنډونې بهیر تشریح کړئ. د ځینو عامو انزایمي ځنډونکو لړلیک جوړ کړئ.
sol Model answer: Enzyme inhibition is a decrease in enzyme activity caused by a molecule (inhibitor). Competitive inhibitors look like the substrate, compete for the active site and block it; more substrate can overcome them (malonate on succinate dehydrogenase). Non-competitive inhibitors bind to another site (allosteric site), change the shape of the active site and reduce the activity; extra substrate cannot overcome them (cyanide on cytochrome oxidase, heavy metals Hg and Pb). Inhibition may be reversible or irreversible. Common inhibitors: cyanide, malonate, heavy metals (mercury, lead), penicillin, nerve gases and some drugs and poisons. ## Unit 3 enzyme inhibition.
qp 3 | Write briefly the mode of action of an enzyme. || خامرے کے عمل کا طریقہ مختصراً لکھیں۔ || د انزایم د عمل بڼه په لنډه توګه ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Mode of action: (1) The substrate collides with the active site of the enzyme and binds, forming an enzyme-substrate complex. (2) The enzyme strains the substrate bonds (induced fit) and lowers the activation energy. (3) The reaction takes place and the substrate changes to products. (4) The products leave the active site and the free enzyme is ready to combine with another substrate molecule. E + S → ES → E + P. ## Unit 3 mode of action.
qp 4 | How do the enzyme and substrate concentrations affect the rate of enzyme action? || خامرے اور سبسٹریٹ کے ارتکاز خامرے کے عمل کی شرح کو کیسے متاثر کرتے ہیں؟ || د انزایم او سبسټریټ غلظتونه د انزایم د عمل په کچه څنګه اغېز کوي؟
sol Model answer: Enzyme concentration: with plenty of substrate, the rate of reaction is directly proportional to the enzyme concentration (more active sites). Substrate concentration: at low substrate the rate rises with substrate concentration; at high concentration all the active sites are occupied (saturation), so the rate levels off at the maximum (Vmax) unless more enzyme is added. ## Unit 3 factors affecting enzyme action.

qp • | List down some common venoms which can act as enzyme inhibitors. || کچھ عام زہر (وینوم) کی فہرست بنائیں جو خامروی روکنے والوں کے طور پر کام کر سکتے ہیں۔ || ځینې عام زهرجن (وینوم) لړلیک کړئ چې د انزایمي ځنډونکو په توګه کار کولای شي.
sol Model answer: Venoms that can act as enzyme inhibitors: snake venoms (neurotoxins such as those of cobra that block acetylcholine receptors; some inhibit acetylcholinesterase, e.g. fasciculin of mamba), scorpion venom, and bee venom (melittin and phospholipase A2).
