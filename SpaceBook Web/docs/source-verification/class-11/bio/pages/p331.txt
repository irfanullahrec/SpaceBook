## B. Write short answers to the following questions. || ب۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیں۔ || ب. د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | How the interstitial fluid is formed? || بین الخلوی سیال کیسے بنتا ہے؟ || د حجرو منځنۍ مایع څنګه جوړېږي؟
sol Model answer: Interstitial (tissue) fluid is formed from blood plasma. At the arterial end of capillaries, the blood pressure is higher than the osmotic pressure, so water and dissolved substances (glucose, amino acids, salts, oxygen) filter out through the thin capillary walls into the spaces between cells, but red blood cells and large proteins stay in. At the venous end most of the fluid returns by osmosis and the rest goes into the lymph vessels. ## Unit 12 tissue fluid.
qp 2 | Why the normal value of BP increases in old age? Explain your answer. || بڑھاپے میں بی پی کی عام قدر کیوں بڑھ جاتی ہے؟ اپنا جواب واضح کریں۔ || په زړښت کې د BP نورمال ارزښت ولې زیاتېږي؟ خپل ځواب تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Blood pressure rises with age because the arteries lose their elasticity: their walls become thick and stiff (arteriosclerosis) and may be narrowed by fatty deposits (atherosclerosis), so the resistance to the flow of blood is higher and the heart must pump harder. Also the lack of exercise, more weight and stress are more common with age. ## Unit 12 blood pressure.
qp 3 | If the baroreceptors are removed from an artery what would be the effect ? || اگر شریان سے بیرورسیپٹرز ہٹا دیے جائیں تو کیا اثر ہو گا؟ || که له شریان څخه بارورسپټرونه لرې شي نو اغېزه به څه وي؟
sol Model answer: Baroreceptors are pressure sensors in the walls of the carotid sinus and aortic arch. If they are removed, the brain would not get information on the blood pressure, so the reflex control (slowing the heart and widening arteries when pressure is high) would not work. Blood pressure would not be regulated and would vary widely, usually remaining high (hypertension) with big swings during changes of posture. ## Unit 12 control of BP.
qp 4 | What changes occur in BP and cardiac out put during a strenuous exercise? || سخت ورزش کے دوران بی پی اور قلبی اخراج میں کیا تبدیلیاں آتی ہیں؟ || د سخت تمرین په مهال په BP او زړه وتنه کې کوم بدلونونه رامنځته کېږي؟
sol Model answer: During strenuous exercise the heart rate and the stroke volume increase, so the cardiac output rises (from about 5 litres to 20-25 litres per minute) to give muscles more oxygen and remove carbon dioxide and heat. The systolic blood pressure rises (for example from 120 to 160 mm Hg or more), while the diastolic pressure stays nearly the same or falls a little because the arterioles in muscles dilate. ## Unit 12 effects of exercise.
qp 5 | If we don't take water the whole day in the month of June, what would be the effect on volume of lymph? || اگر ہم جون کے مہینے میں پورا دن پانی نہ پئیں تو لمف کے حجم پر کیا اثر ہو گا؟ || که موږ د جون په میاشت کې ټوله ورځ اوبه ونه څښو نو د لمف په حجم به څه اغېزه وي؟
sol Model answer: If no water is taken for a whole day in June (hot weather), the body loses water by sweating and urine, the blood plasma becomes more concentrated and its volume falls, and less fluid leaves the capillaries to form tissue fluid. As a result, the volume of lymph falls and it becomes thicker, and the person gets dehydrated. ## Unit 12 lymph.

qp 2 | Name major arteries and veins and discuss the organs to which they target. || بڑی شریانوں اور وریدوں کے نام بتائیں اور ان اعضا پر بحث کریں جن کی طرف وہ جاتی ہیں۔ || لوی شریانونه او وریدونه ونوموئ او هغه غړي وڅېړئ چې دوی ورته ځي.
sol Model answer: Major arteries: the aorta (the largest, from the left ventricle); the coronary arteries (heart muscle); the carotid arteries (head and brain); the subclavian arteries (arms); the hepatic artery (liver); the mesenteric arteries (intestines); the renal arteries (kidneys); the iliac arteries (pelvis and legs, from which the femoral artery supplies the thigh); the pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood to the lungs. Major veins: the superior vena cava (head, neck and arms) and inferior vena cava (trunk and legs) bring blood to the right atrium; the jugular veins (head); the subclavian veins (arms); the hepatic portal vein (intestine to liver); the hepatic veins (liver); the renal veins (kidneys); the femoral and iliac veins (legs); the pulmonary veins bring oxygenated blood from the lungs. ## Unit 12 blood vessels.
qp 3 | Define blood pressure and explain its periods of systolic and diastolic pressure. || بلڈ پریشر کی تعریف کریں اور اس کے سسٹولک اور ڈائیسٹولک دباؤ کے ادوار کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د وینې فشار تعریف کړئ او د سیستولیک او ډایاسټولیک فشار دورې یې تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer: Blood pressure is the force exerted by the blood on the walls of the arteries. It is measured in mm Hg with a sphygmomanometer. Systolic pressure (about 120 mm Hg in a healthy adult) is the highest pressure when the ventricles contract and push blood into the aorta. Diastolic pressure (about 80 mm Hg) is the lowest pressure when the ventricles relax and fill with blood. A normal BP is 120/80 mm Hg. ## Unit 12 blood pressure.
