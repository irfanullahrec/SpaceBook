## B. Write short answers to the following questions || ب۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیں || ب. د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ
qp 1 | Define glycolysis. || گلائیکولائسس کی تعریف کریں۔ || ګلایکولایسس تعریف کړئ.
sol Model answer: Glycolysis (glyco = sugar, lysis = splitting) is the first stage of respiration, in which one glucose molecule (6C) is broken down in the cytoplasm, without oxygen, into two molecules of pyruvic acid (3C), with a net gain of 2 ATP and 2 NADH. ## Unit 4 respiration.
qp 2 | What is photorespiration? || ضیائی تنفس کیا ہے؟ || فوټوتنفس څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Photorespiration is a wasteful process in C3 plants in bright light and high temperature: the enzyme RuBisCO takes up oxygen instead of CO2 and RuBP is partly oxidised to give phosphoglycolate, which passes through chloroplasts, peroxisomes and mitochondria and releases CO2. It makes no ATP and wastes energy and fixed carbon, so it lowers the yield of photosynthesis. ## Unit 4 photorespiration.
qp 3 | What do you mean by chemiomosis? || کیمیو اسموسس سے آپ کی کیا مراد ہے؟ || کیمیواسموسس څخه ستاسو موخه څه ده؟
sol Model answer: Chemiosmosis is the movement of protons (H+) across a membrane through ATP synthase, driven by the proton gradient (the proton motive force), which makes ATP from ADP and phosphate. It occurs across the thylakoid membrane in chloroplasts (photophosphorylation) and across the inner mitochondrial membrane (oxidative phosphorylation); it was explained by Peter Mitchell. ## Unit 4 ATP formation.
qp 4 | State the role of carbondioxide as one of the raw materials of photosynthesis. || ضیائی تالیف کے خام مال میں سے ایک کے طور پر کاربن ڈائی آکسائیڈ کا کردار بیان کریں۔ || د فوتوسنتېز د خام موادو څخه د یوه په توګه د کاربن ډای اکسایډ رول وښیاست.
sol Model answer: Carbon dioxide is the source of carbon for photosynthesis: in the Calvin cycle it is fixed by RuBP (with RuBisCO) and reduced to carbohydrate using ATP and NADPH. Its concentration is a limiting factor: the rate of photosynthesis rises with the CO2 level, up to the point where another factor limits it. The air has only about 0.04 per cent CO2. ## Unit 4 photosynthesis.
qp 5 | Differentiate between the absorption spectra of different photosynthetic pigments. || مختلف ضیائی تالیفی رنگدانوں کے جذبی طیف میں فرق کریں۔ || د بېلابېلو فوتوسنتېزي رنګدانو د جذب سپکټرو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Chlorophyll a absorbs mostly blue-violet (about 430-450 nm) and red (about 660-680 nm); chlorophyll b absorbs blue (about 450-470 nm) and orange-red (about 640-650 nm) and passes the energy to chlorophyll a. Carotenoids (carotene, xanthophyll) absorb blue-green (about 450-500 nm) and protect chlorophyll. Green light (500-600 nm) is mostly reflected, so leaves look green. The graph of absorption against wavelength is the absorption spectrum; the graph of photosynthesis rate against wavelength is the action spectrum. ## Unit 4 photosynthetic pigments.
qp 6 | What is the main difference between cyclic and non cyclic photo… || چکری اور غیر چکری فوٹو … کے درمیان بنیادی فرق کیا ہے … || د حلقوي او غیر حلقوي فوټو … ترمنځ اصلي توپیر څه دی …
sol Model answer: The question is cut off in the book data; it is answered as "cyclic and non-cyclic photophosphorylation". In the cyclic pathway only photosystem I works: the excited electrons go round through carriers and return to the same chlorophyll, only ATP is made, no NADPH and no oxygen. In the non-cyclic pathway both photosystems II and I work: electrons pass from water to NADP+ and do not return, so ATP and NADPH are made, water is split (photolysis) and oxygen is released. ## Unit 4 light reactions. ## C. Write in detail the answers of the following questions. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے جوابات تفصیل سے لکھیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو ځوابونه په تفصیل سره ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Describe the role of sunlight in the process of photosynthesis. || ضیائی تالیف کے عمل میں سورج کی روشنی کا کردار بیان کریں۔ || د فوتوسنتېز په بهیر کې د لمر د رڼا رول بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Sunlight is the source of energy for photosynthesis. Chlorophyll and other pigments absorb photons, which excite electrons in photosystems II and I; this energy is used to split water (photolysis), make ATP and NADPH (light reactions), which are then used to reduce CO2 to sugar. The rate depends on light intensity (up to saturation), colour (red and blue are best) and duration. Without light there are no light reactions, and the dark reactions stop soon after. ## Unit 4 photosynthesis.
qp 2 | Give an account of the events of non-cyclic electron pathway. || غیر چکری الیکٹران راستے کے واقعات کا بیان دیں۔ || د غیر حلقوي الکترون لارې د پېښو شرح ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Non-cyclic electron pathway: (1) Photosystem II absorbs light; its excited electrons leave P680 and are taken by a primary acceptor. (2) Water is split (photolysis) to replace the lost electrons, giving H+ and oxygen. (3) Electrons pass down an electron transport chain (plastoquinone, cytochromes, plastocyanin) and the energy released pumps protons into the thylakoid space; ATP is made by chemiosmosis. (4) Photosystem I absorbs light and its excited electrons (from P700) are passed through ferredoxin. (5) NADP+ reductase gives them to NADP+, which takes H+ and becomes NADPH. Products: ATP, NADPH and O2. ## Unit 4 light reactions.
qp 3 | Write down the main steps of Calvin cycle. || کیلون چکر کے اہم مراحل لکھیں۔ || د کالوین دورې اصلي ګامونه ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: The Calvin cycle (C3 cycle) in the stroma has three stages: (1) Carbon fixation: CO2 combines with ribulose bisphosphate (RuBP, 5C) with RuBisCO to give an unstable 6C compound that splits into 2 molecules of phosphoglyceric acid (PGA, 3C). (2) Reduction: PGA is reduced to phosphoglyceraldehyde (PGAL) using ATP and NADPH. (3) Regeneration: most PGAL is rearranged using ATP to make RuBP again; some PGAL leaves to make glucose and other organic compounds. Six turns fix six CO2 and make one glucose. ## Unit 4 dark reactions.
qp 4 | Write a detailed note on the various steps of Kreb's cycle. || کریبس چکر کے مختلف مراحل پر تفصیلی نوٹ لکھیں۔ || د کربس دورې په بېلابېلو ګامونو یوه مفصله یادداښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: The Krebs cycle (citric acid cycle) takes place in the mitochondrial matrix. Pyruvate is first oxidised to acetyl-CoA (with NADH and CO2). Steps: (1) Acetyl-CoA (2C) joins oxaloacetate (4C) to form citrate (6C). (2) Citrate is changed to isocitrate. (3) Isocitrate is oxidised and loses CO2 to form alpha-ketoglutarate (5C) with NADH. (4) Alpha-ketoglutarate loses CO2 to form succinyl-CoA with NADH. (5) Succinyl-CoA gives succinate with one ATP (substrate-level phosphorylation). (6) Succinate is oxidised to fumarate (FADH2). (7) Fumarate is changed to malate. (8) Malate is oxidised to oxaloacetate (NADH), and the cycle repeats. Per turn: 3 NADH, 1 FADH2, 1 ATP and 2 CO2. ## Unit 4 Krebs cycle.
qp 5 | What is photorespiration and what are its disadvantages? || ضیائی تنفس کیا ہے اور اس کے نقصانات کیا ہیں؟ || فوټوتنفس څه دی او زیانونه یې کوم دي؟
sol Model answer: Photorespiration is the light-dependent uptake of oxygen and release of CO2 in green cells of C3 plants, when RuBisCO binds O2 instead of CO2 (oxygenase activity), most in hot, dry and bright conditions. Disadvantages: it wastes the energy already trapped, produces no ATP or sugar, lowers the net photosynthesis by up to 25-30 per cent and so reduces crop yield. C4 plants avoid it. ## Unit 4 photorespiration.

qp • | Illustrate the net energy output during glycolysis, oxidation of pyruvate and Krebs cycle. || گلائیکولائسس، پائروویٹ کی تکسید اور کریبس چکر کے دوران خالص توانائی کی پیداوار کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د ګلایکولایسس، د پایروویټ د اکسیدېشن او د کربس دورې پر مهال خالصه انرژي تولید انځور کړئ.
sol Model answer: Net energy output per glucose: Glycolysis: 2 ATP and 2 NADH (the NADH gives 4-6 ATP in the electron transport chain). Oxidation of pyruvate (link reaction) for two pyruvates: 2 NADH (about 6 ATP). Krebs cycle for two turns: 2 ATP, 6 NADH (about 18 ATP) and 2 FADH2 (about 4 ATP). The total yield is about 30 to 38 ATP per glucose in aerobic respiration (older books give 38); anaerobic respiration gives only 2 ATP. ## Table of energy; the totals differ slightly between books.
