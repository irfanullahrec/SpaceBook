## B. Write answers to the following short questions. || ب۔ درج ذیل مختصر سوالات کے جوابات لکھیں۔ || ب. د لاندې لنډو پوښتنو ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Define alternation of generation and give its importance. || نسلوں کے تبادل کی تعریف کریں اور اس کی اہمیت بیان کریں۔ || د نسلونو د تناوب تعریف وکړئ او د هغې اهمیت ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Alternation of generation is the regular alternation of two different phases in the life cycle of a plant: a haploid gametophyte (makes gametes by mitosis) and a diploid sporophyte (makes haploid spores by meiosis). Importance: it combines sexual reproduction (variation by meiosis and fertilisation) with spore formation (large numbers of spores for dispersal), and shows evolution from gametophyte-dominant bryophytes to sporophyte-dominant seed plants. ## Unit 8 life cycles.
qp 2 | What is the role of heterospory in the evolution of seed? || بیج کے ارتقا میں ہیٹروسپوری کا کیا کردار ہے؟ || د تخم په تکامل کې د هیټروسپوري رول څه دی؟
sol Model answer: Heterospory is the formation of two kinds of spores, small microspores (male) and large megaspores (female). The megaspore is kept and germinates inside the megasporangium on the parent sporophyte, the female gametophyte is protected and nourished, and the zygote grows into an embryo within it; with an added integument this structure became the ovule and then the seed. So heterospory was a key step in the evolution of the seed habit. ## Unit 8 evolution of seed.
qp 3 | Differentiate between an antheridium and an archegonium. || اینتھیریڈیم اور آرکیگونیم میں فرق کریں۔ || د انتیریډیم او ارکیګونیم ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: The antheridium is the male sex organ of bryophytes and pteridophytes: club-shaped or spherical with a wall of sterile cells, making many motile sperms (antherozoids). The archegonium is the female sex organ: flask-shaped with a swollen venter containing one egg and a neck with canal cells, through which the sperms swim to fertilise the egg. ## Unit 8 reproduction.
qp 4 | Give two differences between bryophytes and tracheophytes. || برائیوفائٹس اور ٹریکیوفائٹس کے درمیان دو فرق بیان کریں۔ || د بریوفایټونو او تراکیوفایټونو ترمنځ دوه توپیرونه ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: (1) Bryophytes have no vascular tissue (no xylem or phloem) while tracheophytes have xylem and phloem. (2) In bryophytes the gametophyte is the dominant, independent plant and the sporophyte depends on it; in tracheophytes the sporophyte is dominant and independent. (Also: bryophytes have no true roots, stems or leaves; they need water for fertilisation and stay small.) ## Unit 8 groups of plants.
qp 5 | Write three examples of sub phylum psilopsida. || ذیلی فائلم سائلوپسڈا کی تین مثالیں لکھیں۔ || د فرعي فایلم سایلوپسیډا درې بېلګې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Three examples of the sub-phylum Psilopsida: Rhynia (an extinct fossil plant), Psilotum (whisk fern) and Tmesipteris. ## Unit 8 Psilopsida.
qp 6 | Write four differences between angiosperms and gymnosperms. || اینجیوسپرمز اور جمنوسپرمز کے درمیان چار فرق لکھیں۔ || د انجیوسپرمونو او جمنوسپرمونو ترمنځ څلور توپیرونه ولیکئ.
sol Model answer: Angiosperms: seeds enclosed in a fruit developed from the ovary; flowers are present; xylem has vessels and phloem has companion cells; double fertilisation gives triploid endosperm; leaves broad. Gymnosperms: seeds naked on cone scales, no fruit; cones, not flowers; xylem has only tracheids and phloem has no companion cells; no double fertilisation, endosperm is the haploid female gametophyte; leaves needle-like, mostly evergreen trees. ## Unit 8 angiosperms and gymnosperms.
qp 7 | What is the difference between microphyllous leaf and megapollylous leaf? || مائکروفلس پتے اور میگافلس پتے میں کیا فرق ہے؟ || د مایکروفیلس پاڼې او میګافیلس پاڼې ترمنځ توپیر څه دی؟
sol Model answer: A microphyllous leaf (microphyll) is a small leaf with a single unbranched vein, with no leaf gap in the stele (Lycopsida: club mosses, Selaginella). A megaphyllous leaf (megaphyll) is a large leaf with a branched vein system and a leaf gap in the stele (ferns, gymnosperms and angiosperms). The book writes megapollylous. ## Unit 8 leaves of vascular plants.
