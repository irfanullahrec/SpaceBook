## B. Write short answers to the following questions. || ب۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیں۔ || ب. د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | What are pyrogens? How they affect the hypothalamus? || پائروجنز کیا ہیں؟ وہ ہائپوتھیلمس پر کیسے اثر ڈالتے ہیں؟ || پایروجنونه څه دي؟ دوی په هایپوتالمس څنګه اغېز کوي؟
sol Model answer: Pyrogens are substances that cause fever: exogenous pyrogens (bacterial toxins, cell wall parts) and endogenous pyrogens (interleukin-1 and other chemicals released by macrophages and white blood cells). They act on the hypothalamus (the thermostat), which then sets the body temperature at a higher level, causing shivering, constriction of skin vessels and a rise in temperature. ## Unit 13 fever.
qp 2 | List three benefits of fever. || بخار کے تین فوائد کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د تبې درې ګټې لیست کړئ.
sol Model answer: Benefits of fever: (1) High temperature slows the growth of many bacteria and viruses. (2) It increases the activity of white blood cells and the production of interferon, and increases the body's defence reactions. (3) It speeds up metabolism and tissue repair. ## Unit 13 non-specific defence.
qp 3 | How interferons inhibit the ability of viruses to infect cells? || انٹرفیرونز وائرس کی خلیوں کو متاثر کرنے کی صلاحیت کو کیسے روکتے ہیں؟ || انټرفیرونونه د ویروسونو د حجرو د اخته کولو وړتیا څنګه مخنیوی کوي؟
sol Model answer: Interferons are proteins released by virus-infected cells. They diffuse to neighbouring healthy cells and bind to receptors on them, which makes those cells produce antiviral proteins (enzymes) that destroy viral RNA and stop protein synthesis, so the virus cannot multiply in them. They also activate natural killer cells and macrophages. ## Unit 13 interferons.
qp 4 | What would happen if mucous of bronchi fails to do it job? || اگر برونکائی کا بلغم اپنا کام کرنے میں ناکام ہو جائے تو کیا ہو گا؟ || که د برونکای مخاط خپله دنده ونه کړي نو څه به پېښ شي؟
sol Model answer: The mucus of the bronchi and the cilia lining the air tubes trap dust, bacteria and viruses and sweep them upward to the throat to be swallowed or coughed out. If the mucus failed, germs and particles would reach the lungs and cause infections such as bronchitis and pneumonia. ## Unit 13 first line of defence.
qp 5 | What are the agent of nonspecific defense and specific defense? || غیر مخصوص دفاع اور مخصوص دفاع کے عوامل کیا ہیں؟ || د غیر ځانګړي دفاع او ځانګړي دفاع عاملین کوم دي؟
sol Model answer: Nonspecific defence (innate): the skin and mucous membranes, mucus and cilia, tears, saliva (lysozyme), stomach acid, normal flora; phagocytes (neutrophils, macrophages), natural killer cells, inflammation, fever, interferons and complement proteins. Specific defence (adaptive): the lymphocytes - B cells (antibodies) and T cells (helper, cytotoxic, suppressor) with memory cells - which respond to a particular antigen. ## Unit 13 defence.
qp 6 | Define allergy and allergens. List down some common allergic conditions. || الرجی اور الرجنز کی تعریف کریں۔ کچھ عام الرجک حالات کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || الرجي او الرجنونه تعریف کړئ. ځینې عام الرجیک حالتونه لیست کړئ.
sol Model answer: Allergy is an abnormally strong (hypersensitive) reaction of the immune system to a harmless substance. The substances that cause it are allergens. Common allergens: pollen, dust mites, animal hair, some foods (egg, nuts, fish), drugs (penicillin), insect stings. Common allergic conditions: hay fever (allergic rhinitis), asthma, skin allergies (eczema, hives), food allergies, and anaphylactic shock. ## Unit 13 allergy.
qp 7 | Differentiate between the two types of acquired immunity. || حاصل شدہ مدافعت کی دو اقسام میں فرق کریں۔ || د ترلاسه شوې معافیت د دوو ډولونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer: Acquired immunity is of two types. Active immunity: the body makes its own antibodies after being exposed to an antigen, naturally by an infection or artificially by a vaccine; it takes time to start but lasts long and has memory cells. Passive immunity: ready-made antibodies are given to the person, naturally from the mother through the placenta and breast milk or artificially by injecting antiserum; it works at once but is short-lived with no memory. ## Unit 13 acquired immunity.
## C. Write answers of the following questions in detail. || ج۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے جوابات تفصیل سے لکھیں۔ || ج. د لاندې پوښتنو ځوابونه په تفصیل سره ولیکئ.
qp 1 | What are the main events of inflammatory response? || سوزشی ردعمل کے اہم واقعات کیا ہیں؟ || د التهابي غبرګون اصلي پېښې کومې دي؟
sol Model answer: Events of the inflammatory response: (1) Tissue damage or infection releases histamine and other chemicals from mast cells. (2) Blood vessels widen and become more permeable, causing redness, heat and swelling, and more blood reaches the area. (3) Phagocytes (neutrophils, then macrophages) leave the capillaries and engulf the germs. (4) Fluid and clotting factors leak out, forming a clot that limits the spread. (5) Pus (dead cells and germs) forms, and the tissue is repaired. The signs are redness, heat, swelling and pain. ## Unit 13 inflammation.
qp 2 | Describe the structural features of human skin which provide effective control against microbes. || انسانی جلد کی ان ساختی خصوصیات کو بیان کریں جو جراثیم کے خلاف مؤثر کنٹرول فراہم کرتی ہیں۔ || د انسان د پوټکي هغه جوړښتي ځانګړتیاوې بیان کړئ چې د مایکروبونو په وړاندې اغېزمن کنټرول برابروي.
sol Model answer: Skin is the first line of defence: the outer layer of the epidermis has dead keratinised cells forming a tough, waterproof barrier that germs cannot enter; it is constantly shed, removing germs; the sweat and sebaceous glands make acids (pH 3-5), salts and fatty acids that kill bacteria; sweat contains lysozyme; and the normal skin flora compete with disease-causing microbes. ## Unit 13 skin.
qp 3 | How macrophages and neutrophils help in killing bacteria? || میکروفیجز اور نیوٹروفلز جراثیم کو مارنے میں کیسے مدد دیتے ہیں؟ || ماکروفاجونه او نیوټروفیلونه د باکتریا په وژلو کې څنګه مرسته کوي؟
sol Model answer: Neutrophils and macrophages are phagocytes. They move to the infection (chemotaxis) and recognise bacteria, often coated with antibodies. They engulf the bacteria by phagocytosis into a vacuole (phagosome); lysosomes fuse with it and their enzymes, together with reactive oxygen compounds, digest and kill the bacteria. Macrophages also show parts of the germ (antigens) to the T cells. ## Unit 13 phagocytes.
qp 4 | How cell mediated immunity is attained? || خلیہ واسطہ مدافعت کیسے حاصل ہوتی ہے؟ || حجروي منځګړی معافیت څنګه ترلاسه کېږي؟
sol Model answer: Cell-mediated immunity is carried out by T lymphocytes. An antigen-presenting cell (macrophage) shows the antigen to a helper T cell, which is activated and releases cytokines. These stimulate cytotoxic T cells that multiply, recognise infected cells (or cancer cells), and kill them by releasing perforin and enzymes; some become memory T cells. The helper T cells also help B cells and macrophages. ## Unit 13 cell-mediated immunity.
qp 5 | What do you mean by autoimmune diseases? Give some examples of autoimmune diseases. || خود مدافعتی بیماریوں سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ خود مدافعتی بیماریوں کی کچھ مثالیں دیں۔ || له آټو معافیتي ناروغیو څخه څه مطلب لرئ؟ د آټو معافیتي ناروغیو ځینې بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: Autoimmune diseases occur when the immune system wrongly attacks the body's own cells and tissues, because it fails to recognise them as self. Examples: rheumatoid arthritis (joints), type 1 diabetes (insulin-making beta cells), multiple sclerosis (nerve sheaths), systemic lupus erythematosus, Hashimoto's thyroiditis, and myasthenia gravis. ## Unit 13 autoimmunity.
qp 6 | Describe the role of T-cells and B-cells in transplant rejections. || پیوند کے رد ہونے میں ٹی خلیوں اور بی خلیوں کا کردار بیان کریں۔ || د پیوند په رد کېدو کې د T حجرو او B حجرو رول بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer: Transplant rejection: the cells of a transplanted organ carry foreign antigens (the MHC or HLA markers) that are different from the recipient's. The recipient's helper T cells recognise them and activate cytotoxic T cells, which attack and destroy the graft; B cells make antibodies against the graft and macrophages join in. For this reason the donor and recipient are matched and immunosuppressant drugs (cyclosporine) are given, which lower the T-cell activity. ## Unit 13 transplants.

qp • | Make a model of a structure of a typical antibody. || ایک عام اینٹی باڈی کی ساخت کا ماڈل بنائیں۔ || د یوې ځانګړې انټي باډي د جوړښت ماډل جوړ کړئ.
sol Model answer: Practical activity. Model of an antibody: shape a Y with four polypeptide chains: two long heavy chains and two shorter light chains, joined by disulphide bonds (use wire, pipe cleaners or clay strips). The two tips of the arms are the variable regions where the antigen binds (antigen-binding sites); the rest is the constant region; the hinge region is at the junction of the arms and the stem (Fc region). Colour the heavy chains one colour, the light chains another, and label the parts. ## Practical work.
