p Nida loves listening to famous rhyme 'Old MacDonald had a Farm'. She often listens to it. One day, she asked her elder brother, "Why does Old MacDonald keep so many animals in his farm?" Her brother said, "These animals have so many benefits for him and us". Let us learn about the importance of animals with Nida. We keep many animals at our homes. They are domestic animals.
