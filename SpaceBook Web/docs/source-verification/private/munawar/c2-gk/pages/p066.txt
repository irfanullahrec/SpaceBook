sol People share things such as toys, food, clothes and comic books, give old books to someone who cannot buy them, help an old man to cross the road, and collect money to help flood victims (any two). ## SpaceBook answer (from pp. 65-66). || People share things such as toys, food, clothes and comic books, give old books to someone who cannot buy them, help an old man to cross the road, and collect money to help flood victims (any two). || People share things such as toys, food, clothes and comic books, give old books to someone who cannot buy them, help an old man to cross the road, and collect money to help flood victims (any two).
qp iii | What are the ways by which we can help other students? || ہم دوسرے طلبہ کی مدد کن طریقوں سے کر سکتے ہیں؟ || موږ نورو زده کوونکو سره په کومو لارو مرسته کولی شو؟
sol We can share our pencils, erasers, scales, toys and books with them, give old books to a student who cannot buy them, and help them in solving problems and in reading other subjects. ## SpaceBook answer (from pp. 65-66). || We can share our pencils, erasers, scales, toys and books with them, give old books to a student who cannot buy them, and help them in solving problems and in reading other subjects. || We can share our pencils, erasers, scales, toys and books with them, give old books to a student who cannot buy them, and help them in solving problems and in reading other subjects.
