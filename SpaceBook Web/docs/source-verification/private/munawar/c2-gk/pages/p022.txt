exercise Test your knowledge (Religious Festivals)
q 1 | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
qp i | When are Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha celebrated? || عیدالفطر اور عیدالاضحیٰ کب منائی جاتی ہیں؟ || د رمضان اختر او د قربانۍ اختر کله لمانځل کېږي؟
sol Eid-ul-Fitr is celebrated on the 1st of Shawwal after fasting in the month of Ramzan; Eid-ul-Azha is celebrated on the 10th of Zilhaj. ## SpaceBook answer (from p. 21). || Eid-ul-Fitr is celebrated on the 1st of Shawwal after fasting in the month of Ramzan; Eid-ul-Azha is celebrated on the 10th of Zilhaj. || Eid-ul-Fitr is celebrated on the 1st of Shawwal after fasting in the month of Ramzan; Eid-ul-Azha is celebrated on the 10th of Zilhaj.
qp ii | How do you celebrate Eid-ul-Fitr? || آپ عیدالفطر کیسے مناتے ہیں؟ || تاسو د رمضان اختر څنګه لمانځئ؟
sol The people greet each other on seeing the Eid crescent, the girls apply henna, sweet dishes are prepared, the poor and needy are included and "Fitrana" is paid before the Eid prayer. On Eid day everyone wears new clothes, offers Eid prayer in the Eid gah or masjid, greets others by saying "Eid Mubarak" and enjoys sweet dishes; the elders give "Eidi" and gifts to kids. ## SpaceBook answer (from p. 21). || The people greet each other on seeing the Eid crescent, the girls apply henna, sweet dishes are prepared, the poor and needy are included and "Fitrana" is paid before the Eid prayer. On Eid day everyone wears new clothes, offers Eid prayer in the Eid gah or masjid, greets others by saying "Eid Mubarak" and enjoys sweet dishes; the elders give "Eidi" and gifts to kids.
qp iii | Why are animals sacrificed on Eid-ul-Adha? || عیدالاضحیٰ پر جانور کیوں قربان کیے جاتے ہیں؟ || د قربانۍ په اختر څاروي ولې قربانیږي؟
sol The people sacrifice animals like sheep, goat and cow following the Sunnah of the Prophet Hazrat Ibrahim, and distribute the meat among the poor, the needy and relatives. ## SpaceBook answer (from p. 21). || The people sacrifice animals like sheep, goat and cow following the Sunnah of the Prophet Hazrat Ibrahim, and distribute the meat among the poor, the needy and relatives. || The people sacrifice animals like sheep, goat and cow following the Sunnah of the Prophet Hazrat Ibrahim, and distribute the meat among the poor, the needy and relatives.

sol For example: Jashn-e-Baharan, Mela Chiraghan, Sibi mela, Shandur Polo Festival, Uchal festival, the Horse and Cattle Show, and local food and sports festivals. ## SpaceBook sample answer from p. 22; the festivals depend on the pupil's own province. || For example: Jashn-e-Baharan, Mela Chiraghan, Sibi mela, Shandur Polo Festival, Uchal festival, the Horse and Cattle Show, and local food and sports festivals. || For example: Jashn-e-Baharan, Mela Chiraghan, Sibi mela, Shandur Polo Festival, Uchal festival, the Horse and Cattle Show, and local food and sports festivals.
