p The Sibi Festival is a show of colours, music and happiness. It is celebrated in February every year. It is a kind of horse and cattle show. Horse and cattle show and cultural shows are famous all over Pakistan. People from all over Balochistan bring their cattle to sell in this festival. It is held in an open and wide field. A temporary market is also set up for this event. The sellers and traders sell their things there. Jugglers and musicians also come there to amuse the children. The children also love to see this colourful event.
