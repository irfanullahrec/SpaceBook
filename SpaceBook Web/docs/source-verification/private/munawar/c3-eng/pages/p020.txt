p Words used for pointing to something or someone to describe their position are called 'Demonstrative Adjectives'.
endbox
p See the following examples:
- Sana put this doll here.
- I can see that cow.
- Smell these flowers.
- He put those books there.
box info | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || په یاد ولرئ!
- 'This' and 'That' point to singular nouns.
- 'This' is used for the thing lying near to the speaker.
- 'That' is used for the thing lying farther from the speaker.
- Similarly, 'These' and 'Those' point to plural nouns.
- We use 'These' for pointing to things lying near to the speaker.
- 'Those' is used for the things lying farther from the speaker.
endbox
## Grammar Structure || گرامر کی ساخت || د ګرامر جوړښت

p A simple sentence has the following structure: Subject + Verb + Object (S + V + O). Subject is the doer of an action. Verb is the action performed by the subject. Object is the person or a thing on which an action has been performed.
endbox

(e) ______ is the window of my classroom away from the door.
qp i | (a) ______ is a piece of chalk in my hand.

qp iii | (c) ______ are the students sitting here in class.
sol These ## The students are near the speaker (plural). || These || These
qp iv | (d) ______ are birds flying high in the sky.
sol Those ## The birds are far from the speaker (plural). || Those || Those
qp v | (e) ______ is the window of my classroom away from the door.
