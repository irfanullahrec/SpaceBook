# Technology to Educate || ٹیکنالوجی تعلیم کے لیے || ټیکنالوژي د زده کړې لپاره
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to articulate, recognize and use some formulaic expressions to show social courtesy.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text by looking at a picture/title.
slo apply while-reading strategies to interact with text to locate specific information to answer short questions based on the text.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text applying world knowledge and own opinion to the text read.
slo use summary skills to provide the missing words in a gapped summary.
slo recognize and change the number of simple naming words by adding or removing 's' (singular/plural).
slo recognize and use words that point to something: 'this', 'that', 'these' and 'those'.
slo recognize and practice that words number to make sentences (a/an/some/lots/many).
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- Why do the people use machines instead of working by hands?
- What are some of the changes in our lives with the use of science and technology?
endbox
p Using technology and machines has made our lives easier and more comfortable nowadays. The technology is all around us in the form of smart phones to smart watches. Technology is borne with scientific progress. It has given boom to comforts and speed of work in life as under:
- It saves time.
- It makes communication and interaction among us quick and more efficient.
- It helps in improving our learning.
- It helps in making our life safe and secure.
- It ensures easy approach to information services and products.
p No doubt, the use of technology has brought betterment in life of the human beings but there are some dark aspects also, of using the technology:
- It causes pollution of atmosphere.
- It causes illness and addictions in lives of the people.
- It consumes natural resources and cause them to finish rapidly.
- The use of technology causes unemployment and shortage of jobs.
p As a whole, the technology is a great tool in the hands of mankind. It can prove a welfare tool for the society. It has become an integral part of industry and livelihood these days. This century is in full boom of technology in every field from productions to space. With the advent of integrated circuits (ICs), the equipment has become more compact and efficient. Technology has completely changed the world and our daily life style. Video conference, the preferred way of communication, is a gift by technology.
