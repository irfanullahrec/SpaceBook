box info | Writing Simple and Interrogative Sentences || سادہ اور سوالیہ جملے لکھنا || ساده او پوښتنیزې جملې لیکل
- For writing any sentence: begin a new sentence with a capital letter.
- Give proper space between words to separate them.
- Include subject, verb and object in the sentence.
- Put a full stop (.) at the end of a simple sentence.
- Use the helping verb before subject and put a sign of interrogation (?) at the end of an interrogative sentence.
