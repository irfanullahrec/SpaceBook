## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to articulate, recognize and use some formulaic expressions to respond to simple instructions and directions.
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to state ability/inability to do something.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text looking at a picture or title.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text using interactive reading strategies (while-reading) to draw through common graphical features such as pictures and tables in texts to increase understanding.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo recognize and use words similar and opposite in meaning.
slo recall and apply the rules for the use of 'a', 'an' and 'the'.
slo identify and use simple sentences that show instructions, commands and requests.
slo make a list of items e.g. vocabulary required for a given task/topic.
slo write an expository paragraph explaining a process or procedure.
slo revise written work for layout, legibility and punctuation.

- Which place did you visit recently?
- What did you see there?

p Iqra and Sana's school was closing for summer vacation. Iqra, her brothers and cousins planned to visit Chitral on a special adventure. It was mountaineering. They arranged necessary equipment for use in mountaineering. It included tents, camping, hammer, helmets, and special boots to wear during mountaineering. They also took clothing according to the weather there. They are only beginners. Therefore, they did not need to take so many tools as the professional mountaineers take with them.
p The children reached Chitral Valley on a fine morning of June. They camped near a hill which was not so high. After taking some rest, they wore special dresses. They put on special boots for safety from slipping on
