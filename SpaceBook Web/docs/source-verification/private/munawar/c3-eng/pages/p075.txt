# A Kabaddi Match || کبڈی کا میچ || د کبډۍ لوبه
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo recognize, discriminate and pronounce three and four syllable words.
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to articulate, recognize and use some formulaic expressions.
slo describe events in a picture or illustration.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text by looking at a picture or title.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text using interactive reading strategies (while-reading) to draw through common graphical features such as pictures and tables in texts to increase understanding.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo identify, differentiate between and use some simple verbs.
slo recognize and use words 'my', 'your', 'his', 'her', 'its', 'our', 'their' as possessive adjectives.
slo write multi-syllable words with correct spelling.

p One day, teachers of our school decided to arrange for a kabaddi match. They made two teams of twelve players each. Both teams reached the ground. Many students were already there waiting for the players. There were students and teachers in colorful dresses. All players were young and active. They were wearing the kabaddi costumes. Their strong bodies were shining with oil. They were hoping to win the match for their teams.
