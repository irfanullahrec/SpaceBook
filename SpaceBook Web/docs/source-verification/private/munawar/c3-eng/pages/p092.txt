box info | Use of joining words || جوڑنے والے الفاظ کا استعمال || د نښلوونکو ټکو کارول
p Some words are used to join the words of sentences. These are called joining words. (i) 'and' joins two nouns / verbs. Examples: Ali and Usman are eating rice. He is reading and writing. (ii) 'but' shows two different things in a sentence. Examples: He is honest but lazy. I will read this lesson but I will not do exercise. (iii) 'or' shows choice. Examples: He will eat it now or keep it for breakfast. Amna or Sarwat will do it. What do you like - juice or tea?
endbox
endexercise
