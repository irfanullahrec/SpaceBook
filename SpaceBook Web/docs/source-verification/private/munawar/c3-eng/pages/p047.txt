q A | Word order in a simple sentence. S+V (Subject and Verb) pattern: Asim sings. Asim: Subject (who performs an action); sings: Verb (the action performed by Asim). This is the basic subject + verb pattern. S+V+O (Subject + Verb + Object) pattern: Talha kicks the ball. Talha: Subject (who performs an action); kicks: Verb (the action performed by Talha); the ball: Object (on which action has been performed). This is a basic subject + verb + object pattern. || سادہ جملے میں الفاظ کی ترتیب۔ فاعل + فعل کی ترتیب: Asim sings۔ فاعل + فعل + مفعول کی ترتیب: Talha kicks the ball۔ || په ساده جمله کې د ټکو ترتیب. فاعل + فعل ترتیب: Asim sings. فاعل + فعل + مفعول ترتیب: Talha kicks the ball.

## 5. Ethical and Social Development || ۵۔ اخلاقی اور سماجی نشوونما || ۵. اخلاقي او ټولنیز وده
p In the context of the contents of the lesson, the teacher is to: Take the example of a recent festival like Eid-ul-Fitr or Eid-ul-Azha and explain its importance to the students. Ask various questions highlighting the social and cultural aspects of the festival. Ask them why Muslims give Fitrana on Eid-ul-Fitr (to help the poor participate in the happy festival). Ask them why Muslims distribute the meat of sacrificed animals among relatives and the needy people. Encourage the students to tell their own experiences when they helped someone with the money they got as Eidee from elders.
