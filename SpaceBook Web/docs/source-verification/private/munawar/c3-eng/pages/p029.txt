# An Other World of Mine || میری ایک اور دنیا || زما بله نړۍ
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo pronounce long and short vowel sounds in words e.g. 'a' as in 'hat', 'a' as in 'cake', 'e' as in 'bed', and 'u' as in 'bus'.
slo recognize and pronounce with reasonable accuracy common two consonant clusters in initial and final position.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text by looking at picture/title.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text using interactive reading strategies (while-reading) to locate specific information to answer simple questions based on it.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo use summary skills to provide the missing words in a gapped summary.
slo make anagrams from simple one-syllable words.
slo complete a simple paragraph using the given words, phrases and sentences.
slo write a few simple sentences to describe/show sequences in a picture (events).

- What is your hobby? || آپ کا شوق کیا ہے؟ || ستاسو شوق څه دی؟
- Do you love animals and birds?

p My name is Ahsan. I love to grow and look after the plants. I live in a small house. It has no lawn to grow plants. I have got a solution to this problem. For growing plants, I have arranged some flower pots. They are suitable for growing small flowering plants. I also get small plants from nursery and plant them in the flower pots. I know that the plants grow well if they get sufficient sunlight, water and air. I place them outside where they are exposed to direct sunlight. They need great care to grow well. The flowers got from these plants help me to decorate the flower vases in my home. They look beautiful. I feel happy to see my plants flowering. Keeping pets is also my hobby.
