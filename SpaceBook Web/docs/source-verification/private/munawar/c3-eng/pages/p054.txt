## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to articulate, recognize and use some formulaic expressions to ask and respond to greetings.
slo use summary skills to write the gapped summary of the text.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict words that might occur in a text looking at a picture/title.
slo apply while-reading strategies to locate specific factual information to answer short questions based on the text.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo recognize and use words similar and opposite in meanings.
slo recognize general naming words or common nouns and particular naming words as proper nouns, and classify nouns as common and proper nouns (names of people, pets, places, holidays, etc.).
slo recognize and use apostrophe (') to show possession and exclamation mark (!) to show strong feelings.
slo write with reasonable accuracy, simple sentences of their own on a given topic.
slo recognize the function of different question words to write short answers.

I give you shadow and comfort. I love you! Wow! It is nice to hear that you love me too. You can also use my wood for making things of great use. Some of my brothers give you fruit. Some of my sisters give you flowers. We look different but we do the same job. We make your life easy and enjoyable. Would you give us water to grow? Would you save us from being cut down?
