p Soon, the match started. One player from both teams moved in the circle. One of them touched the other and tried to come back to his team mates. The other gripped his leg. He had to stop his opponent to score a point. There was a great struggle between the two. The students were shouting and clapping. They wanted their favourite team to win the match. After some struggle, the player succeeded to reach his team to score a point. It went on in turns. The match ended. The headmaster of the school gave away prizes to all players. The teachers encouraged the losing team as well. It was not only a match but a show of patience and sportsmanship.
