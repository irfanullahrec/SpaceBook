# Eid-ul-Fitr (Festivals) || عید الفطر (تہوار) || اختر (جشنونه)
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo identify, pronounce and practise long and short vowels and diphthongs as they occur in practice items.
slo identify paragraph as a larger meaningful unit of expression representing a thought.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text by looking at a picture/title.
slo apply while-reading strategies to locate specific factual information to answer short questions based on the text.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo use summary skills to write a gapped summary of the text.
slo provide the missing letters to complete multi-syllable words.
slo change the number of irregular nouns.
slo complete a simple paragraph with words given in a box.
slo write guided short informal invitations.
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- Have you seen any festival?
- Which is your favourite festival?
endbox
p Every community and religion in the world has special days and cultural events. They celebrate them in their own ways. Muslims also have special days and cultural festivals. Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Azha are special festivals for the Muslims all over the world. Eid-ul-Fitr is celebrated on first of Islamic month 'Shawwal'. It follows the holy month of Ramazan. Its name is taken from a special charity amount called 'Fitrana'. Muslims give away a fixed quantity of wheat, barley or dates, etc or an amount equal to the price of the grains or dates, to the poor. It enables the poor families of Muslims to celebrate the festival with same festivity and pleasure.
p The celebration of Eid-ul-Fitr begins with sighting of the moon. The night before Eid-ul-Fitr is called 'Chaand Raat'. The Muslims decorate their homes. The ladies and girls apply 'mehndi' on hands and wear bangles. Muslims get new clothes for Eid or put on their best
