# Cleanliness || صفائی || پاکوالی
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to articulate, recognize and use some formulaic expressions to listen and respond to commands.
slo describe events in a picture or an illustration.
slo use summary skills to write the gapped summary of the text of the lesson.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text by looking at a picture/title.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text using interactive reading strategies (while-reading) to draw through common graphical features such as pictures and tables in texts to increase understanding.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo use joining words 'and', 'or' and 'but' to show addition, alternative and contrast of various sentences.
slo recognize the function of different question words (e.g. what, when, where, who) to write short answers.
