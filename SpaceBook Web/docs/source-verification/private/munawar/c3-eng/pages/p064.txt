p Formation of past simple tense: Subject + Past form of Verb + Object. Negative sentence = Subject + did not + Present first form of Verb + Object. Example: (i) I played cricket. - I did not play cricket. (ii) We went to school. - We did not go to school. Note: Explain to students the simple past tense with the help of examples.
