# Good Habits || اچھی عادتیں || ښې عادتونه
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo demonstrate conventions and dynamics of oral interactions in group to engage in conversation.
slo describe an item in a picture or an illustration.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text by looking at a picture or title.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text using interactive reading strategies (while-reading) to draw through common grounds of features such as plot and characters in the text.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo spell some words studied in class both orally and in writing.
slo identify simple sentences that show strong feelings.
slo write sentences of their own using correct capitalization, punctuation and spellings.
slo complete a simple paragraph using the given words, phrases and sentences.
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- Tell about your good habits.
- What do you think is a bad habit?
endbox
p Good habits make us dear to everyone. We should adopt good habits. They make us good students. They become part of our life and everyone likes us. Here are some good habits every good child should practice these:
- Good children get up early in the morning.
- Good children brush their teeth twice a day.
- Good children take healthy food.
- Good children share things with others.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Telling lie is the root of all bad habits.
endbox
