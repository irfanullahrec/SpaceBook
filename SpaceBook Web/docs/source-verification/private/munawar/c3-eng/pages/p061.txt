# Common Means of Transportation || نقل و حمل کے عام ذرائع || د ترانسپورت عام وسایل
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo recognize that 'ed' has three sounds i.e. /t/, /d/, /id/ as in looked, danced and landed.
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to articulate, recognize and use some formulaic expressions to express likes and dislikes.
slo use summary skills to write the gapped summary of the text.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text looking at a picture or title.
slo apply while-reading strategies to locate specific factual information to answer short questions based on the text.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo predict what follows in the text using context.
slo provide the missing letter in simple two/three syllable words.
slo illustrate use of different forms of the verb 'be', 'do', '-ing' and 'have' along with their relation forms with their corresponding pronouns (I, we, you, he, she, it and they).
slo identify and use the structure of simple past tense for completed actions, with some indication of specific time.
slo make a list of items e.g. vocabulary required for a given task/topic.
slo fill in the missing information to complete a simple paragraph.
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- Name some means of transportation.
- Which is your favourite means of transportation?
endbox
p We see a lot of motor cars, buses, motorbikes and lorries running on roads. People use them to go to offices and work and their places of business. They also carry things from one place to other in lorries and trucks. All these vehicles are called means of transportation. Motorbike is the most common among other means of transportation.
p Naveed's father bought a new motorbike last week. Naveed wanted to tell his friend Usman about it. They saw a lot of motorbikes running on roads daily while coming to school and back. They are in different colours and sizes. He wanted to tell how his father's motorbike was different from others. He describes its colour and size. It is different from common motorbikes in many ways. It is fine in colour and shape. It is bigger and heavier than any common motorbike. Its wheels and fuel tank is bigger than others. A motorbike is suitable for covering short distances. It uses less fuel than a car. My father drops me at school and picks me up after school every day. Sometimes, he hires a car to take the family on trip to longer distance.
