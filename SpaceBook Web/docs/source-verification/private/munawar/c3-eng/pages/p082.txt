p Remember! Non-Verbal Cues make the task of understanding easier for students. They can understand what their teachers are trying to convey through these cues.
- Facial cues: smile, frown, looking thoughtfully at the pupil, quizzical look etc.
- Head movements: nodding, shaking and tilting of the head.
- Body movements: moving in the classroom.
- Hand movements: pointing to the students, 'continue' cue, 'anything else' cue, 'stop' cue, pointing from one student to another etc.
endbox
