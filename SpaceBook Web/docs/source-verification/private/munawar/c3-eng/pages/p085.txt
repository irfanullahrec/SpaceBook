box info | Tenses || زمانے || زمانې
- Present: indicators today, at the moment, now, at present; helping verbs: present form of verb: do, does, has, have, is, am, are, has been, have been; examples: She plays with dolls. He is working now. Where have they gone today?
- Past: indicators yesterday, last (day, night, week, month), ago; helping verbs: past form of verb: did, was, were, had, had been; examples: He went there yesterday. She was watching a rabbit on her way.
- Future: indicators tomorrow, next (day, week, month, year); helping verbs: present form of verb with be / shall be, will have been / shall have been; examples: He will come to school tomorrow. We shall work for our country. They will have reached home.
