p It is the matter of immense pleasure and satisfaction for Munawar Publishers that our series of English Text and Grammar books have always received tremendous appreciation and commendations from the educationists and faculty who never compromise on standards of teaching/learning material. p For compilation and development of exercises and activities, the latest concepts of 'Bloom's Taxonomy' have been used for making the teaching/learning process the ultimate success. We have produced this series in the pursuit of our commitment to make the teaching/learning activity enjoyable.
