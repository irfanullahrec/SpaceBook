p Muslims get new clothes for Eid or put on their best available dresses on Eid day. This day starts with Fajr prayers as usual. In almost every home, the Muslims take breakfast consisting of traditional sweet dish called Suwayyan or dates. They get together in Eid Gah or central Masjid of the village or town to offer Eid Prayers. After offering the Eid Prayers, the Muslims exchange Eid greetings with one another. Special dishes are prepared in every home. Families and friends exchange Eid gifts and sweets / Eid cakes. They visit one another's homes. Children get Eidee to spend according to their own wishes. There is great rush in the parks and play-lands. The whole day is spent in merry-making and festivity.

q A | Spellings and Pronunciation of vowel Sounds (Diphthongs). What is a Diphthong? A sound formed by the combination of two vowels in a single syllable, in which the sound begins as one vowel and moves towards another (as in coin, loud) is diphthong. In a diphthong each letter gives its sound, e.g. oil, noise, etc. (i) Spellings and Pronunciation: The words with 'oi' and 'oy' sounds. Remember! The letters 'oi' and 'oy' often make similar sounds. Read the following words with 'oy' and 'oi' sounds: boy, toy, boil, coin. Note: The 'oy' sound often comes in the end of a word and 'oi' sound comes in the middle or beginning of the word. || سُروں اور آوازوں کے ہجے اور تلفظ (ڈِپتھانگ)۔ ڈِپتھانگ کیا ہے؟ دو حروفِ علت کے ملنے سے ایک سلیبل میں بننے والی ایسی آواز جو ایک حرفِ علت کی طرح شروع ہو اور دوسرے کی طرف بڑھے (جیسے coin، loud) ڈِپتھانگ ہے۔ || د غږیزو تورو (ډیفتانګ) املا او تلفظ. ډیفتانګ څه شی دی؟ د دوو غږیزو تورو د ګډون څخه په یوه سلیبل کې جوړ شوی غږ چې د یو غږیز توري په څېر پیلیږي او بل ته ځي (لکه coin، loud) ډیفتانګ دی.
