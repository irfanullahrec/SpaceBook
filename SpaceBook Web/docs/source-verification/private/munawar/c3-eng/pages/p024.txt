p Pakistan is our beloved country. It came into being on 14th of August, 1947. It is a beautiful piece of land on earth. The Pakistani people live in peace. They are clean and beautiful. They are very hard working and tough. Most of the people of Pakistan are Muslims. Some people also follow other religions. All Pakistani people are loving and caring people. They also share their sorrows and happiness with one another. They live in four provinces like brothers.
p The people of Pakistan are brave and honest. They love their country and countrymen very much. They can lay down their lives for their country. These people serve their country in different ways. They are doctors, teachers, soldiers and farmers. Some people also do business while others provide services.
