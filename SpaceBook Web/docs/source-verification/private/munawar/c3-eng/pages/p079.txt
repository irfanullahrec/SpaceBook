box info | Possessive Adjectives || ملکیتی صفات || ملکیتي صفتونه
p The words that describe the possession of a noun are possessive adjectives. They come before the noun that they describe. All underlined words are possessive adjectives in the following examples: his friend, her doll, their father, its door, our house, your school, my pen. Note: Possessive adjectives are used according to their subject.
endbox
