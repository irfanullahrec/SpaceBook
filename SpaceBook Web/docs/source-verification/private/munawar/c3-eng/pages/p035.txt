# A Little Argument || ایک چھوٹی سی بحث || یوه کوچنۍ مناقشه
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo identify and use previously learnt and some formulaic expressions of greetings and some routine social courtesies according to the age.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text by looking at a picture/title.
slo apply while-reading strategies to interact with text to locate specific factual information to answer short questions.
slo use post-reading strategies to respond to the text by relating what is read to their own experiences.
slo use summary skills to provide the missing words in a gapped summary.
slo recognize alphabetical arrangement of words as a preparation for glossary or dictionary use.
slo familiarize themselves with dictionary in preparation for using it.
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to articulate, recognize and use formulaic expressions to respond to simple instructions and directions.
slo recall and apply the rules for the use of 'a', 'an' and 'the'.
slo use the reading texts as models for their own writing.
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- How many brothers and sisters do you have?
- Do you take care of your younger siblings?
endbox
p Nasir is playing with his sister, Naila. They have many toys. By mistake, a toy slips from Naila's hands. It is a toy helicopter. Nasir is angry with his sister.
p Nasir: Look mom! Naila has broken my toys. Naila: I also want to play. I don't have any toys. What should I do? Nasir: But you should be careful. Naila: I was careful, the toys got broken by themselves, I didn't break it. (Their mother also comes there.) Mother: What is it Nasir? Why are you teasing your sister?
box note | While-reading || پڑھتے ہوئے || د لوستلو پر مهال
p Do you think Nasir was justified in not sharing his things with his little sister?
endbox
