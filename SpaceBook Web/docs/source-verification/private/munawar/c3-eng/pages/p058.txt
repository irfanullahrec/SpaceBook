box info | Writing a Guided Paragraph || رہنمائی کے ساتھ پیراگراف لکھنا || د لارښوونې سره پراګراف لیکل
- Begin your paragraph with the topic sentence. A topic sentence works as a theme of the paragraph.
- Next write sentences about your topic. If the paragraph needs sentences in favour or against, first write sentences on one aspect and write on the other aspect then.
- In the end, give your concluding views.
endbox
