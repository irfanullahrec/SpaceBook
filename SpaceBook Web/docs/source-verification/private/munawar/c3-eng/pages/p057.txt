p Look at these rabbits. Tell which one is tidy. The opposite of the word 'tidy' is 'untidy'. Words showing opposite meanings are called 'antonyms'. They can be naming words (nouns), action words (verbs) or describing words (adjectives).

exercise Exercise (Talking Tree: Grammar Structure)
box info | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || په یاد ولرئ!
- The naming word used for common persons, places and things is a common noun.
- The naming word used for specific or particular persons, places and things is a proper noun. The first letter of a proper noun is always capitalized.
endbox
