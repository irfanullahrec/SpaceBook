p In the context of the contents of the lesson, the teacher is to: Tell the students some inspiring stories/examples to make them believe that telling lie is a curse. Tell them that they must always speak the truth. Ask the students to turn to tell any incident of their life when they spoke the truth and got the reward for it. They may tell the incident of someone else who told lie and was disgraced or punished for it. Ask each student what he/she will do if they break a precious vase or glass decoration at home.
