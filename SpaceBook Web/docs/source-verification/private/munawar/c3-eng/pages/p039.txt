box info | Possessive pronouns || ملکیتی ضمیر || ملکیتي ضمیرونه
p The possessive pronouns show possession or belonging. They are as follows: my/mine: This is my pen. It is mine. our/ours: We love our homeland. It is ours. your/yours: Show me your pencil. Mine is more beautiful than yours. their/theirs: This is their desk. It is theirs. his: She respects her elders and he respects his. her/hers: She will bring her brother to school tomorrow. This school is also hers. Note: Possessive pronouns are used according to personal pronoun or subject in a sentence.
endbox

box info | Use of Articles 'a' and 'an' || حرفِ تعریف 'a' اور 'an' کا استعمال || د 'a' او 'an' ټکو کارول
- 'a' and 'an' are called indefinite articles. They refer to general (any person, any place or anything).
- We use 'a' before noun beginning with consonant sound. Examples: a pen, a cat, a book, etc.
- We use 'an' before nouns beginning with a vowel sound. Examples: an apple, an egg, an owl, an orange, an umbrella.
- Sometimes, a consonant like 'h' gives the sound of vowels as in words: honest, hour, honour, etc. In such case, we use 'an' before the noun beginning with 'h'.
- Likewise, in some words, 'u' may give the sound of a consonant like in 'university', 'union', etc. In such case, we use 'a' before such nouns.
endbox
q C | Encircle the correct article (a/an). || درست حرفِ تعریف (a/an) پر دائرہ لگائیں۔ || سم ټکی (a/an) ګردی کړئ.
qp i | She has (a/an) glass and (a/an) globe.
sol a glass, a globe || a glass, a globe || a glass, a globe
qp ii | They go to (a/an) university.
sol a university ## 'University' begins with a consonant sound /y/. || a university || a university
qp iii | I am writing (a/an) letter. || میں ایک خط لکھ رہا ہوں۔ || زه یو لیک لیکم.
sol a letter || a letter || a letter
qp iv | Sarwat has (a/an) orange and (a/an) mango.
sol an orange, a mango || an orange, a mango || an orange, a mango
qp v | You bought some toys and (a/an) school bag yesterday.
sol a school bag || a school bag || a school bag
p Remember! Avoid using 'a' and 'an' with plural nouns. The articles 'a' and 'an' are used before a single common noun. They are not used before plural common nouns.
