## Sindh || سندھ || سند
p Sindh is the second largest province of Pakistan. Here, people speak Sindhi, Urdu, Lasi, Kutchi and Saraiki languages. The capital city Karachi is the most famous business hub. It is the largest city of Pakistan. The tomb of Quaid-e-Azam, Port Qasim and coastal areas of Arabian Sea are main features of this city. The Sindhi dress consist of Shalwar kameez with ajrak and Sindhi cap and turban on the head while women wear embroidered kameez shalwar or saree. ## Balochistan || بلوچستان || بلوچستان
p Balochistan is the largest province of Pakistan by area. Here, people speak Balochi, Brahvi, Pashto and Urdu languages. Its capital city is Quetta. Balochistan has many mountains ranges and water falls. Ziarat and Gawadar are tourist spots where thousands of tourists visit to see the beauty of Pakistan. The traditional dress of the people of Balochistan is a turban, loose shalwar kameez and a waistcoat, which is decorated with embroidery and glass work. Women wear a loose dress with elaborate embroidery and round glasswork. They wear a big cloak on their head which covers their face and body.
