## Khyber Pakhtunkhwa || خیبر پختونخوا || خیبر پښتونخوا
p Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is the land of Pathans. The people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa are very brave. They speak Pashto, Urdu, Hindko, Saraikhi and Chitrali languages. Its capital city is Peshawar. It is an ancient and historical province. It still contains the remains of Buddhist period. The traditional dress of Pakhtoons is shalwar kameez and a waistcoat with a cap. Women wear a long cloak over the whole body. Women also love to wear artificial jewelry.
