p Art is another important medium of communication. Different artists communicate sensitive and important aspects of society through their arts. ## Language || زبان || ژبه
p Language is an integral part of communication. We all use language to share our ideas, views and information with the other individuals. There are many languages spoken in the world and people of one country or region communicate in different languages to express their opinions or pass information. The people focus on strong language skills for effective and everlasting communication that requires good use of grammar and punctuation. Allama Iqbal used language in the form of poetry to communicate to Muslims in India and made them realise that they need to struggle for an independent country. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah had good command over English language and his speech influenced and inspired by the British. For an effective communication, learning language and how to use language is very important.
## Letters || خطوط || لیکونه
p We write letters as it is the oldest form of communication and till now we write letters to communicate. People wrote letters with excitement and good spirits to communicate with their loved ones. The people wrote letters and waited for letters anxiously and sometimes it used to take months to send and wait for reply. In old days before internet, the people used to write letters to their old friends and family members. Letters were posted and some people used to collect stamps from the postage on envelopes. The people write both formal and informal letters. The people write letters to express their feelings, share their thoughts, invite people and pass news and important things which they cannot communicate while speaking.
