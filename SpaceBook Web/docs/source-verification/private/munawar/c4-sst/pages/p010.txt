## Tolerance || رواداری || زغم
p Accepting others who are different in their races, cultures, habits and even religious beliefs is tolerance. It is the ability to accept others, with all their differences and experiences at something harmful or unpleasant. It is, in fact, putting up with something you do not like at all. It enables a person to get along better with others.
## Importance of Living in Harmony || ہم آہنگی سے رہنے کی اہمیت || په همغږۍ کې د ژوند ارزښت
p Living in harmony simply means 'living peacefully with those around us and the natural world that sustains our well being. In other words, it implies the rule 'live and let live'. To live in harmony requires that we consider the needs and requirements of others as we think and desire for our own needs and wishes. It is only possible if we are inclined to sacrifice our own needs to fulfill the others' requirements. Living in harmony within a family and also in the society is important because in life, people need to depend on others to fulfill their daily needs. It is only possible if they observe the rule of give and take. When there is harmony among the people of a community, it makes everyone to get its benefits. The harmony within all family members and the entire social set up, is the sign of peace and progress for everyone living in that community. It comes through better relations and good will for one another. It is therefore, necessary for each and every member of a family and community to make best efforts for bringing harmony. It will ensure peaceful life for everyone.
