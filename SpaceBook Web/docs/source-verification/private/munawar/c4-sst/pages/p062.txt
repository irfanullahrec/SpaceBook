## South America || جنوبی امریکہ || سویلي امریکا
p South America is the fourth largest continent of the world. Venezuela, Colombia, Peru, Argentina, Chile and Brazil are a few countries in South America. The Isthmus of Panama separates South America from North America and on the east is Atlantic Ocean and on the west is Pacific Ocean.
## Antarctica || انٹارکٹیکا || انټارکټیکا
p The continent of Antarctica is the fifth largest continent of the world. It is covered by snow all the time and it is very difficult for humans to survive in it's cold weather. There is no country in Antarctica.
## Oceans and Seas || سمندر || سمندرونه
p Oceans are huge water bodies and they cover 71% of the Earth's surface. Many smaller seas join and make a big ocean. Seas are smaller than oceans and are usually located where the land and ocean meet. There are five oceans in the world. Their names are given below.
p The Arctic Ocean: The Arctic is the smallest ocean of the world. The Arctic Ocean shares borders with five seas namely Chukchi, East Siberian, Greenland, Laptev etc.
p The Southern Ocean: It is the newest and the fourth largest ocean of the world.
p The Atlantic Ocean: It is the world's second largest ocean. There are ten seas in Atlantic Ocean and some of their names are The Baltic Sea, The Black Sea, The Mediterranean Sea, Labrador Sea, Gulf of Mexico and Norwegian Sea.
p The Pacific Ocean: The Pacific Ocean is the world's largest ocean. It shares borders with 12 seas e.g. South China Sea, Tasman Sea, Bering Sea and East China Sea.
p The Indian Ocean: It is the world's third largest ocean. There are seven seas in the Indian Ocean, whose other names are Red Sea, Arabian Sea, Persian Gulf and Adaman Sea.
