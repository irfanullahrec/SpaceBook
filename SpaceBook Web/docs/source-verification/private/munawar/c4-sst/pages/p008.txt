p Different people live on earth. They are similar to one another on the basis that they are human beings. On the other side, they are far different from one another. Some are rich, some are poor; and some are young while other are old. Some believe in social unity while others support moral growth. There are some who are workers while some are masters. Some speak their native languages while others national languages for communication. Some (Muslims) worship Allah Almighty while others (non-Muslims) worship many gods. Such a difference amongst people is called diversity.
## Diversity || تنوع || رنګارنګي
p Diversity means the understanding that each individual (person) is unique. Recognizing the individual differences, found among people is called diversity.

## Characteristics of Diverse Groups within a Society || معاشرے میں متنوع گروہوں کی خصوصیات || په ټولنه کې د رنګارنګو ډلو ځانګړتیاوې
p The following elements are included in diversity:
- Age: Different people have different age groups such as children, youngsters, adults and old people.
- Gender: There are two main genders among human beings i.e. male and female.
- Physical Abilities: Different people have different physical abilities. Some may play good while some may read well.
- Religious Beliefs: Different people living in a society, follow different religions. Majority of Pakistani people are Muslims. There live Christians, Hindus and Sikhs.
- Different people have different cultural backgrounds, and they follow their own civilizations and traditions.
