## Establishing Political Parties || سیاسی جماعتوں کا قیام || د سیاسي ګوندونو جوړېدل
p Political party: It is an organized group of citizens having same political ideology and programme and wants to get power through political means. In Pakistan, the political parties have religious, traditional, ethnic and tribal affiliations. They all aim to participate in political changes in the country. Each party has its own manifesto and goals, they wish to achieve. Every party has its leader. They contest in elections from their respective areas. The candidates are representatives of common people and they are aware of problems faced by the people. Some of the political parties in our country are Pakistan Peoples' Party, Muslim League, Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf, Jamiat e Ulma-e-Islam and many more. They take out rallies and address the people. Some of the common manifestos of political parties are as follows:
- eradication of poverty
- providing food, clothing & shelter to people
- corruption free society
- improve the health & safety of people
- bringing educational reforms & making new schools, colleges & universities
- giving subsidies to farmers & small medium industries
- improving law & order situation in the country
- inviting foreigners to invest
- improve exports
- debt management
- improve the security of the country
