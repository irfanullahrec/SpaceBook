## Religious Services || دینی خدمات || دیني خدمتونه
p There have been many personalities who guided the people in religion and education aspects of life and rendered a lot of services for spiritual life of the people of the province. Syed Ali Tirmizi (RA) called Peer Baba (RA), Shah Ismaeel Shaheed (RA), Hazrat KaKa Sahib (RA), Peer of Manki, Haji Sahib (RA), Maulana Abdul Haq (RA), Maulana Ghulam Ghaus Hazarvi (RA), Mian Umar (RA), Main Hassan Jan (RA), Maulana Tahir (RA), Maulana Abdul Hadi (RA), Maulana Arif Hussaini (RA) and many more. These great scholars have made their best efforts in spreading religious and spiritual education by establishing Madrasas and preaching of Islamic teachings among the common people. It is the outcome of the best efforts of these people that we see many people spring out of these religious institutions like great beams of light.
## Literary and Social Services || ادبی اور سماجی خدمات || ادبي او ټولنیز خدمتونه
p The province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has always proved to be the fountain house of social and welfare activities. The popular poets include Khushal Khan Khattak, Rehman Baba, Abdul Hameed Baba, Ameer Hamza Shanwari, Ajmal Khattak, Ghani Khan, Qateel Shafaee and Ahmad Faraz. These poets have composed their poetry for the guidance of the people. The authors have written many valuable books.
p Some of the major personalities of the province are discussed below:
## Khushal Khan Khattak || خوشحال خان خٹک || خوشحال خان خټک
p He was born in Akora Khattak in 1613. He had many qualities of head and heart. He was a great warrior and a great writer as well. That is why people called him the master of pen and sword at the same time. His famous books include, Dastar Nama, Baz Nama, Tib Nama and Deewan-e-Khushal. The English people got translation of many of his creations. Allama Iqbal was very impressed by him. He died in 1689. He used to say that even throwing into the mouth of the hungry lion, should not make you lose heart.
## Haji Sahib Tarangzai || حاجی صاحب ترنگزئی || حاجي صاحب ترنګزی
p Fazal Wahid called Haji Sahib Tarangzai, was born in Charsadda in 1885. He was very famous for his struggle against the slavery of the British rule. He died in 1937. He still lives among the hearts of people due to his bravery, sincerity, patriotism and loyalty.
