## Interdependence || باہمی انحصار || یو بل پورې اړه
p All human beings depend upon their environment to supply them what they need including food, services, shelter, security, etc. This is known as interdependence. In economics, when the producers produce a product, they look for profit. When the consumers need something, they look for the producer who can provide them the goods they need. Neither the producers nor consumers alone can fulfill one another's needs. They have to depend on each other. This is interdependence between them.

## Inflation || مہنگائی || تورم
p When you visit a market place or go for shopping with your elders, you listen to the people saying that the prices of things have gone higher than before. If you bought your toy car for rupees fifty last year, you cannot buy it with the same amount. It shows that the value of fifty rupees has come down as compared to last year. This decrease in the value of money is called inflation. We can observe that our population is increasing day by day. The production of things of daily use is not increasing with same speed. It means there will be more people in need of the same quantity of things. When there is less production and more demand of the things, it causes inflation. The best solution to avoid this problem is to increase the products according to their demand and also increase our income according to the spending.
## The Effect of Inflation || مہنگائی کے اثرات || د تورم اغېزې
p Inflation is a continuous danger for a country's economy. It can affect the people's ability to buy a thing. The following are effects on the people's purchasing power:
p 1. Inflation results in price rise. The people cannot afford to buy, even the most important things they need.
p 2. The inflation affects adversely on the purchasing power of the people. They are unable to afford the most essential facilities of life.
p 3. People have to make economical choices. They have to leave many important necessities of life for the most important needs.
