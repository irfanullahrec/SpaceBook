## Lifestyle of People in Pakistan || پاکستان میں لوگوں کا طرزِ زندگی || په پاکستان کې د خلکو ژوندانه
p The northern parts of Pakistan are mostly covered with high mountains. Most of the mountainous areas are remain covered with snow for most part of the year. It affects the economic activity of the people living in these areas. These area have many beautiful landscapes and hill tops which attract the tourists from all over the world. In this way, the tourism activities remain the source of earning for such areas. Another import source of income for the people living in mountainous areas is rearing the cattle sheep, goats, etc. In the areas which are somewhat suitable for agricultural activity, the people adopt agricultural activity and gardening. The main part of food of these areas consist of dry fruits, meat and Qehwah. The people wear warm and heavy dresses with woolen caps. They build houses with sloppy roofs so that during the snowfall, the snow cannot pile up on roofs.
p The people living in the plain areas of Punjab and Sind mostly adopt agriculture and farming. Relevant to these professions, the trade and industries are established in the areas where raw material is easily available. The climate of such areas is not so hard. Therefore, people wear light dresses and live in airy and well ventilated houses. The people eat foods like vegetables, meat, fruits, and things prepared from wheat and corns.
p In desert areas, there is scarcity of water. They remain extremely hot throughout most part of the year. The sand dunes and least rains do not support agriculture and greenery. Animals rearing is the most popular profession of these areas. The camel is used for carrying loads and people from one place to another. The people are mostly nomadic tribes who remain continue to move in search of water and food resources.
p The land in plateaus areas is not suitable for agriculture. The underground waterways (Karez) are used where some agricultural activity is possible. The most common profession of such areas is farming and mining. In some areas, the fruits farming has also been observed.
p In the coastal areas, most of the people depend on fishing. The people living near the ports and harbours are involved in shipping and trade. The eat sea foods. The climate of such areas is mostly mild, they wear cotton and light clothes. Their houses are airy and well ventilated.
