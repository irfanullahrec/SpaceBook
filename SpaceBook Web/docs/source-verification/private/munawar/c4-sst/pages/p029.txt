## Constitution of Pakistan 1973 || آئینِ پاکستان ۱۹۷۳ || د پاکستان اساسي قانون ۱۹۷۳
p Constitution of Pakistan is also known as 1973 constitution which is the supreme law of Islamic Republic of Pakistan. With a quality of not being too rigid and too flexible, it is the most useful and reliable constitution so far. It was enforced on 14th August, 1973. Let us read some common characteristics of the constitution of Pakistan below.
## Characteristics of the Constitution of Pakistan || آئینِ پاکستان کی خصوصیات || د پاکستان د اساسي قانون ځانګړتیاوې
## 1. Written Form || ۱۔ تحریری شکل || ۱. لیکلې بڼه
p It is written, having 280 articles, 6 schedules, and a few amendments. Political usage and traditions are updated time to time.
## 2. Republican || ۲۔ جمہوری || ۲. جمهوري
p Pakistan is an Islamic Republic, and the head of the state will be a Muslim, who will be elected for five-years.
## 3. Federal System || ۳۔ وفاقی نظام || ۳. فدرالي نظام
p Pakistan is a federation of four provinces and areas. The powers are distribution between federal and provincial government by the constitution.
## 4. Parliamentary System of Government || ۴۔ پارلیمانی نظامِ حکومت || ۴. پارلماني حکومتي نظام
p The constitution of 1973 provides parliamentary system of government. According to this system the parliament enjoys supremacy in the political system of Pakistan. The political executive of the country is taken from the parliament and he is responsible before the parliament. The head of executive (Prime minister) is elected from the National assembly by simple majority. Cabinet members (Ministers) are taken from both the house of legislature the Senate and National Assembly. The executive remain in power till having majority support in the in the National Assembly.
