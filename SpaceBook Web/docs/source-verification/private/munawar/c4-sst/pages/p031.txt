qp i | What is state?
sol A state is a community of people having a definite territory, free of external control and possessing an organized government to which people show habitual obedience. ## From p. 29. || A state is a community of people having a definite territory, free of external control and possessing an organized government to which people show habitual obedience. || A state is a community of people having a definite territory, free of external control and possessing an organized government to which people show habitual obedience.
qp ii | Differentiate between state and government.
sol A state is an organized political community under a single system of government, more or less permanent, immaterial and non-physical; a government is the system by which the state is controlled, temporary and subject to frequent changes, and a group of people that can be observed physically. ## From p. 29. || A state is an organized political community under a single system of government, more or less permanent, immaterial and non-physical; a government is the system by which the state is controlled, temporary and subject to frequent changes, and a group of people that can be observed physically. || A state is an organized political community under a single system of government, more or less permanent, immaterial and non-physical; a government is the system by which the state is controlled, temporary and subject to frequent changes, and a group of people that can be observed physically.
qp iii | What is the role of a constitution? Explain its purposes.
sol A constitution is a set of rules and principles specifying how a country should be governed, how power is distributed and what the rights of citizens are; it establishes the decision makers, explains how they are chosen, limits the power of the government, tells the rights and duties of a citizen and elaborates the aspirations of the society. ## From p. 29 and 30. || A constitution is a set of rules and principles specifying how a country should be governed, how power is distributed and what the rights of citizens are; it establishes the decision makers, explains how they are chosen, limits the power of the government, tells the rights and duties of a citizen and elaborates the aspirations of the society. || A constitution is a set of rules and principles specifying how a country should be governed, how power is distributed and what the rights of citizens are; it establishes the decision makers, explains how they are chosen, limits the power of the government, tells the rights and duties of a citizen and elaborates the aspirations of the society.

sol The 1973 constitution is written (280 articles, 6 schedules), republican (the head of state is a Muslim elected for five years), federal (powers divided between federal and provincial governments), parliamentary, it reflects a welfare spirit, provides an independent judiciary and gives rights to citizens. ## From p. 30 and 31. || The 1973 constitution is written (280 articles, 6 schedules), republican (the head of state is a Muslim elected for five years), federal (powers divided between federal and provincial governments), parliamentary, it reflects a welfare spirit, provides an independent judiciary and gives rights to citizens. || The 1973 constitution is written (280 articles, 6 schedules), republican (the head of state is a Muslim elected for five years), federal (powers divided between federal and provincial governments), parliamentary, it reflects a welfare spirit, provides an independent judiciary and gives rights to citizens.
