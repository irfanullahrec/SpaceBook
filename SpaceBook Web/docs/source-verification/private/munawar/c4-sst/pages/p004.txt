## Global Citizen || عالمی شہری || نړیوال وګړی
p We live in an interconnected world, with a growing focus on becoming more global-minded. Many organizations and universities refer to this phenomenon as enhancing 'global citizenship', or creating 'global citizens', and encourage a variety of international activities. To become a global citizen, you'll have to be creative, flexible, dedicated and proactive. Global citizens get involved as members of the international community and are committed on this community in a meaningful and positive way. This means that aspiring global citizens need to develop skills related to problem-solving, decision-making, critical thinking, communication and collaboration. These five skills are becoming more and more essential to success in the workplace, as well as life in general, and can add to both your personal and professional development.
## Digital Citizenship || ڈیجیٹل شہریت || ډیجیټل اتباعیت
p Our children are very much attached with cyber technology. They use mobile phones and get connected to the world, by using social media with the help of internet. They come to know about what is happening in the world. Hence, they become a part of cyber citizenship. This kind of citizenship demands rules of digital citizenship. All children, youngsters and adults have to follow these rules.

## Six Rules of Digital Citizenship || ڈیجیٹل شہریت کے چھ اصول || د ډیجیټل اتباعیت شپږ اصول
p There are six rules that must be followed to attain the digital citizenship. They are given as follows:
- Treat others with respect: It means avoid making fun of people online.
- Do not steal: It means not to steal any written material, especially with a copyright.
- Shop on secured sites: You should ask your parents and elders before shopping anything on internet website.
- Never disclose personal information: It including phone numbers, addresses and check-ins internet.
- Social media: You should not tell any details to anyone on internet or social media.
- Avoid talking bad on social media: If you do not have something nice to say, never say anything bad at all.
