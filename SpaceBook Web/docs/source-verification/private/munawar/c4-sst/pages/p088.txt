## Entrepreneurs || کاروباری افراد || متشبثان
p The entrepreneurs are people, who start a new business. The risks and rewards are always related to new business. Every year, a number of entrepreneurs develop business and generate jobs for others. Let us know more about entrepreneurs found in Pakistan.
## Different Types of Entrepreneurs in Pakistan || پاکستان میں کاروباری افراد کی مختلف اقسام || په پاکستان کې د متشبثانو بېلابېل ډولونه
## 1. Small Businessman || ۱۔ چھوٹا کاروباری || ۱. واړه سوداګر
p He works as a trader, or service provider to earn profit. For example: a shopkeeper runs a general store or a cable TV operator serve cable services.
## 2. Big Businessman || ۲۔ بڑا کاروباری || ۲. لوی سوداګر
p He is often a person, who runs a company. The company is run by a group of people that produces goods or services or both. It is run according to special terms and conditions settled among all partners. For example: a beverages company or a garments producer.
## 3. Social Businessman || ۳۔ سماجی کاروباری || ۳. ټولنیز سوداګر
p He provides social services to people. He also organizes a group of people for providing social services. It can be an NGO (non-governmental organization) that works for social reforms.
## 4. Private Businessman || ۴۔ نجی کاروباری || ۴. شخصي سوداګر
p He is a hardworking and self-motivated person. He starts his business at a very small scale, and puts every effort to achieve his goals. For example: a private school owner, a small producer or a person dealing in cottage-based industry.
