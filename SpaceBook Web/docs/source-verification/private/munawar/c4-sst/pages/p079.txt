## Over Population || حد سے زیادہ آبادی || ډېر نفوس
p Over Population means the more people and the less resources or basic needs of life in a specific city or area. Over population is a situation that becomes an issue, affects the life of people badly. As a result, people do not get enough food, water, shelter, and clothing. Distributing basic services like healthcare and education becomes a huge challenge.
## Population of Pakistan || پاکستان کی آبادی || د پاکستان نفوس
p The total population was recorded approximately 177 million according to a census survey on 1981 in Pakistan. According to the year 2018, this number has increased to 221 million approximately. It means Pakistan is an over populated and overcrowded country. Let's have a look why the numbers of people are increasing in Pakistan.
## Causes of Over Population in Pakistan || پاکستان میں حد سے زیادہ آبادی کے اسباب || په پاکستان کې د ډېر نفوس لاملونه
p As we know that population may exceed from a certain number when resources fall short. The following are main causes of over population in Pakistan:
## 1. Migration || ۱۔ نقلِ مکانی || ۱. کډوالي
p Migration is a major cause of over population. The movement of people from one area to another area is called migration. Every year, refugees come to Pakistan and settle here. An arrival of refugees from neighbouring countries also results in more population.
## 2. High Growth Rate || ۲۔ بلند شرحِ نمو || ۲. لوړه د ودې کچه
p Rapid growth rate of population in Pakistan is the main reason of over population. The birth rate is very high which shows that Pakistan is over populated.
## 3. Need for More Earning Hands || ۳۔ زیادہ کمانے والے ہاتھوں کی ضرورت || ۳. د ډېرو کمائي کوونکو لاسونو اړتیا
p A single person cannot support a large family in developing countries like Pakistan. He thinks to have more children to get more earning hands. It is thought that more family members in a family will cause the strength of that family. That is also a reason for rapid growth of population.
## 4. Low Death Rate || ۴۔ کم شرحِ اموات || ۴. ټیټه د مړینې کچه
p People get better medical facilities due to advancement in medical fields which causes fewer death. Decrease in death rate is another cause of over population.
## 5. Early Marriages || ۵۔ کم عمری کی شادیاں || ۵. وختي واده
p In rural areas of Pakistan, usually early marriages take place at the age of 16-20. This is also a cause of over population.
