p It was cloudy in the morning. After a few hours, the clouds disappeared and it became sunny. In the afternoon, strong wind began to blow and it became windy. In the evening, cool breeze began to blow which made the atmosphere fine to feel. It shows that in most places, the condition of atmosphere can change from minute-to-minute, hour-to-hour, day-to-day, and season-to-season. This day to day and time to time change in atmosphere is called the 'weather'. When we say what the weather is like over a long period of time in a specific area, we say what the climate of that area is. To describe the climate of a place, we might say what the temperatures are like during different seasons, how windy it usually is, or how much rain or snow typically falls. In short, climate is the average of weather over time and space.
## Elements of Climate || آب و ہوا کے عناصر || د اقلیم عناصر
p There are many elements that affect the climate of any place, it includes temperature, rainfall and wind. We will discuss each of them briefly:
## Temperature || درجۂ حرارت || تودوخه
p Temperature is the hotness or coldness in the weather that is measured on a scale. The rotation of Earth around its axis causes different parts of the Earth to face the Sun at different times. The mornings and evenings are cooler than the afternoon. Temperature of the place changes according to season. During the summer the temperature raises, thus climate is warm and humid. During winters, the temperature gets cold thus climate of that region turns cold.
