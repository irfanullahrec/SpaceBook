## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this chapter, the students will be able to: || اس باب کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا څپرکی بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo define 'trade' and 'business'.
slo identify the major imports and exports of Pakistan.
p We, as human beings, have been created with many desires. We need many things to live our lives. Some of them are called basic needs such as food, shelter, clothing, etc. The study of our necessities and desires is called economics. It helps us to choose necessities when the resources are limited.
## Trade || تجارت || سوداګري
p Trade is the activity of buying, selling, or exchanging goods or services between people, firms, or countries. For example, countries carry resources, such as oil, or skills, or can manufacture that other countries will buy. Trade is beneficial for both people and countries. To fulfill needs, countries try to sell, or export, as much as they buy, or import, from other countries.
## Business || کاروبار || سوداګرۍ
p When you buy the item you want from the shopkeeper, you become an important part of the activity. This activity is called business. Hence, business is an activity related to the exchange, purchase, sale or promotion of goods and services. The aim of any business is to earn profit. A business satisfies the customers' needs to earn profit.
## Major Imports and Exports of Pakistan || پاکستان کی اہم درآمدات اور برآمدات || د پاکستان مهمې واردات او صادرات
## Imports || درآمدات || واردات
p The goods or services bought in one country that are produced in another country are called imports. For example, Pakistan does not produce aluminum and organic chemicals. Therefore, it buys these goods from other countries. So, aluminum and organic chemicals are imports for Pakistan.
