## Family system || خاندانی نظام || کورنۍ نظام
p In Pakistani culture, people mostly live in joint families. Men work and they are head of the family. They earn for their family. Women take care of the house and their children. People live in joint families and children take care of their aged parents. People respect their elders and love the youngsters. Decisions are made by involving the elders. Marriages are performed by asking the will of bride. Marriage invitations are sent by cards and nikkah ceremony and walima ceremony is done where guests come and pay good wishes to the newly married couples. Mehndi and mayon function is also held where friends and family of bride and groom apply hina and sing songs.
## Food, dressing and common values of society || کھانا، لباس اور معاشرے کی مشترکہ اقدار || خواړه، جامې او د ټولنې ګډې ارزښتونه
p People in Pakistan mostly eat simple food e.g. wheat, rice, meat, pulses and vegetables. The food served in marriages is formal. People like milk of cows, buffaloes, goats and camels. People in KP live in the cold region that is why they eat more of mutton and lamb. KP is famous for Namkeen goshti and Chapli Kebab. In Sindh and Punjab, the weather is hot people prefer milk, lassi, curd, butter, dairy products and vegetables. In Baluchistan, people eat mutton, fruits and dry fruits. There are many western fast food chains as well where youngsters love to eat western foods. Pakistan's national dress is shalwar and kameez. Men also wear sherwani, tops and turbans. Women cover their heads and they wear colourful dresses as well. The People wear pant and shirt as a formal dress while going to work. Most people living in villages are farmers and keep cattle. The people living in cities earn livelihood by doing different jobs and business. Family members, relatives and neighbours help each other at the times of marriages and any incidents.

p The handicrafts of Pakistan have gained a lot of importance. The pottery made up of mud, copper and bronze are famous. Fine and good quality carpets are made in Punjab and Baluchistan. In Sindh, people make carpets from the skin of goats. In Kashmir shawls and woollen carpets are made.
