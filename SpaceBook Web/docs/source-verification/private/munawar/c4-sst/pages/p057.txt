## Qazi Hussain Ahmad || قاضی حسین احمد || قاضي حسین احمد
p He was a great religious scholar who was born in a religious family on 17th of January, 1938 in Naushehra. He remained Ameer-e-Jamat-e-Islami for many years. He was impressed by the thoughts and ideas of Syed Abul Ala Maududi. He supported many movements to favour the rights of Muslims living around the world.
## Hayat Muhammad Sheerpao || حیات محمد شیرپاؤ || حیات محمد شیرپاو
p He was born on first February 1937 in the family of Khan Bahadur Ghulam Haider Khan Sherpeo. His father was a leader of Pakistan movement. He belonged to a rich family. He took part in the political activities as a student's leader and as a political leader. He was close friend to Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto. He became Governor of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa at the age of 34 years. He also remained Federal Minister. He became the victim of terrorist explosion on 8th of February, 1975.
## Tahira Qazi Shaheed || طاہرہ قاضی شہید || طاهره قاضي شهیده
p Tahira Qazi was born in Mardan on 1st July, 1954. She got M.A English Degree and started her professional life as teacher. She was appointed Head of English Department in Army Public School Peshawar. She was appointed as Principal APS, Peshawar in 2006. In a terrorist attack on APS, Peshawar, she made efforts to save the students instead of getting to a safe place for herself. She lost her life in saving the students. ## Sheer Khan Shaheed || شیر خان شہید || شیر خان شهید
p He was born in a village of District Sawabi on first January 1970. After his intermediate, he joined the PAF as an Airman in 1987. He joined Army in 1992. He fought bravely on the Kargil front and showed outstanding bravery in fighting against enemy. He laid down his life defending Pak land. He was awarded with Nishan-e-Haider.
## Aitzaz Hassan Shaheed || اعتزاز حسن شہید || اعتزاز حسن شهید
p A little martyr Aitzaz Hassan belonged to a village of Dist. Hangu. He was the student of class 9th in the school of his village. On 6th of January 2014, he had hardly reached the front gate of his school in the morning when he saw a doubtful man entering into the school. He asked him to stop but the boy wasn't sure that the man wanted to make suicide attack. He grabbed the man. When the suicide attacker thought that he was helpless, he burst the bomb. Aitzaz gave his life in saving the lives of his fellow students, teachers and the building of his school. He was awarded with Sitara-e-Shujat for this great valour.
