## Important Historical Events of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa || خیبرپختونخوا کے اہم تاریخی واقعات || د خیبرپښتونخوا مهمې تاریخي پېښې
p The objective of our reading history as a subject is to become aware of past historical events. The important events work as an index for planning about future. Let us study salient historical events of our province. This province has very ancient past. Description of some important events are given here:
## Mujahedin Movement || مجاہدین تحریک || د مجاهدینو غورځنګ
p The pioneer and core personality of Mujahedin Movement was Syed Shah Ahmad Brelvi (Shaheed). He was born in an Indian city named Rae Breli in 1786. At that time, the Great Mughal Empire had declined and many rival powers had begun to rise against Muslims. The Sikhs were an example of rivalry. They believed in crushing and massacre of Muslims of sub-continent. Syed Ahmad Brevli declared Jihad against the Sikhs against the occupation of Sikhs on the land of this province. He made Charkundi and Charsaddha his basecamps and fought against Sikhs in Noshera, Akora Khattak, Sedu and Hund (Sawabi). He travelled to Sawat and Bonar to motivate the Muslims to take part in war against Sikhs. He wrote letters to the Muslim rulers of adjacent states for their favour and help. He defeated Sikhs on many fronts and implemented Islamic system of government which worked for some time but soon, he had to leave for Hazara due to their differences with local Muslim population. He continued his movement against the Sikhs and was martyred while fighting against Sikhs. On 6th May, 1831 at Balakot (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa).
## Sikh Rule (1819-1849) || سکھوں کا دورِ حکومت (۱۸۱۹-۱۸۴۹) || د سکانو واکمني (۱۸۱۹-۱۸۴۹)
p Sikhs were in power in the Punjab province after the downfall of Mughal rule under the leadership of Ranjeet Singh. The Sikhs occupied Hazara also in 1819. They continued including the territories of Dera Ghazi Khan, Bannu, Kohat and Peshawar in 1824 AD. The Sikh regime treated Muslim population very cruelly. In 1849, the English defeated the Sikhs and took over the rule of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa themselves. It enabled the Muslims to get rid of Sikh atrocities.
## The War of Independence 1857 || جنگِ آزادی ۱۸۵۷ || د ۱۸۵۷ د خپلواکۍ جګړه
p The war of independence which the English rulers called mutiny, was fought throughout the subcontinent including areas of present day Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The main objective of that war was of making a collective effort for getting rid from the English occupation of Sub-continent.
