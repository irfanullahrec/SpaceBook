## Human Rights || انسانی حقوق || بشري حقونه
p The rights which are universally accepted, guaranteed and protected by respective state and that are necessary for the human dignity and development are called human rights.
- The right to equality and freedom beyond discrimination
- The right to life, liberty, and personal security
- Freedom from torture and degrading treatments
- The right to equality before the law
- The right to privacy
- Freedom of belief and religion
- Freedom of opinion
## Difference Between Rights and Responsibilities || حقوق اور ذمہ داریوں میں فرق || د حقونو او مسؤلیتونو توپیر
- Rights are legal, social or ethical principles of freedom that people are entitled to by a governing body.
- The duties are responsibilities or obligations of an individual, by the governing body, that are required to be done by the individual.
p Rights and duties are closely related and cannot be separated from each other. Both go side by side. These are the two sides of the same coin. If the state gives the right to life to a citizen, it also imposes an obligation on him to not expose his life to dangers, as well as to respect the life of others. The duties of citizens would be involving in things like paying taxes, serving in the military (during drought /war time), and not spying for a foreign power with an intent to undermine the government. The rights of citizens are far greater than the duties in numbers.
## Qualities / Attributes of a Good Citizen || اچھے شہری کی خصوصیات || د ښه وګړي ځانګړتیاوې
p A good citizen is the one, who follows all religious, social and legal rules of a country. He maintains discipline, and preaches others to do the same. He is helpful to others, and teaches others to be helpful too. The following are the attributes / qualities of a good citizen:
- Fairness: A good citizen is always fair in his dealings with others. He always speaks the truth. He does not tell a lie, or steal things. He narrates, what is true and logical. He respects everyone, and tries his best to comfort others. Similarly, a fair citizen does not cheat others. If he makes any mistake, he confesses it and asks for forgiveness.
