## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this chapter, the students will be able to: || اس باب کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا څپرکی بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo explain civic sense by giving examples (traffic rules, keep the environment clean, etc.).
slo identify common etiquettes (for example, good manners, respect for elders, helping others, dignity of labour, and discipline).
p Etiquette means good behaviour. Etiquettes refer to behaving in a socially responsible way. Etiquette refers to guidelines which control the ways in which a responsible individual should behave in the society.
## Civic Sense || شہری شعور || ښاري شعور
p Do you see the roads, gardens, parks and public places littered all around? Have you seen someone go on a wrong route and curse at you because there might have been an accident? Of course, it wasn't your mistake but you had to listen to the curse. This is all what one can observe in a community or society of which the people lack true sense of living. You might have seen such people blaming the government for all these.
p The awareness and practice about the rules and recognizing one's responsibilities is called the 'Civic Sense'. Another word for civic sense can be social ethics. Human beings are regarded as social animals. They are blessed with common sense. For example; they respect the rules and regulations, observe traffic rules, be polite, have respect and regards for the old, sick, and young, be generous to the needy, are just a few norms in a society with civic sense.
