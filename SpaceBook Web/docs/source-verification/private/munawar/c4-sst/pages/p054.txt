## Afghan Refugees in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa || خیبرپختونخوا میں افغان مہاجرین || په خیبرپښتونخوا کې افغان کډوال
p In 1979, Russia intervened in Afghanistan to establish a government of their own. They sent forces to help the existing Afghan rulers and killed many innocent citizens in Afghanistan. It disturbed the peace in Afghanistan. As a result, thousands of refugees came to Pakistan. It increased the burden on our economy. The government looked after the refugees in all respects.
## Peace and Order in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa || خیبرپختونخوا میں امن و امان || په خیبرپښتونخوا کې سوله او امن
p There have been lots of destruction and security problems in Pakistan after 1979 conflicts between Russian and Taliban volunteers. Many suicide attacks were made into Pakistani lands. The Armed forces and police tried their best to bring peace and security of life and they were ultimately successful in it.
## Merger of FATA with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa || فاٹا کا خیبرپختونخوا میں انضمام || د فاټا له خیبرپښتونخوا سره یوځای کېدل
p There were many problems posed by the people of FATA due to smuggling and security problems. The people of FATA had least basic facilities of life. Therefore in 2018 FATA was merged into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The people are being given equal facilities of life.
