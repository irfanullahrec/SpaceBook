## Contribution of Baluchistan || بلوچستان کا کردار || د بلوچستان ونډه
p The history of Baluchistan shows that the people of this land have always loved freedom and never accepted the foreign domination. This part of sub-continent has always been less developed as compared to other parts of this land. Perhaps, it is the reason that the movement for struggle for Pakistan also started in Baluchistan a bit later than other provinces. Qazi Muhammad Essa strengthened the working of Baluchistan Muslim League on the instructions of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah in 1939. The Muslim ladies of Baluchistan like Begum Saeedah Qazi and others also worked hard to strengthen the working of the Muslim League in Baluchistan to work in the Freedom movement. When Quaid-e-Azam visited Baluchistan to preside over the Annual Session of Baluchistan Muslim League, the ladies took an active part to organize the meeting. The people of Baluchistan strongly upheld and favoured the Pakistan Resolution-1940. The Royal Jirgah of Baluchistan decided in favour of joining the province of Baluchistan with Pakistan on its independence.

## Contribution of Punjab || پنجاب کا کردار || د پنجاب ونډه
p Punjab has been the biggest province in population even before the creation of Pakistan. Its role in the Independence Movement has also been leading one. The political awakening against the English domination gained momentum in Punjab. It was under the dynamic leadership of Allama Iqbal that Muslim leaders of Punjab decided to unite on one platform and reconstitute the Punjab provincial Muslim League in 1926. The Muslims of Punjab favoured the separate electorate for Muslims to have their separate identity as Muslim nation. Dr. Allama Muhammad Iqbal, addressing the annual session of Muslim League in Allahabad in November, 1930, presented the clear idea of Two Nation Theory which led to the creation of Pakistan within a few years of struggle for Independence. The Annual session of All India Muslim League was held in Lahore, Punjab, on 23rd March, 1940. In this session, a resolution was unanimously passed that the areas with majority of Muslims in the Sub-continent, should constitute a separate Muslim state where Muslims can live according to their own ideology of Islam. The Muslim women leaders and the students of Punjab also participated actively in spreading the message of Quaid-e-Azam about Pakistan movement throughout Punjab. The movement was successful and Punjab declared to annex with Pakistan on 3rd June, 1947.
