## Government || حکومت || حکومت
p The government is an elected body of the country. It takes care of all the matters. In Pakistan we have federal government that works as a central or the care-taker. We also have a local provincial government, who closely looks at provincial government matters. The government officials run other departments such as health, education, defence, transport, etc. It provides services to the people for their betterment.
## Organs of the Government || حکومت کے اعضاء || د حکومت ارکان
p You all have seen the government functioning in the country. Let us see how it department, the governments work and how different organs work smoothly to run the country. There are three basic organs of the government. They are:
- Legislature - Makes laws
- Executive - Carries out and implement laws
- Judiciary - Evaluates laws, provides justice
p The basic function of these organs is to make rules, implement and enforce them. These three basic organs of the government perform their own tasks. Let us look in depth what do they do.
