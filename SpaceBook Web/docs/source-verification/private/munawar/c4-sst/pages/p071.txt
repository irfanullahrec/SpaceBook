## Industries || صنعتیں || صنعتونه
p Industries are vital for the economy of Pakistan. They provide employment opportunities to the people. The raw material from agriculture and mining etc is utilized in the industries. The industrial products are source of bringing foreign exchange to the country to establish the economy of Pakistan on firm basis. The important industries of Pakistan include cotton or textile industry, sugar industry, cement industry, fertilizers manufacturing industry and sports gear industry.
## Mining || کان کنی || کان کیندنه
p Mining is bringing out the minerals from underground. Pakistan has been blessed with rich mineral resources of salt, coal, mineral oil, natural gas, diamonds, copper, gold, chromate, limestone, gypsum, and precious stones. Mining is the source of income and main profession of the people of areas where there are mines and mineral resources.
