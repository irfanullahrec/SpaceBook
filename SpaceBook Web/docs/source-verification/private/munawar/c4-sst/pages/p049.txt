## Contribution of Khyberpukhtunkhwa || خیبرپختونخوا کا کردار || د خیبرپښتونخوا ونډه
p The people and land of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have brilliant history. This part of Sub-continent was the last to accept the foreign domination of British East India Company. Perhaps, it was the reason that the land and people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa remained neglected thought, the British period of government on this land. When the movement for an Independent state for Muslims of India started, the people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa also participated actively in it. The Educational institutions like Islamia College Peshawar and others produced many student leaders who worked as a team of Quaid-e-Azam to spread the message of Quaid throughout the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The people and leaders of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa showed open rejoice on the Pakistan Resolution. They thought it to be the destination for them like every Muslim in sub-continent. The leaders like Sardar Abdul Rab Nishtar and many others worked for Pakistan movement with Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.

## Gilgit Baltistan || گلگت بلتستان || ګلګت بلتستان
p At the time of division of Sub-continent into two independent states of India and Pakistan, the land of Gilgit Baltistan was under a Hindu Raja. Under the 3rd June Plan, it was decided that the people of every princely state will decide whether to join India or Pakistan. The Hindu Raja decided to join India against the will of people of Gilgit Baltistan. The people and Armed Forces of Gilgit Baltistan revolted against Hindu Raja and abolished his rule. They established their rule and decided to join Pakistan. It established the rule of Islamic Republic of Pakistan on this land.
## Azad Jammu and Kashmir || آزاد جموں و کشمیر || آزاد جموں او کشمیر
p At the time of Independence of Sub-continent from British rule and establishment of Islamic Republic of Pakistan as an independent state, the Azad Jammu & Kashmir was also in the grip of the movement for independence as it was also moved by the wave of independence. Syed Zia-ud-Din Indarabi was a prominent leader of Pakistan movement from this area. He led a delegation of Kashmiris to represent the people of the valley in the Pakistan Resolution on 23rd March, 1940 and strongly applauded the resolution. Under the 3rd June Plan, it was decided that the people of every princely state would decide whether to join India or Pakistan. The Sikh Raja Hari Singh of Kashmir decided to join India against the will of people of Jammu & Kashmir. The people of Kashmir revolted against the decision because Kashmir was Muslim majority region and people wanted to be annexed with Pakistan. They freed a part of Kashmir which is now called Azad Jammu and Kashmir.
## Role of Quaid-e-Azam, Allama Iqbal and Fatima Jinnah in the creation of Pakistan || پاکستان کے قیام میں قائداعظم، علامہ اقبال اور فاطمہ جناح کا کردار || د پاکستان په جوړېدو کې د قائد اعظم، علامه اقبال او فاطمه جناح رول
## Quaid-e-Azam || قائداعظم || قائد اعظم
p Quaid-e-Azam, Muhammad Ali Jinnah is the founder of Pakistan. We are indebted to him for his freedom struggle, for an independent Muslim State. He was a man with extra-ordinary communication skills, and convincing power. He practiced law in Bombay.
