## Disagreements at Various Levels || مختلف سطحوں پر اختلافات || په بېلابېلو کچو اختلافات
p As we have discussed above that peace stands for living with harmony and without any kind of unrest. A peaceful life in society creates a feeling of goodwill and confidence among the people. When people live together at a place, it is natural that they may have differences of opinion and interests which becomes the cause of differences and disagreements among them. These conflicts may take place at personal level among the people living around you. If not settled timely, they may lead to peers level. Then they cause to spoil the peaceful living in society.
## Meanings of 'Peace' and 'Conflict' || امن اور تنازع کے معنی || د سولې او شخړې مانا
p The word 'peace' stands for living with a feeling of safety and security of life and property. The 'Conflict' is serious disagreement and argument about something important.
## Causes of Conflict at Different Levels || مختلف سطحوں پر تنازع کے اسباب || په بېلابېلو کچو د شخړې لاملونه
p The following are very common causes for conflicts in a society:
- Individual Differences: No two men are alike in their nature, attitudes, ideals, opinions and interests. These differences lead them to clash or the other sort of conflict to fulfill their individual interest. Because of these differences, they fail to live peacefully with each other.
- Cultural Differences: Culture differs from society to society and also group to group. These differences sometimes cause tension and lead to conflict. The religious differences have often led to wars and persecution in history.
- Clash of Interests: The interests of different people or groups occasionally clash. For example, the interests of workers clash with those of the employers which lead to conflicts.
