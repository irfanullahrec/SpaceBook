p from the Latin language, and it means 'people who write laws.' Thus, it is that branch of the government that makes laws. In Pakistan, the legislature consists of the President, the National Assembly (lower house) and Senate (the upper house). They are public represented members, who formulate laws by transforming the demands of people. They devise rules keeping in mind desires, issues and problems faced by the public. Furthermore, it also controls the finances of the nation. Even the national budget needs the approval of the legislature. Prime Minister, and all the cabinet members are also the members of the legislature. The executive is also answerable to the legislature. It also has the power to change laws in the constitution. It also has powers to go through high profile cases in the court like treason, big crimes and removing people from their posts.
## b. Executive || ب۔ انتظامیہ || ب. اجرائیه
p The second important organ is the executive. The function of the executive is to enforce laws that are made by the legislature. The executive organ of the government includes the Political Executive (the Prime Minister and his ministers) and Non Political Permanent executive (bureaucracy or the civil servants). The political executive serves for the period of five years. They make policies and get the policies approved by the legislature. The Permanent executives work in the government departments and administer day to day affairs. They look after the law and order in the country. Police works under their jurisdiction. It also functions as liaison between other countries which treaties to sign with them. It is their responsibility to take approval of the legislature. It is also responsible to keep a strong ties with other countries and maintain peace and analyze threat of war. It also grants titles and honors to people in recognition of their prestigious works.
## c. Judiciary || ج۔ عدلیہ || ج. قضا
p Judiciary is the third organ of the government and it is an independent body whose function is to provide justice, apply the laws and resolve disputes and controversies. It is called as the custodian of the constitution. It punishes those who are not abiding laws of the country and provide justice to the citizens. They carry fair trials and do not favour any party or individual. Supreme Court, Sharia Courts and five high courts are included in the judiciary of Pakistan. District courts and sessions courts: There are district courts and sessions courts in each province. These courts try their level best to settle disputes and disagreements among two parties/ people or groups. If disagreement does not get settled and a party is not satisfied with the decision. It can appeal to the high court.
p High court: The High Court is the highest court of the province. The decision of the High Court is taken by the chief justice.
