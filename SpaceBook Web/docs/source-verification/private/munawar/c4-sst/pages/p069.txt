## Impacts on Human Activities || انسانی سرگرمیوں پر اثرات || په انساني فعالیتونو اغېزې
p As we know that different areas of Pakistan have considerable variation of climate and lifestyle. The areas consist of high mountains, plateaus, plains, deserts and coasts. The natural environments affects the dresses, food and houses of the people living in different areas. In these areas, the life of the people is also affected by some negative activities of the people. For example; the deforestation and cutting of mountains bring changes in the climate of the areas. Same is the case in plain areas. Most of the land is being converted to housing and colonies, leaving less land for agriculture and crops. Near the glaciers, dams are constructed which affect the life the people of such areas.
## Deforestation || جنگلات کی کٹائی || د ځنګلونو پرې کول
p Trees and forests save the Earth from pollution. They produce oxygen and absorb Carbon dioxide. They are also habitats for wildlife and birds. They are source of food for people and animals also. They save Earth from being affected by floods. The deforestation and cutting of trees leave negative effects on the lives of the areas.
## Dams || ڈیم || بندونه
p The dams are constructed to store water for use when there are less or no rains. Canals network is used to carry dams water to the farms for irrigation and drinking purposes. The silt from dams when moves to plain land, it brings fertility there which is very useful for crops.
## Industry || صنعت || صنعت
p The industries in Pakistan play a very important role in the economy of the country and bringing opportunities of employment for the people living around. But at the same time, the poisonous smoke and polluted water cause pollution of atmosphere which is dangerous for the health of people.
