## Population and Census || آبادی اور مردم شماری || نفوس او سرشمېرنه
p When the government of a country wants to know how many people live in a certain area, it records the population by counting it. Population tells the number of people living in an area, city or country. The government carries out a survey to record the population. This is called census. In Pakistan, census is conducted by Census Bureau. When the Census Bureau records the population, it needs to collect data from all over the country. For this purpose, it collects information from each family and gathers this information about people. Census tells about the people who they are, what they are. In simple words it provides information about age, gender, religion, education, income, etc. The government publishes this data. The data is used to make plans for the betterment of the people. For example, government estimates about the hospitals, roads, schools, etc. to be built.
## Distribution of Population in the Region || علاقے میں آبادی کی تقسیم || په سیمه کې د نفوس وېش
p Population is never distributed evenly on the land. Number of living people in different areas are not the same everywhere. Some areas are highly populated while other areas are having less population. There are several reasons of population in different provinces and cities. Better chances of employment, set up of factories and industries are the reasons of big number of population. Moreover, better quality and standards of life such as transport, health, education and facilities of life in a province or city cause an increase in population.
- Punjab is the most thickly populated province while Balochistan is a thinly populated province.
- Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Peshawar are highly populated cities of Pakistan.
