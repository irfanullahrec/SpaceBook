## Citizen || شہری || شهروند
p A legal member of a country, who is given certain rights and privileges by the state and also has some responsibilities.
## Native Citizen || پیدائشی شہری || زېږېدلی وګړی
p In this way, a citizen can gets citizenship at the time of birth. If, parents have the nationality of a country, children also get it by birth. If someone is born in any part of Pakistan, he or she will be the citizen of this country. Similarly, if someone is born in England, he or she will be the citizen of England. If someone's parents live in a country, he or she may get its citizenship by birth.
p After the end of British rule from certain states, and economic revolution in the states like the Gulf States, such states offer citizenship on the basis of investment and skill to those who are interested in their citizenship. But such citizens do not enjoy all the privileges like those who are 'by birth' citizens of such states.
## By Applying for Nationality || قومیت کے لیے درخواست دے کر || د ملیت لپاره غوښتنلیک ورکولو سره
p The other way to get nationality is by applying through a legal process. There are some requirements of a country or state that have to be fulfilled by the citizen. After fulfilling all the legal requirements, the citizen can get nationality of a particular country.
