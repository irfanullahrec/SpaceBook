## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this chapter, the students will be able to: || اس باب کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا څپرکی بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo define History.
slo trace early settlements and their drive.
slo identify ancient civilizations along major rivers and coastal areas of the world (Mesopotamia, Nile Valley (Egyptian) and Indus Valley along with their time lines).
slo describe the artifacts, lifestyle and religion of Mesopotamia, Egyptian and Indus valley.
p It was Mrs. Henry's class and all children were anxiously waiting for her. Today, she wanted to tell the students about the people in the past, the early settlements along with major rivers they lived and how the civilizations declined. She entered the class and started her lesson by explaining the term History. She said, 'History is the study of the people and things in the past.' We are able to understand how people at that time lived. We get to know about them by looking at the remains the tools, utensils, artifacts and signs/symbols they used. We all have seen Pyramids in Egypt. The pyramids show that people of that time were very advanced in Science. They knew how to preserve the dead bodies as mummies.
p The students showed deep interest in the class and then she continued her lesson by defining the term settlements. She said, 'the term 'settlement' means 'place where people dwell.' The early settlement in the ancient times is traced back to Indus Valley Civilization. The people of that time developed a well-planned town. They had built proper houses. There was a proper drainage system. The two well-planned cities are Harappa and Mohenjo-daro. The remains of this site are located in South Asia.
p Then teacher further told them about ancient civilizations, along major rivers and coastal areas in the world. People lived in the areas near by different water bodies as the land was usually fertile and life existed near those areas. They would sow the crops and make their livelihood. There was sufficient grass and pastures for their cattle to feed. The three major civilizations in the past were Mesopotamia, Nile Valley and Indus Valley Civilization. She told them how they came into being, their lifestyle and how they declined. The teacher started with Mesopotamia.
