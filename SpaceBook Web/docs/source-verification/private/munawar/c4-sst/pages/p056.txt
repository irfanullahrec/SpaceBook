## Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan || خان عبدالغفار خان || خان عبدالغفار خان
p He was born in Charsadda in 1890. His father was a well-known landowner of Charsadda. He joined Army as an officer but left the service when an English officer insulted him. He was a freedom fighter from the very beginning in life. He started his career as a social worker. He established independent Madrassas and Khidmatgar Tehrik in 1929. He continued his struggle even among the odds of life and severe problems. He died on 20th of January 1988.
## Sahibzada Abdual Qayyum Khan || صاحبزادہ عبدالقیوم خان || صاحبزاده عبدالقیوم خان
p Nawab Sahib Zada Abdul Qayyum was born in Sawabi in 1863. He got education and started his career as a teacher. He appeared in competitive exams and was selected as Tehsil Daar. He was well known among the rulers and was appointed as Political agent also. He organized the Muslim league as a political party in the province. He died during the year 1937.
## Sardar Abdur Rab Nishter || سردار عبدالرب نشتر || سردار عبدالرب نشتر
p He was born in Peshawar in 1899. After his early education locally, he went to Ali Gath University for higher education. He participated in politics also and was elected for Assembly as a member. He was appointed as Finance Minister in 1943. He died in December, 1958 and was buried in the boundary of the tomb of Quaid-e-Azam.
## Maulana Mufti Mehmood || مولانا مفتی محمود || مولانا مفتي محمود
p Maulana Mufti Mehmood was born in January 1919 at Dera Ismael Khan. After getting early education from Peshawar, he went to Deoband for religious education. He started his political life by participating in 'Leave India movement' in 1944. He was an expert scholar in Islamic research, Fiqh, Quranic education, history and philosophy. He criticized the wrong policies of Iskandar Mirza and Ayub Khan. He became the Chief Minister of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in 1977. He played an important role in getting Islamic acts to be included in the Constitution of 1973. He died on 14th of October 1980.
## Muhammad Jalal Khan (Jalal Baba) || محمد جلال خان (جلال بابا) || محمد جلال خان (جلال بابا)
p Muhammad Jalal Khan (Jalal Baba) was born in a poor family in March 1903. He took part in welfare and social activities and was appointed as president of Anjuman-e-Islamia from 1930 to 1952. He was appointed as president of Muslim league Hazara in 1939. He remained provincial and central Minister in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and voted for joining Pakistan after Partition in 1947.
