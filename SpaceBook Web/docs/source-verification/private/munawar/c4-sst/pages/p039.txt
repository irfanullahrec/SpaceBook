## Mesopotamia || میسوپوٹیمیا || میسوپوتامیا
p The word Mesopotamia is made by combining two ancient words 'meso' which means 'in the middle or centre of' or 'in between' and 'potamos' is meant for 'river'. It means land between two rivers. Mesopotamia was once the centre of civilization. It was flourished between river Tigris and Eccephrates.
- The areas in the 'Mesopotamia' are Middle East and the modern day Iraq, Turkey, Kuwait and Syria were part of South Western Asia and the region around eastern Mediterranean Sea were included in it.
- The early civilization formed around the Neolithic Revolution around 12,000 BCE. It is thought that the civilization might have existed between 4000 BCE or 6000 years ago.
- The land was very fertile because of Tigris and Euphrates River. Sumerian people were the rulers. They made separate states which are known as Ur, Uruk, Lagash and Kish. The oldest city considered at that time there was 'Uruk' dating back to 3200 BCE. It is believed that approximately 50,000 people lived at that time there. The people were mostly farmers and benefitted from the fertile valleys along the banks of river.
- The houses were made of mud and bricks. Temples were built which served the purpose of education. They worshiped nature and had their own gods and goddesses, they worshiped. They also developed an artificial irrigation system called shadoof. They made very strict laws and implemented them.
- They had made strict rules. Women were given special rights. The people were divided into four groups namely Nobles, Royals, The Priests, Rich Landlords and Slaves. The Nobles were influential people. Their priest had great respect in the society and held important positions in the state. Slaves included the farmers, merchants and artists. They were bought and sold frequently.
- It is believed that the civilization disappeared around 4000 years ago. There were many reasons for it's decline as there was lot of chaos. There were frequent wars for power, Sumerian Dynasty was replaced by Amorites and they captured the region and this became rulers.

## Egyptian Civilization || مصری تہذیب || مصري تمدن
p Ancient Egypt was one of the oldest civilizations of the world. It was located along the Nile River in the northeast part of Africa. It lasted for three thousand years.
