## The Balochistan Plateau || سطح مرتفع بلوچستان || د بلوچستان سطح
p The location of Balochistan plateau is towards the south-west of Pakistan. This area is mostly barren land due to little rainfall and rocky area. The area is covered with a number of mountains and hills. The famous mountain ranges are as follows:
- Ras Koh
- Makran Coast Range
- Toba kakar range
- Siahan Range
- Hala Range
- Central Brahui Range
p These ranges are barren but rich in mineral resources with copper, gold and sulphur deposits.
## The Indus Plain || میدانِ سندھ || د سند دښتې
p The Indus plain is located towards the eastern and southern part of the River Indus. It consists of Upper Indus Plain and Lower Indus Plain. The Northern part of the Upper Indus Plain covers the area of Punjab and the Lower Indus Plain occupies major area of Sindh.
p The Indus Plain mostly consists of alluvial plains which are highly suitable for cultivation. River Indus is the main river of the Indus Plain. The plain area got the name after River Indus. River Indus with its tributaries, the Ravi, the Jhelum, the Chenab and the Satlej travel across the whole plain. The water of these rivers used for irrigation purpose. The plain areas of Pakistan are the most suitable areas to live. That is the reason that the population of the plain areas is high because the land of the Indus Plain is highly suitable for living. Vegetation is also not possible as the landscape is rugged. Mostly before winters, people move to the less cold areas. Communication, transportation, road and rail networks are not developed as the places are much higher. While plain areas are suitable to this infrastructure. As a result these areas are developed. The people wear light clothes during summers and woolen clothes during winter. In deserts, life is difficult due to lack of water and rain. Sand dunes and strong winds do not allow farming. The people wear turbans and clothes that save them from sun rays.
