p Dr. Rooh Ul Amin Khattak holds his PhD in English (Applied Linguistics) from the University of Memphis, TN, USA. Dr. Amin has completed his M.Phil in American Studies from Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad and his M.A (English) from University of Peshawar. He has 16 years of university teaching experience at both the undergraduate and graduate level. He has an extensive research in the field of language, pedagogy, and education. He also has expertise in editing and reviewing for national and international journals.
