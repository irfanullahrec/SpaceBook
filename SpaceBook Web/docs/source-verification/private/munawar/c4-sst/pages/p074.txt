p Wind is the air that moves. The direction of the wind determines the climate of any region. The winds coming from humid regions result in cool air and those coming from dry regions warm the air.

## The Factors affecting Climate || آب و ہوا پر اثر انداز ہونے والے عوامل || پر اقلیم اغېزمن عوامل
p There are many factors that affect climate and these are as follows:
p Ocean current: There are two different types of ocean currents and they are warm and cold. The ocean currents carry warm water from equator and transport warm water from south and north to the high altitude. Cold ocean currents originate at high latitude and transport cold water to the equator. Warm ocean currents raise average coastal temperatures and make coastal places more humid and wet. The cold ocean currents lower coastal temperatures and make coastal areas extremely dry. Deserts are formed around these areas.
p Latitude: Latitude is the measurement of distance north or south of equator. Those areas of the Earth with low latitude are near the equator. These areas are very hot as they receive direct sunlight. The areas with high latitude near the South and North Pole receive very less sunlight and result in colder weather.
p Altitude: Altitude or height is the measurement of a place from sea-level. Temperatures decrease with increasing height above sea level. These places have lower temperatures and cold climate. Similarly lower altitudes increase the temperature and climate is warm in such places.
