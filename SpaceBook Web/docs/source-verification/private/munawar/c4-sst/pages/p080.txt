## 6. Urbanization || ۶۔ شہرکاری || ۶. ښار ته کډه کول
p There is a cause of increase in population in urban areas as these areas have more facilities as compared to rural areas. So, villagers move to cities for the purpose of employment. That causes a high rate of population in cities.
## Problems Caused by Over Population || حد سے زیادہ آبادی سے پیدا ہونے والے مسائل || د ډېر نفوس له امله رامنځته شوې ستونزې
## 1. Shortage of Food || ۱۔ خوراک کی کمی || ۱. د خوړو کموالی
p Very fast growth rate of population causes the shortage of food. Shortage of food leads to poverty.
## 2. Unemployment || ۲۔ بے روزگاری || ۲. بېکاري
p Unemployment is a major problem of over population. It is impossible to provide jobs to a great number of people in Pakistan.
## 3. Unplanned settlements and pollution || ۳۔ بے ترتیب آبادیاں اور آلودگی || ۳. بې پلانه استوګنځایونه او ککړتیا
p The rapid growth is a cause of pollution, unplanned settlements and many other environmental issues affect human lives badly.
## 4. Less Saving and Low Investment || ۴۔ کم بچت اور کم سرمایہ کاری || ۴. لږ سپما او ټیټ پانګونه
p A number of people in a country increase the expenditure of government. It reduces the saving and investment rates.
## 5. High prices of goods || ۵۔ اشیا کی بلند قیمتیں || ۵. د توکو لوړې بیې
p Due to more population, there is more demand of household items, eatables and other goods. That results in inflation.
## 6. Social Problems || ۶۔ سماجی مسائل || ۶. ټولنیزې ستونزې
p Rapidly growing population also creates many social problems such as housing, education, health, transport, water etc.
## 7. Low Living Standard || ۷۔ پست معیارِ زندگی || ۷. ټیټ د ژوند معیار
p Rising population cannot be provided with the basic facilities of life in developing countries like Pakistan. So, rising population means low living standard.
## 8. Wash Problems || ۸۔ پانی، صفائی اور حفظانِ صحت کے مسائل || ۸. د اوبو، پاکوالي او روغتیا ستونزې
p Over Population also limits the access of population to the clean drinking water, hygiene, and sanitation.
## Importance of Clean Drinking Water, Hygiene and Sanitation || صاف پانی، حفظانِ صحت اور صفائی کی اہمیت || د پاکو اوبو، روغتیا او پاکوالي اهمیت
p Water causes life. Clean drinking water, hygiene, and sanitation play a vital part in maintaining health of the population. When there is over-population, the access to safe drinking water and good sanitation will be limited. These facilities are most important for the well-being of the living population. These resources control the diseases and boost up the child's health who can enjoy better learning and earning ability in the future.
