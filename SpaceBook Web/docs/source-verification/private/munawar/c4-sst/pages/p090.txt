## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this chapter, the students will be able to: || اس باب کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا څپرکی بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo apply simple and basic money management in personal life (pocket money, savings).
slo define banking.
slo identify services provided by banks.
p When you leave for your school, your parents often give you some money. You carefully keep this money in your pocket. During recess time, you may take something from the school canteen. Sometimes, you do not buy anything at all. After coming back to home, you keep your savings in a box. We call it money box.
p Similarly, you get money from your parents, siblings and relatives on Eid on special occasion such as result day and also after winning a speech contest. This all goes to your money box and you become richer by every passing year. Don't you?
p Then someday, you suddenly hear a hawker selling something you like. You rush to your money box and take out the money you need. It is interesting that you still have some money left in your money box. Do you know you have a banking system of your own? Let us see what banking actually means!
## Bank & Banking || بینک اور بینکاری || بانک او بانکداري
p Banking is a service that makes people save and access their money safely. The people who earn a lot of money every month can use the banking system. People often deposit their excessive amount of money in banks. Bank is a place where you can keep your money and other assets safe.
p In other words, the banking system is involved in the deposit and withdrawal of money. This system also involves in repaying money and savings to the account holders on demand. The banking system also issues loans to people. The account holders can get a huge amount of money for different purposes.
