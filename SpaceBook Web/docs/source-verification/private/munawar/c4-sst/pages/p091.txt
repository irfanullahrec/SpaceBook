p b. Ensuring Payments through Cheques: The bank is responsible to cash the legal and valid cheques. Sometimes, an account holder gets a cheque and deposits it in his bank. The bank gives cash in return.
p c. Ensuring Consultancy: The bank also provides consultancy to its clients. For example, the bank helps its clients to choose between different options to enjoy all services.
p d. Issuing Credit cards: The bank also issues credit cards to its account holders. Credit cards are of great importance when a person cannot carry huge amount of money in his pocket. When he buys any valuable thing from shop or a mall, he swaps his credit card and gets everything set for himself.
p e. Issuing Debit cards: The bank also issues debit cards to its clients. The debit cards have all the money that people have deposited in their bank. They can use this card to withdraw the desired amount of money from an ATM.
