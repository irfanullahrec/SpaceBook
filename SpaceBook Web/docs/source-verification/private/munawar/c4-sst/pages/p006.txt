p Trustworthiness: A good citizen is always trustworthy. People trust in his dealings and character. He earns great respect, through his deeds and actions. He likes and supports justice under all conditions. Similarly, a good citizen displays his trustworthiness in the society. He encourages the ones, who are doing better than him. As a citizen, he supports the deserving ones and earns respect among his social class.
p Honesty: A good citizen displays honesty throughout his life. He remains honest under all circumstances. He fulfills the task, assigned to him honestly on time. He does not takes others' rights and belongings. Similarly, a good citizen displays honesty to enjoy respect in his social class.
box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- A person who legally belongs to a country and has the rights and protection of the state is called a citizen.
- Every citizen has some rights and responsibilities that he enjoys while living in a country.
- Citizenship of a country can be acquired by birth or by applying for it.
- The students should take good care of their rights, and responsibilities in their country.
- The qualities of a good citizen include fairness, trustworthiness and honesty. These qualities make him a good, responsible and respectable citizen.
