## Exports || برآمدات || صادرات
p The goods or services produced in one country and sold in another country are called exports. For example: Pakistan produces cotton and wheat products in huge quantities. Many countries buy cotton and wheat from Pakistan. Therefore, cotton and wheat are exports of Pakistan.
