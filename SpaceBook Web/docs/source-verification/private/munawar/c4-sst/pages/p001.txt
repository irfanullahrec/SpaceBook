## Preface || پیش لفظ || سریزه
p This series of books on Social Studies from class-4 and 5 has been compiled and developed as per the guiding rules, benchmarks and students learning outcomes as envisaged in the Single National Curriculum 2020 (SNC). We acknowledge with great concern that Social Studies is the systematic study of the interaction among people and their environment to promote civic competence. Our young learners are to make the future of the 'Nation'. The books are systematically developed with a view to fostering patriotism, faith and to encourage the inculcation of tolerance and empathy towards others as required by Islam. The main purpose of each book is to instill the necessary knowledge, skills, attitudes and competencies to mold students into informed, concerned and active national and global citizens. The subject matter and activities have been developed to engage students in a rigorous and student-centered learning environment that fosters social understanding and helps students to understand multiple perspectives, think critically, communicate effectively and collaborate in diverse communities in order to understand the past, engage in the present, and impact the future, including how decisions are made at the individual, local, provincial and national levels.
p In order to make the teaching/learning process in Social Studies as an interesting activity, we have compiled and presented the subject matter wrapped up in colourful and lively illustrations. Exercises have been developed exactly according to the Bloom Taxonomy and most modern trends as per the guiding rules prescribed in SNC-2020. The entire effort encompasses a range of concepts included in its domains. We firmly hope that each book in this series will prove instrumental for the teachers in imparting knowledge and concepts most effectively.
p The process of development of books is a collective effort on the part of authors who compile the books and the worthy teachers who put it across to make the teaching/learning process as an ultimate success. Your suggestions will go a long way towards further improving our books. Therefore, we shall welcome your suggestions and incorporate the same to keep our books up-dated.
p Publishers || ناشرین || خپروونکي
