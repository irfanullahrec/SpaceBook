## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this chapter, the students will be able to: || اس باب کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا څپرکی بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo describe key events (Sunnah and Politics) during the Life of Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) and Khalafat-e-Rashida.
p The life of Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) is the perfect role model for all mankind. Every aspect of His pious life guides us all in every aspect of life. The Muslims entered Makkah as victorious after ten years of migration. A unique revolution had occurred in the lives of the people of entire Arabia.
p Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) was born in Makkah in 571 A.D. He spent most of the years of life in Makkah; his childhood and youth were spent there. In the year of his birth, a Christian king of Yemen named Abraha invaded Makkah. He had a big army which was led by elephants. Allah sent flocks of birds with pebbles in their beaks. They threw pebbles on the army of Abraha. Within moments, the whole army and elephants were crushed and trashed to death. It shows that Allah Almighty Himself protects Ka'aba the Sacred House.
p The building of Ka'aba had become very old and needed the walls to be rebuilt and repaired. The time came when the sacred black stone (Hajr-e-Aswad), which was sent by Allah Almighty from heavens, was to be affixed in the wall as it is located now. Every tribe in Makkah wanted that the honour of affixing sacred Black Stone (Hajr-e-Aswad) rested with their head of tribe. It was going to start big conflict among them. Luckily, it was decided that the person who appeared first in the early next morning in Kabatullah, should be given the right to decide about it. Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) was just a young boy at that time. He (PBUH) was first to appear in Kabatullah the next morning. Everyone showed confidence in him because he had always been truthful, honest and pure in manners. He (PBUH) spread a sheet of cloth near the Black Stone (Hajr-e-Aswad), lifted it with his hands and put it on the sheet. Then, he (PBUH) asked the heads of tribes to lift the sheet from corners and take the Hajr-e-Aswad to the place where it was to be affixed. Then he (PBUH) took it in his hands and put it at its place where it exists even at present. His decision was highly appreciated by every one present there.
