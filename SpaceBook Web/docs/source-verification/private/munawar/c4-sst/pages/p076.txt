## Cyclones || سمندری طوفان || توپانونه
p A cyclone is a large scale air mass that rotates around a strong centre of low atmospheric pressure. In the Atlantic and North East Pacific region, it is called Hurricane. In the North West Pacific region, it is called Typhoon. In the South Pacific and Indian Ocean region, it is called cyclone. The winds in cyclones move clock wise. A cyclone happens when the atmospheric pressure is low in the ocean and the water is more than 26.5 degrees. The air circulates and strengthens into greater speed at longer distances. The effects of cyclone are strong winds, heavy rainfall and big storms. Because of heavy rains and storms, the property and life of people is at risk. It results in flash floods, land sliding and tornadoes. We must take precautionary measures against cyclones. Some of them are as follows:

## Avalanches (Snow-slides) || برفانی تودے (برف کے تودے گرنا) || واورین توده (واورې خوځېدل)
p An avalanche is gigantic mass of snow that rolls down quickly on an inclined plane like mountains or roof of building. They are also called snow slides. An avalanche takes place when a layer of snow collapses and slides down the mountains. Avalanche can travel 90 km/hour. The people get buried under the snow and they have suffocation, wounds and bruises. Avalanches can occur due to snow, wind, warm temperatures and heavy rainfall. There are some precautionary measures we should take during avalanche:
p i. Grab on something solid like a tree and rock. ii. Watch for cracking and collapsing snow. iii. Try to keep your head above the surface. iv. Avoid going for skiing and sledging.
