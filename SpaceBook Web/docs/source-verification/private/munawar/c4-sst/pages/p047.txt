## Contribution of Sindh || سندھ کا کردار || د سند ونډه
p Sindh remained important centre of activities during various movements initiated by the Muslim leaders like the Khilafat Movement, and Jehad Movement, etc. The separation of Sindh from the Bombay Presidency was the first step towards the foundation of Pakistan. When the Muslims living in Sindh realized the importance of separation of Sindh, the All India Muslim League passed a resolution in 1925 for separation of Sindh province.
p Subsequently, under the Government of India Act of 1935, Sindh was made a province with its own Legislative Assembly on April 1, 1936. After its initial reorganization, the Sindh Provincial Muslim League Conference held its first session at Karachi in October 1938 under the presidentship of Quaid-e-Azam. The conference adopted a resolution which recommended to the All India Muslim League to devise a scheme of constitution under which Muslims may attain full independence. It was the province of Sindh
