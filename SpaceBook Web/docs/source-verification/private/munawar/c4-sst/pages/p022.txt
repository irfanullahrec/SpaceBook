## Nation and Nationality || قوم اور قومیت || ملت او ملیت
p The word 'nation' means a community of people living together in a country having common history, culture and language. We are a sovereign nation and we have our own identity. The term 'nationality' means the state of legal membership of a country.
p We live in different provinces of Pakistan. We have certain cultural variations. But our religion Islam is the strongest bond that keeps us united like a family. We are like different parts of same body. When one part of body suffers from pain, the whole body becomes restless. We love Pakistan more than anything else.
## Feeling pride on Being Pakistanis || پاکستانی ہونے پر فخر || په پاکستاني کېدو ویاړ
p Pakistan is situated on a piece of land that had been the house of the richest civilizations since the prehistoric times. It has all that is considered ideal for a country to emerge as best in the world. It has beautiful landscapes, the most beautiful and highest mountain peaks, vast and fertile plains, safe and beautiful coasts, glaciers and lush green forests that attract the tourists from all over the world. It has all seasons with variable attractions. It has all kinds of resources for making its economy to flourish by leaps and bounds. It is situated on the crossroads of trade and business with all countries of the world. It is the first Islamic Nuclear power in the world. The Pakistani people have proved that they can show the best performance in education, sports and all the skills of life. It all makes us proud Pakistanis.
box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- Culture is a set of learned and shared behaviors of how people live, behave, think and celebrate different things.
- Pakistan has a diverse culture and it ranges from province to province.
- Components of culture are religion, style, dressing, festivals, arts, architecture and language.
- People in Pakistan practice interfaith harmony and they are given freedom to practice and celebrate their own festivals.
- The word 'nation' means a community of people living together in a country in common and having common history, culture and language.
- The term 'nationality' means belonging to a particular country.
- We, as Pakistanis, must feel proud because of our culture and blessings of Allah that Pakistan is blessed with.
