p We as human beings, have been created with many desires. We need many things to live our lives. Some of them are called basic needs such as food, shelter, clothing, etc. The study of our necessities and desires is called economics. It helps us to choose necessities when the resources are limited.
## Economy of a Country || ملک کی معیشت || د هیواد اقتصاد
p Economy comprises of all the activities related to production, consumption, and trades of goods and services of a specific area. Economy of a country includes national necessities and desires and the available resources to fulfil them. It is all about carefully using the use money, resources and means of production. It also deals with the way of making money for a nation and using it for particular purposes.
p In the past, the people lived a simple life. They had limited desires and needs. They were satisfied with basic necessities of life such as food, clothes and shelter. As time passed, their way of living changed and their basic necessities began to increase. At present, people's needs have become unlimited and countless. Everyone is trying to have the best in life. For example, if somebody wants to have a little house, they would start thinking of a big one and then a house in the posh area. But, man cannot fulfill all of his desires with the little money he has. Hence, he has to make economic choices to live a balanced life.

## Economic Choice || معاشی انتخاب || اقتصادي انتخاب
p Suppose you have one hundred rupees. You want to buy a beautiful pen and a vase. If the price of both items is the same, you cannot buy both of them. So, you have to decide which item you need more at the moment.
