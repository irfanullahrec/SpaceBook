## Defining and locating Major Land Masses /Continents and Oceans/Seas || بڑے زمینی خطوں/براعظموں اور سمندروں کی تعریف اور مقام || لوی وچې/براعظمونو او سمندرونو تعریف او موقعیت
p The term land mass simply means surrounded by land or area of land surrounded by ocean sea for example; an island or a continent.
p Continent: The term continent refers to a large area of land which is separated by oceans. It simply means the division of land into different areas. There are seven continents in the world and each continent comprises of many countries. Let us read about the details of these continents:
## Asia || ایشیا || اسیا
p Asia is the biggest continent according to the land and population. The continent of Asia has borders with Africa and Europe from the west and Pacific Ocean to the east. Pakistan is in the continent of Asia. A few countries in Asia, are Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, China, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, Indonesia, Bhutan, Myanmar, Nepal and Russia.

## Africa || افریقہ || افریقا
p The continent of Africa is the second largest continent in terms of land and population. It is situated in south of Europe and south west of Asia. There are 54 countries in Africa. Some of the countries in Africa are Algeria, Egypt, Morocco, Somalia, and South Africa. Can you name some more countries in Africa?
## Europe || یورپ || اروپا
p Europe is the second smallest continent in the world. It is surrounded by Arctic Ocean in the north, Atlantic Ocean in the West, and Mediterranean Sea in the south. The continent of Africa is situated in the south and Asia is in the east. A few countries in Europe are England, France, Germany, Spain, Switzerland and Austria.
## Australia/Oceania || آسٹریلیا/اوشیانا || استرالیا/اوشیانا
p Australia/Oceania: It is the smallest continent of the world. There are 14 countries in the continent of Australia. The continent of Australia has no land borders.
## North America || شمالی امریکہ || شمالي امریکا
p The continent of North America is the third largest continent of the world. Arctic Ocean lies in the North and in the south is the Caribbean Sea and in the west is Pacific Ocean. U.S.A and Canada are also situated in North America.
