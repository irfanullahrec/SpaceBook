## Democracy as most Popular System of Government || جمہوریت بطور مقبول ترین نظامِ حکومت || دیموکراسي د حکومت د تر ټولو مشهور نظام په توګه
p The word democracy is derived from Greek language. The word 'demos' means people living in a country and 'kratos' means rule or power. Thus democracy is the system of government, that is chosen by the people, for the people and by the people. The people have the freedom to choose their own representatives who then govern the country. They are given the right to follow their own religion, they have freedom of speech, movement and right to vote. It is the preferred form of government and famous system because people are given the right to vote through fair elections and they can choose their own representatives.
## Leader and Qualities of a Leader || رہنما اور رہنما کی خوبیاں || مشر او د مشر ځانګړتیاوې
p In simple terms, a leader is a person who leads. He has the qualities of leadership. He motivates the people and influences them. Some of the qualities of a good leader are as follows:
- Honest & Trust Worthy: The leader should be honest that people could trust him. He makes good decisions and policies for people which benefit them. The taxes taken from the people will be used for betterment of the country.
- Takes Risks & Problem Solving Skills: He should be a person who tackles any problem. He can see opportunities in the risks and is a hard task master. He should make right decisions at right time, especially during war, or natural disasters.
- Brave: The leader must be a brave person having steal nerves. He does not come under pressure. He completes the projects he has initiated.
- Leads by example: He influences people by his actions. His actions should prove that he is honest and polite. He favourable and people will benefit from it.
- Empathetic & Compassionate: He understands the problems of people and has a soft corner for it.
- Integrity & Character: He is a person with high morals. People trust him and he is liked by people. He should have a charismatic character.
- Experienced: He has proven records of experience working in similar roles and has sound grip on world politics and economic situations.
## General Elections || عام انتخابات || عمومي ټاکنې
p In Pakistan according to 1973 constitution, general elections are held after every five years for the transition of the government. In these elections the members of national and provincial assemblies are elected throughout the country. Elected members of National Assembly and Provincial Assemblies elect the Prime Minister and Chief Ministers.
