## The North Mountains and North-Western Mountains || شمالی اور شمال مغربی پہاڑ || شمالي او شمال لویدیځ غرونه
p These mountains come first in the list of major landscapes. They have three main ranges, which ranges lie to the north, north-west and north-east of Pakistan. These three main ranges are:
- The Karakoram Range
- The Hindu Kush
- The Himalayas

## 1. The Karakoram Range || ۱۔ سلسلہ کوہِ قراقرم || ۱. د قراقرم لړۍ
p The Karakoram range lies in the north of Pakistan. The average height of Karakoram ranges is 6000 metres. K2 is the highest peak of this range. Most of the part of this range consists of glaciers. Sometimes, road get blocked due to heavy snowfall and land sliding. It is always attracted by tourists and mountaineers for its scenic beauty and challenging geography.
## 2. The Hindu Kush || ۲۔ سلسلہ کوہِ ہندوکش || ۲. د هندوکش لړۍ
p The Hindu Kush Range lies towards the north-west of Pakistan. Its average height is about 5000 metres. Chitral and Dir are famous valleys of this range. Here, the roads get blocked due to heavy snow fall during winter season. Tirich Mir is the highest peak of this range, which is 7690 metres high. Generally, crops can not be grown here. Forests can be seen here.

## 3. The Himalayas || ۳۔ سلسلہ کوہِ ہمالیہ || ۳. د هیمالیا لړۍ
p The average height of Himalayas is 4000 metres. It is located towards north-east of Pakistan. It is always attracted by tourists due to its famous hill stations, such as Murree, Kaghan, Nathia Gali, Ghora Gali, etc. Most of the part of Himalayas lie in Kashmir 'The heaven on Earth'. It's highest peak is 'Nanga Parbat' which is 8126 metres high.
## The Western Mountains || مغربی پہاڑ || لویدیځ غرونه
p The Western Mountains consist of the following ranges.
- Kirthar Hills
- Sulaiman Range
- Waziristan Hills
- Safed Koh (spin ghar)
p Cultivation is not possible here due to rocky and hard landscape. Climate of these mountains is also not suitable for farming. Due to these reasons, there are barren lands.
