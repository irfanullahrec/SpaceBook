## Important Movements || اہم تحریکیں || مهم غورځنګونه
## Caliphate Movement || تحریکِ خلافت || د خلافت غورځنګ
p Turkey was the only state which was a symbol of Caliphate in the early years of 20th century. After the defeat of Germany in first world war, the allied force including England decided to abolish Khilafat and divide Turkey. Muslims launched Khilafat Movement in Sub-continent to save the Khilafat. In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan joined Khilafat Movement in 1920. It strengthened the spiritual ties of the Muslims of the Sub-continent with Turkish Muslims. Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan was elected president of a district Khilafat Committee. It shows how the Muslims of this province were concerned with the cause of Muslims.
## Khudaee Khidmatgar Movement || خدائی خدمتگار تحریک || د خدایي خدمتګار غورځنګ
p As elaborated earlier, the British rulers had taken over the rule of areas included in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa from 1849 onward. The people of this province were against the British rule. Abdul Ghaffar Khan, nicknamed Bacha Khan who was a social worker was a leading personality in these movement. He founded the Khudaee Khidmatgar Tehreek. He remained the severe opponent of the British till the British rule came to an end on 14th August, 1947. Their particular dress gave them distinction. Khudai Khidmatgars were later on called Red Shirts and their movement as the Red Shirts Movement. Within a few years after its creation, the membership rose to the 200,000, which shows its fame and popularity. Later on the organization joined hands with All India Congress in N.W.F.P for their political motives and got majority in every election till through referendum the people of N.W.F.P. cast their vote in favor of Pakistan. The spirit of the movement took its beginning when it was proclaimed as a political party after the creation of Pakistan. The aims and objectives were changed and gradually people lost their interest in the movement.
## Civil Disobedience Movement || سول نافرمانی کی تحریک || د مدني نافرماني غورځنګ
p Like the other parts of India, the civil disobedience movement was also launched in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in 1930. The congress leader wanted help from Khudaee Khidmatgar which was the very popular socio-political organization at that time. There occurred worst kind of massacre in Peshawar followed by some other parts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The movement did not achieve its goals but it infuriated the people against the British rule.
## Inclusion of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in Pakistan || خیبرپختونخوا کا پاکستان میں شمول || د خیبرپښتونخوا په پاکستان کې شاملېدل
p As a result of long and constant struggle of Muslims for independence for Muslims to live according to their own religion Islam, the subcontinent was divided into two independent states Pakistan and India. The states and provinces were asked to join the state which they liked. The people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa decided in favour of Pakistan. Therefore, it became part of Pakistan.
