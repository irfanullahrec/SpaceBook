## The Potwar Plateau and the Salt Range || سطح مرتفع پوٹھوہار اور کوہستانِ نمک || د پوټوهار سطح او مالګینې لړۍ
p The Potwar Plateau and the Salt Range are located between the River Indus and River Jhelum. The average height of Potwar Plateau is 300 to 600 metres above the sea level while the Salt range raises to the height of 700-900 metres.
## Deserts || صحرا || صحرا ګانې
p Deserts are the locations on earth that receive less than ten inches of rain per year. Deserts can be hot or cold. The following are the main deserts of Pakistan. These are as follows:
- Thal Desert
- Thar Desert
- Cholistan Desert
- Kharan Desert
box info | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهېږئ؟
p The Thar Desert is the largest desert of Pakistan.
endbox
p The Cholistan and Thal deserts are in the area of Punjab. Kharan Desert is located in Balochistan. Thar Desert is located in Sindh. In Pakistan, deserts are extremely hot places. Rainfall and water is scarce. Due to these reasons, vegetation is not possible. Mostly, sand dunes can be seen in deserts. People live nomadic life and move from place to place in search of water.
## Coastal Areas || ساحلی علاقے || ساحلي سیمې
p Coastal areas of Pakistan are located in the south of Pakistan. It is consisted of southern part of Sindh and Balochistan. These are the most important areas of Pakistan on which the economy of the country depends. Famous ports of this area are Karachi, Gawadar and Pasni. The length of the coastal areas of Pakistan is almost 1000 km long.
