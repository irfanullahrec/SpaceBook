## Allama Iqbal || علامہ اقبال || علامه اقبال
p Allama Iqbal was born on November 9, 1877. He was a Muslim scholar and our national poet. He got higher studies in England and Germany. He was very upset after seeing the behaviour of Hindus and British towards Muslims. He had written poetry in Urdu and Persian Language. He wished to awaken the Muslims to come out of slavery and remember the glorious past of their Muslim ancestors. He was the man who dreamed of Pakistan. He wrote many books for the youth. He also wrote poems for children. He died on April 21, 1938. His tomb is in Badshahi Mosque.
## Fatima Jinnah || فاطمہ جناح || فاطمه جناح
p Fatima Jinnah was born on July 31, 1893. She is called Mother of the Nation. She was a dental surgeon by profession. She was the younger sister of Quaid-e-Azam and supported her brother throughout the freedom struggle. She was a strong supporter of Two Nation Theory and represented women in All Indian Muslim League. She was strong advocate of women rights. She opened many educational institutions for women as she wanted women to prosper in every field of life. She also settled the refugees in Pakistan. She also provided assistance in funding and maintaining scholarships in schools. She took care of her brother, when he was very ill before his death. She died on July 9, 1967. Her resting place is Mazar-e-Quaid.
