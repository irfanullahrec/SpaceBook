## Resolving the Conflicts || تنازعات کا حل || د شخړو حل
p There are many ways and means to settle down and resolve the conflicts among people at different levels. The best way to resolve them is the mutual understanding and negotiations. Such matters may be resolved locally so that they do not cause big conflicts and unrest in society. In case, such conflicts are not possible to resolve through negotiations and 'give and take' policy, then the laws of the state come to action and matter is referred to the courts of law.
p We may avoid such conflicts by bringing positivity in our attitudes and behaviour with others. Nobody likes conflicts. The people try to avoid conflicts to live peacefully. They want to promote peace in society. Small conflicts cause bigger conflicts and become reason for serious problems for peaceful living. Managing interpersonal conflict allows people to work together in finding a resolution. Colleagues can also improve their relationships and even come up with all new strategies or solutions to problems. Start from identifying exactly what the conflict is about, including what event started it and how each party reacted to the situation. Look at the situation from each person's point of view to determine what each party wants and needs for the resolution.
## Managing Conflict at Peers Level || ساتھیوں کی سطح پر تنازع کا انتظام || د ملګرو په کچه د شخړې اداره کول
p Openly discuss what caused the conflict and how each party feels about it. This step ensures everyone is involved in coming up with a solution and can talk through the issue honestly. Have each team member clarify why they hold their position and discuss what information is the basis of these beliefs. Make sure everyone is satisfied with the decision and committed to the proposed strategy.
