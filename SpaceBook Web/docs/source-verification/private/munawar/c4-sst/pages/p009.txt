## Advantages of Diversity || تنوع کے فوائد || د رنګارنګۍ ګټې
## Social Coherence and Well-being || سماجی ہم آہنگی اور بھلائی || ټولنیز همغږي او هوساینه
p Diversity makes all of us more open-minded, both in our communities and working places. It means that we accept others with different talents and abilities. We come to know that all people working and living around us are different but they are all human beings. We give them proper respect and appreciate their works. It brings prosperity and unity among people. We help each other in doing different things. We praise others' positive thoughts and ideas.
## Collective Effort and Inclusive Society || اجتماعی کوشش اور شمولیتی معاشرہ || ډله ییزه هڅه او ټولنه
p Diversity helps in building a collective society. It creates a society, in which every person has an active role to play. It leads to the foundation of a society that is based on basic values of equality, social justice and human rights. It ensures freedom, for everyone. As a result, every person works hard for his society and fellow beings.
## Rapid Progress || تیز ترقی || چټکه پرمختګ
p When people work and try different things together, the outcome is always a brilliant success. Success leads to a rapid progress. For example, if the teachers and students work equally hard in their capacities, the result is always a great success. Similarly, diversity in a society leads to a rapid progress and the prosperity of people.
## Maintenance of Tolerance and Patience || رواداری اور صبر کی بقا || د زغم او صبر ساتنه
p When there is diversity in a society, people should accept others with open hearts. They become patient and humble. They also accept others' faults and forgive them. In fact, they become good human beings. They look at the brighter side of things happening around them. They know, that every one does not have similar abilities. Hence, they forgive each other's faults and become united.
