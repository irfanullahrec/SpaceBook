## The Indus Valley Civilization || وادیٔ سندھ کی تہذیب || د سند دره تمدن
p The Indus Valley Civilization is the oldest and yet modern civilization of it's time. It was located around the Indus River. It dates back between 1700-2500 BCE. It had two main big cities, that are Harappa and Mohinjo-daro. Traces of civilization were also found in Sutkagan Dor in the province of Balochistan, Ropar in North Western India, Gulf of Khambat in South East of Karachi.
- The people had a great taste of art and were literate. They were good at making good houses and town planning, making proper drainage system.
- Water reservoir system and tanks were made by them.
- They had developed their own script which consisted of 200-250 characters. The people benefited from the Land of Indus River. The land was fertile.
- People used to grow wheat and other crops which had foundation of good agriculture. Geologists think that this area was inhabited by humans around 7,000 years back.
- People earned their livelihood by herding sheep, cattle and goats. They are grams, barley, wheat and flax. The people had good knowledge about astronomy and solar, lunar eclipses.
- The archaeologists believe that the Early Dynastic period revolved from 3050 to 2686 BC. The civilization was famous for its symbolic language, farming, arts, great architecture building pyramids in the deserts and preserving the bodies etc.
- People widely grew mustard, peas, and sesame. After excavation in 1921 unique and different sort of artifacts were found like the seals of different animals made of talc like tigers, antelopes, elephants and rhinoceros. Sculptures made of stone were also found.
- Historians believe that the civilization disappeared from the Earth, after a heavy flood or Earthquake.
- The reasons are not evident.
