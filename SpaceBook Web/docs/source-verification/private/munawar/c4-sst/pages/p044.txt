p Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) got the first revelation when he was forty years of age. On preaching the message of Allah Almighty, many pagans of Makkah went against him. They treated him and his companions very cruelly. As a result, Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) decided to migrate to Yathreb which was named as Madinatun Nabi after that. The people of Madina cooperated with him (PBUH) and Islam spread with leaps and bounds. The Quraish of Makkah fought three big battles with Muslims of Madina but they failed to stop the spreading of Islam. The Muslims entered Makkah as victorious after ten years of migration. A unique revolution had occurred in the lives of the people of entire Arabia.
p Let's have a look on some major political and social measures of his (PBUH) life.
## Leadership || قیادت || مشرتوب
p Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) proved to be a dynamic, creative and noble leader who united the tribes of Arab and established one state. His (PBUH) diplomatic strategies to save the country from its enemies were outstanding. Moreover, he (PBUH) sent representatives to various heads of states and tried to create understanding of Islam. Madina had no formal army, however, he (PBUH) made incredibly amazing defense plans and personal army who fought 28 battles Rasulullah (PBUH) himself took participation in most of them. He (PBUH) promoted harmony, peace and free society where there was no suppression. He (PBUH) said, 'Whoever kills a non-Muslim who has an agreement with the Muslims, he will not smell the fragrance of Paradise.'
## Economic Achievements || معاشی کامیابیاں || اقتصادي لاس ته راوړنې
p Madinah Munawwarah (Yathrib) was of significance importance because it was on the trade route across the Red Sea to Egypt and Africa. The state of Madina under the guidance of Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) utilized the trade for making the economy strong. Many battles between the Muslims of Yathrib with Quraish of Makkah (The non-believers) interrupted the economic growth but stress working on trade and agriculture sustained strong economic position of the state of Madina. The concept of ownership as given by Islam, establishment of Baitul Maal, system of Zakat, Ushr, Fitrana and implementation of Jizia (a kind of tax on non-Muslims for their protection and welfare) had positive impacts on the economy of the State of Madina and uplift of the common man.
## Hazrat Abu Bakar (632-634) || حضرت ابوبکرؓ (۶۳۲-۶۳۴) || حضرت ابوبکر (۶۳۲-۶۳۴)
p Abu Bakar (RA) was the closest friend, advisor, companion and father in law of Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH). He (RA) became the first rightly guided Caliph after his death. Hazrat Abu Bakar (RA) was the first among adults men who embraced Islam. He (RA) was soft hearted, courageous, and rich. He (RA) used to help people and loved simple clothing. During his reign, he (RA) suppressed the tribal political uprising. The Iraq and Syrian conquest was also started. Battle of Yamama was fought in his supervision, in which multiple Huffaz were martyred so he decided to pen down the Holy Quran according to its revelation order.
## Hazrat Umar (634-644) || حضرت عمرؓ (۶۳۴-۶۴۴) || حضرت عمر (۶۳۴-۶۴۴)
p The second Muslim caliph was Hazrat Umar (RA) who had first opposed Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) but became a Muslim and later the Caliph.
