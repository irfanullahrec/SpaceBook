box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Explain the term 'Diversity' / 'Tolerance' to the students by giving them examples.
- Ask the students to share their ideas about children living in other provinces of Pakistan. If they are arranged about it, correct their ideas and promote the concept of national identity.
- Explain the term 'Tolerance' giving examples from everyday life.
- Explain the importance of tolerance in a society.
- Detail the causes of conflicts.
- Discuss with the students how conflicts can be resolved among people.
