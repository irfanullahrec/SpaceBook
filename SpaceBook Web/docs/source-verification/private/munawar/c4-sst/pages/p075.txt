p Some safety measures must be taken before, during and after earthquake in order to save the lives and properties of people. Let us look at some of the precautions we must take.
p i. Structure of building: Always use good quality material to make homes, offices and buildings. The buildings will not collapse.
p ii. Use the staircase and be careful about the falling debris: We must use stairs and avoid going near the building from where debris is falling.
p iii. Store and secure all harmful/breakable items and flammable materials: Those things that catch fire, harm and break must be kept at a proper place.
p iv. Move out of the building quickly: The building must be left quickly. Move to an open area. Avoid going near the transformers and electric poles.
p v. Avoid driving over bridges and landslides: Do not attempt to go across bridges as they may fall. It is preferred to park car on road side while driving.
p vi. Prepare first aid box, water bottles and perishable things with you: Always prepare a first aid box where you have pain killers and bandages. Water is must as it stops dehydration and some snacks that can be eaten to carry out functions.
## Floods || سیلاب || سیلابونه
p Floods occur due to excessive rainfall that raises water levels in rivers, streams and channels. Floods damage the buildings, bridges, roads and canals. People die and drown in water. The houses and buildings come under the water that results in huge losses. The stagnant and long hold water also increases diseases. There are certain precautions that we must take during floods.
