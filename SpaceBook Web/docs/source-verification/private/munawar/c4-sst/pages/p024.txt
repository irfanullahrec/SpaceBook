## Communication || ابلاغ || اړیکې
p Mustafa was not attending school for a month or so. His friends were worried and they did not know how to talk and tell him about daily school tasks. The teacher told them that they need to do communication with him in order to tell him about his missed work. The students were a little surprised to hear the word 'communication' as it was a new word for them. The teacher told them, 'communication' means the act of transferring information from one individual to another. You often receive and exchange information.

Visual communication is done by seeing the other person while conveying your point. Written communication means by writing to someone. It can be writing letters, and email.
