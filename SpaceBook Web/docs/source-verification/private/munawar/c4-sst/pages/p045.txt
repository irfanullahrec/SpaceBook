## Hazrat Usman (644-656) || حضرت عثمانؓ (۶۴۴-۶۵۶) || حضرت عثمان (۶۴۴-۶۵۶)
p The third Caliph was Hazrat Usman (RA) who was one of those Muslims who embraced Islam early. He (RA) was very rich and spent his wealth for the Islam and the poor Muslims. During his reign, he (RA) annexed many surrounding areas and extended the Islamic state very much. He (RA) was wise, pious and kind hearted. His modesty was unique. He (RA) worked for the welfare of the people, carpeted roads, constructed inns, made drinking water wells, made navy and one of his major contribution is the extension of the Masjid-e-Nabwi. He (RA) is critically important in Islamic history because his death marked the beginning of open religious and political conflicts within the Islamic community. The official version of the Holy Quran was developed in his era. A strong rebellion was opened against him and he (RA) was assassinated at his home while reciting the Holy Quran.
## Hazrat Ali (656-661) || حضرت علیؓ (۶۵۶-۶۶۱) || حضرت علي (۶۵۶-۶۶۱)
p The last Caliph was Hazrat Ali (RA) who was the cousin and son in law of Rasoolullah (PBUH). At the age of ten he (RA) embraced Islam and participated in all battles with Rasoolullah (PBUH). As a warrior, he (RA) was fearless. As an educator, he (RA) was matchless in his service to Islam. He (RA) was assassinated after five years of his rule. He (RA) established the rule of Allah Almighty and worked according to the will of the people. He (RA) would get consent of his pious companions before making the decision as a head of Islamic state.
