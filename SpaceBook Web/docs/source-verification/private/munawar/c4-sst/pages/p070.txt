## Prevention of Negative Impacts of Human Activity || انسانی سرگرمیوں کے منفی اثرات کی روک تھام || د انساني فعالیتونو د منفي اغېزو مخنیوی
p The following preventive measures may be adopted for negative impacts of human activities on the life of the animals and people:
- Strict laws be made and implemented to prevent deforestation and cutting of trees. New trees should be planted and existing trees are to be properly looked after.
- The industries and factories be established away from residential areas.
- Wastage of mineral and water resources be minimized to barest limits.
- Rain water be preserved through construction of dams and lakes so that it can be utilized when there is scarcity of water for agriculture and drinking.

## Agriculture || زراعت || کرنه
p Pakistan is an agricultural country. Most of the people of Pakistan are directly or indirectly involved in the profession of agriculture. Agriculture involves mostly the growing of crops. The plain areas of Pakistan are suitable for agriculture. The water from dams is provided for irrigation through a network of canals. The main crops are Rabi and Khareef.
p Khareef crops are cultivated in the months from April to June and harvested in the months from October to December. Rice, cotton, sugarcane, and maize are main Khareef crops.
p Rabi crops are cultivate in the months from October to December and harvested in the months of April and May. Wheat, grams, pulses, barley are main Rabi crops.
