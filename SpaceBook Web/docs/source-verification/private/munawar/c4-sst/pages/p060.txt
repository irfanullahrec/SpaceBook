## Border || سرحد || پوله
p The border in a map is a thick line which separates different cities, countries and it also tells where the map stops. They are also called the real lines.
## Orientation || سمت || سمت
p Orientation is called the directions on the map. The directions help in understanding, where to go in order to reach desired location. The four cardinal points on maps are North, South, East and West. A straight arrow pointing northward, is called North Arrow. A compass is an instrument used for navigation in a map.
## Legend || علامات کی کنجی || نښې
p Legend is also called 'key'. It helps us to understand symbolic representation on a map. It tells us what each symbol means.
## Title || عنوان || سرلیک
p It is a brief description or summary of the map. It tells us about the content of the map. This picture shows us that this is the world map.
