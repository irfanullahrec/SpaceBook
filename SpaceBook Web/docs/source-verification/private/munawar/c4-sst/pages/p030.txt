## 5. Pakistan to be a Welfare State || ۵۔ پاکستان ایک فلاحی ریاست || ۵. پاکستان یو هوساینه دولت
p The constitution reflects a welfare spirit and emphasizes the following elements:
- Removing illiteracy, and promote educational and economical welfare
- Provision of good human conditions for work
- Prevention from gambling and liquor consumption
- Provision of basic necessities like food, housing, clothing, education, and medical relief to the citizens
## 6. Independent Judiciary || ۶۔ آزاد عدلیہ || ۶. خپلواکه قضا
p The constitution of Pakistan provides independence of Judiciary. The independence of judiciary implies the ability of courts and judges to perform their duties free of influence or control by governmental or private actors. The term also refers to the kind of independence that courts and judges ought to possess.
## Rights of Pakistani Citizens || پاکستانی شہریوں کے حقوق || د پاکستاني وګړو حقونه
p Constitution of Pakistan provide certain rights to its citizens. These rights have been listed below.
- Right to life, property, profession
- Liberty of thought and expression
- Freedom of association, religion, equality of citizens
