p You are a student indeed. You will surely buy a pen. Hence, the pen will be your economic choice. Therefore, the act of choosing and giving importance to the needs that are most important and giving up the less important desires is called economic choice.
p Preference plays an important role in making economic choices. When there are two needs in mind with same price but with different importance, everybody makes a reasonable choice. He/she buys more important item and leaves the other. Similarly, He/she wants to buy two items with different prices, he chooses the one that doesn't affect his budget. This is also called economic choice.

## Services || خدمات || خدمتونه
p In this world, nobody can live independently. Every one has to live in cooperation with each other. In the modern age, we have to benefit others to enjoy benefits from them. People give these benefits to one another by using their mental, physical and technical abilities. They are actually providing services to each other. A service is something that someone does for you, like treat you as a patient or even teaches you in your studies. You can say a service provider can be a doctor or a teacher.
p A teacher teaches the students, the plumber repairs the sanitary items, the electrician repairs electricity, the hair-dresser cuts hair and the mason builds houses for others. People working in the different banks and public offices also provide services. These services are not material goods but fulfill others' needs.
box info | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهېږئ؟
p Telling someone the way to an office or place is also a service.
endbox
