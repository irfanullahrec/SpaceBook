## Languages || زبانیں || ژبې
p The most important aspect of culture is the language. Urdu is the national language of Pakistan that emerged when Muslims settled in sub-continent. Balochi, Pashto, Sindhi, Punjabi and Kashmiri are the languages of different areas. Rehman Baba, Bullay Shah and Shah Abdul Latif Bhattai are great poets who used language as a way to teach religion to people. Altaf Hussain Hali and Allama Muhammad Iqbal used the language skills to influence lives of people. Poetry, drama, criticism and journalism are developed because of language.
## Interfaith Harmony and Festivals of Minorities || بین المذاہب ہم آہنگی اور اقلیتوں کے تہوار || د ادیانو ترمنځ همغږي او د اقلیتونو جشنونه
p Interfaith harmony is a condition in which people of different religions live and practice their faith and religion peacefully without any violence. Pakistan's flag has a white part that represents minorities living in Pakistan and their rights. There are many Hindus, Sikhs, Christians and Buddhists who live in Pakistan. Every person has the right to celebrate their religious festivals. They have the right to go to church, Mandir, Gurdwara and temples.
## Interfaith Harmony in Celebrating Festivals || تہواروں کی تقریبات میں بین المذاہب ہم آہنگی || په جشنونو کې د ادیانو ترمنځ همغږي
p Some of the festivals celebrated by the people of other religions like Dewali, Christmas, Holi, etc. Pakistan is the land where people belonging to various religions are living with complete sense of security and well being. As our state religion is Islam, the other communities are called minorities. But they enjoy religious, cultural and political liberties under our constitution. They are represented by the 'white portion' in our National Flag. They celebrate their festivals with complete fervour and religious sanctity. The government has taken a great initiative by giving public holidays
