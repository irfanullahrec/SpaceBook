p The history of Ancient Egypt has been presented with the mention of 'Dynasties' that ruled Egypt. They were the families that passed the leadership down from one family member to another. There were about 30 dynasties that ruled ancient Egypt during the course of 3000 years.
p Kingdoms and Periods: There were three primary kingdoms that historians used to define periods of ancient Egypt. They were the Old, Middle, and New Kingdoms.
p Early Dynastic (I - III) Period (2950 - 2575 BC): It was the beginning of ancient Egyptian Civilization. The first pharaoh of Egypt, Menes, united the Upper and Lower parts of Egypt into a single civilization. He chose the midpoint of the two lands in a city called Memphis to establish his capital. During this time the Egyptians developed writing which they used for making records and running the government. The first pyramid was built by the Pharaoh Djoser and the famous Egyptian architect Imhotep.
p Old Kingdom (IV - VIII) (2575 - 2150 BC): In the fourth dynasty, the Great Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx were built. This is often called the Age of the Pyramids. The end of the Old Kingdom is a time of poverty and famine.
p First Intermediate Period (2150 - 1975 BC) Dynasties IX - XI: Egypt was split back into two countries. The Old Kingdom ended and the first Intermediate period began.
p Middle Kingdom (1975 - 1640 BC) Dynasties XI - XIV: The Pharaoh Mentuhotep II reunites the two parts of Egypt under one rule marking the start of the Middle Kingdom. The Egyptians started to use irrigation to carry water from the Nile to their crops.
p Second Intermediate Period (1640 - 1520 BC) Dynasties XV - XVII: The Middle Kingdom ended and the Second Intermediate Period began. Some of the dynasties at the end of the middle kingdom and during this period only last for a short time.
p Third Intermediate Period (1075 - 653 BC) Dynasties XXI - XXIV: The New Kingdom came to end when Egypt became divided. Egypt grew weaker and eventually it was conquered by the Assyrian empire near the end of this period.
p Late Period (653 - 332 BC) Dynasties XXV - XXX: The late period began in 525 B.C. The Persians conquered Egypt and ruled for over 100 years, till 332 B.C. Alexander the Great and the Greeks conquer Egypt. He founded the great city of Alexandria which became the new capital. 30 B.C. The last pharaoh, Cleopatra VII, died and civilization was ended.
