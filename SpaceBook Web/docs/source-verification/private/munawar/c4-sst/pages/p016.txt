## a. By Following Traffic Rules || الف۔ ٹریفک قوانین پر عمل کر کے || الف. د ترافیکي مقرراتو په تعقیب
p People, who live in a civilized society, respect the laws. Following traffic rules shows how much they respect the laws of their country. Good citizens always respect all laws and principles. These rules save us from accidents on the roads. Good citizens:
- walk on footpath.
- follow traffic lights called signals and traffic sign boards.
- always cross roads by using zebra crossing when a signal is red.
- always keep safe distance and do not cross the speed limit.
## b. By Keeping the Environment Clean || ب۔ ماحول کو صاف رکھ کر || ب. چاپېریال پاک ساتلو سره
p Good citizens keep their environment clean. They do not throw litters on roads; they also try to use the vehicles that do not emit extra smoke. They take great care of trees and plants to reduce pollution. Everything in our surroundings creates our environment. Good citizens:
- keep the environment clean by throwing trash into the bins.
- collect garbage from the ground. Take care of the plants at public places such as in park, on roads, and ground and public places.
- Grow plants and trees because they clean the air and provide oxygen to our environment.
## c. WASH || ج۔ واش (WASH) || ج. WASH
p 'WASH' is the abbreviation of 'Water' Sanitation and Hygiene. It aims at prevention against diseases caused by unclean water and sanitation irregularities. The basic objects of 'WASH' at local level are as follows:
- Arrangements for the provision of clean drinking water.
- Awareness and education about the physical cleanliness and hygienic living (Sanitation and use of toilet etc.)
- Assurance that people are aware and better informed of the principles of hygienic living
## Common Etiquettes || عام آداب || عام اداب
p Good citizens have good etiquettes. Look at the following manners:
- They speak politely and never hurt others' feelings.
- They eat peacefully and share things with others.
- They respect their elders and love the children.
