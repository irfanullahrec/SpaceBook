## Mr. Ahmed || جناب احمد || ښاغلی احمد
p Mr. Ahmed is a soft spoken and humble person. One day he noticed that dirty water ran across the streets. He got very upset. He knew that Mr. Zafar had thrown garbage into the gutter that might cause blockage. Instead of fighting, he called for the janitor and fixed the issue. Mr. Zafar realized his mistake. He apologized and promised that he would never repeat this mistake.
