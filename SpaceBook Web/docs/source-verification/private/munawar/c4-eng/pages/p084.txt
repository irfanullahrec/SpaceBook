p When a house catches fire, people may worry about their pets or a favourite toy. If there is fire, they have to leave them behind. They can tell the fire rescue people about any pets or costly things that were left behind. They may be able to save them for the owners. Their life is more precious thing than anything else. The first priority is people's personal safety.
p Knowing the cause of fire is important for the fire fighters. When people are safe, their co-operation with fire-fighting staff will be very useful in controlling the fire. They should not waste a single moment in calling the fire brigade.
p It is very important to get awareness for rescue and escape in case of emergency fire. A person's clothes may catch fire by the accident. In this situation, he should avoid running. Instead, he should stop and drop himself to the ground, covering his face with his hands and roll. This will cut off the air and put out the flames. An easy way to remember this drill is: 'Stop, drop, and roll!'
p Nobody should play with matches, lighters, and other fire sources. Everybody should stay away from fireplaces, candles, and stoves. By following this piece of advice, people will be able to keep themselves safe from sudden fire.
