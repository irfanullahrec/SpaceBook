p The function started with recitation from the Holy Quran and Naat-e-Rasool-e-Maqbool ﷺ. The students made speeches highlighting the importance of education for women. Then, the audience appreciated the ideas and expressions of student speakers. Then, Sana was called to come and deliver her speech. She started her speech by referring to the earlier times of Islamic history. She talked about the status of women as a mother, sister, daughter and as the guardian of household. Talking about the importance of education for women, she said that it has been unanimously agreed by Islamic scholars that the first word revealed of the Holy Qur'an was "Iqra" meaning "Read". Knowledge is an important part of the Islamic faith, that makes no distinction between men and women. It is the duty for every Muslim. For example; one Hadith states: "Seeking knowledge is a duty of every Muslim, man or woman". (Al-Tirmidhi Hadith 218)
p About the gender equity in Islam, the Holy Rasool Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ said: "If a daughter is born to a person and he brings her up, gives her a good education and trains her in the art of life, I myself stand between him and the hell-fire." (Kanz-ul-Ummal, reported by Abdullah ibn Mas'ud)
p Sana said that it was the confidence of Muslim women inferred by Islam that we could see great women leaders of Muslim Ummah like great Ummahat-ul-Momineen (RA). Later, we could see the women like Fatima Jinnah, Begum Ra'ana Liaqat Ali Khan, Begum Shah Nawaz and many more. They worked side by side with Quaid-e-Azam and got an independent state called Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
p She continued that gender equity is the key to establish a balanced society. Depriving more than half of population from equal rights is the practical demise of a nation. You can see the results of academic exams as well as competitive examinations at national level. They prove that girls students excel in every field of life. Depriving them from equal opportunities can never be justified. We can see women working with equal efficiency with men. They are working as women fighter pilots, engineers, architects and as defenders of frontiers of the country.
p She concluded her speech with the recitation of verses of the Holy Quran, she said that women need to put in their best and prove that they don't lack in any field of life. The audience gave her a big applause by clapping and cheering.
