The garden became lifeless (describe the garden without children: trees had no leaves, birds flew away, no flowers, no colour and no life). qp i | Retell the story of 'The Selfish Giant' in your own words.
sol Once upon a time there lived a big and angry giant. He had a beautiful garden between a town and the school, where the children liked to play. The giant built a wall around it because he did not want to share it. The garden became lifeless: the trees had no leaves, the birds flew away and spring never came. One morning the giant saw the children sitting in the trees and the garden full of flowers. He realized his mistake, knocked down the wall and let the children play. The garden became evergreen and the children called him 'Generous Uncle'. The moral is that sharing with others brings happiness. ## SpaceBook answer from the story on p. 91 and 92. || Once upon a time there lived a big and angry giant.
