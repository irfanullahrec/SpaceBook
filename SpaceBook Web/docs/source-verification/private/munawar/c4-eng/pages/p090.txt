p Once upon a time, there was a very beautiful garden between a town and the school. Every day, the children liked to play in that beautiful garden after their school time. The selfish man did not allow them to play there. The children did not like him because he was selfish. They called him 'Selfish Giant' for his selfishness. The garden looked bare without children.
p All of a sudden, the selfish giant went to visit his friend who lived far away. In his absence, the children got a chance to play in the garden. They were happy. In spring, the trees had pink flowers on their branches. In the autumn, the trees bore round golden fruits.
p The selfish giant came back after seven years. He saw the children in his garden. He became furious. He shouted at them in a gruff voice. He asked them to run away. He built a wall around the garden and put up a big notice with a warning "KEEP OUT!"
