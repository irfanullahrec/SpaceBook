box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- In English, there are two main types of questions which are: Yes / No Questions and Wh - Questions.
- Forming Questions with 'Yes' or 'No': the questions beginning with 'No' or 'Yes' are made using the verb is, am, are, were, have, had, etc. in the beginning of question sentence.
- We shall learn to make sentences beginning only with 'do', 'did' or 'does'.
- Examples: Do we come to school together? Does your friend come to visit you every day? Am I writing correct English? Were the students sitting in library yesterday? Had Ali reached when you left home? Did you walk in the park yesterday? Is the teacher teaching us English? Are you the students of grade-IV? Have you done your homework? Was Ali speaking English in the last period?
endbox
