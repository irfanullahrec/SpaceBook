## 1. Oral Communication Skill || ۱۔ زبانی ابلاغ کی مہارت || ۱. خولنۍ اړیکې مهارت
exercise Exercise (Tolerance: Oral Communication)
box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
p Silent letters are letters of the English alphabet used in words but they are not pronounced.
endbox
q A | Silent Letters in Words. Read and say the words with silent letters aloud. || الفاظ میں خاموش حروف۔ خاموش حروف والے الفاظ پڑھیں اور بلند آواز سے کہیں۔ || په ټکو کې چپ توري. هغه ټکي ولولئ او په لوړ غږ ووایاست چې چپ توري لري.
qp i | Letter 'b' is not pronounced when it comes after 'm' or before 't' at the end of a word.
sol comb, thumb, limb, debt, doubt || comb, thumb, limb, debt, doubt || comb, thumb, limb, debt, doubt
qp ii | Letter 'c' is often not pronounced in the combination 'sc'.
sol scissors, scene, muscle || scissors, scene, muscle || scissors, scene, muscle
qp iii | Letter 'd' is not pronounced in the following common words.
sol Wednesday, sandwich, handsome, handkerchief, pledge, hedge, bridge, etc. || Wednesday, sandwich, handsome, handkerchief, pledge, hedge, bridge, etc. || Wednesday, sandwich, handsome, handkerchief, pledge, hedge, bridge, etc.
qp iv | Letter 'e' is most commonly not pronounced at the end of these words.
sol peace, home, made, horse || peace, home, made, horse || peace, home, made, horse
qp v | Letter 'g' is often not pronounced when it comes before n.
sol foreign, design, align || foreign, design, align || foreign, design, align
qp vi | Letter 'h' is not pronounced when it comes after w or in the beginning of many of these words.
sol when, white, where, hour, honest, heir || when, white, where, hour, honest, heir || when, white, where, hour, honest, heir
qp vii | Letter 'k' is not pronounced when it comes before n in the beginning of a word.
sol knee, know, and knock || knee, know, and knock || knee, know, and knock
qp viii | Letter 'l' is not pronounced after the vowels a, o and u.
sol calm, would, should, could, etc. || calm, would, should, could, etc. || calm, would, should, could, etc.
qp ix | Letter 'n' is not pronounced when it comes after m at the end of a word.
sol column, autumn, hymn || column, autumn, hymn || column, autumn, hymn
qp x | Letter 's' is not pronounced after 'i'.
sol island, isle || island, isle || island, isle
qp xi | Letter 't' is often silent in some words.
sol often, listen, depot, whistle, hustle and bustle, etc. || often, listen, depot, whistle, hustle and bustle, etc. || often, listen, depot, whistle, hustle and bustle, etc.
qp xii | Letter 'w' is silent in the words like:
sol who, wrong, write, two, sword, wrist, answer, etc. || who, wrong, write, two, sword, wrist, answer, etc. || who, wrong, write, two, sword, wrist, answer, etc.
