box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- There are some words to connect thoughts, sentences and paragraphs together like 'and', 'but', 'or' etc.
- We generally call them joining words or conjunctions. They work as transitional words when they connect thoughts, sentences and paragraphs.
endbox
p Examples of Transitional words: I ran to school, and I got there just in time. ('and' is a transition word that connects two occurrences equally.) I ran to school, but I was still late. ('but' introduces contrast.) I could run to school, or I could stay at home and work. ('or' presents two different options.)
