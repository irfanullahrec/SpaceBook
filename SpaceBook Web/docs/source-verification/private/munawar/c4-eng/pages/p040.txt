q C | Recognize and use words similar and opposite in meaning. || ہم معنی اور متضاد الفاظ کو پہچانیں اور استعمال کریں۔ || هم معنی او متضاد ټکي وپېژنئ او وکاروئ.
sol Synonyms are words with similar meaning (quick - fast); antonyms are words with opposite meaning (good - bad). ## From p. 41. || Synonyms are words with similar meaning (quick - fast); antonyms are words with opposite meaning (good - bad). || Synonyms are words with similar meaning (quick - fast); antonyms are words with opposite meaning (good - bad).
box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- The words that convey similar meanings are called synonyms. Examples: quick - fast, answer - reply, pretty - beautiful, old - aged.
- The words that convey different or opposite meaning are called antonyms. Examples: morning - evening, good - bad, famous - unknown.
endbox
