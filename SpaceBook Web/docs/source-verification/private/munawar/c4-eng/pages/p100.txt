box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- In English, there are two main types of questions which are: Yes / No Questions and Wh - Questions. Forming Questions with 'Yes' or 'No': the questions beginning with 'no' or 'yes' are made using the verb is, am, are, were, have, had, etc. in the beginning of a question sentence.
- We will learn to make sentences beginning with 'do', 'did', 'does' only. Examples: Do we come to school together? Did you walk in the park yesterday? Does your friend come to visit you every day?
endbox
