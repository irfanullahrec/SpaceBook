## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
p (The list is in very small type and is not read.)
p Trees can be short, trees can be tall,
p Whatever is the kind, I like them all.
p They give us oxygen, they give us shade,
p We give them love, we give them care.
p From berries to cherries, from apples to grapes,
p They give us different fruits, and grow in different shapes.
p They need water, soil, space and light,
p We give them those things day and night.
box info | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهېږئ؟
p Trees give out oxygen which we breathe in to live.
endbox
box note | While-reading || پڑھتے ہوئے || د لوستلو پر مهال
- What is the use of oxygen we get from trees?
- What do we give the trees all the time?
endbox
