p This lesson is about giving equal chances for men and women in every field of life. There must not be any discrimination between boys and girls. Our religion, Islam, insists upon gender equality. Depriving more than half of the population of the country (women) of their equal rights can never be justified. The women have shown great qualities of head and heart in Islamic history. There must be given equal opportunities in life to prove their capabilities of head and heart.

## 1. Oral Communication Skill || ۱۔ زبانی ابلاغ کی مہارت || ۱. خولنۍ اړیکې مهارت
exercise Exercise (Educating Generations: Oral Communication)
q A | Dialogue. The English teacher of class-iv arranged a discussion in classroom the next day on 'Gender Equality'. All students participated in turns. Teacher: Dear students, you listened the speeches on 'Gender Equity'. Express your views. Ahmad: Sir, I feel really depressed when I saw girls out of school working in short age not getting education. Waleed: Sir, we are a developing nation. We should focus on women education specially. Ayesha: Sir, the government should provide equal chances, for both genders to make Pakistan strong. Teacher: Your opinions are really appreciating. I would like all of you to prepare posters to display at various places for general awareness of the common people. (The students responded immediately on their teacher's instructions and brought very impressive posters the very next day. The teacher appreciates their efforts.) || مکالمہ۔ || خبرې اترې.
qp i | Practise the dialogue in groups and express your own views on 'Gender Equality'.
