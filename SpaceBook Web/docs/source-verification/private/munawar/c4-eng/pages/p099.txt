p Irregular plural nouns are the nouns that do not become plural by adding -s or -es, as most nouns in English language do. For example: The plural form of man is men, not mans. The plural form of woman is women.
endbox
box info | The most common irregular plural nouns are || عام ترین بے قاعدہ جمع اسم || ډېر عام بې قاعدې جمع نومونه
- (i) Nouns ending in -f and -fe: To make a plural of a word ending in -f, change the f to a v and add es. Similarly, if a word ends in -fe, change the f to a v and add es. The result for both types is a plural that ends in -ves. knife - knives, leaf - leaves, calf - calves; wife - wives, life - lives, wolf - wolves.
- (ii) Nouns Ending in -o: potato - potatoes, hero - heroes; tomato - tomatoes, veto - vetoes.
endbox
