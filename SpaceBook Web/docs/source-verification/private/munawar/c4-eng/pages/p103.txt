p Mr Nazir Ahmad Gondal has done M.A, B.Ed. (English Language and Literature) from University of the Punjab. He has served in the Education Branch of Pakistan Air Force. During his tenure of service, he has been performing instructional as well as school administration duties at various P.A.F. Schools/Colleges and training institutions. After his retirement, he remained associated with some well-established and renowned Publishers of text books. p Mr Muhammad Anwar Shaz has done M.Phil in English Literature. He has valuable experience of teaching English at renowned educational institutions like COMSATS University, Wah Cantt, Degree College for Men, Wah Cantt and The Scholars Science College Wah Cantt. He is an established English author and has written more than thirty textbooks including books for Punjab Textbook Board and Kashmir Textbook Board. He has also worked as a writer and an editor with leading educational publishers like (the name is under the watermark), IBD.
p Dr. Rooh Ul Amin Khattak holds his PhD in English (Applied Linguistics) from the University of Memphis, TN, USA. Dr. Amin has completed his MPhil in American Studies from Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad and his MA (English) from University of Peshawar. He has 16 years of university teaching experience at both the undergraduate and graduate level. He has an extensive research in the field of language, pedagogy, and education. He also has expertise in editing and reviewing for national and international journals.
