## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo use appropriate expressions in conversation to express opinions and respond to instructions and directions.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict the content/vocabulary of a text from pictures and titles by using prior knowledge.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text using intensive reading strategies (while-reading) to locate specific information to answer short questions.
slo use critical thinking to respond to the text (post-reading) by applying world knowledge and own opinions to the text read.
p It is rightly said that 'Necessity is the mother of invention'. The computer was invented and it is still being used in almost every field of life. In order to make its use even more effective, there must be a communication system to send and receive the information from one computer to another. In 1970's, the scientists in United States of America developed a system to link the computers worldwide. They called it internet, which is a data communication system. It has the ability to send and receive messages, letters, documents, pictures, videos and sounds as easily as making a call. Now, millions of people worldwide are using the internet to share information and communicate with one another.
p People rightly think that the invention of internet has a huge impact on their lives. It has brought information and knowledge on their fingertips. Earlier, they used to go to libraries in search of required information but now they get that information in just a few clicks. It provides the people with useful data, information and knowledge that is useful for studies, social contacts and business. It is up to them to utilize their time on the internet in a positive and productive way.
p There are many uses of the internet. It helps people to connect with each other socially. It also helps them to talk to the people living at any distance. They can share messages, photos and videos with one another through the internet.
