box info | Long Vowel Sounds || لمبی حرکتی آوازیں || اوږده غږیز آوازونه
- The long vowel sound is the same as the name of the vowel itself. Follow these rules:
- Long /a/ sound is /ay/ as in cake.
- Long /e/ sound is /ee/ as in sheet.
- Long /i/ sound is /ahy/ as in like.
- Long /o/ sound is /oh/ as in bone.
- Long /u/ sound is /yoo/ as in human or /oo/ as in rude.
endbox
box info | Short Vowels Sounds || چھوٹی حرکتی آوازیں || لنډ غږیز آوازونه
- If a word consists of only one vowel, and that vowel appears in the middle of the word, the vowel is usually pronounced as a short vowel.
- Examples of Short vowels Sounds: at, bat, mat, bet, wet, red, hit, fix, rob, hot, cup, but, chat, slept, fled, chip, strip, flop, hung.
endbox
