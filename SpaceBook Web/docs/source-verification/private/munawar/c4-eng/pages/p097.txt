p Asma: Are you talking about the luck which we also call the wealth?

p Nida: You know that man becomes mad after wealth and has to look after it. On the other
p Uzma: side, see the treasure of knowledge. A person who owns it, he has no fear of losing it. The people bow more respectfully before the men of knowledge than those who has only collected wealth by hook or crook. Okay! Tell me what do you intend to do in future life?
p Nida: I would like to do a job that involves services to human beings and creatures of Allah, Almighty.
p Uzma: Who are such people?
p Nida: They are our great teachers, engineers, doctors, and many of such kind.
p Uzma: If you are unable to achieve your aim, what will you do?
p Nida: I firmly believe that Allah, Almighty never deprives a person who strives for something.
