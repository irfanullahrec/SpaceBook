p An adjective is a describing word for a noun. Examples: big animal, small insect, long pencil, tall tree, hot day, etc. big, small, long, tall and hot describe the objects (nouns) with which they are used.
endbox
box info | Types of Adjectives || صفات کی اقسام || د صفتونو ډولونه
- i. Descriptive Adjectives: Adjective used for describing a certain quality of the subject such as large, small, good, bad, etc. is called a 'descriptive adjective'.
- ii. Proper Adjectives: Proper adjectives are formed from proper nouns. Proper adjectives also name languages or people. e.g. We are Pakistani children. I like Islamic way of life. The English people live in England. Chinese are known for hard work.
- iii. Quantitative Adjectives: Quantitative adjectives show how many or how much of something is there. Examples: There are two birds in that tree. Ali has many friends at his school. There are a lot of people in the market.
endbox
