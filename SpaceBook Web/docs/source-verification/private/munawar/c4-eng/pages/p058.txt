p Another useful feature of internet is video calling. The teachers can also increase their knowledge and skills and share them with their students.
p Internet is also being used to promote business. Consumer products can be sold by using the Internet. Use of internet technology is growing fast in the world. Online shopping is a huge platform for buying and selling products. People can buy and sell clothes, electronic gadgets, hair products, health products etc. through internet in just a few clicks. One of the major benefits of buying goods online is that people get the goods delivered to their doorsteps in just a few hours. It saves their time and efforts in exploring markets physically.
p Social networking is one of the major achievements of the internet because it has made the world a global village.
p There are some negative effects of using internet too. The young learners get involved in excessive chatting. It may bring them in contact with some bad characters in society. The social contacts between boys and girls without proper supervision of parents and teachers may lead to some unhealthy use of internet. Spending most of their time on internet may leave less time for studies. Excess of everything is bad. Elders and teachers should supervise the young learners while using internet.
