## B. Grammar Structure: Adverbs || ب۔ گرامر کی ساخت: متعلقِ فعل || ب. د ګرامر جوړښت: قیدونه
exercise Exercise (Our Festivals: Grammar Structure)
box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
p A word that tells something about 'how, when or where' an action has been done, is called an 'adverb'.
endbox
q E | Read these sentences and note the functions of underlined words. The dog barked loudly in the park. (How did it bark?) She entered the room quietly. (How did she enter the room?) She parked the car near the park. (Where did she park the car?) She will visit the park tomorrow. (When will she visit the park?) || ان جملوں کو پڑھیں اور خط کشیدہ الفاظ کے کام پر غور کریں۔ || دا جملې ولولئ او د لاندې کرښې ټکو دندې ته پام وکړئ.
qp i | Name the adverb in each sentence.
sol loudly (manner), quietly (manner), near (place), tomorrow (time) || loudly (manner), quietly (manner), near (place), tomorrow (time) || loudly (manner), quietly (manner), near (place), tomorrow (time)
box info | There are mainly three types of adverbs || متعلقِ فعل کی بنیادی طور پر تین اقسام ہیں || قیدونه په عمده توګه درې ډوله دي
- Adverbs of Manner (They tell how an action happens or takes place).
- Adverbs of Time (They tell when an action takes place).
- Adverbs of Place (They tell where an action has taken place).
endbox
box note | Note || نوٹ || یادښت
- We mainly form an adverb of manner by adding -ly to an adjective.
- An adverb may be without -ly (adverbs of time, adverbs of place). Examples: slow - slowly, possible - possibly, careful - carefully.
endbox
