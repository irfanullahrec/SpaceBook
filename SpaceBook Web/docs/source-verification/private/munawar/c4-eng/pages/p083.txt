p Fire has always been our friend as well as foe at the same time. We mostly cook our food on fire. We keep our bedrooms warm with it during winters. The fire produced by fuel or electricity gives energy to our factories. We use fire in many ways. Sometimes, it may become our enemy. It happens when we are careless.
p We need to know how to handle an emergency in case of fire. It may be at our home, school, in office or anywhere else. At schools, the students are guided on taking immediate action in case of fire. It is to tell them what to do in case of fire. A trained fire fighting staff is invited to give instructions to the students. They arrange a firefighting drill for the students. They try to give practical demonstration for putting out the fire in emergency. They call it the 'Fire Drill'. They tell how the students and staff can use fire appliances installed at various places at the school. They also show how to use sand buckets available there. They may use water from the water tank at the school built for emergency use. In the fire drill, the firefighting staff tells the students what to do in case of fire.
