box info | Gender of nouns || اسم کی جنس || د نومونو جنس
- i. Masculine means male nouns. Examples: man, boy, lion, bull, rooster.
- ii. Feminine means female nouns. Examples: woman, girl, lioness, cow, hen.
- iii. Neuter (neither male nor female) genders are lifeless things. Examples: bag, school, book, chair, flag.
endbox
