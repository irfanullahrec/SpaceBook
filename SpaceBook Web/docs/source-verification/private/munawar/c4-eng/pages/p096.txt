p Uzma and Asma were sitting in their school library. They were busy in reading books on general knowledge as usual. Both of them wanted to improve their knowledge and vocabulary. Their friend Nida also joins them.
p Nida: My dear friends! I always see you busy in reading books. Do you never become tired of them?
p Uzma: My dear, whatever you do as per your own will and wish, it never becomes burden on you. Yes, I agree but, after all, what do you want to get out of books?
p Nida: Every successful person has an ideal in mind. We have our own ideals. They are
p Asma: neither rich nor players. We have ideals who excelled in Education and became great people in their career.
p Nida: You know, there is a lot of time for you to reach that stage of life. Just enjoy like me.
p Uzma: My dear, time and tide wait for none. The time once wasted carelessly has no compensation.
p Asma: We have seen so many people repenting after they had wasted their precious time.
p Uzma: It is the matter of luck. If it favours you, it can make everything possible.
