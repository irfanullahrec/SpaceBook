box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- A naming word for a person, place or a thing is called a noun. The naming words (nouns) may be divided into two main groups:
- A noun that can be counted, whether singular (a man, a school, a student, etc.) or plural (a few men, many schools) is a countable noun.
- The noun that cannot be counted is an uncountable noun. For example, milk, sugar, love, rice. NOTE: Uncountable nouns have no plural form.
endbox
