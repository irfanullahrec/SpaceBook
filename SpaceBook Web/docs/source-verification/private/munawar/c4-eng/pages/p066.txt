p Irregular plural nouns are the nouns that do not become plural by adding -s or -es, as most nouns in English language. For example: The plural form of man is men, not mans. The plural form of woman is women.
endbox
box info | The Most Common Irregular Plural Nouns are || عام ترین بے قاعدہ جمع اسم || ډېر عام بې قاعدې جمع نومونه
- a. Nouns ending in -f and -fe: To make a plural of a word ending in -f, change the f to a v and add es. Similarly, if a word ends in -fe, change the f to a v and add an 'es'. The result for both types is a plural that ends in -'ves'. knife - knives, life - lives, wife - wives, calf - calves, leaf - leaves, wolf - wolves.
- b. Nouns Ending in -o: Plurals of words ending in -o are usually made by adding -es. potato - potatoes, tomato - tomatoes, hero - heroes, veto - vetoes.
- c. Many English words become plural by changing the vowels: foot - feet, tooth - teeth, goose - geese, mouse - mice, ox - oxen, child - children.
endbox
