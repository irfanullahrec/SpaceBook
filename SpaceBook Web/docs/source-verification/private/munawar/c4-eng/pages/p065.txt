exercise Exercise (Justice of Rasoolullah: Oral Communication)
box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
p We have previously learnt some formulaic expressions of greetings, routine social courtesies and some communicative functions for specific occasions and purposes. For examples:
- When we meet another Muslim, we say "Assalam-o-Alaikum" (May the peace and blessings of Allah Almighty be upon you!).
- The other Muslim replies with: "Wa alaikum assalam" (and may the peace, mercy and blessings of Allah Almighty be upon you!).
- We praise Allah Almighty, we say "Alhamdulillah" and "Subhan Allah" (All the praises is due for Allah Almighty, who is free from any kind of imperfection).
- When we write, or say the name of Hazrat Muhammad Rasoolullah ﷺ we must utter ﷺ.
- Say 'Jazak Allah Khaira' to pay thanks for someone's favour.
- When appreciating or admiring someone or something, say 'Masha Allah'.
- When you intend or plan to do something, say 'Insha Allah'.
endbox

## 2. Reading and Critical Thinking Skills || ۲۔ پڑھنے اور تنقیدی سوچ کی مہارتیں || ۲. لوستلو او انتقادي فکر مهارتونه
exercise Exercise (Justice of Rasoolullah: Reading)
q A | Answer the following questions: || درج ذیل سوالوں کے جواب دیں: || لاندې پوښتنې ځواب کړئ:
qp i | Whose life is the most perfect example for everyone in life worldly and hereafter?
sol The life of the Holy Rasool Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ. ## From p. 64.
qp ii | Why did the Holy Rasool Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ not say anything to the Jews for killing Hazrat Abdullah ibn Sehl (RA)?
sol Because there were no witnesses to identify the guilty; so he ﷺ did not accuse the Jews and paid the blood-money out of the state revenue. ## From p. 65.
qp iii | How did Rasoolullah ﷺ treat his family?
sol He ﷺ was always just in treating his wives. ## From p. 65.
qp iv | How can we make our lives successful?
sol By following the life and the Sunnah of Rasoolullah ﷺ, always following the truth and doing justice. ## From p. 64 and 65.
qp v | How can we do justice to ourselves?
sol By doing our duties honestly and justly, for example by taking our studies above all, and by being fair while distributing things among others. ## From p. 65. || By doing our duties honestly and justly, for example by taking our studies above all, and by being fair while distributing things among others. || By doing our duties honestly and justly, for example by taking our studies above all, and by being fair while distributing things among others.
