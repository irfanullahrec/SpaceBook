## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
p (The list is in very small type and is not read.)
p I can keep on working,
p Until my task is through;
p And I enjoy taking pride,
p In all the things I do.
p I can adjust when things,
p Don't go as planned;
p And I can ask for help,
p When I don't understand.
p I can do more and more,
p If I think I can;
p And if at first I don't succeed,
p I will try again and again.
p (Frederick Hallbach) (the poet's name is read as far as it shows)
