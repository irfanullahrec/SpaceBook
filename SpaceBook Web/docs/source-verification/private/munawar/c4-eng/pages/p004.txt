## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo pronounce and practise simple words with more silent letters such as 'b' in 'comb'.
slo articulate and practise words containing digraphs, trigraphs and silent letters.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict the content/vocabulary of a text from pictures and titles, by using prior knowledge.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text using intensive reading strategies (while reading) to locate specific information to answer short questions.
slo use critical thinking to respond to the text (post reading) by applying world knowledge and own opinion to the text read.
p It was Musa's first day in his new school. His teacher asked Musa to introduce himself to his new classmates. Musa told briefly about himself and his previous school. His teacher asked him what he had liked the most about his previous school.
p Musa: Madam, the best thing at my previous school was, no doubt, the peaceful atmosphere. There were ideal relations of love and respect among students, teachers and the staff working at the school.
p Hassan: How was such an ideal atmosphere created and maintained?
p Musa: There used to be a speech by a teacher regularly in the morning assembly. He would give examples from everyday life and from Islamic history. He would tell how the Arabs changed to the most civilized society in a few years. He would also explain that it was tolerance, mutual respect and equality that changed the life of people.
p Saarim: But, my friend, you know that it was the guidance and perfect leadership of the Holy Rasool, Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ that brought such a unique revolution among them.
p Musa: My dear, you are absolutely right but the message and teachings of Islam are not bound to specific time. They are eternal and for every kind of people.
