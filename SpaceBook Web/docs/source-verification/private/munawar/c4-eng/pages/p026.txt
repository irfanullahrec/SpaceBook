box note | Summary of the Poem || نظم کا خلاصہ || د نظم لنډیز
p This poem is a lyrical description of things of nature in beautiful words. The poet says that everything is the creation of Allah Almighty. Beautiful flowers, colourful singing birds, the pleasant weather and tasty fruits in gardens are all created by Allah Almighty. Allah has given us eyes to see things of nature and lips to praise His blessings.
endbox

## 1. Oral Communication Skill || ۱۔ زبانی ابلاغ کی مہارت || ۱. خولنۍ اړیکې مهارت
exercise Exercise (All Things Bright and Beautiful: Oral Communication)
q A | A conversation between two friends. Situation: (Asad and Fahad are trying to draw pictures in their drawing period.) Asad: Assalam-o-Alaikum! Fahad! Fahad: Wa-Alaikum Assalam! Why are you so upset? Asad: Do you have a pencil because I forgot my pencil at home. Fahad: Sure! Here it is. Asad: Thank you! Fahad: You are welcome! || دو دوستوں کے درمیان گفتگو۔ || د دوو ملګرو ترمنځ خبرې اترې.
qp i | Practise the conversation between Asad and Fahad in pairs.
sol Asad greets Fahad, tells him that he forgot his pencil at home and asks for one; Fahad gives him a pencil, Asad thanks him and Fahad says "You are welcome!". ## Practise it in pairs with polite greetings and thanks. || Asad greets Fahad, tells him that he forgot his pencil at home and asks for one; Fahad gives him a pencil, Asad thanks him and Fahad says "You are welcome!". || Asad greets Fahad, tells him that he forgot his pencil at home and asks for one; Fahad gives him a pencil, Asad thanks him and Fahad says "You are welcome!".
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Arrange a short role-play in the classroom. Divide the class in pairs.
- Ask the students to use positive greetings.
- Guide them about showing expression of politeness, gratitude, request, etc. in their conversation.
- Tell them about correct intonation.
endbox
