box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- The naming words used commonly for anything, any place or any person are called 'common nouns', e.g. boy, girl, garden, city, clock, lion, table, house, etc.
- The specific naming word used particularly for a person, place or a thing is called 'Proper Noun', e.g. Ali, Naima, Jinnah Garden, Lahore, Koh-e-Noor (special name of a diamond).
- Proper nouns start with capital letters.
endbox
