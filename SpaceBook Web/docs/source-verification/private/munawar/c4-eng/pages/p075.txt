box note | Summary of the lesson || سبق کا خلاصہ || د درس لنډیز
p In this poem, the poet wants to teach us the lesson of constant struggle in life. If a person is not successful in doing something in first attempt, he/she should try again and again. The time shall come when he/she achieves what he/she desires. In this process, there is no harm in seeking help from others.
endbox

## 1. Oral Communication Skill || ۱۔ زبانی ابلاغ کی مہارت || ۱. خولنۍ اړیکې مهارت
exercise Exercise (I Think I Can: Oral Communication)
q A | After reading the poem 'I Think I Can Do', the teacher asked the students to express their opinions, ideas and thoughts about it one by one. All students started talking. The teacher asked them to keep order in the classroom and first listen and learn the dynamics and conventions of conversation: i. They must wait for their turn to speak. ii. They must avoid speaking irrelevant and remain positive. iii. They must use simple language, short sentences and as concise as possible. iv. They must wait for their turn to speak. Never interrupt when someone is speaking. v. They must address the teacher while expressing ideas or opinion. vi. They must avoid saying the same thing over and over again. Now, the conversation has started. The students promised to abide by the rules. Ahmad: Sir, I agree with the poet that consistency of aim leads to success. Fahad: Yes teacher, I think the poet has depicted what each successful person feels. Sana: Sir, I am sorry to say that some people lose heart when they fail. It is negative approach. Sehr: Sir, I would like to say that determination is the key to one's success. Raza: Sir, the poet has left a positive message for us all to follow it. Teacher: Well said everyone! Let's promise to be firm on it to continue our efforts. All students raised their hands and said unanimously: In-Sha-Allah. || نظم پڑھنے کے بعد طلبہ کی گفتگو۔ || د نظم له لوستلو وروسته د زده کوونکو خبرې اترې.
