p Bilal: I am sorry, sir, that I was careless in eating. I faced the consequences.
p Doctor: I am prescribing some medicine for you. Take it three times a day.
p Bilal: Sir, what about precautions, please?
p Doctor: Here are some safety precautions about the food you eat.
- Avoid fatty foods in your diet.
- Avoid chocolate and fast food items.
- Avoid spicy and unripe foods.
- Drink sufficient amount of clean water.
- Eat whole grains, fruits, vegetables and fresh dairy foods.
- Try to walk after taking every meal. Sleeping just after meals is harmful for health.
p Mother: Thank you sir! Where should I pay the bill?
p Doctor: Please, go to the counter. The cashier will give you the copy of bill and pay the amount it shows.
p Bilal's mother paid the bill. They bade goodbye to the doctor and went back to their home.

box note | Summary of the lesson || سبق کا خلاصہ || د درس لنډیز
p In this lesson, we have learnt about the troubles one has to face as a result of carelessness in eating. During birthdays and wedding parties, people eat carelessly. Consequently, they may become sick and have to visit a doctor. One has to eat medicines and get painful injections. Therefore, it is better to take care of eating habits. We should eat fresh and nicely-cooked food more instead of fast food and snacks. We should also have a walk after every meal.
