p The students were preparing for Independence Day celebrations at school. 'Love for Pakistan' was a common topic among the students and teachers in almost all classes. Ahmad was preparing a speech on 'Patriotism'. In the opening paragraph, there was a proverb: "Actions speak louder than words".
p Ahmad could not understand the idea behind it during his English lesson, he asked the teacher to explain its meanings. His teacher liked his question. He wrote the proverb on blackboard for the whole class to say something about it. The students expressed their views according to their knowledge and understanding. Ahmad could not get a clear concept about this proverb.
p The teacher elaborated it with examples of deeds from the life of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah. He said that all great people believe that the words lose meanings when there is no action on them. Quaid-e-Azam said that Muslims needed an independent country to live according to their religion. Then, he struggled to realize words. His determination and sincerity to his cause brought him success. Pakistan came into being on 14th of August, 1947.
