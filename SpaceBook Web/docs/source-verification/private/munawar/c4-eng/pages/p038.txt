p The day of Eid-ul-Azha starts with Fajr prayer. The Muslims wear new clothes and go to Eid Gah for Eid prayers. They offer Eid prayer and exchange Eid greetings with one another. After Eid prayers, the Muslims who can afford to buy a halal cattle like the sheep, goat, cow, camel, etc., sacrifice in the name of Allah Almighty, following the Sunnah of Hazrat Ibrahim (AS). They keep only one third part of its meat for their family and distribute the other two parts among the poor, neighbours and relatives. The children like to go to play lands and parks for enjoyment. The two Eid festivals are also a display of Islamic culture and traditions.

p This lesson gives brief description of the celebrations of the two Eids. Sacrifice is a common feature in the celebrations of the two Eids. The Muslims give Fitrana to help the poor on Eid-ul-Fitr and give out third of its meat to the poor and relatives/neighbours on Eid-ul-Azha. They use one part of it for their own family. The Muslims spend the festival day in happiness and merry-making.
endbox
