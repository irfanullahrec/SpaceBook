## 1. Oral Communication Skill || ۱۔ زبانی ابلاغ کی مہارت || ۱. خولنۍ اړیکې مهارت
exercise Exercise (A Trip to Murree Hills: Oral Communication)
q A | After trip to Murree Hills, the teacher of Grade-IV arranged a discussion in the class next morning. Read it carefully. Teacher: Sa'ad, how was your tour to Murree Hills? Sa'ad: Sir, it was really fantastic and enjoyable. Teacher: Rafey, how was your journey by other than school bus? Rafey: Sir, we really enjoyed our journey by a hired bus. It was very comfortable. Teacher: Salman, tell us about your arrival and stay at Murree? Salman: Sir, we were so much involved in sight seeing, enjoying chair lifts and having fun over there that time passed in an eye's wink. Teacher: That's nice. Nida, please tell us something you didn't like there. Nida: Sir, one thing was really dismaying. The people were creating a mess all around. They didn't show sense of responsibility at that public place. Teacher: Oh! That's not good. Sana, tell us about the behavior of the people around there? Sana: The local people and official staff were very cooperative. Teacher: Shams, tell something about the surroundings of Murree. Shams: Sir, the lush green tall trees and thick bushes on hills add beauty to the surroundings of Murree Hills. Teacher: Sehr, what kind of animals did you see around? Sehr: Sir, there were many animals like over here but the monkeys on trees were really a fun to watch. Teacher: Umer, did any one of you watch any mishap? || مری کی پہاڑیوں کے سفر کے بعد چوتھی جماعت کے استاد نے اگلی صبح کلاس میں گفتگو کرائی۔ || د مري غرونو ته له سفر وروسته د څلورم ټولګي ښوونکي بله سهار په ټولګي کې خبرې اترې تنظیم کړې.
qp i | Practise the discussion in groups, answering the teacher's questions about your own trip.
