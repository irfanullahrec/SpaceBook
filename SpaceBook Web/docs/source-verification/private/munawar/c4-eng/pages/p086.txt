exercise Exercise (Safety First: Grammar Structure)
box note | Verbs (Tenses): Remember! || افعال (زمانے): یاد رکھیں! || فعلونه (مهالونه): یاد ولرئ!
- Tense of the verb is the form of verb that shows when something happens.
- If it shows that an action always happens, it is the Present Indefinite Tense.
- If it shows what is happening at the present moment, it is the Present Continuous Tense.
- If it happened sometimes ago, it is the Past Tense.
- If it will happen in the time to come, it is the Future Tense.
