box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- Dictionary is a reference book for learning spellings, pronunciation and meanings of words.
- All words in a dictionary are listed in alphabetical order that means all words that start with 'a' are in the beginning pages of the book and words that start with 'z' are in the end pages of a dictionary.
endbox
box note | Guide Words and Entry Words || رہنما الفاظ اور اندراجی الفاظ || لارښود ټکي او داخلي ټکي
- Guide words are the words placed on the top of every page. They help to 'guide' in the right page.
- The first guide word 'snowflake' is the first entry word on the page.
- The second guide word 'soggy' is the last entry word on the page.
- Each word in a dictionary is termed an entry word; spellings are listed as 'entry words'.
endbox
