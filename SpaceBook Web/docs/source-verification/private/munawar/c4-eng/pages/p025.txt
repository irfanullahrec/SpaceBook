p All things bright and beautiful,
p All creatures great and small,
p All things wise and wonderful,
p Allah Almighty made them all.
p Each little flower that opens,
p Each little bird that sings,
p He made their glowing colours,
p He made their tiny wings.
p The cold wind in the winter,
p The pleasant summer sun,
p The ripe fruits in the garden,
p He made them every one.
p He gave us eyes to see them,
p And lips that we might tell,
p How great is Allah Almighty,
p Who has made all things well!
