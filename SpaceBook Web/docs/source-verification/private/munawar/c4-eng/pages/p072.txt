box note | Auxiliary Verbs: Note || معاون افعال: نوٹ || مرستندویه فعلونه: یادښت
p Auxiliary or helping verbs help in forming various tenses and moods of other verbs. Auxiliary verb 'be' (is, are, am, was, were) is used to form present continuous or past continuous tenses. Examples: I am learning English now. The children are playing outside. Our teacher was teaching us yesterday. The children were learning English.
endbox
