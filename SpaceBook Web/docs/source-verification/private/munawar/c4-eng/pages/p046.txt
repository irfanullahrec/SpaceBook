exercise Exercise (Gifts of a Tree: Grammar Structure)
box note | Agreement of Pronouns with their Genders and Numbers || ضمیروں کی جنس اور تعداد سے مطابقت || د ضمیرونو له جنس او شمېر سره سمون
- A pronoun must agree with its number (singular or plural). Example: Ali talks to his teacher, Mrs Sana. She teaches him English.
- i. The two friends saw their teacher who was coming to them.
- ii. A pronoun must agree with its gender (masculine, feminine, neuter).
- iii. I hope each girl will remember her lesson.
- iv. Each boy has agreed to bring his own lunch.
- v. The ball loses its shine after some time.
- Above examples show that: (a) A pronoun is a word that replaces a noun. (b) The pronoun which replaces the noun must agree with it in the following ways: (i) A feminine pronoun must replace a feminine noun. (ii) A masculine pronoun must replace a masculine noun. (iii) A singular pronoun must replace a singular noun. (iv) A plural pronoun must replace a plural noun.
