box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- (a) Among 26 letters of English alphabet, five letters (a, e, i, o, u) are vowels and other 21 are consonants. They are: B C D F G H J K L M N P Q R S T V W X Y Z.
- A consonant cluster in a word is a group of consonants with no vowels between them.
- The longest possible cluster in English is three consonant sounds at the start, each as in 'school', and four at the end, as in 'twelfths'.
- A few of the words with three consonant clusters in the beginning are: school - scratch, screen - scrub, stripe - scroll, scheme - scribe.
endbox
