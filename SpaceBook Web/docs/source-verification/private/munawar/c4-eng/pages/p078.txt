p The verbs 'am, is, are, was, were, have been' show the state of being. They are 'being verbs'.

box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
p Being verbs state what something is. It means they give information and link between the subject of the sentence and what is said about it in a sentence.

box note | Sentence Structure: Remember! || جملے کی ساخت: یاد رکھیں! || د جملې جوړښت: یاد ولرئ!
p The group of words which gives a complete sense is called a sentence. Arrangement of words in a sentence: Subject + Verb + Object (SVO). Read these groups of words carefully: Bilal writes a letter.
- Bilal is the noun who performs some action. He is the subject of this sentence.
- Writes shows the action. It is a verb.
- Letter is a noun which is affected by the action of the subject. It is object.
endbox
