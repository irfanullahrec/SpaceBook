p We went from Peshawar to Rawalpindi by train on January 24 last year. It was on a fine Saturday morning that we started our journey. It was a fascinating experience for those who had never travelled by train before. The train went through cities, green fields on both sides of the railway track. We reached Rawalpindi before noon. A bus was hired to travel from Rawalpindi to Murree Hills. When the bus started going up the hills, it was even more enjoyable. Lush green towering trees on both sides of the road seemed to welcome us. On one side, we could see tops of the hills and on other side, there was deep lowland areas.
p The bus reached the rest house at about 3:30 pm. We took already prepared lunch for us in the guest house. We rested for an hour or so. It was going to be evening. The sun changed to golden and set behind the hills. It made the hill tops as if made of gold. We were on the Mall of Murree. It was freezing cold. Therefore, we took rest in our rooms at night. After breakfast in the morning, we went to Patriata and New Murree. We enjoyed riding the chair lifts and cable cars. The valley looked like a fairyland. Then, we went to Ayyubia and Nathia Gali. Every moment spent in that dreamland was enjoyable. I took many photographs and made videos of best scenes to add to my album. The tall hills were covered with snow. We could see people throwing snow balls on one another and having a lot of fun.
p The next day, we visited some more places and did some shopping. We could see many people coming to Murree in cars and vans. Motorbikes and rickshaws were rarely seen because they are not suitable for travelling in hilly areas. We started our journey back to Lahore in a bus. It travelled through Motorway. The journey through motorway has its own charm. The bus was running so smoothly that one could not feel whether it was travelling on road. We reached Lahore late in the evening. It was a memorable journey.
