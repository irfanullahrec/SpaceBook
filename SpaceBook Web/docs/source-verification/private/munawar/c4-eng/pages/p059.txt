exercise Exercise (An Amazing Invention: Oral Communication)
q A | During the computer studies period of grade-IV, the teacher wanted to know about the students' awareness regarding the use of internet. He arranged a discussion about advantages and disadvantages of using internet among students. Here is the discussion among students on this topic. Teacher: Dear students, what do you see most of the people around you busy in? Umar: Sir, almost everyone has a mobile phone and uses it for chatting or entertainment. Teacher: Ahmad! What is your opinion about the use of internet by the students? Ahmad: Sir, it is the blessing of science. We must utilize it for our studies. Teacher: Sana, what would you like to say about it? Sana: Sir, the students at our stage are too young to make its correct use. In my opinion, they don't need it yet. Salman: Sir, we should only use internet when needed for studies. Teacher: Sure! The students must make maximum of their time in studies. Sufyan: Yes sir, they can get guidance from teachers and elders in studies. Teacher: The internet is a blessing of science. We should not misuse it just to waste our time. We should make use of internet for knowledge and information. || کمپیوٹر کے پیریڈ میں انٹرنیٹ کے استعمال پر طلبہ کے درمیان گفتگو۔ || د کمپیوټر په پیرېیډ کې د انټرنیټ د کارونې په اړه د زده کوونکو ترمنځ خبرې اترې.
