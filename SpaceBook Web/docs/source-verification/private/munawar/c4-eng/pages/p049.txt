## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
p (The list is in very small type and is not read.)
p Educational and recreational trips are part of extra-curricular activities of our school. Such trips give us first hand knowledge about places, people and their ways of life. The information got through these trips increases our knowledge. They are sources of recreation and enjoyment as well. We also learn about the most suitable means of transportation to be used for travelling to a specific place.
p Last year, a trip to Murree Hills was planned in the month of January. The school arranged the booking of a rest house for us for couple of days. All students were very excited because most of them were going to visit Murree Hills for the first time in their life. I was one of them.
