# My Promise (Poem) || میرا وعدہ (نظم) || زما ژمنه (نظم)
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- How can we show love to our fellows?
- What do you feel after helping someone?
endbox
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo pronounce and practise long and short vowels and diphthongs as they occur in words and sentences in reading lessons and speech.
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict the content/vocabulary of a text from pictures and title, etc. by using prior knowledge.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text using intensive reading strategies (while-reading) to locate specific information to answer short questions.
slo use critical thinking to respond to the text (post-reading) by applying world knowledge and own opinion to the text read.
slo create a short poem using the suggested rhyming words.
p By putting smile on a crying face,
p A smile blooms on my face too.
p By giving shelter to a tired traveller,
p Tiredness of my mind disappears too.
p By giving water to a thirsty man,
p Desires of my heart are fulfilled;
p By bringing happiness in others' lives,
p The pitcher of my happiness is filled.
p By listening problems of others,
p I often forget my troubles;
p By solving difficulties of others,
p My problems turn into bubbles.
p Serving others is a noble quality,
p That brings welfare in the society;
p We should share what we have to others,
p As it brings out the best in us.
p Helping others is a great feeling;
p Believe me it's really amazing;
p When you will help people who are in need,
p Satisfaction and happiness will come to you indeed.
box info | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهېږئ؟
p Islam teaches us to help others facing problems.
endbox
