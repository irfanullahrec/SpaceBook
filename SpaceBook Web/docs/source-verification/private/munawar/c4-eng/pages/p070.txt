box note | Non-verbal Communication: Remember! || غیر لفظی ابلاغ: یاد رکھیں! || غیر لفظي اړیکه: یاد ولرئ!
p Nonverbal communication is the use of body language including eye contact, facial expressions and various gestures to communicate with others. It means speaking without using words. Example: The traffic policeman does not say a word but drivers understand his instructions. He makes gestures and blows whistle. He does not speak at all. It is an example of communication through nonverbal cues.
endbox
box info | Following are some of the very common nonverbal cues || چند عام غیر لفظی اشارے || ځینې ډېر عام غیر لفظي اشارې
- Our eye contact shows that we are listening attentively.
- Nodding our head (up and down movement of head) shows our agreement and shaking (side to side movement of head) shows disagreement.
- Smiling shows our interest and happiness.
- Leaning forward shows our respect and agreement/interest to the speaker.
- Using tone of voice shows our love, hatred, anger, etc.
endbox
