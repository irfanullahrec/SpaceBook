box note | Regular and Irregular Verbs || باقاعدہ اور بے قاعدہ افعال || منظم او بې قاعدې فعلونه
- Note the change of forms of verbs from 'present tense to past tense'. Examples: Asad walks to school everyday. He walked to school yesterday. Sana writes a letter to her friend every week. She wrote a letter last week.
- Note: The root word 'walk' remains the same while changing the tense (walked). The root word 'write' is changed while changing the tense (wrote).
- Remember! When the main part of a verb (root word) remains the same by changing it to the past tense, the verb is said to be in its 'Regular Form', e.g. talk - talked, bake - baked, jump - jumped, wash - washed. When the main part (root word) of a verb is changed when written in the past tense, the verb is said to be in its 'Irregular Form', e.g. break - broke, come - came, go - went.
endbox

- Read these sentences. See changes in action words (verbs). We went to the zoo yesterday. Mother baked a cake for evening tea. I wrote a letter to my friend.
- Note: The verb 'went' is past tense of go. The verb 'baked' is past tense of bake. The verb 'wrote' is past tense of 'write'.
- Remember! The action words (verbs) that tell us what happened some time ago (in the past) represent 'past indefinite tense'. They are in past forms of verbs.
- More examples: The children played cricket last Sunday. Our teacher told us a story in our last lesson. We listened to her silently. She asked questions about the story.
