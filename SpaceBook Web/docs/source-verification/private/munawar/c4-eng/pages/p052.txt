box note | Compound Words: Remember! || مرکب الفاظ: یاد رکھیں! || مرکب ټکي: یاد ولرئ!
p Compound words are formed when two or more words are put together to form a new word with a new meaning. They can function as different parts of speech. For example: the word 'blackboard' is the combination of black (an adjective) and board (a noun). Likewise, in the word 'countdown' the word 'count' is verb and 'down' is a preposition.
endbox
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Guide the students that during the educational trips and visits, the students should follow the instructions of their teachers.
- Ask them to express their feelings about what they experienced and saw during the visit. It may include events and observations which caused them joy, anger or good/bad feelings.
- Show the students the map of Murree Hills and explain/ask what the various symbols in a map stand for.
