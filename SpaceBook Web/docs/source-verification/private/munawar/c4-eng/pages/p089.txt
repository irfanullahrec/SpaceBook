box note | Note || نوٹ || یادښت
- The examples given above show that we use second form of verb to indicate the 'Past Indefinite Tense'.
- The second form of a verb may be just adding 'd' or 'ed' after the base form of verb, e.g. cook - cooked, bake - baked, play - played, talk - talked, or by bringing some change in the root word such as: come - came, go - went, eat - ate, write - wrote.
endbox
