## B. Grammar Structure: Collective Nouns || ب۔ گرامر کی ساخت: اسمِ جمع || ب. د ګرامر جوړښت: ګډ نومونه

box note | We know that || ہم جانتے ہیں کہ || موږ پوهېږو چې
- Naming words for persons, places and things are called nouns. Examples: student, bird, wolf, sheep, book, stone, etc.
- The name given to a group of persons, places, animals and things collectively is called Collective Noun. Example: a class of students, a flock of birds, a pack of wolves, a herd of sheep, a library of books, a heap of stones, etc.
- Here, the words, class, flock, pack, herd, library, heap are group names or collective nouns.
endbox
