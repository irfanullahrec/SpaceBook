tr what || کیا؟ || څه؟ | asking for information about anything | What is your name? What do you like to become in future?
tr when | asking about time | When do you come to school?
tr where | asking about place or position | Where do you live? || آپ کہاں رہتے ہیں؟ || تاسو چېرته اوسېږئ؟
tr which | asking about choice | Which colour do you like the most?
tr who | asking about person or people (subject) | Who opened the door?
tr whom | asking about person or people (object) | Whom did you see?
tr whose | asking about ownership | Whose are these books? Whose turn is it to open the door?
tr why | asking for reason, asking what for | Why did you come late yesterday?
tr how | the way to do something | How do you make tea?
