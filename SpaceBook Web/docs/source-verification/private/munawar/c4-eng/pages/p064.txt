p Hazrat Abdullah ibn Masood (RA) narrated as:
p Hazrat Abdullah ibn Sehl (RA) was sent to collect rent from the Jews of Khyber. His cousin, Mahisah (RA) was also with him. On reaching Khyber, they got separated. Hazrat Abdullah ibn Sehl (RA) was killed by the Jews. Mahisah (RA) reported this sad incident to Rasoolullah ﷺ. As there were no witnesses to identify the guilty, he ﷺ did not say anything to the Jews, and paid the blood-money out of the state revenue. (Al-Bukhari 3173)
p The Jews, inspite of their differences with Rasoolullah ﷺ were so impressed by his ﷺ justice that they used to bring their cases to him ﷺ and he ﷺ would decide their cases according to Jewish laws. (Al-Bukhari, the number is in very small type)
p Another example of the justice of Rasoolullah ﷺ is evident from his family life also. He ﷺ was always just in treating his ﷺ wives. Our success lies in following the life of our Holy Rasool ﷺ.
- Always follow the truth and always do justice, even if it is against yourself or your dear ones.
- If you are given some responsibility, do it honestly and justly.
- For example: your parents send you to school for studies. Your justice to your duty is to take your studies above all.
- Follow the example of Holy Rasoolullah ﷺ and be fair and just while distributing things among your brothers, sisters and friends. Moreover, ask others to do the same.

box note | Summary of the lesson || سبق کا خلاصہ || د درس لنډیز
p In this lesson, we learnt that life of Rasoolullah ﷺ is the perfect and unique role model for all of us forever. If we want to live a successful life, we must follow the Sunnah of Rasoolullah ﷺ. Justice is the most prevailing attribute of His Sunnah. There are everlasting examples of his justice (read as far as it shows); he ﷺ would say that previously, the nations were destroyed by Allah Almighty because they showed discrimination between the rich and the poor in making decisions.
