box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- The sentence in which words are arranged as Subject + Verb + Object is called a simple sentence.
- The subject of a verb always agrees in person and number. (Singular subject follows single verb and plural subjects follows plural verb.) Examples: A boy plays with his toys. The boys play with their toys.
- Now read this sentence: Sana plays with her dolls. Sana is the subject of this sentence. 'plays with her dolls' is something said about the subject. It is called the 'predicate' of the subject (Sana).
endbox

box note | Kinds of sentences: Remember! || جملوں کی اقسام: یاد رکھیں! || د جملو ډولونه: یاد ولرئ!
p Generally, there are four kinds of sentences.
- i. Declarative: It makes a statement or gives information. It ends with a full stop. Examples: The Sun rises in the East. We learn English from our teacher.
- ii. Exclamatory: It expresses strong feeling of joy, sorrow or excitement. It ends with an exclamation mark (!). Examples: Hurrah! We have won the match! Alas! I have lost my pen!
- iii. Imperative: It gives a command, request or advice. It ends with a period or exclamation mark. Example: Go and fetch me a glass of water. Please, give me your pen. Don't go there.
- iv. Interrogative: It asks a question. It ends with a question mark (?). Example: How are you today? When will you come? Are you a student?
endbox
