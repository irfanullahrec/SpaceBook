p The word 'Festival' means a special event that people celebrate together. Some festivals are religious. For example, Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Azha are Islamic festivals. Muslims all over the world celebrate these festivals on specific dates of Islamic year. They celebrate Eid-ul-Fitr on first of 'Shawwal' which is the tenth month of Islamic Lunar Calendar. It comes after the month of fasting, called Ramadan.
p Pakistan is an Islamic country. Religion has great influence on Islamic life and culture. The Muslims lead their lives according to the teachings of Islam. The ultimate aim of celebrating religious festivals is seeking to will of Allah Almighty.
p Muslims celebrate Eid-ul-Azha on 10th of Zul Hajj every year. It is the last month of Islamic calendar or Lunar year. Eid-ul Azha is also called 'Eid-e-Qurbaan' because Muslims sacrifice animals like goats, sheep, cows and camels in the memory of great sacrifice of Hazrat Ismaeel (AS) by his father, Hazrat Ibrahim (AS). The meat of the sacrificed animal is distributed evenly in three parts. One part is for the relatives. One part is for the poor and the remaining one part is for the donor and his family. On Eid-ul-Fitr, Muslims give out Fitrana and distribute meat on Eid-ul-Azha to help the poor. It shows that the main activity on both Eids is to follow the ways of Islamic teachings.
