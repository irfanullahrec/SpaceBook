box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- The form of a verb that shows the time of action i.e. something happens, is happening, happened in the past or will happen in time to come is called tense of the verb.
- There are three main tenses: present tense, past tense and future tense.

box note | The simple present or present indefinite tense is used for || سادہ حال کا استعمال || ساده اوسنی مهال کارول
- Describing habits, routines and permanent features. Examples: He ... daily (habit). We go to school daily (routine). Allah helps those who help themselves. The Earth revolves around the Sun.
endbox
box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- We add 's' or 'es' to the verb for third person singular (he, she, it or name of one person, place or thing). Examples: He works hard. She plays with dolls. It rains in summer. Saarim loves his mother. The tiger jumps at the deer.
- We use only the first form of verb i.e. without adding 's' or 'es' in case the subject is first person (I, we), second person (you) or third person plural (they). Examples: I go to school daily. We speak the truth. You read story books. They tell stories. All the sentences given above are in 'present indefinite tense'.
endbox
