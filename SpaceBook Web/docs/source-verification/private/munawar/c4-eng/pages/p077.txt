q D | How to look up the meanings of a word in a dictionary. Proceed to the letter of the alphabet that your word begins with. The word "succeed" begins with "s". Words starting with 's' are located in the upper corner of each page. We call them 'guide words'. Once you are close to find it, use the second letter of your word to run down the page and locate your word. The word "succeed" will be your 'entry word'. After the first letter 's' the second one is 'u'. See all the words with second letter as 'u' and all the "su" words alphabetically until you reach the "suc" area of the page. This is at last where you will find "succeed". Once you have found the word you are looking for, you will find all its meanings too. Note: You have to be very particular about the spellings and pronunciation of words. It is because the stress in the words may cause to change the meanings of a word having the same spellings, e.g. PreSENT (offer) and PREsent (gift) have different meanings despite their same spellings. || لغت میں لفظ کے معنی کیسے تلاش کریں۔ || په قاموس کې د یو ټکي معنا څنګه ومومئ.

- A verb usually tells what someone or something is doing (action) or its state of being (to be).
- We cannot make a meaningful sentence without using a 'verb'.
