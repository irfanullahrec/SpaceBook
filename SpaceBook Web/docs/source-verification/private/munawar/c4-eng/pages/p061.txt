box info | More types of Adjectives || صفات کی مزید اقسام || د صفتونو نور ډولونه
- iv. Interrogative Adjectives: Interrogative adjectives are used to ask questions. Examples: Which kind of books do you read? What colour do you like the most?
- Possessive Adjectives: Possessive adjectives indicate possession. They include my, your, his, her, its, our, and their. Examples: It is my house. I like your dress. Pakistan is our homeland.
- v. Adjective of Number: Adjective of number indicates the number of object under discussion. Examples: six pencils, two friends, second row, etc.
endbox

box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- English language has two types of articles: 'the' and 'a/an'.
- 'The' is used to refer to specific or particular nouns.
- We use 'a/an' to refer to non-specific or non-particular nouns.
- We call 'the' definite article and 'a/an' the indefinite article. Examples: I have a pen, a book and an inkpot in my bag. The book I am reading is my English Book-IV.
endbox
box info | Rules of articles || حروفِ تعریف کے قاعدے || د مقالو قاعدې
- Rule 1: A single countable noun takes an article before it. e.g. a chair, a flower, an egg, an orange.
