p high boundary walls around the garden. The children recalled the time when there were flowers and birds everywhere. Now, the spring never came nor the summer. In spring season, the other gardens had golden fruits but giant's garden had nothing. It was eternal autumn in selfish giant's garden. The cold north wind blew harder to dry the leaves if any were left. No birds sang and no children played there.
p One morning, the selfish giant woke up early. He heard beautiful music. It was a bird singing cheerfully outside his window. The selfish giant went out. He looked into the garden. There he saw an opening in the wall around the garden. He was astonished to see a wonderful sight. In every tree, there sat a little child. The flowers were smiling on the branches of trees. The birds were flying about and chirping. The garden was looking very beautiful. The selfish giant realized his mistake.
p Now, he understood why spring never came to visit his garden. He knocked down the wall. He allowed the children to come into the garden. They played there ever after. The garden became evergreen in presence of happy faces of children. He was no more called 'Selfish Giant'. He would be friend to everyone who visited the garden. The birds also started singing around him. The children, now, call him 'Generous Uncle'.
p Adopted: Stories by Oscar Wilde
