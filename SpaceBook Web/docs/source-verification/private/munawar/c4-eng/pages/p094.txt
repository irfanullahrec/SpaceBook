q B | Change the sentences as directed in brackets. Example: We are playing cricket in the playground. (past continuous tense) - We were playing cricket in the playground. I go to the park for morning walk. (simple future tense) - I shall go to the park for morning walk. || ہدایت کے مطابق جملے بدلیں۔ || د لارښوونې له مخې جملې بدلې کړئ.
qp a | When I reach home, my mother is sleeping. (past continuous tense)
sol When I reached home, my mother was sleeping. || When I reached home, my mother was sleeping. || When I reached home, my mother was sleeping.
qp b | She talks to her mother. (simple future tense)
sol She will talk to her mother. || She will talk to her mother. || She will talk to her mother.
qp c | The children are cleaning their rooms. (past continuous tense)
sol The children were cleaning their rooms. || The children were cleaning their rooms. || The children were cleaning their rooms.
qp d | My friend comes to me on Sunday. (simple future tense)
sol My friend will come to me on Sunday. || My friend will come to me on Sunday. || My friend will come to me on Sunday.
qp e | Our teacher is teaching us English. (past continuous tense)

box info | Functions of 'Wh' forms used in questions || سوالیہ الفاظ 'Wh' کے کام || د پوښتنیزو 'Wh' ډولونو دندې
- who: it is used to ask about people. It is never used to ask about things.
- which: it is used to ask about people and things when there is a choice to make.
- what: it is used to ask about people, animals and things.
- whose: it is used to ask about the possession of something.
- when: we use it when we want to ask about time.
- where: we use it when we want to ask about place.
- why: we use it to ask about reason.
- how: we use it to ask about people, events and the way things are or have been done.
endbox
