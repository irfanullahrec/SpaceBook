## 1. Oral Communication Skill || ۱۔ زبانی ابلاغ کی مہارت || ۱. خولنۍ اړیکې مهارت
exercise Exercise (Gifts of a Tree: Oral Communication)
q A | Here is a conversation between two friends about value of trees. Hamid: Assalam-o-Alaikum and good morning! Samad: Wa-Alaikum Assalam and good morning to you too! Hamid: Happy to see you here this morning. Samad: Same here. How nice one feels among trees and plants in the morning! Hamid: Yes, you are right. They are blessings of Allah Almighty for us. Samad: May I ask a personal question to you, if you don't mind? Hamid: Sure! We are friends. You can ask whatever you want. Samad: I have visited your house many times. Why could I not find any tree or plant at your house? Hamid: In fact, trees and plants require time to look after them. Everyone in our family is too busy and have no time to spare for gardening. Samad: It doesn't need so much time and effort to grow trees and plants. You may spare time on Sundays and holidays. It will be a very useful activity. Hamid: Sure! I will adopt gardening as my hobby. I shall grow plants in my lawn. Samad: That's nice. Wish you all the best. Let's go now. It's time for school. Hamid: Yes, you are right. Allah Hafiz! || درختوں کی قدر کے بارے میں دو دوستوں کے درمیان گفتگو۔ || د ونو د ارزښت په اړه د دوو ملګرو ترمنځ خبرې اترې.
