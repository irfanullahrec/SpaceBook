## B. Grammar Structure: Singular and Plural Nouns || ب۔ گرامر کی ساخت: واحد اور جمع اسم || ب. د ګرامر جوړښت: مفرد او جمع نومونه
exercise Exercise (My Promise: Grammar Structure)
box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- Singular noun stands for one person, place and thing.
- Plural nouns means more than one persons, places and things.
endbox
box info | Rules for plural nouns || جمع بنانے کے قاعدے || د جمع جوړولو قاعدې
- (a) Most nouns are changed from singular to plural by adding 's' in the end of a singular noun. Examples: bottle - bottles, cup - cups, pencil - pencils, desk - desks, sticker - stickers, window - windows.
- (b) For nouns that end with 'ch', 's', 'x' or 'ss' sounds, require 'es' at their end. Examples: box - boxes, watch - watches, bush - bushes, glass - glasses, bench - benches.
- (c) For nouns ending in 'f' or 'fe' change 'f' to 'v' and add 'es'. Examples: wolf - wolves, wife - wives, leaf - leaves, life - lives, knife - knives, half - halves.
- (d) Some nouns have different plural forms. They are called irregular nouns. Examples: child - children, woman - women, man - men, mouse - mice, goose - geese, tooth - teeth.
- (e) With nouns ending in 'y' but preceded by a vowel, we add 's' in the end of that noun. Examples: boy - boys, toy - toys, monkey - monkeys, money - moneys, key - keys, donkey - donkeys.
- (f) With nouns ending in 'y' preceded by a consonant, replace these ending 'y' with 'ie' and add 's' after it. Examples: city - cities, story - stories, fly - flies.
- (g) A few nouns have the same singular and plural forms. Examples: sheep - sheep, deer - deer, series - series, rice - rice, fish - fish, hair - hair, advice - advice, aircraft - aircraft, trout - trout, cattle - cattle, news - news, sugar - sugar.
endbox
