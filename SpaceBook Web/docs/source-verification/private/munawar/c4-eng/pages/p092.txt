box note | Summary of the lesson || سبق کا خلاصہ || د درس لنډیز
p It is a story of a selfish giant who did not understand that true and evergreen happiness lies in sharing things with others. It also shows that the children and nature make a beautiful whole together. The selfish giant did not allow children to play in his garden. They were apart. It made the garden barren. When the selfish giant saw that playing of the children in the garden has turned the garden into fully loaded with flowers and full of life, he realized his mistake. Then, the children started playing there in the garden and the garden has become evergreen. (read as far as it shows)
endbox

box note | Quarter to and Quarter Past Clock Time: Remember! || پونے اور سوا وقت: یاد رکھیں! || پاؤ کم او پاؤ تېر وخت: یاد ولرئ!
- There are 60 minutes in an hour.
- 30 minutes is half an hour, we say "half past".
- 15 minutes is quarter of an hour.
- We say "quarter past" ... or "quarter to" ...
- If your school opens at 15 minutes before 8 O' clock, we say that it is quarter to eight.
- If your school closes at 15 minutes after 2 O' clock, we say that it is quarter past two.
endbox
