## B. Grammar Structure: Pronouns || ب۔ گرامر کی ساخت: ضمیر || ب. د ګرامر جوړښت: ضمیرونه
box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- 'Pro' means 'in place of' and 'noun' is the naming word for a person, place or a thing.
- A pronoun is a word that replaces a noun in the sentence.
- Pronouns are used to avoid repeating the same nouns over and over again.
- For example: Asad is a student. He studies in class-iv. Sehr is his sister. She is younger than Asad. They are students. Fahad is his brother. He is in class-2. They all play together.
endbox

box note | Remember! || یاد رکھیں! || یاد ولرئ!
- A paragraph comprises a few sentences on one idea given as in topic.
- We plan to write it with suitable vocabulary.
- We make use of 'transitional devices' to join various sentences in it. These sentences, when written as a unit, make a paragraph.
