sol In the memory of the great sacrifice of Hazrat Ismaeel (AS) by his father, Hazrat Ibrahim (AS), following his Sunnah. ## From p. 38 and 39. || In the memory of the great sacrifice of Hazrat Ismaeel (AS) by his father, Hazrat Ibrahim (AS), following his Sunnah. || In the memory of the great sacrifice of Hazrat Ismaeel (AS) by his father, Hazrat Ibrahim (AS), following his Sunnah.
qp v | How do Muslims distribute the meat of sacrificed animal?
sol The meat is distributed evenly in three parts: one part for the relatives, one part for the poor and one part for the donor and his family. ## From p. 38. || The meat is distributed evenly in three parts: one part for the relatives, one part for the poor and one part for the donor and his family. || The meat is distributed evenly in three parts: one part for the relatives, one part for the poor and one part for the donor and his family.
