## Food and Water Hygiene || کھانے اور پانی کی صفائی || د خوراک او اوبو پاکوالی
p We can also avoid germs of various diseases by following food and water hygiene rules.
- Drink clean water. If it does not appear clean, boil or filter and then consume it.
- Do not purchase and consume fruits and vegetables which have been cut and kept in the open air for a long time.
- Keep all food items covered to prevent contamination by flies.
## Environment || ماحول || چاپېریال
- Throw waste in dustbin to avoid upbringing of flies.
- Clean the dustbins regularly.
- Regularly check the places where water is collected.
- The container of drinking water must be kept at a clean and safe place.
## Vaccination || ٹیکہ کاری || واکسین
p Vaccine increase the ability of our body to fight disease-causing germs. A large number of infectious diseases can be prevented by taking vaccines at the appropriate time, such as, Covid-19, polio, tetanus, rabies, measles, chickenpox, etc.

box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- Illness is an unhealthy condition of body or mind.
- Some of the common signs of illness are fever, cough, influenza, stomach aches.
- There are two major categories of diseases, i.e. non-contagious and contagious.
- This type of diseases do not transmit easily. Examples include cancer, diabetes and heart diseases.
- A disease that is caused by a microorganism or germ and is spread from an infected organism or the environment to another organism is called a contagious disease.
- T.B, hepatitis, influenza, Covid-19 and polio are examples of contagious diseases.
- There are several ways in which germs of contagious diseases transmit from one infected person to many people.
- The best way to prevent a disease is to avoid its possible causes.
endbox
