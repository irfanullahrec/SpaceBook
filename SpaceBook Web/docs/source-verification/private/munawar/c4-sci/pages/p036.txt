## iii. Fats || iii۔ چکنائی (فیٹ) || iii. غوړ
p They provide more energy than carbohydrates. Our body stores these substances for future use. Fats may be obtained from animal or vegetable sources. The major sources of animal fats are ghee, butter, milk, cheese, eggs, fish and meat.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Fast food items like pizzas and burgers are not sources of fats. Therefore, children who consume only fast food items tend to become chubby.
endbox
## iv. Vitamin and Minerals || iv۔ حیاتین اور معدنیات || iv. ویټامینونه او منرالونه
p Our body needs a certain amount of vitamins for good health. Our body, however, cannot synthesize them. Therefore, we need to get them from natural sources such as fruits and vegetables.
p Minerals such as calcium, sodium, potassium, iron and iodine are required for the proper growth and development of our body.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Each vitamin has a specific function and its deficiency leads to a particular deficiency disease.
endbox
## 1.2 Nutrients and Food Groups || ۱.۲ غذائی اجزاء اور غذائی گروہ || ۱.۲ غذايي توکي او خوراکي ډلې
p To understand which kind of food will give us the right balance of nutrients, food items have been divided into six major groups given in the table below.
