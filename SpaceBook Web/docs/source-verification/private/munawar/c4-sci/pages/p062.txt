## Celsius (centigrade) Scale || سیلسیئس (سینٹی گریڈ) پیمانہ || سیلسیوس (سانتي ګراډ) پیمانه
p This scale shows temperature in centigrade or Celsius °C. It is usually used in scientific laboratories and defines the freezing point of water as 0 °C and the boiling point of water as 100 °C.
## Fahrenheit Scale || فارن ہائیٹ پیمانہ || فارنهایټ پیمانه
p This scale shows temperature in Fahrenheit °F. It is used for clinical and meteorological purposes. It measures the melting point of ice at 32 °F and boiling point of water at 212 °F.

## 4. Electricity and Simple Electric Circuits || ۴۔ بجلی اور سادہ برقی دور || ۴. برېښنا او ساده برېښنايي دورې
p The energy of moving electric charges within a circuit is called electrical energy. Electric energy produces electricity. Electricity is widely used to power many things such as televisions, bulbs, machines and fans, etc.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Lightning is an example of electrical energy found in nature.
endbox
## 4.1 What are Electric Circuits? || ۴.۱ برقی دور کیا ہیں؟ || ۴.۱ برېښنايي دورې څه دي؟
p Electric current can only flow in a circuit. A circuit can transfer electrical energy from place to place over hundreds of kilometers. A simple electric circuit can consist of a battery (or other energy source), a light bulb (or other device that uses energy), and wires that connect the two terminals of the battery to the two ends of the light bulb. It may also include a switch which opens or closes a gap in the circuit. The switch turns the circuit on or off.
## 4.2 How Electric Circuits Work? || ۴.۲ برقی دور کیسے کام کرتے ہیں؟ || ۴.۲ برېښنايي دورې څنګه کار کوي؟
p When the connections are made properly, the circuit will 'close' and current will flow through the circuit and light the bulb.
p Once one of the wires is removed from the power source or a 'break' is made in the flow. The circuit is now 'open' and the bulb will no longer give light. (Pictures: open circuit and close circuit.)
