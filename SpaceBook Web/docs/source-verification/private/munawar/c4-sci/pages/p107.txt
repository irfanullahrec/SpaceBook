## Wheel and Axle Model || پہیہ اور دھرا ماڈل || څرخ او محور ماډل
p Wheel and axle is a type of simple machine we use daily in many tools and complex machines. Step 1: Make two sphere or balls from play dough or clay. Step 2: Press the sphere from both sides to make it flat and round circles/wheels. Step 3: Separately, make a cylinder having a thickness less than wheels. Step 4: Break a matchstick into two halves. Insert each half in the one end of the cylinder and join wheels at the both ends with the help of match sticks. Step 5: You may attach a white coloured flat circle at the outer side of wheels.

## Rollers and Gears || رولر اور گیئر || رولر او ګیرونه
p Step 1: Use various flower cookie cutters to cut the modeling clay or play dough into different shapes. Step 2: Now use a circle cookie cutter to cut a suitable circle to cut. Step 3: Once that is done, use a small round straw to cut out the center. You will have different kinds of gears. Step 4: Make two cylinders out of a modelled paper and insert in two gear models. Hold or place the rollers in a way that the two cylinders are meshed together.
