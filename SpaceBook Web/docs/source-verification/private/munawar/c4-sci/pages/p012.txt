## 1.6 Bones || ۱.۶ ہڈیاں || ۱.۶ هډوکي
p All vertebrate animals have a framework of bones called the skeleton. The skeleton supports animals' body and helps them in movement. (Pictures: skeletons of a cow, a chicken and a fish.)
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Each bone of the skeleton is an organ having a specific function. For example, the skull protects the brain whereas the bones constituting the ribcage protect lungs and heart.
endbox
## 1.7 Muscles || ۱.۷ پٹھے || ۱.۷ عضلات
p Muscle is a soft and flexible part that can contract and relax. There are different types of muscles that perform different tasks.
- Our bones are covered with skeletal muscles. They pull our bones and help in movement.
- Heart is made up of special type of muscles that help it to pump the blood in our bodies.
- Stomach and lungs are also made of other type of muscles that help the food to move and air to get in and out by contracting and relaxing.

box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Take a human outline or trace your friend on a large chart paper. Then start drawing the human body parts (such as brain, eyes, nose, lips, ears, heart, lungs, and stomach) in the outline. Colour and label the body parts and display in the classroom.

box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- When students are familiar with a variety of animal internal organs, assign them the activities that encourage them to scientific how one organ is similar to or contributes to the work of another organ. Ask the students questions such as: How is the Human heart a similar organ? This encourages the students to consider similarities and differences which they may not generally be taken for granted.
