## 1. Major Organs of Animals || ۱۔ جانوروں کے اہم اعضاء || ۱. د څارویو مهم غړي
p Eyes, nose, arm, leg, foot and hand are examples of external parts of body. All animals also have internal body organs.
## 1.1 Teeth || ۱.۱ دانت || ۱.۱ غاښونه
p Animals have different types of teeth to chew their food. Herbivores have flat and wide teeth, carnivores have sharp and pointed teeth while omnivores have combination of all type of teeth.
- Incisors are sharp teeth that are used for biting and cutting.
- Canines are pointed teeth that are used for tearing food.
- Premolars are flat teeth that that are used to chew food.
- Molars are broad and flat teeth that help in grinding food into tiny particles that are suitable to swallow.
p Herbivores such as horses and rabbits have flat teeth to chew and grind plants. Carnivores such as tigers and lions have sharp teeth to rip and tear meat. Omnivores such as humans have combination of flat and pointed teeth to eat plants and meat. (Pictures: skulls and a mouth, Fig. 1.5 Types of teeth.)
## 1.2 Brain || ۱.۲ دماغ || ۱.۲ مغز
p Brain is the control center of a body. It is protected by the skull. The brain is made up of nerve cells. A network of nerves connect the whole body to the brain. In this way, brain controls all the actions of an animal's body such as seeing, hearing, smelling, walking, etc.
