## 2.5 Conductors or Insulators || ۲.۵ موصل یا غیر موصل || ۲.۵ تیریدونکي یا نه تیریدونکي
- The materials that allow electricity to flow through them easily are called conductors. e.g. copper, gold, iron etc.
- Opposite to conductors are the insulators which do not let the electric current pass through them. e.g. rubber, wood, glass, etc.
p Have you ever wondered why the handles of cooking utensils are mostly made up of wood or plastic? Why the electric wires have plastic coating? It is because wood, plastic, glass and rubber do not allow heat and electricity to pass through them. Some materials such as water or metals are good conductors of heat and electricity. Therefore, only metals are used to make cooking utensils. Similarly, many metals such as copper is used in making electrical wires, silver and gold are used in computers and mobiles to conduct electricity.
