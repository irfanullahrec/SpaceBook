## 2. Plants || ۲۔ پودے || ۲. نباتات
p Plants are living things. They do much more than just adding beauty to our world. They give us the food we eat and some of the clothes we wear. They even give off a gas that we breathe, called oxygen. Without plants, Earth would be an empty place for the animals. Different plants are different from each other. Some plants have flowers while others do not have any.
## 2.1 Flowering Plants || ۲.۱ پھول دار پودے || ۲.۱ ګل لرونکي نباتات
p The plants, which produce flowers during the course of its life cycle, are called flowering plants. A flowering plant is also called an angiosperm. A few examples include rose, hibiscus, sunflower, mango, bananas, and papaya. (Pictures: rose plant, sunflower plant, mango tree, banana tree, papaya tree.)
## 2.2 Non-Flowering Plants || ۲.۲ بغیر پھول والے پودے || ۲.۲ بې ګله نباتات
p Non-flowering plants do not carry flowers. However, these plants are divided into two main groups - those that reproduce with dust like particles called spores and those that use seeds to reproduce.
## I. Spore-bearing plants || I۔ بیضے (سپور) پیدا کرنے والے پودے || I. سپور لرونکي نباتات
p Spore-bearing plants are simple plants for example mosses and ferns. These plants release thousands of tiny spores, which are spread by wind or water. All spore-bearing plants mostly live in damp or shady areas.
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Explain the differences between vertebrates and invertebrates.
- Show to the students various pictures of animals and let them sort those pictures as vertebrates or invertebrates.
- Ask the students about their choices and reasons behind their choices.
endbox
