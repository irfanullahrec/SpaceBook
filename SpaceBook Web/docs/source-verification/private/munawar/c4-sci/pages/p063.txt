## 4.3 Transformation of Electrical Energy || ۴.۳ برقی توانائی کی تبدیلی || ۴.۳ د برېښنايي انرژۍ بدلون
p Have you ever noticed a bulb become hot after we switch it on for long period of time? This is because as charges flow in a circuit, some electrical energy always changes to heat energy.
p Many household appliances act like resistors when they are in a circuit. A resistor can change electrical energy to other forms of energy.
- A light bulb is a resistor that transforms electrical energy to light energy.
- Bells and buzzers are resistors that transform electrical energy to sound energy.
- A toaster is a resistor that transform electrical energy to heat energy.
p These appliances transform electrical energy to heat, sound and light. (Pictures: a hair dryer, an oven, a toaster, a heater.)

box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
p 1. Light: Light is the visible form of energy. We see things around us because of light. When light falls on an opaque object, it cannot reach at opposite side of the object but a formed dark area called shadow. Some opaque objects bend the light in another direction. This is called reflection of light. Change in medium and speed causes the light to change its direction. This is called refraction of light. When white light travels through a different medium, it splits up and its colours become visible. This is called dispersion of light. Rainbows are formed due to dispersion of light.
