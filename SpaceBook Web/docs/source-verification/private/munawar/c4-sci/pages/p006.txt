## II. Seed-bearing non-flowering Plants || II۔ بیج پیدا کرنے والے بغیر پھول کے پودے || II. تخم لرونکي بې ګله نباتات
p The second group of non-flowering plants produce seeds. This type of plants are called gymnosperms. This group includes large coniferous trees such as cedar, spruce and firs. They have woody cones instead of flowers where seeds are produced. (Pictures: cedar, spruce, Douglas fir.)
box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Use small pots to plant few seeds. Provide water and sunlight to them. Observe their growth. Record changes.
endbox
## Comparison between Flowering and Non-Flowering Plants || پھول دار اور بغیر پھول والے پودوں کا موازنہ || د ګل لرونکو او بې ګله نباتاتو پرتله
p Non-Flowering Plants: pine cones (seeds, cone scales). Flowering Plants: tomato and apple (seeds inside the fruit, flesh, seed coat). (Pictures with parts named.)
## 3. Diversity in Life || ۳۔ زندگی میں تنوع || ۳. په ژوند کې رنګارنګي
p Life exists almost everywhere on the Earth from bottom of the oceans to the top of the highest mountains. There is a variety of organisms such as blue whales, giant redwood trees and also tiny life forms such as microorganisms such as germs. This variety of organisms is called diversity in life or simply biodiversity (bio means life and diversity means variety).

p Even the animals or plants which belong to the same group have many differences among them. These differences are also studied under biodiversity. Look at the given picture. How are these dogs similar and how are they different?
