## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo describe a few food chains and analyse their structure to understand its function.
slo describe the role of living things at each link in a simple food chain e.g., plants produce their own food, some animals eat plants, while other animals eat the animals that eat plants.

slo recognize and explain that some living things in an ecosystem compete with each other for food and space.
slo recognize the value of a balanced ecosystem.

slo identify various actions and roles that humans can play in preserving various ecosystems.

## Relationship in Simple Food Chains || سادہ غذائی زنجیروں میں تعلق || په ساده خوراکي زنځیرونو کې اړیکه
p Food is the basic need of all living things. It provides organisms with energy. This energy is required to sustain life and carry out all other important functions of living things.
p Some organisms make their own food whereas others do not. In every ecosystem there is a feeding relationship between the organisms. A food chain is the model of how energy in food is passed from organism to organism in an ecosystem. Each organism is like a link in food chain.
p Vocabulary: feeding, relationship, food chain, producers, primary consumer, secondary consumer, tertiary consumer, prey, predator, food web, population, competition, pollution, urbanization, deforestation. (Pictures: a child eating, a goat eating leaves; a food chain: Sun, Plant, Deer, Lion.)
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Construct a model or create a chart by pasting pictures of animals and plants in a simple food chain using common plants and animals from familiar ecosystem such as a forest or a desert. Using these models/charts allow the students to observe and discuss the food chains so that they can understand how a food chain actually works.
endbox
