## 2.2 Surface Water Pollution || ۲.۲ سطحی پانی کی آلودگی || ۲.۲ د سطحې اوبو ککړتیا
p Many people get water from freshwater sources such as rivers, lakes and streams. Nearly half of our rivers and more than one-third of our lakes are polluted and unfit for swimming, fishing, and drinking due to following reasons: Farm waste and fertilizers. Municipal and industrial waste discharges.
## 2.3 Purifying Water || ۲.۳ پانی کو صاف کرنا || ۲.۳ د اوبو پاکول
p It is extremely important to confirm that water is clean and safe before drinking. There are many ways in which we can make water clean and suitable for drinking. Some of them have been given below:
## i. Boiling || i۔ ابالنا || i. جوشول
p Boiling water is the cheapest and safest method of water purification. In this method, clean water should be brought to boil and left boiling for 1-3 minutes. Boiled water should be covered and left to cool before drinking.
## ii. Filtration || ii۔ چھاننا (فلٹریشن) || ii. چاڼول
p Filtration is another way of water purification. This method uses different layers of rocks, sand and charcoal to purify water and make it safe for drinking. Filtration can remove large and small particles along with a few disease causing germs.
