# Ecosystems || ماحولیاتی نظام || ایکوسیستمونه
## A. Diversity of the Conditions for Life on Earth || الف۔ زمین پر زندگی کے حالات کا تنوع || الف. په ځمکه د ژوند د شرایطو رنګارنګي
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo recognize what is an ecosystem (e.g., forests, ponds, rivers, grasslands and deserts).
slo explain biotic (plants, animals and humans) and abiotic factors (light, temperature, soil and water) and their linkages.
slo analyse the way these biotic and abiotic conditions create a balance to sustain any ecosystem.
slo recognize the interactions between animals and plants and the importance of maintaining balance within an ecosystem.

- What do you know about an ecosystem?
- Name any three non-living things you see in your surroundings.
- Where does a marine grow?
endbox
p Plants need soil to grow, fish needs water to live and we cannot live without breathing air. This is how living and non-living things interact every day.
box note | Activity 2A.1 || سرگرمی 2A.1 || فعالیت 2A.1
p Ecosystems are made of both living and non-living things. What living things do you see in the given picture? What non-living things do you see? (A picture of a lake, a forest and a deer.)
endbox
p An ecosystem is all the interacting parts in an environment. An ecosystem can be large such as a forest or small such as a pond. Large or small ecosystems are made up of living and non-living things. The living things of an ecosystem are called biotic factors. All plants, animals and microorganisms are biotic factors. The non-living things of an ecosystem are called abiotic factors. They include temperature, rainfall, soil, sunlight and air.
p Vocabulary: ecosystem, biotic, abiotic, climate, environment
box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Look around your local environment. What plants and animals do you see? What abiotic factors are important in your ecosystem? Make a chart to record your observations.
endbox
