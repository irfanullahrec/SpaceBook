p Earth's natural resources can be grouped by the processes and the time needed to produce them.
## 4. Nonrenewable Resources || ۴۔ ناقابلِ تجدید وسائل || ۴. نه نوي کېدونکې سرچینې
p These resources take so long to replace that they are considered nonrenewable. Nonrenewable resources are very limited or are used up more quickly than they can be replaced. Fossil fuels are nonrenewable because they take millions of years to form. Once the existing supply of fossil fuels has gone, that fuel cannot be replaced. Minerals such as copper and gold are also nonrenewable resources.

## 5. Renewable Resources || ۵۔ قابلِ تجدید وسائل || ۵. نوي کېدونکې سرچینې
p Resources that can be replaced by the nature are called renewable resources. For example; if trees are cut down, their seeds can be planted. Water is constantly replenished through the water cycle. Some resources, such as solar energy and wind energy are almost endless - there is virtually no end to their supply.
p However, some renewable resources takes hundreds of years to form, such as soil. It takes 500 to 2000 years to replace a few centimetres of topsoil.
