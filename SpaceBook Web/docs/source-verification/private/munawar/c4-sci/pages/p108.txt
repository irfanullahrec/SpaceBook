## Using Alarm Clock || الارم کلاک کا استعمال || د الارم ساعت کارول
p All smart phones have a built-in clock for keeping the track of time and setting alarms. From Apps, tap Clock or tap time on the Home screen to access it.
box note | Activity: 10.3 || سرگرمی: 10.3 || فعالیت: 10.3
p Look at the phone's alarm clock and set an alarm for Fajar prayer or 1 hour before school. 1. Use the AM with time (AM is used for the time from 12 O' clock at midnight till 12 O' clock in the noon). 2. Select the option repeat (all week days). 3. For school alarm, uncheck the Sunday (it means alarm will not be called on Sundays). 4. Select the alarm tone of your own choice. 5. Add the alarm title such as Fajar prayer time or school time.
endbox
