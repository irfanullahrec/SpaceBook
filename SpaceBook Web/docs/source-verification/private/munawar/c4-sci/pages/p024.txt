## 5. Value of a Balanced Ecosystem || ۵۔ متوازن ماحولیاتی نظام کی قدر || ۵. د متوازن ایکوسیستم ارزښت
p Living things in a food web depends on one another. Suppose the population of one organism in a food web changes. A population consists of all members of a single type of organism in an environment. When one population in the food web changes, all other populations in the food web can also be affected. This is what happened in the ocean's kelp forests. Kelp is a type of seaweed. Kelp forests are home to thousands of ocean creatures. Sea otters eat several animals in their ocean food web. (A picture of the kelp forest food web.)

p In the 1800s, too many sea otters were hunted for their fur. Without sea otters, fewer sea animals were eaten. Hence, they increase in number. More animals ate kelps and destroy the whole forest. In the 1900s, many countries agreed to stop hunting sea otters. The kelp forest web became balanced again.
