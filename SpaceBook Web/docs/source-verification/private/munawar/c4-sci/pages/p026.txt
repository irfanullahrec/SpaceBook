## 5.2 Urbanization and Deforestation || ۵.۲ شہری آبادی اور جنگلات کی کٹائی || ۵.۲ ښاري کېدل او ځنګلونو پرې کول
p The growing human population demands more space to live on the Earth. Thus, human population growth in and around cities is called urbanization. The urbanization often destroys natural habitats.
p Sometimes, people construct roads which cross a natural habitat, dividing into two sections. This prevent animals to safely roam in their territory. When animals cannot interact with each other, ecosystem face the problems.
p An ecosystem or a forest may be converted into a housing scheme or a shopping area. It completely destroys an ecosystem. Clearing of forest is called deforestation.
p Every ecosystem has its own number and variety of organisms. If an ecosystem is damaged or destroyed, many animals loose their lives. This is because the whole ecosystem is connected with each other in such a way that may affect the entire ecosystem.

## 6. How do Humans protect the Ecosystem? || ۶۔ انسان ماحولیاتی نظام کی حفاظت کیسے کرتے ہیں؟ || ۶. انسانان ایکوسیستم څنګه ساتي؟
p There are many ways that humans can protect ecosystems. One way is by using Earth's resources in a careful manner. The resource of an ecosystem include all of its living and nonliving parts.
p Wise use of Natural Resources. Humans can protect ecosystems by using fewer natural resources and reducing the waste. The famous 'three R' are three ways to protect natural resources.
- Reduce what you buy and use - this is the first goal to protect natural resources.
