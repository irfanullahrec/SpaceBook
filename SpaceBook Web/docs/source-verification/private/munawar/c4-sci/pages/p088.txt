box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- Weather refers to the day-to-day conditions of a certain location.
- Climate is the long term average weather pattern of an area. Humidity is the amount of water vapour in the air.
- Weather can be described through temperature, humidity, precipitation and wind.
- The water that falls from cloud in the form of rain, snow or hail is called precipitation.
- As we move above the sea level, the temperature becomes low and winds become strong.
- Mountain regions are generally cold, humid and have more precipitation.
- The areas near the sea are called coastal areas. These areas often have moderate weather (neither too hot nor too cold).
- During the day, winds usually blow from the water towards the land. It is called sea breeze.
- At night, the wind changes direction and blows from the land towards the water. It is called land breeze.
- Cycles of weather changes in the year are called seasons. There are four seasons: winter, spring, summer and autumn.
- The Earth is divided into three climatic zones depending upon the amount of sunlight each zone receives, i.e. tropical zone, temperate zone and polar zone.
