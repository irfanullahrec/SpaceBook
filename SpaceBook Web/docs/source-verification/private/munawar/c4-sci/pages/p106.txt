## 2. Basic Shapes - 3D models || ۲۔ بنیادی شکلیں - تین ابعادی ماڈل || ۲. بنسټیزې بڼې - درې اړخیز ماډلونه
p Sphere: Take a small piece of modeling clay. Squeeze it into a rough ball with your fingers. Roll it around and around between the palms of your hands. Make it into a smooth, round ball.
p Cylinder: Squeeze a small ball of clay into a sausage shape. Roll out a short, thick coil and gently tap the ends onto the placemat to make them flat and smooth.
p Cone: Start with a ball. Continue to squeeze the top end until it becomes a point (simplet). Gently tap the base on the slab until it is smooth and flat.
p Prism: Start with a cone. Place a ruler or flat object at the sides of the cone and gently press down to flatten it. Make four sloppy flat faces.
p Cube: Start with a very thick cylinder. Place a ruler or flat object at the top of it and gently press down to flatten it. Turn it so the flat side is the top and repeat. Keep doing all sides until you have created a cube with six faces.
## 3. Clay Models of Simple Machines || ۳۔ سادہ مشینوں کے مٹی کے ماڈل || ۳. د ساده ماشینونو د خټو ماډلونه
p The history of play-dough is very interesting. It was actually invented by accident. In the 1930s, Noah McVicker made a new kind of wallpaper cleaner. In early 90s, most of the people burn coal at their homes for cooking and warmth. It resulted in the formation of a layer of black coal residue settled on walls which was easily removed by the play-dough.
p Modeling clay was invented before play-dough and used to make correction in the sculptures. Now it is used in many educational activities.
p Clay model of a hammer: Step 1: Start with a thick cylinder. Step 2: Cover one end of the cylinder with a different coloured clay. Step 3: Roll another small cylinder. Step 4: Place the small cylinder over coloured end of the large cylinder horizontally. Step 5: Insert a matchstick in the centre of the small cylinder. Step 6: Make a small clay circle and join it at the one end of the matchstick. Step 7: Roll a thin loop of clay/play dough and bend it to make horns of a goat. Join it with the other end of the matchstick. Step 8: Model of a hammer is ready.
