## Friction || رگڑ || کشش (رگړ)
p Do you know what slows things down? It is friction. Friction is a force that slows down moving things. It is produced when two things rub against each other. For example; if we push a book on the table, the pores present on two surfaces will be meshed together and oppose the movement of the book.
box note | Activity: 6.3 || سرگرمی: 6.3 || فعالیت: 6.3
p Roll a ball on the floor and observe its movement. 1. In which direction does it move? 2. Did the ball keep moving or stop after sometime? 3. How can you describe its motion and reason to stop?
endbox
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Explain to the students that gravity do not belong on the Earth alone. Anything, which has mass, also has a gravitational pull. However, we can only observe the gravitational pull of huge size bodies. This is because the mass of the objects is the strongest will have its gravitational pull.
endbox
box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Rub your hands with each other and feel the force. The hands are acting in opposite directions which resist the motion. If you keep rubbing for a minute you feel your hands become warmer. This is because friction also produces heat.
endbox
p There is more friction on rough surfaces than on smooth ones. It is harder to push or pull something on a rough surface than on a smooth surface.
box note | Activity: 6.4 || سرگرمی: 6.4 || فعالیت: 6.4
p Types of surfaces and friction. Material: cardboard, books, toy car, ruler, sandpaper, glue or tape. Procedure: 1. Make a ramp out of books and cardboard. 2. Put the car in the top of the ramp and let it go (do not push it). 3. Measure with the help of ruler that how far the car went. 4. Now, stick the sandpaper in the cardboard by means of glue or tape. Repeat the activity. Inquiry Question: Which ramp slowed the car down more? Predict: What would happen if you put cloth on the ramp?
endbox
## Where is friction necessary? || رگڑ کہاں ضروری ہے؟ || رگړ چېرته اړین دی؟
p Friction is helpful. If an object starts moving, it would never stop if there were no friction. Therefore, running shoes and tyre have treads to add friction. These treads help people and cars to move over slippery surfaces easily.
