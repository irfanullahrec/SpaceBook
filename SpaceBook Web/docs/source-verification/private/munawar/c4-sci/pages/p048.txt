box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- Matter is anything that takes up space and has mass.
- The three states of matter are solid, liquid and gas.
- The solid is matter with a definite shape and volume. The liquid is matter that has definite volume but no definite shape.
- Gas is matter that does not have definite shape or volume.
- Physical properties are the characteristics of matter which we can observe without changing the identity of a substance.
- The amount of matter present in a material is called its mass. The amount of space taken up by an object is called its volume.
- The density tells us about how heavier or lighter an object is compared to its size (volume).
- The materials which allow heat and electricity to pass through them are called conductors. The materials which do not allow heat and electricity to pass through them are called insulators.
