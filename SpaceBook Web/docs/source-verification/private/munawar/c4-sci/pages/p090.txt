# Earth in the Solar System || نظامِ شمسی میں زمین || په لمریز سیستم کې ځمکه
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo describe and demonstrate the solar system with the sun at the center and the planets revolving around the sun.
slo identify the Sun as a source of heat and light for the Solar System.
slo recognize that the Earth has a unique day and night caused by it and from Earth the moon looks different at different times of the month.
slo investigate and describe how day and night are related to Earth's daily rotation about its axis, and provide evidence of the rotation from the changing appearance of shadows during the day.
slo describe how seasons in Earth's Northern and Southern hemispheres are related to Earth's annual movement around the Sun.
slo illustrate and explain how solar and lunar eclipses occur.
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- What is Sun?
- How many planets are there in a Solar system?
- How moon is different from planets?
- Why does moon appear to have different phases in a month?
endbox
p The Sun is in the center of the solar system. The word Solar means 'of the Sun'. The Solar System is a system of the Sun and eight planets, moons and other bodies travelling around it.
p In the space, Sun is fixed at its position and planets revolve around it. From near to farthest from the Sun, the planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
p Our Solar System (a picture of the Sun and the planets). Vocabulary: planet, moons, rotation, revolution, eclipse, phase, solar, lunar.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p A simple way to recall sequence of the planets is: My Very Efficient Mother Just Served Us Nuts (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune).
endbox
box note | Amazing Fact! || حیرت انگیز حقیقت! || په زړه پورې حقیقت!
p Pluto was dispensed on February 18th, 1930. It was considered the ninth and most distant planet from the Sun. After 76 years, in 2006, Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet.
endbox
