## 5. Human Impact on Ecosystem || ۵۔ ماحولیاتی نظام پر انسانی اثرات || ۵. پر ایکوسیستم د انسان اغېزه
p Human activities can change and even harm ecosystems. Any group of living and non-living things interacting with each other can be considered as an ecosystem. Changing one thing in an ecosystem can affect many other things, because everything is connected.
## 5.1 Pollution || ۵.۱ آلودگی || ۵.۱ ککړتیا
p Human can affect ecosystems through pollution. Pollution is caused by any material that harms the environment. For example; factories and vehicles burn fossil fuels and release harmful chemicals into the environment. Humans use pesticides on their crops that may run off and pollute the waterways. Humans can also litter, polluting the environment.
