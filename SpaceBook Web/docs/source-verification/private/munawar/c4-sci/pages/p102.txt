p You can turn into a different character with a mask, and they're beneficial for social development and confidence if used in role-play.
p Things you may need: Paper plates with a hole on either side, pen/marker/crayons, strips of coloured scrap papers, glue, pieces of string, or elastic thread to keep them on. If you want to make it easier for ask an older to cut out some shapes of eyes and noses, etc. You can even cut the plates in to different shapes.
