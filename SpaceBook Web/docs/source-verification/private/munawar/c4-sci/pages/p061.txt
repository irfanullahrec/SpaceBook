p Thermal means heat. Heat Energy is also known as thermal energy. Warmer objects have a higher temperature than cooler objects.

box note | Fact Time || حقیقت کا وقت || د حقیقت وخت
p Some materials conduct or transfer heat better than others. For example, metals are much better conductors of heat than wood or cloth.
endbox
box note | Let's Do It! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Material needed: 2 containers, warm and cold water, 2 thermometers. Procedure: 1. Take two containers (jars, glass, label one as warmer and other as cooler). 2. Add warm (not hot) water in the first container and cool water in the other one. 3. Place a thermometer in both containers for 60 sec. 4. Compare the level of mercury/liquid of both thermometers. 5. Write your observations in your notebook and share it with your class teacher.
endbox
## 3.1 Heat Transfer || ۳.۱ حرارت کی منتقلی || ۳.۱ د تودوخې لېږد
p Heat energy always moves from the hotter material to the colder material. For an example, when you hold an ice cube, it might feel like your hand is absorbing the coldness, but it is actually the other way around - the heat of your body transfers to the ice cube, which results in the ice cube obtaining a higher temperature and eventually melting.
box note | Activity: 5B.6 || سرگرمی: 5B.6 || فعالیت: 5B.6
p What happens when a hot object is brought in contact with a cold object? Materials needed: hot water container, long metal spoon. Procedure: 1. With the help of an elder, place a hot water container on the shelf. 2. Dip one end of the long metal spoon into the hot water. 3. Wait for a while. Do you feel the other end (hold) with your hand? 4. Write your observation in your notebook and share with your class teacher.
endbox
## 3.2 Measurement of Heat || ۳.۲ حرارت کی پیمائش || ۳.۲ د تودوخې اندازه
p Temperature is a measure of hotness or coldness of a body. The tool used to measure temperature is called thermometer. There are many different types of thermometers but the most common types which are often used are laboratory and clinical thermometers.
box note | Amazing Fact! || حیرت انگیز حقیقت! || په زړه پورې حقیقت!
p Thermometers work because mercury in the thermometer expands when it is hot and contracts when it is cold.
endbox
## Laboratory Thermometer || لیبارٹری تھرمامیٹر || لابراتوار ترمامیټر
p The thermometer which is used in the laboratory to measure accurate temperature is known as a laboratory thermometer. It ranges from -10 °C to 110 °C.
## Clinical Thermometer || کلینیکل تھرمامیٹر || کلینیکي ترمامیټر
p The thermometer which is used to measure human body temperature is known as Clinical Thermometer. It ranges from 35 °C to 42 °C and 94 °F to 108 °F.
## 3.3 Common Units for measuring Temperature || ۳.۳ درجہ حرارت ناپنے کی عام اکائیاں || ۳.۳ د تودوخې د اندازې عام واحدونه
p The common units for measuring temperature are Celsius (centigrade) and Fahrenheit.
