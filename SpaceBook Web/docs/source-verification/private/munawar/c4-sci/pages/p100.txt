## 3. Treasure out of Trash || ۳۔ کچرے سے خزانہ || ۳. له زباله څخه خزانه
p Waste: Waste is the material that is left over, rejected or thrown away from anything. Waste is known as trash, rubbish, or garbage. In this section, we will be making crafts by reusing or recycling items that may have been sent to the bins, garbage dump or landfill. We will be utilizing a variety of packaging items to transform these materials into useful or attractive items.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p How long some items take to break down in a landfill. Newspaper - between 2 to 6 months or sometimes more. Plastic bottles - up to 200 years. Styrofoam cup - does not decay at any rate of breaking down!
endbox

## 4. Making a Paper Bag || ۴۔ کاغذ کا تھیلا بنانا || ۴. د کاغذ کڅوړه جوړول
p One way to reduce the amount of plastic we use is by using paper bags instead of plastic bags. These paper bags can be designed from wasted paper or old calendar/magazine/newspaper and reused a number of times before they become garbage.
p Materials required: Paper from an old calendar/magazine/newspaper, ruler, pencil, scissors, and glue stick.
