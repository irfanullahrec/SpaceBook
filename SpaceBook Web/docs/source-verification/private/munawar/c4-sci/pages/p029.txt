## 1. Symptoms of Illness || ۱۔ بیماری کی علامات || ۱. د ناروغۍ نښې
p When were you ill last time? How do we know that we are sick? There are some common signs which tell us that we need medical care.
- Fever - When our body temperature is above normal, our body becomes hot and we feel body pain and headache.
- Cough - Cough is another sign of illness, it tells us something is wrong inside our throat.
- Influenza - Generally known as flu, comes with runny nose and watery eyes. Too many sneezes are also a symptom of illness.
- Stomach aches - Pain in stomach also shows some disturbance in the body.
