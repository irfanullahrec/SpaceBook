## Moderate Weather of Coastal Areas || ساحلی علاقوں کا معتدل موسم || د ساحلي سیمو معتدل موسم
p The areas near the sea are called coastal areas. These areas often have moderate weather (neither too hot nor too cold). It happens because of the temperature difference between the sea water and nearby land.
## Sea Breeze || سمندری ہوا || سمندري نسیم
p On warm summer day, the Sun heats the land to a higher temperature than nearby water. The warm air above the land expands and rises. When it happens, the colder air above the sea water moves in to replace the rising warm air above the land. This is called a sea breeze. (Picture: during the day, the wind usually blows from the sea toward the land.)
## Land Breeze || خشکی کی ہوا || وچکې نسیم
p At night the reverse may take place. The land cools more quickly than the nearby water, so the cool air is above the land now and warm air is above the sea. So, a land breeze develops as the wind blows off the land towards the water. (Picture: at night, the wind changes direction and blows from the land toward the water.)

p Land and Water Temperature. Predict: Whether water or land holds heat longer. Material Required: sand, water, 2 containers, 2 thermometers, tub of ice water. Procedure: 1. Fill one container with room-temperature water and other container to the same height with sand. 2. Measure the initial temperature of each container with thermometers by hanging them into the container (make sure they do not touch bottom). 3. Place both containers in a tub of ice water. 4. Record the temperature of each container every 2 minutes. Conclusion: ______ had a faster drop in temperature.

## Cycle of the Weather - Season || موسم کا چکر - فصل || د موسم دوره - فصل
p Weather changes in cycles known as seasons. There are four seasons; winter, spring, summer and autumn. Each season brings changes in temperature and precipitation, as well as changes to the life pattern of living organisms in the area.
