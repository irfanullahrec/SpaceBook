## 2.2.1 Primary Consumers || ۲.۲.۱ ابتدائی صارفین || ۲.۲.۱ لومړني مصرفوونکي
p The animals that eat only plants to get energy are known as primary consumers or herbivores. Caterpillar, deer, sheep, cow, rabbit, giraffe and elephant are few examples of primary consumers.
## 2.2.2 Secondary Consumers || ۲.۲.۲ ثانوی صارفین || ۲.۲.۲ دویمي مصرفوونکي
p The consumers that get their food by eating primary consumers or the animals that eat plants are called secondary consumers. For example; fox eats rabbit and sparrow eats caterpillar.
## 2.2.3 Tertiary Consumers || ۲.۲.۳ تیسرے درجے کے صارفین || ۲.۲.۳ دریمي مصرفوونکي
p The animals that eat secondary consumers for getting energy are called tertiary consumers. For example, a fox may be eaten by a lion and a sparrow may be eaten by sparrow hawk. Thus, the lion and sparrow hawk are tertiary consumers.
