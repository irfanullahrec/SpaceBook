## 2.3 How do we use Soil and Plants? || ۲.۳ ہم مٹی اور پودے کیسے استعمال کرتے ہیں؟ || ۲.۳ موږ خاورې او نباتات څنګه کاروو؟
p Soil and plants are natural resources. You know that you need soil and plants to live. Plants use the soil to grow. We need plants for food and to make things such as furniture, paper and clothes. (Picture: plants grow in the soil, which is formed from rocks.)
## 2.4 How do we use Oil and Natural gas? || ۲.۴ ہم تیل اور قدرتی گیس کیسے استعمال کرتے ہیں؟ || ۲.۴ موږ تیل او طبیعي ګاز څنګه کاروو؟
p A fuel is something that gives off energy (heat) when it burns. We burn wood to get heat. We also burn coal, oil and natural gas. Long ago coal, oil and natural gas formed underground from the remains of dead plants and animals. We use coal, oil and natural gas to cook and heat our homes. Gasoline is a fuel extracted from oil. (Pictures: people use natural gas to cook food at their home; cars and trucks burn gasoline to move.)
p Coal, oil and natural gas are known as fossil fuels. Do you know what a fossil is?
## 3. Fossils || ۳۔ فوسلز (رکازیات) || ۳. فوسیلونه
p A fossil is what is left of a living thing from the past. Some fossils are bones or teeth of animals that lived long ago. Other fossils are prints of whole plants and animals.
## 3.1 Where do we find fossils? || ۳.۱ ہمیں فوسلز کہاں ملتے ہیں؟ || ۳.۱ موږ فوسیلونه چېرته مومو؟
p The scientists find fossils of plants and animals in many places. Some are found in rocks, while others may found in ice, soil or under the sea.
p How are fossils formed? (Pictures: an animal buries under the layers of soil after dying; after sometimes, its skin, muscles and soft body parts decay leaving behind the skeleton; millions of years later, more and more layers are formed over the skeleton forming a fossil.)
box note | Amazing Fact! || حیرت انگیز حقیقت! || په زړه پورې حقیقت!
p Fossils give us clue about what Earth was like long ago. We can learn about the animal size, structure and its habits even if it is extinct.
