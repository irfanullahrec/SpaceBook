p Desert ecosystem has lower rainfall and scorching sunlight. Therefore, only those plants and animals can survive that have adapted to this environment well. Examples of living organisms are snakes, camels and lizards etc.

p Abiotic and Biotic Factors (Desert). Climate: dry and hot; generally very hot days and cool nights. Soil: poor in animals and plant decay products but often rich in minerals. Plants: limited type of plants grow; cacti, bunch grasses, shrubs and a few trees. Animals: camels, antelopes, small foxes, rodents, snakes, lizards, insects and some birds.

box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- All living and non-living things in an area where we organisms live make their ecosystem.
- Forests, grassland, deserts and ponds are some of the common ecosystems.
- In a balanced ecosystem, the biotic and abiotic factors work together.
- Climate describes the typical weather pattern of an area over time.
- Rainforest ecosystem has higher rainfall and mild sunlight; therefore, it provides home to a huge variety of animals and plants.
- Grassland has lesser rainfall than forest ecosystem. It provides an ideal environment for grazing animals such as elephants, giraffe and zebra, etc. Desert ecosystem has lower rainfall and scorching sunlight.
endbox
