## Summer and Winter || گرمی اور سردی || دوبی او ژمی
p Winter is the cold season. Summer is the hot season. Summer and winter seasons are often dry (have less precipitation). (Pictures: summer - hot and sunny; winter - cold and dry.)
## Spring and Autumn || بہار اور خزاں || پسرلی او مني
p Spring and autumn are the moderate seasons. The air begins to warm in the spring and it begins to cool in the autumn. Spring and fall are often wet (have more precipitation). (Pictures: spring - wet and warm; autumn - cool and windy.)

## Long-term average Weather Conditions - Climate || طویل مدتی اوسط موسمی حالات - آب و ہوا || اوږدمهاله اوسط موسمي حالتونه - اقلیم
p Climate depends upon the position and location of an area with respect to the Sun. The Earth is divided into three climatic zones depending upon the amount of sunlight each zone receives. Due to this uneven distribution of sunlight, different zones have different average temperature and rate of precipitation.
## Climatic Zones of the Earth || زمین کے موسمی خطے || د ځمکې اقلیمي سیمې
p There is an imaginary line on the surface of the Earth called equator. The region above and below the equator is called tropical zone. It receives most direct sunlight and has hot and humid climate with high rainfall most of the year.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Pakistan is situated in the tropical zone of the Earth. Climate in a region generally varies a country that is hot and humid with high precipitation. Since there are other factors that affect a region's climate, Pakistan enjoys a big variation in rainfall and temperatures depending on the height and distance from the sea.
endbox
p Temperate zone starts after tropical zone. It receives less direct sunlight and cycle through all four seasons - winter, spring, summer, and autumn.
p The polar zone is located in far northern and far southern regions of the Earth. It has usually cold and dry climate most of the year. Antarctica is in the polar climate zone.
