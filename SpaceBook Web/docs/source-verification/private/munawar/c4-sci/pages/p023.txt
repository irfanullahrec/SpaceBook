## 3. Predator-Prey Relationship || ۳۔ شکاری اور شکار کا تعلق || ۳. ښکاري او ښکار اړیکه
p One animal can be a food for another animals. An animal that is hunted by another animal for food is called prey. An animal that hunts another animal for food is called a predator.

## 4. Competition in an Ecosystem || ۴۔ ماحولیاتی نظام میں مقابلہ || ۴. په ایکوسیستم کې سیالي
p Competition is the struggle of several organisms for the same resource. The animals living in an area compete with each other for food and space.
## 4.1 Competition for Space || ۴.۱ جگہ کے لیے مقابلہ || ۴.۱ د ځای لپاره سیالي
p Wood pecker and squirrels live in the holes and space in the trees. They often compete with each other for nesting rights.
## 4.2 Competition for Food || ۴.۲ خوراک کے لیے مقابلہ || ۴.۲ د خوراک لپاره سیالي
p In an ecosystem, there may be many mice. One morning, a fox eats a mouse. The next day fox eats something else, say a rabbit but a falcon comes to eat the mouse. What food chain is the mouse part of? Is it part of fox's food chain or the falcon's? Is it part of both food chains? Yes it is part of both food chains. Similarly, many food chains in an environment can overlap with each other. A food web shows how a group of food chains are linked together.
