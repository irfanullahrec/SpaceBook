p Miss Bushra Yousaf did her BS Hons in chemistry from Forman Christian College. She has an ample experience in curriculum and textbook development. She is the author of more than twenty textbooks of general science based on National and International curricula. She is one of the author of federal board General science IV-V (2018) and Science VI AJK board. She has also worked with government bodies for the development of curriculum at various levels including the development of single national curriculum (SNC) of general science (IV-V). Her name is published at the official website of ministry of education Pakistan under curriculum experts. She is also a member of review committee of Curriculum of Chemistry (IX-XII), which is published on the official website of Punjab curriculum and textbook board. Currently, She is doing MA in Educational Planning and Management and providing her services as a senior officer curriculum in a renowned organization.
p Dr. Rooh Ul Amin Khattak holds his PhD in English (Applied Linguistics) from the University of Memphis, TN, USA. Dr. Amin has completed his MPhil in American Studies from Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad and his MA (English) from University of Peshawar. He has 16 years of university teaching experience at both the undergraduate and graduate level. He has an extensive research in the field of language, pedagogy, and education. He also has expertise in editing and reviewing for national and international journals.
