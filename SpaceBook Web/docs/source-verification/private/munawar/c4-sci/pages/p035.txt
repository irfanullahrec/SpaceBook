## 1. Eat Healthy || ۱۔ صحت مند غذا کھائیں || ۱. روغ خوراک وخورئ
p Food is our basic need. It nourishes our body and maintains our health. It gives us energy that is required for everything we do including walking, thinking, playing games and fighting the disease causing germs.
p The various food items that we eat make our diet. Our diet must consist of basic nutrients including proteins, carbohydrates, fats, minerals, vitamins and water. A healthy diet helps to protect against many diseases including non-contagious diseases (NCDs) such as diabetes, heart diseases and cancer. The diet which has correct portion of all nutrients is called a balanced diet.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p The food we consume is broken down into simpler products before it is absorbed and utilized by the body. These simpler substances are called nutrients.
endbox
## 1.1 Nutrients in a Balanced Diet || ۱.۱ متوازن غذا میں غذائی اجزاء || ۱.۱ په متوازن خوراک کې غذايي توکي
## i. Proteins || i۔ لحمیات (پروٹین) || i. پروټینونه
p Proteins are called building blocks of our body. Muscles are made up of proteins. We need to eat protein-containing food to keep our muscles strong and healthy. Meat, milk, eggs, pulses and nuts are some common sources of proteins.
## ii. Carbohydrates || ii۔ نشاستہ (کاربوہائیڈریٹ) || ii. کاربوهایډریټونه
p We eat a large number of carbohydrates to get sufficient energy. Bread, cereals, grains, sweet fruits and vegetables provide us carbohydrates. Excessive carbohydrates are converted into fat and stored in our body.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Fibre is also an important part of a healthy diet. It is found in vegetables, whole grains and fruit. We cannot digest fibre but it helps our body digest food.
endbox
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Ask the students what they have eaten today. The students will write down in self-sticky notes. Discuss what is good food and what is bad food. Draw a T chart on the board, categorizing between good and bad food. Let students will place their food samples they noted down on their sticky notes in correct category.
endbox
