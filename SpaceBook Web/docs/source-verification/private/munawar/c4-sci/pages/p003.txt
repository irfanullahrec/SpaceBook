## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo compare and contrast characteristics that distinguish major groups of living things (plants and animals).
slo classify animals in terms of vertebrates and invertebrates with examples and analyse the differences and similarities in vertebrates and invertebrates.
slo classify plants in terms of flowering and non-flowering with examples and analyse the differences and similarities in flowering and non-flowering plants.
slo recognize and appreciate diversity in life (the living plants and animals) and identify ways to protect diversity.
