# Matter and its Characteristics || مادہ اور اس کی خصوصیات || ماده او د هغې ځانګړتیاوې
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo describe matter and its states (solid, liquid, gas).
slo describe characteristics of each state of matter with examples.
slo compare and sort objects and materials on the basis of physical properties (e.g., mass, volume, state of matter, ability to conduct heat or electricity, ability to float or sink in water).
slo explain the properties of metals (i.e. appearance, texture, colour, density).
slo identify properties of metals (conducting heat and electricity) and relate those properties to use of metals (e.g., a copper electrical wire, an iron cooking pot).
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- What is matter?
- What is the air made up of?
- What are some solids you can see right now?
endbox
p Take a look at the given picture. What do you see? Perhaps you notice the water and ice. This is an example of matter. Matter is anything that takes up space and has mass. Matter does not have to be visible - even air is matter.
p Everything in the world is made of 'matter'. All matter is made from very small particles.
p Vocabulary: matter, physical, property, state, volume, density, sink, float, conductor, insulator
box note | Think and tell! || سوچیں اور بتائیں! || فکر وکړئ او ووایاست!
p Matter exists in all four states in this scene. Identify the solid, liquid and gas in the photograph given above.
endbox
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Assess the students' prior knowledge of states of matter by reading the title aloud, and inviting students to share what they know about the topic. Link their personal experiences and hands-on science explorations.
