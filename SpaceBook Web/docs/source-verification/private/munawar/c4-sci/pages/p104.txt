## 2. Using Thermometers || ۲۔ تھرمامیٹر کا استعمال || ۲. د ترمامیټر کارول
p Thermometer is an important part of a first aid box. There are different types of thermometers:
p Clinical Thermometer: This thermometer most familiar and is made up of glass. It is advised to avoid these thermometers to check oral temperature. Before using, one should always check the tip of the thermometer to make sure it is not broken or cracked. If it is broken or cracked, do not use the thermometer. A thermometer in right condition can be used by placing at the neck or in the armpit. Step 1: Clean the thermometer with cold water and soap, then rinse it. Step 2: Hold the thermometer at eye level with the numbers facing you. Adjust it to see the silver line. Step 3: The thermometer's temperature should always start below the first number on the line. If needed, shake the glass thermometer down.
p Digital Thermometer: An electronic heat sensors is used to record body temperature. A digital thermometer records temperature readings in decimal points, for accuracy and specific reading. They are faster to calculating readings. Step 1: Clean the tip with cold water and soap, then rinse it. Step 2: Turn the thermometer on. Step 3: Place the tip under your tongue, towards the back of your mouth. You can also use a thermometer in your armpit. Make sure you keep your arm tight against your body. Step 4: Close your lips around the thermometer and wait for the beep/flash. Step 5: Check the temperature on the screen.

## 3. Using Digital Blood Pressure Monitor || ۳۔ ڈیجیٹل بلڈ پریشر مانیٹر کا استعمال || ۳. ډیجیټل د وینې فشار مانیټر کارول
p Every time your heart beats, it pumps blood through your circulatory system, putting pressure on your blood vessels. This is known as blood pressure. High blood pressure, also known as hypertension, occurs when the force against your blood vessels becomes too high, which can lead to serious health problems like heart attack and stroke. Thus, regular blood pressure monitoring is necessary to ensure that blood pressure is normal and always well-controlled.
p Picture: the cuff should be placed on you arm at the same level as your heart; sit upright with your back straight; relax your hand; feet flat on the floor. Maintain correct sitting posture before monitoring your blood pressure.
