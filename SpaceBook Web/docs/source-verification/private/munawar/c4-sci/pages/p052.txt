## 3. Importance of Saving Energy || ۳۔ توانائی بچانے کی اہمیت || ۳. د انرژۍ د سپمولو ارزښت
p Most of the energy we use comes from fossil fuels: oil, coal, and natural gas. Fossil fuels are made up of plants and animals that lived millions of years ago. They take millions of years to form and they can only be used once. Someday, people may not be able to find enough oil, coal, and natural gas to create energy. Without energy we can no longer power our lighting and heating devices that require plugging into the electricity mains.

## 4. How to Save Energy || ۴۔ توانائی کیسے بچائیں || ۴. انرژي څنګه وساتو
p Here are a few tips you can do to start saving energy:
- The lights and television use electrical energy, so when you leave the room, shut them off.
- During the day, when it is brighter outside, open the curtains and use sunlight instead of turning on the lights.
- When you have a sunny day, help hang the clothes outside instead of using the dryer.
- Avoid leaving the water dripping in the tap.
- Avoid leaving the refrigerator door open. Decide what you want before you open the door.
- Plant trees and shrubs (trees that lose their leaves are best to block the sun in the summer and, when the leaves fall, they let the sun shine through in the winter helping to warm your home).
