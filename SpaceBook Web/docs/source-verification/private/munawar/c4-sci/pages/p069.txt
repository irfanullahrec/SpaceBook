## 2.2 Changing Motion || ۲.۲ حرکت بدلنا || ۲.۲ حرکت بدلول
p Force can change the motion of an object in different ways.
## Force changes the direction || قوت سمت بدلتی ہے || ځواک لوری بدلوي
p Use of force can change the direction of a moving object. Example: In tennis, player hits the ball and changes its direction.
## Force stops motion || قوت حرکت روکتی ہے || ځواک حرکت درولي
p Force also has the ability to stop a moving object. Example: The boy has stopped the moving ball by applying force.
## 2.3 Changing Shape || ۲.۳ شکل بدلنا || ۲.۳ بڼه بدلول
p By applying force, we can change shape of objects. For example; the size and shape of spring can be changed by using force. Similarly, when we apply force on an inflated balloon, its shape and size can be changed. However, spring and balloon come at their original shape when we leave applying force on them.
p What would happen if we apply force to change the shape of a tin can? Can it come to its original shape after removing force? No, tin can cannot come to its original shape at all.
box note | Activity: 6.2 || سرگرمی: 6.2 || فعالیت: 6.2
p Collect kitchen sponges, rubber bands, springs, an elastic band, etc. from home. Apply and remove force on all items one by one. Observe how these things regain their shape and size after release of force.
endbox
## 2.4 Types of Force || ۲.۴ قوت کی اقسام || ۲.۴ د ځواک ډولونه
p There are many kinds of natural forces which exist almost everywhere on the Earth. Friction and gravity are two common forces. Gravity works on all resting and moving objects on the Earth while friction only works on the moving objects.
## Gravity || کششِ ثقل || ثقل
p Rise a ball up in the air and then just leave it. What will happen? The ball will automatically move downwards until it reaches at the ground. It happens due to gravity. Gravity of the Earth pull objects towards its center. (Pictures: we stand on Earth due to its force of gravity; gravity pulls water downwards; the apple is falling to the ground due to gravity.)
