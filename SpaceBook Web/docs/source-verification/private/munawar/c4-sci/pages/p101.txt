p Making a paper bag: Step 1: Lay your paper on a flat clean surface. Step 2: Fold the both unevenly and paste the two sides together with glue. Step 3: Fold the bottom of the paper and crease it well. Step 4: Open the top layer of the base towards you and press the corners into a triangle shape as shown in the photo. Step 5: Fold up the bottom edge of the base to meet the fold above it. Step 6: Then, fold the top edge of the bag down in the same fold and glue the edges together. Optional: Using a punching machine, make two holes on both sides of the bag to attach the handle (any string/thin rope/ribbon handle of old bag).

p Many times, we have calendars and gift wrapping that are too pretty to throw away. What can we do? How about making envelopes?
p Materials Required: Any paper including old cardstock, instruction paper/newspaper/gift wrapping and a glue stick.
