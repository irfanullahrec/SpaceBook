box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- Motion is a change in position.
- A push or a pull is called a Force.
- A force can cause a change in the position, motion and even shape of an object.
- Gravity of the Earth pull objects towards its center.
- Friction is a force that slows down moving things. It is produced when two things rub against each other.
- There is more friction on rough surfaces than on smooth ones.
- Friction is helpful. If an object start moving, it would never stop if there were no friction.
- We need friction to walk, move vehicles on road and for nails to be fixed in the wood or wall.
- Friction produces heat and wears out the materials.
- Due to friction tyres may burst, machines use more fuel, and shoes become old and rough.
- Simple machines make doing work easier by changing the direction of force or reducing the amount of force by increasing the distance.
- Lever is a long bar or a plank that can rotate around a fixed point called fulcrum.
- A Pulley consists of a wire, rope, or cable moving on a grooved wheel. Pulleys help to move a load up and down using less force.
- Gear consists of a pair of wheels that have teeth which are interlinked with each other. Inclined planes or ramps make moving a load to higher places easier.
endbox
