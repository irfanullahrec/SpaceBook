## 1. Animals || ۱۔ جانور || ۱. څاروي
p Almost all animals move freely and depend on other living organisms for their food. However, animals are different from each other.
box note | Think and Tell! || سوچیں اور بتائیں! || فکر وکړئ او ووایاست!
p Are all animals in the picture same? How are they different from each other? (A picture of many animals.)
endbox
p We can group all animals into two major classes on the basis of presence or absence of backbone.
## 1.1 Invertebrate || ۱.۱ غیر فقاریہ || ۱.۱ بې شاته ملا
p Animals without backbone are called invertebrates. They are usually small and slow-moving animals. They either have a soft body, like worms and jellyfish, or a hard outer covering called the exoskeleton, like spiders and crabs. (Pictures: worm, crab, spider, jellyfish.)
## 1.2 Vertebrate || ۱.۲ فقاریہ || ۱.۲ شاته ملا لرونکي
p Run your hand along the central portion of your back. It is your backbone. Animals which have backbone are called vertebrates. They have internal skeleton and bones.
