box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- The Earth's surface consists of three-quarter water and one-quarter land. Air is present around Earth like a blanket.
- Most of the water bodies are connected with each other and form a cycle in which water flows.
- Natural resources are the materials people take from the Earth. A fossil is what is left of a living thing from the past.
- Earth's natural resources that are used in everyday life are water, wind, rocks, soil, plants, minerals, oil and gas.
- Earth's natural resources can be grouped by the processes and the time needed to produce them.
- The resources that take so long to replace are considered as nonrenewable resources. Fossil fuels are nonrenewable because they take millions of years to form. Minerals such as copper and gold are also nonrenewable resources.
- Resources that can be replaced by the nature are called renewable resources. Water, trees, wind and solar energy.
- People use many different types of natural resources daily. They are misusing nonrenewable resources to meet their growing needs.
- There are many ways that each individual can conserve natural resources by turning off lights, recycling and reusing.

qp ii | How do we use Earth's resources?

qp iii | Differentiate between renewable and nonrenewable resources.
sol Renewable resources can be replaced by nature (trees, water, solar and wind energy); nonrenewable resources take so long to replace that they are limited or used up faster than replaced (fossil fuels, copper, gold). ## From p. 81. || Renewable resources can be replaced by nature (trees, water, solar and wind energy); nonrenewable resources take so long to replace that they are limited or used up faster than replaced (fossil fuels, copper, gold). || Renewable resources can be replaced by nature (trees, water, solar and wind energy); nonrenewable resources take so long to replace that they are limited or used up faster than replaced (fossil fuels, copper, gold).
