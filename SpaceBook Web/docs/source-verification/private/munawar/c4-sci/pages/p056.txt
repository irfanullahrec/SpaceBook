## Unit 5 B: Forms of Energy and Energy Transfer || یونٹ ۵ ب: توانائی کی اقسام اور توانائی کی منتقلی || پنځم څپرکی ب: د انرژۍ بڼې او د انرژۍ لېږد
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo relate familiar physical phenomena (i.e., shadows, reflections, and rainbows) to the behaviour of light.
slo relate familiar physical phenomena (i.e., vibrating objects, echoes) to the production and behaviour of sound.
slo recognize that warmer objects have a higher temperature than cooler objects.
slo investigate the changes that occur when a hot object is brought in contact with a cold object.
slo identify ways to measure temperature and understand its unit.
slo describe and demonstrate that electrical energy in a circuit can be transformed into other forms of energy (e.g., heat, light, sound).
slo explain and provide reasoning that a simple electric circuit requires a complete electrical pathway.

## Forms of Energy in Everyday Life || روزمرہ زندگی میں توانائی کی اقسام || په ورځني ژوند کې د انرژۍ بڼې
p We have learned that energy is part of everything that happens in the world and everything we do. Energy exists in the form of light, sound, heat or electricity.
p Vocabulary: shadow, reflection, refraction, dispersion, vibration, echo, thermal, temperature, chemical, circuit, switch, source.
box note | Think and tell! || سوچیں اور بتائیں! || فکر وکړئ او ووایاست!
p Each picture below shows a form of energy. Write the form of energy being used in each picture given here. (Pictures: a cooking fire, a bulb, a loudspeaker, a plug.)

## 1. Light Energy || ۱۔ روشنی کی توانائی || ۱. د رڼا انرژي
p Light is the visible form of energy. We see things around us because of light. Sun is the biggest source of light energy for us. Other natural sources include stars and lightening. While man-made sources of light energy include electric bulbs, tubelights, flashlights, lamps, etc.
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Talk to the students about how we need energy to move around and do work. Ask them if they can name some energy forms around us? Discuss with them how light, heat, sound and electrical energies are important in our daily lives.
endbox
