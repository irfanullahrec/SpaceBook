## Types of Ecosystems || ماحولیاتی نظام کی اقسام || د ایکوسیستمونو ډولونه
p All organisms have their own ecosystems such as lion lives in grassland ecosystem, evergreen trees grow in forest ecosystem and frogs live in freshwater ecosystem.
## 1.1 Freshwater Ecosystems || ۱.۱ میٹھے پانی کا ماحولیاتی نظام || ۱.۱ د خوږو اوبو ایکوسیستم
p Freshwater ecosystems include streams, rivers, springs, ponds and lakes. They are sub-divided into two classes: i. Those in which the water is nearly stationary, such as ponds. ii. Those in which the water flows, such as rivers.
## Life in a Pond || تالاب میں زندگی || په حوض کې ژوند
p Think of a pond ecosystem with only its abiotic factors. You might think of water and soil. Now add the biotic factors in and around the pond. Both of these factors together make up the ecosystem. In a balanced ecosystem, the biotic and abiotic factors work together. For example, plants in a pond need a lot of water. They also need a certain kind of climate to grow well. Pond animals need a special climate. Climate describes typical weather patterns of a specific area over a long time.
## 1.2 Forest Ecosystem || ۱.۲ جنگل کا ماحولیاتی نظام || ۱.۲ د ځنګل ایکوسیستم
p Forest ecosystems are classified according to their climate. Rainforest ecosystem has higher rainfall and mild sunlight. Therefore, it provides home to a huge variety of animals and plants. The trees grow tall and plants are lush green in rainforest ecosystems.
p Abiotic and Biotic Factors. Climate: hot and wet all year round. Soil: nutrient-poor. Plants: vines, orchids, ferns and a wide variety of trees. Animals: monkeys, jaguars, huge variety of birds, snakes, lizards and insects.
## 1.3 Grassland Ecosystem || ۱.۳ گھاس کے میدان کا ماحولیاتی نظام || ۱.۳ د رغ ایکوسیستم
p Grassland ecosystem is a large ecosystem like a forest. It has lesser rainfall than forest ecosystem. Trees are almost nonexistent, but flowers can bloom on grasses. Grasslands provide an ideal environment for grazing animals such as elephants, giraffe, zebra, etc.
