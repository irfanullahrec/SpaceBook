p Procedure: 1. Cut the bottom of the hanger in the middle (adult help needed) and insert the spool into the open ends of the wire. Then tape the ends tightly to join the hanger's broken ends. After cutting, ends of the wire in the hanger can be sharp, so be careful when working with them, as well as with the wire cutter. Children should be closely supervised for this activity, and an adult may want to help with this step. 2. Hang the hanger on a fixed place or ask your partner to hold it in his/her hand. 3. Attach the weight to the string/thread and loop it over the spool. 4. Pull the string/thread to lift the weight. Inquiry Question: How does pulley make the weight lifting easier? Predict: What would happen if you add another hanger with spool to loop and lift the same load?
## Gears || گیئر || ګیرونه
p Gear is the most commonly used simple machine. It is used in almost every complex machine. It consists of a pair of wheels that have teeth. These toothed wheels are interlinked with each other. Gears help us in many ways.
p Gears are used to change the direction of motion such as in an eggbeater. Gears can be used to increase or decrease force or speed.

p Bicycle, clocks, toys, etc. have gears to perform their functions. Machines typically use two or more gears of different sizes; one gear is often larger than the other.
## Inclined Planes || ڈھلوانی سطح || ځکه سطحه
p Inclined planes or ramps make it easier to move a load at higher places. An inclined plane makes it lifting heavy objects possible using a smaller force but we have to move object over a much longer distance.
p We use many forms of inclined planes daily, for example staircases, wheelchair ramps, slanted roofs, slanted roads, slides in the playground, etc.
