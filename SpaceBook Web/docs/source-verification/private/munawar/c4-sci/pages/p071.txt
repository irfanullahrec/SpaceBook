## Where is friction not good? || رگڑ کہاں اچھی نہیں ہے؟ || رگړ چېرته ښه نه دی؟
p Although friction is necessary to make things move with control. But it wears out the materials whether they are screws, ball bearings or sole of the shoes.
p On the other hand, friction also produces heat. When large machines run, friction produces huge amount of heat and causes energy wastage. Therefore, in many cases, we try to have less friction to make things easier to move. (Pictures: tyres burst due to friction; machines use more fuel to overcome friction; our shoes become old and rough due to friction.)
box note | Think and tell! || سوچیں اور بتائیں! || فکر وکړئ او ووایاست!
p Have you ever slipped on a wet floor? How is it related to friction?
endbox
## 3. Simple Machine || ۳۔ سادہ مشین || ۳. ساده ماشین
p We need less effort to make something move when we use a machine. All simple machines make doing work easier by: changing the direction of force, or reducing the amount of force by increasing the distance.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p All complex machines are manufactured by the combination of one or more simple machines. Examples include sewing machine, fan, etc.
endbox
## Lever || لیور || لیور
p Lever is a long bar or a plank that can rotate around a fixed point called fulcrum. It helps us to lift and move very large and heavy load easily by applying lesser force.
