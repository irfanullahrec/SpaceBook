box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- The Sun is at the center of the solar system. The word Solar means 'of the Sun'. The Solar System is a system of the Sun and eight planets, moons and other bodies travelling around it.
- The Sun is a star that produces its own energy, including heat and light.
- The moon is an object that circles around a planet. The phase of the moon is the shape of lighted part of the moon that we see at any given time.
- The Earth revolves around the Sun and rotates around its own axis.
- The movement of the earth around its own axis is called rotation resulting in formation of day and night.
- The changing appearance of shadows on the Earth is the evidence that the Earth is rotating continuously around its own axis.
- Movement of the Earth around the sun in its fixed orbit is called revolution. Earth takes 365 days (a year) to make a full trip around the Sun. Earth is slightly tilted on its axis. Due to the Earth's revolution and its tilt, change in seasons happen.
- When the Earth casts a shadow on the Moon, the moon appears to be dark for a time until it gets out of the Earth's shadow; this is called lunar eclipse. When Moon comes between the Sun and the Earth, it blocks the path of a few sunrays to reach the Earth; this process is called solar eclipse.
