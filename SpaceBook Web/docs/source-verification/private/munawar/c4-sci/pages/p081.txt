## 6. Human Impact on Natural Resources || ۶۔ قدرتی وسائل پر انسانی اثرات || ۶. پر طبیعي سرچینو د انسان اغېزه
p People use many different types of natural resources daily. They are misusing nonrenewable resources to meet their growing needs.
- Oil is used to run vehicles every day.
- Natural gas is used in homes and many factories to produce heat.
- Most of the world's electricity comes from burning of fossil fuels.
p Such widespread use of fossil fuels is reducing these limited resources rapidly. Burning fossil fuels also impacts other natural resources such as air, water, and soil by causing pollution.
p Even many human activities are also impacting renewable resources badly. Urbanization, deforestation and destroying ecosystems are burning soil, forests and water bodies.
box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Investigating Human impact of Natural Resources. Work in a group of five (might be class fellows, friends, siblings, family members, etc.). List 10 things that you see people do at home, at school, or in the community that you think is a waste of natural resources. Each group member should list at least two items. (A table of ten rows.) Predict/Infer: The scientists believe that we will get oil (fossil fuels) at the present rate for 30 to 40 more years. What do you think the impact of oil resources will be? Suggest an alternative of oil consumption.
endbox
p Whether a resource is renewable or nonrenewable, all natural resources must be regarded as Earth's treasures. To protect these resources, people must make wise choices.
## 7. Conservation of Natural Resources || ۷۔ قدرتی وسائل کا تحفظ || ۷. د طبیعي سرچینو ساتنه
p There are many ways in which each individual can conserve natural resources.
- Turning off extra lights and choosing to walk or ride a bike instead of riding a car saves energy.
- Recycling - the creation of new products by the use of waste or trash materials, is another way to decrease the demand on Earth's natural resources.
- Reusing items, instead of throwing them away, also helps to conserve resources.

box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Introduce the term 'sustainability'. Break the word into fragments (non-re-newable), translate each fragment (not again-new-able) and then arrange them to make their concept clear. Similarly, explain the word 'renewable' and let the students understand and memorise the terms.
