## 1.3 Bending of Light || ۱.۳ روشنی کا مڑنا || ۱.۳ د رڼا کږېدل
p Imagine trying to walk through air, water, and quicksand. How would your movement be differ? Yes! Your speed will vary in each medium.
p Similarly light waves pass through different objects at different speeds. For example; when light travels through water, it moves much slower than it does in air. This change in medium and speed cause the light waves to change their direction as well. This is called refraction of light.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p The object from which a certain thing is passed is called a medium.
endbox

## 1.4 Dispersion and Rainbows || ۱.۴ روشنی کا بکھرنا اور قوسِ قزح || ۱.۴ د رڼا خورول او پرانګ
p White light is not as simple as it looks. It is actually made up of all the colours in the rainbow. When this light travels through a different medium, it splits up and its colours become visible. (A picture of white light split by a prism into its colours.)
p When it rains, light has to travel through rain drops before it reaches our eyes. First, the light is refracted as it enters from air to the water droplet. Second, it reflects from the inner surface of the water droplet and comes back into the environment as a rainbow in the sky.
box note | Activity: 5B.3 || سرگرمی: 5B.3 || فعالیت: 5B.3
p Take a prism from the school lab and shine a small beam of light (torchlight) through it. If the light is angled correctly, you will get a rainbow when light passes through the prism.
endbox
