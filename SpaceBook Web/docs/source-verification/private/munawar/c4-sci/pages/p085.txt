## 1.1 Temperature || ۱.۱ درجہ حرارت || ۱.۱ تودوخه
p The temperature is high when it is hot. The temperature is low when it is cold. We measure temperature by means of a thermometer.
## 1.2 Humidity || ۱.۲ نمی || ۱.۲ رطوبت
p It is the amount of water vapours in the air. A hygrometer is used to measure the humidity in the air.
## 1.3 Precipitation || ۱.۳ بارش || ۱.۳ ورښت
p The water that falls from cloud in the form of rain, snow or hail is called precipitation.
## 1.4 Wind || ۱.۴ ہوا || ۱.۴ باد
p Wind is moving air. Breeze are light and gentle winds and gales are strong winds. Wind storms are extremely strong and destructive winds. We measure the speed of wind with an anemometer, while a weather vane is used to know about its direction.
