- 2. Sound: Sound is a type of energy that we hear. Sound is produced when something vibrates. Echo is just a reflection of sound. Hard and smooth surfaces are good reflectors of sound.
- 3. Heat: Heat is the energy that flows from a warm material to a cool material due to differences in temperature. Warmer objects have a higher temperature than cooler objects. Heat energy always moves from the hotter material to the colder material. Temperature is the measure of hotness or coldness of a body. There are many different types of thermometers but the most common type which are often used are laboratory thermometers and clinical thermometer. The common units for measuring temperature are Celsius (centigrade) and Fahrenheit.
- 4. Electrical energy: The energy of moving electric charges within a circuit is called electrical energy. Electrical current can only travel in a circuit. A simple circuit contains a source of energy (battery) and at least one conductor (wires) that includes a switch. Many household appliances act like resistors when they are in a circuit. A resistor can change electrical energy to other forms of energy.

qp i | How is a rainbow formed in the sky after rainfall?
