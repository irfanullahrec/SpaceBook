box note | Going Beyond! || اس سے آگے! || له دې هاخوا!
p In many mobiles, weather is also displayed along with time at the Home screen. If it is not showing, ask an elder to turn it on from the Settings. Observe the weather for ten days. Record your observations and the weather forecast given in the mobile's home screen in your notebook (make sure that internet is on while checking the weather from home screen). How accurate is the weather forecast given by the mobile?
endbox
## Using Camera || کیمرے کا استعمال || کامره کارول
p All smartphones have Cameras for taking photos and shooting videos. There are multiple ways to open the Camera app. A common way is; From Apps, tap Camera to access it.
box note | Activity: 10.4 || سرگرمی: 10.4 || فعالیت: 10.4
p 1. Study the changes in shadows with Earth Rotation. 2. Fix a pole in the center of a lawn (garden) and take pictures of the pole's shadow during the day sunlight reaches. 3. Take snapshots of pole and shadow after every hour. 4. Compare all the pictures by making a collage in the mobile phone.
endbox
box note | Activity: 10.5 || سرگرمی: 10.5 || فعالیت: 10.5
p Try to focus the moon in your camera. Is its shape visible or you see only a scattering light source? Scattering Light Source: write a note on how our eyes and the eye of a mobile phone (camera) are similar and how are they different. Add a few lines of gratitude due to the perfection of our eyes created by Allah Almighty. Moon and its shape: observe the phases of the moon for a month. Take a snapshot after one week (4 snapshots in a month). Compare all pictures by making a collage in your mobile phone.
endbox
