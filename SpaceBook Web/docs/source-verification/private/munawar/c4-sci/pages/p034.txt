## Ways of Maintaining Good Health || اچھی صحت برقرار رکھنے کے طریقے || د ښې روغتیا د ساتلو لارې
p Good health is a great blessing. A healthy body enables a person to live an active and joyful life. Many people do not look after their health in their youth and get many health issues in the older age. Therefore, if you want to be healthy when you will be old, adopt a healthy lifestyle from your childhood.
p Many things such as a balanced diet, drinking enough water, exercising regularly, brushing teeth, getting enough sleep are parts of healthy lifestyle.
p Vocabulary: health, lifestyle, diet, balanced, nutrients, plants, carbohydrates, protein, minerals, vitamins, fibre, filtration, distillation, physical.
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Write the word 'healthy habits' in the middle of the board. Ask the students to tell at least one healthy habit. Make a concept map from the responses. Add a few missing habits if needed. Start a class discussion of healthy lifestyle.
endbox
