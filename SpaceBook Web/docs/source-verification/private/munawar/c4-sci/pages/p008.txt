qp i | How can you differentiate between a rose plant and cat?
sol A rose plant cannot move from one place to another and prepares its own food by chlorophyll, while a cat can move freely and depends on other animals for its food. ## From p. 5. || A rose plant cannot move from one place to another and prepares its own food by chlorophyll, while a cat can move freely and depends on other animals for its food. || A rose plant cannot move from one place to another and prepares its own food by chlorophyll, while a cat can move freely and depends on other animals for its food.
qp ii | What are vertebrates? Give examples.
sol Animals which have backbone, an internal skeleton and bones are called vertebrates; for example a monkey, a fish and a bird. ## From p. 5 and 6. || Animals which have backbone, an internal skeleton and bones are called vertebrates; for example a monkey, a fish and a bird. || Animals which have backbone, an internal skeleton and bones are called vertebrates; for example a monkey, a fish and a bird.
qp iii | Define exoskeleton. Name two animals having exoskeleton.
sol An exoskeleton is the hard outer covering of some invertebrates; spiders and crabs have it. ## From p. 5. || An exoskeleton is the hard outer covering of some invertebrates; spiders and crabs have it. || An exoskeleton is the hard outer covering of some invertebrates; spiders and crabs have it.
qp iv | Some plants do not produce seeds. How do these plants reproduce (make new plants)?
sol They reproduce with dust-like particles called spores, for example mosses and ferns. ## From p. 6. || They reproduce with dust-like particles called spores, for example mosses and ferns. || They reproduce with dust-like particles called spores, for example mosses and ferns.
qp v | What is meant by the diversity in life?
sol The variety of organisms is called diversity in life or biodiversity. ## From p. 7. || The variety of organisms is called diversity in life or biodiversity. || The variety of organisms is called diversity in life or biodiversity.

qp i | Describe the characteristics that distinguish animals from plants.
sol Plants cannot move from one place to another because they are rooted into the ground, and they have chlorophyll so they prepare their own food. Animals can freely move from one place to another and depend on plants and other animals for their food, either directly or indirectly. ## From the table on p. 5. || Plants cannot move from one place to another because they are rooted into the ground, and they have chlorophyll so they prepare their own food. Animals can freely move from one place to another and depend on plants and other animals for their food, either directly or indirectly. || Plants cannot move from one place to another because they are rooted into the ground, and they have chlorophyll so they prepare their own food. Animals can freely move from one place to another and depend on plants and other animals for their food, either directly or indirectly.
qp ii | Describe the characteristics of flowering and non-flowering plants.
sol Flowering plants (angiosperms) produce flowers during their life cycle and make seeds in fruits, for example rose, sunflower, mango, banana and papaya. Non-flowering plants do not carry flowers; some reproduce with spores (mosses and ferns) and some use seeds in woody cones (gymnosperms such as cedar, spruce and firs). ## From p. 6 and 7. || Flowering plants (angiosperms) produce flowers during their life cycle and make seeds in fruits, for example rose, sunflower, mango, banana and papaya. Non-flowering plants do not carry flowers; some reproduce with spores (mosses and ferns) and some use seeds in woody cones (gymnosperms such as cedar, spruce and firs).
qp iii | Write down three ways to protect diversity in life.
sol Use your own reusable cloth or paper bag instead of plastic, reduce, reuse and recycle, and save water; also join a project to plant trees or clean local rivers. ## From p. 8. || Use your own reusable cloth or paper bag instead of plastic, reduce, reuse and recycle, and save water; also join a project to plant trees or clean local rivers. || Use your own reusable cloth or paper bag instead of plastic, reduce, reuse and recycle, and save water; also join a project to plant trees or clean local rivers.
