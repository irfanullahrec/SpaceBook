## 2. Common Types of Diseases || ۲۔ امراض کی عام اقسام || ۲. د ناروغیو عام ډولونه
p Any disease remains dangerous until we know its causes and treatment. Causes of a disease help us to take preventive measures. However, if we get the disease we should consult a doctor to get proper medical treatment. There are two major types of diseases, i.e. non-contagious and contagious.
## 2.1 Non-Contagious Diseases (NCD's) || ۲.۱ غیر متعدی امراض || ۲.۱ نه خپریدونکې ناروغۍ
p The type of diseases that you cannot catch from another person is NCD's. Examples include cancer, diabetes and heart diseases. Some diseases such as malaria spread through an insect (mosquito). The most common causes of non-contagious diseases are unhealthy life styles. (Major Types of NCD's: heart disease, diabetes, lung diseases, cancer.)
## 2.2 Contagious Diseases (CD's) || ۲.۲ متعدی امراض || ۲.۲ خپریدونکې ناروغۍ
p The diseases that spread from an ill/ infected person organism or the environment to another organism. They are also known as infectious diseases. This type of diseases spread rapidly through many ways and infect many people. Examples include T.B, hepatitis, influenza, Covid-19 and polio. (Pictures: influenza, Covid-19, polio.)
