## iii. Distillation || iii۔ کشیدگی (ڈسٹلیشن) || iii. تقطیر
p Distillation is a method that uses heat to collect pure water in the form of vapour. In this method, water is brought to boil. It is then left at the boiling point until it turns into gas/vapour. Upon cooling, vapour is converted back into liquid water that is clean and safe for drinking. This method is effective in removing germs, salts and other chemicals such as lead, mercury and arsenic.
## iv. Chlorine || iv۔ کلورین || iv. کلورین
p Chlorine is a powerful chemical that has been in use for many years to treat water for home usage. It is an effective water purification method that kills germs and other disease-causing organisms found in ground or tap water. Water can be purified using chlorine tablets or liquid chlorine. ## 3. Move Healthy || ۳۔ صحت مند حرکت کریں || ۳. روغ حرکت وکړئ
p Regular physical activity is important for the healthy growth, development and well-being. Kids and teenagers should do physical activities such as climbing, jumping, running or playing tug-of-war at least three days of the week. There are many benefits of being physically active. Some of them are:
- strong muscles and bones
- stronger heart
- more energy throughout the day
- help your body break down food easier and get rid of waste.
