## 1.3 Heart || ۱.۳ دل || ۱.۳ زړه
p The main function of a heart is to pump the blood to all parts of body. (Pictures: the heart of a cow, a chicken and a human compared with a melon, a lemon and a bell pepper.)
## 1.4 Lungs || ۱.۴ پھیپھڑے || ۱.۴ سږي
p Most of the animals have a pair of lungs. When an animal breathes in air through nose and mouth, it goes to the lungs. The lungs exchange oxygen from air into the blood and carbon dioxide from the blood in to the air. When the animal exhales, it gives out carbon dioxide. (Pictures: lungs of a salamander, a chimpanzee and a pigeon.)
## 1.5 Stomach || ۱.۵ معدہ || ۱.۵ معده
p All animals have a sac-like structure which breaks down the food and helps in digestion. It is called stomach. Animals have stomach according to their body sizes and diets. (Pictures: the stomach of a herbivore, a carnivore and an omnivore.)
