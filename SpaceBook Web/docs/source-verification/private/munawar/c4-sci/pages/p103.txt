## 10.2 Elementary First Aid || ۱۰.۲ ابتدائی طبی امداد || ۱۰.۲ بنسټیزه لومړنۍ مرسته
p Accidents or emergencies can happen anywhere, at any time. First aid is the early care or treatment given to a person who is injured or suddenly becomes sick. It is usually performed by a non-expert person to a sick or injured person until appropriate medical treatment can be accessed in a hospital or by going to a doctor.
p The basic objectives of the First Aid are: to give immediate care; to protect the victim from further harm; to relieve pain; to promote recovery.

## 1. First Aid || ۱۔ ابتدائی طبی امداد || ۱. لومړنۍ مرسته
p The first aid kit is a portable container of medicines, supplies and information. You can buy a first aid kit or make one out of a lunch box or shoe box. A standard first aid kit should include:
p Pictures: a first aid manual (it contains many instructions to provide first aid under different situations); a thermometer (used to check and calculate temperature); a BP apparatus (used to calculate blood pressure of a patient).

th Information || معلومات || معلومات | Supplies | Medicines
tr Emergency phone numbers: rescue, ambulance, fire/police, poison control, doctor, dentist; first aid book/manual | Gloves, adhesive bandages, triangular bandages, cotton swabs and balls, gauze, safety pins, adhesive tape/cloth tape, antiseptic wipes, instant cold pack, scissors, tweezers, thermometer | Aspirin (do not give aspirin to children under 12), ibuprofen, paracetamol, anti-diarrhea medicine, iodine, antibiotic ointment
endtable
p Variety of bandages, gauze roll, pads, adhesive tape and safety pins are used to manage different wounds and injuries. The pair of scissors are used to cut bandages and gauzes. An emergency blanket is used to provide warmth to the patient. Instant cold packs are used to provide cold treatment. Gloves must be used by the first aid provider before giving any treatment.
