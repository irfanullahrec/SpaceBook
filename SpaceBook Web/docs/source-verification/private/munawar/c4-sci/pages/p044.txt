## 1. Three States of Matter || ۱۔ مادے کی تین حالتیں || ۱. د مادې درې حالتونه
p The three states of matter are solid, liquid and gas. Let us investigate the properties of all three states of matter.
## 1.1 Solid || ۱.۱ ٹھوس || ۱.۱ جامد
p What makes a substance a solid? The solid is matter with a definite shape and volume. For example; when you pick up a rock from the ground and place it in a bucket, it does not change shape or size. A solid does not take the shape of a container in which it is placed. This is because the particles of a solid are packed closely together.
p Example: Few other examples of solids are table, computer and bricks, etc.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p If you pour 50 mL of juice from a bottle into a pitcher, the pitcher will contain some 50 mL of juice. If you then pour that same juice into a glass, its shape will change but volume will remain the same.
endbox
## 1.2 Liquid || ۱.۲ مائع || ۱.۲ مایع
p The liquid is matter that has a definite volume but no definite shape. For an example, if we pour a liquid from one container to another, the liquid takes the shape of the container. The volume of a liquid, however, is the same no matter what the shape of the container.
p Examples: Common examples are water, milk, honey, juices and drinks.
box note | Think & tell! || سوچیں اور بتائیں! || فکر وکړئ او ووایاست!
p We can pour a salt from one container to other. In which state of matter is a powder?
endbox
## 1.3 Gas || ۱.۳ گیس || ۱.۳ ګاز
p Gas is matter that does not have definite shape or volume. The particles are much further apart than in a liquid or solid. The particles of gas has the ability to spread out in every direction. For example; in case of leakage of a gas pipe, the gas spread all around and we can detect it by its smell. However, many gases do not have any smell.
