# Forces and Motion || قوتیں اور حرکت || ځواکونه او حرکت
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo describe force and motion with examples from daily life.
slo identify gravity as a force that draws objects to Earth.
slo investigate that friction force works against the direction of motion.
slo provide reasoning with evidence that friction can be either detrimental or useful under different circumstances.
slo recognize that simple machines (e.g., levers, pulleys, gears, ramps) help make motion easier (e.g., make lifting things easier, reduce the amount of force required, change the distance, or change the direction of the force).
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- How can you make something move?
- Why do things fall on ground?
- How can we make our daily work easier?
endbox
p All around us, things move. People walk up and down the street, vehicles move and leaves fall of a tree. When something moves, it starts from one position. Then, it ends at another position.
## 1. Motion || ۱۔ حرکت || ۱. حرکت
p Motion is a change in position. Things move in different ways. These are called types of motion. Common types of motion are:
p Types of Motion: a train moves in a straight line (straight line motion, linear); a seesaw moves up and down (up and down motion, oscillation); a merry-go-round moves round and round (circular motion, rotation); a swing moves back and forth (back and forth motion, swing); a snake moves in a zigzag (zigzag motion, snake).
p Vocabulary: position, motion, force, gravity, friction, simple machine, push, pull, lever, wheel, pulley, slope.
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Introduce the concept of position to the students. Tell them about different words describing the position of an object such as on, under, in, near, etc. Relate it with the concept of changing position, i.e. motion. Show them different types of motion using simple things such as books, clock in a few volunteers.
endbox
