## Preface || پیش لفظ || سریزه
p The main objectives and aims of writing this series of books on science and technology for classes from Pre-primary to Elementary levels is to ensure that students remain equipped with latest knowledge of science and technology and its conceptual understanding which helps them to answer scientific questions about the world around them. Each book has been compiled and developed in the light of Guiding Rules, Benchmarks and Students Learning Outcomes as envisaged in SNC-2020. It aims at making the pupils to comprehend the key concepts in easy language. This series also enables them to become familiar with, and use, technical terminology accurately and precisely. Each book makes the students to seek answers to questions through collecting, analyzing and presenting the knowledge gained through these books for working scientifically.
p Our books developed for beginners enable the students to learn through asking simple questions, make close observations of objects and performing simple tests. It makes them explore the world around them. At further stages, the scientific approach among the students is enhanced through knowledge, observations and experiments on the basis of which it becomes possible to understand the laws of science and technology.
p The activities and exercises are based on real life observations which help in improving their learning of scientific skills and applying their knowledge practically. The books have been designed with lively illustrations, attracting colours, cheerful pictures and perfect layout. All these factors make the books appealing and attractive for students to make the teaching/learning process of science a healthy and lively activity.
