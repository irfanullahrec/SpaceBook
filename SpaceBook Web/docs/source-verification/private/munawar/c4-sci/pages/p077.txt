## Water on Earth || زمین پر پانی || په ځمکه کې اوبه
p Major blue areas of the Earth as shown on world map or globe shows the water present in the oceans. Only a small amount of water is present in the rivers, lakes and streams. Most of the water bodies are connected with each other and form a cycle in which water flows.
p The water starts flowing as a stream of water high in the mountains. Some streams of water end up into lakes. A lake is a small to medium-sized body of water surrounded by land. Other streams join together and become a river that flows into an ocean. Let us have a closer look at these water bodies. (Drawing: stream - water that flows in the above along a fairly defined path, called a channel; lake - body of water surrounded by land; river - rivers are found on the margins of the ocean; ocean.)

p Write about what would happen to the ocean if the ice sheet on the mountains melt rapidly. Discuss the possible consequences of this sudden change.
endbox
box note | Amazing Fact || حیرت انگیز حقیقت || په زړه پورې حقیقت
p There is a very high chance that we drink the same water, once dinosaurs drank. Because our Earth does not produce new water. From centuries, water is circulating around our planet through water cycle.
endbox
box note | Activity: 7.2 || سرگرمی: 7.2 || فعالیت: 7.2
p Suppose you are going into the space. What would you need to live in space? Make a list of all the things you may need there. Now, circle the things you get from the Earth (e.g. water). The result will show you the natural resources of the Earth.
endbox
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Talk about sources of water on the Earth. Make a flow chart to show the path of water from the mountains to the oceans. Elaborate that from the oceans water evaporates and forms clouds. Then, water comes back on the mountains in the form of rain, snow and cycle continues.
endbox
