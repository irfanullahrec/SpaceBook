## 10.1 Basic Craft Making || ۱۰.۱ بنیادی دستکاری || ۱۰.۱ بنسټیز لاسي کار
p Paper craft is the broad term for making objects out of paper. A lot of crafts and practices fit under this general category. So, we'll take a look at some of them including origami and animal's face-mask, using various techniques of folding, cutting, tearing and pasting paper to make items.
## 1. Paper Folding - Origami || ۱۔ کاغذ تہہ کرنا - اوریگامی || ۱. د کاغذ ټولول - اوریګامي
p Origami is a Japanese art form that involves folding, cutting or tearing paper and then folding it into objects. It is the most famous paper craft practice. The idea is to create a figure folding a flat piece of paper with no additional tools.
box note | Be Careful || احتیاط کریں || احتیاط وکړئ
p With any sharp or pointed tools, and always be conscious of where you put it down after using it. You may want to use a safety scissors or sharp tools. (Read as far as it shows.)
endbox
p Make a square sheet of paper (Folding, tearing/cutting). Step 1: Take a rectangular sheet of paper. Fold it down diagonal at the left corner and fold it down to the right side. Make sure the right corner is exactly at the very tip. Step 2: Take the upper right corner and fold it down to the left, connecting the corner to the left corner. Where the original fold line made. Step 3: Fold the bottom rectangle backwards and crease it well by using your fingernails to run along the edge. Step 4: Unfold the whole paper and cut out the bottom rectangle or if you can tear it neatly. Well with your fingernails, you can tear it off.
p Tearing Paper: To tear off, place the paper flat on a table and with each hand firmly placed on the rectangle and the rest of the paper, slowly spread the process apart. You may also use ruler or scale to tear the paper.
## 2. Origami Butterfly || ۲۔ اوریگامی تتلی || ۲. اوریګامي پروانه
p The most popular origami shapes are animals and flowers. Step 1: Start with a square piece of paper. Fold the paper in half by folding the upper left corner to the lower right corner and unfold. Repeat it with the right corner.
