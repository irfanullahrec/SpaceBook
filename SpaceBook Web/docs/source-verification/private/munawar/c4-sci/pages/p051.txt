## 2. Transformation of Energy || ۲۔ توانائی کی تبدیلی || ۲. د انرژۍ بدلون
p Energy has very useful property that it can be converted from one form into another. As we have studied, we can transform electricity into heat, light and sound. This is called energy transformation.
p All forms of energy can be converted into each other. Such as a burning matchstick converts chemical energy into heat and light energy. Our sun converts solar energy into heat and light energy. A dam converts hydropower into electrical energy.
