## 2.2 Echoes || ۲.۲ گونج || ۲.۲ انعکاس غږ
p Have you ever enjoyed echo sound, on a trip to any mountainous region or in an empty room? An echo is just a reflection of sound.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Bats survive in dark caves because of their echoes. If there were no echoes bats would not be able to live in caves, because they are blind. They fly around by hearing their echoes.
endbox
p Actually, when sound waves strike any hard surface, they reflect and travel back to us. Thus, we hear the sound again. This is called reflection of sound.
box note | Amazing Fact! || حیرت انگیز حقیقت! || په زړه پورې حقیقت!
p There is complete silence in space where there is no air. Sound waves cannot travel without any material. Even a huge blast in the space cannot be heard by anyone.
endbox
p Hard and smooth surfaces are good reflectors of sound. This is why empty rooms produce many echoes. Soft and rough surfaces are bad reflectors of sound. This is why rooms with carpets and curtains do not produce echoes.
box info | Tech-Fact || تکنیکی حقیقت || تخنیکي حقیقت
p The scientists use sound waves to learn about the bottom of the oceans. The sound waves travel down, hit the ocean floor, and return to the surface as echoes. By measuring the total time that passes between sending the sound wave and receiving the echo, the scientists can figure out how deep the ocean is. (A picture of a ship sending sound waves.)
endbox
box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Design a Musical Instrument. Material needed: empty box, rubber bands, pencil, paper tube/stick, marker colours. Procedure: 1. Cut an oval shaped hole in the shoebox cover. 2. Stretch the rubber bands around the box and over the hole. 3. Put a pencil under the rubber bands on one side. 4. Attach the stick/tube or stick on one end to act as the arm of the guitar. Decorate the guitar any way you choose. 5. Pluck the rubber bands and observe how vibrations produce sound.
endbox
## 3. Heat || ۳۔ حرارت || ۳. تودوخه
p Heat is the energy that flows from a warm material to a cool material due to differences in temperature. An object is said to be heated when heat is added to it. An object that is heated has an increase in thermal energy as well as an increase in temperature. The reverse is true when an object is cooled. When an object is hot, its particles have a lot of energy and therefore move fast. When an object is cold, it moves slowly because of less energy. Warmer objects have a higher temperature than cooler objects.
