## 3.1 Importance of Diversity in Life || ۳.۱ زندگی میں تنوع کی اہمیت || ۳.۱ په ژوند کې د رنګارنګۍ ارزښت
p Every day, each of us come across a number of life forms. We get many benefits from plants and animals around us, such as: They provide us food. They make oxygen. They clean the air. They control disease. They give us medicine. This list of ways in which we appreciate the organisms (animals and plants), or materials made from them, is seemingly endless.
## 3.2 Ways to Protect Diversity in Life || ۳.۲ زندگی میں تنوع کے تحفظ کے طریقے || ۳.۲ په ژوند کې د رنګارنګۍ د ساتنې لارې
p Due to some natural and man-made reasons many important animals and plants die every year. You can do a lot to protect biodiversity. Some of the ways are:
- Use your own reusable cloth/paper bag instead of taking a plastic one.
- Reduce, reuse, recycle! Think before you use extra lights, consider what you buy; be an environment-friendly shopper! Make sure you and your family recycle everything you can.
- Many animals and plants depend on the abundance of fresh water. Taking five-minute shower and turning the water off while washing your hands, washing the dishes, or brushing your teeth etc. are all easy ways to save water.
- Join a project to plant trees or a butterfly garden, clean local rivers, or clear litter from a local park.

box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- Animals can move from one place to another freely. However, they depend on plants and other animals for their food.
- Plants have chlorophyll due to which they prepare their own food. However, they cannot move.
- Animals without backbone are called invertebrates. Some invertebrates have outer hard covering called exoskeleton.
- Animals which have backbone are called vertebrates. All vertebrates have internal skeleton and bones.
- The plants which produce flowers are called flowering plants. A flowering plant is also called an angiosperm.
- Some non-flowering plants reproduce with dust like particles called spores.
- Some non-flowering plants use seeds to reproduce; this type of plants are called gymnosperms.
- The variety of organisms is called diversity in life or simply biodiversity (bio means life and diversity means difference).
- We get many benefits from plants and animals around us. We should identify different ways to protect diversity.
endbox
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Discuss the concept of biodiversity and its importance with the students.
- Explain the variation among different species of animals and plants with reference to the groups they have studied in this chapter. Also elaborate variations among individuals of same species.
