p We hear about energy everywhere. This word has many meanings but in scientific terms it is the ability to do work or get energy from different sources. For example; the Sun, flowing water, wind, coal, oil and gas are all natural sources of energy whereas electricity is a man-made source of energy.
p Vocabulary: energy, source, work, heating, lighting, transformation, fossil fuels, fission, refrigerator, planting, wind, appliances, electrician, light bulbs.

## 1. Why do We need Energy? || ۱۔ ہمیں توانائی کی ضرورت کیوں ہے؟ || ۱. موږ ولې انرژۍ ته اړتیا لرو؟
p We have learned that energy is the ability to do work. This definition of energy is correct but incomplete. We use energy everywhere. Energy is used for three major purposes, i.e. to do work, heating and lighting.
