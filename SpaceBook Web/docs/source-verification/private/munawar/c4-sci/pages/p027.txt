box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- A food chain is the model of how energy in food is passed from organism to organism in an ecosystem.
- Plants make their own food by combining carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight. This process is called photosynthesis.
- The organisms that cannot produce their own food and take it from other living things are called consumers.
- The animals that eat only plants to get energy are known as primary consumers or herbivores.
- The consumers that obtain their food by eating primary consumers or the animals that eat plants are called secondary consumers.
- The animals that eat secondary consumers for getting energy are called tertiary consumers.
- An animal that is hunted by another animal for food is called prey. An animal that hunts another animal for food is called a predator.
- Competition is the struggle of several organisms for the same resource.
- A food web shows how a group of food chains are linked together.
- A population is all the members of a single type of organism in an environment.
- Changing one thing in an ecosystem can affect many other things, because everything is connected.
- Pollution is caused by any material that harms the environment.
- Human population growth in and around cities is called urbanization. The urbanization often destroys natural habitats. Clearing of forest is called deforestation.
- The famous 'three R' (reduce, reuse and recycle) are three ways to protect natural resources.
endbox
