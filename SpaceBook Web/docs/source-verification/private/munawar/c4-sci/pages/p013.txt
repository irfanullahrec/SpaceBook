## 2. Major Parts of Plants || ۲۔ پودوں کے اہم حصے || ۲. د نباتاتو مهم برخې
p Plants have different parts to help them get what they need.
box note | Think and tell! || سوچیں اور بتائیں! || فکر وکړئ او ووایاست!
p Look at the picture here and tell why does this tree not fall over? What is holding it in place?
endbox
## 2.1 Leaves and Stem || ۲.۱ پتے اور تنا || ۲.۱ پاڼې او ډنډ
p Leaves use sunlight to make food for the plant. Leaves take in air and water too. The stem holds up the plant. Water and food move through stem to all parts of the plant.
box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Take two similar plants, and place one in regular water and the other in coloured (ink) water for a few days. Observe the changes. Cut the stems and discuss the differences.
endbox
## 2.2 Flowers, Fruit and Seeds || ۲.۲ پھول، پھل اور بیج || ۲.۲ ګلان، میوې او تخمونه
p Some plants have flowers. Inside a flower, there is a powder called pollen. Plants use pollen to make seeds. Thus, all flowers make seeds. Plants that have flowers also make fruits. Most of the time a seed grows inside a fruit. The fruit keeps the seed safe and helps them grow. A seed can grow into a new plant.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Some fruits such as pineapple or tomatoes have seeds outside their structure.
endbox
## 2.3 Roots || ۲.۳ جڑیں || ۲.۳ ریښې
p Roots hold the plants in the ground. The plant's roots also take in water and nutrients from the soil. Nutrients help the plants to grow.
box note | Activity || سرگرمی || فعالیت
p A tree named General Sherman in California's Sequoia National Park is said to be the largest in the world. It is 275 feet tall. That is as tall as a building with 27 stories. Write about a tall plant that you have seen. Draw and label its parts.
endbox
