## 2. Properties of Matter || ۲۔ مادے کی خصوصیات || ۲. د مادې ځانګړتیاوې
p You can stretch a rubber band, but you can not stretch a piece of string. You can bend a piece of wire, but you can not easily bend a matchstick. In each case, the materials change their shapes but identity the materials that do not change at all.
p The colour, shape, odour are some of the physical properties of matter. We can say that physical properties are the characteristics of matter which we can observe without changing the identity of a substance. We can sort out objects according to their physical properties.
## 2.1 Mass || ۲.۱ کمیت || ۲.۱ کتله
p The amount of matter present in a material is called its mass. The mass of a substance can be measured by using weighing balance. The scientists use grams (g) or kilograms (kg) as unit of mass. For example, a football has more mass than a beach ball. Similarly, we can sort things according to their mass.
box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Collect some heavy and light items from your home or school and ask your teacher or an elder to help you to measure the mass of each item. Write down the mass in grams in front of the name of each item in a table. Then, find the answers of the following questions: Do all objects have the same amount of matter? Do bigger objects always have a bigger mass? Do all objects have the same mass? Do smaller objects always have a smaller mass?
endbox
## 2.2 Volume || ۲.۲ حجم || ۲.۲ حجم
p The amount of space taken up by an object is called its volume. The volume of an object can be measured by using a graduated cylinder or beaker. The units of volume commonly used are millilitre (mL) or cubic centimeter (cm³).
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Encourage your students to talk and share their experiences about measuring matter and materials in their lives. For example, measuring how much petrol, weighing the baby, buying a certain amount of food, measuring how much building material, buying clothing of the right size.
endbox
