box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- An organ is actually a part of body that has a specific function.
- Animals have different types of teeth to chew their food.
- Brain is the control center of a body. It controls all the actions of an animal's body such as seeing, hearing, smelling, walking, etc.
- The main function of a heart is to pump the blood to all parts of body.
- The lungs exchange oxygen from air into the blood and carbon dioxide from the blood in to the air.
- Stomach breaks down the food and helps in digestion.
- The skeleton supports the body of animal and helps in movement.
- There are different types of muscles having different jobs.
- Plants have different parts to help them get what they need.
- Leaves use sunlight, water and air to make food for the plant. The stem holds up the plant and transports food and water.
- All flowers make seeds. A seed can grow into a new plant. The fruit keeps the seeds safe and helps them grow.
- Roots hold the plants in the ground. They also absorb water and nutrients.
