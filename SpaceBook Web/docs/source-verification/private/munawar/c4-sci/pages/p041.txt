box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- If you want to be healthy when you will be old, adopt a healthy lifestyle from your childhood.
- The various food items that we eat make our diet.
- The diet which consists of basic nutrients including proteins, carbohydrates, fats, minerals, vitamins and water is called a balanced diet.
- Meat, milk, eggs, pulses and nuts are some of the common sources of proteins. Bread, cereals, grains, sweet fruits and vegetables provide us carbohydrates.
- Major sources of animal fats are ghee, butter, milk, cheese, eggs, fish and meat. Sources of vegetable oils are olive, mustard, sesame, etc. We get vitamins from fruits and vegetables. Fruits, vegetables and nuts provide us various minerals.
- A food pyramid is designed to make a healthy diet plan. We can get sick by drinking polluted water.
- Groundwater can be polluted by burning or leakage of sewage pipes, fertilizers and chemicals dissolved in rainwater.
- Freshwater get polluted due to farmwaste runoff, municipal and industrial waste discharges, Rubbish that industries and individuals dump directly into waterways.
- Boiling, filtration, distillation and chlorination are some common ways through which we can make water clean and suitable for drinking.
- Regular physical activity is important for the healthy growth, development and well-being.
- Getting enough sleep is just as important as eating good food and exercising regularly. Personal hygiene is another important part of healthy lifestyle. Thus, brushing teeth twice a day and keeping our body and surroundings clean save us from germs that cause diseases.
endbox
