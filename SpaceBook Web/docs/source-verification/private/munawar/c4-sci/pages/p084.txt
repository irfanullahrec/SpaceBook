# Earth's Weather and Climates || زمین کا موسم اور آب و ہوا || د ځمکې موسم او اقلیم
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo understand the difference between weather and climate.
slo relate that weather (i.e., daily variations in temperature, humidity, precipitation in the form of rain or snow, clouds, and wind) changes with changing geographical location.
slo recognize that average temperature and precipitation can change with the seasons and location.
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- Which time of the day is warmest?
- How is the weather today?
- Why are Polar Regions very cold?
endbox
p Many people use the terms weather and climate as having the same meaning. However, they actually mean two very different things. Weather refers to the day-to-day conditions of a certain location. Climate is the long term average weather conditions of an area.
## 1. Weather || ۱۔ موسم || ۱. موسم
p The weather often changes day to day and from one year to the next. For example; it was raining when you went to the school and sunny day when you came back to the house. Weather can be described through temperature, humidity, precipitation and wind.
p Vocabulary: weather, climate, humidity, precipitation, wind, season, tropical, temperate, polar. (Pictures: sunny, stormy, cloudy, rainy, windy, snowy.)
box teach | Guidelines for Teachers || اساتذہ کے لیے رہنمائی || د ښوونکو لپاره لارښوونې
- Lead a discussion about the weather today: Is it hot? Is it cloudy? What do you think the temperature is today? Ask the students whether they would wear a bathing suit or a winter coat according to the weather condition today? Introduce the topic that the lesson will be about weather and climate.
endbox
