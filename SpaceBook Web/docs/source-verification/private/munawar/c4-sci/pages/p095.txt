## 5. What are Eclipses? || ۵۔ گرہن کیا ہیں؟ || ۵. ګرهنونه څه دي؟
p Recall that Moon revolves around the Earth. Recall also the same time, Earth revolves around the Sun. The Moon's orbit is tilted to the path of the Earth's orbit around the Sun.
## 5.1 Lunar Eclipse || ۵.۱ چاند گرہن || ۵.۱ د سپوږمۍ ګرهن
p Due to the tilted orbits, Moon usually remains above or below the Earth's orbit. Only twice a month, Moon crosses the path of Earth's orbit. When this takes place at full Moon, the Earth casts a shadow on the Moon. So the moon appears to be dark for a time until it gets out of the Earth's shadow. This is called lunar eclipse.
## 5.2 Solar Eclipse || ۵.۲ سورج گرہن || ۵.۲ د لمر ګرهن
p During revolution sometimes, Moon comes between the Sun and the Earth and blocks the path of a few sunrays to reach the Earth. In this case, we see a red Sun with no or partial light. This process is called solar eclipse.
p Solar eclipse is observed in some parts of Earth only because Moon is quite smaller than the Earth. So complete Earth cannot be shadowed by the Moon.
box note | Precautions || احتیاطیں || احتیاطونه
p Never observe solar eclipse with bare eye because during solar eclipse, Sun emits harmful rays. Never observe solar eclipse with ordinary glass or on the surface of water. However, solar eclipse can be observed by using special spectacles fitter with blackish blue glasses.
endbox
