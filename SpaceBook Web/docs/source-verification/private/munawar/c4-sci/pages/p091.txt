## 1. Sun || ۱۔ سورج || ۱. لمر
p The Sun is a star. A Star is an object that produces its own energy, including heat and light. The planets and other objects in the Solar system are not stars because they do not produce their own light. Therefore, the Sun is the only source of heat and light for the whole solar system.
## 2. Moon || ۲۔ چاند || ۲. سپوږمۍ
p A moon is an object that circles around a planet. The Earth's moon is the only moon that can be seen without a telescope. Have you ever observed moon in the sky at night? It appears different at different times of the month.

p Different planets have different numbers and sizes of moons. The dwarf planet Pluto has at least three moons. (A table of the numbers of moons of Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.)

## 2.1 Why does the Moon change its appearance? || ۲.۱ چاند اپنی شکل کیوں بدلتا ہے؟ || ۲.۱ سپوږمۍ ولې خپله بڼه بدلوي؟
p We know that the moon revolves around the Earth. We see only the part of the Moon which is lighted by the Sun. It is called a phase of the moon. The phase of the moon is the shape of lighted part of the moon that we see at any given time.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p The Moon takes 29 days to complete its orbit around the Earth. It changes its shape every day. This cycle is called as lunar month. We can determine the date of a lunar month by looking at the moon.
endbox
p Half of the moon is always lighted by the Sun. However, you cannot always see all of that half. Sometime you can see only a small part of the moon. Sometime you can see a lot. The phase, or shape, of the moon that you see depends on the position of the Sun, the moon, and the Earth with respect to each other.
## i. New Moon Phase || i۔ نیا چاند (ہلال سے پہلے) || i. نوې سپوږمۍ
p At new Moon the Moon is between Earth and the Sun. The lighted part of the Moon is opposite to the half that faces Earth. As a result, you cannot see any of the Moon's sunlit part of the Moon.
