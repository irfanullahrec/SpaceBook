p Things cannot move on their own. You have to use a force to put something in motion. When you play soccer, you kick the ball to move it across the field. Your kick is a force. If you do not kick the ball, it will stay in the same place.
## 2. Force || ۲۔ قوت || ۲. ځواک
p A push or a pull is called a Force. It can cause a change in the position, motion and even shape of an object.
## 2.1 Changing Position || ۲.۱ مقام بدلنا || ۲.۱ ځای بدلول
p The position of things can be changed by applying force. We can move an object with the help of force. For example; We can move a book on a table by just pushing it. Similarly, we can move the trolley suitcase by pulling it.
