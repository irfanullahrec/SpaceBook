## 3.2 Earth's Revolution || ۳.۲ زمین کا سورج کے گرد چکر || ۳.۲ د ځمکې څرخېدنه
p Our Earth does not only rotate, it also revolves around the Sun in a fixed path. This fixed path is called orbit. Movement of the Earth around the Sun in its fixed orbit is called revolution. The Earth takes 365 days (a year) to make a full trip around the Sun.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p All planets revolve around the Sun in their own orbits. They take different time to complete one revolution around the Sun.
endbox
p As the year progresses, you observe the seasonal change as the temperature rises and falls. This change is due to many reasons including Earth's revolution around the Sun.
## Two Halves of the Earth || زمین کے دو نصف || د ځمکې دوه نیمايي
p We know that equator is the imaginary central line on the Earth. The part of the Earth between equator and North Pole is called Northern Hemisphere. The other half which means the area between equator and South Pole is called Southern Hemisphere.
## 4. How seasons change || ۴۔ موسم کیسے بدلتے ہیں || ۴. فصلونه څنګه بدلیږي
p Our Earth is slightly tilted on its axis. Due to the Earth's revolution and its tilt, various parts of the Earth get different amount of sunlight in different parts of the year. It causes change in seasons.
p As the diagram shows, when Earth is at a position A in its orbit, the Northern Hemisphere is tilted towards the Sun. This causes the Sun's incoming rays to strike the Northern Hemisphere at maximum. As a result Northern Hemisphere has summer.
p Sunlight in Summer and Winter (diagram): Position A - Northern Hemisphere is tilted towards the Sun, the Sun's rays hit at a high angle and it is summer in the Northern Hemisphere; Position B - Northern Hemisphere is tilted away from the Sun, the Sun's rays hit at a low angle and it is winter in the Northern Hemisphere.
p Six months later, Earth is now at position B in its orbit. Now the Northern Hemisphere is tilted away from the Sun. Incoming rays from the Sun are slanted. As a result, it is now winter in the Northern Hemisphere.
