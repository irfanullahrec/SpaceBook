p Origami butterfly: Step 2: Fold in half again by folding the top half back and then unfold again. Step 3: With your finger, push down on the middle so that it pops in. Step 4: Bring the left and right creases together to form this triangle. Step 5: Fold the left corner of the top layer to the top corner of the triangle and right corner to its top corner. Step 6: Turn over and fold the bottom part of the triangle up with a portion of the top corner as shown in the picture. Step 7: Turn over and pull down the left flap of the triangle and the side will be forced to bend in. Fold and flatten the side down. Step 8: Repeat the step 6 with the other side. Step 9: Turn over and fold down the top triangle. Step 10: Fold a portion of the triangle back up, but leave some parts folded down. Step 11: Mountain fold in half. In other words, fold the left side under the right side. Step 12: Diagonally fold the wing as shown. Step 13: Open the wings and you have a beautiful butterfly.

p Material needed: Chart paper, marker, safety scissors, gum/glue, old magazine. Procedure: 1. Take a chart paper and make two columns on the chart paper, write vertebrates in one column and invertebrates in the other. 2. Get an old animal magazine from old book house. Find the pictures of different animals in the magazine. 3. Cut out the pictures of animals with safety scissors. 4. Place a picture on a flat surface with its front side facing downwards and apply glue/gum evenly on the back side of the animal cut out. 5. Then carefully place the animal cut out in the right column of the chart paper. Gently press it with your fingers to paste it correctly. 6. Repeat the step 4-5 with each animal picture to complete poster. 7. Display/present your poster in the classroom.
