## 2.3 State of Matter || ۲.۳ مادے کی حالت || ۲.۳ د مادې حالت
p The most common states of matter are solid, liquid and gas. A material like water can change from one state of matter into another without changing its identity. For example; liquid water turns into solid ice on cooling. This solid ice turns back into liquid on heating. Thus, state of matter is an important physical property of a substance.
## 2.4 Density || ۲.۴ کثافت || ۲.۴ کثافت
p Have you ever wondered why does oil float over water? Take two beakers of 100 mL and add 50 mL water in one beaker and 50 mL oil in other beaker. Weigh both beakers one by one. You will notice that water is heavier than oil, although the volume of both liquids was same. Thus, lighter oil float over water.
p The property of a material which enables it to float or sink is called its density. The density tells us about how heavier or lighter an object is compared to its size (volume). Density of a substance can be measured by: Measuring the mass. Measuring the volume. And dividing the mass and volume.
p Density = mass / volume
