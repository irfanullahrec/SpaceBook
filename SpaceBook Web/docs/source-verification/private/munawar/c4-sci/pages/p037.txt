## 2. Drink Healthy || ۲۔ صحت مند پانی پئیں || ۲. روغ اوبه وڅښئ
p Water is the most important liquid. Clean water is a blessing of Almighty. We can get ill by drinking polluted water. There are many ways in which water become polluted and unsafe for drinking.
box note | Activity: 3B.2 || سرگرمی: 3B.2 || فعالیت: 3B.2
p Why should we drink water? With a friend, go to your kitchen or bathroom sink. Place a large container under the tap and turn the water on full. Then count 15 seconds and load or have someone time 15 seconds. The amount of water that comes out of the sink in that 15 seconds is approximately how much water your body loses each day! Your body loses this water through sweating, breathing, and going to the washroom. Therefore, we need to drink a 6-8 glass of water daily in order to keep our bodies hydrated.
endbox
