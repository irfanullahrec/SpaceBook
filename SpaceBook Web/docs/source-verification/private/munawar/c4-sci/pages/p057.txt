## 1.1 Shadow Formation || ۱.۱ سایہ بننا || ۱.۱ د سیوري جوړېدل
p Light travels in straight line. When an opaque object comes in its way, it stops light rays from travelling through it. Therefore, light cannot reach on the opposite side of the object and form a dark area. We call that dark area as shadow. For example; when our back is facing direct light we have our shadow at our front.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p The objects which do not allow light rays to pass through them are called opaque objects. Some opaque objects such as wood and cloth absorb light. This is called absorption of light.
endbox
box note | Let's conclude! || آئیں نتیجہ نکالیں! || راځئ پایله ترلاسه کړو!
p Shadows are formed because of two properties of light, i.e. Light travels in straight line. Light does not pass through opaque objects.
endbox
box note | Activity: 5B.1 || سرگرمی: 5B.1 || فعالیت: 5B.1
p Talha claimed that he can see the light of a fixed candle from a long pipe when the pipe is straight, but when the pipe is bent he cannot see the light of the candle. Design an experiment to verify this statement by using a long pipe and a candle at a certain point. Make a paper with and try to see the candle. Then, bend the paper into tube and again try to see the candle. Share the result with your teacher. (Two pictures: a boy looking through a straight pipe and a bent pipe.)
endbox
## 1.2 Mirrors and Reflection || ۱.۲ آئینہ اور انعکاس || ۱.۲ هینداره او انعکاس
p Mirrors and aluminium foils do not absorb light and instead, they bend the light waves in another direction which make their surface shiny. When this bent light reach our eyes we see an image in the mirror. This is called the reflection. We can see reflections of all objects which are in front of the mirror.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Shadow gives outline of an object while reflection gives details.
endbox
box note | Let's do it! || آئیں کر کے دیکھیں! || راځئ وکړو!
p Mirror changes the direction of light falling on it. 1. In a dark room, ask one of your friends to hold a mirror in his/her hand at one corner of the room. 2. Stand at another corner with a torch in your hand. 3. Cover the glass of torch with your fingers and switch it on. 4. Adjust your fingers with a small gap between them so that you can get a beam of light. 5. Direct the beam of the torch light onto the mirror that your friend is holding. 6. You can see a patch of light on the other side. Now, adjust the direction of the torch so that the patch of light falls on another friend standing in the room.
endbox
