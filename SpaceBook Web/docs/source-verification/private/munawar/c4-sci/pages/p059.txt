## 2. Sound || ۲۔ آواز || ۲. غږ
p Sound is a type of energy that we hear. The beep of a car, whistle of traffic police, siren from an ambulance, the quack of a duck, a sound of coin dropping on the floor and birds chirping on a tree are different sounds.
## 2.1 How is Sound Produced? || ۲.۱ آواز کیسے پیدا ہوتی ہے؟ || ۲.۱ غږ څنګه جوړیږي؟
p Sound is produced when something moves backward and forward quickly. In other words, when things vibrate they produce sound.
box note | Activity: 5B.5 || سرگرمی: 5B.5 || فعالیت: 5B.5
p 1. Take a ruler and hold the one end of a ruler at the edge of a table. Press down the free end and let it go quickly. The ruler will make sound by moving backwards and forwards again and again. This movement is called vibration. 2. Check if you can feel vibration in your throat while speaking. Put your hand on your throat and hum softly and then become quiet. Can you feel any difference?
endbox
p For example, if you pluck a guitar string, the string will vibrate. The vibrating string pass energy to the air that surrounds it, so the air vibrates too. The vibrations travel through the air as sound waves. If the waves reach our ears, we hear the sound made by the guitar string.
box note | Think and tell! || سوچیں اور بتائیں! || فکر وکړئ او ووایاست!
p Read the graph to know difference of speed of sound in different medium and answer the question. (A bar chart of the approximate speed of sound in air, fresh water, steel and iron.) A bird perched in a tree hears the chirping of another bird. A whale hears the songlike sound made by another whale swimming near it. Which sound travels faster, the bird's chirping or the whale's singing? Explain how you decided.
