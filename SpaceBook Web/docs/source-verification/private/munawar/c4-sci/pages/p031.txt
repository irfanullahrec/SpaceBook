p Ways of transmission of contagious diseases:
- By direct contact with the infected person - Touching the skin or a wound of an infected person allows germs to transmit and spread disease.
- By germ through air, water, food or objects - One way for germs to spread from the infected person is through his/her coughing or sneezing without covering mouth. Then, these germs travel in the air. From air, they can land anywhere including water, food, any object, and clothes or at another healthy person.
- Through disease carrying animals - many diseases spread due to animals or insects. For example; when flies land on an infected person, they carry his germs and then land on food. In this way, they transport germs from one location to another.

## 4. Ways to prevent Diseases || ۴۔ بیماریوں سے بچاؤ کے طریقے || ۴. د ناروغیو مخنیوي لارې
p The best way to prevent a disease is to avoid its possible causes. The following measures help in the prevention and control the spread of diseases:
## Personal Hygiene || ذاتی صفائی || شخصي پاکوالی
p Following are most common ways of personal hygiene to prevent diseases:
- Cutting nails and taking bath regularly.
- Washing hands with soap frequently; before touching food or water, before eating or drinking, before touching your face, eyes and mouth, also before and after using the toilet.
