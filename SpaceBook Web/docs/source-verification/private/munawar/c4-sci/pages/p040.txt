## 4. Sleep Healthy || ۴۔ صحت مند نیند || ۴. روغ خوب
p Your body needs rest in order to work properly every day. Therefore, getting enough sleep is just as important as eating good food and exercising regularly. Some of the major benefits of healthy sleep are as follows:
- It lets your body repair itself from damages or illnesses.
- It makes your body stronger to fight against germs.
- Sleep gives you more energy for the next day.
- Sleep makes your mind rest and refresh itself.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Although it is recommended that the average adult needs 7-9 hours of sleep each night, every person is different. Some people need more and some people need less.
endbox

p Think back to the last time you did not get enough sleep. Below are some things that may happen when you do not get enough sleep. Did you experience any of these? bad mood/grouchy, sad; lack of strength; hard to pay attention; poor memory; lack of energy; sleep more during the day. If you remember other experiences that you had when you were tired, write them down.
