## 3.1.1 How Days and Nights occur || ۳.۱.۱ دن اور رات کیسے ہوتے ہیں || ۳.۱.۱ ورځ او شپه څنګه جوړیږي
p We have studied that shape of the Earth is round. Due to its round shape all parts of Earth cannot face the Sun at one time. However, Earth keeps spinning that expose different parts of its to the Sun. It takes almost 24 hours or one day to complete one rotation of the Earth. During rotation, the side of the Earth that faces the Sun experiences daytime and the side of the Earth that is turned away from the Sun experiences night.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p You can't feel the Earth spinning on its axis as everything including you, is moving at the same speed.
endbox
p Example: When it is daytime in Britain, it is night-time on the opposite side of the Earth in New Zealand.

## 3.1.2 How Do we Know Earth is Rotating? || ۳.۱.۲ ہمیں کیسے پتا چلتا ہے کہ زمین گھوم رہی ہے؟ || ۳.۱.۲ موږ څنګه پوهیږو چې ځمکه څرخي؟
p As a day progresses, what does the Sun appear to do? We get that the Sun rises in the east. It seems to travel west, rising higher in the sky and then get lower on the other side. We can observe all these changes by looking at the shadows of objects on the Earth.
p However, we know that the Sun is stationary. Then what will be moving to change the position of the Sun and appearance of shadows on the Earth? Yes it is the evidence that the Earth is rotating continuously around its own axis.
