# Earth and its Resources || زمین اور اس کے وسائل || ځمکه او د هغې سرچینې
## Students' Learning Outcomes || طلبہ کے حاصلاتِ تعلّم || د زده کوونکو د زده کړې پایلې
p After completing this lesson, the students will be able to: || اس سبق کی تکمیل کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || دا درس بشپړولو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo recognize that Earth's surface is made up of land and water and is surrounded by air.
slo recognize that water in rivers and streams flows from mountains to oceans or lakes.
slo identify some of Earth's natural resources that are used in everyday life (e.g., water, wind, soil, forests, oil, natural gas, minerals).
slo recognize that some remains (fossils) of animals and plants that lived on Earth a long time ago are found in rocks, soil and under the sea.
slo differentiate between renewable and non-renewable resources.
slo investigate the impact of human activities on Earth's natural resources.
slo suggest the ways to conserve the natural resources. || قدرتی وسائل بچانے کے طریقے تجویز کریں۔ || د طبیعي سرچینو د ساتلو لارې وړاندیز کړئ.
box note | Pre-reading || پڑھنے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
- What is Earth called other planet?
- How much of the Earth's water do you think is in the ocean?
- How can we make light from the Sun?
endbox
p Our Earth is the only planet where life exists. Do you know why is it so? Mainly, this is because of the presence of water on Earth. What is other main component of the Earth's surface?
box note | Think and tell! || سوچیں اور بتائیں! || فکر وکړئ او ووایاست!
p Look at the picture of the Earth. What two things you can identify in this picture?
endbox
p Vocabulary: stream, lake, pressure, fossils, minerals, renewable, non-renewable.
p Our Earth's surface is made up of land and water. If we divide the whole Earth into 100 equal parts. Its 71 parts would consist of water and remaining 29 parts would consist of land.
p Air is present around the Earth like a blanket. It is a mixture of many gases such as oxygen, carbon dioxide and water vapours. (A pie chart of land 29 % and water 71 %.)
