box note | Activity: 6.5 || سرگرمی: 6.5 || فعالیت: 6.5
p Build a lever and find out best position of fulcrum. Material: tape, ruler, pencil, stack of five large coins, a small book. Procedure: 1. Tape the coins together to make a stack of coins. 2. Put the pencil under the ruler and place the coin stack on one end. 3. Apply a little force on the other end with your finger. 4. Repeat the step 3 after changing the position of the pencil (fulcrum). 5. Observe carefully, where you put minimum force to lift the maximum movement. Inquiry Question: What was the effect of change in position of the pencil (fulcrum)? Predict: What would happen if you put an object with a larger diameter than the pencil under the ruler?
endbox
## Pulley || چرخی (پُلی) || چرخۍ
p A Pulley consists of a wire, rope or cable, moving on a grooved wheel. Pulleys help to lift larger loads in two ways:
- It can decrease the amount of force needed to lift the object.
- It can change the direction of force.
p Pulleys are used in flagpoles, blind cords, sailboats, etc.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p As you add more pulleys in a system you need to put less effort in lifting the same load.
endbox
