## 2. Earth's Natural Resources || ۲۔ زمین کے قدرتی وسائل || ۲. د ځمکې طبیعي سرچینې
p Natural resources are the materials people take from the Earth. Almost everything people use directly or indirectly come from natural resources. Some of Earth's natural resources that are used in everyday life are water, wind, rocks, soil, plants, minerals, oil and gas.
## 2.1 How do we use Water and Wind? || ۲.۱ ہم پانی اور ہوا کیسے استعمال کرتے ہیں؟ || ۲.۱ موږ اوبه او باد څنګه کاروو؟
p Water and wind are most precious natural resources. We use water to drink, cook and clean. We use water to grow plants. We also use water for energy. We use flowing water to produce electricity. This electricity lights and heats homes.
p We breathe in air to get oxygen. Moving air is called wind. Wind can make things move such as a boat move across the water or a flag wave in the wind. Therefore, like water, we can also produce electricity from wind.

## How do we use Rocks and Minerals? || ہم چٹانیں اور معدنیات کیسے استعمال کرتے ہیں؟ || موږ ډبرې او منرالونه څنګه کاروو؟
p Rocks break down and become part of the soil. We also use rocks to build homes. Concrete is made by mixing rocks, sand and water. We use concrete to make buildings and sidewalks. We use the minerals in sand to make glass. We use minerals such as gold and silver to make jewelry. We even eat some minerals such as iron.
