## 2. Organisms in the Food chain || ۲۔ غذائی زنجیر میں جاندار || ۲. په خوراکي زنځیر کې ژوي
p We can classify organisms into two major groups on the basis of the way they get their food in an ecosystem.
## 2.1 Producers || ۲.۱ پیدا کرنے والے || ۲.۱ تولیدوونکي
p Have you ever seen plants eating any fruit or vegetable? No, plants do not eat food. They make their own food. They combine carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight to make their food. This process is called photosynthesis. Photosynthesis is the combination of two words. 'Photo' means 'light' and 'synthesis' means 'to make'. As a result of photosynthesis, plants produce food (sugar) and oxygen inside their leaves. Thus, the plants are called producers.
box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Plants take in carbon dioxide for photosynthesis and release oxygen in the surroundings. In this way, plants make the air clean and better for other living organisms.
endbox
## 2.2 Consumers || ۲.۲ استعمال کرنے والے || ۲.۲ مصرفوونکي
p The organisms that cannot produce their own food and take it from other living things are called consumers. Different animals have different diets. Therefore, we can further classify them according to their position in a food chain.
box note | Quick Review || فوری دہرائی || چټکه بیاکتنه
p Animals that eat only meat are called carnivores whereas the animals that eat both meat and plants are called omnivores.
endbox
