qp ii | Compare the abiotic factors of grassland and desert ecosystem.
sol Grassland has cool winters, hot summers and rich fertile topsoil with some rainfall; the desert has lower rainfall and scorching sunlight, very hot days and cool nights and poor soil. ## From p. 18 and 19. || Grassland has cool winters, hot summers and rich fertile topsoil with some rainfall; the desert has lower rainfall and scorching sunlight, very hot days and cool nights and poor soil. || Grassland has cool winters, hot summers and rich fertile topsoil with some rainfall; the desert has lower rainfall and scorching sunlight, very hot days and cool nights and poor soil.
