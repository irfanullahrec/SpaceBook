- Introduce the concept of conservation of energy with a working analogy. The humans are quickly using up some of the Earth's natural sources of energy, like coal, oil and gas, that are limited in quantity. That's like a chicken lays eggs: a lot of oil and energy; but what about the chickens when the eggs are all gone?

box note | Do you know? || کیا آپ جانتے ہیں؟ || ایا تاسو پوهیږئ؟
p Another way to save energy is to use less plastic. Making plastic in factories uses a lot of oil and energy. Try to use plastic packages more than once. It takes less energy to make things from recycled plastic than from new plastic.

box note | Summary || خلاصہ || لنډیز
- Energy is the ability to do work. || توانائی کام کرنے کی صلاحیت ہے۔ || انرژي د کار کولو وړتیا ده.
- The Sun, flowing water, wind, coal, oil and gas are all natural sources of energy whereas electricity is a man-made source of energy.
- Energy is used for three major purposes, i.e. to do work, heating and lighting.
- Energy has very useful property that it can be converted from one form into another. This is called energy transformation.
- Fossil fuels are made up of plants and animals that lived millions of years ago.
- Someday, people may not be able to find enough oil, coal, and natural gas to create energy.
- We need to use less energy to make sure these natural resources stay and do not disappear in the future.
endbox
