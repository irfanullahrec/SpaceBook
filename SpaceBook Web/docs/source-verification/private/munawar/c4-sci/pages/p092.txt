## ii. Waxing Phase || ii۔ بڑھتا ہوا چاند || ii. وده کوونکې سپوږمۍ
p After the new Moon phase, more of its sunlit part become visible each night. It is called Waxing Moon. Waxing means 'growing larger'. When half of moon is visible, it is called first quarter phase. The sunlit part of the Moon keeps growing untill it reaches to the Full Moon.
## iii. Waning Phase || iii۔ گھٹتا ہوا چاند || iii. کمېدونکې سپوږمۍ
p After full moon, the sunlit part you can see gets smaller. The phases you see are the same as from new to full, only in reverse. It is called Waning Moon. Waning means 'growing smaller'. When the left half of the Moon is visible, the phase is called third quarter. The sunlit part of the Moon keeps getting smaller until it reaches to the New Moon again.

## 3. The Earth || ۳۔ زمین || ۳. ځمکه
p The Earth is the third planet in the solar system. This planet is the home of all living things. It revolves around the Sun and rotates around its own axis.
## 3.1 Earth's Rotation || ۳.۱ زمین کی گردش (محوری) || ۳.۱ د ځمکې څرخېدنه
p Have you ever played with a spinning top? It spins about its centre of movement or axis. Every spinning object has an axis similarly our Earth also spin continuously around its own axis. This movement of the Earth is called rotation and its axis is called axis of rotation. Axis of rotation is an imaginary line that runs from the North Pole to the South Pole of the Earth.
box note | Activity: 9.1 || سرگرمی: 9.1 || فعالیت: 9.1
p Spin the ball to find the axis. This experiment will tell you that how Earth spins on its axis. For this: take a thread ball; pierce a knitting needle through the centre of a ball; rotate the ball around the needle. Conclusion: The needle is the axis around which the ball rotates.
endbox
