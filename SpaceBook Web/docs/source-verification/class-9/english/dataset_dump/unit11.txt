# 11 Safety First (dialogue, Prof. Hamidullah Khan & Aamna Aamad (KPK Textbook Board Peshawar))

## [0] 1. Introduction to First Aid & The First Aider's Role
Babar: Asalam-o-Aliakum my dearest friend! Where were you yesterday? We had a terrific cricket match in the stadium.

Asim: Walaik-um-Asalam! I could not come for the match because I took a First Aid class yesterday.

Babar: Really? What is first aid? What did you learn?

Asim: First aid is the help given to a sick or injured person until full medical treatment is available. First aid is about using your commonsense in ways that will keep that person safe without doing harm.

Babar: You mean if a friend has an accident or someone collapses perhaps from a heart attack then the first person there will be the first aider or the person who will help the casualties till help arrives.

Asim: Yes, my dear friend.

Babar: It's a great idea for older children and adults to do a first aid course so that they know what to do to help others. Tell me more, what else did you learn?

Asim: A lot of stuff. We were taught to have a first aid kit at home, school and work place.


## [1] 2. Essential Contents of a First Aid Kit & Proper Use
Babar: What is a first aid kit? What should I keep in my first aid kit?

Asim: A basic first aid kit may contain:
• adhesive plasters in a variety of different sizes and shapes
• small, medium and large sterile gauze dressings
• at least two sterile eye dressings
• triangular bandages
• crêpe rolled bandages
• safety pins
• disposable sterile gloves
• tweezers
• scissors
• medicines

Babar: When and where do you use a first aid kit?

Asim: Make sure you know how to properly use all the items in your kit, especially the medications. Train others in your family to use the kit. You may be the one who needs first aid.

Babar: You are right, just having a first aid kit is not enough. One must know how to use it.

Asim: Exactly, first aid is all about what to do in emergency situations.


## [2] 3. Emergency Protocols: Minor Burns & Choking
Babar: For example, what should you do if you burn yourself on a hot stove? My grandma says that you should put butter on a burn.

Asim: No! That's the worst thing to do!

Babar: Oh. Then maybe you should put ice on the burn.

Asim: No, the first thing you should do when you get a minor burn is run cool (not cold) water over the burn area for about 20 minutes. Later, cover it with a sterile, non-adhesive bandage or clean cloth.

Babar: How about this? What should you do if someone is choking?

Asim: That's easy. Hit them on the back.

Babar: No. You might lodge the object in their throat more and make things worse. First you should make sure they are really choking. If they can talk or cough that means that air is getting through and you should just let them cough. If they can't talk or cough, you should do the abdominal thrust.

Babar: How do you do that?

Asim: You wrap your arms around the person and quickly pull in and up with your fist. The air that's forced up dislodges the object.


## [3] 4. Managing Severe Bleeding & Heart Attack Emergencies
Babar: Similarly, what would you do if someone cuts his arm and is bleeding really badly?

Asim: Panic.

Babar: Take a deep breath to calm down a little. Call on the emergency phone number for help and in the mean while you should put a cloth over the wound, apply pressure, and elevate it.

Asim: Great. My grandfather is old and ill, God forbid if he gets a heart attack. What should I do?

Babar: Call medical help as soon as possible. In the meanwhile, you need to ease the strain on his heart, so make him as comfortable as possible. A half-sitting position, with his head and shoulders supported and his knees bent is best.

Asim: That is good information, otherwise we normally make the patient lie down.


## [4] 5. First Aid for Nosebleeds & Conclusion
Babar: Please tell me another thing, often when we play in sun or accidently hurt a person, the nose bleeds. What should be done in that case?

Asim: Sit the person up straight and drop their head slightly forward. Apply finger and thumb pressure on the soft part of nostrils below the bridge of the nose for at least 10 minutes.

Babar: Wow! There is so much to learn. I think I need to take this class.

Asim: Everyone should take a first aid class.

Babar: Thank you for all the information. Allah Hafiz.

Asim: Allah Hafiz.
