# 12 The Old Woman (poetry, Joseph Campbell (1906–1987))

## [0] Stanza 1: The Sacred Purity of Old Age
As a white candle
In a holy place,
So is the beauty
Of an aged face.


## [1] Stanza 2: The Gentle Glow of Finished Labor
As the spent radiance
Of the winter sun,
So is a woman
With her travail done,


## [2] Stanza 3: The Deep Still Waters of Maternal Fulfillment
Her brood gone from her,
And her thoughts as still
As the waters
Under a ruined mill.
