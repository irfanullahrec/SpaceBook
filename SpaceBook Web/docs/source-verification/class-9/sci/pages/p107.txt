p ii. Polio: Polio (also called poliomyelitis) is a contagious, historically devastating disease that was almost eliminated from the Western hemisphere in the second half of the 20th century. Polio is an acute viral infection caused by polio virus. The disease occurs among young children especially under 5 years of age. The virus enters the body through the mouth. When it reaches the intestine it crosses the wall of the gut and enters the blood stream; thus it spreads to various organs of the body, specially the nervous system.
