p Ringworm: The causative agents of ringworm are collectively known as dermatophytes. They may infect skin, hair and nails. If the fungi invade the skin they produce a circular white area, which grows in all directions. Nail destruction and hair loss may occur. Ringworm spreads in the community by direct contact with the patient or infected animals. Use of common showers and swimming pools or sharing of towels and sports wear are the cause of transmission.
