p In Pakistan hydroelectric power projects are contributing significantly to electricity production. Although Pakistan has huge hydel power generation capacity, mainly on river Indus, it has yet to exploit it to its maximum capacity. However, three main dams, Tarbela, Mangla and Warsak, constructed for the purpose of generating electricity and irrigating agricultural land, are vital for the energy sector in Pakistan. || ↑ || ↑
p c. Solar Energy: Most of the energy known to man is derived in some way from the sun. When we burn wood or other fuels, we actually release the stored energy of the sun.
