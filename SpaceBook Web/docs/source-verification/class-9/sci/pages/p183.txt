p Famous accidents of civil nuclear power generation: The three significant accidents in the 50-year history of civil nuclear power generation are: • Three Mile Island (USA 1979), where the reactor was severely damaged but radiation was contained and there were no adverse health or environmental consequences. • Chernobyl (Ukraine 1986): on 26 April 1986, reactor no. 4 of the Chernobyl nuclear power station exploded.
