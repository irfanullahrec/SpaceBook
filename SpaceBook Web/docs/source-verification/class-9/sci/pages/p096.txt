p Signs and Symptoms of TB in the lungs may include: a bad cough that lasts 3 weeks or longer; weight loss; coughing up blood or mucus; weakness or fatigue; fever and chills. If not treated properly, TB can be deadly. You can usually cure active TB by taking several medicines for a specific period of time. Unhygienic conditions, over crowding and dark, damp houses with poor aeration expose a person to contracting tuberculosis. Prevention and Treatment - the disease can be prevented by: 1) vaccinating infants with BCG vaccine; 2) isolating infectious patients; 3) improving hygienic condition and housing; 4) using pasteurized and properly boiled milk.
