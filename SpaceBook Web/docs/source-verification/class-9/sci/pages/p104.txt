p Prevention and treatment: The disease can be prevented by "improving hygiene". Food and water should be properly covered. Hands should always be washed properly before eating and after going to toilet. A patient of cholera requires plenty of fluids e.g. ORS (Oral rehydration salt). Antibiotics are used to kill the bacteria. Travelers should always take precautions with food and drinking water, even if vaccinated.
