qp 2 | Briefly describe the composition of the blood. || خون کی ترکیب مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || د وینې ترکیب په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ.
sol See the unit text ## An adult has about 5 litres of blood. Plasma (about 55% by volume) is about 90% water with 7-8% proteins (fibrinogen, antibodies), 0.9% salts, 0.1% glucose and 2-3% digested products. Blood cells (about 45%): RBCs/erythrocytes (about 5 million per mm3, biconcave, no nucleus, haemoglobin carries oxygen, life about 120 days); WBCs/leucocytes (7000-8000 per mm3, nucleated, defend the body, live 3-4 days); platelets (250,000-500,000 per mm3, oval, no nucleus, help clotting). || An adult has about 5 litres of blood.
