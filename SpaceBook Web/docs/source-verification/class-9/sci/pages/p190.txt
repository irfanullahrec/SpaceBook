q 2 | Answer the following short questions. || درج ذیل مختصر سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې لنډې پوښتنې ځواب کړئ.
qp 1 | What would happen if there was no electricity? || اگر بجلی نہ ہو تو کیا ہوگا؟ || که برېښنا نه وي څه به کېږي؟
sol See the unit text ## Lights, fans, appliances, water pumps, communication, hospitals and industries would stop; production and employment would fall and the economy and standard of life would decline (p.175-177).
qp 2 | What is overuse of electricity? || بجلی کا زیادہ استعمال کیا ہے؟ || د برېښنا ډېره کارونه څه ده؟
sol See the unit text ## Using more electricity than needed, e.g. leaving appliances on when not required or using high-consumption appliances; it wastes limited resources and adds to shortages and pollution (p.182).
qp 3 | What are the advantages and disadvantages of using petroleum? || پیٹرولیم کے استعمال کے فوائد و نقصانات کیا ہیں؟ || د پټرولیم د کارونې ګټې او زیانونه څه دي؟
sol See the unit text ## Advantages: liquid and easy to use; used for electricity and to make medicines, plastics and other goods; easy energy by direct combustion. Disadvantages: non-renewable and limited; extraction alters the environment; burning causes air pollution and greenhouse gases; Pakistan must import it (p.158, 161, 176).
qp 4 | Give two examples of ways to conserve energy at home. || گھر میں توانائی بچانے کے دو طریقے بتائیں۔ || په کور کې د انرژۍ ساتلو دوه لارې ووایاست.
sol See the unit text ## Switch off appliances and close gas and water valves when not required; use energy-saving appliances such as tube lights and energy saver bulbs; design homes for sunlight and ventilation (p.182).
