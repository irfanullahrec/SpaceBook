p a. Burning: Burning or combustion reactions are examples of chemical change. These always involve molecular oxygen O2. Anytime anything burns (in the usual sense), it is a combustion reaction. Combustion reactions are almost always exothermic (i.e., they give off heat). For example when wood burns, it must do so in the presence of O2 and a lot of heat is produced. Wood as well as many common items that combust are organic (i.e., they are made up of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen).
