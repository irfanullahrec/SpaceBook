## 5.5 Energy Consumption and Conservation || 5.5 توانائی کا استعمال اور تحفظ || 5.5 د انرژۍ مصرف او ساتنه
p After obtaining preliminary information, you would have realized the value and importance of energy in modern society. You may have also noticed that conventional sources of energy are limited. Exploitation of non-conventional energy sources is still in the initial stages of research and development. The fuel that burns in factories and transport vehicles or performs other activities is mainly obtained from underground deposits in the form of coal, oil, natural gas and other similar raw forms.
