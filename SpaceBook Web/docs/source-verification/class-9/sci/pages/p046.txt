p Hydrogen versus Helium in balloons: Although not used much anymore, hydrogen balloons were once quite popular. However, it is highly flammable, so the slightest spark can cause a huge explosion. Hydrogen is explosive and helium is inert. Helium is less dense. Because helium is lighter than air, a helium balloon rises.
