p In this process heat exchange between system and surroundings may occur. A combustion reaction is an example of a thermal process. In a combustion reaction carbon dioxide (CO2), water (H2O), light and heat are produced. If oxygen supply to the reaction is insufficient, then carbon monoxide (CO) may also be produced. Certain fuels also contain sulphur. When they burn, sulphur dioxide (SO2) is given off. Coal and fuel oil contain up to 3% sulphur and petrol about 0.05%. Quite a low concentration of SO2 can damage the leaves of plants and stunt their growth, and it can also make people's respiratory illness much worse. Carbon monoxide (CO) is poisonous and can cause death, even if its concentration is in decimals. High concentration of CO2 also has adverse effects on the environment; it helps in increasing the temperature of our atmosphere due to the greenhouse effect. || ↑ || ↑
