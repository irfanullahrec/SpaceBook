p v. AIDS: Acquired immunodeficiency syndrome (AIDS) is a fatal disease caused by a virus called Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV). AIDS was first described in 1981 and HIV was identified as the causative agent in 1983. The virus enters the body through blood products, shared needles and razors and sexual relationship with a patient or carrier of AIDS. Signs and Symptoms: The virus destroys the defense system of the body by damaging the white blood cells. The immune system, i.e. body defense system, is weakened so much that even a mild infection can cause death. Prevention and treatment: There is no cure for AIDS as yet.
