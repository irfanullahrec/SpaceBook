p Nowadays electric current is obtained from the sun's energy. A device called a solar cell can change the energy of sunlight to electrical energy. Such solar cells are frequently used in calculators, watches etc. Batteries made up of solar cells are used to run radios, televisions and equipment in artificial satellites going around the earth. Solar cookers, solar geysers and solar house heating systems are successfully working by using solar energy. Solar power has an exciting future ahead of it, because solar power utilizes sunlight, a resource that is everywhere. Solar panels can be attached to moving objects, such as automobiles, to power those objects. Solar powered cars are being experimented with these days. || ↑ || ↑
