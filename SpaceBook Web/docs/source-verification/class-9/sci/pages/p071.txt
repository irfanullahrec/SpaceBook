qp 1 | Write in detail the chemical composition and some of the prominent chemical and physical properties of the following material: a. Plastic b. Polythene c. Glass || درج ذیل اشیا کی کیمیائی ترکیب اور نمایاں کیمیائی و طبعی خصوصیات تفصیل سے لکھیں: الف۔ پلاسٹک ب۔ پولیتھین ج۔ شیشہ || د لاندې توکو کیمیاوي ترکیب او مهم کیمیاوي او فزیکي خواص په تفصیل ولیکئ: الف. پلاستیک ب. پولیتین ج. ښیښه
sol See the unit text ## Plastic: a synthetic high-molecular-weight chain molecule (polymer), mostly carbon/organic, sometimes mixed with other ingredients; versatile, light, flexible, moisture resistant, durable, strong, cheap, clear or opaque; chains flow past each other on moderate heating and it decomposes around 200°C (392°F). Polythene: formed from polymers of ethene; tough, white, translucent, waxy thermoplastic that softens on heating; low-density form by high-pressure polymerization of ethene, high-density form at lower pressure with catalysts; does not readily biodegrade. Glass: amorphous (non-crystalline) solid; man-made glass is silica (SiO2) + soda + lime + colouring additives, heated to about 982°C; brittle, transparent, strong, minimally conducting and unreactive with stored materials; soda lowers silica's fusion point; it behaves as a rigid liquid and can be remelted and recycled.
qp 2 | Compare hydrogen with helium. Which element is better to be used in balloons? || ہائیڈروجن اور ہیلیم کا موازنہ کریں۔ غباروں کے لیے کون سا عنصر بہتر ہے؟ || هایډروجن او هیلیم پرتله کړئ. په بالونونو کې کوم عنصر غوره دی؟
sol See the unit text ## Hydrogen: most abundant element, colourless, diatomic, lightest gas, highly flammable and forms explosive mixtures with air. Helium: noble (inert) gas, second most abundant, forms no compounds, atom of 2 protons, 2 neutrons and 2 electrons. Both are lighter than air so balloons rise, and hydrogen is even lighter, but a slight spark can make a hydrogen balloon explode. Helium is therefore better and safer for balloons.
