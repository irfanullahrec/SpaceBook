sol See the unit text ## Plastic: a synthetic high-molecular-weight chain molecule (polymer), mostly carbon/organic, sometimes mixed with other ingredients; versatile, light, flexible, moisture resistant, durable, strong, cheap, clear or opaque; chains flow past each other on moderate heating and it decomposes around 200°C (392°F).
