qp 3 | Write the chemical and physical properties of copper and compare it with aluminum. Justify which element is better suited to be used in wiring. || تانبے کی کیمیائی اور طبعی خصوصیات لکھ کر ایلومینیم سے موازنہ کریں۔ وائرنگ کے لیے کون سا بہتر ہے؟ || د مس کیمیاوي او فزیکي خواص ولیکئ او له المونیم سره یې پرتله کړئ. سیم کشۍ ته کوم غوره دی؟
sol See the unit text ## Copper: malleable (sheets 1/500 inch), ductile, second only to silver in electrical conductivity, excellent heat conductor, corrosion resistant, moderately active, dissolves in alkalis and forms hydrated copper carbonate Cu2(OH)2CO3 in moist air. Aluminum: shiny, silvery, light and strong, protected by an oxide layer, burns in oxygen to Al2O3. For wiring copper is better: 1.6 times the current-carrying capacity, easier to install, resists corrosion, and its full life-cycle cost is lower, whereas aluminum cable is harder to install and more prone to failure.
qp 4 | Give a brief description about different materials which can be used in thermometers. Based on their nature justify which is the most suited material for thermometer. || تھرمامیٹر میں استعمال ہونے والے مختلف مادوں کی مختصر وضاحت کریں اور بتائیں کہ کون سا سب سے موزوں ہے۔ || په تودوخې میچ کې د کارېدونکو بېلابېلو توکو لنډ بیان وکړئ او ثابت کړئ چې کوم تر ټولو مناسب دی.
sol See the unit text ## Water freezes at 0°C and its boiling/freezing points vary with pressure, so it is unsuitable (it is used only as a calibration standard). Alcohol has a low freezing point and expands well in capillary tubes, so it suits low temperatures, but its low boiling point makes high temperatures hard to measure. Mercury has a low freezing point and a high boiling point, so it is ideal for measuring most temperatures, though it is expensive and dangerous. Mercury is therefore the most suited (alcohol for very low temperatures).
qp 5 | Write three examples of chemical changes which we encounter in our daily life. || روزمرہ زندگی میں کیمیائی تبدیلیوں کی تین مثالیں لکھیں۔ || په ورځني ژوند کې د کیمیاوي بدلون درې بېلګې ولیکئ.
sol See the unit text ## 1. Burning: wood and other organic matter combine with O2, giving carbon dioxide, water and heat. 2. Rusting: iron combines with atmospheric oxygen to form iron oxide. 3. Fermentation: yeast or bacteria convert sugar into acid or alcohol in oxygen-free conditions (dough, curds). (Others: respiration, decomposition.)
qp 6 | Describe the harmful impacts of CFCs, pesticides and fertilizers. || سی ایف سی، کیڑے مار ادویات اور کھادوں کے نقصان دہ اثرات بیان کریں۔ || د CFCs، د آفتونو ضد درملو او سرو زیانمن اغېز بیان کړئ.
sol See the unit text ## CFCs released from refrigerators, freezers and air conditioners react with ozone in the upper atmosphere and weaken its protection against UV radiation; more UV causes skin cancer and weakens the immune system and skin defenses. Pesticides: organophosphates and carbamates affect the nervous system; others irritate skin and eyes, may be carcinogens or affect the hormone (endocrine) system. Fertilizers: toxic chemicals enter the food chain and seep into groundwater; nitrates and nitrites cause blue-baby syndrome, and heavy metals (mercury, lead, cadmium, uranium) can damage kidneys, lungs and liver.
qp 7 | Recycling is the need of the day. Different types of waste material like plastic, glass, rubber etc if not recycled can pose serious threats to environment. Elaborate the statement. || ری سائیکلنگ وقت کی ضرورت ہے۔ پلاسٹک، شیشہ، ربڑ وغیرہ اگر ری سائیکل نہ ہوں تو ماحول کے لیے سنگین خطرہ ہیں۔ اس بیان کی وضاحت کریں۔ || ریسایکلینګ د وخت اړتیا ده. پلاستیک، ښیښه، رابړ او نور که ریسایکل نه شي چاپېریال ته جدي ګواښ دي. دا خبره تشریح کړئ.
sol See the unit text ## Earth's resources are being used up too fast; recycling with reduced consumption is our best defence. Unrecycled plastic and polythene do not readily biodegrade and stay in landfills for hundreds of years, harming health and environment; rubber waste (tyres) is hard to dispose of. Recycling saves raw materials and energy and cuts CO2: glass is 100% recyclable without loss of quality; recycled copper uses only 15% of the energy of mining; aluminum cans return to shelves in about 60 days; recovered rubber costs about half and saves petroleum; recycled iron is cheaper and less damaging than extraction from ores; plastic can be remade into pellets and new bottles.
