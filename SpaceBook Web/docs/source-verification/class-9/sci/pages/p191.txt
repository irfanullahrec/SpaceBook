qp 5 | What is a conventional source of energy? || روایتی ذریعۂ توانائی کیا ہے؟ || دودیزه انرژي سرچینه څه ده؟
sol See the unit text ## A fully developed source in established use, e.g. fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas) and nuclear power; they have limited supply and cause pollution (p.158).
qp 6 | What is the importance of conserving energy? || توانائی بچانے کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د انرژۍ ساتلو اهمیت څه دی؟
sol See the unit text ## Conventional sources are limited and decreasing while population pressure grows; conserving energy makes resources last, reduces cost, shortages and environmental damage (p.181-182).
qp 7 | What is mmbtu? || mmbtu کیا ہے؟ || mmbtu څه دی؟
sol See the unit text ## MMBTU = one million British thermal units, the unit used to bill natural gas. Since 1 BTU = 1055 J, 1 MMBTU is about 1.055 x 10^9 J (p.175).
q 3 | Answer the following questions in detail. || درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته مفصل ځواب ورکړئ.
qp 1 | Describe various types of fossil fuels. || رکازی ایندھن کی اقسام بیان کریں۔ || د فوسیلي سون توکو ډولونه بیان کړئ.
sol See the unit text ## Coal: from prehistoric plants decomposed without oxygen and compressed; states lignite, bituminous, anthracite (graphite if pressed further); used for electricity. Petroleum (crude oil): liquid from remains of sea animals buried under silt; used for electricity, medicines, plastics. Natural gas: mainly methane CH4, found with petroleum; used for heating, transported by pipelines, relatively clean-burning (p.159-162).
qp 2 | Write short notes on: a. Coal as fossil fuel b. Petroleum c. Wind as source of energy || مختصر نوٹ لکھیں: الف کوئلہ ب پیٹرولیم ج ہوا بطور ذریعہ || لنډ یادښتونه ولیکئ: الف ډبرسکاره ب پټرولیم ج باد
sol See the unit text ## a. Coal formed about 300 million years ago from ferns and plants decomposed without oxygen and compressed by sand and dirt; more carbon means cleaner burning; lignite < bituminous < anthracite; Thar coal is a huge reserve. b. Petroleum is liquid crude oil formed from sea animals covered by silt and compressed; drilled with rigs; used for fuel, electricity, medicines, plastics. c. Wind has been used since about 3000 BC for sail boats and later windmills; today wind turbines on 100 ft towers generate electricity; fastest-growing clean source; Pakistan uses it for pumping water and electricity (p.159-164).
qp 3 | Write in detail the nature and utility of any two non conventional sources of energy. || دو غیر روایتی ذرائع کی نوعیت اور افادیت تفصیل سے لکھیں۔ || د دوو نا دودیزو سرچینو طبیعت او ګټه په تفصیل ولیکئ.
sol See the unit text ## Solar: sunlight converted by solar thermal heating or photovoltaic cells; used in calculators, watches, satellites, solar cookers, geysers, house heating and experimental cars; available everywhere and clean. Wind: moving air turns turbines on tall towers to generate electricity; clean, fastest-growing; used for pumping water and electricity in Pakistan. Both are renewable and clean but limited by weather and cost (p.164-169).
qp 4 | Compare renewable energy sources with non-renewable sources. || قابلِ تجدید اور ناقابلِ تجدید ذرائع کا موازنہ کریں۔ || نوې او نه نوې کېدونکې سرچینې پرتله کړئ.
sol See the unit text ## Renewable (solar, wind, hydro, biomass, geothermal): virtually inexhaustible, environmentally clean, but limited output and costly to build. Non-renewable (coal, petroleum, natural gas, nuclear fuel): limited and will be used up, easy direct combustion and round-the-clock output, but cause pollution and greenhouse gases (p.157-164).
qp 5 | Differentiate between conventional energy sources and non-conventional energy sources. || روایتی اور غیر روایتی ذرائع میں فرق کریں۔ || د دودیزو او نا دودیزو سرچینو توپیر وکړئ.
sol See the unit text ## Conventional: fully developed (fossil fuels, nuclear), established technology, round-the-clock output, limited supply, polluting. Non-conventional: renewable (solar, hydropower, wind) still being developed, much smaller environmental impact, still expensive and dependent on conditions like sunny or windy days (p.158).
qp 6 | Briefly write the process of formation of fossil fuels. || رکازی ایندھن بننے کا عمل مختصراً لکھیں۔ || د فوسیلي سون توکو د جوړېدو بهیر لنډ ولیکئ.
sol See the unit text ## Plants (coal) died in swamps, were covered with water and decomposed without oxygen, losing hydrogen and leaving carbon-rich material that sand and dirt compressed into coal. Sea animals (petroleum) died and were covered by silt on the ocean floor; pressure of the layers compressed them into oil; natural gas forms with petroleum (p.159-162).
qp 7 | Describe the role of energy in the development of a country. || ملک کی ترقی میں توانائی کا کردار بیان کریں۔ || د هېواد په پرمختګ کې د انرژۍ رول بیان کړئ.
sol See the unit text ## Development is judged by energy consumption; industries need uninterrupted energy, which creates jobs and economic activity and raises living standards; agriculture, health, education and industry all need energy, so access to energy is a prerequisite for sustainable development. Pakistan relies on imported oil and gas, faces severe shortages harming industry and livelihoods, and leaves wind, solar and biomass untapped (p.175-177).
qp 8 | How critical is hydel power generation for Pakistan? Discuss. || پاکستان کے لیے پن بجلی کتنی اہم ہے؟ بحث کریں۔ || پاکستان ته اوبیزه برېښنا څومره مهمه ده؟ بحث وکړئ.
sol See the unit text ## Hydel is the cheapest source and gives about 30% of generation; Pakistan has huge unexploited potential mainly on the Indus; Tarbela, Mangla and Warsak and Ghazi Barotha (1450 MW) provide electricity and irrigation. Since oil must be imported and shortages are severe, developing hydel power with proper planning is critical (p.165-166, 176-177).
qp 9 | Nuclear power is regarded as one of the strategies for fulfilling the gaps of power shortages. How has Pakistan been able to use nuclear technology successfully, and what hazards are associated with the use of nuclear energy? || جوہری توانائی بجلی کی کمی پوری کرنے کی حکمتِ عملی ہے۔ پاکستان نے اسے کیسے کامیابی سے استعمال کیا اور اس کے کیا خطرات ہیں؟ || اټومي ځواک د برېښنا کمښت د ډکولو یوه ستراتیژي ده. پاکستان څنګه بریالی کارولی او خطرونه یې څه دي؟
sol See the unit text ## Pakistan runs KANUPP (137 MW, Paradise Point near Karachi) and CHASNUPP-1 (325 MW, near Chashma Barrage, started 1992 with Chinese help); nuclear gives about 3% of generation. Hazards: alpha, beta and gamma radiation penetrates matter, uncontrolled reactions damage whole areas, radiation causes cancer and birth defects; accidents at Three Mile Island, Chernobyl and Fukushima. Remedies: careful operation and waste disposal, cooling, physical barriers and reactor designs that allow for operator error and equipment failure (p.169-181).
