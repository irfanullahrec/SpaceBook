p a. Why quit smoking? Smoking is regarded as a first step before someone gets into some serious drug addiction. Although the harms related to smoking are not seriously considered and very easily ignored, especially among teenagers who consider it to be one of the fashionable things to be proud of, in fact it is the first step towards self destruction.
