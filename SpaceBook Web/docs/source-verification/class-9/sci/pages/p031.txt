sol See the unit text ## Science is pure (explains phenomena) or applied (puts them to use). Pure science: physical sciences (physics - time, light, gravity; chemistry - properties, composition, structure and reactions of matter; astronomy - universe beyond earth), earth sciences (geology, oceanography, meteorology, paleontology) and life sciences/biology (botany, genetics, zoology, medicine); mathematical science includes arithmetic, algebra, geometry and calculus. The branches overlap: light (physics) drives photosynthesis (chemistry) in plants (biology).
