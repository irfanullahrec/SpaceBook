sol See the unit text ## Crude birth rate = live births in a year / total population x 1,000 (births per thousand people). qp 5 | What is the significance of carrying capacity of an area? || کسی علاقے کی گنجائش کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د یوې سیمې د ظرفیت اهمیت څه دی؟
sol See the unit text ## Carrying capacity is the maximum number of people (or animals) an area can support with its resources; e.g. a goat needing 50 sq yards of grass means 200 sq yards supports 4 goats. For humans it is more complex because technology, imported resources and infrastructure can raise it. When it is exceeded people leave, health and reproduction decline, pressure can lead to war, and unsanitary crowding causes disease outbreaks and environmental degradation; overgrazing beyond a grassland's capacity causes desertification. || Carrying capacity is the maximum number of people (or animals) an area can support with its resources; e.g. a goat needing 50 sq yards of grass means 200 sq yards supports 4 goats.
