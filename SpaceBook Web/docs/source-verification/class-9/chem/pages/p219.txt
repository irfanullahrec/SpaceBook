qsolution Corrosion can be prevented using the following six methods:
ss 1. Removal of stains, dents, and surface impurities.
ss 2. Application of paints, varnishes, and enamel coatings containing stabilizers.
ss 3. Alloying (e.g. producing Stainless Steel from Fe, Cr, and Ni).

q i | a. What is electroplating? b. Distinguish between the nature of the anode and cathode in such a process. || a. برقی ملمع کاری کیا ہے؟ b. اس میں اینوڈ اور کیتھوڈ کی نوعیت کا فرق بتائیں۔ || a. الکتروپلېټینګ څه دی؟ b. په دې بهیر کې د انود او کاتود د ماهیت توپیر بیان کړئ.
qsolution a. Definition of Electroplating:
ss Electroplating is an electrolytic process in which a thin, uniform, adherent coating of a superior or corrosion-resistant metal (such as Tin, Zinc, Nickel, Chromium, Gold, or Silver) is deposited on the surface of an inferior metallic object by the action of an electric current in an electrolytic cell.
ss b. Nature and Role of Electrodes in Electroplating:
ss 1. The Anode (+ Terminal):
ss • Material: Made of a pure sheet, plate, or rod of the coating metal (the metal to be deposited, e.g. pure Tin, pure Zinc, pure Chromium, or pure Silver).
ss • Function & Reaction: Connected to the positive terminal of the DC battery. During electrolysis, the anode dissolves continuously by undergoing oxidation, converting neutral metal atoms into positive metal cations that enter the electrolyte solution:
ss M(s) ──> M^n+(aq) + n e-
ss • Mass Change: The anode gradually loses mass over time.
ss 2. The Cathode (- Terminal):
ss • Material: Made of the thoroughly cleaned base metal object on which electroplating is desired (e.g. steel bumper, iron cutlery, brass trinket).
ss • Function & Reaction: Connected to the negative terminal of the DC battery. Metallic cations from the solution migrate to this electrode, gain electrons, and are reduced to solid metal atoms that deposit as a smooth, uniform protective layer:
ss M^n+(aq) + n e- ──> M(s) (Deposited on object)
ss • Mass Change: The cathode gains mass over time.
endsolution
q ii | Differentiate between the processes of oxidation and reduction. Write an equation to illustrate each. || تکسید اور تخفیف میں فرق کریں۔ ہر ایک کی وضاحت کے لیے مساوات لکھیں۔ || د اکسیدېشن او ریډکشن د بهیرونو توپیر بیان کړئ. د هر یوه لپاره مساوات ولیکئ.
qsolution Comprehensive Comparison between Oxidation and Reduction:
ss 1. In terms of Oxygen:
ss • Oxidation: Addition of Oxygen to a substance.
ss Equation: 2Mg(s) + O2(g) ──> 2MgO(s)
ss • Reduction: Removal of Oxygen from a substance.
ss Equation: CuO(s) + H2(g) ──> Cu(s) + H2O(l)
ss 2. In terms of Hydrogen:
ss • Oxidation: Removal of Hydrogen from a substance.
ss Equation: H2S(g) + Cl2(g) ──> 2HCl(g) + S(s)
ss • Reduction: Addition of Hydrogen to a substance.
ss Equation: N2(g) + 3H2(g) ──> 2NH3(g)
ss 3. In terms of Electrons (Electronic Concept):
ss • Oxidation: Loss of one or more electrons by an atom, ion, or molecule.
ss Equation: Fe2+(aq) ──> Fe3+(aq) + e- or Na(s) ──> Na+(aq) + e-
ss • Reduction: Gain of one or more electrons by an atom, ion, or molecule.
ss Equation: Cl2(g) + 2e- ──> 2Cl-(aq) or Cu2+(aq) + 2e- ──> Cu(s)
ss 4. In terms of Oxidation State:
ss • Oxidation: Increase in oxidation number (e.g. from 0 to +2).
ss • Reduction: Decrease in oxidation number (e.g. from +2 to 0).
endsolution
q iii | a. What is corrosion? Explain rusting of iron as an example of corrosion. b. Differentiate between electrolytic cell and voltaic cell. c. Discuss the method of recovering/extracting of metal from its ore. || a. خوردگی کیا ہے؟ لوہے کے زنگ کو اس کی مثال کے طور پر سمجھائیں۔ b. برق پاشی اور وولٹائی سیل کا فرق بتائیں۔ c. کچ دھات سے دھات حاصل کرنے کا طریقہ بیان کریں۔ || a. خوردګي څه ده؟ د اوسپنې زنګ وهل یې د بېلګې په توګه تشریح کړئ. b. د الکترولیتیک او ولټایي حجرې توپیر بیان کړئ. c. له کاني څخه د فلز د ترلاسه کولو یا ایستلو لاره بیان کړئ.
qsolution a. Corrosion & Rusting of Iron:
ss Corrosion is the spontaneous, slow electrochemical deterioration of a metal caused by reaction with its surrounding atmosphere (oxygen and moisture). Rusting is the corrosion of iron to form hydrated ferric oxide (Fe2O3·xH2O).
ss • Anodic Region (Stains/Dents): 2Fe(s) ──> 2Fe2+(aq) + 4e-
ss • Cathodic Region (Moist O2): O2(g) + 2H2O(l) + 4e- ──> 4OH-(aq)
ss • Overall Precipitation: 2Fe2+ + 4OH- ──> 2Fe(OH)2 ──(+O2)──> Fe2O3·xH2O (Rust).
ss b. Differentiation: Electrolytic Cell vs Voltaic (Galvanic) Cell:
ss 1. Energy Conversion: Electrolytic converts electrical energy into chemical energy; Voltaic converts chemical energy into electrical energy.
ss 2. Reaction Spontaneity: Electrolytic drives non-spontaneous redox reactions; Voltaic operates via spontaneous redox reactions.
ss 3. Electrodes: Electrolytic (Anode is +, Cathode is -); Voltaic (Anode is -, Cathode is +).
ss 4. Salt Bridge: Electrolytic uses a single container without a salt bridge; Voltaic requires two separate half-cells connected by a salt bridge.
ss c. Recovering/Extracting Metal from Ore (Electrolytic Copper Refining):
ss Impure blister copper cast into thick anodes is suspended in an acidified CuSO4 + H2SO4 electrolyte opposite thin pure copper cathode sheets (at 50°C, 0.3 V). Cu dissolves from the anode as Cu2+ and deposits on the cathode as 99.99% pure copper. Active metal impurities remain in solution, while noble metals (Au, Ag) settle below the anode as valuable Anode Mud.
endsolution
q v | Discuss the preparation of Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH) from Brine along with diagram and reactions at cathode and anode. || برائن سے سوڈیم ہائیڈرو آکسائیڈ (NaOH) کی تیاری خاکے اور اینوڈ و کیتھوڈ کے تعاملات سمیت بیان کریں۔ || له براین څخه د سوډیم هایدروکسایډ (NaOH) جوړول له رسم او د انود او کاتود له تعاملونو سره بیان کړئ.
qsolution 1. Industrial Preparation of NaOH in Nelson's Cell:
ss Sodium hydroxide (caustic soda) is manufactured on a large scale by the electrolysis of concentrated aqueous sodium chloride (Brine) in the Nelson Cell (Fig. 7.10).
ss 2. Construction of Nelson Cell:
ss • Cathode: A U-shaped perforated steel tube lined internally with a porous asbestos diaphragm.
ss • Anode: A graphite/carbon rod suspended in the center of the U-tube.
ss • Electrolyte: Saturated aqueous NaCl (Brine) filling the U-tube.
ss • Catch Basin: Steel vessel placed below the perforated tube to catch dripping NaOH solution.
ss • Steam Inlet: Steam is blown into the cathode compartment to keep the perforations clear and maintain cell temperature.
ss 3. Ionization in Brine Solution:
ss 2NaCl(aq) ──> 2Na+(aq) + 2Cl-(aq)
ss 2H2O(l) ──> 2H+(aq) + 2OH-(aq)
ss 4. Electrode Reactions:
ss • At Anode (Oxidation): Chloride ions discharge preferentially over OH- ions at the graphite anode, releasing chlorine gas:
ss 2Cl-(aq) ──> Cl2(g) + 2e-
ss • At Cathode (Reduction): H+ ions discharge preferentially over Na+ ions at the perforated steel cathode, releasing hydrogen gas:
ss 2H+(aq) + 2e- ──> H2(g)
ss • In Solution: Unreduced Na+ cations and OH- anions accumulate and drip through the porous asbestos into the catch basin as aqueous caustic soda:
ss 2Na+(aq) + 2OH-(aq) ──> 2NaOH(aq)
ss 5. Overall Chemical Equation:
ss 2NaCl(aq) + 2H2O(l) ──> 2NaOH(aq) + H2(g) + Cl2(g)
ss This process yields three commercially vital industrial products: caustic soda (NaOH), chlorine gas (Cl2), and hydrogen gas (H2).
endsolution
