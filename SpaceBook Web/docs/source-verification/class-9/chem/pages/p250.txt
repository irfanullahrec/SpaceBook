q 3 | LONG QUESTIONS. Answer the following questions in detail. || طویل سوالات۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات دیں۔ || اوږدې پوښتنې. لاندې پوښتنو ته په تفصیل ځواب ورکړئ.
q i | Compare and contrast the properties of alkali and alkaline earth metals, with the help of reactions. || تعاملات کی مدد سے الکلی اور الکلائن ارتھ دھاتوں کی خصوصیات کا موازنہ کریں۔ || د تعاملونو په مرسته د القلي او القلي خاورینو فلزاتو ځانګړنې پرتله کړئ.
qsolution Comprehensive Comparison between Group IA (Alkali Metals) and Group IIA (Alkaline Earth Metals):
ss 1. Electronic Configuration & Valency:
ss • Group IA: ns^1 configuration; lose 1 electron to form univalent M+ cations.
ss • Group IIA: ns^2 configuration; lose 2 electrons to form divalent M2+ cations.
ss 2. Ionization Energy & Reactivity:
ss • Group IA: Very low 1st IE (e.g. Na = 496 kJ/mol); most reactive metals, react violently at room temperature.
ss • Group IIA: Higher 1st & 2nd IE (e.g. Mg = 738 and 1451 kJ/mol); less reactive, often require heating.
ss 3. Hardness, Melting & Boiling Points:
ss • Group IA: Very soft (can be cut with a knife), low density (Na floats on water), low melting points (Na mp = 97.6°C).
ss • Group IIA: Harder, denser (Mg = 1.74 g/cm^3), higher melting points (Mg mp = 651°C, Ca mp = 851°C).
ss 4. Reaction with Water:
ss • Group IA: Reacts vigorously/explosively with cold water, floating on surface:
ss 2Na(s) + 2H2O(l) ──> 2NaOH(aq) + H2(g) + Heat
ss • Group IIA: Magnesium reacts very slowly with cold water but rapidly with steam; Calcium reacts steadily with cold water:
ss Mg(s) + H2O(g, steam) ──> MgO(s) + H2(g)
ss Ca(s) + 2H2O(l) ──> Ca(OH)2(aq) + H2(g)
ss 5. Reaction with Oxygen:
ss • Group IA: Burns readily forming basic monoxides, peroxides, or superoxides:
ss 4Na(s) + O2(g) ──> 2Na2O(s)
ss • Group IIA: Burns on heating forming normal basic oxides:
ss 2Mg(s) + O2(g) ──(Heat)──> 2MgO(s) [Dazzling white flame]
ss 2Ca(s) + O2(g) ──(Heat)──> 2CaO(s) [Brick-red flame]
ss 6. Reaction with Halogens:
ss • Group IA: 2Na(s) + Cl2(g) ──> 2NaCl(s) [1:1 ionic halides]
ss • Group IIA: Mg(s) + Cl2(g) ──> MgCl2(s) [1:2 ionic halides]
endsolution
q ii | a. Differentiate between hard and soft metals. b. Give the reaction of magnesium with (i) H₂ (ii) HCl (iii) O₂ (iv) H₂O (v) Cl₂. || الف۔ سخت اور نرم دھاتوں میں فرق کریں۔ ب۔ میگنیشیم کے ساتھ H₂، HCl، O₂، H₂O اور Cl₂ کے تعاملات لکھیں۔ || الف. د سختو او نرمو فلزاتو توپیر وکړئ. ب. له مګنیزیم سره د H₂، HCl، O₂، H₂O او Cl₂ تعاملونه ولیکئ.
qsolution a. Differentiation between Soft and Hard Metals:
ss • Soft Metals: Alkali and alkaline earth metals (Groups IA and IIA). They have low ionization energies, low densities (<2 g/cm^3), low tensile strength, weak metallic bonding due to few valence electrons, and can be cut easily with a knife (e.g. Na, K).
ss • Hard Metals: Transition metals (d-block, e.g. Fe, Cr, Ni, Ti, W). They have high ionization energies, high densities (>7 g/cm^3), strong metallic bonds involving delocalized d-electrons, high tensile strength, and very high melting points (e.g. Iron mp = 1538°C).
ss b. Reactions of Magnesium (Mg):
ss (i) With Hydrogen (H2):
ss Mg(s) + H2(g) ──(Heat)──> MgH2(s) [Magnesium Hydride]
ss (ii) With Hydrochloric Acid (HCl):
ss Mg(s) + 2HCl(aq) ──> MgCl2(aq) + H2(g) [Magnesium Chloride + Hydrogen gas]
ss (iii) With Oxygen (O2):
ss 2Mg(s) + O2(g) ──(Heat)──> 2MgO(s) [Magnesium Oxide, Magnesia]
ss (iv) With Water (H2O, steam):
ss Mg(s) + H2O(g, steam) ──> MgO(s) + H2(g) (or Mg + 2H2O(l, boiling) ──> Mg(OH)2 + H2)
ss (v) With Chlorine (Cl2):
ss Mg(s) + Cl2(g) ──> MgCl2(s) [Magnesium Chloride]
endsolution
q iii | Discuss why some elements exist as free elements in nature while others occur in combined states as compounds. Give two examples of each type. || بیان کریں کہ کچھ عناصر قدرت میں آزاد حالت میں جبکہ دوسرے مرکبات کی صورت میں کیوں ہوتے ہیں۔ ہر قسم کی دو مثالیں دیں۔ || تشریح کړئ چې ځینې عناصر ولې په طبیعت کې آزاد او نور د مرکباتو په ګډ حالت کې وي. د هر ډول دوه بېلګې ورکړئ.
qsolution 1. Scientific Reasons for Free vs Combined Existence:
ss A. Elements Occurring in Combined State:
ss • Causes: Elements that possess very low ionization energies (high electropositivity) or high electron affinities/electronegativity have incomplete valence shells and high chemical reactivity. They spontaneously lose, gain, or share electrons with surrounding oxygen, water, sulphur, or halogens to achieve stable noble gas octets and lower potential energy.
ss • Examples:
ss 1. Sodium (Na): Highly reactive alkali metal; exists as rock salt (NaCl), soda ash (Na2CO3), and Glauber's salt (Na2SO4·10H2O).
ss 2. Calcium (Ca): Highly reactive alkaline earth metal; exists as limestone/marble (CaCO3) and gypsum (CaSO4·2H2O).
ss B. Elements Occurring in Free (Native) State:
ss • Causes: Elements that have completely filled valence electron shells (Noble gases) or very high ionization energies with low electropositivity and positive standard reduction potentials (Noble metals) do not readily react with oxygen, moisture, or environmental acids. They are thermodynamically stable in elemental form.
ss • Examples:
ss 1. Gold (Au): Noble precious metal that resists oxidation, corrosion, and acids, found as pure gold nuggets.
ss 2. Helium / Argon (He, Ar): Noble gases with complete duplets/octets, existing as uncombined monoatomic atmospheric gases.
endsolution
q iv | Define metal and non-metal and compare the physical and chemical properties of metals and non-metals. || دھات اور غیر دھات کی تعریف کریں اور ان کی طبعی و کیمیائی خصوصیات کا موازنہ کریں۔ || فلز او غیر فلز تعریف کړئ او فزیکي او کیمیاوي ځانګړنې یې پرتله کړئ.
qsolution 1. Definitions:
ss • Metal: An electropositive element that readily loses valence electrons to form cations, forms basic oxides, and is a lustrous conductor of heat and electricity.
ss • Non-metal: An electronegative element that gains electrons to form anions, forms acidic oxides, and is a non-conductor/insulator.
ss 2. Comprehensive Comparison Table:
ss A. Physical Properties:
ss • Appearance & Luster: Metals have shiny metallic luster; Non-metals are dull (except shiny iodine and diamond).
ss • Malleability & Ductility: Metals are malleable (sheets) and ductile (wires); Non-metals are brittle when solid, non-malleable, and non-ductile.
ss • Electrical & Thermal Conductivity: Metals are excellent conductors (mobile delocalized electrons); Non-metals are poor conductors / insulators (except graphite).
ss • Physical State at STP: Metals are all solids (except liquid Hg); Non-metals are gases (11), liquid (Br2), or solids (C, S, P, I2).
ss • Melting & Boiling Points: Metals generally have high mp/bp; Non-metals generally have lower mp/bp (except carbon diamond/graphite).
ss • Density: Metals have high densities; Non-metals have low densities.
ss B. Chemical Properties:
ss • Electron Behavior: Metals are electron donors (Reducing agents); Non-metals are electron acceptors (Oxidizing agents).
ss • Nature of Oxides: Metals form basic oxides (e.g. Na2O, CaO) which form alkalies in water; Non-metals form acidic oxides (e.g. SO2, CO2) which form acids in water.
ss • Reaction with Dilute Acids: Metals displace hydrogen gas from acids (Zn + 2HCl ──> ZnCl2 + H2); Non-metals do not react with dilute non-oxidizing acids.
ss • Nature of Chlorides: Metals form electrovalent ionic chlorides (NaCl); Non-metals form covalent chlorides (PCl3, CCl4).
ss • Hydrides: Metals form ionic hydrides (NaH, CaH2); Non-metals form stable covalent hydrides (CH4, NH3, H2O).
endsolution
q v | Halogens are very reactive elements; write down their reactions with hydrogen, oxygen, metals, non-metals and other compounds. || ہیلوجنز بہت فعال عناصر ہیں؛ ہائیڈروجن، آکسیجن، دھاتوں، غیر دھاتوں اور دیگر مرکبات کے ساتھ ان کے تعاملات لکھیں۔ || هالوجنونه ډېر فعال دي؛ له هایدروجن، اکسیجن، فلزاتو، غیر فلزاتو او نورو مرکباتو سره یې تعاملونه ولیکئ.
qsolution Comprehensive Chemical Reactivity of Halogens (Group VIIA):
ss 1. Reactions with Hydrogen (H2 ──> HX):
ss • With Fluorine: H2(g) + F2(g) ──(Dark, -200°C, Explosive)──> 2HF(l)
ss • With Chlorine: H2(g) + Cl2(g) ──(Diffused Sunlight)──> 2HCl(g)
ss • With Bromine: H2(g) + Br2(g) ──(Heat, 200°C)──> 2HBr(g)
ss • With Iodine: H2(g) + I2(s) ⇌(Heat, 400°C, Reversible) 2HI(g)
ss 2. Reactions with Oxygen (Oxide Formation):
ss • Fluorine forms oxygen difluoride: 2F2(g) + O2(g) ──> 2OF2(g) and F2 + O2 ──> O2F2
ss • Chlorine forms dichlorine heptoxide: 2Cl2 + 7O2 ──> 2Cl2O7
ss • Bromine and Iodine form BrO2 and I2O5.
ss 3. Reactions with Metals (Ionic Halide Formation):
ss • With Copper: Cu(s) + Br2(l) ──> CuBr2(s)
ss • With Potassium: 2K(s) + I2(s) ──> 2KI(s)
ss • With Sodium: 2Na(s) + Cl2(g) ──> 2NaCl(s)
ss • With Magnesium: Mg(s) + Cl2(g) ──> MgCl2(s)
ss 4. Reactions with Non-Metals (Phosphorus Halides):
ss • Formation of Trihalides: 2P(s) + 3Cl2(g) ──> 2PCl3(l) ; 2P(s) + 3Br2(l) ──> 2PBr3(l)
ss • Formation of Pentahalides: 2P(s) + 5Cl2(g) ──> 2PCl5(s) ; 2P(s) + 5Br2(l) ──> 2PBr5(s)
ss 5. Reactions with Compounds & Oxidizing Action:
ss • Oxidation of Hydrogen Sulphide: H2S(aq) + Cl2(g) ──> 2HCl(aq) + S(s)
ss • Oxidation of Ammonia: 2NH3(g) + 3Cl2(g) ──> 6HCl(aq) + N2(g)
ss • Reaction with Slaked Lime (Bleaching Powder): Ca(OH)2(aq) + Cl2(g) ──> CaOCl2(s) + H2O(l)
ss 6. Displacement Reactions (Order: F2 > Cl2 > Br2 > I2):
ss • 2NaBr(aq) + Cl2(g) ──> 2NaCl(aq) + Br2(l)
ss • 2KI(aq) + Cl2(g) ──> 2KCl(aq) + I2(s)
ss • 2KI(aq) + Br2(l) ──> 2KBr(aq) + I2(s)
endsolution
