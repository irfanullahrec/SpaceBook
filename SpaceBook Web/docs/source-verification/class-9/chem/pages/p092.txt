q 2 | SHORT QUESTIONS. Answer briefly the following questions. || مختصر سوالات۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات دیں۔ || لنډې پوښتنې. لاندې پوښتنو ته لنډ ځوابونه ورکړئ.
q i | Which element of group IA is not an alkali metal and why? || گروہ IA کا کون سا عنصر الکلی دھات نہیں ہے اور کیوں؟ || د IA ډلې کوم عنصر القلي فلز نه دی او ولې؟
qsolution Hydrogen (H) is the element in Group IA that is NOT an alkali metal.
ss Reasons:
ss 1. Non-Metallic Nature: Hydrogen is a diatomic non-metal gas (H2), whereas all other Group IA elements (Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs, Fr) are soft, highly reactive solid metals.
ss 2. Ionization Energy: Hydrogen has a very high ionization energy (1312 kJ/mol) compared to alkali metals (e.g. Li = 520 kJ/mol).
ss 3. Covalent Bonding: Hydrogen predominantly forms covalent bonds (e.g. H2O, CH4) and can also gain an electron to form a hydride ion (H-), behaving like a halogen.
endsolution
q ii | Place the following elements in order of increasing ionization energy: Na, S, Mg and Ar. || درج ذیل عناصر کو بڑھتی ہوئی آئن سازی کی توانائی کے مطابق ترتیب دیں: Na، S، Mg اور Ar۔ || لاندې عناصر د زیاتېدونکې ایون کېدو انرژۍ له مخې ترتیب کړئ: Na، S، Mg او Ar.
qsolution All four elements belong to Period 3: Na (Z=11), Mg (Z=12), S (Z=16), Ar (Z=18).
ss Across a period from left to right, ionization energy generally increases due to increasing nuclear charge and decreasing atomic radius.
ss • Sodium (Na): 496 kJ/mol
ss • Magnesium (Mg): 738 kJ/mol
ss • Sulphur (S): 1000 kJ/mol
ss • Argon (Ar): 1521 kJ/mol
ss Order of Increasing Ionization Energy:
ss Na < Mg < S < Ar
endsolution

ss • (b) Neon (Ne): Group VIIIA (Group 18) — Noble Gases Family.
ss • (c) Beryllium (Be): Group IIA (Group 2) — Alkaline Earth Metals Family.

q iv | Which element is the most electronegative among C, N, O, Br and S? Which group does it belongs to? || C، N، O، Br اور S میں سب سے زیادہ برق منفی عنصر کون سا ہے؟ یہ کس گروہ سے تعلق رکھتا ہے؟ || په C، N، O، Br او S کې تر ټولو ډېر الکترونګاتیف عنصر کوم دی؟ په کومه ډله پورې اړه لري؟
qsolution Oxygen (O) is the most electronegative element among C (2.5), N (3.0), S (2.5), Br (2.8), and O (3.5).
ss • Electronegativity value of Oxygen = 3.5 on the Pauling scale (second only to Fluorine).
ss • Group: Oxygen belongs to Group VIA (Group 16) — the Oxygen Family (Chalcogens).
endsolution
q v | How do the first ionization energies of representative elements vary across a period and down a group? || نمائندہ عناصر کی پہلی آئن سازی کی توانائیاں ایک دور میں اور ایک گروہ میں نیچے جانے پر کیسے بدلتی ہیں؟ || د استازو عناصرو د لومړي ایون کېدو انرژۍ په یوه دوره کې او په ډله کې ښکته څنګه بدلېږي؟
qsolution 1. Across a Period (Left to Right):
ss First ionization energy INCREASES across a period. As atomic number increases, nuclear charge increases while electrons enter the same shell. The atomic radius decreases, binding valence electrons more tightly.
ss 2. Down a Group (Top to Bottom):
ss First ionization energy DECREASES down a group. Successive electron shells are added, increasing atomic radius and inner electron shielding effect, making it much easier to remove the valence electron.
endsolution
q vi | With the help of periodic table, show which element is found in: (a) Period 2, group VII (b) Period 4, group III (c) Period 5, group VI (d) Period 1, group VIII || دوری جدول کی مدد سے بتائیں کہ کس عنصر کا مقام درج ذیل ہے: (a) دور 2، گروہ VII (b) دور 4، گروہ III (c) دور 5، گروہ VI (d) دور 1، گروہ VIII || د دوراني جدول په مرسته وښایئ چې کوم عنصر په لاندې ځای کې دی: (a) ۲ دوره، VII ډله (b) ۴ دوره، III ډله (c) ۵ دوره، VI ډله (d) ۱ دوره، VIII ډله
qsolution • (a) Period 2, Group VIIA: Fluorine (F, Z = 9).
ss • (b) Period 4, Group IIIA / IIIB: Gallium (Ga, Z = 31 in IIIA) / Scandium (Sc, Z = 21 in IIIB).
ss • (c) Period 5, Group VIA / VIB: Tellurium (Te, Z = 52 in VIA) / Molybdenum (Mo, Z = 42 in VIB).
ss • (d) Period 1, Group VIIIA: Helium (He, Z = 2).
endsolution
q vii | How will you differentiate between representative and transition elements? || آپ نمائندہ اور انتقالی عناصر کے درمیان فرق کیسے کریں گے؟ || د استازو او انتقالي عناصرو ترمنځ څنګه توپیر کوئ؟
qsolution 1. Representative Elements (Main Group Elements):
ss • Belong to s-block and p-block (Groups IA to VIIIA).
ss • Have their outermost s or p subshells incompletely filled or filled.
ss • Show fixed valencies and regular periodic trends.
ss 2. Transition Elements:
ss • Belong to d-block (Groups IIIB to IIB) and f-block (inner transition).
ss • Have incompletely filled d or f inner subshells.
ss • Show variable oxidation states/valencies, form colored compounds, and act as industrial catalysts.
endsolution
q viii | Make a general sketch of the periodic table showing s, p, d and f-block elements (without showing the symbols of elements). || دوری جدول کا عمومی خاکہ بنائیں جس میں s، p، d اور f بلاک کے عناصر دکھائے گئے ہوں (عناصر کی علامات دکھائے بغیر)۔ || د دوراني جدول عمومي نقشه جوړه کړئ چې د s، p، d او f بلاکونو عناصر وښيي (د عناصرو د سمبولونو له ښودلو پرته).
qsolution General Block Demarcation:
ss • Left side (2 columns): s-block (Groups IA & IIA)
ss • Middle region (10 columns): d-block (Transition metals, Periods 4–7)
ss • Right side (6 columns): p-block (Groups IIIA to VIIIA)
ss • Bottom 2 rows (14 columns each): f-block (Lanthanides 4f and Actinides 5f).
endsolution
q ix | Why the s-block elements have two groups only? || s بلاک کے عناصر میں صرف دو گروہ کیوں ہیں؟ || ولې د s بلاک عناصر یوازې دوه ډلې لري؟
qsolution An 's' subshell consists of only ONE spherical orbital. According to Pauli's exclusion principle, a single orbital can hold a MAXIMUM of TWO electrons with opposite spins.
ss • Elements with 1 valence s-electron (ns1) form Group IA (Alkali metals).
ss • Elements with 2 valence s-electrons (ns2) form Group IIA (Alkaline earth metals).
ss Since no more than 2 electrons can fit into the s-subshell, the s-block contains only two groups.
endsolution
q x | What type of element is Sulphur (S), a representative element, a transition element or lanthinide element? || سلفر (S) کس قسم کا عنصر ہے: نمائندہ، انتقالی یا لینتھانائیڈ عنصر؟ || سلفر (S) څه ډول عنصر دی: استازی، انتقالي که لانتانایډ عنصر؟
qsolution Sulphur (S, Z = 16) is a REPRESENTATIVE ELEMENT.
ss Reason:
ss Its electronic configuration is 1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^4. The outermost electrons are filling the 3p subshell, placing it in the p-block (Group VIA / 16, Period 3). All p-block elements are representative elements.
endsolution
