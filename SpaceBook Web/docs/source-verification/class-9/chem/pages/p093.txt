q i | How modern periodic table is different from the Mendeleev periodic table? || جدید دوری جدول مینڈلیف کے دوری جدول سے کیسے مختلف ہے؟ || عصري دوراني جدول د مینډلیف له دوراني جدول څخه څنګه توپیر لري؟
qsolution Comparison between Mendeleev's and Modern Periodic Table:
ss 1. Basis of Arrangement:
ss • Mendeleev's Table (1869): Elements were arranged in increasing order of their Atomic Masses (Atomic Weights).
ss • Modern Table (Moseley, 1913): Elements are arranged in increasing order of their Atomic Numbers (Z).
ss 2. Periodic Law:
ss • Mendeleev: Properties are periodic functions of atomic masses.
ss • Modern: Properties are periodic functions of atomic numbers.
ss 3. Position of Isotopes:
ss • Mendeleev: Could not explain where isotopes should be placed (since isotopes have different atomic masses but same chemical properties).
ss • Modern: All isotopes of an element have the identical atomic number, so they occupy the same position naturally.
ss 4. Anomalous Pairs (Irregular Mass Orders):
ss • Mendeleev: Certain elements with higher atomic masses had to be placed before lower mass elements to maintain chemical groupings (e.g. Ar-40 before K-39, Te-128 before I-127, Co-59 before Ni-58.7).
ss • Modern: When arranged by atomic number (Ar=18, K=19; Co=27, Ni=28; Te=52, I=53), all mass anomalies vanish completely.
ss 5. Electronic Structure & Subshell Blocks:
ss • Mendeleev: Had no connection to electronic configurations.
ss • Modern: Clearly divided into s, p, d, and f blocks reflecting valence electron subshells.
endsolution
q ii | Differentiate between atomic radii and covalent radii. Explain the trends of atomic radius in the periodic table. || ایٹمی اور اشتراکی نصف قطروں کے درمیان فرق کریں۔ دوری جدول میں ایٹمی نصف قطر کے رجحانات کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د اټومي او کووالنټ شعاعو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ. په دوراني جدول کې د اټومي شعاع تمایلونه تشریح کړئ.
qsolution (a) Differentiation:
ss 1. Atomic Radius (r): The distance between the center of the nucleus and the outermost valence shell of an isolated single atom. Expressed in pm or Å.
ss 2. Covalent Radius: Exactly one-half of the distance between the nuclei of two identical atoms bonded together in a homonuclear single covalent bond (r = d / 2, e.g. for Cl2 bond length 199 pm → r = 99.5 or 100 pm).
ss (b) Periodic Trends:
ss 1. Trend Across a Period (Left to Right):
ss Atomic radius DECREASES across a period from alkali metals to halogens.
ss • Reason: Nuclear charge (Z) increases by +1 unit with each successive element while additional electrons enter the same valence shell. The increased positive pull of the nucleus draws the electron cloud closer, shrinking the atomic volume.
ss 2. Trend Down a Group (Top to Bottom):
ss Atomic radius INCREASES down a group.
ss • Reason: An entire new principal electron shell (n) is added with each step down a group. The increased physical distance and the screening effect of inner core electron shells reduce the effective nuclear pull, expanding atomic size.
endsolution
q iii | What is electronegativity? Identify the most and least electronegative groups of elements in the periodic table. Why is fluorine special in terms of electronegativity? || برق منفیّت کیا ہے؟ دوری جدول میں عناصر کے سب سے زیادہ اور سب سے کم برق منفی گروہوں کی شناخت کریں۔ برق منفیّت کے لحاظ سے فلورین خاص کیوں ہے؟ || الکترونګاتیویټي څه ده؟ په دوراني جدول کې د عناصرو تر ټولو ډېر او تر ټولو لږ الکترونګاتیف ډلې وپېژنئ. فلورین د الکترونګاتیویټۍ له مخې ولې ځانګړی دی؟
qsolution 1. Definition of Electronegativity (EN):
ss Electronegativity is the relative tendency or ability of an atom in a molecule to attract the shared pair of electrons towards itself in a covalent bond (measured on the Linus Pauling scale from 0.7 to 4.0).
ss 2. Most and Least Electronegative Groups:
ss • Most Electronegative Group: Group VIIA (Halogens, Group 17). They have small atomic radii, high effective nuclear charge, and only need 1 electron to achieve noble gas stability.
ss • Least Electronegative Group: Group IA (Alkali metals, Group 1). They have large atomic radii, low nuclear attraction, and readily lose electrons.
ss 3. Why Fluorine (F) is Special:
ss • Fluorine has the highest electronegativity value of ALL 118 elements (assigned exactly 4.0 on the Pauling scale).
ss • Reason: Fluorine (Z = 9, 1s2 2s2 2p5) has the smallest atomic radius among all halogen atoms with an extremely high effective nuclear charge acting on its compact valence shell. It attracts shared electron pairs more strongly than any other element, making it an extraordinarily aggressive oxidizing agent.
endsolution
q iv | Define shielding effect and draw its affects upon the ionization energy, electron affinity and electronegativity. || پردہ داری کے اثر کی تعریف کریں اور آئن سازی کی توانائی، الیکٹرانی میلان اور برق منفیّت پر اس کے اثرات کا خاکہ بنائیں۔ || د حایل کېدو اغېز تعریف کړئ او د ایون کېدو پر انرژۍ، الکتروني میلان او الکترونګاتیویټۍ یې اغېزې رسم کړئ.
qsolution 1. Definition of Shielding Effect (Screening Effect):
ss The phenomenon in which inner core electron shells repel the outer valence electrons and screen them from the full positive attractive pull of the nucleus is called the Shielding Effect.
ss 2. Impact of Shielding Effect on Periodic Properties:
ss • On Ionization Energy (IE): Inversely proportional. Greater shielding down a group weakens nuclear attraction on valence electrons, causing Ionization Energy to DECREASE.
ss • On Electron Affinity (EA): Inversely proportional. Greater shielding reduces the nucleus's ability to attract an incoming extra electron, causing Electron Affinity to DECREASE (become less negative).
ss • On Electronegativity (EN): Inversely proportional. Increased shielding screens the positive nucleus from attracting shared bonded electron pairs, causing Electronegativity to DECREASE.
ss 3. Summary Trend Chart (Fig 3.9):
ss • Down a Group: Shielding increases → IE decreases, EA decreases, EN decreases, Atomic radius increases.
ss • Across a Period: Shielding remains constant while Nuclear Charge increases → IE increases, EA increases, EN increases, Atomic radius decreases.
endsolution
q v | Explain the following terms: (a) Periodicity of properties (b) Electron affinity (c) Modern periodic law || درج ذیل اصطلاحات کی وضاحت کریں: (a) خصوصیات کی دوریت (b) الیکٹرانی میلان (c) جدید دوری قانون || لاندې اصطلاحات تشریح کړئ: (a) د ځانګړتیاوو دوراني تکرار (b) الکتروني میلان (c) عصري دوراني قانون
qsolution (a) Periodicity of Properties:
ss The repetition of similar physical and chemical properties of elements at regular atomic number intervals when elements are arranged in increasing order of their atomic numbers in periods and groups.
ss (b) Electron Affinity (EA):
ss The amount of energy released (or absorbed in 2nd EA) when an electron is added to the outermost valence shell of an isolated neutral gaseous atom in its ground state to form a uninegative anion: X(g) + e- → X-(g) + Energy (e.g. Cl + e- → Cl-, EA = -349 kJ/mol).
ss (c) Modern Periodic Law:
ss Formulated by Henry Moseley in 1913, it states: 'The physical and chemical properties of the chemical elements are the periodic functions of their atomic numbers (Z).'
endsolution
