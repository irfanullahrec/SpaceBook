q ii | Cm is the chemical symbol for curium, named after the famous scientist Madam Curie. Why wasn't the symbol C, Cu or Cr used instead? || Cm، کیوریم کی کیمیائی علامت ہے، جس کا نام مشہور سائنس دان میڈم کیوری کے نام پر ہے۔ اس کی جگہ C، Cu یا Cr کی علامت کیوں استعمال نہیں کی گئی؟ || Cm د کیوریم کیمیاوي نښه ده چې د مشهورې ساینسپوهې مادام کیوري په نوم نومول شوی. ولې پر ځای یې C، Cu یا Cr نښه نه ده کارول شوې؟
qsolution According to the rules of chemical nomenclature proposed by Berzelius:
ss 1. The symbol 'C' was already assigned to Carbon.
ss 2. The symbol 'Cu' was already assigned to Copper (from Latin Cuprum).
ss 3. The symbol 'Cr' was already assigned to Chromium.
ss To avoid ambiguity and ensure each element has a unique chemical identity, Curium was assigned the unique symbol 'Cm'.
endsolution
q iii | What is the atomic number of an element? How does it differ from the mass number? || کسی عنصر کا ایٹمی نمبر کیا ہے؟ یہ کمیتی نمبر سے کیسے مختلف ہے؟ || د یوه عنصر اټومي شمېره څه ده؟ د کتلې له شمېرې سره څنګه توپیر لري؟
qsolution 1. Atomic Number (Z): The total number of protons present in the nucleus of an atom of that element. It determines the chemical identity and position of the element in the periodic table.
ss 2. Mass Number (A): The total sum of protons and neutrons (nucleons) present inside the nucleus (A = Z + N).
ss Difference: Atomic number counts only protons (Z), whereas mass number counts both protons and neutrons (A = Z + N). Atomic number is unique and identical for all isotopes of an element, while mass number varies among isotopes.
endsolution

q vi | Calculate the number of moles of butane, C4H10, in 151 g of butane (Atomic masses: C = 12 amu, H = 1 amu). || بوٹین C₄H₁₀ کے 151 g میں مولوں کی تعداد حساب کریں۔ (ایٹمی کمیتیں: C = 12 amu، H = 1 amu)۔ || د بیوټان C₄H₁₀ په151 g کې د مولونو شمېر محاسبه کړئ. (اټومي کتلې: C = 12 amu، H = 1 amu).
qsolution Solution:
ss • Given: Mass of butane (w) = 151 g
ss • Molar Mass of C4H10 (MM) = 4(12) + 10(1) = 48 + 10 = 58 g/mol
ss • Formula: Number of moles (n) = Mass (w) / Molar Mass (MM)
ss • Calculation: n = 151 g / 58 g/mol = 2.603 moles
ss Answer: 2.603 moles of butane (C4H10).
endsolution
q vii | What is the mass of 5 moles of ice? (Atomic masses: H = 1 amu, O = 16 amu) || برف کے 5 مول کی کمیت کیا ہے؟ (ایٹمی کمیتیں: H = 1 amu، O = 16 amu)۔ || د یخ د5 مولونو کتله څومره ده؟ (اټومي کتلې: H = 1 amu، O = 16 amu).
qsolution Solution:
ss • Given: Number of moles of ice (H2O), n = 5 moles
ss • Molar Mass of ice (H2O), MM = 2(1) + 16 = 18 g/mol
ss • Formula: Mass (w) = Number of moles (n) × Molar Mass (MM)
ss • Calculation: w = 5 mol × 18 g/mol = 90 g
ss Answer: 90 g of ice.
endsolution
q viii | Calculate the number of molecules in 6.50 mol of CH4. || CH₄ کے 6.50 مول میں سالموں کی تعداد حساب کریں۔ || د CH₄ په6.50 مولونو کې د مالیکولونو شمېر محاسبه کړئ.
qsolution Solution:
ss • Given: Number of moles of methane (CH4), n = 6.50 mol
ss • Avogadro's Number (NA) = 6.023 × 10^23 molecules/mol

q x | Calculate the mass of 6.68 × 10²³ molecules of PCl₃. || PCl₃ کے 6.68 × 10²³ سالموں کی کمیت حساب کریں۔ || د PCl₃ د6.68 × 10²³ مالیکولونو کتله محاسبه کړئ.
qsolution Solution:
ss • Given: Number of molecules (N) = 6.68 × 10^23 molecules
ss • Avogadro's Number (NA) = 6.023 × 10^23
ss • Molar Mass of PCl3 (MM) = 1(31) + 3(35.5) = 31 + 106.5 = 137.5 g/mol
ss • Step 1: Calculate moles (n) = N / NA = 6.68 × 10^23 / 6.023 × 10^23 = 1.10908 moles
ss • Step 2: Calculate mass (w) = n × MM = 1.10908 mol × 137.5 g/mol = 152.50 g (or 152.524 g)
ss Answer: 152.524 g of PCl3.
endsolution
