q i | What is the molarity of a solution composed of 5.85 g of potassium iodide (KI) dissolved in enough water to make 0.125 dm³ of solution? (Ans: 0.28M) || اتنے پانی میں حل شدہ5.85 g پوٹاشیم آیوڈائیڈ (KI) سے بنے0.125 dm³ محلول کی مولیریٹی کیا ہے؟ (جواب: 0.28M) || د هغه0.125 dm³ محلول مولاریت څومره دی چې5.85 g پوټاشیم آیوډایډ (KI) په کافي اوبو کې حل وي؟ (ځواب: 0.28M)
qsolution Molarity = 0.28 M
ss 1. Calculate moles of KI:
ss Moles = 5.85 g / 166 g/mol = 0.03524 mol
ss 2. Calculate Molarity:
ss M = 0.03524 mol / 0.125 dm^3 = 0.2819 M ≈ 0.28 M
endsolution
q ii | How many moles of H₂SO₄ are present in 0.500 dm³ of 0.150 M H₂SO₄ solution? (Ans: 0.075 moles) || H₂SO₄ کے0.150 M محلول کے0.500 dm³ میں کتنے مول H₂SO₄ ہیں؟ (جواب: 0.075 مول) || دH₂SO₄ د0.150 M محلول په0.500 dm³ کې دH₂SO₄ څو مولونه شته؟ (ځواب: 0.075 مولونه)
qsolution Moles of H2SO4 = 0.075 moles
ss 1. Apply formula:
ss Moles = 0.150 mol/dm^3 × 0.500 dm^3
ss Moles = 0.075 moles
endsolution
q iii | Suppose you wanted to dissolve 40.0 g NaOH in enough H₂O to make 6.00 dm³ of solution. a. What is the molar mass of NaOH? b. What is the molarity of this solution? (Ans: a. 40; b. 0.166 M) || فرض کریں40.0 g NaOH اتنےH₂O میں حل کریں کہ6.00 dm³ محلول بنے۔ a. NaOH کی مولر کمیت کیا ہے؟ b. محلول کی مولیریٹی کیا ہے؟ (جواب: a. 40; b. 0.166 M) || فرض کړئ40.0 g NaOH په دومرهH₂O کې حل کړئ چې6.00 dm³ محلول جوړ کړي. a. دNaOH مولاري کتله څومره ده؟ b. د محلول مولاریت څومره دی؟ (ځواب: a. 40; b. 0.166 M)
qsolution a. Molar mass of NaOH = 40 g/mol b. Molarity = 0.166 M
ss a. Molar mass of NaOH = 23 + 16 + 1 = 40.0 g/mol
ss b. Moles of NaOH = 40.0 g / 40.0 g/mol = 1.0 mol
ss Molarity (M) = 1.0 mol / 6.00 dm^3 = 0.1666... M ≈ 0.166 M

q v | Suppose you want to produce 1.00 dm³ of 3.50 M solution of H₂SO₄. a. Which one is the solute? b. Which one is the solvent? c. How many grams of solute are needed to make this solution? (Ans: a. H₂SO₄; b. H₂O; c. 343 g) || فرض کریںH₂SO₄ کے3.50 M محلول کا1.00 dm³ بنانا چاہتے ہیں۔ a. حل پذیر مادّہ کون سا ہے؟ b. محلّل کون سا ہے؟ c. محلول کے لیے مادّے کے کتنے گرام درکار ہیں؟ (جواب: a. H₂SO₄; b. qsolution a. Solute: H2SO4 b. Solvent: H2O c. Mass of solute = 343 g

q i | Is seawater a solution? How would you prove with a simple experiment whether it is pure water or a solution? || کیا سمندری پانی محلول ہے؟ سادہ تجربے سے کیسے ثابت کریں گے کہ یہ خالص پانی یا محلول ہے؟ || د سمندر اوبه محلول دي؟ په ساده تجربه څنګه ثابتوئ چې خالصې اوبه دي که محلول؟
qsolution Yes, seawater is a true liquid solution containing various dissolved mineral salts (such as NaCl, MgCl2, MgSO4) in water.
ss Experimental Proof:
ss 1. Evaporation / Distillation Test: Take a sample of seawater in an evaporating dish and heat it to dryness over a Bunsen burner.
ss 2. Observation: Water evaporates away, leaving behind a white crystalline residue of solid salts at the bottom of the dish.
ss 3. In contrast, pure water leaves no solid residue upon complete evaporation and boils at a constant 100.0°C at 1 atm, whereas seawater boils at a slightly higher temperature (>100°C).
endsolution
q ii | A bottle in a drug store contains a label “3 percent hydrogen peroxide.” What does it mean? || دوا کی دکان میں بوتل پر “3 فیصد ہائیڈروجن پرآکسائیڈ” لکھا ہے۔ کیا مطلب ہے؟ || د درملو په پلورنځي کې پر بوتل “3 سلنه هایدروجن پراکسایډ” لیکل شوي. معنا یې څه ده؟
qsolution The label '3 percent hydrogen peroxide' indicates the concentration of the antiseptic solution:
ss • On a mass by volume (% m/v) basis: It means there are 3.0 grams of pure hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) solute dissolved in every 100 cm^3 (mL) of aqueous solution.
ss • On a volume by volume (% v/v) basis: It means there are 3.0 cm^3 of liquid H2O2 dissolved per 100 cm^3 of total solution.
endsolution
q iii | Classify the following as a solution, colloid or suspension and explain why: (i) Milk (ii) Hot cup of tea (iii) Orange juice with pulp (iv) Mayonnaise (v) Listerine mouthwash (vi) Milk of Magnesia (vii) Cheese (viii) Mist (ix) Mineral water || درج ذیل کو محلول، کولائیڈ یا معلق آمیزہ میں تقسیم کر کے وجہ بتائیں: (i) دودھ (ii) گرم چائے کا کپ (iii) گودے والا مالٹے کا رس (iv) مایونیز (v) لسٹرین ماؤتھ واش (vi) ملک آف میگنیشیا (vii) پنیر (viii) کہر (ix) معدنی پانی || لاندې په محلول، کولویډ یا تعلیق کې ووېشئ او ولې یې تشریح کړئ: (i) شیدې (ii) د ګرم چای پیاله (iii) له پلپ سره د مالټې جوس (iv) مایونیز (v) د لسټرین خولې مینځونکی (vi) د مګنیزیا شیدې (vii) پنیر (viii) نرۍ لړه (ix) منرالي اوبه
qsolution Classification with Rationale:
ss (i) Milk ── Colloid (Liquid emulsion of butterfat globules and protein dispersed in water; shows Tyndall effect, does not settle).
ss (ii) Hot cup of tea ── Solution (Clear homogeneous liquid solution of dissolved tea tannins and aroma compounds in water).
ss (iii) Orange juice with pulp ── Suspension (Heterogeneous mixture with large, visible pulp particles that settle over time and are retained on filtration).
ss (iv) Mayonnaise ── Colloid (Stable liquid emulsion of oil dispersed in vinegar/water stabilized by egg yolk lecithin).
ss (v) Listerine mouthwash ── Solution (Clear homogeneous aqueous-alcoholic solution of antiseptics, oils, and water).
ss (vi) Milk of Magnesia ── Suspension (Heterogeneous suspension of undissolved magnesium hydroxide Mg(OH)2 particles in water that settle on standing).
ss (vii) Cheese ── Colloid (Solid emulsion containing liquid water droplets dispersed in a solid protein/fat matrix).
ss (viii) Mist ── Colloid (Liquid aerosol consisting of microscopic liquid water droplets dispersed in air).
ss (ix) Mineral water ── Solution (Homogeneous, transparent true solution of dissolved mineral ions in water).
endsolution
