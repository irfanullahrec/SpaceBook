q i | Aluminium is represented as ²⁷₁₃Al. Draw the structures of Aluminium. Write its electronic configuration. || ایلومینیم کو ²⁷₁₃Al سے ظاہر کیا جاتا ہے۔ ایلومینیم کی ساختیں بنائیں۔ اس کی الیکٹرانی ترتیب لکھیں۔ || المونیم په ²⁷₁₃Al ښودل کېږي. د المونیم جوړښتونه رسم کړئ. الکتروني ترتیب یې ولیکئ.
qsolution 1. Composition of Aluminium (27_13 Al):

ss • Shell notation: K = 2, L = 8, M = 3

q ii | The energy of an electron in K and L shells is the same or different. Explain. || K اور L خولوں میں الیکٹران کی توانائی یکساں ہے یا مختلف؟ وضاحت کریں۔ || په K او L قشرونو کې د الکترون انرژي یو شان ده که توپیر لري؟ تشریح یې کړئ.
qsolution The energy of an electron in K and L shells is DIFFERENT.
ss Reason:
ss According to Bohr's 2nd postulate, the energy of an electron in an orbit is directly proportional to its distance from the nucleus.
ss • K-shell (n = 1) is closest to the nucleus and is strongly attracted by nuclear charge, possessing the lowest energy.
ss • L-shell (n = 2) is further away from the nucleus and possesses higher energy than the K-shell.
ss Therefore, an electron in the L-shell has significantly higher energy than an electron in the K-shell (E_L > E_K).
endsolution
q iii | Draw the structures of hydrogen isotopes. || ہائیڈروجن کے ہم جاؤں کی ساختیں بنائیں۔ || د هایدروجن د ایزوټوپونو جوړښتونه رسم کړئ.
qsolution Hydrogen has three isotopes:
ss 1. Protium (^1_1 H): Nucleus has 1 Proton and 0 Neutrons; 1 electron revolving in K-shell.
ss 2. Deuterium (^2_1 H or D): Nucleus has 1 Proton and 1 Neutron; 1 electron revolving in K-shell.
ss 3. Tritium (^3_1 H or T): Nucleus has 1 Proton and 2 Neutrons; 1 electron revolving in K-shell.
endsolution

q v | Why an atom is considered as neutral particle? Give reason(s). || ایٹم کو غیر جانب دار ذرّہ کیوں سمجھا جاتا ہے؟ وجہ یا وجوہ بیان کریں۔ || ولې اټوم بې‌چارج ذره ګڼل کېږي؟ دلیل یا دلیلونه ورکړئ.
qsolution An atom is considered electrically neutral because:
ss 1. It contains an equal number of positively charged protons inside the nucleus and negatively charged electrons revolving in the shells outside the nucleus (Number of Protons = Number of Electrons).
ss 2. The positive electrical charge on one proton (+1.6022 × 10^-19 C) is exactly equal in magnitude to the negative charge on one electron (-1.6022 × 10^-19 C).
ss 3. Neutrons carry zero charge (neutral).
ss Thus, the opposite charges completely balance and cancel each other out, resulting in a net electrical charge of zero.
endsolution
q vi | The mass of an atom is present in its nucleus. Can you explain it? || ایٹم کی کمیت اس کے مرکزے میں موجود ہوتی ہے۔ کیا آپ اس کی وضاحت کر سکتے ہیں؟ || د اټوم کتله د هغه په هسته کې شته. دا تشریح کولای شئ؟
qsolution Yes. An atom consists of three fundamental subatomic particles: protons, neutrons, and electrons.
ss • Mass of a proton = 1.00728 amu (1.6726 × 10^-27 kg)
ss • Mass of a neutron = 1.00867 amu (1.6749 × 10^-27 kg)
ss • Mass of an electron = 0.0005486 amu (9.11 × 10^-31 kg)
ss A proton is ~1837 times heavier, and a neutron is ~1842 times heavier than an electron. The mass of electrons is negligible compared to protons and neutrons. Because all protons and neutrons reside inside the tiny nucleus, more than 99.95% of the total mass of the atom is concentrated in the nucleus.
endsolution
q vii | What is the reason that physical properties of the isotopes are different but their chemical properties are the same? || کیا وجہ ہے کہ ہم جاؤں کی طبعی خصوصیات مختلف لیکن کیمیائی خصوصیات یکساں ہوتی ہیں؟ || ولې د ایزوټوپونو فزیکي ځانګړنې توپیر لري، خو کیمیاوي ځانګړنې یې یو شان دي؟
qsolution 1. Why chemical properties are the SAME:
ss Chemical properties of an element depend entirely on the number of valence electrons and electronic configuration. Since all isotopes of an element have the same atomic number (same number of protons and electrons), they have identical electronic configurations and form identical chemical bonds.
ss 2. Why physical properties are DIFFERENT:
ss Physical properties (such as mass, density, melting point, boiling point, and rate of diffusion) depend on atomic mass. Because isotopes possess different numbers of neutrons, their atomic masses differ, leading to different physical constants (e.g. D2O boils at 101.42 °C while H2O boils at 100.00 °C).
endsolution
