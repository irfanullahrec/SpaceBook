table
tr | Chemistry | The study of the interaction of matter and energy and the changes that matter undergoes.
tr | Compound | A chemical combination of elements that has a definite composition and its own set of properties.
tr | Concentration | The quantity of solute per unit volume of solution or per unit mass of solvent.
tr | Condensation | A change of phase from gaseous to liquid or solid.
tr | Covalent Bond | A bond resulting from electron sharing.
tr | Crystalline Solid | A solid with a regular internal structure of repeating units and a definite melting point.
tr | Dalton’s Atomic Theory | The theory that matter is made up of small particles (atoms) that have properties which are characteristic of an element.
tr | Daniel cell | A cell with zinc and copper electrodes.
tr | Definite composition | The given ratio by mass of each element in a compound to any other element in the compound.
tr | Density | The mass per unit volume of a sample of matter.
tr | Diatomic Molecule | A molecule containing two atoms e.g. H₂.
tr | Direct Proportionality | The relationship in which one variable changes by the same factor as another.
tr | Dissociation | Separation of ions from their close proximity in a solid lattice to a distance when dissolved in a solvent.
tr | Dissolve | To go into solution, making a homogeneous mixture.
tr | Double Bond | The sharing of two pairs of electrons between two atoms.
tr | Electrochemistry | The branch of chemistry dealing with the interaction of chemical reactions and electricity.
tr | Electrode | Electrodes are the conductors i.e. metallic plates, wires or rods, through which electrons enters or leaves the electrolytes in a cell.
tr | Electrolysis | Production of a chemical reaction by means of an electric current.
