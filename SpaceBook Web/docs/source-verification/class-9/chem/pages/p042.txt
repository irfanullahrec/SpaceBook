q i | State and explain with examples: (a) The empirical formula of a compound. (b) The molecular formula of a compound. || مثالوں کے ساتھ بیان کریں اور وضاحت کریں: (a) کسی مرکب کا تجربی فارمولا۔ (b) کسی مرکب کا سالماتی فارمولا۔ || له بېلګو سره بیان او تشریح کړئ: (a) د یوه مرکب تجربوي فورمول. (b) د یوه مرکب مالیکولي فورمول.
qsolution (a) Empirical Formula:
ss 1. Definition: The empirical formula of a chemical compound is the simplest chemical formula that shows the lowest whole-number ratio of atoms of each element present in the compound.
ss 2. Characteristics:
ss • It represents the simplest atomic composition but not necessarily the actual total number of atoms.
ss • For ionic compounds, the empirical formula is identical to the formula unit (e.g. NaCl, CaCl2).
ss 3. Examples:
ss • Benzene: Molecular formula is C6H6. The ratio of C:H is 6:6, which simplifies to 1:1. Hence, empirical formula = CH.
ss • Glucose: Molecular formula is C6H12O6. The ratio of C:H:O is 6:12:6, which simplifies to 1:2:1. Hence, empirical formula = CH2O.
ss • Hydrogen Peroxide: Molecular formula is H2O2. Ratio of H:O is 2:2 → 1:1. Hence, empirical formula = HO.
ss (b) Molecular Formula:
ss 1. Definition: The molecular formula is the chemical formula that expresses the actual number of atoms of each element present in one molecule of a compound.
ss 2. Mathematical Relationship with Empirical Formula:
ss • Molecular Formula = n × (Empirical Formula)
ss • Where 'n' is an integer (n = 1, 2, 3, 4...) calculated as: n = Molar Mass of Compound / Empirical Formula Mass
ss 3. Examples:
ss • Benzene: Empirical formula = CH (mass = 12 + 1 = 13). Molecular mass = 78 amu. n = 78 / 13 = 6. Molecular formula = 6 × (CH) = C6H6.
ss • Glucose: Empirical formula = CH2O (mass = 12 + 2 + 16 = 30). Molecular mass = 180 amu. n = 180 / 30 = 6. Molecular formula = 6 × (CH2O) = C6H12O6.
ss • Compounds with Identical Formulae: For compounds like water (H2O), methane (CH4), and ammonia (NH3), n = 1, so their empirical and molecular formulas are identical.
endsolution
q ii | What do you understand by the terms mole and Avogadro's number? Explain with suitable examples. || مول اور ایووگاڈرو کے عدد کی اصطلاحات سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ مناسب مثالوں کے ساتھ وضاحت کریں۔ || د مول او د اووګاډرو د شمېر له اصطلاحاتو څخه څه پوهېږئ؟ په مناسبو بېلګو یې تشریح کړئ.
qsolution 1. Avogadro's Number (NA):
ss • Definition: Avogadro's number is defined as the exact number of particles (atoms, molecules, ions, or formula units) present in exactly one mole of any pure chemical substance.
ss • Numerical Value: NA = 6.023 × 10^23 particles.
ss • Significance: Since individual atoms and molecules are sub-microscopic and cannot be counted individually, Avogadro's number serves as the fundamental counting bridge connecting macroscopic laboratory masses (grams) to microscopic particle quantities.
ss 2. The Mole Concept (mol):
ss • Qualitative Definition: A mole is the amount of a substance that contains exactly 6.023 × 10^23 representative particles of that substance.
ss • Quantitative Definition: The atomic mass, molecular mass, or formula mass of any chemical substance expressed in grams is termed as one mole (or molar mass).
ss 3. Comprehensive Illustrative Examples:
ss • Gram Atomic Mass: Atomic mass of Carbon = 12 amu → 12.0 g of Carbon = 1 mole of C = 6.023 × 10^23 carbon atoms.
ss • Gram Molecular Mass: Molecular mass of Water (H2O) = 18 amu → 18.0 g of Water = 1 mole of H2O = 6.023 × 10^23 water molecules.
ss • Gram Formula Mass: Formula mass of Sodium Chloride (NaCl) = 58.5 amu → 58.5 g of NaCl = 1 mole of NaCl = 6.023 × 10^23 NaCl formula units (containing 6.023 × 10^23 Na+ ions and 6.023 × 10^23 Cl- ions).
ss 4. Fundamental Stoichiometric Mathematical Relationships:
ss • Number of Moles (n) = Mass in grams (w) / Molar Mass (MM)
ss • Number of Particles (N) = Number of Moles (n) × Avogadro's Number (NA)
endsolution
q iii | (a) Compare and contrast a mixture and a compound. Give examples of each of them. (b) How will you classify molecules? Support your answer with at least two examples of each. || (a) آمیزے اور مرکب کی مماثلتوں اور فرق کا موازنہ کریں۔ دونوں کی مثالیں دیں۔ (b) آپ سالموں کی درجہ بندی کیسے کریں گے؟ ہر قسم کی کم از کم دو مثالوں سے اپنے جواب کی تائید کریں۔ || (a) د مخلوط او مرکب ورته والی او توپیر پرتله کړئ. د هر یوه بېلګې ورکړئ. (b) مالیکولونه به څنګه ډلبندي کړئ؟ د هر ډول لږ تر لږه په دوو بېلګو خپل ځواب روښانه کړئ.
qsolution (a) Comparison between Compound and Mixture:
ss 1. Formation: Compound is formed by chemical combination of constituent elements with bond formation. Mixture is formed by simple physical mixing without chemical bonds.
ss 2. Properties: In a compound, elements lose their original properties and form entirely new substances (e.g. H2 and O2 gases form liquid H2O). In a mixture, components retain their original properties (e.g. sugar dissolved in water retains sweetness).
ss 3. Composition: Compound has a strict, fixed ratio by mass (e.g. H2O is always 2:16 or 1:8 by mass). Mixture has variable composition in any proportion.
ss 4. Separation: Compounds cannot be separated by physical methods; they require chemical decomposition. Mixtures can be separated by physical methods (filtration, distillation, magnet, evaporation).
ss 5. Representation: Compounds have a definite chemical formula (e.g. NaCl, CaCO3). Mixtures have no chemical formula.
ss 6. Melting/Boiling Point: Compounds have sharp, fixed melting and boiling points. Mixtures do not have sharp melting points.
ss Examples: Compounds = Water (H2O), Sodium Chloride (NaCl), Carbon Dioxide (CO2). Mixtures = Air, Brass alloy, Sand in water, Petroleum.
ss (b) Classification of Molecules:
ss 1. Classification Based on Atomicity (Number of Atoms):
ss • Monoatomic molecules: Helium (He), Argon (Ar), Neon (Ne).
ss • Diatomic molecules: Hydrogen (H2), Oxygen (O2), Carbon Monoxide (CO), Hydrogen Chloride (HCl).
ss • Triatomic molecules: Water (H2O), Carbon Dioxide (CO2), Ozone (O3).
ss • Polyatomic molecules: Sulphuric Acid (H2SO4), Methane (CH4), Octasulphur (S8), Glucose (C6H12O6).
ss • Macromolecules: Giant molecules with thousands of atoms e.g. Haemoglobin, DNA, Starch.
ss 2. Classification Based on Nature of Atoms:
ss • Homoatomic (Homonuclear) Molecules: Composed of identical atoms of the same element (e.g. O2, N2, Cl2, P4, S8).
ss • Heteroatomic (Heteronuclear) Molecules: Composed of atoms of different elements chemically bonded (e.g. H2O, NH3, CO2, CH4).
endsolution
q iv | (a) What is the molecular mass of a compound? How will you differentiate it from formula mass? (b) Calculate the molecular mass or formulae mass, as the case may be of the following compounds in amu: (i) Benzene, C6H6 (ii) Ethane gas, C2H6 (iii) Aluminum chloride, AlCl3 (iv) Iron oxide, Fe2O3 || (a) کسی مرکب کی سالماتی کمیت کیا ہے؟ آپ اسے فارمولائی کمیت سے کیسے الگ کریں گے؟ (b) حسبِ صورت درج ذیل مرکبات کی سالماتی کمیت یا فارمولائی کمیت amu میں معلوم کریں: (i) بینزین، C₆H₆ (ii) ایتھین گیس، C₂H₆ (iii) ایلومینیم کلورائیڈ، AlCl₃ (iv) آئرن آکسائیڈ، Fe₂O₃ || (a) د یوه مرکب مالیکولي کتله څه ده؟ له فورمولي کتلې څخه یې څنګه توپیر کوئ؟ (b) د اړتیا له مخې د لاندې مرکبونو مالیکولي یا فورمولي کتله په amu کې محاسبه کړئ: (i) بینزین، C₆H₆ (ii) ایتان ګاز، C₂H₆ (iii) المونیم کلورایډ، AlCl₃ (iv) د اوسپنې اکسایډ، Fe₂O₃
qsolution (a) Molecular Mass vs Formula Mass:
ss 1. Molecular Mass: The sum of the relative atomic masses of all the atoms present in one discrete molecule of a covalent substance (e.g. H2O, CH4, C6H6).
ss 2. Formula Mass: The sum of the atomic masses of all the ions present in one representative formula unit of an ionic crystal lattice (e.g. NaCl, AlCl3, Fe2O3).
ss Difference: Molecular mass applies strictly to molecular/covalent compounds existing as individual discrete molecules. Formula mass applies to ionic compounds that exist as infinite 3D crystalline arrays without separate individual molecules.
ss (b) Step-by-Step Calculations:
ss (i) Benzene (C6H6) — Molecular Mass:
ss • 6 C = 6 × 12.0 amu = 72.0 amu
ss • 6 H = 6 × 1.0 amu = 6.0 amu
ss • Molecular Mass = 72.0 + 6.0 = 78.0 amu
ss (ii) Ethane gas (C2H6) — Molecular Mass:
ss • 2 C = 2 × 12.0 amu = 24.0 amu
ss • 6 H = 6 × 1.0 amu = 6.0 amu
ss • Molecular Mass = 24.0 + 6.0 = 30.0 amu
ss (iii) Aluminum chloride (AlCl3) — Formula Mass:
ss • 1 Al = 1 × 27.0 amu = 27.0 amu
ss • 3 Cl = 3 × 35.5 amu = 106.5 amu
ss • Formula Mass = 27.0 + 106.5 = 133.5 amu
ss (iv) Iron oxide (Fe2O3) — Formula Mass:
ss • 2 Fe = 2 × 56.0 amu = 112.0 amu
ss • 3 O = 3 × 16.0 amu = 48.0 amu
ss • Formula Mass = 112.0 + 48.0 = 160.0 amu
endsolution
