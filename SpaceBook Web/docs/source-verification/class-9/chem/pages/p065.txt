q i | Summarize Rutherford's model of an atom and explain how he developed this model based on the results of his famous gold-foil experiment. || ردرفورڈ کے ایٹم کے ماڈل کا خلاصہ بیان کریں اور وضاحت کریں کہ اس نے اپنے مشہور سونے کے ورق کے تجربے کے نتائج کی بنیاد پر یہ ماڈل کیسے تیار کیا۔ || د رادرفورډ د اټوم ماډل لنډیز بیان کړئ او تشریح کړئ چې د سرو زرو د پاڼې د خپلې مشهورې تجربې د پایلو پر بنسټ یې دا ماډل څنګه جوړ کړ.
qsolution 1. Experimental Setup:
ss In 1911, Lord Rutherford bombarded a thin gold foil (thickness 0.00004 cm) with ~20,000 high-energy alpha particles (^4_2 He^2+) emitted from a radioactive Polonium source. A circular fluorescent Zinc Sulphide (ZnS) screen detected particle deflections.
ss 2. Experimental Observations:
ss • Majority (~19,990 out of 20,000) passed straight through the gold foil with zero deflection.
ss • A small fraction (~8 particles) suffered small-angle deflections.
ss • A very rare fraction (~2 particles) bounced straight back along their original trajectory.
ss 3. Rutherford's Deductions & Atomic Model Postulates:
ss • Central Nucleus: An extremely small, dense, positively charged core called the 'nucleus' is located at the center of the atom.
ss • Empty Volume: Since almost all alpha particles passed undeflected, most of the volume of an atom is empty space.
ss • Planetary Electrons: Negatively charged electrons revolve around the nucleus at high speeds, resembling planets orbiting the sun.
ss • Electrical Neutrality: The total positive charge of nuclear protons exactly equals the total negative charge of orbital electrons.
ss • Mass Concentration: The entire mass and positive charge of the atom are concentrated in the nucleus.
endsolution
q ii | State the postulates which Bohr suggested to overcome the shortcomings of the Rutherford's atomic model. || وہ بنیادی نکات بیان کریں جو بوہر نے ردرفورڈ کے ایٹمی ماڈل کی خامیاں دور کرنے کے لیے پیش کیے۔ || هغه بنسټیز ټکي بیان کړئ چې بوهر د رادرفورډ د اټومي ماډل د نیمګړتیاوو د لرې کولو لپاره وړاندیز کړل.
qsolution In 1913, Neil Bohr formulated his quantum atomic model based on Max Planck's quantum theory of radiation to overcome Rutherford's instability defects:
ss 1. Fixed Quantized Orbits: Electrons revolve around the nucleus only in specific, fixed circular paths called stationary shells or energy levels (n = 1, 2, 3... or K, L, M, N).
ss 2. Energy Proportional to Radius: The energy of an electron in an orbit is directly proportional to its distance from the nucleus. K-shell has minimum energy; energy increases outward.
ss 3. Non-Radiating Stationary States: As long as an electron remains in a particular orbit, it neither radiates nor absorbs energy. Its energy remains strictly constant.
ss 4. Energy Absorption and Emission: Energy is absorbed only when an electron jumps from a lower energy orbit (E1) to a higher energy orbit (E2), and energy is emitted (as a photon) when it falls from a higher orbit (E2) to a lower orbit (E1):
ss ΔE = E2 - E1 = hν (where h = 6.6262 × 10^-34 J·s)
ss 5. Quantized Angular Momentum: An electron can revolve only in those orbits where its orbital angular momentum (mvr) is an integral multiple of h / 2π:
ss mvr = nh / 2π (where n = 1, 2, 3, 4...)
ss 6. Stability Explanation: Because angular momentum and orbits are quantized, electrons cannot spiral continuously into the nucleus, ensuring atomic stability and explaining discrete line spectra.
endsolution

q iv | (a) Define energy level and sub-energy level. (b) Explain the distribution of electrons in various energy levels and sub energy levels for first four elements of the periodic table. || (a) توانائی کی سطح اور ذیلی توانائی کی سطح کی تعریف کریں۔ (b) دوری جدول کے پہلے چار عناصر کے لیے مختلف توانائی کی سطحوں اور ذیلی توانائی کی سطحوں میں الیکٹرانوں کی تقسیم کی وضاحت کریں۔ || (a) د انرژۍ کچه او د انرژۍ فرعي کچه تعریف کړئ. (b) د دوراني جدول د لومړیو څلورو عنصرونو لپاره په بېلابېلو انرژي کچو او فرعي انرژي کچو کې د الکترونونو وېش تشریح کړئ.
qsolution (a) Definitions:
ss 1. Energy Level (Shell): A principal fixed circular path around the nucleus in which electrons revolve with definite quantized energy. Described by principal quantum number 'n' (n = 1, 2, 3, 4...) and lettered K, L, M, N. Maximum electron capacity is 2n^2.
ss 2. Sub-energy Level (Sub-shell): Subdivisions or regional energy sublevels within a principal shell designated as s, p, d, and f (standing for sharp, principal, diffused, fundamental) capable of holding 2, 6, 10, and 14 electrons respectively.
ss (b) Distribution of Electrons for First Four Elements:
ss 1. Hydrogen (H, Z = 1, 1 electron):
ss • Energy Level: K-shell (n = 1) = 1 electron
ss • Sub-energy Level: 1s^1
ss 2. Helium (He, Z = 2, 2 electrons):
ss • Energy Level: K-shell (n = 1) = 2 electrons (duplet complete)
ss • Sub-energy Level: 1s^2
ss 3. Lithium (Li, Z = 3, 3 electrons):
ss • Energy Levels: K-shell = 2 electrons, L-shell = 1 electron
ss • Sub-energy Levels: 1s^2 2s^1
ss 4. Beryllium (Be, Z = 4, 4 electrons):
ss • Energy Levels: K-shell = 2 electrons, L-shell = 2 electrons
ss • Sub-energy Levels: 1s^2 2s^2
endsolution
