p Epoxy is polymer that is formed from two different chemicals. These are referred to as resin and the hardener. Epoxy adhesives are called structural adhesives. These high performance adhesives are used in the construction of aircraft, automobiles, bicycles, boats and golf clubs, where high strength bonds are required. Epoxy adhesives can be developed to suit almost any application. They can be made flexible or rigid, transparent or opaque even colored as well as fast or slow setting. Epoxy adhesives are good heat and chemical resistant. Because of these properties, they are given the name of engineering adhesives. The large family of epoxy resins represents some of the highest performance resins of those available at this time. Epoxies generally out-perform most other resin types in terms of mechanical properties and resistance to environmental degradation, which leads to their almost exclusive use in aircraft components. As a laminating resin their increased adhesive properties and resistance to water degradation make these resins ideal for use in applications such as boat building. Epoxies are widely used as a primary construction material for high-performance boats or as a secondary application to sheath a hull or replace water-degraded polyester resins and gel coats. || ### (i) Applications of Hydrogen Bonding || (i) ہائیڈروجن بند سازی کے استعمال || (i) د هایدروجن اړیکو استعمالونه
p These intermolecular forces are extremely important in determining properties of water, biological molecules such as proteins, DNA etc. and synthetic materials such as glue, paints, resins etc. The adhesive action of paints and dyes is developed due to hydrogen bonding. Synthetic resins also bind two surfaces together by hydrogen bonding or dipole-dipole interactions. || یہ بین سالماتی قوتیں پانی، حیاتیاتی سالموں جیسے پروٹین اور DNA، اور مصنوعی مادّوں جیسے گوند، پینٹ اور ریزن کی خصوصیات طے کرنے میں انتہائی اہم ہیں۔ پینٹ اور رنگوں کی چپکنے کی خاصیت ہائیڈروجن بند سازی سے پیدا ہوتی ہے۔ مصنوعی ریزن بھی ہائیڈروجن بندوں یا دو قطبی تعاملات سے دو سطحوں کو جوڑتے ہیں۔ || دا د مالیکولونو ترمنځ قوې د اوبو، بیولوژیکي مالیکولونو لکه پروټینونو او DNA، او مصنوعي موادو لکه سریښ، رنګونو او رېزینونو د ځانګړتیاوو په ټاکلو کې ډېرې مهمې دي. د رنګونو چسپناک عمل د هایدروجن اړیکو له امله پیدا کېږي. مصنوعي رېزینونه هم دوه سطحې د هایدروجن اړیکو یا ډایپول–ډایپول تعاملونو په وسیله نښلوي.
