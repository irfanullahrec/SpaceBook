q i | Identify at least two groups which contain only metallic elements. || کم از کم دو ایسے گروپوں کی نشان دہی کریں جن میں صرف دھاتی عناصر ہوں۔ || لږ تر لږه دوه داسې ډلې وښایئ چې یوازې فلزي عناصر لري.
qsolution 1. Group IIA (Alkaline Earth Metals): Contains exclusively metals ── Beryllium (Be), Magnesium (Mg), Calcium (Ca), Strontium (Sr), Barium (Ba), and Radium (Ra).
ss 2. Transition Elements (d-block Groups 3 to 12): All elements in the d-block (e.g. Sc, Ti, V, Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Ni, Cu, Zn) as well as the Lanthanides and Actinides (f-block) are 100% metallic elements.
endsolution
q ii | Write down the reaction of group -IA metals with oxygen, with balanced equations. || آکسیجن کے ساتھ گروپ IA دھاتوں کے تعامل کو متوازن مساواتوں کے ساتھ لکھیں۔ || له اکسیجن سره د IA ګروپ د فلزاتو تعاملونه په متوازنو معادلو ولیکئ.
qsolution Alkali metals burn vigorously in oxygen/air to form oxides:
ss 1. Lithium forms normal oxide (monoxide):
ss 4Li(s) + O2(g) ──> 2Li2O(s) [White solid]
ss 2. Sodium forms sodium oxide / peroxide:
ss 4Na(s) + O2(g) ──> 2Na2O(s) (and 2Na + O2 ──> Na2O2)
ss 3. Potassium forms potassium oxide / superoxide:
ss 4K(s) + O2(g) ──> 2K2O(s) (and K + O2 ──> KO2)
ss These oxides dissolve in water to produce strong alkalies (e.g. Na2O + H2O ──> 2NaOH).
endsolution
q iii | State the physical properties of metals. || دھاتوں کی طبعی خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ || د فلزاتو فزیکي ځانګړنې بیان کړئ.
qsolution Key physical properties of metals:
ss 1. Metallic Luster: Freshly cut metal surfaces are shiny and lustrous.
ss 2. Malleability & Ductility: Metals can be hammered into thin sheets (malleable) and drawn into long wires (ductile) without fracturing.
ss 3. Electrical & Thermal Conductivity: Excellent conductors of heat and electricity due to mobile delocalized valence electrons.
ss 4. High Melting & Boiling Points: Generally possess high melting and boiling points due to strong metallic bonds (except alkali metals and Hg).
ss 5. High Density & Rigidity: Heavy and hard solids at room temperature (except liquid Mercury, and soft alkali metals).
endsolution

q v | Ionization energy of alkaline earth metals is higher than alkali metals, why? || الکلائن ارتھ دھاتوں کی آئنائزیشن توانائی الکلی دھاتوں سے زیادہ کیوں ہوتی ہے؟ || د القلي خاورینو فلزاتو آیونایزېشن انرژي ولې تر القلي فلزاتو لوړه ده؟
qsolution Alkaline earth metals (Group IIA) have higher ionization energies than alkali metals (Group IA) in the same period because:
ss 1. Greater Nuclear Charge: Group IIA atoms have an additional proton (+2 nuclear charge), pulling valence electrons more tightly toward the nucleus.
ss 2. Smaller Atomic Radius: The increased nuclear attraction contracts the electron shells, making atomic radii smaller.
ss 3. Stable ns^2 Configuration: The paired s-electrons in Group IIA are more stable and require greater energy to remove than the single ns^1 electron of Group IA.
endsolution

q vii | What are the uses of magnesium? || میگنیشیم کے استعمالات کیا ہیں؟ || د مګنیزیم کارونې کومې دي؟
qsolution Major uses of Magnesium:
ss 1. Alloy Manufacturing: Lightweight, strong alloys like Duralumin (Al + Cu + Mg + Mn) and Magnalium (Al + Mg) for aircraft, car engines, and missile structures.
ss 2. Pyrotechnics: Used in photographic flash powders, military signal flares, and fireworks due to its dazzling white flame.
ss 3. Metallurgy Deoxidizer: Used as a reducing agent in extracting Titanium (Ti) and Uranium (U).
ss 4. Refractory Bricks: Magnesium oxide (MgO) is used to line high-temperature industrial furnaces.
ss 5. Pharmaceuticals: Epsom salt (MgSO4·7H2O) and milk of magnesia [Mg(OH)2] for medicinal uses.
endsolution
