# GLOSSARY || لغت || ګلوسري
table
tr | Absolute temperature | A temperature, measured on the Kelvin scale that must be used for gas law and certain other types of calculations.
tr | Alcohol | An organic compound with the - OH functional group e.g. CH₃OH.
tr | Alkali Metals | Metals in group IA of the periodic table e.g. Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs and Fr.
tr | Allotrope | One of two or more physical forms of an element; for example, diamond and graphite are allotropes of carbon.
tr | Alpha Particle | A helium nucleus generated in a nuclear reaction; a stream of such particles is referred to as an alpha ray.
tr | Analytical Chemistry | The branch of chemistry dealing with the composition of samples.
tr | Angular Molecule | A three-atom molecule in which the atoms do not lie on a line e.g. H₂O molecule.
tr | Anion | A negatively charged ion.
tr | Anode | The electrode where oxidation occurs in an electrochemical reaction.
tr | Aqueous Solution | Any solution in which water is the solvent.
tr | Atmosphere | The envelope of air surrounding the Earth.
tr | Atmospheric Pressure | The pressure of the atmosphere. A unit equal to 760 torr and abbreviated as atm that is the pressure of the atmosphere on a “normal” day at sea level.
tr | Atom | The smallest particle that retains the characteristic composition of an element.
tr | Atomic Mass | The weighted average of the masses of the naturally occurring isotopes of an element, compared with one twelfth of the mass of a ¹²C atom.
tr | Atomic Mass Unit | A mass equal to one twelfth of the mass of a C-12 atom; abbreviated as amu.
tr | Atomic Number | The number of protons in the nucleus of each atom of an element.
endtable
