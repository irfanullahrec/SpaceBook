# Authors Profile || مصنفین کا تعارف || د لیکوالانو پېژندنه
## Dr. Muhammad Ibrahim
p Dr. Muhammad Ibrahim is working in the Department of Elementary and Secondary Education as Senior Subject Specialist in Chemistry, since 2003. He has obtained his Master degree in chemistry from University of Peshawar. He got his Ph.D degree in education from Sarhad University of Science & Information Technology, Peshawar. He has attended a number of seminars and workshops on curriculum and textbook development. He is an author of several articles, which have been published in national and international journals.
## Mushtaq-ur-Rehman
p Mushtaq-ur-Rehman is working as lecturer in Chemistry Department at Govt. College Shabqader, Charsadda. He got his M.Phil Degree in Chemistry from N.C.E in Physical Chemistry from Peshawar University. He has attended numerous seminars and workshops related to education and development of Textbooks.
## Dr. Fida Muhammad
p Dr. Fida Muhammad did Ph.D in physical chemistry from university of California. He worked as Associate Professor in Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute for science and technology Swabi and N.C.E in Physical Chemistry Peshawar University. His numerous research paper has been published. He has extensive research experience in and outside the country. He worked as an advisor in the development of Chemistry curriculum 2006 for grade IX and X.
## Prof. Nawab Khan
p Prof. Nawab Khan worked as Chairman BISE Malakand. He also served as Subject Specialist (Science) in Textbook Board. He has got extensive experience of textbooks writing and editing. He has got his master degree in Chemistry from Peshawar University.
