### Paper Cutter || پیپر کٹر || د کاغذ کټر
p Cutter is a tool with a sharp blade usually used for cutting different kinds of paper and sometimes can be used to sharpen pencils and crayons. Because of its sharp blade, cutter case has been designed in such a way that the blade can be taken out while using. By using it, the blade can be blunt but it does not mean to dispose it. The blade has been marked by several lines which can be seen as pieces. The blunt piece can be cut by a tool which is fixed with the cutter case. After cutting the blade's blunt piece, the cutter will work as new. When the blade finishes, don't dispose the cutter case. ### Sand paper || ریگمال || ریګمال
p Sand paper is a kind of paper having hard rough surface and is used for smothening wooden surfaces. You can use sand paper to smoothen and make your pencil sharp. || ریگمال سخت کھردری سطح والا کاغذ ہے جو لکڑی کی سطح ہموار کرنے میں استعمال ہوتا ہے۔ آپ ریگمال سے پنسل کی نوک ہموار اور تیز کر سکتے ہیں۔ || ریګمال د کلکې زیږې سطحې کاغذ دی چې د لرګیو سطحې هوارولو ته کارېږي. په ریګمال د پنسل څوکه هواره او تېره کولای شئ.
