### Basic technique || بنیادی تکنیک || بنسټیز تخنیک
p Wet and stretch water colour paper is used for water colour before starting. Apply water colour rapidly as it is a quick drying medium. In water colour wash technique is fundamental as shown in figure 1.35 wash is thinned down paint made by adding a small amount of paint to plenty of water, for covering a large area. Light colour can not be applied on dark colour because of its transparency. Minimum colo[ur] should be mixed to avoid losing transparency. So, work from light to dark a[nd] leave the white area and highlight unpainted.
