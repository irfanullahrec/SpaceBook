p The Caliph offered to pay three times the price of the flour, then four times and even five times. Hazrat Usman rejected all these offers. Hazrat Umar got up angrily and said, "Usman, why have you become so hard hearted now, whereas you used to be generous in the days of Hazrat Muhammad (رسول اللہ خاتم النبیین ﷺ)".
p He answered, "I am only doing business and I would sell my goods to the one who would give me the greatest profit." The Caliph raised his offer higher and higher until he was willing to pay nine times the price of the flour as profit.
p Hazrat Usman then stood up calmly and said, "Umar, I have already sold my stock. The buyer is Allah who has promised to pay me at least ten times the price as profit and may raise it to seventy or even seven hundred times. Go and distribute the flour among the people in the name of Allah."
p The Caliph was so touched by the generosity of Hazrat Usman that he wept. He then embraced him and begged forgiveness for the harsh words he had used earlier. Then it was distributed among the people happily and generously.
