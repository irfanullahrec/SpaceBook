# Unit 8 — Hope is the Thing with Feathers || یونٹ 8 — امید پروں والی ایک شے (پرندہ) ہے || 8 څپرکی — امید د بڼکو لرونکی یو شی (مرغۍ) دی
p After reading the unit the students will be able to: || اس یونٹ کو پڑھنے کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || د دې څپرکي له لوستلو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo identify line and stanza.
slo read poem and give orally or in writing: main idea; theme and its development; summary; personal response with justification; paraphrase.
slo use paraphrasing skills to paraphrase stanzas: mark thought groups in the stanza; restate the message in simple prose; replace poetic words with simple ones.
slo read and analyze how the poet uses language to: appeal to the senses through use of figurative language including similes and metaphors; affect meaning through use of synonyms with different connotation and denotation; create imagery.
slo describe the object being personified.
slo deduce meaning of difficult words from context.
slo Illustrate use of prepositions of position, time, movement and direction.
slo Use prepositional phrases and clauses.
## Pre-reading || مطالعے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
p What is hope?
p Hope is the enduring emotional power within the human soul that expects good outcomes and sustains courage through trials.
p Why is hope compared to a bird?
p Because like a bird, hope sings sweet melodies, has wings to rise above difficulties, perches in the soul, and demands nothing in return.
