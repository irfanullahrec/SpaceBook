q 10 | Prepositions A
qp 1 | The princess lives _____ the castle.
sol Solution ## Preposition: in · Solved: The princess lives in the castle. · Rule: Preposition of place/location inside an enclosed structure.
qp 2 | I bought some milk _____ the grocery store.
sol Solution ## Preposition: from · Solved: I bought some milk from the grocery store. · Rule: Preposition of origin/source.
qp 3 | My sister sat _____ my mom _____ the bus.
sol Solution ## Preposition: with / on · Solved: My sister sat with my mom on the bus. · Rule: Preposition of accompaniment ('with') and public transport ('on').
qp 4 | When we got ready for dinner, I had to take my books _____ the table.
sol Solution ## Preposition: from / off · Solved: When we got ready for dinner, I had to take my books from the table. · Rule: Preposition of removal or separation.
qp 5 | We took a trip _____ town last week.
sol Solution ## Preposition: to · Solved: We took a trip to town last week. · Rule: Preposition of direction/destination.
qp 6 | He is _____ home.
sol Solution ## Preposition: at · Solved: He is at home. · Rule: Preposition of specific place/location.
qp 7 | Her apartment is _____ the second floor.
sol Solution ## Preposition: on · Solved: Her apartment is on the second floor. · Rule: Preposition of surface/level.
qp 8 | The boat will take you _____ the river.
sol Solution ## Preposition: across · Solved: The boat will take you across the river. · Rule: Preposition of movement from one side to the other.
qp 9 | The street runs _____ the seafront.
sol Solution ## Preposition: along · Solved: The street runs along the seafront. · Rule: Preposition of parallel movement/direction.
qp 10 | I prefer to ride my bike _____ the hill.
sol Solution ## Preposition: down · Solved: I prefer to ride my bike down the hill. · Rule: Preposition of downward slope movement.
