qp 1 | A. Paragraph on the Patience of Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH)
sol Solution ## Topic Sentence: Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) demonstrated unparalleled patience and sublime forbearance throughout his sacred life. · Content: Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) demonstrated unparalleled patience and sublime forbearance throughout his sacred life. When preaching Islam in Makkah, he endured fierce hostility, cruel boycotts, and bitter persecution from the Quraish with quiet serenity. For three agonizing years in the barren valley of Shab-Abi-Talib, he faced severe starvation and economic embargo without uttering a single curse against his tormentors. Furthermore, at the Conquest of Makkah, he humbly forgave his bitterest enemies and granted general amnesty to all. Indeed, his noble patience conquered hearts and established a timeless moral standard for humanity.

qp 3 | C. Tolerance in Current World Scenario (100+ Words)
sol Solution ## Topic Sentence: In today's interconnected yet divided world, tolerance is essential for peaceful coexistence and global harmony. · Content: In today's interconnected yet divided world, tolerance is essential for peaceful coexistence and global harmony. Modern societies are increasingly diverse, with people of varying cultures, religions, and ideologies living side by side. Without tolerance, misunderstandings quickly escalate into hatred, prejudice, and destructive conflict. Practicing tolerance does not mean compromising one's values; rather, it means respecting the fundamental human rights and dignity of others. Following the example of Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH), we must replace anger with dialogue, empathy, and mutual understanding to build inclusive, stable, and prosperous communities worldwide.
