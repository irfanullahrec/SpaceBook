p Perhaps he was thinking of the thousands who were being attacked and killed on the roads and railways as they tried to reach their homeland. There was much in those days of violence to sadden Jinnah who was a man of very strong feelings although he rarely showed it.
p Yet those who were close to him on Independence Day sometimes saw for a few moments a happier man.
