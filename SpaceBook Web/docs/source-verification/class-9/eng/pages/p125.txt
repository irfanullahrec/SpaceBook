q 6 | Punctuation Mcqs
qp 1 | Choose the correct sentence. (a) a. this is what i ate for breakfast an apple and a bowl of cereal. (b) b. This is what I ate for breakfast, an apple and a bowl of cereal. (c) c. this is what i ate for breakfast: an apple and a bowl of cereal. (d) d. This is what I ate for breakfast: an apple and a bowl of cereal.
sol d. This is what I ate for breakfast: an apple and a bowl of cereal. ## Capitalization of 'This' and 'I' combined with a colon (:) to introduce an explanatory list makes (d) the correct punctuated sentence.
qp 2 | Choose the correct sentence. (a) a. he knocked several times, no one came to the door. (b) b. He knocked several times; no one came to the door. (c) c. he knocked several times: no one came to the door. (d) d. he knocked several times no one came to the door.
sol b. He knocked several times; no one came to the door. ## A semicolon (;) correctly links two independent clauses in a compound sentence without a coordinating conjunction, along with proper capitalization of 'He'.
qp 3 | Choose the correct sentence. (a) a. my sister loves mysteries; my brother likes comics. (b) b. My sister loves mysteries, my brother likes comics. (c) c. my sister loves mysteries my brother likes comics. (d) d. My sister loves mysteries; my brother likes comics.
sol d. My sister loves mysteries; my brother likes comics. ## Capital 'My' and a semicolon separating the two coordinate independent clauses makes (d) correct.
qp 4 | Choose the correct sentence. (a) a. here is what you need to get at the supermarket bananas bread milk and biscuits. (b) b. here is what you need to get at the supermarket: bananas bread milk and biscuits. (c) c. here is what you need to get at the supermarket; bananas, bread, milk and biscuits. (d) d. Here is what you need to get at the supermarket: bananas, bread, milk and biscuits.
sol d. Here is what you need to get at the supermarket: bananas, bread, milk and biscuits. ## A capital 'Here', a colon after the introductory clause, and commas separating items in a series.
qp 5 | Choose the correct sentence. (a) a. the window was open I looked out over the empty fields. (b) b. The window was open, I looked out over the empty fields. (c) c. the window was open: I looked out over the empty fields. (d) d. The window was open; I looked out over the empty fields.
sol d. The window was open; I looked out over the empty fields. ## Semicolon connects two closely related independent clauses; starting with capital 'The'.

p Solved Text: Pasta, a large family of shaped, dried wheat pasta, is a basic staple in many countries. Its origins are obscure. Rice pasta were known very early in China; pasta made of wheat were used in India and Arabia long before they were introduced into Europe in the 11th or 12th century. According to legend, Marco Polo brought a pasta recipe with him from Asia in 1295. Pasta quickly became a major element in the Italian diet, and its use spread throughout Europe. Pasta is made from durum wheat flour, which makes a strong, elastic dough. Hard durum wheat has the highest wheat protein value. The flour is mixed with water, kneaded to form a thick paste, and then forced through perforated plates or dies that shape it into one of more than 100 different forms.
