q 3 | Look up the meaning of the following words in the dictionary and make sentences to clearly bring out the meaning. (difference between 'look' and 'search'; 'buy' and 'purchase'; 'hide' and 'conceal'; 'learn' and 'educate'; 'reward' and 'award')
qp 1 | Difference between 'look' and 'search'
sol Solution ## Explanation: 'Look' means to turn one's eyes in a particular direction to see something visible. 'Search' means to examine a place or person thoroughly to find something hidden or lost. · Sentences: Look: Look at the beautiful rainbow in the sky., Search: The police searched the building thoroughly for the missing keys.
qp 2 | Difference between 'buy' and 'purchase'
sol Solution ## Explanation: 'Buy' is an everyday informal word for acquiring something by paying money. 'Purchase' is a formal term often used in business, commerce, or large investments. · Sentences: Buy: I need to buy some bread and milk from the corner grocery., Purchase: The government finalized a contract to purchase new aircraft.
qp 3 | Difference between 'hide' and 'conceal'
sol Solution ## Explanation: 'Hide' is the general word meaning to put out of sight or stay in a secret place. 'Conceal' is more formal and implies intentional, deliberate hiding of facts, weapons, or emotions. · Sentences: Hide: The children like to hide behind the large oak tree during play., Conceal: He tried hard to conceal his disappointment behind a forced smile.
qp 4 | Difference between 'learn' and 'educate'
sol Solution ## Explanation: 'Learn' means to gain knowledge, comprehension, or skill through study or experience (done by the student). 'Educate' means to provide schooling, instruction, and moral training to someone else (done by teachers/institutions). · Sentences: Learn: Children learn foreign languages very quickly at an early age., Educate: Parents make great sacrifices to educate their children in reputable institutions.
qp 5 | Difference between 'reward' and 'award'
sol Solution ## Explanation: 'Reward' is something given in recognition of hard work, good service, or effort (often informal or financial). 'Award' is a formal prize, certificate, or trophy conferred by a panel or authority after an official decision. · Sentences: Reward: The shopkeeper gave the boy a monetary reward for returning the lost wallet., Award: The young scientist received the prestigious national award for her cancer research.

box activity | Natural Disaster Essay
p A. Write an essay of about 100–150 words on "Natural Disaster".
p Content: Natural disasters are catastrophic events caused by natural forces of the Earth that result in massive loss of human lives, destruction of infrastructure, and ecological damage. Major natural disasters include earthquakes, floods, hurricanes, volcanic eruptions, tsunamis, and droughts.  Pakistan is particularly vulnerable to seismic activities and devastating monsoon floods due to geographical features and changing global climate patterns. For instance, the 2005 Kashmir earthquake and the 2022 mega-floods submerged vast areas, displaced millions, and disrupted agriculture and livelihoods.  While humans cannot stop natural phenomena from occurring, disaster management preparedness can significantly minimize their destructive impact. Early warning systems, resilient infrastructure, public evacuation drills, reforestation, and widespread first-aid training equip communities to respond effectively. Collective humanitarian solidarity and proactive planning are essential to save lives during natural calamities.
endbox
box activity | School Preparedness
p B. In your opinion, how should schools be equipped to face floods, earthquake and fire (natural and man-made)?
p Content: To safeguard students and faculty against natural and man-made emergencies, schools must implement a comprehensive disaster preparedness framework:  1. Structural Safety & Emergency Infrastructure: - School buildings should comply with seismic building codes and have well-marked, unobstructed emergency exits on every floor. - Multi-purpose, fully charged fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, and fire alarms must be installed in science laboratories, cafeterias, and corridors.  2. Emergency Equipment & Medical Supplies: - Each classroom and administrative office should maintain a fully stocked First Aid Kit with sterile bandages, antiseptic lotions, burn dressings, and emergency contact directories.  3. Regular Drills and First Aid Training: - Mandatory termly fire evacuation and earthquake 'Drop, Cover, and Hold On' drills should be practiced. - Senior students and teachers must receive certified first aid and CPR training from rescue organizations (such as Rescue 1122 or Red Crescent).  4. Disaster Management Committee: - Establishing a designated school safety committee ensures swift communication with parents and local emergency services during flash floods, fires, or tremors.
endbox
