### Review of the Lesson 'Nasiruddin'
p The lesson 'Nasiruddin' is a delightful literary selection that blends humor with profound folk wisdom. Through five concise anecdotes, the author showcases Nasiruddin's quick wit in deflating human arrogance, greed, and pretense. Whether outsmarting a haughty farmer with a turban or turning an awkward house robbery into comic self-deprecation, Nasiruddin exemplifies how humor can be a dignified weapon of truth. The prose is crisp, engaging, and accessible to Grade 9 students, providing excellent opportunities to study character dynamics, irony, and conversational English.
q 5 | Adjectives Classification
qp 1 | We had to take a ________ route to reach our destination. (Adjective of quality)
sol Solution ## Solved: We had to take a <b>safe / scenic / long</b> route to reach our destination. · Category: Adjective of Quality · Selected: safe
qp 2 | We should be ________ citizens. (Adjective of quality)
sol Solution ## Solved: We should be <b>responsible / loyal / honest</b> citizens. · Category: Adjective of Quality · Selected: responsible
qp 3 | He is wearing a ________ suit. (Adjective of colour)
sol Solution ## Solved: He is wearing a <b>blue / black / grey</b> suit. · Category: Adjective of Colour · Selected: blue
qp 4 | Burj Al-Khalifa is the ________ building in the world. (Adjective of size / height)
sol Solution ## Solved: Burj Al-Khalifa is the <b>tallest / largest</b> building in the world. · Category: Adjective of Size / Height · Selected: tallest
qp 5 | The earth is ________. (Adjective of shape)
sol Solution ## Solved: The earth is <b>round / spherical</b>. · Category: Adjective of Shape · Selected: round
q 6 | Degrees Of Adjectives
qp 1 | He is ________ than his neighbors. (rich)
sol Solution ## Solved: He is <b>richer</b> than his neighbors. · Degree: Comparative Degree (-er) · Rule: Comparing two entities ('he' and 'his neighbors') requires comparative degree 'richer'.
qp 2 | The brides were much ________ than the grooms. (young)
sol Solution ## Solved: The brides were much <b>younger</b> than the grooms. · Degree: Comparative Degree (-er) · Rule: Comparison between brides and grooms uses 'younger'.
qp 3 | He is ________ student of the class. (intelligent)
sol Solution ## Solved: He is <b>the most intelligent</b> student of the class. · Degree: Superlative Degree (the most...) · Rule: Multi-syllable adjective comparing one among the entire class takes 'the most intelligent'.
qp 4 | He is ________ than I thought him to be. (clever)
sol Solution ## Solved: He is <b>cleverer</b> than I thought him to be. · Degree: Comparative Degree (-er) · Rule: Comparison of expectation vs reality uses 'cleverer'.
qp 5 | When the old woman became ________ she began to move about. (strong)
sol Solution ## Solved: When the old woman became <b>stronger</b> she began to move about. · Degree: Comparative Degree (-er) · Rule: Expresses progressive increase in strength compared to previous weakness.
