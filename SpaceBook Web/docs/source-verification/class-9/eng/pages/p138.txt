qp 2 | What is meant by misuse of media? How can it be stopped?
sol Misuse of media occurs when broadcasting channels and digita ## Misuse of media occurs when broadcasting channels and digital platforms induce baseless ideas through exaggerated advertisements, mislead consumers into buying substandard goods, distort truth to create sensation, or propagate sectarian and divisive propaganda. It can be stopped through strict regulatory oversight (PEMRA/Cybercrime laws), independent fact-checking, ethical journalistic codes, and developing critical media literacy among citizens.
