## Writing
q A | Write an essay of about 100 – 150 words on “Generosity” using the mind-map below.
p Mind map: Generosity — being sympathetic towards people · share money, time and energy with others · save in order to be able to give · to make others' lives easier and pleasant · be kind and encouraging · not expect anything in return
sol Model answer ## Generosity means giving to others willingly: money, time and energy. A generous person is sympathetic towards people, saves in order to be able to give, is kind and encouraging, and expects nothing in return. Such a person makes the lives of others easier and more pleasant, and society becomes a happier place. Hazrat Usman (RA) is a great example: he bought the well of Roma and gave it to the people of Madina. Other essays that follow the mind-map are possible.
q B | Your grandfather has left you money but you have to spend some amount in helping mankind / underprivileged. How will you do it?
sol Model answer ## I would give part of the money to a school or hospital that serves poor families, pay for the education of a few needy children and help people in my neighbourhood who cannot afford food or medicine. Other sensible plans are possible.
q C | Write down the summary of the lesson “The Two Bargains”.
sol Model answer ## A Jew owned the well of Roma in Madina and sold its water at a high price. Hazrat Usman (RA) bought half of the well for twelve thousand dirhams, the water was used by the Muslims free of cost on his days, and the Jew's business failed. The Jew then offered the other half, and Hazrat Usman (RA) paid whatever he asked and dedicated the well to the people of Madina. The well still exists as Baire Usman. Other correct summaries are possible.
q D | Read the passage and answer the following questions.
p The Jew learnt a bitter lesson. Whereas he had hoped to make more money, he made none at all. He went to Hazrat Usman (RA) and offered to sell the other half of the well also. Hazrat Usman (RA) could have refused to buy it, for the Muslims had no longer any difficulty, but such was his generosity that he paid the Jew whatever he asked for his share. The well was dedicated as an endowment to the people of Madina. The well exists in Medina to this day and is known as Baire Usman or Usman's well.
qp I | What is the main idea of the passage?
sol The generosity of Hazrat Usman (RA): he paid the Jew for the other half of the well too and gave the well to the people of Madina. ## Model answer.
qp II | Locate at least three adjectives.
sol bitter (lesson), other (half), such (generosity) ## "bitter lesson", "the other half", "such generosity"; "more" in "more money" and "no longer" are also acceptable as determiners/adjectives in the passage.
qp III | Identify the tense of the passage.
sol Past simple ## The events (learnt, made, went, offered, paid) are told in the past tense; "exists" and "is known" are present simple for the well today.
qp IV | Why did the Jew go to Hazrat Usman (RA)?
sol To sell him the other half of the well also. ## He had hoped to make more money but made none at all, so he offered his half.
p Teacher's guideline:
p • Discuss with the students the difference between financial and moral help.
p • Tell the students that underprivileged or less fortunate means those who have less money and fewer opportunities than most people in society.
p • Discuss ways through which they can be helpful to mankind.
