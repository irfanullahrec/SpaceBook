q 6 | Participles A
qp 1 | His ______ coat needs mending. (a) tattered (b) tattering (c) tatter (d) tatters
sol (a) tattered ## 'Tattered' is a past participle used as an adjective modifying the noun 'coat'.
qp 2 | A ______ stone gathers no moss. (a) rolling (b) roll (c) rolls (d) rolled
sol (a) rolling ## 'Rolling' is a present participle used as an adjective modifying the noun 'stone'.
qp 3 | A ______ opportunity never returns. (a) lost (b) losing (c) loss (d) lose
sol (a) lost ## 'Lost' is a past participle (V3 of lose) used as an adjective modifying 'opportunity'.
qp 4 | He kept me ______. (a) waiting (b) waited (c) wait (d) waits
sol (a) waiting ## 'Waiting' is a present participle functioning as an object complement showing ongoing action.
qp 5 | She looked ______. (a) worried (b) worrying (c) worry (d) worries
sol (a) worried ## 'Worried' is a past participle functioning as a predicate adjective describing the subject's emotional state.
q 7 | Participles B
qp 1 | He was sitting in an armchair ______ a magazine.
sol Solution ## Participle: reading · Type: Present Participle · Explanation: Describes an action happening simultaneously with sitting.
qp 2 | ______ in the company for many years, he knew everyone and everything.
sol Solution ## Participle: Having worked (or Working) · Type: Perfect / Present Participle Clause · Explanation: Explains the cause/reason for his comprehensive knowledge.
qp 3 | The cup ______ with milk was on the table.
sol Solution ## Participle: filled · Type: Past Participle · Explanation: Passive participle phrase modifying the noun 'cup'.
qp 4 | ______ each other for ages, they had a lot to talk about.
sol Solution ## Participle: Having known (or Knowing) · Type: Perfect / Present Participle Clause · Explanation: Indicates familiarity established over a long prior duration.
qp 5 | ______ into a rich family, she got everything she wished for.
sol Solution ## Participle: Born · Type: Past Participle · Explanation: Passive participle clause modifying the subject 'she'.
q 8 | Infinitives Underline
qp 1 | The best plumber to call is Mr. Amir.
sol Solution ## Infinitive: to call · Infinitive Phrase: to call · Function: Adjective modifying the noun 'plumber'.
qp 2 | His only ambition was to succeed at his job.
sol Solution ## Infinitive: to succeed · Infinitive Phrase: to succeed at his job · Function: Predicate noun (subject complement) renaming 'ambition'.
qp 3 | Each year Mansoor tried to find more deductions for his tax return.
sol Solution ## Infinitive: to find · Infinitive Phrase: to find more deductions for his tax return · Function: Direct object of the transitive verb 'tried'.
qp 4 | To patch the hole in the tyre, the mechanic inserted a plug.
sol Solution ## Infinitive: To patch · Infinitive Phrase: To patch the hole in the tyre · Function: Adverbial modifier of purpose explaining why the mechanic acted.
qp 5 | She learnt to read and write English in coaching academy.
sol Solution ## Infinitive: to read and (to) write · Infinitive Phrase: to read and write English · Function: Direct object of the verb 'learnt'.
