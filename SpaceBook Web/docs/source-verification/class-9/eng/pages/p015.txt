qp 3 | The Rasool (PBUH) said, "A Muslim is he from whose hand and tongue the Muslims are safe". This hadith means that: (a) a true Muslim does not hurt Muslims physically and emotionally (b) a true Muslim does not bad mouth or curse anybody (c) a true Muslim does not beat or injure anybody with his hands (d) a true Muslim does not trouble anybody mentally
sol (a) a true Muslim does not hurt Muslims physically and emotionally ## Safety from 'hand and tongue' encompasses both physical protection (hand) and emotional/verbal safety (tongue).
qp 4 | Truly, he is an epitome of tolerance, forgiveness, patience and compassion. The word 'epitome' in this sentence means: (a) essence (b) embodiment (c) contrary (d) example
sol (b) embodiment ## 'Epitome' means a perfect personification or embodiment of a quality.
qp 5 | The Rasool (PBUH) granted a general amnesty to the entire population of Makkah. The word 'amnesty' in this sentence means: (a) patience (b) forgiveness (c) tolerance (d) acceptance
sol (b) forgiveness ## 'Amnesty' means an official universal pardon and general forgiveness.
