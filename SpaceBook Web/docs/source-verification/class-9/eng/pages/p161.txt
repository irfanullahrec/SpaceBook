box teach | Rhyming Couplets and Heroic Meter in Narrative Poetry
p The poem is written in rhyming couplets (AABBCCDD...) in iambic pentameter (Heroic Couplets). Every pair of lines rhymes: increase/peace (A), room/bloom (B), gold/bold (C), said/head (D), accord/Lord (E), so/low (F), then/men (G), night/light (H), blest/rest (I).
- 'An angel writing in a book of gold:—' (C)
- 'Exceeding peace had made Ben Adhem bold,' (C)
endbox
box teach | Archaic Pronouns and Verb Forms (Thou, Thee, Writest)
p Poetic English frequently uses Early Modern English archaic forms: 'thou' (subject: you), 'thee' (object: you), 'writest' (verb with -est ending for second person singular).
- 'What writest thou?' = 'What are you writing?'
- 'I pray thee' = 'I pray you / I request you'
endbox
### Moral Essay: Service to Humanity as the Highest Manifestation of True Worship
p Leigh Hunt's immortal poem 'Abou Ben Adhem' provides a profound ethical philosophy: that love for the Creator is inseparable from love for His creation. In religious practices across world faiths, individuals often focus solely on ritualistic devotion while remaining indifferent to the poverty, illness, and suffering around them. However, authentic faith requires transforming spiritual devotion into practical humanitarian action. Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) taught: 'The best of people are those who are most beneficial to people.' When we feed the hungry, comfort the bereaved, shelter the orphan, and fight against injustice, we perform the highest form of worship. By embodying the spirit of Abou Ben Adhem, society can replace religious fanaticism with universal brotherhood, empathy, and enduring peace.
