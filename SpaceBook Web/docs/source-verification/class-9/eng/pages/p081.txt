q 6 | Adverbs Correct Place
qp 1 | She is at home. (rarely)
sol Solution ## Solved: She is <b>rarely</b> at home. · Rule: Adverbs of frequency are placed after the verb 'to be' (is/am/are/was/were).
qp 2 | He opened the door. (quietly)
sol Solution ## Solved: He opened the door <b>quietly</b>. (or: He <b>quietly</b> opened the door.) · Rule: Adverbs of manner are placed at the end of the clause or immediately before the main verb.
qp 3 | Can I sit down? (here)
sol Solution ## Solved: Can I sit down <b>here</b>? · Rule: Adverbs of place ('here') are placed at the end of the verb phrase.
qp 4 | The students listened. (attentively)
sol Solution ## Solved: The students listened <b>attentively</b>. · Rule: Adverbs of manner modifying intransitive verbs are placed after the verb.
qp 5 | We live in Abbottabad. (now)
sol Solution ## Solved: We live in Abbottabad <b>now</b>. (or: <b>Now</b>, we live in Abbottabad.) · Rule: Adverbs of time are placed at the end (or for emphasis at the beginning) of a sentence.
q 7 | Adverbs Comparative Superlative
qp 1 | Our father arrived ________ than the rest of the family. (late)
sol Solution ## Solved: Our father arrived <b>later</b> than the rest of the family. · Degree: Comparative Degree (-er) · Rule: Short adverb 'late' adds '-er' -> 'later'.
qp 2 | My sister looked ________ than Amara did. (beautifully)
sol Solution ## Solved: My sister looked <b>more beautifully</b> than Amara did. · Degree: Comparative Degree (more...) · Rule: Adverbs ending in '-ly' take 'more' -> 'more beautifully'.
qp 3 | Your husband, Kamal, writes ________ of all. (well)
sol Solution ## Solved: Your husband, Kamal, writes <b>best</b> of all. · Degree: Superlative Degree (irregular) · Rule: Irregular adverb 'well' has comparative 'better' and superlative 'best'.
qp 4 | Mrs. Haseeb speaks ________ than Mrs. Amir. (clearly)
sol Solution ## Solved: Mrs. Haseeb speaks <b>more clearly</b> than Mrs. Amir. · Degree: Comparative Degree (more...) · Rule: Adverbs ending in '-ly' take 'more' -> 'more clearly'.
qp 5 | Mother prepares chicken ________ than grandmother does. (well)
sol Solution ## Solved: Mother prepares chicken <b>better</b> than grandmother does. · Degree: Comparative Degree (irregular) · Rule: Comparison of two actions uses irregular comparative 'better'.
