q 3 | Look up the meaning of the following words in the dictionary and make sentences to clearly bring out the meaning. (difference between 'fetch' and 'bring'; 'famous' and 'notorious'; 'crime' and 'sin'; 'desert' and 'barren')
qp 1 | fetch vs. bring
sol Solution ## Distinction: 'Fetch' means to go somewhere, collect something/someone, and bring it back (a two-way movement: go + get + come back). 'Bring' means to carry or lead something/someone towards the speaker (a one-way movement). · Sentence1: fetch: The villagers had to walk miles every morning to <b>fetch</b> fresh drinking water from the well. · Sentence2: bring: Please <b>bring</b> your English grammar textbook to class tomorrow.
qp 2 | famous vs. notorious
sol Solution ## Distinction: 'Famous' means widely known and recognized for positive, noble, or admirable achievements. 'Notorious' means widely known for bad, shameful, or criminal acts. · Sentence1: famous: Hazrat Usman (رضی اللہ عنہ) is <b>famous</b> throughout Islamic history for his boundless philanthropy. · Sentence2: notorious: The <b>notorious</b> bandit was finally captured by the state police.
qp 3 | crime vs. sin
sol Solution ## Distinction: 'Crime' is an illegal act that violates civil or statutory laws established by the state and is punished by courts. 'Sin' is a moral transgression that violates divine commandments of God. · Sentence1: crime: Black marketing and hoarding food during a national emergency is a punishable <b>crime</b>. · Sentence2: sin: Telling lies and breaking promises is a grave <b>sin</b> in the eyes of Allah.
qp 4 | desert vs. barren
sol Solution ## Distinction: 'Desert' is a geographical noun/biome characterized by arid climate, extreme temperatures, and minimal rainfall. 'Barren' is an adjective describing land that is infertile and incapable of producing vegetation or crops. · Sentence1: desert: The Sahara is the largest hot <b>desert</b> in the world. · Sentence2: barren: Due to decades of soil erosion, the rocky hillside remained completely <b>barren</b>.

qp 4 | The Caliph begged forgiveness for the hasty words he had uttered.
sol Solution ## Word: begged · Pos: verb (past) · Contextual Meaning: Earnestly and humbly asked for pardon or forgiveness.
