table Informal and formal email layout
th | Part of the email | Informal Email | Formal Email
tr | Header | From: To: Subject: | From: To: Subject:
tr | Greeting | Hi/ Hello | 1. Dear Mr. ______ /Mrs. ______ 2. Dear Sir or Madam
tr | Text | When you write an INFORMAL EMAIL you can use informal language and contractions. | When you write a FORMAL EMAIL you shouldn't use informal language and contractions. Usual beginning: I am writing to + purpose
tr | Ending | Please write soon/ looking forward to hearing from you. Best wishes/ kind regards/ love | I look forward to hearing from you. Best wishes/ kind regards/ 1. Yours sincerely 2. Yours faithfully
endtable
box activity | Application Remission Fine
p Exercise A: Write an application to your principal for remission of fine.
p Content: To, The Principal, Government High School No. 1, Peshawar.  Subject: Application for Remission of Absence Fine  Respected Sir, Most respectfully, it is submitted that I could not attend school for three consecutive days (from 15th to 17th October) due to severe seasonal flu and fever. Because of my sudden illness, I was unable to submit a timely leave application. Consequently, a fine of Rs. 300 has been imposed on me.  Sir, my father is a low-income laborer and it is very difficult for him to pay this fine. I have always been a punctual, disciplined, and studious student with an unblemished academic record.  I kindly request you to remit my absence fine on medical grounds. I shall be immensely grateful for your benevolent consideration.  Yours obediently, Muhammad Hamza Class: 9th-B, Roll No: 24 Date: October 18, 2026
endbox
box activity | Email Friend Exam
p Exercise B: Write an email to your friend congratulating him/her on success in exam.
p Content: From: bilal.khan@email.com To: zarak.ahmed@email.com Subject: Heartiest Congratulations on Your Outstanding Board Exam Result!  Dear Zarak,  I hope this email finds you in the best of health and spirits.  I was absolutely thrilled to learn today that you secured 1st position in our district in the Class 9 Board Examinations, scoring 96% marks! Please accept my heartiest congratulations on this spectacular achievement.  Your tireless dedication, late-night study sessions, and perseverance have truly paid off. You have made your parents, teachers, and all of us immensely proud. We must plan a celebration get-together soon.  Convey my warmest regards to uncle and aunt.  Your sincere friend, Bilal Khan
endbox
box activity | Email Editor Road Accidents
p Exercise C: Write an email to Editor of a Newspaper on road accidents and rash driving.
p Content: From: rashid.citizen@email.com To: editor.dailyexpress@email.com Subject: Urgent Need to Curb Rash Driving and Reckless Traffic Violations in Peshawar  Dear Sir,  Through the esteemed columns of your widely circulated newspaper, I wish to draw the urgent attention of the City Traffic Police and municipal authorities toward the alarming surge in road accidents caused by rash driving in Peshawar.  Daily commuters, especially school children and elderly pedestrians, face grave peril due to underage motorcycling, speeding dumpers, and blatant disregard for red lights on major arteries like University Road and Ring Road. The lack of strict helmet enforcement and reckless overtaking create constant hazards.  I urge the traffic administration to deploy electronic speed-monitoring cameras, install prominent pedestrian zebra crossings, impound vehicles driven by unlicensed juveniles, and organize road safety awareness campaigns.  Thank you for your consideration.  Yours sincerely, Rashid Mahmood Resident of Hayatabad Phase 4, Peshawar
endbox
box activity | Letter Director Sanitary
p Exercise D: Write a letter to Director, Peshawar Development Authority (PDA) to take measures to improve sanitary conditions in your area. Follow the format of a formal letter.
p Content: House No. 42, Sector F-3, Phase 6, Hayatabad, Peshawar. October 20, 2026  The Director, Peshawar Development Authority (PDA), Commercial Complex, Hayatabad, Peshawar.  Subject: Deteriorating Sanitary Conditions and Garbage Disposal in Phase 6  Respected Sir, I wish to bring to your immediate notice the appalling sanitary conditions currently prevailing in Sector F-3 of Hayatabad Phase 6.  For the past three weeks, municipal garbage collection trucks have failed to visit our neighborhood regularly. As a result, huge heaps of domestic refuse have accumulated along street corners, giving off foul odors and turning into breeding grounds for mosquitoes and stray animals. The clogged open drains are overflowing with wastewater, posing severe health hazards of dengue and typhoid.  I earnestly request your good office to dispatch sanitation squads for immediate garbage removal and fumigation of the locality.  Thanking you in anticipation.  Yours faithfully, Kamran Tariq President, Residents' Welfare Association, Phase 6
endbox
