# Unit 11 — Safety First || یونٹ 11 — حفاظت سب سے پہلے (ابتدائی طبی امداد) || 11 څپرکی — لومړی خوندیتوب (لومړنۍ مرستې)
p After reading the unit the students will be able to: || اس یونٹ کو پڑھنے کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || د دې څپرکي له لوستلو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo analyze passages in the text to identify the theme / general subject, key idea / central thought (a statement about general subject), and supporting details.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text and use intensive reading strategies to scan to answer short questions.
slo use summary skills to extract salient points and develop a mind map to summarize a text.
slo use dictionary to choose appropriate word definition, identify part of speech and recognize abbreviation used in a dictionary.
slo translate passages from Urdu to English.
slo apply rule of capitalization wherever applicable.
slo illustrate use of punctuation marks learned earlier.
slo recognize and rectify faulty punctuation marks in given passages and own work.
slo recognize and use comma to separate: subordinate clause which precedes a main clause; a non-defining relatives clause; coordinate clauses joined by one of the pure conjunction.
slo recognize and use colon to separate indefinite clauses when there is a sharp antithesis.
slo recognize and use semi colon between: parts of a compound sentence when no conjunction is used; the clauses of a compound sentence before conjunctive adverbs.
