# Unit 13 — Letter to the Newspaper Editor || یونٹ 13 — اخبار کے مدیر کے نام خط (میڈیا کے اثرات) || 13 څپرکی — د ورځپاڼې مدیر ته لیک (د رسنیو اغیزې)
p After reading the unit the students will be able to: || اس یونٹ کو پڑھنے کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || د دې څپرکي له لوستلو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo write and revise formal letters to people in extended social and academic environment for various purposes. Write the address on the envelope clearly and in proper format.
slo write and revise application to people in extended environment using correct format, layout and tone.
slo analyze letters to editor to recognize effective ways of explaining and presenting an idea, a point or an opinion.
slo write and revise letters to editor expressing ideas, points or opinions by supporting it with anecdotes, quotations, examples, analogies etc.
slo analyze and compare various informal and formal emails to note differences of conventions, vocabulary, style and tone.
slo write informal and formal e-mail in extended social and academic environment.
slo recognize and use quotation marks to enclose: both parts of an interrupted quotation; usual or peculiar terms to which attention is directed to make the meaning clear.
slo recognize and use hyphen to indicate the division of a word at the end of the line.
slo recognize and use dash as a separator to indicate that a sentence has been broken off, or to indicate a new direction of thought.
slo express agreement (fully and partially) politely.
