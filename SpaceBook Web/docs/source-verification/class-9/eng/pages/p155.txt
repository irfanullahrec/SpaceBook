## Pre-reading || مطالعے سے پہلے || له لوستلو مخکې
p Who was Abou Ben Adhem?
@sec 15-s1 | Stanza 1: The Angelic Encounter in Moonlight || پہلا بند: چاندنی رات میں فرشتے کا سنہری کتاب لکھنا || لومړی بند: د سپوږمۍ په رڼا کې د فرښتې راتګ او په سروزرو کتاب کې لیکل
## Reading || مطالعہ || لوستل
p Abou Ben Adhem (may his tribe increase!)
p Awoke one night from a deep dream of peace,
p And saw, within the moonlight in his room,
p Making it rich, and like a lily in bloom,
p An angel writing in a book of gold:—
p Exceeding peace had made Ben Adhem bold,
p And to the presence in the room he said,
p "What writest thou?"—The vision raised its head,
p And with a look made of all sweet accord,
p Answered, "The names of those who love the Lord."

@sec 15-s2 | Stanza 2: Abou's Humble Plea for Fellow Men || دوسرا بند: ابو بن ادہم کی عاجزانہ التجا اور انسانیت سے محبت || دویم بند: د ابو بن ادهم عاجزانه غوښتنه او د انسانانو مینه
p "And is mine one?" said Abou. "Nay, not so,"
p Replied the angel. Abou spoke more low,
p But cheerly still; and said, "I pray thee, then,
p Write me as one that loves his fellow men."

@sec 15-s3 | Stanza 3: The Supreme Triumph of Divine Grace || تیسرا بند: الٰہی رحمت کا ظہور اور فہرست میں اول مقام || دریم بند: د الهي رحمت بریالیتوب او د نوملړ په سر کې ځای نیول
p The angel wrote, and vanished. The next night
p It came again with a great wakening light,
p And showed the names whom love of God had blest,
p And lo! Ben Adhem's name led all the rest.
