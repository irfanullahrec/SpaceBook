box teach | Modal Auxiliary Verbs: Can, Could, May, Might, Must, Should, Ought to
p Modal auxiliaries express ability (can/could), permission/possibility (may/might), obligation/necessity (must/ought to), or advice (should). They do not change form with grammatical subjects and are followed by base verbs.
- We must follow the Quaid's motto of Unity, Faith, and Discipline.
- Citizens should actively contribute to national welfare.
- A true leader can inspire millions through honesty.
endbox
box teach | Rules of Punctuation & Capitalization
p Capitalize proper nouns, historical events, official titles, and the first word of direct quotations. Use commas for parenthetical clauses, semicolons to separate independent clauses without conjunctions, and quotation marks for direct speech.
- Quaid-e-Azam said, 'Unity, Faith, and Discipline.'
- Pakistan was founded on August 14, 1947.
endbox
### Speech: The Relevance of Quaid-e-Azam's Principles for Modern Pakistan
p Respected teachers and fellow students! Today, Pakistan stands at a crucial crossroads. To overcome our economic hardships, social divisions, and institutional challenges, we need look no further than the visionary principles of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah. The Quaid showed us that an invincible nation is built on three unbreakable pillars: Unity among all ethnic groups, Faith in our collective destiny and Almighty Allah, and strict Discipline in public life. Furthermore, his commitment to social justice, constitutional supremacy, and minority rights provides the ultimate roadmap for making Pakistan a progressive and respected leader in the comity of nations. Let us pledge today to embody his integrity and hard work in our daily lives. Pakistan Zindabad!
