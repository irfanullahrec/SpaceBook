q 9 | Articles Activity
qp 1 | I like ________ blue T-shirt over there better than ________ red one.
sol Solution ## Solved: I like <b>the</b> blue T-shirt over there better than <b>the</b> red one. · Explanation: 'The' is used for specific items indicated by the speaker ('over there').
qp 2 | Their car does 150 miles ________ hour.
sol Solution ## Solved: Their car does 150 miles <b>an</b> hour. · Explanation: 'An' is used before 'hour' because it begins with a vowel sound /aʊər/.
qp 3 | Where's ________ USB drive I lent you last week?
sol Solution ## Solved: Where's <b>the</b> USB drive I lent you last week? · Explanation: 'The' is used because the USB drive is specifically identified by the relative clause.
qp 4 | Do you still live in ________ Bannu?
sol Solution ## Solved: Do you still live in <b>X (no article)</b> Bannu? · Explanation: No article (zero article 'X') is used before names of cities (proper nouns).
qp 5 | Is your mother working in ________ old office building?
sol Solution ## Solved: Is your mother working in <b>an</b> old office building? · Explanation: 'An' is used before the vowel sound in the adjective 'old'.
q 10 | Modal Verbs
qp 1 | They are able to speak English well. (a) They can speak English well. (b) They may speak English well. (c) They must speak English well.
sol (a) They can speak English well. ## 'Can' expresses ability, matching 'are able to'.
qp 2 | There is a possibility of rain tomorrow. (a) It may rain tomorrow. (b) It must rain tomorrow. (c) It will rain tomorrow.
sol (a) It may rain tomorrow. ## 'May' expresses possibility, matching 'there is a possibility'.
qp 3 | Do I have to clean the kitchen? (a) Can I clean the kitchen? (b) Must I clean the kitchen? (c) Should I clean the kitchen?
sol (b) Must I clean the kitchen? ## 'Must' expresses requirement and obligation, matching 'do I have to'.
qp 4 | Are you able to drive a tractor? (a) Can you drive a tractor? (b) Must you drive a tractor? (c) Should you drive a tractor?
sol (a) Can you drive a tractor? ## 'Can' asks about capability/ability, matching 'are you able to'.
