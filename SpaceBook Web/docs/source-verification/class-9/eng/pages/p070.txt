q 7 | Order Of Adjectives
qp 1 | He was wearing a ________ shirt. (a) dirty old flannel ✅ (b) flannel old dirty (c) old dirty flannel
sol dirty old flannel ## Order: Opinion/Condition (dirty) -> Age (old) -> Material (flannel).
qp 2 | Pass me the ________ cups. (a) big blue plastic ✅ (b) plastic big blue (c) big plastic blue
sol big blue plastic ## Order: Size (big) -> Color (blue) -> Material (plastic).
qp 3 | He sat behind a ________ desk. (a) big brown wooden ✅ (b) big wooden brown (c) wooden big brown
sol big brown wooden ## Order: Size (big) -> Color (brown) -> Material (wooden).
qp 4 | I used to drive ________ car. (a) an old blue German ✅ (b) a blue old German (c) an old German blue
sol an old blue German ## Order: Age (old) -> Color (blue) -> Origin (German).
qp 5 | She gave him a ________ vase. (a) small black Egyptian ✅ (b) small Egyptian black (c) black Egyptian small
sol small black Egyptian ## Order: Size (small) -> Color (black) -> Origin (Egyptian).
q 8 | Formation Of Adjectives
qp 1 | Hameed writes in a simple and ________ way. (understand)
sol Solution ## Solved: Hameed writes in a simple and <b>understandable</b> way. · Suffix: -able · Derived: understandable
qp 2 | The party was much more ________ than I had expected. (enjoy)
sol Solution ## Solved: The party was much more <b>enjoyable</b> than I had expected. · Suffix: -able · Derived: enjoyable
qp 3 | I wish I had ________ powers. (magic)
sol Solution ## Solved: I wish I had <b>magical</b> powers. · Suffix: -al · Derived: magical
qp 4 | Maria came to me in a very ________ state. (emotion)
sol Solution ## Solved: Maria came to me in a very <b>emotional</b> state. · Suffix: -al · Derived: emotional
qp 5 | My sister is ________ to cats. (allergy)
sol Solution ## Solved: My sister is <b>allergic</b> to cats. · Suffix: -ic · Derived: allergic
q 9 | Comparative Superlative Table

sol Solution ## Comparative: more famous · Superlative: most famous · Rule: Multi-syllable adjective takes 'more' and 'most'.
qp 2 | amusing
sol Solution ## Comparative: more amusing · Superlative: most amusing · Rule: Participle adjective takes 'more' and 'most'.
qp 3 | fine
sol Solution ## Comparative: finer · Superlative: finest · Rule: Single-syllable ending in -e adds -r and -st.

sol Solution ## Comparative: newer · Superlative: newest · Rule: Single-syllable adds -er and -est.
qp 5 | old
