q 4 | Parentheses Exercise
qp 1 | My mother likes although I don't know why old folk music.
sol Solution ## Corrected: My mother likes (although I don't know why) old folk music. · Rule: Encloses parenthetical supplementary clause interrupting the sentence.
qp 2 | All tiger species especially the South China tiger are endangered.
sol Solution ## Corrected: All tiger species (especially the South China tiger) are endangered. · Rule: Encloses specific explanatory example.
qp 3 | I'll get back to you tomorrow Friday.
sol Solution ## Corrected: I'll get back to you tomorrow (Friday). · Rule: Marks off clarifying day of the week.
qp 4 | Which country countries do you want to visit?
sol Solution ## Corrected: Which country (countries) do you want to visit? · Rule: Indicates alternative singular/plural grammatical form.
qp 5 | You put fifty-four 54 books on that shelf.
sol Solution ## Corrected: You put fifty-four (54) books on that shelf. · Rule: Expresses an amount in numerals previously written in words.
