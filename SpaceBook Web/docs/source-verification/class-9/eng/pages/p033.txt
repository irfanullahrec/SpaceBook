@sec 3-s1 | 1. The Historic Landing at Mauripur, Karachi (August 7, 1947) || ماڑی پور کراچی کے ہوائی اڈے پر تاریخی آمد (۷ اگست ۱۹۴۷ء) || د ماړي پور کراچۍ په هوایي ډګر کې تاریخي راکوزېدل (۷ اګست ۱۹۴۷)
## Reading || مطالعہ || لوستل
p It was August 7th, 1947, when a silver aircraft circled over the airport at Mauripur, Karachi. It was watched by a vast, excited crowd while smoothly landing.
p First to step out of the aeroplane was a tall and dignified man wearing a pure white Sherwani. As soon as he appeared, the whole crowd roared with one voice, "Pakistan Zindabad! Pakistan Zindabad!" And every one pushed forward to welcome him, to get as close to him as possible in order to see him clearly.
p For here was their great leader, Quaid-e-Azam, the founder and first Governor General of their new country. Here was their leader who had won Pakistan for them—Muhammad Ali Jinnah.

@sec 3-s2 | 2. The Calm, Serious and Sober Statesman || پروقار، سنجیدہ اور متین رہنما || ارام، هوښیار او سنجیده لارښود
p As he drove through the great sea of people that stretched from the airport to the city, through all the cheers and tears of joy and the never ending slogans of "Pakistan Zindabad", the Quaid showed no signs of his feelings. He rarely did so.
p If he felt pride or joy in what he had done, he did not let anybody see it. Through all the excitement of those first days in the life of the young nation, the man who had made it possible remained calm, serious and sober. Perhaps he was thinking of the millions of people who had become homeless when India and Pakistan became separate countries.
