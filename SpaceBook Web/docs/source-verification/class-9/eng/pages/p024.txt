q 1 | Dictionary Words
qp 1 | exploration
sol Solution ## Pos: n. · Meaning: The act of travelling through an unfamiliar area in order to learn about it. · Example: Space exploration expands human knowledge.
qp 2 | invention
sol Solution ## Pos: n. · Meaning: The creation of a novel product, device, or method. · Example: The invention of electricity transformed civilization.
qp 3 | innovation
sol Solution ## Pos: n. · Meaning: The introduction of new and creative ideas or methods. · Example: Technological innovation drives economic growth.
qp 4 | Hamlet
sol Solution ## Pos: n. (prop.) · Meaning: A Shakespearian character representing excessive hesitation and overthinking. · Example: Be a man of action rather than a hesitant Hamlet.
qp 5 | perpetual
sol Solution ## Pos: adj. · Meaning: Never ending or changing; occurring continually. · Example: The spirit of Ishq demands perpetual struggle.
qp 6 | foresight
sol Solution ## Pos: n. · Meaning: The ability to predict what will happen or be needed in the future. · Example: A wise leader plans with great foresight.
qp 7 | enthusiastic
sol Solution ## Pos: adj. · Meaning: Showing intense and eager enjoyment, interest, or approval. · Example: The students were enthusiastic about the science fair.
qp 8 | emperor
sol Solution ## Pos: n. · Meaning: A sovereign ruler of great power and rank. · Example: When a man masters himself, he becomes an emperor of his fate.
qp 9 | thy
sol Solution ## Pos: pron. (arch.) · Meaning: Archaic poetic word meaning 'your'. · Example: "Know thyself and success shall be thine."
qp 10 | forge
sol Solution ## Pos: v. · Meaning: To create or shape something strong with concerted effort. · Example: Iqbal inspired youth to forge their own destiny.
qp 11 | brilliance
sol Solution ## Pos: n. · Meaning: Exceptional talent, intelligence, or intense brightness. · Example: His intellectual brilliance was recognized globally.
qp 12 | nova
sol Solution ## Pos: n. · Meaning: A star that suddenly increases enormously in brightness. · Example: The youth soars toward a brilliant nova of inspiration.
qp 13 | inspiration
sol Solution ## Pos: n. · Meaning: The process of being mentally stimulated to do something creative. · Example: Iqbal's poetry is a source of eternal inspiration.
