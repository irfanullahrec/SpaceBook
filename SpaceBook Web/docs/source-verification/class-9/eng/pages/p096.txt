qp 1 | Why is Zarin Gul different from other businessmen?
sol Zarin Gul is fundamentally different from conventional busin ## Zarin Gul is fundamentally different from conventional businessmen because he treats shoemaking as a sacred art rather than a commercial mass-production trade. He never made ready-made stock for speculative sale, never compromised on premium leather quality, never advertised or hired cheap laborers, and valued customer satisfaction and durability far above monetary profit.

sol The author came in touch with Zarin Gul Brothers during his ## The author came in touch with Zarin Gul Brothers during his youth in the holy month of Ramadan, when his father—a prominent tribal chieftain—took him to Zarin Gul's shop in Qissa Khwani Bazaar to order a pair of custom Peshawari chappals for the upcoming Eid festival.
