q 4 | Transition Words Sentences
qp 1 | The dog barked all night; as a result (consequently), the neighbors had no sleep.
sol Solution ## Word Used: as a result / consequently · Function: Shows cause and effect.
qp 2 | Humera bought a new dress; furthermore, she also bought shoes and a purse.
sol Solution ## Word Used: furthermore · Function: Adds supplementary information.
qp 3 | The police had no clues for the case; nonetheless, tips were still coming in.
sol Solution ## Word Used: nonetheless · Function: Shows contrast or concession.
qp 4 | I have five hundred rupees; however, I don't want to spend it.
sol Solution ## Word Used: however · Function: Introduces a contrasting condition.
