@sec 5-s2 | 2. The Main Clauses of the Medina Charter || میثاقِ مدینہ کی بنیادی دفعات اور شرائط || د مدینې د تړون بنسټیزې مادې او شرایط
p The main points of the charter are:
p All the communities signing the charter would form the common nationality.
p If any signatory of the charter was attacked by an enemy, others would defend him collectively.
p The Muslims, the Jews and other communities of this republic should be free to profess their own respective religion and perform religious ceremonies.
p Individual and personal offences of trivial nature of any non-Muslim would be treated as such and no general liability would fall on the community to which the offender belonged.
p The oppressed should be protected. Henceforth, bloodshed, murder and violence should be Haram (abominable) in Medina.
p All disputes should be referred for decision to Hazrat Muhammad (رسول اللہ خاتم النبیین ﷺ) the Rasool of Allah.

@sec 5-s3 | 3. The Importance of the Charter & Alignment with Pakistan || میثاق کی اہمیت اور نظریہ پاکستان کے ساتھ ہم آہنگی || د تړون اهمیت او د پاکستان له ایډیالوژۍ سره سمون
p The importance of the charter lies in the fact that it announces the great principles of civic equality, freedom of worship and religious and racial tolerance.
p This principle can also be seen in the perspective of the creation of Pakistan. Our founder, Quaid-e-Azam clearly told his people in 1947, at the time of creation of Pakistan: "You may belong to any religion, caste or creed — we are all equal citizens of one state".
