box teach | Relative Clauses: Defining (Restrictive) vs. Non-Defining (Non-Restrictive)
p Relative clauses start with relative pronouns (who, whom, whose, which, that): - Defining clauses provide essential identifying information without commas: 'The animal *that has flared horns* is the Markhor.' - Non-Defining clauses provide extra non-essential information separated by commas: 'The Markhor, *which is our National Animal*, lives in steep mountains.'
- Defining: Students who study ecology will protect nature.
- Non-Defining: The Deosai Plateau, which is known as the Land of Giants, hosts brown bears.
endbox
box teach | Compound Adjectives in Technical Descriptions
p Formed by joining two or more words with hyphens (e.g., 'spiral-horned', 'snow-covered', 'freshwater-dwelling', 'community-based').
- The snow-covered peaks shelter leopards.
- Community-based conservation saved the species.
endbox
### Report: An Environmental Action Plan for Protecting Endangered Fauna in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
p Khyber Pakhtunkhwa possesses world-renowned wildlife assets, including the Markhor in Chitral Gol, the Snow Leopard in the Hindu Kush, and western tragopan pheasants in Kohistan forests. However, unsustainable deforestation and illegal hunting threaten these ecosystems. This action plan proposes four critical pillars: (1) Expansion of Community Protected Areas where local youths are employed as wildlife game-watchers, (2) Strict enforcement of heavy fines and prison terms under the KP Wildlife Act for illegal timber smuggling and poaching, (3) Implementation of eco-tourism corridors generating sustainable revenue for local communities, and (4) Mandatory environmental education in schools to instill conservation ethics in the next generation.

q 7 | SLO-based short questions
qp 1 | Q1. Define biodiversity and explain its ecological significance.
sol Solution ## Marks: 4 Marks · Answer: Biodiversity (biological diversity) refers to the rich variety of all living organisms—including plants, animals, fungi, and microorganisms—and the ecological complexes and ecosystems they inhabit. It is vital because diverse ecosystems provide essential life services: oxygen generation, freshwater purification, pollination of crops, climate moderation, and soil regeneration.
qp 2 | Q2. Why is the Indus River Dolphin classified as an endangered freshwater cetacean?
sol Solution ## Marks: 4 Marks · Answer: The Indus River Dolphin (Platanista minor) is endangered because: 1. Habitat Fragmentation: Construction of irrigation barrages has split the population into isolated, vulnerable subgroups. 2. Water Diversion: Upstream agricultural dams cause critical low water levels during dry seasons. 3. By-Catch Mortality: Dolphins get entangled in illegal monofilament fishing nets and drown. 4. Pollution: Untreated industrial effluents and toxic agrochemicals degrade river water quality.
qp 3 | Q3. What ecological services do the mangrove forests of Sindh and Balochistan provide?
sol Solution ## Marks: 4 Marks · Answer: Mangrove forests provide four critical services: 1. Coastal Shield: Their dense root systems absorb wave energy, protecting coastal settlements against tsunamis and cyclonic surges. 2. Marine Nursery: They serve as breeding grounds for 80% of commercially harvested fish, crabs, and shrimps. 3. Carbon Sequestration: Mangroves store up to four times more carbon per hectare than terrestrial forests. 4. Livelihood Support: They provide sustainable fodder and firewood for coastal communities.
qp 4 | Q4. Describe the physical habitat and conservation status of the Markhor.
sol Solution ## Marks: 4 Marks · Answer: The Markhor (Capra falconeri), Pakistan's national animal, is a majestic wild goat characterized by large, spiraling horns. It inhabits steep, arid, and rocky cliffs in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan, and Balochistan. Due to regulated community-based trophy hunting conservation programs, its status has improved from 'Endangered' to 'Near Threatened' (IUCN).
qp 5 | Q5. State three primary rules for placing parentheses in written English.
sol Solution ## Marks: 4 Marks · Answer: 1. Enclosing Supplementary Info: Setting off non-essential explanatory remarks (e.g., 'My mother likes (although I don't know why) folk music.'). 2. Expressing Numerals: Writing digits corresponding to numbers spelled in words (e.g., 'fifty-four (54) books'). 3. Enclosing Enumerations: Marking off numbers/letters in lists (e.g., '(a)', '(b)', '(1)', '(2)').
qp 6 | Q6. What is the function of an ellipsis (...) in academic writing and dialogue?
sol Solution ## Marks: 4 Marks · Answer: An ellipsis (...) serves two key functions: 1. Academic Quoting: Signifies the omission of words, phrases, or sentences from an original text without altering its intended meaning. 2. Fictional Dialogue: Indicates a dramatic hesitation, trailing off of speech, or an incomplete thought in conversational exchange.
qp 7 | Q7. Outline the three-part structure of a Persuasive/Argumentative Essay.
sol Solution ## Marks: 4 Marks · Answer: 1. Introduction: Hook, background context of the controversial issue, thesis statement, and three supporting reasons. 2. Body Paragraphs (1–3): Topic sentence presenting a distinct reason, specific factual examples, addressing counter-arguments, and concluding link. 3. Conclusion: Restatement of thesis in fresh words, summary of core arguments, and a decisive call to action.
