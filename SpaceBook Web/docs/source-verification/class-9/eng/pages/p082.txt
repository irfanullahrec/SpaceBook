box teach | Transitive and Intransitive Verbs
p A Transitive verb requires a direct object to complete its meaning ('Hazrat Usman bought *the well*'). An Intransitive verb expresses an action that does not pass over to an object ('The trade caravan *arrived*', 'The crops *withered*'). Some verbs can be both depending on context.
- Transitive: He distributed *the food*.
- Intransitive: The starving children cried.
- Transitive: The merchant sold *water*.
endbox
box teach | Regular and Irregular Past Tense Verbs
p Regular verbs form past tense by adding '-ed' (negotiate -> negotiated, declare -> declared). Irregular verbs change root vowels (buy -> bought, arrive -> arrived, give -> gave, strike -> struck).
- Regular: He purchased the well.
- Irregular: The famine struck the region.
endbox
### Essay: Islamic Concept of Philanthropy and Modern Wealth Redistribution
p The inspiring narrative of 'The Two Bargains' provides a profound blueprint for social economics. In contemporary capitalist systems, economic crises frequently result in price-gouging, hoarding, and monopolies by elite cartels. In stark contrast, Hazrat Usman Ghani (RA) demonstrated that Islamic philanthropy views material wealth as a sacred trust from Almighty Allah to be deployed for societal welfare. By dedicating the well of Beer-e-Rumah as a universal public waqf and freely distributing 1,000 camel-loads of grain during a devastating famine, he averted starvation and stabilized the economy. If modern affluent individuals and corporations adopt this spirit of ethical investment and philanthropy, extreme poverty, clean water shortages, and economic inequality can be eradicated globally.
