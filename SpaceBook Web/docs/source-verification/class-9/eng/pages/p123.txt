q 1 | Comprehension
qp 1 | What is first aid?
sol First aid is the immediate assistance and help given to a si ## First aid is the immediate assistance and help given to a sick or injured person using common sense and basic medical supplies until full, professional medical treatment becomes available. Its primary aims are to preserve life, prevent the condition from worsening, and promote quick recovery without causing further harm.
qp 2 | Discuss a situation in which you provided first aid to someone.
sol Once during a school football match, my classmate tripped an ## Once during a school football match, my classmate tripped and severely scraped his knee on the gravel. I immediately washed the wound with clean water to remove dirt particles, applied gentle direct pressure with a clean cotton gauze to stop the bleeding, disinfected it with an antiseptic wipe, and applied a sterile adhesive plaster from our school first aid kit.
qp 3 | What will you do if someone cuts his foot and is bleeding?
sol If someone cuts their foot and is bleeding, you should: 1. R ## If someone cuts their foot and is bleeding, you should: 1. Remain calm and reassure the injured person. 2. Place a clean cloth or sterile gauze pad directly over the cut and apply steady, direct pressure to halt blood flow. 3. Elevate the wounded foot above the level of the heart if possible to reduce blood pressure at the wound site. 4. Call emergency medical services if the cut is deep or bleeding does not stop after 10 minutes.
qp 4 | You're having lunch with a friend and he begins to choke on some food. How can you help?
sol First, verify whether the person is truly choking. If he can ## First, verify whether the person is truly choking. If he can talk, cough, or breathe, encourage him to cough forcefully to expel the food. If he cannot talk, cough, or make any sound (airway fully blocked), perform the Heimlich maneuver (abdominal thrusts): - Stand behind him and wrap your arms around his waist. - Make a fist with one hand and place it just above his navel. - Grasp your fist with the other hand and perform quick, upward and inward thrusts until the lodged food is dislodged.
qp 5 | A passenger travelling in bus with you is complaining of breathlessness and a tight pain in the chest. You suspect a heart attack. What should you do?
sol If a passenger exhibits symptoms of a suspected heart attack ## If a passenger exhibits symptoms of a suspected heart attack: 1. Immediately alert the bus driver to call emergency ambulance services or divert to the nearest hospital. 2. Help the passenger into a comfortable half-sitting posture with head and shoulders well-supported and knees bent to reduce strain on the heart. 3. Loosen tight clothing around the neck and chest, ensure good ventilation, and keep the person calm and reassured until medical personnel take over.
q 2 | Textbook Mcqs
qp 1 | A burn should be immersed or placed under cold water for at least (a) a. 5 minutes. (b) b. 10 minutes. (c) c. 15 minutes. (d) d. 20 minutes.
sol d. 20 minutes. ## According to the dialogue, running cool (not ice-cold) water over a minor burn for about 20 minutes cools the damaged tissues and prevents deep burning.
qp 2 | If your friend cut his finger and it bleeds profusely, you should (a) a. put the injured finger in some cold water. (b) b. apply direct pressure over the wound. (c) c. give him an aspirin. (d) d. let him bleed till emergency doctor arrives.
sol b. apply direct pressure over the wound. ## Applying direct continuous pressure with a clean cloth over the wound stops blood loss effectively until professional help arrives.
qp 3 | If one's nose bleeds, he should (a) a. lie upside down. (b) b. run towards home. (c) c. give jerks to one's head. (d) d. press his/her nostrils in finger and thumb.
sol d. press his/her nostrils in finger and thumb. ## Sitting up straight, leaning slightly forward, and pinching the soft part of nostrils with finger and thumb for 10 minutes controls epistaxis (nosebleeds).
qp 4 | Which one of the following should be avoided if someone gets a heart attack: (a) a. call medical help as soon as possible. (b) b. ease the strain on the heart. (c) c. rest him/her in half sitting position. (d) d. press his/her chest.
sol d. press his/her chest. ## Unless trained in CPR for cardiac arrest when there is no pulse, unguided heavy chest pressing during a conscious heart attack must be avoided; the patient needs a supported half-sitting position and immediate emergency medical services.
