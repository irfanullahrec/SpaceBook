q 6 | Present Perfect Continuous A
qp 1 | Why is Ali at night school? (learn, french)
sol Solution ## Answer: He has been learning how to speak French. · Rule: Subject 'He' takes 'has been' + 'learning' (action in progress).
qp 2 | Why hasn't Abrar done his homework? (take, dogs, walk)
sol Solution ## Answer: He has been taking the dogs for a walk. · Rule: Subject 'He' takes 'has been' + 'taking' (ongoing activity).
qp 3 | Why did Nawab buy you a present? (help, project)
sol Solution ## Answer: I have been helping him with his project. · Rule: Subject 'I' takes 'have been' + 'helping' (continuous helpful action).
qp 4 | Why is Shehla busy on Saturday? (go, cooking lesson)
sol Solution ## Answer: She has been going to a cooking lesson. · Rule: Subject 'She' takes 'has been' + 'going'.
qp 5 | Why are you so angry? (wait, four hours)
sol Solution ## Answer: I have been waiting for four hours. · Rule: Subject 'I' takes 'have been' + 'waiting' + duration 'for four hours'.
qp 6 | How long has Gul Khan been an artist? (paint, since high school)
sol Solution ## Answer: He has been painting since high school. · Rule: Subject 'He' takes 'has been' + 'painting' + starting point 'since high school'.
### Past Perfect Continuous Tense
q 7 | Examples
qp 1 | She had been studying in that university since 2008.
sol Solution ## Tense: past perfect continuous (had been + studying). · The action went on in the past up to another past time; "since 2008" is the starting point.
qp 2 | He had not been eating vegetables for three years.
sol Solution ## Tense: past perfect continuous, negative (had not been + eating). · For three years, up to a time in the past, he did not eat vegetables; "for three years" is the length of time.
qp 3 | Had you been helping your parent for two years?
sol Solution ## Tense: past perfect continuous, question (Had + you + been + helping). · The question asks about an action that went on for two years up to a time in the past.
q 8 | Past Perfect Continuous B
qp 1 | She ________ (sleep) for ten hours when I awoke her.
sol Solution ## Solved: She had been sleeping for ten hours when I awoke her. · Verb: had been sleeping · Explanation: Action was in progress in the past for a duration ('for ten hours') before another past event ('when I awoke her').
qp 2 | They ________ (live) in Bannu for three years when he lost his job.
sol Solution ## Solved: They had been living in Bannu for three years when he lost his job. · Verb: had been living · Explanation: Continuous living in Bannu occurred before he lost his job.
qp 3 | They ________ (wait) at the station for 90 minutes when the train finally arrived.
sol Solution ## Solved: They had been waiting at the station for 90 minutes when the train finally arrived. · Verb: had been waiting · Explanation: Ongoing waiting continued until the train's arrival.
qp 4 | They were very tired in the evening because they ________ (help) on the farm all day.
sol Solution ## Solved: They were very tired in the evening because they had been helping on the farm all day. · Verb: had been helping · Explanation: Continuous farming activity caused tiredness in the evening.
qp 5 | Tahir ________ (cycle) so his legs were sore in the evening.
sol Solution ## Solved: Tahir had been cycling so his legs were sore in the evening. · Verb: had been cycling · Explanation: Prolonged cycling in the past resulted in sore legs.
