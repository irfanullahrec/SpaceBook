q 7 | Pronouns Multiple Choice
qp 1 | We all told the boss that we wanted to have our salaries paid in cash, but he just ignored _____. (a) us (b) we (c) our (d) his
sol (a) us ## 'us' is the objective pronoun referring to 'we'.
qp 2 | When the man asked me how I had got his address, I told him that I was given it by a relative of _____. (a) me (b) his (c) he (d) him
sol (b) his ## 'his' is the possessive pronoun used in double possessive 'a relative of his'.
qp 3 | Although no one in the room seemed to follow _____ said by the speaker, he never intended to simplify his language. (a) anything (b) anybody (c) nothing (d) anyone
sol (a) anything ## 'anything' is the indefinite pronoun referring to words/content spoken.
qp 4 | I hope you will enjoy yourself at the re-union party this weekend because I won't be able to be there _____. (a) myself (b) mine (c) oneself (d) yourself
sol (a) myself ## 'myself' is the reflexive pronoun emphasizing the subject 'I'.
qp 5 | We decided to do all the cooking _____ instead of hiring a catering company for the party. (a) of our own (b) oneself (c) by ourselves (d) ours
sol (c) by ourselves ## 'by ourselves' is the correct idiom meaning without external help.
q 8 | Relative Pronouns
qp 1 | The shop ________ I go to is closed.
sol Solution ## Answers: which / that, where · Solved: The shop <b>that / which</b> I go to is closed.
qp 2 | Your shop, ________ will probably be shut down next year, is really old.
sol Solution ## Answers: which · Solved: Your shop, <b>which</b> will probably be shut down next year, is really old.
qp 3 | I've met most of the people ________ are eager to take part in the competition.
sol Solution ## Answers: who / that · Solved: I've met most of the people <b>who / that</b> are eager to take part in the competition.
qp 4 | Do you know the reason ________ our offer was rejected?
sol Solution ## Answers: why / that · Solved: Do you know the reason <b>why</b> our offer was rejected?
qp 5 | Do you remember the town ________ you were born in?
sol Solution ## Answers: which / that / where · Solved: Do you remember the town <b>which / that</b> you were born in?
