# Unit 3 — Quaid - A Great Leader || یونٹ 3 — قائدِ اعظم — ایک عظیم رہنما || 3 څپرکی — قائدِ اعظم — یو ستر لارښود
p After reading the unit the students will be able to: || اس یونٹ کو پڑھنے کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || د دې څپرکي له لوستلو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict the content of a text from topic / picture / title / headings, key words and visuals etc. by using prior knowledge, asking questions and contextual clues.
slo analyze passages in the text to identify the theme / general subject, key idea / central thought (a statement about general subject), and supporting details.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text and use intensive reading strategies to scan to answer short questions.
slo use summary skills to extract salient points and develop a mind map to summarize a text.
slo apply rules for use of articles (a / an / the) wherever applicable in speech and writing.
slo illustrate use and functions of all modal verbs.
slo illustrate use of regular and irregular verbs in speech and writing.
slo illustrate use of transitive and intransitive verbs.
slo use dictionary to: choose appropriate word definition; identify part of speech; locate synonyms in a thesaurus.
slo translate passages from English to Urdu: use the knowledge of literal and figurative meaning, grammatical gender and syntax to translate passage from English to Urdu; understand that most phrases and idioms do not translate literally from one language to another.
