# Unit 7 — The Two Bargains || یونٹ 7 — دو تاریخی سودے || 7 څپرکی — دوه تاریخي معاملې (د حضرت عثمان رضي الله عنه سخاوت)
p After reading the unit the students will be able to: || اس یونٹ کو پڑھنے کے بعد طلبہ اس قابل ہوں گے کہ: || د دې څپرکي له لوستلو وروسته به زده کوونکي وکولای شي چې:
slo use pre-reading strategies to predict the content of a text from topic / picture / title / headings, key words and visuals etc. by using prior knowledge, asking questions and contextual clues.
slo analyze passages in the text to identify the theme / general subject, key idea / central thought (a statement about general subject), and supporting details.
slo apply critical thinking to interact with text and use intensive reading strategies to scan to answer short questions.
slo use summary skills to extract salient points and develop a mind map to summarize a text.
slo illustrate use of adverbs learnt earlier.
slo recognize varying positions of adverbs in sentence according to their kind and importance.
slo identify and use degrees of comparison of adverbs.
slo use adverbials phrases and clauses.
slo use dictionary to: choose appropriate word definition; identify part of speech; recognize abbreviation used in a dictionary.
slo comprehend a text and answer questions on it.
