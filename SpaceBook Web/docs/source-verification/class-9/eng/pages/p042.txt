@sec 4-s1 | Stanza 1: The Golden Host beside the Lake || پہلا بند: جھیل کے کنارے سنہرے پھولوں کا ہجوم || لومړۍ بند: د جهيل تر څنګ د نرګس ګلانو زړه راښکونکی لښکر
## Reading || مطالعہ || لوستل
p I wandered lonely as a cloud
p That floats on high o'er vales and hills,
p When all at once I saw a crowd,
p A host, of golden daffodils;
p Beside the lake, beneath the trees,
p Fluttering and dancing in the breeze.

@sec 4-s2 | Stanza 2: The Continuous Milky Way of Flowers || دوسرا بند: کہکشاں کی طرح پھیلی ہوئی قطار || دویم بند: لکه د کهکشان ستوري او ښایسته نڅا
p Continuous as the stars that shine
p And twinkle on the Milky Way,
p They stretched in never-ending line
p Along the margin of a bay:
p Ten thousand saw I at a glance,
p Tossing their heads in sprightly dance.

@sec 4-s3 | Stanza 3: Outdoing the Sparkling Waves in Glee || تیسرا بند: موجوں پر سبقت اور مسرت کی صحبت || دریم بند: د څپو ماتول او د خوشحالۍ ملګرتیا
p The waves beside them danced, but they
p Out-did the sparkling waves in glee:
p A Poet could not but be gay,
p In such a jocund company:
p I gazed—and gazed—but little thought
p What wealth the show to me had brought:

@sec 4-s4 | Stanza 4: The Inward Eye & The Bliss of Solitude || چوتھا بند: چشمِ تصور اور تنہائی کی روحانی راحت || څلورم بند: باطني سترګه او د یوازیتوب ستر نعمت
p For oft, when on my couch I lie
p In vacant or in pensive mood,
p They flash upon that inward eye
p Which is the bliss of solitude;
p And then my heart with pleasure fills,
p And dances with the daffodils.
