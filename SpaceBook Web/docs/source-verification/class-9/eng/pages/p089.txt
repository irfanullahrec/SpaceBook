q 9 | Literary Devices Specify
qp 1 | I wandered lonely as a cloud.
sol Solution ## Device: Simile · Reason: Uses 'as' to compare the solitary poet to a wandering cloud.
qp 2 | He stirred his Velvet Head.
sol Solution ## Device: Metaphor & Imagery · Reason: Directly refers to the bird's soft feathers as velvet; appeals to touch.
qp 3 | Even if they are a crowd of sorrows,
sol Solution ## Device: Metaphor · Reason: Directly compares multiple sorrows to a crowd without 'like' or 'as'.
qp 4 | I faked a small smile and then shifted away.
sol Solution ## Device: Imagery · Reason: Visual imagery depicting facial expression and body movement.
qp 5 | Which is the bliss of solitude;
sol Solution ## Device: Metaphor · Reason: Identifies peaceful isolation as blissful joy.
qp 6 | They looked like frightened Beads, I thought-
sol Solution ## Device: Simile · Reason: Uses 'like' to compare eyes to frightened round beads.
qp 7 | A host of golden daffodils;
sol Solution ## Device: Imagery & Personification · Reason: Visual imagery evoking bright yellow flowers assembled as a welcoming army.
qp 8 | This being human is a guest house.
sol Solution ## Device: Metaphor · Reason: Directly states the human experience is a temporary guest house.
qp 9 | The weed before me was dying or dead.
sol Solution ## Device: Imagery · Reason: Visual imagery of decay and mortality in nature.
qp 10 | Ten thousand saw I at a glance,
sol Solution ## Device: Hyperbole & Imagery · Reason: Poetic exaggeration creating vivid visual abundance.
