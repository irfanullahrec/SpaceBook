## B. Persuasive Essay
## Structure
p I. Introduction
p 1. Topic and controversial issue introduced effectively
p 2. Both sides of opinions presented with brief reason
p 3. Your own position stated clearly
p 4. Three supporting reasons stated in parallel structure
p II. Body
p Paragraph 1
p 1. Topic sentence (stating 1st reason) presented clearly
p 2. Examples sufficient, relevant and specific
p 3. Closing remarks relating the examples to topic sentence
p Paragraph 2
p 1. Topic sentence (stating 2nd reason) presented clearly
p 2. Examples sufficient, relevant and specific
p 3. Closing remarks relating the examples to topic sentence
p Paragraph 3
p 1. Topic sentence (stating 3rd reason) presented clearly
p 2. Examples sufficient, relevant and specific
p 3. Closing remarks relating the examples to topic sentence
p III. Conclusion
p 1. Three reasons summed up in a unique way
p 2. Point out solution or call for action, giving the reader a sense of ending
q C | Write a persuasive/argumentative essay on Preservation of Nature.
sol Model answer ## Introduction: nature is being damaged by cutting forests, pollution and waste; some say development matters more, others say nature must come first; I believe we must preserve nature for three reasons. Body: (1) clean air, water and food depend on nature, with examples such as trees and rivers; (2) animals and plants keep the balance of life and are the heritage of future generations; (3) floods and climate change grow worse when forests and wetlands are lost. Conclusion: nature, health and our future depend on it, so everyone should plant trees, save water and avoid waste. Other essays that follow this structure are possible.
