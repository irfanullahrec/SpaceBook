## Writing
q A | Compare Madina Charter with the UN Human Rights Charter.
sol Model answer ## Both documents guarantee equality of citizens, freedom of religion and protection of minorities and the oppressed. The Madina Charter (622 CE) was the first written constitution that gave Muslims, Jews and others one community with equal rights and duties; the UN Charter of human rights (1948) is a worldwide declaration. Other valid comparisons are possible.
q B | Develop a mind map and write down the summary of the lesson “The Madina Charter”.
sol Model answer ## Mind map: centre "The Madina Charter" — branches: background (migration, the Muhajireen and the Ansar), main clauses (one community, equality, freedom of religion, mutual defence, justice), importance (peace, social harmony). Summary: the Prophet (ﷺ) drew up a charter that united the people of Madina, guaranteed everyone's rights and religious freedom and made all responsible for defending the city. Other valid mind maps and summaries are possible.
q C | Read the passage and answer the following questions.
p When Hazrat Muhammad (ﷺ) migrated to Medina, this city was inhabited by different sections of people. They were the faithful followers of Hazrat Muhammad (ﷺ) who had abandoned the ties of kith and kin and followed him (ﷺ). They were known as Muhajireen or “Refugees”. The new converts of Medina who had helped the Rasool (ﷺ) through thick and thin were known as Ansar or “Helpers”.
qp I | State whether the reference in the first sentence is anaphoric or cataphoric?
sol Anaphoric ## "this city" refers back to Medina, which was named earlier in the same sentence.
qp II | Locate at least two transitive and two intransitive verbs.
sol Transitive: abandoned (the ties), followed (him), helped (the Rasool). Intransitive: migrated, were known (passive: "known as"). ## A transitive verb takes an object; an intransitive verb does not. Other verbs of the passage may also be given.
qp III | What is the main idea of the passage?
sol The migrants (Muhajireen) who left everything for the Prophet (ﷺ) and the helpers of Medina (Ansar) who supported him through good and bad times made up the community of Medina. ## Model answer: the passage tells who the Muhajireen and the Ansar were.
qp IV | Identify the tense of the passage.
sol Past simple (migrated, was, had abandoned: past perfect inside the story) ## The passage describes events that happened in the past.
