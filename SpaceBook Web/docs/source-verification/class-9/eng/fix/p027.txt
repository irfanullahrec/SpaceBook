## Writing
q A | Discuss Iqbal’s youth with reference to today’s Pakistani youth. Write a paragraph of at least 100 words.
sol Model answer ## Allama Iqbal saw the youth as the hope of the nation: brave, hard-working, honest and full of faith, like the shaheen (eagle) that flies high and builds no nest. He wanted them to know their own worth (khudi), to love knowledge and to serve Pakistan. Many young Pakistanis today are talented and active in education, sport and technology, but some waste time on useless things or look for easy ways. If today’s youth follow Iqbal’s message – work hard, stay honest, keep high ideals and never give up – they can make Pakistan strong and prosperous. Other paragraphs on this topic are possible.
