box teach | Transitional Devices & Connectors of Cause, Effect, and Contrast
p Transitional words link paragraphs and sentences cohesively: - Cause & Effect: therefore, consequently, as a result, hence ('The pipes leaked; *consequently*, the water was contaminated'). - Contrast: however, nevertheless, on the other hand, whereas ('Complaints were filed; *however*, no action was taken'). - Addition: furthermore, moreover, in addition ('*Moreover*, the power outages damaged laboratory appliances').
- Furthermore, open drains breed dangerous mosquitoes.
- Consequently, schools were forced to close early.
- However, the authorities remained indifferent.
endbox
box teach | Subject-Verb Inversion after Negative Adverbials
p When sentences begin with negative or restrictive adverbs (rarely, seldom, nowhere, not only), the auxiliary verb precedes the subject ('Rarely *have we witnessed* such administrative negligence').
- Not only did they ignore complaints, but they also delayed repairs.
- Seldom has the community faced such acute load-shedding.
endbox
