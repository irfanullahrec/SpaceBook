q 3 | Choose the correct answer for the following.
qp 1 | "The faithful followers of Hazrat Muhammad (ﷺ) who had abandoned the ties of kith and kin and followed him..." In the sentence above, the phrase 'abandoned the ties' means that they: (a) held onto relationships (b) kept relationships (c) gave up relationships (d) maintained relationships
sol (c) gave up relationships ## 'Abandoned the ties' means relinquishing or giving up family and tribal relationships for the sake of following Islam.
qp 2 | "The new converts of Medina who had helped Hazrat Muhammad (ﷺ) through thick and thin were known as Ansar..." In the sentence above, 'new converts of Medina' refers to: (a) the people of Medina, who newly accepted Islam (b) the people who newly migrated to Medina (c) the people of Medina, who newly refused to accept Islam (d) the people who left Medina to accept Islam
sol (a) the people of Medina, who newly accepted Islam ## 'New converts of Medina' refers to the native inhabitants of Yathrib (Medina) who embraced Islam upon the Prophet's arrival.
qp 3 | "The Muslims, the Jews and other communities of this republic should be free to profess their own respective religion." The opposite word of the word 'profess' in this sentence is: (a) own up (b) give up (c) put up (d) take up
sol (b) give up ## 'Profess' means to openly declare, practice, or affirm; its opposite is to 'give up', renounce, or disown.
qp 4 | Which of the following is not a clause of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights by the UN? (a) We are all free and equal, we are all born free. (b) We all have the right to live, and to live in freedom and safety. (c) Everybody has the right to hurt or to torture each other. (d) Education is a right, primary education should be free.
sol (c) Everybody has the right to hurt or to torture each other. ## Clause 4 explicitly affirms: 'Nobody has any right to hurt us or to torture us.' Torturing each other is completely prohibited.
qp 5 | "The oppressed should be protected." The word 'oppressed' in this sentence means the: (a) free (b) ill-treated (c) untroubled (d) exempted
sol (b) ill-treated ## 'Oppressed' means people who are subjected to harsh, unjust, or cruel treatment (ill-treated / victimized).

### Analytical Report: Comparing the Madina Charter (622 CE) with the United Nations Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948)
p A comparative analysis between the Madina Charter (622 CE) and the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR, 1948) reveals extraordinary parallels. While separated by more than thirteen centuries, both landmark documents establish fundamental human rights, judicial equality, and the rejection of discrimination. The Madina Charter preceded the UDHR by explicitly guaranteeing freedom of religious conscience (Article 18 UDHR), personal security and protection from arbitrary arrest (Article 9 UDHR), equal protection under the law (Article 7 UDHR), and collective civic responsibility for peacekeeping (Article 29 UDHR). The genius of the Prophet's constitution lay in implementing these progressive ideals within a volatile 7th-century tribal desert, establishing a peaceful, pluralistic state that remains a beacon for international constitutional law.
