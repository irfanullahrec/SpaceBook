q 17 | Exercise 9: Articles Activity (a, an, the, X) Fill in the blanks with the article 'a', 'an' or 'the' where necessary. Put a cross (X) where no article is used.
qp 1 | Do you live in ________ Peshawar?
sol Solution ## Solved: Do you live in <b>[X]</b> Peshawar. · Article: X (No Article) · Rule: Proper nouns of cities take no article.
qp 2 | He is ________ teacher who taught us English.
sol Solution ## Solved: He is <b>the</b> teacher who taught us English. · Article: the · Rule: Definite article 'the' is used because the relative clause specifies the teacher.
qp 3 | I bought ________ umbrella.
sol Solution ## Solved: I bought <b>an</b> umbrella. · Article: an · Rule: 'Umbrella' starts with a vowel sound (/ʌ/).
qp 4 | He has ________ lovely cat.
sol Solution ## Solved: He has <b>a</b> lovely cat. · Article: a · Rule: 'Lovely' starts with a consonant sound (/l/).
