## Vocabulary
## Imagery
p Imagery means to use figurative language to represent objects, actions, and ideas in such a way that it appeals to our physical senses.
p The word “imagery” is associated with imaginary pictures that we draw in our minds, by use of particular words that create visual representation of ideas.
p Example:
p 1. It was dark and dim in the forest.
p The words “dark” and “dim” appeal to our sense of sight.
p 2. The children were screaming and shouting in the fields.
p “Screaming” and “shouting” appeal to our sense of hearing.
p 3. He smelled the aroma of coffee.
p “Smelled” and “aroma” appeal to our sense of smell.
