## Writing
q A | Write description of Chitral Valley with the help of the following mind map.
p Mind map: Describing Places — observe carefully · mention the place and people · be selective about description and events · be specific · give significance · mention interesting things · choose words carefully
sol Model answer ## Chitral Valley lies in the north of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa among high mountains. The air is cool and clean, the rivers are clear, and the green orchards and snow-covered peaks of the Hindu Kush rise behind the villages. The people are friendly and proud of their culture, music and the Kalash valleys nearby. The valley matters because it is a place of beauty, peace and old traditions. Other descriptions that use the mind map are possible.
