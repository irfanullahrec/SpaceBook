## How to write an autobiography?
p 1. Choose a story that you really want to tell like winning an award, getting a job, first day in school, an injury, losing a loved one, discovering a hobby, meeting your best friend, going away to camp, or learning something about yourself.
p 2. Generate ideas for your autobiography through brainstorming or mind map and write an outline.
p 3. Use vivid details to describe the setting of your autobiography.
p 4. Include clear statement of personality traits at that time.
p 5. Include significant details and an incident used to portray personally.
p 6. Use the first person perspective (I, me, my, mine) when writing your autobiography.
p 7. Consider organizing your story in a chronological manner.

## Sentence Structure
p Independent clause: An independent clause can stand alone as a sentence. It contains a subject and a verb and is a complete idea.
p • I like spaghetti.
p • He reads many books.
p Dependent clause: A dependent clause is not a complete sentence. It must be attached to an independent clause to become complete. This is also known as a subordinate clause.
p • Although I like spaghetti,...
p • Because he reads many books,...
p Subject: A person, animal, place, thing, or concept that does an action.
p Linking verb: A linking verb is a verb that joins the subject of a sentence to the complement. It is sometimes called a copula or a copular verb.
