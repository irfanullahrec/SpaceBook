q 2 | How data is represented in a computer, discuss briefly? || کمپیوٹر میں ڈیٹا کیسے ظاہر ہوتا ہے، مختصراً بیان کریں؟ || په کمپیوټر کې ډېټا څنګه ښودل کېږي، په لنډه توګه یې وڅېړئ؟
sol Model answer ## A computer stores all data (numbers, text, pictures, sound) as binary digits, 0 and 1 (bits), because its circuits have two states, off and on. 8 bits make a byte. Text is coded with ASCII/Unicode, numbers in the binary system, pictures as pixels (bits for colours) and sound as sampled binary values.
q 3 | What are the three basic gates? Draw their circuit diagrams and truth table? || تین بنیادی گیٹس کون سے ہیں؟ ان کے سرکٹ ڈایاگرام اور ٹروتھ ٹیبل بنائیں؟ || درې بنسټیزې دروازې کومې دي؟ سرکټ ډیاګرامونه او truth tables یې رسم کړئ؟
sol Model answer ## The three basic gates are AND, OR and NOT. AND: output 1 only when all inputs are 1 (A B: 00 gives 0, 01 gives 0, 10 gives 0, 11 gives 1). OR: output 1 when any input is 1 (00 gives 0, 01 gives 1, 10 gives 1, 11 gives 1). NOT: output is the inverse of the single input (0 gives 1, 1 gives 0). Circuit symbols: AND is D-shaped, OR is curved-D shaped and NOT is a triangle with a small circle.
q 4 | Convert the following Boolean expression to logic gate. f(A, B, C) = ABC+ ĀB̄ + C̄ + ĀB || درج ذیل بولین اظہار کو لاجک گیٹ میں بدلیں۔ f(A, B, C) = ABC+ ĀB̄ + C̄ + ĀB || لاندې بولین افاده منطقي دروازې ته واړوئ. f(A, B, C) = ABC+ ĀB̄ + C̄ + ĀB
sol Model answer ## f = ABC + A'B' + C' + A'B. Simplify: A'B' + A'B = A', so f = ABC + A' + C'. Since A' + ABC = A' + BC and C' + BC = C' + B, f = A' + B + C'. Gate diagram: NOT on A, NOT on C, and a three-input OR gate that takes A', B and C' (or the original expression with AND gates for ABC, A'B' and A'B joined to an OR gate with C').
