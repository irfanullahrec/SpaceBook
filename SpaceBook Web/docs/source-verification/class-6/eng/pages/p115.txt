q 2 | Fill in the blanks with the correct phrasal verb. || درست فعلِ مرکب سے خالی جگہیں پُر کریں۔ || تشې په سم مرکب فعل ډکې کړئ.
qp a | If he doesn't stop working so hard, he'll ________ himself ________.
sol burn … out ## "burn himself out" = become extremely tired. || بہت تھک جانا || ډېر ستړی کېدل
qp b | The contract was ________ last year.
sol drawn up ## || تیار کیا گیا || چمتو شو
qp c | The meeting was ________ at the last minute.
sol called off ## || منسوخ کر دی گئی || لغوه شوه
qp d | We need to ________ more research before we decide on this product.
sol carry out ## || کرنا || ترسره کول
qp e | You must all ________ your projects by the end of next week.
sol hand in ## || جمع کرانا || سپارل
qp f | I am going to have to ________ to some serious study.
sol knuckle down / get down (none of the listed verbs fits exactly; "work out" is the book's option for "start working harder") ## || سنجیدگی سے محنت شروع کرنا || په جدیت سره کار پیلول
qp g | The new management decided to ________ a number of workers from the factory.
sol lay off ## || ملازمت سے نکالنا || له دندې ایستل
qp h | His father intended that he should ________ the business soon.
sol take over ## || سنبھالنا || واک اخیستل
qp i | You are a new employee. You will be ________ as a trainee.
sol taken on ## || ملازم رکھا جانا || استخدامېدل
qp j | You need to ________ by your line manager first.
sol run it by ## "run it by your line manager" = tell them so they can give their opinion. || منیجر سے رائے لینا || له مدیر نظر اخیستل
