## Dr. Dania Qazi || ڈاکٹر دانیہ قاضی || ډاکټر دانیه قاضي
p Dr. Dania Qazi is a distinguished educator and prolific author with over two decades of expertise in the field of education. From teaching and training to supervising and consulting, her influence spans across all levels of education, from early years to Higher Education. Dr. Dania has written over 70 educational textbooks and materials, which have been widely used by schools in Pakistan and beyond. Her dedication to advancing educational discourse is further evidenced by her extensive research contributions. Dr. Dania Qazi has authored and presented numerous research papers, both domestically and internationally, covering a wide spectrum of topics including education, leadership, management, and professional development. ## Rabia Sajjad || رابعہ سجاد || رابعه سجاد

She holds a Masters Degree in English Language Teaching and an M.Phil Degree in Education. Ms. Rabia Sajjad has extensively worked in the field of curriculum development and designs standards and assessment frameworks for language teaching.
