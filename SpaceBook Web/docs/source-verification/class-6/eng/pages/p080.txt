# Listening || سننا || اورېدل
exercise 4 Listening
q 1 | Work in pairs. Look at the pictures and answer the questions. || جوڑیوں میں کام کریں۔ تصاویر دیکھیں اور سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || په جوړو کې کار وکړئ. انځورونو ته وګورئ او پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
sol a. The sports are polo (on horses), swimming, archery, running (athletics), tennis and karate. b. Answers will vary: each pupil says which of these sports they have played, for example swimming or running. c. The event is the Olympic Games (the Olympic rings and "Tokyo 2020" can be seen in the pictures). d. The five circles (rings) are the logo of the Olympic Games. The Games are held every four years; athletes from many countries take part and the winners get gold, silver and bronze medals. ## Pair talk: the sports are named from the pictures; b and d are oral and personal, so a sample is given.

qp d | The logo with five circles represent the Olympic Games? Do you know anything about these games? Discuss with your partner and share your thoughts with the whole class. || پانچ دائروں والا نشان اولمپک کھیلوں کی نمائندگی کرتا ہے۔ کیا آپ ان کھیلوں کے بارے میں کچھ جانتے ہیں؟ ساتھی سے گفتگو کریں اور جماعت سے شیئر کریں۔ || د پنځو کړیو نښه د المپیک لوبې ښيي. ایا د دې لوبو په اړه څه پوهېږئ؟ له ملګري سره بحث وکړئ او له ټولګي سره یې شریک کړئ.
sol The Olympics are held every four years; athletes from almost every country compete in many sports; the five rings stand for the five continents joined in friendship. ## Open discussion. || کھلی گفتگو۔ || خلاص بحث.
