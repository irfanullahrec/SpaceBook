q 1 | Match the meanings of the following words. || درج ذیل الفاظ کے معانی ملائیں۔ || د لاندې کلمو ماناوې سمې کړئ.
table
th | Word || لفظ || کلمه | Meaning || معنی || مانا
tr | first aid | not awake and aware of and responding to one's environment
tr | nosebleed | having enough water or moisture in your body to maintain a certain body temperature
tr | heatstroke | help given to a sick or injured person until full medical treatment is available
tr | exhaustion | an attack of bleeding from the nose
tr | unconscious | a condition when exposed to excessively high temperatures
tr | hydrated | a state of extreme physical or mental tiredness.
tr | emergency | a serious, unexpected, and often dangerous situation requiring immediate action.
