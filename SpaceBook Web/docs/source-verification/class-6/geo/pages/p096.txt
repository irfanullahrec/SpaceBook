## 3. Give Detailed Answers to the following questions: || 3۔ تفصیلی جوابات دیں: || 3. تفصیلي ځوابونه ورکړئ:
q i | How do wind, water and glacier cause erosion? Discuss in detail. || ہوا، پانی اور گلیشیئر کٹاؤ کیسے کرتے ہیں؟ تفصیل سے بیان کریں۔ || باد، اوبه او یخچال څنګه تخریب کوي؟ تفصیل ورکړئ.
sol Wind erosion moves dust, sand and soil from place to place, mostly in dry and plain areas and deserts (dust storms); the 1930s dust storms stripped topsoil from the US Great Plains, though wind can also deposit fertile loess (Yellow River basin). Water erosion — rain, rivers, streams and sea waves are the most important agent; flowing water cuts into soil and rock and wears down and shapes landforms (Swat valley, Grand Canyon). Glacial erosion occurs in cold areas; moving glaciers carve U-shaped valleys, form lakes and wear down mountain tops (Khaplu valley). ## || ہوا: گرد اور ریت؛ پانی: سب سے اہم، وادیاں اور کینین؛ گلیشیئر: سرد علاقوں میں وادیاں اور جھیلیں۔ || باد: دوړې او شګې؛ اوبه: تر ټولو مهم؛ یخچال: درې او ډنډونه.
q ii | Define weathering and explain the difference between physical and chemical weathering. || شکست و ریخت کی تعریف کریں اور طبعی اور کیمیائی شکست و ریخت کا فرق بیان کریں۔ || سولېدل تعریف کړئ او د فزیکي او کیمیاوي سولېدو توپیر وښیاست.
sol Weathering is the breaking down of rocks into smaller pieces in their place on the Earth's surface by agents of weathering. Physical (mechanical) weathering breaks rocks without any chemical change — e.g. water freezes and expands in cracks and splits the rock. Chemical weathering changes the rock's composition — minerals react with water, oxygen and carbon dioxide to form new substances (decomposition), e.g. forming most natural caves. ## || طبعی: بغیر کیمیائی تبدیلی کے ٹوٹنا؛ کیمیائی: ترکیب بدل جانا۔ || فزیکي: بې کیمیاوي بدلونه ماتېدل؛ کیمیاوي: ترکیب بدلېږي.
q iii | How do human activities contribute to erosion? || انسانی سرگرمیاں کٹاؤ میں کیسے حصہ ڈالتی ہیں؟ || انساني فعالیتونه څنګه تخریب زیاتوي؟
sol Human activities cause about 10 times more erosion than natural processes: deforestation exposes soil and upsets nutrients; agriculture (ploughing, harvesting, chemicals, intensive use) strips fertile topsoil and causes desertification; industry and mining clear vegetation and can speed erosion several times; urbanization clears land for roads, buildings and housing schemes, reducing forests and cultivable land and threatening food security. ## || جنگلات کی کٹائی، زراعت، صنعت و کان کنی اور شہر کاری۔ || ځنګل وهل، کرنه، صنعت او ښارتوب.
endexercise
