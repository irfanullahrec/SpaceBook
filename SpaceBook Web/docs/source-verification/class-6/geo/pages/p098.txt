She completed her master's degree in geography from the University of Punjab and also achieved a role-of-merit. She holds a certificate of merit in professional studies from the University of Bradford. She attended many workshops held by The University of Cambridge International examination She has the experience of preparing Geography curriculum for middle school and 'O' levels.
