## 3. Give detailed answers to the following questions: || 3۔ تفصیلی جوابات دیں: || 3. تفصیلي ځوابونه ورکړئ:
q i | Why are developing countries more affected by natural disasters than developed countries? || ترقی پذیر ممالک ترقی یافتہ ممالک کے مقابلے میں قدرتی آفات سے زیادہ کیوں متاثر ہوتے ہیں؟ || ولې پرمختیایي هېوادونه تر پرمختللو ډېر زیانمنېږي؟
sol (1) Poverty — many people live in unsafe places (riverbanks, steep slopes, flood plains, crowded illegal settlements); disasters push about 26 million people into extreme poverty each year. (2) Lack of resources — few early-warning systems and poorly equipped, untrained disaster management. (3) Poor governance — weak planning and implementation; development funds diverted to relief; buildings not built to withstand disasters; unreliable water, power, food and medicine supplies. So most deaths (about 80%) occur in developing countries. ## || غربت، وسائل کی کمی اور کمزور حکمرانی۔ || بې وزلي، د سرچینو کمښت او کمزوره حکومتولي.
q ii | How do human activities contribute to natural disasters? Explain. || انسانی سرگرمیاں قدرتی آفات میں کیسے حصہ ڈالتی ہیں؟ وضاحت کریں۔ || انساني فعالیتونه څنګه ناورین زیاتوي؟ تشریح کړئ.
sol Overpopulation, deforestation, growing settlements and burning fossil fuels raise greenhouse gases and cause global warming, which changes weather patterns — more heatwaves, cyclones, floods and forest fires, and melting glaciers raise sea level. Deforestation also causes soil erosion and landslides; chemical fertilisers, ploughing and poor irrigation degrade land and bring drought; overgrazing causes desertification; industry pollutes air, water and land; and dams releasing excess water in heavy rain can cause floods. ## || آبادی، جنگلات کی کٹائی، فوسل ایندھن، زیادہ چرائی، صنعت اور ڈیم۔ || نفوس، ځنګل وهل، فوسیلي سون، ډېر څرول، صنعت او بندونه.
q iii | What measures can be taken to reduce the impact of natural disasters? || قدرتی آفات کے اثرات کم کرنے کے لیے کیا اقدامات کیے جا سکتے ہیں؟ || د ناورین د اغېز د کمولو لپاره کوم ګامونه پورته کېدای شي؟
sol Preparedness — early-warning systems for floods, storms and tsunamis; awareness campaigns and mock drills. Response — quick evacuation, urgent medical help, technology such as drones to find survivors. Rehabilitation and recovery — restore clean water, food and shelter; seek international help. Prevention and mitigation — better bridges, roads, dams and levees; strict land-use and building-design rules; use satellite information. ## || تیاری، ردِ عمل، بحالی، روک تھام۔ || چمتووالی، غبرګون، بیا رغونه، مخنیوی.
endexercise
