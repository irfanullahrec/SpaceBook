## 3. Give detailed answers to the following questions: || 3۔ تفصیلی جوابات دیں: || 3. تفصیلي ځوابونه ورکړئ:
q i | Name three types of plateaus and explain how they were formed. Give one example of each. || سطح مرتفع کی تین اقسام کے نام لکھ کر بتائیں کہ وہ کیسے بنیں؛ ہر ایک کی ایک مثال دیں۔ || د هوارو درې ډولونه ونوموئ، جوړېدل یې تشریح کړئ او بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol (1) Intermontane plateau — enclosed by mountains, raised between ranges as they formed; e.g. Tibetan Plateau (also Bolivian). (2) Piedmont plateau — mountains on one side and a plain or ocean on the other, formed at the foot of mountains; e.g. Potwar Plateau (also Patagonia). (3) Volcanic plateau — built by successive sheets of molten lava spreading over a vast area; e.g. Deccan Plateau of India. ## || بین الجبالی (تبت)، دامنِ کوہ (پوٹھوہار)، آتش فشانی (دکن)۔ || د غرونو منځنۍ (تبت)، لمنه (پوټوهار)، اورغورځونکې (دکن).
q ii | What are different types of valleys? Explain any two of them. || وادیوں کی مختلف اقسام کیا ہیں؟ کوئی سی دو بیان کریں۔ || د درو ډولونه کوم دي؟ دوه یې تشریح کړئ.
sol River valleys (V-shaped) — deep, narrow, long depressions cut by the erosion of rivers or streams, e.g. Swat, Kaghan, Neelum valleys. Glacial valleys (U-shaped) — steep sides and a flat floor carved by glaciers, e.g. Baltoro and Hispar valleys. Rift valleys (graben) — formed when rock between two normal faults sinks, with straight parallel walls, e.g. the Great Rift Valley of East Africa. ## || دریائی (V)، برفانی (U) اور شگافی (گرابن) وادیاں۔ || سیندیزې (V)، یخچالي (U) او رفټ درې.
q iii | What are the effects of physical environment on humans? || طبعی ماحول کے انسانوں پر کیا اثرات ہیں؟ || پر انسانانو د طبیعي چاپېریال اغېزې څه دي؟
sol Physical environment gives the resources and base for human activities: mountains provide pastures, forests, tourist spots and rivers; plains are used for farming; plateaus give minerals; climate shapes house types, clothing and food habits and strongly affects vegetation, cropping patterns, livestock and some industries. It also affects lifestyle — e.g. low population and transhumance in mountains, barani farming on the Potwar, Karez irrigation in Balochistan. ## || وسائل، زراعت، معدنیات، رہائش، لباس، خوراک، فصلیں اور طرزِ زندگی۔ || سرچینې، کرنه، منرالونه، کورونه، جامې، خواړه او د ژوند بڼه.
endexercise

@issue The glossary says distribution maps show "aerial distribution"; the correct word is "areal" (by area). In a normal fault the movement is mainly vertical/down-dip along a sloping fault plane. Recorded as printed.
