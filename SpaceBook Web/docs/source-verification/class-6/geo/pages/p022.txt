## 3. Give detailed answers to the following questions: || 3۔ تفصیلی جوابات دیں: || 3. تفصیلي ځوابونه ورکړئ:
q i | Explain theory of plate tectonic in detail. || پلیٹ ٹیکٹونک نظریہ تفصیل سے بیان کریں۔ || د پلیټ ټکټونیک نظریه تفصیل سره تشریح کړئ.
sol About 250 million years ago there was one supercontinent, Pangaea (idea of Alfred Wegener); internal movements broke it into today's continents and oceans. The plate tectonic theory (McKenzie and Parker, 1967) says the lithosphere is divided into large blocks — tectonic plates — that float over the asthenosphere and are moved by currents of hot, plastic rock in it, at 0–100 mm a year. Plates are continental (light, thick) or oceanic (heavier, thinner). Seven major plates: Pacific, North American, Eurasian, African, Antarctic, Indo-Australian, South American. At boundaries they converge (collide — mountains and earthquakes, e.g. Himalayas), diverge (move apart — magma rises, e.g. Mid-Atlantic Ridge) or transform (slide past — shallow earthquakes). ## || پینجیا، پلیٹیں ایستھینوسفیئر پر حرکت کرتی ہیں؛ متقارب، متباعد اور ٹرانسفارم سرحدیں۔ || پانجیا؛ پلیټونه پر استینوسفیر خوځېږي؛ درې ډوله پولې.
q ii | Classify different layers of Earth and draw a labelled diagram. || زمین کی مختلف تہوں کی درجہ بندی کر کے نام والا خاکہ بنائیں۔ || د ځمکې پوړونه ډلبندي او رسم کړئ.
sol 1 Crust — outermost solid layer, 8–40 km, mainly silicon and aluminium (SIAL), density 3 g/cm³. 2 Mantle — about 2900 km, very hot dense rock (SIMA), 80% of volume; lithosphere above and asthenosphere (70–250 km, source of magma) below. 3 Core — iron and nickel (NIFE), densest: liquid outer core (about 2250 km) and solid inner core (radius 1220 km). Boundaries: Moho (crust–mantle), Gutenberg (mantle–core). Draw concentric circles labelled crust, mantle, outer core, inner core. ## || قشر، مینٹل اور مرکزہ (بیرونی مائع، اندرونی ٹھوس)۔ || پوښ، مینټل او هسته.
q iii | Rocks play an important role in the economy of a region. Give reasons. || چٹانیں کسی خطے کی معیشت میں اہم کردار ادا کرتی ہیں۔ وجوہات بتائیں۔ || ډبرې د سیمې په اقتصاد کې مهم رول لري؛ دلیلونه ورکړئ.
sol They are raw material for industries (cement, limestone kilns) that create jobs and taxes; they supply building materials (granite, marble, slate); precious stones (Swat emerald, Mardan topaz, Hunza ruby) and petroleum are exported for foreign exchange; rock formations attract tourists (Khewra, Princess of Hope); rock salt gives food minerals; metallic minerals (copper and gold at Reko Diq, iron, chromite) strengthen industry; mining earns foreign exchange. ## || صنعت، تعمیر، قیمتی پتھر، سیاحت، نمک، دھاتیں اور کان کنی۔ || صنعت، ودانۍ، قیمتي ډبرې، ګرځندوی، مالګه، فلزات.
endexercise
