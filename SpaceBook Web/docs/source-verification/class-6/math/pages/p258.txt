# Important Formulas || اہم فارمولے || مهم فورمولونه
eq \text{LCM} = \text{Product of common prime factors of 2 or more numbers} \times \text{Product of non-common prime factors}
eq a \times b = \text{HCF} \times \text{LCM}
eq a + b = b + a
eq (a + b) + c = a + (b + c)
eq (a \times b) \times c = a \times (b \times c)
eq a \times (b + c) = (a \times b) + (a \times c)
eq a \times (b - c) = (a \times b) - (a \times c)
eq a \times (b - c) = (a \times b) - (a \times c)
eq \text{Perimeter of rectangle} = 2(\text{length} + \text{width})
eq \text{Perimeter of a square} = 4 \times L
eq \text{Area of a square} = \text{length} \times \text{length}
eq \text{Area of a rectangle} = \text{length} \times \text{width}
eq \text{Area of border} = \text{Area of large rectangle} - \text{Area of small rectangle}
eq \text{Area of parallelogram} = \text{base} \times \text{altitude}
eq \text{Area of trapezium} = \text{altitude} \times \left(\frac{b_1 + b_2}{2}\right)
eq \text{Area of triangle} = \frac{1}{2}\,(\text{altitude} \times \text{base})
eq \text{Volume of cube} = \text{length} \times \text{length} \times \text{length}
eq \text{Volume of cuboid} = l \times w \times h
eq \text{Volume of cuboid} = l \times w \times h
eq \text{Area of cube} = 6 \times l^2
eq \text{Total surface area of cuboid} = 2 \times [(l \times w) + (l \times h) + (w \times h)]
eq \text{Angle of the sector} = \frac{\text{number of observations of a class}}{\text{total number of observations in all classes}} \times 360^\circ
eq \text{Arithmetic mean} = \frac{\text{sum of values}}{\text{number of values}}
eq \text{Probability of an event} = P(E) = \frac{\text{Number of favorable (desired) outcomes}}{\text{Total number of possible outcomes}}
p Urdu/Pashto: the formulas above use the same symbols in every language. || اردو: اوپر کے فارمولے ہر زبان میں انہی علامتوں سے لکھے جاتے ہیں۔ || پښتو: پورته فورمولونه په هره ژبه کې په همدې نښو لیکل کېږي.
