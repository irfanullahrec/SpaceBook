q 2 | Define the following. || درج ذیل کی تعریف کریں۔ || لاندې تعریف کړئ.
qp a | Transversal || قاطع || قاطع
sol A line that passes through two or more given lines at different points. ## Page 178. || صفحہ 178۔ || پاڼه 178.
qp b | Corresponding angles || متناظر زاویے || متناظرې زاویې
sol Angles in the same position at each crossing when a transversal cuts two lines (for example ∠2 and ∠6). For parallel lines they are equal. ## Page 178. || صفحہ 178۔ || پاڼه 178.
qp c | Alternate angles || متبادل زاویے || متبادلې زاویې
sol A pair of angles on opposite sides of the transversal, both between the lines (alternate interior, e.g. ∠3 and ∠5) or both outside them (alternate exterior, e.g. ∠1 and ∠7). For parallel lines they are equal. ## Page 178. || صفحہ 178۔ || پاڼه 178.
qp d | Parallel lines || متوازی خطوط || موازي کرښې
sol Lines that never meet, however far they are extended in the same direction; the distance between them stays the same. ## Page 174. || صفحہ 174۔ || پاڼه 174.
qp e | Perpendicular lines || عمودی خطوط || عمودي کرښې
sol Lines that intersect each other at 90°. ## Page 175. || صفحہ 175۔ || پاڼه 175.
