example 2 | Simplify: $68 - [6 + 4 \times (35 - 2 \times 12)]$ || سادہ کریں: $68 - [6 + 4 \times (35 - 2 \times 12)]$ || ساده کړئ: $68 - [6 + 4 \times (35 - 2 \times 12)]$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $= 68 - \{6 + 4 \times (35 - 2 \times 12)\}$
m $= 68 - \{6 + 4 \times (35 - 24)\}$
m $= 68 - \{6 + 4 \times 11\}$
m $= 68 - \{6 + 44\}$
m $= 68 - 50 = 18$

example 3 | Simplify: $1213 - [30 \times \{20 + 7 \times 21 \div 7 - (7 + \overline{5 - 3})\}]$ || سادہ کریں: $1213 - [30 \times \{20 + 7 \times 21 \div 7 - (7 + \overline{5 - 3})\}]$ || ساده کړئ: $1213 - [30 \times \{20 + 7 \times 21 \div 7 - (7 + \overline{5 - 3})\}]$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
m $1213 - [30 \times \{20 + 7 \times 21 \div 7 - (7 + \overline{5 - 3})\}]$
m $= 1213 - [30 \times \{20 + 7 \times 21 \div 7 - (7 + 2)\}]$
m $= 1213 - [30 \times \{20 + 7 \times 21 \div 7 - 9\}]$
m $= 1213 - [30 \times \{20 + 7 \times 3 - 9\}]$
m $= 1213 - [30 \times \{20 + 21 - 9\}]$
m $= 1213 - [30 \times \{41 - 9\}]$
m $= 1213 - [30 \times 32]$
m $= 1213 - 960 = 253$
