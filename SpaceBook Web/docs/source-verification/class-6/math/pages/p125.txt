example 3 | $[12a - \{4b - 2(5a - b)\}]$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
eq \begin{aligned} &[12a - \{4b - 2(5a - b)\}] && \leftarrow \text{First solve the operation within parentheses} \\ &= [12a - \{4b - 10a + 2b\}] && \leftarrow \text{Now solve the like terms in curly brackets} \\ &= [12a - \{6b - 10a\}] && \leftarrow \text{Then open the curly bracket and change the sign} \\ &= [12a - 6b + 10a] && \leftarrow \text{Next add the like terms} \\ &= 22a - 6b \end{aligned}
endexample
example 4 | $[4x + \{z - y + (x - \overline{3x + z})\}]$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
eq \begin{aligned} &[4x + \{z - y + (x - \overline{3x + z})\}] && \leftarrow \text{First solve the operation under the vinculum} \\ &= [4x + \{z - y + (x - 3x - z)\}] && \leftarrow \text{Now solve the like terms in parentheses} \\ &= [4x + \{z - y + (-2x - z)\}] && \leftarrow \text{Then remove the parentheses} \\ &= [4x + \{z - y - 2x - z\}] && \leftarrow \text{Next solve the like terms in the curly brackets} \\ &= [4x + \{-y - 2x\}] && \leftarrow \text{Remove the curly brackets} \\ &= [4x - y - 2x] && \leftarrow \text{Next solve the like terms} \\ &= 2x - y \end{aligned}
endexample
