@issue Measurements: the book says the bowling crease is "a 6-ft line 4 ft from the wicket". In the Laws of Cricket the bowling crease runs through the stumps (8 ft 8 in long), and the popping crease is 4 ft in front of the wicket. The stated ground size "75 to 80 yards" is presumably the boundary radius. The figure's labels (وکٹیں، باؤلنگ کریز، پوپنگ کریز، ریٹرن کریز) are small; read from the visible shapes.
