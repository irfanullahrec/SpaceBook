@issue Goalpost: the second line prints "6 فٹ زمین سے متوازی کورٹ کے اندر" (6 ft into the court parallel to the ground). In the rules the ring projects 15 cm (about 6 in) from the post; "فٹ" (feet) appears to be a misprint for inches. The bracketed words in the translation are SpaceBook's.
