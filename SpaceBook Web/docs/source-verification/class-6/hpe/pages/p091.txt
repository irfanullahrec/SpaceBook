@deco A drawing of football players running for the ball, with a goalkeeper's gloved hands.
@issue Measurements: some printed figures are rounded or slightly off — the penalty area is 18 × 44 yd (16.5 × 40.3 m), not "40 × 17 m"; the goal area is 6 × 20 yd (5.5 × 18.3 m); the corner arc has a radius of 1 yd, not a diameter; and posts are at most 5 in (12 cm) wide. The half-time break under the Laws of the Game is up to 15 minutes. Transcribed as printed.
