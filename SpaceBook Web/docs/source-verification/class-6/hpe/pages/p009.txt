@issue Key points: AIDS is listed here as a "communicable disease" (متعدی مرض), although page 2 of the book gives AIDS as an example of a non-communicable disease. AIDS is in fact communicable (it spreads through blood and body fluids). The key point also says albumin "helps blood to clot"; page 4 lists albumin and the clotting proteins separately, which is correct (albumin itself does not clot blood). Transcribed as printed.
