@deco Drawing of a badminton player lunging low at the net to return a shuttlecock.
@issue Measurements: the official net height is 5 ft at the centre (5 ft 1 in at the posts) and the net is about 2 ft 6 in deep, as printed. The book calls the extra strips "galleries"; the singles court is 44 × 17 ft and doubles 44 × 20 ft, matching the official court.
