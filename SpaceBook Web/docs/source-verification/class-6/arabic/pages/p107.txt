@issue Glossary page 94: **سَاعِدٌ** is glossed "کلائی" (wrist); in Arabic it usually means the forearm, while the wrist is **رُسْغٌ**. **رِجْلٌ** is glossed "ٹانگ" (leg), though in the lesson it is used for the foot. Kept as printed.
