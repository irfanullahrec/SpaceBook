## B. Short Questions: || ب۔ مختصر سوالات: || ب. لنډې پوښتنې:
q B1 | Give one example of mixtures with the following combination. One is done for you. a. Liquid–liquid (Vinegar in Water) b. Solid–Solid c. Liquid-Liquid d. Solid-Liquid e. Gas-liquid || درج ذیل ملاپ والے آمیزوں کی ایک ایک مثال دیں۔ ایک حل ہے۔ || د لاندې ترکیبونو د مخلوطونو یوه یوه بېلګه ورکړئ.
sol b. Solid–solid: sand and iron filings (or rice and beans). c. Liquid–liquid: oil and water (or milk and water). d. Solid–liquid: salt in water (or sugar in tea). e. Gas–liquid: carbon dioxide in a soft drink (or oxygen in water). ## || ||
q B2 | Which process would you use to separate the following? Give the reason. a. Sand and water b. Alcohol and water c. Vinegar (a solution of acetic acid (liquid) in water) || درج ذیل کو کس عمل سے الگ کریں گے؟ وجہ بتائیں۔ || لاندې به په کوم بهیر جلا کړئ؟ دلیل ووایاست.
sol a. Filtration (or decantation) — sand does not dissolve. b. Distillation — both are liquids that mix; alcohol boils at a lower temperature. c. Distillation — acetic acid and water are mixed liquids in a solution. ## || ||
q B3 | Why water is considered as a universal solvent? || پانی کو عالمگیر محلل کیوں کہتے ہیں؟ || اوبه ولې نړیوال محلل ګڼل کېږي؟
sol Because it can dissolve a very wide range of substances — its molecules can stick to the molecules or atoms of many other substances. ## || ||
q B4 | Can you use a polythene bag for filtration. If not Why? || کیا فلٹریشن کے لیے پولی تھین بیگ استعمال ہو سکتا ہے؟ اگر نہیں تو کیوں؟ || ایا د فلټر لپاره پلاستیکي کڅوړه کارولای شو؟ که نه ولې؟
sol No. Polythene has no pores, so the liquid cannot pass through it; filter paper has tiny pores that let the liquid through but stop the solid. ## || ||
q B5 | Why air is considered a mixture and not a compound? || ہوا کو مرکب کے بجائے آمیزہ کیوں سمجھا جاتا ہے؟ || هوا ولې مخلوط ګڼل کېږي نه مرکب؟
sol Its gases are not chemically combined, they keep their own properties, their amounts can vary, and they can be separated by physical methods. ## || ||
q B6 | Identify substances in the following table: Is this a heterogeneous mixture or a solution? Oil and water; Flour and water; Milk and water; Honey and water; Orange juice || جدول کے مادوں کی شناخت کریں: غیر یکساں آمیزہ یا محلول؟ || په جدول کې مواد وپېژنئ: ناهمجنس مخلوط که محلول؟
sol Oil and water — heterogeneous mixture; Flour and water — heterogeneous mixture (suspension); Milk and water — solution (looks uniform); Honey and water — solution; Orange juice (with pulp) — heterogeneous mixture. ## || ||
## C. Long Questions: || ج۔ تفصیلی سوالات: || ج. اوږدې پوښتنې:
q C1 | Explain the different separation techniques to get a pure substance from a mixture? || آمیزے سے خالص مادہ حاصل کرنے کے مختلف طریقے بیان کریں۔ || له مخلوط څخه د خالصې مادې ترلاسه کولو بېلابېلې لارې تشریح کړئ.
sol Hand sorting (by hand), sieving (different particle sizes), winnowing (lighter solid blown away), filtration (insoluble solid from liquid), decantation (pour off liquid after solid settles), evaporation (dissolved solid from liquid), distillation (liquid from solution by evaporation and condensation), chromatography (dyes separated on paper). ## || ||
q C2 | Justify why the process of filtration cannot be used for separating soluble solids. || ثابت کریں کہ حل پذیر ٹھوس کو فلٹریشن سے الگ کیوں نہیں کیا جا سکتا۔ || ثابت کړئ چې ولې حلېدونکي جامدات په فلټر نه جلا کېږي.
sol A dissolved solid breaks into particles as small as the liquid's particles; they pass through the pores of the filter paper together with the liquid, so nothing is left behind. Evaporation is used instead. ## || ||
## D. Project Work || د۔ پراجیکٹ ورک || د. پروژه
q D1 | Take a mixture of salt and sand. Perform an experiment to separate the salt from sand. || نمک اور ریت کا آمیزہ لیں اور نمک کو ریت سے الگ کرنے کا تجربہ کریں۔ || د مالګې او شګو مخلوط واخلئ او د مالګې د شګو څخه د جلا کولو تجربه وکړئ.
sol 1. Add water and stir — salt dissolves, sand does not. 2. Filter — sand stays on the filter paper (residue). 3. Evaporate the filtrate — salt is left behind. ## Dissolving, filtration and evaporation. || حل کرنا، فلٹر، بخارات۔ || حلول، فلټر، تبخیر.
