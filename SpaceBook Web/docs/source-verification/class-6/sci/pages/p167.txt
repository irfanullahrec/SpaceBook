# GLOSSARY || فرہنگ || لغتنامه
## Unit 1 || یونٹ 1 || یونټ 1
table
th | Term || اصطلاح || اصطلاح | Meaning || معنی || مانا
tr | **Cell membrane** || خلوی جھلی || حجروي پرده | Outer thin membrane surrounding the cell which allows substances to pass through.
tr | **Cell organelles** || خلوی عضیات || حجروي غړي | Small parts of cell in the cytoplasm e.g. mitochondria, ribosome, vacuole etc.
tr | **Cell wall** || خلوی دیوار || حجروي دېوال | Rigid outer coat of plant cells which gives support to the cell.
tr | **Cell** || خلیہ || حجره | Building unit of living things.
tr | **Cellular respiration** || خلوی تنفس || حجروي تنفس | The release of energy in a living organism when food materials are broken down into simpler substances.
tr | **Cellulose** || سیلولوز || سلولوز | A large molecule in the form of tough fibres; found in cell wall of plant cell; wood, paper, cotton and dietary fibre are mainly made up of cellulose.
tr | **Chlorophyll** || سبزینہ || کلوروفیل | Green pigment which traps sunlight energy needed for photosynthesis.
tr | **Chloroplast** || سبز مایہ || کلوروپلاست | Green structure inside the cytoplasm of plant cells; contains chlorophyll.
tr | **Cytoplasm** || سائٹوپلازم || سایتوپلازم | Jelly like substance between the cell membrane and nucleus where chemical reactions take place.
tr | **Leaf cell** || پتے کا خلیہ || د پاڼې حجره | Part of the photosynthetic tissue that make food.
tr | **Life process** || حیاتیاتی عمل || د ژوند پروسه | Various functions carried out by living things.
tr | **Mitochondrion** || مائٹوکونڈریا || مایټوکاندریا | Rod shaped cell organelle; responsible for cellular respiration.
tr | **Multicellular organism** || کثیر خلوی جاندار || څو حجروي ژوندی | Organism made up of many cells.
tr | **Nerve cell** || عصبی خلیہ || عصبي حجره | Long, thin cells of the nervous system that carry messages.
tr | **Nucleus** || مرکزہ || هسته | Control centre of the cell.
tr | **Organ system** || نظامِ اعضاء || د غړو سیستم | The body organs working together to carry on a complete life function.
tr | **Organ** || عضو || غړی | Part of the organism, e.g., stomach, made from different tissues.
tr | **Organism** || جاندار || ژوندی موجود | A living thing made up of a cell/cells.
tr | **Photosynthesis** || ضیائی تالیف || ضیايي ترکیب | The process by which green plants make food from carbon dioxide and water using light energy.
tr | **Red blood cell** || سرخ خونی خلیہ || سره وینه حجره | Red, flat cells filled with haemoglobin to carry oxygen; found in blood.
tr | **Ribosome** || رائبوسوم || رایبوزوم | Tiny round cell part that makes protein.
tr | **Root hair cell** || جڑ کے بال کا خلیہ || د ریښې وېښته حجره | Part of root epidermal tissue; responsible for absorption of water and salts.
tr | **Tissue** || نسیج || نسج | Group of cells that perform a similar function.
tr | **Unicellular organism** || یک خلوی جاندار || یو حجروي ژوندی | Organism that exists as a single cell.
tr | **Vacuole** || خلا (ویکیول) || واکیول | A space in the cytoplasm of a cell containing air, liquid, or food; vacuoles in plant cells contain cell sap made up of mainly water and salts.
endtable
