p Brightness of bulb in a circuit can be affected by many factors. Make six circuits according to the given table. As this is activity, so it will show that changing the nature of the wire, will change the conductivity or brightness of bulbs. Use first circuit as standard and compare the brightness of bulb in all circuits with the first one.
