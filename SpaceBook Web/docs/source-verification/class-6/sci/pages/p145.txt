q 11.3 | How many drops does it take to make the milk start to curdle? || کتنے قطروں سے دودھ پھٹنا شروع ہوتا ہے؟ || څو څاڅکي شیدې شلوي؟
sol One or two drops are not enough; for half a litre of hot milk the curdling usually starts after about a teaspoon (roughly 30–60 drops) and is complete with about one tablespoon. More juice gives faster and more complete curdling. ## Results depend on the milk and the lemon. || نتیجہ دودھ اور لیموں پر منحصر ہے۔ || پایله په شیدو او لیمو پورې اړه لري.
