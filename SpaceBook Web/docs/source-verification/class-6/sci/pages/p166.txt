q D4 | Differentiate between stars and planets. || ستاروں اور سیاروں میں فرق کریں۔ || د ستورو او سیارو توپیر وکړئ.
sol Stars are huge balls of hot gas that make their own heat and light and twinkle; they do not orbit a star. Planets are smaller, do not make their own light (they reflect sunlight), do not twinkle and revolve around a star. ## Answer from Unit 12 text. || یونٹ 12 کے متن سے۔ || د 12 یونټ له متن.
