q B6 | [Differentiate] between permanent magnet and temporary magnet? || مستقل اور عارضی مقناطیس میں فرق کریں۔ || د دایمي او لنډمهاله مقناطیس توپیر وکړئ.
sol A permanent magnet keeps its magnetism without any other magnet or current (made of steel/alloys of iron, nickel, cobalt). A temporary magnet acts as a magnet only while in a strong magnetic field or while current flows, and loses it afterwards (made of soft iron). ## Answer from Unit 10 text. || یونٹ 10 کے متن سے۔ || د 10 یونټ له متن.

q C1 | [What do you] understand by magnetic power and discuss briefly, will magnets lose [their power with time?] || مقناطیسی طاقت سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ کیا وقت کے ساتھ مقناطیس اپنی طاقت کھو دیتے ہیں؟ || له مقناطیسي ځواک څه پوهېږئ؟ ایا مقناطیسونه له وخت سره ځواک له لاسه ورکوي؟
sol Magnetic power is the ability of a magnet to attract magnetic materials and to attract/repel other magnets; it is strongest at the poles. Yes — magnets slowly lose strength over time (demagnetization occurs naturally), and faster if heated, hammered, dropped or placed in alternating current. ## Answer from Unit 10 text. || یونٹ 10 کے متن سے۔ || د 10 یونټ له متن.
q C2 | [How many poles of m]agnets are there and how can you tell which is the North Pole or South pole if it's not marked? || مقناطیس کے کتنے قطب ہیں اور نشان نہ ہو تو شمالی یا جنوبی قطب کیسے پہچانیں گے؟ || مقناطیس څو قطبه لري او که نښه نه وي شمالي یا جنوبي قطب څنګه پېژنئ؟
sol Two poles, North and South. Hang the magnet freely by a thread (or float it on water): the end that points to geographic North is the North pole. Or bring it near a compass — the compass's north end is repelled by the magnet's North pole. ## Answer from Unit 10 text. || یونٹ 10 کے متن سے۔ || د 10 یونټ له متن.
q C3 | Describe an experiment to illustrate that like poles repel while the unlike poles, attract. || تجربہ بیان کریں جس سے ثابت ہو کہ یکساں قطب دھکیلتے اور مخالف قطب کھینچتے ہیں۔ || تجربه بیان کړئ چې ثابته کړي ورته قطبونه شړي او مخالف راکاږي.
sol Hang one bar magnet freely by a thread. Bring the N pole of a second magnet near its N pole — the hanging magnet moves away (repulsion). Bring the S pole near the same N pole — it moves towards it (attraction). ## Answer from Unit 10 text. || یونٹ 10 کے متن سے۔ || د 10 یونټ له متن.
q C4 | Explain in your own words, how to make an electromagnet. Draw a labelled diagram to show the circuit of the electromagnet. || اپنے الفاظ میں برقی مقناطیس بنانے کا طریقہ بیان کریں اور لیبل والا خاکہ بنائیں۔ || په خپلو ټکو د برېښنايي مقناطیس جوړول تشریح کړئ او نښه شوی ډیاګرام رسم کړئ.
sol Wind insulated copper wire many times round an iron nail. Join the ends of the wire to a battery through a switch. When the switch is closed, current flows and the nail becomes a magnet that picks up pins; when it is opened, the magnetism disappears. Diagram as in Figure 10.5 (iron nail, coil, battery, switch). ## Answer from Unit 10 text. || یونٹ 10 کے متن سے۔ || د 10 یونټ له متن.
