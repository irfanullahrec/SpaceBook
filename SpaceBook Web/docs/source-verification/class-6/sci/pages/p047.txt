## B. Short Questions || ب۔ مختصر سوالات || ب. لنډې پوښتنې
q B1 | Define digestion. Why do we need to digest food? || ہاضمے کی تعریف کریں۔ ہمیں خوراک ہضم کرنے کی ضرورت کیوں ہے؟ || هضم تعریف کړئ. خواړه ولې باید هضم شي؟
sol Digestion is the breakdown of food into smaller molecules. We need it because large food molecules cannot pass through the cell membrane; only small molecules can be absorbed and used by cells. ## || ||
q B2 | What role do the muscles play in digestion? || ہاضمے میں پٹھوں کا کیا کردار ہے؟ || په هضم کې عضلې څه رول لري؟
sol Smooth muscles of the oesophagus push food down; stomach muscles churn and mix food (physical digestion) and push it into the small intestine; intestine muscles move food along. ## || ||
q B3 | What is the benefit of breaking up food into smaller pieces? || خوراک کو چھوٹے ٹکڑوں میں توڑنے کا کیا فائدہ ہے؟ || د خوړو د کوچنیو ټوټو کولو ګټه څه ده؟
sol Smaller pieces are easier to swallow and have more surface area, so enzymes can act on them faster. ## || ||
q B4 | Explain digestive enzymes. Give examples. || ہاضم انزائمز کی وضاحت کریں۔ مثالیں دیں۔ || هاضمي انزایمونه تشریح کړئ. بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Digestive enzymes are special molecules that break large food molecules into small ones. Examples: the enzyme in saliva (digests starch), enzymes of gastric juice in the stomach (digest proteins), and pancreatic and intestinal enzymes (digest all types of food). ## || ||
q B5 | How can villi help in absorption of food? || ولائی خوراک کے جذب میں کیسے مدد دیتے ہیں؟ || ویلای د خوړو په جذب کې څنګه مرسته کوي؟
sol Villi are finger-like projections that greatly increase the surface area of the small intestine, so more digested food passes into the blood. ## || ||
## C. Long Questions || ج۔ تفصیلی سوالات || ج. اوږدې پوښتنې
q C1 | Explain the different types of digestion. || ہاضمے کی مختلف اقسام بیان کریں۔ || د هضم بېلابېل ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Physical (mechanical) digestion breaks big pieces into smaller pieces by teeth (cutting, crushing), tongue (rolling, mixing) and stomach muscles (churning). Chemical digestion breaks large molecules into small molecules using enzymes — in the mouth (saliva on starch), stomach (gastric juice on protein) and small intestine (pancreatic/intestinal enzymes on all foods). ## || ||
q C2 | Draw and describe human digestive system. || انسانی نظامِ انہضام بنائیں اور بیان کریں۔ || د انسان هاضمي سیستم رسم او بیان کړئ.
sol Draw Figure 4.2 and describe: mouth (teeth, tongue, saliva) → oesophagus (pushes food) → stomach (churning, gastric juice) → small intestine (digestion completes, absorption by villi; liver, gall bladder and pancreas add bile and enzymes) → large intestine (absorbs water) → rectum (stores faeces) → anus. ## || ||
