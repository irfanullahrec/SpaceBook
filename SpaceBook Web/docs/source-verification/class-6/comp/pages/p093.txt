## 6.2.3 Innovators || 6.2.3 موجد (جدت پسند) || 6.2.3 نوښتګر
p Innovators are the entrepreneurs who come up with new ideas and turn them into a hands-on business. This type of entrepreneurs seems to be more passionate and highly motivated to turn their ideas into successful business. Innovative entrepreneurs always introduce new ideas or products to attract their customers. ## 6.2.4 Imitators || 6.2.4 نقال || 6.2.4 مقلدین
