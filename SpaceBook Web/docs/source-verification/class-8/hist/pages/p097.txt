## Industry || صنعت || صنعت
p One of the important indicators of progress of a country is its industrial development. At the time of independence, Pakistan had only 34 industries out of total 921 industrial units which was around four percent of the total industries established in the Subcontinent. Since then Pakistan has been progressing in this field. Now Pakistan is the third largest exporter of cotton textile in the world. Sugar industry developed greatly during last few decades. At the time of independence, there were only two sugar mills in the country whereas now there are more than 100 sugarmills. In Sports, Pakistan exports more than 70% of the total world demand for hand stitched footballs, the number of which reaches to more than 40 million footballs a year. Similarly, telecom industry has developed in recent years with spread of mobile phones and internet facilities, reaching every corner of the country. ## Science and Technology || سائنس اور ٹیکنالوجی || ساینس او ټیکنالوژي
p Like many other fields, Pakistan has also developed significantly in the fields of science & technology. After recovering from initial crises of economy, Pakistan progressed rapidly in this field. Today there are more than 700 technical and vocational institutions in the country. Moreover, every year there are number of graduates in the fields of science & technology, playing their role in progress of the country. In the fields of Physics, Nuclear Science, Engineering, and especially in Information Technology, Pakistan has made exemplary progress. All this progress was because of hard work of Pakistani nation. p Hockey is the national game of Pakistan. After independence in 1947, Pakistan Hockey team started participating in international events. The period between 1960 to 1990 was the time of Pakistan's dominance in world hockey. During this period Pakistan won the hockey World Cup four times and made it to the finals six times. Sohail Abbas holds the world record of highest number of goals with 348 goals for Pakistan. || ہاکی پاکستان کا قومی کھیل ہے۔ 1947 میں آزادی کے بعد پاکستان ہاکی ٹیم نے بین الاقوامی مقابلوں میں حصہ لینا شروع کیا۔ 1960 سے 1990 کے درمیان کا عرصہ عالمی ہاکی میں پاکستان کے غلبے کا دور تھا۔ اس دوران پاکستان نے ہاکی ورلڈ کپ چار بار جیتا اور چھ بار فائنل تک پہنچا۔ سہیل عباس 348 گولز کے ساتھ پاکستان کے لیے سب سے زیادہ گولز کا عالمی ریکارڈ رکھتے ہیں۔ || هاکي د پاکستان ملي لوبه ده. په ۱۹۴۷ کې د خپلواکۍ وروسته د پاکستان د هاکۍ ټیم په نړیوالو سیالیو کې برخه اخیستل پیل کړل. د ۱۹۶۰ څخه تر ۱۹۹۰ پورې موده د نړیوالې هاکۍ کې د پاکستان د برلاسۍ وخت و. په دې موده کې پاکستان د هاکۍ نړیوال جام څلور ځل وګټل او شپږ ځل فاینل ته ورسېد. سهیل عباس د پاکستان لپاره ۳۴۸ ګولونو سره د تر ټولو زیاتو ګولونو نړیوال ریکارډ لري.
p Pakistan dominated the squash courts throughout the second half of 20th century. This success story began with Hashim Khan who won British Open for seven times in the 1950s. Later, Roshan Khan and Azam Khan won British Open for four times. In late 70's Pakistan got the glory back when Jahangir Khan won the British Open in 1979. This began the era of this great player who won 10 British Open titles and 6 World Opens.
