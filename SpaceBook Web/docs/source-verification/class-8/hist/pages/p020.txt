q B | Answer the following questions briefly. || درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات دیں۔ || د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp 1 | What were the main causes of the defeat of Siraj ud Daulah in the battle of Plassey? || جنگِ پلاسی میں سراج الدولہ کی شکست کے بنیادی اسباب کیا تھے؟ || د پلاسي په جګړه کې د سراج‌الدوله د ماتې اصلي لاملونه کوم وو؟
sol Siraj ud Daulah was betrayed by his own commander Mir Jafar and other nobles who had been won over by Robert Clive of the East India Company; most of his army did not fight; the Company's army was small but better trained, better armed and well led; and the Nawab was young and had many enemies at his court. ## The treachery of Mir Jafar decided the battle of Plassey (1757).
qp 2 | Briefly describe the transfer of British power in India from the East India Company to the Crown. || ہندوستان میں برطانوی اقتدار کی ایسٹ انڈیا کمپنی سے تاجِ برطانیہ کو منتقلی کو مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || په هند کې د برتانوي واک له ختیځې هند کمپنۍ څخه تاج ته لېږد لنډ بیان کړئ.
sol After the War of Independence of 1857 the British Parliament passed the Government of India Act 1858. The rule of the East India Company ended and India came directly under the British Crown. The Governor-General became the Viceroy, and a Secretary of State for India with a council in London controlled Indian affairs. Queen Victoria announced the change in her Proclamation of 1858. ## From the Company to the Crown.
qp 3 | Outline the structure of the British administration in India. || ہندوستان میں برطانوی انتظامیہ کے ڈھانچے کا خاکہ پیش کریں۔ || په هند کې د برتانوي ادارې جوړښت لنډیز وړاندې کړئ.
sol At the top was the British Parliament and the Secretary of State for India in London; in India the Viceroy (Governor-General) ruled with his Executive Council. The country was divided into provinces under governors, the provinces into divisions under commissioners and districts under a Deputy Commissioner (Collector), who was helped by officers in the tehsils. The Indian Civil Service ran the administration, and the army, police and law courts supported it. ## The chain of command from London to the district.
qp 4 | What were the objectives of the British education policy in India? || ہندوستان میں برطانوی تعلیمی پالیسی کے مقاصد کیا تھے؟ || په هند کې د برتانوي زده‌کړیزې پالیسۍ موخې کومې وې؟
sol The British wanted to produce a class of Indians who would serve as clerks and junior officers in their administration at low cost, to spread the English language and Western ideas, to make Indians loyal to British rule, and to replace the old Persian, Arabic and Sanskrit learning with English education. ## Macaulay wanted people Indian in blood but English in taste, opinions and morals.
qp 5 | Specify the advantages of communication networks introduced by the British. || انگریزوں کے متعارف کرائے گئے مواصلاتی نیٹ ورکس کے فوائد بیان کریں۔ || د انګرېزانو لخوا معرفي شویو مخابراتي شبکو ګټې ومنئ.
sol Railways, roads, the post and the telegraph made travel and the movement of goods faster, helped trade and the sale of farm products, let news and letters reach quickly, helped the government to control the country and move its army, and also brought people of different regions closer, which spread new ideas and national awareness. ## Railways, telegraph, post and roads.
q C | Answer the following questions in detail. || درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات دیں۔ || د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp 1 | Describe the causes and results of the battle of Plassey. || جنگِ پلاسی کے اسباب اور نتائج بیان کریں۔ || د پلاسي د جګړې لاملونه او پایلې بیان کړئ.
sol Causes: the Company misused its trade privileges and strengthened Fort William in Calcutta without permission; Siraj ud Daulah captured Calcutta in 1756; Robert Clive made a secret plan with Mir Jafar and other nobles to put Mir Jafar on the throne. Result: in the battle of 23 June 1757 Mir Jafar's troops stood aside, Siraj ud Daulah was defeated and killed, Mir Jafar became a puppet Nawab, the Company got wealth and power in Bengal, and this laid the foundation of British rule in India. ## Plassey, 1757.
qp 2 | Identify Tipu Sultan as a symbol of resistance. || ٹیپو سلطان کو مزاحمت کی علامت کے طور پر شناخت کریں۔ || ټیپو سلطان د مقاومت د نښې په توګه وپېژنئ.
sol Tipu Sultan, the ruler of Mysore (1782 to 1799), was the most determined enemy of the British. He strengthened his army with modern weapons including rockets, tried to build a navy, made reforms in trade and administration, and fought the Mysore wars against the British. He refused to accept their rule and died fighting at Seringapatam in 1799, saying that to live one day as a lion is better than to live a hundred years as a jackal. ## He is remembered as a symbol of courage and freedom.
qp 3 | What are the salient features of Queen Victoria's proclamation? || ملکہ وکٹوریہ کے اعلان کی نمایاں خصوصیات کیا ہیں؟ || د ملکې ویکتوریا د اعلامیې مهمې ځانګړتیاوې کومې دي؟
sol It ended the rule of the East India Company and put India under the Crown; the British promised not to take more land from the Indian states and to respect the rights and dignity of the Indian rulers; the subjects were promised freedom of religion and no interference in their beliefs; all were to be equal before the law and to get government jobs on merit without regard to race or religion; those who took part in the revolt were pardoned except murderers. ## Proclamation of 1 November 1858.
qp 4 | Write a note on Lord Macaulay's Minute (1836-37). || لارڈ میکالے کی یادداشت (1836-37) پر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || د لارډ مکاولي د یادښت (۱۸۳۶-۳۷) په اړه یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Lord Macaulay, law member of the Governor-General's council, wrote his Minute on education in 1835 (the book gives 1836-37). He said that European knowledge was better than Eastern learning, that English should be the medium of higher education, and that the government should create a class of Indians 'Indian in blood and colour but English in taste, opinions, morals and intellect'. The government accepted it, English became the language of education and official work, and the Persian and Arabic schools declined. ## English replaced Persian as the official language.
qp 5 | What measures for the social transformation were taken since the British accession to power? || انگریزوں کے اقتدار میں آنے کے بعد سماجی تبدیلی کے لیے کون سے اقدامات کیے گئے؟ || د انګرېزانو له واک ته رسېدو راهیسې د ټولنیز بدلون لپاره کوم اقدامات وشول؟
sol The British stopped the burning of widows (sati) in 1829 and infanticide, allowed widows to marry again (1856), ended slavery, introduced English and modern education and schools and colleges, set up modern law courts and a uniform legal system, hospitals and the press, and built railways, canals and roads, which changed the old society. ## Social reforms under British rule.
qp 6 | What does the picture given below tell us about British rule in India? || نیچے دی گئی تصویر ہمیں ہندوستان میں برطانوی راج کے بارے میں کیا بتاتی ہے؟ || لاندې ورکړل شوی انځور موږ ته په هند کې د برتانوي واکمنۍ په اړه څه وايي؟
sol The painting shows an official of the East India Company sitting on a richly decorated elephant under a canopy with servants walking and riding around him. It tells us that the Company's officers lived like rulers, with great wealth, power and show, and had many Indian servants and attendants; the people looked up to them as masters. ## The picture is titled 'An official of the British East India Company'.
