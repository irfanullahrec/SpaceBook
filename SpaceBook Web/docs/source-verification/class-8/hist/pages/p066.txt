q B | Answer the following questions briefly. || درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات دیں۔ || د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp 1 | Enlist primary objectives of All India Muslim League. || آل انڈیا مسلم لیگ کے بنیادی مقاصد کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د آل انډیا مسلم لیګ اصلي موخې ولیکئ.
sol The Muslim League was founded at Dhaka in 1906 with these objectives: to create among the Muslims of India loyalty to the British government and remove misunderstandings; to protect and advance the political rights and interests of the Muslims and put their needs before the government; and to prevent ill feeling between the Muslims and other communities. ## Aims of the Muslim League (1906).
qp 2 | What was the significance of the Khilafat Movement in the creation of Pakistan? || قیامِ پاکستان میں تحریکِ خلافت کی کیا اہمیت تھی؟ || د پاکستان په رامنځته کېدو کې د خلافت خوځښت اهمیت څه و؟
sol The Khilafat Movement (1919 to 1924) united the Muslims of India for the first time in a mass political movement, awakened their political awareness and taught them organisation and sacrifice. When the Hindu-Muslim unity ended with the failure of the movement and the riots that followed, the Muslims saw that they must look after their own interests, which strengthened the idea of a separate Muslim homeland. ## It strengthened Muslim identity.
qp 3 | Do you think Nehru Report paved the way for the creation of Pakistan? Give reasons. || کیا آپ سمجھتے ہیں کہ نہرو رپورٹ نے قیامِ پاکستان کی راہ ہموار کی؟ وجوہات دیں۔ || ایا تاسو فکر کوئ چې د نهرو راپور د پاکستان د رامنځته کېدو لار هواره کړه؟ دلایل ورکړئ.
sol Yes. The Nehru Report (1928) rejected separate electorates and reserved seats for Muslims and a federal system with strong provinces, and the Congress refused Jinnah's amendments. The Muslims felt that the Hindu majority would not protect their rights, so Jinnah answered with his Fourteen Points (1929) and the Muslims moved towards the demand for a separate homeland. ## Reasons: the Muslims lost trust in the Congress.
qp 4 | How did the Congress Ministries come to an end in 1939? || 1939 میں کانگریسی وزارتوں کا خاتمہ کیسے ہوا؟ || په ۱۹۳۹ کې د کانګرس وزارتونه څنګه پای ته ورسېدل؟
sol When the Second World War began in September 1939 the Viceroy, Lord Linlithgow, declared India at war without asking the Indian leaders. The Congress protested and its ministries resigned in all the provinces in October-November 1939. The Muslims, relieved, celebrated 22 December 1939 as the Day of Deliverance (Yaum-e-Nijat) on the call of Quaid-e-Azam. ## Resignation of the Congress ministries.
qp 5 | Why is the Pakistan Resolution an important landmark in Pakistan Movement? || قراردادِ پاکستان تحریکِ پاکستان میں ایک اہم سنگِ میل کیوں ہے؟ || د پاکستان قرارداد د پاکستان په خوځښت کې مهم کېږدی ولې دی؟
sol It was the first clear demand for separate independent states for the Muslims of India in the areas where they were in a majority, passed on 23 March 1940 at Lahore. It gave the Muslims a goal and a name for their struggle, and the Pakistan Movement became a mass movement that achieved Pakistan within seven years. ## The Lahore (Pakistan) Resolution, 1940.

qp 1 | Write a note on the Congress Ministries after 1937 elections. || 1937 کے انتخابات کے بعد کانگریسی وزارتوں پر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || د ۱۹۳۷ ټاکنو وروسته د کانګرس وزارتونو په اړه یادښت ولیکئ.
sol In the elections of 1937 the Congress won a clear majority in five provinces and formed ministries in seven (later eight) of the eleven provinces, while the Muslim League did poorly. The Congress ministries treated the Muslims unfairly: Bande Mataram was made compulsory in schools, the Wardha and Vidya Mandir schemes tried to hide the Muslim culture, Urdu was pushed down in favour of Hindi, cow slaughter was banned and Muslims were harassed. The Muslim League collected such complaints in reports (the Pirpur Report). The ministries resigned in 1939. ## Rule of the Congress, 1937 to 1939.
qp 2 | Describe the Lahore Resolution and its significance. || قراردادِ لاہور اور اس کی اہمیت بیان کریں۔ || د لاهور قرارداد او د هغه اهمیت بیان کړئ.
sol The Lahore Resolution was moved by A. K. Fazlul Haq and passed on 23 March 1940 at the annual session of the Muslim League at Minto Park, Lahore, under Quaid-e-Azam. It said that the areas where the Muslims are in a majority, such as the north-western and eastern zones of India, should be grouped to form independent states, with their units autonomous and sovereign, and that safeguards must be given to the minorities. Its significance: it officially declared the aim of a separate Muslim homeland (the Hindu press called it the Pakistan Resolution) and turned the freedom movement into the Pakistan Movement. ## Lahore Resolution, 1940.
qp 3 | How did the Muslim League obtain success in the 1945-46 elections? Why were these elections important for the fate of the country? || مسلم لیگ نے 1945-46 کے انتخابات میں کامیابی کیسے حاصل کی؟ یہ انتخابات ملک کی تقدیر کے لیے کیوں اہم تھے؟ || مسلم لیګ په ۱۹۴۵-۴۶ ټاکنو کې بریا څنګه ترلاسه کړه؟ دا ټاکنې د هېواد د برخلیک لپاره ولې مهمې وې؟
sol The Muslim League won all 30 Muslim seats in the Central Assembly and about 90 percent of the Muslim seats in the provinces. It succeeded because of the leadership of Quaid-e-Azam, the clear demand for Pakistan, the unity of the Muslims in the League's campaign, the work of the students and women, and the Congress rule that had made the Muslims distrust it. These elections were important because they proved that the Muslim League was the only representative of the Muslims, so the British and the Congress had to accept the demand for Pakistan. ## Elections of 1945-46.
qp 4 | Analyze the factors that led to landmark victory of All India Muslim League in 1945-46 elections. || 1945-46 کے انتخابات میں آل انڈیا مسلم لیگ کی تاریخی فتح کا باعث بننے والے عوامل کا تجزیہ کریں۔ || هغه فکتورونه تحلیل کړئ چې د ۱۹۴۵-۴۶ ټاکنو کې د آل انډیا مسلم لیګ د کېږدي بریا لامل شول.
sol Factors: the strong and honest leadership of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah; the Pakistan slogan and the Lahore Resolution that united the Muslims; the unfair rule of the Congress ministries (1937 to 1939); the organisation of the League in every province and the help of students, women, the ulema and the press; and the fear of the Muslims that they would be ruled by the Hindu majority. As a result the League won all the Muslim seats of the Central Assembly and about 90 percent of the Muslim seats of the provinces. ## Why the Muslims voted for the League.
