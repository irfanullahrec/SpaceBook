p The National Socialist German Workers' Party, commonly known as the Nazi Party, came to power in Germany in 1933 under the leadership of Adolf Hitler. Hitler promised Germans that he would restore the old glory of the country in all fields. Under him, Germany started arming itself against all treaties and even annexed some neighbouring countries such as Austria and Czechoslovakia in 1938. Between 1936 and 1938, Britain and France used diplomacy against Germany to resolve issues without a war.
