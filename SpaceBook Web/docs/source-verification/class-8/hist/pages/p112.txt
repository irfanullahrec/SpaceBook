### Tahir Mehmood || طاہر محمود || طاهر محمود
p Tahir Mehmood is an Assistant Professor in Federal College of Education, Islamabad having M. Phill in Education and Master's degrees in Economics and Pakistan Studies. He has accumulated more than 30 years teaching experience with different educational setups in Pakistan. He is a national level teacher trainer who conducted several trainings both in public and private sector. As a curriculum expert and instructional material developer he has collaborated with renowned international and national organizations. As an assessment expert, he worked with AKU-IED and NEAS in several projects. He has reviewed textbooks of Matric Technical scheme, Social Studies, and History of different levels.
