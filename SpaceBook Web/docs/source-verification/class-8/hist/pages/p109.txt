q B | Answer the following questions briefly. || درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات دیں۔ || د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp 1 | Justify Pakistan's development in the field of agriculture. || زراعت کے شعبے میں پاکستان کی ترقی کا جواز پیش کریں۔ || د کرنې په برخه کې د پاکستان د پرمختګ توجیه وکړئ.
sol Agriculture gives 18.5 percent of the GDP and 38.5 percent of the jobs of Pakistan, feeds the people and earns foreign exchange. Since 1947 production has increased manifold: wheat from 3.30 to 25.49 million tonnes, cotton from 0.188 to 11.93 million bales, rice from 6.92 to 7.44 million tonnes, citrus from 0.08 to 2.39 million tonnes and sugarcane from 5.52 to 81.10 million tonnes by 2018. ## From the table of the book (1947 and 2018).
qp 2 | Briefly describe development of Pakistan in the field of Industry. || صنعت کے شعبے میں پاکستان کی ترقی مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || د صنعت په برخه کې د پاکستان پرمختګ په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ.
sol At independence Pakistan had only 34 industries out of 921 in the subcontinent (about four percent). Since then it has built the textile industry (the largest), sugar, cement, fertilizer, iron and steel (Pakistan Steel Mills), the Heavy Mechanical Complex and Port Qasim, and is now the third largest exporter of cotton textile in the world. ## From the book's section on industry.
qp 3 | What do you think is the most important factor behind low literacy rate in Pakistan? || آپ کے خیال میں پاکستان میں کم شرحِ خواندگی کے پیچھے سب سے اہم عنصر کیا ہے؟ || ستاسو په فکر په پاکستان کې د ټیټې سواد کچې شاته تر ټولو مهم فکتور څه دی؟
sol Poverty and the shortage of schools, teachers and facilities, especially in villages, together with the fast growth of population and little money spent on education, so that more than 22 million children are out of school; many poor families send their children to work instead. (Sample answer: your opinion with reasons is also accepted.) ## Opinion question; the literacy rate was 58% in 2018.
qp 4 | Discuss the role of the Executive in the country. || ملک میں انتظامیہ کے کردار پر بحث کریں۔ || په هېواد کې د اجرایې د رول په اړه بحث وکړئ.
sol The Executive is the branch of government that carries out the laws made by the Parliament. It consists of the President, the Prime Minister and the Cabinet with the ministries and the civil servants. It runs the administration, makes and carries out the policies, collects taxes, looks after defence and foreign affairs, and keeps law and order. ## The three organs: legislature, executive, judiciary.
qp 5 | Why should judiciary be separate from executive? || عدلیہ کو انتظامیہ سے الگ کیوں ہونا چاہیے؟ || قضا ولې باید له اجرایې څخه بېله وي؟
sol So that judges can decide cases freely and fairly without pressure or fear from the government, protect the rights of the citizens even against the government, and check that the executive does not misuse its power. If the executive controlled the courts, there would be no real justice. ## Separation of powers.
qp 6 | With which countries Pakistan has always had warm relations? || پاکستان کے کن ممالک کے ساتھ ہمیشہ گرم جوش تعلقات رہے ہیں؟ || پاکستان له کومو هېوادونو سره تل تودې اړیکې درلودې؟
sol China (an all-weather friend), Turkey, Saudi Arabia and the other Muslim countries such as Iran, the United Arab Emirates and Azerbaijan. ## Pakistan has friendly relations with many countries.
qp 7 | Describe the role of Pakistan in the United Nations Organization. || اقوامِ متحدہ میں پاکستان کے کردار کو بیان کریں۔ || په ملګرو ملتونو کې د پاکستان رول بیان کړئ.
sol Pakistan has been a member of the UN since 30 September 1947. It is one of the largest contributors of troops to the UN peacekeeping missions in many countries, has been elected several times to the Security Council, supports the right of self-determination of the Kashmiris and Palestinians, and works for disarmament, human rights and development. ## Pakistan and the UN.
q C | Answer the following questions in detail. || درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات دیں۔ || د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ورکړئ.
qp 1 | How much progress Pakistan has made in the field of industry? Give some examples. || صنعت کے شعبے میں پاکستان نے کتنی ترقی کی ہے؟ کچھ مثالیں دیں۔ || په صنعت برخه کې پاکستان څومره پرمختګ کړی دی؟ ځینې بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Pakistan has made great progress: from only 34 industries at independence to a large industrial base. Examples: the textile industry (the largest, Pakistan is the third largest exporter of cotton textile), sugar mills, cement, fertilizer, iron and steel (Pakistan Steel Mills at Karachi), the Heavy Mechanical Complex at Taxila, oil refineries and Port Qasim. ## Examples of industries.
qp 2 | Suggest some steps to bring improvement in Sports in Pakistan. || پاکستان میں کھیلوں میں بہتری لانے کے لیے کچھ اقدامات تجویز کریں۔ || په پاکستان کې په لوبو کې د ښه والي د راوستلو لپاره ځینې ګامونه وړاندیز کړئ.
sol Build and repair playgrounds, stadiums and sports complexes in every town; make sports a regular part of school and college life; give good coaching, scholarships and jobs to players; choose teams on merit without favour; give them sponsors and money and send them to international tournaments; encourage girls and women; and provide proper food and training. (Sample answer: other sensible steps are accepted.) ## Suggestions.
qp 3 | What do you know about different houses of Parliament? || پارلیمنٹ کے مختلف ایوانوں کے بارے میں آپ کیا جانتے ہیں؟ || د پارلمان د بېلابېلو ماڼیو په اړه تاسو څه پوهېږئ؟
sol The Parliament of Pakistan is the law-making body and has two houses: the National Assembly (lower house) and the Senate (upper house); the President is also a part of it. The National Assembly has 342 members (272 elected by direct vote, 60 seats for women and 10 for religious minorities) elected for five years. The Senate has equal representation of all the federating units (100 members in the book, elected for six years) so that all provinces have an equal voice. ## As printed in the book.
qp 4 | Write a note on Judiciary of Pakistan. || پاکستان کی عدلیہ پر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || د پاکستان د قضا په اړه یادښت ولیکئ.
sol The judiciary is the organ of the state that interprets the laws and gives justice. At the top is the Supreme Court of Pakistan at Islamabad, below it are the High Courts of the provinces and Islamabad, the Federal Shariat Court, and the district and session courts and the civil courts. The judges are appointed by the President on the advice of the Judicial Commission, are independent, protect the constitution and the fundamental rights, and decide disputes. ## Courts of Pakistan.
qp 5 | With which countries Pakistan has pleasant relations? Give some examples of these relations. || پاکستان کے کن ممالک کے ساتھ خوشگوار تعلقات ہیں؟ ان تعلقات کی کچھ مثالیں دیں۔ || پاکستان له کومو هېوادونو سره خوندورې اړیکې لري؟ د دې اړیکو ځینې بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol China: friendship and the CPEC, the Karakoram Highway and defence cooperation; Turkey: close cultural, trade and defence ties; Saudi Arabia: Hajj, jobs for Pakistanis, oil and help in need; Iran and other Muslim countries: religion, trade and cooperation in the OIC. ## Examples of friendly relations.
qp 6 | Compare Pakistan's role in SAARC and OIC. || سارک اور او آئی سی میں پاکستان کے کردار کا موازنہ کریں۔ || په SAARC او OIC کې د پاکستان رول پرتله کړئ.
sol SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation, founded in 1985) is a regional organisation of eight countries for cooperation and friendship in South Asia; Pakistan has played an important role, hosting the 4th summit (1988) and the 12th summit (2004) at Islamabad and being the second largest contributor to its budget. The OIC (Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, founded in 1969) is the organisation of Muslim countries for unity and the solving of their problems; Pakistan, a founding supporter, hosted the Lahore summit in 1974 and always speaks for Kashmir and Palestine. SAARC is regional, the OIC is based on religion and spread over many continents. ## Pakistan's role in both.
qp 7 | How can Pakistan maintain amicable relations with other countries? || پاکستان دیگر ممالک کے ساتھ دوستانہ تعلقات کیسے قائم رکھ سکتا ہے؟ || پاکستان له نورو هېوادونو سره دوستانه اړیکې څنګه ساتلای شي؟
sol By following a policy of peace and friendship: respecting the independence and borders of other countries, not interfering in their affairs, solving disputes through talks, increasing trade, cultural and sports exchange, giving and getting help in time of need, and playing an active part in the UN, SAARC and OIC. ## Peaceful foreign policy.
