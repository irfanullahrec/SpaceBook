### Hafsa Akhtar || حفصہ اختر || حفصه اختر
p Hafsa Akhtar is a senior Cambridge teacher with an MPhil and dual Master's degrees in Pakistan Studies and Peace & Conflict Studies. She received the President Gold Medal from National Defense University Islamabad, and holds international professional credentials from the University of Plymouth (UK), Oxford University and Cambridge University UK. She is a teacher trainer, curriculum expert of History and O-level Pakistan Studies, exam paper setter and head of department in The City School system. ### Suhail Bin Aziz || سہیل بن عزیز || سهیل بن عزیز
p Suhail Bin Aziz is an educational professional from Quetta with 22 years of experience. After receiving his dual Masters degrees, he also got teaching certificates from University of Cambridge and University of Oxford. He joined Education Department as a lecturer in the year 2000 and got promoted as Assistant Professor in 2009. He also served Ministry of Federal Education Islamabad, where he played important role in national assessments, revision of curriculum, and development of textbooks. He is a Gold Medallist of International Teaching Contest in 2013. As a Teacher Trainer, he got certification from Arizona State University. Later, he trained more than 5000 teachers for different national and international organizations on various topics such as Assessment Design, Paper Marking, and Life Skills Based Education. He is an author of five textbooks and a reviewer of several books on various topics. He is also a translator of seven books on different topics.
