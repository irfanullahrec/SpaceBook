qp 5 | Write a short note on role of Pakistan Navy in war time. || جنگ کے وقت پاکستان نیوی کے کردار پر مختصر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || د جګړې پر مهال د پاکستان سمندري ځواک د رول په اړه لنډ یادښت ولیکئ.
sol The Pakistan Navy defends the coast and the sea routes, protects the ports through which about 95 percent of the trade passes, and keeps the supplies and trade going in war. In the 1965 war it attacked the Indian radar station at Dwarka, and in 1971 it fought bravely, with the submarine Hangor sinking the Indian frigate Khukri. ## Defence of the sea frontier.
qp 6 | Why has Objectives Resolution become a compulsory part of all the constitutions of Pakistan? || قراردادِ مقاصد پاکستان کے تمام آئینوں کا لازمی حصہ کیوں بن گئی ہے؟ || د مقاصدو قرارداد د پاکستان د ټولو اساسي قوانینو لازمي برخه ولې شوې؟
sol The Objectives Resolution (1949) states the basic principles on which Pakistan was founded: the sovereignty belongs to Allah, the people use their power within the limits set by Him, democracy, freedom, equality and tolerance according to Islam, the rights of the minorities and an independent judiciary. Because it expresses the ideology of Pakistan it was made the preamble of the constitutions of 1956, 1962 and 1973, and a binding part of the 1973 constitution (Article 2A). ## The guiding principles.

qp 1 | Briefly describe the nature of Kashmir conflict between India and Pakistan. How can it be resolved? || بھارت اور پاکستان کے درمیان تنازعہ کشمیر کی نوعیت مختصراً بیان کریں۔ اسے کیسے حل کیا جا سکتا ہے؟ || د هند او پاکستان ترمنځ د کشمیر د شخړې ماهیت په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ. دا څنګه حل کېدای شي؟
sol Kashmir was a princely state with a Muslim majority. In 1947 its Hindu Maharaja Hari Singh joined India against the wish of the people, the Kashmiris rose up and the first war followed (1947-48). The matter went to the UN Security Council, which passed resolutions in 1948-49 giving the Kashmiris the right to decide their future through a plebiscite, but India has never held it; a ceasefire line (now the Line of Control) divides the state, and the 1965 war was fought over it. It can be resolved by talks and by giving the Kashmiris their right of self-determination under the UN resolutions. ## A dispute between Pakistan and India.
qp 2 | What were the causes of War on Terror? How did Pakistani armed forces get success? || دہشت گردی کے خلاف جنگ کے اسباب کیا تھے؟ پاکستانی مسلح افواج کو کامیابی کیسے ملی؟ || د ترهګرۍ پر وړاندې د جګړې لاملونه کوم وو؟ پاکستاني وسله‌والو ځواکونو بریا څنګه ترلاسه کړه؟
sol After the attacks of 11 September 2001 in the USA, the USA and its allies began a war on terrorism and attacked Afghanistan, and Pakistan became the frontline state. Terrorists then attacked Pakistan itself. The Pakistani armed forces fought them in operations such as Rah-e-Nijat in South Waziristan, Zarb-e-Azb (2014) in North Waziristan and Radd-ul-Fasaad, cleared the terrorists from the tribal areas and brought back peace, with great sacrifices of soldiers and civilians. ## 9/11 and the operations of the army.
qp 3 | Identify causes and events of the War of 1971. || 1971 کی جنگ کے اسباب اور واقعات کی نشاندہی کریں۔ || د ۱۹۷۱ د جګړې لاملونه او پېښې وپېژنئ.
sol Causes: the Bengalis of East Pakistan felt economic and political injustice; the Awami League of Sheikh Mujib won a majority in the 1970 elections but power was not handed over to it; the army action began on 25 March 1971; India helped the Mukti Bahini with arms and training. Events: India attacked East and West Pakistan on 3 December 1971; the Pakistani army in the east was surrounded, and on 16 December 1971 it surrendered at Dhaka, and East Pakistan became Bangladesh. ## 1971 war.
qp 4 | Compare the constitution of 1962 with the constitution of 1973. || آئینِ 1962 کا آئینِ 1973 سے موازنہ کریں۔ || د ۱۹۶۲ اساسي قانون له ۱۹۷۳ اساسي قانون سره پرتله کړئ.
sol The constitution of 1962 gave a presidential system in which the President had very wide powers and was elected by the Basic Democrats (indirectly), with a one-house National Assembly and weak fundamental rights and provinces. The constitution of 1973 gives a parliamentary federal system in which the Prime Minister is the real head of government and the President is head of state, with a two-house Parliament (the National Assembly and the Senate), direct elections, fundamental rights, independent judiciary and provincial rights; it is the permanent constitution. ## 1962 presidential, 1973 parliamentary.
qp 5 | "A peaceful Pakistan is the guarantee of a prosperous Pakistan." Evaluate this statement. || "پرامن پاکستان خوشحال پاکستان کی ضمانت ہے۔" اس بیان کا جائزہ لیں۔ || "سوله‌ییز پاکستان د هوسا پاکستان تضمین دی." دا څرګندونه وارزوئ.
sol The statement is true. When there is peace, factories and farms work, investors and tourists come, children go to school, and the government can spend on development instead of fighting; during the war on terror the country lost thousands of lives and billions of dollars. A peaceful Pakistan also keeps its people united and respected in the world. (Sample answer: your opinion with reasons is also accepted.) ## Evaluate the statement.
