p Pakistani forces fought gallantly and offered supreme sacrifices for the defence of country. However, faced with heavy odds and surrounded by Indian forces and Mukti Bahini on three sides and Bay of Bengal on one side Pakistani armed forces had no option but to lay down arms on 16 December 1971 to stop further bloodshed of civil population of East Pakistan. This gave birth to a new state Bangladesh. Pakistani forces numbering about 35,000 to 40,000 along with civil officials and their families totalling about 93,000 became prisoners of war.
