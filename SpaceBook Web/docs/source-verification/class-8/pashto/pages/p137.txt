He was a great spiritual figure and scholar, wrote many books and composed poetry in Pashto and Persian. His two sons Sahibzada Muhammadi and Sahibzada Ahmadi were also great scholars. On his advice Ahmad Shah Abdali defeated the Marathas at Panipat and Muslims in India were saved from destruction.
