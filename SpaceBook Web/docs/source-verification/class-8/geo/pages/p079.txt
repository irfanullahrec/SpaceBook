q 2 | Give brief answers to the following questions: || درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات دیں: || د لاندې پوښتنو لنډ ځوابونه ورکړئ:
qp 1 | Define the following terms: i. Mobility ii). Infrastructure || درج ذیل اصطلاحات کی تعریف کریں: i۔ نقل و حرکت (Mobility) ii۔ بنیادی ڈھانچہ (Infrastructure) || لاندې اصطلاحات تعریف کړئ: i. حرکت (Mobility) ii. زیربنا (Infrastructure)
sol i. Mobility: the movement of people and goods from one place to another, a basic human need carried out by transportation. ii. Infrastructure: the basic structures and facilities that a country needs for its transport and economy, such as roads, railways, ports, airports, bridges and power and communication networks. ## Answer based on the text of the unit.
qp 2 | Write the names of sea ports of Pakistan and their importance in trade. || پاکستان کی بندرگاہوں کے نام اور تجارت میں ان کی اہمیت لکھیں۔ || د پاکستان د سمندري بندرونو نومونه او په سوداګرۍ کې د هغو اهمیت ولیکئ.
sol Karachi Port, Port Qasim (Bin Qasim) and Gwadar Port are the major sea ports of Pakistan. About 95% of Pakistan's international trade goes through the ports on its coast on the Arabian Sea: imports and exports are loaded and unloaded there, and Gwadar is a deep-sea port linked with CPEC. ## Answer based on the text of the unit.
qp 3 | Write any three long term benefits of CPEC. || CPEC کے کوئی تین طویل مدتی فوائد لکھیں۔ || د CPEC درې اوږدمهالې ګټې ولیکئ.
sol Better roads, railways and ports (infrastructure) linking Pakistan with China and the region; new power projects and industrial zones that create jobs; more trade and economic growth. ## China-Pakistan Economic Corridor.
qp 4 | How does transport play a role to develop a settlement? || نقل و حمل بستی کی ترقی میں کیسے کردار ادا کرتی ہے؟ || ترانسپورټ د میشت ځای په پرمختګ کې څنګه رول لوبوي؟
sol Transport links a settlement with other places, so people, raw materials and goods can move easily. Settlements grow along roads, railways, ports and river crossings because trade, jobs, markets, schools and hospitals develop where transport reaches. ## Transport and settlements.
qp 5 | What are the strengths of transport infrastructure in Pakistan? || پاکستان میں نقل و حمل کے بنیادی ڈھانچے کی خوبیاں کیا ہیں؟ || په پاکستان کې د ترانسپورټي زیربناوو ځواکونه کوم دي؟
sol Pakistan has a wide network of roads, highways and motorways, a railway network linking the provinces, sea ports on the Arabian Sea (Karachi, Port Qasim, Gwadar), international airports, the Karakoram Highway to China, and CPEC is improving and extending these links. ## Answer based on the text of the unit.
q 3 | Give detailed answers to the following questions: || درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات دیں: || د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ورکړئ:
qp 1 | What are modes of transportation and their characteristics? || نقل و حمل کے ذرائع اور ان کی خصوصیات کیا ہیں؟ || د ترانسپورټ وسایل او د هغو ځانګړتیاوې کومې دي؟
sol Modes of transport are the ways of moving people and goods: road, railways, air and water. Road: flexible, door-to-door service and one of the cheapest, vital for internal trade. Railways: carry heavy goods and many passengers over long distances cheaply and safely. Air: the fastest but the most expensive, for urgent goods and long distances. Water (inland and ocean): slow but the cheapest way to carry bulky goods between countries. ## Answer based on the text of the unit.
qp 2 | Evaluate the importance of effective transportation for international trade. || بین الاقوامی تجارت کے لیے مؤثر نقل و حمل کی اہمیت کا جائزہ لیں۔ || د نړیوالې سوداګرۍ لپاره د اغېزناک ترانسپورټ اهمیت ارزوئ.
sol Effective transport carries goods quickly, safely and cheaply between countries, links producers with world markets, makes imports and exports possible, lowers prices, supports industries and jobs, and increases a country's income. Sea ports, shipping, air cargo, roads and railways all help trade, so a country with weak transport loses trade and competitiveness. ## Transport is the base of international trade.
qp 3 | Explain how transport is a key measure for economic development. || بیان کریں کہ نقل و حمل معاشی ترقی کا ایک کلیدی پیمانہ کیسے ہے۔ || تشریح کړئ چې ترانسپورټ د اقتصادي پرمختګ یو کلیدي پیمانه څنګه ده.
sol Economic development means better income, education, transport, infrastructure, utilities and technology. Investment in transport improves capacity, efficiency and reliability, lowers transport costs and travel time, lets businesses expand, increases productivity and competitiveness and so leads to economic growth; it also moves labour easily from homes to jobs. ## Answer based on the text of the unit.
