qp 1 | What are the causes of the Ocean Currents? || سمندری دھاروں کے اسباب کیا ہیں؟ || د سمندري جریانونو لاملونه کوم دي؟
sol Surface currents are caused by winds, and their direction is changed by the rotation of the Earth and by the edges of the continents. Deep currents are caused by differences in the salinity (saltiness) and temperature of the water. ## Ocean currents are continuous, directed movements of ocean water.
qp 2 | What are waves? || لہریں کیا ہیں؟ || څپې څه دي؟
sol Waves are series of crests and troughs formed on the surface of water by the dragging effect of winds. A wave does not carry water from one place to another; it moves energy through the water. ## Waves, tides and currents are the three motions of ocean water.
qp 3 | How are Spring and Neap tides caused? || بڑے اور چھوٹے مدوجزر کیسے پیدا ہوتے ہیں؟ || لوی او وړوکي جزر و مد څنګه رامنځته کېږي؟
sol Spring tides: when the Sun, Moon and Earth are in a straight line (full moon and new moon) their pulls add up, so high tides rise higher and low tides fall lower than normal. Neap tides: when the Sun and Moon are at right angles to the Earth their pulls work against each other, so high tides do not rise so high and low tides do not fall so low; they occur on the 7th and 21st day of the Moon. ## Both are caused by the position of the Sun and Moon.
