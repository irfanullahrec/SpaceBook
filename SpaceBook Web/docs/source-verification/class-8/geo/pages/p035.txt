qp 3 | Define Global warming. || عالمی حدت کی تعریف کریں۔ || نړیواله تودوخه تعریف کړئ.
sol Global warming is the gradual increase in the average temperature of the Earth, caused by the increase of greenhouse gases in the air (burning of fossil fuels, deforestation); temperatures have risen by about 1 degree Celsius in the last 100 years. ## Section 2.3.
qp 4 | What are consequences of Global warming? || عالمی حدت کے نتائج کیا ہیں؟ || د نړیوالې تودوخې پایلې کومې دي؟
sol Polar ice and glaciers melt, sea levels rise and flood coasts and low-lying islands, weather patterns change with more floods, droughts, heat waves and storms, crops and wildlife are threatened and diseases may spread. ## Effects of the rise in temperature.
qp 5 | What is distinct about the lifestyle of people of the coastal area? || ساحلی علاقے کے لوگوں کے طرزِ زندگی میں کیا منفرد ہے؟ || د ساحلي سیمې د خلکو په ژوندانه طرز کې څه ځانګړی دی؟
sol People of coastal areas depend on the sea: they work in fishing, boat building, ports, shipping and sea trade, salt making and tourism, and their food is mostly fish and seafood. Their weather is mild and humid, and they face storms, cyclones and floods. ## Their life is shaped by the sea.

qp 1 | What are the main causes of climate change? || موسمیاتی تبدیلی کے بنیادی اسباب کیا ہیں؟ || د اقلیمي بدلون اصلي لاملونه کوم دي؟
sol Natural causes: volcanic eruptions, changes in the energy of the Sun, changes in ocean currents. Human causes (the main cause now): burning coal, oil and gas in industries, power stations and vehicles; cutting forests; waste and farming, which add carbon dioxide, methane and other greenhouse gases to the air so that the Earth becomes warmer. ## Answer based on the text of the unit.
qp 2 | Explain relationship between extreme weather and climate change. || انتہائی موسم اور موسمیاتی تبدیلی کے درمیان تعلق بیان کریں۔ || د سختې هوا او اقلیمي بدلون ترمنځ اړیکه تشریح کړئ.
sol Climate is the average weather of a place over many years; extreme weather is a severe deviation from it. As the climate warms because of greenhouse gases, extreme weather events become more frequent and stronger: more heat waves, heavier rain and floods, stronger hurricanes and longer droughts. So extreme weather is the visible result of climate change. ## Sections 2.1 to 2.4.
qp 3 | Differentiate climate patterns of Karachi and New York. || کراچی اور نیویارک کے موسمی نمونوں میں فرق بتائیں۔ || د کراچۍ او نیویارک اقلیمي بڼې توپیر کړئ.
sol Karachi has extremely hot summers and mild, dry, windy winters; it is mostly clear all year, the temperature is usually 18 to 30 degrees Celsius, the hottest month is June (average high 34 degrees) and July is the wettest month. New York has a humid subtropical climate: cool, wet winters with snow and hot, humid summers with plentiful rain all year round (about 1267 mm a year, February the driest month). ## Section 2.5.

## Glossary || فرہنگ (Glossary) || فرهنګ (Glossary)
- Static Electricity: Electric current produced due to friction. || جامد بجلی (Static Electricity): رگڑ سے پیدا ہونے والا برقی کرنٹ۔ || ثابته برېښنا (Static Electricity): د رګړا له امله جوړ شوی برېښنايي جریان.
- Oppressive: too hot with no wind. || جابرانہ (Oppressive): بہت گرم اور ہوا کے بغیر۔ || ځورونکی (Oppressive): ډېر تود او بې‌بادي.
- Wobble: move or cause to move unsteadily from side to side. || ڈگمگانا (Wobble): ایک طرف سے دوسری طرف غیر مستحکم طریقے سے حرکت کرنا یا کروانا۔ || ولړزېدل (Wobble): له یوې خوا بلې خوا ته ناثابته حرکت کول یا کول.
- Jet stream: High speed air flow in narrow bands located at higher levels of troposphere. || جیٹ اسٹریم: ٹروپوسفیئر کی بلند سطحوں پر تنگ پٹیوں میں تیز رفتار ہوا کا بہاؤ۔ || جټ سټریم: په ټروپوسفیر په لوړو کچو کې په تنګو پټیو کې د هوا چټک بهیر.
- Orbit: Path of a planet around a star. || مدار (Orbit): کسی سیارے کا ستارے کے گرد راستہ۔ || مدار (Orbit): د ستوري شاوخوا د یوې سیارې لار.
- Cumulo-nimbus: A towering cloud famous for thunder and lightening. || کیومولو نمبس: بلند اٹھتا ہوا بادل جو گرج اور بجلی کے لیے مشہور ہے۔ || کیومولو-نمبس: لوړه ورېځ چې په تندر او برېښنا مشهوره ده.
- Funnel: Tube like. || قیف (Funnel): نلکی نما۔ || قیف (Funnel): نل‌ډوله.
- Antarctica: Southern most continent. || انٹارکٹیکا: سب سے جنوبی براعظم۔ || انټارکټیکا: تر ټولو سویلي قاره.
