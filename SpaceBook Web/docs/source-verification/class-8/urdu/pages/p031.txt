The footmen hidden in the chest heard all their conversation. So morning came and when the goldsmith and his wife were again presented before the judge, the judge again said to the goldsmith: "I gave you the night to think and reflect. Now say, are you willing to return the soldier's money or not?" The goldsmith said with great impudence: "Sir! When the chest was opened two government footmen came out of it. Pointing to them the judge said to the goldsmith: "To give a verdict without witness and proof is not the way of a court. The footmen repeated all the talk that had taken place between the goldsmith and his wife. The goldsmith's face turned pale.
