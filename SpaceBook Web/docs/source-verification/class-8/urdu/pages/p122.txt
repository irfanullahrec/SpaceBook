His brain was working fine. He remembered very well that when this gentleman, that is Nabeel Sahib, had come from the hospital in Mummy's lap, that day too similar things had been told to Ammar: "This is the little boy." "Allah has sent him to you." "He is your brother", and so on... But in a short while Ammar Mian had realised that this "Munne Mian" was getting somewhat more attention. Whoever comes brings toys, money and sweets etc. only for him. Abbu, uncles, aunts too have begun to go around carrying "Munne Mian" in their lap more than Ammar. Ammar was shocked for several days at the lessening of his importance. Several times he expressed this in different ways. For example he asked his uncle: "Uncle!
