Daily "Chattan" Karachi: Through your newspaper I wish to draw the attention of the general public to an important issue which most people ignore. Nowadays traffic on the roads has greatly increased and often traffic is seen halted at one place or another. Sometimes it gets so tangled that it takes several hours for the way to open. I only wish to submit that everyone's time is important, everyone is in a hurry to arrive and nobody wants to spend his precious time on the roads. Therefore along with ourselves we should also care for others and should drive carefully. In concern for saving our own time we should not cause trouble to others. Everyone should obey traffic signals and if all of us follow the principles of traffic we can save others' time along with our own. I shall be very grateful if you print my letter. In this way the attention of many people can be drawn to an important matter.
