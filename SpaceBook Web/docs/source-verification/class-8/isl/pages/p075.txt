After the Prophet ﷺ, the Companions (RA) remained active in continuing this process. Many conscious scholars launched many movements for national unity and obtained fruitful results. The unity and agreement of the Muslim Ummah is the most important demand of the present age. Although the Muslim Ummah is united ideologically, it needs to be united practically. The need is that we avoid disagreeing over small matters, respect one another and keep good feelings in our hearts for every Muslim. Mosques should be used for the work of reforming society. Religious madrasas should be regarded as centres of true religious education. Our curriculum should be arranged in such a way that, as a result, feelings of moderation, balance, brotherhood and unity, instead of extremism, can grow in the minds of the young generation.
