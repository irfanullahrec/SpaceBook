Active voice and Passive voice
Voice refers to the form of a verb that indicates when a grammatical subject
performs the action or is the receiver of the action.
When a sentence is written in the active voice, the subject performs the action.
When the sentence is written in the passive voice, the subject receives the action.
Example:
The emperor called Birbal. (Active voice)
Birbal was called by the emperor. (Passive voice)
Sometimes the doer of the action is omitted in passive voice sentences.
Example:
Birbal was called.
