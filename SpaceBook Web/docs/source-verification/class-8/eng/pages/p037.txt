p Forests cover 31 percent of the earth's surface or around 4.06 billion hectares. Only 18% of the world's forests are protected against destruction. Since 1990, around 420 million hectares of forest have gone. Every year between 1990 and 2010, an average of 15.5 million hectares of forest were lost. Between 2010 and 2015, 12 million hectares of forest were lost each year, representing a 22.58 percent decrease from 1990 to 2010. Between 2015 and 2020, 10 million hectares of forest were burned each year, representing a 35.48 percent decline from 1990 to 2010 and a 16.67 percent decline from 2010 to 2015.
