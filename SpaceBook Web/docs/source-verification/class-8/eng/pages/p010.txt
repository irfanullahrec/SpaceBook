exercise 1 Comprehension Checkpoint
q A | Answer the questions. || سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
qp 1 | Describe an event of justice from the life of Hazrat Muhammad Rasoolullah (ﷺ). || حضرت محمد رسول اللہ ﷺ کی زندگی سے انصاف کا ایک واقعہ بیان کریں۔ || د حضرت محمد رسول الله ﷺ د ژوند څخه د عدالت یوه پېښه بیان کړئ.
sol Once two heirs disputed the ownership of a cottage built on land that belonged to two brothers. They came to the Prophet (PBUH), who sent Hazrat Huzaifa bin Yaman (RA) to the site. Hazrat Huzaifa investigated, recorded statements and took evidence from witnesses, and then gave his decision to the Prophet (PBUH), who considered it and approved it. (Another event: a Quraish woman found guilty of stealing was punished, and the Prophet refused to let Hazrat Osama (RA) plead for her, saying earlier nations were ruined because they punished the poor and forgave the rich.) ## From the lesson.
qp 2 | What is referred to as "A piece of fire" by Hazrat Muhammad Rasoolullah (ﷺ)? || حضرت محمد رسول اللہ ﷺ نے "آگ کا ٹکڑا" کس کو فرمایا؟ || حضرت محمد رسول الله ﷺ د "اور ټوټه" څه ته ویلي دي؟
sol Deceiving a judge to get a decision in one's favour (by false witnessing or unfounded evidence) is what the Prophet (PBUH) called "a piece of fire", because a wrong cannot be made right by the judge's decision. ## From the lesson.
qp 3 | Why was an inquiry initiated by Hazrat Muhammad Rasoolullah (ﷺ) while resolving the land dispute? || زمین کا تنازعہ حل کرتے وقت حضرت محمد رسول اللہ ﷺ نے تحقیق کیوں شروع کی؟ || د ځمکې شخړې د حل پر مهال حضرت محمد رسول الله ﷺ ولې څېړنه پیل کړه؟
sol Because in the justice system of the Prophet (PBUH) a judge decides according to evidence, so the facts had to be found first: the investigator went to the site, recorded the statements of people and took evidence from witnesses before the decision was made. ## From the lesson.
qp 4 | How does the Holy Quran emphasize justice? || قرآنِ پاک انصاف پر کیسے زور دیتا ہے؟ || قرآن کریم په عدالت څنګه ټینګار کوي؟
sol The Holy Quran commands believers to stand firm for justice as witnesses for Allah even against themselves, their parents or close relatives, whether the person is rich or poor (4:135), and not to let hatred of a people make them act unjustly, even with enemies, because being just is nearer to righteousness (5:8). It also tells us that the Prophet (PBUH) is an excellent example for us (33:21). ## From the lesson.
qp 5 | What is the main idea of the lesson? || سبق کا مرکزی خیال کیا ہے؟ || د لوست مرکزي مفکوره څه ده؟
sol The Prophet (PBUH) gave everyone his or her due without fear or favour, whether the person was rich or poor, friend or foe, and he showed that no one is more firm for justice than he was. His example guides people of all times. ## Main idea of the lesson.
qp 6 | What role can justice play in our lives? || ہماری زندگی میں انصاف کیا کردار ادا کر سکتا ہے؟ || زموږ په ژوند کې عدالت کوم رول لوبولی شي؟
sol Justice protects the weak from the strong, gives everyone equal rights, creates peace and trust in society, stops crime and revenge, and makes people honest in their dealings; without justice a society is ruined. ## Sample answer: answers will vary.
endexercise
