p People travel for many different reasons. Others enjoy learning about different cultures, tasting new cuisines, and observing different lifestyles. p Tourism has many forms. It can be domestic or international. Cultural Tourism where people travel for sightseeing or visit historical places and life styles of different countries. Tourism can be Educational which is also known as Edutourism. It is to travel for the purpose of formal or informal education and life-long learning in unique natural, historical and multicultural environment. Adventure Tourism is another popular form of tourism. At times, people like to take risk, to do some extreme activities.
