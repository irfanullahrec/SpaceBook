q b | Write your interesting story. (ruled lines continue on the next page) || اپنی دلچسپ کہانی لکھیں۔ (لکیر دار جگہ اگلے صفحے پر جاری رہتی ہے) || خپله په زړه پورې کیسه ولیکئ. (کرښه لرونکې ځای په راتلونکې مخ کې دوام لري)
sol The Honest Woodcutter: A poor woodcutter was cutting wood by a river when his iron axe fell into the water. He sat down and cried. A river spirit rose and showed him a golden axe, but he said, "That is not mine." Then the spirit showed a silver axe and he said, "That is not mine either." At last the spirit showed his own iron axe and he said, "Yes, this is mine." The spirit was pleased with his honesty and gave him all three axes. Moral: honesty is always rewarded. ## Sample story: answers will vary.
