## Happiness and Health || خوشی اور صحت || خوښي او روغتیا
p Happiness and health go hand in hand. But how do we measure happiness? Perhaps health is the best way to know how happy people are. It is generally said, "Happy people generally don't get sick." Some people say that laughter is the best medicine. A number of scientists have agreed on this. It is said that 15 muscles in our face are used when we laugh and it is good for every organ in our body. Some of the benefits of laughter are that it relaxes the whole body. A good, hearty laugh relieves physical tension and stress, leaving our muscles relaxed for up to 45 minutes after. Laughter boosts the immune system and decreases stress hormones and increases immune cells and infection-fighting antibodies, thus improving our resistance to disease. Laughter triggers the release of endorphins, the body's natural feel-good chemicals. Endorphins promote an overall sense of well-being and can even temporarily relieve pain. Laughter protects the heart. It improves the function of blood vessels and increases blood flow, which can help protect us against a heart attack and other cardiovascular problems. Laughter lightens anger's heavy load. Nothing diffuses anger and conflict faster than a shared laugh. Looking at the funny side can put problems into perspective and enable you to move on from confrontations without holding onto bitterness or resentment. Laughter may even help you to live longer. A study in Norway found that people with a strong sense of humour outlived those who don't laugh as much.
