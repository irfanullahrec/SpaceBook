exercise 5G Comprehension Checkpoint
q - | Answer the questions. || سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
qp 1 | Why was the Happy Prince sad? || خوش شہزادہ اداس کیوں تھا؟ || خوشحاله شهزاده ولې خفه و؟
sol Because from his high column he could see all the ugliness and misery of the city: poor and hungry people, a sick child, a cold writer and a match-girl who would be beaten. He had lived in a palace where sorrow was not allowed, so he never knew of them while alive. ## From the lesson.
qp 2 | How did the swallow help the Happy Prince? || ابابیل نے خوش شہزادے کی کیسے مدد کی؟ || ګلې د خوشحاله شهزاده سره څنګه مرسته وکړه؟
sol The swallow carried the ruby from the Prince's sword to the poor seamstress, took his sapphire eyes to the young writer and to the match-girl, took off his gold leaves one by one and gave them to the poor, and stayed with the blind Prince and told him stories. ## From the lesson.
qp 3 | Why did the swallow feel warm? || ابابیل کو گرمی کیوں محسوس ہوئی؟ || ګلې ولې ګرمي احساس کړه؟
sol He felt warm because he had done a good deed; helping others warms the heart even when the weather is cold. ## The Prince tells him that this is what a good action feels like.
qp 4 | The story of the happy prince tells a number of things about the city. Can you identify and list them? || خوش شہزادے کی کہانی شہر کے بارے میں کئی باتیں بتاتی ہے۔ کیا آپ انہیں پہچان کر فہرست بنا سکتے ہیں؟ || د خوشحاله شهزاده کیسه د ښار په اړه ډېر شیان وايي. ایا تاسو یې پېژندلی او لیست کولی شئ؟
sol The city has rich people who enjoy themselves in beautiful houses while beggars sit at the gates; there are poor workers like the seamstress whose child is ill; starving children in dark streets; a young writer who is cold and hungry; a match-girl who may be beaten by her father; and a Mayor and Councillors who care for appearance, not for the people. The city is full of injustice and inequality. ## From the lesson.
qp 5 | Why was the statue of the Happy Prince taken down? || خوش شہزادے کا مجسمہ کیوں گرایا گیا؟ || د خوشحاله شهزاده مجسمه ولې راښکته شوه؟
sol The statue looked shabby, dull and grey after the Prince had given away his gold leaves, ruby and sapphire eyes, and a dead bird lay at its feet, so the Mayor called it ugly and had it pulled down and melted. ## From the lesson.
qp 6 | What do you think is the moral of this story? || آپ کے خیال میں اس کہانی کا سبق کیا ہے؟ || ستاسو په فکر د دې کیسې درس څه دی؟
sol The moral is that kindness, sacrifice and helping the poor are worth more than gold, beauty and position; real happiness comes from caring for others. ## Sample answer: answers will vary.
qp 7 | How does the Prince change from the beginning to the end of the story? Is he happier at the beginning or the end? Why? || کہانی کے آغاز سے اختتام تک شہزادہ کیسے بدلتا ہے؟ کیا وہ آغاز میں زیادہ خوش ہے یا اختتام پر؟ کیوں؟ || په کیسه کې له پیل تر پایه شهزاده څنګه بدلېږي؟ ایا په پیل کې ډېر خوښ دی که په پای کې؟ ولې؟
sol At the beginning the Prince lived a pleasant life in a palace and did not know sorrow; he was happy only in the sense of pleasure. As a statue he saw the misery of the city and became sad and gave everything away. At the end he is truly happy, because his lead heart is chosen by God and he praises God in the city of gold; so he is happier at the end, because his happiness comes from doing good. ## From the lesson.
endexercise
