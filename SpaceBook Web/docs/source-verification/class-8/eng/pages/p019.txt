qp B | Why can it be scary to meet new people and make new friends? || نئے لوگوں سے ملنا اور نئے دوست بنانا ڈراؤنا کیوں ہو سکتا ہے؟ || نویو خلکو سره لیدل او نوي ملګري جوړول ولې وېروونکي کېدای شي؟
sol Meeting new people can be scary because we fear being rejected or laughed at, we do not know their language, culture or habits, we feel shy and out of place, and we do not know if they will like us. ## From the lesson.
qp C | Why is it important to welcome others when they are in a new situation? || جب لوگ نئی صورتحال میں ہوں تو ان کا استقبال کرنا کیوں اہم ہے؟ || کله چې خلک په نوې حالت کې وي نو د هغوی هرکلی ولې مهم دی؟
sol Welcoming others makes them feel accepted and safe, takes away their loneliness and fear, helps them settle in and learn, and starts friendships. Omar was happy again only when Oliver welcomed him. ## From the lesson.
