q B | Short response questions || مختصر جوابی سوالات || لنډ ځوابي پوښتنې
qp 1 | Explain the features of information processing model. || معلومات کی پروسیسنگ کے ماڈل کی خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ || د معلوماتو د پروسس ماډل ځانګړتیاوې تشریح کړئ.
sol The computer's information processing model has three steps: some kind of input is obtained (data entered by the user or a device), the input is processed according to the given instructions (the program), and output is generated (the result is shown or stored). ## Input, then processing, then output.
qp 2 | Demonstrate the main steps in writing a pseudocode. || سوڈو کوڈ لکھنے کے اہم مراحل کا مظاہرہ کریں۔ || د سوډو کوډ د لیکلو اصلي ګامونه وښایاست.
sol a. Develop the solution logic in your mind by arranging the sequence of tasks. b. At the start of the pseudocode write a statement that clearly shows its main goal. c. Write the algorithm by defining sequences, repetitions (loops) or conditional statements as the solution needs, in plain English. ## Steps from section 3.2.1.
qp 3 | How nested loops are useful in arranging a calendar? || کیلنڈر ترتیب دینے میں نیسٹڈ لوپس کیسے مفید ہیں؟ || په کیلنډر ترتیبولو کې نیسټ شوي لوپونه څنګه ګټور دي؟
sol A calendar has weeks (rows) and days (columns). An outer loop repeats once for every week and an inner loop runs through the seven days of that week, printing the dates one after another, so a few lines of code arrange the whole month. Nested loops are useful when a repeated task has to be done many times inside another repetition. ## Outer loop: weeks; inner loop: days.
qp 4 | Differentiate between scope and limitations of a problem. || مسئلے کی گنجائش اور حدود میں فرق بتائیں۔ || د ستونزې د پراخوالي او محدودیتونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol The scope defines the boundaries of what a solution must provide to meet the requirements; the limitations are the aspects that are beyond the study or implementation of the problem. For a calculator, the four operations (add, subtract, multiply, divide) are its scope and other operations are its limitations. ## Example 1 of section 3.1.4.
