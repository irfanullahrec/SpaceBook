q B | Short Response Questions || مختصر جوابی سوالات || لنډ ځوابي پوښتنې
qp 1 | Describe the basic components of business plan. || بزنس پلان کے بنیادی اجزا بیان کریں۔ || د سوداګرۍ پلان بنسټیزې برخې بیان کړئ.
sol A business plan is a written document describing the company's goals and strategy. Its five basic components are: market need (customer needs), product design, costing, operations and marketing. ## The book lists customer needs, product design, costing, operations and marketing.
qp 2 | Describe the four elements of marketing. || مارکیٹنگ کے چار عناصر بیان کریں۔ || د بازارموندنې څلور عناصر بیان کړئ.
sol The four elements (4 Ps) of marketing are: Product (what is sold), Price (what the customer pays), Promotion (advertising and telling people about it) and Place (where and how the product reaches the customer). ## Product, price, promotion and place.
qp 3 | What is the difference between payment and transaction? || ادائیگی اور ٹرانزیکشن میں کیا فرق ہے؟ || د تادیې او معاملې ترمنځ توپیر څه دی؟
sol A transaction is an agreement between a buyer and a seller to exchange goods, services or financial assets for money; it is the act of conducting or carrying out business. A payment is the transfer of money against the purchase; it is the act of paying. ## Section 6.9 and 6.10.
qp 4 | Why a quality assurance is important to assess the quality of a product or service? || مصنوعہ یا خدمت کے معیار کا جائزہ لینے کے لیے کوالٹی ایشورنس کیوں اہم ہے؟ || د محصول یا خدمت د کیفیت د ارزولو لپاره د کیفیت تضمین ولې مهم دی؟
sol Quality assurance ensures the quality of products or services given to customers by improving the development process and making it efficient and effective according to the quality standards. It helps the company make a product that meets customers' expectations, which builds trust and loyalty. ## Section 6.5.
qp 5 | Write down three advantages and three disadvantages of digital marketing. || ڈیجیٹل مارکیٹنگ کے تین فوائد اور تین نقصانات لکھیں۔ || د ډیجیټل بازارموندنې درې ګټې او درې زیانونه ولیکئ.
sol Advantages: it removes geographical barriers; costs fall through automation and electronic media; responses are faster for marketers and users (also extremely low risk, customer loyalty, more exposure). Disadvantages: it depends fully on technology; security and privacy issues may arise; maintenance cost is high in a constantly changing environment (also price competition and worldwide competition). ## From the lists in section 6.11.
