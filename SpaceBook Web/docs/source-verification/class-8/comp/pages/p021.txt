q B | Short response questions || مختصر جوابی سوالات || لنډ ځوابي پوښتنې
qp 1 | Differentiate between LAN and WAN. || LAN اور WAN میں فرق بتائیں۔ || د LAN او WAN ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol A LAN (Local Area Network) covers a small area such as a room, building or campus; it is fast, cheap and owned by one organization. A WAN (Wide Area Network) covers a large area such as cities or countries; it is slower and costlier and uses public or leased lines. The Internet is the biggest WAN. ## A LAN joins computers close together, a WAN joins networks far apart.
qp 2 | What are the benefits of using networks? || نیٹ ورکس استعمال کرنے کے کیا فوائد ہیں؟ || د شبکو د کارولو ګټې کومې دي؟
sol Networks let users share resources (printers, files, the Internet connection), send messages and data quickly, keep data in one place with central backup and security, save money on hardware and software, and work together on the same task. ## These are the main benefits of connecting computers.
qp 3 | What is the role of network switch? || نیٹ ورک سوئچ کا کیا کردار ہے؟ || د شبکې سویچ رول څه دی؟
sol A switch connects the computers of a LAN. It learns the MAC address of every device and sends each message only to the computer it is meant for, while a hub sends it to all computers. This makes the network faster and reduces traffic. ## The switch directs data to its destination.
qp 4 | Differentiate between wired and wireless network. || تار والے اور وائرلیس نیٹ ورک میں فرق بتائیں۔ || د تار لرونکې او بېسیمې شبکې ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol A wired network connects devices with cables (twisted pair, fiber optic): it is fast, stable and secure but the devices are fixed and cabling costs money. A wireless network uses radio waves (Wi-Fi): devices can move and it is easy to set up, but it is usually slower, can be interfered with and is easier to attack. ## Cables against radio waves.
qp 5 | List different tasks that can be performed by robots. || ان مختلف کاموں کی فہرست بنائیں جو روبوٹس انجام دے سکتے ہیں۔ || هغه بېلابېل کارونه لیست کړئ چې روبوټونه یې ترسره کولای شي.
sol Robots can assemble, weld and paint cars in factories, pack and carry goods, clean homes, work in laboratories, help in surgery, explore space and the sea, defuse bombs and do dangerous or repeated jobs. ## Robotics is used in automobile manufacturing, packaging, laboratory research and cleaning homes.
qp 6 | Why distributed applications are used? || تقسیم شدہ ایپلی کیشنز کیوں استعمال کی جاتی ہیں؟ || ویشل شوي اپلېکېشنونه ولې کارول کېږي؟
sol A distributed application runs on several computers in a network at the same time. It is used when the workload is too much for a single server, so the work is shared; it also keeps working if one computer fails and serves users in different places. Examples: cellular networks, social networks, online banking, supermarket check-out systems. ## The book: used when the workload is too much for a single server.
qp 7 | What is the purpose of blockchain technology? || بلاک چین ٹیکنالوجی کا مقصد کیا ہے؟ || د بلاک چین ټکنالوژۍ موخه څه ده؟
sol Blockchain records digital information (for example bank transactions) in blocks that are closed and linked into a chain; the information cannot be changed or deleted, so the record is secure and can be shared safely without a middleman. ## The purpose is secure recording and sharing of digital information.
qp 8 | Describe 3D holographic imaging. || تھری ڈی ہولوگرافک امیجنگ بیان کریں۔ || 3D هولوګرافیک انځورګري تشریح کړئ.
sol 3D holographic imaging uses laser beams to create three-dimensional images called holograms. A hologram shows objects and animations that seem real; it exists freely in space and a person can walk around it and see it from all sides. ## Definition from the book.
