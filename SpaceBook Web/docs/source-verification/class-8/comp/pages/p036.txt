q B | Short Response Questions || مختصر جوابی سوالات || لنډ ځوابي پوښتنې
qp 1 | Write down the uses of Microsoft Excel. || مائیکروسافٹ ایکسل کے استعمالات لکھیں۔ || د مایکروسافټ ایکسل کارونې ولیکئ.
sol Excel is used to enter and store data in rows and columns, do calculations with formulas and functions, sort and filter data, draw charts and graphs, and prepare budgets, marks sheets, accounts and other reports. ## Excel is a spreadsheet program.
qp 2 | What is the purpose of formula bar? || فارمولا بار کا مقصد کیا ہے؟ || د فورمول بار موخه څه ده؟
sol The formula bar shows the contents (data or formula) of the active cell and is used to enter or edit them. ## It sits above the worksheet next to the Name Box.
qp 3 | Differentiate between worksheet and workbook. || ورک شیٹ اور ورک بک میں فرق بتائیں۔ || د ورک شیټ او ورک بوک ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol A worksheet is a single sheet, a grid of rows and columns, in which data is entered. A workbook is an Excel file that holds one or more worksheets. ## One workbook contains many worksheets.
qp 4 | What are the steps to create a Pie chart? || پائی چارٹ بنانے کے مراحل کیا ہیں؟ || د پای چارټ د جوړولو ګامونه کوم دي؟
sol 1. Enter the categories and their values in cells. 2. Select the data. 3. Click the Insert tab and, in the Charts group, choose Pie and the pie type. 4. The chart appears on the sheet. 5. Add or edit the chart title and data labels from Chart Design / Chart Elements. ## A pie chart shows each value as a slice of the whole.
qp 5 | Label the picture. || تصویر پر لیبل لگائیں۔ || انځور ته لیبلونه ولیکئ.
sol The Excel window has these parts: Title Bar (top, shows the file name), Ribbon (tabs and commands), Name Box (shows the address of the selected cell), Formula Bar (shows and edits the cell content), Column headings (A, B, C ...), Row headings (1, 2, 3 ...), Cell Address (such as A1), Active Cell (the cell that is selected), Vertical Scroll Bar, Horizontal Scroll Bar and Sheet Tab (names the worksheets at the bottom). Write each name in the box its arrow points to. ## The eleven boxes are filled with these eleven parts.
