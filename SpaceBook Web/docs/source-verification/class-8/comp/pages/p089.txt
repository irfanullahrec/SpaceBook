q B | Short response questions || مختصر جوابی سوالات || لنډ ځوابي پوښتنې
qp 1 | Who is cyber scout? How can he/she assist in prevention of cyber crime? || سائبر اسکاؤٹ کون ہے؟ وہ سائبر کرائم کی روک تھام میں کیسے مدد کر سکتا/سکتی ہے؟ || سایبر سکاوټ څوک دی؟ هغه د سایبري جرم په مخنیوي کې څنګه مرسته کولای شي؟
sol A cyber scout is a person who works to spread awareness about cybercrime in society. He or she is trained to identify cybercrime activities and knows how to prevent them, so he or she can help by teaching others the risks of virtual socializing, promoting cyber safety, and reporting suspicious activity. ## Section 5.8 of the book.
qp 2 | Why we need cybercrime laws? || ہمیں سائبر کرائم کے قوانین کی ضرورت کیوں ہے؟ || موږ ولې د سایبري جرم قوانینو ته اړتیا لرو؟
sol Cybercrime laws are needed to protect users and organizations from crimes such as hacking, identity theft, online fraud and piracy, to define what is illegal and give punishments, to protect privacy and property, and to let the police (FIA) take action against criminals. Without laws, criminals could misuse computers without fear. ## Laws prevent and punish cybercrime.
qp 3 | Why computer systems should be protected from unauthorized access? || کمپیوٹر سسٹمز کو غیر مجاز رسائی سے کیوں محفوظ رکھنا چاہیے؟ || کمپیوټري سیستمونه ولې باید له غیرمجاز لاسرسي وساتل شي؟
sol Unauthorized persons (hackers) can steal or change information, get credit card or bank account details for money, damage or delete data, and invade privacy. Protecting systems with passwords, antivirus and firewalls keeps data safe. ## Hackers gain unauthorized access to steal or change information.
qp 4 | How creator of copyright work financially benefits from it? || کاپی رائٹ والے کام کا تخلیق کار اس سے مالی فائدہ کیسے اٹھاتا ہے؟ || د کاپي رائټ کار جوړونکی له هغه څخه مالي ګټه څنګه اخلي؟
sol Copyright prevents others from copying the creative work without permission, so the creator can sell or license the work and earn money from every legal copy, use or performance. ## Copyright prevents copying of creative work and allows the creators to financially benefit from their work.
qp 5 | Why digital piracy is a crime? || ڈیجیٹل پائریسی جرم کیوں ہے؟ || ډیجیټل پایرسي ولې جرم دی؟
sol Digital piracy means illegally copying digital content (music, movies, software, games, e-books) without the consent of the owner. It violates the owner's copyright, takes away the owner's income and so it is a crime. ## Definition and reason from section 5.1.7.
qp 6 | Why hackers break into computer systems? || ہیکرز کمپیوٹر سسٹمز میں کیوں گھستے ہیں؟ || هیکران ولې کمپیوټري سیستمونو ته ننوځي؟
sol Hackers gain unauthorized access to computer systems to steal or change information, or to get credit card or bank account details for financial benefit, and sometimes to damage systems. ## Hacking is a cybercrime.
qp 7 | How spyware can be harmful? || اسپائی ویئر کیسے نقصان دہ ہو سکتا ہے؟ || سپایویر څنګه زیانمن کېدای شي؟
sol Spyware spies on a computer user by gathering information about their activities. It is used to steal passwords, banking PINs and other personal information, and it gets in during installation of software from the Internet. Anti-spyware or antivirus software detects and removes it. ## Spyware is a kind of malware.
