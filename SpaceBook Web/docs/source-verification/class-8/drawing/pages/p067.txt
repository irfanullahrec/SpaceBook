## Plasticine
p The original Plasticine was developed by an art teacher William Harbutt in 1897; Harbutt wanted clay that would not dry out so that his students could correct their sculptures. Plasticine clay is the trademarked name for non-toxic oil-based clay. Plasticine is similar to polymer clay in its quality to form realistic shapes and textures.
