## Model of Garlic
- Make six to eight small curved tubes of clay for each garlic piece.
- Form the inner stem of the bulb from a piece of clay slightly wider at the base.
- Fix the pieces around the stem with a touch of water to moisten, leaving the stem extending a little above the pieces.

## Model of Potato
- Form a piece of clay into an irregular oval shape to resemble potato. Randomly indent the surface of the clay with the point of a kitchen knife to form the eyes of the potato. colour it when it becomes dried. You can use poster paints or food colours.
