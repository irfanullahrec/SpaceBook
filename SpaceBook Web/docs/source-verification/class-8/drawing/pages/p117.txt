## 2nd step
p Start sketching on rough paper or news print paper which is suitable for rough sketches. Do many rough sketches from different angles. Put all these sketches on a table in front of you. Now select the best sketch. Time for this step should not be more than 15 minutes.
## 3rd Step
p After selecting the best composition start drawing it on scholar or drawing sheet by observing the subject directly. Sketch lightly while drawing, avoid dark outlines. Do not use eraser too much, it will spoil your drawing surface. 12 to 15 minutes will be enough for this sketch.
## 4th step
p Complete your work by adding full details including highlights, shadows and cast shadows in about 25 minutes.
