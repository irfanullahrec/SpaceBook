## How to Display
p Remember the following points about exhibiting your artwork.
- Display in a place with sufficient and proper light.
- It is very important to display the work on eye level. The viewer must be comfortable to watch the art work.
- The painting should not be placed too close to each other. Let there be a proper space so that the display does not look congested and every artwork gets due time and attention.
- Viewers should be asked not to touch the artwork.
- A tag with the title of the artwork and price (if it is for sale) should be stuck nearby.
- If it is a group exhibition, the artworks must be displayed in separate groups with artists' name.
- There should be variety in the works and sizes to avoid monotony.
