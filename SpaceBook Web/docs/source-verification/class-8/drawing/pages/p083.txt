## Step-3: Erasing the lines of construction
p Once you satisfied with the shape, proportion and composition of the still life, you can erase the lines of transparent construction. This will leave you with an accurate visible outline of each form and that all the objects are positioned correctly. You are now ready to work on the details of each object.
