## Light, Shade and Shadow
p It is the study of the appearance of an object and observation of light, shade and shadow. Light creates different tones and tints of a colour on an object. The correct observation and the right placement of the tones and tints is the key point to create the form or the illusion of volume. Keenly observe the sphere in fig: 2.25.
