# About the Authors
## Ajmal Shah Ajmalain
p Ajmal Shah Ajmalain got his BFA and MFA degrees from Department of Fine Arts University of Peshawar in 2003. Since then he has been working as an artist and art teacher. He has participated in various Art exhibitions in Pakistan and has been awarded with many awards and certificates from different Art councils.
## Muhammad Yousaf
p Muhammad Yousaf is working as Senior Drawing Master in a government school. He attended several training courses relating to art and drawing. He also contributed as reviewer in several textbooks of art and drawing and has vast experience in the field of art and drawing.
