q 1 | Fill in the blanks!
qp i | A response to any kind of artwork is called ______________.
sol art communication ## A response to an artwork (painting or drawing) and talking and writing about it is art communication.
qp ii | ______________ remained the source of enjoyment and communication among humans throughout the history.
sol Images (art) ## Images are the source of enjoyment, personal expression and communication among humans throughout the history.
qp iii | Artist express his feelings through colours ______________ and ______________.
sol shapes and images ## Artists express their feelings, observations and ideas through colours, shapes and images.
qp iv | ______________ is important to preserve and display an artwork.
sol Framing ## Framing is important to preserve and display an artwork.
qp v | Artwork must be displayed in a place with sufficient and proper ______________.
sol light ## Display in a place with sufficient and proper light.
q 2 | Answer the following questions.
qp 1 | What is art communication?
sol A response to any kind of artwork (painting or drawing), and then talking and writing about it, is called art communication. It is important for art education, and for it we must learn and use the appropriate terms related to art. ## Definition from the book.
qp 2 | Define exhibition.
sol The display of paintings or any kind of art works for the public in art galleries, halls or open places is called an exhibition. ## It links the public and the artist.
qp 3 | What are the essential conditions for exhibition?
sol Display the works in a place with sufficient and proper light; at eye level so that the viewer is comfortable; not too close to each other, with proper space so that it does not look crowded; ask the viewers not to touch the artworks; stick a tag with the title (and the price if for sale) nearby; in a group exhibition show the works in separate groups with the artists' names; and have variety in the works and sizes to avoid monotony. ## How to display.
qp 4 | Why it is necessary to frame the picture?
sol Framing is important to preserve and display an artwork: it protects the picture from damage and dust, and a frame of suitable colour improves the look and attraction of the painting. ## Unframed work is kept in a portfolio.
