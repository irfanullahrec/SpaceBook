q 1 | Fill in the blanks!
qp i | Arrangement of objects in a still life drawing is called ______________.
sol composition ## Arrangement of objects in a still life drawing is called composition.
qp ii | Most artists always carry a ______________ with them.
sol sketchbook ## It allows them to observe and sketch landscapes, portraits, architecture and nature.
qp iii | In an artwork the area covered by the object is called ______________ space.
sol positive ## Positive space is the area that is the object; negative space is the area around it.
qp iv | Tonal contrast gives the objects a ______________ look.
sol three dimensional (3D) ## Light and dark tones (tonal contrast) build up the form of the objects and make them look solid, three dimensional and realistic.
q 2 | Answer the following questions.
qp 1 | Why observation is necessary in drawing.
sol Observation is the most important element to draw an object or a landscape. We observe whether lines are vertical, horizontal, diagonal, straight or curved, and also colours, tones and tints, shades and shadows, textures, shapes and sizes. Artists make their sketches on the basis of keen observation, which also builds ideas and the sense of observation. ## Observation comes before drawing.
qp 2 | What is positive and negative space?
sol Space shows the relation between different objects in a drawing. Positive space is the area in a work of art that is the object itself; negative space is the area around the object. ## See the figures of the book.
qp 3 | What is landscape drawing?
sol Landscape drawing is drawing a natural view (trees, hills, sky, a path) with free hand. First make a light outline sketch (horizon line, masses of the trees, the path), then add basic tone and body shadows, then details, cast shadows and textures in the nearest objects; objects farther away are lighter and less detailed, which gives the feeling of depth and distance. ## Keen observation of shapes, angles, colours and tones is needed.
