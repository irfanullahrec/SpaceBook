## ii) Emphasis
p In art emphasis is defined as an area or object within the artwork that draws attention and becomes a focal point. An artist use many ways to show emphasis in his or her work for example by shape, texture or colour contrast. See fig: 2.5 white circle is placed in colourful strips which attract our attention but not because of colour contrast. Eye travel is towards circle by shape of the strips.
