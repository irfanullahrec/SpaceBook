## Recording observation
p Most artists always carry a sketchbook with them. It allows us to observe and sketch landscapes, portraits, architecture, nature etc. This is all visual research that stimulate our ideas and develop sense of observation. The artist or designer draw sketches on the basis of their keen observations. They make several rough sketches continuously changing and rethinking their ideas.
