## Block Making
p Material: Soft wooden board, engraving tools, paper, pencil, etc.
p Using blocks for printing on clothes is a very ancient technique. To make a block, take a slab of soft wood 3x3 or 4x4 inches. Make your designs on paper first then transfer it to the wooden block. Engrave the negative areas of the design using engraving tool carefully to avoid injury. Left positive areas of the image untouched. After engraving the negative areas image will be left raised as relief work.
