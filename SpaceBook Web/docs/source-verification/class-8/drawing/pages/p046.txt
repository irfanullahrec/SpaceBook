Combining each colour on the general palette with each of the other colour gives you around 50 new colours. The illustration above shows the results when blue, red and yellow are mixed with each of the other colours on the palette. You can take the experiment further and do the same using every other colour on the palette. The colours are laid out in the same order as the basic palette above.
