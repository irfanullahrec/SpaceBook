@deco Fig. 5.1: a painting of four women's faces in a stained-glass style of red, green, yellow and blue blocks
- 1. Cultural and historical touch has been given by adding some local decorative motifs.
- 2. Symbolic shapes are the source of enjoyment and communication which are shown at the background.
- 3. The focal point of the painting are the women faces.
- 4. The expressions are full of sorrow and stress.
- 5. Generally the painting looks colourfull and decorative but the question is "why these women are in sorrow and stress!"
