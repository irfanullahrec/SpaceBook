## Making model from Card Board
p Children like to make models from card board e.g. making models of boat, birds, and aeroplane etc. Model making from card board is easy. Place the object to be drawn on card board and cut its outlines first, then by folding techniques bring it to the required shape. This method is used for making 3D models.
