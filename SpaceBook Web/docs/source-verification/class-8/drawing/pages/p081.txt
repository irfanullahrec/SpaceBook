## Materials

## Step - 1: Composition
p Arrangement of objects in a still life drawing is called composition. When composing a still life, try to introduce the qualities that make an interesting arrangement. You need to be aware of the abstract structure of your arrangement, its rhythms and contrasts of line, shape, tone, colour, pattern, texture and form.
