## Making Textures
## By Using Salt
p Texture can be obtained by dropping granules of rock salt onto wet colour. Remove the salt granules when the paint is completely dry. It will become marbled texture.
@deco Fig. 2.33: a bowl of salt, an orange wash with salt marks and a green marbled texture
## Sponging
p Apply colour with a small natural sponge. This texture is ideal for tree foliage.
@deco Fig. 2.34: a sponge dabbing blue and orange paint
