## 1. Composition
p Composition is the arrangement of Art elements e.g. line, shape, volume, colour, value, texture according to the principles of art.
## Principles of Composition
p The principles of visual art are the rules/ guidelines that artists use to organize the elements of art in an artwork.
p Some common principles of art are, movement, harmony, variety, balance, rhythm, emphasis and contrast. When these principles are successfully combined with elements of art. They help creating an interesting work of art.
## i) Balance
p Balance in art means visual balance. It is the arrangement of different art elements in such a way as to give a sense of visual balance. See fig: 2.1(a), this composition has been balanced by placement of trees at both the sides. In the landscape given in Fig. 2.1(b), trees are indicated in yellow outline showing simple balance.
