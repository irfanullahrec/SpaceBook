## Application of light, shade and shadow
p Light, shade and shadow helps you to simplify something as complex as the rocks along the coast of a sea, it helps if you classify groups of planes that are roughly parallel. The rocks in fig: 2.27 can be grouped in four different plans.
- 1. Top plans in highlight
- 2. Side plans in lighter mid tone
- 3. Front plans in darker mid tone
- 4. Side plans in shadow
p Observe how the complex detail is avoided and the general impression of the scene is painted by showing larger area of light, shade, and shadow.
