## Allocation of time
p To complete and art work or your assignment in time you need to learn how to allocate time for different steps of an assignment. For this purpose the given assignment should be divided in various steps, for example selection of subject and its composition through rough sketches, drawing and shading and final touches. All these steps need different time duration. You need practice to allocate proper time for each step.
## Finishing an assignment in time
## 1st step
p For example you have been given an assignment of drawing fruits, observe for 5 minutes that how to arrange these fruits. Should there be distance between the fruits or they should overlap, overlapping will look better.
