q 2 | Answer the following questions.
qp i | What is stencil and for what purpose it is used.
sol A stencil is a sheet of cardboard or x-ray sheet in which the positive area of a motif has been cut out. Colour is applied through it to print the same motif again and again on almost any surface, including 3D and round surfaces such as vases and pots; the technique saves time. ## Stencil printing.
qp ii | How will you make a block?
sol Material: a soft wooden board (3 x 3 or 4 x 4 inches), engraving tools, paper and pencil. Draw the design on paper first and transfer it to the wooden block. Carefully engrave (cut away) the negative areas of the design with the engraving tool, avoiding injury, so that the positive design stands out. Colour the raised design and press it on the cloth or sheet. ## Block making.
qp iii | For what purpose plasticine is being used.
sol Plasticine is a non-toxic oil-based modelling clay that does not dry out. It is used to make models and sculptures and to form realistic shapes, and mistakes can be corrected easily. ## It was invented by William Harbutt in 1897.
