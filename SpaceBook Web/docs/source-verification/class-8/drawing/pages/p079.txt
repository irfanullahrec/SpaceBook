## Positive and Negative Space
p Space has great importance in drawing. It shows relation between different objects in a drawing. Positive space is described as the areas in a work of art that are the object. Negative space are areas around the object. See the following figures.

## Observing and sketching a still life
p Still life is the best subject in art for learning and teaching the skills of drawing and painting. It teaches you how to look at objects like an artist with a perceptive awareness of their outline, shape, proportions, tone, colour, texture, form and composition.
