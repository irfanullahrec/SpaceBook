## iii) Harmony
p Harmony in art is defined as the combine effect of similar or related art elements in an artwork which create visual satisfaction. An art work is consists of different objects. Observe fig: 2.8. Every landscape consists of the same elements like trees, leaves, land and sky but each one looks different from one another, because they have their own strong harmony or oneness.
