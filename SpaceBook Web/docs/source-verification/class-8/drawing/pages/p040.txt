## vi) Contrast
p Contrast referred to the difference in two related elements such as colour, shape, value, texture, directions and movement. Contrast is often created by using complementary colours or extremely light and dark tones. The more contrast is shown the more a viewers attention is obtained.
p Observe fig. 2.13, consisting of grapes, bananas with red apple and cherry's. The painter has used complementary colours red and green as well as a very strong contrast of light and dark.
