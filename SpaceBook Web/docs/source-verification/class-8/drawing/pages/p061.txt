## Stencil and its Uses
p Stencil as a printing technique, can be used on almost any surface. The purpose of printing techniques is to cover the surface with repetition of the same motif. This technique saves time.
p Material: card board or x-ray sheet, cutter, masking tape, glass slab.
p First of all, sketch and finalize your motif on paper. Take a thick sheet or card board, x-ray sheet is more suitable for this technique. Transfer the motif to the card board or x-ray sheet. Now put it on glass slab for cutting. This will make the cutting much easier. After cutting from all sides take out the positive area, now stencil is ready.
p Unlike block printing stencil can be used on 3D and round surfaces e.g. vases, pots etc.
