## iv) Variety
p Variety is the quality or state of having different elements in an art work, but they are placed in such a way that they do not give an impression of monotony or sameness. Variety can be easily created in an artwork by using different colours, sizes and shapes of objects. See Fig: 2.9a and 2.9b.
