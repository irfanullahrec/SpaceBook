## Exhibition/ Display
p Display of paintings or any kind of art works for public in Art galleries, hall or open places is called exhibition.
p Exhibitions serve many purposes for example public gets a chance to enjoy art work. It is a good source for public to be educated about art. The artist and his work is known to the public. By selling artist's work artist gets economic benefit. Exhibition makes link between public and the artist.
