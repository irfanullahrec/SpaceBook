## Space
p Realistic painting and drawing usually has three sections. Background, mid-ground and foreground. On picture plane the main object/ objects create mid-ground and background is the area behind the objects. The frontal area of the object is known as foreground. The whole area surrounding the object including mid, back and foreground is called space.
## Colour
p Colour is one of the most important elements of art. We are surrounded by colours, nothing is colourless. You can produce all these colours by mixing three basic colours i.e. red, yellow, and blue.
