## Polygons
p A shape bounded by more than two straight lines e.g. triangle, rectangle, squares etc. are called polygons.
## Types of Polygons
p Numerous regular and irregular polygons may be seen in nature. In minerals, crystals are often arranged in triangular, square or hexagonal shaped polygons. The most beautiful types of hexagons are the honey comb made by honeybees. There are animals also having polygonal patterns e.g. giraffe, leopard, turtle etc.
