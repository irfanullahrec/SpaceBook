## Art Communication
p A response to any kind of artwork, (painting or drawing) and then talking and writing about it, is called art communication which is important for art education. In this regard, you must learn and use the appropriate terms related to art.
p An English letter (H) is the combination of two vertical and one horizontal line and the same lines with different positions and combinations make a geometrical shape, triangle and other numerous organic and natural images. These Images are the source of enjoyment, personal expression and communication among humans throughout the history. Artists express their feelings, observations and ideas through colours, shapes and images. An arrangement of these images in different ways with colourfull texture and composition conveys meanings to communicate the inner view, observation and experiences of the artist.
