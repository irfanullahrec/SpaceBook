## Model Making
p Making objects from modelling clay is one of the favorite activity of many students. Commercial varieties of modelling clay are available, but is usually expensive, and can even contain chemicals and other toxic ingredients. Making homemade or traditional modelling clay is simple.

## Making peas in a pod
- 1. Make a piece of clay into a leaf-like shape to make the pea pod.
- 2. Hollow the center of the clay into a boat shape using the handle of a teaspoon.
- 3. Pinch the ends into points for the stalk and the tip of the pod.
