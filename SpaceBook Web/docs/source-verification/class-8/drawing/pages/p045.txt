## Primary colours
p The three basic colours i.e. red, yellow, and blue are called primary colours. All other colours are made by mixing these three colours.
@deco Fig. 2.21: three colour wheels showing primary colours, secondary colours and tertiary colours
## Colour Mixing
p Practice is the most effective way to develop your own colour sense, so do get out your paints and try to make the mixtures for yourself. Be systematic with your colour mixing and keep your palette clean. As a general rule, use only two or three colours in each mixture while you are getting used to mixing.
