## v) Rhythm
p Rhythm can be described as timed movement through space. It is showing consistency with colours or lines. It is the repetition of art elements in a way to create interest and movement. In fig: 2.10 coloured wavy strips create an impression of consistency and move the eye along the image. Rhythm can make an art work seem active. See also fig. 2.11 and 2.12.
@deco Fig. 2.10: green, pink, white and black wavy strips
