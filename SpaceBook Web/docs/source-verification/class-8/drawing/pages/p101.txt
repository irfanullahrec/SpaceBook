## How to Draw a Mountain View
p In this section, you will learn how to draw a mountain view. Here is an illustration of the scene.
@deco Fig. 4.32: the finished mountain view with snow-capped mountains, sun, a fence and a winding road
## Step: 1
p Lightly draw a line across the page for the horizon. Place it about one-third of the way from the top. Add four curved lines as shown in Fig: 4.33. Show that the road goes off into the distance by bringing the lines closer together at the top. This use of lines that come together to show depth is known as perspective.
