## Texture
p Texture is defined as the tactile quality of a surface. It refers to the smoothness or roughness of a surface. Everything around us has texture, whether it is bark of a tree, rock, your classroom wall, or your desk. Close your eyes and rub your hand on a tree stem. Is the tree stem smooth, or rough? Now go near to your class window and rub your hand on window glass. What do you feel? Window glass is much smoother as compare to tree stem. Every surface has its own quality called texture see fig: 2.29 and 2.30.
