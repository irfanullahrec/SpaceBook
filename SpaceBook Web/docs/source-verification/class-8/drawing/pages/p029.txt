q 1 | Fill in the blanks.
qp i | Drawing without using geometrical instrument is called ______________.
sol free hand sketching ## Drawing without using geometrical instruments is called free hand sketching.
qp ii | ______________ paper is used to reduce or enlarge the size of an image.
sol Graph ## Graph paper (equally spaced horizontal and vertical lines making equal squares) is used to copy an image and to enlarge or reduce its size.
qp iii | Honey comb is a good example of ______________ shape.
sol hexagonal (polygon) ## The most beautiful hexagons in nature are the honey combs made by honeybees.
qp iv | "3D" stands for ______________.
sol three dimensional ## A 3D form has length, width and depth, for example a cube, cone or cylinder.
qp v | Combination of two or more than two solids form a ______________ solid.
sol compound ## A combination of two or more solids creates a compound solid.
q 2 | Answer the following questions.
qp i | What is free hand sketching?
sol Drawing without using geometrical instruments is called free hand sketching. It needs a good deal of observation and practice; the key to good sketching is to identify the position and direction of the lines. ## Done with the hand only.
qp ii | What do you mean by perspective?
sol Perspective is a technique used by artists and designers to show three dimensional (3D) objects on a two dimensional picture plane so that they look realistic and natural. The size of objects changes with distance (near objects look larger and far-away objects smaller), which creates the illusion of depth. ## Perspective gives depth to a flat drawing.
qp iii | How compound solids are formed?
sol A solid is a three dimensional form such as a cube, cone or cylinder. When two or more such solids are combined (joined, placed on or inside each other), they form a compound solid. ## For example a cylinder with a cone on top.
qp iv | Define polygons.
sol A polygon is a shape bounded by more than two straight lines, for example a triangle, rectangle or square. Polygons may be regular or irregular and are seen in nature, such as the hexagonal honey comb and crystals. ## Definition from the book.
qp v | Explain the term "eye level".
sol The horizontal line in front of our eyes is called the eye level. An object can be seen on the eye level, above the eye level or below the eye level. ## Basic of perspective drawing.
