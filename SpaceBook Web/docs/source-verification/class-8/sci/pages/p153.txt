q B | Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
qp 1 | How can we see ordinary, nonluminous objects? || ہم عام غیر منور اشیا کو کیسے دیکھ سکتے ہیں؟ || موږ عادي، غیر روښانه شیان څنګه لیدلای شو؟
sol Non-luminous objects do not make their own light. We see them because light from a luminous source (sun, bulb) falls on them, is reflected from their surface and enters our eyes. ## Reflection of light.
qp 2 | Do you need a full-length mirror to see your hair and your feet at the same time? Does it depend on how far you stand from the mirror? || کیا اپنے بال اور پاؤں ایک ہی وقت میں دیکھنے کے لیے آپ کو پورے قد کا آئینہ چاہیے؟ کیا یہ اس پر منحصر ہے کہ آپ آئینے سے کتنی دور کھڑے ہیں؟ || ایا د خپلو ویښتانو او پښو په یو وخت لیدلو لپاره تاسو ته د بشپړ قد هینداري ته اړتیا ده؟ ایا دا په دې پورې اړه لري چې تاسو له هینداري څخه څومره لرې ولاړ یاست؟
sol No: a mirror only half your height is enough to see your whole image (hair to feet). It does not depend on how far you stand from the mirror, because the image gets smaller as you go back but still is seen in the same mirror length. ## Plane mirror geometry.
qp 3 | What is the minimum number of mirrors needed to see back of your head? || اپنے سر کا پچھلا حصہ دیکھنے کے لیے کم از کم کتنے آئینے درکار ہیں؟ || د خپل سر شاته برخه د لیدلو لپاره لږ تر لږه څو هینداروو ته اړتیا ده؟
sol Two mirrors are needed, one in front and one behind (inclined at the right angle), so that the light from the back of the head is reflected twice into the eyes. ## As in a barber's shop.
qp 4 | At which position does the image and object has same magnification? || کس مقام پر تصویر اور شے کا سائز برابر ہوتا ہے؟ || په کوم ځای کې انځور او شی په ورته اندازه وي؟
sol The image and object have the same magnification (the same size) when the object is placed at the centre of curvature (2F) of a concave mirror; a plane mirror always gives an image of the same size. ## Magnification equal to 1.
qp 5 | Can a convex mirror produce a real image of an object? || کیا محدب آئینہ کسی شے کی حقیقی تصویر بنا سکتا ہے؟ || ایا محدب هینداره د یو شي حقیقي انځور جوړولای شي؟
sol No. A convex mirror always forms a virtual, erect and smaller image behind the mirror, whatever the position of the object, because it makes the reflected rays diverge. ## Convex mirror.
q C | Long Questions || طویل سوالات || اوږدې پوښتنې
qp 1 | State laws of reflection of light. Discuss how a plane mirror forms a virtual image. || روشنی کے انعکاس کے قوانین بیان کریں۔ بحث کریں کہ سطح آئینہ خیالی تصویر کیسے بناتا ہے۔ || د رڼا د انعکاس قوانین بیان کړئ. وڅېړئ چې یوه مستوي هینداره خیالي انځور څنګه جوړوي.
sol Laws of reflection: (1) the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection; (2) the incident ray, the reflected ray and the normal at the point of incidence lie in the same plane. In a plane mirror the reflected rays diverge, but when they are extended backwards they appear to meet at a point behind the mirror: this is the virtual image. It is upright, of the same size as the object, laterally inverted and as far behind the mirror as the object is in front; it cannot be taken on a screen. ## Laws of reflection and virtual image.
qp 2 | What is white light? How is the colour of light related to reflection and absorption of light? || سفید روشنی کیا ہے؟ روشنی کا رنگ روشنی کے انعکاس اور جذب سے کیسے متعلق ہے؟ || سپینه رڼا څه ده؟ د رڼا رنګ د رڼا له انعکاس او جذب سره څنګه تړاو لري؟
sol White light is a mixture of the seven colours (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet). An opaque object has the colour of the light it reflects and it absorbs the other colours: a red object reflects red and absorbs the rest, a white object reflects all colours and a black object absorbs all. ## Colour, reflection and absorption.
qp 3 | Explain the image formation by convex and concave mirrors. || محدب اور مقعر آئینوں سے تصویر کی تشکیل کی وضاحت کریں۔ || په محدب او مقعر هینداره کې د انځور جوړېدل تشریح کړئ.
sol Concave mirror (converging): beyond C it forms a real, inverted, smaller image between F and C; at C a real, inverted image of the same size at C; between C and F a real, inverted, magnified image beyond C; at F the rays go parallel (no image); between F and the pole a virtual, erect, magnified image (shaving mirror). Convex mirror (diverging): for every position of the object the image is virtual, erect and smaller, formed behind the mirror between the pole and the focus (rear-view mirror). ## Images of spherical mirrors.
q D | Structured Response Questions || ساختی جوابی سوالات || جوړښتي ځوابي پوښتنې
qp 1 | Series of plane mirrors and starting point for a light beam is shown in the figure. You are required to draw the path of light, showing (incident ray, reflected ray and normal) in each reflection. Note that the angle of incidence must be equal to angle of reflection and that all the mirrors may not be used. || سطح آئینوں کی ایک سیریز اور روشنی کی بیم کا نقطۂ آغاز شکل میں دکھایا گیا ہے۔ آپ کو روشنی کا راستہ بنانا ہے جس میں ہر انعکاس میں (واقع شعاع، منعکس شعاع اور عمود) دکھائیں۔ نوٹ کریں کہ زاویۂ وقوع زاویۂ انعکاس کے برابر ہونا چاہیے اور یہ کہ تمام آئینے استعمال نہ بھی ہوں۔ || د سطحي هینداروو لړۍ او د رڼا شعاع پیل ټکی په انځور کې ښودل شوي. تاسو باید د رڼا لار رسم کړئ، په هر انعکاس کې (پېښېدونکې وړانګه، منعکسه وړانګه او نارمل) وښیئ. یادونه وکړئ چې د پېښېدو زاویه باید د انعکاس له زاویې سره برابره وي او دا چې ټولې هینداري ښايي نه کارول شي.
sol The beam from the torch meets the upper-left slanting mirror first. At that point draw the normal (a line at 90 degrees to the mirror) and then the reflected ray on the other side of the normal at the same angle as the incident ray: the beam meets the mirror at only about 21 degrees to its surface, so the angle of incidence is about 69 degrees and the angle of reflection is also about 69 degrees. The reflected ray goes off to the right, rising gently at about 21 degrees above the horizontal. From the figure this ray passes above the upper end of the slanting mirror on the right and does not meet the other mirrors, so only the first mirror is used. ## Drawing question: the first reflection is worked out from the angles measured on the figure.
