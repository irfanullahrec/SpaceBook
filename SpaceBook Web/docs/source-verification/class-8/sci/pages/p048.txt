q B | Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
qp 1 | Why is meiosis important in organisms that reproduce sexually? || جنسی تولید کرنے والے جانداروں میں میوسس کیوں اہم ہے؟ || په جنسي تولید کوونکو موجوداتو کې مییوسیس ولې مهم دی؟
sol Meiosis makes the gametes (sperms and eggs) with half the number of chromosomes (haploid). When they join in fertilisation the full (diploid) number is restored, so the chromosome number stays the same from generation to generation; meiosis also makes new combinations of genes, so the offspring show variations. ## Importance of meiosis.
qp 2 | State the importance of mitosis in human life. || انسانی زندگی میں مائٹوسس کی اہمیت بیان کریں۔ || په انساني ژوند کې د مایټوسیس اهمیت بیان کړئ.
sol Mitosis gives growth of the body, repair of wounds and damaged tissue, and replacement of dead and worn out cells (like skin and blood cells); it also makes the single fertilised egg grow into a whole human body. ## Importance of mitosis.
qp 3 | What is the significance of spindle fibres in cell division? || خلیاتی تقسیم میں سپنڈل ریشوں کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || په حجروي ویش کې د سپینډل تارونو اهمیت څه دی؟
sol Spindle fibres attach to the chromosomes and pull the chromatids to opposite poles of the cell, so that each daughter cell gets the same, complete set of chromosomes. ## Spindle fibres.
qp 4 | You never see frogs, snakes and lizards during extreme winter as they hibernate. What type of adaptation is it? Explain. || آپ شدید سردی میں مینڈک، سانپ اور چھپکلیاں کبھی نہیں دیکھتے کیونکہ وہ سرمائی نیند میں چلے جاتے ہیں۔ یہ کس قسم کی موافقت ہے؟ وضاحت کریں۔ || په سخت ژمي کې تاسو چنګښتې، مارانې او ګوګلان نه ګورئ ځکه چې هغوی ژمنۍ خوب ته ځي. دا کوم ډول تطابق دی؟ تشریح یې کړئ.
sol It is a behavioural (and physiological) adaptation: in the cold season these animals go into a deep sleep (hibernation) in burrows, their body processes slow down and they live on stored food, so they can survive the winter when food is scarce and it is too cold. ## Hibernation.
q C | Long Questions || طویل سوالات || اوږدې پوښتنې
qp 1 | What are variations? Give different causes of variations. || تغیرات کیا ہیں؟ تغیرات کی مختلف وجوہات بیان کریں۔ || تغیرات څه دي؟ د تغیراتو بېلابېل لاملونه ورکړئ.
sol Variations are the differences among the individuals of the same species (e.g. height, skin colour, shape of the nose). Causes: genetic causes, i.e. mutations, crossing over and independent assortment of chromosomes in meiosis, and the random joining of gametes in fertilisation; and environmental causes, i.e. climate, food, sunlight, exercise and disease. ## Variations and their causes.
qp 2 | Describe the process of mitosis in detail. || مائٹوسس کے عمل کو تفصیل سے بیان کریں۔ || د مایټوسیس پروسه په تفصیل سره بیان کړئ.
sol Mitosis: the cell has already copied its DNA in the interphase. Prophase: the chromosomes become short and thick and visible (each of two chromatids), the nuclear membrane disappears and the spindle forms. Metaphase: the chromosomes line up at the equator of the cell and the spindle fibres attach to them. Anaphase: the chromatids separate and are pulled to opposite poles. Telophase: the chromosomes reach the poles and become thin again, nuclear membranes re-form around them, and the cytoplasm divides (cytokinesis) to give two identical daughter cells with the same number of chromosomes as the parent. ## Stages of mitosis.
q D | Structured Response Questions || ساختی جوابی سوالات || جوړښتي ځوابي پوښتنې
qp 1 | Look at the coloured spiral structure of DNA. i) Show units of DNA molecule in different colours. Adenine ______ (colour); Guanine ______ (colour); Cytosine ______ (colour); Thymine ______ (colour). ii) Explain how these units pair to form DNA molecule. || ڈی این اے کی رنگین گھومتی ساخت دیکھیں۔ i) ڈی این اے مالیکیول کی اکائیوں کو مختلف رنگوں میں دکھائیں۔ ایڈینین ______ (رنگ)؛ گوانین ______ (رنگ)؛ سائٹوسین ______ (رنگ)؛ تھائمین ______ (رنگ)۔ ii) وضاحت کریں کہ یہ اکائیاں ڈی این اے مالیکیول بنانے کے لیے کیسے جوڑا بناتی ہیں۔ || د ډي اېن اې رنګینه مارپیچ جوړښت وګورئ. i) د ډي اېن اې مالیکول واحدونه په بېلابېلو رنګونو وښیئ. اډېنین ______ (رنګ)؛ ګوانین ______ (رنګ)؛ سایټوسین ______ (رنګ)؛ تایمین ______ (رنګ). ii) تشریح کړئ چې دا واحدونه څنګه د ډي اېن اې مالیکول جوړولو لپاره جوړه کېږي.
sol (i) Any four different colours can be used, with the bases that pair given matching colours, e.g. Adenine: red; Thymine: green; Guanine: blue; Cytosine: yellow. (ii) The bases pair in a fixed way: adenine pairs with thymine (two hydrogen bonds) and guanine pairs with cytosine (three hydrogen bonds); these base pairs make the steps of the twisted ladder (double helix) whose sides are sugar and phosphate. ## The colours are the student's choice; the pairing is fixed.
