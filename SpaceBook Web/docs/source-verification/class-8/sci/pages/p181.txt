p Now the parabola needs a frame to complete the parabolic solar cooker construction. The frame should allow it to stay stable while in use. Keep in mind that the parabolic solar cooker can accumulate wind pressure due to its shape. The frame should be stiff enough and fixed to the ground, in such cases. A simple example of a frame is shown in figure 11.8.
