qp 1 | What is the most important material in toothpaste? || ٹوتھ پیسٹ میں سب سے اہم مادہ کون سا ہے؟ || په د غاښو پیسټ کې تر ټولو مهم مواد کوم دی؟
sol An alkali (a weak base). It neutralises the acid made by bacteria from food particles in the mouth, and so it prevents tooth decay. ## From the lesson on toothpaste.
qp 2 | Why does milk turn into plastic when vinegar is added to it? || سرکہ ڈالنے پر دودھ پلاسٹک میں کیوں بدل جاتا ہے؟ || کله چې سرکه ورزیاته شي شیدې ولې په پلاستیک بدلېږي؟
sol Milk contains a protein called casein. When an acid such as vinegar is added, the casein molecules join to form a long chain product called casein plastic (milk plastic), which separates as curds and can be moulded. ## From the lesson on milk plastic.
qp 3 | What is saponification? || صابونیت (سیپونیفیکیشن) کیا ہے؟ || صابونیت (سپونیفیکېشن) څه دی؟
sol Saponification is the reaction in which oil or fat is heated with an alkali (sodium hydroxide) to form sodium salts of fatty acids, which are the soaps (and glycerol). ## From the lesson on soap.
qp 4 | Why it is better to use concave mirror rather than convex mirror in solar cooker? || سولر کوکر میں محدب آئینے کے بجائے مقعر آئینہ استعمال کرنا بہتر کیوں ہے؟ || په لمریز پخلي ماشین کې د محدب هینداري پر ځای مقعر هینداره کارول ولې ښه دي؟
sol A concave mirror converges the parallel rays of the sun to a single point, the focus, where the cooking pot is placed, so the heat is concentrated and the food cooks; a convex mirror spreads the rays out (diverges them) and cannot concentrate the heat. ## Focusing of sunlight.
qp 5 | What are the advantages and disadvantages of using a solar cooker? Are there places where solar cookers would have limited utility? || سولر کوکر استعمال کرنے کے کیا فوائد اور نقصانات ہیں؟ کیا ایسی جگہیں ہیں جہاں سولر کوکر کی افادیت محدود ہوگی؟ || د لمریز پخلي ماشین د کارولو ګټې او زیانونه کوم دي؟ ایا داسې ځایونه شته چېرته چې لمریز پخلي ماشینونه محدوده ګټه ولري؟
sol Advantages: it uses free, clean, renewable energy, needs no fuel, makes no smoke or pollution, and is safe. Disadvantages: it works only in bright sunshine in the daytime, cooking is slow, it must be turned towards the sun and it is not good for frying. It has limited use in cloudy, rainy or foggy places and in cold regions with little sunshine, and at night. ## Solar cooker.
qp 6 | What potential you see for the solar cooker to be used in Pakistan? || آپ پاکستان میں سولر کوکر کے استعمال کی کیا صلاحیت دیکھتے ہیں؟ || تاسو په پاکستان کې د لمریز پخلي ماشین د کارونې کوم ظرفیت ګورئ؟
sol Pakistan receives plenty of sunshine in most of the year, so solar cookers can save wood, gas and money, especially in villages and in Sindh, Balochistan and Punjab, and reduce the cutting of trees. They have good potential for cooking and for heating water. ## Sample answer: answers will vary.
qp 7 | When can wind power be used? || ہوائی طاقت کب استعمال کی جا سکتی ہے؟ || د باد ځواک کله کارېدای شي؟
sol Wind power can be used in places where strong winds blow steadily: open plains, coastal areas, hilltops and the sea (offshore), e.g. Jhimpir and Gharo in Sindh. ## Wind power.
qp 8 | Why might engineers be interested in developing wind power? || انجینئر ہوائی طاقت تیار کرنے میں کیوں دلچسپی رکھ سکتے ہیں؟ || انجینران ولې د باد د ځواک په پراختیا کې لیوالتیا لرلای شي؟
sol Wind is a free, renewable and clean source of energy: it does not give out smoke or greenhouse gases, it saves fossil fuels and it can supply electricity in remote places, so engineers want to develop it. ## Wind power.
qp 9 | If you want to install a windmill for your home, where will you place your wind turbine? || اگر آپ اپنے گھر کے لیے ہوائی چکی لگانا چاہیں تو آپ اپنی ہوائی ٹربائن کہاں رکھیں گے؟ || که تاسو غواړئ د خپل کور لپاره باد ګرځنده ماشین ولګوئ نو خپل باد توربین به چېرته کېږدئ؟
sol I would place it on a high tower or on a high open place, free from buildings and trees that block the wind and where the wind blows strongly and steadily. ## Placing a wind turbine.
q C | Long Questions || طویل سوالات || اوږدې پوښتنې
qp 1 | What is spherical mirror type solar cooker? How it can be used to cook food? || کروی آئینے والا سولر کوکر کیا ہے؟ اسے کھانا پکانے کے لیے کیسے استعمال کیا جا سکتا ہے؟ || د کروي هینداري ډوله لمریز پخلی ماشین څه دی؟ دا د خوړو د پخولو لپاره څنګه کارېدای شي؟
sol It is a solar cooker with a large concave (spherical) mirror as reflector. The sunlight falling on the mirror is reflected and focused at the focus of the mirror; a dark pot of food is placed there and the focused heat cooks it. The mirror has to be turned towards the sun from time to time. ## Spherical mirror solar cooker.
qp 2 | What is a wind turbine? How it is used to produce electricity? || ہوائی ٹربائن کیا ہے؟ یہ بجلی بنانے کے لیے کیسے استعمال ہوتی ہے؟ || باد توربین څه دی؟ دا د برېښنا د تولید لپاره څنګه کارېږي؟
sol A wind turbine is a machine with large blades fixed on a shaft at the top of a tall tower. The wind turns the blades and the shaft, and the shaft turns a generator that changes the kinetic energy into electricity. Many turbines together make a wind farm. ## Wind turbine.
qp 3 | What is UPS? Where and how is it used? || یو پی ایس کیا ہے؟ یہ کہاں اور کیسے استعمال ہوتا ہے؟ || UPS څه دی؟ دا چېرته او څنګه کارول کېږي؟
sol UPS (uninterruptible power supply) is a device with a rechargeable battery and an inverter. It charges the battery when the mains supply is on and, when the electricity goes off (load shedding), it changes the stored direct current into alternating current to run lights, fans and computers. It is used in homes, offices and hospitals. ## UPS.
