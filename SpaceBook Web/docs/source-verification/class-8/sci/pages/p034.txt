q B | Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
qp 1 | Name three types of neurons and give their functions. || نیورانز کی تین اقسام کے نام بتائیں اور ان کے افعال لکھیں۔ || د نیورونونو درې ډولونه ونوموئ او دندې یې ولیکئ.
sol Sensory (afferent) neurons carry impulses from the receptors (sense organs) to the brain and spinal cord; motor (efferent) neurons carry impulses from the brain and spinal cord to the effectors (muscles and glands); interneurons (relay neurons) lie in the brain and spinal cord and connect sensory neurons with motor neurons and pass on the information. ## Three types of neurons.
qp 2 | Which receptors and effectors are involved in the reflex actions of: (a) Blinking of eyes in light spark (b) Withdrawal of hand on touching hot object. || درج ذیل انعکاسی افعال میں کون سے ریسیپٹرز اور عمل کنندے شامل ہیں: (الف) روشنی کی چمک میں آنکھیں جھپکنا (ب) گرم چیز کو چھونے پر ہاتھ کھینچ لینا۔ || په لاندې انعکاسي عملونو کې کوم ریسپټورونه او اغېزمنوونکي برخه لري: (الف) د رڼا په جرقه کې د سترګو رپول (ب) د ګرم شي په لمس لاس ایستل.
sol (a) Blinking of the eyes in a light spark: receptor: the light-sensitive cells of the retina of the eye; effector: the muscles of the eyelids. (b) Withdrawal of the hand on touching a hot object: receptors: the heat and pain receptors in the skin of the hand; effectors: the muscles of the arm. ## Receptors and effectors of reflex actions.
qp 3 | What is the significance of peripheral nervous system? || اطرافی اعصابی نظام کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د محیطي عصبي سیستم اهمیت څه دی؟
sol The peripheral nervous system (the cranial and spinal nerves) joins the brain and spinal cord to all parts of the body: it carries messages from the receptors to the central nervous system and carries the commands from it to the muscles and glands, so the body can sense and respond. ## Peripheral nervous system.
qp 4 | Differentiate between receptors and effectors. || ریسیپٹرز اور عمل کنندوں میں فرق کریں۔ || په ریسپټورونو او اغېزمنوونکو کې توپیر وکړئ.
sol Receptors are the cells or organs (eye, ear, skin, nose, tongue) that detect a stimulus, a change in the surroundings, and start an impulse. Effectors are muscles and glands that receive the impulses from the nervous system and respond by moving or secreting. ## Receptors detect, effectors respond.
qp 5 | What is the significance of interneurons? || بین نیورانز کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د منځګړو نیورونونو اهمیت څه دی؟
sol Interneurons are found in the brain and spinal cord. They connect the sensory and motor neurons, process the information and decide the response; they are needed for reflex arcs, thinking, memory and learning. ## Significance of interneurons.
q C | Long Questions || طویل سوالات || اوږدې پوښتنې
qp 1 | Give the structure of human brain and roles of different parts of brain. || انسانی دماغ کی ساخت اور دماغ کے مختلف حصوں کے کردار بیان کریں۔ || د انسان د دماغ جوړښت او د دماغ د بېلابېلو برخو رول بیان کړئ.
sol The human brain has three main parts. The cerebrum (forebrain) is the largest part with two hemispheres: it controls thinking, memory, intelligence, speech, voluntary movements and the senses. The cerebellum (at the back, below the cerebrum) controls balance, posture and the coordination of movements. The brain stem (midbrain, pons and medulla oblongata) joins the brain with the spinal cord: the medulla controls involuntary actions such as heartbeat, breathing, swallowing and vomiting, the pons passes messages and helps in breathing, and the midbrain controls reflex movements of eyes and head. The thalamus and hypothalamus (also in the forebrain) pass sensory messages and control body temperature, hunger and thirst. ## Structure and functions of the brain.
qp 2 | Differentiate between reflex arc and reflex action and explain the pathway followed by nerve impulse in a reflex arc. || انعکاسی قوس اور انعکاسی عمل میں فرق کریں اور انعکاسی قوس میں عصبی تحریک کے راستے کی وضاحت کریں۔ || په انعکاسي قوس او انعکاسي عمل کې توپیر وکړئ او په انعکاسي قوس کې د عصبي تکان لاره تشریح کړئ.
sol A reflex action is a quick, automatic, involuntary response to a stimulus that happens without thinking, e.g. pulling the hand from a hot object. A reflex arc is the pathway followed by the nerve impulse in the reflex action: stimulus, receptor, sensory neuron, interneuron (in the spinal cord), motor neuron, effector (muscle), response. ## Reflex action is the response; the reflex arc is its pathway.

qp 1 | Medulla oblongata, pons and midbrain collectively form brain stem. a. Look at the functions controlled by these parts and suggest what will happen if brain stem dies? b. What will happen if connection between cerebellum and brain stem is damaged in an accident? || میڈیولا اوبلونگاٹا، پونز اور درمیانی دماغ مل کر دماغی تنا بناتے ہیں۔ الف۔ ان حصوں کے کنٹرول کردہ افعال دیکھیں اور بتائیں کہ اگر دماغی تنا مر جائے تو کیا ہوگا؟ ب۔ اگر کسی حادثے میں سیریبیلم اور دماغی تنے کا تعلق خراب ہو جائے تو کیا ہوگا؟ || میډولا اوبلانګاټا، پونز او منځنی دماغ یوځای دماغي ساقه جوړوي. الف. د دغو برخو کنټرول شوي فعالیتونه وګورئ او ووایئ که دماغي ساقه مړه شي نو څه به پېښ شي؟ ب. که په یوه پېښه کې د سیریبیلم او دماغي ساقې اړیکه زیانمنه شي نو څه به پېښ شي؟
sol (a) The brain stem controls breathing, heartbeat, blood pressure and swallowing, so if it dies breathing and the heartbeat stop and the person dies (brain death). (b) If the cerebellum and the brain stem are cut off from each other the person loses balance, posture and the coordination of movements: he would not be able to walk or stand steadily and his movements would be shaky. ## Functions of the brain stem and the cerebellum.
