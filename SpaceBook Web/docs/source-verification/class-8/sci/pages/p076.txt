qp 4 | Write three properties that make gold suitable for making jewellery. || تین خصوصیات لکھیں جو سونے کو زیورات بنانے کے لیے موزوں بناتی ہیں۔ || درې ځانګړتیاوې ولیکئ چې سره زر د ګاڼو جوړولو لپاره مناسب کوي.
sol Gold is suitable for jewellery because it is shiny and attractive, it does not rust or tarnish (unreactive), and it is malleable and ductile so it can be shaped into ornaments; it is also rare and valuable. ## Properties of gold.
qp 5 | In which portion of the periodic table are present: a. Metals b. Non-metals || دوری جدول کے کس حصے میں پائی جاتی ہیں: الف۔ دھاتیں ب۔ غیر دھاتیں || د دوراني جدول په کومه برخه کې شته: الف. فلزات ب. غیر فلزات
sol a. Metals are on the left side and in the centre of the periodic table (Groups IA, IIA and the transition elements). b. Non-metals are on the right side (upper right), e.g. Groups VIA, VIIA and VIIIA; hydrogen is at the top. ## Position of metals and non-metals.
qp 6 | Metals wires can be transformed into different shapes without breaking. Why? || دھاتوں کی تاروں کو ٹوٹے بغیر مختلف شکلوں میں ڈھالا جا سکتا ہے۔ کیوں؟ || د فلزاتو تارونه پرته له ماتېدو په بېلابېلو بڼو بدلېدای شي. ولې؟
sol Metals are ductile and malleable: their atoms are in layers that slide over one another without breaking the metallic bond, so the wires can be bent and shaped without breaking. ## Ductility and malleability.
qp 7 | Why electrical cables are covered with plastics? || بجلی کی تاروں پر پلاسٹک کیوں چڑھایا جاتا ہے؟ || د برېښنا کېبلونه په پلاستیکونو ولې پوښل کېږي؟
sol Plastic is an insulator, so it stops the current from leaking, prevents electric shocks and short circuits, and protects the wire. ## Plastic is a bad conductor of electricity.

qp 1 | How can you locate an element in the periodic table? Identify the location of following elements in the Periodic Table? a) Aluminium (Atomic number 13) b) Silicon (Atomic number 14) c) Fluorine (Atomic number 9) || آپ دوری جدول میں کسی عنصر کا مقام کیسے معلوم کر سکتے ہیں؟ دوری جدول میں درج ذیل عناصر کا مقام بتائیں: الف) ایلومینیم (ایٹمی نمبر 13) ب) سلکان (ایٹمی نمبر 14) ج) فلورین (ایٹمی نمبر 9) || تاسو په دوراني جدول کې د یو عنصر ځای څنګه موندلای شئ؟ په دوراني جدول کې د لاندې عناصرو ځای وټاکئ: الف) المونیم (اتومي نمبر 13) ب) سیلیکون (اتومي نمبر 14) ج) فلورین (اتومي نمبر 9)
sol The period of an element is the number of its shells and its group is the number of electrons in its outermost shell. a) Aluminium (13): 2, 8, 3: Period 3, Group IIIA. b) Silicon (14): 2, 8, 4: Period 3, Group IVA. c) Fluorine (9): 2, 7: Period 2, Group VIIA. ## Electronic configuration decides the position.
qp 2 | Justify that properties of metals make it suitable for making many objects or its parts. Explain with three examples. || ثابت کریں کہ دھاتوں کی خصوصیات انہیں بہت سی اشیا یا ان کے حصے بنانے کے لیے موزوں بناتی ہیں۔ تین مثالوں سے وضاحت کریں۔ || وښیئ چې د فلزاتو ځانګړتیاوې یې د ډېرو شیانو یا د هغو د برخو جوړولو لپاره مناسبوي. په درېیو بېلګو یې تشریح کړئ.
sol Properties of metals decide their use: (1) copper is a good conductor of electricity, so it is used for electric wires; (2) aluminium is light and malleable, so it is used for aircraft parts and cooking foil; (3) iron and steel are strong and hard, so they are used for bridges, buildings and machines (gold, being shiny and unreactive, is used for jewellery). ## Three examples.
q D | Structured Response Questions || ساختی جوابی سوالات || جوړښتي ځوابي پوښتنې
qp 1 | Following figure shows some elements in the Periodic Table (figure below; questions a–h on the next page) || درج ذیل شکل دوری جدول میں کچھ عناصر دکھاتی ہے (نیچے شکل؛ سوالات الف تا ح اگلے صفحے پر) || لاندې انځور په دوراني جدول کې ځینې عناصر ښيي (لاندې انځور؛ پوښتنې الف تر ح بل مخ کې)
sol 1. a) A and F (Group IA). b) A and C are in Period 2; B and D in Period 3; E and F in Period 4. c) C is in Group IIIA. d) A and F (one electron in the outermost shell). e) E is a noble gas. f) B is the alkaline earth metal. g) E is in Group VIIIA (the noble gases). h) B (two electrons in its outermost shell). 2. a) Carbon has four electrons in its outermost shell, and the group number is the number of outer electrons. b) It needs four more electrons to complete its valence shell. c) It can make four single bonds. 3. Oxygen (8): 2, 6; Fluorine (9): 2, 7; Neon (10): 2, 8. Oxygen has six outer electrons (needs 2 more), fluorine seven (needs 1 more), and neon eight, a full shell, so it is stable and unreactive. ## From the figure and electronic configurations (the elements are read from their positions).
