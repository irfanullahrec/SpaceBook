@issue The page title prints "TABLE 9.1 - RAY TRACING FOR CONCAVE MIRRORS" although the previous page refers to "Table 9.2" (a numbering inconsistency in print; kept as printed). The table has three columns (Description, Comments, Illustration); the Illustration cells show a concave mirror with C, F, P and an object arrow, with the named ray drawn: Light ray A (parallel to the axis, reflects through F), Light ray P (to the pole, reflects at equal angle), Light ray F (through F, reflects parallel), Light ray C (through the centre of curvature, reflects back on the same path). The illustrations are described in the text of each row.

tr | Light ray A: Draw a ray from the top of the object to the mirror parallel to the principal axis. Draw the reflected ray through the focal point. | Since this incident ray is parallel to the principal axis, it will reflect through the focal point. | A ray parallel to the axis hits the mirror at A and reflects through F (C, F, P marked).
tr | Light ray P: Draw a ray from the top of the object to the pole P. tr | Light ray F: Draw a ray from the top of the object through the focal point and on to the mirror. tr | Light ray C: Draw a ray from the top of the object through the center of curvature and on to the mirror. Draw the reflected ray antiparallel over it. | The ray of light passing through the center of curvature of concave mirror is reflected back along the same path. | C ray passes through C, hits the mirror perpendicular, and reflects back on the same path.
