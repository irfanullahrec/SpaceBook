qp 1 | What is a chemical reaction? Give two examples. || کیمیائی تعامل کیا ہے؟ دو مثالیں دیں۔ || کیمیاوي تعامل څه دی؟ دوه بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol A chemical reaction is a change in which one or more substances (the reactants) change into new substances (the products) with different properties. Examples: burning of magnesium, 2Mg + O2 gives 2MgO; rusting of iron; burning of methane, CH4 + 2O2 gives CO2 + 2H2O. ## Chemical reaction.
qp 2 | State the law of conservation of mass. || کمیت کی بقا کا قانون بیان کریں۔ || د کتلې د ساتنې قانون بیان کړئ.
sol The law of conservation of mass says that in a chemical reaction mass is neither created nor destroyed; the total mass of the reactants is equal to the total mass of the products. ## Law of conservation of mass.
qp 3 | Write a balanced chemical equation for the reaction between methane and oxygen. || میتھین اور آکسیجن کے درمیان تعامل کے لیے متوازن کیمیائی مساوات لکھیں۔ || د میتان او اکسیجن ترمنځ تعامل لپاره انډول شوې کیمیاوي معادله ولیکئ.
sol CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O ## Balanced: 1 carbon, 4 hydrogen and 4 oxygen atoms on each side (methane burns in oxygen to give carbon dioxide and water).
qp 4 | Show formation of NaCl by electron-dot and electron-cross structures. || الیکٹران ڈاٹ اور الیکٹران کراس ساختوں سے NaCl کی تشکیل دکھائیں۔ || د NaCl جوړېدل د الکټرون ټکي او الکټرون کراس جوړښتونو په وسیله وښیئ.
sol Sodium (2, 8, 1) has one electron in its outer shell and chlorine (2, 8, 7) has seven. In the electron-dot and cross structure the sodium atom gives its one outer electron (cross) to the chlorine atom, which receives it (dot) to make eight: Na+ (2, 8) and Cl- (2, 8, 8). The two oppositely charged ions attract each other and form the ionic bond of NaCl. ## Na x + Cl (with dots) becomes [Na]+ [Cl (8 electrons)]-.
qp 5 | Show the formation of MgO by electron-dot and electron-cross structure (Atomic number Mg=12, O=8) || الیکٹران ڈاٹ اور الیکٹران کراس ساخت سے MgO کی تشکیل دکھائیں (ایٹمی نمبر Mg=12، O=8) || د MgO جوړېدل د الکټرون ټکي او الکټرون کراس جوړښت په وسیله وښیئ (اتومي نمبر Mg=12، O=8)
sol Magnesium (Z = 12; 2, 8, 2) gives its two outer electrons (crosses) to oxygen (Z = 8; 2, 6), which takes them (dots) to complete its octet. Mg becomes Mg2+ (2, 8) and O becomes O2- (2, 8); the attraction between Mg2+ and O2- is the ionic bond of MgO. ## Transfer of two electrons.
qp 6 | Define the following: a) Double covalent bond b) Triple covalent bond || درج ذیل کی تعریف کریں: الف) دوہرا کوویلنٹ بانڈ ب) تہرا کوویلنٹ بانڈ || لاندې تعریف کړئ: الف) دوه ګونی همزوجي بانډ ب) درې ګونی همزوجي بانډ
sol a) A double covalent bond is formed when two atoms share two pairs (four) of electrons, as in oxygen, O=O. b) A triple covalent bond is formed when two atoms share three pairs (six) of electrons, as in nitrogen, N≡N. ## Multiple covalent bonds.
