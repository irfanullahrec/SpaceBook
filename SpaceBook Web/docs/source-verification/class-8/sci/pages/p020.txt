qp 1 | What are the major causes of pollution? || آلودگی کے بڑے اسباب کیا ہیں؟ || د ککړتیا اصلي لاملونه کوم دي؟
sol The major causes of pollution are burning fossil fuels (vehicles, factories and power plants), deforestation, industrial waste and sewage thrown into water, overuse of pesticides and fertilisers, dumping of plastic and other solid waste, loud noise, and the growing population. ## Causes of air, water and land pollution.
qp 2 | Differentiate between primary and secondary consumers. || بنیادی اور ثانوی صارفین کے درمیان فرق کریں۔ || د لومړني او دوهم درجه مصرفوونکو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Primary consumers are herbivores that feed directly on producers (green plants), e.g. rabbit, goat, grasshopper. Secondary consumers are carnivores (or omnivores) that feed on primary consumers, e.g. a snake that eats a rabbit, a frog that eats a grasshopper. ## Food chain: plant, primary consumer, secondary consumer.
qp 3 | What is mutualism? Give some examples. || میوچوئلزم کیا ہے؟ کچھ مثالیں دیں۔ || متقابله ګټه څه ده؟ څو بېلګې ورکړئ.
sol Mutualism is a relationship between two organisms in which both get benefit. Examples: Rhizobium bacteria in the roots of pulse plants fix nitrogen for the plant and get food from it; bees collect nectar from flowers and in return pollinate them; lichen (an alga and a fungus living together). ## Both partners benefit.
qp 4 | Enlist the ways to control air pollution. || ہوا کی آلودگی پر قابو پانے کے طریقے لکھیں۔ || د هوا د ککړتیا د کنټرول لارې لیست کړئ.
sol Air pollution can be controlled by using public transport, CNG, electric or well-tuned vehicles, fitting filters and chimneys in factories, planting more trees, using clean sources of energy (solar, wind), not burning rubbish and crop residue, and making and obeying laws against smoke. ## Ways to control air pollution.
qp 5 | Why sewage water must be treated before releasing it into streams? || گندے پانی کو ندیوں میں چھوڑنے سے پہلے اس کی صفائی کیوں ضروری ہے؟ || فاضله اوبه ولې باید ویالو ته د پرېښودو مخکې تصفیه شي؟
sol Sewage contains harmful germs (bacteria and viruses), organic waste and chemicals. If it is released untreated it pollutes streams and drinking water, spreads diseases such as cholera and typhoid, uses up the oxygen of the water and kills fish and other water life. Treatment removes the solids, waste and germs first. ## Sewage treatment.
qp 6 | How can you grow plants of warm area in a region with low environmental temperature? || کم ماحولیاتی درجہ حرارت والے علاقے میں گرم علاقے کے پودے آپ کیسے اگا سکتے ہیں؟ || په هغه سیمه کې چې د چاپېریال تودوخه یې ټیټه ده د تودې سیمې نباتات څنګه روزلی شئ؟
sol Plants of a warm area can be grown in a cold region inside a greenhouse (glasshouse): its glass or plastic walls let sunlight in and trap the heat, so the temperature inside stays warm and can also be controlled with heaters and ventilation. ## Greenhouse.
q C | Long Questions || تفصیلی سوالات || اوږدې پوښتنې
qp 1 | Explain carbon cycle and find the reasons for the gradual increase of carbon dioxide in atmosphere. || کاربن کے چکر کی وضاحت کریں اور فضا میں کاربن ڈائی آکسائیڈ کے بتدریج اضافے کی وجوہات تلاش کریں۔ || د کاربن دوره تشریح کړئ او په اتموسفیر کې د کاربن ډای اکسایډ د تدریجي زیاتوالي لاملونه ومومئ.
sol Carbon cycle: plants take in carbon dioxide from the air for photosynthesis and make food (carbon compounds); animals get carbon by eating plants or other animals; carbon dioxide goes back to the air by respiration of living things, by the decay of dead plants and animals, and by burning of wood and fossil fuels; the dead remains of living things buried for millions of years also become coal, oil and gas (fossil fuels), and the oceans dissolve some carbon dioxide. The carbon dioxide in the air is increasing because of burning of fossil fuels in vehicles, factories and power stations, deforestation (fewer trees to take it in), forest fires, the growing population and industry. ## The carbon cycle keeps the amount of carbon dioxide balanced; human activities upset it.
qp 2 | How positive activities of human can help to restore an ecosystem? || انسان کی مثبت سرگرمیاں ماحولیاتی نظام بحال کرنے میں کیسے مدد دے سکتی ہیں؟ || د انسان مثبتې کړنې څنګه کولی شي ایکوسیستم بیرته جوړ کړي؟
sol Positive human activities that help to restore an ecosystem: planting trees (afforestation), protecting forests and wildlife by national parks and reserves, controlling hunting and fishing, cleaning rivers and treating waste, recycling and reusing things, using clean energy, keeping balance in food chains by saving predators and prey, and raising awareness. ## Restoring an ecosystem.

qp 1 | Blind Indus dolphin is endemic to river Indus. It is an endangered species of water animal in Pakistan. || اندھی انڈس ڈالفن دریائے سندھ کی مقامی نوع ہے۔ یہ پاکستان میں آبی جانور کی خطرے سے دوچار نوع ہے۔ || ړوند سند ډالفین د سند سیند اصلي ډول دی. دا په پاکستان کې د اوبو ځناور یو ګواښ لرونکی ډول دی.
sol (a) The dolphin is threatened by dams and barrages that break up its river, water diversion that lowers the river flow, pollution, fishing nets and hunting. (b) It is an endangered species: only about 2,000 are left, mostly in the Indus between Guddu and Sukkur barrages. (c) To save it: protect its habitat (Indus Dolphin Reserve), keep enough water and clean water in the river, control fishing nets and hunting, rescue stranded dolphins and teach people about it. ## Answers of parts a, b and c.
