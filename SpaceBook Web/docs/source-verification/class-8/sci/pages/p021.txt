qp a | Enlist the factors which lead to the threat of extinction of Indus dolphin. || انڈس ڈالفن کے معدوم ہونے کے خطرے کا باعث بننے والے عوامل لکھیں۔ || هغه عوامل لیست کړئ چې د سند ډالفین د ورکېدو ګواښ لامل کېږي.
sol Factors leading to the extinction of the Indus dolphin: dams and barrages cut the river into pieces and stop its movement; less water in the river and its diversion for irrigation; pollution from industry, farm chemicals and sewage; entanglement in fishing nets; hunting for oil and meat; noise of boats. ## Threats to the Indus dolphin.
qp b | What is the present status of this mammal? || اس ممالیہ کی موجودہ حیثیت کیا ہے؟ || د دې تی لرونکي اوسنی وضعیت څه دی؟
sol It is an endangered species (IUCN): only about 2,000 Indus river dolphins survive, mainly between the Guddu and Sukkur barrages in Sindh. ## Present status.
qp c | Suggest the steps to help conservation of Indus dolphin. || انڈس ڈالفن کے تحفظ میں مدد کے لیے اقدامات تجویز کریں۔ || د سند ډالفین د ساتنې د مرستې لپاره ګامونه وړاندیز کړئ.
sol Steps for its conservation: protect and manage its habitat (reserves), make sure enough clean water flows in the river all year, control pollution, ban hunting and the use of harmful nets, rescue and release stranded dolphins, count them regularly and make people aware of its importance. ## Conservation steps.
qp 2 | Most of the islands of Maldives are less than one meter above the sea level. || مالدیپ کے زیادہ تر جزائر سطحِ سمندر سے ایک میٹر سے بھی کم بلند ہیں۔ || د مالدیپ ډېر ټاپوګان د سمندر له کچې یو متر ټیټ لوړ دي.
sol (a) Global warming melts the polar ice and makes the sea water expand, so the sea level rises; low islands like the Maldives would be flooded and finally go under water, people would lose homes and fresh water, and the land would be eroded. (b) Coastal cities can be saved by building sea walls and flood barriers, planting mangrove forests, making good drains and pumps, raising buildings, controlling the emission of greenhouse gases and giving early warnings. ## Answers of parts a and b.
qp a | What could be the effect of global warming on such islands across the globe? || دنیا بھر میں ایسے جزائر پر عالمی حدت کا کیا اثر ہو سکتا ہے؟ || په ټوله نړۍ کې په داسې ټاپوګانو د نړیوال ګرمېدا اغېز څه کېدای شي؟
sol Global warming melts glaciers and polar ice and warms the sea, so the sea level rises. Islands less than a metre above sea level would be flooded and could be submerged, the salty water would spoil the soil and fresh water, and the people would have to leave. ## Effect of global warming on low islands.
qp b | Suggest the ways to save coastal cities from flooding or being submerged. || ساحلی شہروں کو سیلاب یا ڈوبنے سے بچانے کے طریقے تجویز کریں۔ || ساحلي ښارونه له سیلاب یا ډوبېدو د ژغورلو لارې وړاندیز کړئ.
sol Ways to save coastal cities: build sea walls and flood barriers, plant mangrove forests along the coast, build proper drainage and water pumps, build houses on higher ground or on stilts, protect wetlands and reduce the greenhouse gases that cause global warming, and give early warnings of floods. ## Protecting coastal cities.
