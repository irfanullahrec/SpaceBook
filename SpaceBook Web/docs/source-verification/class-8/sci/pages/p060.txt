q B | Short Questions || مختصر سوالات || لنډې پوښتنې
qp 1 | What could be the advantages of microbes produced dyes over synthetic dyes? || مصنوعی رنگوں کے مقابلے میں خرد حیاتیات سے بننے والے رنگوں کے کیا فوائد ہو سکتے ہیں؟ || د مصنوعي رنګونو په پرتله د مایکروبونو لخوا جوړ شویو رنګونو ګټې څه کېدای شي؟
sol Dyes made by microbes are natural, biodegradable and safe for the environment and for people, and are made from renewable materials, while synthetic dyes are made from chemicals that pollute water and soil and may harm health. ## Microbe-made dyes.
qp 2 | Give the advantages of using biodegradable plastic. || حیاتیاتی طور پر تحلیل پذیر پلاسٹک کے استعمال کے فوائد بیان کریں۔ || د بایو تجزیه کېدونکي پلاستیک د کارولو ګټې ورکړئ.
sol Biodegradable plastic is broken down by microbes, so it does not pile up as waste, it does not choke drains or harm animals and it reduces pollution of land and water; it can be made from renewable materials like starch or milk. ## Biodegradable plastic.
qp 3 | What is the significance of fermented food? || خمیر شدہ خوراک کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د تخمیر شویو خوړو اهمیت څه دی؟
sol Fermented foods (yoghurt, cheese, bread, pickles) are made with the help of microbes; they last longer, taste better, are easier to digest and are rich in vitamins and useful bacteria, and fermentation also makes products like vinegar. ## Significance of fermented food.
qp 4 | How biotechnology can transform the way of treating diseases? || حیاتی ٹیکنالوجی بیماریوں کے علاج کے طریقے کو کیسے بدل سکتی ہے؟ || بایوټکنالوجي د ناروغیو د درملنې طریقه څنګه بدلولای شي؟
sol Biotechnology makes medicines such as insulin, growth hormone and vaccines by genetic engineering of bacteria; it helps in the early diagnosis of diseases and in gene therapy for hereditary diseases, and gives antibiotics, so many diseases can be cured or prevented. ## Biotechnology and diseases.
qp 5 | What could be the best solution to mineral deficiency diseases in third world countries? || تیسری دنیا کے ممالک میں معدنی کمی کی بیماریوں کا بہترین حل کیا ہو سکتا ہے؟ || په دریمې نړۍ هېوادونو کې د منرالونو د کمښت ناروغیو غوره حل څه کېدای شي؟
sol The best solution is to grow biofortified crops (genetically improved rice, wheat or maize rich in iron, zinc and vitamins) and to give foods fortified with minerals, along with mineral supplements. ## Mineral deficiency diseases.
q C | Long Questions || طویل سوالات || اوږدې پوښتنې
qp 1 | Explain how biotechnology is helping to solve issues of food shortage? || وضاحت کریں کہ حیاتی ٹیکنالوجی خوراک کی کمی کے مسائل حل کرنے میں کیسے مدد دے رہی ہے؟ || تشریح کړئ چې بایوټکنالوجي د خوړو د کمښت ستونزو په حل کې څنګه مرسته کوي؟
sol Biotechnology increases the food supply by crops that give high yields and resist pests, diseases and drought (genetically modified crops such as Bt corn), crops with more nutrients, tissue culture to grow many plants quickly, better animal breeds and vaccines for animals, and food that keeps longer. ## Biotechnology and food shortage.
qp 2 | What are the applications of biotechnology? || حیاتی ٹیکنالوجی کے استعمالات کیا ہیں؟ || د بایوټکنالوجۍ کارونې کومې دي؟
sol Applications of biotechnology: in agriculture (GM crops, tissue culture, biofertilisers), medicine (insulin, vaccines, antibiotics, gene therapy), food (yoghurt, cheese, bread), industry (enzymes, biodegradable plastic, dyes, biofuels), environment (cleaning oil spills and waste with microbes) and forensic science (DNA fingerprinting). ## Applications of biotechnology.
