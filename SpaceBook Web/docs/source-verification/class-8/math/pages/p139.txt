example 10
p Simplify the following: || درج ذیل کو مختصر کریں: || لاندې ساده کړئ:
qp i | $(x + 6)(x - 6)$
qp ii | $\left(2p + \frac{1}{2q}\right)\left(2p - \frac{1}{2q}\right)$
## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m i. $(x + 6)(x - 6)$
m $= (x)^2 - (6)^2$
m $= x^2 - 36$
m ii. $\left(2p + \frac{1}{2q}\right)\left(2p - \frac{1}{2q}\right)$
m $= (2p)^2 - \left(\frac{1}{2q}\right)^2$
m $= 4p^2 - \frac{1}{4q^2}$
endexample
example 11
p Simplify: $108 \times 92$ with the help of formula. || فارمولے کی مدد سے مختصر کریں: $108 \times 92$ || د فورمول په مرسته ساده کړئ: $108 \times 92$
## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m $108 \times 92 = (100 + 8)(100 - 8)$
m $= (100)^2 - (8)^2$
m $= 10000 - 64 = 9936$
endexample
