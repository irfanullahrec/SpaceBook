example 8
p If $x - y = 4$ and $xy = 2$, find $x^2 + y^2$ || اگر $x - y = 4$ اور $xy = 2$ ہو تو $x^2 + y^2$ معلوم کریں || که $x - y = 4$ او $xy = 2$ وي، $x^2 + y^2$ معلوم کړئ
## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m $x - y = 4$ (given)
m $(x - y)^2 = (4)^2$ (taking square of both sides)
m $x^2 - 2xy + y^2 = 16$
m $x^2 - 2(2) + y^2 = 16$
m $x^2 - 4 + y^2 = 16$
m $x^2 + y^2 = 16 + 4$
m $x^2 + y^2 = 20$
endexample
example 9
p If $x - \frac{1}{x} = 4$, find $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$ || اگر $x - \frac{1}{x} = 4$ ہو تو $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$ معلوم کریں || که $x - \frac{1}{x} = 4$ وي، $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$ معلوم کړئ
## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m $x - \frac{1}{x} = 4$
m $\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 = (4)^2$ (taking square of both sides)
m $x^2 - 2(x)\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) + \frac{1}{x^2} = 16$
m $x^2 - 2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = 16$
m $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = 16 + 2$
m $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = 18$
endexample

### Identity IV: $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$ || مماثلت IV || څلورم مطابقت
p **Algebraic Proof:** || **الجبری ثبوت:** || **الجبري ثبوت:**
m RHS. $= (a - b)(a + b)$
m $= a(a + b) - b(a + b)$
m $= a^2 + ab - ba - b^2$
m $= a^2 + ab - ab - b^2$
m $= a^2 - b^2$
m $=$ LHS
m Hence proved $(a - b)(a + b) = a^2 - b^2$
p Thus, LHS. = RHS. || اس طرح LHS. = RHS. || نو LHS. = RHS.
p **Geometric Proof:** || **ہندسی ثبوت:** || **هندسي ثبوت:**
