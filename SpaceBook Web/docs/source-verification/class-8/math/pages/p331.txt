# Important Formulas || اہم فارمولے || مهم فورمولونه
- Profit = selling price − cost price = S.P − C.P || منافع = قیمت فروخت − قیمت خرید = S.P − C.P || ګټه = د پلورلو بیه − د پېرودنې بیه = S.P − C.P
- Loss = cost price − selling price = C.P − S.P || نقصان = قیمت خرید − قیمت فروخت = C.P − S.P || زیان = د پېرودنې بیه − د پلورلو بیه = C.P − S.P
eq \text{Profit \%} = \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{cost price}} \times 100\%,\qquad \text{Loss \%} = \frac{\text{Loss}}{\text{cost price}} \times 100\%
eq \text{Discount} = M.P - S.P,\qquad \text{Percentage Discount} = \frac{\text{Discount}}{M.P} \times 100\%
eq (a+b)^2 = a^2 + b^2 + 2ab,\quad (a-b)^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab,\quad (a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2
eq \pi = \frac{\text{circumference}}{\text{diameter}} = \frac{C}{d},\quad C = \pi d,\quad \text{Diameter} = 2 \times \text{radius}
eq \text{Area of a circle} = \pi r^2,\qquad \text{Circumference} = 2\pi r
eq \text{Total surface area of a cylinder} = 2\pi r(h + r),\quad \text{each flat face} = \pi r^2,\quad \text{curved face} = 2\pi r h,\quad \text{Volume} = \pi r^2 h
eq \text{Total surface area of a rectangular prism} = 2 \times [(l \times w) + (l \times h) + (w \times h)]
eq \text{Area of cube} = 6 \times l^2,\qquad \text{Area of square prism} = 6 \times l^2
eq \text{Total surface area of a right prism} = bh + (s_1 + s_2 + s_3) \times L
eq \text{Volume of right rectangular prism} = l \times w \times h,\qquad \text{Volume of right square prism} = l \times l \times l
eq \text{Volume of the triangular prism} = \text{base area} \times \text{length of the prism} = \tfrac{1}{2}\, bh \times L
- 1 kilometre (km) = 1000 metres (m); 1 metre (m) = 100 centimetres (cm); 1 centimetre (cm) = 10 millimetres (mm) || 1 کلومیٹر = 1000 میٹر؛ 1 میٹر = 100 سینٹی میٹر؛ 1 سینٹی میٹر = 10 ملی میٹر || 1 کیلومتر = 1000 متره؛ 1 متر = 100 سانتي متره؛ 1 سانتي متر = 10 ملي متره
- 1 hour = 60 minutes; 1 minute = 60 seconds; 1 hour = 60 × 60 seconds = 3600 seconds || 1 گھنٹہ = 60 منٹ؛ 1 منٹ = 60 سیکنڈ؛ 1 گھنٹہ = 60 × 60 سیکنڈ = 3600 سیکنڈ || 1 ساعت = 60 دقیقې؛ 1 دقیقه = 60 ثانیې؛ 1 ساعت = 60 × 60 ثانیې = 3600 ثانیې
eq \text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}},\quad \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}},\quad \text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}
eq \text{Average speed} = \frac{\text{total distance covered}}{\text{total time taken}}
eq \text{Sum of interior angles} = 180^\circ \times (n-2),\ \text{where } n \text{ is the number of sides of the polygon}
eq \text{Each interior angle of a regular polygon} = \frac{\text{Sum of interior angles of the regular polygon}}{\text{Number of interior angles of the regular polygon}} = \frac{180^\circ \times (n-2)}{n}
eq \text{The sum of exterior angles of an } n\text{-sided polygon} = (\text{sum of interior and exterior angles of an } n\text{-sided polygon}) - (\text{sum of interior angles of an } n\text{-sided polygon})
eq \text{Measure of an exterior angle of any polygon} = \frac{360^\circ}{\text{Number of sides of the polygon}}
eq \text{Angle of each sector} = \frac{\text{Frequency of each observation}}{\text{Total number of observations}} \times 360^\circ
eq \bar{X} = \frac{\sum x}{n},\qquad \text{Mean of grouped data} = \bar{X} = \frac{\sum f x}{\sum f}
eq \text{Probability of an event} = P(E) = \frac{\text{Number of favourable (desired) outcomes}}{\text{Total number of possible outcomes}}
