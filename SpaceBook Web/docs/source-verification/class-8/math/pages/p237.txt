## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m Height of a conical flask ($h$) $= 6$cm
p Now, first we find radius ($r$) and slant height ($l$) of the conical flask. || اب پہلے ہم مخروطی فلاسک کا نصف قطر ($r$) اور ترچھی اونچائی ($l$) معلوم کرتے ہیں۔ || اوس لومړی د مخروطي فلاسک شعاع ($r$) او ځوړ لوړوالی ($l$) مومو.
m Area of the base of the conical flask $= \pi r^2$
m $50\text{ cm}^2 = \pi r^2$
m $\Rightarrow (3.14)r^2 = 50\text{cm}^2$
m $r^2 = \frac{50}{3.14}\text{ cm}^2$
m $r^2 = 16\text{cm}^2$
m $r^2 = 16\text{cm}^2$
m $r = 4\text{cm}$
m Slant height of the conical flask ($l$) $= \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$
m $= \sqrt{(4)^2 + (6)^2}$
m $= \sqrt{16 + 36}$
m $= \sqrt{52}$
m $l = 7.21\text{cm}$
m i. Total surface area of conical flask $= \pi r (l + r)$
m $= 3.14\,(4)\,(7.21 + 4)$
m $= 3.14\,(4)\,(11.21)$
m $= 140.79\text{cm}^2$
m ii. Volume of the conical flask $= \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$
m $= \frac{1}{3}(3.14)(4)^2(6)$
m $= (3.14)(4)(4)(2)$
m $= 100.48\text{cm}^3$
endexample
