example 1
p Expand the following: || درج ذیل کو پھیلائیں: || لاندې پراخې کړئ:
qp a | $(2a + 3b)^3$
qp b | $(4x + y)^3$
## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m a) $(2a + 3b)^3$
m $= (2a)^3 + 3(2a)^2(3b) + 3(2a)(3b)^2 + (3b)^3$
m $= 8a^3 + 3(4a^2)(3b) + 6a(9b^2) + 27b^3$
m $= 8a^3 + 36a^2b + 54ab^2 + 27b^3$
m b) $(4x + y)^3$
m $= (4x)^3 + 3(4x)^2(y) + 3(4x)(y^2) + y^3$
m $= 64x^3 + 3(16x^2)(y) + 12xy^2 + y^3$
m $= 64x^3 + 48x^2y + 12xy^2 + y^3$
endexample

example 2
p Expand the following: || درج ذیل کو پھیلائیں: || لاندې پراخې کړئ:
qp a | $(5a - 3)^3$
qp b | $(4p - 5q)^3$
## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m a) $(5a - 3)^3$
m $= (5a)^3 - 3(5a)^2(3) + 3(5a)(3)^2 - (3)^3$
m $= 125a^3 - 3(25a^2)(3) + 15a \times 9 - 27$
m $= 125a^3 - 225a^2 + 135a - 27$
m b) $(4p - 5q)^3$
m $= (4p)^3 - 3(4p)^2(5q) + 3(4p)(5q)^2 - (5q)^3$
m $= 64p^3 - 3(16p^2)(5q) + 12p(25q^2) - 125q^3$
m $= 64p^3 - 240p^2q + 300pq^2 - 125q^3$
endexample

example 3
p Find the value of $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3}$ when $x + \frac{1}{x} = 3$ || $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3}$ کی قیمت معلوم کریں جب $x + \frac{1}{x} = 3$ ہو || د $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3}$ ارزښت معلوم کړئ کله چې $x + \frac{1}{x} = 3$ وي
## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m As, $x + \frac{1}{x} = 3$
m So, $\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)^3 = 3^3$ (Take the cube of the both sides)
m $x^3 + 3(x)^2\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) + 3(x)\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^3 = 27$
m $x^3 + 3 \times x \times x \times \frac{1}{x} + 3 \times x \times \frac{1}{x} \times \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x^3} = 27$
m $x^3 + 3x + 3 \times \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x^3} = 27$
m $x^3 + 3\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right) + \frac{1}{x^3} = 27$
m $x^3 + 3 \times 3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = 27 \quad$ [Put $\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right) = 3$]
m $x^3 + 9 + \frac{1}{x^3} = 27$
m $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = 27 - 9$
m $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = 18$
endexample
