example 4
p Find the value of $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3}$ when $x - \frac{1}{x} = 7$ || $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3}$ کی قیمت معلوم کریں جب $x - \frac{1}{x} = 7$ ہو || د $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3}$ ارزښت معلوم کړئ کله چې $x - \frac{1}{x} = 7$ وي
## Solution: || حل: || حل:
m As, $x - \frac{1}{x} = 7$
m $\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)^3 = 7^3$ (Take cube of the both sides of the equation)
m $x^3 - 3(x)^2\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) + 3(x)\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 - \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^3 = 243$
m $x^3 - 3 \times x \times x \times \frac{1}{x} + 3 \times x \times \frac{1}{x} \times \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x^3} = 243$
m $x^3 - 3x + 3 \times \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x^3} = 243$
m $x^3 - 3\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right) - \frac{1}{x^3} = 243$
m $x^3 - 3 \times 7 - \frac{1}{x^3} = 243 \quad$ [Put $x - \frac{1}{x} = 7$]
m $x^3 - 21 - \frac{1}{x^3} = 243$
m $x^3 - \frac{1}{x^3} = 243 + 21$
m Thus, $x^3 - \frac{1}{x^3} = 264$
endexample
