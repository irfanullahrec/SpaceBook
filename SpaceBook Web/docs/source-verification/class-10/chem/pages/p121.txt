q B | Short questions. || مختصر سوالات۔ || لنډې پوښتنې.
qp 1 | Differentiate between glucose and fructose? || گلوکوز اور فرکٹوز میں فرق کریں۔ || د ګلوکوز او فرکټوز ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Glucose is an aldohexose (has an aldehyde group) with a six-membered ring; it is the blood sugar and the main source of energy. Fructose is a ketohexose (has a ketone group) with a five-membered ring; it is found in fruits and honey and is the sweetest sugar. Both have the formula $C_6H_{12}O_6$ (they are isomers).
qp 2 | Write down four uses of lipids. || لپڈز کے چار استعمالات لکھیں۔ || د لیپیډونو څلور کارونې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Uses of lipids: (1) storage of energy (they give about 9 kcal per gram); (2) insulation and protection of organs against heat loss and shock; (3) parts of cell membranes; (4) carry fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) and make hormones.
qp 3 | How would you differentiate between simple and complex lipids. || سادہ اور پیچیدہ لپڈز میں فرق کیسے کریں گے؟ || د ساده او پېچلو لیپیډونو ترمنځ به څنګه توپیر وکړئ؟
sol Model answer ## Simple lipids are esters of fatty acids with an alcohol only (fats, oils and waxes; e.g. triglycerides). Complex (compound) lipids contain other groups besides fatty acids and alcohol, such as phosphate (phospholipids) or sugar (glycolipids).
qp 4 | What is meant by denaturing of protein? || پروٹین کی ڈی نیچرنگ سے کیا مراد ہے؟ || د پروټین له ډی نېچرېشن څه مراد دی؟
sol Model answer ## Denaturing is the loss of the natural three-dimensional structure of a protein (without breaking the peptide bonds) caused by heat, acids, bases, alcohol or heavy metals, so the protein loses its biological activity. Example: the white of an egg turns solid when boiled.
qp 5 | How would you classify vitamins? || آپ حیاتین کی درجہ بندی کیسے کریں گے؟ || ویټامینونه به څنګه ډلبندي کړئ؟
sol Model answer ## Vitamins are classified by their solubility: fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E and K) and water-soluble vitamins (the B-complex and C).
qp 6 | Vitamins are vital for us, why? || حیاتین ہمارے لیے کیوں ضروری ہیں؟ || ویټامینونه زموږ لپاره ولې حیاتي دي؟
sol Model answer ## Vitamins are needed in small amounts to regulate metabolism, help enzymes work, keep skin, eyes, bones and nerves healthy and protect against diseases. The body cannot make most of them, and their lack causes deficiency diseases.
