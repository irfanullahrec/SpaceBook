q 1 | Model answers for the four pictures. || چاروں تصاویر کے نمونہ جوابات۔ || د څلورو انځورونو نمونه ځوابونه.
sol See answer ## Open sewage: germs reach rivers and drinking water, spreading cholera, typhoid and hepatitis. Rubbish by water: leaching releases chemicals into rivers and groundwater and blocks drains. Crowded littered market: plastic and food waste block drains and breed flies and disease.
