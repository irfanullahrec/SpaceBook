q B | Short questions. || مختصر سوالات۔ || لنډې پوښتنې.
qp 1 | How ozone layer is depleted in the atmosphere? || کرۂ ہوائی میں اوزون کی تہہ کیسے کم ہوتی ہے؟ || په اتموسفیر کې د اوزون طبقه څنګه کمېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) from refrigerators, aerosols and foams rise to the stratosphere, where ultraviolet light breaks them and releases chlorine atoms. Each chlorine atom destroys many ozone molecules ($Cl + O_3 \rightarrow ClO + O_2$, then $ClO + O \rightarrow Cl + O_2$), which thins the ozone layer.
qp 2 | Differentiate between primary and secondary pollutants. Write the name and sources of greenhouse gases to the atmosphere. || بنیادی اور ثانوی آلودگیوں میں فرق کریں۔ کرۂ ہوائی میں گرین ہاؤس گیسوں کے نام اور ذرائع لکھیں۔ || د لومړنیو او دوهمیو ککړوونکو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ. د شنه خونې د ګازونو نومونه او سرچینې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Primary pollutants are released directly into the air ($CO$, $SO_2$, $NO$, smoke); secondary pollutants form from reactions of primary pollutants (ozone, smog, acid rain, PAN). Greenhouse gases: $CO_2$ (burning fuels, deforestation), methane (cattle, rice fields, landfills), $N_2O$ (fertilizers), CFCs (refrigerants) and water vapour.
qp 3 | Define environmental chemistry and atmosphere. || ماحولیاتی کیمیا اور کرۂ ہوائی کی تعریف کریں۔ || چاپېریالي کیمیا او اتموسفیر تعریف کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Environmental chemistry is the study of chemical processes in the environment (air, water, soil), the sources of pollutants and their effects. The atmosphere is the envelope of gases that surrounds the earth.
qp 4 | Sketch and briefly discuss the composition of atmosphere, by mentioning the percentage of each component. || ہر جزو کا فیصد بتا کر کرۂ ہوائی کی ترکیب کا خاکہ بنائیں اور مختصر بحث کریں۔ || د هر جز سلنه په ښودلو د اتموسفیر جوړښت رسم او په لنډه توګه بحث کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The atmosphere has layers: troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, thermosphere. Dry air by volume: nitrogen about 78%, oxygen about 21%, argon about 0.9%, carbon dioxide about 0.04%, and traces of neon, helium, methane and others, plus a variable amount of water vapour. qp 5 | How the acid rain affect the building material or statues? || تیزابی بارش عمارتی مواد یا مجسموں کو کیسے متاثر کرتی ہے؟ || تېزابي باران د ودانیو توکي یا مجسمې څنګه اغېزمنوي؟
sol Model answer ## Acid rain reacts with the limestone or marble of buildings and statues: $CaCO_3 + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow CaSO_4 + H_2O + CO_2$. The surface dissolves and is eaten away, and metal parts corrode.
