qp 7 | Make the distinction between soft and hard water. || نرم اور سخت پانی میں فرق کریں۔ || د نرمو او سختو اوبو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Soft water lathers easily with soap and has no dissolved calcium or magnesium salts (rain water, distilled water). Hard water does not lather easily because $Ca^{2+}$ and $Mg^{2+}$ ions form a scum with soap; it contains their bicarbonates (temporary hardness) or chlorides and sulphates (permanent hardness).
qp 8 | Why water is universal solvent? || پانی ہمہ گیر محلل کیوں ہے؟ || اوبه ولې نړیوال محلل دی؟
sol Model answer ## Water is a polar liquid with a high dielectric constant and forms hydrogen bonds, so it dissolves more substances (ionic compounds, polar covalent compounds, gases) than any other liquid; for this reason it is called the universal solvent.
qp 9 | Give some of the disadvantages of the detergents? || ڈٹرجنٹس کے کچھ نقصانات بتائیں۔ || د ډیټرجنټونو ځینې زیانونه ووایئ.
sol Model answer ## Detergents are not biodegradable if they have branched chains, so they remain in rivers and cause foam; their phosphates cause eutrophication; they harm fish and aquatic life; they may irritate skin and they pollute the water.

qp 1 | a) Enlist the main sources of water? b) How would you categorize physical properties of water? c) Predict the product of reaction, (i) K (ii) Cl₂ (iii) CaO (iv) CH₃COONa (v) C, with water. || a) پانی کے بڑے ذرائع لکھیں۔ b) پانی کی طبعی خصوصیات کی درجہ بندی کیسے کریں گے؟ c) پانی کے ساتھ (i) K (ii) Cl₂ (iii) CaO (iv) CH₃COONa (v) C کے تعامل کے حاصلات کی پیش گوئی کریں۔ || a) د اوبو اصلي سرچینې ولیکئ. b) د اوبو فزیکي ځانګړنې به څنګه ډلبندي کړئ؟ c) له اوبو سره د (i) K (ii) Cl₂ (iii) CaO (iv) CH₃COONa (v) C د تعامل محصولات وړاندوینه کړئ.
sol Model answer ## a. Sources: rain, rivers, lakes, springs, wells, oceans, glaciers. b. Physical properties: colourless, odourless and tasteless liquid; boils at 100 °C and freezes at 0 °C; maximum density at 4 °C; high specific heat and surface tension; a good solvent. c. (i) $2K + 2H_2O \rightarrow 2KOH + H_2$. (ii) $Cl_2 + H_2O \rightarrow HCl + HOCl$. (iii) $CaO + H_2O \rightarrow Ca(OH)_2$. (iv) $CH_3COONa + H_2O \rightleftharpoons CH_3COOH + NaOH$ (hydrolysis). (v) $C + H_2O \rightarrow CO + H_2$ (steam over red-hot carbon).
qp 2 | Water is an excellent solvent. Explain how this property is beneficial for life but sometimes harmful for us. || پانی بہترین محلل ہے۔ وضاحت کریں کہ یہ خاصیت زندگی کے لیے کیسے مفید مگر کبھی ہمارے لیے نقصان دہ ہے۔ || اوبه غوره محلل دی. تشریح کړئ چې دا ځانګړنه د ژوند لپاره څنګه ګټوره خو کله کله زموږ لپاره زیانمنه ده.
sol Model answer ## Water dissolves many minerals and gases, which carry food to plants and animals, allow chemical reactions in cells, and let fish breathe dissolved oxygen: this is beneficial. It is harmful when it dissolves poisonous substances such as arsenic, lead and industrial wastes, and when it dissolves calcium and magnesium salts (hard water), which block pipes and boilers.
qp 3 | a) Classify temporary hard and permanent hard water. b) What methods could be adopted to remove the temporary hardness of water. c) What methods could be adopted to remove the permanent hardness of water. || a) عارضی اور مستقل سخت پانی کی درجہ بندی کریں۔ b) عارضی سختی دور کرنے کے کون سے طریقے اپنائے جا سکتے ہیں؟ c) مستقل سختی دور کرنے کے کون سے طریقے اپنائے جا سکتے ہیں؟ || a) لنډمهاله او دایمي سختې اوبه ډلبندي کړئ. b) د لنډمهاله سختوالي د لرې کولو کومې طریقې کارول کېدای شي؟ c) د دایمي سختوالي د لرې کولو کومې طریقې کارول کېدای شي؟
sol Model answer ## a. Temporary hard water contains the bicarbonates of calcium and magnesium and is softened by boiling; permanent hard water contains the chlorides and sulphates of calcium and magnesium, which boiling does not remove. b. Temporary hardness: boiling ($Ca(HCO_3)_2 \rightarrow CaCO_3 + H_2O + CO_2$) or adding slaked lime (Clark's method). c. Permanent hardness: adding washing soda ($Na_2CO_3$), using ion exchange resins or the permutit process, or distillation.
qp 4 | a) Why water is important for us? b) Write the disadvantages of hard water? c) Enlist the advantages of wastewater treatment. || a) پانی ہمارے لیے کیوں اہم ہے؟ b) سخت پانی کے نقصانات لکھیں۔ c) گندے پانی کی صفائی کے فوائد لکھیں۔ || a) اوبه موږ ته ولې مهمې دي؟ b) د سختو اوبو زیانونه ولیکئ. c) د فاضلو اوبو د درملنې ګټې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## a. Water is needed for drinking, cooking, washing, agriculture, industry and all life processes. b. Hard water wastes soap, leaves scum, makes scale in boilers and pipes, wastes fuel, and spoils clothes. c. Wastewater treatment protects rivers and the environment, gives water for reuse in irrigation and industry, saves fresh water, reduces diseases and recovers useful materials.
