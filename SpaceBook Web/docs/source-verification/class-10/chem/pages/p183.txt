sol Model answers ## 1: Polarity of water molecules, hydrogen bonding, and a high dielectric constant (80). 2: Boiling kills bacteria, viruses and other germs that cause waterborne diseases, and removes temporary hardness. 3: In ice, hydrogen bonds hold molecules in open hexagonal rings, so ice (0.91 g/cm³) is less dense than water (1 g/cm³). 4: Water is needed for life, body functions, digestion, temperature control, agriculture, industry, transport and power; poor quality spreads disease and harms crops and aquatic life (printed 160–162). 5: Enrichment of water with nutrients (nitrates, phosphates) from fertilizer run-off or rock weathering; it causes algal blooms that use up dissolved oxygen and kill fish and other aquatic animals. 6: Chlorine reacts with water to form HOCl, which releases atomic oxygen that kills germs; it is kept between 0.5 and 1.5 mg/dm³. 7: Soft water lathers easily with soap and forms no scum; hard water (with Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺ salts) gives little lather and forms scum. 8: It dissolves more substances than any other solvent because of its polarity, hydrogen bonding and high dielectric constant. 9: Detergents are non-biodegradable, so they persist in water, form foam and harm aquatic life. 10: Detergents, medicines, acids, bleaches, dyes, waxes and hair colours, plus pathogens in flushed water. || 1: پانی کے سالمات کی قطبیت، ہائیڈروجن
