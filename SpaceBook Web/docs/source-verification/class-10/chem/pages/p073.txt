qp 4 | Write down examples of three unsaturated hydrocarbons with structural and condensed formulae. || تین غیر سیر شدہ ہائیڈروکاربنز کی مثالیں ساختی اور مختصر فارمولوں کے ساتھ لکھیں۔ || د دریو نامشبوعو هایدروکاربنونو بېلګې د جوړښتي او لنډو فورمولونو سره ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Ethene: structural $H_2C=CH_2$, condensed $CH_2=CH_2$. Propene: structural $H_2C=CH-CH_3$, condensed $CH_2=CHCH_3$. Ethyne: structural $HC\equiv CH$, condensed $CH\equiv CH$.
qp 5 | Define hydrocarbons, and briefly discuss their importance? || ہائیڈروکاربنز کی تعریف کریں اور ان کی اہمیت پر مختصر بحث کریں۔ || هایدروکاربنونه تعریف کړئ او په لنډه توګه یې اهمیت بحث کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Hydrocarbons are organic compounds made of carbon and hydrogen only (alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, aromatics). They are the main constituents of fuels (natural gas, petrol, diesel, LPG), the raw materials for plastics, synthetic fibres, detergents and solvents, and so are very important to industry and daily life.
qp 6 | How alkyl radicals are formed. Discuss it with examples. || الکائل ریڈیکلز کیسے بنتے ہیں؟ مثالوں کے ساتھ بحث کریں۔ || الکایل رادیکالونه څنګه جوړېږي؟ د بېلګو سره یې بحث کړئ.
sol Model answer ## An alkyl radical is formed when an alkane loses one hydrogen atom, $C_nH_{2n+2} - H \rightarrow C_nH_{2n+1}$-. Examples: methane gives methyl $CH_3-$, ethane gives ethyl $C_2H_5-$, propane gives propyl $C_3H_7-$.
qp 7 | List some uses of organic compounds. || نامیاتی مرکبات کے کچھ استعمالات لکھیں۔ || د عضوي مرکباتو ځینې کارونې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Uses: fuels (petrol, diesel, LPG, natural gas), plastics and synthetic fibres (polythene, nylon, polyester), medicines, dyes and paints, solvents, food (sugar, oils, proteins), soaps and detergents, cosmetics, pesticides and fertilizers (urea).
qp 8 | Give the general molecular formulae of the following homologus series: a. alkanes b. alkenes c. alkynes || درج ذیل ہم ساخت سلسلوں کے عمومی سالماتی فارمولے دیں: (a) الکینز (b) الکینز (alkenes) (c) الکائنز || د لاندې همجنس لړیو عمومي مالیکولي فورمولونه ورکړئ: (a) الکېنونه (b) الکینونه (c) الکاینونه
sol Model answer ## (a) Alkanes: $C_nH_{2n+2}$. (b) Alkenes: $C_nH_{2n}$. (c) Alkynes: $C_nH_{2n-2}$.
qp 9 | Why organic compounds are volatile in nature? || نامیاتی مرکبات اڑنے والے کیوں ہوتے ہیں؟ || عضوي مرکبات ولې الوتونکي دي؟
sol Model answer ## Organic compounds are mostly covalent and made of non-polar or weakly polar molecules with weak intermolecular (van der Waals) forces. Little energy is needed to separate the molecules, so they have low melting and boiling points and are volatile.

qp 1 | a. List the different characteristics of organic compounds. b. Which of these is not an unsaturated molecule? (i) C₄H₆ (ii) C₆H₆ (iii) C₈H₁₈ (iv) C₃H₆ c. sol Model answer ## a. Organic compounds are covalent, have low melting and boiling points, are mostly insoluble in water but soluble in organic solvents, are poor conductors, burn easily, show catenation and isomerism, and react slowly. b. (iii) $C_8H_{18}$ is not unsaturated: it fits $C_nH_{2n+2}$, an alkane. c. Destructive distillation of coal is heating coal strongly in the absence of air. Products: coal gas (gas), coal tar and ammonia liquor (liquids) and coke (solid).
qp 2 | a. What is catenation? b. How does catenation contribute to the diversity of organic compounds? || a. کیٹینیشن کیا ہے؟ b. کیٹینیشن نامیاتی مرکبات کے تنوع میں کیسے حصہ ڈالتی ہے؟ || a. کیټینېشن څه دی؟ b. کیټینېشن د عضوي مرکباتو په تنوع کې څنګه ونډه لري؟
sol Model answer ## a. Catenation is the ability of carbon atoms to join each other by covalent bonds to form long chains and rings. b. Because carbon is tetravalent and forms strong C-C single, double and triple bonds, catenation gives straight and branched chains and rings and isomers, which leads to millions of different organic compounds.
qp 3 | a. What information about a compound is provided by a structural formula? b. How are structural formulae used in organic chemistry? || a. ساختی فارمولا کسی مرکب کے بارے میں کیا معلومات دیتا ہے؟ b. نامیاتی کیمیا میں ساختی فارمولے کیسے استعمال ہوتے ہیں؟ || a. جوړښتي فورمول د یوه مرکب په اړه کوم معلومات ورکوي؟ b. په عضوي کیمیا کې جوړښتي فورمولونه څنګه کارېږي؟
sol Model answer ## a. A structural formula shows which atoms are present, how they are joined (all bonds) and the arrangement of groups. b. It is used to show the functional group, to tell isomers apart, to name the compound and to predict its properties and reactions.
qp 4 | a. What do the terms saturated and unsaturated mean when applied to hydrocarbons? b. What other meanings do these terms have in chemistry? c. Classify alkenes, alkanes, alkynes and aromatic hydrocarbons as either saturated or unsaturated. || a. ہائیڈروکاربنز کے لیے سیر شدہ اور غیر سیر شدہ اصطلاحات کا کیا مطلب ہے؟ b. کیمیا میں ان اصطلاحات کے اور کیا معنی ہیں؟ c. الکینز (alkenes)، الکینز، الکائنز اور ایرومیٹک ہائیڈروکاربنز کو سیر شدہ یا غیر سیر شدہ میں درجہ بند کریں۔ || a. د هایدروکاربنونو لپاره د مشبوع او نامشبوع اصطلاحاتو معنا څه ده؟ b. په کیمیا کې د دې اصطلاحاتو نورې معناوې څه دي؟ c. الکینونه، الکېنونه، الکاینونه او اروماتیک هایدروکاربنونه د مشبوع یا نامشبوع په توګه وډلبندي کړئ.
sol Model answer ## a. Saturated hydrocarbons contain only single C-C bonds; unsaturated hydrocarbons contain at least one double or triple C-C bond. b. In general chemistry saturated also means a solution that cannot dissolve more solute at that temperature, and unsaturated means it can dissolve more. c. Alkanes are saturated; alkenes and alkynes are unsaturated; aromatic hydrocarbons (benzene) are classified as unsaturated.
