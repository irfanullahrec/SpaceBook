sol Model answer ## An amino acid has the general formula $H_2N-CHR-COOH$. Parts: the amino group $-NH_2$ (basic), the carboxyl group $-COOH$ (acidic), a hydrogen atom, and the side chain R attached to the central (alpha) carbon.

qp 1 | What functions do carbohydrates perform in living organisms? || جانداروں میں کاربوہائیڈریٹس کیا کام انجام دیتے ہیں؟ || کاربوهایدریټونه په ژوندیو موجوداتو کې کومې دندې ترسره کوي؟
sol Model answer ## Carbohydrates are the main source of energy (glucose); they are stored as glycogen in animals and starch in plants; cellulose gives structure to plant cell walls; they are parts of nucleic acids (ribose, deoxyribose) and save proteins for body building.
qp 2 | a. Distinguish between monosaccharides, disaccharides and polysaccharides and also give examples of each one. b. How are carbohydrates important to living organisms? || a. مونو، ڈائی اور پولی سیکرائیڈز میں فرق کریں اور ہر ایک کی مثالیں دیں۔ b. کاربوہائیڈریٹس جانداروں کے لیے کیوں اہم ہیں؟ || a. د مونو، ډای او پولي سکرایډونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ او د هر یوه بېلګې ورکړئ. b. کاربوهایدریټونه ژوندیو موجوداتو ته څنګه مهم دي؟
sol Model answer ## a. Monosaccharides are simple sugars that cannot be hydrolysed (glucose, fructose, galactose). Disaccharides give two monosaccharides on hydrolysis (sucrose, maltose, lactose). Polysaccharides give many monosaccharides (starch, glycogen, cellulose). b. They supply energy, are stored for later use, form cell wall material and are present in nucleic acids.
qp 3 | a. How are proteins important to living organisms? b. Describe the nature of bonding in protein? || a. پروٹینز جانداروں کے لیے کیوں اہم ہیں؟ b. پروٹین میں بندش کی نوعیت بیان کریں۔ || a. پروټینونه ژوندیو موجوداتو ته څنګه مهم دي؟ b. په پروټین کې د اړیکو ماهیت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## a. Proteins are the building material of cells and tissues, make enzymes and many hormones, give immunity (antibodies), carry oxygen (haemoglobin), cause muscle movement and can give energy. b. The amino acids in a protein are joined by peptide bonds (-CO-NH-) formed between the carboxyl group of one amino acid and the amino group of the next with loss of water; hydrogen bonds, disulphide bridges and other forces hold the chain in shape.
qp 4 | a. Define the term lipid. b. Distinguish between fats and oils. c. List four foods you eat that contain lipids. d. How are lipids important to your body? || a. لپڈ کی تعریف کریں۔ b. چکنائیوں اور تیلوں میں فرق کریں۔ c. چار غذائیں لکھیں جن میں لپڈز ہوں۔ d. لپڈز آپ کے جسم کے لیے کیوں اہم ہیں؟ || a. لیپیډ تعریف کړئ. b. د غوړیو او تېلو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ. c. څلور هغه خواړه ولیکئ چې لیپیډونه لري. d. لیپیډونه ستاسو بدن ته څنګه مهم دي؟
sol Model answer ## a. Lipids are organic compounds insoluble in water but soluble in organic solvents, made mainly of esters of fatty acids. b. Fats are solid at room temperature and mostly saturated (animal fat, butter); oils are liquid at room temperature and mostly unsaturated (olive, corn oil). c. Butter, cheese, eggs, meat, nuts, vegetable oil. d. They store energy, insulate the body, protect organs, form cell membranes and carry vitamins A, D, E and K.
qp 5 | a. How would you justify DNA as genetic code of life? b. Distinguish between DNA and RNA c. Explain the functions of DNA. || a. آپ DNA کو زندگی کا جینیاتی کوڈ کیسے ثابت کریں گے؟ b. DNA اور RNA میں فرق کریں۔ c. DNA کے افعال بیان کریں۔ || a. DNA به د ژوند د جینیټیک کوډ په توګه څنګه ثابت کړئ؟ b. د DNA او RNA ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ. c. د DNA دندې تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## a. The sequence of the four bases (A, T, G, C) in DNA is a code: groups of three bases (codons) specify amino acids and so the proteins of the cell; DNA copies itself and passes information from parents to offspring. b. DNA: deoxyribose sugar, bases A, T, G, C, double helix, stays in the nucleus. RNA: ribose sugar, bases A, U, G, C, single strand, found in nucleus and cytoplasm. c. DNA stores hereditary information, replicates before cell division, and directs protein synthesis through RNA.
