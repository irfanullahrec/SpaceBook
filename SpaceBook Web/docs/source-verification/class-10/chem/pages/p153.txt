sol Model answers ## 1: CFCs reach the stratosphere, UV frees Cl atoms, and each Cl destroys many O₃ molecules ($Cl + O_3 \rightarrow ClO + O_2$, $ClO + O \rightarrow Cl + O_2$); NOₓ, atomic O and SO₂ also destroy ozone. 2: Primary pollutants are released directly (CO, SO₂, NOₓ, ash); secondary ones form in the air from them (ground-level ozone, acid rain). Greenhouse gases: CO₂ (fuel burning, deforestation), CH₄ (wetlands, cattle, termites, fossil-fuel extraction), NO₂ (vehicles, power plants), O₃. 3: Environmental chemistry studies the environment and changes in it; atmosphere is the thick protective blanket of gases round the earth that sustains life. 4: N₂ 78.1%, O₂ 20.9%, Ar 0.9%, CO₂ 0.03%, traces of H₂, Ne, He, O₃; water vapour 0–4% (pie chart, Fig. 14.1). 5: Acids dissolve limestone and marble ($CaCO_3 + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow CaSO_4 + CO_2 + H_2O$); soluble CaSO₄ washes away, so statues and buildings are eaten away. 6: CO₂ lets sunlight in but absorbs the heat radiated back by the earth; more CO₂ traps more heat and the average temperature rises. 7: Below pH 5 fish eggs do not hatch, lower pH kills adult fish, and food organisms such as mayflies die. 8: CO – vehicle exhaust (75%), incomplete burning, forest fires, steel industry, volcanoes; CO₂ – respiration, decay, fuel burning, cooking, industry, power plants, volcanoes. 9: Desertification, melting ice, sea level rise, stronger storms, harm to crops, rainfall and health. 10: It absorbs harmful UV radiation and protects living things from skin cancer, cataract and other harm. || 1: CFCs اسٹریٹوسفیئر پہنچتے ہیں، UV سے Cl ایٹم آزاد ہوتے ہیں جو بہت سے O₃ سالمات توڑتے ہیں؛ NOₓ، ایٹمی O اور SO₂ بھی۔ 2: بنیادی براہِ راست خارج (CO، SO₂، NOₓ)، ثانوی ہوا
