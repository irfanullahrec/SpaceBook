qp 6 | Increase in concentration of CO₂ cause greenhouse effect, justify it. || CO₂ کے ارتکاز میں اضافہ گرین ہاؤس اثر کا سبب بنتا ہے، ثابت کریں۔ || د CO₂ د غلظت زیاتوالی د شنه خونې اغېزه رامنځته کوي، ثابت یې کړئ.
sol Model answer ## $CO_2$ absorbs the infrared (heat) radiation sent out by the earth and re-radiates part of it back, so the heat is trapped. More $CO_2$ from burning fuels and cutting forests traps more heat and raises the average temperature of the earth: the greenhouse effect.
qp 7 | Briefly discuss how the acid rain affects the aquatic life. || مختصراً بتائیں کہ تیزابی بارش آبی حیات کو کیسے متاثر کرتی ہے۔ || په لنډه توګه بحث وکړئ چې تېزابي باران اوبیز ژوند څنګه اغېزمنوي.
sol Model answer ## Acid rain lowers the pH of lakes and rivers. Fish eggs and young fish die, plants and plankton are damaged, aluminium is released from soil into water, and the food chain is broken, so aquatic life declines.
qp 8 | List the sources which produce CO and CO₂ to the atmosphere. || کرۂ ہوائی میں CO اور CO₂ پیدا کرنے والے ذرائع لکھیں۔ || هغه سرچینې ولیکئ چې اتموسفیر ته CO او CO₂ ورزیاتوي.
sol Model answer ## Sources of CO: incomplete burning of fuel in vehicles, burning of wood and coal, cigarette smoke, forest fires. Sources of $CO_2$: complete combustion of fuels, respiration, decay, volcanoes, factories, power plants and burning forests.
qp 9 | What are the adverse effects of global warming? || عالمی حدت کے مضر اثرات کیا ہیں؟ || د نړیوالې تودوخې زیانمنې اغېزې کومې دي؟
sol Model answer ## Global warming causes melting of glaciers and polar ice, rise of sea level and flooding of coastal areas, floods and droughts, changed rainfall, heat waves, loss of crops and of animal and plant species, and spread of diseases.

qp 1 | a) Sketch and identify the different layers of atmosphere. b) Differentiate between stratosphere and troposphere. || a) کرۂ ہوائی کی مختلف تہوں کا خاکہ بنائیں اور شناخت کریں۔ b) اسٹریٹوسفیئر اور ٹروپوسفیئر میں فرق کریں۔ || a) د اتموسفیر بېلابېلې طبقې رسم او وپېژنئ. b) د سټراټوسفیر او ټروپوسفیر ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## a. Layers from the ground up: troposphere (up to about 12 km), stratosphere (to about 50 km, contains the ozone layer), mesosphere (to about 85 km), thermosphere (above), then the exosphere. (Sketch: concentric layers with temperature curve.) b. The troposphere holds most of the air, clouds and weather and its temperature falls with height; the stratosphere has little water vapour, no weather, contains the ozone layer and its temperature rises with height.
qp 2 | a) Air is polluted in the big cities of Pakistan, justify the statement. b) Enlist the main sources of air pollution. c) Prove that the oxides of nitrogen and sulphur causes air pollution. d) Enlist the effects of air pollution. || a) پاکستان کے بڑے شہروں میں ہوا آلودہ ہے، ثابت کریں۔ b) فضائی آلودگی کے بڑے ذرائع لکھیں۔ c) ثابت کریں کہ نائٹروجن اور سلفر کے آکسائیڈز فضائی آلودگی کا سبب ہیں۔ d) فضائی آلودگی کے اثرات لکھیں۔ || a) د پاکستان په لویو ښارونو کې هوا ککړه ده، ثابت یې کړئ. b) د هوا د ککړتیا اصلي سرچینې ولیکئ. c) ثابت کړئ چې د نایټروجن او سلفر اکسایډونه د هوا ککړتیا رامنځته کوي. d) د هوا د ککړتیا اغېزې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## a. The big cities have heavy traffic, factories, burning of waste and fuel, and dust, so smoke, CO, $NO_x$, $SO_2$ and particles are high. b. Sources: vehicles, factories, power stations, burning of coal, wood and waste, dust, volcanoes. c. $SO_2$ and $NO_2$ dissolve in rain to form acids ($SO_2 + H_2O \rightarrow H_2SO_3$; $2NO_2 + H_2O \rightarrow HNO_3 + HNO_2$), causing acid rain and breathing problems. d. Effects: respiratory diseases and eye irritation, acid rain, smog, damage to plants and buildings, global warming and ozone depletion.
qp 3 | a) What would be the result if Rainwater is acidic? b) What could be done to minimize the formation of acid rain? c) Write down the effects of acid rain on, (i) Humans (ii) Plants (iii) Soil (iv) Materials || a) اگر بارش کا پانی تیزابی ہو تو کیا نتیجہ ہوگا؟ b) تیزابی بارش کو کم کرنے کے لیے کیا کیا جا سکتا ہے؟ c) تیزابی بارش کے اثرات لکھیں: (i) انسان (ii) پودے (iii) مٹی (iv) مواد || a) که د باران اوبه تېزابي وي پایله به څه وي؟ b) د تېزابي باران د کمولو لپاره څه کېدای شي؟ c) د تېزابي باران اغېزې ولیکئ: (i) انسانان (ii) بوټي (iii) خاوره (iv) توکي
sol Model answer ## a. Acidic rain damages plants, soil, lakes and buildings. b. Reduce burning of coal and oil, use clean fuels, fit scrubbers and catalytic converters, use more renewable energy. c. Humans: breathing problems, irritation of eyes and skin; plants: leaves are damaged and growth is reduced; soil: nutrients are washed away and it becomes acidic; materials: marble and limestone dissolve and metals corrode.
qp 4 | a) Explain ozone layer depletion. b) Where does ozone layer lie in the atmosphere? c) What evidence can you find that depletion of ozone occurs in the atmosphere? d) Recommend few ways to protect the ozone layer. || a) اوزون کی تہہ میں کمی بیان کریں۔ b) اوزون کی تہہ کرۂ ہوائی میں کہاں ہے؟ c) کرۂ ہوائی میں اوزون کی کمی کا کیا ثبوت ملتا ہے؟ d) اوزون کی تہہ کی حفاظت کے چند طریقے تجویز کریں۔ || a) د اوزون د طبقې کمښت تشریح کړئ. b) د اوزون طبقه په اتموسفیر کې چېرې ده؟ c) د اوزون د کمښت کوم ثبوت موندلای شئ؟ d) د اوزون د طبقې د ساتنې څو لارې وړاندیز کړئ.
sol Model answer ## a. Ozone depletion is the thinning of the ozone layer caused by chlorine and bromine from CFCs. b. It lies in the stratosphere, at about 15-35 km. c. Satellite and balloon measurements show an ozone hole over Antarctica every spring. d. Stop using CFCs (use HFC or other alternatives), recycle old refrigerators, avoid aerosols with CFCs and follow the Montreal Protocol.
