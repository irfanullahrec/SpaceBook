qp 5 | What is the function of froth floatation process. || فروتھ فلوٹیشن عمل کا کام کیا ہے؟ || د فروت فلوټېشن پروسې دنده څه ده؟
sol Model answer ## Froth flotation concentrates sulphide ores. The finely powdered ore is stirred with water, oil and a frothing agent while air is blown through; the ore particles stick to the oil and rise in the froth, while the gangue sinks to the bottom, so the ore is separated.
qp 6 | On what basis the different fractions of petroleum are separated? || پیٹرولیم کے مختلف اجزاء کس بنیاد پر الگ کیے جاتے ہیں؟ || د پټرولیم بېلابېلې برخې په کوم بنسټ جلا کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## The fractions are separated on the basis of their different boiling points by fractional distillation: the small molecules with low boiling points rise higher in the column and are collected at the top, and the heavy fractions with high boiling points are collected near the bottom.
qp 7 | What is slaked lime? How slaked lime is produced? || بجھا چونا کیا ہے؟ یہ کیسے بنتا ہے؟ || مړه چونه څه ده؟ څنګه جوړېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Slaked lime is calcium hydroxide, $Ca(OH)_2$. It is made by adding water to quicklime: $CaO + H_2O \rightarrow Ca(OH)_2 + heat$.
qp 8 | Assess the composition of urea and calculate the percentage of nitrogen in it. || یوریا کی ترکیب کا جائزہ لیں اور اس میں نائٹروجن کا فیصد معلوم کریں۔ || د یوریا جوړښت وارزوئ او په هغې کې د نایټروجن سلنه محاسبه کړئ.
sol $46.7\%$ N ## Urea is $CO(NH_2)_2$, molar mass $= 12 + 16 + 2(14 + 2) = 60$ g/mol. Mass of nitrogen $= 2\times14 = 28$ g. Percentage of N $= \dfrac{28}{60}\times100 = 46.7\%$.
qp 9 | What is gangue and where it is found? || گینگ کیا ہے اور کہاں پایا جاتا ہے؟ || ګینګ څه دی او چېرې موندل کېږي؟

qp 1 | a) How could you convert the concentrated ore to its oxide? b) How would you use the Roasting in extraction of copper? c) What inference can you make of smelting in extraction of copper? d) Can you elaborate the reason of electro-refining of copper. || a) مرتکز کچ دھات کو آکسائیڈ میں کیسے بدلیں گے؟ b) تانبا نکالنے میں روسٹنگ کیسے استعمال کریں گے؟ c) تانبا نکالنے میں اسمیلٹنگ سے کیا نتیجہ نکالیں گے؟ d) تانبے کی برقی صفائی کی وجہ بیان کریں۔ || a) تغلیظ شوې کاني ډبره به په اکسایډ څنګه بدله کړئ؟ b) د مسو په ایستلو کې به روسټینګ څنګه وکاروئ؟ c) د مسو په ایستلو کې د سمېلټینګ څه پایله اخلئ؟ d) د مسو د برېښنايي پاکولو لامل تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## a. The concentrated sulphide ore is converted to its oxide by roasting, heating strongly in air: $2Cu_2S + 3O_2 \rightarrow 2Cu_2O + 2SO_2$. b. Roasting also removes sulphur as $SO_2$ and arsenic and other volatile impurities. c. Smelting is heating the roasted ore with coke and silica in a furnace to reduce the oxide to the metal and to remove iron as slag (FeSiO₃), giving molten blister copper. d. Electro-refining removes the remaining impurities and gives 99.9% pure copper: the impure copper is the anode, a thin pure copper sheet is the cathode, and the electrolyte is $CuSO_4$ acidified with $H_2SO_4$; pure copper deposits on the cathode and the impurities fall as anode mud.

sol Model answer ## a. Raw materials: sodium chloride (brine), limestone, ammonia. b. $NH_3 + CO_2 + H_2O \rightarrow NH_4HCO_3$; $NH_4HCO_3 + NaCl \rightarrow NaHCO_3 + NH_4Cl$; $2NaHCO_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} Na_2CO_3 + H_2O + CO_2$; $CaCO_3 \rightarrow CaO + CO_2$. c. The by-product is calcium chloride (from $2NH_4Cl + Ca(OH)_2 \rightarrow CaCl_2 + 2NH_3 + 2H_2O$); ammonia is recycled. d. (Flow sheet: brine + ammonia in the ammonia tower, carbonating tower with $CO_2$ from the lime kiln, filter to get $NaHCO_3$, calciner to give soda ash, ammonia recovery tower with slaked lime.)

sol Model answer ## a. Raw materials: ammonia and carbon dioxide. b. $2NH_3 + CO_2 \rightarrow NH_2COONH_4$ (ammonium carbamate), then $NH_2COONH_4 \rightarrow CO(NH_2)_2 + H_2O$ at about 180 °C and 150 atm. c. qp 4 | a) Define refining of petroleum. Describe the composition of petroleum? b) What are the two theories about the origin of petroleum? c) Write a detail note on fractional distillation of petroleum. || a) پیٹرولیم کی صفائی کی تعریف کریں اور اس کی ترکیب بیان کریں۔ b) پیٹرولیم کی ابتدا کے دو نظریات کیا ہیں؟ c) پیٹرولیم کی جزوی کشید پر تفصیلی نوٹ لکھیں۔ || a) د پټرولیم تصفیه تعریف او جوړښت یې بیان کړئ. b) د پټرولیم د اصل دوه نظریې کومې دي؟ c) د پټرولیم د جزوي تقطیر په اړه مفصل یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## a. Refining of petroleum is the separation of crude oil into useful fractions by fractional distillation. Crude petroleum is a mixture of many hydrocarbons (alkanes, cycloalkanes and aromatics) with small amounts of compounds of sulphur, nitrogen and oxygen. b. The organic (biogenic) theory: it formed from the remains of marine plants and animals buried under sediments under heat and pressure over millions of years. The inorganic (abiogenic) theory: it formed from metal carbides and water deep in the earth. c. Crude oil is heated to about 400 °C and enters a fractionating tower, hotter at the bottom and cooler at the top. Fractions rise until they condense at levels where the temperature matches their boiling points: petroleum gas and LPG at the top, then petrol, naphtha, kerosene, diesel, lubricating oil and fuel oil, and bitumen (residue) at the bottom.
