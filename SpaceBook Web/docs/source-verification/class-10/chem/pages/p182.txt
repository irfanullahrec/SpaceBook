qp 1 | Identify the factors which are responsible for dissolving polar substances in water? || پانی میں قطبی مادوں کے حل ہونے کے ذمہ دار عوامل کون سے ہیں؟ || په اوبو کې د قطبي توکو د حلېدو مسؤل عوامل وپېژنئ.
sol Model answer ## Polar substances dissolve in water because water molecules are polar and form strong ion-dipole and dipole-dipole attractions and hydrogen bonds with them, which overcome the forces holding the solute together; hydration of the ions releases energy.
qp 2 | Explain why it is advisable to drink boiled water. || وضاحت کریں کہ ابلا ہوا پانی پینا کیوں بہتر ہے۔ || تشریح کړئ چې ولې د اېشېدلو اوبو څښل غوره دي.
sol Model answer ## Boiling kills germs (bacteria, viruses, parasites) and removes temporary hardness and some dissolved gases, so it makes water safe to drink.
qp 3 | Why ice floats on the surface of water? || برف پانی کی سطح پر کیوں تیرتی ہے؟ || یخ ولې د اوبو پر سر لامبو وهي؟
sol Model answer ## Ice is less dense than water because the hydrogen bonds hold the molecules in an open hexagonal lattice with empty spaces, so the molecules in ice are farther apart than in liquid water. Ice therefore floats.
qp 4 | Explain the importance of water and its quality? || پانی اور اس کے معیار کی اہمیت بیان کریں۔ || د اوبو او د هغو د کیفیت اهمیت تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Water is essential for life: it dissolves nutrients, carries them in the body, regulates temperature, takes part in photosynthesis and digestion and is needed for agriculture and industry. Good quality water (free of germs, harmful chemicals and excessive hardness) prevents diseases such as cholera, typhoid and diarrhoea.
qp 5 | What is eutrophication? How does it pollute water? || یوٹروفیکیشن کیا ہے؟ یہ پانی کو کیسے آلودہ کرتا ہے؟ || یوټروفیکېشن څه دی؟ اوبه څنګه ککړوي؟
sol Model answer ## Eutrophication is the over-enrichment of lakes and rivers with nutrients (nitrates and phosphates from fertilizers and detergents), which makes algae grow rapidly. When the algae die and decay, bacteria use up the dissolved oxygen, so fish and other life die and the water becomes polluted.
qp 6 | How chemistry helps to maintain clean swimming pools? || کیمیا سوئمنگ پول کو صاف رکھنے میں کیسے مدد دیتی ہے؟ || کیمیا څنګه د لامبو حوضونه پاک ساتي؟
sol Model answer ## Chlorine compounds (sodium hypochlorite, chlorine tablets) kill germs; acids or bases are added to keep the pH at 7.2-7.8, and chemicals are used to remove algae and to filter the water; testing kits measure chlorine and pH.
