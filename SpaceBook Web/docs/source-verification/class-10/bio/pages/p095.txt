q 7 | How are wind-pollinated flowers different from insect-pollinated flowers? || ہوا کے ذریعے زیرگی والے پھول کیڑوں کے ذریعے زیرگی والے پھولوں سے کیسے مختلف ہیں؟ || د باد په واسطه ګرده افشانه کېدونکي ګلونه له حشراتي ګرده افشانه کېدونکو ګلونو څنګه توپیر لري؟
sol Model answer ## Wind-pollinated flowers are small, have no bright colour, scent or nectar, have long, feathery stigmas and large exposed stamens that give much light pollen. Insect-pollinated flowers are large and brightly coloured, scented, have nectar, and have small sticky or spiny pollen and stigmas.
q 8 | Why do seeds need water and oxygen for germination? || بیجوں کو اگنے کے لیے پانی اور آکسیجن کی ضرورت کیوں ہوتی ہے؟ || ولې تخمونه د زرغونېدو لپاره اوبو او اکسیجن ته اړتیا لري؟
sol Model answer ## Water softens the seed coat and activates enzymes that digest stored food, and the embryo takes up water to grow; oxygen is needed for aerobic respiration to release the energy that the growing embryo requires.

q 1 | Describe the alternation of generation in a flowering plant. || پھول دار پودے میں نسلوں کی تبدیلی بیان کیجیے۔ || په ګل لرونکي بوټي کې د نسلونو بدلون بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A flowering plant shows two generations alternating: the sporophyte (diploid, the plant body) forms spores by meiosis in the anther and ovule; spores develop into the gametophytes (haploid): pollen grain (male) and embryo sac (female). The gametophytes produce gametes by mitosis; fertilization gives a zygote (diploid) that grows into the embryo and a new sporophyte.
q 2 | Explain the different plant structures modified for vegetative propagation. || نباتاتی افزائش کے لیے تبدیل شدہ پودوں کی مختلف ساختوں کی وضاحت کیجیے۔ || د نباتي تکثر لپاره بدل شوي د بوټو بېلابېل جوړښتونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Modified structures for vegetative propagation: runners (strawberry, grass), stem tubers (potato), rhizomes (ginger), bulbs (onion), corms (colocasia), suckers (mint), leaves (Bryophyllum) and roots (sweet potato); they bear buds that grow into new plants.
q 3 | Explain gametogenesis. || گیمیٹوجینیسس کی وضاحت کیجیے۔ || ګامیټوجنیزس تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Gametogenesis is the formation of gametes. Spermatogenesis (testes): spermatogonia divide by mitosis, grow into primary spermatocytes, divide by meiosis into spermatids that become four sperms. Oogenesis (ovary): oogonia grow into primary oocytes, meiosis gives one large ovum and small polar bodies. In plants pollen and the egg cell form by mitosis from the haploid spores.
q 4 | How are seeds produced? What is the structure of a seed? || بیج کیسے بنتے ہیں؟ بیج کی ساخت کیا ہے؟ || تخمونه څنګه جوړېږي؟ د تخم جوړښت څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## After pollination, a pollen tube carries the male nuclei to the ovule; double fertilization forms the zygote and the endosperm. The ovule becomes a seed and the ovary becomes the fruit. A seed has a seed coat (testa), embryo (plumule, radicle, cotyledons) and stored food in the endosperm or cotyledons; a micropyle and hilum are visible on the coat.
q 5 | Write a comprehensive note on seed germination and its types. || بیج کے اگنے اور اس کی اقسام پر جامع نوٹ لکھیے۔ || د تخم د زرغونېدو او ډولونو په اړه جامع یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Germination is the growth of the embryo into a seedling when it gets water, oxygen and a suitable temperature. Types: epigeal (cotyledons come above the ground as in bean and sunflower; the hypocotyl grows) and hypogeal (cotyledons stay under the soil as in pea and maize; the epicotyl grows). First the radicle emerges, then the plumule forms the shoot.
