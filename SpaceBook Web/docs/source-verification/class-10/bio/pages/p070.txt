q 7 | What are ligaments and tendons? What function do they perform? || لیگامنٹس اور ٹینڈنز کیا ہیں؟ یہ کیا کام انجام دیتے ہیں؟ || لیګامنټونه او وترونه څه دي؟ کومه دنده ترسره کوي؟
sol Model answer ## Ligaments join bone to bone and hold the joint in place and stabilize it; tendons join muscle to bone and transmit the pull of the muscle to the bone to move it. Both are made of strong collagen fibres.
q 8 | State five functions of bones. || ہڈیوں کے پانچ افعال بیان کیجیے۔ || د هډوکو پنځه دندې وښایاست.
sol Model answer ## Functions of bones: support the body, protect delicate organs (brain, heart, lungs), movement (levers for muscles), make blood cells in the bone marrow, store minerals (calcium and phosphorus) and fat.
q 3 | C. Write detailed answers to the following questions: || C. درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات لکھیے: || C. لاندې پوښتنو ته مفصل ځوابونه ولیکئ:
q 1 | Describe the role of the skeleton and muscles in locomotion. || نقل و حرکت میں ڈھانچے اور عضلات کا کردار بیان کیجیے۔ || په لوکوموشن کې د اسکلېټ او عضلو رول بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The skeleton gives a rigid framework and levers; muscles are attached to bones by tendons and pull on them when they contract. Muscles work in antagonistic pairs (flexor and extensor) around joints, so the bones move at the joint like levers, and the movement of the limbs makes the body walk, run or swim.
q 2 | Differentiate between the axial and appendicular skeleton and describe the major components of both. || محوری اور ضمیمہ ڈھانچے میں فرق کیجیے اور دونوں کے بڑے اجزا بیان کیجیے۔ || د محوري او ضمیموي اسکلېټ ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ او د دواړو لوی اجزا بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The axial skeleton (80 bones) forms the central axis: the skull (cranium and face), vertebral column (33 vertebrae), ribs (12 pairs) and sternum. The appendicular skeleton (126 bones) consists of the limbs and girdles: the pectoral girdle (scapulae and clavicles), the arms (humerus, radius, ulna, carpals, metacarpals, phalanges), the pelvic girdle and the legs (femur, tibia, fibula, tarsals, metatarsals, phalanges).
q 3 | Discuss different types of joints and their role. || جوڑوں کی مختلف اقسام اور ان کے کردار پر بحث کیجیے۔ || د بندونو بېلابېل ډولونه او رول یې وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer ## Fixed joints (skull) allow no movement; slightly movable joints (vertebrae) allow limited movement; synovial joints allow free movement: ball and socket (shoulder, hip) in all directions, hinge (elbow, knee) in one plane, pivot (neck) for rotation, gliding (wrist, ankle) for sliding. Joints permit movement, give flexibility and absorb shock.
q 4 | Write the causes and treatments of arthritis and osteoporosis. || آرتھرائٹس اور آسٹیوپوروسس کی وجوہات اور علاج لکھیے۔ || د ارترایټس او اوسټیوپوروزس لاملونه او درملنې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Arthritis: inflammation of the joints; causes are age, injury, infection, uric acid and autoimmune diseases; treatment includes pain killers and anti-inflammatory drugs, exercise and physiotherapy, and joint replacement in severe cases. Osteoporosis: bones become weak and brittle because of loss of calcium, due to age, menopause, poor diet and lack of vitamin D and exercise; treatment is calcium and vitamin D, medicines, exercise, and a balanced diet.

q 1 | Identify and draw labelled diagrams of different bones of the axial and appendicular skeleton from real specimen models or charts. || اصل نمونوں کے ماڈلز یا چارٹس سے محوری اور ضمیمہ ڈھانچے کی مختلف ہڈیوں کی شناخت کیجیے اور لیبل والی شکلیں بنائیے۔ || له اصلي نمونو، ماډلونو یا چارټونو د محوري او ضمیموي اسکلېټ بېلابېل هډوکي وپېژنئ او نښه شوي انځورونه یې جوړ کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Axial: skull, vertebrae, ribs, sternum. Appendicular: scapula, clavicle, humerus, radius, ulna, hip bone, femur, tibia, fibula. Draw each from a specimen and label its main parts.
q 2 | Describe the movement of various human joints through observation of models. || ماڈلز کے مشاہدے کے ذریعے انسان کے مختلف جوڑوں کی حرکت بیان کیجیے۔ || د ماډلونو په کتلو سره د انسان د بېلابېلو بندونو حرکت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Observe that a ball and socket joint (shoulder, hip) moves in all directions, a hinge joint (elbow, knee) moves only like a door in one plane, a pivot joint allows rotation, and fixed joints do not move.
q 3 | Describe the movement of biceps and triceps through presentation of the movement of the elbow. || کہنی کی حرکت پیش کر کے بائسپس اور ٹرائسپس کی حرکت بیان کیجیے۔ || د څنګل د حرکت په ښودلو سره د بایسپس او ټرایسپس حرکت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## When the biceps contracts the triceps relaxes and the forearm is raised (flexion); when the triceps contracts and the biceps relaxes the arm straightens (extension): the muscles work as an antagonistic pair.
q 5 | Science, Technology and Society || سائنس، ٹیکنالوجی اور معاشرہ || ساینس، ټکنالوژي او ټولنه
q 1 | Relate your skeleton and its functioning with daily life. || اپنے ڈھانچے اور اس کے کام کو روزمرہ زندگی سے منسلک کیجیے۔ || خپل اسکلېټ او دنده یې له ورځني ژوند سره وتړئ.
sol Model answer ## The skeleton supports us in sitting and standing, the joints let us walk, run, write and lift things, the skull and ribs protect the brain and organs, and good posture, calcium-rich food and exercise keep bones strong.
q 2 | Relate the principle of leverage to the action of the elbow joint. || لیور کے اصول کو کہنی کے جوڑ کے عمل سے منسلک کیجیے۔ || د لیور اصل د څنګل د بند له عمل سره وتړئ.
sol Model answer ## The elbow joint works as a lever of the third class: the fulcrum is the elbow joint, the effort is the biceps force close to the joint, and the load is the weight in the hand.
q 3 | State the principles of arthroplasty for the replacement of joints. || جوڑوں کی تبدیلی کے لیے آرتھروپلاسٹی کے اصول بیان کیجیے۔ || د بندونو د بدلون لپاره د ارتروپلاسټۍ اصول وښایاست.
sol Model answer ## Arthroplasty replaces a damaged joint with an artificial one. The damaged bone ends are removed and replaced by metal, plastic or ceramic parts shaped like the natural joint, fixed with bone cement or by bone growth; it relieves pain and restores movement (hip and knee replacement).
