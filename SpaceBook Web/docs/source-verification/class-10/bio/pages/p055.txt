q 3 | What happens if Islets of Langerhans secrete insulin but not glucagon? || اگر آئلٹس آف لینگرہینز انسولین خارج کریں لیکن گلوکاگون نہیں تو کیا ہوگا؟ || که د لانګرهانس ټاپوګي انسولین افراز کړي خو ګلوکاګون نه، نو څه به پېښ شي؟
sol Model answer ## If insulin is released but not glucagon, the blood glucose level would keep falling because glucose is taken up and stored as glycogen but not released back from the liver when needed; this causes hypoglycaemia, weakness and fainting.
q 4 | Why is the pituitary gland known as the master gland? || پیچوٹری غدود کو ماسٹر گلینڈ کیوں کہا جاتا ہے؟ || ولې پیټیوټري غدې ته ماسټر غده ویل کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## The pituitary gland is called the master gland because it secretes hormones that control the activity of other endocrine glands (thyroid, adrenals, ovaries, testes) and also controls growth and water balance; it is itself controlled by the hypothalamus.
q 5 | Differentiate between the two types of coordination system. || ہم آہنگی کے نظام کی دو اقسام میں فرق کیجیے۔ || د همغږۍ د سیسټم د دوو ډولونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## The two types are nervous coordination and chemical (hormonal) coordination. Nervous: by electrical impulses along neurons, very fast, short-lived effect, acts on specific muscles or glands. Chemical: by hormones carried in the blood, slow, long-lasting effect, acts on target organs far from the gland.
q 6 | What is the role of insulin and glucagon? || انسولین اور گلوکاگون کا کیا کردار ہے؟ || د انسولین او ګلوکاګون رول څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## Both hormones are produced by the pancreas (islets of Langerhans) and control blood glucose. Insulin (beta cells) lowers blood glucose by making cells take up glucose and by converting it to glycogen in the liver. Glucagon (alpha cells) raises blood glucose by converting stored glycogen back into glucose.
q 7 | What characters are controlled by the ovarian hormones? || بیضہ دانی کے ہارمونز کون سی خصوصیات کو کنٹرول کرتے ہیں؟ || د تخمدان هورمونونه کومې ځانګړنې کنټرولوي؟
sol Model answer ## Ovarian hormones (oestrogen and progesterone) control the female secondary sexual characters: growth of breasts, widening of hips, voice and body hair pattern, the menstrual cycle, preparation of the uterus for pregnancy and the maintenance of pregnancy.
q 8 | State the hormones of the pituitary and thyroid glands and write their functions. || پیچوٹری اور تھائرائیڈ غدود کے ہارمونز بیان کیجیے اور ان کے افعال لکھیے۔ || د پیټیوټري او تایرایډ غدو هورمونونه وښایاست او دندې یې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Pituitary: growth hormone (growth of bones and muscles), TSH (stimulates the thyroid), ACTH (stimulates the adrenal cortex), FSH and LH (control the gonads), prolactin (milk production), ADH (water reabsorption in the kidney), oxytocin (uterine contraction). Thyroid: thyroxine (controls the metabolic rate, growth and development) and calcitonin (lowers blood calcium).

q 1 | Explain the different types of receptors in human beings. || انسانوں میں ریسیپٹرز کی مختلف اقسام بیان کیجیے۔ || په انسانانو کې د ریسیپټرونو بېلابېل ډولونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Receptors detect stimuli. Photoreceptors (eye, rods and cones) detect light; mechanoreceptors detect touch, pressure and sound (skin, ear); chemoreceptors detect chemicals (taste buds and nose); thermoreceptors detect temperature; proprioceptors detect position of muscles and joints; and pain receptors (nociceptors) detect injury.
q 2 | Describe the structure of the human brain. || انسانی دماغ کی ساخت بیان کیجیے۔ || د انسان د دماغ جوړښت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The human brain (about 1.4 kg) is protected by the skull and three membranes (meninges) with cerebrospinal fluid. Parts: the forebrain (cerebrum with two hemispheres and a folded cortex for thinking, memory, senses and voluntary action; thalamus; hypothalamus), the midbrain, and the hindbrain (cerebellum for balance and coordination; pons; medulla oblongata for involuntary actions such as breathing and heartbeat).
q 3 | Write a comprehensive note on chemical coordination in human beings. || انسانوں میں کیمیائی ہم آہنگی پر جامع نوٹ لکھیے۔ || په انسانانو کې د کیمیاوي همغږۍ په اړه جامع یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Chemical coordination is carried out by hormones secreted by endocrine glands directly into the blood. Examples: pituitary (master gland), thyroid (thyroxine, metabolism), parathyroid (calcium), adrenal (adrenaline for emergencies), pancreas (insulin and glucagon for glucose), ovaries (oestrogen, progesterone) and testes (testosterone). Hormones work at low concentrations on target cells, act slowly and for longer and are controlled by negative feedback.
q 4 | Discuss any two disorders of the human eye. || انسانی آنکھ کی کوئی سی دو بیماریوں پر بحث کیجیے۔ || د انسان د سترګې هرې دوه ناروغۍ وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer ## Short sightedness (myopia): the eyeball is too long or the lens too strong, so the image forms in front of the retina and distant objects are blurred; corrected by concave lenses. Long sightedness (hypermetropia): the eyeball is too short, so the image forms behind the retina and near objects are blurred; corrected by convex lenses. (Cataract, clouding of the lens, is another disorder.)
q 5 | Describe the causes, symptoms and treatments of paralysis. || فالج کی وجوہات، علامات اور علاج بیان کیجیے۔ || د فلج لاملونه، نښې او درملنې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Paralysis is the loss of the ability to move a part of the body. Causes: spinal cord injury, stroke, brain injury, polio and nerve diseases. Symptoms: loss of movement and sensation, weakness, muscle stiffness. Treatment: physiotherapy, medicines, surgery, special devices and rehabilitation; prevention includes vaccination against polio and safe driving.
q 6 | Describe the causes, symptoms and treatments of epilepsy. || مرگی کی وجوہات، علامات اور علاج بیان کیجیے۔ || د مرګي لاملونه، نښې او درملنې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Epilepsy causes: brain injury, infection, tumour, stroke, genetic factors. Symptoms: seizures with loss of consciousness, convulsions (jerking), staring spells and confusion. Treatment: anti-epileptic drugs, surgery in some patients, avoiding triggers such as lack of sleep and flashing lights, and care of the patient during a seizure.
q 7 | Explain the mechanism of vision by the eyes. || آنکھوں کے ذریعے بینائی کا طریقۂ کار بیان کیجیے۔ || د سترګو له لارې د لید میکانیزم تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Light from an object passes through the cornea, aqueous humour, pupil (the iris adjusts its size), lens and vitreous humour. The cornea and lens focus the rays to form a real, inverted, smaller image on the retina. Rods (dim light) and cones (colour) change light into nerve impulses that go through the optic nerve to the visual area of the brain, which interprets the image as upright.

q 1 | Analyse why plants (like sunflower) have a very slow response to stimuli. || تجزیہ کیجیے کہ پودوں (جیسے سورج مکھی) کا محرکات پر ردِعمل بہت سست کیوں ہوتا ہے۔ || وشنئ چې ولې بوټي (لکه لمر ګلی) محرکاتو ته ډېر ورو غبرګون ښيي.
sol Model answer ## Plants have no nervous system or muscles; they respond by growth changes (tropisms) controlled by hormones (auxins) that spread slowly from cell to cell, so the response takes hours or days.
q 2 | Visualise nervous and hormonal coordination by comparing electrical transmission in wires with the transmission of nerve impulse in neurons and by comparing convection currents in liquids to the hormonal transmission in blood. || تاروں میں برقی ترسیل کا نیورونز میں عصبی پیغام کی ترسیل سے اور مائعات میں حرارتی بہاؤ (کنویکشن کرنٹس) کا خون میں ہارمونی ترسیل سے موازنہ کر کے عصبی اور ہارمونی ہم آہنگی کا تصور کیجیے۔ || په سیمونو کې د برقي لېږد پرتله په نیورونونو کې د عصبي سیګنال له لېږد سره او په مایعاتو کې د کنوکشن جریانونو پرتله په وینه کې له هورموني لېږد سره کولو سره عصبي او هورموني همغږي تصور کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A nerve impulse travels like an electrical signal in a wire, quickly and in one direction along a specific path (neuron), whereas hormones are carried by blood like heat is carried by convection currents in a liquid: slowly, spread everywhere, and acting only on target cells.
q 3 | Compare the BGC (blood glucose concentration) of a healthy person with a patient suffering from Diabetes mellitus. || ایک صحت مند شخص اور ذیابیطس میلیٹس کے مریض کے BGC (خون میں گلوکوز کا ارتکاز) کا موازنہ کیجیے۔ || د یوه روغ کس BGC (د وینې ګلوکوز غلظت) د شکرې ناروغ له BGC سره پرتله کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A healthy person's blood glucose is about 70-110 mg/dL (fasting) and returns to normal after meals; in a diabetic patient it stays high (above 126 mg/dL fasting and often more than 200 after meals) because insulin is lacking or ineffective.
