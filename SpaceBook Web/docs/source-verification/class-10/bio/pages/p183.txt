q 1 | What is drug addiction and what are the effects of addiction? || منشیات کی لت کیا ہے اور لت کے اثرات کیا ہیں؟ || د نشه یي توکو روږدتیا څه ده او د روږدتیا اغېزې کومې دي؟
sol Model answer ## Drug addiction is a habit of using a drug regularly and not being able to stop, because the body and mind depend on it. Effects: damage to health (liver, heart, brain), mental disorders, weak work and studies, crime, loss of money, broken family and death by overdose.
