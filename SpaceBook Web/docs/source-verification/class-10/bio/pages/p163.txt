q 4 | Write any five advantages of biotechnology in the field of agriculture. || زراعت کے شعبے میں بائیو ٹیکنالوجی کے کوئی پانچ فوائد لکھیے۔ || د کرنې په برخه کې د بایوټکنالوژي هرې پنځه ګټې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Advantages in agriculture: crops resistant to pests, disease and herbicides; higher yield; crops tolerant to drought and salinity; improved nutrition (golden rice); longer shelf life; and biofertilizers and biopesticides that reduce chemical use.
q 5 | How is yogurt produced through the use of biotechnology? || بائیو ٹیکنالوجی کے استعمال سے دہی کیسے تیار کیا جاتا ہے؟ || د بایوټکنالوژي په کارولو مستې څنګه تولیدېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Milk is heated to kill germs, cooled to about 40-45 °C and a little yogurt starter (Lactobacillus and Streptococcus) is added; the bacteria change lactose into lactic acid, which makes the milk thicken and sour, giving yogurt after several hours.

q 1 | Write a comprehensive note on the procedure of recombinant DNA technology. || ری کمبیننٹ DNA ٹیکنالوجی کے طریقۂ کار پر جامع نوٹ لکھیے۔ || د ریکمبیننټ DNA ټکنالوژي د کړنلارې په اړه جامع یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Steps of recombinant DNA technology: (1) isolate the gene of interest (e.g. the human insulin gene); (2) cut it out with a restriction enzyme; (3) cut a vector (plasmid) with the same enzyme; (4) join the gene into the plasmid with DNA ligase to make recombinant DNA; (5) put it into a host bacterium (transformation); (6) grow the bacteria in a fermenter so that they multiply and make the product, e.g. insulin; (7) separate and purify the product.
q 2 | How are single cell proteins produced and what is their significance? || سنگل سیل پروٹینز کیسے تیار کی جاتی ہیں اور ان کی اہمیت کیا ہے؟ || یو حجروي پروټینونه څنګه تولیدېږي او اهمیت یې څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## Single cell proteins (SCP) are protein-rich biomass of microorganisms (bacteria, yeast, algae, fungi) grown on cheap wastes such as molasses in fermenters, then harvested, dried and purified. Significance: a cheap and fast source of protein for humans and animal feed, uses waste, little land, and helps fight protein shortage.
q 3 | What is a fermenter and how does it work? || فرمینٹر کیا ہے اور یہ کیسے کام کرتا ہے؟ || فرمینټر څه دی او څنګه کار کوي؟
sol Model answer ## A fermenter (bioreactor) is a large closed vessel in which microorganisms grow under controlled conditions to make products. It has a stirrer, an air inlet (sparger), temperature and pH controls with a cooling jacket, a nutrient inlet, sensors and an outlet for harvesting. The nutrient medium and microorganisms are mixed; the conditions are kept optimal so that the microbes multiply and make the product.
q 4 | Describe the advantages of using fermenters for getting products from genetically modified organisms. || جینیاتی طور پر تبدیل شدہ جانداروں سے مصنوعات حاصل کرنے کے لیے فرمینٹرز کے استعمال کے فوائد بیان کیجیے۔ || له جنیټیکي بدلو شوو موجوداتو د محصولاتو د ترلاسه کولو لپاره د فرمینټرونو د کارولو ګټې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Fermenters give controlled temperature, pH, oxygen and nutrients, so growth and yield are high; they are sterile, so there is no contamination; they work on a large scale, continuously, and the product is easily separated and purified.
q 5 | Describe how biotechnology is helping humankind in the fields of food and health. || بیان کیجیے کہ بائیو ٹیکنالوجی خوراک اور صحت کے شعبوں میں انسانیت کی کیسے مدد کر رہی ہے۔ || بیان کړئ چې بایوټکنالوژي د خوړو او روغتیا په برخو کې څنګه له انسانیت سره مرسته کوي.
sol Model answer ## Food: yogurt, cheese, bread, single cell protein, GM crops with higher yield and nutrients, longer shelf life. Health: insulin, growth hormone, vaccines (hepatitis B), interferon, antibiotics, gene therapy, DNA tests and diagnosis, clotting factors.
