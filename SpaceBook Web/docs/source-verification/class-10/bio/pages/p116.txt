q 4 | How is artificial selection used for the improvement of crops? || فصلوں کی بہتری کے لیے مصنوعی انتخاب کیسے استعمال ہوتا ہے؟ || د فصلونو د ښه کولو لپاره مصنوعي انتخاب څنګه کارول کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## In artificial selection farmers choose plants with desired characters (high yield, disease resistance, drought tolerance, good taste) and breed only them over many generations, so those characters become common; this is how wheat, rice, maize and other improved varieties were developed.
q 5 | Differentiate between different types of dominant relations. || غلبے کے مختلف تعلقات میں فرق کیجیے۔ || د غلبې د بېلابېلو اړیکو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Complete dominance: the dominant allele completely hides the recessive (tall Tt is tall). Incomplete dominance: the heterozygote is intermediate (red × white four o'clock gives pink). Codominance: both alleles show fully in the heterozygote (blood group AB).
q 6 | Write down the phenotypes of the individuals in the following table. || درج ذیل جدول میں افراد کے فینوٹائپ لکھیے۔ || په لاندې جدول کې د افرادو فینوټایپونه ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## $I^AI^A$ (or $I^Ai$): blood group A; $I^BI^B$: blood group B; $I^Bi$: blood group B; $I^Ai$: blood group A; $ii$: blood group O; $I^AI^B$: blood group AB. The alleles $I^A$ and $I^B$ are dominant over $i$, and they are codominant with each other, so $I^AI^B$ shows both and gives group AB.

q 1 | Relate the structure of chromosome with DNA and gene. Draw a concept chart to explain your answer. || کروموسوم کی ساخت کو DNA اور جین سے منسلک کیجیے۔ اپنے جواب کی وضاحت کے لیے تصوراتی چارٹ بنائیے۔ || د کروموزوم جوړښت له DNA او جین سره وتړئ. د خپل ځواب د تشریح لپاره مفهومي چارټ جوړ کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A chromosome is made of one very long DNA molecule coiled round histone proteins. A gene is a particular segment of DNA with the instruction for making one protein or trait. Concept chart: Nucleus, then chromosomes, then DNA, then genes, then a sequence of nucleotides (A, T, G, C).
q 2 | Explain with the help of a diagram the relationship amongst DNA, RNA and proteins. || شکل کی مدد سے DNA، RNA اور پروٹینز کے درمیان تعلق کی وضاحت کیجیے۔ || د انځور په مرسته د DNA، RNA او پروټینونو ترمنځ اړیکه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## DNA carries the genetic information. During transcription an mRNA copy of a gene is made in the nucleus (DNA to RNA). The mRNA moves to the ribosomes, where tRNA brings amino acids according to the codons (translation) and the amino acids join to form a protein: DNA to RNA to protein (the central dogma). (Draw this flow with the nucleus and the ribosome.)
q 3 | Explain why Mendel selected peas as a subject for studying genetics? || وضاحت کیجیے کہ مینڈل نے جینیات کے مطالعے کے لیے مٹر کو کیوں منتخب کیا؟ || تشریح کړئ چې ولې مینډل د جنیټیک د مطالعې لپاره مټر غوره کړل؟
sol Model answer ## Mendel chose the garden pea because it has easily distinguished contrasting characters (tall/dwarf, round/wrinkled), is self-pollinating but can be cross-pollinated, grows fast with a short life cycle, gives many seeds, is cheap and easy to grow, and has true-breeding varieties.
q 4 | Explain the law of independent assortment with the help of an example and chart. || مثال اور چارٹ کی مدد سے قانونِ آزادانہ ترتیب کی وضاحت کیجیے۔ || د یوې بېلګې او چارټ په مرسته د خپلواک ترتیب قانون تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Law of independent assortment: the alleles for different traits separate independently of each other during gamete formation. Example: round yellow (RRYY) × wrinkled green (rryy) gives F1 all RrYy (round yellow); selfing gives gametes RY, Ry, rY, ry and an F2 of 9 round yellow : 3 round green : 3 wrinkled yellow : 1 wrinkled green (a 4 × 4 Punnett square).
q 5 | Describe the sources of variations. Explain the importance of variations in organisms. || تغیرات کے ذرائع بیان کیجیے۔ جانداروں میں تغیرات کی اہمیت کی وضاحت کیجیے۔ || د توپیرونو سرچینې بیان کړئ. په موجوداتو کې د توپیرونو اهمیت تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Variations arise from mutations, crossing over and independent assortment in meiosis, random fertilization, and from environmental effects. They are important because they give a population differences that natural selection can act on, so those best adapted survive, and the species can evolve and survive changes in the environment.
q 6 | Draw a cross between two pea plants. One of them has round green seeds (RRyy) while the other has wrinkled yellow seeds (rrYY). || مٹر کے دو پودوں کے درمیان کراس بنائیے۔ ان میں سے ایک کے گول سبز بیج (RRyy) ہیں جبکہ دوسرے کے جھریوں والے زرد بیج (rrYY) ہیں۔ || د دوو مټر بوټو ترمنځ کراس رسم کړئ. یو یې ګرد شنه تخمونه (RRyy) لري په داسې حال کې چې بل یې ګونجې لرونکي ژېړ تخمونه (rrYY) لري.
sol Model answer ## RRyy (round green) × rrYY (wrinkled yellow): the gametes are Ry and rY, so all F1 plants are RrYy (round yellow). When F1 is selfed the gametes RY, Ry, rY and ry give the F2 in the ratio 9 round yellow : 3 round green : 3 wrinkled yellow : 1 wrinkled green.

q 3 | Rationalise life as a product of the diversity brought about by chromosomes, genes and DNA. || زندگی کو کروموسومز، جینز اور DNA سے پیدا ہونے والے تنوع کی پیداوار کے طور پر ثابت کیجیے۔ || ژوند د کروموزومونو، جینونو او DNA له لارې رامنځته شوي تنوع د محصول په توګه ثابت کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Life's diversity arises because chromosomes carry genes made of DNA; different combinations of alleles from crossing over, independent assortment and mutations give different traits in every individual.
q 4 | Outline the scientific findings and some of the technological advances that led to the modern concept of gene. || ان سائنسی دریافتوں اور کچھ تکنیکی ترقیوں کا خاکہ پیش کیجیے جو جین کے جدید تصور کا باعث بنیں۔ || هغه ساینسي موندنې او ځینې تخنیکي پرمختګونه په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ چې د جین عصري مفکورې ته یې لار هواره کړه.
sol Model answer ## Mendel's laws (1866), the discovery of chromosomes (Sutton), DNA as the genetic material (Griffith, Avery, Hershey-Chase), the double helix (Watson and Crick, 1953) and the genetic code and recombinant DNA led to the modern concept of the gene as a DNA sequence coding for a protein.
q 5 | Analyse the concept of the gene to produce various proteins of the body. || جسم کے مختلف پروٹینز پیدا کرنے کے لیے جین کے تصور کا تجزیہ کیجیے۔ || د بدن د بېلابېلو پروټینونو د تولید لپاره د جین مفکوره وشنئ.
sol Model answer ## A gene is a segment of DNA that is transcribed into mRNA and translated into one protein (or polypeptide); different genes thus make the enzymes, hormones and structural proteins of the body.
q 6 | Describe the importance of scientific investigation and mathematical knowledge in genetics. || جینیات میں سائنسی تحقیق اور ریاضیاتی علم کی اہمیت بیان کیجیے۔ || په جنیټیک کې د ساینسي څېړنې او ریاضیاتي پوهې اهمیت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Mendel used experiments and mathematical analysis of the ratios (3:1, 9:3:3:1) to find the laws of heredity; probability helps to predict the results of crosses, so mathematics and scientific investigation are important in genetics.
q 7 | Explain how genetics can predict the progeny of two individuals which are crossed. || وضاحت کیجیے کہ جینیات کراس کیے گئے دو افراد کی اولاد کی پیش گوئی کیسے کر سکتی ہے۔ || تشریح کړئ چې جنیټیک څنګه د دوو کراس شویو افرادو اولاد وړاندوینه کولای شي.
sol Model answer ## Using the genotypes of the parents, the possible gametes and a Punnett square, genetics predicts the genotypes and phenotypes of the children and their ratios, e.g. $Tt \times Tt$ gives 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt, which is 3 tall : 1 dwarf.
q 8 | Describe the effects of the environment that lead to the selection of a variant which is more adapted to it. || ماحول کے ان اثرات کو بیان کیجیے جو ایسے تغیر کے انتخاب کا باعث بنتے ہیں جو اس سے زیادہ مطابقت رکھتا ہو۔ || د چاپېریال هغه اغېزې بیان کړئ چې د داسې توپیر انتخاب ته لار هواروي چې ورسره ډېر برابر وي.
sol Model answer ## The environment selects the variants that are better adapted: e.g. in a polluted area dark moths are better camouflaged and survive, so more of them reproduce and the dark variant becomes common (natural selection).
