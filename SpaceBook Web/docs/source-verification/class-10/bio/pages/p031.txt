q 2 | Differentiate between the adaptations of hydrophytes and xerophytes for osmoregulation. || اوسمو ریگولیشن کے لیے ہائیڈروفائٹس اور زیروفائٹس کی موافقتوں میں فرق بیان کیجیے۔ || د اوسمو ریګولیشن لپاره د هایډروفایټونو او زیروفایټونو د تطابقونو ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Hydrophytes (water plants) live in water and have thin cuticle, stomata on the upper surface, large air spaces and reduced roots, since they have to get rid of excess water. Xerophytes (desert plants) have thick waxy cuticle, sunken stomata, reduced or spiny leaves, deep or wide roots and fleshy stems that store water, to reduce water loss.
q 3 | Briefly describe how kidneys control the composition of blood. || مختصراً بیان کیجیے کہ گردے خون کی ترکیب کو کیسے کنٹرول کرتے ہیں۔ || په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ چې پښتورګي د وینې جوړښت څنګه کنټرولوي.
sol Model answer ## The kidneys filter the blood in the nephrons: useful substances (glucose, amino acids, most water and salts) are reabsorbed, and wastes (urea, extra water and salts) are removed in the urine. They adjust the amounts of water and ions and the pH, so the composition of blood is kept constant.
q 4 | Enlist materials in our diet which are more likely to cause kidney stones? || ہماری خوراک میں ان مادوں کی فہرست بنائیے جو گردے کی پتھری کا زیادہ امکان پیدا کرتے ہیں؟ || زموږ په خواړو کې هغه مواد لست کړئ چې د پښتورګي د تیږو د رامنځته کولو ډېر احتمال لري؟
sol Model answer ## Foods rich in oxalates and calcium, such as spinach, tomatoes, chocolate, tea, nuts, cheese and milk products, and too much salt and meat protein, are more likely to cause kidney stones, especially if little water is drunk.
q 5 | Define lithotripsy? || لیتھوٹرپسی کی تعریف کیجیے؟ || لیتوټریپسي تعریف کړئ؟
sol Model answer ## Lithotripsy is a treatment that uses shock waves (ultrasound) from outside the body to break kidney stones into small pieces that pass out with the urine.
q 6 | What is the role of the skin in thermoregulation? || تھرمو ریگولیشن میں جلد کا کیا کردار ہے؟ || په ترمو ریګولیشن کې د پوستکي رول څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## The skin controls body temperature: in heat, sweat glands release sweat that cools the body as it evaporates and blood vessels in the skin widen (vasodilation); in cold, sweating stops, vessels narrow (vasoconstriction) and hair stands up, which keeps heat in.

q 1 | Describe the structure of kidneys in human beings. || انسانوں میں گردوں کی ساخت بیان کیجیے۔ || په انسانانو کې د پښتورګو جوړښت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Human kidneys are a pair of bean-shaped organs, about 10 cm long, on either side of the backbone below the ribs. Each has an outer cortex, an inner medulla with cone-shaped pyramids and a central cavity called the pelvis that leads into the ureter. Each kidney contains about a million nephrons, each made of a Bowman's capsule with a glomerulus, a proximal tubule, loop of Henle, distal tubule and a collecting duct. The renal artery brings blood and the renal vein takes it away.
q 2 | Why do plants excrete? What are the different mechanisms through which plants excrete different substances? || پودے اخراج کیوں کرتے ہیں؟ وہ کون سے مختلف طریقۂ کار ہیں جن کے ذریعے پودے مختلف مادے خارج کرتے ہیں؟ || بوټي ولې اطراح کوي؟ هغه بېلابېل میکانیزمونه کوم دي چې بوټي پرې بېلابېل مواد اطراح کوي؟
sol Model answer ## Plants excrete the wastes of metabolism such as $CO_2$, oxygen, excess water and other substances. Mechanisms: gases ($O_2$, $CO_2$) leave through stomata and lenticels by diffusion; excess water leaves as water vapour by transpiration; waste substances are stored in leaves, bark and fruits that are shed (leaf fall), or as crystals in vacuoles, or as resin, gums, latex and tannins; some are secreted through roots into the soil.
q 3 | What are kidney stones and how are they formed? Suggest ways in which these stones can be removed from the body. || گردے کی پتھری کیا ہے اور یہ کیسے بنتی ہے؟ وہ طریقے تجویز کیجیے جن سے یہ پتھریاں جسم سے نکالی جا سکیں۔ || د پښتورګي تیږې څه دي او څنګه جوړېږي؟ هغه لارې وړاندیز کړئ چې دا تیږې پرې له بدن څخه لرې کېدای شي.
sol Model answer ## Kidney stones are hard deposits of salts such as calcium oxalate, calcium phosphate and uric acid that crystallize in the kidney when urine is concentrated, e.g. by little water intake or too much of certain foods. They can be removed by drinking plenty of water, by medicines, lithotripsy (shock waves), endoscopic removal through the urethra or surgery.
q 4 | Define haemodialysis. How is it performed? || ہیمو ڈائیلاسس کی تعریف کیجیے۔ یہ کیسے کیا جاتا ہے؟ || هیموډیالیزس تعریف کړئ. دا څنګه ترسره کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Haemodialysis is the cleaning of blood by a machine for people whose kidneys have failed. Blood is taken from an artery in the arm, passed through the dialyzer where wastes (urea, excess salts) diffuse across a semi-permeable membrane into dialysis fluid, and the cleaned blood is returned to a vein. It is done for about 4 hours, three times a week.
q 5 | How does a dialyzer work? Relate the function of a dialyzer with that of the kidney. || ڈائیلائزر کیسے کام کرتا ہے؟ ڈائیلائزر کے فعل کو گردے کے فعل سے منسلک کیجیے۔ || ډیالایزر څنګه کار کوي؟ د ډیالایزر دنده د پښتورګي له دندې سره وتړئ.
sol Model answer ## The dialyzer has tubes with a partially permeable membrane surrounded by dialysis fluid flowing in the opposite direction. Urea, salts and water pass from the blood into the fluid by diffusion, while blood cells and proteins stay in. Like the kidney's nephron it filters blood and removes wastes, though it cannot reabsorb useful substances actively, so the fluid contains normal levels of glucose and salts to prevent their loss.
