q 1 | How do reflex action work in human beings? || انسانوں میں ریفلیکس ایکشن کیسے کام کرتا ہے؟ || په انسانانو کې انعکاسي عمل څنګه کار کوي؟
sol Model answer ## A reflex action is a quick, automatic response. Path: stimulus, receptor (sense organ), sensory neuron, spinal cord (relay neuron in the grey matter), motor neuron, effector (muscle or gland), response; it does not wait for the brain, e.g. jerking the hand away from a hot object.
q 2 | What are the causes of epilepsy and paralysis? || مرگی اور فالج کی وجوہات کیا ہیں؟ || د مرګي او فلج لاملونه کوم دي؟
sol Model answer ## Epilepsy is caused by abnormal electrical discharges in the brain due to brain injury, infection, tumour, stroke, genetic factors or lack of oxygen at birth. Paralysis is caused by damage to the brain or spinal cord or nerves through accidents, stroke, infection (polio), tumours or diseases.
