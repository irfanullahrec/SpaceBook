q 1 | Define homeostasis and osmoregulation. || ہومیوسٹیسس اور اوسمو ریگولیشن کی تعریف کیجیے۔ || هومیوسټازس او اوسمو ریګولیشن تعریف کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Homeostasis is the maintenance of a constant internal environment of the body (temperature, water, glucose, pH, ions). Osmoregulation is the control of the water and salt balance of body fluids so that the osmotic pressure stays constant.
