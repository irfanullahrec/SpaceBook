q 2 | B. Write short answers to the following questions. || B. درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیے۔ || B. لاندې پوښتنو ته لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
q 1 | Why are bones considered as dynamic structures? || ہڈیوں کو متحرک ساختیں کیوں سمجھا جاتا ہے؟ || ولې هډوکي متحرک جوړښتونه ګڼل کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Bone is living tissue: it has a blood supply and cells (osteoblasts and osteoclasts) that continually build, reshape and repair it, store and release calcium, and make blood cells in the marrow; it grows and mends fractures, so it is dynamic.
q 2 | What is the contribution of Vesalius in understanding the human skeleton? || انسانی ڈھانچے کو سمجھنے میں ویسالیئس کی کیا خدمات ہیں؟ || د انسان د اسکلېټ په پوهېدو کې د ویسالیوس ونډه څه ده؟
sol Model answer ## Andreas Vesalius (1514-1564) dissected human bodies and wrote *De Humani Corporis Fabrica*, correcting many errors of Galen and giving the first accurate description and drawings of the human skeleton and muscles; he founded modern anatomy.
q 3 | Describe the function of three major types of joints and give an example of each. || جوڑوں کی تین بڑی اقسام کا کام بیان کیجیے اور ہر ایک کی مثال دیجیے۔ || د بندونو د درېیو لویو ډولونو دنده بیان او د هر یو بېلګه ورکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Immovable (fixed) joints hold bones firmly together and protect organs, e.g. the skull sutures. Slightly movable (cartilaginous) joints allow limited movement and absorb shock, e.g. between vertebrae. Freely movable (synovial) joints allow wide movement, e.g. the ball and socket joint of the shoulder and hip, the hinge joint of the elbow and knee.
q 4 | How are different types of arthritis caused? || آرتھرائٹس کی مختلف اقسام کیسے ہوتی ہیں؟ || د ارترایټس بېلابېل ډولونه څنګه رامنځته کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Osteoarthritis is caused by wear and tear of the cartilage with age or injury; rheumatoid arthritis is an autoimmune disease in which the body attacks the synovial membrane; gout arthritis is caused by deposition of uric acid crystals in the joints; infection can cause septic arthritis.
q 5 | Differentiate between a skeletal muscle’s origin and insertion. || ڈھانچوی عضلے کے اوریجن اور انسرشن میں فرق کیجیے۔ || د اسکلېټي عضلې د اوریجن او انسرشن ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## The origin is the attachment of a muscle to a fixed (stationary) bone and the insertion is the attachment to the bone that moves when the muscle contracts; for the biceps the origin is on the scapula and the insertion is on the radius.
q 6 | State the functions of flexors and extensors. || فلیکسرز اور ایکسٹینسرز کے افعال بیان کیجیے۔ || د فلکسرونو او اکسټنسرونو دندې وښایاست.
sol Model answer ## Flexors contract to bend a joint, decreasing the angle between the bones (biceps at the elbow); extensors contract to straighten a joint and increase the angle (triceps). They work as an antagonistic pair.
