q 1 | If a man with type AB blood group marries a woman with type O blood group. What type of blood group do you expect in the offsprings? || اگر AB بلڈ گروپ والا مرد O بلڈ گروپ والی عورت سے شادی کرے۔ آپ اولاد میں کس قسم کے بلڈ گروپ کی توقع کرتے ہیں؟ || که د AB د وینې ګروپ لرونکی سړی د O د وینې ګروپ لرونکې ښځې سره واده وکړي. په اولاد کې د کوم ډول وینې ګروپ تمه لرئ؟
sol Model answer ## Blood group AB has genotype $I^AI^B$ and O has $ii$. The gametes are $I^A$ or $I^B$ and $i$. The children are $I^Ai$ (group A) or $I^Bi$ (group B): 50% group A and 50% group B; no child will have group AB or O.
q 2 | How can crossing bring variations? || کراسنگ کیسے تغیرات لا سکتی ہے؟ || کراسنګ څنګه توپیرونه راوستلای شي؟
sol Model answer ## During meiosis, crossing over exchanges segments between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes, which makes new combinations of alleles on the chromosomes of the gametes, so offspring differ from their parents and from each other.
q 3 | How are DNA and proteins packed to form a chromosome? || DNA اور پروٹینز کروموسوم بنانے کے لیے کیسے پیک ہوتے ہیں؟ || DNA او پروټینونه د کروموزوم د جوړولو لپاره څنګه بسته کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## DNA winds twice around proteins called histones to form nucleosomes (beads on a string); these coil into a chromatin fibre and then fold and coil further to form chromatin, and when the cell divides the chromatin condenses into the compact chromosome.
