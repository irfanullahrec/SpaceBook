q 2 | B. Write short answers to the following questions. || B. درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیے۔ || B. لاندې پوښتنو ته لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
q 1 | Define the terms: species, biosphere and ecosystem. || اصطلاحات کی تعریف کیجیے: نوع، حیاتی کرہ اور ماحولیاتی نظام۔ || اصطلاحات تعریف کړئ: نوع، ژوندۍ کره او ایکوسیسټم.
sol Model answer ## Species: a group of similar organisms that can interbreed and produce fertile offspring. Biosphere: the part of the earth where life exists (land, water and air). Ecosystem: a community of living organisms interacting with each other and with the non-living (abiotic) environment in an area.
q 2 | Differentiate between population and community. || آبادی اور کمیونٹی میں فرق کیجیے۔ || د نفوس او ټولنې ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## A population is a group of organisms of the same species living in the same area at the same time; a community is all the populations of different species living and interacting in one area.
q 3 | Differentiate between food chain and food web. || غذائی زنجیر اور غذائی جال میں فرق کیجیے۔ || د غذايي ځنځیر او غذايي جال ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## A food chain is a single, straight path of food and energy from producer to consumers (grass, grasshopper, frog, snake, eagle). A food web is a network of many interconnected food chains in an ecosystem, which gives organisms alternative food and makes the ecosystem stable.
q 4 | How does deforestation affect the environment? || جنگلات کی کٹائی ماحول کو کیسے متاثر کرتی ہے؟ || د ځنګلونو پرې کول چاپېریال څنګه اغېزمنوي؟
sol Model answer ## Deforestation reduces oxygen and raises $CO_2$ (global warming), causes soil erosion, floods and landslides, reduces rainfall, destroys the habitats of animals, and decreases biodiversity.
