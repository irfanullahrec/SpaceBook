q 5 | Distinguish between herbivore, carnivore, and omnivore. || سبزی خور، گوشت خور اور ہمہ خور میں فرق کیجیے۔ || د واښه خوړونکي، غوښه خوړونکي او هر څه خوړونکي ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Herbivores eat only plants (cow, goat); carnivores eat only other animals (lion, eagle); omnivores eat both plants and animals (human, crow, bear).
q 6 | Outline the role of bacteria in the nitrogen cycle. || نائٹروجن کے چکر میں بیکٹیریا کے کردار کا خاکہ پیش کیجیے۔ || د نایټروجن په دوره کې د باکتریاوو رول په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Nitrogen-fixing bacteria (Rhizobium in root nodules, Azotobacter) change atmospheric nitrogen into ammonia; nitrifying bacteria (Nitrosomonas, Nitrobacter) change ammonia into nitrites and nitrates that plants take up; decomposers return ammonia from dead matter; denitrifying bacteria return nitrogen to the air.
q 7 | How can overpopulation affect the environment? || آبادی میں حد سے زیادہ اضافہ ماحول کو کیسے متاثر کر سکتا ہے؟ || د نفوس ډېر زیاتوالی چاپېریال څنګه اغېزمنولای شي؟
sol Model answer ## Overpopulation increases the demand for food, water, land and energy, leads to deforestation, pollution, loss of biodiversity, waste problems, shortage of resources, global warming and spread of diseases.
q 8 | Name any five sources of water pollution. || آبی آلودگی کے کوئی پانچ ذرائع کے نام لکھیے۔ || د اوبو د ککړتیا هر پنځه سرچینې نوم کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Sources of water pollution: industrial wastes, sewage, fertilizers and pesticides from farms, oil spills, solid waste and detergents.
q 9 | Differentiate between parasitism and mutualism. || طفیلیت اور باہمی فائدے میں فرق کیجیے۔ || د پرازیتیزم او ګډې ګټې ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Parasitism: one organism (parasite) lives on or in another (host) and harms it (tapeworm in humans, lice). Mutualism: both organisms benefit (nitrogen-fixing bacteria in the roots of legumes, lichens).
q 10 | Define nitrogen fixation and nitrification. || نائٹروجن کے جکڑاؤ اور نائٹریفکیشن کی تعریف کیجیے۔ || د نایټروجن تثبیت او نایټریفیکېشن تعریف کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Nitrogen fixation is the conversion of atmospheric nitrogen into ammonia or nitrates by bacteria or lightning. Nitrification is the conversion of ammonia into nitrites and then nitrates by nitrifying bacteria.

q 1 | Describe the nitrogen cycle in detail. What would happen if the nitrogen cycle stopped working? || نائٹروجن کے چکر کو تفصیل سے بیان کیجیے۔ اگر نائٹروجن کا چکر کام کرنا چھوڑ دے تو کیا ہوگا؟ || د نایټروجن دوره په تفصیل سره بیان کړئ. که د نایټروجن دوره کار ودروي څه به وشي؟
sol Model answer ## Nitrogen gas is fixed into ammonia by bacteria (Rhizobium) and lightning; nitrifying bacteria change ammonia to nitrites and nitrates; plants take up nitrates and make proteins; animals get nitrogen by eating plants; when plants and animals die decomposers return ammonia (ammonification); denitrifying bacteria change nitrates back to nitrogen gas which returns to the atmosphere. If the cycle stopped, plants would lack nitrates, proteins could not be made and life would end.
q 2 | Compare the flow of materials and the flow of energy in the ecosystem. || ماحولیاتی نظام میں مادوں کے بہاؤ اور توانائی کے بہاؤ کا موازنہ کیجیے۔ || په ایکوسیسټم کې د موادو جریان او د انرژۍ جریان پرتله کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Materials (carbon, nitrogen, water) are recycled again and again between living things and the environment, while energy flows in one direction only, from the sun through producers to consumers and is lost as heat at each step; it is not recycled.
q 3 | Write a note on the energy flow through different components of an ecosystem. || ماحولیاتی نظام کے مختلف اجزا میں توانائی کے بہاؤ پر نوٹ لکھیے۔ || د ایکوسیسټم د بېلابېلو اجزاوو له لارې د انرژۍ په جریان یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Sunlight energy is trapped by producers in photosynthesis (about 1% fixed), passes to primary consumers, secondary and tertiary consumers, and decomposers. At each trophic level only about 10% passes on, the rest is lost as heat in respiration and in waste, so food chains are short and energy decreases at each step.
q 4 | What are the different ways through which organisms of the ecosystem interact with each other? || وہ مختلف طریقے کون سے ہیں جن کے ذریعے ماحولیاتی نظام کے جاندار ایک دوسرے سے تعامل کرتے ہیں؟ || هغه بېلابېلې لارې کومې دي چې د ایکوسیسټم موجودات پرې له یو بل سره متقابل عمل کوي؟
sol Model answer ## Organisms interact by predation (predator and prey), competition (for food, space), symbiosis (mutualism, commensalism, parasitism), and by feeding relationships in food chains.
q 5 | Discuss the biotic components of the ecosystem. || ماحولیاتی نظام کے حیاتیاتی اجزا پر بحث کیجیے۔ || د ایکوسیسټم ژوندي اجزا وڅېړئ.
sol Model answer ## The biotic components are producers (green plants and algae that make food by photosynthesis), consumers (herbivores, carnivores, omnivores) and decomposers (bacteria and fungi that break down dead matter and return nutrients to the soil).
q 6 | Write notes on the three types of symbiosis. || سمبیوسس کی تین اقسام پر نوٹ لکھیے۔ || د سمبیوسس په درې ډولونو یادښتونه ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Mutualism: both partners benefit (Rhizobium and legume roots; lichens). Commensalism: one benefits and the other is neither helped nor harmed (orchids growing on a tree; remora fish with a shark). Parasitism: one benefits and the host is harmed (tapeworm, ticks, mistletoe).
q 7 | Enlist the causes of water pollution and also describe how water pollution can be controlled. || آبی آلودگی کی وجوہات کی فہرست بنائیے اور یہ بھی بیان کیجیے کہ آبی آلودگی پر کیسے قابو پایا جا سکتا ہے۔ || د اوبو د ککړتیا لاملونه لېست کړئ او دا هم بیان کړئ چې د اوبو ککړتیا څنګه کنټرولېدای شي.
sol Model answer ## Causes: sewage, industrial wastes, fertilizers and pesticides from farms, oil spills, detergents, garbage and heat from industries. Control: treat sewage and industrial wastewater before disposal, reduce use of fertilizers and pesticides, stop dumping garbage in water, laws and awareness, use biodegradable detergents and plant trees along rivers.
q 8 | Write a comprehensive note on land pollution. || زمینی آلودگی پر جامع نوٹ لکھیے۔ || د ځمکې په ککړتیا یو جامع یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Land pollution is the contamination of soil by solid wastes, plastics, chemicals, fertilizers and pesticides, industrial and mining wastes and sewage. It reduces soil fertility, harms crops and animals, contaminates ground water and spreads diseases. Control: reduce, reuse and recycle, proper disposal, use of organic manure and bio-pesticides, and afforestation.
q 9 | Explain, by making diagrams, the pyramids of number and biomass. || شکلیں بنا کر تعداد اور بایوماس کے اہرام کی وضاحت کیجیے۔ || د انځورونو په جوړولو د شمېر او بایوماس هرمونه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A pyramid of numbers shows the number of organisms at each trophic level, with many producers at the base and few top carnivores at the top (an inverted pyramid occurs in a tree-insect-bird chain). A pyramid of biomass shows the total dry mass at each level, which decreases at each higher level. (Draw both as stacked bars with grass, rabbit and fox.)
q 10 | Write a note on the causes and effects of air pollution. || فضائی آلودگی کی وجوہات اور اثرات پر نوٹ لکھیے۔ || د هوا د ککړتیا د لاملونو او اغېزو په اړه یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Air pollution is caused by vehicle exhaust, factories, burning of fuels and wood, dust, smoking and burning of wastes. Effects: respiratory diseases, acid rain, smog, global warming, ozone depletion and damage to plants and buildings.

sol Model answer ## Competition for limited resources can lead to shortage of food, water, jobs and housing, poverty, conflict, migration and the spread of disease and pollution.

sol Model answer ## Pakistan's population grew from about 132 million (1998) to about 208 million (2017), an increase of about 57% (2.4% per year); this puts pressure on food, water, jobs, housing, health and education, and the environment.
