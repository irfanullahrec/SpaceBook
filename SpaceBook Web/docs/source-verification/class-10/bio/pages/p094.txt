q 2 | B. Write short answers to the following questions. || B. درج ذیل سوالات کے مختصر جوابات لکھیے۔ || B. لاندې پوښتنو ته لنډ ځوابونه ولیکئ.
q 1 | How can vegetative methods of reproduction, be used to achieve better yield? || تولید کے نباتاتی طریقے بہتر پیداوار حاصل کرنے کے لیے کیسے استعمال کیے جا سکتے ہیں؟ || د تکثر نباتي طریقې څنګه د ښه حاصل لپاره کارول کېدای شي؟
sol Model answer ## Vegetative propagation (cuttings, grafting, layering, bulbs, tubers) gives offspring identical to the parent plant with the desired qualities (disease resistance, high yield, good fruit), earlier flowering and fruiting and without depending on seeds, so farmers get a uniform, better crop.
q 2 | Differentiate between internal and external fertilization. Which type of fertilization will ensure better chances of fusion of gametes? || اندرونی اور بیرونی فرٹیلائزیشن میں فرق کیجیے۔ کون سی قسم کی فرٹیلائزیشن گیمیٹس کے ملاپ کے بہتر امکانات یقینی بنائے گی؟ || د دنني او بهرني القاح ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ. کوم ډول القاح به د ګامیټونو د یوځای کېدو ښه چانس ډاډمن کړي؟
sol Model answer ## In external fertilization the gametes fuse outside the body, in water (fish, frogs); in internal fertilization sperms are put into the female body and fusion takes place inside (reptiles, birds, mammals). Internal fertilization gives better chances of fusion because the gametes are close together and protected.
q 3 | What is population planning? Why is it important? || آبادی کی منصوبہ بندی کیا ہے؟ یہ کیوں اہم ہے؟ || د نفوس پلان جوړونه څه ده؟ ولې مهمه ده؟
sol Model answer ## Population planning is the control of the number of children born by choice (spacing and limiting births with family planning methods). It is important to keep population within the resources of food, water, housing, education and health, to improve the quality of life and to protect the environment and the health of mothers.
q 4 | How does self-pollination bring more variations in plants? || خود زیرگی پودوں میں زیادہ تغیرات کیسے لاتی ہے؟ || ځاني ګرده افشاني څنګه په بوټو کې ډېر توپیرونه راولي؟
sol Model answer ## In self-pollination the same plant gives both gametes, so it gives little variation; the statement in the question is not correct for most plants, but self-pollination can bring variations by mutation or when the plant is heterozygous and segregation of alleles takes place during meiosis, so that new combinations appear in the offspring.
q 5 | How can the spread of HIV be controlled? What is the role of the community and NGOs in the control of AIDS? || HIV کے پھیلاؤ کو کیسے کنٹرول کیا جا سکتا ہے؟ ایڈز کے کنٹرول میں معاشرے اور غیر سرکاری تنظیموں کا کیا کردار ہے؟ || د HIV خپرېدل څنګه کنټرولېدای شي؟ د ایډز په کنټرول کې د ټولنې او نادولتي موسسو رول څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## HIV spreads by unsafe sexual contact, infected blood and needles, and from an infected mother to the baby. It is controlled by using screened blood, disposable syringes, safe behaviour and testing; the community and NGOs give awareness and education, support the infected without discrimination, give counselling and free testing, and help with treatment.
q 6 | Name the four whorls present in a flower and also describe the components of each whorl. || پھول میں موجود چار دائروں کے نام بتائیے اور ہر دائرے کے اجزا بھی بیان کیجیے۔ || په ګل کې موجودې څلور حلقې ونوموئ او د هرې حلقې اجزا هم بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The four whorls from outside to inside: calyx (sepals, usually green, protect the bud), corolla (petals, coloured to attract insects), androecium (stamens, the male part, each with anther and filament) and gynoecium (carpels, the female part, with stigma, style and ovary containing ovules).
