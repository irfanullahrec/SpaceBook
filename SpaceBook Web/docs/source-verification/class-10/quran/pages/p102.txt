(B): And tell them about the guests of Ibrahim, when they came to him and said "Peace". (Ibrahim) said: "We feel afraid of you." (The guests) said: "Do not be afraid; we bring you good news of a knowledgeable son." Angels came to Ibrahim in human form and greeted him; he felt afraid of them, and they said: "Do not fear; we give you good news of a knowledgeable son (Ishaq)." He wondered at it because of his old age; they said they had given him good news in truth and he should not despair; he replied that none despairs of his Lord's mercy except the astray. Then the angels told him they had been sent to the people of Lut (with punishment). 2(A): Then when the messengers (angels) came to the family of Lut, (Lut) said: "You seem to be strangers." (B): So set out with your family in a part of the night, and follow behind them yourself; let none of you look back, and go where you are commanded." That is, to leave with his family in part of the night, walk behind them himself, let none look back, and go where commanded; by morning the root of that people would be cut off. 3(A): And the People of Al-Hijr certainly denied the messengers. And We gave them Our signs, but they kept turning away from them. (B): The Blast seized them in the morning, and their strong houses carved from the mountains and all they had earned availed them nothing. Lesson: turning away from Allah's signs and denying the messengers ends in ruin; worldly wealth cannot save anyone from Allah's punishment. || ۱(الف): او څه کینه چې د هغوی په سینو کې وي، موږ به یې وباسو؛ هغوی به ورونه شي او د (عزت او کرامت) پر تختونو به مخامخ ناست وي.
