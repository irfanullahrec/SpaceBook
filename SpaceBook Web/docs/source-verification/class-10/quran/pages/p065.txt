So agree on your plan, you and your partners; then let no aspect of your plan be hidden from you; then carry out what you will against me and give me no respite." (B): (1) Nuh admonished his people with Allah's verses and trusted in Allah; (2) he asked no reward for his call; (3) when his people denied him, Allah saved Nuh and the believers in the ship and made them successors in the land; (4) those who denied were drowned — the end of those warned was exemplary. 2(A): Then after them We sent Musa and Harun to Pharaoh and his chiefs with Our signs, but they were arrogant and were a criminal people. (B): (1) Allah brought the Children of Israel across the sea, and Pharaoh and his army pursued them in oppression and enmity; (2) while drowning, Pharaoh professed belief; (3) he was told: "Now?
