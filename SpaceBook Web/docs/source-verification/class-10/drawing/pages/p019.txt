p Now study some still life compositions in your sketchbook. Arrange simple still life. Consider the lighting and arrangement of the objects in particular. Observe the given examples. Remember, you don't have to draw from them. It is just to give you an idea of what you are supposed to do. Also note the difference in the styles of sketching in the examples.
