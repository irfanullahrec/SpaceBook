q B | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
qp (i) | Describe the four steps of evaluating an artwork. || فن پارے کو پرکھنے کے چار مراحل بیان کریں۔ || د هنري کار د ارزونې څلور پړاوونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The four steps of evaluating an artwork are: describe (what you see), analyse (how the elements and principles are used), interpret (what the artist means) and judge (decide its value with reasons).
qp (ii) | What is the difference between functional wood work and decorative woodwork. || کارآمد اور آرائشی لکڑی کے کام میں کیا فرق ہے؟ || د کاري او ښکلاییزې لرګي کارۍ ترمنځ توپیر څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## Functional woodwork is made for use (chairs, tables, doors, cupboards); decorative woodwork is made only for beauty (carved panels, frames, ornaments, wall hangings), though some items are both.
qp (iii) | Write notes on! I. Shahid sajjad ii. Rabia Zuberi iii. Makli Hills || نوٹ لکھیں: i. شاہد سجاد ii. رابعہ زبیری iii. مکلی کا پہاڑ || یادښتونه ولیکئ: i. شاهد سجاد ii. رابعه زبیري iii. د مکلي غونډۍ
sol Model answer ## (i) Shahid Sajjad (1936-2012) was a leading Pakistani sculptor known for abstract works in stone and marble. (ii) Rabia Zuberi (1948-2020) was a Pakistani artist, sculptor and teacher known for her work in stone and mixed media. (iii) Makli Hills near Thatta in Sindh is one of the world's largest necropolises (graveyards) with thousands of tombs showing fine stone carving and brick work from the 14th-18th centuries; it is a World Heritage Site.
