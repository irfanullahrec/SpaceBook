@deco Sequence of pencil sketches (artwork) building a head from a ball. A sphere is pierced by a pin as an axis; "middle line" and "brow line" bands are drawn around it; the sides are sliced off; the sphere is turned to different angles; and the jaw is added to make skull forms seen from the front, three-quarter and side views.
