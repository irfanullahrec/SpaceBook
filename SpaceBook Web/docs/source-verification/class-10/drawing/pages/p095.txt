qp vi | Describe the qualities of a good poster? || اچھے پوسٹر کی خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ || د ښه پوسټر ځانګړنې بیان کړئ.
sol i: A mosaic creates images or patterns from small pieces of coloured glass, stone or other materials; a collage is made by pasting bits of newspaper, cloth, coloured paper, parts of other artworks or photographs. ii: The technique of representing three-dimensional objects on a two-dimensional surface so that they look realistic, creating an illusion of space and depth. iii: The area or element of an artwork made most prominent through emphasis (contrast, isolation, location or unusualness), where the eye is drawn first. iv: An instrument (two cardboard L-shapes clipped into a frame) used to choose a good view or composition by framing part of a scene or picture. v: A drawing, painting or photograph of a person, specially showing the face. vi: It must be readable (clear, large fonts in good contrast to the background), well organized (using the elements and principles of design) and to the point (brief and prominent, since viewers give it only 11–15 seconds). ##
