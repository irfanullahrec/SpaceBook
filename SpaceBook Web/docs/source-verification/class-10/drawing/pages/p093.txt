@issue The "Value" entry runs two definitions together ("…of a colour Intensity. Purity of a colour."); it probably means Value = lightness or darkness of a colour and Intensity = purity of a colour. Kept as printed. Also kept as printed: "arround", and "Mosaic" defined as using "coloured paper" (the chapter text says glass, stone or other materials).
