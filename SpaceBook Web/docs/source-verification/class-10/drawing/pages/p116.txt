q B | Answer the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
qp Q.1 | What is the difference between strip and window mounting? || پٹی والی اور کھڑکی نما ماؤنٹنگ میں کیا فرق ہے؟ || د پټه ییز او کړکۍ ډوله نصب ترمنځ توپیر څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## Strip mounting: a thin strip of card or mount board is used as a border around the picture and the work is attached to it. Window mounting: a mount with a cut-out opening (window) in a board is placed over the picture so that only the picture is seen with a clean frame around it, and it protects the edges.
qp Q.2 | State five points to be kept in mind while displaying? || نمائش کرتے وقت ذہن میں رکھنے کے پانچ نکات بیان کریں۔ || د نندارې پر مهال پنځه هغه ټکي بیان کړئ چې باید په یاد وساتل شي.
sol Model answer ## Points for displaying: (1) arrange the pictures at eye level; (2) keep suitable space between them; (3) use good lighting without glare; (4) group works by theme or size; (5) label each work with title and artist's name and keep the display neat and safe.
qp Q.3 | What is the job of an interior designer? || داخلی ڈیزائنر کا کام کیا ہے؟ || د دننه ډیزاینر دنده څه ده؟
sol Model answer ## An interior designer plans and designs the inside of homes, offices and shops: arranging space, colours, furniture, lighting, curtains and decoration to make them useful, comfortable and attractive.
qp Q.4 | Would you like to become a professional artist? Why? Or why not? || کیا آپ پیشہ ور فنکار بننا چاہیں گے؟ کیوں؟ یا کیوں نہیں؟ || ایا غواړئ مسلکي هنرمند شئ؟ ولې؟ یا ولې نه؟
sol Model answer ## Open question. A model answer: Yes, I would like to become a professional artist because I enjoy creating things and can earn a living by painting, designing and teaching, and I can express ideas. (A student may also say no and give a reason such as another career choice.)
qp Q.5 | What is graphic designing? || گرافک ڈیزائننگ کیا ہے؟ || ګرافیک ډیزاین څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## Graphic design is the art of communicating ideas through visual elements such as text, images, colour and layout, as in logos, posters, books, magazines, websites and packaging.
qp Q.6 | What is advertising ? || اشتہار بازی کیا ہے؟ || اعلان څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## Advertising is promoting a product, service or idea to the public through posters, newspapers, radio, television and the internet so that people will buy or support it.
qp Q.7 | Write guideline for presentation of Art work! || فن پارے کی پیشکش کی رہنما ہدایات لکھیں! || د هنري کار د وړاندې کولو لارښوونې ولیکئ!
sol Model answer ## Guidelines for presentation: choose the best works, mount them neatly, label them with title, medium and name, arrange them at eye level in a logical order with enough space, use good lighting, keep the display clean and attractive and be ready to explain your work.
