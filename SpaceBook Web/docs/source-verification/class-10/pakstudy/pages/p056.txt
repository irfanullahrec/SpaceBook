But civil war broke out among the Afghan mujahideen. Pakistan made great efforts to end this civil war, but they were not fruitful, and in 1996 the Taliban set up a government in Kabul. At that time Pakistan was the first country to recognise the Taliban government and establish diplomatic relations. After the attacks of 11 September 2001 on the World Trade Center and the Pentagon in America, America and its allies attacked Afghanistan, ended the Taliban government and set up Hamid Karzai's government in its place. As always, Pakistan recognised Hamid Karzai's government too and established diplomatic relations with it. Thus Pakistan–Afghanistan relations have been set on good lines.
