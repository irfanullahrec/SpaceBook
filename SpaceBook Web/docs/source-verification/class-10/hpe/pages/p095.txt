@issue Kept as printed: the court is "59 فٹ (18 میٹر)" (18 m is 59 ft 1 in) and the service area is described as "15 cm behind the end line". The volleyball antenna is normally 1.8 m long and extends 80 cm above the net; the book's wording is kept.
