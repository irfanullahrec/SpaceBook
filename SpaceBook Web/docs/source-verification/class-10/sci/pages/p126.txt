## 10.2 NATURAL DISASTERS || 10.2 قدرتی آفات || ۱۰.۲ طبیعي پېښې
p Planet Earth at times encounters tremendous changes which occur deep inside it or on its surface. Such changes are called natural disasters. These have the capacity to bring significant changes in the ecological systems. Natural disaster produce such a huge force that it can cause damage to the shape of the land or to the lives of people and other living things living in it.
