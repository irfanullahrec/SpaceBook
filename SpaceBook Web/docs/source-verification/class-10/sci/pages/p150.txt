q C | Write in detail answers of the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات لکھیں۔ || د لاندې پوښتنو تفصیلي ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | What are the main causes of ozone depletion? List some of the major effects of ozone depletion on the living organisms. || اوزون کی کمی کی بڑی وجوہات کیا ہیں؟ جانداروں پر اوزون کی کمی کے چند بڑے اثرات کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د اوزون د کمښت اصلي لاملونه کوم دي؟ پر ژوندیو موجوداتو د اوزون د کمښت ځینې لویې اغېزې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Causes: CFCs from refrigerators, air conditioners, aerosols and foams, halons from fire extinguishers, nitrogen oxides from jets and carbon tetrachloride. Effects: more ultraviolet radiation reaches the earth causing skin cancer, cataracts and weak immunity in humans, damage to plants and crops, harm to plankton and aquatic life, and global warming.
qp 2 | Describe global warming and its consequences. || عالمی حدت اور اس کے نتائج بیان کریں۔ || نړیواله تودوخه او پایلې یې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Global warming is the rise of the earth's average temperature caused by greenhouse gases ($CO_2$, methane, $N_2O$, CFCs) from burning fossil fuels, deforestation and farming that trap heat. Consequences: melting of glaciers and ice caps, rise in sea level, floods and droughts, heat waves, damage to crops, loss of species and spread of diseases.
qp 3 | How acid rain is caused? What impacts this rain can have on the life of living organisms of a particular region? || تیزابی بارش کیسے ہوتی ہے؟ یہ بارش کسی خاص علاقے کے جانداروں کی زندگی پر کیا اثرات ڈال سکتی ہے؟ || تېزابي باران څنګه رامنځته کېږي؟ دا باران د یوې ځانګړې سیمې د ژوندیو موجوداتو پر ژوند کومې اغېزې لرلی شي؟
sol Model answer ## Acid rain is caused when sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides from factories, power plants and vehicles dissolve in rain to form sulphuric and nitric acids. It damages forests and crops, makes lakes acidic so fish die, corrodes buildings, statues and metals, harms soil and causes human health problems.
qp 4 | What do you mean by Climate change? Describe some of its effects. || موسمیاتی تبدیلی سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ اس کے چند اثرات بیان کریں۔ || له اقلیمي بدلون څخه څه پوهېږئ؟ ځینې اغېزې یې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Climate change is a long-term change in the average weather of the earth. Effects: higher temperatures, melting glaciers, rising seas, changed rainfall, more floods, droughts, storms and heat waves, loss of biodiversity, shortage of food and water and spread of disease.
qp 5 | Describe natural disasters caused by El-Nino and La-Nina. || ال نینو اور لا نینا سے ہونے والی قدرتی آفات بیان کریں۔ || د ال نینو او لا نینا له امله طبیعي پېښې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## El Nino is the warming of the Pacific Ocean near the equator, which brings droughts to some areas (Australia, South Asia) and floods to others (South America). La Nina is the cooling of this water, which brings heavy rain and floods (as in Pakistan in some years) and cold winters. They cause crop loss, floods, droughts and forest fires.
qp 6 | Identify the legislation or laws on environmental problems. || ماحولیاتی مسائل سے متعلق قانون سازی یا قوانین کی نشاندہی کریں۔ || د چاپېریالي ستونزو په اړه قانون جوړونه یا قوانین وپېژنئ.
sol Model answer ## Laws and agreements for the environment include the Pakistan Environmental Protection Act 1997 and the Environmental Protection Agencies, the Montreal Protocol (ozone), the Kyoto Protocol and Paris Agreement (climate change), and the Basel Convention (hazardous waste).
qp 7 | Describe the control strategies that can be used for treating air pollutants. || فضائی آلودگیوں کے علاج کے لیے استعمال ہونے والی کنٹرول حکمتِ عملیاں بیان کریں۔ || د هوا د ککړوونکو د درملنې لپاره د کنټرول تګلارې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Air pollutants can be controlled by fitting filters and scrubbers in chimneys, catalytic converters on vehicles, using clean fuels (CNG, unleaded petrol), renewable energy, planting trees, proper waste disposal, public transport, vehicle checks and laws on emissions.
qp 8 | Describe the harmful effects of the excessive use of Television on individual's health. || ٹیلی وژن کے حد سے زیادہ استعمال کے انفرادی صحت پر نقصان دہ اثرات بیان کریں۔ || د ټلویزیون د ډېرې کارونې زیانمنې اغېزې د فرد پر روغتیا بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Too much television causes eye strain and headaches, obesity from sitting, poor sleep, weak studies, neck and back pain, less physical activity and social contact, and aggression from violent programmes.
