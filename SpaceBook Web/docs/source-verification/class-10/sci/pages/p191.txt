qp 1 | What are ultrasound waves? Why these waves are less harmful than X-rays. || الٹراساؤنڈ لہریں کیا ہیں؟ یہ لہریں ایکس ریز سے کم نقصان دہ کیوں ہیں؟ || الټراساونډ څپې څه دي؟ دا څپې ولې تر اکسرې لږ زیانمنې دي؟
sol Model answer ## Ultrasound waves are sound waves of frequency above 20,000 Hz that cannot be heard. They reflect from the boundaries of body organs and give images (sonography) of the heart, womb and liver. They are less harmful than X-rays because they are not ionizing: they do not damage cells or cause cancer and are safe even for the unborn baby.
