- Plastic is used in various daily use products. Plenty of plastic waste is generated on daily basis in almost every home. ### 3. Disposal of wastes in land based disposal areas || 3. زمینی تلفی کے علاقوں میں فضلے کو ٹھکانے لگانا || ۳. د ځمکني لرې کولو په ځایونو کې د کثافاتو اچول
p One of the easiest way for the disposal of hazardous wastes is to throw them in a landfill, waste pile, injection well or other land based disposal areas. However, this disposal method must be supported by proper precautions and necessary regulations which can help protect the people living adjacent to it.
