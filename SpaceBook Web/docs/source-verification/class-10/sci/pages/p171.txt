## 11.3 INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (IT) || 11.3 انفارمیشن ٹیکنالوجی (آئی ٹی) || ۱۱.۳ معلوماتي ټکنالوژي (IT)
p The rapid advancements in electronics have provided new tools such as television, computer, internet, telephone, satellites etc. Our society is changing due to developments in information technology. While watching TV or reading a newspaper or magazine, it is impossible to miss coverage on seemingly diverse topic related to information technology, internet, World Wide Web (WWW), digital TV, online learning, corporate restructuring, wireless communication, medicine and so on. IT is based on the hardware and software of a computer and the telecommunication infrastructure.
