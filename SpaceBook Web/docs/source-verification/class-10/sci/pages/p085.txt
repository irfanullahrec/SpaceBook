q C | Write detail answers of the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات لکھیں۔ || د لاندې پوښتنو تفصیلي ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Describe the structure and function of nucleus. || مرکزے کی ساخت اور افعال بیان کریں۔ || د هستې جوړښت او دندې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The nucleus is the control centre of the cell, surrounded by a double nuclear envelope with pores. It contains chromatin (DNA with histones, forming chromosomes in division), the nucleolus (makes ribosomal RNA) and nucleoplasm. Function: it controls the activities of the cell, stores hereditary information and passes it to new cells in division, and makes mRNA and ribosomes.
qp 2 | Describe the importance of hereditary material found in nucleus. || مرکزے میں پائے جانے والے موروثی مادے کی اہمیت بیان کریں۔ || په هسته کې د موندل شوو ارثي موادو اهمیت بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The hereditary material (DNA) in the nucleus carries the genes that control all the characteristics of an organism; it passes them from parents to offspring, directs the making of proteins and enzymes, and controls the growth, repair and working of the cell.
qp 3 | Identify the relationships among nucleic acids (DNA and RNA), genes and chromosomes. || نیوکلیک ایسڈز (DNA اور RNA)، جینز اور کروموسومز کے درمیان تعلق کی نشاندہی کریں۔ || د نوکلیک اسیدونو (DNA او RNA)، جینونو او کروموزومونو ترمنځ اړیکې وپېژنئ.
sol Model answer ## DNA is a long molecule; a gene is a part of DNA that codes for a protein; a chromosome is a structure of DNA tightly coiled with histone proteins, so each chromosome has many genes. RNA is made from DNA (transcription) and carries the instruction to the ribosomes to make proteins.
qp 4 | Describe the molecular basis of heredity. || وراثت کی سالماتی بنیاد بیان کریں۔ || د وراثت مالیکولي بنسټ بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The molecular basis of heredity is DNA: it is a double helix of two strands of nucleotides held by bases (A pairs with T and G with C). The sequence of bases forms the genetic code in triplets (codons) for amino acids; DNA is copied exactly in replication and passed on, and it is used to make proteins through RNA, so parents' traits are passed to children.
qp 5 | Give an overview of the process of DNA replication. || DNA کی نقل سازی کے عمل کا جائزہ پیش کریں۔ || د DNA د تکثیر د بهیر عمومي کتنه وړاندې کړئ.
sol Model answer ## DNA replication is semi-conservative: the double helix unwinds and unzips by the enzyme helicase; each old strand acts as a template and new nucleotides pair with it (A-T, G-C) by DNA polymerase, so two identical DNA molecules form, each having one old and one new strand.
qp 6 | Describe the process of mitosis in detail. || مائٹوسس کے عمل کو تفصیل سے بیان کریں۔ || د مایټوسس بهیر په تفصیل بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Mitosis gives two identical daughter cells with the same number of chromosomes (growth and repair). Prophase: chromosomes condense, the nuclear envelope breaks down, spindle forms. Metaphase: chromosomes line up at the equator. Anaphase: the sister chromatids separate and move to opposite poles. Telophase: nuclear envelopes form again and chromosomes uncoil. Cytokinesis then divides the cytoplasm.
qp 7 | Describe the process of meiosis in detail. || میاسس کے عمل کو تفصیل سے بیان کریں۔ || د مایوسس بهیر په تفصیل بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Meiosis forms four haploid gametes in two divisions. Meiosis I: homologous chromosomes pair (prophase I with crossing over), line up, and separate (anaphase I), giving two cells with half the number of chromosomes. Meiosis II: the chromatids separate as in mitosis, giving four haploid cells. It halves the chromosome number and gives variation.
qp 8 | Explain thalassemia and Down's syndrome as common genetic disorders. || تھیلیسیمیا اور ڈاؤن سنڈروم کی عام جینیاتی عوارض کے طور پر وضاحت کریں۔ || تالاسیمیا او ډاون سنډروم د عامو جنتیکي اختلالونو په توګه روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Thalassemia is an inherited blood disorder in which the body makes abnormal or too little haemoglobin; the patient suffers from anaemia and needs frequent blood transfusions. Down's syndrome is caused by an extra copy of chromosome 21 (trisomy 21; 47 chromosomes) and causes mental disability and typical facial features and heart problems.
qp 9 | Analyze the role of genetic engineering and biotechnology in the fields of agriculture and food processing. || زراعت اور خوراک کی پروسیسنگ کے شعبوں میں جینیاتی انجینئرنگ اور بائیو ٹیکنالوجی کے کردار کا تجزیہ کریں۔ || د کرنې او خوړو د پروسس په برخو کې د جنتیکي انجنیرۍ او بایوټکنالوژۍ رول وشنئ.
sol Model answer ## In agriculture genetic engineering gives crops that resist insects, disease and herbicides (Bt cotton), tolerate drought and salt, give higher yield and better nutrition (golden rice). In food processing, microbes and enzymes are used for yogurt, cheese, bread, juices and sweeteners, and food can be preserved longer; biotechnology also gives single cell protein.
