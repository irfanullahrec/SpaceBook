### 7.1.1 SCIENTIFIC NOMENCLATURE AND COMMON CONSUMER PRODUCTS || 7.1.1 سائنسی نام اور عام صارف مصنوعات || ۷.۱.۱ علمي نومونه او عام مصرفي محصولات
p Different chemical compounds have scientific names based upon their chemical structure. Chemical nomenclature or scientific nomenclature is the term given to the naming of chemical compounds. Often, these names are complicated, long and understandable only to those who have background knowledge of chemistry. Commonly used chemical compounds may have several names. For example, Baking soda is white substance usually available in powder form. It is one of the important edible items used in every kitchen. Chemical name of this compound is sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃).
