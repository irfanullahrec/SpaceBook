qp 1 | What are the basic characteristics of static charges? || ساکن چارجز کی بنیادی خصوصیات کیا ہیں؟ || د ساکنو چارجونو بنسټیزې ځانګړنې کومې دي؟
sol Model answer ## Static charges stay at rest on an insulator; they are produced by friction, conduction or induction; there are two kinds, positive and negative; like charges repel and unlike charges attract; a charge accumulates on the surface (sharp points leak it most); and charge is conserved and quantised, measured in coulombs.
qp 2 | Describe the effects of static charges. || ساکن چارجز کے اثرات بیان کریں۔ || د ساکنو چارجونو اغېزې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Static charge makes hair stand up after combing, attracts small pieces of paper, causes sparks and shocks (a car door or a carpet), and lightning in clouds. It can start a fire where there is petrol vapour, damage electronic parts, and makes dust stick to surfaces and clothes cling.
qp 3 | What is meant by resistance? How resistors are connected in a circuit? || مزاحمت سے کیا مراد ہے؟ سرکٹ میں ریزسٹرز کیسے جوڑے جاتے ہیں؟ || له مقاومت څه مراد دی؟ په سرکټ کې مقاومتونه څنګه نښلول کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Resistance is the opposition a material offers to the flow of electric current, $R = V/I$, measured in ohms. Resistors can be connected in series (end to end: the same current, $R = R_1+R_2+\dots$) or in parallel (across the same two points: the same voltage, $1/R = 1/R_1+1/R_2+\dots$).
qp 4 | Describe the practical applications of static electricity. || ساکن بجلی کے عملی استعمالات بیان کریں۔ || د ساکنې برېښنا عملي کارونې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Practical uses: photocopiers and laser printers, electrostatic paint spraying, electrostatic precipitators that remove dust and smoke from chimneys, spraying of pesticides, removal of dust from air, and lightning conductors that protect buildings.
qp 5 | Write about practical uses of current electricity in household appliances? || گھریلو آلات میں کرنٹ بجلی کے عملی استعمالات کے بارے میں لکھیں۔ || په کورنیو وسایلو کې د جریاني برېښنا د عملي کارونو په اړه ولیکئ.

qp 6 | What safety measures should be adopted to avoid hazards of electricity? || بجلی کے خطرات سے بچنے کے لیے کون سی حفاظتی تدابیر اختیار کرنی چاہئیں؟ || د برېښنا له خطرونو د مخنیوي لپاره کوم خوندیتوب اقدامات باید وشي؟
sol Model answer ## Use fuses and circuit breakers; earth all metal appliances; never touch switches or appliances with wet hands; keep wires insulated and replace damaged ones; do not overload sockets; keep children away from sockets; switch off the mains before repair; and use proper plugs and lightning conductors.
