qp 10 | What is meant by polarization of charge? || چارج کی پولرائزیشن سے کیا مراد ہے؟ || د چارج له قطبي کېدو څه مراد دی؟
sol 1: To carry static charge from the tanker's body to the ground; charge built up by friction could otherwise cause a spark, fire or explosion. 2: Charging a neutral conductor, without touching it, by bringing a charged body near it: the near side gets the opposite charge and the far side a like charge. 3: A fuse is a thin wire of low melting point in the live wire, in series with the supply; if current exceeds the limit it melts and breaks the circuit, protecting wiring and appliances from damage and fire. 4: A circuit is the path for the flow of current; a closed circuit has a complete path (switch on, current flows); an open circuit has a broken path (switch off or supply disconnected, no current). 5: A switch that automatically turns the circuit off when the current exceeds a set value; it can be reset, so it protects like a fuse without replacement. 6: The flow of charges (electrons or ions) through a conductor; direct current (one direction) and alternating current (changes direction regularly, 100 times a second in homes). 7: Pulling the cap transfers electrons from the cap to the hair; all the hairs get the same charge and repel each other. 8: Lightning takes the shortest path to the ground; it strikes the rod and is carried by the wire safely to the earth instead of through the building. 9: Charges produced on objects by rubbing or by pressing and pulling apart; they stay on the rubbed area and cannot move to other parts, so they are static. 10: Near a charged object the centres of positive and negative charge in neutral molecules shift slightly,
