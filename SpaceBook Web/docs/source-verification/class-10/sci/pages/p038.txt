p Human hairs have natural colour and shades. From black to brown different types of colours are present. Although hair are mainly composed of a protein called keratin, the natural colour of hair depends on the ratio and quantities of two other proteins, phaeomelanin and eumelanin. Phaeomelanin controls the expression of golden blond, ginger, and red colors while eumelanin is responsible for brown to black hair shades. If one of these two proteins is lacking, hair colour turns white/grey.
