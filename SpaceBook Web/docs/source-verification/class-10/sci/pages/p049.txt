qp 1 | What do you mean by the term scientific nomenclature? Explain it with examples. || سائنسی نام دہی کی اصطلاح سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ مثالوں سے واضح کریں۔ || د علمي نوم ایښودنې له اصطلاح څه پوهېږئ؟ د بېلګو سره یې روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Scientific nomenclature is the system of naming compounds according to the rules of IUPAC so that each compound has one unique name worldwide. Examples: $NaCl$ is sodium chloride; $CH_3OH$ is methanol; $H_2SO_4$ is sulphuric acid; $CuSO_4$ is copper sulphate; $C_2H_5OH$ is ethanol.
qp 2 | How soap is prepared from oil and sodium hydroxide? || تیل اور سوڈیم ہائیڈرو آکسائیڈ سے صابن کیسے تیار کیا جاتا ہے؟ || له تېلو او سوډیم هایدروکساید څخه صابون څنګه جوړېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Soap is made by saponification: an oil or fat is heated with sodium hydroxide solution. The oil is hydrolysed into soap (sodium salt of fatty acids) and glycerol: fat + 3NaOH → 3 soap + glycerol. Common salt is added to separate the soap (salting out), which is then filtered, washed and moulded.

qp 4 | List the methods of chemical disposal and briefly describe each method. || کیمیائی فضلہ ٹھکانے لگانے کے طریقوں کی فہرست بنائیں اور ہر طریقہ مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || د کیمیاوي کثافاتو د لرې کولو طریقې ولیکئ او هره یوه په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Methods of disposal: (1) Landfill: waste is buried in specially lined pits; (2) Incineration: waste is burnt at high temperature to reduce its volume, with gases treated; (3) Neutralization: acids and alkalis are neutralized before release; (4) Recycling and reuse: the useful material is recovered; (5) Chemical treatment: harmful chemicals are changed into harmless ones; (6) Deep-well injection and storage for hazardous waste.
qp 5 | Describe how old tyres can be recycled and reused. || بیان کریں کہ پرانے ٹائروں کو کیسے ری سائیکل اور دوبارہ استعمال کیا جا سکتا ہے۔ || بیان کړئ چې زاړه ټایرونه څنګه بیا جوړېدای او بیا کارېدای شي.
sol Model answer ## Old tyres are cut into pieces and crushed to make rubber crumb that is used for road surfaces, playground floors, mats and shoe soles; they can be burnt as fuel in cement kilns, treated by pyrolysis to give oil and carbon black, or reused as swings, boats fenders and retaining walls; this reduces waste and pollution.
qp 6 | Explain the impacts and safety consequences of chemical disposal in Pakistan. || پاکستان میں کیمیائی فضلہ ٹھکانے لگانے کے اثرات اور حفاظتی نتائج کی وضاحت کریں۔ || په پاکستان کې د کیمیاوي کثافاتو د لرې کولو اغېزې او خوندیتوب پایلې روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer ## In Pakistan, factory wastes, hospital waste and chemicals are often dumped in rivers, drains and open land, which pollutes drinking water, soil and food, causes skin, stomach and cancer diseases, kills fish and plants, and spreads toxic fumes when burnt. Safe disposal needs laws, treatment plants, trained workers and public awareness.
