p Optical fibers are used for the transmission of internet data, especially graphical data. They are also used for the transmission of broadband signals, such as high-definition television (HDTV) telecasts. For this reason cable television companies also use fiber for delivery of digital video and data services. Telemedicine devices are used for the transmission of digital diagnostic images used by the doctors to diagnose and treat the patients effectively.
