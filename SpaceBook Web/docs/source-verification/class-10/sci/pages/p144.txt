p Excessive use of computer can cause some serious health problems, for example, headache and backache, pain in hand and fingers joints, vision abnormalities and eye strains. The implications for growing children are even more serious. For example, sitting for longer periods can delay physical as well as mental development of the child. Sitting in room with no social interaction with the society can hinder the interpersonal skills of the child.
