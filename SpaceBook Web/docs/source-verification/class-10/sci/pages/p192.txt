qp 3 | Describe the principle of Lasers and its uses in different fields with examples. || لیزر کا اصول اور مثالوں کے ساتھ مختلف شعبوں میں اس کے استعمالات بیان کریں۔ || د لېزر اصل او په بېلابېلو برخو کې یې کارونې د بېلګو سره بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## LASER stands for Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation: atoms are excited and emit photons of the same wavelength and phase which are amplified between two mirrors to give a narrow, intense, coherent, monochromatic beam. Uses: eye and other surgery, cutting and welding metals, barcode readers, CD/DVD players, communication through optical fibres, measuring distances and in printers.
qp 4 | Describe the functions and uses of ECG. || ای سی جی کے افعال اور استعمالات بیان کریں۔ || د ECG دندې او کارونې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The electrocardiograph (ECG) records the electrical signals produced by the heart through electrodes on the chest, arms and legs. Uses: to diagnose irregular heartbeat, heart attack, enlargement and other heart diseases and to monitor the patient.
qp 5 | Describe the functions and uses of EEG. || ای ای جی کے افعال اور استعمالات بیان کریں۔ || د EEG دندې او کارونې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The electroencephalograph (EEG) records the electrical activity of the brain through electrodes on the scalp. Uses: to diagnose epilepsy, brain tumours, injuries and sleep disorders, and to confirm brain death.
qp 6 | Describe the main components of a computer. || کمپیوٹر کے بڑے اجزاء بیان کریں۔ || د کمپیوټر اصلي برخې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## The main components of a computer are the input devices (keyboard, mouse), the CPU (control unit and ALU) that processes data, memory (RAM, ROM) and storage (hard disk), and the output devices (monitor, printer); the software (operating system and programs) tells the hardware what to do.
qp 7 | Explain the contribution SUPARCO has made in the development of our country and list their future plans. || ہمارے ملک کی ترقی میں سپارکو کی خدمات کی وضاحت کریں اور اس کے مستقبل کے منصوبوں کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || زموږ د هېواد په پرمختګ کې د سپارکو ونډه روښانه کړئ او راتلونکي پلانونه یې ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## SUPARCO (Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission) has launched Pakistani satellites (Badr-1, Paksat-1R, PakSat-MM1, remote sensing and communication satellites), helps weather forecasting, flood and crop monitoring, mapping and communication. Its future plans include more earth-observation and communication satellites and a Pakistani astronaut flight with China.
qp 8 | Write a note on the working of a telephone. || ٹیلی فون کے کام کرنے پر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || د ټیلیفون د کار په اړه یادښت ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## When a person speaks into the transmitter (microphone) the sound is changed into electrical signals that travel through wires or the mobile network to the receiver at the other end, where the receiver (speaker) changes the signals back into sound. The exchange connects the two telephones; a mobile phone uses radio waves.
qp 9 | What is the role of information technology in our society? How it has revolutionized the human life? || ہمارے معاشرے میں انفارمیشن ٹیکنالوجی کا کیا کردار ہے؟ اس نے انسانی زندگی میں کیسے انقلاب برپا کیا ہے؟ || زموږ په ټولنه کې د معلوماتي ټکنالوژۍ رول څه دی؟ د انسان ژوند یې څنګه بدل کړی؟
sol Model answer ## Information technology helps communication (internet, e-mail, mobile phones), education (e-learning), business and banking (online shopping, e-banking), medicine, entertainment, government services and research, saving time and bringing the world together; it has revolutionized human life by making work faster and information available everywhere.
