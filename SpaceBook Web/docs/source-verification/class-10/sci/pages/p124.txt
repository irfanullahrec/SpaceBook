p In the absence of greenhouse effect the temperature of Earth would have gone low enough to make life difficult. Global temperatures in the past one hundred years have been increasing more rapidly than the historic record shows and this increase has been attributed to increasing amounts of the greenhouse gases which are trapping heat. p In addition to a rise in average global temperature, there have been other changes that indicate that climate indeed is changing.
