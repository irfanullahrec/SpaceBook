q C | Write in detail answers of the following questions. || درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جوابات لکھیں۔ || د لاندې پوښتنو تفصیلي ځوابونه ولیکئ.
qp 1 | Identify the amount of fresh water available on Earth with an emphasis on Pakistan. || زمین پر دستیاب میٹھے پانی کی مقدار کی نشاندہی کریں، پاکستان پر خصوصی توجہ کے ساتھ۔ || په ځمکه د شته خوږو اوبو مقدار وپېژنئ، په پاکستان ټینګار سره.
sol Model answer ## About 97% of the earth's water is salty sea water and only about 3% is fresh; most of the fresh water is frozen in glaciers and ice caps, so less than 1% is available in rivers, lakes and ground water. Pakistan's water comes mainly from the Indus river system and its glaciers, rainfall and ground water, with a large share used in agriculture.
qp 2 | The level of agricultural production is directly related to the availability and effective use of water as a major input. Justify the statement. || زرعی پیداوار کی سطح بنیادی جزو کے طور پر پانی کی دستیابی اور مؤثر استعمال سے براہِ راست وابستہ ہے۔ اس بیان کی توجیہ کریں۔ || د کرنیز تولید کچه د یوه لوی توکي په توګه د اوبو له شتون او اغېزمنې کارونې سره مستقیمه تړلې ده. دا وینا ثابته کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Crops need water for growth, photosynthesis and transport of nutrients, and most of Pakistan is dry, so agriculture depends on irrigation from canals, tube wells and rainfall. Where water is enough and used efficiently (drip, sprinkler) the yield rises, and shortage of water reduces the production, so production is directly related to water.
qp 3 | Describe the existing ways of utilization of water resources in Pakistan. || پاکستان میں آبی وسائل کے استعمال کے موجودہ طریقے بیان کریں۔ || په پاکستان کې د اوبو سرچینو د کارونې اوسنۍ لارې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## In Pakistan water is used mostly for irrigation (about 90%) through the large canal system of the Indus basin, then for drinking and domestic use, industry, and hydropower and power generation from dams (Tarbela, Mangla), also for fisheries and navigation.
qp 4 | Explain the issues and problems Pakistan is facing in utilizing the water resources effectively for its people. || پاکستان کو اپنے عوام کے لیے آبی وسائل کے مؤثر استعمال میں جن مسائل اور مشکلات کا سامنا ہے ان کی وضاحت کریں۔ || پاکستان د خپلو خلکو لپاره د اوبو سرچینو په اغېزمنه کارونه کې له کومو مسلو او ستونزو سره مخ دی، روښانه یې کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Problems: wastage in irrigation (seepage and unlined canals), water-logging and salinity, falling ground water, pollution of water, lack of dams and storage, climate change and glacier melt, population growth, unequal distribution among provinces and lack of awareness.
qp 5 | Explain the methods of reclamation of water. || پانی کی بحالی کے طریقوں کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د اوبو د بیا ترلاسه کولو طریقې روښانه کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Reclamation of water: treatment and reuse of waste water, desalination of sea water, rain-water harvesting, recharging ground water, filtering and chlorination, drainage of waterlogged land, and use of treated water for gardens and industry.
qp 6 | Write an essay on conservation of water. || پانی کے تحفظ پر مضمون لکھیں۔ || د اوبو د ساتنې په اړه مقاله ولیکئ.
sol Model answer ## Water conservation means using water wisely. Water is vital for life, and only a little is fresh. We can save it by repairing leaks, closing taps, using a bucket instead of a hose, drip irrigation, rain-water harvesting, reuse of waste water, building dams, preventing pollution and teaching people; saving water today ensures supply for future generations.
qp 7 | Explain how water resources are being threatened by urbanization and deforestation. || وضاحت کریں کہ شہر کاری اور جنگلات کی کٹائی سے آبی وسائل کو کس طرح خطرہ لاحق ہے۔ || روښانه کړئ چې د ښاري کېدو او ځنګلونو د پرې کولو له امله د اوبو سرچینې څنګه ګواښل کېږي.
sol Model answer ## Urbanization covers land with roads and buildings so rain does not soak in to refill ground water, and cities pollute water with sewage and wastes and use huge amounts. Deforestation reduces rainfall and ground water recharge, increases run-off, floods and soil erosion that fills reservoirs with silt, so water supply is threatened.
qp 8 | Describe the implications of growing demand of water in the world. || دنیا میں پانی کی بڑھتی ہوئی طلب کے اثرات بیان کریں۔ || په نړۍ کې د اوبو د زیاتېدونکې غوښتنې اغېزې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## With growth of population, industry and farming the demand for water rises faster than the supply, causing scarcity, falling water tables, drying rivers and lakes, conflict between countries and regions, higher prices, food shortage, disease from unsafe water and damage to the environment.
