qp 7 | What do you mean by genetic disorders? || جینیاتی عوارض سے آپ کیا سمجھتے ہیں؟ || له جنتیکي اختلالونو څخه څه پوهېږئ؟
sol 1: Nuclear pores provide a passage for exchange of materials, such as proteins and ribonucleic acids (RNA), between the nucleus and the cytoplasm. 2: Stores hereditary material (genes); forms mRNA and ribosomes for protein synthesis; exchanges hereditary molecules with the rest of the cell; during cell division its chromatin forms chromosomes that pass equally to daughter cells. 3: mRNA carries DNA's instructions for making a protein; tRNA brings amino acids to the site of protein synthesis; rRNA in ribosomes works with tRNA to translate mRNA into a polypeptide (transcription and translation). 4: Adenine, guanine, thymine and cytosine. 5: The resting phase between two mitotic divisions, in which the nucleus and cytoplasm are active and the nucleus prepares for division. 6: It brings about growth, keeps the chromosome number constant, passes hereditary characters equally to daughter cells, and helps heal wounds and form new cells. 7: Disorders transmitted from parents to offspring through genes or chromosomes, e.g. thalassemia, Down's syndrome and sickle cell anaemia. ##
