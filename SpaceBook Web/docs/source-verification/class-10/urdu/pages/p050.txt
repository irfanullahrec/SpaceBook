The neighbour lady thought, what claim has the poor woman on the boys? If only there had been a daughter, she would have gone with her; and wiping her eyes and nose she went to her in the other room. Now silence and the darkness of a winter evening were descending on the room; the sparrow again began to fly everywhere and strike the ventilator glass.
