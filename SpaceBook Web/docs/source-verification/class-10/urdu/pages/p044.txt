Pouring tea into the cups, the Begum thought how long she had wanted to buy a tea trolley but could not save enough change; her husband complained about rising prices and forgot to give the daily household money. Everyone busied themselves with tea and samosas. Suddenly a sparrow struck the ventilator glass; while taking down the cobwebs the gap in the ventilator had probably been closed, and the sparrow had come in and could not get out. Her husband began to prime the aunt: "Ask her today…"; the Begum broke in, "If only you had ever asked!" The aunt took offence: "We come thinking of you as our own." The Begum wrapped an old warm shawl around herself. Her husband's voice rose: "What have I not asked you? What don't you have? Have I left you sitting in the street? Don't you get food, clothes, medicine?" The neighbour lady's husband said, "Yes, yes, daughter, say what you have to say."
