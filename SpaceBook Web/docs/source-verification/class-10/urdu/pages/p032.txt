He has no topic of conversation except the illnesses of his wife and children. However hard I try, he does not come out of that subject. If I mention the weather he says, "Yes, the weather is very bad. My youngest child has got a fever. My middle daughter is suffering from a cough." If I start a conversation about literature, Tahseen Sahib at once apologises that, brother, the whole household is ill these days; where would I find the time to read the newspaper?
