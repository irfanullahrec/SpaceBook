It is a beautiful picture of Turkish architecture and refinement. Attached to it is a large library with the biggest collection of manuscripts — an estimated hundred thousand handwritten books arranged in excellent order. Among Istanbul's museums Topkapı is world-famous: it holds thousands of precious objects from the Roman, Byzantine and Ottoman periods, relics of the Ottoman sultans, jewels, garments and ornaments, as well as Chinese, Japanese, Arab and European curiosities. Besides a museum of antiquities, Topkapı has a military museum, "Awqaf", with amazing examples of Islamic Turkish art, literature, design and painting. The Ottoman rulers played a prominent role in advancing Islamic sciences, arts and culture; through their love of learning Topkapı became a treasury of rarities and rare books, including some of which only one copy exists in the world. As examples of calligraphy, Topkapı holds Quranic verses written by famous calligraphers, and an important Turkish manuscript on the life of the Prophet ﷺ.
