Maulvi Sahib came to Delhi and rented Dr Ansari's mansion in Darya Ganj. At that time Dr Ansari's mansion was the largest in Darya Ganj. It was a very historic mansion: as long as Dr Ansari lived, all the great leaders of the Congress gathered there for consultations. Maulvi Sahib made this very mansion the stronghold of Urdu and moved the office of the Anjuman into it. The people of Delhi gave Maulvi Sahib a grand welcome and brought him in a procession from the station to the mansion. This place too was temporary for the Anjuman's office. Maulvi Sahib had applied for a very large plot of land at a peaceful spot in New Delhi, and later he got it. The plan was to build a splendid building for the Anjuman on it, but the Anjuman did not have enough money to build it.
