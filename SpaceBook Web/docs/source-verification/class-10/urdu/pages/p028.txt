# مجھے میرے دوستوں سے بچاؤ || Save me from my friends || ما زما له ملګرو وژغورئ

After a pause of two or three minutes this speech full of pain was repeated in the same words and the same manner. This style struck me as so special that I stopped to look at the man and listen to his words. The beggar was tall, his body well-fed and stout, and his face would have been fairly handsome, but roguery and shamelessness had disfigured it. That was his appearance. As for his call, I am not so hard-hearted as to write only a short summary of it; it deserves to be written word for word.
