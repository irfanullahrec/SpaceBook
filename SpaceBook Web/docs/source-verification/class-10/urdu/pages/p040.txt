She shook the dust out of the curtains — curtains she had had made a few weeks earlier by saving paisa by paisa from the household money. The doctor had told her to eat eggs, milk and butter every day, but she ate less of these so the house could be furnished. Old things did not look good in the new house, so she had bought a table, chairs and a sofa too, and the room was very dear to her. Coughing all the while she cleaned, rearranged the chairs, wiped the pictures of the children, her husband and herself on the walls, and stood a long time before her own picture thinking that people do not lie when they say she is beautiful. Back in her own room she remembered she had to knit Bakhshu a new sweater; only the neck was left.
