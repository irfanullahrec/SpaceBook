On the way he met many animals, big and small, which he recognised were not human. Then he saw a camel, which he had never seen before; seeing its strength and height he thought it must be a human and challenged it: "Get ready — I won't leave you alive!" When the camel asked why, the cub said, "You are a human, and I want to hunt a human first because he is strong." The camel said, "You are mistaken; I am not a human. A human is very strong: he keeps me as a slave, loads burdens on me, milks me and sacrifices me and eats my meat." The cub was astonished and asked where he could find a human; the camel told him to go on — he would find one somewhere in the forest.
