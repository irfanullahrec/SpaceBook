### شکل نمبر 7.33 نیسٹڈ لسٹ بنانا || Figure 7.33 Making a nested list || ۷.۳۳ شکل ځالې لېست جوړول
m <html>
m <head>
m <title>Nested List</title>
m </head>
m <body>
m <h3>Types of Computer Memories</h3>
m <ol>
m <li>Primary Memory</li>
m <ul>
m <li>Random Access Memory</li>
m <li>Read Only Memory</li>
m </ul>
m <li>Secondary Memory</li>
m <ul>
m <li>Magnetic Memory</li>
m <li>Optical Memory</li>
m <li>USB Flash Drive</li>
m <li>Memory Card</li>
m </ul>
m </ol>
m </body>
m </html>
@issue In Figure 7.33 each <ul> is placed directly inside <ol>, after a closed </li>. Browsers display it as intended, but in valid HTML a nested list must go inside the <li> it belongs to (…<li>Primary Memory<ul>…</ul></li>).
