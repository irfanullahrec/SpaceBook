### پروگرام 3.5 طالب علم کے رول نمبر، حاصل کردہ نمبر، کل نمبر اِن پُٹ کروا کے فیصد معلوم کرنے کے لیے پروگرام۔ || Program 3.5 A program that inputs a student's roll number, obtained marks and total marks and finds the percentage || پروګرام ۳.۵ د زده کوونکي رول نمبر، ترلاسه شوې نمرې او ټولې نمرې داخلوي او سلنه پیدا کوي
m #include<stdio.h>
m void main()
m {
m int rn,tm,om;
m float pm;
m printf("\nEnter Roll number= ");
m scanf("%d",&rn);
m printf("\nEnter Total marks= ");
m scanf("%d",&tm);
m printf("\nEnter Obtained marks= ");
m scanf("%d",&om);
m pm=om/tm*100;
m printf("\nPercentage marks=%f",pm);
m }
box info | آؤٹ پُٹ || Output || Output
m Enter Roll number = 10
m Enter Total marks = 800
m Enter Obtained marks = 650
m Percentage marks = 81.25
endbox
@issue Program 3.5 has a logic error: om and tm are int, so om/tm is integer division (650/800 = 0) and pm becomes 0, printed as 0.000000. To get 81.25 write pm = (float)om/tm*100; (or om*100.0/tm). Even then %f prints 81.250000; "%.2f" would print 81.25 as shown.
