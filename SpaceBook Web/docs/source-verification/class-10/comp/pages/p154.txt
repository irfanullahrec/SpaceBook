People do not have much time to read yesterday's printed newspaper, so they always turn to news sites for the latest news. They are an easy and reliable way of getting information, and they also let you express your opinions and ideas on any news item. Some news websites also provide videos and pictures of the latest breaking news happening around us. The most important part of a news site is its archive section, holding all the news and articles, so you can search for any past news item at any time.
