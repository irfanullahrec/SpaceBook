qp 1 | Why electrons are emitted from an electrically heated metal filament? || برقی طور پر گرم دھاتی فلامنٹ سے الیکٹران کیوں خارج ہوتے ہیں؟ || ولې له برېښنايي ګرم شوي فلزي فلمنټ الکترونونه وځي؟
sol Model answer ## When a metal filament is heated electrically, its free electrons gain thermal energy; some of them gain enough energy to overcome the attraction of the metal surface (the work function) and escape into the surrounding space. This process is called thermionic emission.
qp 2 | What are cathode rays? How are cathode rays produced? || کیتھوڈ شعاعیں کیا ہیں؟ کیتھوڈ شعاعیں کیسے پیدا ہوتی ہیں؟ || کاتوډي وړانګې څه دي؟ کاتوډي وړانګې څنګه جوړېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Cathode rays are streams of fast-moving electrons that travel from the cathode in a vacuum tube. They are produced when a hot cathode emits electrons by thermionic emission and a high voltage between the cathode and the anode accelerates them. Properties: they travel in straight lines, carry negative charge (deflected by electric and magnetic fields), produce fluorescence on a screen, and can produce X-rays when they hit a metal target.
qp 3 | Describe the construction and working of electron gun? || الیکٹران گن کی ساخت اور کام بیان کریں۔ || د الکترون ټوپک جوړښت او کار بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## An electron gun consists of a heated filament that heats the cathode so that it emits electrons by thermionic emission, a control grid (a cylinder with a small hole at negative potential that controls the number of electrons and so the brightness), and one or more anodes at high positive potential that accelerate the electrons and focus them into a narrow beam that leaves through a hole in the anode.
qp 4 | What effect does an electric field and magnetic field have on the electron beam? || برقی میدان اور مقناطیسی میدان کا الیکٹران بیم پر کیا اثر ہوتا ہے؟ || برېښنايي ساحه او مقناطیسي ساحه پر الکترون شعاع څه اغېز لري؟
sol Model answer ## An electric field deflects the electron beam towards the positive plate, in the direction opposite to the field, with a force $F = eE$. A magnetic field deflects it in a direction perpendicular to both the field and the beam (given by Fleming's left-hand rule for the conventional current), with a force $F = evB$ that bends the beam into a circular path. These effects are used to move the beam on the screen of a CRO.
qp 5 | Describe the working principle of Cathode Ray Oscilloscope (CRO) and make list of its uses. || کیتھوڈ رے آسیلوسکوپ (CRO) کا اصولِ کار بیان کریں اور اس کے استعمالات کی فہرست بنائیں۔ || د کاتوډ رې اوسیلوسکوپ (CRO) د کار اصل بیان کړئ او د کارونو لېست یې جوړ کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A CRO consists of an electron gun, deflecting plates (X and Y plates) and a fluorescent screen in an evacuated tube. The electron beam makes a bright spot on the screen. A voltage on the Y plates moves the spot vertically and the time-base (a saw-tooth voltage) on the X plates sweeps it horizontally, so a waveform of voltage against time is displayed. Uses: measuring voltage, time period and frequency of signals, displaying waveforms, testing electronic circuits and, in medicine, ECG and EEG displays.
qp 6 | What is the difference between analogue and digital electronics? || اینالاگ اور ڈیجیٹل الیکٹرانکس میں کیا فرق ہے؟ || د انالوګ او ډیجیټل الکترونیک ترمنځ څه توپیر دی؟
sol Model answer ## Analogue electronics deals with signals that vary continuously in value (like the sound wave from a microphone), while digital electronics deals with signals that have only two levels, high (1) and low (0). Digital circuits are less affected by noise, easier to store and process (computers, mobile phones), while analogue circuits represent the real signal directly (old radio, record players).
qp 7 | Define logic gates. Describe the operation of AND, OR, NOT, NAND and NOR logic gates by drawing their symbols and truth tables. || لاجک گیٹس کی تعریف کریں۔ AND، OR، NOT، NAND اور NOR لاجک گیٹس کا عمل ان کی علامات اور ٹروتھ ٹیبل بنا کر بیان کریں۔ || منطقي دروازې تعریف کړئ. د AND، OR، NOT، NAND او NOR منطقي دروازو عمل د هغو د نښو او د ریښتیا جدولونو په رسمولو بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A logic gate is an electronic circuit with one or more inputs and a single output that follows a logical rule. AND: output 1 only when all inputs are 1 ($X = A\cdot B$). OR: output 1 when any input is 1 ($X = A + B$). NOT: output is the opposite of the input ($X = \overline{A}$). NAND: AND followed by NOT; output 0 only when all inputs are 1 ($X = \overline{AB}$). NOR: OR followed by NOT; output 1 only when all inputs are 0 ($X = \overline{A+B}$). Truth tables, inputs A B giving output: AND 00→0, 01→0, 10→0, 11→1; OR 00→0, 01→1, 10→1, 11→1; NAND 00→1, 01→1, 10→1, 11→0; NOR 00→1, 01→0, 10→0, 11→0; NOT 0→1, 1→0.
