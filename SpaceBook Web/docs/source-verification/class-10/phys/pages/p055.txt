q 5 | What is the speed of sound in air at −20°C?
sol 319 m/s ## $v = 331 + 0.6\,T = 331 + 0.6(-20) = 331 - 12 = 319$ m/s.
q 6 | Army man wearing binoculars see the flash from enemy tank fire 5 s before the fire is heard, he records 26°C temperature on his personal thermometer. What is the distance of the tank from him?
sol About 1.73 km ## At 26 °C, $v = 331 + 0.6(26) = 346.6$ m/s. The flash arrives practically at once, so $d = vt = 346.6\times5 \approx 1733$ m $\approx 1.73$ km.
q 7 | Calculate the wavelengths of sounds at the extremes of the audible range, 20 Hz and 20,000 Hz, at normal room temperature of 20°C?
sol 17.2 m and 1.7 cm ## At 20 °C, $v = 331 + 0.6(20) = 343$ m/s. For 20 Hz: $\lambda = v/f = 343/20 = 17.2$ m. For 20,000 Hz: $\lambda = 343/20000 = 0.0172$ m $= 1.7$ cm.
q 8 | Ishfaq stands between two high rise buildings A and B, such that he is at 33 m distance from building A. When he blows the whistle, he hears the first echo after 0.2 s and second echo after 0.8 s. Calculate (a) the speed of sound and (b) distance of building B from him.
sol (a) 330 m/s (b) 132 m ## (a) The first echo comes from building A: the sound travels $2\times33 = 66$ m in 0.2 s, so $v = 66/0.2 = 330$ m/s. (b) The second echo comes from building B: $2d = vt = 330\times0.8 = 264$ m, so $d = 132$ m.
endexercise
