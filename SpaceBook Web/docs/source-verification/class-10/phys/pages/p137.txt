q C | COMPREHENSIVE QUESTIONS — Give an extended response to the following questions || جامع سوالات — درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جواب دیں || جامع پوښتنې — لاندې پوښتنو ته پراخ ځوابونه ورکړئ
qp 1 | What is electric charge? How objects can be electrified? Describe with the help of experiments. || برقی چارج کیا ہے؟ اجسام کو کیسے برقی بنایا جا سکتا ہے؟ تجربات کی مدد سے بیان کریں۔ || برېښنايي چارج څه دی؟ شیان څنګه برېښنايي کېږي؟ د تجربو په مرسته یې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Electric charge is the property of matter that makes it experience a force in an electric field; it is of two kinds, positive and negative, measured in coulombs (C). Objects are electrified by friction, conduction and induction. Experiment: rub a glass rod with silk and an ebonite rod with fur; each rod then picks up small pieces of paper, and a hanging glass rod is repelled by another rubbed glass rod but attracted by a rubbed ebonite rod. This shows two kinds of charge: like charges repel and unlike charges attract. In friction, electrons move from one material to the other, so one becomes positive and the other negative; in conduction a charged body touching a neutral one shares its charge with it.
qp 2 | What is electrostatic induction? Explain. || ساکن برقی امالہ کیا ہے؟ وضاحت کریں۔ || الکتروستاتیک القا څه ده؟ تشریح یې کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Electrostatic induction is the process of charging a conductor without touching it. Bring a negatively charged rod near, but not touching, an insulated neutral metal sphere: electrons in the sphere are repelled to the far side, so the near side becomes positive and the far side negative. If the far side is earthed (touched by a finger) the repelled electrons flow to the ground; removing the earth connection first and then the rod leaves the sphere with a net positive charge, opposite to that of the rod.
qp 3 | What is the function of electroscope? How can we use electroscope to find the presence and nature of charge on a body? || الیکٹروسکوپ کا کام کیا ہے؟ کسی جسم پر چارج کی موجودگی اور نوعیت معلوم کرنے کے لیے الیکٹروسکوپ کیسے استعمال کیا جاتا ہے؟ || د الکتروسکوپ دنده څه ده؟ د یوه جسم د چارج د شتون او ډول موندلو لپاره څنګه کارول کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## An electroscope detects the presence of charge on a body and shows its nature. It has a metal cap joined by a rod to two thin gold leaves in a glass case. A charged body brought near the cap makes the leaves diverge (charge is present). To find the sign, first charge the electroscope with a known charge (say positive). Bring the test body near: if the leaves diverge further, the body has the same (positive) charge; if they collapse (come closer), it has the opposite (negative) charge.
