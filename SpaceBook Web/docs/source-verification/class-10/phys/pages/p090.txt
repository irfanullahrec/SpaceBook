p As shown in figure 12.26 (a) and the answers in this example it is confirmed that when the object distance is greater than twice the focal length (dₒ > 2f), for convex lens
- the image distance is less than twice the focal length, (i.e 15 cm < 20 cm)
- the image is real, (distance positive)
- the image is inverted, (magnification negative) and
- the image is smaller than the object. (magnification less than 1)
