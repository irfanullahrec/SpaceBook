q 1 | MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS
qp 1 | If the angle of incidence is 30° then the angle of reflection will be (A) 30° (B) 45° (C) 90° (D) 210°
sol A. 30° ## By the law of reflection the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence, so it is 30°.
qp 2 | When r is the radius of curvature of concave mirror. Real diminished image will be formed when the object is at (A) dₒ > r (B) dₒ = r (C) r > dₒ > f (D) dₒ = f
sol A. $d_o > r$ ## A concave mirror forms a real, inverted, diminished image between F and C when the object is beyond the centre of curvature (object distance greater than $r$).
qp 3 | The focal length of convex mirror with radius of curvature 10 cm is (A) +10 cm (B) +5 cm (C) −10 cm (D) −5 cm
sol D. −5 cm ## $f = r/2 = 10/2 = 5$ cm, and the focus of a convex mirror is virtual (behind the mirror), so with the sign convention $f = -5$ cm.
qp 4 | An object is placed 7 cm from a concave mirror whose radius of curvature is 10 cm, the image formed will be (A) real and upright (B) virtual and upright (C) real and inverted (D) virtual and inverted
sol C. real and inverted ## The focal length is $f = r/2 = 5$ cm. An object at 7 cm lies between F and C (5 cm < 7 cm < 10 cm), so the image is real, inverted and magnified, formed beyond C.
qp 5 | Which one of the following materials will refract light more (A) water (B) glass (C) air (D) diamond
sol D. diamond ## A material refracts light more when its refractive index is larger. Diamond (n ≈ 2.42) has a higher index than glass (≈ 1.5), water (1.33) or air (1.0003).
