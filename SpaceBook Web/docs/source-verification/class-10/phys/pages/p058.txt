## 12.1.1 Laws of Reflection
p 12.1.1 Laws of Reflection: The laws of reflection describes the behavior of the incident and reflected rays.
- First Law: The incident ray, the reflected ray and the normal to the surface all lie in the same plane
- Second Law: the angle of reflection θᵣ equals the angle of incidence θᵢ:
m θᵢ = θᵣ   (12.1)
p 12.1.2 Image characteristics: We can completely describe any image by defining four characteristics.
- The magnification is the ratio of the image size to the object size. If the magnification is greater than one, the image is larger than the object. When it is equal to one, the object and image are the same size. If the magnification is less than one, the image is smaller than the object.
- The attitude of an image shows whether the image is oriented the same way as the object (upright) or upside down (inverted) with respect to the object.
- The image location (or position) is the distance between the image and the optical device — mirror or lens.
