qp 1 | What is Information and Communication Technology (ICT)? Explain briefly its components. || انفارمیشن اینڈ کمیونیکیشن ٹیکنالوجی (ICT) کیا ہے؟ اس کے اجزا مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || د معلوماتو او مخابراتو ټکنالوژي (ICT) څه ده؟ برخې یې په لنډه توګه تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## ICT is the technology used for collecting, storing, processing and communicating information. qp 2 | Explain the flow of information. Describe how information is transmitted as electric signal through wires, light signals through optical fibres and radio waves through air/vacuum? || معلومات کے بہاؤ کی وضاحت کریں۔ بیان کریں کہ معلومات تاروں کے ذریعے برقی سگنل، آپٹیکل فائبر کے ذریعے روشنی کے سگنل اور ہوا/خلا کے ذریعے ریڈیائی موجوں کی شکل میں کیسے منتقل ہوتی ہیں۔ || د معلوماتو بهیر تشریح کړئ. بیان کړئ چې معلومات د سیمونو له لارې د برېښنايي سیګنال، د نوري فایبرونو له لارې د رڼا سیګنال او د هوا/تشیال له لارې د راډیويي څپو په توګه څنګه لېږدول کېږي.
sol Model answer ## Information flows from a sender through a channel (medium) to a receiver. In wires, it is changed into electrical signals that travel along the conductor (telephone line, coaxial cable). In optical fibres, it is changed into pulses of light that travel by total internal reflection. In air or vacuum, it is sent as radio waves (electromagnetic waves) from an antenna and picked up by the receiving antenna, and is then changed back into sound, text or pictures.
qp 3 | Describe function and use of fax machine, cell phone, photo phone and computer. || فیکس مشین، سیل فون، فوٹو فون اور کمپیوٹر کا کام اور استعمال بیان کریں۔ || د فکس ماشین، ګرځنده تلیفون، فوټو فون او کمپیوټر دنده او کارونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A fax machine scans a document, changes it into electrical signals and sends them over a telephone line to another machine that prints a copy. A cell phone is a portable radio transmitter and receiver for calls, messages and internet access through cell towers. A photo phone (video phone) transmits pictures along with voice so that callers can see each other. A computer processes, stores and displays data, and is used for writing, calculations, communication, design and entertainment.

qp 5 | What are information storage devices? Describe the use of information storage devices such as audio cassettes, video cassettes, hard discs, floppy, compact discs and flash drive. || معلومات ذخیرہ کرنے والے آلات کیا ہیں؟ آڈیو کیسٹ، ویڈیو کیسٹ، ہارڈ ڈسک، فلاپی، کمپیکٹ ڈسک اور فلیش ڈرائیو جیسے آلات کا استعمال بیان کریں۔ || د معلوماتو د زېرمه کولو وسیلې څه دي؟ د اډیو کیسټونو، ویډیو کیسټونو، هارډ ډیسکونو، فلاپي، کمپیکټ ډیسکونو او فلش ډرایف کارونه بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Information storage devices keep data so that it can be used later. Audio cassettes and video cassettes store sound and pictures on magnetic tape; hard disks store large amounts of data magnetically inside a computer; floppy disks stored small files magnetically on a portable disk (now obsolete); compact discs (CDs, DVDs) store sound, video and data that is read by a laser; flash drives store data in memory chips and are small, fast and portable.
