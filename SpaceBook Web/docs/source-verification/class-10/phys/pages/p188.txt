p To make the coil continue to rotate, the ends of the coil are attached to a split ring called the commutator. The power supply is connected to the commutator using a pair of brushes which allows the ring to rotate while maintaining electrical contact. Again consider the motor starting in the position in fig. 15.7. The coil rotates clockwise. When the coil becomes vertical (green conductor on the top, red on the bottom, coil perpendicular to the magnetic field), the gaps in the commutator align with the brushes and so the current stops flowing. The inertia of the coil carries it forward and now the green half is closer to the South pole. But the rotation of the commutator now means that the green half is connected to the positive terminal of the power supply instead of the negative.
