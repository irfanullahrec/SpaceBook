p 1. A - ray parallel to the principal axis is reflected as if it came from a focal point.
p 2. F - ray directed toward the focal point is reflected parallel to the principal axis.
p 3. P - ray incident on the vertex of the mirror reflects at an equal angle to the axis.
p 4. C - ray along a radius is reflected back on itself.

p Capturing solar energy with mirrors. Parabolic mirrors are also used in one method of collecting solar energy for commercial purposes. Figure shows a long row of concave parabolic mirrors that reflect the sun's rays to the focal point. Located at the focal point and running the length of the row is an oil-filled pipe. The focused rays of the sun heat the oil.
@deco Photograph of a row of parabolic solar mirrors
p In a solar-thermal electric plant, the heat from many such rows is used to generate steam. The steam, in turn, drives a turbine connected to an electric generator.
