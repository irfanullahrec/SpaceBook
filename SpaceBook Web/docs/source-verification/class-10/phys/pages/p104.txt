@sec 12-keypoints | Key points
box teach | KEY POINTS
p The Reflection of Light: When light reflects from a smooth surface, the reflected light obeys the law of reflection.
p Spherical Mirrors: A spherical mirror has the shape of a section from the surface of a hollow sphere.
p The Mirror Formula: The formula specifying the relation between the object distance dₒ, the image distance dᵢ, and the focal length f of the mirror.
p Refraction: The changing of a light ray's direction when it passes through variations in matter.
p Snell's Law: The ratio of sine of angle of incidence and sine of angle of refraction is constant for a given pair of media.
p Total Internal Reflection: When the angle of incidence exceeds the critical angle, all the incident light is reflected back into the material from which it came.
p Lens: A transmissive optical device that focuses or disperses a light beam by means of refraction.
p Thin Lens Formula: The formula specifying the relation between the object distance dₒ, the image distance dᵢ, and the focal length f of thin lens.
p Power of Lens: The degree of convergence or divergence of light rays falling on lens.
p Short Sightedness: Defect of an eye so that distant object are not seen clearly.
p Long Sightedness: Defect of an eye so that nearby object are not seen clearly.
p Simple Microscope: A converging lens, which works by allowing an object to be viewed at a distance less than the near-point distance.
p Compound Microscope: Instruments for enlarging the detail that we cannot see with the unaided eye.
p Telescope: Device meant for viewing distant objects, producing an image that is larger than the image that can be seen with the unaided eye.
endbox
