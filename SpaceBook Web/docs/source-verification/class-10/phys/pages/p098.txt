example 12.7 | INSECT EXAMINATION
p A biologist with a near-point distance of N = 26 cm, examines an insect wing through a magnifying glass whose focal length is 4.3 cm. Find the angular magnification when the image produced by the magnifier is (a) at the near point and (b) at infinity.
@deco Photograph of a magnifying glass over flowers
### GIVEN
p Focal length of magnifying glass 'f' = 4.3 cm. Near-point distance of N = 26 cm. Object Distance 'dₒ' = 1.1 cm
### REQUIRED
p Angular Magnification 'mθ' = ?
### SOLUTION
p The magnification when the image is at near point for magnifying glass is
m mθ = N/f + 1   Putting values   mθ = 26 cm / 4.3 cm + 1
p Hence mθ = 7   [Answer]
p The magnification when the image is at infinity for magnifying glass is
m mθ = N/f   Putting values   mθ = 26 cm / 4.3 cm
p Hence mθ = 6   [Answer]
p The relaxed eye results in decrease in magnification of 1.0—from 7.0 to 6.0.
