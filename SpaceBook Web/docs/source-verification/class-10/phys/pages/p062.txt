p The concave mirror has the ability to converge the beam of light parallel to principal axis. Therefore it is also termed as converging mirror. The convex mirror has the ability to diverge the beam of light parallel to principal axis. Therefore it is also termed as diverging mirror.
p After reflecting from the mirror, the rays converge or diverge as if they originate from a single point called the focal point F (or principal focus) as shown in figure 12.6.
