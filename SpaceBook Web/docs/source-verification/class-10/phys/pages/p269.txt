qp 3 | What are nuclear transmutations? What changes in the composition of the nucleus is observed when alpha or beta particles are emitted? Explain by symbolic equations. || مرکزی تبدیلیاں کیا ہیں؟ الفا یا بیٹا ذرات کے اخراج پر مرکزے کی ترکیب میں کیا تبدیلیاں دیکھی جاتی ہیں؟ علامتی مساواتوں سے وضاحت کریں۔ || هستوي بدلونونه څه دي؟ کله چې الفا یا بېټا ذرې خپرې شي د هستې په ترکیب کې کوم بدلونونه لیدل کېږي؟ په سمبولیکو معادلو یې تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A nuclear transmutation is the change of one element into another when the nucleus emits a particle. Alpha emission: the nucleus loses 2 protons and 2 neutrons, so $Z$ decreases by 2 and $A$ by 4, e.g. ${}^{238}_{92}U \rightarrow {}^{234}_{90}Th + {}^{4}_{2}He$. Beta emission: a neutron changes into a proton and an electron, so $Z$ increases by 1 and $A$ stays the same, e.g. ${}^{14}_{6}C \rightarrow {}^{14}_{7}N + {}^{0}_{-1}e$.
qp 4 | Radioactive sources are said to have half life. What is the meaning of half life? || کہا جاتا ہے کہ تابکار ذرائع کی نصف عمر ہوتی ہے۔ نصف عمر کا کیا مطلب ہے؟ || ویل کېږي چې راډیواکټیو سرچینې نیم عمر لري. د نیم عمر معنا څه ده؟
sol Model answer ## The half life is the time taken for half of the radioactive nuclei in a sample to decay (equivalently, for the activity to fall to half). After one half life half remains, after two half lives a quarter, after three an eighth. For example, if the half life is 5 days, 80 g of the sample becomes 40 g after 5 days and 20 g after 10 days.
qp 5 | What are radioisotopes? Explain their uses for various applications? || ریڈیو آئسوٹوپس کیا ہیں؟ مختلف کاموں میں ان کے استعمالات کی وضاحت کریں۔ || راډیو ایزوټوپونه څه دي؟ په بېلابېلو کارونو کې د هغو کارونې تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Radioisotopes are isotopes of an element whose nuclei are radioactive. Uses: in medicine (cobalt-60 for cancer treatment, iodine-131 for the thyroid, technetium-99m for scans), in industry (checking thickness and leaks in pipes, gauges), in agriculture (tracers, crop improvement, preserving food), in archaeology (carbon dating) and in sterilizing medical equipment.
qp 6 | How is carbon-14 used to determine the ages of wood, bones and other artifacts? || لکڑی، ہڈیوں اور دیگر نوادرات کی عمر معلوم کرنے کے لیے کاربن-14 کیسے استعمال ہوتا ہے؟ || د لرګیو، هډوکو او نورو لرغونو شیانو د عمر د ټاکلو لپاره کاربن-14 څنګه کارېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Living plants and animals take in carbon-14 along with ordinary carbon, so their C-14 to C-12 ratio is fixed. When they die, no new C-14 is taken in and the C-14 decays with a half life of 5730 years. By measuring the fraction of C-14 left in a wooden, bone or fibre sample and comparing it with that in living matter, the number of half lives that have passed, and so its age, can be calculated.
qp 7 | What are fission and fusion? || انشقاق اور ادغام کیا ہیں؟ || فیشن او فیوژن څه دي؟
sol Model answer ## Fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus (e.g. uranium-235) into two lighter nuclei, after absorbing a neutron, with release of a large amount of energy and more neutrons; it is the basis of nuclear reactors and atomic bombs. Fusion is the joining of two light nuclei (e.g. hydrogen isotopes) to form a heavier nucleus, releasing even more energy per unit mass; it needs very high temperature and pressure and takes place in the sun and stars.
qp 9 | What are background radiations? What are its major sources? || پس منظر کی اشعاع کیا ہیں؟ ان کے بڑے ذرائع کیا ہیں؟ || د شالید وړانګې څه دي؟ لویې سرچینې یې کومې دي؟
sol Model answer ## Background radiation is the low level of ionizing radiation that is always present around us. Natural sources: cosmic rays from space, radioactive rocks and soil (radon gas, uranium, thorium), building materials and the food and water we take (potassium-40, carbon-14). Artificial sources: medical X-rays, nuclear power and fallout from weapons testing. Natural sources give most of the exposure.
