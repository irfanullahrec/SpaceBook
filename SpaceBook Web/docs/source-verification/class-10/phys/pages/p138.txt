qp 4 | State and explain Coulomb's Law. || کولمب کا قانون بیان کریں اور وضاحت کریں۔ || د کولمب قانون ووایئ او تشریح یې کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Coulomb's law: the electric force between two point charges is directly proportional to the product of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them, $F = k\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r^2}$ with $k = 9\times10^{9}$ N m² C⁻². The force acts along the line joining the charges; it is attractive for unlike charges and repulsive for like charges. Doubling one charge doubles the force; doubling the distance reduces the force to one quarter.
qp 5 | What is meant by electric field and electric field intensity? How the field lines represent the electric field for isolated positive and negative point charges? || برقی میدان اور برقی میدان کی شدت سے کیا مراد ہے؟ الگ تھلگ مثبت اور منفی نقطہ چارجز کے برقی میدان کو میدانی لکیریں کیسے ظاہر کرتی ہیں؟ || له برېښنايي ساحې او د هغې له شدت څه مراد دی؟ د یوازې مثبت او منفي ټکي چارجونو ساحه کرښې څنګه ښيي؟
sol Model answer ## The electric field is the region around a charge in which another charge experiences a force. The electric field intensity at a point is the force per unit positive test charge, $E = F/q_0$ in N/C (V/m). Field lines of an isolated positive point charge point radially outward and those of an isolated negative charge point radially inward; the lines never cross, they start on positive and end on negative charges, and they are closer together where the field is stronger.
qp 6 | What is electric potential? In what units we measure electric potential? || برقی پوٹینشل کیا ہے؟ اسے کن اکائیوں میں ناپا جاتا ہے؟ || برېښنايي پوټانشیل څه دی؟ په کومو واحدونو اندازه کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Electric potential at a point is the work done in bringing a unit positive charge from infinity to that point, $V = W/q_0$. Its unit is the volt (V): 1 V = 1 J/C. The potential difference between two points is the work done per unit charge in moving a charge from one to the other.
qp 7 | Give some practical applications in which electric field is useful. || چند عملی استعمالات بتائیں جن میں برقی میدان مفید ہے۔ || ځینې عملي کارونې ووایئ چې پکې برېښنايي ساحه ګټوره ده.
sol Model answer ## Practical uses of electric fields: electrostatic painting and spray painting (charged paint droplets are attracted to the object), photocopiers and laser printers (charged drum attracts toner), electrostatic precipitators that remove smoke and dust in chimneys, inkjet printers that deflect charged ink drops, capacitors that store energy, and lightning rods.
qp 8 | How lightning occurs? How can we safeguard ourselves from lightning hazard? || آسمانی بجلی کیسے پیدا ہوتی ہے؟ ہم اس کے خطرے سے خود کو کیسے محفوظ رکھ سکتے ہیں؟ || تندر څنګه رامنځته کېږي؟ له خطر یې ځان څنګه ساتلای شو؟
sol Model answer ## Lightning occurs when friction between ice particles and air in a storm cloud separates charge: the cloud bottom becomes negative and induces positive charge on the ground. When the potential difference becomes very large the air breaks down and charge flows in a giant spark. Safety: stay inside a building or a car, avoid open fields, tall trees and water, do not use metal objects or phones outdoors, and fit buildings with lightning rods connected to the ground.
qp 9 | What is capacitor? Define capacitance and its units. || کیپیسیٹر کیا ہے؟ کیپیسیٹنس اور اس کی اکائیوں کی تعریف کریں۔ || خازن څه دی؟ ظرفیت او واحدونه یې تعریف کړئ.
sol Model answer ## A capacitor is a device that stores electric charge and energy; it consists of two conducting plates separated by an insulator (dielectric). Capacitance is the ability of a capacitor to store charge: $C = Q/V$, the charge stored per unit potential difference. Its unit is the farad (F): 1 F = 1 C/V (smaller units: μF = 10⁻⁶ F, pF = 10⁻¹² F).
qp 10 | What is series combination of capacitors? How we can determine equivalent capacitance for different capacitors connected in series? || کیپیسیٹرز کا سلسلہ وار ملاپ کیا ہے؟ سلسلہ وار جڑے مختلف کیپیسیٹرز کی مساوی کیپیسیٹنس کیسے معلوم کی جاتی ہے؟ || د خازنونو لړۍ ییز ترکیب څه دی؟ د لړۍ ییزو خازنونو معادل ظرفیت څنګه معلومېږي؟
sol Model answer ## In a series combination the capacitors are connected end to end so that the same charge $Q$ is on each, while the potential differences add up, $V = V_1 + V_2 + V_3$. Since $V = Q/C$, $\dfrac{Q}{C_{eq}} = \dfrac{Q}{C_1} + \dfrac{Q}{C_2} + \dfrac{Q}{C_3}$, so $\dfrac{1}{C_{eq}} = \dfrac{1}{C_1} + \dfrac{1}{C_2} + \dfrac{1}{C_3}$. The equivalent capacitance is smaller than the smallest capacitor.
