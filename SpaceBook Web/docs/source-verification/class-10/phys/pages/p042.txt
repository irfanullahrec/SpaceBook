p In both examples, sound is traveling in a material other than air. You may have watched distant lightning and noticed the time lapse before the sound of thunder reaches you. This is an example of the relatively slow speed of sound compared to the speed of light. The speed of sound in water is almost five times than its speed in air. Sound travels most rapidly in many solids, and quite slowly in many gases. Some values are given in Table 11.3.
