p As in the microscope, the image of the objective is the object for the eyepiece, which is basically a magnifier. Thus, if the image of the objective is placed at the focal point of the eyepiece, it will form an image that is at infinity, as indicated in Figure 12.37. In this configuration, the observer can view the final image of the telescope with a completely relaxed eye.
p The angular size of the image formed by the eyepiece is approximately
m θ′ = hᵢ / fₑ   ②
p To find the total angular magnification of the telescope, we consider the definition of angular magnification as
m mθ = θ′ / θ   ③
p putting equation 1 and equation 2 in equation 3, we get
m mθ = (hᵢ / fₑ) / (−hᵢ / fₒ)   Therefore   mθ = −fₒ / fₑ   (12.14)

example 12.9 | WORLD'S LARGEST TELESCOPE
p The largest optical refracting telescope in the world is located at the Yerkes Observatory in Wisconsin. The objective lens has a focal length of 19 m, and the eyepiece has a focal length of 10 cm. Calculate the total magnifying power of this telescope.
@deco Photograph of the Yerkes Observatory refracting telescope
### GIVEN
p Focal length of objective 'fₒ' = 19 m. Focal length of eye piece 'fₑ' = 10 cm = 0.10 m
### REQUIRED
p Angular magnification 'mθ' = ?
### SOLUTION
p (a) The magnification of telescope is
m mθ = −fₒ / fₑ
p Putting values
m mθ = −19 m / 0.10 m
p Therefore mθ = −190   [Answer]
endexample
