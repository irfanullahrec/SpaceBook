q C | COMPREHENSIVE QUESTIONS — Give an extended response to the following questions || جامع سوالات — درج ذیل سوالات کے تفصیلی جواب دیں || جامع پوښتنې — لاندې پوښتنو ته پراخ ځوابونه ورکړئ
qp 1 | What is nucleus? How a nuclide is represented symbolically? || مرکزہ کیا ہے؟ نیوکلائیڈ کو علامتی طور پر کیسے ظاہر کیا جاتا ہے؟ || هسته څه ده؟ نیوکلایډ په سمبولیک ډول څنګه ښودل کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## The nucleus is the small, dense, positively charged centre of an atom containing protons and neutrons (nucleons); it holds nearly all of the mass of the atom. A nuclide is written as ${}^{A}_{Z}X$, where X is the chemical symbol, $Z$ the atomic (proton) number and $A$ the mass (nucleon) number; the number of neutrons is $N = A - Z$. Example: ${}^{14}_{6}C$ has 6 protons and 8 neutrons.
qp 2 | What is radioactivity? Give the nature, ionizing and penetration abilities of the three types of radioactive emissions. || تابکاری کیا ہے؟ تین قسم کے تابکار اخراج کی نوعیت، آئنائزنگ اور نفوذ کی صلاحیتیں بیان کریں۔ || راډیواکټیویټي څه ده؟ د دریو ډوله راډیواکټیو خپرونو طبیعت، ایونایزینګ او نفوذ وړتیاوې بیان کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Radioactivity is the spontaneous emission of radiation from unstable nuclei. Alpha ($\alpha$): a helium nucleus (2 protons + 2 neutrons), charge +2, strongly ionizing, least penetrating (stopped by paper). Beta ($\beta$): fast electrons, charge −1, medium ionizing, stopped by a few mm of aluminium. Gamma ($\gamma$): electromagnetic waves of very high energy, no charge, weakly ionizing, most penetrating (reduced only by thick lead or concrete).
