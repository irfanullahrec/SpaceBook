example 12.3 | SPEED OF LIGHT IN WATER
p What is the speed of light in water having index of refraction as 1.33?
### GIVEN
p Speed of light in vacuum 'c' = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s. Index of refraction for water 'n' = 1.33
### REQUIRED
p Speed of light in water 'v' = ?
### SOLUTION
p The index of refraction is  n = c / v  or  v = c / n
p Putting values
m v = (3.00 × 10⁸ m/s) / 1.33
p Therefore v = 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s   [Answer]
p Therefore the speed of light in water is 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s
endexample
