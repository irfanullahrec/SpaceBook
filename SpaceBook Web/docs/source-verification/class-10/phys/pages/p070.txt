example 12.2 | REAR VIEW MIRROR
p An external rearview car mirror is convex with a radius of curvature of 16.0 m (Fig.). Determine (a) the focal length of the mirror, (b) location of the image and (c) its magnification for an object 10.0 m from the mirror.
### GIVEN
p Object distance 'dₒ' = 10 m. Radius of curvature = R = 16.0 m
### REQUIRED
p (a) focal length 'f' = ? (b) image distance 'dᵢ' = ? (c) magnification 'M' = ?
### Solution
p (a) radius of curvature and focal length for convex mirror are related by equation f = −½ R. Putting values f = −½ 16 m
p Therefore f = −8.0 m   [Answer]
