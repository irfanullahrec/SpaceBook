qp 6 | A convex lense with focal length 8.00 cm has the power of lens (A) 2.05 D (B) 4.00 D (C) 12.5 D (D) 16.0 D
sol C. 12.5 D ## $P = \dfrac{1}{f(\text{m})} = \dfrac{1}{0.0800} = 12.5$ D.
qp 7 | If the distance from your eye's lens to the retina is shorter than for a normal eye, you will struggle to see objects that are (A) nearby (B) colorful (C) far away (D) moving fast.
sol A. nearby ## If the eyeball is too short, the image of a near object forms behind the retina (long-sightedness), so the person struggles to see nearby objects clearly.
qp 8 | Who benefits more from using a magnifying glass, a person whose near point is located at a distance away from the eyes of (A) 75 cm (B) 50 cm (C) 35 cm (D) 25 cm
sol A. 75 cm ## The simple magnifier gives $M = 1 + N/f$, which is larger when the near point $N$ is larger; so a person whose near point is 75 cm benefits most.
qp 9 | The human eye forms the image of an object at its (A) iris (B) retina (C) pupil (D) cornea
sol B. retina ## The cornea and lens of the eye focus the light so that a real, inverted image is formed on the retina, which carries the signal to the brain.
q 2 | CONCEPTUAL QUESTIONS. Give a brief response to the following questions
q 1 | Which type of lens would you use to start fire from light from sun concave or convex, would work best? At what distance from the lens should the paper be held for best results?
sol Model answer ## A convex (converging) lens works best, because it brings the parallel rays of the sun to a point. The paper should be held at the focal point, a distance equal to the focal length behind the lens, where the bright spot is smallest and hottest.
q 2 | If a concave mirror produces a real image, is the image necessarily inverted? Explain.
sol Model answer ## Yes. A real image is formed where light rays actually meet, and for a concave mirror (object outside the focal length) a real image is always inverted. Only a virtual image, formed with the object inside F, is upright.
q 3 | Are rearview mirrors used in cars concave or convex?
sol Model answer ## Convex mirrors. They give a wide field of view and an upright, diminished image of the traffic behind the car.
q 4 | A magician during a show makes a glass lens with n = 1.47 disappear in a trough of liquid. What is the refractive index of the liquid? Could the liquid be water?
sol Model answer ## The lens disappears when the liquid has the same refractive index as the glass, so no refraction occurs at the surface: the liquid has $n = 1.47$. It could not be water, because water has $n = 1.33$.
q 5 | Suppose that you were handed a lens and a ruler and told to determine the focal length of the lens. How would you proceed?
sol Model answer ## Focus the light of a distant object (the sun or a far window) on a sheet of paper or a wall. When the image is sharpest, measure the distance from the lens to the image with the ruler; this is the focal length. Alternatively measure an object distance and the sharp image distance and use $1/f = 1/d_o + 1/d_i$.
q 6 | Can we achieve total internal reflection from optically rare medium to optically dense medium?
sol Model answer ## No. Total internal reflection happens only when light travels from an optically denser medium to a rarer medium ($n_1 > n_2$) at an angle of incidence greater than the critical angle. Going from rare to dense, the ray bends towards the normal and is always refracted.
q 7 | Will a nearsighted person who wears corrective lenses in her glasses be able to see clearly underwater when wearing those glasses?
sol Model answer ## No. Her spectacles are shaped for use in air; under water the difference between the refractive index of glass (about 1.5) and water (1.33) is small, so the lenses bend light much less, and the eye itself loses most of its focusing power. She will not see clearly.
q 8 | When you use a simple magnifying glass, does it matter whether you hold the object to be examined closer to the lens than its focal length or farther away? Explain.
sol Model answer ## Yes. To get an enlarged, upright, virtual image the object must be placed closer to the lens than its focal length. If it is farther than the focal length the lens forms a real, inverted image and does not work as a magnifier.
q 9 | In blind turns on hilly roads, mirrors are used to help drivers. Are these mirrors plane mirrors, concave mirrors or convex mirrors? Explain.
sol Model answer ## Convex mirrors. They give a wide field of view and an upright, smaller image, so the driver can see traffic coming around the bend from a long way off; plane mirrors give a narrow view and concave mirrors give an inverted or magnified view.
