qp 1 | Define electric current. In what units it is measured? Discuss briefly the direction of current through the conductor. || برقی کرنٹ کی تعریف کریں۔ اسے کن اکائیوں میں ناپا جاتا ہے؟ موصل میں کرنٹ کی سمت پر مختصر بحث کریں۔ || برېښنايي جریان تعریف کړئ. په کومو واحدونو اندازه کېږي؟ په هادي کې د جریان پر لوري لنډه بحث وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Electric current is the rate of flow of charge through a conductor, $I = Q/t$. Its unit is the ampere (A): 1 A = 1 C/s. By convention the direction of conventional current is from the positive to the negative terminal of the battery (the direction positive charges would move), whereas the electrons in a metal actually move in the opposite direction, from negative to positive.
qp 2 | What is potential difference and emf? Differentiate between them. || پوٹینشل ڈفرنس اور emf کیا ہیں؟ ان میں فرق بیان کریں۔ || د پوټانشیل توپیر او emf څه دي؟ توپیر یې ووایئ.
sol Model answer ## Potential difference is the work done per unit charge in moving a charge between two points of a circuit, $V = W/Q$; it is the energy transferred to the load. The emf of a source is the total energy supplied per unit charge by the source (including the energy used in driving charge through the cell's own internal resistance). Difference: emf is the cause that drives the current and is measured when no current flows (open circuit), whereas potential difference is the effect measured across a component, and it is less than the emf when current flows because of the internal resistance. Both are measured in volts.
qp 3 | State and explain Ohm's Law. What are its limitations? || اوہم کا قانون بیان اور واضح کریں۔ اس کی حدود کیا ہیں؟ || د اوم قانون ووایئ او تشریح یې کړئ. محدودیتونه یې څه دي؟
sol Model answer ## Ohm's law: the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across its ends provided temperature and other physical conditions stay constant, $V \propto I$ or $V = IR$, where $R$ is the resistance. A graph of V against I is a straight line through the origin. Limitations: it holds only for ohmic (metallic) conductors at constant temperature; it does not apply to devices such as diodes, thermistors, filament lamps, transistors and electrolytes, where V-I is not linear.
qp 4 | What is resistance and in what units we measure resistance? || مزاحمت کیا ہے اور اسے کن اکائیوں میں ناپا جاتا ہے؟ || مقاومت څه دی او په کومو واحدونو اندازه کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Resistance is the opposition a conductor offers to the flow of current, $R = V/I$. Its unit is the ohm (Ω): 1 Ω = 1 V/A.
qp 5 | What is series combination of resistors? How we can determine equivalent resistance for different resistors connected in series? || مزاحمتوں کا سلسلہ وار ملاپ کیا ہے؟ سلسلہ وار جڑی مختلف مزاحمتوں کی مساوی مزاحمت کیسے معلوم کی جاتی ہے؟ || د مقاومتونو لړۍ ییز ترکیب څه دی؟ د لړۍ ییزو مقاومتونو معادل مقاومت څنګه معلومېږي؟
sol Model answer ## In a series combination the resistors are joined end to end, so the same current $I$ flows through each and the voltages add: $V = V_1 + V_2 + V_3 = IR_1 + IR_2 + IR_3 = IR_{eq}$. Hence $R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2 + R_3$. The equivalent resistance is greater than the largest resistor.
qp 6 | What is parallel combination of resistors? How we can determine equivalent resistance for different resistors connected in parallel? || مزاحمتوں کا متوازی ملاپ کیا ہے؟ متوازی جڑی مختلف مزاحمتوں کی مساوی مزاحمت کیسے معلوم کی جاتی ہے؟ || د مقاومتونو موازي ترکیب څه دی؟ د موازي مقاومتونو معادل مقاومت څنګه معلومېږي؟
sol Model answer ## In a parallel combination the resistors are connected across the same two points, so each has the same potential difference $V$ and the currents add: $I = I_1 + I_2 + I_3$. Since $I = V/R$, $\dfrac{V}{R_{eq}} = \dfrac{V}{R_1} + \dfrac{V}{R_2} + \dfrac{V}{R_3}$, so $\dfrac{1}{R_{eq}} = \dfrac{1}{R_1} + \dfrac{1}{R_2} + \dfrac{1}{R_3}$. The equivalent resistance is smaller than the smallest resistor.
qp 7 | Explain the factors on which the resistance of metallic conductor depends. || ان عوامل کی وضاحت کریں جن پر دھاتی موصل کی مزاحمت منحصر ہے۔ || هغه عوامل تشریح کړئ چې د فلزي هادي مقاومت پرې اړه لري.
sol Model answer ## The resistance of a metallic wire depends on (1) its length $L$: $R \propto L$; (2) its cross-sectional area $A$: $R \propto 1/A$ (a thicker wire has less resistance); (3) the material, through its resistivity $\rho$; and (4) its temperature: resistance of metals rises with temperature. Together $R = \rho\dfrac{L}{A}$.
qp 8 | What are ohmic and non-ohmic devices? Sketch and interpret the V-I characteristic graph to justify metallic conductor, filament lamp and thermistor as ohmic or non-ohmic materials. || اوہمک اور غیر اوہمک آلات کیا ہیں؟ دھاتی موصل، فلامنٹ لیمپ اور تھرمسٹر کو اوہمک یا غیر اوہمک ثابت کرنے کے لیے V-I خصوصیاتی گراف بنا کر واضح کریں۔ || اومیک او غیر اومیک وسیلې څه دي؟ د فلزي هادي، فلامنټ څراغ او ترمیستور د اومیک یا غیر اومیک ثابتولو لپاره د V-I ګراف رسم او تشریح کړئ.
sol Model answer ## Ohmic devices obey Ohm's law, so their V-I graph is a straight line through the origin (resistance constant); non-ohmic devices do not obey it and their V-I graph is curved. A metallic conductor at constant temperature gives a straight line: ohmic. A filament lamp gives a curve that bends towards the voltage axis because the filament heats up and its resistance increases: non-ohmic. A thermistor gives a curve bending towards the current axis because its resistance falls as it heats: non-ohmic.
qp 9 | Explain the concept of electric energy and electric power. What is the commercial unit for the consumption of electric energy? || برقی توانائی اور برقی طاقت کے تصور کی وضاحت کریں۔ برقی توانائی کے استعمال کی تجارتی اکائی کیا ہے؟ || د برېښنايي انرژۍ او برېښنايي توان مفهوم تشریح کړئ. د برېښنايي انرژۍ د مصرف تجارتي واحد څه دی؟
sol Model answer ## Electric energy is the work done by the current: $E = VIt$ in joules. Electric power is the rate of using energy, $P = E/t = VI = I^2R = V^2/R$, measured in watts (W). The commercial unit of electric energy is the kilowatt-hour (kWh), the energy used by a 1 kW appliance in one hour: 1 kWh = $3.6\times10^{6}$ J.
qp 10 | State the functions of live, neutral and earth wires in domestic main supply. || گھریلو مین سپلائی میں لائیو، نیوٹرل اور ارتھ تاروں کے کام بیان کریں۔ || په کورني اصلي رسونه کې د ژوندي، بې طرفه او ځمکې سیمونو دندې ووایئ.
sol Model answer ## The live wire (brown) carries current from the supply to the appliance at high potential through the fuse and switch. The neutral wire (blue) completes the circuit by returning the current to the supply at about zero potential. The earth wire (green/yellow) is connected to the metal case of the appliance and to the ground; if a fault touches the case to the live wire, a large current flows to earth, blowing the fuse and protecting the user from shock.
qp 11 | Explain why the domestic appliances are connected in parallel? || وضاحت کریں کہ گھریلو آلات متوازی کیوں جوڑے جاتے ہیں؟ || تشریح کړئ چې کورنۍ وسیلې ولې موازي تړل کېږي؟
sol Model answer ## Appliances are connected in parallel so that each gets the full supply voltage (220 V), each can be switched on and off independently without affecting the others, and each draws only the current it needs; in series all would share the voltage and all would stop if one failed.
qp 12 | Differentiate between alternating current and direct current. || آلٹرنیٹنگ کرنٹ اور ڈائریکٹ کرنٹ میں فرق بیان کریں۔ || د متناوب او مستقیم جریان ترمنځ توپیر وکړئ.
sol Model answer ## Direct current (DC) flows in one direction only, with constant polarity (cells, batteries, solar cells); its graph against time is a straight line. Alternating current (AC) reverses its direction periodically (50 Hz in Pakistan) and varies sinusoidally; it comes from AC generators and the mains supply. AC is easily changed in voltage by transformers and transmitted over long distances; DC is needed for electronics and battery charging.
