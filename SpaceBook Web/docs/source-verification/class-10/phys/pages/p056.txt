# Unit 12: Geometrical Optics || اکائی 12: جیومیٹریکل آپٹکس || څپرکی 12: جیومیټریکي اپټیکس
p How a human eye works?
@deco Photograph of a human eye behind the unit title
p After studying this unit you should be able to:
slo describe the terms used in reflection including normal, angle of incidence, angle of reflection and state laws of reflection.
slo solve problems of image location by spherical mirrors by using mirror formula.
slo define the terminology for the angle of incidence θᵢ and angle of refraction θᵣ and describe the passage of light through parallel-sided transparent material.
slo solve problems by using the equation sin θᵢ / sin θᵣ = n (refractive index).
slo state the conditions for total internal reflection.
slo describe the passage of light through a glass prism.
slo describe how total internal reflection is used in light propagation through optical fibres.
slo describe how light is refracted through lenses.
slo define power of a lens and its unit.
slo solve problems of image location by lenses using lens formula.
slo define the terms resolving power and magnifying power.
slo draw ray diagram of simple microscope and mention its magnifying power.
slo draw ray diagram of compound microscope and mention its magnifying power.
slo draw ray diagram of a telescope and mention its magnifying power.
slo draw ray diagrams to show the formation of images in the normal eye, a shortsighted eye and a long-sighted eye.
slo describe the correction of short-sight and long-sight.
