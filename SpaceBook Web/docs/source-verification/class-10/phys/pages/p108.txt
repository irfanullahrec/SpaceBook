q 4 | A light ray strikes an air/water surface at an angle of 46° with respect to the normal. The refractive index for water is 1.33. Find the angle of refraction when the direction of the ray is (a) from air to water and (b) from water to air.
sol (a) 32.7° (b) 73.1° ## (a) Air to water: $\sin\theta_2 = \dfrac{\sin 46^\circ}{1.33} = \dfrac{0.7193}{1.33} = 0.5408$, so $\theta_2 = 32.7^\circ$. (b) Water to air: $n_1\sin 46^\circ = n_2\sin\theta_2$ gives $\sin\theta_2 = 1.33\times0.7193 = 0.9567$, so $\theta_2 = 73.1^\circ$ (the critical angle of water is 48.8°, so 46° is below it and the ray emerges).
q 5 | An optical fiber is made from flint glass with index of refraction 1.66 and is surrounded by a cladding made of crown glass with index of refraction 1.52. What is the critical angle?
sol $\theta_c = 66.3^\circ$ ## $\sin\theta_c = \dfrac{n_2}{n_1} = \dfrac{1.52}{1.66} = 0.9157$, so $\theta_c = \sin^{-1}(0.9157) = 66.3^\circ$.
q 6 | Suppose the book page is held 7.50 cm from a convex lens of focal length 10.0 cm and concave lens of focal length −10 cm. What magnification is produced in each case?
sol $m = +4.0$ (convex), $m = +0.57$ (concave) ## Convex lens, $f = 10.0$ cm, $d_o = 7.50$ cm: $\dfrac{1}{d_i} = \dfrac{1}{10.0} - \dfrac{1}{7.50} = -\dfrac{1}{30.0}$, so $d_i = -30.0$ cm (virtual) and $m = -\dfrac{d_i}{d_o} = \dfrac{30.0}{7.50} = +4.0$. Concave lens, $f = -10.0$ cm: $\dfrac{1}{d_i} = -\dfrac{1}{10.0} - \dfrac{1}{7.50} = -\dfrac{7}{30}$, so $d_i = -4.29$ cm and $m = \dfrac{4.29}{7.50} = +0.57$.
q 7 | Gulalai is viewing a flea using a magnifier with f = 3.0 cm. If her near point is at N= 25 cm then calculate the maximum magnification she can get.
sol $M \approx 9.3$ ## For the image formed at the near point, $M = 1 + \dfrac{N}{f} = 1 + \dfrac{25}{3.0} = 9.3$.
q 8 | A telescope has a magnification of 40.0 and a length of 1230 mm. What are the focal lengths of the objective and eyepiece?
sol $f_o = 1200$ mm, $f_e = 30$ mm ## $M = \dfrac{f_o}{f_e} = 40$ and $L = f_o + f_e = 1230$ mm. So $f_e + 40 f_e = 1230$, giving $f_e = 30$ mm and $f_o = 40\times30 = 1200$ mm.
