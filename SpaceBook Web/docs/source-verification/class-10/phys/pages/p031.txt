# Unit 11: Sound
slo Explain that sound is produced by vibrating bodies and requires a material medium.
slo Describe the longitudinal nature of sound waves in terms of compressions and rarefactions.
slo Define loudness, pitch, quality (timbre) and intensity of sound.
slo Explain the factors affecting loudness (amplitude, surface area, distance).
slo Describe sound intensity level and use the decibel scale.
slo Explain the reflection of sound (echo) and calculate speed/distance using echo method.
slo Define audible frequency range and describe applications of ultrasound in medicine and industry.
