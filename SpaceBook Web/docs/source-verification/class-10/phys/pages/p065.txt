sol Model answers ## 1: No. Water as a whole is neutral; its protons and electrons move together at the same speed, so there is no net flow of charge and hence no current. 2: In parallel, because each headlight has its own branch; a break in one branch does not stop the current in the other. 3: Connect three 300 Ω resistors in parallel: $1/R_{eq} = 3/300$, so $R_{eq} = 100\ \Omega$. 4: In parallel: each bulb gets the full supply voltage, so each draws more current and glows brightly; in series the voltage is shared, the current is smaller and all bulbs are dim. 5: When the bulb is cold its filament resistance is low, so a large current flows at switch-on; this surge heats and stresses the filament, which then breaks. 6: Both feet of the bird touch the same wire at (almost) the same potential, so there is no potential difference across its body and no current flows through it. 7: Insulated shoes give a high resistance between the body and earth, and keeping one hand away stops current flowing from one hand through the chest (heart) to the other hand. 8: Wet skin and water lower the body's resistance and the water can connect the body to earth; a fault in the lamp or switch could then drive a large, possibly fatal, current through the body. 9: So that when a switch is off or a fuse/breaker has operated, the appliance is disconnected from the live wire and is no longer at a high potential; on the neutral wire the appliance would stay live and could give a fatal shock. || 1: نہیں؛ پانی مجموعی طور پر نیوٹرل ہے اور اس کے پروٹون اور الیکٹران ساتھ ساتھ ایک ہی رفتار سے حرکت
