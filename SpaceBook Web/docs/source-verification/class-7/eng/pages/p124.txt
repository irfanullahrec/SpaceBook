## The Danyang-Kunshan Grand Bridge-China: || دانیانگ-کنشان گرینڈ برج، چین: || د دانیانګ-کونشان لوی پل، چین:
p The 164.8 km long Danyang-Kunshan Grand Bridge is a railway bridge used for carrying a considerable part of the Beijing–Shanghai High-Speed Railway. The bridge is a viaduct type and a really long kind of bridge, with the total length estimated to be exceeding 100 miles. The bridge is considered to be one of the world's few engineering marvels presently. The bridge runs roughly parallel to the Yangtze River, about 8 to 80 km (5 to 50 miles) south of the river. It passes through the northern edges of population centers (from west to east) beginning in Danyang, Changzhou, Wuxi, Suzhou, and ending in Kunshan.
