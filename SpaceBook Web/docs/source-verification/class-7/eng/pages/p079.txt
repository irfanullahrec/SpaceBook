qp b | What do you think will happen if all these animals go extinct? || آپ کے خیال میں اگر یہ تمام جانور معدوم ہو جائیں تو کیا ہوگا؟ || ستاسو په نظر که دا ټول ژوي ورک شي څه به وشي؟
sol Food chains would break, nature would lose its balance, and future generations would never see these animals.
qp c | What will be the effect of the extinction of these animals in Pakistan and in the world? || پاکستان اور دنیا میں ان جانوروں کی معدومی کا کیا اثر ہوگا؟ || په پاکستان او نړۍ کې د دې ژویو د ورکېدو اغېز به څه وي؟
sol Pakistan would lose part of its natural heritage (e.g. its national animal), ecosystems would be harmed, and the world's biodiversity would become poorer.

# Use of Language || زبان کا استعمال || د ژبې کارونه
table
th | Tense || زمانہ || مهال | Structure and Example
tr | Simple Future Tense || مستقبل مطلق || ساده راتلونکی | Structure: Will/shall + verb. Example: We **will complete** the campaign to preserve the endangered animals. The government **shall assist** in supporting the project.
tr | Future Continuous Tense || مستقبل جاری || روان راتلونکی | Structure: Will be + present participle; Am/is/are + going to be + present participle. Example: Indus dolphins **will be extinct** if we don't do something. Markhors **are going to be endangered** if not given proper protection.
tr | Future Perfect Tense || مستقبل تام || بشپړ راتلونکی | Structure: Will have + past participle; Am/is/are + going to have + past participle. Example: I **will have played** the card of protecting the animals. We **are going to have played** our card in protecting the animals.
endtable
@issue The future continuous examples ("will be extinct", "are going to be endangered") use adjectives, not present participles; kept as printed.
