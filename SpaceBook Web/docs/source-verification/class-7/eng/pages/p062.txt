## Comprehension Questions || فہم کے سوالات || د پوهې پوښتنې
q 2 | Answer these questions. || ان سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || دې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ.
qp a | Why do Muslims not eat or drink anything during the month of Ramadan? || مسلمان رمضان کے مہینے میں کچھ کیوں نہیں کھاتے پیتے؟ || مسلمانان د روژې په میاشت کې ولې هېڅ نه خوري او نه څښي؟
sol Because they fast during daylight hours in the holy month of Ramadan, as Allah has commanded; Eid-ul-Fitr celebrates the end of this month of fasting. ## p. 55. || ص 55۔ || مخ 55.
qp b | Why is Eid-ul-Adha known as the 'Festival of Sacrifice'? || عید الاضحیٰ کو 'قربانی کا تہوار' کیوں کہا جاتا ہے؟ || عید الاضحی ته ولې 'د قربانۍ اختر' وايي؟
sol Because it remembers Hazrat Ibrahim's (علیہ السلام) readiness to sacrifice his son Hazrat Ismail (علیہ السلام), and families sacrifice an animal and share its meat.
qp c | How do Chinese people celebrate their new year? Explain in detail. || چینی لوگ اپنا نیا سال کیسے مناتے ہیں؟ تفصیل سے بیان کریں۔ || چینایي خلک خپل نوی کال څنګه لمانځي؟ په تفصیل یې بیان کړئ.
sol For fifteen days from the new moon to the full moon: they visit family, children get money, red lanterns are hung, traditional food is cooked, firecrackers are let off, and it ends with the Lantern Festival and dragon dances. ## pp. 51, 55–56. || ص 51، 55–56۔ || مخ 51، 55–56.
qp d | State some of the popular traditions of Christmas. || کرسمس کی کچھ مقبول روایات بیان کریں۔ || د کرسمس ځینې مشهور دودونه بیان کړئ.
sol Giving presents (from Santa Claus), sending cards, parties with good food, singing carols, and decorating a Christmas tree with lights and ornaments.
qp e | How do Hindus celebrate their new year? || ہندو اپنا نیا سال کیسے مناتے ہیں؟ || هندوان خپل نوی کال څنګه لمانځي؟
sol With Diwali, the five-day festival of lights: visiting family, giving gifts, eating sweets, wearing new clothes, lighting oil lamps, setting off firecrackers and holding religious ceremonies.
qp f | What do all festivals teach us? || تمام تہوار ہمیں کیا سکھاتے ہیں؟ || ټول اخترونه موږ ته څه راښيي؟
sol To share happiness, love our families and friends, give to others (especially the poor) and respect different cultures.
