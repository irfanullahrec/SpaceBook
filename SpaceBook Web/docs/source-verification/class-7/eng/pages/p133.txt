sol Model answer: A Robot at Home. If I had a robot, it would clean my room, wash the dishes and water the plants. It could even carry my school bag and remind me of my homework. I would have a lot more time to read, play and spend with my family. My mother would be happier because she would not have to do every task. However, there are problems. A robot costs a lot of money and needs electricity. If it did all my schoolwork I would not learn anything. So I think a robot is a good helper for chores, but I must still do my own studying. ## Introduction, advantages, disadvantages, conclusion; one possible answer.
