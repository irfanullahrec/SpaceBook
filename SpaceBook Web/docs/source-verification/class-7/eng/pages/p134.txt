# Glossary || فرہنگ || لغت نامه
- **Abundant** (n.) - Existing or occurring in large quantities; plentiful. || **Abundant** — وافر؛ بڑی مقدار میں موجود۔ || **Abundant** — ډېر؛ په لوی مقدار کې شته.
- **Adjacent** (adj.) - Next to or adjoining something else. || **Adjacent** — ملحق؛ کسی چیز کے ساتھ لگا ہوا۔ || **Adjacent** — نږدې؛ له بل شي سره نښتی.
- **Aimlessly** (adv.) - Without a clear purpose or direction; wandering. || **Aimlessly** — بے مقصد؛ بغیر سمت کے۔ || **Aimlessly** — بې موخې؛ پرته له لوري.
- **Ancient** (adj.) - Extremely old; belonging to the distant past. || **Ancient** — قدیم؛ بہت پرانا۔ || **Ancient** — لرغونی؛ ډېر زوړ.
- **Ascend** (v.) - To go up or climb. || **Ascend** — اوپر چڑھنا۔ || **Ascend** — پورته ختل.
- **Astonishment** (n.) - A feeling of great surprise or wonder. || **Astonishment** — شدید حیرت۔ || **Astonishment** — ډېره حیرانتیا.
- **Aurora** (n.) - A natural light display in the Earth's sky, predominantly seen in high-latitude regions. || **Aurora** — قطبی روشنی؛ آسمان میں قدرتی روشنیوں کا منظر۔ || **Aurora** — قطبي رڼا؛ په اسمان کې د طبیعي رڼاوو ننداره.
- **Bridge** (n.) - A structure built to span a physical obstacle like a river, road, or railway. || **Bridge** — پل۔ || **Bridge** — پل.
- **Bustling** (adj.) - Full of activity, noise, and energy. || **Bustling** — گہما گہمی سے بھرپور۔ || **Bustling** — ګڼه ګوڼه او فعاله.
- **Buttermilk** (n.) - The liquid remaining after butter has been churned from cream; often used in cooking. || **Buttermilk** — لسی/چھاچھ۔ || **Buttermilk** — شلومبې.
- **Canyon** (n.) - A deep, narrow valley with steep sides, often with a river or stream running through it. || **Canyon** — گہری تنگ گھاٹی۔ || **Canyon** — ژوره تنګه دره.
- **Catastrophic** (adj.) - Involving or causing a sudden and widespread disaster. || **Catastrophic** — تباہ کن۔ || **Catastrophic** — ویجاړوونکی.
- **Ceremony** (n.) - A formal event or ritual often involving a series of prescribed actions or speeches. || **Ceremony** — تقریب؛ رسم۔ || **Ceremony** — مراسم.
- **Chimney** (n.) - A vertical structure, typically made of brick or metal, used to carry smoke and gases from a fireplace or stove out of a building. || **Chimney** — چمنی۔ || **Chimney** — لوګیکښ.
- **Clamber** (v.) - To climb or move with difficulty, typically using one's hands and feet. || **Clamber** — ہاتھ پاؤں کے سہارے مشکل سے چڑھنا۔ || **Clamber** — په لاسونو او پښو په سختۍ ختل.
- **Collide** (v.) - Hit by accident when moving. || **Collide** — ٹکرانا۔ || **Collide** — ټکر کول.
- **Combination** (n.) - The act or process of combining two or more things to create a unified whole. || **Combination** — امتزاج؛ ملاپ۔ || **Combination** — ترکیب.
- **Communication** (n.) - The exchange of information or ideas between people, often through speech, writing, or other means. || **Communication** — ابلاغ؛ رابطہ۔ || **Communication** — اړیکه.
- **Concrete** (n.) - A building material made of cement, water, and aggregates like sand and gravel. || **Concrete** — کنکریٹ۔ || **Concrete** — کانکرېټ.
- **Congested** (adj.) - Blocked or overcrowded, typically used to describe traffic or areas with too many people or vehicles. || **Congested** — گنجان؛ رکا ہوا۔ || **Congested** — ګڼ؛ بند.
- **Consumption** (n.) - The act of using or using up something, often referring to goods, services, or resources. || **Consumption** — صرف؛ استعمال۔ || **Consumption** — مصرف.
- **Cornbread** (n.) - A type of bread made from cornmeal, often associated with Southern cuisine in the United States. || **Cornbread** — مکئی کی روٹی۔ || **Cornbread** — د جوارو ډوډۍ.
- **Delicate** (adj.) - Easily damaged, sensitive, or requiring careful handling. || **Delicate** — نازک۔ || **Delicate** — نازک.
- **Delightful** (adj.) - Extremely pleasing, charming, or enjoyable. || **Delightful** — نہایت خوشگوار۔ || **Delightful** — ډېر خوندور.
- **Determine** (v.) - To ascertain, decide, or find out through investigation or analysis. || **Determine** — طے کرنا؛ معلوم کرنا۔ || **Determine** — ټاکل؛ معلومول.
- **Ditches** (n.) - Narrow channels or trenches often dug in the ground to convey water or for drainage. || **Ditches** — کھائیاں؛ نالیاں۔ || **Ditches** — ویالې؛ کندې.
- **Distraction** (n.) - A thing that prevents someone from concentrating on something else or causes diversion. || **Distraction** — توجہ ہٹانے والی چیز۔ || **Distraction** — پام اړوونکی شی.
- **Drown** (v.) - To die through submersion in water; to be overwhelmed or suffocated by a large quantity of something. || **Drown** — ڈوبنا۔ || **Drown** — ډوبېدل.
- **Driverless** (adj.) - Autonomous or self-driving usually referring to vehicles that can operate without human intervention. || **Driverless** — بغیر ڈرائیور کے۔ || **Driverless** — بې چلوونکي.
- **Elderly** (adj.) - Old or aging, typically used to describe people of advanced age. || **Elderly** — بزرگ؛ عمر رسیدہ۔ || **Elderly** — زاړه؛ مشران.
- **Elevation** (n.) - The height or altitude above a given level, often referring to the distance above sea level. || **Elevation** — بلندی۔ || **Elevation** — لوړوالی.
- **Endangered** (adj.) - In danger of becoming extinct or no longer existing. || **Endangered** — معدومی کے خطرے سے دوچار۔ || **Endangered** — د ورکېدو په خطر کې.
- **Evaporate** (v.) - To turn from a liquid or solid state into vapor or gas. || **Evaporate** — بخارات بن کر اڑ جانا۔ || **Evaporate** — تبخیرېدل.
