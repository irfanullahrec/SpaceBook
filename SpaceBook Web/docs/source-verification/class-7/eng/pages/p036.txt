qp 1 | A walk that takes half an hour → __Half-an-hour walk__
sol a half-an-hour walk (given)
qp 2 | A mountain that stood more than twenty thousand feet → ____
sol a twenty-thousand-foot mountain
qp 3 | A waterfall that is the largest in the world → ____
sol the world's-largest waterfall (a world-famous waterfall)
qp 4 | Northern lights that make your mouth water → ____
sol mouth-watering Northern Lights
qp 5 | A grand canyon that is famous through out the world → ____
sol a world-famous Grand Canyon
q 2 | Use the following compound adjectives in sentences. || درج ذیل مرکب صفات کو جملوں میں استعمال کریں۔ || لاندې مرکب صفتونه په جملو کې وکاروئ.
