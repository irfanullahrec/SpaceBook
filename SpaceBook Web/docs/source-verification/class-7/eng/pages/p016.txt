Use a dictionary to look for the denotative meanings and read the text for the connotative meanings. || کالم 1 کے الفاظ پڑھیں اور ان کے ضمنی (connotative) اور لغوی (denotative) معانی لکھیں۔ لغوی معانی کے لیے لغت دیکھیں اور ضمنی معانی کے لیے متن پڑھیں۔ || په لومړۍ ستنه کې کلمې ولولئ او د هغو ضمني (connotative) او لغوي (denotative) ماناوې ولیکئ. د لغوي ماناوو لپاره قاموس وګورئ او د ضمني ماناوو لپاره متن ولولئ.
sol thrive: meaning: grow strongly and healthily; connotation: Nature is doing well and full of life; falter: meaning: lose strength; move unsteadily; connotation: Nature becomes weak or damaged; starved: meaning: made to suffer or die from lack of food; connotation: Nature let whole species die out; determine: meaning: decide or settle; connotation: your own actions will decide your future; fate: meaning: what happens to someone in the end; connotation: the future of human beings; evolve: meaning: develop gradually; connotation: Nature is ready to change and adapt; typhoons: meaning: violent tropical storms; connotation: the sky's anger shown in storms; tornadoes: meaning: violent spinning columns of wind; connotation: the sky losing control; unpredictable: meaning: not able to be known in advance; connotation: weather that changes without warning; weathered: meaning: came safely through a difficulty; connotation: the sky has survived damage before; congested: meaning: blocked or overcrowded; connotation: the sky is choked with smoke and gases ## Denotative meaning = the dictionary meaning; connotative meaning = the feeling or idea the word carries in the text.
