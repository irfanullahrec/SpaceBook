p Building an airport on water in itself was one of the few challenges that the engineers faced. Another big challenge was the condition of the ground below the water. The underwater clay was the soft clay also known as alluvial clay, which holds 70% of water. To deal with this, engineers used sand drains. Constructing a sea wall around the sand drain was another challenge, else the chances of the airport collapsing were quite high. However, engineers and technicians made the impossible possible and airport was operational in 1994.
