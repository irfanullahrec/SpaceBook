exercise 2 Use of Language: Adjectives
q 1 | Underline the adjectives in the given paragraph and identify them as definitive, indefinite or distributive adjective. || دیے گئے پیراگراف میں صفات کے نیچے لکیر لگائیں اور انہیں معین، غیر معین یا تقسیمی صفت کے طور پر پہچانیں۔ || په ورکړل شوي پراګراف کې صفتونو لاندې کرښه وکاږئ او ټاکلي، ناټاکلي یا وېشونکي صفت په توګه یې وپېژنئ.
qp a | Some call me Nature. ____
sol Some — indefinite
qp b | Others call me Mother Nature. ____
sol Others — indefinite (used here as a pronoun)
qp c | I've been here for over four and a half billion years. ____
sol four and a half (billion) — definitive
qp d | Everyone depends on me. ____
sol Everyone — distributive (used here as a pronoun)
qp e | I've been here for aeons. ____
sol No quantitative adjective in this sentence.
qp f | I have fed every species greater than you. ____
sol every — distributive
qp g | They all can take you or leave you. ____
sol all — indefinite
qp h | One way or the other, your actions will determine your fate, not mine. ____
sol One — definitive
q 2 | Use the following quantitative adjectives in sentences. || درج ذیل مقداری صفات کو جملوں میں استعمال کریں۔ || لاندې مقداري صفتونه په جملو کې وکاروئ.
m enough · every · third · fifty · none · most
sol We have enough water for the trip. · Every tree gives us oxygen. · She came third in the race. · Fifty birds sat on the wire. · None of the rivers was clean. · Most children love the mountains. ## Any correct sentences are acceptable. || کوئی بھی درست جملے قابلِ قبول ہیں۔ || هره سمه جمله د منلو وړ ده.
q 3 | a. Identify the adjective in each sentence and write down its position (before or after the noun). || a. ہر جملے میں صفت پہچانیں اور اس کا مقام لکھیں (اسم سے پہلے یا بعد)۔ || a. په هره جمله کې صفت وپېژنئ او ځای یې ولیکئ (له نوم مخکې یا وروسته).
qp i | The swift bird flew across the sky. Adjective: ____ Position: ____ || تیز رفتار پرندہ آسمان میں اڑ گیا۔ || چټکه مرغۍ د اسمان پر سر والوته.
sol Adjective: swift; Position: before the noun "bird"
qp ii | The tall trees provided shade on a hot day. Adjective: ____ Position: ____ || اونچے درختوں نے گرم دن میں سایہ دیا۔ || لوړو ونو په ګرمه ورځ سیوری ورکړ.
sol Adjectives: tall, hot; Position: before the nouns "trees" and "day"
qp iii | The roaring waves crashed against the rocky shore. Adjective: ____ Position: ____ || گرجتی لہریں پتھریلے ساحل سے ٹکرائیں۔ || غړمپېدونکې څپې له ډبرین ساحل سره ولګېدې.
sol Adjectives: roaring, rocky; Position: before the nouns "waves" and "shore"
qp iv | A gentle breeze blew through the open meadow. Adjective: ____ Position: ____ || ہلکی ہوا کھلے سبزہ زار میں چلی۔ || نرم باد د پرانیستي څړځای له منځه تېر شو.
sol Adjectives: gentle, open; Position: before the nouns "breeze" and "meadow"
@issue In Q1 the eight items are separate sentences, not "the given paragraph". "Others" and "Everyone" are pronouns here; the answers note this.
