q 1 | **Your turn to Write:** Prepare a letter to the Wildlife Conservation Organisations in Pakistan and give your concrete suggestions on how to save these beautiful animals before they become extinct. || **اب آپ لکھیں:** پاکستان میں جنگلی حیات کے تحفظ کی تنظیموں کو خط لکھیں اور ان خوبصورت جانوروں کو معدوم ہونے سے پہلے بچانے کے لیے ٹھوس تجاویز دیں۔ || **اوس تاسو ولیکئ:** په پاکستان کې د ځنګلي ژوند ساتنې سازمانونو ته لیک ولیکئ او د دې ښکلو ژویو د ورکېدو مخکې د ژغورنې لپاره مشخص وړاندیزونه ورکړئ.
sol Model answer: To the Director, Wildlife Conservation Organisation, Islamabad. 5 May 2025. Dear Sir, I am a student of Class 7 and I am worried that many beautiful animals of Pakistan, such as the snow leopard, markhor and Indus dolphin, may become extinct. I request you to take the following steps: stop illegal hunting and trade with strict punishments; protect forests and rivers where these animals live; create more national parks and wildlife sanctuaries; and run awareness programmes in schools and villages. I also suggest that local people should be given jobs as guards so that they help protect wildlife. Thank you for your valuable work. Yours sincerely, Ahmed. ## A formal letter with specific, practical suggestions.
