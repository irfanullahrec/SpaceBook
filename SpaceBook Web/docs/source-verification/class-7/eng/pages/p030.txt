sol Model answer: In the forest I would hear a soft, gentle rustle as the wind brushed through the tall leaves. Birds chirp cheerfully overhead and a woodpecker taps loudly on a hollow trunk. Somewhere far away a stream babbles and splashes over smooth stones. Dry twigs crackle under my slow steps and a hidden insect hums endlessly. Every sound is calm and peaceful, and after listening for a while I would feel relaxed and happy, as if the whole forest were whispering a lullaby to me. ## Uses adjectives and verbs that show feeling; one possible answer.
