After breakfast of fruit and honey I run with Bagheera, the panther, along the river. Today we hide from Shere Khan, the tiger, who hunts me. This is a challenge because I must stay silent and clever. In the afternoon I help my wolf brothers find food. I learn that courage, friendship and respect for the jungle keep us safe. At night I sleep under the stars and feel proud of my family. ## First person, past or present tense, describing adventures, challenges and lessons; one possible answer.
