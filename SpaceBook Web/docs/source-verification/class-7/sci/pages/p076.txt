qp 1 | Differentiate between heat and temperature on the basis of kinetic molecular theory. || حرکی سالماتی نظریے کی بنیاد پر حرارت اور درجۂ حرارت میں فرق کریں۔ || د حرکي مالیکولي تیورۍ پر بنسټ د تودوخې او د تودوخې درجې توپیر وکړئ.
sol Temperature is the average kinetic energy of the particles — it tells how hot a body is and does not depend on the amount of matter (unit K). Heat is the energy transferred from a hotter to a colder body because of a temperature difference; the total thermal energy depends on both temperature and amount of matter (unit J). Half a cup of tea at the same temperature has half the heat.
qp 2 | Solids are good conductors but gases are not. Explain in detail on the basis of kinetic molecular theory. || ٹھوس اچھے موصل ہیں لیکن گیسیں نہیں۔ حرکی سالماتی نظریے کی بنیاد پر تفصیل سے وضاحت کریں۔ || جامدات ښه لېږدوونکي دي خو ګازونه نه. د حرکي مالیکولي تیورۍ پر بنسټ په تفصیل تشریح کړئ.
sol In solids particles are packed very close, so a vibrating hot particle quickly passes energy to its neighbours (and metals also have free electrons). In gases particles are far apart, so collisions are rare and energy passes slowly — gases such as air are insulators.
qp 3 | What is conduction? How is heat transferred by conduction? Why are metals good conductors of heat? || ایصال کیا ہے؟ ایصال سے حرارت کیسے منتقل ہوتی ہے؟ دھاتیں حرارت کی اچھی موصل کیوں ہیں؟ || لېږد (کنډکشن) څه دی؟ تودوخه د لېږد له لارې څنګه لېږدول کېږي؟ فلزات ولې د تودوخې ښه لېږدوونکي دي؟
sol Conduction is transfer of heat from hot to cold by vibrations of particles. Heated particles at one end vibrate harder, collide with neighbours and pass energy along the rod. Metals have free electrons that move fast and carry energy quickly through the metal.
qp 4 | What is convection? How is heat transferred by convection? || حمل کیا ہے؟ حمل سے حرارت کیسے منتقل ہوتی ہے؟ || جریان (کنوېکشن) څه دی؟ تودوخه د جریان له لارې څنګه لېږدول کېږي؟
sol Convection is transfer of heat by the actual movement of particles in fluids. Heated fluid expands, becomes less dense and rises; cooler, denser fluid sinks to take its place, forming convection currents that heat the whole fluid (e.g. water in a pot, sea breeze).
