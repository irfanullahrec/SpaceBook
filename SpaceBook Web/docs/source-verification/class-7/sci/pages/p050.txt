table Answer (a) || جواب (a) || ځواب (a)
th | Box | Definition
tr | Solution | A homogeneous mixture of two or more substances (solute + solvent).
tr | Dilute solution | A solution with a relatively small amount of solute dissolved.
tr | Concentrated solution | A solution with a relatively large amount of solute dissolved.
tr | Solvent | The substance in greater amount in which the solute dissolves.
tr | Solute | The substance in less amount that dissolves.
tr | Saturated solution | Holds the maximum amount of solute at a given temperature.
tr | Unsaturated solution | The solute has completely dissolved and more can still dissolve.
endtable
sol (b) 1. Nature of solute and solvent — like dissolves like. 2. Temperature — higher temperature increases the solubility of solids and liquids but decreases that of gases. 3. Pressure — higher pressure increases the solubility of gases (little effect on solids and liquids).
