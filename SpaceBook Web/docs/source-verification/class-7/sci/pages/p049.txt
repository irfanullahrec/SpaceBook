qp 1 | What is the difference between saturated and unsaturated solution? || سیر شدہ اور غیر سیر شدہ محلول میں کیا فرق ہے؟ || د مشبع او غیر مشبع محلول توپیر څه دی؟
sol A saturated solution holds the maximum amount of solute at that temperature — extra solute stays undissolved. An unsaturated solution can still dissolve more solute.

sol Yes — the sugar and coffee dissolve completely and evenly in the water, making a homogeneous mixture. Solutes: sugar and coffee; solvent: hot water.
qp 3 | How does an increase in temperature speed up the dissolving process? || درجۂ حرارت میں اضافہ حل ہونے کے عمل کو کیسے تیز کرتا ہے؟ || د تودوخې زیاتوالی د حل کېدو بهیر څنګه چټکوي؟
sol Heat makes the solvent particles move faster, so they hit and pull apart the solute particles more quickly and mix them through the solvent.
qp 4 | What happens when a solution becomes supersaturated? || جب محلول فوق سیر شدہ ہو جائے تو کیا ہوتا ہے؟ || کله چې محلول فوق مشبع شي څه کېږي؟
sol It holds more solute than it normally can; it is unstable, and on cooling (or when a seed crystal is added) the extra solute comes out as crystals.
qp 5 | In gases, solubility increases with increase in pressure. Give a daily life example. || گیسوں میں دباؤ بڑھنے سے حل پذیری بڑھتی ہے۔ روزمرہ زندگی سے مثال دیں۔ || په ګازونو کې د فشار په زیاتېدو حل کېدل زیاتېږي. د ورځني ژوند بېلګه ورکړئ.
sol Soft drinks: CO₂ is dissolved in them under high pressure; when the bottle is opened the pressure drops and the gas bubbles out.
qp 6 | How does the nature of solutes have an effect on the solubility? || حل پذیر کی نوعیت حل پذیری پر کیسے اثر انداز ہوتی ہے؟ || د منحلې طبیعت پر حل کېدو څه اغېز لري؟
sol "Like dissolves like": a solute dissolves well in a solvent with similar chemical nature (salt and sugar in water), but not in an unlike one (oil in water).
qp 7 | Khushi added some olive oil to a cup of water. Why was a layer of oil formed at the surface of water? || خوشی نے ایک کپ پانی میں زیتون کا تیل ڈالا۔ پانی کی سطح پر تیل کی تہہ کیوں بنی؟ || خوشي یوې پیالې اوبو ته د زیتون غوړي واچول. د اوبو پر سر د غوړیو پوړ ولې جوړ شو؟
sol Oil and water have different chemical natures, so oil does not dissolve. Water molecules attract each other and pack closer (water is denser), so water stays below and oil floats on top.
qp 8 | How does increase in temperature affect the solubility of gases? || درجۂ حرارت میں اضافہ گیسوں کی حل پذیری پر کیسے اثر ڈالتا ہے؟ || د تودوخې زیاتوالی د ګازونو پر حل کېدو څنګه اغېز کوي؟
sol It decreases it — less gas dissolves at a higher temperature (e.g. a warm soda goes flat faster).
q D | Long questions || طویل سوالات || اوږدې پوښتنې
qp 1 | What is the effect of increasing temperature on the solubility of solids in liquids? || مائع میں ٹھوس کی حل پذیری پر درجۂ حرارت بڑھانے کا کیا اثر ہے؟ || په مایعاتو کې د جامداتو پر حل کېدو د تودوخې د زیاتولو اغېز څه دی؟
sol Generally, solubility of solids increases with temperature: more sugar dissolves in hot water than in cold water. Particles of a hot solvent move faster and have more space between them, so more solute can fit and it dissolves faster.
qp 2 | Explain the formation of saturated and supersaturated solutions. || سیر شدہ اور فوق سیر شدہ محلول کے بننے کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د مشبع او فوق مشبع محلولونو جوړېدل تشریح کړئ.
sol Keep adding solute to a solvent at a fixed temperature: when no more dissolves and extra stays at the bottom, the solution is saturated (e.g. 36 g NaCl in 100 mL water). If the solution is heated, more solute dissolves; when it is cooled carefully it holds more than it normally can — a supersaturated solution, which is unstable and forms crystals (e.g. rock candy).
