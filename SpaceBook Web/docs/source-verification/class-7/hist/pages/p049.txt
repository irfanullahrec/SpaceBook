p Shirkuh served Nur al-Din Mahmud as commander of the expeditionary force in Egypt. After death of Shirkuh in 1169, power passed to his brother Ayyub's son, Salah al-Din Yusuf (Saladin) as both commander of the Syrian troops in Egypt and vizier of the Fatimid caliph there.
