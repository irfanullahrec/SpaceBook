example 3 | Simplify $3(14x^2 \div 6x) + 2(x + 5)$ || سادہ کریں $3(14x^2 \div 6x) + 2(x + 5)$ || ساده کړئ $3(14x^2 \div 6x) + 2(x + 5)$

eq \begin{aligned} &= 3\left(\frac{14x^2}{6x}\right) + 2x + 10 && \leftarrow \text{Solve the operation within parentheses} \\ &= 3\left(\frac{7x}{3}\right) + 2x + 10 \\ &= 7x + 2x + 10 && \leftarrow \text{Simplify the like terms} \\ &= 9x + 10 \end{aligned}
