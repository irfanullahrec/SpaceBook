example 1 | Verify the distributive property. $\left(\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{7}\right) \times \frac{7}{11} = \left(\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{7}{11}\right) + \left(\frac{2}{7} \times \frac{7}{11}\right)$ || تقسیمی خاصیت کی تصدیق کریں۔ $\left(\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{7}\right) \times \frac{7}{11} = \left(\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{7}{11}\right) + \left(\frac{2}{7} \times \frac{7}{11}\right)$ || توزیعي ځانګړتیا تایید کړئ. $\left(\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{7}\right) \times \frac{7}{11} = \left(\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{7}{11}\right) + \left(\frac{2}{7} \times \frac{7}{11}\right)$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
p **LHS** || **بایاں طرف (LHS)** || **کیڼ اړخ (LHS)**
eq \left(\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{7}\right) \times \frac{7}{11} = \left(\frac{21 + 16}{56}\right) \times \frac{7}{11} = \frac{37}{56} \times \frac{7}{11} = \frac{37 \times 1}{8 \times 11} = \frac{37}{88}
p **RHS** || **دایاں طرف (RHS)** || **ښي اړخ (RHS)**
eq \left(\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{7}{11}\right) + \left(\frac{2}{7} \times \frac{7}{11}\right) = \left(\frac{3 \times 7}{8 \times 11}\right) + \left(\frac{2 \times 7}{7 \times 11}\right) = \frac{21}{88} + \frac{2}{11} = \frac{21 + 16}{88} = \frac{37}{88}
m LHS = RHS
endexample

p Similarly, multiplying a rational number by a group of rational numbers subtracted together is the same as doing each multiplication separately and then subtracting their products. If a, b, c are three rational numbers then: || اسی طرح کسی ناطق عدد کو تفریق کیے گئے ناطق اعداد کے ایک گروہ سے ضرب دینا ویسا ہی ہے جیسے ہر ضرب الگ الگ کر کے پھر ان کے حاصلِ ضرب تفریق کرنا۔ اگر a، b، c تین ناطق اعداد ہوں تو: || په همدې ډول، د یوه ناطق عدد ضربول د ناطق عددونو په یوه تفریق شوې ډله کې، همغه دی لکه هر ضرب جلا جلا کول او بیا د هغوی حاصل ضربونه تفریق کول. که a، b، c درې ناطق عددونه وي نو:
eq (a - b) \times c = (a \times c) - (b \times c)
example 2 | Verify that: $\left(\frac{5}{9} - \frac{12}{13}\right) \times \frac{7}{10} = \left(\frac{5}{9} \times \frac{7}{10}\right) - \left(\frac{12}{13} \times \frac{7}{10}\right)$ || تصدیق کریں کہ: $\left(\frac{5}{9} - \frac{12}{13}\right) \times \frac{7}{10} = \left(\frac{5}{9} \times \frac{7}{10}\right) - \left(\frac{12}{13} \times \frac{7}{10}\right)$ || تایید کړئ چې: $\left(\frac{5}{9} - \frac{12}{13}\right) \times \frac{7}{10} = \left(\frac{5}{9} \times \frac{7}{10}\right) - \left(\frac{12}{13} \times \frac{7}{10}\right)$
p **Solution:** || **حل:** || **حل:**
eq \text{LHS} = \left(\frac{5}{9} - \frac{12}{13}\right) \times \frac{7}{10} = \left(\frac{65 - 108}{117}\right) \times \frac{7}{10} = \left(\frac{-43}{117}\right) \times \frac{7}{10} = \frac{-301}{1170}
eq \text{RHS} = \left(\frac{5}{9} \times \frac{7}{10}\right) - \left(\frac{12}{13} \times \frac{7}{10}\right) = \left(\frac{5 \times 7}{9 \times 10}\right) - \left(\frac{12 \times 7}{13 \times 10}\right) = \frac{35}{90} - \frac{84}{130} = \frac{-301}{1170}
endexample
