# Important Formulas || اہم فارمولے || مهم فورمولونه
eq \text{Profit} = \text{selling price} - \text{cost price} \qquad \text{Profit} = \text{S.P} - \text{C.P}
eq \text{Loss} = \text{cost price} - \text{selling price} \qquad \text{Loss} = \text{C.P} - \text{S.P}
eq \text{Profit \%} = \dfrac{\text{Profit}}{\text{cost price}} \times 100\% \qquad \text{Loss \%} = \dfrac{\text{Loss}}{\text{cost price}} \times 100\%
eq \text{Discount} = \text{M.P} - \text{S.P} \qquad \text{Percentage Discount} = \dfrac{\text{Discount}}{\text{M.P}} \times 100\%
eq (a+b)^2 = a^2 + b^2 + 2ab \qquad (a-b)^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \qquad (a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2
eq \pi = \dfrac{\text{circumference}}{\text{diameter}} = \dfrac{C}{d}
eq \text{Circumference} = \pi \times \text{diameter}, \quad C = \pi d \qquad \text{Diameter} = 2 \times \text{radius}
eq \text{Area of a circle} = \pi r^2 \qquad \text{Circumference} = 2\pi r
eq \text{Total surface area of a cylinder} = 2\pi r(h + r)
eq \text{Area of each flat face of a cylinder} = \pi r^2 \qquad \text{Area of the curved face of a cylinder} = 2\pi r h
eq \text{Volume of a cylinder} = \pi r^2 h
eq \text{Total surface area of a rectangular prism} = 2 \times [(l \times w) + (l \times h) + (w \times h)]
eq \text{Area of cube} = 6 \times \ell^2 \qquad \text{Area of Square prism} = 6 \times \ell^2
eq \text{Total surface area of a right prism} = bh + (s_1 + s_2 + s_3) \times L
eq \text{Volume of right rectangular prism} = l \times w \times h \qquad \text{Volume of right square prism} = l \times l \times l
@issue "Area of cube" means total surface area of a cube; "Area of Square prism = 6 × ℓ²" and "Volume of right square prism = l × l × l" describe a cube; kept as printed.
