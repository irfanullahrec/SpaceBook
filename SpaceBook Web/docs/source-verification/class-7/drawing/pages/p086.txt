@deco A website watermark (awaz e inqilab.com) is overprinted and is not book content; the glossary of 31 words is printed in two aligned columns (word and meaning); the rows of the meanings are slightly displaced from the words in the print, so a few meanings run one line off their word, and some English glosses are wrong or merged. They are given as printed: "Cadmium — A metal like tin" and the following phrase "figure whose opposite sides are parallel" belongs to "Parallelogram" (printed one line up against Cadmium); "Diagonal — A line drawn from angle to angle" runs on into "four sided rectilinear" which belongs to Parallelogram; "Fig — Full dress" (printed so; "Fig" here means "figure"); "Pretty — Neatly arranged"; "Monochromatic — Painting executed in deferent shades of one colour" ("deferent" for "different"); "Tactile — Susceptible of touch".
@issue The glossary meanings are misaligned with their words by one line at Cadmium/Parallelogram: the phrase "figure whose opposite sides are parallel" (printed on the Cadmium line) belongs to Parallelogram, and "four sided rectilinear" (printed on the Diagonal line) belongs to Parallelogram too. The table below gives each word the meaning that fits it and records the printed fragments here.
