### Focal Point or Centre of Interest || مرکزی نقطہ یا دل چسپی کا مرکز || مرکزي ټکی یا د لیوالتیا مرکز
p Suppose you are walking in countryside. Suddenly you see a tree that attracts your attention for some reason. Although you have come across hundreds of trees but they did not stop you. This one is very beautiful. You want to draw or paint it. According to the principles of composition, you have learned in class 6th, this tree will have a certain position on your sheet. It will not cover the whole page. There may be other trees, houses, rocks etc here and there which you may not be very interested in. In this case you need not to paint everything as it is. Because you were caught by the beauty of that particular tree, you will like to make it more prominent than other objects. Thus that tree will be your focal point or centre of interest.
