p After the above activity, you would have come to know that drawing from a model is more difficult than drawing from a picture. Sometimes, in life drawing, you can not judge the angle of a line at all. Fortunately this complicated problem has a very simple solution. Hold your pencil exactly horizontal or vertical in front touching one end of the problematic line as in the picture below. Now you can easily see and judge the angle.
