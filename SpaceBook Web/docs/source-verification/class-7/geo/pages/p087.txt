p Ms. Raheela Awais is an educationist serving as a Principal in a public-sector institution in Islamabad. She holds a Master's degree in Geography from the University of Punjab and a postgraduate degree in Educational Planning and Management, and is a master trainer of the Federal Directorate of Education who has taken part in curriculum and textbook work. p Prof. Muhammad Javaid is Chairman of the Department of Geography at Islamabad Model Postgraduate College H-8. He holds an M.Sc. in Geography from the University of Punjab, Lahore and an M.Ed. from AIOU Islamabad, joined the federal education department in 1997, has taught geography at intermediate, degree, M.Sc. and BS levels, and is co-author of geography and Pakistan studies books for middle and higher secondary levels.
