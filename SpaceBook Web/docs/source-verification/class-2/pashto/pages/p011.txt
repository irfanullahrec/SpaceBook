Today this necklace is kept in the Peshawar Museum. According to experts' research, it was made a hundred years before Hazrat Isa (Jesus, peace be upon him). Its design is neither Indian nor Iranian, though it certainly shows some influence of Central Asia. The horses made on it have the same shape as those found carved on rocks in the Chilas area. Both sides of the necklace (front and back) are the same, with the same patterns and pictures. It is a rare example. But the question arises: what was such a heavy necklace for, since a human neck cannot carry so much weight?
