An old woman named Khakho found it while gathering dry firewood. Aunt Khakho lived in Pattan, in Kohistan district; Pattan is two hundred and fifty kilometres from Mansehra. The necklace was lying in an old fort, of which only traces remain now. This precious necklace weighs fourteen and a half kilograms; its gold is pure and it is made of fourteen strands. Besides its fourteen and a half kilograms of gold, it is a very historic object. On every strand there are pictures of people and of various animals such as horses and lions …
