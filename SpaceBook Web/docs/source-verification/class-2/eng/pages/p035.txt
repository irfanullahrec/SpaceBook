exercise Learning to Write

Whenever he visits Zain, he brings ___ vegetables from his garden. || دیے گئے الفاظ سے پیراگراف مکمل کریں۔ || د ورکړل شویو کلمو په مرسته پاراګراف بشپړ کړئ.
sol garden, flowers, colours, care, fruit, fresh ## Zain loves his grandpa's garden. It has many pretty flowers. They are of different colours. Zain's grandpa always takes care of them.
