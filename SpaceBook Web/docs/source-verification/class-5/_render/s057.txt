q 4.4 | Fill in the effects of air, water and land pollution. || ہوا، پانی اور زمین کی آلودگی کے اثرات لکھیں۔ || د هوا، اوبو او ځمکې د ککړتیا اغېزې ولیکئ.
sol Air pollution: throat, skin, eye and lung diseases, allergy, smog and acid rain. Water pollution: diseases from germs in dirty water; fish and other aquatic animals die.
