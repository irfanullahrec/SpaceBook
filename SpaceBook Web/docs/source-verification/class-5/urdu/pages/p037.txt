Together these make our life healthy and pleasant. If any of them is not available in balance, our life is surrounded by many diseases and unbearable troubles. Yet we humans are cutting down trees and plants with our own hands and putting up factories and machines that produce poisonous gases, which harm our health and life. According to environmental experts, forests must cover a quarter of any country's area. Trees give us oxygen and also absorb dense, dirty air, making the environment pleasant.
