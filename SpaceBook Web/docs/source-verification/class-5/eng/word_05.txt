Markhor lives in the mountains. It is mostly found in Northern Pakistan and the Himalayas. There are three species of Markhor found in Asia. 
It is known for its long and curly horns. These attractive horns of males are nearly 63 inches long and of females around 10 inches long. Males and females can be easily identified by the colour of their coats. Males have light 
brown to black coloured coats. Females have 
reddish coats. These coats shorten in summer and elongate in winter. A Markhor male measures around 52 to 73 inches in length and weighs 71 to 240 pounds. Females are much smaller than males. 
Markhor's strong and powerful legs give it the ability to maintain balance when climbing or simply walking on hills.. 
It eats grass in spring and summer but feeds on leaves, herbs, fruits and flowers in winter.
