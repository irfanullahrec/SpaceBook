### Regular and Irregular Plural Nouns || باقاعدہ اور بے قاعدہ جمع اسما || منظم او غیر منظم جمع نومونه
q ii | Read the given rules for regular and irregular plural nouns. || باقاعدہ اور بے قاعدہ جمع اسما کے دیے گئے اصول پڑھیں۔ || د منظمو او غیر منظمو جمع نومونو ورکړل شوې قاعدې ولولئ.
table Plural rules || جمع کے اصول || د جمع قاعدې
th | Rule | Singular | Plural
tr | Add -s to the most of the nouns | book, parcel | books, parcels
tr | Add -es to the nouns ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, or -z | inch, box | inches, boxes
tr | Add -s to the most of the nouns that end in -o | radio, video | radios, videos
tr | Add -es to a few nouns that end in -o | tomato, hero | tomatoes, heroes
tr | Change -y to -i and add -es to most nouns ending in -y | city | cities
tr | If a vowel comes before -y, add -s | boy, day | boys, days
tr | Change -f to -v and add -es to most nouns that end in -f or -fe | leaf, wolf | leaves, wolves
endtable
