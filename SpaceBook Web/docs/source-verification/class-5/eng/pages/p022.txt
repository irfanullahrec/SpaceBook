sol Model story: An ant fell into a stream and was drowning. A dove on a tree saw it and dropped a leaf into the water. The ant climbed onto the leaf and was saved. One day a hunter came to catch the dove. The ant bit the hunter's foot, he missed, and the dove flew away. The dove thanked the ant. Moral: one good turn deserves another. ## Write the events in order using first, then, next and finally. || واقعات کو first، then، next اور finally کے ساتھ ترتیب سے لکھیں۔ || پېښې د first، then، next او finally په کارولو په ترتیب ولیکئ.
