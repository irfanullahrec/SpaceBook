sol Brave, honest and hard-working people (not gold) make a nation strong. ## "Not gold but only men can make a people great and strong." || ||
qp b | What is the message of the poem in your opinion? || آپ کی رائے میں نظم کا پیغام کیا ہے؟ || ستاسو په نظر د شعر پیغام څه دی؟
