# B) Reading and Critical Thinking || B) مطالعہ اور تنقیدی سوچ || B) لوستل او انتقادي فکر
## 1. Reading Comprehension || 1۔ فہمِ عبارت || 1. د لوست پوهه
q i | Answer these questions. || ان سوالات کے جواب دیں۔ || دې پوښتنو ته ځواب ووایاست.
qp a | What was wrong with the parcel? || پارسل میں کیا غلط تھا؟ || په پارسل کې څه ستونزه وه؟
sol The company had sent the wrong books — not the storybooks that had been ordered. ## From p.129. || ||
qp b | What type of stories does Hafsa like to read? || حفصہ کس قسم کی کہانیاں پڑھنا پسند کرتی ہے؟ || حفصه څه ډول کیسې لوستل خوښوي؟
sol Adventure stories. ## "It would be real fun to read a new adventure story each day." || ||
qp c | Which storybooks do you like? Why? || آپ کو کون سی کہانیوں کی کتابیں پسند ہیں؟ کیوں؟ || تاسو ته کوم د کیسو کتابونه خوښ دي؟ ولې؟
sol Open answer, e.g. "I like the stories of Sindbad the Sailor because they are full of adventure." ## A personal response. || ||
q ii | Read the lesson again and fill in the blanks with the correct options. || سبق دوبارہ پڑھیں اور درست اختیار سے خالی جگہیں پُر کریں۔ || لوست بیا ولولئ او تشې په سم انتخاب ډکې کړئ.
qp a | Hafsa was anxiously waiting for her \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_. (i) food   ii) storybooks   iii) dress   iv) flowers) || ||
sol ii) storybooks ## || ||
qp b | 'Doorbell' is an example of a/an \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ noun. (i) compound   ii) abstract   iii) proper   iv) collective) || ||
sol i) compound ## door + bell. || ||
qp c | Hafsa's mother wrote a/an \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ letter to the company. (i) invitation   ii) complaint   iii) greetings   iv) thank you) || ||
sol ii) complaint ## "Let's write a complaint to the seller". || ||
qp d | Her \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ ordered storybooks for Hafsa. (i) father   ii) mother   iii) friend   iv) cousin) || ||
sol ii) mother ## "the storybooks her mother had ordered for her". || ||
## 2. Analytical Reading || 2۔ تجزیاتی مطالعہ || 2. تحلیلي لوستل
q 2 | Read a formal and an informal letter from the internet or a children's magazine. Note its important features and differences in your notebook. || انٹرنیٹ یا بچوں کے رسالے سے ایک رسمی اور ایک غیر رسمی خط پڑھیں۔ اپنی کاپی میں اس کی اہم خصوصیات اور فرق لکھیں۔ || له انټرنېټ یا د ماشومانو له مجلې یو رسمي او یو غیر رسمي لیک ولولئ. مهمې ځانګړنې او توپیرونه یې په کتابچه کې ولیکئ.
sol Formal letter: written to an office, company or official; has both addresses, a subject line, "Respected Sir/Madam", polite and short language, "Yours truly/faithfully". Informal letter: written to family or friends; has the writer's address and date, "Dear …", friendly language, "Your loving …". ## || ||
