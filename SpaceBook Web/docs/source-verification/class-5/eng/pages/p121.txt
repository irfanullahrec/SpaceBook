p I am Abdullah. Sara, Tahir and Maheen are my friends. We all go to the same school. Every evening, we gather in a park located in our neighbourhood. Last week, our teacher announced that the school would arrange a fun fair next month. We all became excited but we felt that Sara was not happy.
