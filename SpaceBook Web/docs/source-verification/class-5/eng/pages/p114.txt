### Homonyms || ہم نام الفاظ || هم نومه ټکي
q iv | Write the meanings of the given homonyms in your notebook. Also use them in sentences of your own. || دیے گئے ہم نام الفاظ کے معانی اپنی کاپی میں لکھیں۔ انہیں اپنے جملوں میں بھی استعمال کریں۔ || د ورکړل شوو هم نومه ټکو ماناوې په کتابچه کې ولیکئ. په خپلو جملو کې یې هم وکاروئ.
qp a | watch, watch
sol watch (to look at): We watch cartoons. / watch (a small clock): My watch is new. ## || ||
qp b | fly, fly
sol fly (to move in the air): Birds fly high. / fly (an insect): A fly sat on the food. ## || ||
qp c | bat, bat
sol bat (a stick for cricket): He hit the ball with a bat. / bat (a flying animal): A bat flies at night. ## || ||
qp d | fine, fine
sol fine (well): I am fine. / fine (money paid as punishment): He paid a fine for parking. ## || ||
qp e | park, park
sol park (a garden): We play in the park. / park (to leave a car): Park the car here. ## || ||
