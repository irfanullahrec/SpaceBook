q 8 | Write the fable on the topic 'A Lion and the Mouse' with a moral. || 'شیر اور چوہا' کے موضوع پر سبق آموز حکایت لکھیں۔ || د 'زمری او موږک' په موضوع یو فابل له پند سره ولیکئ.
sol Model: Once a lion was sleeping in a forest. A little mouse ran over him and woke him up. The angry lion caught the mouse, but the mouse begged, "Please let me go. One day I will help you." The lion laughed and let him go. Some days later the lion was caught in a hunter's net. The mouse heard him roar, came and cut the net with his sharp teeth. The lion was free and thanked the mouse. Moral: No act of kindness is ever wasted / a friend in need is a friend indeed. ## Include characters, setting, plot, conflict, resolution and moral. || ||
