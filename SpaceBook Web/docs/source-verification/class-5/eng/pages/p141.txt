sol Read the given sentences and notice the use of intonation. ## Practice activity: there is nothing to write.
table Rising and falling intonation (↗ rising, ↘ falling) || اٹھتا اور گرتا لہجہ (↗ اٹھتا، ↘ گرتا) || پورته او ښکته لهجه (↗ پورته، ↘ ښکته)
th | Intonation | Use | Example
tr | Rising ↗ | Our voice rises at the end of a statement. | This scene is beautiful, isn't it? ↗
tr | Rising ↗ | In yes-no questions. | Do you sell clothes? ↗ / Are you hungry? ↗
tr | Falling ↘ | In falling intonation our voice falls on the final stressed syllable of a phrase or a group of words. In wh- questions. | Where's the nearest bank? ↘ / What is your name? ↘
tr | Falling ↘ | In commands | Do not run on roads. ↘
tr | Falling ↘ | In exclamations | That's great! ↘
endtable
box note | Read Me || مجھے پڑھیں || ما ولولئ
p **Intonation** is the rise and fall of the voice in speaking. Intonation is about how we say rather than what we say. There are two types of intonation: rising and falling intonation. || **لہجہ (intonation)** بولتے وقت آواز کا اتار چڑھاؤ ہے۔ لہجہ اس بارے میں ہے کہ ہم کیسے کہتے ہیں نہ کہ کیا کہتے ہیں۔ لہجے کی دو قسمیں ہیں: اٹھتا اور گرتا لہجہ۔ || **لهجه (intonation)** د خبرو پر مهال د غږ پورته او ښکته کېدل دي. لهجه دا ده چې څنګه وایو نه دا چې څه وایو. لهجه دوه ډوله ده: پورته او ښکته لهجه.
endbox
