p **Ms. Madeeha Nuzhat** has completed her Masters in Mathematics from University of Peshawar and did her B. Ed. from AIOU Islamabad. She also holds a 2-year certificate in 'Professional Manager in Educational Planning and Management' from Pakistan Institute of Modern Studies. She has more than 15 years of experience in the field of education. Her publications include more than 80 textbooks (local and International series) of mathematics for various grades.
