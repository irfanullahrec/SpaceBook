qp i | Define biodiversity. || حیاتیاتی تنوع کی تعریف کریں۔ || ژوندی تنوع تعریف کړئ.
sol The number of kinds of living things present at a particular place is called biodiversity. ## || ||
qp ii | State the importance of classification. || درجہ بندی کی اہمیت بیان کریں۔ || د ډلبندۍ اهمیت بیان کړئ.
sol Classification helps us find the similarities and differences among organisms, identify and study them by their structure and characteristics, and know the relationships among them. ## From "Importance of Classification". || ||
qp iii | Write the differences between: (a) Worms and Insects (b) Amphibians and Reptiles || درج ذیل میں فرق لکھیں: (a) ورمز اور حشرات (b) جل تھلیے اور رینگنے والے || د لاندې توپیرونه ولیکئ: (a) چینجي او حشرات (b) دوه ژوندي او خاپوړي
sol (a) Worms have soft bodies and no legs; insects have jointed legs (six), a hard exoskeleton and a body in three parts. (b) Amphibians have moist, loose skin, breathe through lungs and skin and lay eggs in water; reptiles have thick, dry, coarse skin and lay eggs on land. ## || ||
qp iv | What is the similarity between Fungi and Animals? || فنجائی اور جانوروں میں کیا مماثلت ہے؟ || د فنجای او څارویو ترمنځ څه ورته والی دی؟
sol Neither has chlorophyll, so neither can make its own food; both get food from other sources. ## || ||
qp v | Write one characteristic and one example of Monera and Protista. || مونیرا اور پروٹسٹا کی ایک ایک خصوصیت اور ایک ایک مثال لکھیں۔ || د مونیرا او پروټیسټا یوه یوه ځانګړنه او یوه یوه بېلګه ولیکئ.
sol Monera: made of one simple cell — e.g. bacteria. Protista: usually live in water (many have chlorophyll) — e.g. Amoeba, Paramecium or Spirogyra. ## || ||
