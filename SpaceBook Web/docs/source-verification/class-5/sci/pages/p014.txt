### Birds || پرندے || مرغان
p The birds have feathers and beak. Their bones are hollow, thus their weight is less. Along with lungs, they have air sacs. Birds can fly in air. Some birds can swim in water e.g. duck. Some birds cannot fly and are called running birds e.g., Kiwi, Ostrich. All birds lay eggs. The birds that live in water have webbed feet e.g., duck. Their body temperature does not depend on the external environment.
