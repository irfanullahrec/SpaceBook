From there he passed Class 12 with good marks, and at eighteen joined the Pakistan Air Force as an airman. On completing his training and course he was given the title "Shaheen Bacha" (young falcon). During this time he also passed the army commission exam, and in 1992 went to the Pakistan Military Academy, Kakul (Abbottabad), for training. He completed the course in October 1994. In sports, studies and other stages of the course he was ahead of his fellows, and in his unit he was known as "Shera" (the lion).
