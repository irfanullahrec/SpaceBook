In 1895 Marconi had the idea of making a wireless electrical device, and the radio was invented. Graham Bell invented the telephone. John Logie Baird and some others invented television. In 1940 the computer was invented, but that computer was very large and bulky and did not work very well. Today the computer is small and works much better.
