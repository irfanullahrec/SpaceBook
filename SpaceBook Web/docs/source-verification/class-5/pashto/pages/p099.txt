He was still small when his parents died. They left him only a little house; for the rest he relied on Allah. So Asma'i lived in great hardship, sometimes going hungry and thirsty. But he dearly loved learning. In torn clothes he studied under the great scholars of Basra. Besides the Quran and Hadith, he became an expert in history, mathematics and other sciences.
