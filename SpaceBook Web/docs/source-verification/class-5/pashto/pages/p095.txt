You all know it: one child puts a hand on a particular wall and shuts their eyes, and the others hide here and there. After a moment that child calls out, "Shall I come?" If the others have reached their hiding places they answer, "Come!" Then any child who reaches the special wall is safe from being caught; and if the seeker catches nobody, they have to close their eyes again.
