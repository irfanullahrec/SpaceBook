Scholars of Pashto language, civilisation, culture and history agree that there is no place for negative values in Pashto; any negative values seen in it are the result of outside influences, and what is called Pashtunwali is ethics in the light of Islamic teachings. Pashtun scholars have explained the letters of the words "Pashto" and "Pashtun" like this: پ stands for pat (honour), ښ for khegaṛa (goodness), ت for tura (the sword, bravery), و for wa'da (keeping promises) and ن for nang (honour, dignity). Beyond these five qualities, Pashtunwali has many other aspects covering every part of Pashtun life. We can divide Pashtunwali into three parts:
