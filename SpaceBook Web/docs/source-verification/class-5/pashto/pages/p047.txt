By now he was very hungry. In front of him he saw a plum tree. He shook down a few plums and began to eat, but they were very sour. Mashal shook the tree hard again, meaning to stop other travellers being fooled by these sour plums. When he had given it a few shakes, the plum tree cried out in a pleading voice:

On the way he came to a forest and there he saw a hut. He was very tired, so he went into the hut to rest. Inside, an old dervish was asleep. Mashal sat down quietly beside him so as not to disturb his sleep.
