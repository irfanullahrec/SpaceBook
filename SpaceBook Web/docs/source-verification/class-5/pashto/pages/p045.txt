He was poor, but he firmly believed that hard work, effort and good character always bear fruit one day, and that there is blessing in effort. As he walked along, he saw craftsmen building a magnificent palace for the king; but the strange thing was that whenever one side of the palace was finished and they began work on the other, the first part came crashing down. Mashal greeted the workmen. When the king noticed Mashal, he asked him:
