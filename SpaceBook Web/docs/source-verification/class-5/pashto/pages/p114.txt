At the airport Sultan Jamil Naseem was waiting with his family and children. His brother Shahid, an officer with PIA, took their passports and tickets inside; soon they said goodbye to Ahmad and Jamil's children and went in. Shahid took their suitcases for the customs check and left them with a man who served them tea.
