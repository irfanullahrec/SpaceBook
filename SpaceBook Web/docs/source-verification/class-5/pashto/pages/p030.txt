One day, as he sat in class, Syed Sarwar Shah suddenly came in and signalled him to come out. The writer left his book and went out; a handsome young man was standing there. They were introduced, and the three of them came happily to the writer's room in Chelmsford Hostel.

It crossed his mind that this was too little for such an unknown venture. They came out of the college onto the main road and headed for Peshawar. This is the royal highway (the Grand Trunk Road).
