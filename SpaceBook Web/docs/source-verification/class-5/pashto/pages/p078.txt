Dost Muhammad Khan Kamil Momand was born in Peshawar city on 17 September 1915. He received his early education at the primary school of Reti Mohalla; meanwhile his father was transferred from Peshawar to Mardan, so he studied from Class 6 to Class 10 in Mardan. He did Class 12 at Islamia College, Peshawar, and Class 14 (B.A.) at Edwardes College, Peshawar, passing both with good marks. In 1938 he took an M.A. in Philosophy from Aligarh, and in 1939 an LL.B. (law) degree from the same institution.
