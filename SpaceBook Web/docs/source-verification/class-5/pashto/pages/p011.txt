The Holy Prophet ﷺ borrowed something from him. Several days were still left before the repayment date when he came to demand it; he seized the Prophet's ﷺ shawl and pulled it, spoke rudely and said: "O family of Abdul Muttalib! You always make such excuses." Hazrat Umar (RA), who was present, grew agitated and said to him: "O enemy of God! You are being insolent to Allah's Prophet ﷺ." The Holy Prophet ﷺ smiled and said: "O Umar! You should have told him to ask politely, and told me to repay his debt." Then he said to Hazrat Umar (RA): "Pay his debt, and give him twenty sa' of dates extra."
