Listen to me — learn some trade and earn your living." These words hurt Asma'i deeply, but his only remedy was to leave home in the dark before dawn and come back late after dark, so as not to hear the ignorant man's taunts.

The fame of his learning spread far and wide. One day, as he lay resting at home, someone knocked at the door. Opening it, Asma'i found the personal servant of the governor of Basra, who said: "The governor has sent for you, and I have come to take you now." Asma'i went with him.
