In the beginning, whatever the noble Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) heard, learned or saw from the Noble Prophet ﷺ, they would preserve it in their minds, in practical form and in written form, but there was no regular book. At the end of the Umayyad era and the beginning of the Abbasid era, the tendency to compile collections of ahadith gained strength, and many collections of ahadith were compiled. But along with this, some short-sighted people, for the support of their political and religious views, also began to spread wrong and forged ahadith among them, because of which non-authentic and weak ahadith too got included along with authentic ones in the collection of ahadith. Now the need arose that authentic and sahih ahadith be sifted out from non-authentic and weak ahadith and separated, and collections of purely sahih ahadith be compiled. So in the third century Hijri the great muhaddithin accomplished this work and compiled six books of sahih ahadith, which are called the Sihah Sittah (the six authentic books).
