When you enter into a transaction of debt with one another for a fixed term, then write it down, and the writer (among you) should write fairly, without (causing anyone loss); nor should the writer refuse to write, as Allah has taught him; he should write the document. And the person who takes the loan should dictate the text of the document and fear Allah, who is his Lord, and should not reduce anything from the loan money. And if the one taking the loan is of little intelligence or weak, or not capable of dictating the text, then whoever is his guardian should dictate the text with fairness.
