p Mohammad Khalid is Head of Computer Science Department at OPF Boys College, Islamabad. Throughout his educational career he has got first-class first division. He did his M.Sc in Computer Science from the University of Peshawar in 1996 and earned his B.Ed degree in Science from Institute of Education and Research, University of Peshawar in 1997. He has been instructor and teacher in Computer Science for the last seventeen years. He has vast teaching experience from Secondary to Masters Level.
