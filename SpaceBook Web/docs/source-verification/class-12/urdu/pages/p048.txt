Meanwhile a voice came: “Call Azad.” At once a voice came that perhaps he would not accept to sit in this assembly; but from there someone again said: “Among whichever people you seat him, he will sit.” Meanwhile a few persons raised an outcry that his pen had waged war with a whole world; he should not be given a place in the court of fame. Argument began over this case. I wanted to lift the veil from my face, step forward and say something, but my guide and companion, that is the angel of mercy, took my hand and said quietly: “It is not expedient yet.” Meanwhile my eyes opened.
