These women wear the dress of fairies, but there too I heard it said that in reality they are neither fairies nor fairy-born women. Some among them are heedlessness, some are dissipation; some are self-conceit, some are indifference. When some man of resolve travels on the road of progress, these are sure to meet him. Caught in them, the people of progress remain deprived of their aims. The farther these people advanced, the more pleasing that sound seemed to their ears. It seemed to me that many choice and eminent persons advanced with the resolve to climb to the height of the mountain and, in whatever way possible, to go near and obtain spiritual strength from this heavenly melody. So all the people began to take some things or other with them. It seemed as if they were taking provisions for the road ahead. The provisions too were different for each one.
