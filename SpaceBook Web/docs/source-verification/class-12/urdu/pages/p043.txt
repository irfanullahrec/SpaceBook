His bones and his dress were foreign, and his robe was patterned with blood. He had many precious jewels of Hindustan, but since he was unfamiliar with them, he held some jewels in his hand and some lay on his shoulder. He was Mahmud of Ghazni. Many authors advanced to receive him, but he seemed to be waiting and longing for someone else. Thereupon a young man, houri-like in form, came, took Firdausi by the hand and led him before Mahmud. Mahmud took his hand with great eagerness and gratitude. Although they sat side by side, the eyes of both were lowered in shame. The young man smiled with a strange coquetry and went away.
