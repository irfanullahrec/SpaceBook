Manzoor chirped as usual all day long. Sometimes he talked with the nurses, sometimes with the doctors, sometimes with the sweepers — they too were his friends. But he was also amazed at how he remained so happy in spite of so great a loss. At first they had him treated by hakims, but there was no benefit. Then they resorted to charms and spells, but in vain.
