Under the canopy sat a row of young men — old classmates and playmates — all standing up saying “Recognise us?” Lalu was among them, reminding her of Liaquat Ali Khan’s procession and the master’s beating. Recovering from her surprise she asked where he was and what he did. “I am a bus conductor.” “You’re joking.” “Not at all. What harm is there?
