If you change your past and work together with one spirit, then it does not matter at all which group you belong to; you are first, second and last a citizen of this state, where the rights, privileges and responsibilities of all are equal. If work is done with this spirit you can make immense progress. We are going forward with this very spirit and basic principle that we are all equal citizens of this country.
