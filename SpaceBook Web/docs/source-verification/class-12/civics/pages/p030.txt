To enforce its decisions it can give directions to any High Court. In the constitution of Pakistan Pakistani citizens have been given several fundamental rights. No one has the right to violate these rights. On the application of any citizen the superior court can declare unlawful every law made by Parliament or a provincial assembly, or any order issued by the executive, that conflicts with any fundamental right given in the constitution. In brief it is right to say that the Supreme Court of Pakistan is the guardian of the fundamental rights of citizens according to the constitution. In the democratic periods the political executive represented the important political parties of the country. The principle of collective responsibility operated in it. In the democratic periods the elected Prime Minister of the country remained the executive head, and the highest powers of the state remained in the hands of the Prime Minister. The executive answered to Parliament and the people. In the democratic periods the cabinet usually remained a mixture of different political parties, and the government worked in coalition form. For this reason it remained difficult for the largest party to implement its agenda, manifesto and electoral promises freely. Such elected governments also enjoyed the people’s confidence. In the democratic periods the executive ran according to the people’s wishes and inclinations. Because of the coalition system most democratic administrations also remained unstable.
