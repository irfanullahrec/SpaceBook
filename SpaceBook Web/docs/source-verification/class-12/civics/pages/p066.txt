Therefore there is a need for this, that to become a dignified, free and sovereign nation, the dependence on world institutions, especially the International Monetary Fund and the World Bank, be reduced and gradually ended. The best thing is that life be lived within domestic resources, i.e. there is a need to stretch one’s legs according to one’s sheet. When we go beyond our resources and do not control our unlimited desires and needs, then we have to look towards others. If they are not a basic need, it is very good to ignore them and reduce their use. Unfortunately in our country the trend towards technical education is very low, which is an important obstacle in the way of economic progress.
