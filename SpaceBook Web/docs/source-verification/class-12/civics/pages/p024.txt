The Prime Minister of Pakistan is the head of the executive and the real ruler. The President appoints the ministers on the advice of the Prime Minister, and on the advice of the Prime Minister he removes ministers. Allotting departments (portfolios) to ministers is the Prime Minister’s power. He supervises the activities of the ministers and creates coordination between them. Giving instructions to cabinet members and ending differences between ministers is included in the Prime Minister’s responsibilities. The formulation of the administrative policy of the country is the responsibility of the executive. The enforcement of law and its supervision is the duty of the executive. The Prime Minister presides over the meetings of the cabinet and in it the Prime Minister’s voice is decisive. Although appointments to important posts of the country are made by the President of Pakistan, the real decision or advice is given by the Prime Minister.
