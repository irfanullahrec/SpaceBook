In Pakistan the police has several powers and duties or responsibilities. Basically the work of the police is the establishment of law and order inside the country. However, when the situation goes out of the control of the police, the government can call paramilitary forces or the army to help the police. The police is trained to combat unrest. It is the responsibility of the police to trace terrorist groups and forces spreading unrest and bring them to justice. The protection of the life and property of the people is an important responsibility of the police. The police takes criminals into custody and presents them in court for punishment. Gambling, drinking, drug dealing, theft, murder, dacoity, kidnapping, hoarding and black marketing etc. are crimes for whose control the police is responsible. The police generally keeps a complete record of criminals. Finding out their centres and groups is the responsibility of the police. In this way uprooting crime and establishing law and order is the first responsibility of the police. For the control of traffic a branch of the police works under the name of Traffic Police. The Traffic Police challans vehicles on violation of law and presents them in court.
