In Islam there is a detailed mention of women’s rights. Spiritual rights mean that Islam has given men and women the same rights. No one has any superiority over anyone. According to verse 124 of Surah An-Nisa, “Whoever, whether man or woman, is a believer and does good deeds, such people will surely enter Paradise, and they will not be wronged even as much as the speck on a date-stone.” In the same way, according to verse 97 of Surah An-Nahl, “Whoever does good, whether man or woman, but is a believer, We shall surely give him a very good life, and We shall certainly give them a better reward for their deeds.” According to verse number 195 of Surah Aal-e-Imran, “I do not waste the work of any worker among you, whether man or woman.” In Islam it is the right of the woman to be equal to the man. In Islam moral duties are equal; in the same way moral rights are also equal. In the eyes of Islamic law man and woman are both equal. The Quran’s command is that whether the killer is a man or a woman, he/she will be given the death penalty. If the heir of the murdered, whether man or woman, forgives the killer or takes blood money, then both have this right.
