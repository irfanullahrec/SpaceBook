Key Points
Industrial chemistry is the study of fundamental chemical processes used in industry for transferring raw material to useful commercial products for society. Dye is a substance, which adds value to product for their cost.
Pesticides are chemical substances used for destroying insect and other organisms harmful to cultivated plants or to animals.
Petrochemicals are chemicals derived from petroleum products and used in the
chemical trade.
Petroleum in the unrefined form is called crude oil.
A polymer is a macromolecule formed as a result of a process known as
polymerization.
The small molecules, which undergo polymerization, are called the monomers, Addition polymers are long chain giant organic compounds, which are assembled from many smaller molecules.
Condensation polymer formed through a condensation reaction, releasing small molecules as by-products.
Cosmetics are chemicals, which are intended to be applied to the human body for cleansing, beautifying or altering the appearance without affecting the body&#x27;s functions.
Lipstick is mainly composed of a mixture of non-volatile oil and solid wax. Nail varnish or nail polish is a lacquer applied to human finger nails or toe nails to decorate or protect the nail plate.
Nail polish removers may be acetone base remover or non- acetone base
remover.
Hair dyes are the dyes, used for hair colouring.
The adhesive is a substance usually in liquid or semi-liquid states that is used in binding two or more objects together and resists their separation. Adhesives are of two types non-reactive and reactive adhesives. Reactive adhesives are classified into two classes e.g. multi part adhesives and
one part adhesives.
