Caliph Muqtadir, creating the new post of Amir al-Umara', appointed the commander of the guard army, Mu'nis al-Muzaffar, to it. The Amir al-Umara' Muzaffar deposed the caliph and later had him killed. Caliph al-Qahir was blinded. The same treatment was given to al-Muttaqi. The army had Caliph al-Radi too killed. Muktafi became caliph. In Muktafi's era, in the region of Daylam, the chief Buyah had established a permanent government. He had three sons — Ali, Hasan and Ahmad. The youngest son was Ahmad. He made considerable conquests and established a magnificent sultanate. In 945 CE he entered Baghdad. Caliph Muktafi welcomed him. In January 946 CE Ahmad deposed Muktafi and blinded him. Now in the streets of Baghdad all three caliphs al-Qahir, al-Muttaqi and Muktafi were seen begging. Muti' was made caliph in name only. || په همدې دوره کې عبیدالله په مصر کې فاطمي حکومت جوړ کړ. خلیفه مقتدر د «امیرالامراء» نوې څوکۍ جوړه کړه او د ساتونکي پوځ مشر مونس المظفر یې پرې وټاکه. امیرالامراء مظفر خلیفه لرې کړ او وروسته ووژل شو. خلیفه قاهر ړوند کړای شو؛ له متقي سره هم همداسې چلند وشو؛ پوځ خلیفه راضي هم ووژه. مکتفي خلیفه شو. د مکتفي په دوره کې د دیلم په سیمه کې مشر بویه دایمي حکومت جوړ کړی و؛ درې زامن یې وو: علي، حسن او احمد؛ تر ټولو کشر زوی احمد و. هغه ډېرې فتحې وکړې او لویه سلطنت یې جوړ کړ؛ په ۹۴۵ع کې بغداد ته ننوت؛ خلیفه مکتفي یې هرکلی وکړ. د ۹۴۶ع په جنورۍ کې احمد مکتفي لرې او ړوند کړ. اوس د بغداد په کوڅو کې قاهر، متقي او مکتفي درې واړه خلفاء د سوال کولو په حال لیدل کېدل. مطیع یوازې په نامه خلیفه کړای شو.

Their capital was Shiraz. Iraq had the status of a province. Among the Buyid sultans 'Adud al-Dawlah performed prominent achievements. He extended the conquests up to the extent of the sultanate of the era of Harun al-Rashid. He married the daughter of Caliph al-Ta'i' and gave his own daughter in marriage to al-Ta'i', so that the link with the caliphate would remain. He was the first ruler who was called Malik al-Muluk or Shahanshah. In Baghdad he spent a very large sum on works of public welfare. He had magnificent buildings, mosques and hospitals built, canals cleaned; in the hospitals expert surgeons and physicians remained present. The Sultan greatly valued scholars. Ali al-Qasimi wrote "Kitab al-Idah" for him.
