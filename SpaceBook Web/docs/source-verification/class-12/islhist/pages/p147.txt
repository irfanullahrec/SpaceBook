Saluli used to go to preach the religion. Through his preaching efforts hundreds of non-Muslims accepted Islam. Amir Saluli, advancing into France, began efforts to restore the dignity of the Muslims. Crossing the Pyrenees, military cantonments were made along the river Rhône. The regions of Saint-Paul and Burgundy were conquered. Charles, feeling danger, set up a united front against the Muslims. He incited many tribes against the Muslims. Saluli wanted to fight a decisive war with Charles, but during this very time the Berbers of Africa revolted and the governor-general of Africa called Amir Saluli back. The army appointed Abd al-Malik bin Fihri amir of Andalus again in 739 CE. The flames of the fire of the revolt of the Berber tribes in Africa reached Andalus. In Andalus too various tribes were settled. Among them were Berbers, new Muslims, Mudaris and Yamanis. Distance from Islamic teachings had divided the Muslims into various groups. These groups were busy in conspiracies against one another. In this way disorder and civil war began in Muslim Andalus. This civil war continued for approximately seventeen years. In this period the Muslims became victims of destruction. Their life, property and honour were not safe. The uncertain situation had made people's lives miserable. The civil war caused severe harm to the unity of the Muslims. If these abilities had been used unitedly against non-Muslims, the Muslims would never have been disgraced and humiliated, and decline would not have become their destiny. In these circumstances the Umayyad prince Abd al-Rahman al-Dakhil enters Andalus. By his God-given abilities he restores the dignity of the Muslims in Andalus.
