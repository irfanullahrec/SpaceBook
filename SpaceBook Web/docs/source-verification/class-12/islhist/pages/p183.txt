After Ghalib his successors, enlarging it further, refreshed the memory of the Banu Umayyah and the Banu Abbas. 'Ulama' and learned men began to gather here from far and wide. During this time Granada obtained a distinguished position in Europe commercially. Silk cloth here was very famous. It began to be counted among the richest cities of Europe. Muslim culture began to be restored. The Christians could not bear to see all this; so they began efforts to wipe this small state of the Muslims in Andalus off the face of existence. On the other side the other Muslim states were playing the role of spectators. No Muslim state helped Granada at this difficult time. If the Muslim rulers had succeeded in finishing a small mountain state of the Christians, their descendants would not have had to face this disgrace and humiliation; rather, today Europe would present a different map. In 1230 CE Castile and Leon united. Their union became the cause of the destruction of the Muslims. Their united forces led an expedition against the Muslims, and such a series of their conquests began which at last deprived the Muslims of the power of Andalus for ever. In 1085 CE Toledo was taken. In 1236 CE Cordoba was included in the Christian sultanate. In 1248 CE Seville too was swallowed up.
