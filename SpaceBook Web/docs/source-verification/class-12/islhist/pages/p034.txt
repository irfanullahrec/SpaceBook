Al-Rashid, with the thought that both of them should always take part in my gathering, said to Ja'far that I want to marry 'Abbasah to you, so that looking at 'Abbasah becomes lawful for you according to the Shari'ah and you both can gather without formality; but the condition is that there be no union as husband and wife. So on this condition the marriage took place; but when Harun came to know from his wife Zubaydah's tongue that 'Abbasah is the mother of a child — who had been sent off to Makkah — Harun destroyed and ruined this family." The chief of historians Ibn Khaldun has strongly refuted this event and declared it merely a fable. The authentic historian Ibn Qutaybah wrote his book "al-Ma'arif" 80 years after the killing of Ja'far. According to Ibn Qutaybah, "Harun married 'Abbasah first to Muhammad bin Sulayman, and after his death the second marriage to Ibrahim bin Salih" (research of Mawlana Abd al-Razzaq Kanpuri, book "al-Baramikah"). Mawlana Abd al-Razzaq Kanpuri too, in his book "al-Baramikah", proved through research that this was a baseless fable. ## ہارون الرشید کا شاندار دورِ حکومت || The glorious era of government of Harun al-Rashid || د هارون الرشید د حکومت ښکلې دوره
p ہارون الرشید کا دورِ حکومت تاریخِ اسلام کا شاندار دور کہلاتا ہے۔ اس زمانے میں خلافتِ اسلامیہ معاشرتی، علمی اور سیاسی لحاظ سے پورے عروج پر پہنچی۔ طاقت، دولت اور شان و شوکت میں دنیا کی کوئی دوسری مملکت اس کا مقابلہ نہیں کر سکتی تھی۔ || Harun al-Rashid's era of government is called the glorious era of the history of Islam. In this period the Islamic caliphate reached its full ascendancy socially, academically and politically. In power, wealth and grandeur no other realm of the world could compete with it. || د هارون الرشید د حکومت دوره د اسلام د تاریخ ښکلې دوره بلل کېږي. په دې زمانه کې اسلامي خلافت په ټولنیز، علمي او سیاسي لحاظ بشپړ عروج ته ورسېد؛ په ځواک، شتمنۍ او شان و شوکت کې د نړۍ هېڅ بل مملکت ورسره سیالي نشوه کولی.
