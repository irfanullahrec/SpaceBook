On them, by means of navigation, goods were carried from one place to another. This treasure of sciences kept being transferred with time to Islamic Spain. In every corner of the country schools and colleges and scientific laboratories had been established. For discussions, debates and gatherings of poetry magnificent halls existed everywhere. In them 'ulama' and learned men remained present at all times. A network of higher educational institutions had been spread. The 'ulama' and learned men were given large salaries and stipends. This was the reason that the great 'ulama' and experts of the world had gathered in Islamic Spain. To acquire education students came to Islamic Spain from far-off countries. Among them students of Europe, Egypt, Africa and Eastern countries were included. In Cordoba there were twenty-seven such educational institutions in which higher education was given free. The Jami'ah of Cordoba had a high place in the world. In it the teaching of all the sciences was given. It had obtained superiority even over the Jami'ah al-Azhar of Cairo and the Jami'ah of Baghdad.
