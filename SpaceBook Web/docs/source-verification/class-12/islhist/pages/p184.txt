They picked them off one by one and swallowed them. Now only the lamp of Granada was flickering. The small states of the Christians kept uniting against the Muslims, and at last two states, Aragon and Castile, came into existence. In 1469 CE the Christian ruler of Aragon, Ferdinand, married the queen of Castile, Isabella. In this way both states became further strengthened; rather later these two states joined to become one powerful state. On the other side the only government of the Muslims, Granada, consisted of the Nasrid family. In this family too civil war was going on. The Nasrids remained in power from 1232 CE to 1492 CE. But the condition was that father and son were busy in conspiracies against each other. On the other side the Christians were looking for an opportunity. Sultan Abu al-Hasan, king of Granada, refused to give kharaj to the Christians and attacked Castile.
