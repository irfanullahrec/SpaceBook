The Seljuq Turks for the first time took possession of Asia Minor. Here the Turkish language and Turkish civilisation and culture began to be seen. The Seljuqs appointed Sulayman bin Qutalmish governor here. He was a relative-brother of Alp Arslan. In 1077 CE he established the sultanate of the Seljuqs of Rum. The capital was Konya. During the Crusades his son Qilij Arslan had to leave here. After this Konya was made the capital. It was a very beautiful city. In the beginning the Seljuqs did not live in Baghdad. Alp Arslan never came to Baghdad. || … واکمنان طغرل، الپ ارسلان او ملک شاه وو. سلجوقي ترکانو د لومړي ځل لپاره پر کوچنۍ اسیا قبضه وکړه؛ دلته ترکي ژبه او ترکي تهذیب او تمدن ښکاره شو. سلجوقیانو سلیمان بن قتلمش (چاپ: مطلمس) دلته حاکم وټاکه؛ هغه د ارسلان د خپلوۍ ورور و. هغه په ۱۰۷۷ع کې د روم د سلجوقیانو سلطنت جوړ کړ؛ پلازمېنه یې قونیه وه. د صلیبي جګړو پر مهال د هغه زوی قلیچ ارسلان له دې ځایه وتلو ته اړ شو؛ له دې وروسته قونیه پلازمېنه کړای شوه؛ دا ډېر ښکلی ښار و. په پیل کې سلجوقیان په بغداد کې نه اوسېدل؛ الپ ارسلان هېڅکله بغداد ته رانغی.

Only a Seljuq military governor supervised Baghdad. In the last era of Malik Shah the Seljuqs moved to Baghdad. In 1087 CE Caliph al-Muqtadi married a daughter of Malik Shah. Along with the caliph's name the name of the Seljuq sultan too was included in the khutbah. The ancient Sumerians had presented the concept of the double-headed eagle. In Baghdad the people of Babylon had made it their national emblem. The Seljuqs too reserved this emblem for their family. After them the Byzantine, Austrian and Russian emperors adopted this emblem. Seljuq power over Baghdad remained established from 1055 CE to 1225 CE. These people respected the caliphs instead of giving them pain. During this very time the flood of the Crusades had surged. In stopping it the Seljuq and Abbasid caliphs did not play any special prominent role; they remained mere spectators. At last this duty was performed by 'Imad al-Din Zangi and his son Nur al-Din Zangi. They made numerous attempts to stop this flood of the Cross. To a considerable extent they met with success.
