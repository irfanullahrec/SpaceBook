In Andalus the amirs remained drowned in pleasures. Their life was devoted to eating, drinking and other pleasures. The majority of the public consisted of slaves, who kept working day and night in the fields, but nothing of it was their own. With time their masters kept changing. Between these two classes the most oppressed class was that of the slaves, on whom pressure kept falling from both sides. They had to pay the greater part of the country's taxes. This class provided the means of pleasure for the amirs. The whole business of government too this class had to run. In society such people had disappeared who could face attackers. The amirs and nobles had drowned in their colourful pleasures. Their swords had become rusty. They were running far away from war.
