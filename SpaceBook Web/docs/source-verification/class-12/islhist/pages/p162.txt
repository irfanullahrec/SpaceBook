Not even two years of his government had been completed when his brother had him killed by means of a surgeon with a poisoned lancet. After al-Mundhir's death Abdullah ascended the throne in 888 CE and remained in possession of the throne till 912 CE. His era was an era of revolts and confusion. The whole sultanate had come into the grip of revolts. The Berbers, to establish their own government, spread a net of conspiracies and revolts. On the other side the Muslims of Spain wanted to obtain freedom. The central government kept becoming weaker and weaker. The Christians too began to dream of obtaining government. Some wretched Muslim rebels created difficulties for the government at the signals of the Christians. Among the rebels 'Umar bin Hafsun was the most dangerous. He was a new-Muslim hypocrite rebel. He was called the mountain rat. He belonged to the lineage of a Visigothic count. Joining with robbers, in 880 CE he took possession of the old fort of Jabal Bobastro. Bringing the mountain inhabitants of Asira with him, he established a strong state in southern Andalus. He greatly troubled Muhammad, Mundhir and Abdullah. He tried to obtain the amirate of Andalus. For this purpose he also established contact with other rulers, but no special success came. At last, disappointed, he announced Christianity and named himself Samuel. He went on continuously creating difficulties for the Muslims. His state, progressing, became included among the large states of the Christians. This state played a prominent role in depriving the Muslims of power. In Murcia too a new Muslim announced his autonomy.
