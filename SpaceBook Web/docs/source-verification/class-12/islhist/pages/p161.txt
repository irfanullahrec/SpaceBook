The founder of this movement was a prejudiced priest, Eulogius. A wealthy person, Alvaro, was giving him financial help. According to the planning of this movement, on the day of 'Id al-Fitr 850 CE a person named Perfectus publicly used improper words regarding the dignity of the Holy Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, his family and companions). According to the decision of the jurists he was given the death sentence, but the priests gave him the rank of a holy saint (wali). In the same way another monk, Isaac, used blasphemous words before the qadi regarding the dignity of the Holy Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, his family and companions). He too was given the death sentence. But the priests gave him too the rank of holy saint. In the same way many other Christians too adopted this very method. Al-Awsat informed the Christian priests of Eulogius's impure plan, behind the curtain of which he was preparing simple-minded Christians for blasphemy of the Messenger by showing them the green garden of martyrdom. Eulogius, including many beautiful girls in this movement, brought them to a dreadful end.
