This lake is situated near the city of Sidonia in the province of Cadiz. The river Wadi Lakkah or Wadi Barbat falls into this lake. Tariq too set his ranks right. The number of the enemy was very large, but these sons of Islam were filled with the spirit of faith. Tariq, making a stirring speech before the sons of Tawhid, awakened a new spirit in them. Roderick attacked, but Tariq's vanguard stopped it. In the month of September a bloody battle began. Roderick, fleeing from the battlefield, jumped into the river. The waves of the river swallowed this cruel, bloodthirsty emperor and gave the oppressed public of Andalus deliverance. The news of this victory spread on all four sides. After conquering Cadiz, Tariq advanced towards Sidonia. Seville was conquered. Ecija was taken. By digging the ground at Bi'r al-Tariq sweet water was provided. Tariq made Ecija his headquarters. He began the advance for the conquest of Toledo. Units were sent to take Cordoba, Granada, Valencia, Malaga and Tudmir. Granada was called 'Arus al-Bilad. Musa too set off for Andalus. In Qayrawan he left his son Abdullah. Tariq met his governor. Under Musa the series of conquests continued. Scott, Musa had forbidden plunder. He emphasised respect for the Christian religion. He strictly stopped violence and instructed that no damage be done to property. In Damascus envious and prejudiced amirs began to incite Caliph Walid bin Abd al-Malik.
