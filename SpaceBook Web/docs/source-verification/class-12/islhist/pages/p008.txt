Imam Zayn al-Abidin had already become aware of his cunning, so he (may Allah be pleased with him) refused. After this Mukhtar began efforts for the patronage of Muhammad bin Hanafiyyah, the non-Fatimid son of Hazrat Ali (may Allah be pleased with him). When Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubayr (may Allah be pleased with him) came to know, he arrested Muhammad bin Hanafiyyah. Mukhtar sent an army and got him freed. After this, to strengthen his movement, Mukhtar joined other tribes with himself and attained tremendous strength. When Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubayr (may Allah be pleased with him) came to know, he sent his brother Hazrat Mus'ab bin Zubayr (may Allah be pleased with him) to confront him. It is said that in that year, on the occasion of Hajj, four flags were gathered: one flag of the Banu Umayyah, the second of the Kharijites, the third of Hazrat Abdullah bin Zubayr (may Allah be pleased with him), and the fourth of Muhammad bin Hanafiyyah. Hazrat Mus'ab bin Zubayr (may Allah be pleased with him) put an end to Mukhtar Thaqafi and finished this movement forever. Muhammad bin Hanafiyyah openly gave allegiance to the Caliph Abd al-Malik. The people of this sect were called the Kaysaniyyah. These people also accepted the imamate of Muhammad bin Hanafiyyah. After the death of Muhammad bin Hanafiyyah his son Imam Abu Hashim was accepted as imam. The people of the Kaysaniyyah gave allegiance to Imam Abu Hashim. Imam Abu Hashim had no sons of his own. So he nominated as his successor Ali bin Abdullah, the grandson of Hazrat Abbas (may Allah be pleased with him). By this succession the imamate was transferred to the Banu Abbas. This was the reason that people regarded the caliphs of the Banu Abbas, along with being worldly rulers, also as spiritual, religious and doctrinal leaders. Despite incompetence, because of religious leadership these people remained in power for five hundred years.
