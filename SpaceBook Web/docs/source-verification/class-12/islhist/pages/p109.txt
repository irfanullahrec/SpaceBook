After Chingiz Khan's death his grandson Mangu Khan ascended the throne. Mangu Khan instructed his brother Hulagu Khan to conquer the Islamic regions. Unfortunately in those days Shi'a–Sunni enmity was at its height in Baghdad. According to Ibn Khaldun and other reliable historians the Shi'a vizier Ibn al-'Alqami invited Hulagu Khan to attack Baghdad. Hulagu Khan took full advantage of this golden opportunity. He made a full attack on Baghdad. For forty days the siege of the city continued. Caliph Musta'sim laid down his arms. The caliph and his sons were killed. Baghdad, the centre of sciences and arts and of civilisation and culture, was destroyed and ruined. The water of the Tigris was first red with human blood, then it became black with the ink of books. With this attack the caliphate of the Banu Abbas came to an end.
