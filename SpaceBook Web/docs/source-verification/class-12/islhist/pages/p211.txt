She requested al-Nasir that a city be settled in her name, in which there would be magnificent palaces and beautiful buildings. Al-Nasir accepted this request and issued the decree for building the city in 936 CE. Abd al-Rahman spent twenty-five years of his era, and al-Hakam fifteen years, on its adornment; with this the beauty of Cordoba was enhanced fourfold. This city was seven furlongs long and five furlongs wide. It is said that when this city was complete, Zahra' came to see it. She saw marble palaces on one side and an ugly mountain on the other. She complained to al-Nasir about the mountain. So, planting beautiful trees on the mountain, it was made Jabal al-'Arus. But alas! The city whose building took thirty-five years was destroyed and ruined in only twenty-five years at the hands of the Berber armies. p عبدالرحمان الداخل نے رصافہ سے فنِ تعمیر کا جو سلسلہ شروع کیا وہ بنی نصر کے دورِ حکومت میں اپنے عروج کو پہنچ گیا۔ الحمراء کی تعمیر محمد بن الاحمر نے شروع کی۔ اس نسبت سے اسے الحمراء کا نام دیا گیا۔ لیکن بعض مورخین کا خیال ہے کہ اس کی تعمیر میں جو خاص مسالہ استعمال کیا گیا تھا، اس کا رنگ سرخ تھا۔ اس لئے اسے الحمراء کا نام دیا گیا۔ غرناطہ کا یہ محل ابتداء میں ایک پہاڑی پر واقع قلعہ تھا۔ جسے مسمار کر کے الحمراء کی تعمیر کی گئی۔ اس کے ایک طرف کوہ سیرانواڈا کی برف پوش چوٹیاں ہیں اور دوسری طرف زرخیز وادی جس میں دریائے صدارہ بل کھاتا ہوا رواں دواں ہے۔ اس محل کا اندرونی حصہ پچی کاری کاشی کاری اور نقش نگاری کا بہترین نمونہ ہے۔ فن || The series of the art of architecture that Abd al-Rahman al-Dakhil began with al-Rusafah reached its height in the era of government of the Banu Nasr. The building of al-Hamra' was begun by Muhammad bin al-Ahmar. From this connection it was given the name al-Hamra'. But some historians think that the special mortar which was used in its building — its colour was red; therefore it was given the name al-Hamra'. This palace of Granada was in the beginning a fort situated on a hill, which was demolished and al-Hamra' was built. On one side of it are the snow-clad peaks of the Sierra Nevada, and on the other side a fertile valley in which the river Darro flows winding. The interior part of this palace is the best specimen of stucco work, tile work and engraving.
