p سوال نمبر 2: درج ذیل سوالات کے مفصل جوابات تحریر کریں: || Question 2: Write detailed answers to the following questions: || ۲ پوښتنه: د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ولیکئ:
qp i | پاکستان کی بیرونی تجارت کی نمایاں خصوصیات بیان کیجئے۔ (گوجرانوالہ بورڈ 2009ء، لاہور بورڈ 2011ء) || State the prominent characteristics of the foreign trade of Pakistan. (Gujranwala Board 2009, Lahore Board 2011) || د پاکستان د بهرنۍ سوداګرۍ ښکاره ځانګړتیاوې بیان کړئ. (ګوجرانواله بورډ ۲۰۰۹، لاهور بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss (1) Persistent deficit — imports exceed exports; (2) Exports concentrated on a few items — cotton, textiles, rice, leather, sports and surgical goods; (3) Imports mainly of petroleum, machinery, chemicals, edible oil, vehicles and iron and steel.
ss (4) Shift from raw materials to manufactured exports; (5) Concentration of markets — USA, EU, China, UAE, Afghanistan; (6) Low value-added and low-quality exports; (7) Exports affected by world prices and quotas; (8) Most trade is by sea through Karachi and Port Qasim.
ss (9) Rising import bill due to oil prices and population growth; (10) Government's control through tariffs, licences and export incentives; (11) Growing trade with China under CPEC and free-trade agreements; (12) Dependence on remittances and loans to finance the trade deficit.
endsolution
qp ii | پاکستانی معیشت میں بیرونی تجارت کی اہمیت بیان کریں۔ || State the importance of foreign trade in the Pakistani economy. || په پاکستاني اقتصاد کې د بهرنۍ سوداګرۍ اهمیت بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss (1) Earning foreign exchange needed for development; (2) Import of machinery, technology and raw materials for industry; (3) Import of petroleum — the main source of energy; (4) Import of food items (edible oil, wheat in shortage years) and medicines.
ss (5) Market for surplus agricultural and industrial products; (6) Employment in export industries; (7) Industrialisation and economies of scale; (8) Government revenue from customs duties; (9) Repayment of foreign loans and interest.
ss (10) Consumers get variety of goods; (11) Improvement in quality through competition; (12) Friendly relations with other countries; (13) Help in defence needs; (14) Raises national income and the standard of living.
endsolution
