qp ii | پاکستان ریلوے کی اہمیت اور کارکردگی پر نوٹ لکھیں۔ || Write a note on the importance and performance of Pakistan Railway. || د پاکستان رېل پر اهمیت او کارکړنې یادښت ولیکئ.
solution جواب
ss Pakistan Railways is the national railway (about 7,800 km of track), run by the Ministry of Railways; it carries passengers and goods across the country, linking Karachi port with the north.
ss Importance: cheap and safe transport for passengers and heavy goods (coal, wheat, oil, cement, fertiliser); links ports with industries and markets; provides employment to thousands; important for defence and national unity; saves fuel compared with road transport.
ss Performance: it has expanded and introduced new trains, dry ports and freight services, but suffers from losses, old locomotives and coaches, shortage of engines, delays, accidents, corruption and competition from road transport. Reforms such as new locomotives, track upgradation and CPEC's ML-1 project aim to improve it.
endsolution
qp iii | کمپیوٹر اور اطلاع ٹیکنالوجی (IT) کی اہمیت واضح کریں۔ || Make clear the importance of the computer and information technology (IT). || د کمپیوټر او معلوماتي ټکنالوژۍ (IT) اهمیت روښانه کړئ.
solution جواب
ss The computer and information technology (IT) means the use of computers, software, the internet and telecommunications to store, process and transmit information.
ss Importance: (1) Faster and accurate work in offices, banks and industries; (2) Banking — online banking, ATMs, mobile wallets; (3) E-commerce — buying and selling online; (4) Education — e-learning, online libraries; (5) Health — hospital records, telemedicine; (6) Communication — e-mail, social media, video calls.
ss (7) Employment and exports — software houses, freelancing and IT exports earn foreign exchange; (8) E-government — NADRA, online tax filing, e-challans; (9) Research and planning — data processing; (10) Industry — automation and design; (11) Agriculture — weather and market information. IT is thus essential for economic development.
endsolution

solution جواب
ss The efficiency (productivity) of labour depends on: (1) Physical health and strength; (2) Mental ability and intelligence; (3) General and technical education and training; (4) Character — honesty, hard work and discipline; (5) Climate.

ss (11) Standard of living — food, housing, recreation; (12) Specialisation and division of labour; (13) Hope of promotion; (14) Peaceful industrial relations; (15) Political and economic stability.

solution جواب
ss Mobility of labour means the ability of workers to move from one place or occupation to another. Factors increasing it: (1) Education and technical training; (2) Better means of transport and communications; (3) Information about jobs (employment exchanges, internet, media).
ss (4) Higher wages and better facilities elsewhere; (5) Industrialisation and urbanisation creating new jobs; (6) Weakening of family ties and caste/social restrictions; (7) Peace and security; (8) Housing facilities in new places; (9) Opportunities of promotion; (10) Government policies encouraging migration (overseas employment).
