q 16 | زکوٰۃ کا نصاب لکھیں؟ || Write the nisab of zakat. || د زکات نصاب ولیکئ.
sol جواب ## اگر کسی شخص کے پاس سونا ½7 تولے، چاندی ½52 تولے اور نقد روپیہ 200 درہم کے برابر ہو اور اس کو ایک سال اپنے پاس رکھتا ہو تا اس پر 2.5% زکوٰۃ عائد ہو گی۔ معدنیات پر 20%۔ مال مویشی، اونٹ 5، گائے 30، بھیڑ بکریاں 40 ہوں تو اس پر زکوٰۃ لاگو ہو گی۔ سامانِ تجارت پر بھی 2.5% کے حساب سے زکوٰۃ عائد ہو گی۔
p جواب: اگر کسی شخص کے پاس سونا ½7 تولے، چاندی ½52 تولے اور نقد روپیہ 200 درہم کے برابر ہو اور اس کو ایک سال اپنے پاس رکھتا ہو تا اس پر 2.5% زکوٰۃ عائد ہو گی۔ معدنیات پر 20%۔ مال مویشی، اونٹ 5، گائے 30، بھیڑ بکریاں 40 ہوں تو اس پر زکوٰۃ لاگو ہو گی۔ سامانِ تجارت پر بھی 2.5% کے حساب سے زکوٰۃ عائد ہو گی۔ || A. q 17 | کیا زکوٰۃ اسلامی ٹیکس ہے؟ || Is zakat an Islamic tax? || ایا زکات اسلامي مالیه ده؟
sol جواب ## جی نہیں! زکوٰۃ اور ٹیکس دو علیحدہ علیحدہ تصور ہیں۔ زکوٰۃ اسلام کا ایک رکن ہے اس کا نصاب ایک مقررہ حد ہے جو اسلام میں مقرر کر دیا گیا ہے۔ زکوٰۃ کی کوئی معافی نہیں۔ جبکہ ٹیکس حکومت کی آمدنی ہے حکومت اس کو عائد کرتی ہے اور وصول بھی کرتی ہے اس کی شرح تبدیل ہوتی رہتی ہے بعض وجوہات میں ٹیکس معاف بھی ہو سکتا ہے۔
p جواب: جی نہیں! زکوٰۃ اور ٹیکس دو علیحدہ علیحدہ تصور ہیں۔ زکوٰۃ اسلام کا ایک رکن ہے اس کا نصاب ایک مقررہ حد ہے جو اسلام میں مقرر کر دیا گیا ہے۔ زکوٰۃ کی کوئی معافی نہیں۔ جبکہ ٹیکس حکومت کی آمدنی ہے حکومت اس کو عائد کرتی ہے اور وصول بھی کرتی ہے اس کی شرح تبدیل ہوتی رہتی ہے بعض وجوہات میں ٹیکس معاف بھی ہو سکتا ہے۔ || A. No! Zakat and tax are separate concepts. Zakat is a pillar of Islam; its nisab is a fixed limit which has been fixed in Islam. There is no exemption from zakat, whereas tax is the income of the government; the government imposes it and also collects it; its rate keeps changing; for some reasons tax can also be exempted. || ځواب: نه! زکات او مالیه دوه جلا تصورونه دي. زکات د اسلام رکن دی؛ نصاب یې یوه ټاکلې پوله ده چې په اسلام کې ټاکل شوې. له زکات معافیت نشته. حال دا چې مالیه د حکومت عاید دی؛ حکومت یې لګوي او اخلي یې هم؛ نرخ یې بدلېږي؛ د ځینو لاملونو له امله مالیه معاف هم کېدای شي.

q 1 | مرکزی حکومت پاکستان کی آمدنی کے ذرائع بیان کریں۔ || State the sources of income of the central government of Pakistan. || د پاکستان د مرکزي حکومت د عاید سرچینې بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss Tax revenue: (1) Income tax and corporation tax — the largest direct tax; (2) Sales tax/General Sales Tax (GST) — the largest single source; (3) Customs duties on imports and exports; (4) Federal excise duty on industrial goods and services; (5) Capital value tax, withholding taxes, petroleum levy.
ss Non-tax revenue: (1) Profits of the State Bank and public enterprises (PTCL, OGDCL, PIA dividends); (2) Interest on loans to provinces and public corporations; (3) Fees, fines and charges — passports, licences, telecom licences; (4) Income of railways and post offices; (5) Gas development surcharge and royalties on oil and gas.
ss Capital receipts: internal borrowing (national savings schemes, treasury bills, Pakistan Investment Bonds), external loans and grants (IMF, World Bank, ADB, friendly countries), privatisation proceeds, and deficit financing.
endsolution
q 2 | پاکستان کے نظامِ محصولات کا جائزہ لیجئے۔ || Take a review of the tax system of Pakistan. || د پاکستان د مالیاتي نظام ارزونه وکړئ.
solution جواب
ss Pakistan's tax system: federal taxes (income tax, sales tax, customs, federal excise) are collected by the Federal Board of Revenue; provinces collect agricultural income tax, property tax, stamp duty, motor vehicle tax, sales tax on services and abiana.
ss Features/weaknesses: (1) Narrow tax base — a very small part of the population files returns; (2) Low tax-to-GDP ratio (about 9–11%); (3) Heavy reliance on indirect taxes (about 60–70%), which is regressive; (4) Large-scale tax evasion and undocumented economy; (5) Exemptions for influential sectors; (6) Agriculture and retail trade are under-taxed.
ss (7) Complicated and frequently changing laws; (8) Weak administration and corruption; (9) Withholding taxes form a large part of direct taxes; (10) Reforms — GST, universal self-assessment, computerisation, NTN registration and the Active Taxpayers List — have been introduced but more improvement is needed.
endsolution
q 3 | پاکستان کے محصولاتی نظام کو بہتر بنانے کے لئے تجاویز پیش کریں۔ || Present proposals for making the revenue system of Pakistan better. || د پاکستان د محصولاتي نظام د ښه کولو لپاره وړاندیزونه وړاندې کړئ.
solution جواب
ss (1) Widen the tax base by bringing all earners, including big landlords, traders and professionals, into the tax net; (2) Use NADRA and bank data to identify non-filers; (3) Reduce dependence on indirect taxes and increase the share of direct taxes; (4) Simplify the laws and make them stable; reduce rates but remove exemptions.
ss (5) Computerise and automate tax administration; e-filing and e-payment; (6) Train and adequately pay tax officials; strict accountability for corruption; (7) Document the economy — encourage banking transactions and discourage cash dealings; (8) Develop a tax culture by awareness campaigns and by spending revenue honestly on public services.
ss (9) Increase non-tax revenue through efficient public enterprises; (10) Control smuggling and under-invoicing; (11) Collect zakat and ushr efficiently; (12) Reduce wasteful expenditure so that taxes are not raised; (13) Improve coordination between federal and provincial revenue authorities.
endsolution
q 4 | مرکزی حکومت کی مالیاتی پالیسی (Fiscal Policy) کے بڑے بڑے مقاصد بیان کیجئے۔ || State the big objectives of the fiscal policy (Fiscal Policy) of the central government. || د مرکزي حکومت د مالي پالیسۍ (Fiscal Policy) لویې موخې بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss (1) Mobilisation of resources — raising revenue for development and public services; (2) Economic growth — encouraging saving, investment and production through tax concessions and development spending; (3) Full employment — public works and incentives to industry.
ss (4) Price stability — controlling inflation and deflation through taxes, expenditure and borrowing; (5) Fair distribution of income and wealth — progressive taxes and spending on the poor; (6) Balance of payments improvement — import duties, export rebates.
ss (7) Reduction of the budget deficit and public debt; (8) Development of backward regions; (9) Provision of social services — education, health, housing; (10) Allocation of resources to priority sectors; (11) Establishment of a welfare state.
endsolution
q 5 | پاکستان کی صوبائی حکومتوں کے ذرائع آمدنی پر بحث کریں۔ (میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورڈ 2010ء، ملتان بورڈ 2011ء) || Discuss the sources of income of the provincial governments of Pakistan. (Mirpur Azad Kashmir Board 2010, Multan Board 2011) || د پاکستان د ولایتي حکومتونو د عاید پر سرچینو بحث وکړئ. (میرپور ازاد کشمیر بورډ ۲۰۱۰، ملتان بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss (1) Share in the federal divisible pool under the National Finance Commission (NFC) Award — the biggest source (more than 80% of provincial revenue), distributed mainly on the basis of population; (2) Straight transfers — royalties and development surcharges on oil and gas, excise on natural gas; (3) Grants and special grants from the federal government.
ss Provincial taxes: agricultural income tax, land revenue, property tax (urban immovable property), stamp duty and registration fees, motor vehicle tax, provincial excise (on liquor, etc.), entertainment tax, professional tax, sales tax on services (collected by PRA, SRB, KPRA, BRA), abiana (water rate), electricity duty.
ss Non-tax sources: fees of courts and educational institutions, income from forests, irrigation, provincial public enterprises, fines, and loans from the federal government and foreign agencies.
endsolution
q 6 | زکوٰۃ کے مستحقین کون کون ہیں؟ || Who are the recipients of zakat? || د زکات مستحقین څوک دي؟
solution جواب
ss The recipients of zakat are named in the Holy Quran (At-Taubah 9:60) — eight categories: (1) Fuqara — the poor who have little; (2) Masakin — the needy who have nothing; (3) Amileen — those employed to collect and distribute zakat; (4) Muallafat-ul-Quloob — those whose hearts are to be reconciled (new Muslims).
ss (5) Fir-riqab — freeing slaves and captives; (6) Al-gharimeen — debtors unable to pay their debts; (7) Fi sabeelillah — in the way of Allah (jihad, religious and welfare purposes); (8) Ibn-us-sabeel — travellers in need, away from home.
endsolution
q 7 | زکوٰۃ کی معاشی اور معاشرتی اہمیت پر نوٹ لکھیں۔ (بہاولپور بورڈ 2011ء) || Write a note on the economic and social importance of zakat. (Bahawalpur Board 2011) || د زکات پر اقتصادي او ټولنیز اهمیت یادښت ولیکئ. (بهاولپور بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss Economic importance: (1) Circulation of wealth — it prevents concentration of wealth in a few hands; (2) Reduces poverty and inequality by transferring wealth from the rich to the poor; (3) Discourages hoarding — idle wealth decreases each year by 2.5%, so it is invested; (4) Increases investment, production and employment.
ss (5) Increases demand — the poor spend zakat on necessities, which expands markets; (6) Social security — help for orphans, widows, disabled, unemployed and debtors; (7) Reduces the government's burden of welfare expenditure; (8) Encourages lawful earning and economic stability.
ss Social importance: purifies wealth and the soul from greed and miserliness; creates brotherhood, love and cooperation between rich and poor; reduces crime, jealousy and class conflict; makes society stable and peaceful; and establishes an Islamic welfare state. In Pakistan zakat and ushr are collected under the Zakat and Ushr Ordinance 1980.
endsolution
