q 1 | ملکی اور بین الاقوامی تجارت میں فرق کی وضاحت کریں۔ (سرگودھا بورڈ 2009ء، بہاولپور بورڈ 2009ء، میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورڈ 2010ء، ملتان بورڈ 2011ء) || Explain the difference between domestic and international trade. (Sargodha Board 2009, Bahawalpur Board 2009, Mirpur Azad Kashmir Board 2010, Multan Board 2011) || د هېوادنۍ او نړیوالې سوداګرۍ توپیر روښانه کړئ. (سرګودها بورډ ۲۰۰۹، بهاولپور بورډ ۲۰۰۹، میرپور ازاد کشمیر بورډ ۲۰۱۰، ملتان بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss Domestic (internal) trade is buying and selling within the boundaries of one country; international trade is the exchange of goods and services between different countries.
ss Differences: (1) Currency — domestic trade uses one currency, international trade needs foreign exchange and an exchange rate; (2) Mobility of factors — labour and capital move freely within a country but not between countries; (3) Laws and policies — one set of laws inside, different laws, tariffs and quotas between countries.
ss (4) Distance and transport — international trade involves longer distances, higher transport and insurance costs; (5) Language, culture and tastes differ between countries; (6) Risk — international trade has more political and exchange-rate risks; (7) Documents — imports and exports need licences, letters of credit, bills of lading and customs clearance; (8) Balance of payments problems arise only in international trade.

q 2 | بین الاقوامی تجارت کے فائدے بیان کریں۔ (سرگودھا بورڈ 2009ء) || State the benefits of international trade. (Sargodha Board 2009) || د نړیوالې سوداګرۍ ګټې بیان کړئ. (سرګودها بورډ ۲۰۰۹)
solution جواب
ss (1) Goods not produced at home can be obtained (oil, machinery); (2) International division of labour and specialisation according to comparative advantage raises world output; (3) Consumers get cheaper and better-quality goods and more variety.
ss (4) Surplus production can be sold abroad, expanding markets and industries; (5) Earning foreign exchange for development; (6) Transfer of technology and know-how; (7) Large-scale production and economies of scale; (8) Help in times of famine or scarcity.
ss (9) Competition improves efficiency and quality; (10) Employment increases in export industries; (11) Friendly relations and cultural exchange between nations; (12) Price levels tend to equalise across countries.

q 4 | بین الاقوامی تجارت کے تقابلی مصارف کی برتری کے نظریہ کی تشریح کیجئے۔ (گوجرانوالہ بورڈ 2009ء) || Explain the theory of comparative advantage in costs in international trade. (Gujranwala Board 2009) || د نړیوالې سوداګرۍ د پرتلیزو لګښتونو د برترۍ نظریه تشریح کړئ. (ګوجرانواله بورډ ۲۰۰۹)
solution جواب
ss The theory of comparative advantage (comparative costs) was given by David Ricardo. It says that a country should specialise in producing those goods in which its comparative (relative) cost advantage is greatest, or comparative disadvantage least, and import the others; then both countries gain from trade even if one is more efficient in producing both goods.
ss Assumptions: two countries, two goods; labour is the only factor; perfect competition; labour mobile within a country but not between countries; constant cost; no transport costs; full employment.
ss Example: In Pakistan 10 days of labour produce 1 unit of cloth and 20 days 1 unit of wheat; in England 8 days produce cloth and 10 days produce wheat. England is better in both, but its advantage is greater in wheat (10/20 = 0.5) than in cloth (8/10 = 0.8). So England specialises in wheat, Pakistan in cloth (its disadvantage is least there), and by exchanging, both countries obtain more of both goods than without trade.
