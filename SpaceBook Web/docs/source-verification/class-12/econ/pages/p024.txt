## 3۔ اوسط میلانِ بچت: Average Propensity is Save || 3. Average propensity to save || 3. د سپما اوسط تمایل (Average Propensity is Save)

## 4۔ مختتم میلانِ بچت: Marginal Propensity to Save (MPS) || 4. Marginal Propensity to Save (MPS) || 4. د سپما حاشیوي تمایل (Marginal Propensity to Save, MPS)
