p سوال نمبر 2۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مفصل جوابات تحریر کریں: || Question 2. Write detailed answers to the following questions: || ۲ پوښتنه. د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ولیکئ:
qp i | سرمایہ دارانہ معاشی نظام کی خصوصیات بیان کریں۔ (میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورڈ 2010ء، ملتان بورڈ 2011ء) || State the characteristics of the capitalist economic system. (Mirpur Azad Kashmir Board 2010, Multan Board 2011) || د پانګوال اقتصادي نظام ځانګړتیاوې بیان کړئ. (میرپور ازاد کشمیر بورډ ۲۰۱۰، ملتان بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss Capitalism is an economic system in which the means of production are privately owned and economic activities are guided by the price mechanism and the profit motive.
ss Characteristics: (1) Right of private property; (2) Freedom of enterprise — anyone may start any business; (3) Profit motive; (4) Price mechanism — demand and supply decide what, how and for whom to produce; (5) Competition among producers and sellers; (6) Consumer's sovereignty.
ss (7) Freedom of choice of occupation and consumption; (8) Right of inheritance; (9) Minimum government interference (laissez-faire); (10) Interest-based banking and credit; (11) Inequality of income; (12) Saving and investment by private individuals; (13) Free trade.
endsolution
qp ii | سرمایہ داری نظام (Capitalism) کی خامیاں بیان کیجئے۔ || State the shortcomings of the capitalist system (Capitalism). || د پانګوالۍ نظام (Capitalism) نیمګړتیاوې بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss (1) Unequal distribution of wealth — the rich become richer and the poor poorer; (2) Exploitation of workers by capitalists — low wages and long hours; (3) Monopolies harm consumers by high prices; (4) Trade cycles — booms and depressions cause unemployment.
ss (5) Wastage of resources through competition and advertising; (6) Production of luxuries for the rich instead of necessities for the poor; (7) Interest system — concentration of wealth and exploitation; (8) Class conflict between workers and owners; (9) Neglect of social welfare.
ss (10) Materialism — moral values are ignored for profit; (11) Consumer's sovereignty is limited by low incomes; (12) Hoarding, speculation and black marketing; (13) Environmental damage.
endsolution
qp iii | سوشلزم کی خامیاں لکھیے۔ || Write the shortcomings of socialism. || د سوسیالیزم نیمګړتیاوې ولیکئ.
solution جواب
ss (1) No freedom — individuals cannot choose their occupations or businesses freely; (2) Abolition of private property kills the incentive to work hard; (3) Lack of profit motive leads to low efficiency and productivity; (4) Bureaucracy and red tape in planning.
ss (5) Errors in central planning cause shortages and surpluses; (6) Loss of consumer's sovereignty — the state decides what is produced; (7) Dictatorship and suppression of political freedom; (8) Rigidity — difficulty in adjusting to changing needs.
ss (9) Corruption and favouritism among officials; (10) Denial of religion and moral values; (11) Neglect of quality and innovation; (12) Practical failure — the collapse of the Soviet Union (1991) showed its weaknesses.
endsolution
qp iv | اسلام کے معاشی نظام کی اہم خصوصیات بیان کیجئے۔ (ملتان بورڈ 2009ء، بہاولپور بورڈ 2009ء، میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورڈ 2009ء، بہاولپور بورڈ 2010ء) || State the important characteristics of the economic system of Islam. (Multan Board 2009, Bahawalpur Board 2009, Mirpur Azad Kashmir Board 2009, Bahawalpur Board 2010) || د اسلام د اقتصادي نظام مهمې ځانګړتیاوې بیان کړئ. (ملتان بورډ ۲۰۰۹، بهاولپور بورډ ۲۰۰۹، میرپور ازاد کشمیر بورډ ۲۰۰۹، بهاولپور بورډ ۲۰۱۰)
solution جواب
ss (1) Ownership of everything belongs to Allah; man is His trustee, so property must be used according to His commands; (2) Right of private property within limits; (3) Lawful (halal) earning — prohibition of interest (riba), gambling, hoarding, fraud and black marketing.
ss (4) Zakat, ushr, sadaqat and the law of inheritance for the circulation of wealth; (5) Prohibition of concentration of wealth; (6) Freedom of enterprise with moral limits; (7) Profit-and-loss sharing (mudarabah, musharakah) instead of interest.
ss (8) Moderation in spending — no extravagance or miserliness; (9) Just distribution and rights of workers — pay wages before the sweat dries; (10) Social security — the state provides basic needs to all; (11) Balance between individual and social interests; (12) Dignity of labour; (13) Fair dealing in trade and honest weights and measures.
endsolution
qp v | اسلامی معاشی نظام میں ارتکازِ دولت (Concentration of Wealth) کو ممنوع قرار دیا گیا ہے۔ اس پر نوٹ لکھیں؟ || In the Islamic economic system the concentration of wealth (Concentration of Wealth) has been declared forbidden. Write a note on it. || په اسلامي اقتصادي نظام کې د شتمنۍ تمرکز (Concentration of Wealth) منع ګڼل شوی. پر هغه یادښت ولیکئ.
solution جواب
ss Islam forbids the concentration of wealth in a few hands. The Holy Quran says that wealth should not merely circulate among the rich (Al-Hashr 59:7), and warns those who hoard gold and silver (At-Taubah 9:34).
ss Measures: (1) Zakat and ushr — compulsory transfer from the rich to the poor; (2) Prohibition of interest, which makes the rich richer; (3) Law of inheritance — wealth is divided among many heirs; (4) Prohibition of hoarding, monopoly and speculation; (5) Encouragement of sadaqat and infaq (spending in the way of Allah).
ss (6) Prohibition of gambling, bribery and unlawful earning; (7) Fair wages to labour; (8) Use of idle land — land not cultivated can be taken back by the state; (9) Responsibility of the state to provide basic needs. In this way wealth keeps circulating, poverty is reduced and society remains balanced.
endsolution
qp vi | اسلامی معاشی نظام اور سرمایہ دارانہ نظام کا موازنہ پیش کیجئے۔ || Present a comparison of the Islamic economic system and the capitalist system. || د اسلامي اقتصادي نظام او پانګوال نظام پرتله وړاندې کړئ.
solution جواب
ss Ownership: in Islam, Allah is the real owner and man is a trustee; in capitalism, man has absolute ownership of property.
ss Interest: Islam prohibits interest and uses profit-and-loss sharing; capitalism is based on interest.
ss Earnings: Islam allows only lawful (halal) earnings and forbids gambling, hoarding and fraud; capitalism allows any profitable activity.
ss Distribution: Islam ensures circulation of wealth through zakat, ushr and inheritance; capitalism leads to concentration of wealth and inequality.
ss Freedom: Islam gives economic freedom within moral limits; capitalism gives unlimited freedom (laissez-faire).
ss Motive and welfare: Islam aims at the pleasure of Allah and social welfare; capitalism aims at maximum profit. Islam provides social security and protects workers' rights; capitalism neglects the poor. Islam keeps a balance between individual and society.
endsolution
qp vii | زکوٰۃ کی معاشی اہمیت واضح کریں۔ || Make clear the economic importance of zakat. || د زکات اقتصادي اهمیت روښانه کړئ.
solution جواب
ss Zakat is a compulsory payment of 2.5% on savings and wealth above the nisab held for one year; it is one of the five pillars of Islam.
ss Economic importance: (1) Circulation of wealth and prevention of its concentration; (2) Reduction in poverty and income inequality; (3) Discourages hoarding — idle money is invested, increasing production and employment; (4) Raises demand for goods as the poor's purchasing power increases.
ss (5) Social security for the poor, orphans, widows, disabled and debtors; (6) Reduces the government's welfare burden; (7) Promotes economic stability and growth; (8) Creates brotherhood, cooperation and peace in society, reducing crime and class conflict; (9) Purifies wealth and encourages lawful earning.
endsolution
