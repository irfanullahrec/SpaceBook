p سوال 2۔ درج ذیل سوالات کے مفصل جوابات تحریر کریں۔ || Question 2. Write detailed answers to the following questions. || ۲ پوښتنه: د لاندې پوښتنو مفصل ځوابونه ولیکئ.
q 1 | نجی مالیات اور سرکاری مالیات میں فرق بیان کیجئے؟ (سرگودھا بورڈ 2009ء، لاہور بورڈ 2010ء) || State the difference between private finance and public finance. (Sargodha Board 2009, Lahore Board 2010) || د خصوصي مالیې او دولتي مالیې توپیر بیان کړئ. (سرګودها بورډ ۲۰۰۹، لاهور بورډ ۲۰۱۰)
solution جواب
ss Private finance is the income and expenditure of individuals and firms; public finance is the income, expenditure and borrowing of the government.
ss Differences: (1) Adjustment — an individual adjusts expenditure to his income, while the government first decides expenditure and then raises income. (2) Objective — private finance aims at personal satisfaction or profit; public finance aims at public welfare. (3) Sources — individuals earn from work and property; government gets taxes, fees, loans and can print notes. (4) Borrowing — the government can borrow internally and externally on a large scale; individuals have limited credit.
ss (5) Secrecy — private budgets are kept secret, while the government budget is published and debated in parliament. (6) Time period — individuals plan for the short term; government plans for long periods (five-year plans). (7) Compulsion — government can collect taxes by law; individuals cannot force anyone to pay them. (8) Surplus — a surplus is good for an individual, but a surplus budget is not always good for a government.
endsolution
q 2 | ٹیکس عائد کرنے کے اصول بیان کریں؟ (ملتان بورڈ 2009ء، سرگودھا بورڈ 2010ء) || State the principles of imposing tax. (Multan Board 2009, Sargodha Board 2010) || د مالیې د لګولو اصول بیان کړئ. (ملتان بورډ ۲۰۰۹، سرګودها بورډ ۲۰۱۰)
solution جواب
ss Adam Smith's canons: (1) Equality — tax according to ability to pay; (2) Certainty — the amount, time and method of payment should be clear; (3) Convenience — tax should be collected at a time and in a way convenient for the taxpayer (e.g. tax deducted from salary); (4) Economy — cost of collection should be low.
ss Other principles: (5) Productivity — the tax should yield enough revenue; (6) Elasticity — revenue should rise with need, e.g. by changing rates; (7) Flexibility — the system can be changed easily; (8) Simplicity — easy to understand; (9) Diversity — many kinds of taxes so the burden is spread; (10) Economic growth — taxes should not discourage saving, investment and production.
endsolution
q 3 | ٹیکسوں کی مختلف اقسام کی وضاحت کریں؟ (بہاولپور بورڈ 2010ء) || Explain the different kinds of taxes. (Bahawalpur Board 2010) || د مالیو بېلابېل ډولونه روښانه کړئ. (بهاولپور بورډ ۲۰۱۰)
solution جواب
ss Direct taxes — the burden falls on the person who pays them and cannot be shifted: income tax, wealth tax, property tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax.
ss Indirect taxes — paid first by producers or traders but shifted to consumers through prices: sales tax, excise duty, customs (import/export) duties, value-added tax.
ss By rate: Proportional tax — the same rate on all incomes; Progressive tax — the rate rises as income rises (income tax); Regressive tax — the rate falls as income rises, or it takes a larger share from the poor (many indirect taxes); Degressive tax — progressive up to a limit and then proportional.
ss Other kinds: specific tax (per unit of goods) and ad valorem tax (as a percentage of value); single and multiple tax systems.
endsolution
q 4 | حکومت کے مختلف ذرائع آمدنی کی تفصیل بتایئے؟ || Give the detail of the different sources of income of the government. || د حکومت د عاید د بېلابېلو سرچینو تفصیل ووایاست.
solution جواب
ss Tax revenue: income tax, corporation tax, wealth tax, property tax, sales tax/GST, central excise duty, customs duties on imports and exports, capital value tax.
ss Non-tax revenue: fees (licence, court and registration fees), fines and penalties, prices of government services (railway, post, PTCL), profits of public enterprises (State Bank, WAPDA), interest on loans given to provinces, gifts and grants, escheats.
ss Borrowing and others: internal loans (bonds, savings certificates, treasury bills), external loans and aid (IMF, World Bank, ADB, friendly countries), and deficit financing (borrowing from the central bank/printing notes). Provinces also get their share from the divisible pool under the NFC Award, land revenue, abiana (water rate), excise and stamp duties.
endsolution
q 5 | بالواسطہ ٹیکس کے فوائد اور نقصانات بیان کریں؟ (سرگودھا بورڈ 2011ء) || State the benefits and harms of indirect tax. (Sargodha Board 2011) || د غیر مستقیمې مالیې ګټې او زیانونه بیان کړئ. (سرګودها بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss Benefits of indirect tax: (1) Convenient — paid in small amounts when buying goods; (2) Difficult to evade, as it is included in prices; (3) Everyone, including the poor, contributes to state revenue; (4) Elastic — revenue rises as consumption increases; (5) Can discourage harmful goods (tobacco, alcohol) and luxury imports; (6) Cheap to collect from a few producers and importers.
ss Harms: (1) Regressive — the poor pay a larger share of their income; (2) Uncertain — revenue depends on sales; (3) Raises prices and can cause inflation; (4) Reduces demand and may harm industry; (5) No civic consciousness, as people do not realise they pay tax; (6) Can encourage smuggling.
endsolution
q 6 | براہِ راست ٹیکس کی خوبیوں اور خامیوں پر بحث کریں؟ (بہاولپور بورڈ 2011ء، میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورڈ 2011ء) || Discuss the merits and demerits of direct tax. (Bahawalpur Board 2011, Mirpur Azad Kashmir Board 2011) || د مستقیمې مالیې پر ښېګڼو او نیمګړتیاوو بحث وکړئ. (بهاولپور بورډ ۲۰۱۱، میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss Merits of direct tax: (1) Equitable — based on ability to pay and usually progressive; (2) Certain — taxpayer knows how much and when to pay; (3) Economical to collect; (4) Elastic and productive — revenue rises with income; (5) Creates civic consciousness; (6) Reduces inequalities of income and wealth.
ss Demerits: (1) Inconvenient — paid in a lump sum; (2) Encourages evasion through false returns; (3) May discourage saving, investment and hard work if rates are high; (4) Arbitrary — rates are set by the government; (5) Narrow base in poor countries, since few people have taxable incomes; (6) Complicated assessment procedures.
endsolution
q 7 | پاکستان کے نظامِ محصولات کا جائزہ لیجئے؟ (لاہور بورڈ 2011ء) || Review the tax system of Pakistan. (Lahore Board 2011) || د پاکستان د مالیاتي نظام کتنه وکړئ. (لاهور بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss Features of Pakistan's tax system: (1) Narrow tax base — only a small percentage of people file income-tax returns; (2) Heavy dependence on indirect taxes (about two-thirds of revenue), making the system regressive; (3) Low tax-to-GDP ratio (around 9–11%); (4) Agricultural income is largely untaxed at the federal level.
ss (5) Widespread tax evasion and a large undocumented economy; (6) Complicated laws and frequent changes; (7) Weak and sometimes corrupt tax administration; (8) Many exemptions and concessions; (9) Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) collects federal taxes, provinces collect their own taxes and get shares under the NFC Award; (10) Reforms such as GST, withholding taxes and computerisation have been introduced.
endsolution
q 8 | پاکستان میں نظامِ محصولات بہتر بنانے کے لیے تجاویز پیش کریں؟ || Present proposals to improve the tax system in Pakistan. || په پاکستان کې د مالیاتي نظام د ښه کولو وړاندیزونه وړاندې کړئ.
solution جواب
ss (1) Broaden the tax base — bring all persons with taxable income, including big landlords and traders, into the tax net; (2) Reduce reliance on indirect taxes and increase the share of direct taxes; (3) Simplify tax laws and procedures and make them stable.
ss (4) Reduce tax rates but enforce them strictly; (5) Remove unnecessary exemptions; (6) Computerise records, link NADRA, banks and property data to identify tax evaders; (7) Train honest tax officials and punish corruption; (8) Document the economy and encourage banking transactions.
ss (9) Develop a tax culture through awareness and by spending tax money visibly on public welfare; (10) Tax agricultural income fairly; (11) Self-assessment schemes and easy online filing; (12) Coordinate federal and provincial taxes to avoid double taxation.
endsolution
