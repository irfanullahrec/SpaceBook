qp iii | پاکستان کی برآمدات میں کس طرح اضافہ کیا جا سکتا ہے؟ || How can an increase be made in the exports of Pakistan? || د پاکستان په صادراتو کې څنګه زیاتوالی راوستل کېدای شي؟
solution جواب
ss (1) Increase production of exportable goods — modern agriculture and industry; (2) Improve quality and standardisation to meet international standards; (3) Diversify exports — new products (IT, engineering goods, pharmaceuticals) and value addition to cotton.
ss (4) Find new markets — Africa, Central Asia, Latin America; trade agreements; (5) Reduce cost of production — cheap energy, modern machinery, skilled labour; (6) Export incentives — rebates, tax refunds, cheap export finance; (7) Trade fairs, exhibitions and commercial attachés abroad.
ss (8) Simplify export procedures; one-window facility; (9) Stable and realistic exchange rate; (10) Improve infrastructure — ports, roads, dry ports, cold storage; (11) Control smuggling; (12) Political stability and good law and order; (13) Encourage foreign investment in export industries; (14) Develop SMEs and the export of services and IT.
endsolution
qp iv | پاکستان کے توازنِ ادائیگی میں خرابی کی وجوہات بتایئے۔ (بہاولپور بورڈ 2011ء، میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورڈ 2011ء) || State the reasons for disorder in the balance of payments of Pakistan. solution جواب
ss (1) Imports far exceed exports — large trade deficit; (2) Rising oil import bill; (3) Narrow export base dependent on textiles; (4) Low quality and high cost of exports; (5) Fall in world prices of our exports.
ss (6) Heavy repayment of foreign loans and interest; (7) Import of luxury goods; (8) Rapid population growth increasing demand for imports; (9) Energy shortages reducing exports; (10) Political instability and law-and-order problems; (11) Protectionism by developed countries.
ss (12) Natural disasters damaging export crops; (13) Decline in foreign investment and aid at times; (14) Smuggling and under-invoicing; (15) Devaluation raising the cost of imports and debt.

qp v | پاکستان کے ادائیگیوں کے توازن کو کیسے بہتر بنایا جا سکتا ہے؟ || How can the balance of payments of Pakistan be made better? || د پاکستان د تادیاتو توازن څنګه ښه کېدای شي؟
solution جواب
ss (1) Increase exports through higher production, diversification, quality improvement and new markets; (2) Reduce unnecessary imports — high duties on luxury goods; (3) Import substitution — produce at home goods now imported (edible oil, machinery); (4) Develop local energy resources (hydel, coal, solar) to cut the oil bill.
ss (5) Encourage home remittances through banking channels; (6) Attract foreign direct investment; (7) Promote tourism; (8) Reduce foreign borrowing and use loans productively; (9) Realistic exchange rate; (10) Control smuggling and under-invoicing.
ss (11) Population control; (12) Austerity in government expenditure; (13) Trade agreements with neighbouring countries; (14) Political stability and good governance; (15) Development of IT and services exports.
endsolution
qp vi | سارک ممالک (SAARC) کے اہم مقاصد بیان کیجئے۔ || State the important objectives of SAARC countries (SAARC). || د سارک هېوادونو (SAARC) مهمې موخې بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) was founded in Dhaka in December 1985; its secretariat is in Kathmandu. Members: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives and Afghanistan (since 2007).
ss Objectives: (1) Promote the welfare and quality of life of the people of South Asia; (2) Accelerate economic growth, social progress and cultural development; (3) Promote self-reliance among member countries; (4) Mutual trust and understanding; (5) Cooperation in agriculture, trade, science, technology, transport, health and education.
ss (6) Expansion of regional trade — SAPTA (1995) and SAFTA (2006); (7) Cooperation with other developing countries and international organisations; (8) Joint efforts against poverty, terrorism, drugs and natural disasters; (9) Strengthen cooperation in international forums on common interests.
endsolution
qp vii | عالمی تجارتی تنظیم (WTO) کے پاکستانی معیشت پر اثرات بیان کیجئے۔ || State the effects of the World Trade Organisation (WTO) on the Pakistani economy. || پر پاکستاني اقتصاد د سوداګرۍ د نړیوال سازمان (WTO) اغېزې بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss Positive effects: (1) Removal of textile quotas (2005) gave Pakistan access to world markets; (2) Lower tariffs abroad increase opportunities for exports; (3) Access to cheaper raw materials and machinery; (4) Foreign investment and technology transfer; (5) Competition improves efficiency and quality; (6) Dispute-settlement mechanism protects Pakistan's trade interests.
ss Negative effects: (1) Local industries face strong competition from cheap imports; (2) Pakistan must reduce its own tariffs, cutting government revenue; (3) Subsidies to agriculture and industry must be reduced; (4) TRIPS raises prices of medicines, seeds and software; (5) Strict quality, environmental and labour standards act as barriers; (6) Developed countries continue to subsidise agriculture, harming our farmers; (7) Trade deficit may increase.
endsolution
qp viii | بین الاقوامی سطح پر کرنسیوں کی شرحِ مبادلہ کیسے متعین ہوتی ہے؟ || How is the rate of exchange of currencies determined at the international level? || په نړیواله کچه د اسعارو د تبادلې نرخ څنګه ټاکل کېږي؟
solution جواب
ss The rate of exchange is the price of one currency in terms of another. Under a free (floating) system it is determined by demand for and supply of foreign exchange in the foreign exchange market.
ss Demand for foreign currency (e.g. dollars) comes from importers, those paying foreign loans and interest, students and tourists going abroad, and investors sending money abroad. Supply comes from exporters, foreign investors, remittances of overseas workers, tourists coming in, and foreign aid and loans.
ss Equilibrium: the rate settles where demand equals supply. If demand for dollars rises (more imports), the rupee depreciates; if supply rises (more exports/remittances), the rupee appreciates. Theories: the mint-par theory (gold standard), purchasing power parity theory (rate depends on relative price levels) and balance of payments theory. Under managed float the central bank intervenes by buying or selling foreign currency.
endsolution
qp ix | برآمدات بڑھانے کے لئے حکومت کی کوشش کا ذکر کیجئے؟ (سرگودھا بورڈ 2009ء) || Mention the government’s efforts to increase exports. (Sargodha Board 2009) || د صادراتو د زیاتولو لپاره د حکومت هڅې یادې کړئ. (سرګودها بورډ ۲۰۰۹)
solution جواب
ss (1) Export Promotion Bureau, now the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan (TDAP), to promote exports; (2) Cheap export finance through the State Bank's Export Finance Scheme and Long-Term Financing Facility; (3) Duty drawback and sales-tax refunds; (4) Export processing zones and special economic zones.
ss (5) Participation in international trade fairs and exhibitions; (6) Commercial attachés in embassies; (7) Free-trade and preferential-trade agreements (China, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, GSP Plus with the EU); (8) Devaluation/market-based exchange rate to make exports competitive; (9) Export awards and tax concessions to exporters.
ss (10) Development of new export products — IT, engineering goods, pharmaceuticals, halal food; (11) Improved infrastructure — ports, dry ports, Gwadar; (12) Quality-control and standards institutions; (13) Textile and strategic trade policy frameworks.
endsolution
