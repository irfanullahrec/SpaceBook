qp i | پاکستان میں معیارِ زندگی پست ہونے کے اسباب بیان کیجیے۔ (لاہور بورڈ 2009ء) || State the causes of the standard of living being low in Pakistan. (Lahore Board 2009) || په پاکستان کې د ژوند د معیار د ټیټوالي لاملونه بیان کړئ. (لاهور بورډ ۲۰۰۹)
solution جواب
ss (1) Low per capita income; (2) Rapid population growth — large families with few earners; (3) Unemployment and disguised unemployment; (4) Inflation — rising prices reduce real income; (5) Unequal distribution of income and wealth.
ss (6) Low productivity of agriculture and industry; (7) Illiteracy and lack of technical skills; (8) Poor health facilities and nutrition; (9) Shortage of capital and low saving and investment; (10) Shortage of energy (electricity, gas).
ss (11) Political instability, corruption and bad governance; (12) Heavy burden of foreign debt; (13) Social evils — extravagance on ceremonies, dowry; (14) Under-utilisation of natural resources; (15) Inadequate infrastructure and housing.

qp ii | پاکستان میں فی کس آمدنی کی کمی کے اسباب تحریر کیجیے۔ (ملتان بورڈ 2009ء، لاہور بورڈ 2010ء) || Write the causes of the low per capita income in Pakistan. (Multan Board 2009, Lahore Board 2010) || په پاکستان کې د سړي سر عاید د کمښت لاملونه ولیکئ. (ملتان بورډ ۲۰۰۹، لاهور بورډ ۲۰۱۰)
solution جواب
ss (1) Rapid growth of population (about 2% a year) divides national income among more people; (2) Low productivity in agriculture due to old methods, small holdings and shortage of water; (3) Slow industrial growth; (4) Shortage of capital — low savings and investment.
ss (5) Illiteracy and lack of technical education reduce efficiency of labour; (6) Unemployment and disguised unemployment; (7) Under-utilisation of natural resources; (8) Energy shortages; (9) Unequal distribution of wealth — feudal system and concentration in few hands.
ss (10) Political instability and law-and-order problems discourage investment; (11) Heavy defence expenditure and debt servicing; (12) Inflation; (13) Corruption and inefficient administration; (14) Natural disasters such as floods and droughts.
endsolution
qp iii | پاکستان کی فی کس آمدنی پست ہونے کی وجوہات تحریر کریں۔ (ملتان بورڈ 2011ء) || Write the reasons for Pakistan’s per capita income being low. (Multan Board 2011) || د پاکستان د سړي سر عاید د ټیټوالي لاملونه ولیکئ. (ملتان بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss The reasons are: high population growth rate; dependence on backward agriculture; small and slowly growing industrial base; shortage of capital and technology; low literacy and skills; unemployment; underdeveloped natural resources; shortage of electricity and gas.
ss Also: unequal distribution of income (landlords and industrialists hold most wealth); corruption and bad governance; political instability; heavy foreign debt and defence burden; inflation; low exports and trade deficit; social customs causing wasteful spending; and natural calamities.
endsolution
qp iv | پاکستان کی قومی آمدنی میں اضافہ کس طرح کیا جا سکتا ہے؟ || How can an increase be made in the national income of Pakistan? || د پاکستان په ملي عاید کې څنګه زیاتوالی راتلی شي؟
solution جواب
ss (1) Control population growth through family planning and education; (2) Modernise agriculture — improved seeds, fertilisers, machinery, irrigation and credit; (3) Rapid industrialisation, especially agro-based and small industries; (4) Increase saving and investment; encourage foreign investment.
ss (5) Develop human resources — education, technical training and health; (6) Overcome energy shortages by building dams and using alternative energy; (7) Exploit natural resources (minerals, gas, coal); (8) Increase exports and reduce unnecessary imports.
ss (9) Political stability and good law and order; (10) Remove corruption and improve governance; (11) Fair distribution of wealth through land reforms and progressive taxation; (12) Economic planning; (13) Enforcement of the Islamic economic system (zakat, ushr, interest-free economy); (14) Develop infrastructure — roads, communications, IT.
endsolution
qp v | معاشی ترقی کے لیے کون سے عوامل ضروری ہوتے ہیں؟ || Which factors are necessary for economic development? || د اقتصادي پرمختګ لپاره کوم عوامل اړین دي؟
solution جواب
ss Economic factors: (1) Natural resources — land, minerals, water, forests; (2) Capital formation — saving and investment; (3) Human resources — educated, skilled and healthy labour; (4) Technology and innovation; (5) Entrepreneurship; (6) Infrastructure — transport, communications, energy; (7) Size of the market and foreign trade.
ss Non-economic factors: (1) Political stability and good governance; (2) Law and order; (3) Social values — honesty, hard work, absence of social evils; (4) Education and attitudes towards change; (5) Sound institutions (banks, courts); (6) Population control; (7) Economic planning and sound government policies; (8) International cooperation, aid and peace.
endsolution
qp vi | پاکستان کے صنعتی شعبہ کے بڑے بڑے مسائل بیان کیجیے۔ || State the big problems of the industrial sector of Pakistan. || د پاکستان د صنعتي برخې لویې ستونزې بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss (1) Shortage of energy — load-shedding of electricity and gas; (2) Shortage of capital and high interest rates; (3) Outdated machinery and lack of modern technology; (4) Shortage of skilled labour and managers; (5) Dependence on imported raw materials and machinery.
ss (6) Political instability and inconsistent industrial policies; (7) Law-and-order problems and labour disputes; (8) High taxes and complicated procedures; (9) Limited domestic market and competition from foreign (smuggled) goods; (10) Lack of research and development.
ss (11) Concentration on a few industries (textiles); (12) Sick units and poor management; (13) Inadequate infrastructure; (14) Corruption and red tape; (15) Under-utilisation of installed capacity.
endsolution
qp vii | معاشی منصوبہ بندی کی اہمیت اور فوائد بیان کیجیے۔ (لاہور بورڈ 2009ء، گوجرانوالہ بورڈ 2009ء) || State the importance and benefits of economic planning. (Lahore Board 2009, Gujranwala Board 2009) || د اقتصادي پلان جوړونې اهمیت او ګټې بیان کړئ. (لاهور بورډ ۲۰۰۹، ګوجرانواله بورډ ۲۰۰۹)
solution جواب
ss Importance and benefits of economic planning: (1) Efficient use of limited resources according to national priorities; (2) Rapid economic growth and increase in national and per capita income; (3) Removal of unemployment; (4) Balanced development of all sectors and regions.
ss (5) Reduction in inequalities of income and wealth; (6) Development of infrastructure (dams, roads, power); (7) Industrialisation and modernisation of agriculture; (8) Price stability and control of inflation; (9) Mobilisation of savings and capital formation.
ss (10) Improvement of social services — education, health, housing; (11) Self-reliance — less dependence on foreign aid; (12) Improvement in balance of payments; (13) Coordination between public and private sectors; (14) Achievement of a welfare state.
endsolution
