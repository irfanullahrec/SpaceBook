qp i | تجارتی بینک کے فرائض لکھیے۔ || Write the functions of a commercial bank. || د سوداګریز بانک دندې ولیکئ.
solution جواب
ss Primary functions: (1) Accepting deposits — current (demand), saving and fixed deposits; (2) Advancing loans — overdrafts, cash credit, loans and discounting of bills; (3) Credit creation — creating deposits many times the cash reserve.
ss Agency functions: collecting cheques, bills and dividends; paying insurance premiums, bills and taxes on customers' instructions; buying and selling shares and securities; acting as trustee and executor; transferring money through drafts, pay orders and online transfers.
ss General utility functions: lockers for valuables; traveller's cheques and letters of credit; credit and debit cards, ATMs; issuing references about customers' financial standing; dealing in foreign exchange; collecting economic data; financing foreign trade.

qp ii | بینکوں کی اقسام بیان کیجئے۔ || State the kinds of banks. || د بانکونو ډولونه بیان کړئ.

ss (1) Central bank — controls currency and credit (State Bank of Pakistan); (2) Commercial banks — accept deposits and give short-term loans (HBL, MCB, UBL, NBP); (3) Industrial banks — long-term finance for industry (Industrial Development Bank, PICIC).

qp iii | تجارتی بینک اعتباری زر کس طرح تخلیق کرتے ہیں؟ (میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورڈ 2009ء) || How do commercial banks create credit money? (Mirpur Azad Kashmir Board 2009) || سوداګریز بانکونه اعتباري پیسې څنګه پیدا کوي؟ (میرپور ازاد کشمیر بورډ ۲۰۰۹)
solution جواب
ss Credit creation: commercial banks create credit (deposit) money by lending out a large part of the cash deposited with them, keeping only a fraction as cash reserve. Every loan becomes a new deposit in the banking system, which can be lent again.
ss Example: cash reserve ratio = 20%. A deposits Rs 1000; the bank keeps Rs 200 and lends Rs 800. The Rs 800 is deposited in a bank, which keeps Rs 160 and lends Rs 640, and so on: 1000 + 800 + 640 + 512 + … = Rs 5000.
ss Formula: Credit money = Original deposit ÷ Cash reserve ratio = 1000 ÷ 0.20 = Rs 5000; credit created = 5000 − 1000 = Rs 4000.
ss Limits: the cash reserve ratio, amount of cash in the economy, demand for loans, availability of securities, business conditions, the monetary policy of the central bank, and people's banking habits.
endsolution
qp iv | افراطِ زر کے عمومی اسباب بیان کیجئے۔ || State the general causes of inflation. || د انفلاسیون عمومي لاملونه بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss Inflation is a persistent rise in the general price level. Causes on the demand side (demand-pull): (1) Increase in money supply — printing notes, deficit financing; (2) Increase in bank credit; (3) Rise in incomes and spending; (4) Increase in government expenditure; (5) Population growth; (6) Black money and hoarding.
ss Causes on the supply side (cost-push): (1) Fall in production because of floods, droughts or energy shortages; (2) Rise in costs — wages, raw materials, fuel prices; (3) Indirect taxes raise prices; (4) Devaluation of currency making imports dearer; (5) Monopolies and profiteering; (6) Imported inflation from world prices; (7) Smuggling and black marketing.
endsolution
qp v | پاکستان میں افراطِ زر کے اسباب بیان کریں۔ (بہاولپور بورڈ 2011ء) || State the causes of inflation in Pakistan. (Bahawalpur Board 2011) || په پاکستان کې د انفلاسیون لاملونه بیان کړئ. (بهاولپور بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss (1) Deficit financing and borrowing from the State Bank; (2) Rapid increase in money supply and bank credit; (3) Rising oil prices and electricity/gas tariffs; (4) Devaluation of the rupee raising import costs; (5) Increase in indirect taxes (GST, excise).
ss (6) Fall in agricultural production due to floods, droughts and pests; (7) Population growth increasing demand; (8) Hoarding, smuggling and profiteering by traders; (9) Heavy non-development expenditure (defence, debt servicing); (10) Wage increases without productivity growth.
ss (11) Home remittances and black money increasing spending; (12) Weak price control machinery; (13) Imported inflation — high world prices of edible oil and food; (14) Energy shortages reducing industrial output.
endsolution
qp vi | پاکستان میں رائج افراطِ زر کا علاج بتائیے۔ اس ضمن میں حکومت نے کون کون سے اقدامات کئے ہیں؟ || State the remedy of the inflation prevalent in Pakistan. In this connection which measures has the government taken? || په پاکستان کې د رواج انفلاسیون درملنه ووایاست. په دې برخه کې حکومت کوم ګامونه پورته کړي؟
solution جواب
ss Remedies: Monetary measures — raise interest rate (policy rate), raise cash reserve ratio, open market sales of securities, selective credit control; Fiscal measures — cut unnecessary government expenditure, avoid deficit financing, increase direct taxes, reduce indirect taxes on necessities.
ss Other measures — increase production of agriculture and industry; import scarce goods; control hoarding and smuggling; effective price control; rationing of essential goods; stop wage increases not linked to productivity; reduce population growth.
ss Government steps: the State Bank tightened monetary policy (higher policy rate); restrictions on borrowing from the State Bank; Utility Stores selling goods at subsidised rates; targeted subsidies (BISP/Ehsaas); price control committees and market magistrates; imports of wheat, sugar and edible oil to stabilise prices; agreements with the IMF to reduce the budget deficit.
endsolution
qp vii | افراطِ زر کے اثرات تحریر کریں۔ (ملتان بورڈ 2009ء) || Write the effects of inflation. (Multan Board 2009) || د انفلاسیون اغېزې ولیکئ. (ملتان بورډ ۲۰۰۹)
solution جواب
ss Effects on production: mild inflation raises profits and encourages investment and output, but high inflation creates uncertainty and discourages long-term investment.
ss Effects on distribution: (1) Fixed-income groups (salaried, pensioners, labourers) lose as real income falls; (2) Businessmen, traders and producers gain; (3) Debtors gain, creditors lose; (4) Farmers may gain from higher crop prices; (5) Inequalities of income increase.
ss Other effects: savings fall as money loses value; people buy property and gold; exports become expensive and imports rise, worsening the balance of payments; the value of the currency falls; corruption, hoarding and black marketing increase; social unrest and strikes; government expenditure increases.
endsolution
qp viii | نیشنل بینک آف پاکستان پر ایک نوٹ لکھیے۔ || Write a note on the National Bank of Pakistan. || د پاکستان پر ملي بانک یادښت ولیکئ.
solution جواب
ss The National Bank of Pakistan (NBP) was established in November 1949 to finance the jute trade during the crisis with India; it is the largest commercial bank in the public sector, with branches throughout Pakistan and abroad.
ss Functions: (1) Agent of the State Bank where the SBP has no branch — handles government receipts and payments, treasury operations and collection of taxes; (2) Accepts deposits and gives loans like other commercial banks; (3) Finances agriculture, industry, trade and exports; (4) Pays government salaries and pensions.
ss (5) Deals in foreign exchange and remittances; (6) Handles public debt and government securities; (7) Provides utility bill collection, lockers and modern banking (ATMs, online); (8) Gives loans for small businesses and housing; (9) Plays a role in national development schemes.
endsolution
qp ix | زرعی ترقیاتی بینک لمیٹڈ (ZTBL) کے فرائض بیان کیجئے۔ || State the functions of Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited (ZTBL). || د زرعي ترقیاتي بانک لمیټډ (ZTBL) دندې بیان کړئ.
solution جواب
ss Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited (ZTBL), formerly the Agricultural Development Bank of Pakistan (established in 1961), is a specialised bank for agricultural credit.
ss Functions: (1) Gives short-, medium- and long-term loans to farmers for seeds, fertilisers, pesticides, tractors, tubewells and machinery; (2) Loans for livestock, dairy, poultry, fisheries and orchards; (3) Loans for land development, farm buildings and storage; (4) Easy and interest-subsidised credit for small farmers; (5) Loans for cottage and agro-based industries in rural areas.
ss (6) Technical guidance through mobile credit officers; (7) Accepts deposits to mobilise rural savings; (8) Crop insurance schemes; (9) Helps to raise agricultural productivity and rural income and reduce dependence on moneylenders.
endsolution
qp x | سٹیٹ بینک آف پاکستان کے فرائض بیان کیجئے۔ (میرپور آزاد کشمیر بورڈ 2011ء) || State the functions of the State Bank of Pakistan. (Mirpur Azad Kashmir Board 2011) || د پاکستان دولتي بانک دندې بیان کړئ. (میرپور ازاد کشمیر بورډ ۲۰۱۱)
solution جواب
ss The State Bank of Pakistan (established 1 July 1948) is the central bank. Functions: (1) Issue of currency notes — sole right of note issue; (2) Banker, agent and adviser to the government — keeps government accounts, manages public debt, gives loans and economic advice.
ss (3) Bankers' bank — commercial banks keep cash reserves with it; it supervises and regulates them; (4) Lender of last resort — rediscounts bills and lends to banks in crisis; (5) Clearing house — settles inter-bank claims; (6) Controller of credit — uses bank rate, open market operations, cash reserve ratio and selective controls (monetary policy).
ss (7) Custodian of foreign exchange reserves and manages the exchange rate; (8) Promotional functions — development of the banking system, agricultural and industrial credit, Islamic banking; (9) Collects and publishes economic statistics and reports; (10) Maintains price stability and promotes economic growth; (11) Relations with international institutions such as the IMF.
endsolution
