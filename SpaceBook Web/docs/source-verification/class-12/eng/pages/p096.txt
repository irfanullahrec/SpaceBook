q 6 | How does the poet relate his own action to the mercy of God? || شاعر اپنے عمل کو خدا کی رحمت سے کیسے جوڑتا ہے؟ || شاعر خپله کړنه د خدای له رحمت سره څنګه تړي؟
sol جواب ## The poet compares himself to God: as he, a father, forgave his son after seeing his innocence and sorrow, he hopes that God, who is far more fatherly and merciful than he, will forgive human beings for their childish behaviour and sins on the Day of Judgement.
q 7 | What is the tone of the poem "The Toys"? Do you think it is in conformity with the theme of the poem? How? || نظم ’’کھلونے‘‘ کا لہجہ کیا ہے؟ کیا آپ کے خیال میں یہ نظم کے موضوع سے ہم آہنگ ہے؟ کیسے؟ || د "لوبونې" شعر لهجه څه ده؟ ایا ستاسو په اند د شعر له موضوع سره برابره ده؟ څنګه؟
sol جواب ## The tone is sad, regretful and tender, ending in a prayerful, hopeful note. Yes, it matches the theme: the regret of the father leads to his realisation of God's mercy, so the gentle, sorrowful tone fits the message of love and forgiveness.
q 8 | What feelings does the poem "The Toys" evoke in you? || نظم ’’کھلونے‘‘ آپ میں کون سے جذبات ابھارتی ہے؟ || د "لوبونې" شعر په تاسو کې کوم احساسات راپاروي؟
sol جواب ## The poem evokes feelings of sympathy for the innocent child, regret for harsh treatment, tenderness and love between parent and child, and hope and gratitude for the infinite mercy of Allah. It teaches us to be gentle and forgiving.
