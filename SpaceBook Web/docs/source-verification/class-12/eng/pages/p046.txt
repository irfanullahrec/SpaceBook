p Reading Comprehension || مطالعہ اور فہم || لوستل او پوهه
p Answer the following Questions: || درج ذیل سوالات کے جواب دیں: || لاندې پوښتنو ته ځواب ورکړئ:
q 1 | Explain the connection which the writer makes between the camel caravans of past and modern decoration of trucks. || ماضی کے اونٹوں کے قافلوں اور ٹرکوں کی جدید سجاوٹ کے درمیان مصنفہ کے بیان کردہ تعلق کی وضاحت کریں۔ || د پخوانیو اوښانو د کاروانونو او د ټرکونو د عصري ښکلا ترمنځ هغه اړیکه تشریح کړئ چې لیکوالې جوړه کړې.
sol جواب ## The writer links the decorated trucks of today with the camel caravans of the past: just as camel drivers once decorated their camels with bells, beads and colourful cloth for long journeys on the trade routes, truck drivers now decorate their trucks, which have taken the place of camels as the carriers of goods across the land.
q 2 | Which point is the writer illustrating when she gives examples of the decorations on trucks from Peshawar and Rawalpindi? || پشاور اور راولپنڈی کے ٹرکوں کی سجاوٹ کی مثالیں دیتے ہوئے مصنفہ کون سا نکتہ واضح کر رہی ہیں؟ || لیکواله د پېښور او راولپنډۍ د ټرکونو د ښکلا د بېلګو په ورکولو کوم ټکی روښانه کوي؟
sol جواب ## She is illustrating that truck art has regional styles: trucks from Peshawar, for example, are decorated with wooden carvings and particular motifs, while those from Rawalpindi are known for plastic work and reflective tape — each region has its own distinctive taste.
q 3 | Which point is the writer illustrating in the lesson "Truck Art" by giving examples of the specialists' work? || سبق ’’ٹرک آرٹ‘‘ میں ماہرین کے کام کی مثالیں دے کر مصنفہ کون سا نکتہ واضح کر رہی ہیں؟ || لیکواله په "د ټرک هنر" لوست کې د متخصصینو د کار د بېلګو په ورکولو کوم ټکی روښانه کوي؟
sol جواب ## She shows that truck art is a skilled craft done by specialists — painters, carpenters, metal-workers and calligraphers — each working on a different part of the truck, so decorating a truck is a team effort of skilled artisans.
q 4 | Why do truckers spend so much money on decorating their trucks? || ٹرک والے اپنے ٹرکوں کی سجاوٹ پر اتنا پیسہ کیوں خرچ کرتے ہیں؟ || ټرکوال ولې د خپلو ټرکونو پر ښکلا دومره پیسې لګوي؟
sol جواب ## Truckers spend so much because the truck is their home and their pride for long periods; decoration expresses their personality, beliefs and love for their family and region, and a beautiful truck attracts more business and respect. It is also believed to bring good luck and protection.
q 5 | Briefly describe how truck art developed from the 1940s to the 1970s? || مختصراً بیان کریں کہ 1940 سے 1970 کی دہائی تک ٹرک آرٹ کیسے پروان چڑھا؟ || په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ چې د ټرک هنر له 1940 څخه تر 1970 لسیزې څنګه وده وکړه؟
sol جواب ## In the 1940s trucks were decorated simply by the companies that built the bodies; gradually painters added floral designs and pictures. By the 1950s and 1960s, with the Bedford trucks, decoration became more elaborate with paintings, calligraphy, mirrors and metal work, and by the 1970s it had become a fully developed popular art form with competing workshops.
q 6 | Why are truck drivers 'nostalgic' about the old Bedford trucks? || ٹرک ڈرائیور پرانے بیڈفورڈ ٹرکوں کے بارے میں ’’یادِ ماضی میں کھوئے‘‘ کیوں ہیں؟ || ټرک چلوونکي ولې د زړو بیډفورډ ټرکونو لپاره "د تېر په یاد کې ډوب" دي؟
sol جواب ## They are nostalgic because the old Bedford trucks were the first trucks on which truck art really flourished; they were strong, beautiful and easy to decorate, and they remind the drivers of the golden age of truck art.
q 7 | Explain how truck art at the present time can be seen as having reached 'some kind of apogee'? || وضاحت کریں کہ موجودہ دور میں ٹرک آرٹ کو کس طرح ’’ایک قسم کے عروج‘‘ پر پہنچا ہوا سمجھا جا سکتا ہے؟ || تشریح کړئ چې په اوسني وخت کې د ټرک هنر څنګه "یو ډول لوړې څوکې" ته رسېدلی ګڼل کېدای شي؟
sol جواب ## Truck art has reached 'some kind of apogee' (the highest point) because it is now extremely elaborate and colourful, combining painting, calligraphy, mirror-work, steel and plastic decoration; it is famous all over the world, displayed in exhibitions and copied on objects, and has become a symbol of Pakistani popular culture.
