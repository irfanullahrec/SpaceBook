p Reading Comprehension || مطالعہ اور فہم || لوستل او پوهه
p Answer the Questions || سوالات کے جواب دیں || پوښتنو ته ځواب ووایئ
q 1 | How did the boys come to the jungle island? || لڑکے جنگلی جزیرے پر کیسے پہنچے؟ || هلکان ځنګلي ټاپو ته څنګه راغلل؟
sol جواب ## During a war the boys were being evacuated from Britain by plane; the plane was shot down/crashed on an uninhabited tropical island, and the boys survived with no adults.
q 2 | What did Ralph do to attract the other boys on the island? || رالف نے جزیرے پر دوسرے لڑکوں کو متوجہ کرنے کے لیے کیا کیا؟ || رالف په ټاپو کې د نورو هلکانو د راجلبولو لپاره څه وکړل؟
sol جواب ## Ralph found a large conch shell in the lagoon and, with Piggy's help, blew it like a trumpet; the loud sound called all the scattered boys together to one place for a meeting.
q 3 | Describe the scene of the arrival of Jack and his band at the place where the boys were assembled? || لڑکوں کے جمع ہونے کی جگہ پر جیک اور اس کی ٹولی کی آمد کا منظر بیان کریں؟ || هغه ځای ته چې هلکان راټول وو، د جیک او د هغه د ډلې د راتګ صحنه بیان کړئ؟
sol جواب ## Jack and his choir boys came marching along the beach in two lines, dressed in black cloaks and caps with silver badges, looking like a strange dark creature in the heat; Jack, tall and red-haired, led them with authority and they halted in order before the assembly.
q 4 | What was the first issue to that arose before the boys and how was it tackled? || لڑکوں کے سامنے پہلا مسئلہ کیا آیا اور اسے کیسے حل کیا گیا؟ || د هلکانو مخې ته لومړۍ ستونزه کومه وه او څنګه حل شوه؟
sol جواب ## The first issue was choosing a leader. They held a vote; Ralph, who had the conch, was elected chief, while Jack was given charge of his choir as hunters to satisfy him.
q 5 | How did Ralph think they would be rescued? || رالف کے خیال میں انہیں کیسے بچایا جائے گا؟ || رالف فکر کاوه چې څنګه به وژغورل شي؟
sol جواب ## Ralph believed they would be rescued if they kept a signal fire burning on the mountain so that a passing ship would see the smoke; he also hoped his father, a naval officer, would come for them.
q 6 | What rule regarding the conch was made and why? || سیپی کے بارے میں کیا قاعدہ بنایا گیا اور کیوں؟ || د سیپۍ په اړه کومه قاعده جوړه شوه او ولې؟
sol جواب ## The rule was that whoever held the conch had the right to speak in the meeting and others must listen; it was made to keep order, give everyone a fair chance and make the assemblies democratic.
q 7 | Briefly describe the role of hallucinations scene from 'Loard of the Flies'. || ’’لارڈ آف دی فلائیز‘‘ سے فریبِ نظر (hallucinations) کے منظر کا کردار مختصراً بیان کریں۔ || د "لارډ آف دي فلایز" څخه د خیالي لید (hallucinations) د صحنې رول په لنډه توګه بیان کړئ.
sol جواب ## In the hallucination scene Simon, alone in the forest, imagines the pig's head on the stick (the Lord of the Flies) speaking to him. It tells him that the 'beast' is not outside but inside the boys themselves. The scene reveals the novel's main idea about the evil within human nature.
q 8 | What is the significance of the title 'Loard of the Flies'? || عنوان ’’لارڈ آف دی فلائیز‘‘ کی کیا اہمیت ہے؟ || د "لارډ آف دي فلایز" سرلیک اهمیت څه دی؟
sol جواب ## 'Lord of the Flies' is a translation of 'Beelzebub', a name of the devil. It refers to the pig's head on a stick swarming with flies, which the boys offer to the beast. The title symbolises the evil and savagery that exist within human beings.
q 9 | What happenes to the conch and Piggy and what does its suggest? || سیپی اور پگی کے ساتھ کیا ہوتا ہے اور اس سے کیا ظاہر ہوتا ہے؟ || په سیپۍ او پګي څه کېږي او دا څه ښيي؟
sol جواب ## Roger rolls a huge rock that kills Piggy and shatters the conch into pieces. It suggests the complete end of order, reason and civilisation on the island and the victory of savagery.
q 10 | How are the boys rescued at the end? || آخر میں لڑکوں کو کیسے بچایا جاتا ہے؟ || په پای کې هلکان څنګه ژغورل کېږي؟
sol جواب ## Jack's tribe sets the island on fire while hunting Ralph. The smoke attracts a passing naval ship; an officer lands on the beach just as Ralph falls at his feet, and the boys are rescued.
q 11 | How were Ralph's priorities different from Jack's? || رالف کی ترجیحات جیک سے کیسے مختلف تھیں؟ || د رالف لومړیتوبونه د جیک څخه څنګه توپیر درلود؟
sol جواب ## Ralph's priorities were order, building shelters, keeping the signal fire and being rescued. Jack cared only about hunting, meat, power and fun; he ignored the fire and rules, which led to the split between them.
q 12 | What does Simon represent in the novel? || ناول میں سائمن کس چیز کی نمائندگی کرتا ہے؟ || په ناول کې سایمن د څه استازیتوب کوي؟
sol جواب ## Simon represents natural goodness and spiritual insight — he is kind, helpful and thoughtful, and he understands that the beast is within the boys; some critics see him as a Christ-like figure.
q 13 | Compare and contrast the characters of Ralph and Piggy. || رالف اور پگی کے کرداروں کا موازنہ اور تقابل کریں۔ || د رالف او پګي کرکټرونه پرتله کړئ.
sol جواب ## Both Ralph and Piggy stand for order and civilisation. Ralph is athletic, attractive and a natural leader, but less intelligent; Piggy is fat, asthmatic and short-sighted, mocked by others, but he is the most intelligent and logical. Ralph provides leadership, Piggy provides ideas.
q 14 | What is the purpose of the introduction of the mock-hunts in the novel? || ناول میں فرضی شکار (mock-hunts) کو شامل کرنے کا مقصد کیا ہے؟ || په ناول کې د جعلي ښکارونو (mock-hunts) د راوستلو موخه څه ده؟
sol جواب ## The mock-hunts show how the boys' games gradually become real violence; chanting "Kill the pig!" they lose control, attack Robert and later kill Simon — showing the growth of savagery in human nature.
q 15 | [Quotation from Jack’s speech inviting the boys to join his tribe — abridged text p. 208, beginning “Listen all of you…”] Whose words are these and what's the occasion? || [جیک کی تقریر سے اقتباس جس میں وہ لڑکوں کو اپنے قبیلے میں شامل ہونے کی دعوت دیتا ہے — مختصر متن صفحہ 208، جو “Listen all of you…” سے شروع ہوتا ہے] یہ کس کے الفاظ ہیں اور موقع کیا ہے؟ || [د جیک له وینا څخه اقتباس چې هلکانو ته د خپلې قبیلې د ګډون بلنه ورکوي — لنډ شوی متن 208 مخ، چې په “Listen all of you…” پیلېږي] دا د چا خبرې دي او موقع څه ده؟
sol جواب ## These are Jack's words. On the occasion of his feast after leaving Ralph's group, he invites the boys to join his tribe, promising them meat, hunting and protection from the beast — he is trying to win followers away from Ralph.
q 16 | [Quotation describing Jack’s authority at the feast — abridged text p. 210, beginning “Authority set on his shoulder…”] Explain this statement of William Golding with reference to the context. || [جیک کے اختیار کو بیان کرنے والا اقتباس — مختصر متن صفحہ 210، جو “Authority set on his shoulder…” سے شروع ہوتا ہے] ولیم گولڈنگ کے اس بیان کی سیاق و سباق کے حوالے سے وضاحت کریں۔ || [د مېلمستیا پر مهال د جیک د واک تشریح کوونکی اقتباس — لنډ شوی متن 210 مخ، چې په “Authority set on his shoulder…” پیلېږي] د ویلیم ګولډینګ دا وینا د شرایطو په حواله تشریح کړئ.
sol جواب ## At the feast Jack sits like an idol, painted and garlanded, with power resting on his shoulders. Golding shows that Jack has now become a tyrant ruler; his authority comes from fear and the boys' desire for meat and excitement, not from law or reason.
q 17 | "The fire is the most important thing. Without the fire we can't be rescued." Whose words are these? What do you say about this statement? || "The fire is the most important thing. Without the fire we can't be rescued." یہ کس کے الفاظ ہیں؟ اس بیان کے بارے میں آپ کیا کہتے ہیں؟ || "The fire is the most important thing. Without the fire we can't be rescued." دا د چا خبرې دي؟ د دې وینا په اړه څه وایئ؟
sol جواب ## These are Ralph's words. The statement is true in the story: the fire is the boys' only link with civilisation and hope of rescue; when they neglect it, they lose that hope, and ironically it is a fire (the burning island) that finally brings rescue.
q 18 | What is the dominant theme of the novel " Lord of the flies"? || ناول ’’لارڈ آف دی فلائیز‘‘ کا غالب موضوع کیا ہے؟ || د "لارډ آف دي فلایز" ناول غالبه موضوع څه ده؟
sol جواب ## The dominant theme is the conflict between civilisation and savagery — the instinct to live by rules and morality versus the instinct to seek power and violence. Without the restraints of society, the evil within human nature comes out.
q 19 | What general idea about the psychology of small children do you derive from this novel' Lord of the flies"? || اس ناول ’’لارڈ آف دی فلائیز‘‘ سے آپ چھوٹے بچوں کی نفسیات کے بارے میں کیا عمومی خیال اخذ کرتے ہیں؟ || له دې ناول "لارډ آف دي فلایز" څخه د کوچنیو ماشومانو د ارواپوهنې په اړه کوم عمومي نظر اخلئ؟
sol جواب ## The novel suggests that children are not naturally innocent; they have the same capacity for cruelty, fear and lust for power as adults, and without guidance and rules they can easily turn savage.
q 20 | Explain the following symbols used in the novel: || ناول میں استعمال ہونے والی درج ذیل علامتوں کی وضاحت کریں: || په ناول کې کارول شوي لاندې سمبولونه تشریح کړئ:
sol جواب ## Symbols: the conch shell — order, democracy and authority; Piggy's glasses — intelligence, science and reason (also the means of making fire); the signal fire — hope of rescue and link with civilisation; the beast — the evil and fear inside human beings.
q 21 | The conch shell, Piggy's glasses, the signal fire, the beast. || سیپی، پگی کی عینک، اشارے کی آگ، درندہ۔ || سیپۍ، د پګي عینکې، د نښې اور، ځناور.
sol جواب ## The conch shell symbolises law and democratic order; Piggy's glasses stand for reason and the power of science; the signal fire stands for hope and the connection with civilisation (when it goes out, hope fades); the beast represents the inner evil and savagery of mankind, which the boys wrongly think is outside them.
