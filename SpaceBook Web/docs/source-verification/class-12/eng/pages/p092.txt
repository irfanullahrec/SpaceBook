## C۔ Conditional sentences || شرطیہ جملے || شرطي جملې
p A conditional sentence is a sentence that gives a condition (e.g., If it rains) and the outcome of the condition occurring (e.g., the game will be cancelled). || شرطیہ جملہ وہ جملہ ہے جو ایک شرط (مثلاً اگر بارش ہوئی) اور اس شرط کے پورا ہونے کا نتیجہ (مثلاً کھیل منسوخ ہو جائے گا) بیان کرے۔ || شرطي جمله هغه جمله ده چې یو شرط (لکه که باران وشو) او د شرط د پوره کېدو پایله (لکه لوبه به لغوه شي) ورکوي.
p There are four types of conditional sentences: || شرطیہ جملوں کی چار اقسام ہیں: || د شرطي جملو څلور ډولونه دي:
table
th | Type | Function | Example
tr | zero conditional | Expresses something as a fact | If you sleep, you dream.
tr | first conditional | States the result of a possible future event occurring | If you get some sleep, you will feel better.
tr | second conditional | States the result of an unlikely event occurring or an untruth being true | If you became an insomniac, you would understand. (unlikely event occurring) If you were an insomniac, you would understand. (untruth being true)
tr | third conditional | States how the situation would be different with a different past | If you had slept last night, you would have beaten your record.
endtable
