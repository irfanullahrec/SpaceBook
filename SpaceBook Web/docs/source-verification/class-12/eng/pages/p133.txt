p Reading Comprehension || مطالعہ اور فہم || لوستل او پوهه
q 1 | Why do we need more technical education than general education in Pakistan? || پاکستان میں ہمیں عمومی تعلیم سے زیادہ فنی تعلیم کی ضرورت کیوں ہے؟ || په پاکستان کې موږ ولې له عمومي زده کړې ډېرې تخنیکي زده کړې ته اړتیا لرو؟
sol جواب ## Pakistan needs more technical education because its economy needs skilled workers — technicians, engineers and craftsmen — for industry, agriculture and services. General education produces many graduates who cannot find jobs, while technical education gives practical skills, self-employment and higher productivity.
q 2 | How did technical education help Japan and Germany recover their economy after World War II? || فنی تعلیم نے دوسری جنگِ عظیم کے بعد جاپان اور جرمنی کی معیشت کی بحالی میں کیسے مدد کی؟ || تخنیکي زده کړې له دویمې نړیوالې جګړې وروسته د جاپان او جرمني د اقتصاد په بیا رغونه کې څنګه مرسته وکړه؟
sol جواب ## After World War II both countries were ruined, but they invested heavily in technical and vocational training. Skilled workers rebuilt industries, produced high-quality goods (cars, machines, electronics) and exported them, so Japan and Germany quickly became industrial powers.
q 3 | Why is a linkage between the industries and the technical education training centres necessary? || صنعتوں اور فنی تعلیم کے تربیتی مراکز کے درمیان ربط کیوں ضروری ہے؟ || د صنعتونو او د تخنیکي زده کړې د روزنیزو مرکزونو ترمنځ اړیکه ولې اړینه ده؟
sol جواب ## Linkage is necessary so that training centres teach the skills industries actually need, students get on-the-job training and apprenticeships, industries get trained workers, and the curriculum keeps up with new technology; it reduces unemployment and wastage.
q 4 | What measures do you suggest for improving the technical education structure of Pakistan? || پاکستان کے فنی تعلیم کے ڈھانچے کو بہتر بنانے کے لیے آپ کیا اقدامات تجویز کرتے ہیں؟ || د پاکستان د تخنیکي زده کړې د جوړښت د ښه کولو لپاره کوم اقدامات وړاندیز کوئ؟
sol جواب ## Measures: more polytechnics and vocational institutes in every district; modern equipment and trained teachers; up-to-date curricula linked with industry; apprenticeship schemes; scholarships and loans; respect and good salaries for technical jobs; career counselling; and public-private partnership.
q 5 | How can technical education help in strengthening the economy of Pakistan? || فنی تعلیم پاکستان کی معیشت کو مضبوط بنانے میں کیسے مدد کر سکتی ہے؟ || تخنیکي زده کړه د پاکستان د اقتصاد په پیاوړتیا کې څنګه مرسته کولی شي؟
sol جواب ## Technical education creates skilled manpower, raises industrial and agricultural production, encourages small businesses and self-employment, reduces unemployment and poverty, improves product quality for export, attracts foreign investment and lets Pakistani workers earn more abroad — all of which strengthen the economy.
