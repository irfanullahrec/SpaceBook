p Reading Comprehension || مطالعہ اور فہم || لوستل او پوهه
q 1 | Write in your own words the significance of the title 'Lines from the Deserted Village'? || اپنے الفاظ میں عنوان ’’اجڑے گاؤں سے اشعار‘‘ کی اہمیت لکھیں؟ || په خپلو کلمو د "له وران کلي څخه بیتونه" سرلیک اهمیت ولیکئ؟
sol جواب ## The title shows that the poem is an extract (lines) from Goldsmith's longer poem 'The Deserted Village'. It describes how a once happy and lively village, Auburn, has been deserted because the rich landlords seized the land and the peasants were forced to leave — the title reflects the poet's sorrow at this loss.
q 2 | Describe in your own words the picture of the village portrayed by the poet in first six lines of the poem 'Lines from the Deserted Village'? || نظم ’’اجڑے گاؤں سے اشعار‘‘ کی پہلی چھ سطروں میں شاعر کی پیش کردہ گاؤں کی تصویر اپنے الفاظ میں بیان کریں؟ || د "له وران کلي څخه بیتونه" شعر په لومړیو شپږو کرښو کې د شاعر انځور شوی کلی په خپلو کلمو بیان کړئ؟
sol جواب ## The poet pictures 'sweet Auburn', the loveliest village of the plain, where health and plenty cheered the hard-working peasants; spring came early and summer stayed late; there were humble cottages, cultivated farms, a brook, a busy mill, a church on the hill and a hawthorn bush with seats beneath its shade for lovers and the old.
q 3 | What does the poet mean by the following words and phrases: (a) Loitered over thy green (b) talking age (c) seats of my youth (d) the village train || شاعر درج ذیل الفاظ اور فقروں سے کیا مراد لیتا ہے: (a) Loitered over thy green (b) talking age (c) seats of my youth (d) the village train || شاعر له لاندې کلمو او عبارتونو څه مطلب لري: (a) Loitered over thy green (b) talking age (c) seats of my youth (d) the village train
sol جواب ## (a) 'Loitered over thy green' — wandered leisurely over the village common/green fields. (b) 'Talking age' — the old people who sat together chatting. (c) 'Seats of my youth' — the places where the poet spent his happy childhood. (d) 'The village train' — the group/crowd of villagers who gathered for sports and games.
q 4 | What verse form has been used by the poet in the poem "Lines from the Deserted Village"? Also tell, what's its rhyme scheme? || نظم ’’اجڑے گاؤں سے اشعار‘‘ میں شاعر نے کون سی شعری ہیئت استعمال کی ہے؟ یہ بھی بتائیں کہ اس کی قافیہ بندی کیا ہے؟ || شاعر د "له وران کلي څخه بیتونه" په شعر کې کومه شعري بڼه کارولې؟ دا هم ووایئ چې قافیه یې څنګه ده؟
sol جواب ## The poem is written in heroic couplets — iambic pentameter lines rhyming in pairs; the rhyme scheme is AABBCC…
q 5 | Explain the following couplet: " Ill fares the land, to hast'ning ills a prey, Where wealth accumulates and men decay;" || درج ذیل شعر کی وضاحت کریں: " Ill fares the land, to hast'ning ills a prey, Where wealth accumulates and men decay;" || لاندې بیت تشریح کړئ: " Ill fares the land, to hast'ning ills a prey, Where wealth accumulates and men decay;"
sol جواب ## The land suffers greatly and falls a prey to quickly spreading evils where wealth gathers in the hands of a few rich people while the ordinary men (peasants) decline and suffer. Goldsmith means that a country is truly ruined when riches increase but the people who are its real strength are reduced to poverty and forced away.
q 6 | Give an example of alliteration from the poem "Lines from The Deserted Village". || نظم ’’اجڑے گاؤں سے اشعار‘‘ سے تکرارِ حرفی (alliteration) کی ایک مثال دیں۔ || د "له وران کلي څخه بیتونه" شعر څخه د تورو تکرار (alliteration) یوه بېلګه ورکړئ.
sol جواب ## Examples: 'Sweet smiling village, loveliest of the lawn'; 'the busy mill'; 'decent church that topped the neighbouring hill'; 'Ill fares the land, to hastening ills a prey' (repetition of the l / h sounds).
