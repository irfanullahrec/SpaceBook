qp 1 | A. Find the meanings of the following idioms related to weather in proper reference source and then match them to their exact meanings as given in column B. || A. موسم سے متعلق درج ذیل محاوروں کے معنی کسی مناسب حوالہ جاتی ذریعے میں تلاش کریں اور پھر انہیں کالم B میں دیے گئے ان کے درست معنی سے ملائیں۔ || A. د هوا اړوند د لاندې محاورو ماناوې په یوه مناسبه مرجع سرچینه کې ومومئ او بیا یې د B ستون له دقیقو ماناوو سره برابرې کړئ.
solution To brighten up — to become more cheerful; To take a shine to — to develop a liking for; To make hay when the sun shines — to make the most of an opportunity while it lasts; Come rain or shine — whatever the weather/situation; To chase rainbows — to pursue something impossible/impractical; To save for a rainy day — to put something aside for a future time of need; To wait for a raindrop in the drought — to wait/hope for something which has little chance of happening.
ss Right as rain — fit and healthy; To take a rain check — to refuse an offer while showing willingness to accept it later; Into each life some rain must fall — everyone must experience difficulties at some time; To rain on someone's parade — to spoil someone's plans; It never rains but it pours — misfortunes come all at once; Raining cats and dogs — raining heavily; Dry spell — a period of less success than usual; Windfall — a large amount of money unexpectedly received.
ss To shoot the breeze — to have a casual conversation; To get wind of something — to hear a rumour about something; To know which way the wind blows — to understand changing circumstances and anticipate what will happen; To sail close to the wind — to verge on the limit of what is acceptable; To throw caution to the wind — to take a great risk; Cloud/storm on the horizon — an approaching problem; Every dark cloud has a silver lining — every difficult situation has a hopeful aspect; Cloud nine — a state of great happiness; To have your head in the clouds — to have unrealistic or fanciful ideas; "I don't have the foggiest" — I have no idea.
