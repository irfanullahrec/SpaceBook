p Developing a textbook under the touchstone of a prescribed curriculum is harder than writing at free will. This becomes specifically laborious when one is to organise and arrange material according to the SLOs, Standards and Benchmarks. We have endeavoured to the best of our level to develop a textbook that will, hopefully satiate the needs of the students in the light of the National Education curriculum. We are grateful to those writers, websites and authentic sources whose copyright materials have been freely utilised in the compilation of this textbook.
