p Reading Comprehension || مطالعہ اور فہم || لوستل او پوهه
q 1 | Write down the central idea of the lesson 'Désirée's Baby'? || سبق ’’دیزیرے کا بچہ‘‘ کا مرکزی خیال لکھیں؟ || د "د دېزیرې ماشوم" لوست مرکزي فکر ولیکئ؟
sol جواب ## The central idea is the evil of racial prejudice and pride: Armand rejects his wife and child because he believes they are of mixed race, only to discover that the 'black blood' came from his own mother. The story shows how racism destroys love and humanity, and how pride blinds people.
q 2 | Why did Armand not consider Désirée's origin before marrying her? || آرمانڈ نے شادی سے پہلے دیزیرے کے نسب پر غور کیوں نہیں کیا؟ || ارمانډ له واده مخکې د دېزیرې ریښې ته ولې پام ونه کړ؟
sol جواب ## Armand fell in love with Désirée at first sight, 'as if struck by a pistol shot'; his passion was so strong that he did not care about her unknown origin. He was proud of his own old and noble family name and felt he could give her his name, so her lack of family did not matter to him then.
q 3 | Write a brief character sketch of Armand Aubigny. || آرمانڈ اوبینی کا مختصر کرداری خاکہ لکھیں۔ || د ارمانډ اوبیني لنډ کرکټري انځور ولیکئ.
sol جواب ## Armand Aubigny is a rich, proud and passionate planter of an old Louisiana family. He is hot-tempered, harsh and cruel to his slaves; his love for Désirée is passionate but shallow. When he thinks his child is of mixed race, he becomes cold and cruel and drives Désirée away. He is a slave to racial prejudice and pride, and in the end he is shown to be the one with African ancestry.
q 4 | Why did the behaviour of Armand Aubigny change towards Désirée as the story proceeds? || کہانی آگے بڑھنے کے ساتھ آرمانڈ اوبینی کا رویہ دیزیرے کے ساتھ کیوں بدل گیا؟ || د کیسې په پرمختګ سره د ارمانډ اوبیني چلند له دېزیرې سره ولې بدل شو؟
sol جواب ## His behaviour changed when the baby grew older and began to show features of African descent. Armand believed this came from Désirée, whose parentage was unknown; his racial pride was hurt, so he became cold, avoided her and the child and treated the slaves even more harshly.
q 5 | Why was Désirée assumed a reason for his child not being white? || بچے کے سفید فام نہ ہونے کی وجہ دیزیرے کو کیوں سمجھا گیا؟ || ولې دېزیره د هغه د ماشوم د سپین پوستي نه کېدو لامل وګڼل شوه؟
sol جواب ## Because Désirée was a foundling with no known family, while Armand was proud of his 'pure' white lineage. So the blame for the baby's dark features was put on her unknown origin — Armand never thought that the cause could be in his own family.
q 6 | What is the meaning of the phrase "he was reminded that she was nameless" in the context of Désirée's Baby? || ’’دیزیرے کا بچہ‘‘ کے سیاق میں فقرے "he was reminded that she was nameless" کا کیا مطلب ہے؟ || د "د دېزیرې ماشوم" په شرایطو کې د "he was reminded that she was nameless" عبارت مانا څه ده؟
sol جواب ## It means that Armand remembered, with sudden shame, that Désirée had no family name or known parentage; he felt she had brought disgrace to his proud and ancient name, and he blamed her nameless origin for the child's colour.
q 7 | Suggest another ending for the story, 'Désirée's Baby'? || کہانی ’’دیزیرے کا بچہ‘‘ کا کوئی اور انجام تجویز کریں؟ || د "د دېزیرې ماشوم" کیسې لپاره بل پای وړاندیز کړئ؟
sol جواب ## Alternate ending: Before Désirée leaves, Armand finds his mother's letter. Ashamed of his cruelty, he rushes to the bayou, brings Désirée and the baby back, asks her forgiveness and frees his slaves. He learns that love and humanity are more important than colour and race. (Any logical ending is accepted.)
