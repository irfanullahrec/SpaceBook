p Reading Comprehension || مطالعہ اور فہم || لوستل او پوهه
q 1 | What was the composition of the army of the Qureish that marched to Madina to attack the Muslims? || مسلمانوں پر حملے کے لیے مدینہ کی طرف بڑھنے والے قریش کے لشکر کی ترکیب کیا تھی؟ || د قریشو د هغه لښکر جوړښت څه و چې پر مسلمانانو د برید لپاره مدینې ته وخوځېد؟
sol جواب ## The Quraish army of about three thousand men marched to Madinah under Abu Sufyan; it included about seven hundred men in armour, two hundred horsemen and three thousand camels, with women who sang war songs to encourage the soldiers.
q 2 | Why did the holy Rasool (ﷺ) suggest the defensive strategy in the the battle of a Uhud? || رسولِ پاک (ﷺ) نے غزوۂ احد میں دفاعی حکمتِ عملی کیوں تجویز کی؟ || سپیڅلي رسول (ﷺ) د احد په غزا کې ولې دفاعي ستراتیژي وړاندیز کړه؟
sol جواب ## The Holy Prophet (ﷺ) suggested staying inside Madinah and defending it from within, because the city could be defended easily from its narrow streets and houses, the Muslims were far fewer than the enemy, and fighting in the open would be risky.
q 3 | What was the opinion of youth with respect to facing the army of the Quraish? || قریش کے لشکر کا سامنا کرنے کے بارے میں نوجوانوں کی کیا رائے تھی؟ || د قریشو له لښکر سره د مخامخ کېدو په اړه د ځوانانو نظر څه و؟
sol جواب ## The young Companions, especially those who had missed Badr, were full of zeal and wanted to go out of the city and meet the enemy in the open battlefield; they felt that staying inside would look like weakness and cowardice.
q 4 | Why did the holy Rasool (ﷺ) prefer the opinion of youths regarding facing the enemy in the battle of Uhud? || رسولِ پاک (ﷺ) نے غزوۂ احد میں دشمن کا سامنا کرنے کے بارے میں نوجوانوں کی رائے کو کیوں ترجیح دی؟ || سپیڅلي رسول (ﷺ) د احد په غزا کې له دښمن سره د مخامخ کېدو په اړه د ځوانانو نظر ولې غوره کړ؟
sol جواب ## He preferred the opinion of the youth because the majority favoured it and Islam teaches consultation (shura); he respected their enthusiasm and courage. Once the decision was made he put on his armour and did not go back on it.
q 5 | How did the course of the battle of Uhud change for a time in favour of the Quraish of Mecca? || غزوۂ احد کا رخ کچھ دیر کے لیے مکہ کے قریش کے حق میں کیسے بدل گیا؟ || د احد د غزا بهیر د یو څه وخت لپاره د مکې د قریشو په ګټه څنګه واوښت؟
sol جواب ## At first the Muslims were winning and the Quraish fled. Seeing this, most of the fifty archers placed on the hill (Jabal al-Rumah) left their post to collect the spoils, against the Prophet's (ﷺ) order. Khalid bin Walid, then a commander of the Quraish cavalry, attacked from behind through the gap, and the battle turned against the Muslims.
q 6 | What happened after the false rumour about the martyrdom of the holy Rasool (ﷺ) was spread by the Infidels? || کافروں کی طرف سے رسولِ پاک (ﷺ) کی شہادت کی جھوٹی افواہ پھیلائے جانے کے بعد کیا ہوا؟ || له هغه وروسته چې کافرانو د سپیڅلي رسول (ﷺ) د شهادت دروغجنه اوازه خپره کړه، څه وشول؟
sol جواب ## When the infidels spread the false rumour that the Holy Prophet (ﷺ) had been martyred, many Muslims lost heart and some left the field, while others fought on bravely. When the Companions saw him alive, they gathered around him, protected him and took him to a safe place on the mountain; the Muslims regrouped and the Quraish withdrew.
q 7 | What lesson does lie in the battle of Uhud for the Muslims in general? || غزوۂ احد میں عمومی طور پر مسلمانوں کے لیے کیا سبق ہے؟ || د احد په غزا کې په عمومي ډول د مسلمانانو لپاره کوم لوست دی؟
sol جواب ## The lesson is that obedience to the leader and discipline are essential for success; greed for worldly gain leads to disaster. It also teaches patience in hardship, unity, steadfastness and trust in Allah.
q 8 | What would have been the result of the battle had the archers not left their high post? || اگر تیر انداز اپنی بلند جگہ نہ چھوڑتے تو جنگ کا نتیجہ کیا ہوتا؟ || که غشي‌ویشتونکو خپل لوړ ځای نه وای پرېښی، د جګړې پایله به څه وای؟
sol جواب ## If the archers had not left the hill, Khalid's cavalry could not have attacked from behind; the Muslims would have completed their victory and the Quraish would have suffered a defeat like that of Badr.
q 9 | How would you relate the battle of Uhud to the battle of Badr? || آپ غزوۂ احد کو غزوۂ بدر سے کیسے جوڑیں گے؟ || تاسو به د احد غزا د بدر له غزا سره څنګه وتړئ؟
sol جواب ## At Badr (2 AH) 313 Muslims defeated a much larger Quraish army because of faith, obedience and Allah's help. Uhud (3 AH) was the Quraish's revenge for Badr; the Muslims began winning but suffered losses because of disobedience. Together they show that success comes with faith and obedience, and loss follows disobedience.
