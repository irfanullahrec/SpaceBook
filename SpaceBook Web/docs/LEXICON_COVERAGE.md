# Lexicon coverage

Generated by `node tools/build/lexicon_report.js --write` from the word lists of the books (`js/word_stats_words.js`) and the device lexicon (`js/lexicon_data.js` + the English-Urdu dictionary). A word "has a meaning" when a lookup in that direction returns one. **Types** = distinct words; **tokens** = occurrences in the books (a missing common word hurts more than a missing rare one). The numbers describe what a student gets offline today; nothing here is machine translation.

Lexicon: 38448 rows; word lists: English 61636, Urdu 50330, Pashto 68413, Arabic 30076 distinct words.
