## What changed, and why those

1. **The lexicon table is built in 10 ms slices** (`js/universal_translate.js`): one 0.9 s task after every page load (3.7 s on a phone 4x slower) is gone; a word looked up early finishes the job at once.
2. **A chapter is no longer drawn twice with layout twice** on wide screens: the open-book view (`js/sb_book_spread.js`) replaces what the reader drew; its spreads skip layout while off screen (`content-visibility:auto`), it no longer clones every part to measure it, and a formula is typeset once (`js/core/katexcache.js`).
3. **Class cards: 15 PNGs (2.2 MB) became WebP (0.37 MB)** (`tools/launch/make_webp.js`).
4. **The service worker installs after the first screen**, six files at a time, without fetching again the hashed files the page just loaded; and the first visit **no longer reloads the page** when the worker takes over (it used to, a second or two in).
5. **Hashed files are cached for a year** by the new `.htaccess` (the old rule never applied): the 100+ revalidation requests of a repeat visit disappear on the live host.

## Not measured, and why

* A real low-end phone (the app on the owner's phone was not timed: the screen is the owner's). The throttled Chrome profile is a proxy.
* The live host (LiteSpeed, brotli, a real network): the measurements use a local server with gzip. The transfer sizes are therefore compressed sizes from gzip level 6; the host compresses with brotli (smaller).
* Memory of the page after a long session; the biggest chapters (a Class 11 Maths lesson is about 100,000 DOM nodes) are still heavy on a 2 GB phone: `lazyCards` (off by default, `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`) halves it and should be switched on after a phone check.
