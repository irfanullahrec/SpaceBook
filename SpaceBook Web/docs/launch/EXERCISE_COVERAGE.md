# Exercise completeness and answer coverage, chapter by chapter

Generated by `tools/launch/exercise_coverage.js` from the books as the app holds them. **Do not edit by hand.** The per-chapter table is `exercise_coverage.csv` (one row per chapter); `EXERCISE_COVERAGE.json` has the same data for tools.

What this proves and what it does not: it proves that every question the app holds has an answer (or is named below), that an MCQ answer names one of its options, and that no chapter loses questions in the future (`--check`). It does **not** prove that the questions equal the printed ones: that is the page-by-page work of `docs/source-verification/`, sampled and disclosed in `VERIFICATION_REPORT.md`.

Answer columns: **own** = a record of its own (worked solution or stated answer); **via question** = the question it belongs to has one Ans for all its parts; **key** = the book's printed answer key; **written** = written for the project (`js/answers_extra.js`), not printed in the book; **whole line** = only the printed line of the whole question exists; **none** = nothing to show.
