# Permissions

Written permissions from the holders of rights in the textbooks (the Textbook Board, the Government, authors, publishers) are filed here: the letter or e-mail itself (PDF, scan or a saved e-mail), who gave it, when, for which books and Classes, and exactly what it allows (showing the books, redistributing them, a subscription, removing a mark).

**No permission is recorded yet.** Until one is, nothing may be removed from the books for rights reasons (`docs/launch/CONTENT_RIGHTS.md`, section 2) and `docs/launch/CONTENT_RIGHTS_AUDIT.md` stays the list for review.

A permission here is not enough on its own to remove a name or a rights notice: those stay, and a lawyer decides about the rest.
